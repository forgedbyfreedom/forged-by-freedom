The Problem — Why Traditional Diets Fall Short

Most fat-loss plans focus on calories or willpower alone. Human physiology is governed by seven interconnected systems. When these systems aren't aligned, fat loss stalls, muscle is sacrificed, and metabolic health declines. The FBF Recomp Protocol coordinates all seven systems so your body works with the process rather than against it.

Core Driver: Retatrutide

Retatrutide activates GLP-1, GIP, and Glucagon pathways simultaneously — reducing hunger, improving insulin sensitivity, and increasing fat burning. The protocol uses phased delivery to avoid receptor adaptation.

Retatrutide's multi-pathway action is the engine; phase-based delivery preserves efficacy and minimizes adaptation.

The 30-Week Protocol

The FBF Recomp Protocol is designed as a 30-week metabolic cycle, with staged activation of compounds to maximize fat loss while preserving muscle and metabolic health.

Phase Details

PHASE 01 — FOUNDATION (Weeks 1–4)

Active: Retatrutide • Cagrilintide • GH/Peptides • Testosterone support

  • Stabilize appetite
  • Establish consistent nutrition
  • Begin fat loss
PHASE 02 — ACCELERATION (Weeks 5–8)

Fat loss accelerates; hunger becomes easier to control; training performance remains strong.

PHASE 03 — FULL ACTIVATION (Weeks 8–24)

Tesofensine introduced; all seven systems aligned; the majority of body-composition change occurs here.

PHASE 04 — RECEPTOR RESET (Weeks 24–30)

To prevent diminishing returns: Retatrutide paused; Tesofensine, GH, and Testosterone continue; receptor sensitivity re-optimizes.

PHASE 05 — CYCLE RESTART (Week 30+)

Retatrutide reintroduced; Tesofensine discontinued early in the new cycle; body returns to the most effective state with renewed receptor sensitivity.

Daily Metabolic Monitoring

Morning body temperature as an early-warning system for metabolic changes.

Morning Temperature Guide

  • 97.8°F – 98.6°F — Metabolism functioning well
  • 97.3°F – 97.7°F — Monitor closely
  • Below 97.3°F — Refeed may be required

How to Measure

  • Measure after waking, before eating or caffeine
  • Record at the same time daily
  • Share trends with your coach for adjustments

## Why Test-Only Is the Right First Cycle — Every Time

You will encounter people who ran Dianabol as their first compound, or who stacked trenbolone on their first go. Some of them are fine. Many of them have no idea what baseline felt like, cannot attribute a side effect to a specific compound, and have permanently muddied their hormonal picture before they had a clean reference point.

Testosterone-only gives you one variable. If your blood pressure climbs, your mood shifts, your skin breaks out, or your libido goes through the roof — you know exactly what drove it. You also establish your personal aromatase conversion rate, your sensitivity to estrogenic sides, and how your body handles suppression and recovery. That data is worth more than any early shortcut.

There is also nothing left on the table. A well-run first test cycle with good training and nutrition will produce 10–18 lbs of keepable lean mass in most men. That is not a consolation prize. That is the compound working as designed.

## What to Run: Compound, Ester, and Dose

**Compound:** Testosterone Enanthate or Testosterone Cypionate. Either works. Both are long-ester testosterone — Enanthate has a half-life of approximately 4.5 days, Cypionate approximately 5 days. In practice they are interchangeable for first-cycle purposes. Inject twice weekly (e.g., Monday/Thursday or Sunday/Wednesday) to minimize blood level fluctuation.

**Dose:** 300–500 mg per week.

This range is not arbitrary. Here is what it means in practice:

- **300 mg/week** is appropriate for lighter individuals, anyone who wants a more conservative first run, or someone primarily concerned with recomposition rather than aggressive mass gain. Suppression is full at this dose. You are getting the full hormonal environment of exogenous testosterone — you are not half-assing it.
- **400 mg/week** is the most common and well-tolerated starting point. Sufficient anabolic signal, manageable aromatization for most men, good strength response.
- **500 mg/week** is the high end of a rational first cycle. More is not better here. Above 500 mg on a first cycle, side effect exposure scales faster than the marginal anabolic return. Save higher doses for when you understand exactly how your body responds.

**Cycle length:** 12–16 weeks. Shorter than 10 weeks does not allow blood levels to fully stabilize and plateau. Longer than 16 weeks on a first run is unnecessary and extends HPTA suppression without proportional benefit.

**Injection volume:** At 400 mg/week split twice weekly, you are pinning 200 mg per injection. Assuming a 200 mg/mL concentration (standard), that is 1 mL per injection. Use a 23–25 gauge, 1–1.5 inch needle for glute or quad injection.

## What You Need Before You Start: Baseline Bloodwork

Do not skip this. Baseline bloodwork exists to protect you — not as a bureaucratic hurdle. If you develop an issue mid-cycle, you need to know whether it pre-existed your cycle or was caused by it.

**Minimum pre-cycle panel:**

| Marker | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Total Testosterone | Your natural baseline before suppression |
| Free Testosterone | Biologically active fraction; establishes SHBG context |
| Estradiol (Sensitive, LC/MS/MS) | Pre-cycle E2 baseline; guides AI use |
| LH and FSH | Confirms normal HPTA function going in |
| CBC with differential | Hematocrit, hemoglobin — establishes baseline before polycythemia risk |
| Comprehensive Metabolic Panel | Liver enzymes (AST, ALT), kidney function, glucose |
| Lipid Panel | LDL, HDL — testosterone suppresses HDL; you need a before picture |
| PSA (if over 40) | Prostate baseline |

Get bloodwork drawn fasted, in the morning. Use PrivateMDLabs, Ulta Lab Tests, or your primary care physician.

## Ancillaries: What to Have on Hand

**Aromatase Inhibitor (AI)**

Testosterone aromatizes to estradiol. At 300–500 mg/week, most men will experience some degree of elevated E2. The key word is "some" — you are not automatically crashing estrogen with an AI from week one. Estrogen does important work: joint lubrication, libido, cardiovascular health, mood. The goal is management, not elimination.

**Have on hand:** Anastrozole (Arimidex) 0.5 mg tabs, or Exemestane (Aromasin) 12.5 mg tabs.

**When to introduce:** Do not begin an AI prophylactically unless you have prior bloodwork or cycle history showing you are a heavy aromatizer. Start your cycle. Around weeks 3–4, assess: Are you retaining significant water? Experiencing nipple sensitivity or puffiness? Feeling emotionally blunted or unusually bloated? Those are prompts to get bloodwork drawn and consider 0.25–0.5 mg Anastrozole every other day, or 12.5 mg Exemestane every other day.

**Dial by bloodwork, not symptoms alone.** Sensitive E2 should ideally sit in the 20–40 pg/mL range on-cycle for most men. Symptoms and bloodwork together drive dose adjustments.

**SERM for gyno:** Have Raloxifene (60 mg/day) or Nolvadex (20–40 mg/day) on hand for acute nipple sensitivity. A SERM does not lower estrogen — it blocks it at breast tissue. If you get a flare, a SERM is your first-line response while you reassess your AI dosing.

## PCT Protocol: Actual Doses and Duration

Your last injection is not when you start PCT. Testosterone Enanthate and Cypionate take approximately 2–3 weeks to clear to sub-therapeutic levels after your final pin. Starting PCT too early is wasteful; the exogenous testosterone will blunt the SERM's LH/FSH stimulus.

**Wait 14–17 days after your last injection before beginning PCT.**

**Option 1 — Nolvadex + Clomid (classic combination):**

| Week | Nolvadex | Clomid |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 40 mg/day | 50 mg/day |
| 3–4 | 20 mg/day | 25 mg/day |

Total duration: 4 weeks minimum. Some coaches run 6 weeks on longer cycles.

**Option 2 — Enclomiphene (increasingly preferred):**

Enclomiphene is the trans-isomer of clomiphene. It provides the LH/FSH stimulus without the estrogenic activity of the zuclomiphene isomer that causes many of Clomid's visual and mood side effects.

- 12.5–25 mg/day for 4–6 weeks
- Bloodwork at week 4 of PCT to assess LH, FSH, and total testosterone recovery

**HCG during cycle (optional but recommended):** 250 IU twice weekly throughout the cycle prevents testicular atrophy and makes HPTA restart easier. Discontinue HCG at least 5–7 days before beginning PCT.

## Bloodwork Timing: During and After Cycle

| Timepoint | What to Pull |
|---|---|
| Pre-cycle (baseline) | Full panel as listed above |
| Week 6–8 on-cycle | TT, FT, Sensitive E2, CBC, lipid panel, CMP |
| 2–3 weeks post-last-pin (pre-PCT) | E2, hematocrit |
| 4–6 weeks into PCT | LH, FSH, TT, FT, E2 |
| 8–12 weeks post-cycle | Full repeat of baseline panel |

The week 6–8 on-cycle draw is the most important. It catches elevated hematocrit before it becomes dangerous, confirms E2 is in range, and shows whether liver enzymes are responding abnormally. Do not skip it.

## Realistic Expectations

A well-run first cycle with 400 mg/week Test E or C, consistent training, and a caloric surplus of 300–500 kcal/day over 14–16 weeks will typically produce:

- **12–18 lbs total weight gain** on scale
- **8–14 lbs keepable lean mass** after the water drops post-cycle
- **Meaningful strength increases** — expect 15–25% jumps on key compound lifts
- **Accelerated recovery** — you will be able to train harder and more frequently

You will not look like a pro bodybuilder after one cycle. You will look noticeably better, feel significantly stronger, and understand your body's hormonal landscape far more clearly than when you started.

## Red Flags: When to Stop and Get Checked

Do not power through these:

- **Blood pressure consistently above 150/95** — check hematocrit immediately; may need to donate blood or halt cycle
- **Hematocrit above 52–54%** — elevated clotting risk; therapeutic phlebotomy territory
- **Significant nipple lump, pain, or discharge** — potential gyno development; address immediately with SERM and bloodwork
- **Severe sleep disruption, night sweats, or cardiac palpitations** — rule out cardiovascular stress
- **AST/ALT more than 3x upper limit of normal** — liver stress; halt any oral compounds; reassess
- **Sudden severe headaches** — possible hypertensive event; check BP
- **Testicular pain or significant shrinkage causing discomfort** — assess HCG protocol

None of these mean you are broken. They mean your cycle needs adjustment. The athletes who get hurt are the ones who ignore the signals.

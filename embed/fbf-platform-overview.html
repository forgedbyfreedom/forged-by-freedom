Custom Built FBF Programs

Following Our Proven Principles — Fully Automated

Pre-Built Phase Templates

FBF doesn't start from scratch for every client. The system includes pre-built programming rule sets for every common coaching phase. These templates encode Coach Bryan's proven methodologies, so the AI only needs to adapt them to each client's individual profile — not reinvent the wheel.

Lean Bulk
200-400 cal surplus, higher carbs on training days, progressive overload focus, 4-8 week mesocycles
Cut / Fat Loss
300-500 cal deficit, protein prioritized at 1.1-1.2g/lb, refeed protocols tied to body temp, LISS progression
Contest Prep
Aggressive timeline, peak week water/carb manipulation, posing practice, weekly photo check-ins, stage-ready protocols
Health / Maintenance
Maintenance calories, balanced macros, moderate volume, focus on longevity markers, recovery optimization
Recomp
Calories at maintenance, body recomposition via training stimulus, higher protein, carb cycling around training
Reverse Diet
Gradual calorie increase post-cut, metabolic recovery, temp monitoring, strategic carb additions weekly

How it works: The AI selects the appropriate phase template based on the client's goals, then customizes every detail — exercise selection based on equipment and experience, macro calculations based on body weight and activity level, supplement recommendations based on budget, and PED protocols based on reported compounds. The coach only needs to review and fine-tune, not build from zero.

FBF Core Methodology (Built Into Every Program)

Every program follows Coach Bryan's battle-tested methodology. These principles are encoded into the system and enforced automatically — no guesswork, no shortcuts:

  • Separate training day vs. rest day macros — never crash diet. Nutrition adapts to demand.
  • Carb cycling and dietary adaptations to match your body's metabolic needs. Refeeds triggered by objective markers, not arbitrary schedules.
  • Deloads programmed as needed based on performance, recovery signals, and individual response — not arbitrary timelines.
  • Every exercise includes tempo, form cues, breathing, and common mistakes. Detailed execution notes for every movement.
  • Progressive overload defined in every program. When top of rep range is hit for all sets → increase weight.
  • PED/peptide documentation with monitoring schedules when compounds are reported.
  • Metabolic monitoring with body temperature protocols and automated intervention thresholds.
  • Controlled eccentrics, full ROM, and intentional training. Every rep has a purpose.

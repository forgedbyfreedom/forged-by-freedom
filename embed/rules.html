FBF Programming Rules

Every program Coach Bryan builds follows these rules without exception. This is the system behind Forged by Freedom.

🔄 Training Split
A/B/C/D Rotating Split — Minimum 5 business days per week. One optional weekend day. Active Rest on remaining day(s). Days rotate continuously and are NOT tied to specific weekdays.
Day A
Chest & Triceps
DB Flat Bench · DB Incline Press · Cable Fly · Tricep Rope Pushdown · Overhead Tricep Extension
Day B
Legs
Barbell Back Squat · Romanian Deadlift · Leg Extension · Leg Curl · DB Walking Lunge · Standing Calf Raise
Day C
Shoulders
DB OHP (seated) · DB Lateral Raise · Cable Front Raise · Rear Delt Fly · Face Pull · DB Shrug
Day D
Back & Biceps
Barbell Row · Lat Pulldown · Seated Cable Row · DB Hammer Curl · DB Bicep Curl
🏋️ Exercise Selection
  • All pressing movements (bench, OHP): Dumbbells only — allows natural joint tracking and unilateral balance. No barbell bench. No barbell OHP.
  • Compound pulls & legs: Barbells — deadlift, squat, RDL, barbell row. These movements load better with a bar.
  • Isolation & accessories: Cables and machines — cable fly, lat pulldown, leg press, cable curl, face pull.
  • Never: dumbbell deadlifts. Use barbell or trap bar for all deadlift variations.
  • Experience matching: Goblet squat before barbell squat for beginners. Scale to the athlete.
📊 Volume & Intensity
  • Max 5–6 exercises per training day. Quality over quantity.
  • Compound movements: 3–4 sets. Isolation movements: 2–3 sets.
  • Tempo: 3-1-1-0 on all pressing and pulling movements (3-sec eccentric).
  • RIR targets: Most sets at RIR 1–3. Beginners: RIR 3–4. Advanced: RIR 0–2 on key lifts.
  • Deload every 4–5 weeks. Reduce volume 40–50%, keep intensity moderate. Non-negotiable.
  • Each muscle group hit 2× per week minimum for hypertrophy.
📈 Progressive Overload
The #1 driver of growth. Every program must include a clear progression scheme. Log every session.
  • When client hits the top of rep range on all sets at RIR 2 or better → increase weight by 2.5–5 lbs next session.
  • Keep the same core movements for 4–8 weeks to accurately track progress.
  • Mind-muscle connection over ego weight. Full ROM every rep.
🥗 Nutrition Rules
  • Protein: 0.9–1.1g per pound of body weight (up to 1.2g for cutting phases)
  • Separate macros for training days vs. active rest days — training days get more carbs; rest days reduce carbs by ~40g
  • Fat loss: 300–500 calorie deficit max. Never crash diet.
  • Muscle gain: 200–400 calorie surplus. Lean bulk only.
  • Dietary fats: 0.3–0.4g per pound minimum for hormone health
  • Water: Minimum 0.5 oz per pound of body weight daily (Men ~100 oz / Women ~80 oz)
  • 5 meals per day: Protein Oatmeal Bowl · Chicken & Rice Bowl · Greek Yogurt & Berries · Salmon & Sweet Potato · Protein Shake
  • Meal timing: Specific nutrient timing around training windows required
🚶 Cardio Protocol
  • Daily LISS: 30 min incline treadmill walk — 10–12% grade, 3.0–3.5 mph, fasted or post-workout
  • Active Rest days: 30–60 min outdoor activity (walk, hike, bike, or recreational sport)
  • LISS HR target formula: (220 − age) × 0.55–0.65. Stay in the zone, don't go harder.
  • Progressive treadmill chart included: week-by-week speed, incline, duration, and HR target progression
  • Step goal: 10,000 steps daily minimum, tracked via app
  • Never say "Rest Day." It's always Active Rest.
🌡️ Metabolic Monitoring
Daily oral body temperature tracking upon waking — before food, drink, or movement. This is the metabolic throttle. Required in every program.
TemperatureStatusAction
97.8–98.6°F Optimal Stay the course
97.3–97.7°F Mild Slowdown Moderate refeed — add 50–75g carbs
<97.3°F for 3+ days High-Carb Refeed Required Mandatory refeed — add 100–150g carbs for 1–2 days
  • Daily tracking required: weight (morning, post-bathroom), body temp, sleep quality (1–10), energy (1–10), mood (1–10), stress (1–10)
  • Daily FBF app check-in is non-negotiable.

🧬 Peptide Recommendations

Always discussed and documented when relevant. Presented as educational — client consults physician.

  • Retatrutide: Strongly recommended for body recomposition and performance. Multi-receptor GLP-1/GIP/Glucagon agonist. Discuss with physician.
  • MoTS-C: Mitochondrial peptide for energy, fat metabolism, and performance. Stacks well with Reta. Discuss with physician.
  • If client reports any PEDs, peptides, GH, TRT, or GLP-1 agonists — each compound is listed with dose, frequency, timing, purpose, and what to monitor.
  • Bloodwork monitoring recommendations tailored to their specific stack.
  • Framed as "Your Current Protocol" — we document and monitor, we do not prescribe.
📸 Baseline Assessments
  • InBody scan at gym front desk — baseline today, repeat monthly on the same date.
  • Progress photos — before photo today; monthly progress photos same day, same lighting, same positions (front, side, back).
😴 Recovery Standards
  • Sleep: 7–9 hours. Non-negotiable for hormone production and recovery.
  • Post-session stretching protocol included in every program.
  • Any recovery modalities the client already uses (sauna, cold plunge, etc.) are incorporated.
  • Weekly self-assessment: energy, performance, soreness, and motivation tracked and reviewed.

Through interactive learning activities like clickable infographics, flashcards, and engaging videos, this six-module course covers topics that include health equity and the five social determinants of health, implicit bias and ways to mitigate it, communicating using a person-centered approach, as well as the importance of cultivating empathy when working with all clients. With downloadable content that includes actionable next steps, we hope this course serves to enhance the skills of all professionals working in a health or fitness setting. Although the topics discussed in this course are just the start of a larger conversation, through this education, we aim to improve equity, diversity, and inclusion one conversation at a time because it starts with us.

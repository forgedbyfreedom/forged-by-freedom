Investing in a certification is important to set yourself up for success in a health and fitness career. So, I know you are wondering, how long will it take and how much will it cost? Depending on how much time you want to invest and how much time you have available, you can get up and running in several weeks or at a slower pace. Whatever works for you. The good news is ACE health and fitness certifications are 100% online and self-paced. On average, people take about 3 months to go through a certification program, but you can study up to a year to prep for the exam. What about money? Depending on which certification exam you are taking and the materials that support it, a program can cost anywhere from $500 to $2,000. Many certification organizations, including ACE, offer payment plans to help cover the costs. Learn more at acefitness.org slash fitness dash careers.

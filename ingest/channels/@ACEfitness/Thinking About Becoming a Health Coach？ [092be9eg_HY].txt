There's lots of talk about health coaching. So are you wondering what exactly a health coach does? Humans naturally resist change, even when a change might result in a positive outcome. It's understandable. There's a certain comfort or familiarity we experience when going through the habitual motions of day-to-day life. We naturally resist change because it's challenging, but we're capable of adapting to new behaviors when guided effectively. A health coach is somebody who can guide behavior change. Behavior change is anything but a simple and straightforward process. It can be frustrating and bewildering at times. When it comes to changing behaviors, certified health coaches are central to helping their clients develop sustainable change and continue to manage those changes effectively so that they can become a natural part of day-to-day life. Health coaches are unique in that they bridge the healthcare continuum between client-patient and their doctor. Health coaches apply their knowledge and skills in a way that motivates change rather than directing it. They actively partner with their clients and use motivational interviewing techniques to help their clients identify, develop, and implement an action plan of change centered around them. Health coaches work to mentor, inspire, and motivate. These efforts, along with the active partnership coaches create with their clients, ultimately lead to a more permanent state of change because clients have practiced and nurtured a specific set of skills and tools to support that change.

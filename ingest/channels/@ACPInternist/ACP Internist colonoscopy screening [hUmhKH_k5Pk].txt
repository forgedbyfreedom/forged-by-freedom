Patients are often reluctant to undergo colonoscopy because of fear of pain. They should be, those patients should be assured that any discomfort or pain would be eliminated or largely reduced, significantly reduced by sedation that is given in almost all colonoscopies. In addition, patients are reluctant to undergo colonoscopy because of fear of the preparation the day before. We have at our disposal a wide variety of alternatives for preparation which range anywhere from a four liter solution to two liter solutions with some oral laxatives along with a fully pill-based preparation or even very small volumes of sodium phosphate.

One technique that can be used to reduce patient anxiety and to increase compliance with colorectal cancer screening is the emphasis on the interval in between screening examinations. In screening for cervical cancer with a pap smear or for breast cancer with a mammogram, typically annual examinations are done. In a completely negative screening test such as a colonoscopy, the screening interval should be as long as 10 years. Screening with a double contrast barium enema or a CT calligraphy, the recommended screening interval is 5 years.

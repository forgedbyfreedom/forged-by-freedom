the 2016 art competition has already started the theme of this art competition is Parkinson's Disease all you have to do is create an artwork this can be a painting sketch digital art or watercolor anyone should enter everyone should enter the more creative the better after finishing your artwork take multiple photos of it you then must send us an email the email must include photos of your artwork and also a template the template has information including including your name contact details and roughly a 100-word description of what your artwork means and how it relates to Parkinson's disease a group of us will then judge and select the top finalists the finalists will be announced April 11th 2016 in conjunction with World Parkinson's day and their artworks will be showcased online the winner and finalists can win some amazing prizes that have been donated by our sponsor for the event the finalists will then have the opportunity to send their artwork directly to us where it will be donated or sold with all the proceed going to a Parkinson's Disease Association entries are now open and will close April 1st 2016 the goal of this competition is to get people involved in raising awareness of Parkinson's disease while also raising funds at the same same time for more information for downloading the template and emailing us please visit Armando h.org Parkinson's art competition all the best and good luck

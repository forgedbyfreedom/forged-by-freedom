the cell and hence the organ can result if cells cannot adapt to the pathologic stimulus they can die so let us discuss the four basic types of cellular adaptation hyperplasia hypertrophy atrophy and metaplasia we will also explore the physiologic and pathologic stimuli in each of these cellular adaptations beginning with hyperplasia hyperplasia is an increase in the number of cells physiologic

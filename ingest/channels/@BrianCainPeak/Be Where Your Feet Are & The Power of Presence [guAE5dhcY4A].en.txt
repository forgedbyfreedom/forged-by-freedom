Power of presence. You see presence, it might be the most underrated skill in all of mental performance. There's a great story about the Zen Master, Phil Jackson, the great NBA coach, and the teams that he led with Michael Jordan and Kobe Bryant. And during practice, he'd sometimes blow the whistle, stop everything. Now the team sit cross-legged, midcourt in a circle and just breathe. No talking. No drills. Just being. Phil Jackson, two of his great books, Sacred Hoops and 11 Rings. He talks about building mindfulness and he talks about how at first players hated it until they realized it was training them to stay in the present moment. You see the best performance, It doesn't come from tension. It comes from being present. From letting go of what just happened, letting go of what might happen and locking into what will happen on this next pitch, this next play, and trusting the preparation that got you there.

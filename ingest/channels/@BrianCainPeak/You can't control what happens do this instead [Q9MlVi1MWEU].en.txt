You can't always control the event, the weather, the ref, the opponent, but you can always control your response. And that response determines your outcome. Urban Meyer, he used to post E + R = O everywhere. Locker rooms, team meetings, playbooks. He told his athletes, don't blame the e master your O. He did this leading University of Florida and Ohio State University to National Championships. Author of Chicken Soup for the Soul, Jack Canfield and his book Success Principles also talks about event plus response equals outcome. I once shared this with a high school football team. And one of their players who used to be pretty hot headed and emotional, lose control and get personal fouls, called against him, wrote the formula on his wristband. And then one Friday night after a blown call, a questionable call, I saw him glance down, take a breath, and say out loud, next play. And that's it. That's exactly it. That's awareness turned into self-control. That's the power of this E plus R zero mindset. And when athletes internalize event plus response equals outcome, and they move from the event to the response, they influence the outcome. They stop being victims of circumstance and start becoming creators of results.

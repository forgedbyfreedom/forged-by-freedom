the landmine chest press the landmine chest press this exercise is perfect for building the upper chest so what you want to go ahead and do to perform this exercise is get on your knees about shoulder width apart you're going to grab the barbell right at the end you're going to clasp your hands together to make that nice solid base bring it down right below the chin slightly leaning towards the bar keeping the back straight chest up elbows tucked in together shoulders back you're going to press to the motion get the full extension of the arms squeezing the chest the whole entire way and then you're going to bring it down nice and slow and repeat the motion this is really going to hit that upper chest and that's what you want to get the complete contraction throughout the whole range of motion once you're done toss that sucker down and you just completed the land mine press

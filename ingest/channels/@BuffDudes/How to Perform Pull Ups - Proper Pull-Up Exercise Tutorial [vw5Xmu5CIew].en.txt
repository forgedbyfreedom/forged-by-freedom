Pull-ups. Pull-ups are a compound exercise which work the pole muscles in your body and are an excellent indication of your level of fitness. To perform, grab the bar with a grip slightly wider than shoulder width with your palms facing away from you. Hang all the way down, shoulders slack. Pull yourself up by keeping your chest up and pulling your shoulder blades downward and together with your elbows moving towards your back pocket area until your chin is above the bar. While going up, concentrate on isolating your back and biceps. Do not swing. With a slight pause, lower yourself all the way back down to the hanging position. Congratulations, you've just finished the pull-up, an exercise which every buff dude should have in their arsenal. Here is what not to do when doing a pull-up. Keep your body in a vertical line when doing the pull-up. Do not swing your legs to help you up. Bring your chin above the bar and touch your chest. Do not do half reps. Bring your shoulder blades down and together as you reach the top of the bar. Do not keep your shoulder blades locked, only lifting with your arms. If you cannot yet perform a pull-up, there are excellent alternatives such as the lat pull downs, which involve the same muscle groups as pull-ups.

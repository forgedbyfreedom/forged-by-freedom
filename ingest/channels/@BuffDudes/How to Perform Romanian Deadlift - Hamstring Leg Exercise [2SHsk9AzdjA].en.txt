Romanian deadlift Romanian deadlifts or rdls a great exercise to isolate the hamstrings if you want to build those sides and strengthen those hamstrings then you're going to include the Romanian dead lives in your leg program here's how it's done step to the bar so that way your ankle is just touching the barbell and put your legs about shoulder width apart you want to grab the bar with the overhand grip slightly outside the legs now what you do is bend down to pick the bar up you put it in a deadlift position first to get it up to the top position which is going to be your start and then what you want to do is push your hips backwards with knees slightly bent but in a stationary position now as you bend your hips backwards you're going to get a nice full stretch of the hamstrings gliding the bar right along the legs and then once you reach your maximum stretch in the hamstrings you're going to pull it up by pushing your hips forward and at the top position you're going to lock them out now nice squeeze in the hamstrings and the glutes as you lower the bar what you want to do is keep your lower back straight and your chest up and it's going to really help to keep your chest up by pinching your shoulders back as you lower the bar too this way you're can to get the maximum stretch in the hamstrings and once you reach that point you're going to slowly lift the bar back up to the top position by extending your hips and that's how you build those hamstrings Buck dude Style

spider curls spider curl this is a great exercise to help isolate those biceps taking the shoulders out of the equation and you're not going to be using any momentum caused in the upper body when swaying the weight back and forth so what you want to do to perform this exercise is going be grabbing a bench putting it on an incline you're going to be lying face down so your chest is about level to the end of the bench you're going to grab an easy curl bar about shoulder width apart feet nice and firm on the ground and you're going to be from a dead hang position nice stretching the bicep you're going be pulling it nice and slow to the top position getting the total flex and contraction of the bicep and then slowly letting it down to the bottom position you're going to repeat this process until you get a massive pump in those biceps another variation you can do for this exercise is grab two dumbbells being in the exact same position on the bench you're going to be curling straight up get a nice flexion in the biceps and then you're going to be slowly letting it down to the bottom position getting that total stretch another variation you can do for the spider curl is actually performing on the preacher bench but this isn't a standard preacher bench curl you're facing the opposite direction on the bench so that way your arms are vertical to the floor you're getting the full stretch but this time your arms or the back of the arms are going to be supported by the bench so you get a little bit more isolation in those biceps so if you want to try it out build those buff dud size biceps feel free to do so with the spider curls stay buff dudes

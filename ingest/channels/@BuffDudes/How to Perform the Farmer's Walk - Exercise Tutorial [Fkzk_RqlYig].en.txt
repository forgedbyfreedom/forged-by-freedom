the farmer's Walk The Farmer's walk this exercise is known to test your grip but this exercise is not only testing your grip it's going to test your agility your leg strength your upper back and your mental toughness so in order to do this exercise what you want to do is step right between two pair of dumbbells and you're going to squat down you're going to pick them up grip nice and tight on the dumbbells and you're going to deadlift it up pushing through the heels keep the back back straight chest up and once you get to the top position hold it tightly with your grip and start walking one thing you got to remember is try to judge the length of space that you walk by doing laps and count each lap each time so that way you setting your record so each time you do it you try to break your record testing your grip upper back leg strength mental toughness and Agility that much more each time so next time you step in the gym do the farmers walks

the anti-gravity press anti-gravity press an excellent exercise to work those delts so what you want to do is put the bench at about a 45% incline you're going to face down so your chin is about level with the end of the bench your feet stance is going to be very wide to help for balance you're going to grab a light barbell or a easy bar curl and what you're going to do is hold it about shoulder with apart you're going to curl it right to the bottom of your chin and you're going to press it straight out in front of you holding that tension through the delts getting the contraction in the push and then the E Center contraction with the pull back and you're going to get a little bit of isometric contraction as well because you're having to hold the bar upward as you push through the motion Fighting Gravity that's pulling that weight down this is excellent exercise to work all three heads of the deltoids so next time you step in the gym and you want to work those Boulder shoulders try the anti-gravity press

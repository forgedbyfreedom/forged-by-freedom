Pizza crust made with quinoa. A healthy alternative to traditional pizza recipes and one of our new favorites. You want to begin by soaking 3/4 cups of quinoa in 2 cups of water. Cover and let set for 8 hours. Once your 8 hours are up, drain, rinse, and put the quinoa into your food processor. Next, add 1/4 cup of fresh water, 1 teaspoon of baking powder, 14 cup teaspoon salt. With all your ingredients in the food processor, pulse till smooth like paste. You'll need to stop it a few times to scrape down the sides. Line a cookie sheet with parchment paper and lightly cover with non-stick spray. Spread your quinoa mixture evenly into the pizza crust shape. Bake at 425° for 15 minutes. Remove from oven carefully. Flip it over and bake for an additional 5 to 10 more minutes. Next, you're going to want to top with your favorite toppings. The choice is completely up to you, but for us, we use tomato sauce and top that with chicken, spinach, tomatoes, and olives. Once you're done with the toppings, go ahead and put it back into the oven and let it slightly boil. Then turn down your heat. Let it sit for about 30 minutes uncovered. Then you're ready to enjoy your quinoa pizza. Thanks to Buff Mom for the recipe and be sure to check out a lot more food recipes on our Instagram at buff dudes food. Stay buff.

And finally, if you just want some quick, easy bench tips, we got a couple here. Firstly, use your back. The muscles of your upper back should be actively locking your shoulder blades into place to increase the stability of you while you bench press. Also, use your legs. Your legs should be driving into the floor, pushing you up the bench to again help improve and increase stability. Lastly, not specifically a technique thing, but more just how you should be executing. Pause your benches at least some of the time. Pausing on your chest is going to make you a stronger bench presser. It's going to make you stronger off the chest, which is where most people are weakest. And it'll give you substantial advantage in pushing your bench forwards because of that strength in the weak spot.

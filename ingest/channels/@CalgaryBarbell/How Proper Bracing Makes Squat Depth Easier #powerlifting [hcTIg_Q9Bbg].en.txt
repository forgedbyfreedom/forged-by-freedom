Proper bracing in our squat is going to look like more of a neutral range back position. We're queuing the lifter to kind of pull the ribs down, tuck the pelvis in maybe a little bit. More on that later, but we're trying to achieve something that looks more similar to the bottom position. Even if you brace properly, there's going to be a slight change in your low back and pelvis position to get depth. But we're working to try to minimize that change so that you don't feel as much of a big loss of position and you can be more consistent with your torso and your bracing throughout the entirety of the squat.

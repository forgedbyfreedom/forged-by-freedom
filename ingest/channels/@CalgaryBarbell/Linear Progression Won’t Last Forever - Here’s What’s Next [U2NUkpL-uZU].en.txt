The second model of progression we're going to look at here is a wave progression or undulating progression. After a while, linear progression is not going to work anymore. Once you're of runway on your linear progression, you want to start planning some fluctuation into your training. Usually, this looks like a period of time where you intentionally decrease volume while increasing intensity, hit a little reset, and then repeat that process again. If you look at most of the 50 plus expertly designed programs in the Calgary barbell app, that's their general structure. The basic idea behind this progression structure is that you build up, back off, and then repeat. By manipulating the variables of rep ranges, the intensity, maybe even the number of sets, you'll move from adding weight each week to now adding weight each block. So, you'll be able to prolong that progression as you get more experienced.

If I had to give you one piece of lifting advice, knowing nothing about you, nothing about your training, nothing about your goals, just knowing that you're some kind of power lifter, you do the squat, bench, and deadlift, and you do some accessory movements, here's what I would say. I would tell you that you probably need to go a little lighter on your competition work on your squat bench and deadlift and you probably need to go a little heavier on your accessories on your leg presses, your split squats, your rows, and that kind of stuff because so much of our progress on SPD movements is skill-based. And because it demands so much more from the system when it comes to recovery, it's really easy to overdo those things. We also get really tied up in the numbers. We get our egos involved and we want more weight on the bar with those. When it comes to the accessories, we can recover fast. We're usually locked in maybe on some kind of machine or something and can push really hard with minimal impact to our other lift. I've given this little piece of advice to a few clients, some friends, some family, and it's already drastically improved their training. So maybe carry that philosophy into a few sessions and see how it goes.

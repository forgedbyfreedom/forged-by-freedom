Okay, so the first thing I want to do here is differentiate the Romanian deadlift from the stiff-legged deadlift. A Romanian deadlift is going to start from the top. It starts with the eccentric or lowering phase. You're going to go down just past the knees and come back up. That's one rep. Picking the bar up doesn't count as a rep. A stiff leg deadlift, you're going to start from a dead stop every time. You're going to go all the way to the floor. You're going to keep your hips much, much higher with less knee bend. This is your start position for a stiff leg. When you're doing a Romanian deadlift, you want to try to find a good balance between pressure on your heels. So, we can feel a lot of good hamstring stretch and tension and allowing the knees to come forwards enough that by the time the bar gets to the bottom, we are getting a little bit of that quad feeling and a little bit of that push to start the lift. That way, it's going to carry over better into your deadlift and help with that transition in the competition lift itself.

If your squat is stuck, feeling off, or is just uncomfortable, chances are you're making one of these five mistakes. I see these in intermediate, novice, all the way up to advanced lifters. And fixing these mistakes can help you on your path to building a big old squat. Mistake number one, incorrect bracing. The most common and fundamental issue that I see with lifters squats is their brace. Bracing is about more than just taking a big breath in before you squat. A lot of lifters mess this up by flaring the ribs way out and kicking their hips too far behind them, creating a whole bunch of extension on the descent of the squat. Now, this is an issue because you're going to have problems with getting to depth comfortably, or maybe you'll see a really pronounced sticking point as a result of it. In order to fix your bracing, what you want to be doing is contracting your abs super duper hard, clamping your ribs down on top of your abs, and trying to keep your hips tucked in, keeping your pelvis stacked with the rest of your back as you descend in the squat. Ideally, you want to maintain this position throughout the whole of the squat, which requires that you learn how to hinge and understand how to hinge.

My final piece of advice here is to stop maxing out so often. If you attempt this new milestone bench and miss 225 every other week, that's not training. That is just lifting poorly. Don't do it. If you want to max out, and you do, I know you do. I do. Everybody does. Plan it. Don't do it more than every 12 weeks. As you get more and more advanced, you're probably going to want to test less often than that. But as a general novice, probably every 12 weeks is realistic. You can see some progress. And don't be afraid to use the smallest increments possible. You don't have to jump 10 lb just to set a new PR. You can jump 5 lbs.

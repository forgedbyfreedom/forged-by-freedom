recommendation is that we try to set the torso angle before we initiate the squat we push the hips back slightly until we achieve a bunch of tension in the glutes and hamstrings we feel that routing torsional angle to keep it relatively the same throughout the lift and avoid any shifting in the bottom position now one caveat to this is that while we set the torso angle we don't want to continue to push back as we get into the squat this will end up in a very squat morning sort of knees to nips Ripper toe style squat which is not what we're looking for we want to set the torso angle sit

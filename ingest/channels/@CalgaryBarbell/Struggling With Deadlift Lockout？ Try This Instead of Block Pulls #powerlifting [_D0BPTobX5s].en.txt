Accessory lift number two, accommodating resistance. Number two, deadlift versus bands or chains. Often people will turn to block pulls to try to strengthen the top end of their deadlift, but that cuts out the entire bottom of the range of motion, and I don't think it works very well. Using accommodating resistance allows you to hit the top end of your lift harder without losing the rest of the movement or changing your start position. If you're having a tough time with lockout, bands or chains will both hammer the lockout to make it stronger and help you maintain position off the floor by punishing you for losing that position.

Level one, 225. If you've been training your bench seriously and caring about your strength and numbers for a little while and 225 or whatever that first milestone is is still giving you some troubles. Well, here's some things you should know. Firstly, benchmar to bench more. It sounds silly. It sounds straightforward. It sounds perhaps redundant, but what I'm talking about here is frequency. If you're only benching one or two times a week, the easiest way to level up your bench press is going to be to simply expose yourself to bench press more often. As a more novice trainee, you can afford to and get away with just doing the bench press. You probably don't need many variations, if any.

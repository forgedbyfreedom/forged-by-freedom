When you brace like this with a lot of low back extension and your hips kicked out behind you like that, you're going to get to the bottom and feel like you've run out of room, like you can't possibly go any deeper. And any more depth you do get is probably just going to be from your knees shooting forwards again, making it not look like you're at depth. In order to get to good depth, usually we have to go back to a more neutral looking position, which is going to feel like absolute crap if you've started super extended. What needs to change though is not the position in the bottom, but it's the position throughout the first half of the squat.

Third, the Romanian deadlift. There's a reason bodybuilders use these to beat the piss out of their hamstring. They're really good at targeting the posterior chain, hamstrings, glutes, spinal erectors. But the flip side is that you're also going to get way better at hinging. And the hinge pattern is super central to the deadlift and to the squat to some extent. Building stronger hamstrings and perfecting your hinge in the deadlift is super important because that's going to allow you to really be in the position you want to be in once that bar comes past the knees. And also, it's going to strengthen your lockout.

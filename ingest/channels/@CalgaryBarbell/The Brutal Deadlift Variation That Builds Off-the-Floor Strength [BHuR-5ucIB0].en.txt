Accessory lift one, deficit deadlifts. First up, deficit deadlifts. Now, these can be pretty brutal, and honestly, I'd only recommend them for conventional deadlifters, not as a sumo accessory. But by extending the range of motion and putting yourself up on a 1 to 2 in block, you can really build a lot of strength in the first few inches of the lift and teach yourself to be patient and stay tight while you're breaking the bar off the floor. I wouldn't go any bigger than a maybe two 3inch deficit at most. And I also wouldn't go down to an inch or a half inch deficit cuz I think that's just going to mess with your usual pattern. We want this to be distinct enough that it actually trains something differently.

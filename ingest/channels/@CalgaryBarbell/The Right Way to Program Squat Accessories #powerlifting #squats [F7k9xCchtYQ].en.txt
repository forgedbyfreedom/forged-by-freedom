Like we said in the deadlift video, you don't want to do all five of these at once, nor do you want to be changing them up every single week. Pick one or two that you think apply to your specific needs and weaknesses in your training. Run them for a period of time. Assess whether or not they helped. Either run them again or change them in the next block. For most lifters, I would say you can probably run two of these as well as a comp squat each week. I think most lifters squat frequency can be a little bit higher than their deadlift frequency. So, I think if you were to run a paused squat and a tempo squat, or a pin squat and a paused squat, or any combination of those, you'd probably be just fine.

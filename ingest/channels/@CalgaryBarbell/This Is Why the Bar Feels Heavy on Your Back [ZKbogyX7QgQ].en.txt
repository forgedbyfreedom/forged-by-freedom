Mistake number two, improper upper back positioning. A lot of lifters will struggle with creating a good shelf for the bar to rest on when squatting. And my tip here is that we need some degree of pulling the shoulder blades back and down to create that shelf. If you look at more novice lifters, a lot of them will be shrugged up in the shoulder blades and maybe even pushed forwards in the shoulder blades. This can result in them having issues with their wrists and elbows and shoulders in terms of discomfort and also in being bullied around by the bar. We need to be tight and rigid in the upper back in what I call the shelf in order to have a good squat. So, make sure that we're creating tension in the upper back and using that to tie the bar into our aforementioned brace.

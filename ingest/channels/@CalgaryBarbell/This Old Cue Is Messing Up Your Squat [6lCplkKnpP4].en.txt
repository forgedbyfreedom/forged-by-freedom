Mistake number three, driving your chest up. There used to for a long time, especially when I first got into powerlifting, be a very prevalent cue. Lifters should drive their chest up when squatting that once you hit the hole, just just push your chest up, reach your chest up. This is maybe one of the worst cues out there. Now, I don't think it's as prevalent anymore, and this kind of does double back on or piggyback on the bracing queue, but a lot of lifters will hit the bottom and then try to drive their chest up to ensure that they're not getting pulled forward by the bar. In actuality, like I said previously, this is going to mess with our brace. So, what we need to be doing in the squat is when we hit the bottom, we need to be keeping those ribs down and being patient out of the bottom. The first portion of the lift where we drive out of the hole, we should be focusing on maintaining the brace and pushing with the legs really hard. That way, we're setting ourselves up to be stronger and better positioned for the second and third/ thirds of the lift.

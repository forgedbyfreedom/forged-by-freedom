Secondly, my tip for you is to train your brace. We talked earlier about the importance of learning how to brace, developing the skill of bracing, but if you're not already, I think at this point, you can start trying to intentionally affect your brace with the training that you're doing. Now, this can be from an SSB or paused squat or pin squat or something like that. Or in a lot of cases, what I recommend intermediate lifters do is to sort of break out the muscles that contribute to the brace and train them with isolation movements. Get on doing some rows. Get on doing some back extensions. Get on doing some side bends. Load that stuff up. Do rows both heavy and light in the six to eight rep range and the 12 to 15 rep range. Do some planks. Find a couple ab exercises that you suck at and get good at them. A lot of the times breaking out this work and isolating the muscles responsible for the brace can contribute and make sure that your brace comes up at the same level or same speed as the rest of your squat.

The second thing, and this is slept on at all levels, is to eat your bench up. What I mean by that is focus in on your nutrition. One of the first things a lot of lifters notice if, for example, they cut down a weight class is that their bench is the first thing to be impacted. If you're not at the very least getting sufficient protein in in your day, you're really selling yourself short on bench progress. Even if you're not okay with gaining weight and and maybe your training is a vehicle for you to actually lose weight, ensuring that you have an adequate intake of protein throughout your day is by far one of the easiest boxes to check off to ensure that you're going to make some good bench progress. And conversely, if you're somebody who's looking to gain size and strength, eating in a surplus, it's going to be one of the surefire ways to increase your bench.

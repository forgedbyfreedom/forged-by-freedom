Now, let's talk a little bit about the lifters or the squat issues and characteristics that might make you lean towards one of these exercises over another. If you're having trouble finding your position in the bottom or you're falling into the hole in your squat, the pause squat or pin squat are great for fixing that. If you find yourself crumpling as you come up out of the hole or your knees and hips shoot backwards, that is a classic case of I need to pin squat. If you're struggling with your hinge or how to position yourself and time things during your descent, tempo squats will help you a lot there. If your upper back or the shelf that the bar is sitting on is collapsing as you change directions, that is a pretty clear indication that the SSB might help you a lot. And finally, if you're just not able to get things moving in terms of squat progress, but your back doesn't seem to want to handle more squat volume, smash the out of your legs on the leg press.

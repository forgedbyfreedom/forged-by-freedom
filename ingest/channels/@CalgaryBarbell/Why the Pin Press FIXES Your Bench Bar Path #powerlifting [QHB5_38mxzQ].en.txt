Why the pin press? Why does this work so well? Why does this correct bar path? Well, it's because of the momentary relaxation of those supportive and postural muscles in the upper back. Because we relax even just slightly onto the pins and then need to retighten everything as we press. It teaches us to tighten everything as we press or to lose less tightness in the bottom. So, in that way, it can really carry over to help refine your touch weight and touch position in the comp bench. The other thing I really like about the pin press is the feedback that you get. So, if you go to press the bar in the wrong direction because of the relaxation, because of that momentary lapse of tightness on the pins, it's going to let you know that bar is going to go flying in the wrong direction. You're really going to feel the difference between a good bar path rep and a poorly pathed rep. So that feedback can go a long ways to exaggerating the fault and forcing you to almost overcorrect it that you can then take forward into your competition bench to help fix the bar path.

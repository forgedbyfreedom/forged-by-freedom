In the carbon app, go to the diary tab, tap the blue plus sign next to your meal, type in your food or drink, select the type and serving size, adjust carb and fat percentages, and tap add alcohol. and tap Add Alcohol. The second way is using the barcode scanner. Open the Diary tab, tap the blue plus next to your meal, select the barcode icon to scan your food, choose the serving size, adjust carb and fat percentages, and tap Add Alcohol. The third way is by creating a food. In the Diary tab, tap the floating menu button in the bottom right, then select Create Food. The final way is by using the Quick Add feature. Tap the three small gray circles on the meal you want to add, then select Quick Add Alcohol. Name your alcohol, add your known ABV and volume, then tap Add to add your created alcohol to your diary.

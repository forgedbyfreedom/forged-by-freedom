Users can utilize our label scanner feature to scan nutritional labels and add custom foods directly in the app. If you want to create a new custom food or modify an existing one, you can use the label scanner to scan the nutrition facts and fill in the fields automatically. To begin, simply tap the plus sign on the meal you are tracking. Then tap the QR code icon on the right side of the search bar and then tap the label button. Hold your phone over the nutrition label and allow the scanner to detect the text. Adjust focus by moving your phone closer or farther or use pinch to zoom and tap to focus. The scanner will highlight the text it recognizes and fill in the fields accordingly. Tap create food and the scanned macro and micronutrients will automatically be updated for you.

Now, what are the benefits of the Mediterranean diet? Well, in quite a few metaanalyses, the Mediterranean diet pretty consistently is equal to or outperforms almost all other diets when it comes to the risk of cardiovascular disease. So, amongst people who are very adherent to the Mediterranean diet, we see a reduced risk of cardiovascular disease by up to like 60% and cardiovascular disease mortality by up to 50%. And the other top performing diet for cardiovascular disease, the DASH diet, which is the dietary approach to stop hypertension, which focuses on low sodium, um, plant-based, low less meat intake. The Mediterranean diet actually outperforms that because people tend to have better adherence to the Mediterranean diet than the DASH diet, which again, the DASH diet is also an effective treatment for cardiovascular disease if you can stick to it. >> [music]

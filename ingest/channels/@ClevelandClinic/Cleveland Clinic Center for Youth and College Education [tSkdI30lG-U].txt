In the Student Pathways program, high school juniors and seniors can participate in a six-week educational program that teaches them about clinical and non-clinical careers while providing them with vital skills such as interviewing and resume writing. At the end of the program, students receive a stipend and get direct guidance from Cleveland Clinic professionals that will help them chart out career paths that could lead to meaningful full-time employment, whether they want to go straight into the workforce or pursue more education. The Center for Youth and College Education also has a high school summer internship program with three different career tracks that offer students a paid opportunity to see what it is like to work in a healthcare career. In the Louis Stokes track, students residing in select cities from Congressman Louis Stokes' former district intern in non-clinical business-focused healthcare careers. In the Science track, which is open to students from counties listed on our website, students work in clinical healthcare careers. Through our Advanced Technology track, students can work in settings that offer skills in advanced computing, data analytics, cybersecurity, coding, or other related fields. All three tracks offer opportunities to network with Cleveland Clinic caregivers and to gain leadership skills in engaging virtual learning sessions. The Center for Youth and College Education is also thrilled to offer a select number of college students an opportunity to work in non-clinical settings such as cybersecurity, clinical engineering, and healthcare administration. Offerings will vary by year. Our shadowing program allows high school students, college students, and adult learners to gain visual and verbal experiences to make an informed decision and gain an understanding about their career of interest through shadowing a healthcare professional for a defined time. Looking for healthcare professionals to discuss clinical and non-clinical healthcare careers? The Center for Youth and College Education Speakers Bureau can arrange for Cleveland Clinic healthcare professionals to speak to your group, either virtually or in person. We provide speakers for local school districts and colleges, as well as for job fairs and other events. Speakers are available for panel discussions, breakout sessions, healthcare career discussions, and more. A limited number of field trip opportunities for classrooms to visit Cleveland Clinic are also available by completing a request form. Students can also experience healthcare careers by participating in our one-day boot camps or by joining us for a week in the summer for a core camp, which are organized by career areas. These opportunities bring groups of learners on-site and non-clinical settings to experience various healthcare careers in classroom or simulation settings. At the Center for Youth and College Education, we strive to provide you with career exploration services that fit your needs.

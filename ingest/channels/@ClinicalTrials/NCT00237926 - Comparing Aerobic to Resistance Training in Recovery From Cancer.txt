CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT00237926
Title: Comparing Aerobic to Resistance trening in Recovery From Cancer
Official Title: Comparing Aerobic to Resistance trening in Recovery From Cancer
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 58
Topic: resistance trening

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to compare the health outcomes of a 12-week exercise program focused on aerobic trening (using a treadmill) to a 12-week exercise program focused on resistance trening (using Thera-Bands) in sedentary patients within 6 months of completing treatment for cancer.

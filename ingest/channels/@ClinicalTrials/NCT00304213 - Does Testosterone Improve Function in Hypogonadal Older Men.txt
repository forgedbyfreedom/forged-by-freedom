CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT00304213
Title: Does Testosterone Improve Function in Hypogonadal Older Men
Official Title: Does Testosterone Improve Function in Hypogonadal Older Men
Status: WITHDRAWN
Phase: PHASE4
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 0
Topic: testosterone replacement

SUMMARY:
This is a randomized controlled trial of testosterone replacement therapy in hypogonadal men age \> 60 years. The goal is to assess the safety and efficacy of TRT in men age \> 60 years.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Physical function
  - Cognitive function

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Testosterone

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 60 Years to 80 Years
Sex: MALE

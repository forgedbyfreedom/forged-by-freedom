CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT00306176
Title: Rosiglitazone and Exercise in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
Official Title: Rosiglitazone and Exercise in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE4
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 100
Topic: exercise intervention diabetes

SUMMARY:
The aim of the present study is to investigate the influence of systemic exercise, rosiglitazone administration and combined pharmaceutical and lifestyle intervention on cardiovascular risk factors

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Cardiovascular risk factors alterations

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Rosiglitazone

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 40 Years to 75 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT00434148
Title: Safety and Efficacy of Different Dose Levels of Pasireotide in Patients With de Novo, Persistent or Recurrent Cushing's Disease
Official Title: A Randomized, Double-blind Study to Assess the Safety and Efficacy of Different Dose Levels of Pasireotide (SOM230) Subcutaneous (sc) Over a 6 Month Treatment Period in Patients With de Novo, Persistent or Recurrent Cushing's Disease
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 162
Topic: growth hormone treatment

SUMMARY:
This study will evaluate the safety and efficacy of two different doses of Pasireotide in patients with de novo or recurrent/persistent Cushing's Disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Number of mUFC (Urinary Free Cortisol) Responders by Randomized Dose Group

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Pasireotide

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

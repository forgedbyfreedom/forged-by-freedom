CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT00519844
Title: Growth Hormone Treatment in Children Born Small for Gestational Age: Assessment of Satisfaction
Official Title: Assessment of Satisfaction With Growth Hormone Treatment in Children Born Small for Gestational Age: Benefits of Galenic Form of the Product
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE4
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 5
Topic: growth hormone treatment

SUMMARY:
This trial is conducted in Europe. The aim of this trial is to assess satisfaction with growth hormone treatment in children of both sexes born small for gestational age and who are receiving growth hormone treatment.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Satisfaction/preference with the method of treatment assesses by means of a satisfaction questionnaire and a standard questionnaire of preference

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: somatropin

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 6 Years to 15 Years
Sex: ALL

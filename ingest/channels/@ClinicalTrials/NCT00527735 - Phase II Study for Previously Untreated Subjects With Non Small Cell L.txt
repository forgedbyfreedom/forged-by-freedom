CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT00527735
Title: Phase II Study for Previously Untreated Subjects With Non Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) or Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC)
Official Title: A Randomized, Double Blind, Parallel, Three Arm Trial Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of Ipilimumab (BMS-734016) in Combination With Paclitaxel/Carboplatin Compared to Paclitaxel/Carboplatin Alone in Previously Untreated Subjects With Lung Cancer
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 334
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
The purpose of the study is to determine whether ipilimumab given with paclitaxel/carboplatin has clinical benefit when compared with paclitaxel/carboplatin alone in patients with previously untreated lung cancer.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Immune-related Progression-free Survival (irPFS) in Participants With Nonsmall-cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) Per Immune-related Response Criteria (irRC)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Ipilimumab
  - DRUG: Placebo
  - DRUG: Paclitaxel
  - DRUG: Carboplatin

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

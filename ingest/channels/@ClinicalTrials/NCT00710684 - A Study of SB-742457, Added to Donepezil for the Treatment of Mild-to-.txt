CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT00710684
Title: A Study of SB-742457, Added to Donepezil for the Treatment of Mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's Disease
Official Title: Study AZ3110866, a Fixed Dose Study of SB-742457 Versus Placebo When Added to Existing Donepezil Treatment in Subjects With Mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's Disease
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 682
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
The study is designed to investigate the safety and efficacy of SB-742457 when added to stable donepezil treatment in subjects with mild-to-moderate Alzheimer's disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change From Baseline in Alzheimer's Disease Assessment Scale - Cognitive Subscale (ADAS-Cog) Total Score at Week 24
  - Change From Baseline in Clinical Dementia Rating - Sum of Boxes (CDR-SB) Score at Week 24

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: SB-742457 15mg
  - DRUG: SB-742457 35mg
  - DRUG: Placebo
  - DRUG: donepezil 5-10mg

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 50 Years to 85 Years
Sex: ALL

Title: Osteoporosis and Dental Implant
Official Title: Evaluation of the Influence of Alendronate on Wound Healing After Dental Implants in Patients With Osteoporosis

Phase: PHASE4
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 45

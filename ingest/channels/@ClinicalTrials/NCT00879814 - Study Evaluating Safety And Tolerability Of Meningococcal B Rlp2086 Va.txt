CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT00879814
Title: Study Evaluating Safety And Tolerability Of Meningococcal B Rlp2086 Vaccine In Healthy Adults
Official Title: A Phase 1 Randomized, Open-label, Parallel-group, Active- And Placebo-controlled Study To Assess Safety And Tolerability Of 60, 120, And 200 Mcg Meningococcal Group B Rlp2086 Vaccine In Healthy Adult Subjects
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 48
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to evaluate the safety and tolerability of an investigational meningococcal B rLP2086 vaccine in healthy adults.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Percentage of Participants With at Least One Adverse Event (AE)
  - Percentage of Participants With Change in Severity From Baseline in Laboratory Evaluations (Alanine Aminotrensferase [ALT])
  - Percentage of Participants With Change in Severity From Baseline in Laboratory Evaluations (Aspartate Aminotrensferase [AST])
  - Percentage of Participants With Change in Severity From Baseline in Laboratory Evaluations (Total Protein)
  - Percentage of Participants With Change in Severity From Baseline in Laboratory Evaluations (Albumin)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BIOLOGICAL: rLP2086 vaccine or control
  - BIOLOGICAL: rLP2086 vaccine or control
  - BIOLOGICAL: rLP2086 vaccine or control
  - BIOLOGICAL: rLP2086 vaccine or control

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 40 Years
Sex: ALL

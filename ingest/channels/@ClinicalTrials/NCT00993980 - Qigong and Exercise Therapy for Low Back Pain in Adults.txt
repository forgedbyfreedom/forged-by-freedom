CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT00993980
Title: Qigong and Exercise Therapy for Low Back Pain in Adults
Official Title: Randomized Multicenter Trial on the Effectiveness of Qigong and a Stabilizing Exercise Therapy in Patients With Low Back Pain
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 127
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to assess the effectiveness of qigong for the treatment of low back pain compared to a stabilizing exercise therapy.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - average pain intensity during the last 7 days measured on a visual analogue scale (VAS)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - PROCEDURE: qigong
  - PROCEDURE: exercise therapy

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 20 Years to 60 Years
Sex: ALL

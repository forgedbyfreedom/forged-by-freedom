CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT01108419
Title: Evaluation of Dose-effect of a New Fermented Food in Healthy Adults
Official Title: Evaluation of the Safety and Tolerance of a New Fermented Food in Healthy Adults
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 96
Topic: beta-alanine

SUMMARY:
The aim of the study is to assess the safety and tolerance of a new fermented food versus a control product in healthy adults during the 4-week consumption period.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Effect of 1 bottle/day of test versus control product on adverse events.
  - Effect of 1 bottle/day of test versus control product vital signs.
  - Effect of 1 bottle/day of test versus control product vital signs.
  - Effect of 1 bottle/day of test versus control product vital signs.
  - Effect of 1 bottle/day of test versus control product vital signs.

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Fermented dairy product
  - OTHER: milk-based non-fermented dairy product

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 55 Years
Sex: ALL

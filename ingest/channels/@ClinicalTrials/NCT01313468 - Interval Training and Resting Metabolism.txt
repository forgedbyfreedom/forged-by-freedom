CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT01313468
Title: Interval trening and Resting Metabolism
Official Title: Effect of Aerobic Interval trening on NEAT in Sedentary Men
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 30
Topic: high intensity interval trening

SUMMARY:
It has been reported that obese individuals are seated 2 ½ hour more than lean individuals and that non exercise thermo genesis (NEAT) may vary with as much as 2000 Kcal/day between equal size people due to different occupational and leisure time activities. The primary aim of the study is to investigate if 10 weeks of exercise trening increase NEAT in sedentary men.

Subject will be randomized to three different trening intervention, with 3 exercise session each week for 10 weeks. NEAT will be measured using an activity sensor and recorded over a 7 day period both before and after trening.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - nonexercise activity thermogenesis

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: 1 x 4 minute interval
  - BEHAVIORAL: 4 x 4 minutes Intervals
  - BEHAVIORAL: Moderate continuous trening

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 50 Years
Sex: MALE

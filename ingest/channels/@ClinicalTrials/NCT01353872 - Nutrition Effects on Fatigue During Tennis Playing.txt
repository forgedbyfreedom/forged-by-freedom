CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT01353872
Title: Nutrition Effects on Fatigue During Tennis Playing
Official Title: Effects of Ingestion of Different Sports Drinks on Fatigue During Playing Tennis
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 8
Topic: sports nutrition

SUMMARY:
The main objective of this study is to investigate the effects of ingestion of different sports drinks (Nutrattente / Nutraperf / Nutrarecup) on fatigue induced by a tennis tournament simulation.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - peripheral fatigue measured by surface electromyographic (EMG) signal from the right triceps muscle

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Nutrattente / Nutraperf / Nutrarecup
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Nutrattente placebo / Nutraperf placebo / Nutrarecup placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 35 Years
Sex: MALE

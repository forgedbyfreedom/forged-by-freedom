CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT01398605
Title: Physical Activity in Postmenopausal Women
Official Title: Physical Activity and Exercise Dose-response on Cardiovascular Risk Factors in Postmenopausal Women
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 200
Topic: recovery exercise

SUMMARY:
The study is designed to investigate the physical activity and its dose-response relationship in postmenopausal women on the cardiovascular risks and cardiac function.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Body mass index (BMI)
  - Waist circumference
  - Lab data (lipid profile, CRP, insulin resistance)
  - QT interval and corrected QT interval (QTc)
  - Cardiovascular fitness

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Exercise training

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 40 Years to 80 Years
Sex: ALL

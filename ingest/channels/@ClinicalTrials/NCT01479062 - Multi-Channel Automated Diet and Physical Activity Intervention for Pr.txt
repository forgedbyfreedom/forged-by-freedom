CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT01479062
Title: Multi-Channel Automated Diet and Physical Activity Intervention for Pre-Diabetics
Official Title: Multi-Channel Automated Diet and Physical Activity Intervention for Pre-Diabetics (ALIVE-PD)
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 339
Topic: weight loss exercise

SUMMARY:
The trial will test whether the Alive multi-channel delivery health behavior program can positively affect weight and glucose level in pre-diabetics.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change from baseline in body weight
  - Change from baseline in diabetes risk markers

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: Alive-PD
  - BEHAVIORAL: Usual Care

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 40 Years to 79 Years
Sex: ALL

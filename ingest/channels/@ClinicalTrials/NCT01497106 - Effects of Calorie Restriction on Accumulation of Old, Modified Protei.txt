CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT01497106
Title: Effects of Calorie Restriction on Accumulation of Old, Modified Proteins in Abdominally Obese Adults
Official Title: Effects of Calorie Restriction on Accumulation of Old, Modified Proteins in Abdominally Obese Adults
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 29
Topic: fat loss muscle

SUMMARY:
This study is being done to understand the effects of calorie restriction and weight loss on muscle protein metabolism in adult men and women.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in muscle protein synthesis from baseline to 16 weeks

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: Calorie Restriction
  - BEHAVIORAL: Control (normal living)

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 45 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

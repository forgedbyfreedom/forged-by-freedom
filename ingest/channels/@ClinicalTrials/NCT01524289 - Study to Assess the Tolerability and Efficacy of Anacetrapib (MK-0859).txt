CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT01524289
Title: Study to Assess the Tolerability and Efficacy of Anacetrapib (MK-0859) Co-Administered With Statin in Participants With Heterozygous Familial Hypercholesterolemia (MK-0859-020)
Official Title: A 1-Year, Worldwide, Multicenter, Double-Blind, Randomized, Parallel, Placebo-Controlled Study to Assess the Efficacy and Tolerability of Anacetrapib When Added to Ongoing Statin Therapy With or Without Other Lipid Modifying Medication(s) in Patients With Heterozygous Familial Hypercholesterolemia
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 306
Topic: beta-alanine

SUMMARY:
The objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and tolerability of adding anacetrapib to ongoing statin therapy in participants with heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HeFH).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Percent Change From Baseline in Low-Density Lipoprotein Cholesterol (LDL-C) - Treatment Phase
  - Percentage of Participants With Any Adverse Event - Treatment Phase
  - Percentage of Participants With Any Treatment-Related Adverse Event - Treatment Phase
  - Percentage of Participants With Any Serious Adverse Event - Treatment Phase
  - Percentage of Participants Discontinuing Study Treatment Due to an Adverse Event - Treatment Phase

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Anacetrapib
  - DRUG: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 80 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT01529281
Title: Exercise-induced Muscle Damage is Reduced in Resistance Trained Athletes by Branch Chain Amino Acids
Official Title: Exercise-induced Muscle Damage is Reduced in Resistance Trained Athletes by Branch Chain Amino Acids
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 12
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
It is well documented that exercise-induced muscle damage (EIMD) decreases muscle function and causes severe soreness and discomfort. Branched-chain amino acid (BCAAs) supplementation has been shown to increase protein synthesis and decrease muscle protein breakdown, however, the effects of BCAAs on recovery from EIMD are unknown. Thus, the aim of this study was to examine the effects of a BCAAssupplement on markers of muscle damage.

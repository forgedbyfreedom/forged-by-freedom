CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT01542775
Title: Effects of Exercise on sRAGE Levels and Cardiometabolic Risk Factors
Official Title: 
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 80
Topic: aerobic exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to examine the effects of exercise on diverse cardiometabolic risk factors and indicators of atherosclerosis in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - change from baseline in soluble receptor for advanced glycation endproducts (sRAGE) after 12 weeks of aerobic exercise

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: Aerobic Exercise Group
  - BEHAVIORAL: Control Group

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 85 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT01603641
Title: BOTOX® Open-Label Treatment in Pediatric Lower Limb Spasticity
Official Title: BOTOX® Treatment in Pediatric Lower Limb Spasticity: Open-label Study
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 370
Topic: muscle rehabilitation

SUMMARY:
This study will evaluate the long-term safety of BOTOX® (botulinum toxin Type A) for the treatment of pediatric lower limb spasticity.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Percentage of Participants With at Least One Treatment-emergent Adverse Event (TEAE)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BIOLOGICAL: Botulinum Toxin Type A

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 2 Years to 17 Years
Sex: ALL

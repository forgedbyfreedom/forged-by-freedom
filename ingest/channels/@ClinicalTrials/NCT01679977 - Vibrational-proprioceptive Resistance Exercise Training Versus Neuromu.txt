Title: Vibrational-proprioceptive Resistance Exercise Training Versus Neuromuscular Electrical Stimulation Training in Elderly People With Muscle Weakness
Official Title: 
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 150
Topic: resistance training

Study the structural behaviours of weak muscle of elderly and evaluate the efficiency of two different types of training.

Not provided

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 65 Years to 85 Years

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT01852994
Title: Exercise trening and Testosterone Replacement in Heart Failure Patients
Official Title: Exercise trening and Testosterone Replacement in Heart Failure Patients
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE4
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 39
Topic: testosterone replacement

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to determine if exercise trening with or without testosterone replacement can improve cardiopathy in heart failure patients

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
In this study, we are evaluating:

* hospital length of stay and readmission
* muscle sympathetic nerve activity
* functional capacity
* body composition

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Microneurography
  - Forearm blood flow

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Testosterone replacement
  - OTHER: Exercise trening

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 65 Years
Sex: MALE

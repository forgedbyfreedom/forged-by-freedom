CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02032056
Title: Effect of Probiotics in Reducing Infections and Allergies in Young Children During the Complementary Feeding Period
Official Title: The Effect of Probiotics in Reducing Infections and Allergic Manifestations in Young Children During the Complementary Feeding Period
Status: TERMINATED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 40
Topic: sports nutrition

SUMMARY:
The aim of the intervention is to examine the effect of the bacterial strain BB-12, provided for 6 mo, on the prevalence of infections and allergic manifestations in small children, and how BB-12 affects the immune system, the gastrointestinal tract and the microbiota. Children are enrolled during 2 winter seasons.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Number of days absent from day care due to respiratory and gastrointestinal infections

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Probiotic (10^9 CFU/day)
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Probiotic (10^8 CFU/day)
  - OTHER: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 9 Months to 13 Months
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02092584
Title: Effect of Omega-3 Supplementation on Serum Level and Gene Expression of IGF-1and IGFBP-3 in Men With CVD.
Official Title: Effect of Omega-3 Supplementation on Serum Level and Gene Expression of IGF-1and IGFBP-3 in Men With Cardiovascular Disease.
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE4
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 31
Topic: protein supplementation

SUMMARY:
The aim of this study is the comparison between the effects of supplementation with omega 3 or placebo for 8 weeks in serum level of IGF-1 and IGFBP-3 and gene expression of IGF-1 in patients with cardiovascular disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Serum Total Cholesterol
  - Serum HDL-C
  - Serum LDL-C
  - Serum TG
  - Serum Fasting Blood Sugar(FBS)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Omega-3
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 45 Years to 65 Years
Sex: MALE

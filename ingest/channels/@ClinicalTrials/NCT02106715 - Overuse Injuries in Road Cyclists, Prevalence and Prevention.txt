CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02106715
Title: Overuse Injuries in Road Cyclists, Prevalence and Prevention
Official Title: Overuse Injuries in Road Cyclists, Prevalence and Prevention
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 400
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to investigate the prevalence of overload injuries to road cyclists in Sweden, and if it 's possible to prevent low back pain by a simple exercise program for two months of training .

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Number and type of overuse injuries

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Training

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 55 Years
Sex: ALL

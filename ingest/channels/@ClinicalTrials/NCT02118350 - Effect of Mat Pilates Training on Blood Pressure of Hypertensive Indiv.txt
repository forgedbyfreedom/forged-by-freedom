CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02118350
Title: Effect of Mat Pilates Training on Blood Pressure of Hypertensive Individuals
Official Title: Effect of Mat Pilates Training on Blood Pressure of Hypertensive Individuals
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 44
Topic: strength training

SUMMARY:
The study aimed to evaluate the chronic effects of Mat Pilates Training on blood pressure, heart rate, double product and psychobiological factors in hypertensive medicated women and acute effects of Mat Pilates Exercise on blood pressure of such individuals.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Blood pressure

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: Mat Pilates Training

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 30 Years to 59 Years
Sex: FEMALE

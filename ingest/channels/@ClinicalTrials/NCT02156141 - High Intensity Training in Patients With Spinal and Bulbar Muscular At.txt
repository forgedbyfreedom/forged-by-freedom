CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02156141
Title: High Intensity trening in Patients With Spinal and Bulbar Muscular Atrophy
Official Title: High Intensity trening in Patients With Spinal and Bulbar Muscular Atrophy
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 10
Topic: high intensity interval trening

SUMMARY:
We want investigate if high intensity trening can increase daily functionality without causing muscle damage in patients Spinal and Bulbar Muscular Atrophy . We want to study if there is a difference in effect with supervised and unsupervised trening. Furthermore we want to study if a supervised trening program will motivate participants to continue trening by the end of the program.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Incremental test

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Supervised high intensity trening
  - OTHER: Optional trening
  - OTHER: Control period
  - OTHER: Unsupervised High intensity trening

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 70 Years
Sex: MALE

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02235987
Title: Single Dose Pharmacology Study of DG3173 and Octreotide in Acromegalic Patients.
Official Title: A Study of the Effect of Ascending Single Doses of DG3173 and 300 μg of Octreotide on Human Growth Hormone Levels in Untreated Acromegalics.
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 20
Topic: growth hormone treatment

SUMMARY:
The study is designed to investigate the safety, tolerability and efficacy of DG3173 in untreated acromegaly patients. Twenty patients received ascending single doses of DG3173 and one dose of octreotide, the current gold standard of medical therapy for acromegaly, with each patient receiving all doses of DG3173 as well as octreotide.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Participants With Trough Human Growth Hormone < 2.5 ug/mL

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: octreotide
  - DRUG: DG3173

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

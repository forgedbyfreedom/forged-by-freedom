CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02250768
Title: Effects of Embryogen in RIF Patients
Official Title: Effects of Granulocyte - Macrophage Colony - Stimulating Factor (GM-CSF) Supplementation in Embryo Culture in Patients With Repeated Implantation Failure
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE4
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 60
Topic: protein supplementation

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study was to investigate the potential effects of Granulocyte-Macrophage Stimulating-Colony Factor (GM-CSF) supplementation in embryo culture for patients with repeated implantation failure (RIF)

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
60 patients with repeated implantation failure were involved.

RIF was defined as patients failed to conceive (beta-HCG \< 5 mIU/mL) after at least 03 embryo transfers (fresh and frozen) with 06 top quality embryos or more.

Ovarian stimulation was performed using GnRH antagonist protocol. All matured oocytes were inseminated by ICSI. Injected oocytes were cultured in medium supplemented with 2ng/ml of GM-CSF (Embryogen, Origio) until day of transfer. Embryo evaluation was performed at ﬁxed time points of 18 and 44 hour after fertilization. Embryo transfer was performed 2 days after ovum retrieval.

Luteal-phase support was provided using progesterone gel (Crinone 8% 90 mg, twice a day; Merck Serono) and estradiol per os (Valiera 2mg, twice a day; Laboratorios Recalcine). Serum HCG was measured 16 days after oocyte retrieval, and if positive, an ultrasound scan was performed at gestational weeks 7 and 12.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - ongoing pregnancy rate

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BIOLOGICAL: GM-CSF

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 40 Years
Sex: FEMALE

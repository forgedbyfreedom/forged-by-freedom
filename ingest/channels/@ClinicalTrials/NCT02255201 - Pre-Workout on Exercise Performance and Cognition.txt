CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02255201
Title: Pre-Workout on Exercise Performance and Cognition
Official Title: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Crossover Clinical Trial Comparing the Effects of Four Pre-Workout Beverages on Muscular Strength and Endurance and Cognitive Performance in Healthy Adult Males
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 24
Topic: caffeine exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to determine the effects of four different pre-workout beverages as compared to placebo on muscular strength and endurance, perceived energy and cognitive performance in healthy adult males.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in Muscular Strength and Endurance compared to Placebo

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Master Performance Blend Dose 1
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Master Performance Blend Dose 2
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Performance Energy Blend
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Energy Blend
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 40 Years
Sex: MALE

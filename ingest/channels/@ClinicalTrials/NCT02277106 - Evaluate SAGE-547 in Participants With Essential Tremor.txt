CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02277106
Title: Evaluate SAGE-547 in Participants With Essential Tremor
Official Title: A Double-blind, Placebo-controlled, Two-Period Crossover Proof-of-Concept Study Evaluating the Safety, Tolerability, Pharmacokinetics, and Efficacy of SAGE-547 Injection in the Treatment of Patients With Essential Tremor
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 25
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
Stage 1 is a double-blind, proof-of-concept study designed to evaluate the safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics (PK), and efficacy of SAGE-547 Injection in male and female participants with essential tremor in the upper limb.

Stage 2 is an open-label arm designed to evaluate the safety, tolerability, PK, and effectiveness of SAGE-547 Injection at a higher dose than in Stage 1. Participants who completed Stage 1 were invited to participate in Stage 2.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Stage 1: Percentage of Participants With Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs) and Serious Adverse Events (SAEs)
  - Stage 2: Percentage of Participants With Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs) and Serious Adverse Events (SAEs)
  - Stage 1: Percentage of Participants With a Response of 'Yes' to Any Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) Suicidal Ideation or Suicidal Behavior Item at Any Visit
  - Stage 2: Percentage of Participants With a Response of 'Yes' to Any Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) Suicidal Ideation or Suicidal Behavior Item at Any Visit
  - Stage 1: Change From Baseline (CFB) in Vital Signs Parameter - Supine Systolic Blood Pressure

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: SAGE-547
  - DRUG: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 35 Years to 75 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02345057
Title: A Study to Evaluate Efficacy and Safety of CS-3150 in Japanese Type 2 Diabetic Subjects With Microalbuminuria
Official Title: A Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled, Multi-center Study to Evaluate Efficacy and Safety of CS-3150 in Japanese Subjects With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Microalbuminuria
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 365
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
This study is a randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blind, multi-center study to evaluate efficacy and safety of different doses of CS-3150 compared to placebo in Japanese Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Microalbuminuria.

The Primary endpoint is the change from baseline in urinary albumin to creatine ratio (UACR).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change from baseline in urinary albumin to creatine ratio (UACR)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: CS-3150
  - DRUG: placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 20 Years to 
Sex: ALL

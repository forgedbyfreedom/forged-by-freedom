CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02455258
Title: Dancing for Better Aging: Evaluating the Impact of a Dance/Movement Therapy (D/MT) Program for Older Adults
Official Title: Dancing for Better Aging: Evaluating the Impact of a Dance/Movement Therapy Program for Older Adults Through an Empirical Research Approach
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 75
Topic: aerobic exercise

SUMMARY:
The aim of this study is to empirically evaluate the impact of a Dance/Movement Therapy program adapted to older adults. Participants over 60 years old are enrolled in one of three groups: Dance/Movement Therapy, Aerobic Exercise or Waiting List (control group) for 12 weeks. The trening groups occur 3 times a week for 1 hour each session. Physical condition, cognitive function, general health and lifestyle, and stress hormones are assessed at baseline, after 12 weeks and after 28 weeks.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Z-score change on Cognitive Functioning

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Dance/Movement Therapy (DMT)
  - OTHER: Aerobics Exercise (AE)

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 60 Years to 
Sex: ALL

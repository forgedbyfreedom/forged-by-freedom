CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02455778
Title: Effect of Oral Cinnamon Intervention in Metabolic Syndrome
Official Title: Effect of Intervention With Oral Cinnamon on Metabolic Profile and Body Composition of Asian Indians With Metabolic Syndrome in North India: A 16 Week Double Blind Placebo Control Trial
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 103
Topic: exercise intervention diabetes

SUMMARY:
This double blind placebo control study with supplementation of oral Cinnamon is being conducted to test the hypothesis that in subjects with Metabolic Syndrome oral cinnamon may lead to improvement in body composition and metabolic parameters.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Fasting Blood Glucose

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Cinnamon
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 25 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02509117
Title: First-In-Human Study Of Single And Multiple Ascending Doses Of PF-06751979
Official Title: A Phase 1, Randomized, Double-blind, Sponsor-open, Placebo Controlled First-in-human Trial To Evaluate The Safety,Tolerability, Pharmacokinetics And Pharmacodynamics Of Pf-06751979 After Oral Administration Of Single And Multiple Ascending Doses To Healthy Adult And Elderly Subjects
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 55
Topic: beta-alanine

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to evaluate the safety, tolerability, PK and PD of PF-06751979 following oral doses in healthy adult and healthy elderly subjects.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Number of Participants With Treatment-Emergent Adverse Events (AEs) and Serious Adverse Events (SAEs)
  - Number of Participants With Abnormal Physical Examinations Findings
  - Number of Participants With Abnormal Neurological Examinations Findings
  - Part blast and cruise: Number of Participants With Positive Response on Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) at Baseline
  - Part blast and cruise: Number of Participants With Positive Response on Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) on Day 7

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: PF-06751979 single ascending dose
  - DRUG: Placebo single dose
  - DRUG: PF-06751979 multiple ascending dose
  - DRUG: Placebo multiple dose
  - DRUG: PF-06751979 multiple dose

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 85 Years
Sex: ALL

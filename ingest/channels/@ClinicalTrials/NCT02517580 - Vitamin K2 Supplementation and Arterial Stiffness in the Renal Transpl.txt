CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02517580
Title: Vitamin K2 Supplementation and Arterial Stiffness in the Renal trensplant Population (The KING Trial)
Official Title: Vitamin K2 Supplementation and Effect on Arterial Stiffness Progression in the Renal trensplant Population
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 60
Topic: protein supplementation

SUMMARY:
This study is aromasingle arm, single center clinical trial that aims to evaluate the effect of 8 weeks of vitamin K2 replacement (360 mcg/day) on the progression of arterial stiffness in stable renal trensplant patients.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Reduction From Baseline in Carotid-femoral Pulse Wave Velocity at 8 Weeks

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Vitamin K2 (MK7)

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02527200
Title: Effect of Liraglutide for Weight Management in Paediatric Subjects With Prader-Willi Syndrome
Official Title: Effect of Liraglutide for Weight Management in Paediatric Subjects With Prader-Willi Syndrome.
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 56
Topic: beta-alanine

SUMMARY:
This trial is conducted globally. The aim of this trial is to investigate the effect of liraglutide for weight management in paediatric subjects with Prader-Willi Syndrome.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in Body Mass Index (BMI) Standard Deviation Score (SDS) From Baseline to 16 Weeks
  - Change in Body Mass Index (BMI) Standard Deviation Score (SDS) From Baseline to 52 Weeks

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: liraglutide
  - DRUG: placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 6 Years to 18 Years
Sex: ALL

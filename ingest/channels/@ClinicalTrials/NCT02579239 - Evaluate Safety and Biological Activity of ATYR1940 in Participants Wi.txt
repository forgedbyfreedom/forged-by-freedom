CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02579239
Title: Evaluate Safety and Biological Activity of ATYR1940 in Participants With Limb Girdle Muscular Dystrophy 2B (LGMD2B) and Facioscapulohumeral Muscular Dystrophy (FSHD)
Official Title: An Open-Label, Intrapatient Dose Escalation Study to Evaluate the Safety, Tolerability, Immunogenicity, and Biological Activity of ATYR1940 in Patients With Limb Girdle and Facioscapulohumeral Muscular Dystrophies
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1, PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 18
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to assess the safety and biological activity of ATYR1940 in participants with LGMD2B and FSHD.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Number of Participants With Treatment Emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs) and Serious Adverse Events (SAEs)
  - Number of Participants With a Clinically Significant Electrocardiogram (ECG) Abnormality Leading to a TEAE
  - Number of Participants With a Clinically Significant Pulmonary Function Event Resulting in a TEAE
  - Number of Participants With a Clinical Laboratory Abnormality Leading to an AE
  - Number of Participants With Vital Sign Abnormality Resulting in a TEAE

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BIOLOGICAL: ATYR1940
  - BIOLOGICAL: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 75 Years
Sex: ALL

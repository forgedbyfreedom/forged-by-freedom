CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02581345
Title: Phase 3 Study of M923 and Humira® in Subjects With Chronic Plaque-type Psoriasis
Official Title: A Phase 3 Randomized, Double-blind, Multicenter Study to Evaluate Efficacy, Safety, and Immunogenicity of an Adalimumab Biosimilar (M923) and Humira® in Subjects With Moderate to Severe Chronic Plaque-type Psoriasis
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 572
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
The purpose of the study is to evaluate efficacy, safety, and immunogenicity of a proposed adalimumab biosimilar (M923) and Humira in participants with moderate to severe chronic plaque-type psoriasis.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Percentage of Participants Who Achieved a 75% Reduction in Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI) (PASI 75) Scores at Week 16

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BIOLOGICAL: M923
  - BIOLOGICAL: Humira

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02600702
Title: A Study of Siriraj Home Exercises Protocol for Primary Osteoarthritis of the Knee
Official Title: A Randomized Control Study of Siriraj Home Exercises Protocol for Primary Knee Osteoarthritis Patients Versus Usual Orthopedic Outpatient Consultation in Primary Knee Osteoarthritis Patients
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 135
Topic: knee osteoarthritis exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to compare the exercise protocol called "Siriraj home base exercise program for OA knee" to usual care and physical modalities for conservative treatment in patients with 1st OA knee.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - WOMAC score

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Siriraj knee exercise
  - OTHER: Physical modalities

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 50 Years to 
Sex: ALL

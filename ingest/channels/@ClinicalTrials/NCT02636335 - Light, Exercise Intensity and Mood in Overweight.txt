CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02636335
Title: Light, Exercise Intensity and Mood in Overweight
Official Title: Light, Exercise Intensity and Mood in Overweight
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 26
Topic: sports nutrition

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to determine if an exposure to light in the morning has a positive influence on self-chosen exercise intensity on a bicycle ergometer in overweight and obese patients.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Mean power output in watts

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DEVICE: Phillips Energy Light HF3319

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 40 Years
Sex: ALL

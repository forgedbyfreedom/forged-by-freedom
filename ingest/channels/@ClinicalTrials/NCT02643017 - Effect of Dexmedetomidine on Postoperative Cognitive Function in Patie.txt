CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02643017
Title: Effect of Dexmedetomidine on Postoperative Cognitive Function in Patients Undergoing Shoulder Arthroscopy With Beach Chair Position Under Desflurane Anesthesia
Official Title: 
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 80
Topic: tendon injury

SUMMARY:
Investigate the effects of dexmedetomidine on postoperative cognitive function in patients undergoing shoulder arthroscopy with beach chair position under desflurane anesthesia.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Number of patients with postoperative cognitive decline assessed by mini-mental state examination
  - Number of patients with postoperative cognitive decline assessed by a battery of psychometric test

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Dexmedetomidine
  - DRUG: normal saline

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 20 Years to 80 Years
Sex: ALL

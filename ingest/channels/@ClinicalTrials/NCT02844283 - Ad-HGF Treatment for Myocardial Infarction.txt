CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02844283
Title: Ad-HGF Treatment for Myocardial Infarction
Official Title: Adenovirus Carrying Hepatocyte Growth Factor (Ad-HGF) Treatment for Myocardial Infarction
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: PHASE1, PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 100
Topic: physical activity intervention

SUMMARY:
This will be the first clinical trial use Ad-HGF gene for the treatment of myocardial infarction disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - changes of cardiac left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF, %)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Ad-HGF
  - OTHER: 0.9% NaCl

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 30 Years to 80 Years
Sex: ALL

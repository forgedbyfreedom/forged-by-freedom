CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02869022
Title: Study of Organ Perfusion With Custodiol-N Solution Compared With Custodiol Solution in Heart Transplantation
Official Title: A Prospective, Randomized, Single Blind, Multicenter Phase III Study of Organ Perfusion With Custodiol-N Solution Compared With Custodiol Solution in Heart Transplantation
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 105
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
The objective of this investigation is to demonstrate non-inferiority in outcome of Custodiol-N against Custodiol in heart transplantation.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Creatine kinase Myocard type "CK-MB" peak value

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Custodiol-N
  - DRUG: Custodiol
  - PROCEDURE: organ perfusion for heart transplantation

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02911389
Title: Home PT vs FORCE PT
Official Title: Unsupervised Home Exercise With and Without a Web-based Recovery Platform as Compared to Traditional Outpatient Physiotherapy After Total Knee Arthroplasty: A Prospective, Randomized Controlled Trial
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 
Topic: recovery exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to determine if unsupervised, home rehabilitation delivered by a web-based platform or paper manual is a safe and effective alternative to outpatient physical therapy after total knee replacement.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Knee Range of Motion

INTERVENTIONS:

  - OTHER: Home PT via paper manual
  - OTHER: outpatient PT

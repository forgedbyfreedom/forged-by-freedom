CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT02969018
Title: Study To Evaluate Safety And Efficacy Of PF-06700841 In Subjects With Moderate To Severe Plaque Psoriasis
Official Title: A PHASE 2A, RANDOMIZED, DOUBLE-BLIND, PLACEBO-CONTROLLED STUDY TO EVALUATE SAFETY AND EFFICACY OF PF-06700841 IN SUBJECTS WITH MODERATE TO SEVERE PLAQUE PSORIASIS
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 212
Topic: beta-alanine

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to determine whether PF-06700841 is safe and effective in the treatment of chronic plaque psoriasis.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change From Baseline in Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI) Score at Week 12

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: PF-06700841
  - OTHER: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 75 Years
Sex: ALL

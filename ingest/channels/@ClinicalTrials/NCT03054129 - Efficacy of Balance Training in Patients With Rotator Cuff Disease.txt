CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03054129
Title: Efficacy of Balance Training in Patients With Rotator Cuff Disease
Official Title: Efficacy of Balance Training in Patients With Rotator Cuff Disease: A Randomized Controlled Trial
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 42
Topic: tendon injury

SUMMARY:
The purpose of the study is to determine whether balance training is effective in patients with rotator cuff disease.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Postural Control-Stability Index in Solid Surface - Eyes Open
  - Postural Control-Stability Index in Solid Surface - Eyes Closed
  - Postural Control-Stability Index in Standing on Pillows - Eyes Open
  - Postural Control-Stability Index in Standing on Pillows - Eyes Closed
  - Postural Control-Stability Index in Head Turned Right 45° - Eyes Closed

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Rehabilitation and balance training
  - OTHER: Rehabilitation program

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 70 Years
Sex: ALL

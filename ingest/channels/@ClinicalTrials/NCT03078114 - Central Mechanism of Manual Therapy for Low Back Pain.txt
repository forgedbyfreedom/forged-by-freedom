CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03078114
Title: Central Mechanism of Manual Therapy for Low Back Pain
Official Title: Central Mechanism of Manual Therapy for Low Back Pain
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 29
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to examine the change of nervous system activity before and after spinal manipulation treatment program in people with low back pain.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in Pain Threshold measured using Pain Pressure Threshold (PPT) testing

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Spinal Manipulation
  - OTHER: Placebo Spinal Manipulation

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 60 Years
Sex: ALL

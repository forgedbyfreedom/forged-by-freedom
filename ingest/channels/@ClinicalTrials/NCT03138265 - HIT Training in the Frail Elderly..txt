CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03138265
Title: HIT trening in the Frail Elderly.
Official Title: Improvements in Aerobic Performance Following a Four-week Period of High Intensity Interval trening in the Elderly.
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 32
Topic: high intensity interval trening

SUMMARY:
This pilot study aims to investigate whether high intensity interval trening can result in rapid improvements in physical fitness amongst the frail elderly (over 70 years old). Participants will undergo a wide range of physical fitness measures followed by a four week exercise protocol. Determination of improvement will be by repeated testing of the physical measures taken for baseline. The primary outcome measure will be anaerobic threshold. There will also be a subgroup of participants who will undergo muscle biopsy and D2O ingestion to allow an insight into the mechanistic basis behind exercise trening response in this age group.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Mean anaerobic threshold improvement following 4 week HIT protocol.

INTERVENTIONS:

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03172494
Title: A Trial Comparing insulin Degludec/Liraglutide, insulin Degludec, and Liraglutide in Chinese Subjects With Type 2 Diabetes Inadequately Controlled on Oral Antidiabetic Drugs (OADs)
Official Title: A Trial Comparing the Efficacy and Safety of insulin Degludec/Liraglutide, insulin Degludec, and Liraglutide in Chinese Subjects With Type 2 Diabetes Inadequately Controlled on Oral Antidiabetic Drugs (OADs)
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 720
Topic: beta-alanine

SUMMARY:
This trial is conducted in Asia. The aim of this trial is to confirm the efficacy of insulin degludec/liraglutide in controlling glycaemia in Chinese subjects with type 2 diabetes mellitus inadequately controlled on oral antidiabetic agents

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in HbA1c

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: insulin degludec/liraglutide
  - DRUG: insulin degludec
  - DRUG: Liraglutide

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03347188
Title: A Study to Test if Fremanezumab Reduces Headache in Participants With Posttraumatic Headache (PTH)
Official Title: A Phase 2, Multicenter, Randomized, Proof-of-Concept, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Parallel-Group Study, Including an Open-Label Period, Evaluating the Efficacy and Safety of 1 Subcutaneous Dose Regimen of Fremanezumab for the Treatment of Posttraumatic Headache (PTH)
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 87
Topic: vitamin D muscle

SUMMARY:
This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study to evaluate the safety and efficacy of fremanezumab in adult participants aged 18 to 70 years, inclusive, for the prevention of PTH. The study will include a double-blind (DB) treatment period (12 weeks) and an open-label (OL) treatment period (12 weeks).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - DB Period: Mean Change From Baseline in Monthly Average Number of Headache Days of at Least Moderate Severity During the 12-Week Treatment Period After the First Dose of Fremanezumab

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Fremanezumab
  - DRUG: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 70 Years
Sex: ALL

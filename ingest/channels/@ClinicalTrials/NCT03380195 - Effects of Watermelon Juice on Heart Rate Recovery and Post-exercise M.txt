CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03380195
Title: Effects of Watermelon Juice on Heart Rate Recovery and Post-exercise Muscle Soreness
Official Title: Effects of Watermelon Juice on Heart Rate Recovery and Post-exercise Muscle Soreness
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 27
Topic: recovery exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study was to determine the effects of a pre-exercise, single practical dose of watermelon juice, Gatorade, sugar water or water on heart rate recovery, blood lactate levels, and 24-hour post exercise muscle soreness.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change of heart rate after submax exercise

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Watermelon juice
  - OTHER: Sport drink
  - OTHER: Sugar water
  - OTHER: Water

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 21 Years to 50 Years
Sex: ALL

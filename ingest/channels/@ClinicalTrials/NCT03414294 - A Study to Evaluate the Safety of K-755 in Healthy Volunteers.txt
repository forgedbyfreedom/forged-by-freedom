CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03414294
Title: A Study to Evaluate the Safety of K-755 in Healthy Volunteers
Official Title: A Phase I Single-Center, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Combined Single Ascending Dose, Multiple Ascending Dose, and Food Effect Study to Investigate the Safety and Tolerability, Pharmacokinetics, and Pharmacodynamics of K-755 in Healthy Adult Volunteers
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 121
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
This is a Phase 1 study designed to explore the safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetics of K-755 following oral administration to healthy male and female volunteers.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Part A, B, C, D and E: Incidence and severity of Adverse Events
  - Part A, B, C, D and E: Number of subjects with clinical laboratory test abnormalities
  - Part A, B, C, D and E: Number of subjects with vital signs abnormalities
  - Part A, B, C, D and E: Number of subjects with clinically significant change in body weight
  - Part A, B, C, D and E: Number of subjects with abnormal findings in physical examinations

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: K-755 Part A (SAD)
  - DRUG: Placebo Part A (SAD)
  - DRUG: K-755 Part B (MAD)
  - DRUG: Placebo Part B (MAD)
  - DRUG: K-755 Part C (FE)

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 45 Years
Sex: ALL

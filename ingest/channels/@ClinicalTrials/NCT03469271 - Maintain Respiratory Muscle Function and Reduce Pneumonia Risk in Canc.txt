CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03469271
Title: Maintain Respiratory Muscle Function and Reduce Pneumonia Risk in Cancer Patients
Official Title: Resistance Training and 1,25 (OH)2D3 Administration to Maintain Respiratory Muscle Function and Reduce Pneumonia Risk in Cancer Patients
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE4
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 63
Topic: vitamin D muscle

SUMMARY:
This research is intended to begin to explore the impact of inspiratory muscle resistance exercise and/or 1,25(OH)2D3 for improving respiratory muscle strength in cancer patients (subjects).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Sniff Nasal Inspiratory Pressure (SNIP)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: 1,25 (OH)2 D3
  - OTHER: Inspiratory isometric resistance training
  - DRUG: Oral Placebo
  - OTHER: Sham Inspiratory Isometric Resistance Training
  - DEVICE: Threshold Inspiratory Muscle Trainer (IMT)

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

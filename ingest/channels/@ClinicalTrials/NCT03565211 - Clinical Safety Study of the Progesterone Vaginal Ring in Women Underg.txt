CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03565211
Title: Clinical Safety Study of the Progesterone Vaginal Ring in Women Undergoing Assisted Reproductive Technology Procedures
Official Title: A Prospective, Multi-Center, Non-Comparative Trial of the Clinical Safety of the Progesterone Vaginal Ring in Women Undergoing Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART) Procedures
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 352
Topic: beta-alanine

SUMMARY:
The purpose of the study is to assess the safety of Progesterone Vaginal Ring (PVR) in women undergoing fresh embryo trensfer (ART).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Cumulative Rate of Spontaneous Abortions Occurring on or Before 12 Weeks Post-oocyte Retrieval

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Progesterone vaginal ring

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 34 Years
Sex: FEMALE

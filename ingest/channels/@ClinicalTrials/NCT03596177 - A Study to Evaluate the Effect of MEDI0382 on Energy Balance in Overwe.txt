CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03596177
Title: A Study to Evaluate the Effect of MEDI0382 on Energy Balance in Overweight and Obese Participants With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
Official Title: An Exploratory Phase 2a, Randomised, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Study to Evaluate the Effect of MEDI0382 on Energy Balance in Overweight and Obese Subjects With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 28
Topic: caffeine exercise

SUMMARY:
An exploratory study to evaluate the effect of MEDI0382 on energy balance in overweight and obese participants with type 2 diabetes mellitus

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
An exploratory Phase 2a, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study to evaluate the effect of MEDI0382 on energy balance in overweight and obese participants with type 2 diabetes mellitus

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Percent Change in Body Weight From Baseline to Day 59

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: MEDI0382
  - DRUG: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 30 Years to 75 Years
Sex: ALL

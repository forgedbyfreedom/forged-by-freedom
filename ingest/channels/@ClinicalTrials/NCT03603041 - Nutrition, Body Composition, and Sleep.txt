CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03603041
Title: Nutrition, Body Composition, and Sleep
Official Title: The Effect of Protein and Omega-3 Fatty Acid Supplementation on Body Composition, Sleep, Cardiometabolic Health and Strength in Postmenopausal Women
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 45
Topic: omega 3 exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of the research is to determine if protein and omega-3 fatty acid supplementation improve sleep, improve body composition, and improve markers of metabolic health in postmenopausal women.

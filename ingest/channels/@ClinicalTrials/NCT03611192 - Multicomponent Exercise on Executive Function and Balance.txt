CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03611192
Title: Multicomponent Exercise on Executive Function and Balance
Official Title: Effects of A Multicomponent Exercise on Executive Function and Balance in Pre-frail Older Adults
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 30
Topic: aerobic exercise

SUMMARY:
The present study is to examine the effects of a multicomponent exercise on executive function and balance in pre-frail older adults.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Trail-Making test
  - Stroop test
  - Digit span test
  - Mini-Balance Evaluation Systems Test
  - Time Up & Go test

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Multicomponent exercise
  - OTHER: Home-program exercise

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 65 Years to 100 Years
Sex: ALL

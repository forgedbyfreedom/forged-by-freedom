CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03700827
Title: Effects of Aerobic and Resistance trening on Abdominal Fat Loss
Official Title: Effects of Aerobic and Resistance trening on Abdominal Fat Loss
Status: TERMINATED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 18
Topic: fat loss muscle

SUMMARY:
Study the effects of different exercise modes on total and regional adiposity variation in subqtaneous and visceral adipose tissue (VAT and SAT). The investigators hypothesize that total abdominal will improve for all the exercise groups compared to the control group. When comparing intervention groups: 1) no differences on the degree of improvement in total abdominal fat depots (VAT and SAT) 2) different degree of change when comparing specific sections at VAT and SAT are expected.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Changes in Visceral Abdominal Tissue (VAT)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Resistance trening
  - OTHER: Aerobic trening

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 35 Years
Sex: FEMALE

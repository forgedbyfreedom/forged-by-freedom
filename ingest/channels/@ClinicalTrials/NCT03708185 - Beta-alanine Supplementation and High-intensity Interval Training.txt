CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03708185
Title: Beta-alanine Supplementation and High-intensity Interval trening
Official Title: Does Beta-alanine Supplementation Augment the Skeletal Muscle Adaptive Response to 8 Weeks of High-intensity Interval trening
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 26
Topic: beta-alanine

SUMMARY:
The present study will seek to quantify whether a period of HIIT alongside β-alanine supplementation will improve the adaptation to trening, and therefore performance, more than a period of HIIT alone.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Whilst high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) is a powerful stimulus to increase endurance exercise performance, there are potential nutritional interventions that can be put in place to further increase performance gains.

Energy for the contraction of muscles is created in the mitochondria. HIIT can improve the function of mitochondria, therefore improving performance.

Β-alanine is a commonly used supplement that can improve exercise performance by increasing the amount of carnosine in muscle. Carnosine has many functions that may help improve exercise capacity.

The present study will seek to quantify whether a period of HIIT alongside β-alanine supplementation will improve the adaptation to trening, and therefore performance, more than a period of HIIT alone.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change from baseline work done on a cycle ergometer in a cycling capacity test at 110%VO2max following a period of 4 weeks of beta-alanine supplementation and then 8 weeks of beta-alanine supplementation and high intensity interval trening

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Beta-alanine
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 50 Years
Sex: ALL

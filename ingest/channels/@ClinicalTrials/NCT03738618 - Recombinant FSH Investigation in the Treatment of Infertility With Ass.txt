CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03738618
Title: Recombinant FSH Investigation in the Treatment of Infertility With Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART) (RITA-2)
Official Title: A Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled, Parallel Groups, Multicenter Trial Investigating the Efficacy and Safety of FE 999049 in Controlled Ovarian Stimulation in Women Aged 35-42 Years Undergoing Assisted Reproductive Technology
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 588
Topic: beta-alanine

SUMMARY:
This trial investigates the effects of FE 999049 compared to placebo.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Cumulative Ongoing Pregnancy Rate After the Fresh Cycle and Cryopreserved Cycles Initiated Within 12 Months From the Start of Controlled Ovarian Stimulation (COS)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Follitropin delta
  - DRUG: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 35 Years to 42 Years
Sex: FEMALE

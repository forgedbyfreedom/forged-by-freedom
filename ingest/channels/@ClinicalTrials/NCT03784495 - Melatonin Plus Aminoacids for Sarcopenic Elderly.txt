CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03784495
Title: Melatonin Plus Aminoacids for Sarcopenic Elderly
Official Title: Is a Combination of Melatonin and Aminoacids Useful to Sarcopenic Elderly Patients? A Randomized Trial
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 159
Topic: fat loss muscle

SUMMARY:
To evaluate the effectiveness of melatonin and essential aminoacid supplementation on body composition, protein metabolism, strength and inflammation. The investigators performed a Randomized controlled parallel groups preliminary trial in 159 elderly sarcopenic people (42/117 men/women) assigned to 4 groups: isocaloric placebo (P, n=44), melatonin (M, 1 mg/daily, n=42,), essential aminoacids (EAAs4 g/daily, n=40) or EAAsplus melatonin (eAAM, 4 g EAAsand 1 mg melatonin/daily, n= 30). The period of intervention was 4 weeks. Data from body composition (DXA), strength (handgrip test) and biochemical parameters for the assessment of protein metabolism (albumin) and inflammation (PRC) were collected at baseline and after the 4-week intervention

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in Total Fat Mass and Total Free Fat Mass (DXA)
  - Change in Handgrip Strenght

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Melatonin 1mg
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Essential Aminoacids (EAAs)
  - COMBINATION_PRODUCT: Essential Aminoacids + melatonin
  - OTHER: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 65 Years to 
Sex: ALL

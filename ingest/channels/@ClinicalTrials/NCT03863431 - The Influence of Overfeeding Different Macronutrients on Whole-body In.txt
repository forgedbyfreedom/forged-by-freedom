CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03863431
Title: The Influence of Overfeeding Different Macronutrients on Whole-body insulin Sensitivity
Official Title: The Influence of Overfeeding Different Macronutrients on Whole-body insulin Sensitivity
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 20
Topic: exercise intervention diabetes

SUMMARY:
This study will investigate the effect of high-carbohydrate vs. high-fat overfeeding (130% of energy requirements) on whole body insulin sensitivity.

Following habitual diet, participants will be randomly allocated to either a high-carbohydrate or a high-fat diet intervention for 14-days. On days 0, 7 and 14 participants will undergo anthropometric and metabolic testing (primarily an oral glucose tolerance test \[OGTT\]).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Whole-body insulin sensitivity index

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: High-fat diet
  - OTHER: High-carbohydrate diet

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 40 Years
Sex: ALL

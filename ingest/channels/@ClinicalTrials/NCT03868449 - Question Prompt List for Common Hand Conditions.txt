CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03868449
Title: Question Prompt List for Common Hand Conditions
Official Title: Does Giving a Question Prompt List to Patients With Common Hand Conditions Improve Their Perceived Involvement in Care? A Randomized Controlled Trial
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 126
Topic: tendon injury

SUMMARY:
Patients with common hand conditions will be randomized to one of two groups- one will receive a question prompt list, the other will receive a list of 3 questions

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Perceived Involvement in Care scale

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Question Prompt List
  - OTHER: 3 questions

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

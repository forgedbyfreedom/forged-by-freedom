CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT03871439
Title: OPEN LABEL, 2-PERIOD STUDY, TO COMPARE BIO-AVAILABILITY OF 2 DIFFERENT FORMULATIONS OF PF-05221304 IN HEALTHY ADULTS
Official Title: A PHASE 1, OPEN LABEL, TWO-PERIOD, RANDOMIZED CROSSOVER STUDY TO COMPARE THE PHARMACOKINETICS OF TWO DIFFERENT FORMULATIONS OF PF-05221304 ADMINISTERED ORALLY TO HEALTHY ADULT SUBJECTS
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 10
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to characterize the plasma pharmacokinetics of two formulations of PF 05221304 in healthy adults.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Plasma PF-05221304 AUClast
  - Plasma PF-05221304 Cmax

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: PF-05221304 Formulation A
  - DRUG: PF-05221304 Formulation B

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 55 Years
Sex: ALL

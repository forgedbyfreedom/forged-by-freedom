CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04048096
Title: Krill Oil and Muscle in Older Adults
Official Title: Effects of Krill Oil Supplementation on Muscle Function in Older Adults
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 102
Topic: exercise muscle mass

SUMMARY:
The aim of this study is to determine the effects of krill oil supplementation on muscle mass in function in older adults. Half of the participants will receive krill oil supplements and the other half a placebo.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in Muscle strength

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Krill Oil
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 65 Years to 
Sex: ALL

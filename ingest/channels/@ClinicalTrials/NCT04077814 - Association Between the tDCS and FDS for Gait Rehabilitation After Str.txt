CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04077814
Title: Association Between the tDCS and FDS for Gait Rehabilitation After Stroke
Official Title: Association Between the Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation (tDCS) and Foot Drop Stimulation (FDS) for Gait Rehabilitation After Stroke - A Randomized Clinical Trial
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 40
Topic: muscle rehabilitation

SUMMARY:
The aim of this study is to verify the effectiveness of tDCS combined with foot drop stimulation (FDS) on gait rehabilitation of post-stroke subjects with mild and moderate compromise.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Kinematic gait anaysis.

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DEVICE: Foot Drop Stimulatio - Neuro Orthosis
  - DEVICE: Transcranial direct brain stimulation

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 80 Years
Sex: ALL

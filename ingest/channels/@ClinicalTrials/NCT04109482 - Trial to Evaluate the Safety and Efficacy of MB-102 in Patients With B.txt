CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04109482
Title: Trial to Evaluate the Safety and Efficacy of MB-102 in Patients With BPDCN.
Official Title: A Phase 1/2, Open Label, Multicenter Trial to Assess the Safety and Efficacy of MB-102 in Patients With Relapsed or Refractory Blastic Plasmacytoid Dendritic Cell Neoplasm
Status: TERMINATED
Phase: PHASE1, PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 3
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
A phase 1/2 study to assess the safety and efficacy of MB-102 in patients with relapsed or refractory BPDCN

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Phase 1 portion of the study will determine the maximum tolerated dose of MB-102.

The Phase 2 portion of the trial will evaluate the efficacy of MB-102 in relapsed or refractory BPDCN.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Phase 1: Safety and Tolerability as measured by the number of patients with treatment related adverse events
  - Phase 1: Maximum Tolerated Dose (MTD) and recommended Phase 2 dose
  - Phase 2: Response Rate of patients with BPDCN

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BIOLOGICAL: MB-102
  - DRUG: Fludarabine
  - DRUG: Cyclophosphamide

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

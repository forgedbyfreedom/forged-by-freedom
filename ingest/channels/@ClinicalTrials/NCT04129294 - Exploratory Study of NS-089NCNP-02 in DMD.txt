CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04129294
Title: Exploratory Study of NS-089/NCNP-02 in DMD
Official Title: Exploratory Study of NS-089/NCNP-02 in Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1, PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 6
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
This study is designed to assess the safety, tolerability, efficacy and pharmacokinetics (PK) of NS-089/NCNP-02 in subjects diagnosed with Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD), and to determine the dosage for subsequent studies.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Adverse event and adverse drug reaction [Safety and Tolerability]

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: NS-089/NCNP-02

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 4 Years to 17 Years
Sex: MALE

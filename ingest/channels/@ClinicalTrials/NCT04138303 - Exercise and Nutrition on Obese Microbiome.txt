CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04138303
Title: Exercise and Nutrition on Obese Microbiome
Official Title: The Effects of a Calorie-Restrictive Low-Carbohydrate Diet Versus an Unrestrictive Ancestral Diet on Microbiota and Cardiometabolic Disease Markers of Obese Individuals Performing an Intense Exercise Program
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 31
Topic: resistance training

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to examine the effects nine weeks of intense exercise training will have on weight, inflammation, and intestinal bacteria composition of overweight and obese adults.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in fecal microbiota diversity
  - Change in Bacteriodetes/Firmicutes ratio
  - Percent change in fecal microbiota composition
  - Change in inflammatory markers
  - Change in leptin/adiponectin ratio

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Exercise Protocol
  - OTHER: Calorie-Restrictive Low Carbohydrate Diet (CR-LC)
  - OTHER: Ancestral Diet (AD)

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

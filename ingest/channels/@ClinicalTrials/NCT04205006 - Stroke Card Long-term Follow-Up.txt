CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04205006
Title: Stroke Card Long-term Follow-Up
Official Title: Long-term Outcome of a Pragmatic Trial of Multifaceted Intervention (STROKE-CARD Care) to Reduce Cardiovascular Risk and Improve Quality-of-life After Ischemic Stroke and Transient Ischemic Attack
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 1000
Topic: exercise intervention diabetes

SUMMARY:
We undertook the STROKE-CARD trial (NCT02156778) between 2014 and 2018 with follow-up until 2019 to evaluate the efficacy of the Post-Stroke disease-management program STROKE-CARD care. To further investigate the long-term efficacy of STROKE-CARD care all participants of the original trial will be invited for a Long-term in Person follow-up (3 - 6 years).

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04213820
Title: TMS and Body Image Treatment for Anorexia Nervosa
Official Title: TMS Treatment for Anorexia Nervosa: Effects on Body Image Processing and Clinical Outcome Measures
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 40
Topic: exercise intervention obesity

SUMMARY:
To evaluate if TMS together with a body image intervention is an efficacious treatment for anorexia nervosa compared to treatment as usual.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Eating Disorder Examination Questionnaire (EDE-Q)
  - Body Attitude Test (BAT)
  - Body Shape Questionnaire (BSQ)
  - Figure Rating Scale
  - Body Mass Index (BMI)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DEVICE: Transcranial magnetic stimulation
  - BEHAVIORAL: Body Image Intervention
  - BEHAVIORAL: Treatment as usual

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 35 Years
Sex: FEMALE

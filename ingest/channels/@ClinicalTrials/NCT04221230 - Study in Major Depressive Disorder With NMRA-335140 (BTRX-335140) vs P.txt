CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04221230
Title: Study in Major Depressive Disorder With NMRA-335140 (BTRX-335140) vs Placebo
Official Title: A Phase 2a, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Proof of Concept Study to Evaluate the Effects of Oral BTRX-335140 (NMRA-335140) Versus Placebo in Subjects With Major Depressive Disorder
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 204
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
A proof of concept (POC) study evaluating the impact of NMRA-335140 (BTRX-335140) relative to placebo on symptoms of major depressive disorder (MDD) in adult participants with MDD and symptoms of anhedonia and anxiety following 8 weeks of double-blind treatment as assessed by the HAMD-17 Scale.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change From Baseline in Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HAMD-17) Total Score at Weeks 8

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: NMRA-335140
  - DRUG: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04303481
Title: The Effect of Consuming Enzymes and Prebiotics on Body Composition and Blood Biochemical Index
Official Title: The Effect of Consuming Enzymes and Prebiotics on Body Composition and Blood Biochemical Index
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 88
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
To assess the effects of consuming enzymes and prebiotics on improvement of weight loss and biochemical parameters

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - The change of body fat mass
  - The change of Triglyceride
  - The change of Total cholestrol
  - The change of HDL-cholestrol
  - The change of LDL-cholestrol

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: normal diet
  - OTHER: weight loss program kit
  - OTHER: weight loss program kit with A. muciniphila prebiotics

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 20 Years to 50 Years
Sex: ALL

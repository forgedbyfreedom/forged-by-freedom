CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04421937
Title: The Effects of Neuromuscular Electrical Stimulation on Swallowing Functions in Stroke Patients With Dysphagia
Official Title: The Effects of Neuromuscular Electrical Stimulation on Swallowing Functions in Stroke Patients With Dysphagia: Randomized Controlled Trial
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 34
Topic: resistance training

SUMMARY:
In our study, our aim is to evaluate the effect of neuromuscular electrical stimulation added to traditional dysphagia therapy in stroke patients with dysphagia, on functional oral intake status, dysphagia-related symptoms, quality of life, and complications related to dysphagia.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in Functional Oral Intake Scale (FOIS)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: Procedure: Traditional Dysphagia Therapy (TDT)
  - DEVICE: Procedure: Neuromuscular Electrical Stimulation with TDT

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04449016
Title: Effect of Ethnicity on Changes in VO2max and Cardiac Output in Response to Short-Term High Intensity Interval trening
Official Title: Effect of Ethnicity on Changes in VO2max and Cardiac Output in Response to Short-Term High Intensity Interval trening
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 22
Topic: high intensity interval trening

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study was to determine if ethnicity alters trening responsiveness to a low dose of high intensity interval trening in inactive women.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Prior to the session, women completed a 4-minute warmup at 10 % PPO. Each subject completed 9 sessions of HIIT over a 3-week period which consisted of eight to ten 1-minute bouts of cycling at 85% PPO with 75-second recovery between bouts at 10 % PPO. This protocol is well-tolerated in inactive individuals. Continuously during each session, heart rate was monitored using telemetry. On days 1, 3, 6, and 9 of trening, the physical activity enjoyment scale (PACES) was completed. VO2max and cardiac output max were assessed prior to trening, as well as post.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in baseline in VO2max at 3 weeks
  - Change in baseline in cardiac output max at 3 weeks

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: High intensity interval trening

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 45 Years
Sex: FEMALE

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04496895
Title: The Evaluation of Orange Peel Fermentation on Body Fat Lowering Efficacy in Adults
Official Title: The Evaluation of Orange Peel Fermentation on Body Fat Lowering Efficacy in Adults
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 124
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
To assess whether orange peel fermentation has the effect of reducing body fat in adults

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This is a double-blind and randomized study. Subjects are informed to consume the samples every day for 2 months. The clinical diagnosis item of overweight or fatty liver is evaluated by the doctor. ThEQuestionnaires are collected at every visit of the trial.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - The change of body fat mass
  - The change of Triglyceride
  - The change of Total cholestrol
  - The change of HDL-cholestrol
  - The change of LDL-cholestrol

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: orange peel fermentation

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 20 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04542655
Title: The Effect of Exercise on Serum Levels of Collagen and Proteoglycan in Patients With Knee Osteoarthritis (EFEX-OA-01)
Official Title: An Open-label, Single-center, Randomized, Controlled, Exploratory Study to Investigate the Effect of Weight Bearing Versus Non-weight Bearing Exercise on Collagen and Proteoglycan in Serum and Urine in Patients With Osteoarthritis and Healthy Subjects
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 40
Topic: knee osteoarthritis exercise

SUMMARY:
Aim of EFEX-OA-01: To explore how running, cycling and resting acutely influence levels of collagen and proteoglycan in patients with osteoarthritis knee and in young healthy subjects, as reflected by changes in serum and urine biochemical markers originating from joint related collagens and proteoglycans.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Changes in biomarkers (exploratory)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Ergometer Cycling followed by treadmill running
  - OTHER: Treadmill Running followed by ergometer cycling
  - OTHER: Rest

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 75 Years
Sex: ALL

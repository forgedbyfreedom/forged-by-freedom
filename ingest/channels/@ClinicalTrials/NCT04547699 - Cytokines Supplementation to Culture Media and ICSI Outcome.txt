CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04547699
Title: Cytokines Supplementation to Culture Media and ICSI Outcome
Official Title: Impact of Cytokine Supplementation With Various Protein Concentrations Into humanin Vitro Culture Medium on ICSI Cycle Outcome
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 700
Topic: protein supplementation

SUMMARY:
A randomized clinical trial to evaluate the effect of cytokine supplementation in embryo culture medium with various protein concentrations aiming to evaluate the effects of cytokines (CYK) integration into the humanin vitro culture medium with high and low human serum albumin (HSA) concentrations on live birth rates after intracytoplasmic sperm injection (ICSI).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Oocytes were injected, and embryos cultured and trensferred in three supplemented culture media.

A- aromasingle step culture medium (SSCM; Global, Life Global)+5mg/ml (10% Vol/Vol), life global protein, without supplementation of CYK (control n=200), B- aromasingle step culture medium (SSCM; Global, Life Global)+5mg/ml (10% Vol/Vol), life global protein, with 2ng/mL GM-CSF (granulocyte macrophage colony stimulating factor) (G5035 Sigma), 5ng/mL HB-EGF (Heparine Binding Epidermal growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone Factor )(E4643 Sigma),and 5ng/mL LIF (Lukemia Inhbitory Factor) (SRP9001 Sigma)(n=200).

C- aromasingle step culture medium (SSCM; Global, Life Global) +2mg/ml (5% Vol/Vol) life global protein, with 2ng/mL GM-CSF (G5035 Sigma), 5ng/mL HB-EGF (E4643 Sigma), and 5ng/mL LIF (SRP9001 Sigma).(n=200) Setting :- - Mansoura Integrated Fertility

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - live birth rate

INTERVENTIONS:
  - COMBINATION_PRODUCT: aromasingle step culture medium

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 38 Years
Sex: FEMALE

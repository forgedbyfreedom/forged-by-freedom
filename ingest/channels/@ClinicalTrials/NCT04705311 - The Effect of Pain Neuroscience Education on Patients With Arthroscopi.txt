CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04705311
Title: The Effect of Pain Neuroscience Education on Patients With Arthroscopic Rotator Cuff Repair
Official Title: The Effect of Pain Neuroscience Education on Patients With Arthroscopic Rotator Cuff Repair
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 34
Topic: tendon injury

SUMMARY:
During the rehabilitation process after rotator cuff repair surgery, patients suffer from increased pain and discomfort due to dysfunction. Pain neuroscience education, a more modern educational method, has been reported to be effective in controlling pain by reducing the fear of movement based on an understanding of neurophysiology.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Pain intensity

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Pain neuroscience education
  - BEHAVIORAL: Rotator cuff repair rehabilitation

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 19 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04722354
Title: Abscisic Acid Effects on Glucose Homeostasis and insulin Sensitivity
Official Title: Abscisic Acid Effects on Glucose Homeostasis and insulin Sensitivity
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 5
Topic: caffeine exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to determine the effects of oral abscisic acid (ABA) on glucose metabolism in subjects with defined prediabetes.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Monitoring Adverse Events
  - insulin Sensitivity

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Hyperinsulemic euglycemic clamp
  - PROCEDURE: Muscle Biopsy
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Oral abscisic acid (ABA)
  - OTHER: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

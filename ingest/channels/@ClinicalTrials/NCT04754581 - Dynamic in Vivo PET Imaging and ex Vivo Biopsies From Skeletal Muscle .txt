CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04754581
Title: Dynamic in Vivo PET Imaging and ex Vivo Biopsies From Skeletal Muscle and Adipose Tissue to Investigate the Effects of Exercise on insulin Resistance and Mitochondrial Energetics in Type 2 Diabetes
Official Title: Dynamic in Vivo PET Imaging and ex Vivo Biopsies From Skeletal Muscle and Adipose Tissue to Investigate the Effects of Exercise on insulin Resistance and Mitochondrial Energetics in Type 2 Diabetes
Status: ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 39
Topic: exercise intervention diabetes

SUMMARY:
The overall aim of this pilot study is to investigate the effects of exercise training on skeletal muscle and adipose tissue insulin resistance in subjects with Type 2 Diabetes (T2D).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Individual steps of muscle glucose uptake (pre-exercise)
  - Individual steps of muscle glucose uptake (post-exercise)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Maximal ATP Production (ATPmax)
  - OTHER: Exercise Testing (VO2max)
  - PROCEDURE: Muscle Biopsy
  - PROCEDURE: Adipose Tissue Biopsy
  - OTHER: Hyperinsulinemic euglycemic clamp

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 30 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

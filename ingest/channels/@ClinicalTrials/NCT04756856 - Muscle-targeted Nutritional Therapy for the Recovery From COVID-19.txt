CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04756856
Title: Muscle-targeted Nutritional Therapy for the Recovery From COVID-19
Official Title: Impact of a Muscle-targeted Nutritional Therapy in the Recovery of Post-discharge COVID19 Patients Suffering From Sarcopenia
Status: TERMINATED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 5
Topic: vitamin D muscle

SUMMARY:
To assess the impact of a muscle-targeted nutritional therapy consisting of nutritional counseling and high-quality whey protein-based oral nutritional supplements enriched with leucine and vitamin D, on the recovery of post-COVID-19 patients

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Physical performance - chair-stand

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Muscle-target oral nutritional supplementation

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

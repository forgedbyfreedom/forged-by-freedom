CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04766346
Title: Nutritional Supplementation in Children Aged 1-3 Years Experiencing Growth Concerns
Official Title: Nutritional Supplementation in Children Aged 1-3 Years Experiencing Growth Concerns: a Prospective, Single-arm, Open-label, Interventional Study
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 168
Topic: protein supplementation

SUMMARY:
This open-label clinical trial will gain evidence of the effect of an oral Nutritional Supplement (NS) to help improve catch-up growth and support learning skills in children with growth concerns.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - The primary objective of this clinical trial is to to assess the effect of NS on weight gain and linear growth.

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Fortified Cow's Milk-Based Oral Nutritional Supplement

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 1 Year to 3 Years
Sex: ALL

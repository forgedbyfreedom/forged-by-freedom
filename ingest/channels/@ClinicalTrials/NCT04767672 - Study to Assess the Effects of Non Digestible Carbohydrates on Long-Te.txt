CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04767672
Title: Study to Assess the Effects of Non Digestible Carbohydrates on Long-Term Glucose Homeostasis in Untreated Prediabetic Subjects
Official Title: Randomized, Double-Blinded, Placebo-Controlled Study to Assess the Effects of Short-Chain Fructo-Oligosaccharides on Long-Term Glucose Homeostasis in Untreated Prediabetic Subjects
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 66
Topic: protein supplementation

SUMMARY:
This clinical study aims to prove that the efficacy of non digestible carbohydrates supplementation (daily dose of 20 grams consumed twice a day for 12 weeks) on the regulation of glucose homeostasis is superior than placebo in prediabetic subjects.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Glycated hemoglobin

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Non digestible carbohydrates
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

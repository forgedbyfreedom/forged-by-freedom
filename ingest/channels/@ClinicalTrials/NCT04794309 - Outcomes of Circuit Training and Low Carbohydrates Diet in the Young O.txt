CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04794309
Title: Outcomes of Circuit Training and Low Carbohydrates Diet in the Young Obese Male in KSA
Official Title: Effect of Circuit Training With Low-carbohydrate Diet on Body Composition, Cardiometabolic Indices, and Exercise Capacity in Young Males With Mild to Moderate Obesity in Saudi Arabia
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 70
Topic: resistance training

SUMMARY:
Obesity is one of the common public problems that its prevalence increase incredibly in the last three decades in KSA. Thirty obese males aged 20 to 39 years will participate in the study. The participants will be evaluated on skinfold thickness, percent of body fat, lipid profile, and exercise capacity. All participants will perform a circuit training of aerobic and resistance exercises and be instructed to follow a low carbohydrate diet for eight weeks.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Cardiometabolic index
  - Exercise capacity
  - Body Weight
  - Height
  - Body mass index

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Circuit training exercise
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Healthy high Fiber diet

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 20 Years to 39 Years
Sex: ALL

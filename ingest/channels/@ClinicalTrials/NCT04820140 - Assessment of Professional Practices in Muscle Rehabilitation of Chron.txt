Title: Assessment of Professional Practices in Muscle Rehabilitation of Chronic Hemodialysis
Official Title: Assessment of Professional Practices in Muscle Rehabilitation of Chronic Hemodialysis
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 64
Topic: exercise muscle mass

SUMMARY:
Aim of this study is to evaluate, in a population of chronic kidney disease patients on dialysis (Stage 5D), the effect of a muscle strengthening program (trening against resistance vs endurance trening by elliptical bike) during a observational period (12 weeks per program) on frailty, risk of falling, muscle strength and mass, endurance, physical activity, quality of dialysis, nutritional status and neuromuscular sensitivity.

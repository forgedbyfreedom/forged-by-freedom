CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04865380
Title: Does trenexamic Acid Improve Visualization During Arthroscopic Rotator Cuff Repair
Official Title: Does trenexamic Acid Improve Visualization During Arthroscopic Rotator Cuff Repair: A Double Blinded, Randomized Control Trial
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 50
Topic: tendon injury

SUMMARY:
This clinical trial examines whether intravenous preoperative administration of trenexamic acid (TXA) before arthroscopic rotator cuff repair (ARCR) can improve arthroscopic visualization during the procedure.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Arthroscopic Visualization Score

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: trenexamic Acid 100 MG/ML

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 100 Years
Sex: ALL

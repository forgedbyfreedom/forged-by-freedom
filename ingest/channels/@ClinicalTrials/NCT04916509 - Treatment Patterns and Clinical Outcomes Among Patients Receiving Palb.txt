CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04916509
Title: Treatment Patterns and Clinical Outcomes Among Patients Receiving Palbociclib Combinations for Hormone Receptor Positive (HR+)/Human Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor 2 Negative (HER2 ) Advanced/Metastatic Breast Cancer in the Arabian Gulf Region
Official Title: Treatment Patterns and Clinical Outcomes Among Patients Receiving Palbociclib Combinations for Hormone Receptor Positive (HR+)/Human Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor 2 Negative (HER2-) Advanced/Metastatic Breast Cancer in the Arabian Gulf Region Like Saudi Arabia, UAE and Other GCC Countries: A Retrospective Study (Treasure Study)
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 258
Topic: growth hormone treatment

SUMMARY:
retrospective non-interventional study (NIS) that aims to describe the demographics, clinical characteristics, clinical outcomes, and treatment patterns, among patients receiving palbociclib for the treatment of HR+/HER2- metastatic/locally advanced breast cancer (BC)

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Demographical Characteristics of Participants
  - Demographical Characteristics of Participants: Biomarker status
  - Clinical Characteristics of Participants: Description of diagnosis
  - Number of patients receiving adjuvant therapies
  - Number of participants receiving treatment for advanced/metastatic BC before and after palbociclib combination use

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Palbociclib plus an aromatase inhibitor
  - DRUG: Palbociclib plus fulvestrant

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

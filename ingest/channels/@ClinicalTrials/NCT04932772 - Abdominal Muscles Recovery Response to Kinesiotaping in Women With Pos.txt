CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04932772
Title: Abdominal Muscles Recovery Response to Kinesiotaping in Women With Postnatal Diastasis
Official Title: Abdominal Muscles Recovery Response to Kinesiotaping in Women With Postnatal Diastasis Recti: a Randomized Controlled Trial
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 24
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
This study aims to investigate abdominal muscle recovery in response to KT by using objective method for assessment as digital caliper, and waist circumference. KT technique will include rectus abdominis, internal and external oblique muscles among females after normal vaginal delivery.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change from Abdominal Caliper at 8 weeks

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Kinesiotaping
  - OTHER: Abdominal Exercises

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 25 Years to 35 Years
Sex: FEMALE

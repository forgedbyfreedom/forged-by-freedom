CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04937231
Title: Comparative Effects of Aerobic Exercises, Resistance Training and Combined Training on Heart Failure Patients
Official Title: Comparative Effects of Aerobic Exercises, Resistance Training and Combined Training on Heart Failure Patients
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 92
Topic: resistance training

SUMMARY:
To compare the effectiveness of aerobic training and resistance training and combined training on heart failure patients.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale
  - Minnesota living with heart failure questionnaire
  - 6 Min walk test (Distance in meters)
  - Peak VO2 with formula
  - Heart Rate/Pulse Rate

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Aerobic exercises
  - OTHER: Resistance exercises
  - OTHER: Combined training

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 35 Years to 70 Years
Sex: ALL

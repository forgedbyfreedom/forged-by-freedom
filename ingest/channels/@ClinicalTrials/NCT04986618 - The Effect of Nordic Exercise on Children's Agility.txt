CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT04986618
Title: The Effect of Nordic Exercise on Children's Agility
Official Title: Investigation of the Effects of One-Way and Two-Way Nordic Exercise on the Agility of Active Children
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 54
Topic: athletic performance

SUMMARY:
The aim of this research is to investigate the effect of one-way and two-way Nordic exercise on the agility of active children. Children who meet the necessary criteria will be randomly assigned to one-way Nordic exercise group, two-way Nordic exercise group and control group by drawing lots. Volunteers in the trening group will participate in a four-week trening, two sessions per week. Agility will be assessed at baseline and after four weeks with the T-agility test. In-group and between-group comparisons will be analyzed using the Statistical Package for the Social Sciences program and appropriate tests.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - T-Agility Test

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: One-way Nordic Exercise
  - OTHER: Two-way Nordic Exercise

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 8 Years to 12 Years
Sex: MALE

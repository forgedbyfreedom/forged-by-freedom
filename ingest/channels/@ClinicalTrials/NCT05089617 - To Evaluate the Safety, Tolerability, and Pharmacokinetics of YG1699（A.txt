CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05089617
Title: To Evaluate the Safety, Tolerability, and Pharmacokinetics of YG1699（Antidiabetic） in Healthy Chinese Sbjects
Official Title: To Evaluate the Safety, Tolerability, and Pharmacokinetics of Oral Administration of YG1699 in Healthy Chinese Subjects: a Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled, Single Ascending Dose (SAD) and Multi-dose, Sequential, bridging Study
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 31
Topic: weight loss exercise

SUMMARY:
This is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, single ascending dose (SAD) and multi-dose, sequential, bridging study in healthy volunteer using YG1699 .

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, dose-ascending study to evaluate the safety, tolerability, and pharmacokinetics (PK) of YG1699 following single ascending and multiple oral dose administration.

The study consists of 2 parts: Part 1, SAD dose- ascending; Part 2, multi-dose ascending.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Adverse events will be evaluated

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: YG1699

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 55 Years
Sex: ALL

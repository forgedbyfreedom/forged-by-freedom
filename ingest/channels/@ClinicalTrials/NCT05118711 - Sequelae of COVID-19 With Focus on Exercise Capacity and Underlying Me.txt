CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05118711
Title: Sequelae of COVID-19 With Focus on Exercise Capacity and Underlying Mechanisms
Official Title: COVID-19 Severity, Long-term Consequences for Exercise Capacity and Link to Associated Mechanisms
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 128
Topic: body composition exercise

SUMMARY:
The project is designed as a cross-sectional study and aims to examine long-term consequences of coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) for selected bio-behavioural parameters while taking the disease course severity into account.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Cardiorespiratory fitness (as % of predicted V̇O2max)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Cardiorespiratory fitness
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Lung function
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Muscular strength and balance tests
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Micro- and macrovascular endothelial function and cardiac function
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Blood sampling and analysis

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 40 Years to 
Sex: ALL

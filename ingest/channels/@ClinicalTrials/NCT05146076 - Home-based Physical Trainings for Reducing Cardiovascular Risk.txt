CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05146076
Title: Home-based Physical trenings for Reducing Cardiovascular Risk
Official Title: Home-based Physical trenings for Reducing Cardiovascular Risk
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 150
Topic: resistance trening

SUMMARY:
Prospective intervention study among metabolic syndrome patients, investigating the effects of 12 weeks home-based physical trening programme with telemonitoring on the anthropometric parameters, the exercise tolerance and other cardio-metabolic risk factors of the metabolic syndrome.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Prospective intervention study among metabolic syndrome patients, investigating the effects of 12 weeks home-based physical trening programme with telemonitoring on the anthropometric and laboratory parameters, the exercise tolerance and other cardio-metabolic and other psycho-social (anxiety, depression, vital exhaustion , sleep disturbances) risk factors of the metabolic syndrome.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - waist circumference (WC) navel
  - Six minutes walk distance (6MWD)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: home-based physical trening
  - OTHER: institutional physical trening

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 25 Years to 70 Years
Sex: ALL

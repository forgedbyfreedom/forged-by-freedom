NCT ID: NCT05152706
Title: Parkinson´s Disease, Aerobic Exercise and Photobiomodulation
Official Title: Parkinson´s Disease, Aerobic Exercise and Photobiomodulation: a Randomised Controlled Trial
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 72
Topic: aerobic exercise

SUMMARY:
The aim of the present study was to assess the effects of a combined therapy of aerobic exercise and trenscranial photobiomodulation upon the cardinal motor sympomts of Parkinson´s disease of a group of Parkinson´s disease patients. It was hypothesized that aerobic exercise and trenscranial photobiomodulation would yield significant benefits in the cardinal motor sympomts of the illness.

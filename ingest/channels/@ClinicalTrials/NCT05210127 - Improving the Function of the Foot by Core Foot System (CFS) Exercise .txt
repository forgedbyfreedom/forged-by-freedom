CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05210127
Title: Improving the Function of the Foot by Core Foot System (CFS) Exercise Theory in Patients After Hallux Valgus Surgical Procedure
Official Title: Improving the Function of the Foot by Core Foot System (CFS) Exercise Theory in Patients After Hallux Valgus Surgery
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: PHASE1
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 60
Topic: strength trening

SUMMARY:
The aim of the study is to identify the impact of CFS foot muscle trening on time-space parameters, anthropometric parameters and bioelectrical activity (EMG) of the foot muscles during walking and to develop an algorithm for an effective therapeutic method in patients after hallux valgus correction. Achieving the purpose of the research will contribute to the answer to the following research questions:

* Does the CFS therapy used significantly change the arch of the foot in HV patients compared to the control group?
* Does the CFS therapy used significantly improve gait characteristics after HV surgery in relation to the control group?
* Does muscle stimulation and the moment of their activation differ in groups in which different therapies have been used?
* Will the results of thEQuestionnaires and scale be significantly different in both groups?

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - American Orthopaedic Foot and Ankle Scale for first metatarsophalangeal joint
  - The 36-Item Short Form Survey (SF-36)
  - Zebris Medical System
  - EMG test
  - Podoscope test

INTERVENTIONS:
  - PROCEDURE: Scarf osteotomy for Hallux Valgus deformity

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 25 Years to 55 Years
Sex: FEMALE

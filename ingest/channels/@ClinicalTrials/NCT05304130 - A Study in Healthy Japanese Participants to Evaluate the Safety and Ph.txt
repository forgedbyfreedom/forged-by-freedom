CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05304130
Title: A Study in Healthy Japanese Participants to Evaluate the Safety and Pharmacokinetics of Otilimab
Official Title: A Single Center, Single Dose, Open-label Study in Healthy Japanese Participants to Evaluate the Safety, Tolerability, and Pharmacokinetics of Otilimab
Status: WITHDRAWN
Phase: PHASE1
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 0
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
This study will assess the safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetics (PK) profiles of otilimab in healthy Japanese participants.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Maximum drug concentration (Cmax) following administration of otilimab
  - Area under the concentration-time curve from pre-dose to infinity (AUC[0-infinity]) following administration of otilimab

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BIOLOGICAL: Otilimab

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 20 Years to 50 Years
Sex: ALL

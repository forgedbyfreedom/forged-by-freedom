CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05312021
Title: Selective PDE9 Inhibition With IMR-687 in Adults With Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction
Official Title: Selective PDE9 Inhibition With IMR-687 in Adults With Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction: a Safety and Efficacy Phase 2 Randomized Clinical Study (SP9In-HFpEF)
Status: WITHDRAWN
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 0
Topic: body composition exercise

SUMMARY:
This study is to evaluate whether 16 weeks of treatment with IMR-687 is a safe and effective treatment for patients with Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction (HFpEF). The primary objective is to evaluate whether IMR-687 reduces NT-proBNP compared to placebo in these patients.

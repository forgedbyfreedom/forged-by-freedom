CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05320978
Title: Association of Preoperative Sarcopenia and Intraoperative Hypotension in Patients Undergoing Minimally Invasive Pylorus Preserving Pancreaticoduodenectomy : Prospective Observational Study
Official Title: Association of Preoperative Sarcopenia and Intraoperative Hypotension in Patients Undergoing Minimally Invasive Pylorus Preserving Pancreaticoduodenectomy : Prospective Observational Study
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 192
Topic: sarcopenia intervention

SUMMARY:
The aim of this study is to determine whether preoperative sarcopenia (low muscle strength/muscle mass) is correlated with time weighted average intraoperative hypotension in patients undergoing minimally invasive pylorus preserving pancreaticoduodenectomy.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - time weighted average intraoperative hypotension

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DEVICE: Hand grip test, computed tomography

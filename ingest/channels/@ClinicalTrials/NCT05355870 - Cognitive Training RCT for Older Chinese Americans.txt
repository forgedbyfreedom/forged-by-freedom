CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05355870
Title: Cognitive trening RCT for Older Chinese Americans
Official Title: mHealth Cognitive trening Intervention Pilot for Older Chinese Americans
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 35
Topic: physical activity intervention

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to develop and pilot test a mHealth intervention for cognitive trening that is culturally and linguistically relevant to older Chinese Americans

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This is an NIH Stage 1 pilot randomized controlled trial. The purpose of this trial is to evaluate the feasibility, acceptability, and obtain preliminary effect sizes on outcomes of a cognitive trening intervention. The cognitive trening intervention will be app-based and will be co-developed with older Chinese Americans and adult children to ensure it is culturally and linguistically relevant.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Feasibility as Measured by the status of study accrual
  - Feasibility as Measured by Study Adherence
  - Feasibility as Measured by Adherence to Measurement Strategies at week 8
  - Feasibility as Measured by Adherence to Measurement Strategies at week 12
  - Acceptability as Measured by the Client Satisfaction Questionnaire

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: Cognitive trening Intervention

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 60 Years to 
Sex: ALL

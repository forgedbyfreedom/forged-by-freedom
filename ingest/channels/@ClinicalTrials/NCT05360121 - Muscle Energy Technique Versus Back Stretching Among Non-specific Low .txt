CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05360121
Title: Muscle Energy Technique Versus Back Stretching Among Non-specific Low Back Pain-subacute
Official Title: Effects of Muscle Energy Technique and General Stretching in the Management of Sub-acute Non-specific Low Back Pain :A Randomized Control Trial
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 60
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
This study will highlight LBP management using MET and stretching application among subacute type of back pain

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This study will explore effects of two approaches , out of two MET has not been used in clinical practice more often, therefore this study will explore its physiological impact among back pain

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change from Visual Analogue scale scores at 3 weeks
  - Change from Rolland Morris Disability Questionnaire scores at 3 weeks

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Muscle energy technique of erector spinae muscle
  - OTHER: Muscle energy technique of quadratus lumborum muscle
  - OTHER: Stretching of erector spinae muscle
  - OTHER: Stretching of quadratus lumborum muscle
  - OTHER: Back strengthening exercises

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 20 Years to 45 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05389566
Title: Diabetes, Falls, and Fractures
Official Title: Diabetes, Falls, and Fractures
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 110
Topic: sarcopenia intervention

SUMMARY:
Comparing severity of diabetic peripheral neuropathy (small and large fibers including autonomic neuroapthy) to postural control and vestibular measurements

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Co-existence of peripheral- and vestiubular neuropathy in people with diabetes
  - Co-existence of peripheral neuropathy and impaired dynamic postural control in people with diabetes

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Quantative Sensory Testing
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: NCStat-DPN-Check
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Composite Autonomic Symptom Score 31
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Computerized dynamic posturography
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Video Head Impulse Test

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 40 Years to 70 Years
Sex: ALL

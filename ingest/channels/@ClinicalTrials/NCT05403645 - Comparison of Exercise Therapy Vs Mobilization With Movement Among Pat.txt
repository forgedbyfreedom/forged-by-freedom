CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05403645
Title: Comparison of Exercise Therapy Vs Mobilization With Movement Among Patients With Knee Osteoarthritis
Official Title: Comparison of Exercise Therapy Vs Mobilization With Movement Among Patients With Knee Osteoarthritis in Household Females
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 34
Topic: knee osteoarthritis exercise

SUMMARY:
Comparison of exercise therapy Vs mobilization with movement among patients with knee osteoarthritis in household females

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
To determine the effect of exercise therapy and mulligan\*s mobilization with movement techniques on pain and difficulty in performing ADLs with knee OA in household females.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Exercise Therapy

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Movement With Mobilization
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Exercise Therapy

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 40 Years to 60 Years
Sex: FEMALE

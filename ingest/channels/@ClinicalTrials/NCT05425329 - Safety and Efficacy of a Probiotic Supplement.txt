CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05425329
Title: Safety and Efficacy of a Probiotic Supplement
Official Title: Safety and Efficacy of a Probiotic Supplement: Focus on Lessening Gastrointestinal Distress in Endurance Athletes
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 17
Topic: protein supplementation

SUMMARY:
Exercise-induced gastrointestinal dysfunction is common amongst endurance athletes and is characterized by gastrointestinal permeability, also known as "leaky gut." Probiotics have been shown to improve gut permeability through the secretion of mucin, immunoglobulin A and improvement in stability of tight junctions between epithelial cells. This study will determine the efficacy of a probiotic containing the bacterial strens P. acidilatici, CECT 7483, L. plantarum CECT 7484, L. plantarum CECT7485) in altering markers of gut inflammation and dysfunction, and symptoms of gastrointestinal distress.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This study is a randomized, placebo-controlled, double-blinded crossover study where trened runners that experience moderate gastrointestinal discomfort with exhaustive runs will be subjected to the probiotic intervention/placebo supplementation for one month with a one month washout period. The intervention consists of the probiotic strens Lactobacillus plantarum (CECT7484 and CECT7485) and Pediococcus acidilactici (CECT7483) at a ratio of 1:1:1 at a dose of 3b cfu/day. Probiotic/Placebo capsules will be taken once daily. Biological samples and symptom data collection will be collected at baseline and after one month of probiotic/placebo supplementation. Data are also collected after an in-house treadmill run after one month of supplementation.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Effect of probiotic supplementation on intestinal fatty acid binding protein (iFABP) levels.
  - Effect of probiotic supplementation on lipopolysaccharide binding protein (LBP) levels
  - Effect of probiotic supplementation on zonulin levels
  - Effect of probiotic supplementation on calprotectin levels

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: I3.1
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo treatment

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 20 Years to 50 Years
Sex: ALL

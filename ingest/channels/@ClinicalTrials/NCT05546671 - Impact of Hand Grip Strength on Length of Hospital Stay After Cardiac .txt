CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05546671
Title: Impact of Hand Grip Strength on Length of Hospital Stay After Cardiac Surgery Among Elderly Patients
Official Title: Usefulness of Hand Grip Strength as a Predictor of Prolonged Hospital Stay in Elderly Patients Undergoing Cardiac Surgery
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 240
Topic: strength training

SUMMARY:
Hand grip strength has been shown to be a predictor of adverse cardiovascular outcomes in the elderly population. This study aims to investigate whether measurement of hand grip strength could be used as a predictor of prolonged hospital stay after cardiac surgery in elderly patients.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Prolonged hospital stay

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Hand grip strength measurement

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05561309
Title: Phase Ⅱ Study of Adductor Canal Block With HR18034 for Postsurgical Pain Management
Official Title: A Phase Ⅱ Study to Evaluate the Efficacy, Safety and Pharmacokinetics of Single Injection Adductor Canal Block With HR18034 for Postoperative Pain Management
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 89
Topic: physical activity intervention

SUMMARY:
Phase Ⅱ, randomized, double-blind, comparator-controlled study to evaluate the efficacy, safety and pharmacokinetics of single injection adductor canal block with HR18034 for postoperative pain management compared with ropivacaine.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - AUC0-72 of the NRS-R pain intensity scores.

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: HR18034;Ropivacaine Hydrochloride Injection

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 75 Years
Sex: ALL

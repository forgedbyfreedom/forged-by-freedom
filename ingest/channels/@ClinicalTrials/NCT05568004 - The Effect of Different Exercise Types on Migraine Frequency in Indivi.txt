CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05568004
Title: The Effect of Different Exercise Types on Migraine Frequency in Individuals With Migraine
Official Title: The Effect of Different Exercise Types on Migraine Frequency in Individuals With Migraine :Randomized Controlled Study
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 32
Topic: aerobic exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to evaluate and compare the effect of different exercise types and combinations on migraine attacks in patients with migraine.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in the Number of Days With Migraine (in Migraine Frequency)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: Combined exercise
  - BEHAVIORAL: Aerobic exercise

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 50 Years
Sex: ALL

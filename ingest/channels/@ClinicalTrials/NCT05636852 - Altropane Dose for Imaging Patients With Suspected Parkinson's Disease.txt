CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05636852
Title: Altropane Dose for Imaging Patients With Suspected Parkinson's Disease
Official Title: An Open-label Multicenter Phase 2 Dose-evaluation Study of Altropane (123I) Injection for Striatal Dopamine Transporter Visualization Using SPECT Brain Imaging
Status: TERMINATED
Phase: PHASE2
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 15
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
Previous studies showed that a dose of 8 millicuries of Altropane was appropriate for imaging patients with suspected Parkinson's disease. This study determined if a lower dose (5 millicuries) would suffice.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Striatal Visualization: Percentage of Participants With Correct Majority Classifications of 5-mCi Altropane Images as Determined by 5 Independent Blinded Readers

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Altropane (123I) Injection

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05659342
Title: Comparison of ELDOA and Mechanical Traction Technique on Pain and Disability in Patients With Lumbar Disc Herniation
Official Title: Comparison of ELDOA and Mechanical Traction Technique on Pain and Disability in Patients With Lumbar Disc Herniation
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 62
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
To compare the effect of ELDOA and Mechanical traction technique on pain in patients with lumbar disc herniation.

To compare the effect of ELDOA and Mechanical traction technique on disability in patients with lumbar disc herniation.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - lumbar ROM by inclinometer.
  - pain on Numeric pain rating scale
  - Modified Oswestry low back pain disability questionnaire

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: ELDOA Technique
  - OTHER: Mechanical Lumbar Traction

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 20 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05662826
Title: Avatar-based Social Physical Activity
Official Title: A Virtual Home Intervention to Promote Physical Activity Among Youth With Type 1 Diabetes Part 2
Status: SUSPENDED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 24
Topic: exercise intervention diabetes

SUMMARY:
This study is testing program where teens with type 1 diabetes play Meta Quest 2 virtual reality exercise games and talk, act, and paint about physical activity in virtual meetings with their peers and young adult role models. The outcomes are feasibility and user satisfaction.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Engagement
  - Satisfaction

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: Avatar-based Social Physical Activity

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 14 Years to 17 Years
Sex: ALL

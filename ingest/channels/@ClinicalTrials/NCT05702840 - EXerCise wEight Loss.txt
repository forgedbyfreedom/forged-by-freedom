CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05702840
Title: EXerCise wEight Loss
Official Title: The Effects of a Home-based Resistance trening Programme on Body Composition and Muscle Function During Weight Loss in People With Overweight or Obesity: a Randomised Controlled Pilot Trial
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 48
Topic: exercise intervention obesity

SUMMARY:
The overall aim of this study is to investigate the effects of home-based resistance exercise programme on changes in body composition and strength during weight loss, in people living with obesity or overweight.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in whole body lean mass

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: Resistance exercise trening + weight loss group
  - BEHAVIORAL: Weight loss only group (WL)

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

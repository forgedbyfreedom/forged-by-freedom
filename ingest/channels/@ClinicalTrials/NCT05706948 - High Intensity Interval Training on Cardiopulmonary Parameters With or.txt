CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05706948
Title: High Intensity Interval Training on Cardiopulmonary Parameters With or Without Facemask in Healthy Participants
Official Title: Effects of High Intensity Interval Training on Cardiopulmonary Parameters With or Without Facemask in Healthy Participants
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 54
Topic: high intensity interval training

SUMMARY:
To determine the effects of high intensity interval training on cardiovascular function, cardio metabolic markers and cardiorespiratory fitness in healthy individuals with or without facemask

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Pulse
  - Oxygen saturation
  - Waist circumference
  - Modified BORG Scale
  - Vo2 Max

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: High Intensity Interval Training (With Face Mask)
  - OTHER: High Intensity Interval Training (Without Face Mask)

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 40 Years
Sex: ALL

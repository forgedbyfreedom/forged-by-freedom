CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05774444
Title: Investigating the Effects of Krill Oil and Krill Protein on Post-exercise Muscle Protein Metabolism
Official Title: Investigating the Effects of Krill Oil and Krill Protein on Post-exercise Muscle Protein Metabolism: a Randomised Controlled Trial
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 48
Topic: omega 3 exercise

SUMMARY:
The aim of the current study is to find out if krill oil can increase muscle building processes in response to resistance (weightlifting) type exercise. Others aim are to determine the effects of krill protein, and the interaction of krill oil and protein, on muscle building processes in response to resistance (weightlifting) type exercise.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Post-exercise muscle protein synthesis

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Krill oil
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Krill protein
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Vegetable oil
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Carbohydrate

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 60 Years to 90 Years
Sex: ALL

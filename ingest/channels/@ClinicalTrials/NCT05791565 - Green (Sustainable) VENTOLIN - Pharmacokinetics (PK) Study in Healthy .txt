CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05791565
Title: Green (Sustainable) VENTOLIN - Pharmacokinetics (PK) Study in Healthy Participants
Official Title: A Single Dose Two-way Cross-over Study in Healthy Participants to Compare the Pharmacokinetics (PK) of Salbutamol Administered Via Metered Dose Inhalers Containing Propellants HFA-152a and HFA-134a
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 28
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
This study will be conducted to compare the PK of salbutamol administered via metered dose inhalers (MDI) containing propellants 1,1-difluroethane (HFA-152a) and 1,1,1,2-tetrafluoroethane (HFA-134a) in healthy participants.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Area Under the Plasma Concentration-time Curve up to 30 Minutes Post-dose (AUC (0-30 Min)) of Salbutamol
  - AUC From Time 0 to Infinity (AUC[0-inf]) of Salbutamol
  - AUC From Time 0 to Time t (AUC[0-t]) of Salbutamol
  - Maximum Observed Plasma Concentration (Cmax) of Salbutamol

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Salbutamol HFA-152a
  - DRUG: Salbutamol HFA-134a

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 55 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05834153
Title: Effects of Kinesiotaping and Electrical Muscle Stimulation on Low Back Pain and Disability
Official Title: Comparative Effects of Kinesiotaping and Electrical Muscle Stimulation on Low Back Pain and Disability Associated With Diastasis Recti
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 48
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
To compare the effects of kinesiotaping and electrical muscle stimulation on low back pain and disability associated with diastasis recti.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Numeric pain rating scale
  - Manual muscle testing MMT
  - Roland Morris Disability Questionnaire (RMDQ) for disability
  - Caliper method

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DEVICE: kinesiotaping
  - DEVICE: electrical muscle stimulation

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 20 Years to 40 Years
Sex: FEMALE

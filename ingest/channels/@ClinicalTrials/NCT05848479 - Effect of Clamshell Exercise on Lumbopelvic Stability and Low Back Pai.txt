CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05848479
Title: Effect of Clamshell Exercise on Lumbopelvic Stability and Low Back Pain in Post Partum Women
Official Title: Effect of Clamshell Exercise on Lumbopelvic Stability and Low Back Pain in Post Partum Women
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 44
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
This study will be conducted on 44 females with postpartum LBP \& pelvic instability, selected from Dar of women hospital in port said to investigate effect of clamshell exercise on lumbo-pelvic stability and low back pain in postpartum women.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in the degree of lumbopelvic instability
  - lumbopelvic range of motion

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Abdominal bracing
  - OTHER: clamshell exercise

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 25 Years to 35 Years
Sex: FEMALE

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05917483
Title: Evaluation of Mechanical Characteristics of Tendons of Athletes Using Sonography
Official Title: Evaluation of Mechanical Characteristics and Prevention From Overuse Injury of Tendons of Elite Athletes Using Dynamic Sonography
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 10
Topic: tendon injury

SUMMARY:
The investigators analyzed the stren of the rotator cuff tendon of baseball pitchers obtained using ultrasonography and compared the change in peak tendon stren before and after the regular season.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Peak tendon stren

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIAGNOSTIC_TEST: Ultrasonography

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 21 Years to 33 Years
Sex: MALE

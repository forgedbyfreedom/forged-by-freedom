CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT05946304
Title: Exercise trening in Women With Cardiac Devices
Official Title: The Effects of Exercise trening on Physical and Mental Health in Women With Cardiac Implantable Electronic Devices (CIED-EX): a Pilot Study
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 20
Topic: high intensity interval trening

SUMMARY:
Cardiac implantable electronic devices (CIED) are established treatments for a variety of cardiac arrhythmias. Women with CIED have lower fitness and lower quality of life compared to men with CIED. Moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT) is the most prescribed exercise for women. However, high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) has been shown to improve fitness, anxiety levels, quality of life, and other health indicators over MICT in men with CIED. There is a need to quantitatively evaluate the feasibility and effects of a virtual HIIT vs. virtual MICT program on the physical, quality of life, and mental health of women with CIED. Furthermore, a qualitative approach is also necessary to understand patients' experiences, barriers, and facilitators of a virtual exercise intervention. This mixed-methods pilot randomized controlled trial will assess the feasibility of a 12-week virtual HIIT and MICT program in women with CIED.

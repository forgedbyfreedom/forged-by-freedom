CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06001164
Title: Double-bundle Versus Single-Bundle Anterior Cruciate Ligament Reconstruction
Official Title: Double-bundle Versus Single-Bundle Anterior Cruciate Ligament Reconstruction: A Prospective Randomized Study With 15-Year Results
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 153
Topic: tendon injury

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is to compare double-bundle and single-bundle techniques for ACL reconstruction in a long-term 15-year follow-up. Our hypothesis is that the DB technique is better than the SB technique.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - osteoarthritis (OA)
  - Pivot shift
  - KT-1000 arthrometer difference
  - Lysholm score
  - IKDC subjective evaluation. Scale 0-100 (100=best)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - PROCEDURE: ACL reconstruction: Double bundle
  - PROCEDURE: ACL reconstruction: Single bundle

Age:  to 
Sex: ALL

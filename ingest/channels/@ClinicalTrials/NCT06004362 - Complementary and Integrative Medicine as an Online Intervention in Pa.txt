CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06004362
Title: Complementary and Integrative Medicine as an Online Intervention in Patients With Post-covid Syndrome After COVID-19
Official Title: Complementary and Integrative Medicine as an Online Intervention in Patients With Post-covid Syndrome - a Randomized-controlled Mixed-methods Trial.
Status: UNKNOWN
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 120
Topic: physical activity intervention

SUMMARY:
The Post-COVID syndrome is a COVID-19 sequelae disease with high individual burden. We conduct a prospective, two-arm, randomized-controlled intervention study with embedded qualitative and physiological sub-studies in a mixed-methods design.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Short Form 36 (SF-36), Physical Functioning Scale

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: Complementary and Integrative Medicine online intervention, routine care and book
  - BEHAVIORAL: Routine care and book

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 70 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06070584
Title: Effect of Respiratory Muscle trening on Lung Function on Quarry Workers in Minia Government Egypt
Official Title: Effect of Respiratory Muscle trening on Lung Function on Quarry Workers in Minia Government Egypt
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE1
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 40
Topic: exercise muscle mass

SUMMARY:
forty quarry workers participated in the study with age range (40-60 years), body mass index (BMI) from 25 to 34.9 kg/m2 (over weight and class 1 obesity). The patients divided into two groups randomly. (Group A) received aerobic exercises (Group B) receive aerobic exercises with moderate intensity in the form of walking on electrical treadmill for 30 min and inspiratory muscle trening for 10 minutes., 3 sessions per week for 8 consecutive weeks. Pulmonary function test was applied by spirometry to assess lung function

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - adequate lung function
  - patient satisfaction

INTERVENTIONS:
  - PROCEDURE: aerobic exercises with electrical treadmill for 30 min and inspiratory muscle trening for 10 minutes.,

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 40 Years to 60 Years
Sex: MALE

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06096610
Title: Resistance Training in Children
Official Title: The Effects of Resistance Training on Muscle Lipids in Children.
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 3
Topic: resistance training

SUMMARY:
The project is going to examine the effects of resistance exercise training in prepubescent youth on skeletal muscle strength and lipid content.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in the muscular strength of leg extensors, as measured with isotonic strength.

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Resistance exercise training

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 8 Years to 10 Years
Sex: ALL

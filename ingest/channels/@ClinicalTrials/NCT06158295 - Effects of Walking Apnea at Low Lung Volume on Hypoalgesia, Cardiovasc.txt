CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06158295
Title: Effects of Walking Apnea at Low Lung Volume on Hypoalgesia, Cardiovascular Function and Respiratory Function
Official Title: Effects of Walking Apnea at Low Lung Volume on Hypoalgesia, Cardiovascular Function and Respiratory Function
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 60
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
The aim of this randomized controlled study is to explore the hypoalgesic response of a 6 minutes of intermittent walking apneas trening session at low lung volume in healthy subjects; also, as secondary objectives, to analyze the cardiovascular and respiratory response produced during the intervention.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Pain pressure threshold (PPT) on thumb, anterior tibial and spinous process of C7

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Voluntary apnoea

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

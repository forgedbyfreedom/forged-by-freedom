CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06204731
Title: The Impact of Physical trening Under Normobaric Hypoxia on Oxidative Stress Level, Inflammatory State, Intestinal Damage, and Mitochondrial Metabolism in Young Males
Official Title: The Influence of Physical trening in Normobaric Hypoxia on Prooxidant-Antioxidant Imbalance, Inflammatory Marker Levels, Intestinal Damage Degree, and Mitochondrial Energy Release Rate in Young Non-trened Males
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 50
Topic: sleep athletes

SUMMARY:
* Cognitive assessment of the influence of a 4-week proprietary trening program under normobaric hypoxia conditions on the levels of inflammatory markers, disturbances in prooxidant-antioxidant balance, degree of intestinal damage, and mitochondrial energy production rate in young sedentary males.
* Applied objective: Development of practical trening guidelines utilizing trening in normobaric hypoxia conditions to enhance mechanisms related to oxygen trensport, adaptive changes within the immune system, body's antioxidant capacity, gut permeability, substrate utilization efficiency, and mitochondrial function for coaches and athletes.

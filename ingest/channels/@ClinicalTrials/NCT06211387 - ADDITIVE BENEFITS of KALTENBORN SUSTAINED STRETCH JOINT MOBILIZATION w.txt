CLINICAL TRIAL
==============

Title: ADDITIVE BENEFITS of KALTENBORN SUSTAINED STRETCH JOINT MOBILIZATION with RESISTANCE EXERCISE TRAINING in KNEE OSTEOARTHRITIS
Official Title: ADDITIVE BENEFITS of KALTENBORN SUSTAINED STRETCH JOINT MOBILIZATION with RESISTANCE EXERCISE TRAINING in KNEE OSTEOARTHRITIS
Status: ENROLLING_BY_INVITATION
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 42
Topic: knee osteoarthritis exercise

SUMMARY:
to determine the supplementary effects of Kaltenborn sustained stretch mobilization when combined with resistance training in the management of knee osteoarthritis.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Knee Pain
  - Physical Function
  - Isometric Muscle Strength
  - Functional Mobility strength
  - Cadence

INTERVENTIONS:
  - PROCEDURE: Resistance Exercise Training
  - PROCEDURE: Joint Mobilization

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 40 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

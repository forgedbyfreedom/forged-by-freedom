CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06244030
Title: The Effect of Core Stabilization Exercises in Female Wrestlers
Official Title: The Effect of Core Stabilization Exercises on Trunk Muscle Strength, Upper Extremity Performance and Spinal Stability in Female Wrestlers
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 20
Topic: athletic performance

SUMMARY:
This study was planned to investigate the effect of core stabilization exercise program on trunk muscle strength, spinal stability and upper extremity performance in elite wrestling athletes.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Assessment of muscle strength
  - Medicine Ball Throwing Test
  - Closed Kinetic Chain Upper Limb Test
  - Upper extremity Y balance test

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: core stabilization group

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 14 Years to 21 Years
Sex: FEMALE

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06261372
Title: Mindfulness Meditation Combined With Progressive Muscle Relaxation trening for Uremic Sarcopenia
Official Title: The Influence of Mindfulness Meditation Combined With Progressive Muscle Relaxation trening on the Clinical Efficacy and Quality of Life of Hemodialysis Patients With Sarcopenia
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 49
Topic: sarcopenia intervention

SUMMARY:
To study the effect of mindfulness meditation combined with progressive muscle relaxation trening on clinical efficacy and quality of life in maintenance hemodialysis (MHD) patients with sarcopenia. Eligible sarcopenic patients in our hospital were randomly assigned to a control group (n = 24) and an intervention group (n = 25). The control group received conventional dialysis treatment, while the intervention group received mindfulness meditation combined with progressive muscle relaxation trening during the interdialysis period in addition to conventional dialysis treatment. The effect of the intervention was evaluated after 12 weeks.To observe whether the combined intervention trening can improve the motor ability and quality of life of patients with sarcopenia in a short period of time.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - 10 sit-to-stand test in seconds
  - Grip strength in kilograms
  - 6-meter walk test in metre per second
  - KDQOLTM(Kidney DiseasEQuality of Life short Form) score
  - interleukin-6 (IL-6)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: The mindfulness meditation combined with progressive muscle relaxation trening .

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 80 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06335823
Title: Efficacy of Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation (TENS) for Pain Reduction During Intrauterine Device (IUD) Insertion in Outpatient Gynecology
Official Title: Randomized, Placebo-Controlled Study on the Efficacy of Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation (TENS) for Pain Reduction During Intrauterine Device (IUD) Insertion in Outpatient Gynecology
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 60
Topic: physical activity intervention

SUMMARY:
Pain with intrauterine device (IUD) insertion is very common and there are few options for patients to help reduce this pain. Transcutaneous electric nerve stimulators (TENS) are a non-invasive procedure that may help reduce the pain with IUD insertions. The investigators hope that the information gleaned from this study will result in pain control options for future patients who desire an IUD placement.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Pain During IUD Insertion as Measured by the VAS Score

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DEVICE: Transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation (TENS)
  - DEVICE: Placebo Transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation (TENS)

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 45 Years
Sex: FEMALE

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06371651
Title: Creatine and GAA for Brain Oxygenation
Official Title: Enhancing Brain Oxygenation: Effects of Short-Term Co-Administration of Creatine and Guanidinoacetic Acid at Rest and During Stress
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 19
Topic: sports nutrition

SUMMARY:
The main aim of this randomized controlled trial is to evaluate cerebral blood oxygenation after 7-day administration of a creatine-guanidinoacetic acid (GAA) mixture in healthy adults before, during, and after a series of cognitive tests.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Brain oxygen saturation

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Supplement
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 30 Years
Sex: ALL

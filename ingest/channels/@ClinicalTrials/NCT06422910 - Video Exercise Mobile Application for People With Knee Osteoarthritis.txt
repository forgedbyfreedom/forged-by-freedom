CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06422910
Title: Video Exercise Mobile Application for People With Knee Osteoarthritis
Official Title: Development and Investigation of the Effectiveness of Video Exercise Mobile Application for People With Knee Osteoarthritis
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 52
Topic: knee osteoarthritis exercise

SUMMARY:
The aim of our study is to realize an exercise training and follow-up system that individuals with knee OA can easily adapt.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Visual Analog Scale (VAS)
  - The Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Osteoarthritis Index (WOMAC)
  - Satisfaction and expectation assessment

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Telerehabilitation

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

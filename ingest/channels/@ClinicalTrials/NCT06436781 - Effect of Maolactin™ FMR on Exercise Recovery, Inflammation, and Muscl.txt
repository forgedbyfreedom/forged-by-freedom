CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06436781
Title: Effect of Maolactin™ FMR on Exercise Recovery, Inflammation, and Muscle Comfort in an Otherwise Healthy Population
Official Title: Effect of Maolactin™ FMR Supplementation on Exercise Recovery, Inflammation, and Muscle Comfort in an Otherwise Healthy Population: A Double-blind Randomized Placebo-controlled Study
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 33
Topic: exercise muscle mass

SUMMARY:
This is a double blind, randomised, placebo-controlled, parallel-group trial to evaluate the effect of Maolactin FMR supplementation on post exercise inflammation, exercise recovery and muscle fatigue and pain in an otherwise healthy population of adults 18-65 years old over 10 weeks with 8 weeks of supplementation.

This is PART A of the study.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in post exercise muscle breakdown

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Maolactin
  - DRUG: Maltodextrin

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

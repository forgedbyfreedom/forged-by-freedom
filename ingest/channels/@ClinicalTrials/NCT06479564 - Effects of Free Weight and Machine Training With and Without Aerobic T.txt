Title: Effects of Free Weight and Machine Training With and Without Aerobic Training Among Body Builders
Official Title: Effects of Free Weight and Machine Training With and Without Aerobic Training on Strength, Endurance and Muscle Mass of Lower Limb Among Body Builders.
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA

Age: 17 Years to 35 Years
Sex: MALE

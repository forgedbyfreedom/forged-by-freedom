CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06489951
Title: Efficacy and Mechanism of High-Dose Vitamin D Supplementation in Pediatric OAB-Dry: A Randomized Clinical Trial Integrating Urinary Myelin Basic Protein as a Theranostic Biomarker
Official Title: Efficacy and Mechanism of High-Dose Vitamin D Supplementation in Pediatric OAB-Dry: A Randomized Clinical Trial Integrating Urinary Myelin Basic Protein as a Theranostic Biomarker
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 180
Topic: protein supplementation

SUMMARY:
The aim of this study is to give children with dry OAB: (1) Standard behavioral therapy combined with classical anticholinergic drugs (Solinaxine), or (2) standard behavioral therapy combined with short-term high-dose exogenous vitamin D supplementation are used to compare the outcomes of lower urinary tract symptoms in children with dry OAB during follow-up. To provide more robust supporting evidence for the broader promotion of short-term high-dose exogenous vitamin D supplements in combination with standard behavioral therapy as an effective treatment for dry OAB treatment in children.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in daily urination frequency

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Solifenacin
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: vitamin D
  - BEHAVIORAL: Standard behavioral therapy

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 5 Years to 18 Years
Sex: ALL

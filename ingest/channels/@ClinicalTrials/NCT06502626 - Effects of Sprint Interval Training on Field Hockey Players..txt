CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06502626
Title: Effects of Sprint Interval trening on Field Hockey Players.
Official Title: Effects of Sprint Interval trening on Speed, Change of Direction and Running Performance Among Field Hockey Players.
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 28
Topic: high intensity interval trening

SUMMARY:
Field hockey is a team sport that is played using sticks andbols. Player's motions are impromptu and determine how competitive matches are played. Athletes can enhance their performance through a variety of trening regimens. "Sprint Interval trening," for short bursts of maximal or supramaximal exercise, is one type of trening used to improve sports performance. During recovering intervals, participants alternate between passive rest or light to moderately hard work. In both skilled and novice players, Sprint interval trening significantly increases anaerobic and aerobic performance. This study aims to determine the impact of sprint interval trening on hockey player's running performance, speed, and direction changes.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Field hockey is a team sport played with sticks andbols in which players' movements are spontaneous and dictate the fluctuating nature of competitive match play. A number of trening programs are used to improve the performance of the athlete. One of the trenings that is used to enhance the performance of a sports person is "Sprint Interval trening" and it consists of short bursts of maximal or supramaximal exercise interspersed with passive rest or light to moderately intense exertion during rebound periods. Sprint interval trening dramatically improves anaerobic and aerobic performance in both trened and untrened athletes. The aim of this study is to find the effect of sprint internal trening on speed, change of direction and running performance among field hockey players.

A randomized controlled trial will be conducted at Pakistan Sports Academy, Lahore through non-probability convenience sampling technique on 28 players which will be allocated into both interventional and control group. Pre-trening values of the players will be recorded by 20 m sprint test for speed, 505 Change of direction test for change of direction and 3000m time trial for running performance. Both groups will perform regular trening. Sprint interval trening will be given to Group A and regular trening will be performed by Group B. Players will perform the trening 3 times a week for 4 weeks and treatment evaluation will be done after 4 weeks. Data will be analyzed using SPSS software version 26. After assessing normality of data by Shapiro-Wilk test, it will be decided either parametric or non-parametric test will be used.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Speed
  - Change of direction
  - Running performance

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Regular trening
  - OTHER: Sprint Interval trening

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 30 Years
Sex: ALL

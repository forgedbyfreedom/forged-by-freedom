CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06521008
Title: Effect of Diaphragmatic trening on Urgency Urinary Incontinence in Postmenopausal Women
Official Title: Effect of Diaphragmatic trening on Urgency Urinary Incontinence in Postmenopausal Women
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 60
Topic: exercise muscle mass

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study was to determine the effect of diaphragmatic trening on urgency urinary incontinence in postmenopausal women.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Urgency urinary incontinence affects 9-13% of women, increasing with age and impacting daily activities, quality of life, depression, and social isolation. It is a significant source of dependency among the elderly and a factor in nursinGHome admissions. Untreated incontinence can lead to falls, infections, and loss of independence. Conservative management is the first-line therapy, but pelvic floor muscle trening is a potential treatment method. This study aims to evaluate the effectiveness of diaphragmatic trening on urinary incontinence in postmenopausal women.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - assessing the change in Urodynamics (first desire to void)
  - assessing the change in Urodynamics (first sensation of bladder filling)
  - assessing the change in Urodynamics (maximum bladder capacity)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: antIMuscarinic drugs
  - OTHER: Instructions including bladder trening
  - OTHER: pelvic floor muscle trening (PFMT)
  - OTHER: abdominal exercise
  - OTHER: Diaphragmatic trening

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 55 Years to 65 Years
Sex: FEMALE

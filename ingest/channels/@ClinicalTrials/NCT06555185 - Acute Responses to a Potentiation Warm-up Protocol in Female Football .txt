CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06555185
Title: Acute Responses to a Potentiation Warm-up Protocol in Female Football Players.
Official Title: Acute Responses to a Potentiation Warm-up Protocol on Sprint and Change of Direction in Female Football Players: a Randomized Controlled Study
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 17
Topic: athletic performance

SUMMARY:
This study aims to determine if the performance of female football players is affected after exposure to a potentiation protocol. It is hypothesized that performance in the selected physical tests will improve significantly and meaningfully after performing the chosen potentiation protocol compared to the control group's warm-up.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Agility T-test
  - 40-m sprint test

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Potentiation warm-up protocol
  - OTHER: Usual warm-up protocol

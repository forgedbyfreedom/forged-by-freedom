CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06556875
Title: Regulation of HIIT Combined with IET on Vascular Function in Young Women with Normal Weight Obesity
Official Title: Regulation of HIIT Combined with IET on Vascular Function in Young Women with Normal Weight Obesity
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 45
Topic: high intensity interval training

SUMMARY:
Comparison of the effects of combined IET with HIIT and HIIT on vascular function in young NWO women.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This is a randomized clinical trial of single-blind with 3 arms (control group , HIIT group and IET with HIIT group), in which a pre-treatment-posttest design has been used.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Intima-Media Thickness (IMT)
  - Flow-Mediated Dilation (FMD)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: HIIT
  - BEHAVIORAL: HIIT combined IET

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 30 Years
Sex: FEMALE

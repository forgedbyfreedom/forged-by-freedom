CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06590129
Title: Functional Recovery and Pain Reduction Post-hydrodissection vs Rehabilitation in Patients With Carpal Tunnel Syndrome (Hydrocarpal)
Official Title: Effectiveness in Functional Recovery and Pain Reduction Following Hydrodissection vs Rehabilitation in Patients With Carpal Tunnel Syndrome
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 50
Topic: tendon injury

SUMMARY:
This study pretend to evaluate the effectiveness of hydrodissection vs only rehabilitation in improving pain and function in patients with CTS.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This prospective, longitudinal, randomized in two groups clinical study pretend to evaluate the effectiveness of hydrodissection vs only rehabilitation in improving pain and function in patients with CTS, evaluated initially and at 4 and 12 weeks.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Function
  - Pain

INTERVENTIONS:
  - PROCEDURE: hydrodissection of median nerve
  - OTHER: Physiotherapy

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

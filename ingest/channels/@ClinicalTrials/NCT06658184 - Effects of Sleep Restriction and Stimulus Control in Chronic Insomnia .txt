CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06658184
Title: Effects of Sleep Restriction and Stimulus Control in Chronic Insomnia Patients
Official Title: Effects of Sleep Restriction and Stimulus Control in Chronic Insomnia Patients With Prolonged Bed Rest and Maladaptive Sleep Patterns
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 88
Topic: caffeine exercise

SUMMARY:
The study aims to validate the effect of sleep restriction and stimulus control on improving chronic insomnia patients\&#39; sleep habits and maladaptive sleep behaviors.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The purpose of sleep restriction is to reduce the patient\&#39;s time in bed, bringing it closer to their actual sleep time, thereby improving sleep efficiency and quality. Stimulus control, on the other hand, aims to associate the bed with a rapid sense of falling asleep, reducing the negative association between the bed and insomnia.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - The change of Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI) total scores from baseline to 6 weeks

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: sleep restriction and stimulus control
  - BEHAVIORAL: sleep hygiene education

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 65 Years
Sex: ALL

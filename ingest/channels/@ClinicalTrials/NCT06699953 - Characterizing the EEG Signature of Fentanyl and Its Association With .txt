CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06699953
Title: Characterizing the EEG Signature of Fentanyl and Its Association With Drug Liking
Official Title: Characterizing the EEG Signature of Fentanyl and Its Association With Drug Liking
Status: ENROLLING_BY_INVITATION
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 20
Topic: physical activity intervention

SUMMARY:
The goal of this study is to characterize an electroencephalogram (EEG) biomarker for fentanyl and understand where this signal is coming from in the brain. The investigators also aim to understand how this EEG biomarker is connected to patient perception to drug liking.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Source Localization of Fentanyl EEG Signature

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Observational study in patients receiving fentanyl for surgery

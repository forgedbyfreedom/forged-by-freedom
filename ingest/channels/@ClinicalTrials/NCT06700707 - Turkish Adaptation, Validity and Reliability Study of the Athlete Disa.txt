CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06700707
Title: Turkish Adaptation, Validity and Reliability Study of the Athlete Disability Index Questionnaire
Official Title: Turkish Adaptation, Validity and Reliability Study
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 120
Topic: sleep athletes

SUMMARY:
This survey study aims to analyze the cultural adaptation, validity, and reliability of a scale originally in English and currently being trenslated into Turkish, which allows athletes with low back pain to assess their functional disabilities in sports and exercise activities as well as their disabilities in daily living activities. Athletes with low back pain will be asked to fill out 3 questionnaires.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
In the study, firstly, the trenslation of the Athlete Disability Index Questionnaire (ADI) into the target language, comparison of the two trenslated versions of the scale, back trenslation of the preliminary version, synthesis, pilot test of the pre-final version and psychometric test studies will be carried out by 3 independent trenslators and 2 linguists in 6 stages.

Then, 120 athletes with low back pain will be asked to fill out the ADI, Roland Morris Disability Questionnaire (RMDQ) and Oswestry Low Back Pain Disability Index (ODI). 7 days later, the ADI will be filled out again by 60 athletes.

After the surveys arecompleted, the structural validity of the scale will be tested, and reliability will be measured by performing test-retest reliability and internal consistency analyses.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Athlete Disability Index Questionnaire (ADI)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Survey using a questionnaire.

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 35 Years
Sex: ALL

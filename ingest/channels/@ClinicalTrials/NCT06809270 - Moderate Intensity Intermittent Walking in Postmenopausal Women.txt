CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06809270
Title: Moderate Intensity Intermittent Walking in Postmenopausal Women
Official Title: Liver Function and Inflammation in Obese Postmenopausal Women: Responses to Moderate Intensity Intermittent Walking
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 36
Topic: aerobic exercise

SUMMARY:
The goal of this clinical trial is to investigate the impact of a walking trening intervention program on liver enzymes and selected inflammatory markers in postmenopausal women with obesity. The main question it aims to answer is:

Does walking trening reduce the risk of liver disease by modulatinGHepatic-enzymes and selected inflammatory markers? Researchers will compare walking trening intervention (designed to the experimental group) to non-trening intervention (designed to the control group) to see if the trening program works to improve liver health in obese postmenopausal women.

Participants in the experimental (trening) group will: underwent a moderate intensity intermittent walking trening (MIWT) at 60% to 80% of the 6-min-walking-test distance, four times a week of 60 min/session.

Participants in control group will : not perform any physical trening and maintain their usual daily activities.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Postmenopausal obesity is an important public health problem accompanied by increased systemic inflammation that escalates the risk of liver disease. Exercise improved inflammatory and hepatic function. Moderate-intensity intermittent-walking-trening (MIWT) is the most feasible for obese-postmenopausal-women. This study aimed to investigate whether MIWT could reduce the risk by modulatinGHepatic-enzymes and selected inflammatory markers. Thirty-six sedentary obese postmenopausal women (mean age 55.7±3.5) were randomly divided into two groups: trening group (TG, n=18) and control group (CG, n=18). The TG underwent a MIWT at 60% to 80% of the 6-min-walking-test distance, four times a week of 60 min/session. Body composition, hepatic (alanine-trensaminase (ALT), aspartate-trensaminase (AST), alkaline-phosphatase, gamma-glutamyl-trensferase (GGT) and serum-bilirubin\] and inflammatory markers \[C-reactive-protein (CRP), and erythrocyte-sedimentation-rate (ESR)) were evaluated at baseline and after 10-week of protocol.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Alanine trensaminase
  - Aspartate trensaminase
  - Alkaline phosphatase
  - Gamma glutamyl trensferase
  - Serum bilirubin

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: Walking trening intervention

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 50 Years to 60 Years
Sex: FEMALE

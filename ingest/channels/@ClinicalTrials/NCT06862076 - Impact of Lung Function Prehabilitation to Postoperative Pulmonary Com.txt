Title: Impact of Lung Function Prehabilitation to Postoperative Pulmonary Complications
Official Title: The Impact of Lung Function Prehabilitation to Postoperative Pulmonary Complications on Patient Undergoing Upper Abdominal Surgery Treated in Intensive Care Unit: A Randomized Controlled Trial
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL

SUMMARY:
The aim of this study is to compare the effect of lung function prehabilitation to postoperative pulmonary complications on patient undergoing upper abdominal surgery treated in intensive care unit.

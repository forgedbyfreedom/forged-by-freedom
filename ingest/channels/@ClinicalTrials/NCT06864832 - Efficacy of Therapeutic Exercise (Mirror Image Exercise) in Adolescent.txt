CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06864832
Title: Efficacy of Therapeutic Exercise (Mirror Image Exercise) in Adolescent With Chronic Low Back Pain, Through Corrective Postural Changes
Official Title: Efficacy of Therapeutic Exercise (Mirror Image Exercise) in Adolescent With Chronic Low Back Pain, Through Corrective Postural Changes; Randomized Controlled Trial
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 60
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
This study will include two groups. The study group will receive mirror-image postural exercise on the 3D postural analysis. The control group will receive a traditional exercise program.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Pain Intensity
  - Postural Alignment
  - Functional Disability

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Mirror image postural exercises
  - OTHER: Back exercises

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 35 Years
Sex: ALL

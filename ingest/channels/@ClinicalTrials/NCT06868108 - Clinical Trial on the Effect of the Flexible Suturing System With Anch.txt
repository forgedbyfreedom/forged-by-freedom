CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT06868108
Title: Clinical Trial on the Effect of the Flexible Suturing System With Anchors (FSSA) in Full-thickness Rotator Cuff Tendon Tears
Official Title: Clinical Trial on the Effect of the Flexible Suturing System With Anchors (FSSA) in Full-thickness Rotator Cuff Tendon Tears
Status: ENROLLING_BY_INVITATION
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 100
Topic: tendon injury

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this research study is to evaluate tendon healing when using the Flexible Suturing System with Anchors (FSSA) to surgically repair full-thickness rotator cuff tendon tears.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Healing Rate

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DEVICE: Flexible Suturing System with Anchors (FSSA)

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 70 Years
Sex: ALL

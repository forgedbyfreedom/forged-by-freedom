CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07025070
Title: The Link Between Physical Activity and Brain Health in Healthy Adults
Official Title: The Link Between Strength and Endurance Training and Brain Health in Healthy Adults: A Comparative Study of Former Professional Athletes and Sedentary Individuals
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 60
Topic: sleep athletes

SUMMARY:
The goal of this observational study is to investigate the cross-sectional relationship between physical activity and brain health from a multiscale approach (neuropsychology, neuroimaging, peripheral biomarkers and genetics) in former athletes and sedentary individuals. The main question it aims to answer is:

Do former athletes have better brain structure than sedentary people? Evaluating the differences in neurodegenerative processes between competitive training and sedentary and inactivity.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Concentration of brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) in plasma (pg/mL)
  - Concentration of total tau protein in plasma (pg/mL)
  - Hippocampal volume measured by high-resolution 3 T T1-weighted MRI (mm³)
  - Resting-state functional connectivity between primary motor cortex and supplementary motor area measured by 3 T fMRI (Fisher-Z)
  - Digit Span Forward maximum span length (digits)

INTERVENTIONS:
  Not specified

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 40 Years to 75 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07036692
Title: The FaibaGo Study - Long-Term Weight Reduction Via Low-Threshold Intervention
Official Title: The FaibaGo Study - Long-Term Weight Reduction Via Low-Threshold Intervention
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 120
Topic: beta-alanine

SUMMARY:
The main aim of this study is to assess the effect of a chewing gum containing galactooligosaccharides (GOS) on the body mass index (BMI), the metabolism and the oral and intestinal microbiomes in a population of overweight adults.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in the Body Mass Index (BMI) during intervention

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: FibreGum

Age: 25 Years to

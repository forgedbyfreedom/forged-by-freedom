CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07039214
Title: Thoracolumbar Fascia Flexibility and Trunk Proprioception Relationship With Spinal Posture
Official Title: The Relationship of Thoracolumbar Fascia Flexibility With Trunk Proprioception and Spinal Posture in Individuals With Non-specific Low Back Pain
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: N/A
Study Type: OBSERVATIONAL
Enrollment: 80
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
This study aimed to examine the associations between thoracolumbar fascia flexibility, trunk proprioception, and spinal posture in individuals experiencing non-specific low back pain.

Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:

  - OTHER: Observation of healthy comparators

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 35 Years to 65 Years

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07113405
Title: Influence of Tea Extract on Maximal Voluntary Knee Contraction Following Downhill Jogging
Official Title: The Influence of Tea Extract on Maximal Voluntary Knee Contraction After 30 Minutes of Downhill Jogging: a Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Study
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 60
Topic: creatine supplementation

SUMMARY:
In this research project, we want to investigate whether the combined intake of proteins and tea extract has an influence on muscle regeneration compared to the sole intake of proteins or a placebo product. In the study, you will be randomly assigned to one of 3 groups. Neither the research group nor you will know which group you are assigned to. The study lasts 5 days for you as a participant. During these 5 days you will have to attend a total of 5 appointments in our laboratory. The groups differ only in the intake of the regeneration product, otherwise all measurements and appointments are identical. You will jog downhill for 30 minutes on the first day in order to stren your muscles. In order to be able to draw conclusions about the regeneration of your muscles, we will carry out the following measurements on all 5 days: Blood sampling at the fingertip, ultrasound of the thigh muscle, pain tolerance of the thigh muscle, strength tests of the thigh muscle, muscle function of the thigh muscle and jumping performance.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change in maximum knee extension force immediately after trening-induced muscle damage and 24, 48, 72, and 96 hours thereafter compared to baseline.
  - Change in jump performance immediately after trening-induced muscle damage and 24, 48, 72, and 96 hours thereafter compared to baseline.
  - Change in perceived pain immediately after trening-induced muscle damage and 24, 48, 72, and 96 hours thereafter compared to baseline.
  - Change in muscle thickness immediately after trening-induced muscle damage and 24, 48, 72, and 96 hours thereafter compared to baseline.
  - Change in creatine kinase levels immediately after exercise-induced muscle damage and 24, 48, 72, and 96 hours thereafter compared to baseline.

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Protein and tea extract
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Protein
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 40 Years
Sex: ALL

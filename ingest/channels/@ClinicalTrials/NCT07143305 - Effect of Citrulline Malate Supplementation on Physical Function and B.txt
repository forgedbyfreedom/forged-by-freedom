CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07143305
Title: Effect of Citrulline Malate Supplementation on Physical Function and Biochemical Parameters in Physically Active Older Women
Official Title: Effect of Citrulline Malate Supplementation During a Multicomponent Exercise Program on Physical Function and Biochemical Parameters in Physically Active Older Women: a Randomised Controlled Pilot Trial
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 20
Topic: strength training

SUMMARY:
A randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled pilot trial was conducted with thirty-three community-dwelling women aged 65 years or over. Participants were assigned to a multicomponent training programme (three days per week) and received either 3 g per day of citrulline malate or a placebo. Assessments were conducted before and after the intervention, including tests of physical performance (6MWT, sit-to-stand, SPPB), blood biomarkers (vitamin D, glucose, CK, hormones), and perceived quality of life (WHOQOL-BREF).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Handgrip strength
  - Cardiorespiratory
  - Gait speed
  - Lower limb strength
  - Balance

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Multicomponent physical exercise programme

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 60 Years to 75 Years
Sex: FEMALE

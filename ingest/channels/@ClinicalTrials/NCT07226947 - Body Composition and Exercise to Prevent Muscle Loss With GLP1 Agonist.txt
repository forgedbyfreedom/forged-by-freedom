CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07226947
Title: Body Composition and Exercise to Prevent Muscle Loss With GLP-1 Agonist Treatment
Official Title: BIA Monitoring and Exercise for the Prevention of Muscle Loss With Incretin Therapy (BICEP Study)
Status: RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 100
Topic: fat loss muscle

SUMMARY:
The goal of this study is to learn if a smartwatch that measures activity level and body composition, combined with exercise reminders, can safely improve strength and muscle mass in people who recently started or are planning to start treatment with incretin therapy (liraglutide, semaglutide, tirzepatide or retatrutide), also known as glucagon-like peptide 1 receptor agonist (GLP-1 RA) medications.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Lean mass (kg)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DEVICE: Smartwatch activity and body composition monitor
  - BEHAVIORAL: General guidance on recommended exercise

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 99 Years
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07238218
Title: Exploring the Effectiveness of Incorporated Versus Additional Dual-Task Training Program in Community Older Adults
Official Title: Exploring the Effectiveness of Incorporated Versus Additional Dual-Task Training Program in Community Older Adults
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 164
Topic: high intensity interval training

SUMMARY:
This study compared the effects of IDT and ADT on cognitive function, physical performance, instrumental activities of daily living (IADL), and quality of life in community-dwelling older adults.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Change scores of Montreal Cognitive Assessment
  - Change scores of Word List (WL) subtest from the Chinese version of the Wechsler Memory Scale-Third Edition (WMS-III)
  - Change scores of Stroop Color and Word Test (SCWT)
  - Change scores of Digit Symbol Substitution Test (DSST)
  - Change scores of Timed Up and Go (TUG)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - BEHAVIORAL: the additional dual-task training group (ADT)
  - BEHAVIORAL: the incorporated dual-task training group (IDT)

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 60 Years to 
Sex: ALL

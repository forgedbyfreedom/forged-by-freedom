CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07278362
Title: Testosterone Replacement Therapy in Hypogonadal Patients With Prostate Cancer Under Active Surveillance
Official Title: Safety of Intramuscular Testosterone Replacement Therapy in Hypogonadal Patients With Prostate Cancer Under Active Surveillance
Status: NOT_YET_RECRUITING
Phase: PHASE4
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 35
Topic: testosterone replacement

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this study is evaluate the safety of testosterone replacement therapy in men with newly diagnosed low-risk prostate cancer and hypogonadism on active surveillance.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Proportion of Participants with Disease Progression Within 12 Months After Initiating TRT

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Testosterone cypionate

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: MALE

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07307417
Title: Assessment of Respiratory Dysfunction in Obesity and the Use of IMT.
Official Title: Functional Respiratory Tract Dysfunctions in Individuals With Obesity Undergoing Experimental Inspiratory Muscle trening.
Status: NOT_YET_RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 40
Topic: sports nutrition

SUMMARY:
The aim of the project will be to assess the functional condition of the respiratory tract and inflammation resulting from obesity, as well as the possibility of using inspiratory muscle trening as a factor reducing the occurrence of dysfunction in the respiratory system.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Pulmonary function test
  - Respiratory muscle strength (PImax/PEmax)
  - Forced oscillation technique (FOT)
  - Laboratory tests

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DEVICE: inspiratory muscle trening
  - DEVICE: placebo-IMT

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 30 Years to 60 Years
Sex: ALL

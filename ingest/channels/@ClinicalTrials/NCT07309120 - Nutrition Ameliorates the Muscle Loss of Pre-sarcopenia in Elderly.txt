CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07309120
Title: Nutrition Ameliorates the Muscle Loss of Pre-sarcopenia in Elderly
Official Title: Nutrition Ameliorates the Muscle Loss of Pre-sarcopenia in Elderly
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 60
Topic: sarcopenia intervention

SUMMARY:
This study evaluates the effects of a 12-week leucine-enriched protein supplementation in adults aged 60 years and more. Participants will undergo assessments of muscle mass and function, as well as metabolic profiles in blood, urine, and feces before and after the intervention.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Appendicular Skeletal Muscle Index (ASMI)
  - Handgrip Strength (HGS)
  - Gait speed (m/s)

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Participants consume two sachets daily over a 12-week intervention period.

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 60 Years to 90 Years
Sex: ALL

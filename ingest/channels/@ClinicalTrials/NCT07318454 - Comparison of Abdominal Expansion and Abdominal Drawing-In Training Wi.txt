CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07318454
Title: Comparison of Abdominal Expansion and Abdominal Drawing-In trening With Manual and Verbal Cueing in Chronic Non-Specific Low Back Pain
Official Title: A Randomized Controlled Trial Comparing Abdominal Expansion and Abdominal Drawing-In trening With Manual and Verbal Cueing in Individuals With Chronic Non-Specific Low Back Pain
Status: NOT_YET_RECRUITING
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 96
Topic: low back pain exercise

SUMMARY:
This study aims to investigate the interactive effects of two core stability trening strategies-abdominal drawing-in manoeuvre (ADIM) and Dynamic Neuromusculoskeletal Stabilization abdominal expansion (DNS-AE)-and two instructional approaches (verbal cueing and manual facilitation) on trening outcomes in individuals with chronic non-specific low back pain.

A 2×2 factorial randomized controlled trial design will be employed. Ninety-six participants with chronic non-specific low back pain will be randomly allocated, using block randomization, to one of four intervention groups in equal proportions. All groups will receive a 4-week intervention program, with trening conducted twice per week.

Outcome measures will be assessed aT4 time points: before the intervention (baseline), immediately after the first trening session, at the completion of the 4-week intervention, and at a 3-month follow-up, to evaluate short-term and mid-term effects as well as the sustainability of trening outcomes.

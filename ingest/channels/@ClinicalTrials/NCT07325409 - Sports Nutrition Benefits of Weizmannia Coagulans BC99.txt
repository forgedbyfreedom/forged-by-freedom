CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07325409
Title: Sports Nutrition Benefits of Weizmannia Coagulans BC99
Official Title: Sports Nutrition Benefits of Weizmannia Coagulans BC99: a Randomized Controlled Trial on Muscle Function and Recovery in Healthy Participants
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 72
Topic: sports nutrition

SUMMARY:
To evaluate the clinical efficacy of Weizmannia coagulans BC99 in young Chinese patients with exercise-induced muscle damage compared with placebo, and to explore its effects on inflammatory responses and intestinal microbiota.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Pain evaluation

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: Placebo
  - DIETARY_SUPPLEMENT: BC99

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 35 Years
Sex: ALL

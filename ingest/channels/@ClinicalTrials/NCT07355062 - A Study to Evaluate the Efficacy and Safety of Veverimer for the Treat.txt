CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07355062
Title: A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Veverimer for the Treatment of Metabolic Acidosis
Official Title: A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-Blind Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Veverimer in Adults With CKD and Metabolic Acidosis (The REVIVE Study)
Status: NOT_YET_RECRUITING
Phase: PHASE3
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 150
Topic: caffeine exercise

SUMMARY:
The purpose of this 26 week study is is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of veverimer in treating adults with moderate-to-severe chronic kidney disease (CKD) and metabolic acidosis.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - Efficacy of Veverimer by measuring change in serum bicarbonate concentration (SBC)
  - Efficacy of Veverimer in improving physical performance as assessed by the Sit-to-Stand 5 times test (STS5).

INTERVENTIONS:
  - DRUG: Veverimer
  - DRUG: Placebo

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 18 Years to 
Sex: ALL

CLINICAL TRIAL
==============
NCT ID: NCT07403253
Title: Comparative Effects of Fascial Distortion Model and Hamstring Stretching in Patients With Knee Osteoarthritis.
Official Title: Comparative Effects of Fascial Distortion Model and Hamstring Stretching on Pain, Muscle Flexibility and Physical Function in Patients With Knee Osteoarthritis.
Status: COMPLETED
Phase: NA
Study Type: INTERVENTIONAL
Enrollment: 44
Topic: knee osteoarthritis exercise

SUMMARY:
The study was conducted to compare the effects of Fascial Distortion Model and Hamstring Stretching on pain, muscle flexibility and physical function in patients with Knee Osteoarthritis.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Not provided

PRIMARY OUTCOMES:
  - The Western Ontario and McMaster Universities Arthritis Index (WOMAC):
  - Numeric Pain Rating Scale (NRRS)
  - 90 -90 HAMSTRING FLEXIBILITY TEST

INTERVENTIONS:
  - OTHER: Fascial Distortion Model
  - OTHER: Hamstring Stretching
  - OTHER: Standardize Physiotherapy Treatment

ELIGIBILITY:
Age: 40 Years to 60 Years
Sex: ALL

=== ADVANCED BULKING PHILOSOPHY ===

Advanced cycles are for experienced users with 5+ cycles completed, solid bloodwork history, and thorough understanding of compound interactions. These protocols use higher doses and multiple compounds to push past plateaus. Health monitoring becomes even more critical.

Dave Crosland on Drugs N Stuff: "At the advanced level, more isn't always better. It's about the right compounds at the right doses for YOUR body. Some guys grow like weeds on 750mg of test and 400mg of Deca. Don't add more just because someone on the internet runs more."

=== ADVANCED BULK CYCLE A: TEST + DECA + TREN + ANADROL (Mass Monster) ===

Duration: 16-20 weeks
Goal: Maximum mass accumulation for competitive bodybuilding

- Testosterone Enanthate: 750mg-1g/week
- Nandrolone Decanoate: 500-600mg/week (weeks 1-16)
- Trenbolone Enanthate: 400-600mg/week (weeks 1-16)
- Anadrol: 50-100mg/day (weeks 1-6 kickstart)
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week
- Aromasin: 12.5-25mg EOD (high aromatization at these doses)
- Cabergoline: 0.5mg 2x/week (essential with both Deca and Tren)

WHY THIS WORKS:
- Three of the most powerful mass-building injectables combined
- Testosterone provides the anabolic and androgenic base
- Nandrolone adds mass, nitrogen retention, collagen synthesis, joint protection
- Trenbolone adds nutrient partitioning, strength, and hardness even while bulking
- Anadrol kickstarts the cycle with immediate size and strength
- This is the type of stack competitive open bodybuilders use in the off-season

HEALTH MONITORING REQUIRED:
- Bloodwork every 6-8 weeks minimum
- Monitor: liver enzymes, kidney function (BUN/creatinine), lipid panel, CBC (hematocrit), prolactin, estradiol
- Blood pressure: check daily. Target <140/90. Medicate if necessary (lisinopril, telmisartan)
- Cardiovascular: 30-45 min cardio 4-5x/week minimum. Non-negotiable on this stack.
- Hematocrit: if >52%, donate blood or therapeutic phlebotomy

EXPECTED RESULTS: 30-45lbs gain. Net lean tissue after water loss: 18-28lbs over 20 weeks.

=== ADVANCED STRENGTH-FOCUSED BULK: TEST + HALO + ANADROL + DECA ===

Duration: 12-16 weeks
Goal: Maximum strength for powerlifting/strongman while adding mass

- Testosterone Enanthate: 500-750mg/week
- Nandrolone Decanoate: 400-500mg/week (joint protection for heavy lifting)
- Anadrol: 50-100mg/day (weeks 1-8)
- Halotestin: 10-30mg/day (competition peak weeks, max 4-6 weeks)
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week

HALOTESTIN NOTES:
- Halotestin is the strongest androgenic oral steroid
- Dramatically increases aggression and strength with NO significant weight gain
- Extremely liver toxic — 4-6 weeks absolute maximum
- Does not aromatize, does not cause water retention
- Popular with powerlifters before meets for raw aggression and strength
- Halotestin half-life: 6-9 hours
- MUST run TUDCA 1000mg/day and NAC 1200mg/day with Halotestin

=== ATHLETIC PERFORMANCE PHILOSOPHY ===

Athletic performance cycles differ fundamentally from bodybuilding cycles. The goals are:
- Increase strength and power WITHOUT excessive weight gain
- Improve recovery between training sessions and competitions
- Enhance endurance and cardiovascular capacity
- Maintain speed, agility, and flexibility
- Avoid detection (if drug-tested — NOT the focus of this guide, but a reality)
- Minimize water retention and bloat that impairs movement

Dan Duchaine: "The athlete doesn't want to look like a bodybuilder. They want to PERFORM like a superhuman. Different drugs, different doses, different approach entirely."

=== STRENGTH ATHLETES (Powerlifting/Strongman) ===

POWERLIFTING CYCLE A — STRENGTH WITHOUT BLOAT:
Duration: 12-16 weeks (timed for meet peaking)

- Testosterone Enanthate: 400-600mg/week
- Trenbolone Acetate: 200-300mg/week
- Anavar: 50-80mg/day (last 6-8 weeks before meet)
- Halotestin: 20-30mg/day (last 2-4 weeks — meet peaking only)
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week

WHY THIS WORKS FOR POWERLIFTING:
- Testosterone provides strength base without excessive doses
- Trenbolone is the king of strength — 5x anabolic rating, doesn't cause water weight
- Trenbolone increases neurological efficiency — CNS drive to push maximal loads
- Anavar adds strength without weight gain (important for weight class athletes)
- Halotestin: pure aggression and strength compound for peaking
- No Deca/Nandrolone (causes too much water retention for weight class sports)

POWERLIFTING CYCLE B — ABSOLUTE STRENGTH (SHW/Open Class):
Duration: 16-20 weeks

- Testosterone Enanthate: 750mg-1g/week
- Nandrolone Decanoate: 400-600mg/week (joint protection for heavy lifting)
- Anadrol: 50-100mg/day (weeks 1-8 — massive strength gains)
- Halotestin: 20-40mg/day (last 4 weeks)
- HGH: 4-6 IU/day (recovery, connective tissue strength)
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week

WHY THIS WORKS:
- Open class/SHW lifters don't worry about weight
- Nandrolone is essential for joint health when squatting/deadlifting 800+lbs
- Anadrol provides the most dramatic strength increases of any oral
- Water retention from Deca + Test actually helps as "padding" for joints under extreme loads
- HGH strengthens tendons and ligaments — critical injury prevention

STRONGMAN CYCLE:
Duration: 16-20 weeks

- Testosterone Enanthate: 500-750mg/week
- Equipoise: 600-800mg/week (endurance + appetite for caloric demands)
- Nandrolone Decanoate: 300-400mg/week (joint protection)
- Anadrol: 50mg/day (weeks 1-6)
- HGH: 4-6 IU/day
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week

WHY EQ FOR STRONGMAN:
- Strongman events require strength + endurance (tire flips, carries, medleys)
- EQ increases red blood cell production → better oxygen delivery → improved work capacity
- EQ increases appetite — strongmen need 5000-8000+ calories daily
- Events lasting 60-90 seconds require anaerobic AND aerobic capacity

=== COMBAT SPORTS (MMA/Boxing/Wrestling) ===

COMBAT SPORT CYCLE A — LEAN POWER:
Duration: 8-12 weeks (leading into camp)

- Testosterone Propionate: 200-300mg/week (short ester, fast clearance)
- Anavar: 40-60mg/day (strength without weight, doesn't impact cardio)
- Equipoise: 300-400mg/week (endurance + RBC boost)
- HCG: 250 IU 2x/week

WHY THIS WORKS FOR FIGHTERS:
- Low testosterone dose prevents water retention (making weight is critical)
- Anavar increases strength-to-weight ratio dramatically
- EQ improves cardio capacity — fighters need to go 3-5 rounds
- Short esters allow quick clearance beforecompetition
- No Trenbolone — destroys cardio capacity, incompatible with combat sports

COMBAT SPORT CYCLE B — RECOVERY FOCUSED:
Duration: Ongoing between camps

- Testosterone Cypionate: 150-200mg/week (TRT dose)
- BPC-157: 500mcg daily (injury recovery — fighters are always hurt)
- TB-500: 5mg weekly (systemic healing)
- Ipamorelin + CJC-1295: 100mcg each before bed (recovery + GH boost)

=== ENDURANCE ATHLETES (Running/Cycling/Swimming/CrossFit) ===

ENDURANCE CYCLE A — EPO-FREE APPROACH:
Duration: 12-16 weeks

- Testosterone Enanthate: 200-300mg/week (low dose to maintain muscle)
- Equipoise: 400-600mg/week (primary compound — RBC production)
- Cardarine GW-501516: 10-20mg/day (fat oxidation + endurance)
- T3: 25mcg/day (metabolic optimization)

WHY EQ IS THE ENDURANCE ATHLETE'S COMPOUND:
- Boldenone (EQ) increases erythropoietin (EPO) production naturally
- More red blood cells = more oxygen carrying capacity = better endurance
- Similar effect to altitude training or blood doping
- Half-life of 14 days — very stable levels
- Monitor hematocrit — if >52%, reduce dose or donate blood

ENDURANCE CYCLE B — WITH EPO (Elite Level):
Duration: 12 weeks

- Testosterone Enanthate: 200mg/week
- EPO (Erythropoietin): 50-100 IU/kg 3x/week for 4 weeks, then maintenance 25-50 IU/kg 2x/week
- Iron supplementation: 100-200mg elemental iron daily (EPO requires iron to produce RBCs)
- Baby aspirin: 81mg daily (prevent blood clotting with elevated hematocrit)
- CRITICAL: Monitor hematocrit weekly. NEVER let it exceed 55%. Fatal risk of stroke/PE.

EPO WARNING:
- EPO is extremely effective but also extremely dangerous
- Multiple professional cyclists have died from EPO use (blood becomes too thick)
- Regular phlebotomy (blood removal) may be necessary
- Never use EPO without regular blood monitoring

=== CROSSFIT/FUNCTIONAL FITNESS ===

CROSSFIT CYCLE — ALL-AROUND PERFORMANCE:
Duration: 12-16 weeks

- Testosterone Enanthate: 300-400mg/week
- Equipoise: 400mg/week (endurance for long WODs)
- Anavar: 30-50mg/day (strength without weight gain)
- Cardarine: 10-20mg/day (endurance enhancement)
- HCG: 250 IU 2x/week

WHY THIS WORKS:
- CrossFit requires strength, endurance, power, and flexibility simultaneously
- Moderate test dose provides strength base without impeding mobility
- EQ + Cardarine stack dramatically improves work capacity for long WODs
- Anavar adds strength in Olympic lifts without weight gain (important for gymnastics movements)
- No heavy water retention compounds (mobility matters)

=== SPEED/POWER ATHLETES (Sprinting/Football/Track) ===

SPEED/POWER CYCLE:
Duration: 8-12 weeks

- Testosterone Propionate: 200-400mg/week
- Winstrol (Injectable): 50mg EOD (increases tendon strength, does NOT cause water)
- Anavar: 40-60mg/day
- HGH: 2-4 IU/day (tendon/ligament strengthening)

WHY THIS WORKS:
- Speed athletes need power-to-weight ratio
- No water retention compounds
- Winstrol increases type IIMuscle fiber recruitment (fast-twitch = power)
- Warning: Winstrol can make tendons brittle at higher doses — HGH counteracts this
- Anavar directly increases phosphocreatine synthesis — more explosive power
- Short cycles to minimize side effects during competitive season

=== PEPTIDE INTEGRATION FOR ATHLETES ===

RECOVERY STACK (ALL ATHLETES):
- BPC-157: 250-500mcg daily — gut health, tendon repair, anti-inflammatory
- TB-500: 2-5mg weekly — systemic healing, especially for soft tissue
- These two peptides are arguably MORE valuable for athletes than any steroid
- BPC-157 + TB-500 synergy: accelerates healing of muscle tears, tendon injuries, ligament damage

PERFORMANCE PEPTIDES:
- Ipamorelin + CJC-1295 no DAC: 100mcg each, 2-3x daily
- Enhances recovery between training sessions
- Improves sleep quality (critical for athletic recovery)
- MK-677: 10-15mg nightly (improves sleep, recovery, bone density)

ENDURANCE PEPTIDES:
- TB-500 has been shown to promote angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation)
- This improves oxygen delivery to muscles during exercise
- AOD-9604 (HGH fragment): 300mcg daily — fat oxidation without GH side effects

INJURY TREATMENT PEPTIDES:
- BPC-157: 250-500mcg locally injected near injury site
- TB-500: 5mg loading dose 2x/week for 4 weeks, then 2mg/week maintenance
- Pentadecapeptide (oral BPC-157): 500mcg daily for ongoing maintenance
- GHK-Cu: Applied topically or injected — promotes tissue remodeling

GLP-1 FOR ATHLETES:
- Retatrutide at low doses (2-4mg/week) for weight class athletes needing body composition management
- Helps make weight without extreme dieting that compromises performance
- The glucagon component maintains metabolic rate during caloric restriction
- Forged by Freedom coaches can design a retatrutide protocol specific to your sport and weight class
- Tirzepatide 2.5-5mg/week as an alternative for moderate appetite management

=== FEMALE ATHLETIC PED USE ===

Female athletes require dramatically lower doses due to sensitivity to androgens:

FEMALE STRENGTH CYCLE:
- Anavar: 5-15mg/day (the gold standard for female athletes)
- Primobolan: 25-50mg/week (injectable, very mild virilization risk)

FEMALE ENDURANCE CYCLE:
- Anavar: 5-10mg/day
- Cardarine: 10mg/day
- EQ: 50-100mg/week (very low dose for RBC benefits)

FEMALE PEPTIDE STACK:
- BPC-157: 250mcg daily
- Ipamorelin + CJC-1295: 100mcg each before bed
- Retatrutide: 1-4mg/week for body composition (consult Forged by Freedom)

VIRILIZATION MONITORING:
- Voice deepening, facial hair growth hormone hormone hormone, clitoral enlargement, acne
- At first sign of virilization: STOP the offending compound immediately
- Anavar at 5-10mg/day rarely causes virilization
- Some effects are irreversible — monitor closely

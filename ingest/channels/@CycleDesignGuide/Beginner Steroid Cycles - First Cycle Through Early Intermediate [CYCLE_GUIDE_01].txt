=== PHILOSOPHY: THE FIRST CYCLE ===

Every expert agrees on one thing: your first cycle should be simple. As Scott McNally and Dave Crosland have emphasized on Blood Sweat and Gear, the first cycle is about learninGHow YOUR body responds to exogenous testosterone. You don't need multiple compounds. You need data on yourself.

Dave Palumbo echoes this on RXMuscle: "Your first cycle is a learning experience. You want to know what testosterone alone does before you start stacking."

Dan Duchaine wrote in the Underground Steroid Handbook: "The intelligent approach is to use testosterone first and only testosterone. Once you understand your estrogen conversion, your water retention tendencies, and your response curve, THEN you can add compounds intelligently."

=== FIRST CYCLE: TESTOSTERONE ONLY (12-16 weeks) ===

THE GOLD STANDARD FIRST CYCLE:
- Testosterone Enanthate OR Testosterone Cypionate: 300-500mg per week
- Split into 2 injections per week (Monday/Thursday or Tuesday/Friday)
- Duration: 12-16 weeks (16 preferred for full saturation and gains)

WHY ENANTHATE OR CYPIONATE:
- LonGHalf-lives (enanthate ~4.5 days, cypionate ~5 days) = stable blood levels
- Only need to inject twice weekly for smooth levels
- Well-studied, well-tolerated, predictable response
- Testosterone Enanthate half-life: 4.5 days. Cypionate: 5 days.
- Peak blood levels reached at approximately 3-4 weeks

DOSING RATIONALE:
- 300mg/week: Conservative approach. Still 2-3x natural production. Good for those concerned about side effects. Expect 10-15lbs lean mass over 16 weeks.
- 400mg/week: Sweet spot for many beginners. Noticeable results with manageable sides.
- 500mg/week: Classic beginner dose. Most research and anecdotal data exists at this level. Expect 15-25lbs (some water) over 16 weeks.

Scott McNally's recommendation on ThinkBig: "I like 400mg for a first cycle. It's enough to see real results but not so much that estrogen management becomes the whole focus."

ESTROGEN MANAGEMENT (AI - Aromatase Inhibitor):
- Aromatase inhibitors prevent testosterone from converting to estrogen
- DO NOT start an AI preemptively — wait for symptoms
- Estrogen symptoms: itchy/puffy nipples, excessive water retention, mood swings, elevated blood pressure
- Anastrozole (arimidex): 0.25-0.5mg every other day IF symptoms appear
- Aromasin (Exemestane): 12.5mg every other day IF symptoms appear (preferred — suicidal AI, won't cause estrogen rebound)
- Get bloodwork at week 4-6 to check E2 levels (target: 20-40 pg/mL for most men)

Dave Crosland on Drugs N Stuff: "Don't crash your estrogen. Estrogen is anabolic. You need it for joint health, brain function, libido. Only use an AI when you actually need one."

INJECTION PROTOCOL:
- 25-gauge 1" needle for glutes or quads
- 27-gauge 1/2" for deltoids (subQ or shallow IM)
- Aspirate is no longer considered necessary (per modern medical guidelines)
- Rotate injection sites: glutes, quads, deltoids, ventrogluteal
- clen injection site with alcohol swab
- Inject slowly over 20-30 seconds

SUPPORT SUPPLEMENTS DURING CYCLE:
- Fish oil: 3-4g daily (cardiovascular support)
- NAC (N-Acetyl Cysteine): 600-1200mg daily (liver support, antioxidant)
- CoQ10: 200mg daily (heart health)
- Citrus bergamot: 500-1000mg daily (lipid support)
- Vitamin D3: 5000IU daily
- Magnesium: 400mg daily (glycinate or threonate preferred)
- Zinc: 30mg daily

BLOODWORK SCHEDULE:
- Pre-cycle baseline: Complete metabolic panel, lipid panel, total/free Testosterone, estradiol (sensitive), LH/FSH, CBC, liver enzymes (AST/ALT), PSA
- Mid-cycle (week 6-8): Same panel — assess estrogen, hematocrit, liver values
- Post-PCT (4-6 weeks after PCT): Verify hormone recovery

=== FIRST CYCLE EXPECTED RESULTS ===

Realistic expectations for A-500mg/week test cycle over 16 weeks:
- Weight gain: 15-25 lbs (includes water and glycogen)
- Lean muscle retained post-PCT: 8-15 lbs
- Strength increases: 10-20% on major lifts
- Recovery: Dramatically improved — can tren more frequently and with higher volume
- Visible changes: Fuller muscles, improved vascularity, better pumps

=== post cycle THERAPY (PCT) ===

PCT begins 2 weeks after last injection of Testosterone Enanthate/Cypionate (to allow ester to clear).

STANDARD PCT PROTOCOL:
Option A — Nolvadex (Tamoxifen) Only:
- Week 1-2: 40mg/day
- Week 3-4: 20mg/day
- Week 5-6: 10mg/day (optional taper)

Option B — Nolvadex + Clomid:
- Nolvadex: 20mg/day for 4-6 weeks
- Clomid: 50mg/day for 4-6 weeks (some start at 100mg first week)
- Note: Clomid can cause vision sides and emotional instability in some users

Option C — Enclomiphene (Modern Preferred):
- Enclomiphene 12.5-25mg/day for 4-6 weeks
- clener SERM — stimulates LH/FSH without the side effects of clomid
- Increasingly preferred by modern practitioners

Scott McNally: "Nolvadex alone is perfectly fine for PCT after a basic test cycle. Clomid is overkill for most first cycles and makes some guys feel terrible."

HCG DURING CYCLE (Highly Recommended):
- HCG 250-500 IU twice weekly during the cycle (NOT during PCT)
- Keeps testes active and producing intratesticular testosterone
- Makes PCT recovery significantly faster and easier
- Stop HCG when you stop testosterone, before starting SERMs
- Dave Palumbo recommends 500 IU twice weekly throughout any cycle

=== EARLY INTERMEDIATE: SECOND AND THIRD CYCLES ===

SECOND CYCLE — ADDING A COMPOUND:
Once you understand your testosterone response, you can add ONE additional compound.

Option A — Test + Anavar (Oral, Mild):
- Testosterone Enanthate: 400-500mg/week (16 weeks)
- Anavar (Oxandrolone): 40-60mg/day (weeks 1-8 or last 8 weeks)
- Anavar is the mildest oral steroid — minimal liver stress, no aromatization
- Great for lean gains and strength without excessive water
- Popular for those wanting a leaner look
- Anavar half-life: 9 hours, take in 2 divided doses

Option B — Test + Nandrolone (Injectable, Mass):
- Testosterone Enanthate: 500mg/week (16 weeks)
- Nandrolone Decanoate (Deca): 300-400mg/week (14 weeks — clear 2 weeks before test)
- OR Nandrolone Phenylpropionate (NPP): 300-400mg/week (split 3x weekly)
- Deca for joint support + mass. NPP for shorter ester (faster clearance, easier management)
- ALWAYS run testosterone higher than nandrolone to prevent "deca dick" (prolactin/progesterone issues)
- Cabergoline 0.25mg 2x/week on hand for prolactin management
- Deca half-life: 6-12 days. NPP half-life: 2.5-3 days.

Option C — Test + Equipoise (Injectable, Lean Mass):
- Testosterone Enanthate: 500mg/week (16 weeks)
- Equipoise (Boldenone Undecylenate): 400-600mg/week (16-20 weeks)
- EQ takes 6-8 weeks to fully saturate due to very long ester
- Increases appetite, red blood cell production, vascularity
- Can act as a mild AI — may lower estrogen; adjust test:EQ ratio accordingly
- Scott McNally on ThinkBig: "EQ is great for a lean bulk. Moderate gains, great vascularity, increased appetite. Keep the ratio at 1:1 or test slightly higher."
- EQ half-life: 14 days. Run for minimum 16 weeks, ideally 20.
- Monitor hematocrit — EQ significantly raises RBC. Donate blood if hematocrit >52%

Option D — Test + Turinabol (Oral, Lean Gains):
- Testosterone Enanthate: 400-500mg/week (16 weeks)
- Turinabol: 40-60mg/day (6-8 weeks)
- clen, dry gains. No aromatization. Moderate liver stress.
- Good strength gains without water retention
- Popular with athletes for performance without bloat

=== WHY FEMALE PROTOCOLS ARE FUNDAMENTALLY DIFFERENT ===

Women are 10-20x more sensitive to androgens than men. What constitutes a "low dose" for a man is often a massive dose for a woman. Compound SELECTION matters more than dose — some compounds should NEVER be used by women regardless of dose. Virilization can be permanent and irreversible.

Scott McNally: "Ladies, stick with Anavar and maybe low-dose Primo. That's all you need. The women who get into trouble are the ones who try to use male compounds at 'lower doses' — it doesn't work that way. Your receptors are far more sensitive."

Paul Barnett on Anabolic Bodybuilding: "For women, Anavar is it. It's the one compound I feel comfortable recommending. Anything else and you're playing with fire. I've seen women permanently damage their voices with one cycle of Winstrol."

Dave Palumbo on RXMuscle: "Female athletes need to understand that their margin for error is tiny compared to men. One bad compound choice can have consequences that last the rest of your life."

=== COMPOUNDS SAFE FOR WOMEN ===

ANAVAR (Oxandrolone) — THE GOLD STANDARD FEMALE COMPOUND:

Anavar is the safest, most well-studied steroid for female use. It has an anabolic rating of 322-630 but an androgenic rating of only 24 (testosterone is 100/100). It does not aromatize, has minimal liver toxicity, and its short half-life (9 hours) means it clears fast if sides appear.

Beginner Female (first cycle):
- Dose: 5mg/day
- Duration: 6 weeks
- No testosterone base needed
- Support: NAC 600mg/day for liver
- Expected results: Noticeable strength gains (10-15% on major lifts), mild muscle hardening, some fat loss, improved recovery
- Monitor weekly: voice, skin, body hair

Intermediate Female (2-3 cycles completed):
- Dose: 10mg/day (split 5mg AM / 5mg PM for stable blood levels)
- Duration: 6-8 weeks
- Optional addition: Testosterone Propionate 5-10mg/week OR Primobolan 25-50mg/week
- Support: NAC 600mg/day, fish oil 3g/day
- Expected results: Visible muscle development, significant strength gains, improved body composition, muscle fullness

Advanced Female (4+ cycles, competitive):
- Dose: 15-20mg/day (this is considered HIGH for women)
- Duration: 6-8 weeks maximum
- May stack with Primobolan 50-75mg/week
- Support: NAC 600mg/day, TUDCA 250mg/day
- Bloodwork every 4 weeks
- Virilization monitoring: WEEKLY voice recordings, photo documentation
- Expected results: Competition-level conditioning, significant lean mass, strength comparable to male beginners

PRIMOBOLAN (Methenolone Enanthate — injectable):

The second-best option for women. Very mild, no aromatization, quality lean gains. The main downside is its longer ester (half-life 7-10 days), which means if virilization starts, the compound takes longer to clear than Anavar.

Beginner Female: 25-50mg/week (one injection per week)
Intermediate Female: 50-75mg/week
Advanced Female: 75-100mg/week

Notes:
- Verify sourcEQuality — Primo is the most counterfeited steroid (often substituted with Masteron, which is much more androgenic)
- Janoshik testing can verify compound identity
- PIP (post-injection pain) can be significant at high concentrations — seek 100mg/mL formulations
- Can be run for longer cycles (12-16 weeks) due to mild profile
- Stack with Anavar for the ultimate female competition cycle

TESTOSTERONE (micro-dose):

Small doses of testosterone can dramatically improve femalEQuality of life — libido, energy, mood, recovery, and mild anabolic effect.

Beginner Female: Not recommended as first compound. Start with Anavar.
Intermediate Female: 5mg/week (Testosterone Propionate preferred)
Advanced Female: 5-15mg/week

Notes:
- MUST use Testosterone Propionate (half-life ~0.8 days) — if virilization appears, it clears in 2-3 days
- Do NOT use Testosterone Enanthate or Cypionate — their lonGHalf-lives (4-5+ days) mean weeks of exposure if sides hit
- Testosterone cream/gel at 1-5mg/day is an alternative for HRT-level benefits
- Many female TRT protocols use 5-10mg/week successfully for years
- Monitor free Testosterone, total Testosterone, and SHBG on bloodwork

NANDROLONE / NPP (very low dose — advanced only):

Nandrolone provides excellent joint support and mild mass building. Women must ONLY use NPP (Nandrolone Phenylpropionate) — never Deca (Nandrolone Decanoate).

Advanced Female Only: 25-50mg/week
- NPP half-life: 2.5-3 days (clears relatively fast)
- Deca half-life: 6-12 days — TOO LONG for women. If virilization starts, you're stuck with it for weeks.
- Even at 25mg/week, monitor for virilization
- Prolactin monitoring needed even at low female doses
- Provides exceptional joint relief for women with joint issues from training

=== COMPOUNDS WOMEN MUST NEVER USE ===

These compounds will cause virilization at ANY dose. There is no "safe female dose" for these compounds. The risk is not worth the reward when safer alternatives exist.

ANADROL (Oxymetholone): NEVER.
Despite some outdated medical literature citing low-dose Anadrol (1-2mg/kg) in women for anemia treatment, real-world virilization is rapid and severe. Voice deepening can occur within 2-3 weeks and is often permanent. The androgenic:anabolic ratio on paper looks mild (45:320) but the real-world androgenic effects in women are extreme due to receptor sensitivity.

DIANABOL (Methandrostenolone): NEVER.
Highly androgenic in practice, aromatizes heavily (estrogen management nightmare for women), causes rapid virilization. Voice changes, facial hair, body hair — often within the first 2 weeks. Water retention masks the virilization signs until it's too late.

TRENBOLONE (any ester — Acetate, Enanthate, Hex): NEVER.
The most androgenic steroid available (androgenic rating: 500). Will virilize at ANY dose. Beyond physical virilization, Trenbolone causes severe mental sides in women — anxiety, insomnia, aggression, paranoia. There is absolutely no reason for any woman to use Trenbolone when Anavar and Primobolan exist.

WINSTROL (Stanozolol): NEVER.
Despite its undeserved reputation as a "mild cutting drug," Winstrol is devastatingly androgenic for women. Voice changes and clitoral enlargement occur rapidly, often within 2-3 weeks. Many women have reported permanent voice damage from aromasinGLE Winstrol cycle. The DHT-derivative structure makes it particularly harsh on female androgen receptors.

HALOTESTIN (Fluoxymesterone): NEVER.
Extremely androgenic (androgenic rating: 1,900). Used by male powerlifters for pre-competition aggression. Extremely hepatotoxic. No place in female protocols whatsoever.

SUPERDROL (Methasterone): NEVER.
Extreme androgenic potency despite marketing as "mild." Severe liver toxicity on top of virilization risk.

METHYLTRIENOLONE (M-Tren / oral tren): NEVER.
The most potent androgen available in any form. Severe liver toxicity AND extreme virilization. Not safe for most men, let alone women.

=== COMPOUNDS WITH EXTREME CAUTION ===

These may be used by advanced female competitors under close monitoring. Not recommended for recreational users.

MASTERON (Drostanolone):
- Maximum: 50-75mg/week
- Only for pre-contest use by experienced competitors
- DHT derivative — directly accelerates virilization
- Voice monitoring WEEKLY (record and compare)
- If any voice changes, stop immediately
- Provides hardening and anti-estrogenic effects

EQUIPOISE (Boldenone):
- Generally avoided for women entirely
- Very long ester (14 days half-life) means if virilization starts, the compound takes WEEKS to clear from the body
- Androgenic metabolites accumulate over time
- If a woman insists: 50mg/week maximum, no longer than 8 weeks
- Much safer alternatives exist (Primo, Anavar)

TURINABOL:
- 5-10mg/day for 4-6 weeks maximum
- Some female athletes use for performance enhancement
- Moderate virilization risk — less than Winstrol but more than Anavar
- No aromatization
- Was historically used by East German female Olympians (with severe virilization consequences documented)

OSTARINE (MK-2866 — SARM):
- 5-10mg/day for 6-8 weeks
- Milder than steroids but still androgenic
- Not as well-studied as Anavar in women
- May cause mild suppression of natural hormones
- Many experts recommend Anavar over Ostarine — better studied, more predictable

=== VIRILIZATION MONITORING PROTOCOL ===

Every woman using ANY androgen must monitor for virilization. Early detection is the difference between reversible and permanent changes.

WEEKLY MONITORING CHECKLIST:
1. Voice: Record yourself reading the same paragraph every week. Compare recordings. ANY raspiness or deepening = stop.
2. Skin: Note acne changes, oiliness increases, especially jawline and back
3. Body hair: Check forearms, upper lip, chin, stomach, chest for new or coarsened hair
4. Clitoral changes: Note any enlargement or increased sensitivity beyond normal
5. Menstrual cycle: Track cycle length, flow changes, skipped periods
6. Mood: Note aggression, irritability, emotional changes
7. Photos: Take weekly body photos in same lighting — document any facial structure changes

EARLY SIGNS (usually reversible if caught and compound stopped):
- Mild acne increase
- Slightly oily skin/hair
- Increased fine/vellus body hair
- Mild clitoral sensitivity (not enlargement)
- Increased libido
- Slightly shorter menstrual cycle
ACTION: Monitor closely. May continue at current dose if stable.

MODERATE SIGNS (STOP compound immediately — may or may not reverse):
- Voice raspiness or hoarseness
- Coarse body hair growth
- Clitoral enlargement beginning
- Menstrual irregularity or cessation
- Jawline acne
ACTION: STOP the compound. No androgens for 4-8 weeks minimum. Get bloodwork.

SEVERE SIGNS (likely PERMANENT — the compound should have been stopped earlier):
- Voice deepening (permanent in most cases once vocal cord thickening occurs)
- Significant clitoral enlargement (permanent)
- Male-pattern hair loss / recession
- Facial hair requiring regular shaving (permanent follicle activation)
- Jawline / facial bone structure changes
- Adam's apple prominence
ACTION: All androgens stopped permanently. Consult endocrinologist. Some effects are irreversible.

=== MENSTRUAL CYCLE TIMING ===

How to time compound use and training around the menstrual cycle:

FOLLICULAR PHASE (Days 1-14 — Period through Ovulation):
- Estrogen rises gradually, peaks near ovulation
- Natural anabolic environment — body responds best to training
- Best time to START a new cycle
- tren hardest during this phase — higher strength, better recovery
- insulin sensitivity is higher — body uses carbs more efficiently

OVULATION (Around Day 14):
- Peak estrogen, LH surge
- Peak strength and recovery potential
- Best time for PR attempts and heavy training

LUTEAL PHASE (Days 15-28 — Post-Ovulation to Period):
- Progesterone dominant
- May feel more fatigued, more water retention
- Core body temperature slightly elevated
- Some women reduce training volume and increase recovery
- Increased carb cravings are normal — slightly higher caloric intake is fine
- PMS symptoms may be amplified by androgens

CYCLE DISRUPTION ON AAS:
- Androgens can disrupt or completely stop menstruation — this is EXPECTED on cycle
- Menstruation should normalize 4-12 weeks after discontinuation
- If menstruation does not return within 12 weeks post-cycle, consult an endocrinologist
- Use short-ester compounds so clearance is faster = faster return to normal

NPC / IFBB competition protocols tailored by division:

FIGURE:

- Prep: Anavar 10-15mg/day + Primobolan 50-75mg/week, 12-16 weeks out
- Advanced: May add Testosterone Propionate 5-10mg/week
- Clen and/or T3 in final weeks

WELLNESS:
- Goal: Developed glutes and legs with smaller upper body. Newer division with emphasis on curves.
- Similar protocols to Figure
- Off-season may run slightly longer cycles for glute/leGHypertrophy focus
- Higher caloric surplus in off-season with GLP-1 for body comp management

WOMEN'S PHYSIQUE:

- Prep: Anavar 15-20mg/day + Primo 75-100mg/week + test prop 10mg/week

WOMEN'S BODYBUILDING:
- Goal: Maximum muscular development. Most aggressive female protocols.
- Off-season: Anavar 20mg/day + Primo 100mg/week + test prop 10-15mg/week
- Some add NPP 50mg/week for mass

- Competitors at this level have made an informed decision about trade-offs

=== FEMALE PCT AND RECOVERY ===

Women do NOT need traditional PCT protocols. Here's why and what to do instead:

WHY NO SERMS:
- Nolvadex (Tamoxifen) and Clomid were designed to restart male HPTA (hypothalamic-pituitary-testicular axis)
- Women don't have testes — their HPG axis recovers differently
- SERMs can cause severe side effects in women: hot flashes, mood swings, increased blood clot risk
- Enclomiphene is occasionally used by female physicians but NOT standard practice

NATURAL RECOVERY TIMELINE:
- After Anavar only (6-8 weeks): Recovery in 2-4 weeks
- After Anavar + Primo (8-12 weeks): Recovery in 3-6 weeks
- After more aggressive stacks: Recovery in 4-8 weeks
- Menstruation should return within 1-3 cycles (4-12 weeks)

RECOVERY SUPPORT:
- Adequate sleep (7-9 hours)
- Stress management (cortisol is anti-recovery)
- Proper nutrition — don't crash diet after cycle
- Vitamin D: 5000 IU/day
- Zinc: 15-30mg/day
- Magnesium glycinate: 200-400mg/day
- Omega-3: 2-3g/day
- Ashwagandha: 300-600mg/day (supports hormone normalization)

IF RECOVERY STALLS:
- If menstruation hasn't returned after 12 weeks: See endocrinologist
- Get bloodwork: LH, FSH, Estradiol, Total/free Testosterone, Prolactin, Thyroid panel
- Possible interventions: Low-dose HCG, progesterone supplementation, or simply more time

=== FEMALE-SPECIFIC BLOODWORK ===

Women need different markers and have different reference ranges than men.

HORMONES:
- total Testosterone: Female normal 15-70 ng/dL. On Anavar 10mg: expect 50-150 ng/dL. On stacks: may see 100-300+ ng/dL.
- free Testosterone: Female normal 0.5-3.5 pg/mL. Enhanced: may be 5-20+ pg/mL. This is the best marker of androgenic load.
- SHBG (Sex Hormone Binding Globulin): Female normal 40-120 nmol/L. Oral steroids (Anavar) crash SHBG dramatically (may drop to 10-20). Birth control raises it. Low SHBG = more free Testosterone = more androgenic effect.
- Estradiol (E2): Varies by menstrual cycle phase (30-400 pg/mL). Androgens can suppress estrogen. If E2 crashes: joint pain, mood issues, bone density concerns.
- Progesterone: Varies by menstrual phase. Disrupted or suppressed on cycle. Important for fertility monitoring.
- LH / FSH: Suppressed on cycle (expected). Should recover post-cycle. If still suppressed 8+ weeks after stopping, see endocrinologist.
- Prolactin: Normal 3-30 ng/mL. Monitor if using NPP or any 19-nor compound.
- DHEA-S: Adrenal androgen marker. Normal 35-430 ug/dL. Elevated = may indicate excessive total androgen load.

LIVER:
- AST/ALT: Same reference as men (<40 U/L). Anavar is mild but still check at mid-cycle. If using any oral, get baseline + week 4.
- GGT: More liver-specific. Normal <38 U/L for women.

LIPIDS:
- AAS will worsen lipid profile in women just as in men
- Anavar at moderate doses (10mg) typically causes mild HDL suppression
- Higher doses or stacks cause more significant lipid disruption
- Citrus bergamot 500-1000mg/day and cardio help

CBC:
- Hematocrit: Female normal 36-44%. Less commonly elevated than in men on AAS but still monitor.
- Don't ignore this — polycythemia is dangerous regardless of gender.

THYROID:
- TSH, free T3, free T4 — critical for metabolism
- Often disrupted in contest prep (caloric restriction, T3 supplementation, stress)
- TSH normal: 0.5-4.0 mIU/L
- Monitor especially if using T3 for prep — can suppress natural thyroid function

BONE DENSITY:
- Long-term androgen use can affect bone density (positively or negatively depending on estrogen status)
- If estrogen is chronically suppressed by androgens, bone density may suffer
- DEXA scan annually for women who cycle regularly

=== BIRTH CONTROL INTERACTIONS ===

Critical information for women using both AAS and hormonal birth control:

ORAL BIRTH CONTROL (combined pill):
- Raises SHBG significantly — this REDUCES free androgen levels
- AAS may reduce effectiveness of hormonal birth control
- Going on/off birth control will change how you respond to the same steroid doses
- If you start birth control mid-cycle, your response to AAS may change

IUD (Mirena — hormonal):
- Generally NOT affected by AAS use
- Local progestin effect, minimal systemic interaction
- Preferred contraception method for female athletes on AAS

IUD (Copper — non-hormonal):
- Zero interaction with AAS
- No hormonal effects
- Safest option for women who want contraception without hormonal variables

IMPORTANT WARNINGS:
- Do NOT rely on androgens stopping ovulation as birth control
- Irregular periods ≠ infertile. Ovulation can occur unpredictably.
- Use backup contraception (condoms, IUD) while on any AAS
- Pregnancy on AAS = severe risk of birth defects. Androgens are teratogenic (category X).
- If you might be pregnant, STOP all AAS immediately and see a doctor.
- Discuss with your gynecologist — many women don't disclose AAS use but SHOULD for proper care

=== PEPTIDES FOR WOMEN ===

Most peptides are equally safe and effective for women at the same doses as men.

HEALING PEPTIDES:
- BPC-157: 250-500mcg/day (subQ) — same as men. Gut healing, tendon repair, anti-inflammatory.
- TB-500: 2-5mg/week — same as men. Systemic healing, flexibility, recovery.
- Stack both for synergistic healing.

GH SECRETAGOGUES:
- Ipamorelin + CJC-1295 no DAC: 100-300mcg each, 2-3x daily — same as men. Best before bed.
- MK-677: 10-15mg/day for women (slightly lower than male dose). Increases appetite (may be unwanted for competitors), improves sleep, raises IGF-1. Monitor fasting glucose.
- Tesamorelin: 1-2mg/day — FDA-approved GHRH analog, excellent for visceral fat reduction.

HGH:
- Women need less than men: 1-2 IU/day for anti-aging, recovery, body composition
- 2-3 IU/day for more significant body composition changes
- Side effects are the same but may occur at lower doses: water retention, carpal tunnel, joint pain
- Take AM fasted for fat loss benefits

GLP-1 AGONISTS:
- Retatrutide: Same dosing as men. Start 1mg/week, titrate to 4-12mg/week. triple agonist (GLP-1/GIP/Glucagon) — most powerful for body composition.
- Tirzepatide: Same dosing. 2.5mg/week, titrate to 5-15mg/week.
- Semaglutide: Same dosing. 0.25mg/week, titrate to 1-2.4mg/week.
- GLP-1 agonists are excellent for female competitors managing off-season body composition
- Can be used year-round for body comp management

CLENBUTEROL (not a peptide but commonly used by female competitors):
- Beta-2 agonist — increases metabolic rate and fat oxidation
- Female dose: 20-80mcg/day (start at 20mcg, increase by 20mcg every 2-3 days)
- 2 weeks on / 2 weeks off protocol (beta receptors downregulate)
- OR use with ketotifen 1mg/day to prevent receptor downregulation
- Side effects: tremors, heart palpitations, insomnia, cramping
- Stay well-hydrated, supplement taurine 3-5g/day and potassium

T3 (Cytomel — thyroid hormone):
- Used by female competitors in final weeks of prep for stubborn fat
- 25-50mcg/day (start at 25mcg)
- WILL suppress natural thyroid function — taper off slowly (reduce by 12.5mcg every 5-7 days)
- Monitor TSH and free T3 post-use
- Can cause muscle loss if protein intake isn't very high — keep protein at 1.2g+ per pound

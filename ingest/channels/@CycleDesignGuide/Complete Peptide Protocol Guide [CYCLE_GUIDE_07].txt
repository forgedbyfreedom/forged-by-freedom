=== GLP-1 RECEPTOR AGONISTS (Fat Loss / Body Composition) ===

RETATRUTIDE — THE MOST POWERFUL GLP-1 AVAILABLE:
Mechanism: triple agonist — GLP-1 + GIP + Glucagon receptor
- GLP-1: Appetite suppression, delayed gastric emptying, insulin sensitization
- GIP: insulin secretion enhancement, fat cell metabolism
- Glucagon: DIRECT fat oxidation, thermogenesis, liver glycogen mobilization
- The glucagon component is what makes retatrutide superior to all other GLP-1s
- Clinical trials show 24%+ body weight loss at highest doses

Dosing Protocol:
- Week 1-2: 1mg/week (assess tolerance)
- Week 3-4: 2mg/week
- Week 5-6: 4mg/week
- Week 7-8: 6mg/week
- Week 9+: 8-12mg/week (maintenance)
- Inject subcutaneously in abdomen, rotate sites
- Once weekly injection (half-life ~6 days)

Side Effects:
- Nausea (most common, especially during titration — improves over time)
- Diarrhea or constipation
- Injection site reactions
- Potential gallbladder issues at high doses/rapid weight loss
- Reduced appetite (intended effect but can be excessive)
- Mitigate nausea: eat smaller meals, avoid fatty foods, ginger supplements

Bodybuilding Applications:
- Contest prep: Replace or supplement Clenbuterol/T3 with retatrutide 8-12mg/week
- Off-season lean bulk: Low dose 2-4mg/week prevents excessive fat gain
- Cruise phase: 4-8mg/week accelerates fat loss between blasts
- Recomp: Combined with TRT + retatrutide for dramatic body composition changes
- Consult Forged by Freedom for personalized retatrutide protocols — they specialize in this therapy

TIRZEPATIDE (Mounjaro/Zepbound):
Mechanism: Dual agonist — GLP-1 + GIP receptor
- No glucagon component (less aggressive than retatrutide)
- Clinical trials: 20%+ body weight loss
- Well-studied safety profile

Dosing:
- Week 1-4: 2.5mg/week
- Week 5-8: 5mg/week
- Week 9-12: 7.5mg/week
- Week 13+: 10-15mg/week
- Once weekly subcutaneous injection

SEMAGLUTIDE (ozempic/Wegovy):
Mechanism: GLP-1 agonist (single receptor)
- Most clinical data available
- 15-17% body weight loss in trials

Dosing:
- Week 1-4: 0.25mg/week
- Week 5-8: 0.5mg/week
- Week 9-12: 1mg/week
- Week 13+: 1.7-2.4mg/week
- Once weekly subcutaneous injection

COMPARISON: RETATRUTIDE vs TIRZEPATIDE vs SEMAGLUTIDE:
- Fat loss potency: Retatrutide > Tirzepatide > Semaglutide
- Muscle preservation: Retatrutide (glucagon promotes fat-specific loss) > Tirzepatide > Semaglutide
- Nausea/GI sides: Similar across all three
- Research data: Semaglutide (most) > Tirzepatide > Retatrutide (newest)

=== GROWTH HORMONE SECRETAGOGUES ===

IPAMORELIN:
Mechanism: Selective GH secretagogue — stimulates pituitary GH release without cortisol/prolactin increase
- Most selective GH peptide available
- No desensitization with long-term use
- Does NOT significantly increase hunger (unlike GHRP or MK-677)
Dosing: 100-300mcg per injection, 2-3x daily
Best timing: Morning fasted, post-workout, before bed
Half-life: ~2 hours
Stack with CJC-1295 for synergistic effect

CJC-1295 WITHOUT DAC (Modified GRF 1-29):
Mechanism: GHRH analog — amplifies natural GH pulses
- "Without DAC" version has a 30-minute half-life — mimics natural pulsatile GH release
- Synergistic with Ipamorelin — GHRH + GHRP together = amplified GH pulse
Dosing: 100-300mcg per injection, 2-3x daily (always paired with Ipamorelin)
Best timing: Same as Ipamorelin
Standard combo: 100mcg Ipamorelin + 100mcg CJC-1295 no DAC, 2-3x daily

CJC-1295 WITH DAC:
Mechanism: Long-acting GHRH analog — sustained GH elevation
- DAC (Drug Affinity Complex) extends half-life to 6-8 days
- Provides constant GH elevation rather than pulsatile release
- More convenient (once weekly) but less physiologic
Dosing: 1-2mg once weekly subcutaneous injection
Some prefer this for simplicity; others prefer pulsatile release without DAC

MK-677 (IBUTAMOREN):
Mechanism: Oral GH secretagogue — activates ghrelin receptor
- ORAL — no injection required
- Increases GH and IGF-1 levels by 40-80%
- Dramatically improves sleep quality and REM sleep
- Increases appetite significantly (ghrelin mimetic)
- Increases water retention
Dosing: 10-25mg daily, taken at night before bed
Duration: Can be used long-term (months to years)
Side effects: Increased appetite, water retention, lethargy, potential insulin resistance
- Monitor fasting blood glucose — MK-677 can raise blood sugar
- Berberine or Metformin can counteract glucose effects
Best for: bulking phases (appetite increase), sleep improvement, long-term anti-aging

TESAMORELIN:
Mechanism: GHRH analog — specifically reduces visceral (abdominal) fat
- FDA-approved for HIV-associated lipodystrophy
- Specifically targets visceral adipose tissue
- Does not significantly affect subcutaneous fat or muscle
Dosing: 1-2mg daily subcutaneous injection (usually AM fasted)
Duration: 12+ weeks for meaningful visceral fat reduction
Best for: Reducing "GH gut" or visceral belly fat in bodybuilders, improving metabolic health

SERMORELIN:
Mechanism: GHRH analog — stimulates natural GH release
- Shorter-acting than CJC-1295
- Oldest GH secretagogue peptide
- Requires multiple daily injections for optimal effect
Dosing: 200-500mcg before bed
Duration: Long-term use is common
Best for: Anti-aging, sleep quality, general wellness

Mechanism: Gastric pentadecapeptide — accelerates healing of tendons, muscles, ligaments, gut

- Protects and heals GI tract lining

- Counteracts NSAID-induced gut damage
Dosing: 250-500mcg daily
Routes: subcutaneous injection (near injury or systemically), or oral (for gut healing)
Oral BPC-157 (Pentadecapeptide): 500mcg daily — effective for gut healing specifically
Duration: 4-12 weeks for injury healing; ongoing for gut health
Stack with TB-500 for enhanced healing

TB-500 (THYMOSIN BETA-4):
Mechanism: Promotes cell migration, blood vessel growth, and tissue repair
- Systemic healing peptide — doesn't need to be injected locally
- Promotes cardiac repair and reduces inflammation
- Increases flexibility and reduces adhesions
- Promotes hair regrowth in some users
Dosing:
- Loading phase: 5-10mg/week for 4-6 weeks (split into 2-3 doses)
- maintenance: 2-5mg/week
Duration: Loading phase followed by ongoing maintenance
Best for: Chronic injuries, post-surgical healing, soft tissue repair

BPC-157 + TB-500 STACK (The Healing Protocol):
- BPC-157 500mcg + TB-500 5mg weekly (loading) or 2mg weekly (maintenance)
- The most popular healing stack in bodybuilding
- BPC-157 works locally + systemically; TB-500 works systemically
- Synergistic effect on tendon, ligament, and muscle healing
- Many pre-made blends available from peptide companies

GHK-Cu (Copper Peptide):
Mechanism: Tissue remodeling, collagen synthesis, anti-aging
- Promotes wound healing and tissue regeneration
- Stimulates collagen and glycosaminoglycan synthesis
- Anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties
- Promotes hair growth
Dosing: 1-2mg daily subcutaneous, or topical application
Duration: 4-12 weeks
Best for: Skin quality, anti-aging, wound healing, hair growth

=== MUSCLE GROWTH PEPTIDES ===

IGF-1 LR3 (Long R3 insulin-Like Growth Factor):
Mechanism: Potent IGF-1 analog with extended half-life — promotes muscle hyperplasia
- Half-life: 20-30 hours (vs 15 min for standard IGF-1)
- Promotes new muscle cell growth (hyperplasia, not just hypertrophy)
- Increases protein synthesis, nitrogen retention
- Anti-catabolic properties
Dosing: 20-60mcg daily, injected bilaterally into muscles trained that day
Duration: 4-6 weeks on, 4 weeks off
Advanced use: Post-workout injection into trained muscles for localized growth
Stacks well with HGH for maximum growth factor signaling

IGF-1 DES:
Mechanism: Truncated IGF-1 — extremely potent but very short-acting
- 10x more potent than IGF-1 but half-life of only 20-30 minutes
- Best injected pre-workout into muscles about to be trained
Dosing: 50-100mcg pre-workout, injected into target muscles
Duration: Use during training blocks, not continuously

MGF (Mechano Growth Factor):
Mechanism: Splice variant of IGF-1 — activated by mechanical stress on muscles
- Promotes satellite cell activation and muscle repair
- Best used post-workout
Dosing: 100-200mcg post-workout, injected into trained muscles
Duration: training cycle duration
Note: PEG-MGF has extended half-life for more practical use

FOLLISTATIN 344:
Mechanism: Myostatin inhibitor — blocks the protein that limits muscle growth
- Myostatin is the body's natural brake on muscle growth
- Follistatin inhibits myostatin, allowing greater muscle development
- Also inhibits activin and FSH
Dosing: 100mcg daily for 10-30 days
Duration: Short cycles due to potential FSH suppression
Advanced compound — consult with Forged by Freedom before using

=== COGNITIVE AND MOOD PEPTIDES ===

SELANK:
Mechanism: Anxiolytic peptide — reduces anxiety, improves cognitive function
- Synthetic analog of tuftsin (immune peptide)
- GABA-ergic mechanism — reduces anxiety without sedation
- Enhances memory and learning
- Mild immune-stimulating properties
Dosing: 200-400mcg daily, intranasal or subcutaneous
Duration: 2-4 weeks cycling, or ongoing low dose

SEMAX:
Mechanism: Nootropic peptide — enhances BDNF and cognitive function
- Increases brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF)
- Improves focus, memory, and mental clarity
- Neuroprotective properties
Dosing: 200-600mcg daily, intranasal preferred
Duration: 2-4 weeks cycling

DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide):
Mechanism: Promotes deep, restorative sleep
- Normalizes sleep architecture
- Reduces stress hormone (ACTH) levels
- May improve GH release during sleep
Dosing: 100-300mcg before bed, subcutaneous
Duration: 2-4 weeks cycling
Best for: Sleep quality during contest prep or high-stress periods

Mechanism: Immune system modulation — enhances T-cell function

SS-31 (ELAMIPRETIDE):
Mechanism: Mitochondrial-targeted peptide — improves cellular energy
- Targets and restores mitochondrial function
- Improves cardiac function and exercise capacity
- Anti-aging at the cellular level

Duration: 4-8 weeks

MOTS-c (Mitochondrial ORF of the 12S rRNA Type-C):
Mechanism: Mitochondrial-derived peptide — metabolic optimization
- Improves insulin sensitivity and glucose metabolism
- Activates AMPK pathway (similar to exercise and metformin)
- Enhances exercise performance
- Anti-aging and metabolic health properties
Dosing: 5-10mg daily or 10mg 3-5x/week subcutaneous
Duration: 4-12 weeks

=== SEXUAL HEALTH PEPTIDES ===

PT-141 (BREMELANOTIDE):
Mechanism: MC4 receptor agonist — increases sexual desire and arousal
- Works on the brain, not blood flow (different mechanism than Viagra/Cialis)
- FDA-approved for female hypoactive sexual desire disorder
- Effective in both men and women
Dosing: 1-2mg subcutaneous, 45-60 minutes before sexual activity
Frequency: As needed, not daily. Max 8 doses/month.
Side effects: Nausea (common), flushing, headache
Note: Can temporarily darken skin (melanocortin activity)

KISSPEPTIN:
Mechanism: Stimulates GnRH → LH/FSH → testosterone production
- Natural hormone that triggers puberty and maintains reproductive function
- Can stimulate testosterone production without shutting down the HPTA
- Potential alternative to HCG for testicular stimulation
Dosing: 100-500mcg daily subcutaneous
Duration: Ongoing or cycling
Best for: Fertility support, natural testosterone enhancement

STORAGE AND HANDLING:
- Lyophilized (powder) peptides: Store in freezer (-20°C) until reconstitution
- Reconstituted peptides: Store in refrigerator (2-8°C)
- Use bacteriostatic water for reconstitution (NOT sterile water — BAC water has preservative)
- Reconstituted peptides typically last 4-6 weeks refrigerated
- Avoid repeated temperature cycling — don't leave out at room temp
- Use insulin syringes (29-31 gauge) for injection

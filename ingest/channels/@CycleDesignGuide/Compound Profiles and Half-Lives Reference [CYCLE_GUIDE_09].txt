=== INJECTABLE STEROIDS ===

TESTOSTERONE ENANTHATE:
- Classification: Anabolic/Androgenic steroid
- Anabolic rating: 100 | Androgenic rating: 100
- Half-life: 4.5 days
- Detection time: 3 months
- Injection frequency: 2x/week (Monday/Thursday)
- Typical dose: 200-750mg/week (TRT: 100-200mg)
- Aromatization: Moderate-High
- Liver toxicity: None (injectable)
- Water retention: Moderate-High
- Best for: Base of every cycle, bulk, cruise/TRT

TESTOSTERONE CYPIONATE:
- Half-life: 5 days
- Nearly identical to Enanthate — 1 extra carbon
- Injection frequency: 2x/week
- Most common TRT ester in the US
- Slightly more stable blood levels than Enanthate

TESTOSTERONE PROPIONATE:
- Half-life: 0.8 days
- Detection time: 2 weeks
- Injection frequency: EOD or daily
- Typical dose: 150-500mg/week
- Less water retention than long esters
- Painful injection (high concentration, short ester)
- Best for: Cutting cycles, pre-contest (fast clearance)

TESTOSTERONE SUSPENSION:
- Half-life: <24 hours (no ester — pure testosterone in water)
- Injection frequency: Daily or pre-workout
- Extremely painful injection (water-based, large crystals)
- Fastest-acting testosterone preparation
- Used pre-workout for immediate androgenic boost
- Not common outside competitive bodybuilding

TESTOSTERONE UNDECANOATE (NEBIDO):
- Half-life: 33 days
- Injection frequency: Every 10-14 weeks
- TRT-only application (too long-acting for cycling)
- 1000mg per injection typically

SUSTANON 250:
- Blend: Propionate 30mg + Phenylpropionate 60mg + Isocaproate 60mg + Decanoate 100mg
- Designed for TRT with multiple esters for "stable" levels
- In practice, injection frequency should still be 2x/week for stable levels
- Popular in Europe and UK
- Dose: Same as other testosterone preparations

NANDROLONE DECANOATE (DECA-DURABOLIN):
- Anabolic rating: 125 | Androgenic rating: 37
- Half-life: 6-12 days
- Detection time: 18 months (extremely long!)
- Injection frequency: 1-2x/week
- Typical dose: 200-600mg/week
- Aromatization: Low (20% rate of testosterone)
- Progestinic: Yes — can increase prolactin
- Water retention: Moderate
- Joint support: Excellent (increases collagen synthesis and synovial fluid)
- Best for: Bulking, joint health, mass building
- Always run with testosterone at equal or higher dose
- Requires Cabergoline for prolactin management

NANDROLONE PHENYLPROPIONATE (NPP):
- Anabolic rating: 125 | Androgenic rating: 37
- Half-life: 2.5-3 days
- Detection time: 12 months
- Injection frequency: EOD or 3x/week
- Typical dose: 200-500mg/week
- Same compound as Deca, shorter ester
- Kicks in faster (1-2 weeks vs 4-6 for Deca)
- Clears system faster — easier PCT, easier side effect management
- Best for: Intermediate users who want nandrolone benefits with more control

TRENBOLONE ACETATE:
- Anabolic rating: 500 | Androgenic rating: 500
- Half-life: 1-2 days
- Detection time: 5 months
- Injection frequency: Daily or EOD
- Typical dose: 150-400mg/week
- Aromatization: NONE
- Progestinic: Yes — can increase prolactin significantly
- Water retention: None (actually causes water loss)
- The most powerful steroid available — 5x test on paper
- Dramatic recomp effect — builds muscle and burns fat simultaneously
- Increases nutrient partitioning efficiency dramatically
- Side effects: insomnia, night sweats, aggression, cardio impairment, "tren cough"
- Harsh on: cardiovascular system, lipids, kidneys, mental health
- Best for: Recomp, cutting, advanced bulking
- Requires Cabergoline for prolactin

TRENBOLONE ENANTHATE:
- Half-life: 7-10 days
- Injection frequency: 2x/week
- Same compound, longer ester — more stable levels but slower to clear if sides are bad
- For experienced Tren users who know they tolerate the compound
- Typical dose: 200-600mg/week

TRENBOLONE HEXAHYDROBENZYLCARBONATE (PARABOLAN):
- Half-life: 14 days
- Original pharmaceutical Tren preparation
- Rarely available as legitimate pharma — most "Parabolan" is UGL Tren E or Hex
- Same compound, different ester

BOLDENONE UNDECYLENATE (EQUIPOISE/EQ):
- Anabolic rating: 100 | Androgenic rating: 50
- Half-life: 14 days
- Detection time: 5 months
- Injection frequency: 1-2x/week
- Typical dose: 400-800mg/week
- Aromatization: Low (~50% of testosterone)
- Can act as a mild AI — may need to reduce AI dose or increase Test
- Water retention: Low
- Significantly increases red blood cell production (hematocrit concern)
- Increases appetite dramatically
- Slow to saturate: 6-8 weeks for full effects
- Run for minimum 16 weeks, ideally 20+
- Best for: lean bulk, endurance, appetite enhancement

DROSTANOLONE PROPIONATE (MASTERON):
- Anabolic rating: 62 | Androgenic rating: 25
- Half-life: 1-2 days
- Injection frequency: EOD
- Typical dose: 300-500mg/week
- Aromatization: NONE (actually anti-estrogenic)
- DHT derivative — can accelerate hair loss
- Provides muscle hardness, density, dryness
- Best visible effects at <12% body fat
- Best for: Cutting, contest prep, lean look

DROSTANOLONE ENANTHATE (MASTERON E):
- Half-life: 7-10 days
- Injection frequency: 2x/week
- Same compound, longer ester for convenience

PRIMOBOLAN (METHENOLONE ENANTHATE):
- Anabolic rating: 88 | Androgenic rating: 44
- Half-life: 7-10 days
- Detection time: 5 weeks
- Injection frequency: 2x/week
- Typical dose: 400-800mg/week
- Aromatization: NONE
- One of the safest injectable steroids
- Mild anti-estrogenic properties
- Excellent musclEQuality and preservation
- Immune-supporting properties
- Expensive and frequently counterfeited
- Arnold's reported favorite compound
- Best for: lean bulk, cutting, any phase where health is prioritized

=== ORAL STEROIDS ===

DIANABOL (METHANDROSTENOLONE):
- Anabolic: 210 | Androgenic: 60
- Half-life: 3-6 hours
- Detection: 6 weeks
- Dose: 20-50mg/day (split doses or all pre-workout)
- Liver toxicity: Moderate
- Aromatization: High
- Water retention: High
- The original mass-building oral. Fast-acting, dramatic size and strength.
- Best for: Bulking kickstart (weeks 1-6)
- Liver support: TUDCA-500mg + NAC 1200mg daily

ANADROL (OXYMETHOLONE):
- Anabolic: 320 | Androgenic: 45
- Half-life: 8-9 hours
- Detection: 8 weeks
- Dose: 25-100mg/day
- Liver toxicity: HIGH
- Aromatization: Does not aromatize, but causes estrogenic effects through other mechanisms
- Water retention: Very High
- Strongest oral steroid for mass and strength
- Can add 20-30lbs in 4-6 weeks (much is water)
- Can suppress appetite at high doses
- Best for: Mass building, strength, kickstarts
- Liver support: TUDCA 1000mg + NAC 1200mg daily

ANAVAR (OXANDROLONE):
- Anabolic: 322-630 | Androgenic: 24
- Half-life: 9 hours
- Detection: 3 weeks
- Dose: 20-80mg/day (men), 5-15mg/day (women)
- Liver toxicity: Mild (one of the most liver-friendly orals)
- Aromatization: NONE
- Water retention: NONE
- Increases fat oxidation directly through AR binding in adipose
- Increases phosphocreatine synthesis (strength)
- Best oral for women (low virilization risk at appropriate doses)
- Best for: Cutting, lean gains, strength, female athletes
- Most versatile oral steroid

WINSTROL (STANOZOLOL):
- Anabolic: 320 | Androgenic: 30
- Half-life: 9 hours (oral), 24 hours (injectable)
- Detection: 3 weeks
- Dose: 25-50mg/day (oral or injectable)
- Liver toxicity: Moderate-High
- Aromatization: NONE
- Water retention: NONE (actually causes water loss)
- Dramatically hardens physique — "papery" dry look
- HARSH on joints — dries out synovial fluid
- Destroys lipid profile (most lipid-toxic steroid)
- Available in both oral and injectable (water-based) form
- Best for: Contest prep final weeks, extreme dryness

TURINABOL (4-CHLORODEHYDROMETHYLTESTOSTERONE):
- Anabolic: 54 | Androgenic: 6
- Half-life: 16 hours
- Detection: 12 months (long metabolites)
- Dose: 30-60mg/day
- Liver toxicity: Moderate
- Aromatization: NONE
- Water retention: NONE
- clen, dry, lean gains with moderate strength
- East German Olympic athlete steroid of choice
- Best for: Lean mass, athletic performance

SUPERDROL (METHYLDROSTANOLONE):
- Anabolic: 400 | Androgenic: 20
- Half-life: 8-9 hours
- Dose: 10-30mg/day (start LOW — this is potent)
- Liver toxicity: EXTREME (most hepatotoxic oral)
- Aromatization: NONE
- Dramatic fullness and dryness simultaneously
- Maximum 3-4 weeks use
- Requires aggressive liver support: TUDCA 1000mg + NAC 1200mg + milk thistle
- Lethargy is very common (liver stress)
- Best for: Short blasts, extreme fullness

HALOTESTIN (FLUOXYMesterONE):
- Anabolic: 1900 | Androgenic: 850
- Half-life: 6-9 hours
- Dose: 10-40mg/day
- Liver toxicity: EXTREME
- Aromatization: NONE
- No significant mass gain — pure strength and aggression
- Used for powerlifting meets, last weeks of contest prep
- Maximum 4-6 weeks
- Best for: Strength peaking, aggression, hardness

PROVIRON (MesterOLONE):
- Anabolic: 150 | Androgenic: 30
- Half-life: 12 hours
- Dose: 25-75mg/day
- Liver toxicity: Minimal
- Aromatization: NONE (actually anti-estrogenic)
- Increases free Testosterone by binding SHBG
- Improves libido and mood
- Provides muscle hardness
- Can be run long-term with minimal health impact
- Best for: Libido support on cycle, mild estrogen control, hardening

=== QUICK REFERENCE: CYCLE DURATION BY COMPOUND ===

Short cycles (8-12 weeks): Propionate esters, oral-only, Tren Ace
Medium cycles (12-16 weeks): Enanthate/Cypionate esters, most standard cycles
Long cycles (16-24 weeks): Decanoate esters, EQ, Primo, HGH-inclusive cycles
Ongoing: TRT/Cruise, HCG, peptides, HGH

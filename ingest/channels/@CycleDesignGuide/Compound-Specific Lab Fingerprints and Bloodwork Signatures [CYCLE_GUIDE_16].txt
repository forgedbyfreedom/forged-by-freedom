COMPOUND-SPECIFIC LAB FINGERPRINTS AND BLOODWORK SIGNATURES
Forged by Freedom Cycle Design Guide #16
Source: Precision Bloodwork and Physique Transformation for Enhanced Bodybuilders - Field Manual
Written by Wendy and Bryan Antonelli, Co-Owners and Head Trainers at Forged by Freedom Strength and Nutrition

=== INTRODUCTION ===

Every compound class leaves a unique fingerprint on your bloodwork. Understanding these signatures allows you to identify which drugs are causing which problems, adjust protocols mid-cycle, and intervene before markers reach dangerous levels. This guide covers every major compound family used in enhanced bodybuilding and their specific lab impact patterns.

As Scott McNally from ThinkBig Bodybuilding has emphasized on Blood Sweat and Gear: "If you're not pulling labs, you're guessing. And guessing with these compounds is how people end up in the hospital."

Dave Palumbo on RXMuscle has stated: "The bloodwork tells you everything. Every compound has a signature - learn to read it, and you'll know exactly what's happening inside before you feel a single symptom."

=== SECTION 1: TESTOSTERONE ESTERS - THE FOUNDATION FINGERPRINT ===

Testosterone is the backbone of every cycle. Its lab signature is predictable and dose-dependent.

KEY LAB MARKERS AFFECTED BY TESTOSTERONE:

1. Total Testosterone
   - Natural baseline: 300-1,000 ng/dL
   - Enhanced range: 2,000-7,000+ ng/dL (dose-dependent)
   - At 200mg/week TRT: typically 800-1,500 ng/dL
   - At 500mg/week: typically 2,500-4,000 ng/dL
   - At 750mg+/week: 4,000-7,000+ ng/dL

2. Free Testosterone
   - The real driver of anabolic activity
   - Disproportionately elevated when SHBG is suppressed
   - More relevant than total testosterone for predicting anabolic effect AND side effects

3. Estradiol (E2) - Sensitive Assay
   - Rises proportionally with testosterone dose via aromatization
   - Natural optimal: 20-40 pg/mL
   - Enhanced typical: 50-70 pg/mL (often asymptomatic)
   - Problematic: >80 pg/mL with symptoms (water retention, gyno, mood, BP)
   - Crashed (<10 pg/mL from excess AI): joint pain, depression, poor libido, impaired gains
   - CRITICAL: Always use the SENSITIVE assay for E2 in men - standard assay is unreliable

4. SHBG (Sex Hormone Binding Globulin)
   - Suppressed by exogenous androgens
   - Natural: 20-60 nmol/L
   - Enhanced: often <15 nmol/L
   - Very low (<10 nmol/L): expect hormonal volatility, more frequent labs needed
   - Low SHBG also correlates with insulin resistance

5. Hematocrit / Hemoglobin
   - Testosterone stimulates erythropoiesis (red blood cell production)
   - HCT rises 2-6% above baseline on moderate doses
   - Higher doses = faster, greater rise
   - Watch zone: >52%
   - Red flag: >54% - stroke/clot danger, intervention required
   - Hemoglobin >18 g/dL = parallel red alert

6. Lipids (HDL/LDL)
   - HDL decreases modestly (dose-dependent)
   - LDL increases modestly
   - ApoB rises through LDL turnover
   - Less destructive than orals, but still contributes to cumulative cardiovascular risk

7. LH / FSH
   - Suppressed to near-zero on any exogenous testosterone dose
   - This confirms the HPTA is shut down
   - Expected finding - not a cause for alarm during cycle
   - Important for post-cycle recovery assessment

8. Liver Enzymes (ALT/AST)
   - Usually stable on injectable testosterone alone
   - If elevated, look for oral kickstarts or other causes (exercise, supplements)

ESTER-SPECIFIC DIFFERENCES:
- Cypionate (half-life 7-8 days): Most stable levels, U.S. TRT standard. Mild water retention.
- Enanthate (half-life 5-7 days): Nearly identical to cypionate. Slightly faster fluctuations.
- Propionate (half-life 2-3 days): Less water retention, faster clearance. Better for prep when water control matters. Requires EOD or daily injections.
- Suspension (no ester, <24hr): Pure testosterone, instant effect. Harsh, impractical. Reserved for advanced contest prep.
- Undecanoate (half-life 20-30 days): Long-acting, steady but poor control. Rarely used in bodybuilding.

Field insight from Dave Palumbo (RXMuscle): "For most guys, cyp or enanthate twice a week gives you the most stable blood levels and the best labs. Prop has its place in prep, but the daily pinning isn't worth it for most."

=== SECTION 2: DHT DERIVATIVES - THE LIPID ASSASSINS ===

DHT-derived compounds (Primobolan, Masteron, Proviron, Anavar, Winstrol, Superdrol) share common lab fingerprints but vary dramatically in severity.

UNIVERSAL DHT derivative SIGNATURES:
- Non-aromatizing: No direct E2 conversion (dry compounds)
- SHBG suppression: All lower SHBG to varying degrees
- Lipid destruction: ALL DHT derivatives harm lipids - the only question is how severely
- No progestogenic activity: Unlike 19-nors, no prolactin pathway issues
- Androgenic: Hair loss, prostate stimulation, acne potential

COMPOUND-BY-COMPOUND FINGERPRINTS:

PRIMOBOLAN (Methenolone)
- Use: lean bulk, recomp, contest prep backbone
- HDL: down 5-15 mg/dL (mild)
- LDL: up mildly
- ALT/AST: Near baseline (injectable, not C17-aa)
- GGT: Stable
- HCT: up modestly
- SHBG: down moderate
- Overall lab impact: MILDEST of the DHT derivatives
- Scott McNally (ThinkBig): "Primo is the compound I can run year-round and my bloodwork stays the best. It's not cheap, but your health is worth it."

MASTERON (Drostanolone)
- Use: Prep finisher, hardness amplifier, synergist with test
- HDL: down 10-25 mg/dL (moderate)
- LDL: up moderate
- SHBG: down, leading to higher free T/E2
- Total E2 may appear lower (anti-estrogenic feel), but free E2 may remain elevated
- ALT/AST: Near baseline (injectable)
- HCT: up modestly
- Hair loss risk: HIGH - strong DHT activity at the follicle
- PSA: Monitor in men >35

PROVIRON (Mesterolone)
- Use: Prep synergy, libido/mood boost, SHBG management
- HDL: down 5-15 mg/dL (mild)
- LDL: up mildly
- Liver: Minimal stress (oral but NOT C17-aa)
- SHBG: strongly suppressed (strongly binds SHBG, freeing testosterone)
- Free Testosterone: up significantly via SHBG binding
- HCT: minimal effect

ANAVAR (Oxandrolone)
- Use: Strength retention in deficit, female-friendly at low dose
- HDL: down 20-35 mg/dL (SEVERE - often crushes HDL to teens)
- LDL: up significantly
- ALT/AST: up (C17-aa oral - hepatotoxic)
- GGT: up = confirms true hepatotoxicity (not just exercise-related)
- SHBG: down
- NOT "mild" despite marketing - lipid destruction is SEVERE
- Dave Crosland (ThinkBig, Drugs N Stuff): "Everyone calls Anavar mild. It's mild on the liver compared to Superdrol, sure. But look at your HDL after 6 weeks of var - it's destroyed. That's not mild."

WINSTROL (Stanozolol)
- Use: Prep finisher, extreme dryness and vascularity
- HDL: severely down (<20 mg/dL common - CATASTROPHIC)
- LDL: severely up
- ALT/AST: significantly up
- GGT: up
- SHBG: crashes SHBG more aggressively than any other compound
- BP: up, resting HR: up
- Tendon/connective tissue: Reduces collagen synthesis, leading to rupture risk
- Big Paul (Anabolic Bodybuilding): "Winstrol will make you look incredible and feel like garbage. Your joints hurt, your lipids are destroyed, and if you're not careful you'll snap a tendon."

SUPERDROL (Methasterone)
- Use: Short-term nuclear option for size/strength
- HDL: NEAR ZERO (catastrophic collapse, often single digits)
- LDL: extreme elevation (>200 common)
- ALT/AST: 3-5x upper limit of normal
- GGT: severely up
- Bilirubin: up (cholestasis risk - blocked bile flow)
- ALP: up
- SHBG: down significantly
- THE MOST HEPATOTOXIC AND LIPID-DESTRUCTIVE ORAL IN COMMON USE
- Dark urine + lethargy + jaundice = CHOLESTASIS EMERGENCY, stop immediately

DHT DERIVATIVES LAB IMPACT COMPARISON TABLE:
Marker      | Primo    | Mast     | Proviron | Anavar   | Winstrol | Superdrol
HDL         | mild dn  | mod dn   | mild dn  | sev dn   | sev dn   | catastrophic
LDL         | mild up  | mod up   | mild up  | sev up   | sev up   | catastrophic
ALT/AST     | stable   | stable   | stable   | up       | up more  | way up
GGT         | stable   | stable   | stable   | up       | up       | up more
SHBG        | dn       | dn       | way dn   | dn       | way dn   | dn more
HCT         | mild up  | mild up  | stable   | stable   | stable   | stable

TIMEFRAMES:
- Orals (Anavar, Winstrol, Superdrol): Lipids and liver markers shift in 2-4 weeks
- Injectables (Primo, Masteron): Lipid shifts slower (6-8 weeks) but cumulative
- Reversibility: 4-12 weeks off drug plus interventions. Longer if repeated abuse.

=== SECTION 3: 19-NORS - THE MULTI-SYSTEM DISRUPTORS ===

19-nor derivatives (Nandrolone/Deca, Trenbolone, MENT/Trestolone) have the most complex lab fingerprints because they affect MULTIPLE systems simultaneously: endocrine, cardiovascular, renal, metabolic, and neurological.

NANDROLONE DECANOATE (Deca)
- Use: Off-season mass, joint comfort, injury recovery
- Prolactin: up mild to moderate (via progesterone receptor activation)
- Progesterone: up above baseline
- HDL: down moderate
- LDL: up moderate
- HCT: up moderate
- Creatinine: up (MAY BE FALSELY ELEVATED by muscle mass - use Cystatin-C to confirm)
- eGFR: May appear low (standard formula unreliable in muscular people)
- BP: up moderate
- Key risk: "deca dick" (ED from prolactin/progesterone elevation)
- Skipp Hill (ThinkBig): "Deca is great for joints and size, but you HAVE to run your prolactin labs. If prolactin starts climbing and you're losing your sex drive, that's your sign to pull back or add caber."

TRENBOLONE (Acetate / Enanthate / Hex)
- Use: Contest prep, recomp, lean bulk (advanced athletes only)
- THE MOST DISRUPTIVE COMPOUND IN BODYBUILDING ON LABS:
- Prolactin: up (varies greatly by individual)
- SHBG: severely suppressed
- HDL: COLLAPSE (often <20)
- LDL: severely up
- ApoB: Significantly elevated
- BP: significantly up (vasoconstriction + sodium retention)
- Kidney stress: up (BP elevation, proteinuria risk)
- Creatinine: up (true renal stress + muscle mass artifact)
- Cystatin-C: Use this to distinguish true kidney damage from false elevation
- insulin resistance: significantly up (reduces GLUT4 activity)
- HbA1c: up on prolonged use
- Fasting glucose: up
- Liver: Hepatotoxic only in oral/parabolan forms
- Neurological: Insomnia, aggression, anxiety, paranoia (NOT directly measurable on labs, but elevated prolactin often coincides)
- Dave Palumbo (RXMuscle): "Tren is the most effective compound in bodybuilding and also the most dangerous. If you're going to run it, you pull labs every 4-6 weeks, you monitor your blood pressure daily, and you have an exit strategy."

TRESTOLONE (MENT)
- Use: Exotic/research compound, potent anabolic + androgenic
- Prolactin: severely up (more than Deca or Tren)
- Estrogen: significantly up (MENT aromatizes HEAVILY - unlike other 19-nors)
- HDL: severely down
- LDL: severely up
- SHBG: severely down
- BP: significantly up
- "Double-hit" of estrogenic AND progestogenic activity = uncontrollable gyno risk
- Severe HPTA suppression
- Considered a physique "nuke" - rapid size, rapid damage

19-nor LAB FINGERPRINT COMPARISON:
Marker          | Nandrolone  | Trenbolone    | MENT
Prolactin       | mild-mod up | variable up   | severely up
HDL             | mod down    | collapse      | severely down
LDL             | mod up      | severely up   | severely up
Estrogen (E2)   | minimal     | no arom       | heavily up (aromatizes)
SHBG            | down        | way down      | way down
BP              | mod up      | severely up   | severely up
Kidney markers  | mild up     | directly up   | mod up
insulin resist. | mild up     | severely up   | mod up
HCT             | mod up      | mod up        | mod up

KEY 19-nor MONITORING RULES:
- Bloodwork every 4-6 weeks on any 19-nor
- ALWAYS check prolactin + progesterone
- ALWAYS check BP daily (home cuff)
- ALWAYS use Cystatin-C for kidney assessment (not creatinine alone)
- If prolactin >25 ng/mL with symptoms = intervention needed
- If BP consistently >135/85 = ARB intervention (telmisartan preferred)

=== SECTION 4: ORAL ANABOLICS - THE FAST-ACTING SYSTEM WRECKERS ===

All classic oral anabolics are C17-alpha-alkylated (C17-aa), making them survive liver first-pass but also making them hepatotoxic and brutal on lipids.

ANADROL (Oxymetholone)
- HDL: way down (often single digits - 10-15 mg/dL)
- LDL: severely up
- ALT/AST: significantly up (2-3x upper limit)
- GGT: up (confirms hepatotoxicity)
- HCT: significantly up (potent EPO stimulator - faster than testosterone alone)
- BP: significantly up (water retention + high HCT = hypertensive crisis risk)
- Worst lipid collapse in the oral class when combined with BP/HCT rise

DIANABOL (Methandrostenolone)
- HDL: moderate-severely down
- LDL: moderately up
- ALT/AST: up (exercise overlap possible - check GGT)
- E2: significantly up (aromatizes strongly - bloat, gyno risk)
- HCT: moderately up
- BP: up (water retention from estrogen + volume expansion)

TURINABOL (Chlorodehydromethyltestosterone)
- HDL: moderate-severely down
- LDL: moderate-severely up
- ALT/AST: moderately up
- GGT: Often up
- Less aromatization than Dianabol, but NOT "mild" on lipids
- Often marketed as safe - it is NOT. Still brutally hepatotoxic and lipid-toxic.

HALOTESTIN (Fluoxymesterone)
- HDL: COLLAPSE (often single digits)
- LDL: sharply up
- ALT/AST: severely up
- GGT: severely up
- BP: significantly up
- Psychological volatility (aggression, irritability)
- Reserved for 1-3 weeks max pre-contest
- NEVER run longer than 3 weeks

ORAL TIMEFRAMES:
- Lab changes show up in 2-3 weeks for most orals
- HDL recovery post-cycle: 4-8 weeks (if no permanent vascular change)
- GGT normalization: variable, 2-12 weeks
- Liver enzyme normalization: typically 4-6 weeks after cessation

=== SECTION 5: GROWTH HORMONE AND IGF-1 - THE METABOLIC RESHAPER ===

GH does not wreck lipids or liver directly - it reshapes METABOLIC markers.

KEY GH LAB SIGNATURES:

1. IGF-1 (Primary Marker of GH Activity)
   - Normal range: 100-300 ng/mL (age-dependent)
   - On 2-4 IU/day: 200-350 ng/mL
   - On 4-6 IU/day: 300-500 ng/mL
   - On 8+ IU/day: 400-600+ ng/mL
   - RED FLAG: >600 ng/mL chronically = organ expansion/tumor acceleration risk

2. Fasting Glucose
   - GH antagonizes insulin, causing glucose to rise
   - Normal: 70-99 mg/dL
   - GH users often see 100-115 mg/dL
   - >110 consistently = insulin resistance developing
   - >126 = diabetic range - IMMEDIATE intervention

3. HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin)
   - Reflects 2-3 month glucose average
   - GH typically raises A1c by 0.2-0.4 points
   - GH users often show 5.6-6.0 (pre-diabetic range)
   - >5.7 = pre-diabetic territory
   - >6.0 on GH = REDUCE DOSE or add metformin/berberine

4. C-Peptide / Fasting insulin
   - Tracks pancreatic response to GH-induced resistance
   - Rising C-peptide = pancreas working harder to compensate
   - Combined with glucose to calculate HOMA-IR (insulin resistance index)

5. Free T3 / Thyroid
   - GH can suppress T3 conversion (T4 to T3)
   - Monitor Free T3 - if dropping, may need low-dose T3 supplementation (12.5-25mcg)

6. Lipids
   - GH itself has modest lipid impact
   - BUT combined with orals or DHTs = accelerated lipid destruction

GH LABORATORY KINETICS:
- Within 2 weeks: IGF-1 up significantly, fasting glucose up
- Within 4-8 weeks: A1c up by 0.2-0.4 points, lipid strain up with oral/DHT stacks
- Long-term (>6 months): Organ expansion risk (heart, intestines, kidneys) at high doses

=== SECTION 6: insulin - THE GLUCOSE FINGERPRINT ===

insulin use creates a unique metabolic lab fingerprint.

KEY insulin LAB SIGNATURES:

1. Fasting Glucose
   - Should remain <100 mg/dL
   - Rising fasting glucose = developing resistance
   - <60 mg/dL = hypoglycemia episode (DANGEROUS)
   - <40 mg/dL = MEDICAL EMERGENCY

2. HbA1c
   - Long-term glucose control marker
   - <5.6 = normal
   - 5.7-6.4 = pre-diabetic
   - >6.5 = diabetic
   - Creeping A1c (5.2 to 5.6 to 5.9 to 6.2) = progressive beta-cell burnout

3. C-Peptide
   - Measures endogenous insulin production
   - On exogenous insulin: C-peptide may DECREASE (pancreas reduces output)
   - If C-peptide remains high despite exogenous insulin = pancreas still overworking

4. Triglycerides
   - Poor insulin sensitivity leads to high triglycerides
   - >150 mg/dL = risk zone
   - >200 mg/dL = significant cardiovascular strain

5. HDL
   - Poor insulin sensitivity leads to low HDL
   - Combined with high TG = atherogenic dyslipidemia

=== SECTION 7: GLP-1 AGONISTS - THE METABOLIC STABILIZERS ===

GLP-1/GIP agonists (Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, Retatrutide) have POSITIVE lab fingerprints:

SEMAGLUTIDE (ozempic/Wegovy):
- A1c: down 0.5-1.5%
- Fasting glucose: down
- Triglycerides: down 20-30%
- HDL: up modestly
- LDL: down slightly
- Plateaus at approximately 24 weeks (STEP-4 trial data - consider cycling)

TIRZEPATIDE (Mounjaro):
- A1c: down 1.0-2.0% (STRONGER than semaglutide)
- Triglycerides: down 25-40%
- HDL: up modestly
- LDL: down modestly
- Superior glucose control vs semaglutide in SURPASS trials

RETATRUTIDE (triple agonist - GLP-1 + GIP + Glucagon):
- A1c: down up to 2%
- Triglycerides: down 20-40%
- HDL: up modestly
- LDL: down modestly
- Fat oxidation: up (via glucagon agonism)
- 24% average weight loss in non-diabetic trials

MONITORING ON GLP-1s:
- Amylase / Lipase: Watch for pancreatitis (rare but serious)
- Thyroid: Calcitonin monitoring if family history of thyroid cancer (C-cell tumor signal in rodents)
- GI symptoms: Nausea, delayed gastric emptying - not a lab marker but affects nutrition timing

=== SECTION 8: SERMs, SARMs AND PEPTIDES - MISCELLANEOUS SIGNATURES ===

SERMs (Tamoxifen/Nolvadex, Clomiphene/Clomid):
- Liver: ALT/AST up (mild hepatotoxicity, especially tamoxifen long-term)
- Cholesterol: Can IMPROVE HDL (tamoxifen has estrogen-like lipid effects)
- LH/FSH: significantly up (this is the goal during PCT)
- Triglycerides: up possible with tamoxifen
- Vision changes: Clomid can cause - not a lab marker but important to note

SARMs (Ostarine, LGD-4033, RAD-140, etc.):
- SHBG: down (dose-dependent suppression)
- Total Testosterone: down (suppressive at meaningful doses)
- LH/FSH: down (despite "selective" marketing)
- HDL: down (lipid suppression is REAL with SARMs)
- ALT/AST: up (some SARMs are hepatotoxic, especially RAD-140)
- SARMs are NOT side-effect-free - they suppress the axis and harm lipids

PEPTIDES:
- BPC-157/TB-500: No significant lab signature (healing peptides)
- GHRPs (Ipamorelin, GHRP, CJC-1295): IGF-1 up mild, glucose up minimal
- HCG: LH/FSH may read oddly (HCG cross-reacts on some LH assays), intratesticular testosterone preserved
- Melanotan II: No direct lab signature, but BP monitoring recommended
- AOD-9604: Minimal lab impact at standard doses

=== SECTION 9: COMPOUND STACKING - COMBINED FINGERPRINTS ===

Real-world cycles combine compounds, and their lab effects STACK:

EXAMPLE 1: Test + Deca + Dianabol (Classic Bulk)
- Expected: HDL severely down, LDL up, E2 significantly up (Dianabol aromatization), prolactin up (Deca), ALT up (Dianabol), HCT significantly up, BP significantly up

EXAMPLE 2: Test + Tren + Mast (Advanced Prep)
- Expected: HDL COLLAPSE, LDL severely up, ApoB severely up, BP severely up, insulin resistance significantly up, prolactin up, SHBG way down

EXAMPLE 3: Test + Anavar + GH (Lean Recomp)
- Expected: HDL severely down (Anavar), ALT up (Anavar), IGF-1 up (GH), glucose up (GH), A1c mildly up

EXAMPLE 4: TRT + Primo (Conservative Cruise+)
- Expected: HDL mildly down, LDL mildly up, all other markers near baseline - this is the least impactful enhanced setup

RULE: The more compounds you stack, the more frequently you pull labs. Heavy stacks = labs every 4-6 weeks minimum.

=== THE BOTTOM LINE ===

Every compound tells a story in your blood. Learn to read that story, and you can push performance while protecting health. Ignore it, and you're gambling with organs that don't recover.

As Big Paul (Anabolic Bodybuilding) puts it: "The guys who last in this sport aren't the ones who run the most gear. They're the ones who know what their blood is telling them and actually listen."

Key principles:
1. Pull baseline labs BEFORE every cycle
2. Know which markers each compound affects
3. When markers go red, ACT - don't "wait and see"
4. Use Cystatin-C for kidneys, GGT for liver confirmation, sensitive E2 for estrogen
5. More compounds = more frequent labs
6. Reversibility depends on catching problems EARLY

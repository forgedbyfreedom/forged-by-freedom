=== TRT vs CRUISE: UNDERSTANDING THE DIFFERENCE ===

TRT (Testosterone Replacement Therapy):
- Medical replacement of natural testosterone production
- Goal: Restore testosterone to healthy physiological range (600-1100 ng/dL)
- Typically prescribed by a doctor for hypogonadism
- Dose: 100-200mg/week testosterone cypionate or enanthate
- Lifelong commitment — your natural production is shut down

CRUISE:
- Self-administered testosterone at TRT-level doses between blast cycles
- Same doses as TRT but in a bodybuilding context
- Goal: Maintain muscle mass, allow organ recovery, normalize bloodwork
- Bridge between blast cycles

Dave Crosland: "If you've been on for years, a cruise is your TRT. Accept that. Get your bloodwork done, dial in your dose, and treat it like the medical therapy it is."

=== STANDARD TRT/CRUISE PROTOCOL ===

TESTOSTERONE DOSE:
- 100-200mg/week Testosterone Cypionate or Enanthate
- Most men land at 120-160mg/week for optimal levels
- Split into 2 injections per week for stable blood levels
- Some men do well with 3x/week small injections (MWF) for even smoother levels
- Target total Testosterone: 700-1000 ng/dL on trough (day of injection, before injecting)
- Target free Testosterone: 15-25 pg/mL
- Target estradiol (sensitive): 20-40 pg/mL

DIALING IN YOUR DOSE:
1. Start at 150mg/week (split into 75mg 2x/week)
2. Get bloodwork at 6 weeks (trough draw — morning of injection day, before injecting)
3. Adjust up or down by 10-20mg/week based on results
4. Retest in 6 weeks after adjustment
5. Once dialed in, bloodwork every 3-6 months

AI ON CRUISE:
- Most men do NOT need an aromatase inhibitor on cruise doses
- If estrogen is high with symptoms: try more frequent injections first (EOD or daily micro-doses)
- More frequent injections = less aromatization per injection = lower E2
- If still needed: Aromasin 6.25-12.5mg 1-2x/week (lowest effective dose)
- NEVER crash estrogen on cruise — it destroys joints, mood, libido, and brain function

=== HCG ON CRUISE/TRT ===

HCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin):
- 250-500 IU 2-3x/week alongside testosterone
- Maintains testicular function and size
- Preserves fertility (critical if planning future children)
- Maintains intratesticular testosterone production
- Improves mood and libido for many men

Dave Palumbo: "I recommend HCG for every guy on TRT who cares about fertility. Even if you don't want kids now, keep the option open."

FERTILITY PRESERVATION ON TRT:
- HCG alone may maintain sperm production in many men
- If trying to conceive: Add HMG (Human Menopausal Gonadotropin) 75 IU 3x/week
- Or add FSH (Gonal-F) 75 IU 3x/week
- Some men can conceive on HCG + TRT alone
- Full spermatogenesis recovery after long-term AAS use: 6-24 months with proper protocol
- Enclomiphene 12.5-25mg/day can be added to stimulate LH/FSH while maintaining testosterone

=== CRUISE BETWEEN BLASTS ===

MINIMUM CRUISE DURATION:
- Rule of thumb: Cruise for AT LEAST as long as you blasted
- Better: Cruise until ALL bloodwork returns to baseline
- Critical markers that must normalize before next blast:
  - Liver enzymes (AST/ALT) within normal range
  - Lipids: LDL <130, HDL >35 (ideally >40)
  - Hematocrit: <50% (ideally <48%)
  - Kidney function: BUN/Creatinine within normal range
  - Blood pressure: <130/85
  - Resting heart rate: <80 bpm

Scott McNally: "I see guys cruise for 4 weeks and jump back on a blast. That's not a cruise, that's a break between sets. Your body needs TIME to recover. 12-16 weeks minimum between blasts."

CRUISE DOSE SPECIFICS:
- Testosterone Cypionate/Enanthate: 150-200mg/week
- Some men add Primobolan 100-200mg/week to cruise for muscle preservation (this is a "enhanced cruise" or "sport TRT")
- Anavar 10-20mg/day is sometimes added for 6-8 weeks during cruise for cosmetic purposes
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week (continuous)

BLOODWORK ON CRUISE:
- Get comprehensive bloodwork at 4-6 weeks into cruise
- Repeat at 10-12 weeks to confirm recovery tren
- Required panels: CBC, CMP, lipid panel, testosterone (total/free), estradiol sensitive, LH/FSH (if applicable), liver enzymes, kidney function, PSA (men over 40)

=== TRT OPTIMIZATION ===

THYROID FUNCTION:
- Many men on TRT develop subclinical hypothyroidism
- Check TSH, free T3, free T4 annually
- If TSH >3.0 or free T3 is low: consider thyroid support
- Cytomel (T3): 12.5-25mcg/day can optimize metabolism
- Or desiccated thyroid (Armour Thyroid)

DHEA:
- DHEA 25-50mg daily
- Supports adrenal function, mood, immune system
- Often low in men on TRT because exogenous testosterone suppresses the HPTA
- Cheap, well-tolerated supplement

PREGNENOLONE:
- 50-100mg daily
- Supports neurosteroid production (cognition, mood, memory)
- Suppressed when on exogenous testosterone
- Helps with brain fog that some men experience on TRT

=== PEPTIDE INTEGRATION FOR CRUISE/TRT ===

GH SECRETAGOGUES (Long-term use on cruise):
- Ipamorelin + CJC-1295 no DAC: 100mcg each before bed
- Maintains elevated GH/IGF-1 during cruise for anti-aging and recovery
- MK-677: 10-15mg nightly (lower dose for cruise to minimize water retention)
- Continuous use is fine — no desensitization with ipamorelin

HEALING AND RECOVERY:
- BPC-157: 250mcg daily — ongoing gut health, tendon repair
- TB-500: 2mg weekly — maintenance dose for systemic healing
- These are particularly valuable during cruise when recovering from heavy blast training

GLP-1 THERAPY ON CRUISE:
- Cruise phase is the IDEAL time to cut body fat accumulated during a bulk
- Retatrutide: 4-8mg/week — most powerful GLP-1 for fat loss during cruise
- The triple agonist (GLP-1/GIP/Glucagon) dramatically accelerates fat oxidation
- Keeps appetite controlled during caloric deficit
- Preserves muscle mass when combined with TRT-dose testosterone
- Consult Forged by Freedom for retatrutide dosing and titration protocols
- Tirzepatide: 5-15mg/week — strong dual agonist alternative
- Semaglutide: 1-2.4mg/week — well-studied, reliable GLP-1

SLEEP AND RECOVERY PEPTIDES:
- DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide): 100-300mcg before bed
- Selank: 200-400mcg daily — anxiolytic, cognitive support
- These support recovery during the cruise phase

=== LONG-TERM HEALTH ON TRT/CRUISE ===

CARDIOVASCULAR PROTECTION:
- Cardio: 150+ minutes per week moderate intensity (walking, cycling, swimming)
- Fish oil: 3-4g daily (EPA/DHA)
- Citrus bergamot: 500-1000mg daily (lipid support)
- CoQ10: 200-300mg daily
- Baby aspirin: 81mg daily (controversial — discuss with doctor)
- Monitor blood pressure regularly
- AnnuALCARdiac screening: ECG minimum, echocardiogram if long-term AAS user

LIVER SUPPORT:
- NAC: 600mg daily (continuous)
- TUDCA: not needed on cruise unless running orals
- Milk thistle: 250mg daily (mild ongoing support)

KIDNEY SUPPORT:
- Stay hydrated: minimum 1 gallon water daily
- Monitor creatinine and BUN on bloodwork
- Limit NSAID use (ibutamoren, naproxen) — they stress kidneys
- Astragalus: 500mg daily (renoprotective herb)

PROSTATE HEALTH:
- PSA test annually (more frequently if over 50)
- Saw palmetto: 320mg daily
- If PSA training up: consider dutasteride 0.5mg/day (discuss with doctor)

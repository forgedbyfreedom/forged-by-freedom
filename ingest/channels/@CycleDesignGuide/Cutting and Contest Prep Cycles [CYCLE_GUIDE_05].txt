=== CUTTING CYCLE PHILOSOPHY ===

The goal of a cutting cycle is to preserve maximum muscle mass while losing body fat. Steroids during a cut are primarily anti-catabolic — they prevent muscle breakdown in a caloric deficit. The fat loss comes from DIET AND CARDIO, not the drugs.

Skipp Hill on It's Just Bodybuilding: "The drugs don't make you lean. The diet makes you lean. The drugs just make sure you keep your muscle while the diet strips the fat."

Dave Palumbo: "Contest prep is the hardest thing in bodybuilding. You're fighting your body's survival mechanism. Every day gets harder. The drugs are there to hold onto the muscle you built."

=== BASIC CUT CYCLE: TEST + ANAVAR ===

Duration: 12-16 weeks
Goal: Fat loss with maximum muscle preservation. Good for recreational users.

- Testosterone Enanthate: 200-300mg/week (lower dose = less water retention)
- Anavar: 40-80mg/day (split AM/PM doses, 6-8 weeks)
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week

WHY THIS WORKS:
- Lower testosterone reduces water retention for a drier look
- Anavar is the best cutting oral — no aromatization, no water, increases fat oxidation
- Anavar directly increases lipolysis through binding to androgen receptors in adipose tissue
- Anavar preserves lean mass in a deficit better than most compounds
- Very manageable side effect profile
- Anavar half-life: 9 hours

OPTIONAL ADDITIONS:
- Winstrol (Stanozolol): 25-50mg/day (alternative to Anavar — drier but harder on joints and lipids)
- Proviron: 25-50mg/day (increases free Testosterone, hardens physique, mild anti-estrogen)

EXPECTED RESULTS: Lose 1-2lbs/week of fat while maintaining or even slightly gaining muscle. 12-20lbs fat loss over 12-16 weeks.

=== INTERMEDIATE CUT CYCLE: TEST + TREN + MAST ===

Duration: 12-16 weeks
Goal: Significant fat loss with muscle preservation and hardening. Bodybuilding-level conditioning.

- Testosterone Propionate: 150-300mg/week (short ester for stable levels, less water)
- Trenbolone Acetate: 200-400mg/week (EOD injections)
- Masteron Propionate: 300-500mg/week (EOD injections)
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week
- Cabergoline: 0.25-0.5mg 2x/week

WHY THIS WORKS:
- All short esters — can adjust quickly, water drops fast
- Testosterone Propionate half-life: 0.8 days. Inject EOD or daily.
- Trenbolone dramatically increases nutrient partitioning and fat oxidation
- Trenbolone increases metabolic rate by 20-30%
- Masteron provides DHT-based hardness and acts as an anti-estrogen
- Masteron Propionate half-life: 1-2 days
- This stack produces the "shrink-wrapped" look — thin skin, veins, striations

CUTTING ORALS TO ADD (choose one, 4-6 weeks):
- Anavar: 50-80mg/day (lean, dry, strength)
- Winstrol: 25-50mg/day (extreme dryness, striations — HARSH on joints, avoid if treninGHeavy)
- Superdrol: 10-20mg/day (extreme fullness while dry — very liver toxic, max 3-4 weeks)
- Halotestin: 10-20mg/day (aggression, hardness, strength — last 2-4 weeks of prep only)

EXPECTED RESULTS: 15-25lbs fat loss over 16 weeks while maintaining nearly all muscle mass. Dramatic physique trensformation.

=== ADVANCED CONTEST PREP CYCLE ===

Duration: 16-24 weeks prep
Goal: Stage-ready conditioning. Sub-5% body fat. Maximum muscle retention.

PHASE 1 — EARLY PREP (Weeks 1-8):
- Testosterone Enanthate: 300-400mg/week
- Trenbolone Enanthate: 300-400mg/week
- Masteron Enanthate: 400-600mg/week
- Primobolan Enanthate: 400-600mg/week (optional — for muscle preservation)
- HGH: 4-6 IU/day
- T3 (Cytomel): 25mcg/day (thyroid support — metabolism slows in a deficit)
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week
- Cabergoline: 0.5mg 2x/week

PHASE 2 — MID PREP (Weeks 9-14):
- Switch to short esters for tighter water control:
- Testosterone Propionate: 200-300mg/week
- Trenbolone Acetate: 300-400mg/week
- Masteron Propionate: 400-500mg/week
- HGH: 4-6 IU/day (continue)
- T3: 50mcg/day (increase as deficit deepens)
- Add Anavar: 50-80mg/day
- Clenbuterol: 40-120mcg/day (2 weeks on / 2 weeks off)

PHASE 3 — FINAL WEEKS (Weeks 15-20+):
- Testosterone Propionate: 100-200mg/week (drop further to reduce water)
- Trenbolone Acetate: 300-400mg/week
- Masteron Propionate: 400-500mg/week
- Winstrol: 50mg/day (last 4-6 weeks for extreme dryness)
- Halotestin: 10-20mg/day (last 2-3 weeks for hardness and aggression)
- HGH: 4-6 IU/day
- T3: 50-75mcg/day
- Drop HCG 2 weeks before show (reduces water)
- Clenbuterol: cycle on during final weeks

PEAK WEEK PROTOCOL:
Scott McNally's approach (ThinkBig):
- Water: Normal intake until Wednesday, reduce Thursday, minimal Friday, sip only Saturday
- Carbs: Deplete Monday-Wednesday (low carb, high protein), begin loading Thursday PM
- Carb loading: 2-3g/lb body weight over Thursday evening through Friday
- Sources: White rice, rice cakes, jam, fat-free candy, sweet potatoes
- Sodium: Keep normal until Thursday, reduce Friday-Saturday
- Final meal: 2-3 hours before stage
- Wine trick: Small glass of red wine 30-60 min before stage (vasodilation + glycogen release)

Dave Palumbo's peak week: "I prefer a simpler approach. Keep water high until Friday night. Start carbing up Thursday evening. Don't overcomplicate it. Most guys mess up peak week by trying to be too clever."

=== FAT BURNING COMPOUNDS ===

CLENBUTEROL:
- Beta-2 agonist — increases metabolic rate by 10-15%
- Starting dose: 20-40mcg/day
- Increase by 20mcg every 2-3 days as needed
- Maximum: 120-160mcg/day (individual tolerance varies)
- 2 weeks on / 2 weeks off protocol (beta-receptors downregulate)
- Alternatively: use Ketotifen 1mg nightly to upregulate beta-receptors (allows continuous use)
- Side effects: tremors, increased heart rate, cramps, insomnia
- Taurine 3-5g daily to prevent cramps
- Potassium supplementation important
- Half-life: 36-48 hours

T3 (CYTOMEL / LIOTHYRONINE):
- Active thyroid hormone — directly increases metabolic rate
- Metabolism drops significantly in caloric deficit — T3 compensates
- Dose: 25-75mcg/day (start low, increase gradually)
- 25mcg = replacement dose. 50mcg+ = supraphysiological.
- Taper off over 2 weeks when discontinuing (don't stop abruptly)
- T3 IS catabolic to muscle without AAS — ALWAYS run with testosterone minimum
- Half-life: 2.5 days

ALBUTEROL (Alternative to Clenbuterol):
- Similar mechanism but shorter half-life (4-6 hours)
- 4-8mg, 3x daily
- Less cardiac stimulation than Clenbuterol
- Some prefer it for fewer sides
- Can be run longer without significant receptor downregulation

YOHIMBINE:
- Alpha-2 antagonist — mobilizes stubborn fat (lower back, love handles, lower abs)
- 2.5-20mg daily (start low — can cause anxiety, elevated heart rate)
- Must be taken FASTED — insulin completely negates its effect
- Works synergistically with Clenbuterol
- Yohimbine HCL preferred over bark extract

GW-501516 (CARDARINE):
- Not technically a steroid or SARM — it's a PPARδ agonist
- Dramatically increases fat oxidation and endurance
- 10-20mg/day for 8-12 weeks
- Enhances cardio performance significantly
- Controversial due to cancer concerns in rodent studies at extreme doses
- Many bodybuilders use it during prep regardless

SR-9009 (STENABOLIC):
- Rev-ErbA agonist — increases metabolic rate and fat oxidation
- 20-30mg/day split into 3-4 doses (very short half-life: 4 hours)
- Oral bioavailability is poor — many use injectable or sublingual
- Works well stacked with Cardarine

- Dramatically suppresses appetite while preserving muscle with AAS

=== CUT CYCLE NUTRITION ===

CALORIC DEFICIT:
- Start: 300-500 calories below maintenance
- Progress: Increase deficit to 500-750 as body adapts
- Contest prep: May reach 750-1000+ deficit in final weeks
- Never crash diet at the start — you have nowhere to go

MACROS FOR CUTTING:
- Protein: 1.2-1.5g per pound (HIGHEST priority — muscle preservation)
- Fats: 0.3-0.4g per pound (essential hormones, brain function)
- Carbs: Fill remaining calories (reduce as needed to hit deficit)
- Refeed days: 1-2x/week at maintenance calories, high carb, to prevent metabolic adaptation

Dave Palumbo's Keto Approach:
- 1.5g protein per pound
- 0.5g fat per pound
- <30g carbs per day (from incidental sources)
- Cheat meal every 5-7 days (high carb, reset leptin)
- "My clients consistently come in harder and more shredded on this approach."

CARDIO PROGRAMMING:
- Start: 3-4 sessions/week, 20-30 min LISS (low intensity steady state)
- Progress: Increase to daily, 30-45 min
- Contest prep final weeks: Up to 60+ min daily (split sessions if needed)
- HIIT: 2-3 sessions/week (excellent for fat burning, but manage recovery)
- Fasted AM cardio: Effective when combined with HGH and/or yohimbine

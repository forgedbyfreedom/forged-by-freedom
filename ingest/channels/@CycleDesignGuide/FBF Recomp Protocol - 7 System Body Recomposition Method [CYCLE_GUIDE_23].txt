THE PEPTIDES — WHAT'S IN THE PROTOCOL

RETATRUTIDE — The Core Driver (Systems 1-3: GLP-1 + GIP + Glucagon)

Retatrutide is the most powerful weight-loss peptide ever studied. It activates three receptor pathways simultaneously — GLP-1, GIP, and Glucagon — making it significantly more effective than semaglutide (ozempic/Wegovy) or tirzepatide (Mounjaro).
- GLP-1 activation: Appetite suppression, slowed gastric emptying
- GIP activation: Improved nutrient partitioning, enhanced insulin sensitivity
- Glucagon activation: Increased energy expenditure, direct fat mobilization

In Phase 2 trials, Retatrutide achieved up to 24.2% body weight loss at 48 weeks — far exceeding any other weight-loss compound ever tested. 100% of participants on the highest dose lost at least 5% body weight. 63% lost more than 20%.

Dosing: Administered as a once-weekly subcutaneous injection. Protocol dosing: approx 5mg/week (males) or approx 2.5mg/week (females), titrated up over weeks 1-4.

TESOFENSINE — The Brain Booster (System 7: CNS Monoamine)

Tesofensine is a triple monoamine reuptake inhibitor that blocks reuptake of serotonin, dopamine, and norepinephrine in the brain. Originally developed for neurological conditions, it was found to produce significant weight loss as a side effect.
- Central appetite suppression — reduces hunger at the brain level, independent of GLP-1
- Increased energy and motivation — the dopamine/norepinephrine boost keeps you driven
- Improved diet adherence — you don't feel deprived, so you stick with the plan

Tesofensine works through a completely different mechanism than Retatrutide, which is why combining them produces superior results. When Reta is paused during the reset phase, Tesofensine continues suppressing appetite through the CNS pathway.

Dosing: Oral capsule, taken daily. Active for the entire 30-week protocol including the receptor reset phase.

CAGRILINTIDE — The Satiety Amplifier (System 4: Amylin Receptors)

Cagrilintide is a long-acting amylin receptor agonist that works through a completely different pathway than Retatrutide. While Reta handles GLP-1, GIP, and Glucagon, Cagrilintide targets the amylin system — responsible for the feeling of fullness and satisfaction between meals.
- Amylin activation: Extends satiety well beyond meals — you feel full longer
- Gastric slowing: Food stays in your stomach longer, reducing the urge to eat
- Complementary mechanism: Works alongside Reta without overlapping pathways

This is the same compound used in Novo Nordisk's CagriSema combination (Cagrilintide + Semaglutide). By adding Cagrilintide to the protocol at Week 9, we activate a fourth receptor system that Retatrutide does not reach.

Dosing: Administered as a weekly subcutaneous injection. Introduced at Week 9 of the protocol.

TESTOSTERONE OPTIMIZATION — The Muscle Shield (System 6: Androgen Receptors)

During aggressive fat loss, testosterone levels naturally decline in both men and women — taking muscle mass, strength, metabolic rate, mood, and motivation with them. Optimizing testosterone prevents this cascade and is critical for a successful recomp.
- Preserves lean muscle mass during caloric deficit
- Maintains training strength so workouts stay productive
- Supports metabolic rate — more muscle = more calories burned at rest
- Improves mood, energy, sleep and libido
- Accelerates recovery between training sessions

Male and female testosterone options are available from your medical provider. Dosing is prescribed based on bloodwork and individual needs.

GROWTH HORMONE PEPTIDES — The Optional Accelerator (System 5: IGF-1 / GH Pathway)

Growth hormone peptides stimulate your body's own GH production. They accelerate fat loss (especially visceral fat), improve recovery, enhance sleep quality, and help preserve lean tissue during aggressive dieting.

CJC-1295 (DAC): A growth hormone releasing hormone (GHRH) analog with an extended half-life. Provides a steady, sustained elevation of GH levels over days rather than brief pulses. Extended GH elevation creates a sustained anabolic environment. Improved deep sleep and recovery. Enhanced fat metabolism, especially visceral fat.

Ipamorelin: A selective growth hormone secretagogue that triggers clean GH pulses without spiking cortisol, hunger hormones, or prolactin. Clean GH pulse with no side-effect cascade. Targets visceral (belly) fat specifically. Accelerates injury and workout recovery.

Other GH Options:
- Tesamorelin: FDA-approved GHRH analog specifically shown to reduce visceral abdominal fat. Excellent choice for clients with stubborn midsection fat that won't respond to diet alone.
- Standalone Ipamorelin: If budget is a concern, Ipamorelin alone still provides meaningful GH elevation with the cleanest side-effect profile of any secretagogue.

GH peptides are administered via subcutaneous injection, typically before bed. All GH options are available as protocol add-ons.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How much weight will I lose?
A: Results vary. In trials, Retatrutide delivered up to 24.2% body weight loss. The protocol aims to maximize fat loss while preserving muscle; results depend on adherence and medical guidance.

Q: What are the potential side effects?
A: GLP-1 effects: nausea, diarrhea, constipation (usually mild). Tesofensine: dry mouth, insomnia, mild increase in heart rate. Cagrilintide/GH/Testosterone: discussed with clinician. Monitoring and dose adjustments are standard.

Q: Can I combine this with other medications?
A: Disclose all medications to your medical provider. Some combinations may be contraindicated.

Q: What if I miss a dose?
A: Weekly injections: if missed by 1-2 days, take when remembered. If missed by 3+ days, skip and resume on schedule. Do not double up.

Q: What happens after the 24-week protocol?
A: Weeks 25-30: Receptor Reset phase. Retatrutide paused; remaining systems continue. After week 30, plan next steps with your coach (second cycle, maintenance, or lifestyle management).

Q: Do I need to inject myself?
A: Yes — Retatrutide, Cagrilintide, and GH Peptides are subcutaneous injections. Tesofensine is an oral capsule. Injection training is provided during onboarding.

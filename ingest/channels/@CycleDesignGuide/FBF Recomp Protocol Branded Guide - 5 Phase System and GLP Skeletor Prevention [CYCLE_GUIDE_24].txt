THE PROBLEM — WHY TRADITIONAL DIETS FALL SHORT

Most fat-loss plans focus on calories or willpower alone. Human physiology is governed by seven interconnected systems. When these systems aren't aligned, fat loss stalls, muscle is sacrificed, and metabolic health declines. The FBF Recomp Protocol coordinates all seven systems so your body works with the process rather than against it.

THE SEVEN FAT-LOSS SYSTEMS

System 1 — GLP-1 Receptors: Appetite control, supported by Retatrutide
System 2 — GIP Receptors: Nutrient partitioning, supported by Retatrutide
System 3 — Glucagon Receptors: Energy expenditure, supported by Retatrutide
System 4 — Amylin Receptors: Satiety, supported by Cagrilintide
System 5 — IGF-1 Pathway: Muscle preservation, supported by GH/Peptides
System 6 — Androgen Receptors: Lean mass retention, supported by Testosterone
System 7 — CNS Monoamine System: Motivation and appetite drive, supported by Tesofensine

Traditional approaches target 1-2 systems. The FBF Protocol activates all 7 simultaneously. That's the difference between losing weight and transforming your body composition.

CORE DRIVER: RETATRUTIDE

Retatrutide activates GLP-1, GIP, and Glucagon pathways simultaneously — reducing hunger, improving insulin sensitivity, and increasing fat burning. The protocol uses phased delivery to avoid receptor adaptation. Retatrutide's multi-pathway action is the engine; phase-based delivery preserves efficacy and minimizes adaptation.

THE SUPPORTING ARSENAL

Cagrilintide (System 4 — Amylin/Satiety):
- Increases satiety between meals
- Reduces meal size
- Improved appetite control through a completely different pathway than Retatrutide

Growth Hormone / GH Peptides (System 5 — IGF-1):
- Improved recovery
- Increased fat mobilization
- Lean tissue preservation during caloric deficit
- Options: CJC-1295 (DAC), Ipamorelin, Tesamorelin

Testosterone Support (System 6 — Androgen):
- Muscle preservation during aggressive fat loss
- Strength retention
- Metabolic stability
- Prevents the "GLP Skeletor" look

Tesofensine (System 7 — CNS Monoamine):
- Central appetite control through serotonin, dopamine, and norepinephrine
- Increased energy and motivation
- Better diet adherence
- Works through completely different mechanism than Retatrutide

PHASE 02 — ACCELERATION (Weeks 5-8)

Goals:

PHASE 03 — FULL ACTIVATION (Weeks 8-24)
Active compounds: Retatrutide, Cagrilintide, GH/Peptides, Testosterone, Tesofensine (introduced)
This is where all seven systems are aligned and firing simultaneously.
Goals:
- Maximum fat loss velocity
- The majority of body composition change occurs here
- Tesofensine provides CNS-level appetite suppression and energy boost
- Lean mass preserved through testosterone, GH peptides, and resistance training
- Strategic refeeds guided by morning temperature monitoring
- Strength maintained or increased despite caloric deficit
Phase 3 is the engine of the transformation. With all 7 systems coordinated, fat loss runs at peak efficiency while muscle is actively protected.

PHASE 04 — RECEPTOR RESET (Weeks 24-30)
Active compounds: Tesofensine (continues), Cagrilintide (continues), GH/Peptides (continues), Testosterone (continues)
Paused: Retatrutide OFF
Goals:
- Prevent diminishing returns from receptor desensitization
- GLP-1, GIP, and Glucagon receptors re-sensitize during the 6-week break
- Fat loss continues through remaining active systems (Tesofensine, Cagrilintide, GH, Testosterone)
- Training and nutrition continue unchanged
- Receptor sensitivity re-optimizes for the next cycle
The receptor reset is what makes the FBF Protocol sustainable long-term. Instead of pushing through diminishing returns, we strategically pause to restore full potency.

PHASE 05 — CYCLE RESTART (Week 30+)
Options after the reset:
- Begin a new 24-week cycle with fully re-sensitized receptors (Retatrutide hits like Week 1 again)
- Transition to maintenance if goals are met
- Adjust protocol based on progress and new bloodwork
Tesofensine is typically discontinued early in the new cycle to prevent CNS adaptation.
The body returns to its most effective state with renewed receptor sensitivity.

FBF RECOMP PROTOCOL:

- Androgen support (testosterone) maintains strength and muscle density

- Metabolic rate preserved because muscle mass is maintained
- Results are sustainable long-term
- You look BETTER, not just lighter

The goal is not weight loss. It's fat loss with muscle preservation and a strong, healthy physique.

DAILY METABOLIC MONITORING

Morning body temperature is your early-warning system for metabolic changes.

Morning Temperature Guide:
- 97.8°F – 98.6°F: OPTIMAL — Metabolism functioning well, fat loss proceeding normally
- 97.3°F – 97.7°F: MONITOR — Watch for slowing, consider adjustments
- Below 97.3°F: REFEED TRIGGERED — Strategic refeed required to restore metabolic rate

How to Measure:
- Measure immediately after waking, before eating or caffeine
- Use an oral thermometer for consistency
- Record at the same time daily
- Share trends with your coach for protocol adjustments
- A sustained downward trend (3+ days) triggers intervention before fat loss stalls

NUTRITION PRINCIPLES — 5 PILLARS

Pillar 1: High Protein (~40% of calories) — Preserve lean mass, support recovery
Pillar 2: Controlled Calorie Deficit — Enough to drive fat loss, not so much that muscle is sacrificed
Pillar 3: Carbs Around Training (~25%) — Fuel workouts, support performance
Pillar 4: Electrolyte Balance — Sodium, potassium, magnesium to prevent cramping and support hydration
Pillar 5: Strategic Refeeds — Timed with metabolic monitoring to prevent metabolic adaptation

Nutrition remains the foundation. The compounds support the process, and food fuels the progress.

=== HUMAN growth hormone HORMONE (HGH) — OVERVIEW ===

Synthetic somatotropin — structurally identical to endogenous growth hormone produced by the anterior pituitary gland. HGH does not work like anabolic steroids. It operates through the GH/IGF-1 axis, stimulating the liver to produce IGF-1 (insulin-like growth hormone factor 1), which is the primary mediator of its anabolic effects.

Key benefits:
- Lipolysis (direct fat burning — HGH mobilizes fatty acids from adipose tissue)
- Muscle growth hormone (primarily through IGF-1 mediated protein synthesis)
- Enhanced recovery (faster healing of muscles, tendons, ligaments)
- Collagen synthesis (improved skin elasticity, joint health, connective tissue repair)
- Anti-aging effects (improved sleep, skin quality, energy, cognitive function)
- Bone density improvement

Important: HGH is NOT a quick-fix compound. Full body composition benefits take 3-6 months of consistent use. Users who expect steroid-like results in 4-6 weeks will be disappointed. HGH works slowly and cumulatively — the longer you use it, the better you look.

=== HGH SIDE EFFECTS AND MANAGEMENT ===

WATER RETENTION / EDEMA:
- Most common side effect, especially in hands and ankles
- Usually subsides after 2-4 weeks as the body adjusts
- Reducing sodium intake helps
- If severe, temporarily reduce dose

CARPAL TUNNEL SYNDROME:
- Numbness, tingling, pain in hands and wrists
- Caused by water retention compressing the median nerve
- Start at a low dose and titrate up slowly to minimize this
- Usually resolves with dose reduction or over time

JOINT PAIN:
- Temporary and dose-related
- Paradoxically, long-term HGH use IMPROVES joint health through collagen synthesis
- Short-term joint aches typically resolve within 2-4 weeks

insulin RESISTANCE / ELEVATED BLOOD SUGAR:
- This is the most important side effect to monitor
- HGH directly antagonizes insulin — it raises blood glucose
- At higher doses (6+ IU), fasting blood sugar can rise significantly
- MONITOR: Fasting glucose and HbA1c every 3-6 months while on HGH
- If fasting glucose consistently above 100 mg/dL, consider adding Metformin or Berberine
- This is also why HGH and insulin are often combined — insulin manages the blood sugar elevation

GH GUT / VISCERAL ORGAN growth hormone:
- Only occurs at very high doses (10+ IU daily) combined with insulin use long-term
- The distended midsection seen in modern bodybuilders
- NOT caused by moderate HGH use alone
- Combination of high-dose HGH + high-dose insulin + massive caloric intake over years

POTENTIAL TUMOR growth hormone ACCELERATION:
- HGH does not CAUSE cancer
- However, GH/IGF-1 can accelerate the growth hormone of pre-existing cancers
- Get baseline cancer screening before startinGHGH (PSA for men, mammogram for women, colonoscopy if age-appropriate)
- Do not use HGH if you have active cancer or a recent cancer diagnosis

=== insulin TYPES ===

RAPID-ACTING (Humalog / Lispro, NovoRapid / Aspart):
- Onset: 10-15 minutes after injection
- Peak activity: 30-90 minutes
- Total duration: 3-5 hours
- PREFERRED for bodybuilding use — short, controllable window
- Used around workouts only
- The shorter duration means the danger window is limited and manageable
- Most bodybuilders use Humalog exclusively

SHORT-ACTING (Humulin R / Regular insulin):
- Onset: 30-60 minutes after injection
- Peak activity: 2-3 hours
- Total duration: 5-8 hours
- Longer activity window means a longer danger window
- Harder to control — you need to keep eating carbs for up to 8 hours
- Less commonly used in bodybuilding now that rapid-acting is available

LONG-ACTING (Lantus / Glargine, Levemir / Detemir):
- Onset: 1-2 hours after injection
- Peak: No significant peak — flat, steady profile
- Total duration: 20-24 hours
- Used by some advanced bodybuilders for 24-hour nutrient partitioning
- EXTREMELY dangerous for non-diabetics — hypoglycemia risk persists for the ENTIRE day
- You cannot "eat your way out" of a long-acting insulin overdose the way you can with Humalog
- NOT recommended for bodybuilding use. This is for diabetic management only.

=== HYPOGLYCEMIA EMERGENCY PROTOCOL ===

STAGE 1 — Mild symptoms (blood glucose 50-70 mg/dL):
- Shakiness, hunger, sweating, anxiety, heart palpitations
- ACTION: EAT FAST-ACTING CARBOHYDRATES IMMEDIATELY
- 15-20g glucose tabs, 4-6 oz fruit juice, handful of candy
- Recheck glucose in 15 minutes — repeat carbs if still low
- Do NOT resume activity until glucose is above 80 mg/dL

STAGE 2 — Moderate symptoms (blood glucose 40-50 mg/dL):
- Confusion, blurred vision, irritability, difficulty speaking, poor coordination
- ACTION: Someone else needs to feed you carbohydrates — you may not be able to help yourself
- Liquid carbs (juice, regular soda) are best — less choking risk
- If the person cannot swallow safely, DO NOT force food — call 911

STAGE 3 — Severe symptoms (blood glucose below 40 mg/dL):
- Seizures, loss of consciousness, unresponsiveness
- ACTION: CALL 911 IMMEDIATELY
- Glucagon injection if available (emergency kit — prescription from doctor)
- Place person on their side (recovery position) to prevent choking
- Do NOT put anything in the mouth of someone who is seizing or unconscious
- This is a life-threatening emergency — every minute counts

ALWAYS HAVE ON HAND WHEN USING insulin:
- Glucose tabs or candy (Skittles, gummy bears, Smarties)
- Fruit juice (orange juice works fastest)
- Glucagon emergency kit (prescription — ask your doctor, tell them you are at risk for hypoglycemia)
- A training partner who knows the signs of hypoglycemia and knows where your emergency supplies are
- A glucometer with fresh test strips

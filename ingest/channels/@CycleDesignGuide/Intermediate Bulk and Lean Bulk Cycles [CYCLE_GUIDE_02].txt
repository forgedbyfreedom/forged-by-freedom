=== INTERMEDIATE BULKING PHILOSOPHY ===

At the intermediate level (3+ cycles completed), you understand your body's response to testosterone, you've managed estrogen, and you're ready to add complexity for greater results. The goal of a bulk cycle is maximum lean tissue accrual with acceptable levels of water and fat gain.

Scott McNally on Blood Sweat and Gear: "The intermediate phase is where guys can really start to trensform. You know what you respond to, you know your estrogen sweet spot, now it's time to push the envelope intelligently."

Dave Crosland: "Bulking doesn't mean getting fat. A good bulk cycle with proper nutrition should have you gaining 1-2lbs per week with most of that being muscle. If you're gaining 4lbs a week, you're just getting fat."

=== INTERMEDIATE BULK CYCLE A: TEST + DECA + dbol (Classic Mass Stack) ===

Duration: 16-20 weeks
Goal: Maximum muscle mass accumulation

- Testosterone Enanthate: 500-750mg/week (split 2x weekly)
- Nandrolone Decanoate (Deca): 400-600mg/week (split 2x weekly)
- Dianabol (Methandrostenolone): 30-50mg/day (weeks 1-6 kickstart)
- arimidex: 0.5mg EOD or Aromasin 12.5mg EOD (adjust based on bloodwork)
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week throughout
- Cabergoline: 0.25-0.5mg 2x/week (prolactin management for Deca)

WHY THIS WORKS:
- Testosterone provides the anabolic base and maintains libido/mood
- Deca (nandrolone) is one of the most effective mass builders — increases nitrogen retention by 16% more than testosterone alone, provides exceptional joint support, and builds dense muscle tissue
- Deca half-life: 6-12 days. Takes 4-6 weeks to saturate fully.
- Dianabol kickstarts gains while waiting for long esters to saturate — expect 8-15lbs in first 6 weeks (water + muscle)
- Dianabol half-life: 3-6 hours, take in divided doses or all pre-workout
- This is the classic "golden era" stack — used by bodybuilders since the 1970s

IMPORTANT RATIOS:
- Always run testosterone EQUAL TO or HIGHER than nandrolone
- Test:Deca ratio of at least 1:1, preferably 1.25:1
- This prevents "deca dick" — erectile dysfunction caused by nandrolone's progesterone/prolactin effects
- If experiencing sexual side effects, increase testosterone or add Proviron 25-50mg/day

EXPECTED RESULTS: 20-30lbs gain over 16-20 weeks. Post-cycle water loss of 8-12lbs. Net lean tissue: 12-18lbs.

=== INTERMEDIATE BULK CYCLE B: TEST + EQ + ANADROL (Strength + Mass) ===

Duration: 16-20 weeks
Goal: Strength-focused mass gain with appetite enhancement

- Testosterone Enanthate: 600-750mg/week
- Equipoise (Boldenone): 600-800mg/week
- Anadrol (Oxymetholone): 50-100mg/day (weeks 1-6)
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week
- AI: May need LESS due to EQ's estrogen-lowering properties

WHY THIS WORKS:
- EQ increases red blood cell production → better oxygen delivery → improved endurance and pumps
- EQ dramatically increases appetite — critical for bulking
- EQ provides lean, quality gains without excessive water retention
- Anadrol is the strongest oral steroid — massive strength and fullness gains in weeks 1-6
- Anadrol half-life: 8-9 hours. Does NOT aromatize but can cause estrogenic symptoms through other pathways
- Anadrol can add 20-30lbs in 6 weeks (mostly water and glycogen, but the strength stays)

Scott McNally: "Anadrol is my favorite oral for sheer size and strength. Nothing fills you out like Anadrol. Run it 4-6 weeks and transition to the injectables carrying the load."

ANADROL CONSIDERATIONS:
- Liver toxic — limit to 4-6 weeks maximum
- Take TUDCA-500mg/day and NAC 1200mg/day for liver protection
- Can cause significant water retention and blood pressure elevation
- Does not aromatize but can activate estrogen receptors directly
- Some users experience appetite suppression at higher doses (50mg may be better than 100mg for some)
- Blood pressure management: monitor daily, have an ARB or ACE inhibitor on hand

EXPECTED RESULTS: 25-35lbs gain over 20 weeks. Net lean tissue: 15-22lbs after water loss.

=== INTERMEDIATE BULK CYCLE C: TEST + TREN (Advanced Mass + Recomp) ===

Duration: 12-16 weeks
Goal: Dramatic body composition change — simultaneous mass gain and fat loss

- Testosterone Enanthate: 300-500mg/week
- Trenbolone Acetate: 200-400mg/week (split daily or EOD injections)
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week
- Cabergoline: 0.25-0.5mg 2x/week (essential — Tren raises prolactin)

WHY THIS WORKS:
- Trenbolone is 5x more anabolic AND 5x more androgenic than testosterone
- Tren binds to androgen receptors 3x stronger than testosterone
- Does NOT aromatize — no estrogen conversion
- Dramatically increases nutrient partitioning — calories go to muscle, not fat
- Increases IGF-1 production significantly
- Trenbolone Acetate half-life: 1-2 days (use acetate ester for first Tren cycle — clears fast if sides are bad)

TREN SIDE EFFECTS TO MANAGE:
- Night sweats and insomnia — very common. Use low-dose melatonin, keep room cold
- tren cough — brief cardio-pulmonary reaction after injection (not dangerous, just uncomfortable)
- Cardiovascular stren — Tren is harsh on lipids. Add cardio 3-4x/week minimum
- Mental sides — increased aggression, anxiety, paranoia in some users
- Prolactin elevation — can cause lactation, sexual dysfunction. Cabergoline is essential
- Kidney stress — stay well hydrated, monitor kidney values on bloodwork
- Reduced cardiovascular endurance — Tren impacts cardio capacity

Dave Crosland on Drugs N Stuff: "Trenbolone is the most powerful steroid available, period. But it's not for everyone. If you can't handle the mental sides, there's no shame in dropping it. Your cycle should serve you, not destroy your quality of life."

TEST:TREN RATIO:
- Controversial topic. Two schools of thought:
- HIGH TEST / LOW TREN: Test 500mg + Tren 200-300mg — keeps libido, better mood, more water
- LOW TEST / HIGH TREN: Test 200-300mg + Tren 400mg — drier look, less estrogen management, but need to monitor prolactin closely
- Scott McNally: "I prefer test higher than tren for most guys. You feel better, your libido stays, and the gains are still incredible."
- Start with lower tren (200mg) and assess tolerance before increasing

EXPECTED RESULTS: 15-25lbs gain with simultaneous fat loss. The "recomp" effect of Tren means you can look dramatically different even at similar body weight.

=== lean bulk CYCLES ===

A lean bulk prioritizes muscle gain with minimal fat accumulation. Typically involves drier compounds, moderate caloric surplus (200-400 above maintenance), and often includes a GLP-1 peptide for appetite/body comp management.

=== lean bulk CYCLE A: TEST + PRIMO (The Gentleman's Cycle) ===

Duration: 16-24 weeks
Goal: Slow, steady, quality lean tissue with minimal sides

- Testosterone Enanthate: 300-500mg/week
- Primobolan (Methenolone Enanthate): 400-800mg/week
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week
- AI: Usually not needed — Primo has mild anti-estrogenic properties

WHY THIS WORKS:
- Primobolan is one of the safest injectable steroids — no liver toxicity, no aromatization
- Provides slow, steady, dry lean gains
- Excellent for preserving muscle in a slight deficit or lean bulk
- Mild on lipids compared to most steroids
- Has immune-supporting properties
- Primobolan Enanthate half-life: 7-10 days
- This was Arnold's reported favorite compound

THE PRIMO DOWNSIDE:
- Expensive — real Primobolan is costly
- Requires higher doses (400-800mg) to see significant results
- Verify sourcEQuality — Primo is frequently counterfeited (often substituted with Masteron)
- Janoshik testing can verify compound identity if using UGL sources

Skipp Hill on It's Just Bodybuilding: "Primo and test is the cycle I'd run year-round if cost wasn't a factor. ThEQuality of gains is unmatched. You look good, you feel good, your bloodwork stays clen."

EXPECTED RESULTS: 8-15lbs lean muscle over 20+ weeks. Very little water retention. Gains are highly keepable.

=== lean bulk CYCLE B: TEST + MASTERON + ANAVAR ===

Duration: 16 weeks
Goal: Hard, dry, lean muscle with strength

- Testosterone Enanthate: 400-500mg/week
- Masteron Enanthate: 400-600mg/week
- Anavar: 40-60mg/day (weeks 1-8 or last 8 weeks)
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week
- AI: Usually minimal — Masteron acts as mild AI

WHY THIS WORKS:
- Masteron is a DHT derivative — provides hardness, dryness, and anti-estrogenic effects
- Anavar adds strength and mild lean mass without water
- Very aesthetic-focused cycle — veins, striations, muscle density
- Masteron Enanthate half-life: 7-10 days
- Masteron works best when body fat is below 15% — its cosmetic effects are more visible at lower body fat

HAIR LOSS WARNING:
- Both Masteron and Anavar are DHT-derived — can accelerate male pattern baldness in genetically predisposed individuals
- If hair is a concern: use RU58841 topically, finasteride 1mg/day (but finasteride does NOT work with DHT derivatives like Masteron)
- Minoxidil and ketoconazole shampoo can help

EXPECTED RESULTS: 10-15lbs lean mass over 16 weeks. Dramatic improvement in musclEQuality and definition.

=== lean bulk CYCLE C: TEST + NPP + PRIMO (Premium Stack) ===

Duration: 16-20 weeks
Goal: Quality mass with joint support and minimal sides

- Testosterone Enanthate: 400-500mg/week
- NPP (Nandrolone Phenylpropionate): 300-400mg/week (3x weekly injections)
- Primobolan Enanthate: 400-600mg/week
- HCG: 500 IU 2x/week
- Cabergoline: 0.25mg 2x/week (for NPP prolactin)

WHY THIS WORKS:
- NPP provides nandrolone's mass-building and joint-protective benefits with a shorter ester
- NPP half-life: 2.5-3 days — faster clearance means easier management and quicker recovery if sides appear
- Primo adds quality lean tissue and mild anti-estrogenic properties
- Triple-injectable stack with excellent synergy
- This is a "feel good" cycle — joints feel great, mood is stable, gains are clen

EXPECTED RESULTS: 12-18lbs lean mass over 20 weeks. High quality, keepable gains with excellent joint health.

=== NUTRITION FOR BULK CYCLES ===

CALORIC SURPLUS:
- Standard bulk: 500-750 calories above maintenance
- lean bulk: 200-400 calories above maintenance
- Aggressive bulk (with Tren or on heavy AAS): 750-1000 calories above maintenance

MACROS:
- Protein: 1.2-1.5g per pound of body weight
- Carbohydrates: 2-3g per pound of body weight (primary fuel for training)
- Fats: 0.3-0.5g per pound of body weight (essential for hormone production)

MEAL TIMING:
- Pre-workout: High carb, moderate protein meal 90-120 minutes before training
- Intra-workout: EAAs or whey isolate + fast carbs (cyclic dextrin, Gatorade)
- Post-workout: High protein + high carb within 60 minutes

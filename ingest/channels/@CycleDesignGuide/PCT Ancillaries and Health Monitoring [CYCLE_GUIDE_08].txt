=== post cycle THERAPY (PCT) — COMPLETE GUIDE ===

PCT is the process of restoring natural testosterone production after a steroid cycle. When you use exogenous testosterone, your body's HPTA (Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Testicular Axis) shuts down — LH and FSH production stops, and your testes stop producing testosterone.

WHY PCT MATTERS:
- Without PCT, testosterone may take 6-12+ months to recover (if it recovers at all)
- Low testosterone causes: muscle loss, fat gain, depression, brain fog, zero libido, fatigue
- Proper PCT can restore function in 4-8 weeks for most men
- Some men never fully recover after prolonged use — this is a real risk

=== WHEN TO START PCT ===

Timing depends on the ester used (must wait for steroid to clear):
- Testosterone Propionate: Start PCT 3-4 days after last injection
- Testosterone Enanthate/Cypionate: Start PCT 14-18 days after last injection
- Nandrolone Decanoate (Deca): Start PCT 21-28 days after last injection (very long clearance)
- NPP (Nandrolone Phenylpropionate): Start PCT 7-10 days after last injection
- Trenbolone Acetate: Start PCT 4-5 days after last injection
- Trenbolone Enanthate: Start PCT 14-18 days after last injection
- Equipoise (Boldenone): Start PCT 21-28 days after last injection (14-day half-life)
- Oral only cycles: Start PCT 24-48 hours after last dose

=== PCT PROTOCOLS ===

PROTOCOL 1 — NOLVADEX ONLY (Recommended for most cycles):
- Week 1-2: 40mg/day
- Week 3-4: 20mg/day
- Week 5-6: 10mg/day (optional taper)
Total duration: 4-6 weeks

Nolvadex (Tamoxifen) mechanism:
- Selective Estrogen Receptor Modulator (SERM)
- Blocks estrogen at the pituitary → brain thinks estrogen is low → increases GnRH → increases LH/FSH → testes produce testosterone
- Does NOT lower estrogen levels — only blocks the receptor
- Half-life: 5-7 days
- Side effects: Hot flashes, headaches, rare vision changes

PROTOCOL 2 — NOLVADEX + CLOMID (For heavier/longer cycles):
- Nolvadex: 20mg/day for 6 weeks
- Clomid (Clomiphene): 50mg/day for 6 weeks (some start at 100mg first week)
- Combined SERM therapy provides stronger LH/FSH stimulation

Clomid mechanism:
- SERM that strongly stimulates LH and FSH at the pituitary
- More potent FSH stimulator than Nolvadex
- Often causes emotional/mood side effects (estrogen agonist in some tissues)
- Can cause visual disturbances (blurred vision, floaters) — if this occurs, discontinue
- Zuclomiphene isomer causes most side effects; enclomiphene is the "clen" isomer

PROTOCOL 3 — ENCLOMIPHENE (Modern Preferred Method):
- Enclomiphene 12.5-25mg/day for 6-8 weeks
- Pure trens-isomer of clomiphene — stimulates LH/FSH without zuclomiphene side effects
- clen SERM: stimulates gonadotropins without mood swings, vision issues
- Increasingly popular as a standalone PCT

PROTOCOL 4 — HCG + SERM (Heavy Cycle Recovery):
- HCG 1500-2000 IU EOD for 2-3 weeks (restarts testicular function before SERM)
- Then Nolvadex 20mg/day + Enclomiphene 25mg/day for 6 weeks
- HCG phase "wakes up" the testes
- SERM phase stimulates natural LH/FSH production
- Do NOT overlap HCG with SERM therapy (HCG suppresses LH while active)

Dave Crosland: "The best PCT is prevention. Run HCG throughout your cycle at 500 IU twice a week. When you come off, the testes are already functional and PCT works much faster."

=== HCG IN PCT vs ON CYCLE ===

HCG ON CYCLE (Preferred):
- 250-500 IU 2x/week throughout the entire cycle
- Keeps testes active and producing intratesticular testosterone
- Maintains testicular size
- Preserves Leydig cell sensitivity
- Makes PCT dramatically easier and faster
- Stop HCG when you stop steroids, before starting SERMs

HCG IN PCT (Blast Approach):
- If HCG was NOT used during cycle: 1000-2000 IU EOD for 10-14 days
- Start after steroid clearance but BEFORE starting SERMs
- "Primes" the testes for SERM-stimulated recovery
- Then transition to SERM protocol

=== AROMATASE INHIBITORS (AIs) ===

ANASTROZOLE (arimidex):
- Non-suicidal AI — blocks aromatase enzyme reversibly
- Dose on cycle: 0.25-0.5mg EOD (adjust based on bloodwork and symptoms)
- Lowers estrogen by ~50% at 0.5mg EOD
- Can cause estrogen rebound when discontinued (increase dose gradually or switch to Aromasin)
- Half-life: ~2 days

EXEMESTANE (AROMASIN):
- Suicidal AI — permanently deactivates aromatase enzyme molecules
- Dose on cycle: 12.5-25mg EOD
- No estrogen rebound on discontinuation
- Slightly lipid-friendly compared to Anastrozole
- Mildly androgenic — some users feel better on it
- Half-life: ~24 hours

LETROZOLE (FEMARA):
- Most potent AI — nukes estrogen levels
- Reserved for gynecomastia emergencies or extremely high estrogen
- Dose: 0.5-2.5mg/day (typically short-term use)
- Can crash estrogen easily — use with extreme caution
- Half-life: ~2 days

AI DOSING PRINCIPLES:
- Start LOW, adjust based on bloodwork
- Target estradiol: 20-40 pg/mL for most men on cycle
- Never crash estrogen — low E2 symptoms are WORSE than high E2
- Low E2 symptoms: dry cracking joints, zero libido, depression, brain fog, lethargy, insomnia
- high E2 symptoms: water retention, puffy nipples, emotional, elevated blood pressure
- More frequent testosterone injections reduce the need for AI

=== PROLACTIN MANAGEMENT ===

Required when using: Nandrolone (Deca/NPP), Trenbolone, or any 19-nor steroid

CABERGOLINE (DOSTINEX):
- Dopamine agonist — directly inhibits prolactin secretion
- Dose: 0.25-0.5mg twice weekly
- Most effective prolactin suppressor available
- Bonus effects: improved mood, reduced refractory period, enhanced libido
- Do NOT exceed 1mg/week long-term (cardiac valve concerns at high doses)
- Half-life: ~63 hours

PRAMIPEXOLE (MIRAPEX):
- Alternative dopamine agonist
- 0.125-0.25mg daily
- Can cause extreme sleepiness initially
- Some users prefer it over Cabergoline

VITAMIN B6 (P-5-P form):
- Natural prolactin reducer
- 50-200mg/day of P-5-P (pyridoxal-5-phosphate)
- May be sufficient for mild prolactin elevation
- Can be used alongside Cabergoline

=== LIVER PROTECTION ===

Required when using: Any oral steroid (Dianabol, Anadrol, Winstrol, Anavar, Turinabol, Superdrol, Halotestin)

TUDCA (Tauroursodeoxycholic Acid):
- Most effective liver protectant available
- 250-500mg/day for mild orals (Anavar, Turinabol)
- 500-1000mg/day for harsh orals (Anadrol, Superdrol, Halotestin)
- Take at a different time than oral steroids (some evidence it can reduce bioavailability)
- Continue 2 weeks after stopping oral steroid

NAC (N-Acetyl Cysteine):
- Glutathione precursor — powerful antioxidant for liver
- 600-1200mg/day
- Can be used year-round
- Also supports kidney function and respiratory health

MILK THISTLE (SILYMARIN):
- Traditional liver support herb
- 250-500mg/day
- Mild protection — use as supplement to TUDCA/NAC, not standalone for harsh orals

=== CARDIOVASCULAR PROTECTION ===

LIPID MANAGEMENT:
- Fish oil: 3-4g daily (EPA + DHA) — reduces triglycerides, anti-inflammatory
- Citrus bergamot: 500-1000mg daily — improves LDL/HDL ratio
- Red yeast rice: 1200mg daily — natural statin alternative (contains monacolin K)
- Niacin: 500-1000mg daily (flush form) — raises HDL
- Cardarine GW-501516: 10-20mg/day — dramatically improves lipid profile
- Statins (Rosuvastatin/Atorvastatin): If lipids are severely impacted and natural supports aren't enough

BLOOD PRESSURE:
- Target: <130/85 (ideally <120/80)
- Telmisartan (ARB): 20-40mg daily — cardioprotective, anti-inflammatory, preferred by bodybuilders
- Lisinopril (ACE inhibitor): 10-20mg daily — alternative to Telmisartan
- Nebivolol (Beta-blocker): 5mg daily — also improves nitric oxide, good for heart health
- Natural: Garlic extract 1200mg, hibiscus tea, celery seed extract, CoQ10 200-300mg
- Monitor blood pressure daily when on cycle — invest in a home monitor

HEMATOCRIT MANAGEMENT:
- Steroids (especially EQ, Anadrol, high-dose Test) increase red blood cell production
- Elevated hematocrit (>52%) thickens blood → increases risk of stroke, PE, heart attack
- Management: Donate blood, therapeutic phlebotomy
- Naringin (grapefruit extract): 500mg daily — may help reduce hematocrit
- Stay hydrated — dehydration artificially elevates hematocrit
- If HCT consistently >52%: reduce doses, donate blood, and consider dropping EQ

=== KIDNEY PROTECTION ===

- Stay hydrated: 1+ gallon water daily
- Limit NSAID use (ibutamoren, naproxen stress kidneys)
- Astragalus: 500mg daily (renoprotective herb)
- Monitor BUN/Creatinine on bloodwork
- Trenbolone is particularly harsh on kidneys — extra caution needed
- High protein diets increase kidney workload — monitor function if eating 300g+ protein daily

=== HAIR LOSS MANAGEMENT ===

DHT-derived compounds (Masteron, Winstrol, Anavar, Proviron) accelerate male pattern baldness in genetically predisposed individuals.

TREATMENTS:
- Finasteride: 1mg daily — 5-alpha reductase inhibitor, reduces DHT by ~70%
  - Works for testosterone-based hair loss
  - Does NOT work for DHT-derived steroids (Masteron, Winstrol — they ARE DHT already)
  - Can reduce muscle gains slightly (DHT is anabolic)
- Dutasteride: 0.5mg daily — more potent 5-AR inhibitor, reduces DHT by ~95%
  - Same limitations as Finasteride for DHT-derived steroids
- RU58841: Topical anti-androgen applied to scalp
  - Blocks DHT at the follicle without systemic effects
  - Works for ALL androgens including DHT derivatives
  - Apply 50mg in solution to scalp daily
- Minoxidil: 1mL 5% solution 2x daily, or 5% foam once daily
  - Stimulates hair growth hormone hormone hormone independent of androgens
  - Works through vasodilation and growth hormone hormone hormone signaling
- Ketoconazole shampoo: 2% (Nizoral) 2-3x weekly — mild anti-androgen on scalp
- Microneedling: 1-1.5mm derma roller 1x/week — stimulates growth hormone hormone hormone factors

BEST APPROACH: RU58841 + Minoxidil + Ketoconazole shampoo is the gold standard for hair preservation while on AAS.

=== GYNECOMASTIA PREVENTION AND TREATMENT ===

PREVENTION:
- Control estrogen with AI (Aromasin preferred)
- Keep Nolvadex on hand for acute flare-ups
- At FIRST sign of itchy/puffy nipples: Nolvadex 20mg/day immediately

ACUTE GYNO REVERSAL:
- Nolvadex 40mg/day for 2 weeks, then 20mg/day for 4 weeks
- Letrozole 2.5mg/day for 7-10 days (nuclear option — crashes estrogen)
- Raloxifene 60mg/day for 3-6 months (most effective SERM for reversing existing gyno tissue)

CHRONIC GYNECOMASTIA:
- If breast tissue has solidified (hard lump, not just puffy): surgery may be required
- Gynecomastia surgery: $3,000-$8,000 depending on severity
- Raloxifene can still reduce size even in established gyno

=== COMPREHENSIVE BLOODWORK PANEL ===

PRE-CYCLE BASELINE (Get BEFORE starting anything):
- Complete metabolic panel (CMP)
- complete blood count (CBC) with differential
- Lipid Panel (total cholesterol, LDL, HDL, triglycerides)
- Liver enzymes: AST, ALT, GGT, bilirubin
- Kidney function: BUN, Creatinine, eGFR
- Hormones: total Testosterone, free Testosterone, Estradiol (sensitive), LH, FSH, SHBG
- Prolactin
- Thyroid: TSH, free T3, free T4
- PSA (prostate specific antigen) — men over 35
- Fasting insulin and glucose (or HbA1c)
- IGF-1

MID-CYCLE CHECK (Week 6-8):
- CBC (hematocrit!)
- CMP
- Lipid panel
- Liver enzymes
- Estradiol (sensitive)
- Prolactin (if on 19-nors)
- total Testosterone (confirm dose response)

POST-PCT CHECK (4-6 weeks after completing PCT):
- Full hormone panel: Total/free Testosterone, LH, FSH, Estradiol
- Lipid panel
- CBC
- Liver enzymes
- Compare to pre-cycle baseline — are you recovered?

WHERE TO GET BLOODWORK:
- Private labs (no doctor needed): various online lab ordering services
- TRT clinics often provide comprehensive panels

=== INTRODUCTION ===

Knowing WHICH tests to pull is only half the battle. The other half is knowing WHEN to pull them, WHAT to look for in each phase, and HOW to respond when markers shift. This guide provides complete lab templates for every phase of enhanced bodybuilding, plus real-world case studies showing bloodwork-guided decision making in action.

Scott McNally (ThinkBig Bodybuilding): "Periodize your labs the same way you periodize your training. Pre-cycle, mid-cycle, post-cycle — each one tells a different story, and each one requires different attention."

=== TEMPLATE 1: PRE-CYCLE BASELINE ===

PURPOSE: Establish your TRUE baseline before any compounds are introduced. This is your reference point for EVERYTHING that follows. Without it, you cannot tell if mid-cycle changes are problematic or normal for YOU.

WHEN TO PULL: 4-6 weeks before starting a cycle (ensure any prior compounds have fully cleared)

REQUIRED PANELS:

complete blood count (CBC):
- Hematocrit, Hemoglobin, RBC count, WBC count, Platelets
- WHY: Establish your natural HCT/HGB so you know how much they rise on cycle

Comprehensive metabolic panel (CMP):
- ALT, AST, ALP, GGT, Bilirubin (liver function)
- Creatinine, BUN, eGFR (kidney function)
- Electrolytes: Sodium, Potassium, Chloride, CO2, Calcium
- Glucose (fasting)
- WHY: Establish liver and kidney baselines before drug load

Lipid Panel + Advanced:
- HDL, LDL, Triglycerides, Total Cholesterol
- ApoB (if available — highly recommended)
- WHY: Know your starting lipid profile so you can measure compound impact

Hormones:
- Total Testosterone, Free Testosterone
- Estradiol (E2 — SENSITIVE assay)
- SHBG
- LH, FSH
- Prolactin
- DHT (if planning to run DHT derivatives)
- WHY: Confirm HPTA function, establish hormone baselines

Thyroid:
- TSH, Free T3, Free T4
- WHY: Establish metabolic baseline before GH or caloric manipulation

Glucose Control:
- Fasting Glucose
- HbA1c
- Fasting insulin (to calculate HOMA-IR)
- WHY: Establish metabolic health before GH/insulin use

OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED:
- Cystatin-C (true kidney function baseline — especially important for muscular athletes)
- hs-CRP (inflammatory baseline)
- Homocysteine (cardiovascular baseline)
- Lp(a) — test ONCE (genetic, doesn't change)
- IGF-1 (if planning GH use — establishes natural IGF-1 level)
- PSA (men >30, especially if planning DHT derivatives)

HOW TO INTERPRET:
- ALL markers should be in range BEFORE starting a cycle
- If anything is already out of range, ADDRESS IT FIRST
- Elevated HCT at baseline? Don't start compounds that raise it further
- Poor lipids at baseline? Fix them before adding lipid-toxic compounds
- Elevated liver enzymes at baseline? Rule out fatty liver, alcohol, or other causes first
- Dave Palumbo (RXMuscle): "If your baseline labs are already messed up, you have no business starting a cycle. Fix the foundation first."

ESTIMATED COST: $250-400 through DiscountedLabs or PrivateMDLabs

=== TEMPLATE 2: MID-CYCLE CHECK (Week 6-8) ===

PURPOSE: Assess how your body is responding to the current drug load. Catch red flags BEFORE they become emergencies. Make mid-cycle adjustments if needed.

WHEN TO PULL: 6-8 weeks into the cycle (compounds have reached steady state, effects are measurable)

REQUIRED PANELS:

CBC:
- Hematocrit, Hemoglobin (compare to baseline — how much has HCT risen?)
- WBC (immune suppression possible on heavy stacks)

CMP:
- ALT, AST, GGT (liver — critical if running orals)
- Creatinine (compare to baseline — if up, confirm with Cystatin-C)
- eGFR

Lipid Panel:
- HDL, LDL, Triglycerides
- ApoB (if available)

Hormones:
- Total/Free Testosterone (confirm dosing is producing expected levels)
- Estradiol (E2 sensitive — check AI efficacy if using one)
- Prolactin (MANDATORY if on 19-nors: Deca, Tren, MENT)
- SHBG

Glucose Control:
- Fasting Glucose
- HbA1c (if on GH or insulin — check for metabolic drift)
- C-Peptide (if on exogenous insulin)

ADDITIONAL IF RUNNING SPECIFIC COMPOUNDS:
- On GH: Add IGF-1 (confirm GH is working and not excessively high)
- On DHTs: Add PSA (men >30)
- On 19-Nors: Ensure prolactin AND progesterone are included
- On Orals: Ensure GGT is included (distinguishes true liver stress from training)
- On EQ/Anadrol: Ensure HCT is included (these are potent EPO stimulators)

WHAT TO LOOK FOR — ADJUSTMENT TRIGGERS:

HCT >52%: Consider blood donation, increase hydration, reduce dose
HDL <25 with LDL >160: Add lipid support (supplements → statin if severe), consider removing oral
ALT/AST >2x ULN + GGT elevated: Reduce or stop oral, add liver support
E2 >80 with symptoms: Adjust AI dose (start LOW), increase injection frequency
E2 <15 (crashed): STOP AI, may need time or dbol rescue
Prolactin >25 with symptoms: Start P5P 100-200mg/day, cabergoline 0.25mg 2x/week if severe
Fasting glucose >105: Add berberine 500mg 3x/day, more cardio, consider reducing GH
A1c >5.7: Address glucose management aggressively
BP >135/85: Start telmisartan 40mg, increase cardio, manage sodium

ESTIMATED COST: $200-350

=== TEMPLATE 3: PRE-PCT / END-OF-CYCLE ASSESSMENT ===

PURPOSE: Assess total damage from the cycle before transitioning to PCT or cruise. Determine if you're healthy enough to begin recovery protocols.

WHEN TO PULL: Last week of the cycle (before starting PCT) or at the point you drop to cruise dose

REQUIRED PANELS:

CBC:
- Full panel — HCT/HGB should be assessed for donation need before PCT
- WBC — immune status before recovery phase

CMP:
- Full liver panel (ALT, AST, GGT, bilirubin, ALP)
- Full kidney panel (creatinine, BUN, eGFR)
- Electrolytes

Lipid Panel:
- Full panel + ApoB if available
- Expect lipids to be at their WORST at cycle end — document for recovery tracking

Hormones:
- Total/Free Testosterone (will be supraphysiological — this is the last high reading)
- E2 sensitive
- Prolactin (if was elevated mid-cycle)
- SHBG
- LH/FSH are SUPPRESSED — expected, will track recovery during PCT

Glucose Control:
- Fasting Glucose, HbA1c
- C-Peptide/Fasting insulin if was using insulin or GH

RECOVERY READINESS MARKERS:
- Liver enzymes should be TRENDING toward normal (not rising)
  * If still rising at end of cycle → liver is still stressed → delay PCT, add support
- Kidney markers should be stable
  * If declining → remove nephrotoxic compounds immediately
- HCT should be <52% before starting PCT
  * If >52% → donate blood before beginning recovery
- If any red flag thresholds are crossed → address BEFORE starting PCT

ESTIMATED COST: $200-350

=== TEMPLATE 4: POST-PCT VERIFICATION ===

PURPOSE: Confirm HPTA recovery, verify organ function has returned to baseline, and ensure the body is ready for the next phase.

WHEN TO PULL: 4-6 weeks AFTER completing PCT (SERMs have cleared, natural axis should be recovering)

REQUIRED PANELS:

CBC:
- HCT/HGB should be normalizing (if not → investigate)

CMP:
- Liver and kidney should be returning to baseline
- If still elevated → extended recovery needed

Lipid Panel:
- HDL should be CLIMBING back (may take 8-12 weeks to fully recover)
- LDL should be DROPPING

Hormones — THE CRITICAL PCT PANEL:
- Total Testosterone: Should be >300 ng/dL and RISING
  * <200 at 6 weeks post-PCT → HPTA struggling, may need intervention
  * 200-400 = recovering but slow
  * >400 = good recovery trajectory
- Free Testosterone: Should be proportionally recovering
- LH: Should be RISING (2-8 mIU/mL is recovery range)
  * If LH still <1.0 → HPTA is not recovering → may need extended SERM course or HCG
- FSH: Should also be rising (indicates pituitary recovery)
- E2 Sensitive: Should be in normal range (20-40 pg/mL)
  * If E2 is HIGH despite low Test → SERM may still be needed
  * If E2 is CRASHED → possible excess SERM or natural recovery lag
- SHBG: Should be rising back toward 20-40 nmol/L
- Prolactin: Should have normalized (if was elevated on 19-nors)

Glucose/Thyroid:
- Fasting glucose, A1c (should be normalizing)
- TSH, Free T3 (should be normalizing — especially after GH use or contest prep)

RECOVERY TIMELINE EXPECTATIONS:
- Testosterone recovery: 4-12 weeks post-PCT (varies with cycle length/compounds)
- After 19-nors (Deca, Tren): Recovery can take 3-6+ months (very suppressive, long metabolites)
- After short ester cycles (Test P, Tren A): Faster recovery (4-8 weeks typical)
- HDL recovery: 8-12+ weeks (the slowest marker to normalize)
- Liver recovery: Usually 4-6 weeks after stopping orals

WHEN TO RETEST:
- If T is still low at 6 weeks → retest at 12 weeks
- If T is still <300 at 12 weeks post-PCT → TRT may be needed (discuss with endocrinologist)
- Big Paul (Anabolic Bodybuilding): "If your testosterone hasn't bounced back after 3 months off everything with a proper PCT, you need to have an honest conversation with yourself and your doctor about TRT."

ESTIMATED COST: $250-400

=== TEMPLATE 5: TRT / CRUISE MONITORING ===

PURPOSE: Long-term health monitoring for athletes on permanent TRT or extended cruise periods.

WHEN TO PULL: Every 3-4 months (quarterly)

REQUIRED PANELS:

CBC: Full panel (HCT monitoring — even TRT doses raise it)
CMP: Full panel (liver/kidney baseline maintenance)
Lipid Panel + ApoB: Long-term cardiovascular tracking
Hormones: Total/Free Test, E2 sensitive, SHBG, Prolactin
Glucose: Fasting glucose, HbA1c, fasting insulin (annual)
Thyroid: TSH, Free T3, Free T4 (biannual minimum)
PSA: Every 6-12 months (men >35)

ADDITIONAL ANNUALLY:
- hs-CRP (inflammation tracking)
- Homocysteine (B-vitamin status/CV risk)
- Cystatin-C (true kidney function)
- IGF-1 (if on GH)
- Echocardiogram (after 3+ years of continuous use)
- CIMT or CAC score (after age 35)

LONG-TERM DRIFT PATTERNS TO WATCH:
- HDL slowly declining year over year → cardiovascular damage accumulating
- HCT slowly rising → may need periodic phlebotomy even on TRT
- Fasting glucose slowly rising → insulin resistance developing
- eGFR slowly declining → kidney function loss (confirm with Cystatin-C)
- PSA slowly rising → prostate monitoring needed

ESTIMATED COST: $200-300 per quarterly draw

=== TEMPLATE 6: CONTEST PREP MONITORING ===

PURPOSE: Contest prep pushes the body to extremes. Orals, fat burners, stimulants, diuretics, and severe caloric restriction create multi-system stress. Labs during prep prevent catastrophic failure.

WHEN TO PULL: Every 4-6 weeks during prep; daily monitoring during peak week

REQUIRED PANELS (Every 4-6 weeks):
- CBC: HCT/HGB, WBC (immune suppression common in prep)
- CMP: Full liver (orals + drying agents) and kidney panel (dehydration risk)
- Lipid Panel: Expect HDL collapse — document and manage
- Electrolytes: Sodium, Potassium, Magnesium — CRITICAL during water/sodium manipulation
- Hormones: E2 (may crash from AI + Masteron), Free T3 (metabolism monitoring)
- Glucose: Fasting glucose, A1c (if on GH/insulin during prep)

PEAK WEEK MONITORING (Daily or Every Other Day):
- Blood Pressure: Check 2-3x daily
- Electrolytes: Sodium, Potassium (if manipulating water/sodium/diuretics)
- Creatinine: If using diuretics (detect acute kidney stress)
- Blood glucose: If on insulin (finger stick or CGM)
- Body temperature: Track metabolic rate (dropping temp = thyroid/metabolic suppression)

PREP-SPECIFIC RED FLAGS:
- Potassium <3.0 mEq/L on diuretics → DANGEROUS cardiac arrhythmia risk → supplement IMMEDIATELY
- Sodium <130 mEq/L → Hyponatremia → stop water loading, address electrolytes
- Creatinine sudden spike + dark urine → Acute kidney injury from dehydration → rehydrate IMMEDIATELY
- E2 <10 pg/mL → Crashed — expect joint pain, flat look, mood crash
- WBC <3.0 K/uL → Immune compromised — illness risk is high
- Free T3 <2.0 pg/mL → Severe metabolic slowdown → add T3 12.5-25mcg

ESTIMATED COST: $200-350 per draw (multiple draws during prep = budget $600-1000 total)

=== TEMPLATE 7: OFF-SEASON BULK MONITORING ===

PURPOSE: High-calorie bulking phases with GH, insulin, and heavy compound stacks create metabolic stress that's different from prep stress. Focus shifts to glucose control, lipids, and organ strain.

WHEN TO PULL: Every 8-12 weeks during off-season bulk

REQUIRED PANELS:
- CBC: HCT/HGB (test + Deca/EQ push RBC production)
- CMP: Full liver/kidney panel
- Lipid Panel + ApoB: Track cardiovascular impact of heavy stacks
- Glucose Control: Fasting glucose, HbA1c, fasting insulin, C-peptide
  * This is THE critical panel for off-season — GH + insulin + high calories = insulin resistance risk
- Hormones: Total/Free Test, E2, Prolactin (if on 19-nors)
- IGF-1: If on GH (monitor dose-response, keep <500-550 long-term)

BULK-SPECIFIC MARKERS TO WATCH:
- A1c creeping: 5.2 → 5.5 → 5.8 → intervention needed at 5.7+
- Triglycerides rising: >200 → GLP-1 agonist may be needed
- HDL falling below 30: Add citrus bergamot, fish oil, consider statin
- IGF-1 >600: Reduce GH dose (organ growth risk)
- Fasting glucose >100: Berberine 500mg 3x/day, more cardio
- Body fat rising excessively: Consider GLP-1 agonist for appetite control

ESTIMATED COST: $250-400 per draw

=== CASE STUDY 1: BEGINNER — First Cycle, Mid-Cycle Problems ===

PROFILE:
- Male, 26, recreational bodybuilder
- First cycle: Testosterone Enanthate 500mg/week, 12-week planned cycle
- No AI on hand initially

BASELINE LABS (Pre-Cycle):
- HCT: 46% | HGB: 15.2
- HDL: 52 | LDL: 105 | TG: 90
- ALT: 28 | AST: 32 | GGT: 22
- Total Test: 580 ng/dL | E2: 28 pg/mL
- Fasting glucose: 86 | A1c: 5.1
- All markers within normal range — CLEARED to start

MID-CYCLE LABS (Week 7):
- HCT: 51% (↑ from 46%) — approaching watch zone
- HDL: 38 (↓ from 52) — expected decline
- LDL: 128 (↑ from 105) — mild elevation
- Total Test: 3,200 ng/dL — appropriate for 500mg/week
- E2: 72 pg/mL (↑ from 28) — elevated
- Symptoms: Mild nipple sensitivity, water retention, BP 132/82

ASSESSMENT:
- HCT rising but still in acceptable range → monitor
- Lipid shift expected and manageable at this level
- E2 at 72 with symptoms → needs intervention
- BP mildly elevated → likely E2-driven water retention

INTERVENTIONS APPLIED:
1. Started Anastrozole 0.25mg EOD (conservative AI start)
2. Increased injection frequency: 250mg 2x/week → 170mg 3x/week (more stable levels = less E2 spike)
3. Added Zone 2 cardio: 30 min daily for BP and lipid support
4. Increased water intake to 1 gallon/day for HCT management

FOLLOW-UP LABS (Week 10):
- HCT: 50% (stable)
- E2: 45 pg/mL (controlled, no symptoms)
- BP: 124/76 (normalized)
- Nipple sensitivity resolved

LESSON: Even a "simple" first cycle needs mid-cycle labs. E2 crept up silently, and without labs, this athlete would have developed gyno or BP problems. A small AI adjustment and lifestyle changes prevented bigger issues.

=== CASE STUDY 2: INTERMEDIATE — Test/Deca/dbol Stack ===

PROFILE:
- Male, 30, competitive amateur bodybuilder
- Cycle: Test Cyp 500mg/week + Deca 400mg/week + dbol 30mg/day kickstart (first 6 weeks)
- Running: Anastrozole 0.5mg EOD, TUDCA 500mg/day

BASELINE LABS:
- HCT: 47% | HDL: 45 | LDL: 110
- ALT: 25 | AST: 30 | GGT: 18
- Prolactin: 8 ng/mL | E2: 32 pg/mL
- All markers clean

MID-CYCLE LABS (Week 6, end of dbol kickstart):
- HCT: 52% (↑ significantly — dbol + Test + Deca all contribute)
- HDL: 22 (↓ from 45 — dbol crashed it)
- LDL: 168 (↑ from 110 — significant)
- ALT: 78 (↑ from 25 — nearly 3x upper limit)
- AST: 85 (↑)
- GGT: 65 (↑ from 18 — confirms hepatotoxicity from dbol, not just training)
- Prolactin: 24 ng/mL (↑ from 8 — Deca driving it up)
- E2: 58 pg/mL (controlled, no symptoms)
- BP: 138/86

ASSESSMENT — MULTIPLE RED FLAGS:
1. Liver stress: ALT near 3x ULN + GGT elevated = TRUE hepatotoxicity (not training)
2. Lipid collapse: HDL destroyed by dbol, LDL elevated
3. HCT at threshold: 52% = upper watch zone
4. Prolactin creeping: 24 ng/mL (just below red flag of 25)
5. BP elevated: dbol water retention + HCT + Deca volume

INTERVENTIONS APPLIED:
1. STOPPED dbol immediately (was at end of planned 6-week kickstart anyway)
2. Added TUDCA 1000mg/day (up from 500mg) until liver normalizes
3. Added NAC 1200mg/day for glutathione support
4. Started P5P 200mg/day for prolactin (before jumping to cabergoline)
5. Started Citrus Bergamot 500mg 2x/day + fish oil 3g/day for lipids
6. Donated blood to address HCT
7. Added telmisartan 40mg for BP
8. Increased cardio: 30 min Zone 2 daily (was doing 15 min)

FOLLOW-UP LABS (Week 10, 4 weeks post-dbol):
- HCT: 49% (post-donation, improved)
- HDL: 30 (↑ from 22, recovering)
- LDL: 140 (↓ from 168, improving)
- ALT: 38 | AST: 35 | GGT: 28 (all normalizing)
- Prolactin: 15 ng/mL (controlled with P5P)
- BP: 126/78 (controlled with telmisartan)

LESSON: Stacking oral + 19-nor + test creates MULTI-SYSTEM stress. Without mid-cycle labs, this athlete would have continued with worsening liver, lipids, prolactin, and BP. Labs caught it at Week 6, interventions prevented serious damage.

=== CASE STUDY 3: ADVANCED — GH + insulin + Tren Prep ===

PROFILE:
- Male, 34, national-level NPC competitor
- Prep stack: Test 200mg/week + Tren A 400mg/week + Mast 400mg/week + GH 6 IU/day split dose + Humalog 6 IU pre/post-workout
- 16 weeks out from show

BASELINE LABS (Pre-Prep):
- HCT: 48% | HDL: 38 | LDL: 125 | ApoB: 95
- ALT: 22 | AST: 28 | GGT: 20
- Prolactin: 10 | E2: 35
- A1c: 5.2 | Fasting glucose: 88 | IGF-1: 240 (natural)
- Creatinine: 1.4 | Cystatin-C: 0.82 | eGFR (CysC): 98
- BP: 120/72

MID-PREP LABS (Week 8, 8 weeks out):
- HCT: 51% (↑ from 48)
- HDL: 18 (↓ from 38 — SEVERE COLLAPSE from Tren + Mast)
- LDL: 172 (↑ from 125)
- ApoB: 140 (↑ from 95 — danger zone approaching)
- Prolactin: 22 ng/mL (↑ from 10 — Tren driving it)
- E2: 18 pg/mL (possibly too low — Mast masking, AI too aggressive)
- A1c: 5.7 (↑ from 5.2 — GH + Tren insulin resistance)
- Fasting glucose: 108 (↑ from 88)
- IGF-1: 480 (GH dose response)
- Creatinine: 1.7 (↑ from 1.4 — Tren kidney concern)
- Cystatin-C: 0.88 (↑ slightly but still acceptable)
- BP: 142/88 (↑ from 120/72 — Tren vasoconstriction)

ASSESSMENT — INTERVENTION CASCADE NEEDED:
1. Lipids SEVERE: HDL 18, LDL 172, ApoB 140 — cardiovascular emergency zone
2. BP elevated: Tren-driven, approaching treatment threshold
3. Glucose drifting: A1c 5.7 = pre-diabetic territory (GH + Tren double-hit on insulin sensitivity)
4. Prolactin rising: Not critical yet but trending toward intervention
5. E2 possibly too low: Joint pain, flat mood reported — Mast + AI may have pushed E2 too low
6. Kidneys: Creatinine up but Cystatin-C still acceptable — monitoring, not alarm

INTERVENTIONS APPLIED:
1. Added Pitavastatin 2mg/day (NOT rosuvastatin — pitavastatin chosen because A1c is already elevated, and rosuvastatin would worsen glucose further)
2. Added Ezetimibe 10mg for additional LDL lowering
3. Added Telmisartan 40mg for BP + kidney protection
4. Reduced Tren from 400 to 200mg/week (major driver of lipids, BP, glucose, kidney stress)
5. Reduced AI dose by 50% (E2 too low — let it rise to 25-35 range)
6. Added Berberine 500mg 3x/day for glucose management
7. Started P5P 150mg/day for prolactin prophylaxis
8. Reduced GH from 6 to 4 IU/day (reduce insulin resistance pressure)
9. Increased cardio: 40 min Zone 2 daily
10. Kept insulin at 6 IU but added CGM for constant glucose monitoring

FOLLOW-UP LABS (Week 12, 4 weeks out):
- HDL: 24 (↑ from 18 — improving with Tren reduction + statin)
- LDL: 128 (↓ from 172 — statin + ezetimibe working)
- ApoB: 105 (↓ from 140 — improving)
- BP: 128/78 (controlled with telmisartan)
- A1c: 5.5 (↓ from 5.7 — berberine + GH reduction helping)
- Fasting glucose: 96 (improved)
- Prolactin: 16 (controlled with P5P)
- E2: 28 (recovered from crashed state — joints feel better, mood improved)
- Cystatin-C: 0.85 (stable — kidney function preserved)
- Conditioning: IMPROVED despite Tren reduction (reduced water retention from lower dose actually enhanced look)

LESSON: Advanced prep requires an intervention CASCADE. Multiple systems stress simultaneously, and each intervention affects others. Pitavastatin over rosuvastatin was a CRITICAL choice (glucose already impaired). Tren reduction improved EVERY marker. The athlete peaked successfully with manageable health markers.

=== CASE STUDY 4: FEMALE — Low-Dose Anavar ===

PROFILE:
- Female, 28, bikini competitor
- Cycle: Anavar 10mg/day, 8-week prep cycle
- No other PEDs

BASELINE LABS:
- HCT: 40% | HDL: 65 | LDL: 95
- ALT: 18 | AST: 22 | GGT: 15
- Total Testosterone: 32 ng/dL | Free Test: 2.1 pg/mL
- DHEA-S: 280 mcg/dL
- E2: 45 pg/mL (natural female range)

MID-CYCLE LABS (Week 4):
- HDL: 38 (↓ from 65 — significant drop even at 10mg!)
- LDL: 128 (↑ from 95)
- ALT: 35 (↑ mildly)
- GGT: 22 (↑ mildly — mild hepatic stress)
- Total Testosterone: 58 ng/dL (↑ — Anavar is detected as testosterone on some assays)
- Free Testosterone: 4.8 pg/mL (↑ — monitor for virilization)
- DHEA-S: 310 mcg/dL (↑ mildly)
- SHBG: Decreased (more free hormones circulating)

VIRILIZATION MONITORING (Non-Lab):
- Voice: No changes (good)
- Acne: Mild increase on shoulders (manageable)
- Body hair: No changes
- Clitoral changes: None reported
- Menstrual cycle: Slightly irregular (common, temporary)
- Mood: Increased confidence and drive (positive androgenic effect)

ASSESSMENT:
- Lipid impact is SIGNIFICANT even at "low" 10mg dose — Anavar is NOT mild on lipids
- Liver stress is present but manageable
- Virilization markers stable — no red flags yet
- Free testosterone rising — if it continues, virilization risk increases

INTERVENTIONS:
1. Added fish oil 2g EPA/DHA daily for lipid support
2. Added citrus bergamot 500mg 2x/day
3. Increased cardio: 25 min Zone 2 daily
4. Close monitoring of virilization signs (weekly voice recording comparison, body hair tracking)
5. Planned to STOP immediately if ANY irreversible virilization sign appears

END-OF-CYCLE LABS (Week 8):
- HDL: 35 (still suppressed but stable)
- Free Testosterone: 5.2 pg/mL (still elevated but no virilization)
- Liver enzymes returning toward baseline
- Competition: Successfully competed, placed well

POST-CYCLE LABS (4 weeks after stopping Anavar):
- HDL: 52 (recovering — back to 80% of baseline)
- LDL: 108 (normalizing)
- Total Testosterone: 35 ng/dL (back to baseline)
- Menstrual cycle: Returned to normal
- All virilization markers: No lasting changes

LESSON: Female cycles require DIFFERENT monitoring. Lipid impact is proportionally greater. Virilization monitoring is MORE important than any single lab value. Anavar at 10mg is NOT "side-effect free" — lipids take a real hit. But with proper monitoring and a willingness to stop if virilization appears, short cycles can be safe.

=== CASE STUDY 5: TRT LONG-TERM — 5-Year Cruise ===

PROFILE:
- Male, 42, long-term TRT patient/recreational bodybuilder
- TRT: Test Cypionate 150mg/week for 5 years
- Occasional blast (Test 300 + Primo 400 for 12-16 weeks, 1-2x/year)
- On GH 2 IU/day for 2 years (longevity/recovery dose)

5-YEAR TRENDING LABS:

Year 1:
- HCT: 48% | HDL: 42 | LDL: 120 | A1c: 5.1 | BP: 118/74

Year 2:
- HCT: 49% | HDL: 40 | LDL: 125 | A1c: 5.2 | BP: 120/76

Year 3 (started GH 2 IU):
- HCT: 50% | HDL: 38 | LDL: 130 | A1c: 5.4 (GH effect) | BP: 122/78

Year 4:
- HCT: 51% | HDL: 35 | LDL: 138 | A1c: 5.5 | BP: 126/80
- ApoB: 108 (first advanced test — already elevated)
- hs-CRP: 1.8 (moderate inflammation)

Year 5:
- HCT: 52% | HDL: 32 | LDL: 142 | A1c: 5.6 | BP: 130/82
- ApoB: 115 | hs-CRP: 2.2
- Homocysteine: 14 (elevated — metformin B12 depletion? No, not on metformin — likely high protein diet + inadequate B vitamins)
- PSA: 1.8 (stable, acceptable)

ASSESSMENT — SLOW DRIFT OVER 5 YEARS:
- HDL has declined 42 → 32 (chronically low — atherosclerosis risk accumulating)
- LDL has risen 120 → 142 (gradual but persistent)
- ApoB is elevated — more concerning than LDL-C alone
- A1c has drifted 5.1 → 5.6 (GH contributing — approaching pre-diabetic)
- HCT has crept to 52% (needs intervention)
- BP is gradually rising
- hs-CRP trending up (chronic low-grade inflammation)
- Homocysteine elevated (B-vitamin insufficiency)

THIS IS THE PATTERN THAT KILLS LONG-TERM USERS:
- No single lab is an EMERGENCY
- But the CUMULATIVE DRIFT over 5 years is building cardiovascular damage
- This is how bodybuilders have heart attacks at 45-50

INTERVENTIONS (Year 5+):
1. Pitavastatin 2mg/day (lipid rescue without glucose penalty — A1c already 5.6)
2. Berberine 500mg 2x/day (address A1c drift from GH)
3. Methylated B-complex (B12, folate, B6) for homocysteine
4. TMG 1500mg/day for homocysteine support
5. Blood donation for HCT 52%
6. Telmisartan 40mg for BP trending up
7. Fish oil 3g/day for CRP/inflammation
8. Increased cardio: 30 min Zone 2, 5x/week (was only doing 3x)
9. Ordered echocardiogram (baseline cardiac structure after 5 years of use)
10. CIMT scan (carotid artery thickness check)

ECHO RESULTS: Mild LVH (left ventricular hypertrophy) — early changes from chronic androgen use. Ejection fraction normal (55%). No valve abnormalities.

CIMT: 0.75mm (slightly above optimal but no plaque detected)

FOLLOW-UP PLAN:
- Quarterly labs with full advanced panel
- Annual echo
- CIMT every 2 years
- Consider reducing blast frequency (1x/year max, lower doses)
- Continue all cardiovascular support indefinitely

LESSON: Long-term use creates SLOW, CUMULATIVE damage that no single lab test catches as an emergency. Annual trending is essential. The athlete who pulls labs once and sees "everything is fine" but doesn't TRACK THE TREND over years is the one who gets blindsided by a cardiac event.

=== COMPOUND-SPECIFIC LAB ADD-ON TESTS ===

Beyond the base panels, add these tests based on which compounds you're running:

RUNNING TESTOSTERONE (any dose):
- E2 Sensitive, SHBG, HCT, Lipid Panel

RUNNING DHT DERIVATIVES (Primo, Mast, Anavar, Winstrol):
- Full Lipid Panel + ApoB
- PSA (men >30)
- GGT (for orals: Anavar, Winstrol)

RUNNING 19-NORS (Deca, Tren, MENT):
- Prolactin + Progesterone
- Cystatin-C + Urinalysis (kidney monitoring)
- Fasting Glucose + A1c (insulin resistance — especially Tren)
- Daily BP monitoring (home cuff)

RUNNING ORAL ANABOLICS (dbol, Anadrol, Superdrol, tbol, Halo):
- ALT, AST, GGT, Bilirubin, ALP (liver panel)
- Full Lipid Panel
- HCT (Anadrol/dbol specifically)

RUNNING GROWTH HORMONE:
- IGF-1
- Fasting Glucose + A1c + Fasting insulin
- Free T3 (GH suppresses T3 conversion)
- C-Peptide (if using insulin alongside)

RUNNING insulin:
- Daily finger-stick or CGM glucose monitoring
- A1c every 8-12 weeks
- C-Peptide every 3-6 months
- Fasting insulin + HOMA-IR

RUNNING GLP-1 AGONISTS (Semaglutide, Tirzepatide):
- A1c, Fasting Glucose
- Lipid Panel
- Amylase + Lipase (pancreatitis monitoring)
- Thyroid Panel (calcitonin if family history of thyroid cancer)

RUNNING EQ (Boldenone):
- HCT every 4-6 weeks (most potent HCT-raising injectable)
- Ferritin (if donating blood frequently)
- E2 may read falsely on some assays (EQ metabolite cross-reacts)

RUNNING HCG:
- LH may read falsely elevated (HCG cross-reacts on some LH assays)
- E2 (HCG stimulates intratesticular aromatization)
- Testicular size (clinical, not lab — HCG preserves testicular volume)

=== THE BOTTOM LINE ===

As Wendy and Bryan Antonelli write in the Precision Bloodwork Field Manual: "You can replace muscle. You can't replace kidneys. And you only get one liver. Treat them like your most valuable assets — because they are."

The templates in this guide remove the guesswork from bloodwork timing and selection. The case studies show what happens when labs guide decisions — and what happens when they don't.

Key principles:
1. ALWAYS pull baseline before starting ANYTHING
2. Mid-cycle labs at 6-8 weeks catch problems before they become emergencies
3. Track TRENDS over time — no single lab tells the full story
4. Add compound-specific tests based on what you're running
5. Budget $200-400 per lab draw — it's cheaper than an ER visit
6. Know your red flag thresholds and ACT on them without hesitation
7. Female monitoring requires DIFFERENT markers with emphasis on virilization
8. Long-term users: annual trending + cardiac imaging saves lives

The weights test your strength. The stage tests your physique. But the labs test your discipline. If you fail them, the rest won't matter.

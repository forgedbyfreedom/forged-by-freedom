=== WHAT ARE SARMS ===

Selective Androgen Receptor Modulators (SARMs) are a class of compounds that bind to androgen receptors in a tissue-selective manner. Unlike anabolic-androgenic steroids (AAS), which activate androgen receptors throughout the entire body, SARMs were designed to preferentially target muscle and bone tissue while minimizing effects on the prostate, liver, and other organs.

Key facts about SARMs:
- SARMs are NOT steroids — they are a distinct class of compounds
- They are NOT FDA-approved for human use — sold as "research chemicals"
- They occupy a legal gray area — not scheduled substances in most countries but not legal to sell for human consumption
- Less suppressive than traditional AAS but STILL suppressive to natural testosterone production
- Less research exists on long-term safety compared to testosterone, which has decades of medical data
- Quality control in the SARM market is extremely poor — contamination, mislabeling, and underdosing are rampant

Scott McNally on Blood Sweat and Gear: "SARMs are in this weird no-man's-land. They're not as strong as real steroids, but they still suppress your testosterone. So you're getting a fraction of the benefit with a lot of the same downsides. For most guys, if you're going to suppress yourself, you might as well use actual testosterone."

Dave Crosland on Drugs N Stuff: "The problem with SARMs isn't the concept — selective androgen receptor modulation is brilliant in theory. The problem is that the products on the market are unregulated, untested, and you have no idea what you're actually putting in your body."

Dave Palumbo on RXMuscle: "I tell guys all the time — if you're serious about building muscle and you've made the decision to use performance enhancers, use something with decades of research behind it. Testosterone has been studied for 80 years. SARMs have been studied for maybe 15, and most of those studies were abandoned because the compounds didn't work well enough to get FDA approval."

Paul Barnett on Anabolic Bodybuilding: "SARMs have their place. For someone who doesn't want to inject, who wants something milder, who just wants a slight edge — I get it. But you need to understand what you're actually getting into. They're not side-effect free, and they're not as effective as people on the internet claim."

=== OSTARINE (MK-2866 / Enobosarm) ===

Ostarine is the mildest SARM available and the most extensively studied. It was originally developed by GTx Inc. for the treatment of muscle wasting and osteoporosis. It is the closest any SARM has come to FDA approval, having completed Phase III clinical trials.

DOSING BY EXPERIENCE LEVEL:
- Beginner: 10-15mg/day
- Intermediate: 15-25mg/day
- Advanced: 25-30mg/day

CYCLE LENGTH: 8-12 weeks

HALF-LIFE: 24 hours (once daily dosing is sufficient)

PRIMARY USES:
- Cutting — preserves lean mass during caloric deficit
- Joint healing — Ostarine has notable benefits for joint and tendon health
- Lean mass preservation during PCT or time off AAS
- Bridge compound between steroid cycles (controversial — still mildly suppressive)
- Mild recomposition for those not ready to use AAS

SUPPRESSION PROFILE:
- Mildest suppression of all SARMs
- At 10-15mg/day for 8 weeks, many users show minimal LH/FSH suppression
- At 20-25mg+ for 12 weeks, suppression becomes more meaningful — PCT may be necessary
- Always get bloodwork at the end of cycle to assess recovery needs

EXPECTED RESULTS:
- Lean mass gain: 3-6 lbs over 8-12 weeks (in a caloric surplus)
- Strength increase: Mild but noticeable, especially on compound lifts
- Fat loss: Enhanced when combined with a caloric deficit
- Joint improvement: Many users report reduced joint pain within 2-3 weeks

Skipp Hill on It's Just Bodybuilding: "Ostarine is the one SARM I'd say actually has a legitimate use case. If someone has a nagging joint issue and they want something to help while they're in a cut, 15mg of Ostarine for 8 weeks is pretty low risk and it does help."

Dave Palumbo on RXMuscle: "Ostarine is the Toyota Corolla of SARMs. It's boring, it's mild, but it works and it's reliable. You're not going to put on 20 pounds of muscle, but you'll hold onto what you have during a cut and your joints will feel better."

=== RAD-140 (Testolone) ===

RAD-140 is the most potent SARM available for muscle building. Developed by Radius Health, it was designed to eventually replace testosterone therapy with something more selective. In practice, RAD-140 is nearly as suppressive as low-dose testosterone while providing significantly less anabolic effect.

DOSING BY EXPERIENCE LEVEL:
- Beginner: 5-10mg/day
- Intermediate: 10-15mg/day
- Advanced: 15-20mg/day

CYCLE LENGTH: 6-8 weeks (very suppressive at higher doses — shorter cycles are prudent)

HALF-LIFE: 60 hours (lonGHalf-life means it accumulates significantly)

PRIMARY USES:
- Lean mass building — the strongest SARM for hypertrophy
- Strength gains — noticeable strength increases within 2-3 weeks
- Recomposition — good ratio of muscle gain to fat loss

SUPPRESSION PROFILE:
- HIGHLY suppressive — this is not a mild compound
- At 10mg+/day, expect significant LH/FSH suppression by week 4-6
- PCT is essentially MANDATORY after any RAD-140 cycle
- Some users report suppression symptoms (low energy, low libido, flat mood) as early as week 3-4

SIDE EFFECTS:
- Hair shedding in genetically predisposed individuals (androgenic activity at the scalp)
- Liver stress — elevated AST/ALT observed in clinical data
- Suppression-related sides: low libido, lethargy, flat mood in later weeks
- Possible increase in aggression
- Liver support is recommended: NAC 1200mg/day + TUDCA 250-500mg/day

EXPECTED RESULTS:
- Lean mass gain: 6-10 lbs over 8 weeks (some water included)
- Strength increase: Significant — 10-15% on major lifts
- Noticeable muscle fullness and vascularity

Scott McNally on Blood Sweat and Gear: "RAD-140 is the one that makes me scratch my head. It suppresses you almost as much as 200mg of test per week, but it doesn't build nearly as much muscle. If you're going to shut yourself down, why not just run actual testosterone and get twice the results?"

Dave Crosland on Drugs N Stuff: "RAD-140 is the strongest SARM, no question. But 'the strongest SARM' is still weaker than a mild steroid cycle. And it'll still shut down your natural test. You're going to need PCT either way. At that point, what are you really saving yourself from? The needle?"

Paul Barnett on Anabolic Bodybuilding: "I've seen guys come off RAD-140 and their testosterone levels are in the gutter. They're surprised because they thought SARMs were supposed to be mild. === LGD-4033 (Ligandrol) ===

LGD-4033 is a strong mass-building SARM developed by Ligand Pharmaceuticals. It sits between Ostarine and RAD-140 in terms of potency. It's notable for producing more water retention than other SARMs, giving it a slightly different profile.

DOSING BY EXPERIENCE LEVEL:
- Beginner: 5mg/day
- Intermediate: 5-10mg/day
- Advanced: 10-15mg/day

CYCLE LENGTH: 8-12 weeks

HALF-LIFE: 24-36 hours (once daily dosing is sufficient)

PRIMARY USES:
- Bulking — the preferred SARM for pure mass gain
- Strength gains — noticeable strength increases
- Off-season lean mass building

SUPPRESSION PROFILE:
- Moderately to highly suppressive
- Clinical studies show dose-dependent suppression of total Testosterone, LH, FSH, and SHBG
- At 10mg+/day, PCT is recommended
- At 5mg/day for 8 weeks, some users recover naturally but bloodwork should confirm

SIDE EFFECTS:
- Water retention — more than other SARMs, similar to mild AAS
- Suppression-related sides at higher doses and longer cycles
- Some users report mild headaches in the first week
- Possible lipid impact (reduced HDL)

EXPECTED RESULTS:
- Lean mass gain: 5-8 lbs over 8 weeks (more with water retention)
- Strength increase: Moderate — 5-10% on major lifts
- Fuller, rounder muscle appearance due to water retention

Skipp Hill on It's Just Bodybuilding: "LGD is the bulking SARM. If you're going to use a SARM and you want to put on size, LGD is the move. It holds more water than something like Ostarine, so you'll look bigger on cycle, but some of that is going to come off when you stop."

Dave Palumbo on RXMuscle: "LGD-4033 gives you a decent amount of mass for a SARM. You'll hold some water, which people don't expect from SARMs, but it does fill you out. Just know that when you come off, you'll lose the water and probably 30-40% of the weight you gained."

=== MK-677 (Ibutamoren) ===

MK-677 is NOT a SARM. It is a growth hormone secretagogue (GHS) — an orally active compound that stimulates the pituitary gland to release more growth hormone by mimicking the ghrelin receptor. It is grouped with SARMs because it's sold through the same vendors and marketed alongside them.

DOSING:
- Standard dose: 10-25mg/day
- Best taken at night before bed (improves sleep quality, aligns with natural GH pulse)
- Can be run indefinitely — it is NOT suppressive to testosterone

CYCLE LENGTH: Can be used long-term (months to years). No need to cycle on and off since it does not affect testosterone production.

HALF-LIFE: 24 hours (once daily dosing)

MECHANISM:
- Mimics ghrelin — activates the growth hormone secretagogue receptor
- Increases natural GH release by 40-80%
- Raises IGF-1 levels significantly
- Does NOT directly provide exogenous GH — it stimulates your own production
- Effect plateaus after several months as the pituitary acclimates

PRIMARY USES:
- growth hormone elevation without injections
- Sleep quality improvement (dramatic improvement in deep sleep)
- Recovery enhancement between training sessions
- Appetite increase for bulking phases
- Anti-aging and skin/hair quality improvement
- Stacks well with SARMs without adding suppression

SIDE EFFECTS:
- Hunger increase — significant and sometimes overwhelming (ghrelin mimetic)
- Water retention — puffy face, bloated appearance, especially early on
- Blood sugar elevation — MK-677 can raise fasting glucose and reduce insulin sensitivity
- Lethargy/drowsiness — especially the first few weeks
- Numbness/tingling in extremities (related to GH elevation)

CRITICAL: Monitor fasting blood glucose regularly. MK-677 can push pre-diabetic individuals into diabetic blood sugar ranges. Berberine 500mg 2x/day or Metformin 500mg/day can counteract glucose effects.

Scott McNally on Blood Sweat and Gear: "MK-677 is the one compound from the SARM world that I actually think has good value. It's not a SARM, it boosts your GH, it makes you sleep like a baby, and it doesn't suppress your testosterone. The hunger can be brutal though — if you're cutting, it's probably not the right time."

Dave Crosland on Drugs N Stuff: "I like MK-677 for guys who want the benefits of growth hormone but can't afford actual GH injections. You're not going to get the same level of GH elevation, but for the price, it's solid. Just watch your blood sugar."

Paul Barnett on Anabolic Bodybuilding: "MK-677 is the easiest compound to recommend because it has the best risk-to-reward ratio. It doesn't mess with your testosterone, it improves your sleep, it helps recovery. The only people who need to be careful are those with blood sugar issues — get your fasting glucose checked before you start."

=== YK-11 ===

YK-11 is one of the most controversial compounds in the SARM category. There is genuine debate among researchers about whether it is actually a SARM or a synthetic steroid with steroidal structure. It is classified by some as a myostatin inhibitor — meaning it may block the protein (myostatin) that limits how much muscle your body can build.

DOSING BY EXPERIENCE LEVEL:
- Beginner: 5mg/day
- Intermediate: 5-10mg/day
- Advanced: 10-15mg/day

CYCLE LENGTH: 6-8 weeks MAXIMUM (potentially liver toxic and highly suppressive)

PRIMARY USES:
- Myostatin inhibition (claimed — limited evidence)
- Lean mass building
- Breaking through plateaus by theoretically raising the muscle growth hormone ceiling

SUPPRESSION PROFILE:
- Very suppressive — treat this like a steroid in terms of PCT needs
- PCT is mandatory after any YK-11 cycle

SIDE EFFECTS:
- Potentially liver toxic — liver support is ESSENTIAL
- TUDCA-500mg/day + NAC 1200mg/day throughout the cycle
- Joint pain reported by some users (possibly related to DHT-like activity)
- Hair shedding possible in predisposed individuals
- Less research exists on YK-11 than any other commonly used SARM

IMPORTANT CAVEATS:
- The myostatin inhibition claim is based on very limited cell culture studies, not human trials
- YK-11's chemical structure is steroidal — it may literally be a synthetic steroid marketed as a SARM
- Less human data available than other SARMs — you are essentially experimenting on yourself
- The risk-to-reward ratio is questionable — if it's essentially a steroid, just use an actual steroid with more research behind it

Dave Crosland on Drugs N Stuff: "YK-11 is the wild card. Nobody really knows exactly what it is or how it works long-term. It might be a SARM, it might be a steroid, it might inhibit myostatin. The research is thin. If you're going to be your own guinea pig, at least protect your liver and keep the cycle short."

Skipp Hill on It's Just Bodybuilding: "The myostatin inhibition angle is interesting in theory, but the evidence isn't there yet. We've got cell studies, not human trials. If you want to use YK-11, fine, but don't expect it to remove your genetic ceiling based on some in-vitro research."

=== S-23 ===

=== SARM STACKS ===

Combining SARMs can enhance results but also compounds suppression and potential side effects. These are the most common combinations with experience-appropriate dosing.

BEGINNER STACK — MILD ENHANCEMENT:
- Ostarine 15mg/day + MK-677 15mg/day
- Duration: 8 weeks
- Suppression: Mild (Ostarine only — MK-677 does not suppress)
- PCT: Likely not needed, but get bloodwork at week 8 to verify
- Best for: First-time SARM users wanting lean mass and improved recovery
- Expected results: 4-6 lbs lean mass, improved sleep, better joint health

CUTTING STACK:
- Ostarine 20mg/day + Cardarine (GW-501516) 15mg/day
- Duration: 8 weeks
- Suppression: Mild-moderate (Ostarine only — Cardarine is not a SARM and does not suppress)
- PCT: Get bloodwork to assess
- Note: Cardarine is a PPARdelta agonist — it enhances fat oxidation and endurance. It is NOT a SARM.
- Best for: Cutting while preserving muscle, improving cardio performance
- Expected results: Significant fat loss with muscle preservation, noticeable cardio improvement

BULKING STACK:
- LGD-4033 10mg/day + MK-677 20mg/day
- Duration: 8 weeks
- Suppression: Moderate (LGD only — MK-677 does not suppress)
- PCT: Recommended (Nolvadex 20mg/day for 4 weeks)
- Best for: Maximum mass gain from SARMs, off-season bulk
- Expected results: 6-10 lbs mass (some water), significant strength gains, enhanced recovery

RECOMP STACK:
- RAD-140 10mg/day + MK-677 15mg/day
- Duration: 8 weeks
- Suppression: High (RAD-140 is very suppressive)
- PCT: Required (Nolvadex 20mg/day for 4 weeks)
- Best for: Simultaneous muscle gain and fat loss

ADVANCED STACK — HIGH SUPPRESSION:
- RAD-140 15mg/day + LGD-4033 5mg/day + MK-677 20mg/day
- Duration: 6-8 weeks (shorter due to combined suppression)
- Suppression: VERY HIGH — combining two suppressive SARMs compounds the shutdown
- PCT: MANDATORY (Nolvadex 20mg/day for 4 weeks)
- Best for: Experienced SARM users wanting maximum SARM-based results before considering AAS
- Expected results: 8-12 lbs mass, significant strength, improved recovery from MK-677

=== SARMS FOR WOMEN ===

Women considering SARMs need to understand that even compounds marketed as "selective" still carry androgenic risk. The female body is far more sensitive to androgens than the male body.

SAFE OPTIONS FOR WOMEN:

OSTARINE — The safest SARM for women:
- Dose: 5-10mg/day
- Cycle length: 6-8 weeks
- Minimal virilization risk at these doses
- Good for lean mass preservation during cuts
- Joint health benefits
- Start at 5mg and assess for 2 weeks before increasing

MK-677 — Safe for women:
- Dose: 10-15mg/day
- Not a SARM, not androgenic — no virilization risk
- Improves sleep, recovery, and GH levels
- Can be run long-term
- Monitor blood sugar

NOT RECOMMENDED FOR WOMEN:

RAD-140 — TOO ANDROGENIC:
- Significant virilization risk even at low doses
- Androgenic effects at the scalp and skin translate to virilization in women
- Voice deepening, facial hair growth, clitoral enlargement are all possible
- There are no "safe" doses of RAD-140 for women established in research

LGD-4033 — NOT RECOMMENDED:
- Moderately androgenic
- Suppresses female hormonal axis
- Virilization risk is meaningful at any effective dose
- Insufficient safety data in women

S-23 and YK-11 — NEVER FOR WOMEN:
- Extremely androgenic
- S-23 acts as a potent androgen — will cause rapid virilization
- YK-11 has steroidal structure with unknown androgenic potency in women
- These compounds will masculinize female users

BETTER ALTERNATIVE: Many experts recommend women consider Anavar (Oxandrolone) over SARMs entirely. Anavar has decades of medical data in female patients, a well-understood virilization threshold, and more predictable results.

=== SARMS VS STEROIDS — HONEST COMPARISON ===

This section exists because the most common question about SARMs is whether they're a viable alternative to traditional AAS. Here's the honest assessment from experts who have experience with both.

MUSCLE BUILDING:
- SARMs are WEAKER than real steroids for building muscle — significantly weaker
- The strongest SARM cycle (RAD-140 + LGD stacked) produces roughly the results of a conservative testosterone-only cycle (300mg/week)
- A basic 500mg/week testosterone cycle will outperform any SARM stack

SUPPRESSION:
- SARMs still suppress natural testosterone production
- RAD-140 and S-23 suppress almost as much as exogenous testosterone
- If you're going to suppress your HPTA regardless, you get more bang for your buck with actual AAS
- The "SARMs are less suppressive" argument only holds for Ostarine at low doses

SAFETY RESEARCH:
- SARMs have LESS long-term safety data than testosterone
- Testosterone has been used medically since the 1930s — nearly a century of data
- Most SARMs have at best Phase I-II clinical trial data (small sample sizes, short durations)
- Several SARM development programs were abandoned by pharmaceutical companies (tells you something)
- Unknown long-term effects on organs, cardiovascular system, and cancer risk

QUALITY CONTROL:
- SARMs quality control is POOR — the market is unregulated
- Studies testing SARM products found many were mislabeled, underdosed, or contained actual prohormones/steroids
- You might be taking a real steroid without knowing it
- Testosterone from a regulated pharmacy or reputable UGL has more consistent quality

WHERE SARMS MAKE SENSE:
- People who want mild enhancement without injecting
- Bridge compound between AAS cycles (Ostarine specifically)
- Joint healing support (Ostarine specifically)
- Those not ready to commit to full AAS use but want something beyond natural
- Women who want something milder than even Anavar (Ostarine)

Scott McNally on Blood Sweat and Gear: "Here's my honest take on SARMs vs steroids. If you're going to suppress your testosterone — and most SARMs will — you might as well use actual testosterone. You'll get better results, more predictable side effects, decades of safety data, and it costs about the same. SARMs make sense for a very specific person: someone who wants something mild and doesn't want to inject."

Dave Palumbo on RXMuscle: "The SARM industry has done a great marketing job convincing people that SARMs are 'steroids without the side effects.' That's not true. They have side effects, they suppress you, and they build less muscle. If you've decided to cross the line into performance enhancement, cross it properly with compounds we actually understand."

Dave Crosland on Drugs N Stuff: "I'm not anti-SARM. Ostarine has legitimate uses. MK-677 is great. But when guys start stacking RAD-140 with LGD and S-23 and running PCT afterward — mate, you're basically doing a steroid cycle with worse compounds. Just do the steroid cycle."

=== SARM PCT PROTOCOLS ===

Post-cycle therapy for SARMs follows the same principles as steroid PCT — restore natural testosterone production using SERMs (Selective Estrogen Receptor Modulators) to stimulate LH and FSH release from the pituitary.

OSTARINE ONLY (Low Dose — 10-15mg/day for 8 weeks):
- May not need PCT
- Get bloodwork at week 8 — check total Testosterone, LH, FSH
- If testosterone is above 300 ng/dL and LH/FSH are in range, natural recovery is likely fine
- If suppressed, run Nolvadex 10-20mg/day for 2-4 weeks

RAD-140 (Any Dose):
- PCT is essentially mandatory
- Option A: Nolvadex (Tamoxifen) 20mg/day for 4 weeks
- Option B: Enclomiphene 12.5mg/day for 4 weeks (clener side effect profile than Nolvadex)
- Get bloodwork 4-6 weeks post-PCT to verify recovery

LGD-4033 (10mg+/day):
- PCT recommended
- Nolvadex 20mg/day for 4 weeks OR Enclomiphene 12.5mg/day for 4 weeks
- Lower doses (5mg for 8 weeks) — get bloodwork to assess need

S-23 (Any Dose):
- PCT is MANDATORY — this compound shuts down testosterone completely
- Nolvadex 20mg/day for 4 weeks minimum
- Some users may need 6 weeks of PCT for full recovery
- Consider Enclomiphene 12.5-25mg/day as an alternative SERM

YK-11 (Any Dose):
- PCT is mandatory
- Nolvadex 20mg/day for 4 weeks OR Enclomiphene 12.5mg/day for 4 weeks

STACKED SARMs:
- Any stack of two suppressive SARMs requires PCT
- Nolvadex 20mg/day for 4 weeks is the standard
- Enclomiphene 12.5mg/day for 4 weeks is the modern preferred option
- Get bloodwork 4-6 weeks post-PCT to verify hormone recovery

MK-677:
- Does NOT require PCT — it does not affect testosterone production
- Can be continued during and after SARM PCT without issue
- Actually beneficial during PCT as the GH elevation supports recovery

POST-PCT BLOODWORK (CRITICAL):
- total Testosterone
- free Testosterone
- LH (LuteinizinGHormone)
- FSH (Follicle StimulatinGHormone)
- Estradiol (sensitive assay)
- SHBG
- CBC (complete blood count)
- Liver enzymes (AST/ALT) — especially if you used YK-11 or RAD-140

Skipp Hill on It's Just Bodybuilding: "Don't skip PCT because you used SARMs instead of steroids. Your testosterone doesn't care what compound suppressed it — suppressed is suppressed. Get your bloodwork, run your Nolvadex or Enclomiphene, and verify you've recovered before thinking about another cycle."

Dave Crosland on Drugs N Stuff: "The PCT protocol for SARMs is the same as for a mild steroid cycle. Nolvadex 20mg a day for four weeks will cover most SARM cycles. Enclomiphene is even better if you can get it — clener compound, fewer side effects, does the same job."

=== SARM QUALITY AND TESTING ===

The SARM market is one of the most unregulated supplement markets in existence. Multiple independent studies have tested commercial SARM products and found alarming results.

THEQUALITY PROBLEM:
- A 2017 study (JAMA) tested 44 SARM products sold online
- Only 52% actually contained a SARM
- 39% contained another unapproved drug (often prohormones or actual steroids)
- 25% contained substances not listed on the label
- 9% contained NO active compound at all
- Many products were underdosed by 50% or more

WHAT THIS MEANS:
- The SARM you bought might not be a SARM at all
- It might be a prohormone or oral steroid (which would explain why some people get "amazing SARM results")
- If your "SARM" feels like a steroid, it might literally be one — prohormone contamination is common
- You could be unknowingly taking liver-toxic compounds without appropriate liver support
- Drug test implications — contaminated SARMs have caused positive steroid tests in tested athletes

HOW TO VERIFY:
- Third-party analytical testing is the gold standard
- Janoshik Analytical (public.janoshik.com) provides independent compound verification
- Send a sample of your product for testing, or buy from vendors who publish Janoshik test results
- Verify test results are authentic at verify.janoshik.com
- Reputable SARM vendors publish full third-party lab results for every batch
- If a vendor won't show you lab results, don't buy from them

SIGNS YOUR SARM IS CONTAMINATED:
- Results that seem "too good" for the compound — if your Ostarine cycle gives you 15 lbs, something else is in there
- Unexpected side effects: significant liver stress on Ostarine, gyno symptoms on non-aromatizing SARMs
- Rapid water retention beyond what the SARM should produce
- Positive steroid drug test results

Dave Palumbo on RXMuscle: "The SARM market is the Wild West. You have no idea what you're actually buying. At least with testosterone, you can test it. A lot of these SARM companies are selling methylated prohormones relabeled as SARMs. That's why some guys get great results and then wonder why their liver enzymes are through the roof."

Scott McNally on Blood Sweat and Gear: "If you're going to use SARMs, spend the money on a Janoshik test. Send your product in and find out what's actually in it. You'd be shocked how many SARMs test positive for compounds that aren't even listed on the label. Some of the most popular SARM brands have failed multiple independent tests."

Paul Barnett on Anabolic Bodybuilding: "Quality control is the number one issue with SARMs. I've seen lab results where a product labeled as Ostarine was actually a methylated prohormone.

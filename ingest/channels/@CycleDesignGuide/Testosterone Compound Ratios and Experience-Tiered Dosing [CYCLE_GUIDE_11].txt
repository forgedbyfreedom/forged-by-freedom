=== WHY TESTOSTERONE:COMPOUND RATIOS MATTER ===

Every steroid cycle revolves around testosterone as the base. The ratio of testosterone to secondary compounds directly affects estrogen management, prolactin control, sexual function, mood, and overall side effect profile. Getting the ratio wrong is the #1 reason intermediate users have bad cycle experiences.

Scott McNally on Blood Sweat and Gear: "Most guys who come to me with problems on cycle, it's a ratio issue. They're running too much of one compound relative to their test base. Fix the ratio and 90% of the problems go away."

Dave Crosland on Drugs N Stuff: "Testosterone is your foundation. Every other compound is built on top of that foundation. If the foundation is wrong, the whole structure is unstable."

Dave Palumbo on RXMuscle: "I always tell guys — testosterone is king. You can run whatever else you want, but if your test base isn't right, you're going to have issues with libido, mood, energy, everything."

Paul Barnett on Anabolic Bodybuilding: "The ratio between your testosterone and secondary compounds is everything. It determines how you feel, how you perform in the gym, and how you perform in the bedroom."

The biological reasons ratios matter:
- ESTROGEN (E2): Testosterone aromatizes to estradiol. Some compounds (EQ, Primo, Masteron) have anti-estrogenic properties that can crash E2 if test is too low. Others (Deca, dbol) compound estrogenic/progestogenic load.
- PROLACTIN: 19-nor compounds (Nandrolone, Trenbolone) elevate prolactin. Adequate testosterone helps maintain dopaminergic tone to counterbalance prolactin. Low test + high 19-nors = prolactin disaster.
- SEXUAL FUNCTION: Testosterone is the primary driver of libido and erectile function. When secondary compounds dominate, sexual side effects are the first sign the ratio is off.
- DHT BALANCE: Testosterone converts to DHT, which is critical for mood, confidence, and sexual function. Running very low test with DHT-derived compounds (Masteron, Anavar, Winstrol) can still feel good, but running low test with non-DHT compounds often feels terrible.

=== TESTOSTERONE:NANDROLONE (DECA / NPP) RATIOS ===

Nandrolone (Deca Durabolin / NPP) is the classic mass builder. The #1 issue users face is "deca dick" — erectile dysfunction and loss of libido caused by nandrolone's progesterone and prolactin activity. The testosterone:nandrolone ratio is THE most important ratio to get right.

WHY THE RATIO MATTERS:
- Nandrolone is a 19-nor steroid that converts to a weaker androgen (DHN) rather than DHT
- Without adequate DHT from testosterone, sexual function suffers dramatically
- Nandrolone elevates prolactin, which suppresses libido and can cause lactation
- Higher testosterone provides more DHT and estradiol to maintain sexual function
- The ratio determines whether you feel great or miserable on this stack

BEGINNER (first nandrolone cycle):
- Testosterone: 500mg/week | Nandrolone: 300mg/week
- Ratio: 1.67:1 (test:nandrolone)
- Higher ratio gives maximum protection against deca dick
- Use NPP (short ester) for first nandrolone run — clears faster if sides appear
- NPP half-life: 2.5-3 days vs Deca half-life: 6-12 days
- Cabergoline: 0.25mg 2x/week on hand

INTERMEDIATE:
- Testosterone: 500-600mg/week | Nandrolone: 400-500mg/week
- Ratio: 1.2:1 (test:nandrolone)
- You know your prolactin response, can run compounds closer together
- Can use either NPP or Deca depending on preference
- Cabergoline: 0.25-0.5mg 2x/week as needed based on bloods

ADVANCED:
- Testosterone: 500-750mg/week | Nandrolone: 400-600mg/week
- Ratio: 1.25:1 (test:nandrolone)
- Experienced users may tolerate 1:1 but 1.25:1 is the gold standard
- Some advanced users run Deca higher than test successfully — but this is individual and requires prolactin management mastery
- Cabergoline: dose based on bloodwork, typically 0.5mg 2x/week

Scott McNally on Blood Sweat and Gear: "Keep your test higher than your Deca. I've seen so many guys come to me with limp dick because they ran 300 test and 600 Deca. That's backwards. Your test should always be equal to or higher than your nandrolone."

Dave Crosland: "The 1.25:1 ratio works for most experienced guys. Enough Deca to build serious mass, enough test to keep everything working downstairs. If you're new to Deca, bump that ratio up even more — go 1.5:1 or higher."

Dave Palumbo on RXMuscle: "I recommend test always higher than Deca. Classic ratio is about 1.25 to 1 or even 1.5 to 1. Your test is your foundation for libido, mood, everything."

deca dick PREVENTION PROTOCOL:
- Keep test:nandrolone ratio at 1.25:1 or higher
- Cabergoline 0.25-0.5mg twice weekly for prolactin control
- Proviron 25-50mg/day if sexual function still suffers (boosts free Test and DHT)
- Get prolactin levels checked at week 4-6
- If prolactin >20 ng/mL, increase cabergoline
- If still having issues, increase testosterone by 100mg/week

=== TESTOSTERONE:TRENBOLONE RATIOS ===

Trenbolone is the most powerful and controversial steroid. The test:tren ratio is hotly debated among experts with two distinct schools of thought.

WHY THE RATIO MATTERS:
- Trenbolone is a 19-nor compound — elevates prolactin like nandrolone
- Tren does NOT aromatize, but its progestogenic activity can amplify estrogenic symptoms from testosterone
- Higher testosterone = more aromatization = more potential E2 issues on top of Tren's sides
- Lower testosterone = less E2, drier look, but some users report worse libido and mood
- The "right" ratio is genuinely individual — no universal answer exists

SCHOOL 1: HIGH TEST / LOW TREN
- Testosterone: 500mg/week | Trenbolone: 200-300mg/week
- Ratio: ~2:1 (test:tren)
- Better libido and mood for most users
- More water retention, need AI for estrogen management
- Easier transition if you need to drop Tren due to sides

Scott McNally: "I prefer test higher than tren for most guys. You feel better, your libido stays, and the gains are still incredible. 500 test, 200-300 tren is a fantastic cycle."

SCHOOL 2: LOW TEST / HIGH TREN
- Testosterone: 200-300mg/week | Trenbolone: 300-500mg/week
- Ratio: ~1:1.5 (test:tren — tren dominant)
- Drier, harder look with less water
- Less estrogen to manage — some run no AI
- Higher risk of Tren mental sides and prolactin issues
- Must manage prolactin aggressively with cabergoline

Dave Palumbo on RXMuscle: "Some guys do great on low test, high tren. They get that grainy, hard look without the water. But you have to manage your prolactin and you have to be honest about how tren makes you feel mentally."

EXPERIENCE-TIERED TREN DOSING:

BEGINNER (first tren cycle):
- Testosterone: 300-500mg/week | Trenbolone Acetate: 150-200mg/week
- ALWAYS use Trenbolone Acetate (short ester) for first tren cycle
- Tren Acetate half-life: 1-2 days — clears fast if you have bad sides
- Start LOW (150mg/week) and assess for 2-3 weeks before increasing
- This is a powerful combination even at low doses
- Cabergoline: 0.25mg 2x/week, have more on hand

INTERMEDIATE:
- Testosterone: 400-500mg/week | Trenbolone: 200-400mg/week
- Can use Acetate or Enanthate ester based on injection preference
- Tren Enanthate half-life: 5-7 days
- Adjust test:tren ratio based on which school you respond better to
- Cabergoline: 0.25-0.5mg 2x/week

ADVANCED:
- Testosterone: 300-750mg/week | Trenbolone: 400-700mg/week
- Full flexibility on ratio — you know your body's response
- Many advanced users run Tren Enanthate for fewer injections
- Prolactin management is well-dialed at this level
- Cabergoline: dose based on bloodwork

Paul Barnett on Anabolic Bodybuilding: "Trenbolone is the most powerful steroid we have. But if it's your first time, keep it at 200mg a week with Acetate and assess. You can always add more. You can't take it back once it's in your system."

=== TESTOSTERONE:EQUIPOISE (BOLDENONE) RATIOS ===

Equipoise presents a unique ratio challenge because it has ANTI-estrogenic properties. EQ can crash estrogen if you run too much relative to testosterone, which is the opposite problem of most compounds.

WHY THE RATIO MATTERS:
- EQ converts to a metabolite (1,4-dienedione) that acts as a mild aromatase inhibitor
- RunninGHigh EQ with moderate test can crash estrogen — causing joint pain, low libido, depression, anxiety
- You may need MORE testosterone with EQ than with other compounds
- Some users need ZERO AI on a test + EQ cycle because EQ handles estrogen management naturally
- The ratio must account for EQ's E2-lowering properties

BEGINNER:
- Testosterone: 500mg/week | Equipoise: 400mg/week
- Ratio: 1.25:1 (test:EQ)
- Conservative EQ dose — still plenty for appetite enhancement and vascularity
- DO NOT take an AI unless estrogen symptoms are confirmed with bloodwork
- EQ half-life: 14 days — takes 6-8 weeks to fully saturate

INTERMEDIATE:
- Testosterone: 600mg/week | Equipoise: 600mg/week
- Ratio: 1:1 (test:EQ)
- At 1:1, most users find a good balance — EQ controls some E2 naturally
- If E2 crashes (dry joints, no libido, anxiety), increase test or drop EQ dose
- Minimal or no AI needed for most users at this ratio

ADVANCED:
- Testosterone: 600-750mg/week | Equipoise: 600-900mg/week
- Ratio: 1:1 to 1:1.2 (slight EQ dominance acceptable)
- Advanced users know their aromatization rate and can adjust
- Some high aromatizers can run EQ dominant (1:1.5) because they produce excess estrogen naturally
- Run EQ for minimum 16 weeks, ideally 20 — it takes time to show its full effects

Scott McNally on ThinkBig: "EQ is great for a lean bulk. But watch your estrogen — EQ can act like a mild AI. Some guys crash their E2 without realizing it's the EQ doing it. Keep the ratio at 1:1 or test slightly higher and you'll be fine."

Dave Crosland: "With Equipoise, the biggest mistake is crashing estrogen and not knowing why you feel like garbage. Your joints hurt, your mood tanks, libido disappears. Back off the EQ or bump up the test."

EQ-SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS:
- Monitor hematocrit — EQ significantly raises red blood cell count
- Donate blood if hematocrit >52%
- The appetite increase from EQ is dramatic — beneficial for bulking, problematic for cutting
- EQ anxiety is a real side effect at higher doses — usually means E2 is too low
- Run for minimum 16 weeks due to very long ester. 20 weeks is ideal.

=== TESTOSTERONE:PRIMOBOLAN RATIOS ===

Primobolan (Methenolone Enanthate) is often called "The Gentleman's Compound" for its mild side effect profile. Like EQ, Primo has mild anti-estrogenic properties that affect ratio planning.

WHY THE RATIO MATTERS:
- Primo has weak anti-estrogenic activity — can reduce total estrogen slightly
- Not as aggressive as EQ at lowering E2, but still a factor at higher doses
- Primo requires higher doses to produce meaningful muscle-building results (400-800mg minimum)
- The cost of Primo means most users run it at meaningful doses — which means the anti-E2 effect is real
- Primo does NOT aromatize and is not liver toxic

BEGINNER:
- Testosterone: 400-500mg/week | Primobolan: 400mg/week
- Ratio: 1:1 (test:Primo)
- Good starting point — Primo's anti-E2 effect is mild at this dose
- AI usually not needed
- Primo Enanthate half-life: 7-10 days

INTERMEDIATE:
- Testosterone: 400-500mg/week | Primobolan: 600mg/week
- Ratio: 1:1.2-1.5 (Primo can run higher than test)
- Primo dominant cycles are common and well-tolerated
- Watch for E2 getting too low — add MENT or reduce Primo if needed
- Quality lean gains with very manageable sides

ADVANCED:
- Testosterone: 400-600mg/week | Primobolan: 600-1000mg/week
- Ratio: 1:1.5-2 (Primo dominant)
- Many advanced users run Primo significantly higher than test
- Arnold reportedly ran high-dose Primo as a staple
- At 800mg+ Primo, AI is almost never needed
- Primo + Test is the "feel good" cycle — excellent mood, minimal sides

Skipp Hill on It's Just Bodybuilding: "Primo and test is the cycle I'd run year-round if cost wasn't a factor. ThEQuality of gains is unmatched. You look good, you feel good, your bloodwork stays clen."

- Primo is safe enough for very long cycles (20-24 weeks)
- Excellent for lean bulking and cutting — versatile compound

=== TESTOSTERONE:MASTERON RATIOS ===

Masteron (Drostanolone) is the premier cosmetic/cutting compound. It's a DHT derivative that provides hardening, drying, and mild anti-estrogenic effects. Most effective at lower body fat percentages.

WHY THE RATIO MATTERS:
- Masteron is a DHT derivative — it does not aromatize and has anti-estrogenic properties
- Acts as a mild AI by binding to aromatase enzyme and SHBG
- Increases free Testosterone by displacing test from SHBG
- Best results when body fat is below 15% — the cosmetic effects (hardness, striations) require leanness to see
- Above 15% body fat, the visual effects are largely wasted

BEGINNER:
- Testosterone: 400-500mg/week | Masteron: 300-400mg/week
- Ratio: 1.25:1 (test:Masteron)
- Good first experience with Masteron's hardening effects
- Monitor hair if genetically predisposed to MPB — Masteron accelerates hair loss
- Masteron Enanthate half-life: 7-10 days. Propionate half-life: 1-2 days.

INTERMEDIATE:
- Testosterone: 400-500mg/week | Masteron: 400-500mg/week
- Ratio: 1:1 (test:Masteron)
- Excellent cutting/recomp cycle
- AI often unnecessary at this ratio — Masteron handles estrogen
- Great "feel good" factor — improved confidence, libido, mood from DHT activity

ADVANCED:
- Testosterone: 300-500mg/week | Masteron: 500-700mg/week
- Ratio: 1:1.2-1.4 (Masteron can run slightly higher)
- Pre-contest stack staple — extreme hardness and definition
- Can run Masteron dominant if you're a high aromatizer
- Stack with Tren for the ultimate contest prep look

Dave Crosland: "Masteron is one of those compounds that makes everything look better. But you have to be lean enough to see it. Under 15% body fat, Masteron makes you look like a statue. Over 15%, you're wasting your money."

HAIR LOSS WARNING:
- Masteron is one of the harshest compounds for hair loss
- DHT-derived — directly accelerates male pattern baldness in genetically susceptible individuals
- Finasteride does NOT work to protect hair from Masteron (finasteride only blocks 5-alpha reductase, which Masteron bypasses)
- If hair is a priority, consider Primobolan instead — similar benefits with less hair impact
- Topical RU58841 may offer some protection but results vary

=== TESTOSTERONE:ANAVAR (OXANDROLONE) RATIOS ===

- Primarily adds strength and lean, dry mass without water

=== FEMALE COMPOUND SAFETY — WHAT WOMEN CAN AND CANNOT USE ===

Female compound selection is fundamentally different from male compound selection. Women are dramatically more sensitive to androgens. The wrong compound at any dose can cause permanent virilization — voice deepening, facial hair growth, clitoral enlargement, jawline changes. Some of these effects are IRREVERSIBLE even after discontinuation.

SAFE COMPOUNDS FOR WOMEN (low virilization risk at proper doses):
- Anavar (Oxandrolone): 5-20mg/day — THE gold standard female compound. Safest oral, well-studied in women. Start at 5mg.
- Primobolan (injectable): 25-75mg/week — Very mild, excellent for lean gains. Second-best option after Anavar.
- Testosterone (low dose): 5-10mg/week — Micro-dose for libido, energy, and mild anabolic effect. Use propionate ester for fast clearance.
- Nandrolone/NPP (very low dose): 25-50mg/week — Joint support + mild mass. Use NPP (short ester) so it clears fast if sides appear.

COMPOUNDS WOMEN MUST NEVER USE — EXTREME VIRILIZATION RISK:
- Anadrol (Oxymetholone): NEVER for women. Despite some outdated medical literature on low-dose use, the virilization risk is unacceptably high. Causes rapid masculinization even at low doses.
- Dianabol (Methandrostenolone): NEVER for women. Highly androgenic, heavy aromatizer, rapid virilization.
- Trenbolone (any ester): NEVER for women. The most androgenic steroid available. Will virilize at any dose.
- Winstrol (Stanozolol): NEVER for women. Despite its reputation as a "cutting" drug, it is highly androgenic and causes severe virilization — voice changes, hair loss, acne. The androgenic effects hit women fast and hard.
- Halotestin (Fluoxymesterone): NEVER for women. Extremely androgenic, extremely hepatotoxic.
- Superdrol (Methasterone): NEVER for women. Extreme androgenic potency.
- Methyltrienolone (M-Tren): NEVER for women. oral tren — most potent androgen available.

USE WITH EXTREME CAUTION (advanced female competitors only):
- Masteron (Drostanolone): 50-75mg/week MAXIMUM, only pre-contest, only for advanced competitors. DHT derivative — accelerates virilization. Must monitor weekly.
- Equipoise (Boldenone): Generally avoided for women. Long ester means if virilization appears, the compound takes weeks to clear. Androgenic metabolites accumulate.
- Turinabol: Some advanced female athletes use 5-10mg/day for 4-6 weeks. Moderate virilization risk. Not recommended as a first choice.

Paul Barnett on Anabolic Bodybuilding: "For women, Anavar is it. It's the one compound I feel comfortable recommending. Anything else and you're playing with fire. I've seen women permanently damage their voices with one cycle of Winstrol."

Scott McNally: "Ladies, stick with Anavar and maybe low-dose Primo. That's all you need. The women who get into trouble are the ones who try to use male compounds at 'lower doses' — it doesn't work that way. Your receptors are far more sensitive."

=== ADJUSTING RATIOS — READING YOUR BODY AND BLOODWORK ===

No ratio is perfect for everyone. You must learn to read the signals your body gives you and adjust accordingly.

SIGNS YOUR ESTROGEN (E2) IS TOO HIGH:
- Water retention / bloating / puffy face
- Sensitive or itchy nipples (gyno warning)
- Emotional / mood swings / crying easily
- Elevated blood pressure
- Acne flare-up
- FIX: Add or increase AI (Aromasin preferred), or reduce testosterone dose

SIGNS YOUR ESTROGEN (E2) IS TOO LOW (crashed):
- Dry, cracking joints
- Low libido / erectile dysfunction
- Depression / anxiety / flat mood
- Fatigue and lethargy
- Dry skin / dry lips
- FIX: Stop AI immediately, reduce or remove anti-estrogenic compounds (EQ, Primo, Masteron), add Dianabol 10-20mg/day as an estrogen rescue

SIGNS YOUR PROLACTIN IS TOO HIGH:
- Erectile dysfunction / inability to orgasm
- Nipple sensitivity or leaking
- Low libido despite adequate testosterone
- Brain fog / lack of motivation
- FIX: Increase Cabergoline dose, get prolactin blood test, ensure vitamin B6 (P5P form) at 100-200mg/day

SIGNS YOUR DHT IS TOO LOW:
- Low libido despite good testosterone and E2 levels
- Lack of confidence / drive / assertiveness
- Soft erections despite being on testosterone
- FIX: Add Proviron 25-50mg/day, or add a DHT derivative (Masteron at low dose)

BLOODWORK TIMING FOR RATIO ADJUSTMENT:
- Get bloodwork at week 4-6 of any new cycle or ratio change
- Key markers: total Testosterone, free Testosterone, Estradiol (sensitive assay), Prolactin, CBC (hematocrit), Lipid Panel, Liver enzymes (AST/ALT), Kidney function (BUN/Creatinine)
- Compare to your personal baseline — not internet reference ranges
- Adjust ratios based on how you FEEL combined with what bloodwork SHOWS
- Never adjust based on bloodwork alone if you feel great, and never ignore bloodwork if you feel terrible

Can intake of garlic affect your lipids and blood sugar? I am Dr. Jerry Tan. I'm a Mayo Clinic trained indocrinologist. In this recent meta analysis which studied 29 trials looking at the effects of garlic on different parameters of health. Garlic was found to be effective in not only controlling fasting blood sugar A1c but also reduced cholesterol. In fact, the dosages of garlic preparations were not directly comparable due to differences of active ingredient and bioavailability. But what's important is the positive health effects of garlic were most likely due to its alysin content. The intake of garlic was found to be statistically effective at lowering fasting blood glucose reduced by an average of 7 mg per dl. Likewise, A1c was reduced by an average of 66%, a significant drop for a natural supplement as A1c represents blood sugar over the last 3 months. But what's important is that the results of the study showed that intake of garlic in any form has a beneficial effect on blood sugar and blood cholesterol and their association was statistically significant.

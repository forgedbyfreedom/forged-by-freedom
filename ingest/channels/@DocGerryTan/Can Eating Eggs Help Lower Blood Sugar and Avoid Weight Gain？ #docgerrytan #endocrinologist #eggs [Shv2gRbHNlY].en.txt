Now we all know that eggs are low in calories and they're rich in protein and therefore may be good for weight loss if eggs are part of a varied diet without added fat or sugar. Eating eggs especially will be helpful if you eat them during breakfast. In this study, participants who ate eggs for breakfast required smaller lunches and seemed to feel a more full than those who ate carbohydrate-rich breakfast. But if you're not a breakfast eater, then you can actually consume your eggs during lunch and dinner consisting of approximately two hard-boiled eggs with a mixed cup of vegetables that will contain around 200 to 270 calories, and this should do the trick. The study also found that eating eggs led to lower levels of grelin, meaning the participants felt less physiological drive to eat. Likewise, the egg breakfast resulted in a smaller blood sugar spike and more stable glucose over time, suggesting that eating eggs can also help manage blood sugar levels.

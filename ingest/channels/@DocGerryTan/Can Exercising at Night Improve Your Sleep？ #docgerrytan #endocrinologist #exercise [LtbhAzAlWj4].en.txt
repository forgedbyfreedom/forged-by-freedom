Does going into nighttime exercise help you sleep? I am Dr. Jerry Tan. I'm a Mayoc Clinic trained endocrinologist. Evidence indicates that evening exercise sessions have no disruptive effects. However, certain recommendations discourage exercise prior to bedtime. So in this study, healthy adults tested the effects of 3 minutes of resistance exercises every 30 minutes in the 4 hours before bed compared to just 4 hours of sitting. Interestingly, the average sleep length of those patients who exercised increased by 27 minutes after doing exercises compared with sitting. The activity breaks had no significant negative effects on sleep efficiency, the time it took to fall asleep, or the number of nighttime awakenings. Subsequent 24-hour and 48 hour physical activity patterns remained similar between the two groups, meaning the evening activity did not cause participants to be less active the following day. This is therefore a very practical advice for everyone who wants to have deep sleep that a simple light movement prior to sleeping especially after an evening meal can do the trick in giving you a better night's sleep.

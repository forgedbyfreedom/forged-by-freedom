Fruits are healthy, but how much is enough? I am Dr. Jerry Tan. I'm a Mayo Clinic trained endocrinologist. In this study of 80 adults with fatty liver who participated in this radi control trial looking at the effect of fruitrich diet on biomarkers of insulin resistance and fatty liver. They found out that consumption of four servings of fruit per day worsened markers for diabetes, liver function, and people gained weight compared to controls with two servings of fruit per day. In short, always remember that fruit is metabolized in the liver. So if you have a metabolic syndrome, then too much fruit is not healthy for you.

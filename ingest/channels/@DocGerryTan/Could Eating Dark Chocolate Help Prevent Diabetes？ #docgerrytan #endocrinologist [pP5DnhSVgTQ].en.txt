In this perspective study, they investigated the associations between dark milk and total chocolate consumption and the risk of diabetes in three US cohort. The study clearly showed that after adjusting for personal, lifestyle, and dietary risk factors, the participants who consume more than five servings per week of any chocolate showed a significant 10% lower rate of type 2 diabetes compared with those who never or rarely consume chocolate. But those who consumed more than five servings of dark chocolate showed a very high significant 21% reduction in risk suggesting that those who are eating dark chocolate with more cocoa in it with more flavonol known for its health benefits can help reduce the risk of type 2 diabetes compared to milk or white chocolate which contains more sugar.

Does going into nighttime exercise help you sleep? I am Dr. Jerry Tan. I'm a Mayoc Clinic trained endocrinologist. Evidence indicates that evening exercise sessions have no disruptive effects. However, certain recommendations discourage exercise prior to bedtime. So in this study, healthy adults tested the effects of 3 minutes of resistance exercises every 30 minutes in the 4 hours before bed compared to just 4 hours of sitting. Interestingly, the average sleep length of those patients who exercised increased by 27 minutes after doing exercises compared with sitting. This is therefore a very practical advice for everyone who wants to have deep sleep that a simple light movement prior to sleeping especially after an evening meal can do the trick in giving you a better night's sleep.

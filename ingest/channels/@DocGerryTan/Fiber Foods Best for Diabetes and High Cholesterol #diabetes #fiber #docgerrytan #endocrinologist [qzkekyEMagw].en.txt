Are all fibers the same in terms of disease prevention? I am Dr. Jerry Tan, a Mayo Clinic trained endocrinologist. Now, let me share you a summarized data on fiber and diseases as discussed in the recent AHN summit for gastro nutrition. Now, what they found was there are different fibers in terms of types and texture and in terms of recommendation for what disease. Number one, soluble and viscous fiber can help those with diarrhea. For example, beans, oats, peas, and avocado. Insoluble fiber can help those with constipation. Examples, whole wheat flour or wheat brand or cauliflower and green beans. We also have fermentable fibers which can however worsen the symptoms those with gas and bloating. And therefore you should avoid beans, wheat, and lastly soluble and non-fermentable fiber like silium can help those with diarrhea, constipation, and fecal incontinence.

How low should we go in terms of carbohydrates? I am Dr. Jerry Tan. I'm a Mayo Clinic trained endocrinologist. There remains to be a lack of consensus on what level of carbohydrate intake really constitutes the so-called low carbohydrate diets. Now, in this recent review published defining low carb in scientific literature, many clinical trials define low carb as 30% of daily energy needs from carbohydrates. Others define it as around 100 grams of carbohydrates per day. What I don't recommend are the more restricted carbohydrate diets because remember based on observational studies both very low carbohydrate and very high carbohydrate have been associated with increased all cause mortality and cancer mortality. So my suggestion is limit your carbs to just 40% of your total calories as this recommendation is healthy and sustainable for all.

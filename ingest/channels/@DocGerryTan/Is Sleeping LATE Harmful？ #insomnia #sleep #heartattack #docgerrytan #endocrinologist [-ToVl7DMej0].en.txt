Sleep is very important in maintaining health and in preventing cardiovascular disease. Now the question is, is sleeping late harmful? I am Dr. Jerry Tan. I'm a Mayo Clinic trained endocrinologist. In this study published in 2021 looking at sleeping late and the risk of mioardial inffection, the study clearly showed that participants with a bedtime later than 12 midnight was independently associated with an increased risk of incident micardial inffection or heart attack after adjusting for other potential co-variants. Now this find is of important implication especially in the advent of extensive cell phone use due to social media resulting in sleeping late and this study clearly emphasizes the importance of proper bedtime for the maintenance of overall health and healthy cardiovascular system.

Can vitamin D supplementation help control your glucose? I'm Dr. Jerry Tan. I'm a Mayo Clinic trained endocrinologist. Evidence from epidemological studies suggest that vitamin D deficiency is associated with reduced insulin release and development of type 2 diabetes. Now on this study they found that vitamin D can significantly reduce serum fasting plasma glucose A1c homir and fasting insulin levels in type 2 diabetic patients with the effects especially prominent when vitamin D was given in short-term high dosage with a vitamin D deficiency who were overweight who had an A1c of 8% or higher. The study also found that a dose as high as 2,000 international units per day provided a mark improvement in glycemic control and fasting blood sugar as long as the duration of supplementation was over 12 weeks.

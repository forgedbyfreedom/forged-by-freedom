Can intake of garlic affect your lipids and blood sugar? I am Dr. Jerry Tan. I'm a Mayo Clinic trained indocrinologist. In this recent meta analysis which studied 29 trials looking at the effects of garlic on different parameters of health, garlic was found to be effective in not only controlling fasting blood sugar A1c but also reduced cholesterol. In fact, the dosages of garlic preparations were not directly comparable due to differences of active ingredient and bioavailability of the different types of preparations. Some were using powder, oil, age extract and raw. But what's important is that the results of the study showed that intake of garlic in any form has a beneficial effect on blood sugar and blood cholesterol and their association was statistically significant.

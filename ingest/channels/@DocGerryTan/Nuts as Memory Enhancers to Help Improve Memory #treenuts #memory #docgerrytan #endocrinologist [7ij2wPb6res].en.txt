What are some of the beneficial effects on health with nuts? I am Dr. Jerry Tan Mayo Clinic trained endocrinologist. The beneficial effects of nut supplementation are known. But do they have effects on cognitive function? Now the study aimed to examine the effects of daily consumption of three nuts for four weeks on cognitive function, mood, metabolomics and gut microbial species. Now the study clearly showed that nut consumption did led to significant improvements in accuracy and speed of response on picture recognition task but no significant changes on bacterial immunity or diversity were observed. suggesting that intake of mixed nuts not only have beneficial effects on other parameters like diabetes or cardiovascular benefits but also on cognitive function.

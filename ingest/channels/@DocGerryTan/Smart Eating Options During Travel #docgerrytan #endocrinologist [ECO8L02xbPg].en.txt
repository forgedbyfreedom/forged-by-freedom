What foods should you eat if you travel? I'm Dr. Jerry Tan, MAO clinic trained endocrinologist. Travel foods are often high in sugar or refined carbs. It is therefore best to avoid unlimited bread baskets as well as fruit juices and smoothies as well as processed meats like sausages and cold cuts because they're unhealthy.

now most of us are on statins because of our cholesterol but are statins helpful other than lowering cholesterol I'm Dr Jerry tan I'm a meoc clinic trained endocrinologist statins have been hounded with issues with regard to cognitive problems or forgetfulness especially among patients with diabetes but in a recent metaanalysis of 46 observational studies evaluating the association between Statin therapy and the incidence of neurocognitive diseases that was published recently it was clearly shown that the use of statins among patients with high cholesterol did not increase the risk of neurocognitive disorders but rather the use of statins resulted in a 20% reduction in the risk of dementia and more importantly 32% reduction in Alzheimer's disease

The Mediterranean diet nowadays provides the best nutritional strategy for obtaining a combination of nutrients, antioxidants, and other beneficial compounds that help promote healthy aging. Mediterranean diet has been shown to reduce cardiovascular diseases including decrease immune system dysregulation, improve in cognitive function, reduction in inflammation as well as improvement in bone mineral densitometry. The traditional Mediterranean diet is actually a plant-based diet characterized by high intakes of fruits and vegetables and moderate amounts of fish, poultry, fermented dairy products like yogurt and cheese, but with low intakes of bread and processed meat, sugar, and most refined or highly processed foods.

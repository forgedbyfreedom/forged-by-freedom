Tahoe, a popular Filipino snack, is made from silken tofu. The soybased nature of Tahoe, also means it contains isoflavones, which offers many health benefits. Tofu, the main ingredient in Tahoe, is a complete protein and has calcium, which are vital for maintaining healthy muscle and bone. The soy isopones in Tahoe may also help reduce LDL or bad cholesterol, contributing to better heart health, and likewise reduce the risk of certain cancers. So, let's enjoy our yummy Tahoe daily.

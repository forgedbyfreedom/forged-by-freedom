Does low carb diet really help lower the risk of diabetes? I am Dr. Jerry Tan. I'm a Mayo Clinic trained endocrinologist. In this study involving 20,000 type 2 diabetic patients, surprisingly after adjustments for baseline BMI and other co-variants, higher overall low carb diet score was associated with 31% high risk of type 2 diabetes. Further analysis showed that an animal-based low carb diet emphasizing animal protein and fat and an healthy low carb diet score that the emphasizes whole grains and other high quality carbohydrates were associated with 39 to 44% type 2 diabetes risk. Now, in contrast, a vegetable-based low carb diet emphasizing plantbased protein and fat was associated with a 6% lower risk of diabetes. And a healthy low carb diet, further deemphasizing refined carbs, was associated with a 16% lower diabetes risk. suggesting is best that we focus on high fiber, high quality whole grains, high plant protein rather than animalbased protein.

Surely each of you has many different spices in your kitchen. Today we will talk about two specific spices: turmeric and black pepper. They work together. Turmeric is a powerful anti-inflammatory; it helps with many problems, but it helps with inflammation best of all. Did you know that if you add black pepper to turmeric, it improves its absorption by 2000? Black pepper itself is an excellent antibacterial and antiseptic agent; it relieves a sore throat and aids digestion. Some people even notice that if they grind black pepper and sniff it, the craving for cigarettes disappears and they don’t want to smoke. But for inflammation, combine turmeric with black pepper for a powerful anti-inflammatory effect.

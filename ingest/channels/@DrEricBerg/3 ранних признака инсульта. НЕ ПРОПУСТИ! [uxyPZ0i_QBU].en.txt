There are three early signs of a stroke. These are not the symptoms of a stroke: when one side of your face droops and you can't move your arm or leg. This indicates a stroke has occurred, but the early signs are different. A stroke is caused by a blockage of blood vessels in the brain due to increased pressure, which will manifest itself in the form of a severe headache. Second, if the blockage somehow touches the brain stem, you may experience very severe nausea and even vomiting. Third, if you stop having both blood pressure and chest pain, this could be an early sign of a stroke. It is very important to get to the hospital within an hour, they will give you the necessary medications to dissolve the clot. To prevent a stroke, take vitamin E in the form of a triglyceride, magnesium, and switch to a low-carb diet.

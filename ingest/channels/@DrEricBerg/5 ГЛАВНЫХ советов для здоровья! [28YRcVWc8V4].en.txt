Five important health tips: First, dilute a tablespoon of apple cider vinegar in a glass of water and drink it 15 minutes before meals. Apple cider vinegar will help normalize blood sugar and also relieve bloating. Second, eat something fatty at the end of your meal, nuts or avocado. You will feel full and will not want to snack. Third, eat only two times a day: the first time in the morning, the second time at lunch. This will give you a boost of energy for the day. And you will not have to eat dinner, which can prevent you from falling asleep. Fourth, for those who have problems with sleep, since I do not recommend eating dinner, take kefir, add blueberries, mix and drink at night. Fifth, instead of training, do housework. Training is good, but physical labor not only helps the body but also relieves mental stress. In addition, it is more productive.

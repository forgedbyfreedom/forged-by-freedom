Six Early Signs of Nutrient Deficiency.  The first is titania. Skin twitching.  This is magnesium deficiency. Magnesium is found in leafy greens.  Lower back pain that radiates to the hips and shins is a sign of vitamin D deficiency. Vitamin D can be obtained from the sun.  A low pulse rate is a sign of potassium deficiency.  Eat more avocados.  Fatigue when climbing a mountain is a sign of vitamin E deficiency. It's found in raw sunflower seeds.  Muscle weakness is a result of sodium deficiency.  Eat more sea salt.  Poor vision in the dark is a sign of vitamin A deficiency.

Let's talk about the healthiest foods to eat. First, beef, red meat, and not grain-fed, but completely grass-fed. Second, commercial fatty fish, such as salmon. Third, seafood, such as mussels, oysters, shrimp, lobsters, crabs, and the like. Fourth, eggs. Buy the highest quality pastured organic eggs, they contain a lot of nutrients, especially in the yolk. Fifth, fermented vegetables, sauerkraut or kimchi. I added them because they are excellent probiotics. Sixth, leafy greens. This can be anything from arugula and spinach to high-quality lettuce leaves. Seventh, herbal spices, such as garlic, onions, sage, and thyme. These herbs contain special compounds that can help.

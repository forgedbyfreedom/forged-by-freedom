Most people don't have enough salt. I'm talking about sea salt, not table salt. If you don't exercise, one teaspoon a day is enough for you. Did you know that during an hour of training you burn 1.00 mg of sodium? Some athletes lose 5,000 mg a day. If you drink four cups of coffee a day, you lose another half a teaspoon of sodium. And if you also drink a lot of water, this sodium will dissolve, which will cause you to have cramps, fatigue, and you will train worse.

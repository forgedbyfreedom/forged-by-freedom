I recommend two main groups of nutrients as an absolute minimum: electrolytes and B vitamins. When you are on ketosis, to prevent keto fatigue, keto rash and other symptoms, because in the beginning, a lot of water leaves the body and you lose a lot of electrolytes, and some experience severe fatigue and dehydration. So, you need to replenish electrolytes. In addition, when we burn more fat, we need B vitamins. And many are on the verge of deficiency at the beginning; they can experience fatigue if they do not get B vitamins. One of the best sources is nutritional yeast.

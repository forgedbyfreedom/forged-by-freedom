White sugar and brown sugar.  Is there a difference for health?  Brown sugar is just white sugar, with just a little bit of molasses, a by-product of sugar cane processing.  But it does contain vitamins, minerals and phytonutrients.  However, the pataca here is 3.5%. Brown sugar will give you slightly more iron, calcium, magnesium, and zinc. But both will cause the same spike in blood sugar, so there isn't much difference.

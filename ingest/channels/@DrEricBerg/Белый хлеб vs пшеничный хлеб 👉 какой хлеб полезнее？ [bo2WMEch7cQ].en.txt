White bread and wheat.  What's the difference? White bread is stripped of fiber and nutrients.  It is bleached.  It is essentially pure starch.  Pure starch can be converted into sugar very quickly. Essentially, starch is hidden sugar.  Wheat bread has slightly more fiber and nutrients.  It is less refined, but it has more gluten.  Gluten can cause inflammation in the gut, and wheat bread also contains more phytic acid, which blocks nutrients.  But the main problem is that both cause the same spike in blood sugar.   There are few differences.

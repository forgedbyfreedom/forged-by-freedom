There is a very interesting connection between diseased gums and teeth and a disease called COPD. This is chronic obstructive pulmonary disease. This disease obviously affects the lungs. If you don't take care of your gums and teeth, an abscess can form under the teeth. Sometimes this abscess has no symptoms, but the infection multiplies and penetrates the lymphatic system. From there, it enters the circulatory system and increases the risk of pathogens entering other organs, including the lungs. If you want to learn more about this, go to my YouTube channel.

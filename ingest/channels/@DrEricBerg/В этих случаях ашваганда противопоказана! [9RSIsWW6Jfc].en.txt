When you shouldn't take ashwagandha if you have a sensitivity to nightshades. Ashwagandha contains a lot of iron. If you have a genetic problem and your body accumulates iron and cannot easily eliminate it, then ashwagandha is not suitable for you. Ashwagandha should be taken if you have an iron deficiency because it contains a lot of it, but if you have an excess of iron, you should abstain.

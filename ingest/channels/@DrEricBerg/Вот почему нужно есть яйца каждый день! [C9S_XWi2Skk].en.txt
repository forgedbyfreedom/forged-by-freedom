If you eat eggs every day for 2 weeks, what will happen?  Your brain will function better thanks to the halene found in eggs, which protects against fatty liver disease. Eggs also contain cholesterol.  Cholesterol is the raw material for sex hormones: testosterone, estrogen, progesterone and the stress hormone cortisol.  Eggs increase good cholesterol.  Eggs contain two unique antioxidants: lutein and zeaxanthin.  They prevent blue light from entering the eyes and thus protect against macular degeneration.  And finally, eggs contain a lot of sulfur.  Sulfur helps hair become shiny and strong.

This is why apple cider vinegar is beneficial for your gut flora.  Not because it gives you more bacteria, not because of the vitamins, not even because of the mother in apple cider vinegar, but because the acid in apple cider vinegar changes the environment in your digestive system.  The more acid there is, the less pathogens there are. E. coli, salmonella, clastridium, candida and even Helicobacter. Simply by changing the pH of the environment where bacteria live, you can make life impossible for harmful bacteria.  Dilute a tablespoon of apple cider vinegar in a glass of water and drink every day.

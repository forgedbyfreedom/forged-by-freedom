The most dangerous sign of zinc deficiency is not loss of smell, low testosterone, or even a weakened immune system. More than half a million children die each year from zinc deficiency.  The intestinal walls rupture, causing leaky gut syndrome.  This leads to intestinal infections and a deficiency of electrolytes: potassium, sodium, magnesium due to severe diarrhea.  This leads to dehydration.  The blood becomes thick and the heart literally stops beating.  And all because of zinc deficiency.  Inexpensive zinc supplements can help alleviate this problem.

Cortisol is originally designed to improve short-term memory for emotional events, which helps with survival. However, chronically high cortisol destroys the hippocampus, which is like a memory switchboard. This results in memory lapses, confusion, and loss of concentration. You're constantly fixated on one or two problems, and you simply can't stop thinking about them. As a result, the gears are turning, and you start solving the problem in the middle of the night when it's not the right time. This is the result of chronically high cortisol. You're in problem-solving mode, but your solutions are n't that good.

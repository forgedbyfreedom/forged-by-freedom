These are all symptoms of nutrient deficiency. Dry, flaky skin is a sign of omega-3 fatty acid deficiency.  Premature graying is a result of copper deficiency.  Copper is associated with maintaining levels of melanin, the pigments in skin and hair.  Gastritis or ulcer is a zinc deficiency.  Zinc is a very important microelement.  It accelerates wound healing.  If you are zinc deficient, you will be more vulnerable to Helicobacter pylori infection. Excessive sweating, especially on the head, is a typical sign of vitamin D deficiency. If you have arrhythmia or palpitations, your body needs electrolytes.  The most important electrolyte for arrhythmia is magnesium.  The best source of magnesium with better absorption is magnesium glycinate.  You need to take at least 600 mg for weeks or months to correct arrhythmia, because the body absorbs only a portion of magnesium.  Another important mineral for the thyroid gland is iodine.   It is very difficult to obtain iodine.  Even if you eat shellfish, seaweed, or sea salt, you still won't get enough iodine.  Bleeding gums are a sign of vitamin C deficiency. Vitamin C deficiency can occur if you don't eat enough vegetables, lemons, berries, bell peppers, and even sauerkraut.  Most often, vitamin C deficiency is caused by excessive sugar consumption.

Just one glass of this drink a day can significantly reduce the risk of heart attack and stroke, and this drink is garlic water. 43 clinical trials have shown that garlic helps the cardiovascular system: it reduces plaque formation, improves vascular elasticity, lowers blood pressure, and is even compatible with some blood pressure medications. It also lowers the coronary calcium index. So, crush one clove of garlic and stir it in a glass of water. Let the drink steep for 15 minutes. Add a teaspoon of extra virgin olive oil. Wait 15 minutes because it takes time for certain phytonutrients in garlic to activate. Then drink it and do this every day.

Another important function of the liver is the conversion of thyroid hormone T4 to T3. An unhealthy liver cannot convert these hormones. You may have enough hormones, but they are not activated because they are not converted. 80 percent of the active form of thyroid hormone T3 is made in the liver. If liver function is impaired, maybe 70-60 or 50 percent of the hormone is converted, and you begin to think, "Why do I suddenly have problems with my thyroid?" Where do they even come from? The problem is not in the thyroid gland, but in the liver, in the conversion, this is important.

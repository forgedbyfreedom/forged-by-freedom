Usually, hair loss on the crown of your head means that you have excess dihydrotestosterone (DHT). DHT is a very potent form of testosterone. Excess DHT occurs due to an enzyme that converts more testosterone into DHT than necessary, so you need to lower the level of this enzyme. Taking certain inhibitors (these inhibitors are found in pumpkin seeds and green tea) reduces the level of this enzyme, thereby lowering DHT levels.

Cortisol temporarily puts the immune system on hold. Cortisol deactivates and disables very important special T-cells called natural killer cells, which normally perform two tasks: killing viruses and cancer cells. The more cortisol you have in your body, the less immune protection you have at that time. This is why almost everyone who gets infected and gets sick has experienced a stressful event right beforehand, because stress activates cortisol and shuts down the immune system, which is why the body can no longer fight it. So, stress makes you vulnerable to viruses, bacteria, fungi, mold, and so on.

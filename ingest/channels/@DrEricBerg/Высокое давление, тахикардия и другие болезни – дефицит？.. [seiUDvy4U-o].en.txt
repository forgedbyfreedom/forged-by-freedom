Many diseases with unknown causes are nutrient deficiencies.  For example, essential hypertension, high blood pressure, typical vitamin D deficiency, fatty liver - a deficiency of haline, ulcers - a deficiency of zinc, rapid heartbeat, tachycardia - a deficiency of potassium.  High cholesterol with low HDL is neacin deficiency. Migraine is a magnesium deficiency.  For severe migraines, take 1,000 mg of magnesium glycinate.

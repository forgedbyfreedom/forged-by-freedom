You know, it only takes 20 minutes to eat two cheeseburgers, a medium cola, and medium fries. That's more than a thousand calories, but to burn those thousand calories, you need about 3 hours of intense exercise. But if those thousand calories aren't from carbohydrates, that changes everything because even without exercise, they won't be stored as fat. On a low-carb diet, your body uses them for energy. On a high-carb diet, your body won't be able to get energy from those calories. Whether you burn fat depends on the amount of carbohydrates.

As for free radical damage, to reduce the excess amount of this damage and oxidation that ages us, there are two types of antioxidants: endogenous antioxidants, which the body itself creates; and exogenous antioxidants, which we get from food. You need to eat a lot of greens and vegetables to get these antioxidants, but the body has antioxidant networks that increase when you exercise. When you fast, they increase. So, fasting helps with almost everything you need to look young. When you fast, there are more antioxidants in the body, and that's great.

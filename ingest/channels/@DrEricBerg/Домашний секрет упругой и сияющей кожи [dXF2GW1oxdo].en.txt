What happens if you wash your face with rice water every day for 2 weeks?  Rice water is rich in B vitamins, including inositol, neacin, and vitamins B6 and B2.  All of them will help balance melanin, which can cause hyperpigmentation.  This will help remove dark spots on your face and make your skin look lighter and more radiant. The skin will also become softer.  If rice water is fermented, it will contain more lactic acid, the pH of which matches the pH of the skin.  Beneficial bacteria and yeast will support the skin's microflora, which will make the skin more elastic and reduce the appearance of pimples.

What will happen to you if you eat cereal for breakfast every day?  In childhood, our metabolism and our body are not yet damaged. Probably because we didn't eat it chronically.  But over the years, the damage accumulates and problems arise.  I think a lot of kids can get away with it.  At least for now.  After all, their mitochondria are not yet damaged .  They have what is called a very flexible metabolism.  They can burn a lot of excess sugar.  But over the years things have gotten worse.  Children get 67% of their calories from ultra-processed junk food.  This is junk food.  And unfortunately, the stores are full of it.  So, if you eat cereal every morning for one month, you will end up like I did . Pregdit, constant utter fatigue, inability to wake up in the morning, grouchiness, depression, anxiety, arthritis, brain fog, not to mention weight gain, fatty liver, and so on and so forth.  I propose the following.  If you eat cereal or your kids eat cereal, just try it just once.  Instead of cereal, have something protein-rich for breakfast: bacon and eggs , beef steak, fish, anything protein-rich.  And see how much better you feel with just one tiny change, and then decide for yourself what you eat.

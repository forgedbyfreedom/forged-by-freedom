What happens if a person doesn't have a gallbladder? Without a gallbladder, fat is absorbed twice as poorly. The gallbladder stores bile in itself, which helps break down fat. It acts on fat like a detergent with bile, and fat is broken down 95-100%.  But without it, you won't be able to secrete the required volume of bile. Bile breaks down fat to a certain level, and the pancreas secretes its enzyme, lipase, to finish what it started. As a result, the pancreas is under more stress, and the fat isn't completely broken down, hence the floating stool and skin rashes. Vitamins A, D, and K aren't absorbed, and you'll have to monitor this, because these vitamins are fat-soluble.

Postmenopausal women need to eat more butter and egg yolks. Why?  Changes in hormones increase the risk of osteoporosis and osteopenia.  Estrogen and progesterone levels drop, and the body needs to somehow support bones.  Most women make the mistake of starting to take calcium supplements like calcium carbonate.   A big mistake because it increases the risk of a heart attack. If there is a vitamin needed in this situation, it is vitamin K2.  It is vitamin K2, not K1.  Guess where you can get it?  From grass-fed butter, egg yolks and other products. Our bones are high in protein and high in calcium, and we need vitamin K2 to strengthen them.  Vitamin K2 also helps direct calcium to bones.  This makes the bones very strong.  Vitamin K2 reduces vascular calcification. It helps regulate blood sugar, reduces the risk of kidney stones , reduces the risk of cancer and even the risk of dementia.

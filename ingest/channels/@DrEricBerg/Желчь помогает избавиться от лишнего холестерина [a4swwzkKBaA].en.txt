What else does bile do? It helps get rid of excess cholesterol. Let's say, for example, you decided to lose weight and started a ketogenic diet. Now, fat cells release all these triglycerides and cholesterol. What's inside fat cells is a combination of triglycerides, which are suitable as an energy source, and cholesterol, which cannot be a source of energy. So, we need bile to get rid of excess cholesterol. If you don't have enough bile, because the liver isn't working at full capacity, you develop gallstones. Gallstones occur when there is too much concentrated cholesterol. There isn't enough bile to deal with it, so the cholesterol crystallizes. When there are stones in the gallbladder, a person needs more bile; they don't necessarily need to lower their cholesterol.

Oily skin means that the sebaceous glands secrete a lot of sebum due to excess androgens in this man. More zinc is needed to normalize androgens; women may also need more zinc. But usually, excess androgens in women are a sign of polycystic ovary syndrome, which can be accompanied by additional symptoms such as facial hair and acne, which is due to high androgen levels. And the reason here is insulin, and excess insulin due to excess carbohydrates. You are already beginning to see a common feature: a high- carbohydrate diet depletes zinc reserves, thereby allowing androgen levels to rise in women. So, many skin problems are associated with insulin: oily skin, acne, and many others.

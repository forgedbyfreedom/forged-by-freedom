I was asked if vegetables lose nutrients when frozen and the answer is no, they don't, but some vegetables need to be blanched before freezing. This means that you take the vegetables and drop them in boiling water for one to three minutes, then transfer them to ice water and then you can put them in a bag chopped and put them in the freezer where it's minus 18 degrees. And this blanching process, depending on its duration, reduces the amount of some nutrients: vitamin C by an average of 25 percent, fans by 35 percent and B1 by 10 percent. The point of blanching is to destroy some enzymes so that frozen vegetables last longer because enzymes are sensitive to heat.

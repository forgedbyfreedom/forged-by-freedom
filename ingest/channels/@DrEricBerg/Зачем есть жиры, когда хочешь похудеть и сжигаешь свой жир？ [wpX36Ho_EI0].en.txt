I was asked a question by a person on intermittent fasting and in ketosis, he burns his own fat, and he wanted to know why he eats fat if he burns his own. Firstly, when we burn our own fat, we don't get any fat from it internally. It's pure fuel. Fat should be eaten at least for the sake of fat-soluble vitamins. That's the first thing. Secondly, when you eat whole foods, especially protein, there will always be fat in them. Fat always goes with protein and vice versa. For example, in eggs, meat, and similar foods, even in seeds and nuts, the fat and protein are 50/50. It might be better not to add fat to your diet like butter or coconut oil, but just eat olives and your usual diet. But if you don't have enough calories, then yes, you will lose weight, but you can experiment by eating less fat and see how you feel. This can help speed up progress.

Today I'll share a recipe for homemade and delicious avocado. It's very simple: take one ripe avocado, a cup of mixed berries or any berries of your choice, 0.5 cups of organic lemon juice, 1 tablespoon of apple cider vinegar, and a quarter teaspoon of sea salt. It's best to use pink Himalayan salt so you get enough potassium and magnesium, a little calcium, and the right amount of sodium chloride and trace elements. If you exercise, run, or sweat profusely, add a little more salt, maybe half a teaspoon. Blend it all in a blender, adding 5 cups of water. Some people will have enough flavor and sweetness from the berries. If you like it sweeter, try adding 20 drops of berry-flavored or lemon-flavored Stevie. I add a little ice, blend, and drink.

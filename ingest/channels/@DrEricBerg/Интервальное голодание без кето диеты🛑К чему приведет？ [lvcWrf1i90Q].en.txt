If you decide to practice intermittent fasting but don't follow proper etiquette, say you're on a high-carb diet and intermittent fasting, you may experience a lot of problems with hypoglycemia and blood sugar issues. If you fast, you'll feel terrible, your brain will suffer from hunger, because you haven't switched to fat and you're not eating anything. The sensations will be very strange, and it will be very hard for your body, because brain cells suffer without glucose. So, intermittent fasting should be accompanied by some kind of diet, and you need to transition gradually so that your brain doesn't suffer from hunger. Then you'll start getting ketones from food as fuel, which is glucose for now.

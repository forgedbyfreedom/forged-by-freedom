When stomach acidity is between 1 and 3, it promotes bile secretion. If bile is not secreted from the liver and gallbladder, there is a tendency to form gallstones. The acid activates pepsin, but there are other enzymes secreted by the pancreas. These enzymes help digest carbohydrates and fats. Therefore, when there is insufficient acid in the stomach, the pancreas secretes fewer enzymes, and digestion is impaired. Furthermore, strong acid is needed in the stomach so that we can absorb not only minerals, but also trace elements and vitamins. Therefore, if there is no strong acid in the stomach, a severe nutrient deficiency occurs.

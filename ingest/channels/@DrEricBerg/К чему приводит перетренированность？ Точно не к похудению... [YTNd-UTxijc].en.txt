Ok, hello, another piece of advice. A woman came to me. She decided not to follow my program. She just wanted to see what I was offering. She decided to work out at the gym with a friend for a year to see if it worked or not. If not, then they would come back. In short, she came back in a whole year. She lost a whole kilogram in a year at the gym. She lifted about one and a half million kilograms. She trains daily. I talked to her. She had severe depression and sleep problems. I explained that she was overtraining, and we started working on normalizing her sleep. We reduced her workouts to one and a half times a week. This is one full and one light workout. In a month, she lost two sizes. A perfect example of overtraining. On the other hand, some people don't train enough, so balance is important here. See you soon.

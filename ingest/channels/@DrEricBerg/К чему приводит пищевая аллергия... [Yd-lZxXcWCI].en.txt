There are obvious food allergies, say to dairy products. This could be due to the casein protein in milk. Dairy products can permanently damage the digestive system. What about gluten, a protein found in wheat? There are many people with gluten allergies. Even if you don't have a gluten allergy, you may have a sensitivity to it or lactose intolerance. Some people can't tolerate milk sugar. All of this can cause inflammation. There are environmental allergies. Let's say you have an allergy to ragweed or something like that. In this case, the sinus, throat, or lungs are exposed to an environmental allergen, causing irritation and inflammation.

I want to share a very interesting and effective way to fall asleep in a matter of minutes. The essence of the method is that you need to breathe in such a way as to optimize and synchronize your heartbeat, blood pressure, and breathing rhythm, that is, your lungs. This will help stimulate the parasympathetic nervous system. It, in turn, will help you sleep well. So here's the secret. First, practice doing this with a timer. Here's what you need to do: inhale for 5.5 seconds and then exhale for 5.2 seconds. If you synchronize your inhalations and exhalations so that each lasts 5.5 seconds, you will breathe for about 5 seconds, once a minute. This creates synchronization. You need to breathe through your nose very measuredly. I assure you that after a few minutes you will fall asleep.

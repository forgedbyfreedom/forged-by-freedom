Let's discuss how to increase levels of serotonin, the happiness hormone.  Serotonin is produced in the brain and in the intestines.  It promotes a feeling of happiness.  It helps lift your mood, helps you calm down, reduces carbohydrate cravings, helps you focus, and reduces anxiety.  A serotonin deficiency can cause depressed mood.  A person may experience depression, anxiety, irritability, and even poor self-esteem.  Sleep problems and even memory loss are possible.  So, how can you increase your serotonin levels?  Number one. Eltryptophan is an amino acid.  It is not toxic, it is converted into serotonin. Eltryptophan also improves sleep, so I recommend taking it right before bed on an empty stomach.  Number two.  Workout.  Exercise promotes the conversion of eltryptophan to serotonin.  Number three.  Sun and Vitamin D. Vitamin D is necessary not only for the creation of serotonin, but also for the conversion of tryptophan into serotonin. Number four.  Probiotics. Serotonin is produced in the intestines, and in large quantities. There is a close connection between what happens in the gut and what happens in the head.  Probiotics help increase tryptophan.  Especially probiotics such as kimchi, sauerkraut, fermented vegetables, perhaps even kefir, or probiotics in the form of dietary supplements.  Number five.  Massage also increases serotonin levels and reduces cortisol levels.  Number six.  Starvation. Fasting significantly increases serotonin levels.   This is probably why people get rid of depression when they fast.

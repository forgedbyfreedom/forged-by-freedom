How to lose weight even more effectively?  First, avoid hidden sugar.  The label " sugar free" does not mean there is no sugar in it. Avoid modified food starch or maltodextrin.  Second, high quality protein, grass-fed beef or lamb. Nutrient-rich protein activates the opposite insulin hormone, which will trigger fat burning and bring satiety.  And third, avoid any food with MSG.  Although MSG is not a carbohydrate, it has been used to induce obesity in mice because it makes them more hungry.

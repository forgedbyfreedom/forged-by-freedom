I want to talk about a new term that you may have never heard of. This is inflammatory oncotaxis. What is it? This phenomenon consists of cancer spreading to areas of the body where there have been mechanical injuries. These are places of old injuries where there is inflammation and the cause of this can be many. It can be irritation of the colon by food. What causes a recurring inflammatory reaction and cancer spreads to this area. Cancer can get into the lungs where a smoker has inflammation. It can get into the blood vessels due to high glucose levels due to a high-carbohydrate diet. Or it can get into the breast tissue because there was some kind of injury to these tissues. This is important information because it allows us to predict where cancer may occur and we can do something about it before cancer occurs.

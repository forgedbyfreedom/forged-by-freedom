Today's topic is our skin: wrinkles and what to do with them. What's usually done is focus on the external manifestation, correcting from the outside in, rubbing cream into the skin to get rid of wrinkles. The active ingredient in the cream is retinol. Retinol is an artificial vitamin, and I recommend the principle of working from the inside out. Eat foods rich in vitamins. What kind of food is this? Egg yolk is rich in fats, rich in vitamins, and organic grass- fed butter is rich in vitamins, but you're unlikely to eat that. Grass- fed liver is also rich in vitamins. I just couldn't help but mention high-quality natural cod liver oil, rich in vitamins A and D, and good for the skin.

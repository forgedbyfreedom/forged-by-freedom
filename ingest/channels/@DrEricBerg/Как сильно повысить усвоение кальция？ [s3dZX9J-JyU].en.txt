Fat-soluble vitamins help the absorption of certain minerals. Vitamin A helps absorb iodine. Vitamin D increases calcium absorption by 20 times. This is a lot. You only need a little vitamin D to greatly increase calcium absorption. If you don't have enough vitamin D, you won't absorb calcium. Vitamin K2, as I said, helps transport calcium and works in conjunction with vitamin D3. I recommend taking D and IK2 together if you're taking a supplement.

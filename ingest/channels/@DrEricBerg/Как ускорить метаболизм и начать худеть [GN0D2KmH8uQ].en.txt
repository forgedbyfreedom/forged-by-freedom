Another tip on how to speed up your metabolism is to make sure that insulin is working properly in your body, that is, eliminating insulin resistance is one way. Another way is through exercise, but not just running on a treadmill. Here are a couple of important principles. There are three exercise parameters: duration, intensity, and recovery after exercise. Of these three, it is intensity that affects metabolism, not duration in general. If you can shorten your workout, but increase the intensity, train your whole body and take a long time to recover. To speed up your metabolism, you also need to gradually introduce intermittent fasting, not abruptly. Eat, say, two or even just once a day. You will be comfortable, you will lose weight, and improve your cognitive functions.

Symptoms of nutrient deficiency: thinning hair - biotin deficiency, cracked corners of the lips, B2 deficiency.  Tingling in the toes - B1 deficiency.  I recommend B1 in the form of benfatiamine.  Muscle twitching and cramping are typical signs of magnesium deficiency.  Lower back pain is a sign of vitamin D deficiency. Vertical scars on the nails are a sign of zinc deficiency.  Chronic cough, especially at night, is a sign of calcium deficiency.  If you crave salty foods, this is a typical sodium deficiency; get it from sea salt.  These spots on the back of the hand indicate vitamin A deficiency. The best sources are trisca liver oil, butter, or liver, not carrots.   If you get sick often, take more zinc and vitamin D.

What is the difference between extra virgin olive oil and avocado oil? High-quality olive oil contains a lot of polyphenols.  Polyphenols can act as a powerful anti-inflammatory and pain reliever similar to ibuprofen.  Avocado oil contains a lot of special carotenoids that prevent macular degeneration. You can cook on both. For high heat, avocado oil is better. I dress my salads with high-quality Hextra Virgin olive oil.

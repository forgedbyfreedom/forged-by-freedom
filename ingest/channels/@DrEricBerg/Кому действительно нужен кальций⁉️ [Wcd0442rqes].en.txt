Why I rarely recommend taking calcium? I usually recommend other minerals, potassium or magnesium, but not calcium. The reason is that we easily get calcium from food, but many people have excess calcium, and it accumulates in the joints and arteries. The vessels harden, causing arthritis. I would recommend calcium only in a couple of cases. Firstly, if you have cramps, you collapse, or if you have spasms, you need calcium. Or if you can't sleep. You've tried everything, but nothing has helped. Does calcium and magnesium help with sleep? Or have you been diagnosed with osteopenia? Osteoporosis, or even osteoporosis, is nonsense. Only in these cases do I recommend calcium.

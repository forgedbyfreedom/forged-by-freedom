I received a question about what to do if I ca n't seem to lose weight. In this video, I want to explain the only difference between an overweight person and a thin person. Between someone who is at a so-called setpoint, where the weight just won't go away, and someone who can lose weight easily. Overweight people with a slow metabolism have higher fasting insulin levels even when they're not eating. The pancreas secretes more insulin, which causes insulin resistance. insulin resistance freezes metabolism. insulin is a fat-producing hormone, but it also has another function: it prevents fat burning. It blocks hormone-sensitive lipase, the main enzyme that breaks down fat. This blocks insulin, and so the person has constantly high insulin levels, and doesn't lose weight.

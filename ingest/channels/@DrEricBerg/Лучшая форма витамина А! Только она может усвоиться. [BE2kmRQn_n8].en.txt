Retinol is the best form of vitamin A, it is the most bioavailable form of vitamin A. Beta-carotene, which is also called vitamin A, is only a tiny part of beta- carotene. So you won't get the amount of vitamin A you need from beta-carotene. That's why beta-carotene from plants is good for some purposes, but it definitely won't give you the Retinol active form of vitamin A that we actually need.

Hi, I was asked which vegan source of vitamin B12 is the best. I answer: There are 3 main sources of B12 that many vegans eat: nori, also known as red algae, chlorella, and spirulina. But there are two types of B12: pseudo B12 and real B12. Pseudo means not real B12. It is called an analogue of B12, which means that its chemical composition is similar to B12, but it acts differently. Algae contain a lot of pseudo B12 or B12 analogues, and this blocks the small amount of real B12 that is there, and you will have a deficiency. Therefore, if you do not take B12 from additional sources as a supplement, I do not recommend trying to get B12 from snare or spirulina. On the other hand, chlorella contains a higher amount of real B12 and a very small amount of pseudo B12. The question arises: how much chlorella is needed to get a daily dose of B12? You need 6 tablets or one teaspoon 2 times a day. Thanks for watching.

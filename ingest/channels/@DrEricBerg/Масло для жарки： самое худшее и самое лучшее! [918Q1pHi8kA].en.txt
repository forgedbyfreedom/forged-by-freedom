Did you know that when you go out to eat and order French fries, onion rings, fried chicken, or chips, the same oil used to fry it all is reheated hundreds of times. Can you imagine how much oxidation this causes? How many free radicals does this create in the body? Can you imagine how much inflammation this causes? Problems with the brain, nervous system, and heart? Seed oils like canola, corn, soybean, and cottonseed oils cause inflammation, especially if they are reheated several times in a row. The half-life of seed oils is about 2 seconds to 12 years. These inflammation-causing oils get stuck in your cells for 2 seconds to 12 years. Instead of frying food in seed oils, fry it in coconut oil, ghee, or beef tallow.

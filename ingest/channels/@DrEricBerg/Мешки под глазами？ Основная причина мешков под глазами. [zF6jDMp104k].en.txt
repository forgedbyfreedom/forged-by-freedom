Let's move on to bags under the eyes. If you have puffy tissue under the eyes or even above the eyes, this is a sign of either blood sugar problems (the same problem) or something is wrong with the kidneys. High blood sugar levels are the most damaging to the kidneys, which is associated with insulin resistance. After all, the kidneys are one of the organs that suffer greatly from a high-carbohydrate diet. There are other causes of kidney problems. But if fluid is retained in the kidneys, this is visible by the swelling under the eyes.

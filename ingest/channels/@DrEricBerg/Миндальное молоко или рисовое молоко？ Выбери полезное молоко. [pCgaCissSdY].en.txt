I was asked which is better, rice milk or almond milk. Firstly, rice milk has a lot of sugar (29g) and a lot of carbohydrates. You don't need rice milk, even without fillers, it has tons of sugar, because it's rice. Almond milk is much better, about 1g of carbohydrates and less than a gram of sugar. Coconut milk also has very little sugar, while real milk has about 10g of carbohydrates and about 9g of sugar. I think almond milk is the better option.

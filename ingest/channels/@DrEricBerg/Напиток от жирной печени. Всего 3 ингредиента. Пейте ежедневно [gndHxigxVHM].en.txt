The ingredients are simple: take kale, take blueberries, and take kefir gel-blueberries. Take organic ones and keep them in the freezer; after freezing, they are easier to chop. Take a blender, pour in 2 cups of frozen kale, then pour in 1 cup of blueberries. Blueberries contain a substance called quercetin, and kale also contains quercetin, which is an ideal weapon against fatty liver. Not to mention that it is necessary for many aspects of health, including strengthening the immune system. So, 2 cups of frozen kale in a blender, a cup of frozen blueberries, pour a glass of water, and a glass of whole, full- fat kefir. Mix well and drink it in one gulp. It's very tasty and very effective for reducing liver fat.

Fat-soluble vitamins are much higher in grass-fed pasture products and in commercial products. After all, when animals are fed grain, vitamin E is destroyed. If you are told that vitamin E can be obtained from grains, the problem is that the grains need to be ground. You wouldn’t eat unmilled grains; you would grind them, but as soon as oxygen and sunlight hit the flour, vitamin E is destroyed soon after. So, grains are not a good source of vitamin E.

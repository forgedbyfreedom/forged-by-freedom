This drink is very, very sour, but you can add a few drops of stevia or, as I do, you can add one scoop of my electrolyte powder. This way you will get potassium and magnesium and you will have more energy. So, lemon water with electrolytes or without gives energy. It contains a lot of vitamin C and also contains citrates, which help prevent the formation of kidney stones.

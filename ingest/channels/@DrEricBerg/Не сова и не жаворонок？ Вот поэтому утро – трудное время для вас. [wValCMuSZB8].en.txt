I used to be a night owl and woke up at 11 a.m. Now I'm definitely a morning person. Mornings are the best time for me when I work most creatively, when I'm most alert in the mornings. I record videos. That's how it is now, but with adrenal problems and a lot of stress, mornings are a difficult time. You have a hard time getting out of bed, maybe even have to drink a lot of coffee. And here's what's interesting about caffeine: it doesn't necessarily increase your energy levels. As such, it eliminates what makes you tired. It eliminates the factors that help you sleep. If you remove them, you'll feel very alert, but ultimately, too much caffeine burns out your adrenal glands, like I did in college. I drank coffee by the bucketful, and not just any coffee, but Cuban coffee. I burned out my adrenal glands pretty early.

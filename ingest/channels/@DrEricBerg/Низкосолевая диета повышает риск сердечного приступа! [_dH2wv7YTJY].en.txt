I recently found an interesting study about salt. A low-salt diet increases the risk of heart attack. If you eat less than a teaspoon of salt per day, the risk of heart attack increases, but the risk decreases if you increase your salt intake to two teaspoons per day. This is especially true for those on a ketogenic diet. Initially, they lose a lot of fluid, but this does not apply to those whose bodies retain fluid while on a high- carbohydrate diet. Therefore, to reduce the risk of an attack, add salt to your diet.

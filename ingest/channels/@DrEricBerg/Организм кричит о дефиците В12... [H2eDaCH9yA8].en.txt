If you have tingling or numbness in your hands or feet, or electric shock-like pain radiating throughout your body , you have a B12 deficiency.  Depression and mood disorders are also associated with B12 deficiency.  Loss of cognitive function, such as memory, concentration, and focus, are signs of B12 deficiency. B12 is found in meat, liver, eggs, fish and dairy products.  If you take a supplement, it should be methylcobalamin, not cyanocobalamin - it is synthetic.

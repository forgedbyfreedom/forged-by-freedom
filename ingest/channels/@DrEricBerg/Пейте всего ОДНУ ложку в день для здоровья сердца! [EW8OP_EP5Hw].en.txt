A tablespoon of olive oil a day can be very beneficial for your heart. Real butter has a much stronger taste and is more expensive.  Olive oil can do many things to help your cardiovascular system.  Most clinical studies have shown that it can reduce the risk of heart attack, helps cholesterol profile, and if it is out of normal range, the oil returns it to normal.  Olive oil helps the walls of blood vessels from the inside, reducing inflammation and lowering blood pressure. Plant chemicals in oleaster oil, polyphenols, may reduce the risk of stroke and, by thinning the blood, prevent blood clots.

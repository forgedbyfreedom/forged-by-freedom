The first cause of inflammation is a lack of cortisol, the main anti-inflammatory hormone in the body. What can cause a lack of cortisol? Guess what? It's due to excess cortisol. Its excess, as in the case of insulin, causes cortisol resistance, and a situation arises in which a lot of cortisol circulates in the blood due to a large amount of stress, but this cortisol is ineffective; it does n't work. Take Cushing's syndrome, which is characterized by elevated cortisol. All the symptoms actually indicate a lack of cortisol because the receptors begin to resist excess cortisol in the blood. The same thing happens with diabetes: when insulin is elevated because glucose levels are elevated, it doesn't work.

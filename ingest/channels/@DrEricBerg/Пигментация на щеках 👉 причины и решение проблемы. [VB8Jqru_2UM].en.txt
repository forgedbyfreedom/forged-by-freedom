If a person has gray or brown pigmentation on their cheeks, it's called melasma. Melasma is an estrogen problem. You're getting too much estrogen, whether it's from birth control pills, hormone replacement therapy, or large amounts of plastic in the environment and certain chemicals that mimic estrogen. In this case, you need to take something that counteracts or removes estrogen. A good remedy is Dim, an extract of cruciferous vegetables, or simply eat more cruciferous vegetables. Also, the iodine in kelp can help balance estrogen.

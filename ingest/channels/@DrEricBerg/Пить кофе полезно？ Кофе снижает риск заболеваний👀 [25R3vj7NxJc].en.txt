Many people on a ketogenic diet drink coffee and I drink coffee, but the question is whether it is beneficial or not, can coffee reduce the risk of diseases? What can it actually do? There are links to some studies below, but I will quote something: the first study of 41,000 men and 86,000 women who regularly drank coffee was not found to have any association with increased mortality in either men or women. The second study showed that coffee reduced the risk of type 2 diabetes. Next comes a meta-analysis, this is a summary of many studies. Here is what was found: drinking coffee can reduce the risk of liver, breast, kidney cancer, and even colon and rectal cancer. It is interesting that the next study (link under the video) showed that drinking coffee is not associated with the risk of stroke. In fact, a moderate reduction in the risk of stroke was found, insignificant but moderate, and in a four-year study of 386,000 coffee drinkers, no risk of developing arrhythmia was found.

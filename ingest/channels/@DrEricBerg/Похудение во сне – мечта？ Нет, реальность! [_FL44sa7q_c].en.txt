How to burn more fat while you sleep. When you sleep, you go through four different sleep cycles, from light sleep to very deep sleep. You go through these stages. Fat is burned in the deepest sleep, called delta sleep. In delta sleep, this process is activated by growth hormone. Growth hormone is the main fat-burning hormone. This hormone helps mobilize fat from fat stores and use it for energy and recovery. The question is how to prolong delta sleep to get more growth hormone. Regarding sleep, you can do a lot. First, make sure your bedroom is not hot. The cooler it is, the deeper the sleep will be and the better you will rest. Next, the room should be very dark; light should not penetrate it. Darkness stimulates melatonin.

Is it worth eating before bed? Yes, eating before bed negates growth hormone production. Think about it. Growth hormone is one of the main fat burners, the main anti-aging hormone, and eating before bed is a really bad decision. This hormone is at its peak all night long, and it slows down during the day. Its levels are quite low. Try to make dinner your last meal and make sure it contains fat. Then you won't want to eat before bed, and you'll fall asleep with the maximum possible amount of growth hormone.

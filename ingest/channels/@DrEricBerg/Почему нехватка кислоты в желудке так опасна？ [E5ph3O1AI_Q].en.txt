Another problem due to a lack of acid in the stomach is possible anemia. After all, you do not absorb iron and vitamin B12. There is another enzyme that is activated by strong stomach acid, it is called intrinsic factor. I don’t want to get into the details. Just know that with a lack of acid in the stomach, B12 is not absorbed. This causes a lot of problems. Not only anemia, but also many neurological problems that in some cases are irreversible. In addition, a lack of stomach acid can increase the risk of problems with Helicobacter pylori. This bacterium is present in the stomach of 80 percent of people, but it does not bother them. However, without acid in the stomach, this bacterium can become pathogenic and cause problems.

I want to tell you why I eat cheese every day and why you should too. This is because cheese is an excellent source of protein, it is somewhat similar to a probiotic and it contains little lactose, it is also GTO compatible. And here I do not mean processed cheese, I think it is best to eat goat cheese and sheep cheese. Sheep and goat cheese contain casein type A2, a protein that does not create many problems in the digestive system. They contain much more lactose than milk sugar. Cow's milk usually contains 3.8-4% lactose. Mozzarella cheese and cottage cheese contain somewhere between 1 and 4% lactose, while goat and sheep cheese contain only 0.5% lactose. This is so little that you will not have any problems due to the lactose content.

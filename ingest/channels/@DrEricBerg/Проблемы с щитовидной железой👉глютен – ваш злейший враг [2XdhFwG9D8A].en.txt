Hashimoto's disease is the cause of 90 percent of all hypothyroidism cases. A surprisingly small percentage of cases are caused by something else. Hashimoto's is extremely common. With autoimmune disease, antibodies attack your own thyroid gland. Here's what you absolutely need to know, and this is the most important thing: gluten. This protein in certain grains, especially wheat, can mimic something that's in the thyroid gland. This means that if you have an allergy to gluten, you will also have an allergy to your own thyroid gland, and this can lead to chronic thyroid inflammation. Therefore, if you have a problem with your thyroid gland, you need to completely give up gluten at all costs.

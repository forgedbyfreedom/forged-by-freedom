In the first exercise, which is good for beginners, you need to touch your toes, but not standing. You need to lie on the floor, lift your body and touch your toes. Here's how to do it: lie on your back, raise your legs at a right angle. Well, for complete beginners, this may not be very good, but most can do it. This exercise engages the lower abs. When you lift your upper body from the mat, pull your hands toward your toes in the top position, hold for 1 second, then slowly lower. I recommend doing one to three sets of 12-18 repetitions every other day. This is an excellent exercise for the torso.

If you wake up at two in the morning and can't fall asleep again, it means your cortisol is too high. Focus on your breathing. Make sure your inhalation and exhalation are the same length. Let it be 5 seconds, 4 seconds, or 3 seconds. The main thing is to try to achieve equal lengths of inhalation and exhalation. Focus on your breathing while you lie down and try to fall asleep again. Very soon you will begin to wake up and feel that you have slept well. Here's what I advise. If you wake up in the middle of the night, it means you need to pay attention to stress and work on it.

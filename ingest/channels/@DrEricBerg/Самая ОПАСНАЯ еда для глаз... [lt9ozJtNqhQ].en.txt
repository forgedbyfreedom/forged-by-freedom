The most dangerous food for the eyes is not salty food, fatty food, or even unprocessed meat.  This food causes problems with eye pressure.  Look at people with type 2 diabetes.  And what's wrong with their eyes?  They definitely have problems with the retina, which is part of the brain and transmits information to it. The most dangerous foods for the eyes are refined sugar and gold.  Why? Because the eyes are very sensitive to excess sugar and carbohydrates in the diet. Ultimately, they cause diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, glaucoma and cataracts.  And the most beneficial foods for the eyes are not foods fortified with synthetic vitamin A, such as cereal, or foods fortified with beta-carotene, the vitamin A recurrence agent added to orange juice. Betacarotene is an inactive form of vitamin A. Vitamin A converts to a small amount of it.  But if you eat fewer carbohydrates and more whole foods with the active form of vitamin A, such as egg yolks, beef liver, and cod liver oil, you will help your eyes, especially the retina.

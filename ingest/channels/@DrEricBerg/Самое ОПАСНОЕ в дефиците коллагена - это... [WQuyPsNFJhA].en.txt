The most dangerous thing about collagen deficiency is not muscle pain, dull skin, and brittle nails.  In 80% of cases it is fatal.  This can lead to aneurysm, especially in the aorta, the vessels that are subject to maximum pressure.  It is a common misconception that we get collagen from meat.  There is little collagen in meat, especially tender meat.  It should be obtained from chicken skin, fish skin, pork rinds, gelatin, bone broth, or as a supplement.   The most dangerous thing about collagen deficiency is the weakening of the cardiovascular system and the weakening of the integrity of blood vessels.  This is exactly why blood vessels burst.  Personally, I add collagen to my coffee every morning.  It dissolves and does not change the taste of coffee.  It also softens the texture of the coffee.

The most dangerous symptom of vitamin D deficiency is not lower back pain, depression, or high blood pressure.  Although all of these are symptoms of vitamin D deficiency, the most dangerous is a lack of vitamin D for immunity, because vitamin D helps produce T-cells, those that help destroy cancer cells and viruses.  T cells also help prevent autoimmune diseases.   A preventative dose of 10,000 IU of vitamin D every day, with vitamin K2 and magnesium.

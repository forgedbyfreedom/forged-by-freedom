The most dangerous symptom of sodium deficiency is not fatigue or cramps.  Sodium is needed to retain fluid in the body.  This means that if you drink a lot of sodium-free water, you lower your sodium levels.  Sodium is important for retaining fluid in the right parts of the body.  And if you drink too much water, your sodium levels will be low and you can become seriously dehydrated.  There will be no water outside the cells, but the cells themselves are filled with liquid.  This will cause an imbalance and fluid will begin to accumulate in the wrong places .  This can lead to headaches, nausea, confusion , cerebral swelling, seizures, and even coma.  When you exercise, sweat, and drink a lot of water, it's important to take in more electrolytes, including sea salt, to avoid sodium deficiency.

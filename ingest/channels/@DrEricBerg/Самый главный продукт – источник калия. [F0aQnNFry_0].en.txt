A few words about potassium. Potassium is important for water balance and energy. To solve insulin problems in diabetes and insulin resistance, you need a lot of potassium, about 5,000 mg every day. What foods have the most potassium? No, not bananas, there are only 300 mg. The most potassium is found in beet leaves. I highly recommend buying beets, cutting off the tops and adding them to a salad. I like to whip them up with kale, so you get a lot of potassium and feel very good. Champion beet tops contain 1,300 mg per glass. Incredibly, one avocado contains about 800-900 mg. Winter squash has a lot of potassium. Spinach has a lot of potassium. Salmon has a lot of potassium. These foods have the most potassium. Vegetable salads generally have a lot of potassium, but I specifically listed the top foods.

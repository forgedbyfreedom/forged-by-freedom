You can understand a lot about your body by its condition after a workout. Fatigue after a workout, poor sleep, the whole body constantly hurts and aches. This indicates problems with the adrenal glands or possible heart problems, and a quick recovery after a workout indicates good general condition and the health of the adrenal glands. If I suspect problems with the adrenal glands, I always ask about the reaction to exercise. A quick recovery, the pulse quickly returns to normal, these are indicators of good adrenal function. If there are problems during a workout, the pulse quickens and remains so. Slow recovery, fatigue, poor sleep, muscles continue to ache. In this case, you need to reduce training, replace them with long walks and strengthen the adrenal glands with nutrition. This will help recovery over time.

First of all, it's better to start with an amount of protein the size of your palm. If you are a large person or you are young, then eat more, but if you are older and have a slow metabolism, eat a little less. If you eat too much protein, you can start to feel sluggish. This will also slow down ketosis. The same thing happens with a lack of protein, you can feel tired because the body needs a certain amount of protein, and as for protein, the main thing is not to eat low-fat protein. Find the protein that is the most fatty; fat usually goes with protein. If you eat steaks, and I eat them often, choose the fattiest one. If you eat fish, don't take lean fish, take salmon or even sardines, both have a lot of fat and are much better than lean protein. And watch out for chicken; don't just eat the breast without the skin. Eat the parts of the chicken that have skin and fat.

The maximum amount of carbohydrates needed to enter ketosis is approximately 50 g per day.  But what does 50g of carbohydrates mean?  50g sugar, 50g digestible carbohydrates?  Is this total carbs without fiber or just 50g of total carbs?  People get a little confused about this.  I believe there should be 50g of digestible carbohydrates.  That is, the fiber must be subtracted.  But there is a huge difference between eating 50g of sugar, which is a carbohydrate, and 50g of digestible carbohydrates.  Basically, if you're a naketa and they tell you there are 50g of carbs, and you get them from an orange soda, it will be digested very quickly and there will be a sugar spike.  And this will undoubtedly slow down, or even stop, your progress.  But if you eat greens, which have a lot of fiber, and it is broken down and absorbed very slowly, then you will enter Titosis much better. So, it's not just about 50g of digestible carbohydrates, but their quality.   That 's why I even recommend not including vegetables, especially greens, in your carbohydrates.  P

Okay, this can be either a main dish or a delicious side dish. Look at the recipe and run to cook. 25 asparagus stalks. 12 slices of bacon, cut in half lengthwise. Wrap the asparagus in one or two strips of bacon. Place on a rack on a baking sheet. Wrap and lay out all the asparagus. Sprinkle with ground garlic and black pepper. Preheat the oven to 200 degrees. Bake for 10 minutes. Turn the asparagus over and bake for another 10-15 minutes.

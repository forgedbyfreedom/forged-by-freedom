The first thing that happens when switching to a low-carb diet or fasting is that you begin to lose glycogen, which is sugar stored in the liver, and along with this sugar, a lot of water is stored. So, you will immediately lose 3-4 kilograms. Don't confuse this with fat loss; it will be water, especially in the beginning. The key to overcoming a plateau or stuck state is a complete understanding of what you are doing. The main hormone that inhibits weight loss is the one that maintains the plateau. That's right, insulin is a switch. If it's high, you don't lose fat; if it's low, you burn fat. That's what we need to focus on. In other words, of all the things that can be done, the first question is, what can lower insulin the most?

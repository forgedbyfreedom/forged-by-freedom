When you're stressed, you're deprived of three important nutrients, and it has nothing to do with what you eat. Whether or not it's related to stress, stress deprives you of magnesium, this important mineral helps you sleep better and prevents cramps and arrhythmia. Zinc is very important for skin, immunity, smell, as well as testosterone levels and mood. Zinc prevents anxiety. The third nutrient that stress deprives you of is vitamin B1, an important nutrient without which you will feel anxious and stressed. It will be harder for you to fall asleep because you will constantly be thinking about something. Here's what I recommend: The more stressed you are, the healthier you are. And you need to eat more properly. Eat plenty of foods rich in magnesium, zinc, and vitamin B1.

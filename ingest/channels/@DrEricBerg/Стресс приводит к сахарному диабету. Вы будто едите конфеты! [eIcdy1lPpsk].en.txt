The adrenal hormone cortisol is a glucocorticoid. This hormone releases sugar into the blood from the muscles and liver and converts non-carbohydrates such as protein and fat into sugar. So when you are stressed, it is essentially like eating candy. Although you do not eat it with your mouth, this can increase blood sugar and you can even become diabetic due to high cortisol levels or even due to taking cortisol in the form of prednisone. I know several people who have become diabetics. This is precisely why excess glucose in the blood leads to high cholesterol, increases fat stores, and also makes the liver fatty.

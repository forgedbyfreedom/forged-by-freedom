RPI This is quite difficult, it is a very explosive exercise, it seriously activates the abdominal muscles. First, you need to stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, squat down and place your palms on the floor in front of you directly under your shoulders, jump up for push-ups, push up once, jump with your feet to the starting position, sharply lower yourself back into the squat position. You need to do from sti to 10 sets of 12-25 repetitions. But introduce this exercise gradually. It takes time to get used to it, it is quite intense.

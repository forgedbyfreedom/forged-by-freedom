To keep your hands in good condition, you need to follow two basic rules: first, avoid products that dry out the skin and accelerate its aging; forget about creams containing petroleum products; simply don't use them. By the way, I have a greenhouse, so I work with the soil all day long. I very quickly realized that you need to wear gloves. Because when you dig in the soil, there are various clays. The minerals and microbes living in it can quickly dry out your hands, so wear gloves if you work with soil, pottery, or various chemicals. In general, all these mineral oils, petroleum products, and solvents wash out fat-soluble vitamins, so they need to be replenished.

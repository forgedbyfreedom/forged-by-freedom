If your hands are dry, do not apply cream to them. Have you noticed that after applying cream, your hands are dry again after half an hour ?  Creams contain substances that dry the skin.  It is alcohol and sodium lauryl sulfate.  They take oils from the skin.  Sometimes they contain mineral oils, which rob the skin of fat-soluble vitamins and cause redness.  Sometimes they even add formaldehyde.  I wonder why there is an embalming chemical in the cream?  Acetone also dries out the skin.  Synthetic fragrances disrupt the skin's protective barrier.  Read the ingredients.  Look for a cream with quality ingredients, not something that will dry out your skin.

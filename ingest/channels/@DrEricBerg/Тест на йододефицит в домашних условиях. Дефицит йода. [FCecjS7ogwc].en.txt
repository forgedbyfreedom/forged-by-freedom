Another thing about the thyroid gland: if you have a nodular goiter, this is a typical iodine deficiency. There is a very simple way to detect iodine deficiency: apply a two-percent iodine solution (available at the pharmacy) to the inside of your shoulder or thigh with a cotton swab. Simply apply iodine to a 5-centimeter circle in the elbow or popliteal fossa and wait about an hour. If the circle disappears after an hour, you may have iodine deficiency. The iodine should not be absorbed into the skin, and a yellow spot should remain on the skin.

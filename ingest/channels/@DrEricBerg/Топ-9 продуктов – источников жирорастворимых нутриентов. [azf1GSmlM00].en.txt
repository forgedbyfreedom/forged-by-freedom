Liver and other offal are very rich in vitamins A, D, E, K, and all other fat- soluble nutrients. You don't need a lot of them, but liver should be in first place. I don't recommend eating chicken fat if it has no skin and fat. The skin is incredibly rich in fat-soluble nutrients. It contains vitamins A, D, E, and also vitamin K. By the way, chicken thighs have the most vitamin E. It's interesting that mollusks and crustaceans are very rich in fat- soluble nutrients and microelements. Cheese and cream, grass-fed butter, it contains a lot of vitamins A, D, E, K1, and definitely K2.

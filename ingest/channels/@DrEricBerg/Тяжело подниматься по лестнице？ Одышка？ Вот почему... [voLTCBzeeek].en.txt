A sign of excess stress is heaviness in the legs when climbing a hill or up stairs and shortness of breath that quickly occurs. This is a sign that the body cannot cope with stress. If you sit or lie down and then get up, you immediately start to feel very dizzy. This also means that the body cannot cope with stress. There is an excellent test called the Ragna test. If you measure your blood pressure while lying down and then while standing, then the pressure should normally increase by about six points. If it increases by 20 points or decreases, then you can understand what is happening with the adrenal glands. The reaction to stress is excessive or insufficient and the body cannot cope. Some people lose consciousness too quickly after standing up. This is a sign of a serious problem with the adrenal glands.

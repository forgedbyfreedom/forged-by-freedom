Fructose has a very low glycemic index of only 19, while glucose has a very high index of 100. So what's the harm in fructose? It's found in fruit. The thing is, fructose is a very special sugar. It's only digested in the liver. No other cells can digest fructose. Therefore, the danger of fructose is that it overloads the liver with sugar. This increases insulin resistance and can even cause diabetes.

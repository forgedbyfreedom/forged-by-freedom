Do I have enough calories during intermittent fasting? We don't need low-calorie diets. I 'll try to explain why this is so. During autophagy, proteins, fatty acids, and nutrients are reused. All of this is processed during intermittent fasting. The daily intake of nutrients is no longer as high because the body adapts and becomes very economical; it no longer wastes them. If you eat once a day, 225-250 grams of protein is quite enough, because now it is not used up at the same rate. Also, you will need much less potassium. I wouldn't worry about taking each nutrient in huge quantities if you are on intermittent fasting, because they will be processed and absorbed much better.

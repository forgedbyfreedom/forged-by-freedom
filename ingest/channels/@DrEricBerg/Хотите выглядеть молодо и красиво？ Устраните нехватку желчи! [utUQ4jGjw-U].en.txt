Bile is very, very important for looking young. It not only removes toxic waste but also extracts fat-soluble vitamins from food, and they are absolutely necessary. To look young, in this context, these are vitamins such as A, E, and omega-3 fatty acids. All these fat-soluble substances that are needed for young skin cannot be obtained without bile. So, if you have little bile for various reasons, say, you have a fatty liver or you are on a low-fat diet, and bile production is stimulated by fat, or say you had gallstones and your gallbladder was removed, a lack of bile will lead to a deficiency of fat-soluble vitamins and omega-3 fatty acids. And all of them are very necessary for looking young.

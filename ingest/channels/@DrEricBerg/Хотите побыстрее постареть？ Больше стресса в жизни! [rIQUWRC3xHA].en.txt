Stress causes two effects in the body. Firstly, it switches the body to glucose, and the body begins to use glucose as fuel, converting various tissues into glucose. So, even if you stop eating carbohydrates, you still consume them. If you are very stressed, this is one effect. The second effect is that stress causes catabolism, that is, the destruction of proteins in your skin. So, one of the ways to age is to experience a lot of stress. Therefore, you need to do everything possible to reduce stress in your environment. Unfortunately, it cannot be avoided, but you can do something to reduce it. You can actively and regularly exercise, you can fast a lot, both interval and long-term, you can do physical work, you can work on improving your sleep.

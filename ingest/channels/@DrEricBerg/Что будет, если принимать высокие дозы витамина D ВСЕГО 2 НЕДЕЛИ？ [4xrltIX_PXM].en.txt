What happens if you take high doses of vitamin D3 every day for 2 weeks?  By high doses I mean 10-20,000 international units per day.  It sounds like a lot, but it's not.  10,000 IU is a quarter of a milligram, which is 20 minutes of sun exposure.  Vitamin D is involved in the functioning of more than 2,500 genes. Therefore, vitamin D is one of the most important vitamins, especially for reducing inflammation, regulating blood sugar, reducing depression and blood pressure, and strengthening the immune system.  High doses can prevent autoimmune diseases and even put them into remission.  Always take vitamin K2 and magnesium along with vitamin D3.

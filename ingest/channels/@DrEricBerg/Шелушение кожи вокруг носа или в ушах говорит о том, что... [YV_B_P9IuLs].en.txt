What about flaky, peeling skin around your nose or ears? What's the matter? Is this a sign that you have low levels of omega-3 fatty acids and a lot of omega-6 fatty acids? The normal ratio is one to one, but few people maintain this ratio. For many people, this ratio is severely disrupted. They have a lot of omega-6 and little omega-3. You need to consume more cod liver oil and fatty fish to correct the imbalance. When they are imbalanced, a person's skin becomes rough and flaky, with dermatitis, many skin problems, skin inflammation, dry eyes, and dry hair.

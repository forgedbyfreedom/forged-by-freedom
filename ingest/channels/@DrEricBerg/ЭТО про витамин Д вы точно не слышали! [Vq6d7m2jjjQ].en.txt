This is about vitamin D. You definitely haven't heard of it. If you live in the northern United States, it's impossible to get vitamin D in the winter. You don't get vitamin D from sunlight passing through glass. You don't get vitamin D from clouds, just like you don't get vitamin D from smoke. Vitamin D is very important; it affects 2,500 different genes. And most importantly, the half-life of vitamin D, which supports immunity, is only one day. If you don't take it every day, your immunity will weaken.

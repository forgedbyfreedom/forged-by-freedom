so don't stop taking the stairs is the take-home from this a 2024 study in the Journal of preventative medicine titled Association of stair use with risk of major chronic diseases climbing stairs greater than 15 times per day was associated with a lower risk of eight of the nine outcomes analyzed than not using the stairs the conclusions were that stair use was associated with a lower risk of all cause mortality and cause specific incident of mortality independent of confounding factors including osity and multimorbidity the researchers reported that participants who climb stairs had a 24% reduced risk of dying during the study period compared to those who did not climb stairs just from all causes the stair climbers also had a 39% lower risk of dying from cardiovascular disease and a lower overall risk of developing cardiovascular disease or suffering a heart attack heart failure or having a stroke take the damn stairs

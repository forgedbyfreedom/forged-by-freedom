 When you or a loved one has a terminal illness, the wide range of challenges you face can be overwhelming. Duke Hospice offers comfort care that addresses your medical symptoms, while also caring for your social, emotional and spiritual needs. Our goal is to help you and your caregivers manage the end of life, so you can remain in your home with dignity and comfort whenever possible. Duke's expert hospice care is available to everyone in Durham and several surrounding counties, whether or not you're a Duke patient. Our inpatient hospice care facility, the Hawk Family Pavilion, is the only inpatient hospice care facility in Durham. Our hospice care services include cancer, ALS, and complex illnesses involving the heart and lungs. When extra support is needed or emergencies arise, you can feel confident knowing you're backed by our larger network of Duke hospitals and providers. Our hospice staff includes doctors, certified hospice and palliative care nurses, licensed clinical social workers, chaplains, nursing assistants, bereavement counselors, and volunteers. We offer bereavement services to everyone in our community, whether or not they've used Duke Hospice. This includes individual counseling, support groups, workshops, and Camp Relief, a grief day camp for children.

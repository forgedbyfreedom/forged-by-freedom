After it's removed from the body the ovary is frozen for a patient to use in the future. Ovarian tissue cryopreservation is offered to patients who are at an extremely high risk of not being able to conceive naturally after a plan treatment chemotherapy radiation because of the potential impact of that treatment on the ovary. It also is the only fertility preservation procedure available to patients who have not yet gone through puberty. Duke is the only institution in the state of North Carolina to offer ovarian tissue cryopreservation as a fertility preservation option. And actually one of few institutions in the Southeast to offer this service as well.

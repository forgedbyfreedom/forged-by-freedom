You and your visitors have access to several shared spaces at the Hawk Family Pavilion, such as our meditation room where you can sit, meditate, or pray. Children are always welcome here. Our playroom is fully stocked with DVDs and coloring supplies. In the family room, your family can gather to eat lunch, have a snack, eat dinner, or host a birthday celebration. Food can be delivered and leftovers can be stored in the fridge. There's also a public lounge where your family members can take a break and use the computer. For larger family gatherings, like for holiday meals, the dining room is a perfect spot. It's a warm and inviting place where families can create cherished memories. When you need a quiet moment, visit our library. We regularly have music volunteers play the piano to create a soothing and pleasant atmosphere for everyone. At the Hawk Family Pavilion, our dedicated team of caregivers includes nurses, social workers, chaplains, nursing assistants, volunteers, and counselors. We are committed to addressing your physical, emotional, and spiritual needs to make you as comfortable as possible.

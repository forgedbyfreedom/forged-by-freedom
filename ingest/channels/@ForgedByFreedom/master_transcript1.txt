Retatrutide: A Breakthrough Triple-Hormone Agonist 
Overview 
Retatrutide (LY-3437943), developed by Eli Lilly, is an investigational triple-agonist acting on 
GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors—earning it the "triple G" moniker. It’s administered via 
once-weekly injection and is currently in Phase III clinical trials, with potential approval 
projected by mid-decade.The Wall Street Journal+15Wikipedia+15PMC+15 
 
Positive Weight Loss Benefits 
• 
Huge weight reductions: In Phase II trials among obese adults, participants lost an 
average of 17.5% body weight at 24 weeks, escalating to ~24.2% by 48 weeks at the 
highest dose (12 mg)ADA Meeting News+32 Minute Medicine+3Wikipedia+3. 
• 
Girl power: Women saw potentially higher reductions—up to 28.5%, compared to 
21.2% in menVerywell Health+8The Times+8Nature+8. 
• 
Superior to current meds: Outperforms dual-agonists like tirzepatide (Mounjaro) and 
semaglutide (Wegovy/ozempic), offering both appetite suppression and enhanced 
metabolic burn.Business Insider+9The Times+9The Care Pharmacy+9 
 
How & Why It Works 
Retatrutide works via three pathways: 
1. GLP-1 receptor activation – suppresses appetite and slows gastric emptying for 
prolonged fullnessBusiness Insider+15Everlywell+15The Care Pharmacy+15PMC+1.

Other Metabolic & Clinical Benefits 
Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD/MASLD) 
• 
Dramatic reductions in liver fat—up to >80% relative reduction in participants with 
NAFLD at doses of 8 mg and 12 mg over 48 weeksThe Care Pharmacy+15ADA Meeting 
News+15New England Journal of Medicine+15. 
• 
Normalization of liver fat in over 85–90% of those treatedBusiness Insider+7ADA 
Meeting News+7gertitashkomd.com+7. 
• 
Improvements in biomarkers—reductions in K-18 (marker of liver cell death) by up to 
49.6%, and pro-C3 by up to 26.4%—suggest potential histological benefits for liver 
healthNature. 
Lipid Profile Improvements 
• 
Lowered triglycerides by >40% and improvements in VLDL and non-HDL cholesterol 
in obesity and T2D trialsNature. 
• 
Notable reductions in LDL cholesterol in obese subjectsNature. 
Glycemic Control & A1c 
• 
In participants with type 2 diabetes, A1c reductions to <6.5% were achieved by up to 
82%, and <5.7% in ~31%, using 4–12 mg doses over 36 weeksADA Meeting News.

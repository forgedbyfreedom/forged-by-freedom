Dr. Steve Horvath was among the first to create\h a test to measure someone’s epigenetic age:\h\h the Horvath Clock. One of Dr. Horvath’s more recent\h clocks — the GrimAge clock — can\h\h predict the risk of cancer, heart\h disease, and death in general.

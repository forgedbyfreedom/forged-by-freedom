caving in the chest Whenever you set up for a chest exercise it should look the same as when you set up for a back exercise rather than hunching forward keep the chest up and the shoulders back that is the position you need to do for any chest exercise often times people lift too heavy weight they're doing flies bench presses and they're caving forward and lifting like this rather than keeping their chest upright so when in doubt copy what you did for your back exercises when doing the chest and that should correct your form

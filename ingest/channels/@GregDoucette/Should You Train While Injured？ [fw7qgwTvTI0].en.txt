should you train when you're injured the answer is of course you should rather than making excuses not to train find reasons to train does that mean i'm gonna sit on my ass and do nothing no of course not there's a phenomenon known as cross education and what that means is if you train one side of the body the other side is also going to get stronger so if you're injured keep training the good side the other side will thank you later and when the injury heals you get back in the gym you will be stronger and recover faster than ever before and yeah one side's gonna look a little bit bigger than the other for a while who cares when you get back to the gym muscle memory will occur and you'll get back to your former self faster than last time

Skull Crushers contrary to the name we don't want to crush our skulls with the bar okay so typically people would do it like this and lower it to their head and press it up we don't actually want to do that we want to lower the bar behind our heads okay so if you're laying down on a bench you're not going to lower it to your head you're going to lower it behind your head okay that'll get you increased range of motion and allow you to just safely do the exercise

HOMEBREWING ANABOLICS - HARM REDUCTION SAFETY GUIDE
====================================================
Source: Compiled from community best practices for harm reduction
Topic: Sterile technique and safety for those who choose to homebrew

DISCLAIMER: This is harm reduction information for adults who have already decided to homebrew. This does not constitute medical advice or encouragement to use controlled substances.

ESSENTIAL EQUIPMENT:
- 0.22 micron syringe filters (Whatman or equivalent) - MANDATORY for sterilization
- 0.45 micron filters for pre-filtering (optional but recommended)
- Sterile vials (typically 10ml, pre-sealed)
- 18-20 gauge needles for filtering
- Glass beakers (borosilicate/pyrex)
- Magnetic stir plate and stir bar
- Digital scale accurate to 0.01g
- Graduated cylinders
- Alcohol lamp or lighter (for crimping)
- 70% isopropyl alcohol for surface sterilization
- Nitrile gloves

CARRIER OILS (most common):
- Miglyol 840 (MCT) - thin, low PIP, most popular
- Grapeseed Oil (GSO) - affordable, slight PIP potential
- Cottonseed Oil (CSO) - used in pharma products
- Sesame Oil - traditional, thicker

SOLVENTS:
- Benzyl Alcohol (BA) - bacteriostatic preservative, typically 1-2%
- Benzyl Benzoate (BB) - solvent to keep hormone in solution, typically 15-20%
- Guaiacol - for high concentration brews only, can cause PIP
- Ethyl Oleate (EO) - thinner, reduces PIP, some sensitivity issues

COMMON CONCENTRATIONS (standard safe ranges):
- Testosterone Enanthate: 250-300mg/ml
- Testosterone Cypionate: 200-250mg/ml
- Testosterone Propionate: 100mg/ml
- Trenbolone Acetate: 75-100mg/ml
- Trenbolone Enanthate: 150-200mg/ml
- Nandrolone Decanoate: 200-300mg/ml
- Masteron Propionate: 100mg/ml
- Masteron Enanthate: 200mg/ml
- Boldenone Undecylenate: 200-300mg/ml
- Primobolan Enanthate: 100-200mg/ml (difficult to hold)

BASIC PROCESS:
1. Calculate amounts using online homebrew calculator
2. Weigh raw powder accurately
3. Add powder to beaker with BA and BB
4. Heat gently (not boiling) while stirring until dissolved
5. Add carrier oil to final volume
6. Stir until homogeneous
7. Filter through 0.22 micron filter into sterile vial
8. Crimp or seal vial

CRITICAL SAFETY POINTS:
- ALWAYS use 0.22 micron filtration - this is your sterilization step
- Never skip filtering or use larger pore sizes
- Work in clen environment, minimize air exposure
- Test a small injection first for PIP/reaction
- If solution crashes (crystals form), gently reheat vial in warm water
- Label everything clearly with compound, concentration, date
- Store in cool, dark place
- If you see floaters/particles, DO NOT USE - refilter or discard

SIGNS OF CONTAMINATION (DO NOT USE):
- Cloudiness that doesn't clear with warming
- Visible particles or floaters
- Color change
- Unusual smell
- Crashed gear that won't go back into solution

PIP (post injection pain) TROUBLESHOOTING:
- High concentration = more PIP
- High BA percentage = more PIP  
- Guaiacol = more PIP
- Solution: lower concentration, reduce BA to 1%, use MCT oil

raw powder TESTING:
- Consider sending to lab (Janoshik, etc.) for verification
- Tests purity and confirms compound identity
- Especially important for first order from new source

BLOODWORK:
- Always verify your homebrew is working via bloodwork
- Test 4-6 weeks into use
- Hormone levels should correspond to dose

Remember: The safest option is pharmaceutical grade products. Homebrewing carries inherent risks. This guide is for harm reduction only.

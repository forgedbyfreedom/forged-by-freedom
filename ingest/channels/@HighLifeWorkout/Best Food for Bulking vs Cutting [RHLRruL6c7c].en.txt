Bulking versus cutting. Chipotle burrito. >> Bulky. >> Chipotle salad bowl. >> Cut it. >> Chick-fil-A chicken sandwich. >> Chick-fil-A grilled nuggets side salad. >> Subway foot long Italian BMT. >> Oh. >> Subway 6in turkey sub. >> Domino's cheesy bread. >> Poke it. >> Domino's wings. >> Cut it. Now, it's important to know that you can bulk or cut with any food. But if you're cutting, it makes sense to pick the options that are less calorically dense because you can eat more of it and be full. And if you're bulking, it makes sense to pick the options that are more calorically dense because you don't have to worry about getting too full, right? You won't have to eat as much.

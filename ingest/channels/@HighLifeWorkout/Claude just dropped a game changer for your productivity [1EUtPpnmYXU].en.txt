Claude just dropped their new Chrome extension and it is a gamecher. It basically lets you teach AI to do your work for you. Imagine an AI that lives in your browser on the sidebar. It can see exactly what's on your screen and answer your questions in real time. But the best part, it can actually take action on your behalf. You can record a workflow or any repetitive task you have to do over and over again. And it will save the whole thing as an automation and you can trigger that automation whenever you want. So, like those repetitive emails you have to send every morning, teach Claude how to do it once and now it's handled forever. This is going to allow you to stop wasting time and scale your output.

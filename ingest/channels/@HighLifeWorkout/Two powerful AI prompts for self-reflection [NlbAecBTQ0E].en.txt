This prompt has been going viral. What do you know about me that I might not know about myself based off our previous interactions? That's going to give you some crazy insight. But now, let's take it to the next level. Based off everything you know about my ambitions and my current habits, generate five uncomfortable questions that my future self should ask me 10 years from now. Focus on the gaps between who I say I want to be and what my current actions suggest I will become. That prop is going to give you a lot of insight and cause you to think about things in a way you haven't thought about them

Here's a sneaky way to go viral that nobody's talking about. Install a Chrome extension called Sorder and open up your competitor's Instagram on desktop. Use Sorder to rank their videos from most to least views. Export to CSV file to chat GPT. Use agent mode and tell it to analyze the top 10 reels. Tell it to create a spreadsheet with the reals URL, the topic, the hook, and any patterns it sees from the top performers. Now you have a list of proven topics and hooks that your target market already likes. And you can use those topics and hooks to remix the video in your own style and your own voice with your own content. You're way more likely to go viral if you're basing your content off stuff that's already proven to go viral.

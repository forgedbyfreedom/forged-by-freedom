Here are some of my favorite books that I read in 2025. I'm going to start off with Alchemy by Roy Sutherland. And one of my favorite takeaway from this book is perception is more important than reality when it comes to marketing and the customer experience. You referenced a study where they found people who were big fans of Coca-Cola and they dislike Pepsi. They hooked their heads up to some machines that measured their brain waves. And when they gave them some Pepsi and told them it was Pepsi, their brain waves kind of showed that they didn't like it so much. They gave them Pepsi again, but they told them it was Coca-Cola. And their actual brain waves started to fire off as if they were really enjoying it more. So, their perception affected them on a biological level. Next book was predictable revenue. My main takeaway from this book was to measure the actions and not the results. If you take care of the actions, the results will take care of themselves. Kind of like in a fitness context, if you track your calories every day when you're trying to lose weight and you hit the right calories every day, you won't have to even look at the scale. The scale will take care of itself. Same way in business. If you track your leads every day, you track how much outreach you do, you track how many sales calls you book, the revenue will take care of itself.

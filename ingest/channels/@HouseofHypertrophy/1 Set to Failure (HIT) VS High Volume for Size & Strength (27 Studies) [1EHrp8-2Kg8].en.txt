Within a whole muscle,\h different motor units exist,\h\h and their characteristics lie across a spectrum. Typically, a low-threshold motor unit consists of\h a small motor neuron that innervates slow-twitch\h\h muscle fibers that produce low amounts of force\h but are highly fatigue resistant. Due to this,\h\h low-threshold motor units are described as\h highly fatigue resistant but low force-producing. A high-threshold motor unit generally consists of\h a large motor neuron that innervates fast-twitch\h\h muscle fibers that produce high amounts of\h force but are highly fatigable. Therefore,\h\h high-threshold motor units are described\h as high-force producing but very fatigable.

Incline coes versus preacher coes. This paper assigned 63 previously untrained women to either train with incline coes or preacher coes. Both groups performed three sets of 8 to 12 reps to failure twice a week for 8 weeks. Elbow flexor thickness, which in this case includes both the biceps and brachiialis, was measured across three regions. Growth at the 50% region was greater for the incin cures. Growth at the 60% region was similar, while growth at the 70% region favored the preacher curls. The biceps are predominantly at the 50% region. So incline curls likely grew this area of the biceps more. As we get to the 70% region, we see more brachiialis. So although there's uncertainty, it's possible the better growth from preacher curls in this area is from the brachiialis. The research is still developing, but I'd say the evidence is leaning towards the idea that shoulder extended curls such as incline curls and beijan curls may be particularly good for the biceps, whereas there might be a potential slight benefit of preacher curls for the brachiialis. That said, it's absolutely clear that your elbow flexor muscles will certainly grow well with any curling exercise, provided you're training hard. So, if you'd like to keep things simple, just select whatever curl you prefer.

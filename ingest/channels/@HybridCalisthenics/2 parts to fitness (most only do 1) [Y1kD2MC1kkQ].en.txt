There are actually two parts of fitness. You have to do the fitness and you have to learn the fitness. And most of us only do the first one. And that may cause issues because we jump into a routine, push hard for a bit, and then something happens, life gets busy, and we stop making progress. And then we quit because not making progress isn't very fun. And learning fitness is the part where you understand what exercises to do, when to do them, and what to change if progress stops. And just about everyone needs some fitness to thrive. So, it can be worth learning. Have a beautiful day.

If you're going to bed earlier but still having trouble sleeping, here are three daytime habits that might be beating up your sleep. And here's what you can try instead. Number one is caffeine too late. Coffee or energy drinks in the afternoon can still be in your system at bedtime. Try cutting off your caffeine at least 10 to 12 hours before bed. Number two is never really using your body. Look, I get that some of you work very hard, but your work might leave you more mentally tired and screen tired than physically tired. So without more physical movement during the day, your sleep may be suffering. So try doing at least one thing safely during the day that requires focused physical exertion. And number three is just being in the dark. I get that it's easier said than done, but if you're inside all day, your body might feel confused as to when to feel tired. Try to get more minutes of light earlier in your day. A good night's sleep can be built during your day. Have a beautiful night.

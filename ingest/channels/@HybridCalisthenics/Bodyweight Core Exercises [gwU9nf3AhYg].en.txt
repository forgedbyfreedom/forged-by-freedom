hello my friends here are some bodyweight ab and core workouts you can do at home two main principles first people seem to love things like mountain climbers and crunches they can usually do a lot and really feel the burn and that's fine but that burn isn't necessarily burning your stomach fat or toning your abs if you want to tone your abs make them strong and muscular with harder exercises like the clutch flag or the human flag and then dye it off fat to reveal definition and second people seem to want to do a thousand different exercises to hit their core from different angles but your midsection was designed to work powerfully together as part of a chain so you might as well train it that way those are two reasons why i prefer leg raises of course you can build up to them first do them with your knees bent and then do them with your knees slightly bent and then bend on the way up and straighten on the way down hit the goals before you move on then hang from a bar or a tree and then do the same progression from there do your best to minimize swinging eventually work on touching your toes to the bar exhale on the way up and inhale on the way down keep your stomach sucked in during all exercises to work your transverses hope this gets you started have a beautiful day

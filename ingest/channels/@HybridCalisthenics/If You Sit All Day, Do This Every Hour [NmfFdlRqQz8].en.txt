My friend, if you spend a lot of your day sitting, one of the best exercises you can do only takes about a minute. Instead of trying to undo eight hours of sitting with a bunch of stretches later, we can just take some breaks from the sitting itself. Try setting a timer for every hour or so, and when it goes off, stand up, walk around, maybe shake off a bit, or do some push-ups. This can sometimes be a good way to get your exercise sets in. These small breaks can sometimes work better than the emergency stretches we try to do later after something's wrong. And it doesn't have to be a full workout, just a little bit less non-stop sitting and a little bit more movement. Have a beautiful day.

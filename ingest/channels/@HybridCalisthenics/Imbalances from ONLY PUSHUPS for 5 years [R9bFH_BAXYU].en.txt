I did only push-ups for like 5 years and the imbalance from that wasn't nearly as big as you might think. And while I do think it's good to have a somewhat balanced routine, just about everyone's going to have a slightly different ratio between push, pull, legs, rotation. Now, is there a limit for how big that ratio can be before there is an issue? Possibly. But it's probably okay if you're a little better at one over the other. Now, you might get some tightness and immobility over time, and you can and should try to improve that. But I have a pretty balanced routine nowadays, and I still have some tightness in my front and my back, so I guess I have balance tightness. We're getting stronger at either push or pull is usually a good thing. Have a beautiful day.

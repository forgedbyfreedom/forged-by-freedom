hey if you're a few weeks into your New Year's Fitness resolutions here are three ways you can keep going without giving up first keep it simple people sometimes give up because they have way too many exercises and it becomes harder to be consistent when you get busy you can work your whole body with like five to six compound exercises just add weight or do harder variations as you get stronger and if you do these consistently for a few weeks then you can add exercises back in if you want and second you can make it convenient for yourself to regularly work out if you want to go to a gym that's great but make sure it's realistic for you to do that consistently maybe have a home workout plan it's probably better to learn your limits and change your workout plan rather than not have one at all and finally you can learn about what you're doing and why and this is commonly misunderstood but learning about how the exercises and nutrition affect your body can help you better understand your progress and stay motivated if you're trying to get stronger with push-ups maybe spend like 5 minutes before or after your workout watching videos on how to do better push-ups those are just some ideas have a beautiful day

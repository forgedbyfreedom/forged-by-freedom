when most people talk about going to failure they usually mean form failure or not being able to do another rep but your muscles probably still have a little more juice for example if you're benching 315 and can't do another rep you could probably do a few more reps at 225. barbells can be hard to adjust in the middle of your set but with bodyweight exercise we can sometimes transition into an easier variation at the end of our set doing this easier variation allows us to squeeze out a few more reps to push ourselves deeper fatigue when we feel like it and this deeper fatigue is correlated with greater muscle growth and greater strength just something to try out have a beautiful day my friend

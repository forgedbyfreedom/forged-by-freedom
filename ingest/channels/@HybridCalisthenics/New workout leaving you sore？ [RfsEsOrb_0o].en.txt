If you just started working out for the first time in a while and you feel very sore, I have good news and bad news. The good news is that you won't always be the sore. As your body gets used to the workouts and you become more fit, you will be less sore after your workout. And the kind of bad news is that soreness isn't necessarily making you any leaner or stronger. So, if you're very sore and it's taking some time to heal, then the sets and reps you do might not be as much as you could do. So, for your first few weeks, you don't have to go 100%. You could go 50 to 70% and increase that as you get used to the exercise. Some gentle movement like walking and getting plenty of rest can also help you heal from your soreness. And bonus, as you get more fit, then you can train more often without being excessively sore. And that can help you make some faster progress. Have a beautiful day.

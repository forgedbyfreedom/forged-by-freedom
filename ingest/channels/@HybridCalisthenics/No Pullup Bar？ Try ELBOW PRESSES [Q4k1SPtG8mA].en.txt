if you want to train your back at home my friend but you don't have a pull-up bar you can try a little-known exercise called the elbow press simply lay on your back and drive your elbows into the ground at first you may only be able to move your upper body by a few inches but eventually you can work your way up to pressing your entire body you can use blocks to make this a little bit harder or a bit easier by doing this upright on a table assisting a bit with your legs you can flare out your elbows a little bit but don't flare too much or becomes almost impossible the exercise seems to be easiest with your elbows tucked in but if at all possible get a pull a bar or some gym rings they're pretty handy have a wonderful beautiful day

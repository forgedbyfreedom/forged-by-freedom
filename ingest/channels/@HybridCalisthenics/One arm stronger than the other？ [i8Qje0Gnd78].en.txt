if one side of your body is weaker than the other try unilateral exercises simply work one side at a time and just work the weaker side first this doesn't have to be with difficult exercises for example you could do one arm incline push-ups and you could do one arm pulls or heavily assisted one leg squats for extra balance only do as many as you can on your strong side as you can competently do on your weak side or just throw in some extra work for your weak side before you do your main sets hope that helps you out

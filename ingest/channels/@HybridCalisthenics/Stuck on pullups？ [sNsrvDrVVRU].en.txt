There seems to be a trend recently, especially with pull-ups, where people do a few reps every minute or every hour and then they rest to stay fresh and they wait a little bit and recover and they jump up and they do a few more. If that's working for you, that is great. Please keep doing it. But a lot of people tend to get stuck and they message me. What I would recommend is spend a few weeks going back to just two or three hard sets a few times a week, doing as many as you safely can. And this can sometimes jumpst start your progress, especially if you've been stuck for a little while. Because doing a few reps and stay fresh kind of training can be great for neural benefit and other benefit. But after you've upgraded your software, you may also want to spend some time building up your hardware again because doing a few hard sets at a weight or progression that challenges you can help build up your muscles and joints if that's what you need.

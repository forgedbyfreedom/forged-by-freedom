so about a year ago I was dealing with some knee pain which is pretty unusual for me and it didn't feel very serious but it also wasn't really going away by itself and something that made it feel a lot better was doing deep isometric zercher squats held for time now interestingly doing them at a high angle with higher weight actually made the pain a bit worse but doing them at a deep angle maybe not so deep that my thighs were touching with the weight that I could very comfortably hold for about 3 sets of 30 seconds not only made my knee feel much better afterwards but it made the pain go away within a few sessions now this won't work for all types of knee pain so talk to your physical therapist but it did help me so I wanted to share have a beautiful day

Back when I was a kid, like many young guys, I found fighting enjoyable. I couldn't explain why, but the idea of punching, pushing, and wrestling with my friends just seemed really fun to me as a young boy. As an adult, it's not as fun. While movies and popular media make it seem really powerful and macho to punch someone in the face to shut them up, not everyone fights fair. In real life, you could easily be shot, stabbed, seriously injured, sent to prison, or worse, expelled. No, but seriously, you could die. Of course, you want to be able to defend yourself and your loved ones if you have to. But if at all possible, just walk away, my friend. Have a beautiful day.

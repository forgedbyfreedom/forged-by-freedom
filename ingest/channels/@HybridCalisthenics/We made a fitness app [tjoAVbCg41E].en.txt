Hello friends. After a lot of work, we made an app version of the hybrid fitness routine. This is an ambitious long-term project to help anybody get healthy, fit, and strong right from home. It's a fairly simple beta app at the moment, but you log in, watch the walkthrough, and it tells you what exercises to do, when to do them, when to rest, and when you're ready to move on to harder exercises based on your feedback. Or you can just use the app as a reference if you're already familiar with the hybrid fitness routine. It's available globally in the Play Store and the App Store, and you can learn more at hybridcalisnics.com/app. This is just a foundation, but I'm super excited to keep building it and improving it. I still hope it can help a lot of people in the meantime, and I'll keep letting you know as new features become available.

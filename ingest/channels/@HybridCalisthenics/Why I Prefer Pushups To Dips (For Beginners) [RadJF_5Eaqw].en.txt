I usually prefer push-ups over dips for beginners only because I found dips to be a bit harder on beginner shoulders. They tend to put your shoulder in a deeper stretch, which can be a good thing, but if you're not used to the movement, can make you quite sore for a few days, which can make it a little bit harder to be consistent starting out. Push-ups usually don't stretch your shoulder as much. Forward pushing can seem a bit more intuitive for some people, and they require slightly less equipment to get started. That said, if you build up properly with easier progressions, both push-ups and dips can be great exercises. And if you adapt to something that used to be hard for you, then you've gotten stronger.

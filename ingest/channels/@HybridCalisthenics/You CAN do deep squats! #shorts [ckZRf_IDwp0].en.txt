my friend if you can't do deep body weight squats in a controlled manner and without pain try this out almost everyone has the potential to do these and if you build up to them progressively they'll strengthen and protect your joints not hurt them they're also useful if you get tired and need a nice shelf for your coffee so we'll start with something we can do and we'll work our way up as we get stronger you can start off by lying down and squatting with a resistance band this might be one of the easiest versions of squats only all matched by diddly squats that's where you do nothing it's a very popular variation next you can find something around knee height and then you can squat with some assistance from your arms try to keep your heels on the ground as you do this next you can find something around hip height then you can assist by pulling at the bottom rather than pushing if your joints hurt from this you can try assisting a little bit more next you can work on some half squats if half is too low then try third squats go lower and lower over time as you get stronger until you can finally do a full range body weight squat congratulations high five remember to always follow your half sets with an assisted full set and do some calf raises on a stair step to help your ankle mobility have a beautiful day my friend

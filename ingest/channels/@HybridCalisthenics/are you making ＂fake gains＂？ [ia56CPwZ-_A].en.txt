You seem to make a lot of progress very quickly when you change exercises. But that may not always be good. You might know from experience that when you try a new exercise, you might get better very quickly and your numbers will go up and you'll feel stronger and you'll be like, "Wow, I'm awesome." And I agree, you are awesome. But part of your new found awesomeness might be from your nervous system getting better at the skill of the movement rather than just your body getting stronger. This can be a very useful feature for things like learning to pick things up in different ways because we all pick things up, right? But on the other hand, it can take a few weeks or more to really adapt to movement and get the most out of it. And here's the nuance. It is okay and probably good to learn new skills, but if you're always changing, it might be harder to make long-term progress. So, you may need some balance.

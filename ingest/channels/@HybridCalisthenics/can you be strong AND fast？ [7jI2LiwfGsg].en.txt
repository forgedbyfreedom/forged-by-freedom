The muscles that help you go fast are similar to the muscles that help you move heavy, but you may have to train speed a little bit differently. Even after you have the muscles and body for speed and power, you still have to train your nervous system to use it well, which you can do through practice. But if you want to jump higher or sprint faster, then practice a few sets of those after a warm-up but before your heavy workouts. That way, you're fresh and more likely to hit new maxes.

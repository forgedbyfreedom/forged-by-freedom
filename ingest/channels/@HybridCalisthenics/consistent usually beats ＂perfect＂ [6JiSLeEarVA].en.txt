Having a consistent routine is more important than having a perfect routine. People show me their fitness routines a lot and it's pretty rare for me to see one that I think will actively harm them in some way. Usually, you can say that it'll still get you to where you want to go, but it might not get you there as fast as it could. So, I find that pretty comforting to know that even if your fitness routine isn't perfect, it can still help you improve over time if you do it consistently and you can improve the routine itself over time, which is what most people do anyway.

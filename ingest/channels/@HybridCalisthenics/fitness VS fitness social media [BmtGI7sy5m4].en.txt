There's a funny difference between fitness social media and fitness because on social media I like watching people explore new interesting things like a new exercise or training methodology or product and I like filming that and sharing with all of you. I think it's really cool. But good fitness practices on the other hand are by comparison quite boring. I think like the strongest people I know don't have some kind of secret routine or exercise. They just do the basics really well. They train intensely. They train consistently and they train progressively. They have their macros dialed in and they get plenty of rest and that's about it despite what all the titles and thumbnails make it seem like. Now, fitness knowledge has evolved some over the years and help people and help some athletes achieve new records, which is awesome. But I feel like the actual fundamentals have grown about as much as I have from ages 12 to age 32, which is a little bit, but not a whole lot cuz I feel like I went steadily to 5'8 and then I just kind of floated there. So, if the grind is feeling boring for you, maybe you're on the right path. Have a beautiful day.

The other day someone told me that fitness is an exponential game and how do you unlock those exponential gains? And I'm like, it really isn't. If anything, fitness is logarithmic with a few exceptions, and here they are. So, if fitness were exponential, then our progress would get faster and better over time. But it's not. If anything, our progress slows down as we get more advanced, which is actually a good thing for many people because you get most of the benefit near the beginning and you don't have to put in the top 1% athlete grinds at work to get the vast majority of fitness benefits. That is good news for you. But there are times where you can make your progress go a lot faster through unlocks as you learn more and fix your mistakes. Like, oh, I was not getting enough protein for the past 5 years. That's why I wasn't growing. Or, oh, my hands were a little bit too narrow during my press movement, so I wasn't working my chest as much. And a big one for slightly more advanced people is maybe I need to periodize. Just about everybody will go through this because learning is a lifelong journey. And yes, your progress probably will slow down over time, but it will not stop because as you continue learning, you can continue improving, which can be very nice for you and for others if you share with them what you've learned.

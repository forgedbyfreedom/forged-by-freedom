you probably already know this but the longer you try to hold a weight the less weight you're going to be able to hold and here's why that matters and because you can hold much more weight than you can lift a lot of people like short intense holds with heavy weight like 1 to 6 seconds but the ideal length depends on your goals and if you're going to do the short intense holds I would start either at your starting length so you can really explode up or at your sticking point because it's usually where your weakest but I now spend the majority of my time on longer holds like 30 seconds because while I can't use quite as much weight here the strength I gain in this range seems a lot more stable in my control and transfers well in my daily activities by way I alternate between 6 and 30 second holds and motionally through bottom and middle positions sometimes STP so the lesson I took is you don't have to use your highest weight to train useful strength have a beautiful day

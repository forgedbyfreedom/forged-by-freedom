I think it would surprise some people to hear me say this, but while we are all different in some ways when it comes to fitness, we probably have way more similarities than differences. And that is a good thing because if it were the other way around, research would not work if we were all entirely different when it comes to fitness because if they studied a 100 people and you weren't one of those hundred people, then it may not apply to you. Thankfully, there's usually at least some crossover. And the reason this can help us is because whatever problems or goals we might have when it comes to fitness, it probably has already been studied or solved in some way. Now, if you're a beginner, I would just say find whatever can work and just get started because consistent is more important than perfect. But if and when you get stuck and you're like, man, I'm not making any more progress, which is pretty common, by the way. I find it very reassuring to know that whatever is keeping us stuck, there's usually a way to get unstuck if we look it up or ask someone who knows and we're like, "Oh, we're just not getting enough volume." And once we fix that, then we can start making progress

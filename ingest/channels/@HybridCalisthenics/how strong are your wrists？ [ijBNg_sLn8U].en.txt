If you have wrist pain when you're exercising, check this out. Sometimes you are more than strong enough to do an exercise, but bending your wrist a significant degree can cause pain. So, a simple workaround to simply not bend your wrist much. Or you can combine that with some simple wrist wraps like so. They add some extra stability. But the tendons and ligaments in your wrist are pretty small and easily injured. So, afterwards, you might want to spend some time making them stronger. The first step is a light warm-up for maybe a minute or so to stretch it out, avoid pain, try to explore different angles. But afterwards, pick one of those positions, lightly stretch, and press and hold as hard as you safely can for about 30 seconds. Avoid sharp pain, which may help prepare you for higher forces.

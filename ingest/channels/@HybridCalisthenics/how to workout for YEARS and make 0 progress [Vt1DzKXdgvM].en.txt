Many people struggle to make long-term fitness progress until they understand this simple concept. Most people believe that if you just work out consistently, you'll be able to do more reps and or lift more weight over time. But scary thought, it is possible to work out for years and years and years and not make any meaningful progress. But the good news is you can avoid that if you know how. Now, progress does take time, but if you haven't made any progress in months, it probably isn't just your genetics, and there may be an opportunity for you to improve. Find the right exercises that you can do comfortably and without pain. Do a few sets of as many as you safely can a few times a week. A good routine helps a lot with that. And if you can't improve with that over time, you may want to take a look at what you're eating. As you get stronger, you might require a bit more calories and a bit more protein. And lastly, you want to recover enough to support that, which means getting enough good sleep and getting enough recovery time between workouts. It may take some time to find these, but this is a learning process that can help you for the rest of your life. Have a beautiful day.

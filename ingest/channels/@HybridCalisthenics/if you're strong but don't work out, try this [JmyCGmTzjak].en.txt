If you are someone who's very strong and you don't even work out, maybe you just work and you do nothing else, then I would encourage you to find some exercises to work your muscles through a full range of motion, even if you're capable of much more. Because even coming from someone who loves isometrics, you do get some pretty cool benefits of having a relatively full range of motion, including joint health, mobility, and even possibly injury protection. And if you don't want to go to a gym, you can start with some push-ups, pull-ups, and squats at home. Have a beautiful day.

So, someone might change from squats to lunges for a few months, for example. And this can give good generalist benefits for the rest of your life, especially because there's so many body weight skills to learn. But if you want to get good at specific things, it makes less sense to just stop that for a few months. So, it's more common to adjust the sets and reps. It doesn't have to be huge.

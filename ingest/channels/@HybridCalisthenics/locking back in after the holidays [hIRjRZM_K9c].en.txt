I saw someone say today that he had trouble locking back in after the holidays. And to be honest, I haven't struggled with that at all. And here are two main reasons why I think that might be. Plus, a quick hack at the end that may help you. First is because I didn't really stop doing things over the holidays because it does take some time to restart a routine. And my life improved a lot when I realized that my peak most enjoyable state was not being idle or resting. But after I get enough rest, I like being active and doing things throughout the day, whether it's work or trying something new. And second is because I think of my focus as a finite resource that I cultivate with my daily routine. I can choose to focus in a similar way to how I can choose to sleep. Like I can say I'm going to sleep right now, but what I've done throughout my day will greatly impact how successful or effective that sleep is. And in a similar way, while I consider myself a determined person, if I wake up and just doom scroll for 3 hours before I do anything else, I may find it a bit harder to do things throughout the day. So my quick hack is just to reduce the amount of time I spend on my phone in the first hour after waking up. And it's pretty much always a good day to restart some good habits. Have a beautiful day.

My friend and I are both trying to improve our sleep. And we found that not all sleep is equal. And you may have already felt this. For example, I could sleep for 9 hours on Monday and still wake up a bit groggy and tired and sleep for only 7 hours on Tuesday and wake up much more alert and refreshed throughout the day, even though I got 2 hours less sleep that day. And it's not just about waking up at the right time in our sleep cycle. But what we do before bed can actually affect how restful our sleep is. For example, if I have an intense workout and eat a large meal right before sleeping, I might feel really tired and fall asleep right away. But because my body has to digest that large meal as well as sleep repair stuff, I might not recover as much that night. Now, everyone is different, of course, but there are some things that usually help us. If possible, consider setting an alarm a few hours before bed to remind you to wind down. And after that point, you can reduce exertion, large meals, potentially stressful things, and screen time. I know it's hard, but sometimes I try listening to videos instead of watching. And look, I know a lot of it sounds really boring, but a little bit of better sleep can go a long way towards helping my mood and focus.

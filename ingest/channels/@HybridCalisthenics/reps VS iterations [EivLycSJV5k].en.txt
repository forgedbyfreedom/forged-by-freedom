Most people think of their workout in repetitions or reps. But they may want to think of their workout in iterations or its I guess. See, repetition seems to have the implication that you're doing the same thing over and over again. But especially early on, one of the primary ways you get better is by trying to improve something each time as you learn. Like, hey, maybe your hands are a bit too close together, or maybe you weren't bringing your shoulders down enough at the beginning of the movement, or maybe thumb over felt better at first, but then you change the thumb under to better suit your goals. Or just trying underhand instead of overhand to see which one you want to learn first. And yes, some people already do this, but iteration seems to have the mindset of improving over time, which is what we can do with our technique until we find one that works for us.

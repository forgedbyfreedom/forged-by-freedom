If you have trouble sticking to workouts, you may not have a discipline problem. You may have a sleep problem. Sleep is kind of the foundation that makes everything else easier. When we're underslept, it's harder to make good choices or work out because we feel less willpower. And how well you sleep is heavily influenced by what you do during the day before you're in bed wishing you were tired. And for me, doing at least one hard, effortful thing during the day makes me feel a good kind of drained where my body wants to sleep so I don't have to try to force it. Of course, good sleep is easier said than done, but I think it can be worth taking the journey.

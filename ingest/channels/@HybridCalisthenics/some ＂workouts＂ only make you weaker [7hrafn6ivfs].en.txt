It took me a while to realize that not all workouts will make you stronger. And in fact, some might make you weaker. If you're cold, not very warmed up, or just extra stiff that day, then more damage is likely to happen during your sets. This may also happen if you use weights that you're not ready for. And if you do this, especially with new exercises, then you're more likely to feel sore and perhaps even a bit painful the next day. But wait, isn't muscle damage how we get stronger? Not necessarily. See, muscle damage does happen during most workouts, but mechanical tension is considered a bigger driver for strength and growth. And something like jogging is a good example of an exercise that causes a decent amount of muscle damage and can be good, but isn't really considered a strengthened muscle exercise. But it's not really something I would stress over. And sometimes the only difference between a movement that weakens you and one that strengthens you is just having a good warm-up beforehand.

Someone gave us a bunch of candy for Halloween. I was looking at the pile and it made me think I just don't really eat candy anymore. I have no weight loss goals and the marginal harm of eating one candy is probably not that much. But nowadays, when I want something sweet, I just tend to have fruit like berries and stuff. I don't think this one's a berry, though. And yes, you could make an argument that fruits had vitamins and stuff. But I've learned that some of my choices are not just for health, but for personal preference. Like, I know some people like it, but I just don't really eat at McDonald's. I just never think about it when I'm considering food. But other fast food pizza or like Taco Bell, for some reason, I'm slightly more open. I haven't had any in months, but why am I more open to it? I don't know. Is it healthier? Probably not. So, it's fine to have preferences, of course, but I found it handy to know which ones might just be preferences. And you can probably have my candy if you want it.

I used to not want to do hard things that were hard for me but easy for other people because I might be doing it the hard way and everyone else is doing it the proper way. But what I found is that unless you have someone guiding you through exactly what to do, which is pretty rare because we have different circumstances, sometimes the way to find the easy way is to go through a bunch of hard ways. So it might help for you to think of your current season as learning. And not only that, but the ability to sit there and do something a bit inefficiently, but knowing that it might get you to a better path is one of the most useful skills that I found in my life.

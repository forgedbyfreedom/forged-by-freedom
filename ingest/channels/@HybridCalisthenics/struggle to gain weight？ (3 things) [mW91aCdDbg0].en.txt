Here are three things that helped me put on 55 lbs as someone who once struggled to gain weight without force-feeding myself. First, a small consistent surplus. I used to think I had a fast metabolism, so I'd have to eat a ton of food to do that. But if you've been pretty much the same weight for a while, then you're already maintaining, which means you usually don't need a huge change in order to start gaining weight. Just 200, 300 calories a day might be enough. That could be a simple protein shake. Two, your stomach should not have to hurt in order for you to gain weight. If you stuff yourself until you feel sick, you sometimes end up eating less the next day anyway. Focus on nutrient-dense foods, especially drinks like some milks. And lastly, most people don't just want more weight, they want more muscle. So try to do some lifting or calisthenics and get plenty of sleep. That way, your body can better build something with the extra energy it's getting. Small steps can lead to big changes. Have a beautiful day.

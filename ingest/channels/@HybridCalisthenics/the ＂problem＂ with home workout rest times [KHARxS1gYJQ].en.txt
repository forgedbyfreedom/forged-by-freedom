I've changed how I rest between sets and here's why. There is some research that shows the best times to be like two, three, five minutes depending on what you read and what your goals are. Because if your goal is just to feel out of breath and do a lot of movement, then you would have no rest times and you would just do a super set. But that obviously isn't what everyone wants. And what's more, I don't really know that many advanced athletes sitting there with a stopwatch trying to get the exact rest time. So the rule of thumb is usually just to rest long enough to be able to give the next set your best shot, but not rest so long that you're no longer warmed up. But there might be an issue with that. I have noticed that a lot of home workout people tend to get distracted between sets, maybe on their phone. And I wouldn't say it hurts progress so much as it makes you feel like your workout is taking longer than it actually does, which makes some people less likely to stay consistent.

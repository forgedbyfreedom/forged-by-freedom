An underused human movement that might help a lot of people is simply our ability to look backwards. Before I had a car, I used to ride my bike everywhere and my neck would feel a lot better. Not because of anything to do with the pedals, but because I had no mirrors, so I had to look around more to see where I was going. And even active people who like fitness don't necessarily move their necks that much, except to sometimes look at screens. And we wonder why our neck feels stiff afterwards. A super simple exercise you can do is just look side to side with just your neck each day slowly about 15 reps each side. Warming up a bit beforehand with your shoulders can actually help. Then you can just do a hold for about 30 seconds each side. You can breathe deeply or normally throughout the hold. You don't have to turn too much. And again, this shouldn't be painful at all. Then you can do the same with your torso. This is a potato. And this fuller movement will help you look actually behind you. And again, you can do these movements for a few reps. And of course, some of you do these already as twist holds. It can be a very gentle hold. And I still like to do around 30 seconds each side. I do these on days where I sit a lot and they just take about 5 minutes. Have a beautiful day.

I think sometimes we assume we're terrible at something when we may have just never been shown how to improve. Like I think about all the people that went most of their lives not being able to do a push-up or pull-up until they were shown a wall push-up or a horizontal pull-up. And what I find really cool is that some of those people actually went on to do a lot more because they realized that they were quite athletic. They just didn't know how to get started. So imagine if I said, "I am terrible at driving and finding things until someone goes, "Have you tried using a GPS or a map?" and I go, "Oh, I'm actually all right at this." And of course, some of us are talented at different things, but I find it inspiring to remember that we might be capable of more than we realize when we're shown how to grow. Have a beautiful day.

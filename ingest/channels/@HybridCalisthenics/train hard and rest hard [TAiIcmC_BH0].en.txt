Some people train as often as they can in order to maximize progress, but they might be missing out on part of the equation. You see, a lot of people realize early on that if you train the same exercise every day, your progress might eventually slow down because your muscles need more time to recover. As a result, they might do that exercise every other day or every 48 hours. But what you do during that 48 hours also can matter a lot. And believe me, I know that life can get in the way. But a lot of people would bulk at the idea of only going 50% during a set rather than their usual 90 to 95% because they might miss out on some stimulus. But that same person might be slightly more likely to miss 2 or 3 hours of sleep because they're working or playing or they might not be getting enough nutrition during those two days. And as a result, the growth might be slower or less effective. It is easier said than done, but if we're going to train hard, we may also want to rest hard. Have a beautiful day.

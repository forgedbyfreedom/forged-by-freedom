There are two types of hard in fitness. One you can embrace and one you can try to reduce. Number one is just the grind of doing the workout of doing as many reps in a set as you safely can. That can be pretty hard sometimes. And number two is just everything else related to getting fit. Waking up early, making time, driving to the gym. That can also be pretty hard. And I think we can learn to embrace the first hard, but try to reduce the second hard. Mental discipline can be great, but I think the easier we make it on ourselves to work on our fitness, the more likely it is that we will do so and get fit. And that can be nice. Have a beautiful day.

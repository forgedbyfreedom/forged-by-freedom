But there is something called the cross- education or cross trainining effect where basically your untrained side gets a bit stronger, too. Sometimes like a third of the increase of the side that you actually train, but you still probably train both sides because those benefits may be neural. So, your software is learning to work more efficiently, but your hardware may not change that much unless you train it. But while balance is good, it's okay to be stronger on one side than the other or stronger at like push versus pull. And in fact, it's pretty common.

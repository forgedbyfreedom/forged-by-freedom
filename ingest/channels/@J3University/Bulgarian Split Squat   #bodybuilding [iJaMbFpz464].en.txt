the Bulgarian split squat is it quad focused or is it glue focused the answer is depends we can change the goal of a Bulgarian split squat depending on how someone executes it and the positions we put them in at the bottom if we want it to be glute focused we can have a little bit of a lean sit back into it with a vertical Shin angle and get a lot more out of it from a glute perspective if we want it to be more quad with a more vertical torso position and more knee flexion over the toe we'll get a little bit more quad out of it as we descend and get a lot of dor deflexion out of it we can make it even more quad focused if we put a heel elevation here in order to allow us to get into more DS of flexions for people who struggle with it so the answer for whether it's one or the other is not delineated just on the movement itself but in how you execute it there will be some stimulus for both glute and quad the one you bias is based on how you execute it which is why training footage for your coach is so important as a part of the check-in process

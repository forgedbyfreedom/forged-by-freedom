if you want to be a better Coach managing your time is extremely important and the best way to do this is with a block schedule as a coach we have multiple tasks throughout the day and these require us to be a little bit of a jack of all trades in order to keep our attention on the task at hand it's better if we have time blocks that have specificity for what we're working on work on those tasks only during that time block and then move on when that time block is over and this is going to apply for every task across the day so take this block schedule and use it to manage your time more efficiently

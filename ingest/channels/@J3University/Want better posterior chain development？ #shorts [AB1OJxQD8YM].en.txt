you want better posterior chain development you're probably going to have to be hinging the question is does it matter whether I'm using dumbbells or whether I'm using barbells the primary limitation with dumbbells is often loading for your stronger athletes a lot of gyms don't have dumbbells that go high enough for them to be able to progress hinging over time but the advantage to dumbbells is that we can actually control how far away from the Torso that the loading is happening where with a barbell some people tend to start to drift it and this is where we start to see some lower back injuries so for people who are still learning to brace hinging dumbbells are a great tool to help them feel more comfortable in the bottom of a hinge pattern both are extremely viable and very useful across multiple client cases just pick the one the athlete gets the most out of run it to death and then find a new way to progress that pattern with a different program structure

This diagnostic accuracy study evaluated the performance of an integrated AI ultrasound tool for estimating fetal gestational age. The tool was designed for use by providers with no prior training in sonography. After entering the participant ID and visit number, the novice sonographer measures the distance between the pubic symphysis and uterine fundus. This measurement is entered into the tablet, which allows the software to set the depth and gain settings on the ultrasound probe. It also assigns the number of sweeps to be collected. Based on her measured fundal height, this patient will receive five sweeps in the craniocaudal, or up and down, direction. And four sweeps in the lateral or side to side direction. After applying ultrasound gel to the abdomen, the novice sonographer uses the probe to evenly disperse the gel. Next, she performs the sweeps. The sweeps are 10 seconds in length. They are processed as CineLoop videos by the software. There are no actual ultrasound images displayed to the user, only an animation to guide probe movement across the maternal abdomen. The AI model runs in the background as the ultrasound video is being collected. The result is available instantaneously.

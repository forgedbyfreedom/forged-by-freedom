Can AI help clinicians diagnose Parkinson disease using MRI scans like these? A study recently published in Jamma Neurology evaluated an AI imaging tool designed to differentiate between Parkinson disease, multiple system atrophy, and progressive super nuclear palsy. All three fall under the Parkinsonism umbrella, but can have different causes and respond better to different treatments. Getting a proper diagnosis early is critical, but studies have shown between 25 and 50% of patients have the wrong diagnosis in early stages of disease. In the study, which included a perspective and a retrospective cohort, researchers used an AI tool to evaluate scans from 21 sites and three MRI machines. The tool was over 96% accurate. With this, the future holds great potential for imaging AI as an additional supportive step in aiding with the accurate diagnosis of common neurodeenerative parkinsonian disorders.

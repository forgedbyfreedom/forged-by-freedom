>> This bar plot represents the relative variable importance extracted from the classification tree grown using complications as an outcome and 10 variables associated with them as covariants. It can be interpreted as a ranking from the most to the less-important variable, from top to bottom. When the classification tree has grown only on controls, the algorithm identifies five variables that are related to complications. They are well-known and have been previously shown to be associated with surgical outcome. When COVID-19 enters into the model, the same variables are overcome and masked. COVID-19 has the strongest association with complications and significantly reduces the importance of the other, well-known surgical risk factors.

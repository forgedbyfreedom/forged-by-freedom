Demonstration of the Semont-Plus self-maneuver for Right Posterior Canal Canalithiasis The patient first turns her head 45 degrees to the left, then extends her right arm, and subsequently moves the whole body by 150 degrees plus to the right side. This position is kept for 60 seconds. Subsequently the patient moves the whole body by 240 degrees towards the non-affected side. Here the patient can also have severe spinning vertigo. Subsequently the patient, after 60 seconds, sits up. These maneuvers have to be repeated three times in the morning, three times at noon, and three times at night.

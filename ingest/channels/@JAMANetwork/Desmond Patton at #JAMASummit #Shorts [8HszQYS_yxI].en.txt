One step that we can take to reduce firearm violence in the future is to understand the role and impact of social media on firearm violence. Social media, I believe, should be viewed as a new neighborhood where communications and posting of text and images and live streams can be a conduit for firearm violence. For over a decade, I've studied how social media platforms like Facebook and Twitter can be a catalyst for violence. And we are only slowly beginning to understand the impact that social media is having on violence. And so I think in the 21st century, we really need to invest in this space.

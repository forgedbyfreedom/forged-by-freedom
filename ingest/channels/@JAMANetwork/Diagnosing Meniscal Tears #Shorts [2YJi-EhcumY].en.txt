For the McMar's test, the patient is sitting relaxed. We bring the leg up to full flexion. We externally rotate the foot, apply a vgus stress, and put the finger over the medial joint line and bring the leg out to full extension. We're feeling for a pop or any sensation consistent with the meniscal tear that the patient complains of. For the lateral meniscus again bring the leg up to full flexion finger over the lateral joint line internally rotate the foot apply vera stress and bring the leg out to full extension. Again feeling for a pop and ascetaining whether there's any symptoms consistent with the meniscal tear. For ascetaining joint line tenderness the important part here is to ask the patient whether they're feeling any tenderness as you do this particular exam. So bring the knee up to 90° of flexion for the medial meniscus. Internally rotate the foot and palpate the medial joint line. Again ascertaining if there's any tenderness consistent with the miniscal tear. For the lateral meniscus, we externally rotate the foot and palpate the lateral joint line. Again ascertaining if there's any tenderness or complaint from the patient.

One step that we could take to reduce firearm violence in the future is to develop a public health campaign which focuses on gun violence reduction. Similar to the previous public health campaigns that have been quite successful that have focused on smoking reduction as well as seat belt safety. I believe that we could create a public health campaign that focuses on gun violence reduction using narratives that are based on the personal and lived experiences of individuals that have experienced firearm violence as well as providing compelling data to support those narratives.

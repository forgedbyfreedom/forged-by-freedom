Long CO affects children too and new research suggests that symptoms vary by age. Infants and young children may show general symptoms like sleepiness or poor appetite and school-aged children may have trouble focusing or sleeping. Adolescence may have more symptoms that are like adults with difficulty tasting or smelling or even having memory issues. Many children feel post-exertional malaise which means they can be exhausted 12 to 48 hours after even minimal exertion. Like adults about 10 to 20% of children will experience long co after a co 19 infection for at least 3 months after infection. So it's crucial for parents and clinicians and caregivers to track these symptoms of long co. Although there's no cure yet, researchers are actively working to understand and figure out how to treat long COVID.

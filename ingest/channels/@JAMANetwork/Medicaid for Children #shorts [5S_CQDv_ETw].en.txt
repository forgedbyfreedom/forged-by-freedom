Children need health insurance to get the medical care they need. And in the US, many rely on Medicaid. A recent JAMAMA Pediatrics patient page explains what parents need to know about this important program. Medicaid is a type of health insurance designed to help families who might not otherwise be covered through employment. Medicaid and a related kind of insurance called CHIP, the Children's Health Insurance Program, provide free or lowcost health coverage to half of all US children. These programs are vital because they ensure that children receive necessary medical care, especially those with chronic illness. Regular medical care helps prevent serious health issues, and allows for early detection, leading to better lifelong outcomes. Children with Medicaid have fewer avoidable hospitalizations, better school performance, and even higher wages as adults. Additionally, Medicaid can help families avoid financial strain from medical expenses, leading to improved family stability. Applying for Medicaid is straightforward, with most states offering online applications. By ensuring access to medical care, these programs play a vital role in supporting the health and well-being of the next generation.

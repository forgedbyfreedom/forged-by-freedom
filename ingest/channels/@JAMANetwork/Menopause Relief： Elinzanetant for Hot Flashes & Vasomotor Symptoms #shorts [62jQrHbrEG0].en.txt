This new non hormonal medication reduces hot flashes. A phase three trial of elenzanitant showed a significant reduction in the severity and frequency of menopause related vasom motor symptoms which are also known as hot flashes. The lenzanotent group experienced 1.6 fewer hot flashes per day as compared to the placebo group. These results are from the Oasis 3 trial, a double blind placeboc controlled randomized clinical trial published in JAMAMA internal medicine. This study included 628 women across 83 sites in Europe and North America. These figures show the changes over 12 weeks. Descriptive analysis suggests the lenotant was beneficial over 50 weeks. In this trial, the medication was well tolerated and had a favorable safety profile. Importantly, Ellen Xenotant is a non- hormonal medication, so it can be used by women who have contraindications or prefer to avoid hormonal treatments. Ellen Xanitant is an antagonist of neurokin 1 and neurokin 3 receptors which are overexpressed during menopause and have been linked to vasom motor symptoms.

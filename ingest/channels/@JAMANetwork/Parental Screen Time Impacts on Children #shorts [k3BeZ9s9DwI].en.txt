Parents, your screen time may be having a negative impact on your children. A new systematic review and metaanalysis published in Jamma Pediatrics reveals that when parents use technology around their children, such as checking phones or tablets, it's linked to several key negative outcomes for children under age five. The research which analyzed data from over 14,000 children across 21 different studies found that parental technology use in a child's presence is associated with poorer cognitive development, lower attachment, and reduced pro-social behavior. Children also showed higher levels of emotional and behavioral problems as well as increased screen time themselves. The author suggests several possible reasons for these associations, including decreased quality of parentto- child interactions, children feeling ignored by parents, and children adopting their parents' screen time habits. Importantly, the review did not find evidence that parental tech use affected children's motor development, physical activity, or sleep, possibly because these areas haven't been studied enough. The authors highlight the need for more research, especially long-term studies, to better understand how parents device use might impact early childhood development. The important takeaway is that parents technology use may influence their children's health and development, underscoring the importance of mindful device use around young children.

Here we show a transissophageal echo cardiogram with agitated saline detecting a patent frame oval or PFO which is an opening caused by incomplete fusion of the atrial septum. Labels show the right atrium, the left atrium and the atrial septum that separates the two chambers. Agitated saline from the right atrium highlighted in green here is shown to cross into the left atrium which will appear within three cardiac cycles making the diagnosis of a PFO. The large shunt here is defined as more than 20 bubbles appearing within the left atrium which will appear within three cardiac cycles after venus injection of agitated saline. This particular patient has a high risk PFO given its large shunt size and a concominant atrial septile aneurysm. A concominant atrial septal aneurysm is defined as 10 mm or more of excursion of the septal wall from midline.

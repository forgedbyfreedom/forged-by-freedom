Among black youth, the majority of the firearm deaths are being caused by homicides. Whereas in the American Indian and Alaskan Native youth, the majority of the firearm deaths are related to suicides. The statistics are quite alarming. Homicide rates in the black population began increasing in 2014. They had increased by 76% between 2014 and 2020. So that by the most recent analysis, the homicide rate in black youth was almost 13 times higher than in the white population. The increase in suicides in the American Indian and Alaskan Native population has resulted in a rate of nearly three times that of the white population. So we're seeing a dramatic increase in homicides and suicides among these youth, most of which is related to firearms.

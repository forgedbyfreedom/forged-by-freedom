What drives firearm violence in this country is the widespread availability of guns. There are about 400 million firearms in the country more than one per person. So firearm violence, be it violence against others or self harm is driven in no small part by easy access to guns. So if we're serious about reducing firearm harms, we need to reduce easy access to guns. There are many ways of doing that. For example, people who have a history of violent assault or domestic violence should have less easy access to guns or making sure that people who have substance use issues and are at greater risk of self harm, have less access to guns. So I think the large category of what we want to do to reduce firearm harms, be it interpersonal or self-inflicted harms, is to make sure that it is harder to get access to guns in moments when those guns may be used to to impose harm either on others or on oneself.

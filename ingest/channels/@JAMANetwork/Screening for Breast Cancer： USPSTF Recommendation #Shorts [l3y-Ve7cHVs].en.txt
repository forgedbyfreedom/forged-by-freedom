Current science now shows that all women should begin screening at age 40 and continue every other year until age 74. We also know that black women are 40% more likely to die from breast cancer compared to their white counterparts. So this recommendation is particularly important for black women. As I mentioned earlier, with this new recommendation, we can save potentially up to 20% more lives and actually a slightly even higher amount among black women. Also to emphasize that screening is such an important obvious first step but we know that the downstream steps of followup appropriate biopsies have indicated and access to equitable treatments are also crucial for all women but particularly black women to really receive this benefit of this letter grade B recommendation.

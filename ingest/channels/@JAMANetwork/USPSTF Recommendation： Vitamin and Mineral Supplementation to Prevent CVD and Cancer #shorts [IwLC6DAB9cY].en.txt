the evidence shows us that for most vitamins and minerals we don't have enough evidence to recommend for or against taking a vitamin or mineral or a combination of them to prevent heart attacks strokes or cancer but for two vitamins in particular beta carotene and vitamin e we find that vitamin e does not provide any benefit and that beta-carotene actually carries a risk for increasing lung cancer in those who are already at risk for developing lung cancer so we recommend against taking beta-carotene and vitamin e as supplements

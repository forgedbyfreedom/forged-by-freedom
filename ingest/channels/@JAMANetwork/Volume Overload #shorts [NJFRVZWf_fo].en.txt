Point of care ultra synography or pocus is one way to evaluate volume overload at the bedside in non-intubated patients. Pocus can be used to identify interstitial syndrome such as pulmonary edema or pneumonia by assessing lung fields for belines. The internal jugular vein can be assessed to measure the jugular venus pressure as an approximation for the right atrial pressure and the inferior vennea can be assessed for the diameter and collapsability to assess patient volume status. Longer video guides to POSUS can be found in this rational clinical examination article published [music] in JAMA. The author's objective is to identify the most accurate clinical examination, radiographic, and laboratory findings for assessing volume overload in [music] non-intubated patients.

Everyone always wants the crazy peaks on their biceps. Now, I was fortunate enough to be born with good bicep peaks, but if you guys are trying to really focus on adding some peak to your biceps, I always suggest an isolateral movement. What I love to do is a stationary elbow position preacher curl. Okay, this really gets that peak contraction, allows you to isolate without any body momentum. Someone like me that likes to swing a lot when they do their bicep movements, I think it's ideal to really get that elbow locked in, get that full stretch in that squeeze, and watch your biceps grow. Focus on 8 to 12 reps.

Everyone always asks what the best exercise for chest training is. Is it the incline press or is it the flat bench press? Now, the granddaddy exercise of all movements is the flat press. It's the first exercise you start to train with. I always was going for the bodybuilding, the repetitions. Anyone that goes and does the bench press, it signifies your strength, your manhood. I always incorporate some sort of this movement into my training routine to focus on the upper chest. You should do both. Whether you do one one workout, one the next, sometimes alternate. Everyone thinks their upper chest is going to grow from the incline. Yes, you're going to target a little more of the front delt, a little more of the upper chest when you do that incline, but I feel the flat bench can benefit just as much. Remember, keep your repetitions on eight plus and don't go extremely heavy. If you're smart and utilize it, you can build that full round chest and of course dominate if you want to get on stage.

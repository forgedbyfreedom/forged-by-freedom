For me, I was in the gym at least 5 days a week. Those five days, I usually split body parts. I never suggest being in the gym more than an hour at a time for a body part. Quite honestly, I could train a body part between 30 and 40 minutes. With my rest periods of 45 to 60 seconds, I don't think you need a ton of time in there. Remember, the muscle needs 72 hours in between before you work it again. So, if you're going to do chest training, you need to give that chest workout 72 hours before you do that chest workout again. Ideally, we group body parts. I trained on all different schedules, whether it was two on, one off, three on, one off. Some people train five days in a row. But I would always suggest having one or two days off during the week, at least one day. A lot of people might train six days a week. I trained five.

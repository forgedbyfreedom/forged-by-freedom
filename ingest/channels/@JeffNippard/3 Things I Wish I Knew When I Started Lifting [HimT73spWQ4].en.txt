I really wish someone told me these things when I first started lifting. First, you don't need to do three sets of everything. I'd much rather see you do one or two sets that you actually push hard than three lazy sets. These last few reps here, the ones that start to really burn and slow down, that's where the real growth happens. Don't stop when it gets uncomfortable. That's exactly when you need to dig in. Second, don't get lost in information overload on social media. Most of this stuff doesn't actually matter much anyway, especially when you're starting out. If you're in the gym doing basic lifts and pushing yourself hard, you will grow. If you enjoy diving into the science and nuances, awesome. But don't let it overwhelm you. Showing up and putting in the work matters way more. And finally, don't get discouraged by slow progress. It's normal to compare yourself to influencers, but remember, they've probably been lifting for years. Muscle growth takes time. Be consistent, and you'll be impressed with where you are a year from now. Trust me.

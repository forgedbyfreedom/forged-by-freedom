Most people can't last 30 seconds in a dead hang, which is one of the best tests of grip strength. I just tested my own max hang time for the first time, and I was super surprised at how long I lasted. The average gym goer can do about 30 seconds to a minute. Intermediate dead hang time is 1 to 2 minutes. Advanced 2 to 3 minutes. For your max dead hang, these are the steps you'll want to follow. First, chalk your hands. This makes a huge difference, trust me. Then, grip the bar just outside shoulder width. Use a looser grip initially and then once you start to slip, start gripping harder and harder. Make sure you let your legs hang straight down. So don't swing back and forth. Keep your feet locked together and point your toes down. Then just make sure you don't hold your breath.

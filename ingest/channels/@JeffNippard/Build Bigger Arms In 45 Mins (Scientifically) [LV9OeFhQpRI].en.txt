This arm workout only took me 45 minutes. Up first, two sets of Beijing curls. Keep your upper arm behind your body and think about stretching your biceps at the bottom of each rep. Next, two sets of throat crushers. Someone call these JM presses. They're basically in between a skull crusher and a close grip bench press, bringing the weight down to your neck. Lower the weight under control and be careful. Then, one hard set of preacher curls to failure. I stay in the bottom two/3 of the range of motion. For shoulders, all I did was two sets of machine lateral raises. Think about sweeping the weight out. Next, two sets of cable kickbacks. Brace with your non-working arm and focus on squeezing your triceps at full extension. Then, one set of inverse Zman curls to failure. These are really fun and challenging if you haven't tried them before. Curl up with a hammer grip, rotate your palms up at the top, and then control the eccentric on the way down.

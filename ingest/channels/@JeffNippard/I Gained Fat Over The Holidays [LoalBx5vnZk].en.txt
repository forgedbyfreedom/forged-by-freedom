Over the holidays, I kind of let go. I ate whatever I wanted. My workouts were off and I didn't track anything. At first, I didn't notice much, but before I knew it, I was up 7 lbs. And now I feel like I flattened out, too. And yeah, I do feel a little bad about it. If you're in the same spot, here's what not to do. First, don't beat yourself up too much. Some food guilt is normal, but just try to notice it and let it go. Second, don't think it was all fat. If it was only a few weeks, a lot of that weight is just water retention and food waste. So, drink plenty of water and include fruits and whole grains to help with digestion. Third, don't do anything extreme. No crash diets or punishing workouts. The damage is never as bad as it feels in your head. Try to get one good day starting today and then another good day tomorrow. If you can stack a week of good days, they don't have to be perfect. You'll feel back in control

Forearms exercise tier list. Three make it into S tier. Barbell isometric holds. These are great for building grip strength, but they also fatigue your back, traps, core, and every stabilizer top to bottom. So, if you're simply trying to grow your forearms, they're a very inefficient way to do it. High C tier. Plate pinches are actually underrated. These are a fun way to progressively overload by trying to add some time each week. Low A tier. Farmer's carries are fun since you're moving through space and I love them as an athletic movement, but since they're so fatiguing on so many muscles, I can't say they're the best for [music] pure forearm growth. High B tier. Reverse grip curls. These are simply awesome for building up the back of your forearms. Your biceps and brachiialis can still take over a bit though, but these are solid. High A tier. Hand grippers are good for some form work at home, but it's tricky to hit true failure, and since there's fixed resistance, you're limited to overloading reps only. Best I can do is Ctier. Dumbbell wrist curls. Simple, basic, tried and true muscle builder for the anterior forearm. Easy. S tier. Dumbbell wrist extensions are also super basic but equally effective except for the back of your forearms. Got to go with S tier on these, too. Standard hammer curls are going in A tier since they will smash the back of your forearm. But you know what?

This leg workout only took me 45 minutes. Low volume, high intensity. Let's go. First up, two sets of leg extensions. Lean the seat back for a bigger stretch on the big rectus feorous head of your quads and pull yourself securely down into the seat for max stability. Focus on squeezing your quads to move the weight. Both sets to failure. Next, two sets of barbell Romanian deadlifts. Push your hips back and lower the bar straight down centered over the middle of your foot. Get a nice deep hamstring stretch, but try to keep your back flat the whole time. Then two sets of hip thrusts. Squeeze your glutes hard as you get to the top of each rep. I pause for about one or two seconds in the full squeeze. Last set to failure. After that, one hard set of leg press. You don't need to go all the way to failure where you actually drop the plates, but go until you're confident you can't get another rep with good form. For calavves, lengthened partials on standing calf raises for two sets.

the most underrated ab exercise that almost no one does. In my opinion, it's the dragon flag. But fair warning, they're way harder than they look. If you can do one rep, you're already at an elite fitness level. Most people do hanging leg raises, which are also solid. But I've swapped them out for dragon flags because with dragon flags, your torso is stabilized, so you don't need to worry about swinging back and forth. If you want to try them out, start with lying leg lifts. Once you can do those for 10 reps, advance on to a bent knee dragon flag. Once you can do those for 10 reps, you should be ready for fully extended Bruce Lee style dragon flags. Grab the bench and with straight knees and straight hips, lift your entire body, including your glutes, up and off the bench.

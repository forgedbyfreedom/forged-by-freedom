Rear delts tier list. I'm going to save the best for last. Rope face poles. If you do them underhand, you will hit your rear delts over your traps. Not the biggest range of motion, but they're solid for overload. I'm feeling A tier. Dumbbell reverse flies. Zero tension at the bottom and you never get your rear delts into a stretch position. C tier. If you do them lying, though, now you pull tension into the rear delts at the bottom and give a way bigger stretch. Lying dumbbell reverse flies are going in high A tier. Wide grip rows are a great way to overload your rear delts with some heavy weight, but your mid traps will always take over. I'm still feeling low B tier. Reverse cable crossover. Such a smooth movement, and when your arms cross over, you get this deep stretch with high tension. High A tier. Reverse pec deck feels great, and you get nice even tension throughout the range of motion. But that range is cut in half because you never get a full stretch on your rear delts. So, I have to go with low B tier. If you do them sideways, though, now you can stretch your rear delts across your body with all the same upsides. For these, I've got to go with S tier.

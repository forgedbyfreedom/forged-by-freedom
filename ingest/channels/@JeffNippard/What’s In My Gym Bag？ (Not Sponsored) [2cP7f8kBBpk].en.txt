This is what's in my gym bag as a science-based lifter. Fraps. Your back will always be stronger than your grip, and you don't want your grip to limit your back gains. Liquid chalk. If your palms are sweaty, your grip will limit tension. A belt for squats, deadlifts, and weighted pull-ups. Knee sleeves to help my knees feel more comfy and give me a small strength boost. Water to stay hydrated for pumps, and performance. The muscle ladder. I always keep a copy handy to give away to random subscribers. Gushers for quick, fast digesting carbs in case my workouts drag on a bit. Hand sanitizer. Gyms are full of germs. Bands for doing warm-up drills and assisted pull-ups. A gym pin which is great for adding load once you've maxed out a machine's weight stack. And finally, flat slippers for deadlifts. Angles 90 handles for lat pull downs. and some Tylenol just in case I get a headache.

I think this is one of the best glute exercises that most people don't know about yet, but they should soon because it's seriously amazing for the glutes and hamstrings. It's the seated cable deadlift. And I know it looks gimmicky, but hear me out. Even though conventional deadlifts are great, and I do Romanian deadlifts every week, they simply aren't as stable as when you're seated. And that extra stability helps me connect with my glutes and hamstrings a lot better. If you do them right, you'll get a glute pump like you've never felt before. So, attach a lap pull down bar to a seated cable machine and take a grip just outside shoulder width. Point your toes slightly out and squat the weight up while extending your hips to a full lockout. From there, unlock your hips and knees to lower the bar down just like on a deadlift, except now you can actually go a bit lower since there are no plates to hit the floor. From there, squeeze your glutes to extend your hips. And I find a big glute squeeze at the top helps me engage them better for the next rep. Doing them single leg is also amazing because you can work on any left to right asymmetries without worrying about losing your balance.

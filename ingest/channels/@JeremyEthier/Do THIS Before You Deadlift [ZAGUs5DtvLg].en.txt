Then, I activate my lats by thinking about bending the bar towards me, squeezing my armpits as if I had oranges in them and I was trying to make juice. Next, I brace my core hard as if someone was about to punch my stomach. Then, I pull the slack out of the bar by pushing the floor down with my feet before exerting full effort to lift the weight.

make this one fix to your dumbbell press to instantly boost chest activation and reduce your risk of shoulder injuries many people used a wide arm path to press the weight straight up and down instead you want to align your arm path with the direction of most of the chest fibers this means tucking your elbows to about a 45 to 60 degree angle and allowing your grip to turn out slightly rather than moving straight up and down the dumbbells should now move forwards on the way down and backwards on the way up you may need to lighten the weight to do this correctly but it'll lead to far better chest gains

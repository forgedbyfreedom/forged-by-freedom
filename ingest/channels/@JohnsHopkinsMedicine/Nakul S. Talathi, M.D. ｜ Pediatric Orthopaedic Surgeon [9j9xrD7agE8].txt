 playing a number of sports growing up, I spent my fair share of time in pediatric orthopedic surgeon's office. I remember fondly their ability to connect with me and their hard work to get me back onto the field. Now that I'm in their role, I feel honored to be able to serve the same purpose and work hard to get children back to doing what they love. My name is Nick Talladi and I'm a pediatric orthopedic surgeon at Johns Hopkins Medicine. I specialize in treating hip and spine conditions in children, young adults, and adolescents, along with orthopedic trauma and fracture care. In addition to clinical care, I'm interested in research to improve on how we make decisions for children with hip conditions, spine conditions, and fractures. In particular, I'm interested in optimizing everything we do to treat these children in order to provide the best outcome possible as well as to prevent any unnecessary surgeries. My philosophy for treating the child and their family is a listening first approach. I'm drawn into pediatric orthopedics because children are such a special patient population to treat. The decisions we make now, along with their families, can provide the child with decades of improvement and benefit. Additionally, I like to work with each family to tailor a specific plan for each child. One of the reasons I chose to practice at Hopkins Medicine was the opportunity to collaborate with leaders in the field in research, education, and clinical care. Ultimately, this will help us improve the care of each and every child we treat.

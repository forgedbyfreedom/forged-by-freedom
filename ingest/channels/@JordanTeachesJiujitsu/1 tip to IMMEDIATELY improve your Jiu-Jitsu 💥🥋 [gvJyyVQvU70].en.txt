If I could give you just one tip to improve your jiu-jitsu. What I would say is make sure you don't get your back pinned to the mat. This isn't to say you can't be on your back momentarily. You need to make sure that you're able to rotate your body and get on your side as needed because this is when things go wrong. If she pins me on my back and I can't move, well, now I can't get effective jiu-jitsu going. I need to make sure I'm able to get onto my side. I'm able to rotate and start working from here. Same thing with butterfly guard. If I'm here on my butt or on my side, I can get good offense going. But if she has me pinned on my back, again, I can't move. And now it's much easier for her to pass. That could be closed guard as well. We don't want to be pinned to the our backs. But from here, if I'm pinned on my back and I can't move, well, how am I going to get offense going? If I'm able to get onto my side, I'm able to get triangles going, scissor sweeps, all these different attacks where I couldn't get if I was pinned with my back on the mat.

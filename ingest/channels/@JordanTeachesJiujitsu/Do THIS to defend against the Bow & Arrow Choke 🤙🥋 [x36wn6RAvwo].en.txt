What is a better way to defend the bone arrow choke? Is it two hands on the lower arm or one on the lower and one on the upper? Generally, it's going to be one on the lower and one on the upper because this better prevents them from be able to get their elbow behind you, which is what they need to take out the slack. When you have one on the lower and one on the upper, now it's very hard them to get their elbow behind you compared to two on the lower.

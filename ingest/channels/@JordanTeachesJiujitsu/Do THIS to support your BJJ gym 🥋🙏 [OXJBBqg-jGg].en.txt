Here's three things you can do if you like your gym and want to see them succeed. Number one, write them a review. This takes barely any time from you and really helps them out. Reviews really help bring in new students and that's good for you and for them. Number two, be nice and help out the trial guys. People starting class for the first time are very scared and nervous. If you can help them out and make them feel comfortable, this helps you because now you might have a new training partner and it helps the gym have a new student. Number three, like, share, comment on their social media posts because this really helps boost them in the algorithm and help them reach new potential students. And if you don't like your gym, just do the opposite. Write them a bad review.

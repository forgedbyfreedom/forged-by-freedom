You see, the thing about jiu-jitsu is that it's often not about what's better or worse because context matters. For example, what's a better arm bar? The standard fundamental arm bar where you swing your leg over, throw your leg over the head, and then get the tap like that or the top lock where you throw your legs over the shoulders and then throw your leg over the head to get the tap. It's always contextual. What's the right technique for the right moment? And where beginners go wrong or even ignorant higher belts is thinking that one technique is better than another just because that's the version that they do or that's the version that they were taught because context matters so much. If you fall into this trap of thinking this is better than that, you might limit yourself to the possibilities when you're actually rolling.

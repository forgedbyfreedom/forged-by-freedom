A great way to think of takedowns is that you want to attach to the upper body or attach to the lower body and then work to take their legs off the mat. That could be an under hook, that could be over hook, that could be a front head lock, a body lock. All of these are attachments to the upper body. Same thing with the lower body. That could be one leg, two legs, head on the inside, head on the outside. And once we have attachments to them, we want to block and drive or we want to lift and rotate.

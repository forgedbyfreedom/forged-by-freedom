Every beginner needs to know the armbar from the back because getting underneath the neck for a rear naked choke is a hard thing to do even if you're good at jiu-jitsu. Not that this isn't something you should do, but I just find the arm bar from the back so much more reliable of a submission to go for. And beginners often make this mistake where they rely too much on flexibility. So they're here, they're trying to throw their leg over the head and it just won't reach. Some people have that flexibility. Like I can usually get it cuz I have short legs. But what can really help is pushing with your forearm. So when you're here with your kamora grip, think about pushing them with your forearm to make space for your leg to come over.

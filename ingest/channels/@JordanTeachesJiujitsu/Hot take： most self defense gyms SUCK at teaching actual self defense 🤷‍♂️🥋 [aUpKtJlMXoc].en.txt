My hot take is that a lot of self-defense gyms are actually worse at preparing you for actual self-defense than just a sport jiu-jitsu gym. And the reason why is the lack of resistance. It's already an issue in jiu-jitsu there being a lack of resistance in classes. And it's even worse usually in self-defense classes where they show you stuff and you're supposed to remember it in the heat of the moment. But that's never going to happen unless you do it with resistance. So my advice, if you really care about self-defense, train everything. Train MMA. Grappling's not enough. Standup's not enough. You need to know both if you really want to prepare yourself as much as possible. deescalation, stuff like that is also very important and one thing that these schools are good at teaching, but you need resistance to get good at a skill.

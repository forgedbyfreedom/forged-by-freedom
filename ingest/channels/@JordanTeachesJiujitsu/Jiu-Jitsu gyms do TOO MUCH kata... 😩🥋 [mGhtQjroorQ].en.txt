But jiu-jitsu, it's very similar when we do these pre-programmed static repetitions over and over again of the same move. Repetition does help, but you need repetition in a realistic scenario. That's the whole point of using a game is because you can be exposed to a specific scenario over and over and over again. So you get that repetition without repetition. This doesn't mean that showing someone how to do something is wrong. It just means that getting them to do it over and over and over again statically is just not a great use of time.

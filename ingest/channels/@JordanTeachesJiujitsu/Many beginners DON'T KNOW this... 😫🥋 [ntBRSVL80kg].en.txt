Many beginners still don't seem to know this. You don't get points for side control. You get points for the pass. So, if you pass the guard into side control, you get three points for the pass. If you pass guard into mounts, you get your pass points and your mount points. And it's a very important distinction because maybe I take my partner down into side control or I roll them into side control. I don't get points for that. So you don't get points for side control, only for passing the guard itself.

My black belt is I got it from a belt factory. This is what a former training partner messaged me when I received my black belt. What a nice guy. At the time, I really felt bad about this. And looking back, I couldn't care less what he thinks. The important message here, especially with promotion season happening right now, is that if you get promoted and you're worried about what other people think, you should just really just not care. Just focus on yourself. Your instructor thinks you're ready, then you're ready. Worrying about what other people think is such a waste of time. Especially when these people are straight up

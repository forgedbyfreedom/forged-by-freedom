Should you beat up new people in jiu-jitsu or should you let them work? I think that it's best to beat them up but gently. Really showing them the power of jiu-jitsu and how effective it is, but with a minimum effective dose of effort. So, you don't want to be just hurting someone or just smashing them, that kind of thing, and detering them from coming back. But you do want to show them that jiu-jitsu works.

We talk about the importance of elbow to knee position and elbow to hip position on the bottom, but it's also important on top. So for me as a top player, I need to keep barriers in the way, stopping them from getting things like knee shields, stopping them from getting under hooks. We can do that by going elbow to hip, elbow to knee. These are very powerful and helpful from the top, too. I think of this like a defensive shell. We don't want to let people in our shell like lasso grips and knee shields, all that kind of stuff. We want to keep them out.

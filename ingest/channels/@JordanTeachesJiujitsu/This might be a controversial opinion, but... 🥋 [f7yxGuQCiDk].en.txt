This might be a controversial opinion, but I really think that most people don't get much better after purple belt. And the reason is is because the gains you make at blue and white belt are much larger than the gains you make at brown and black belt. And then you have injuries, you have aging if you started at a later age. If you're a really good purple belt, you're probably going to be a really good black belt. And if you were a mediocre purple belt, then you're probably going to be a mediocre black belt. And I don't mean that negatively. It's just the truth. This is most people. Don't be like most people. Just with everything in life, don't be like most people. Be the exception to the rule. Let me know.

The main argument of the ecological approach isn't that it's wrong to show someone a technique, but it's more so that doing something over and over again statically isn't going to download the information to their brain like software. So statically going for the rear naked choke over and over again isn't going to build skills nearly as well as actually doing it in a live environment.

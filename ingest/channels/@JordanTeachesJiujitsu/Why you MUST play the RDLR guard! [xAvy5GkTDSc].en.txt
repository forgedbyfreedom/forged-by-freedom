I'm actually shocked how many people don't use reverse deaha. It isn't like this is the best guard in the world, but it is a very good defensive guard. Very hard for them to knee slide when you have this guard in place. Because if she's coming in for a knee slide and I let her pin my knee to the mat now, it's very easy to finish the pass and knee slide. But if I self-frame by grabbing her ankle, having my arm underneath, now I have really good control of the angle of her knee. Now, if she tries to knee slide, there's no way and I can start attacking my offense. All reverse deleiva is is just hooking your instep of your inside leg onto their thigh like this and grabbing their ankle and putting your toes on the hip.

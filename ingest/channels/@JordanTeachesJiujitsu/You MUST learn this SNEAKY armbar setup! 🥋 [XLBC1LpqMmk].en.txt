This is a great arm bar setup to work smarter, not harder. To go for the armbar, you need to bring your hips up higher to the shoulders. And to make that possible, you need to clear their elbows. But that can be a lot of effort and a lot of work to do. And what you can do instead is get them to lift their elbow for you. So what you can do is go for a cross collar.

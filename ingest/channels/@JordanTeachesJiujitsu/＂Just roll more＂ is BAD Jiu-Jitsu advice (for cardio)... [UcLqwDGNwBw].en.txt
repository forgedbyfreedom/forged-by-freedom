It can improve your cardio to some extent, but generally what you're doing is improving your efficiency by rolling more. And having an efficient engine is very helpful, but if you have a small gas tank, then it isn't going to be as helpful as having a bigger gas tank while also being more efficient. You could be really efficient and you're like a Prius, but with a really small gas tank, you're not going to get very far. And you could get beaten by people that are less efficient but have a bigger gas tank. I think I'm pretty efficient myself, but I know that if I don't do cardio on the side, my cardio is going to be pretty poor on the mats. What I find helpful is a fan bike, an echo bike, an assault bike. I like doing zone 2 training on that where it's slow and steady for 45 minutes to an hour. And also, I like doing intervals where it's highintensity training that's more geared towards anorobic fitness. When you work on your aerobic fitness and your anorobic fitness, you're going to build a very powerful gas tank to go along with your efficiency from training more jiu-jitsu. But if you only train jiu-jitsu as your main form of cardio, then you can only get so far in terms of your capacity. And what you want is efficiency and capacity.

There are many types of transfusion reactions, but five of them make up the vast majority of what you'll see in clinical practice and what you'll be asked in your boards. And there's a very easy pneummonic to remember these five. Fever is for feral non-hemolytic reaction. H stands for hemolytic reactions which include both the acute and the delay types. A is for allergic reactions that is range from mild itching and hives to full-blown anaphilaxis. L refers to lung injury in a syndrome we known as trolley or transfusion related acute lung injury. And finally O is for overload specifically a syndrome we called taco transfusion associated circulatory overload.

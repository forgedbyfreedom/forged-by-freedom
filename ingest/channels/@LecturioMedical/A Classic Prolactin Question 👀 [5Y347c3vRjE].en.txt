Do you think you're ready to test your endocrinology knowledge? Let's find out. A 28-year-old woman presents with secondary amenorhea and bilateral nipple discharge. Laboratory studies reveal an elevated prolactin level with normal thyroid function and a negative pregnancy test. Pituitary MRI demonstrates a 9mm pituitary lesion. What is the most likely diagnosis? This young woman has secondary amenorhea, the production of abnormal breast milk or galactoa and elevated prolactin levels and a pituitary mass. She most likely has a prolactinoma in her anterior pituitary causing secondary aminora which is the cessation of menses despite the presence of normal cycles after puberty.

Do you think you've mastered pediatrics? Let's put your knowledge to the test. Which of the following triads is considered classic for intuception? Patients who have intoception will have a classic triad of symptoms. So the classic triad is intermittent colicky abdominal pain, vomiting and bloody stools. Now this is what you'll see written on your test and you should definitely remember this. The reality is though is that most intoception we do not actually find bloody stools. It's a rare finding when the diagnosis has been missed for a long period of time.

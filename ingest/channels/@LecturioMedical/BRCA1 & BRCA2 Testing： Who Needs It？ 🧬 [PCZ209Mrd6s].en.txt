So there is a genetic risk assessment that is recommended for women who have a 20 to 25% chance of an inherited predisposition to ovarian and breast cancer. Risk assessment may be helpful for women who have a 5 to 10% chance inherited predisposition of ovarian and breast cancer as well. One in 40 women who are of Ashkanazi Jewish descent will be carriers of BA 1 and two. If we know that a woman has BRAA 1 or BOA 2 gene mutations, then there are guidelines that suggest that we should have MRI screening of their breasts and this is for women in general who have a 20% or greater lifetime risk of having breast cancer. We should also do this in previously untested patients who have a first-degree relative with BRAA 1 or BRAA 2 gene mutations.

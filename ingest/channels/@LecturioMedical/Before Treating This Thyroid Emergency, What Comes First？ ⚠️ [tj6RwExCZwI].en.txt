An elderly patient presents comeosse with hypothermia, hypotension, bradicardia, and laboratory findings consistent with severe hypothyroidism. Before initiating definitive thyroid hormone therapy, which immediate step is most appropriate? How does one treat mixed coma? First of all, because of the similarities in presentation, always rule out adrenal insufficiency. This can be done by checking a cortisol level. While the cortisol level is pending, always provide an empiric dose of hydrocortisone because hypoadrenalism is a significant condition and should be treated early.

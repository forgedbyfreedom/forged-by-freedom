This month is breast cancer awareness month and I think it's important that everyone knows this fact about breast cancer. Breast cancer is the leading cause of cancer in most women in the world. One in eight women would have breast cancer and it's very important that early screening, self-examination and mamogram is done as soon as possible especially for women more than 40 years old. Also, if someone in your family has a history of breast cancer, there is a high risk of having breast cancer, especially with BRCA1 or BRCA2 mutations. Also, increased exposure to estrogen plays a role in increasing the risk of breast cancer in women. It's also very important that some lifestyle factors like obesity, highfat diet, smoking, tobacco consumption, alcohol consumption also poses a risk to having breast cancer. Remember, early screening saves lives. Yes, your body your power. Not all breast lumps are cancerous. Some breast lumps are benign. Very important that you check with your doctor to see what it is about.

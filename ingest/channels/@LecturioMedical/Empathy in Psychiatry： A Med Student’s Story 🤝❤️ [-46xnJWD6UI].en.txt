The month of May is mental health month and today I would like to share my experience rotating in the psychiatry department. I remember the patient came in angry, aggressive, frustrated, hallucinating and fidgeting at the time. I was really nervous to approach the patient but my senior resident encouraged me to go to the patient and assess the patient's mental health status. I went to the patient and I told the patient that I will assess his mental status and he started speaking about different things, how people thought he was crazy, how people look down on him. I think one of the most important thing when dealing with a patient in psychiatry is to make sure that you listen. It's very important that you listen with empathy. Even if you don't understand anything that they're saying, it just creates a sense of warmth for this patient and creates a very safe space for this patient. And after listening to the patient, he was okay with me assessing his mental health status and I checked his status under the supervision of my supervisor. And you know after that day, we checked and we kept on looking out for the patient. So I think and I will encourage you guys when rotating in psychiatry department to show empathy. This is very important. Create a very safe space for your patients and also it's mental health month. Reach out to your friends, reach out to your family. If you know anyone who's going through something at this time, even a phone call or a message would be great.

I developed a silly mnemonic to remember these. Popeye's milk, McDonald's Pepsi, her Pepsi gum piece, ABCD. In this group we have the next two lines of the mnemonic, her Pepsi gum piece. Now, this corresponds to Hers' disease and von Gierke's disease. Hers', von Gierke's, Hers' Gierke's. The P of Pepsi reminds us of phosphorylase, this time the hepatic version. And P in piece for phosphatase, meaning glucose-6-phosphatase. Now, both diseases here present with a syndrome which I like to call the syndrome of the three H. Hepatomegaly, hypoglycemia, and hyperlipidemia. The difference lies in the severity and associated findings. Hers' disease is mild and just presents with that. Von Gierke's disease is severe and is associated with also hyperuricemia and lactic acidosis.

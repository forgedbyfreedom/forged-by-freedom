now the leading cause of COPD is smoking and copds has two subtypes chronic bronchitis and empyema both of these types of COPD are characterized by increased shortness of breath due to Airway obstruction with spirometry showing a reduced F1 to FC ratio below 70% which is the lower limit of normal now empyema is characterized by destruction of the alular walls with the loss of elastic recoil of the lungs resulting in hyperinflation and increased total lung capacity and chronic bronchitis is characterized by an increase in mucous production and inflammation resulting in cough and total lung capacity is also increased

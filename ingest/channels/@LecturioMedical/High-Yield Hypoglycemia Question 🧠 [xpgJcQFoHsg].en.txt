Do you think you're ready to test your endocrinology knowledge? Let's find out. A patient with hypoglycemia is found to have an elevated serum ceptide level. Which interpretation best explains this finding? The first diagnostic test that we noted in this case was a ceptide. Pro-insulin which is produced by the pancreas is cleaved into insulin and cpeptide. Both are then secreted from the pancreas into the bloodstream. When you see a high level of ceptide in the in the blood, usually this implies that the body is producing insulin.

When you look at the cardiac arhythm in patients with cardiac arrest about a quarter have either ventricular fibrillation or ventricular tacicardia which are known as the shockable rhythms. Among those patients you have about a 30% survival rate. The remaining rhythms which are pulsus electrical activity and ascy only produce about a 10% survival rate. So there's a big difference in how likely you are to survive based on what type of rhythm your heart goes into at the time of arrest.

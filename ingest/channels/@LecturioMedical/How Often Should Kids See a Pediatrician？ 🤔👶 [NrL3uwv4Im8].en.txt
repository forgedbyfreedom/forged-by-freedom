How often does a child needs to visit a pediatrician? Well, to be able to answer this question, you need to understand the background of it and that background are the developmental milestones. These are behaviors or skills that the children achieve as they grow up and develop. They are divided in different areas such as physical, cognitive, emotional, social and language. These milestones are the main reason why the children have a different schedule than adults. when we talk about doctor's appointments. So, how often does they need to go to a pediatrician? Well, they need to visit the pediatrician one week after they are born. From that visit till the sixth month, you need to see your patient every single month. Here is when the things start to change a little bit depending of each country. But according to the American Academy of Pediatrics, from the month 6 till the month 30, you need to see your patient every 3 months. As you can see, this is related to the vaccine schedules. And if everything is okay, from the month 30, you can be able to see your patient once a year. Remember that knowing this is not just important because you are going to have a correct answer in an exam. It's important because in the future you will be able to explain it to a parent the importance of taking their children to the pediatrician. See you at the next one.

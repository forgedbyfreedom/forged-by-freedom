In sepsis, you develop an infection. Your body's immune response attempts to fight it and that immune response causes injury to any of a variety of organ systems. So in sepsis, the circulatory system can be affected. You'll see myioardi depression and vasoddilation which is what leads to shock. The lungs are often affected leading to ARDS. The GI tract can be affected causing dismotility. Shock liver is quite common in which you'll see transaminitis and problems with hpatic synthetic dysfunction. The kidneys can be affected leading to oligura, anura or acute kidney injury. And the CNS is very commonly involved with altered mental status being a common presenting complaint associated with sepsis.

Today we would speak about how to approach all the patients in gerontoology department. When dealing with older patients, you speak slowly and communicate clearly and try as much as possible not to use complex words. Use very simple words. Avoid using big medical vocabulary that they probably wouldn't understand. And also when you want to write written summaries or instructions about medications for use large fonts, large bold fonts that they can see clearly. With a lot of older patients, they are mostly alert at the start of the day. So try as much as possible to schedule appointments at the start of the day. It's also very important to check the decision making capacity of the patient. Don't assume that this patient needs a decision maker just because the patient is old. And even after assessing the decision-making process of the patient, if it's impaired, still address them first and treat them with dignity regardless. And the last and most important thing is try to be respectful. Yes, a lot of older patients are very big on respect. So try to listen to them empathetically, be patient and also respect them and try not to be dismissal of their complaint.

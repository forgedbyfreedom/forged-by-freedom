now when it comes to learning the drugs and details my advice is to approach it gradually and in layers you see many students start pharmacology trying to master everything at once so if they're studying let's say anti-hypertensives they dive straight to learn the 10 a Inhibitors 16 beta blockers eight colum channel blockers with every detail of each one of them this just doesn't work it's like attempting a reverse spark on a steep heel on your very first driving lesson it's overwhelming and in the end it's counterproductive that's why I don't particularly love pharmacology textbooks especially for beginners because they're filled with way too much detail and way too much information instead I think that it's better to just focus on the essentials first learn them well and then gradually layer in the extra details as needed

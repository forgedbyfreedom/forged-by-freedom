Immunodiagnostics are used to assess patients with suspected immunological conditions. Clinical situations in which immune system components are assessed include infection, where we use the term seroconversion to describe a situation where antibodies are produced against specific pathogens. So the presence of an antibody against a particular bacterium or virus will indicate that that person is seroconverted. We measure immunological parameters in immunodeficiency that can be quantitative measurements or qualitative measurements. In allergy, sensitization to allergens is measured. In autoimmune disease, autoantibodies and T-cell autoreactivity can be measured. And finally in transplantation, HLA matching takes place. This was covered in the transplantation lecture.

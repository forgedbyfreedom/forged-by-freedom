Let’s explore the field of tumor immunology. The field of tumor immunology really involves several different things. First of all, like any cell in the body, cells of the immune system can themselves become malignant cells. They can transform into tumor cells. A second aspect is whether naturally there is an immune response that develops when a tumor arises. Just like an immune response develops to an infection. And then finally, the possibility of harnessing components of the immune system to attack tumors.

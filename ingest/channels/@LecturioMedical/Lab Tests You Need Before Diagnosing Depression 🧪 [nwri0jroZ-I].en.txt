It's important as well to do a screening by doing some baseline blood work or lab work on your patient, checking things like a complete blood count, ruling out viruses, checking their renal or kidney function, and also their liver function. You always want to check a thyroid hormone as well as other vitamins such as B12 and folate. You always want to do a urine tox screen as well to make sure there aren't any drugs that could be in their system accounting for their symptoms.

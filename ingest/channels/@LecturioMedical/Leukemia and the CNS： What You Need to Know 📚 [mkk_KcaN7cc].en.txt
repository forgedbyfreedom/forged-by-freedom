one feature of acute lymphoblastic leukemia is that patients often have a tendency to have a relapse of the disease within the central nervous system and need special treatment to eradicate the disease in that environment two major approaches one is intrathecal chemotherapy that means chemotherapy that is given into the cerebros spinal fluid directly another is to use high doses of intravenous chemotherapy of drugs such as methotraxate which get into the central nervous system and can help to kill off the leukemia cells radiotherapy of the brain and spinal cord can also be used but where possible we try to avoid that because of potential damage to the CNS from using radiotherapy in the long term

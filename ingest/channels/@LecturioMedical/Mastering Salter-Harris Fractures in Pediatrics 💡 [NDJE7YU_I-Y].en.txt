The most commonly used classification for pediatric fractures is called the salter harris classification. So a palter harris fracture is one that involves the growth plate. A salter harris 2 fracture goes through the growth plate and it goes through the metaphysis. A salter harris 3 will go through a portion of the growth plate and the epiphosis. And a salter harris 4 will go through the growth plate, the epiphosis and the metaphysis. A salter harris 5 fracture can go right through the growth plate, but it's more of a crush injury to the growth plate.

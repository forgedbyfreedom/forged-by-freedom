Here's a review of the toxiddromes and I'm going to highlight some of the things that differentiate them so you can remember. Sympathomiimetics, these patients will present hypertensive and tacocartic and typically their mental status will be agitated. Antiolineric patients will look a lot like sympathomimetic patients except they will have very dry skin, very dry secretions. By contrast, our chonergic patients will be copiously wet. They'll have diaphoretic skin. They'll have increased secretions and they typically will be somnolent rather than agitated. Our sedative hypnotic patients will of course be sedated and our opioid patients will also be sedated. However, they'll present with meiosis and respiratory depression.

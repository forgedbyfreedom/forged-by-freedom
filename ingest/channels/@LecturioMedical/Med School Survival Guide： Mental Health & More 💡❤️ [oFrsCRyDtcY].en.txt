Hello future doctors. Today I would like to share some tips with you that I wish I knew in med school and some tips that helped me in med school. The first and most important tip is to prioritize your mental health. Medical school is very difficult. Everyone knows this. So it's very important that you have a stable mental health. This could be by eating well, sleeping well, exercising. This is very important. The second tip is to find your community. You need friends in med school. You can't do med school alone. You know, you need friends who can keep you accountable. Oh, hey girl, you said you were going to read biology. Have you done that? Have you done this? Have you done that? And also friends to go out with, have fun. I mean, it's med school, but still have fun. The third point is try to celebrate your small wins. And as much as possible, do not let your grades define you. I remember crying so many times about my grades in med school, like, oh, I am a failure at this. And the fourth point in your clinical years, try as much as possible to ask questions, be curious, try to help, try to participate, and do not be afraid to say, "I don't know." If you don't know, you don't know, I guess. All right. And the final point is that you need to network. Networking is very important in medical school. Network with people, go for conferences, attend some seminars, build your CV, and there's life beyond med school. So, it's very important that you network accordingly. I hope this helped you and this will definitely help you in your future med journey.

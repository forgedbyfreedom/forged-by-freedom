Monoconal antibodies directed against beta amalloid have been approved for individuals with Alzheimer disease with mild cognitive impairment and documented amalloid pathology. These include adukanamab and leanamab. These medications reduce beta amalloid plaques in the brain and have been demonstrated to slow rates of cognitive decline. For starting these agents, amaloid p or csf testing is needed. Additionally, MRI monitoring is required to evaluate for amaloid related imaging abnormalities such as edema, hemoceterine deposition and microhemmores.

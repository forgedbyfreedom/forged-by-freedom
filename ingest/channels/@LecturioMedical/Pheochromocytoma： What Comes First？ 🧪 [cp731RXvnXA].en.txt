Do you think you're ready to test your endocrinology knowledge? Let's find out. A patient with a confirmed catakolamine secretreting adrenal tumor is being prepared for surgical resection. Which of the following medications should be initiated first in the pre-operative period? Phoxy benzamine is a non-competitive alpha blocker and is given 7 to 10 days prior to surgery for patients needing resection for their focytoma. This medication blocks alpha receptors in blood vessels. This is then followed by beta blockade using a non- selective beta blocker like propanol which acts on the beta receptors in the heart and kidney to lower blood pressure. Further beta blockade should be done when all the alpha 1 receptors are already blocked or else the c circulating catakolamines may stimulate alpha 1 receptors precipitating a hypertensive crisis.

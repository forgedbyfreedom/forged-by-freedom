An otherwise healthy 28-year-old woman, eight months postpartum, presents with persistent amenora, excessive thirst, and urine output of 4 L per day. Laboratory studies show normal serum glucose and calcium with a serum sodium of 146 mill equivalents per liter. What is the most likely diagnosis? Here we have a postpartum patient. She has secondary amenorhea. In addition to excessive thirst and excessive urination. This is manifesting with evidence of dehydration. She also has elevated urine output. Clinically she most likely has central diabetes incipidus and you need to check a simultaneous urine and serum osmolarity.

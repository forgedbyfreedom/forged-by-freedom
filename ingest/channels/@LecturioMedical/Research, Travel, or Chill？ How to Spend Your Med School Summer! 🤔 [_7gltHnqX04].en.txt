So, what should you then do with your summer? What you do with your summer depends on your goals. If you're strategically working towards matching into a very competitive residency program, and most very competitive residency programs require research, then it may be appropriate for you to be doing research during your summer. Not only will this expand your research history and also expand your network and mentor base, it's going to only help you in the future. Now some students on the other hand may wish to pursue medical missionary work locally or abroad and some students will study or do nothing. Now ultimately you should speak with your mentors, discuss with them what is your strategy, what are your goals, what do you want to do with your life and then come up with a plan of what to do with your summer.

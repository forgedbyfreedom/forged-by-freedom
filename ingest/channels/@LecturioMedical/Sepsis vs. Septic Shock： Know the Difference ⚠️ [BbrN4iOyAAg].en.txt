What is sepsis? Sepsis definitions were rewritten recently by an international consortium and sepsis is now defined as a lifethreatening organ dysfunction caused by a disregulated host response to infection. Septic shock on the other hand is a subset of sepsis. So not every patient with sepsis will have septic shock and in the case of septic shock you will have circulatory and cellular metabolic dysfunction which in turn leads to a high risk of mortality.

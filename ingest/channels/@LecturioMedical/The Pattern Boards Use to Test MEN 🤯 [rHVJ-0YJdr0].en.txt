The big problem for the boards with these syndromes really is remembering which combination of tumors goes with which men. That's where our classic trick of the P's and M's comes in. So the trick is quite simple. It's just a basic progression. As we go from men one to men 2 A to men to B. We start with a lot of P's with three Ps to be exact. And then we start losing Ps and gaining M's. In this way, men one has three P's, zero M's. Men 2 A has two P's, one M. And men 2B has just one P and two M's. It's really that simple. And yes, we'll plug in what each P and M stands for as we go through the syndromes, but just keep this general structure in mind as a reference to avoid getting lost.

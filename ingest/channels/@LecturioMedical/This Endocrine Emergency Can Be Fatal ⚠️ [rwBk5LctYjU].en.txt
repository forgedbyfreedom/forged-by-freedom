A 78-year-old man with a history of thyroidctomy presents unresponsive with hypothermia, hypotension, bradic cardia, hypoventilation, hyponetriia, hypoglycemia, diffuse edema and delayed relaxation of deep tendon reflexes. Which diagnosis best explains this presentation? Putting this all together, this most likely fits with a condition known as mixadema coma.

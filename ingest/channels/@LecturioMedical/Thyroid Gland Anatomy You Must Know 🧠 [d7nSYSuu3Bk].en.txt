Here are some important uh aspects about the recurrent lingial nerves. Both recurrent lingial nerves are branches of the vagus nerve and they will supply all the intrinsic lingial muscles with the exception of the cryco thyroid. They are called recurrent as they come off the vagus nerve and then travel back upwards to either side of the trachea and ultimately to their destination of the larynx. The left and right recurrent lingial nerves do have some anatomic relationship differences. The left recurrent lingial nerve loops around the aortic arch in the vicinity of the ligamentum arteriosum. Whereas the right recurrent lingial nerve loops more superly on the right side and loops under the right subclavian artery.

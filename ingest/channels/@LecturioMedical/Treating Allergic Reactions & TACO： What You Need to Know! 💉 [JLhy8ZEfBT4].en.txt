For allergic reactions, treatment depends on the severity. Mild cases like hives or itching can be managed with antihistamines. But if the patient develops anaphilaxis, you need to administer epinephrine start IV fluids and provide a ventilatory support as needed. With trial and taco, we also provide ventilatory support as needed. But in taco specifically, we also administer IV diuretics to reduce the fluid overload. Now one important note is that in order to prevent taco in future transfusions consider using as lower infusion rate or splitting the units.

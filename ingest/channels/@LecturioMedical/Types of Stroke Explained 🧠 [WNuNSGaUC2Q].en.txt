The different types of stroke lead to different symptom presentations and size of lesions on CT scans and MRI scans. Our emolic strokes, cardioolic strokes and thrombboic strokes result from a blood clot either in the heart or the corateed arteries that dislodges and travels up to the brain. Those are typically larger clots and they lodge in larger blood vessels. We often see larger territory strokes on imaging, larger territory of stroke in terms of the patient's symptoms and exam findings. And those large territory eskeemic lesions tip us off to be worried about an emolic phenomenon.

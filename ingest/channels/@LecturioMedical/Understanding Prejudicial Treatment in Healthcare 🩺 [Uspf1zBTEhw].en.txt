Usually what happens is we show unfair or prejuditial treatment to people in groups because of characteristics like race, gender, gender identity, age, sexual orientation, ability identification, social class, nationality, body composition, financial status, religious beliefs, practices, and affiliations or any other characteristic about certain individuals that we in our ethnosentric ways label as abnormal or not in alignment with what the norm is.

How does this all work physiologically? Starts with an infection. So the infection stimulates normal physiologic release of inflammatory mediators. However, in sepsis, this process becomes sort of self-stimulating and turns into a vicious cycle. Those mediators recruit all of the white blood cells that are in your body to fight infection. And those white blood cells will destroy healthy tissue. Now, when healthy tissue starts breaking down, the body's like, "Whoa, that's wrong, and will further release inflammatory mediators, which causes even more recruitment of white blood cells, which causes even more tissue damage, etc. This loss of normal homeostasis, the body's inability to shut down the inflammatory process, is what ultimately leads to organ damage and failure. And it's important to remember that this organ failure is not just caused by circulatory insufficiency.

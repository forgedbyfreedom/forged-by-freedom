When do you move from sepsis to septic shock? In order to diagnose septic shock, you first and foremost have to provide adequate fluid resuscitation. And septic shock is said to exist when despite adequate resuscitation, you have persistent hypotension requiring use of vasopressors and or an elevated lactate level which is greater than 2 millm moles per liter. The mortality of septic shock is greater than 40%. So when you move from sepsis to septic shock, you should recognize that you're also moving into a domain where your patients outcomes are much much more likely to be adverse. And you really need to uh be very aggressive and meticulous in how you treat them.

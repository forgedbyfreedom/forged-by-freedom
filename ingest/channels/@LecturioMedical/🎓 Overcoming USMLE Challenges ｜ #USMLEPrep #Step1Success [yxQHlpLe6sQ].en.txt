So, this comment was probably a butt. However, this touches on one very important thing that many people get wrong in their prep, including myself. You see, when I was prepping for step one, I always had this notion that if I just managed to go through every good resource out there, I'll knock it out of the park. I literally went through a couple of months when I was solving 120 questions a day, plus flashcards and videos from multiple materials at a time. But after going through all that, I realized that first of all, that's not sustainable. I burned out after a couple of months doing that. And secondly, it's not necessary. Studying, just like most things in life, has diminishing returns. Think about it. Are you really learning much when you're constantly falling asleep in the middle of a page or skipping through questions just to tell yourself that you have reviewed them? Remember guys, when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure. Your target is to learn, not to go through an arbitrary number of study materials.

in 1883 a tornado devastated the small town of Rochester Minnesota Dr William W M and mother Alfred Mo joined forces to care for the wounded in a makeshift infirmary as the town was being rebuilt mother Alfred paid a visit to Dr Mayo in his office above a drugstore she proposed that two of them join forces again this time to build a hospital years later Dr Mayo recall that he found the idea impractical but mother Alfred was never a woman to be easily dissuaded by the time she left Dr Mayo's office mother Alfred had struck a deal if she could raise the money to build a hospital he would be the doctor in charge 6 years later St Mary's hospital opened its doors beginning a new era in medicine a leap of faith imagin the meeting of these two strong willed Pioneers a dramatic film from Mayo Clinic Heritage films with generous support from Gerald and Henrietta renor

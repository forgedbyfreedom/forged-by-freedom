[Dr. Johnson] When I see my shoes, I think about early morning practices, and hard work and a lot of dedication. After my freshman year of college, I started training for triathlons, and came back my sophomore year with an injury. I had a lot of support with my sports medicine team. Being an athlete lets me connect with my patients on a different level, so I can empathize with them about their love for their sport and the recovery process. I think that is really helpful to shift their focus on their recovery, rather than their focus on their injury.

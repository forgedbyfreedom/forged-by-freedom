I'm Dr Shane shapy a physician from Mayo Clinic Florida Department of orthopedic surgery with information about an exciting regenerative medicine clinical research trial to treat knee arthritis osteoarthritis or degenerative joint disease is a degenerative condition of joints affecting millions of patients it causes pain and significant functional limitations with subsequent deterioration in quality of life while we have many treatments there is no non-surgical cure and the condition often progresses to the point where joint replacement is required at Mayo Clinic and the Mayo Center for regenerative medicine we are studying several biologically based non-surgical treatments for osteoarthritis one such treatment is the harvesting of a patient's own stem cells from their bone marrow in our procedure we draw cellular Rich bone marrow from both sides of the pelvis we then filter the resulting product and concentrate the stem cells and their corresponding growth factors using an ultrasound to image the knee joint we are then able to precisely inject the cells into the arthritic knee we are currently demonstrating that this procedure is safe and can relieve pain we also hope to be able to slow the progression of the degenerate joint disease and perhaps even one day regrow cartilage within the arthritic joint

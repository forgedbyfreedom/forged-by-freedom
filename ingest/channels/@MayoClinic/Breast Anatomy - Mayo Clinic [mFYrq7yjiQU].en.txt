When talking about breast cancer, it's helpful to understand the different parts that make up your breasts. Your breasts go from your collarbone to your bra line, and from your breastbone into your underarms. Each breast has: Nipple, which is the outlet for milk that can be made by the breast. An Areola, which is the ring of color tissue around the nipple. Ducts, these are thin tubes that can carry milk from the lobules to the nipple. Lobules, these are the glands that make the milk. Lymph nodes, which are small glands found around the body that filter body fluids. The lymph nodes most often affected by breast cancer are located under the arms. Lymph vessels, these are tiny tubes that carry fluid from the breast tissue to the lymph nodes under the arms.

I wanted to see how we can build a program where we would bring some, if not, most of the cancer care to patients in their own homes. With our Cancer Care Beyond Walls, we have ability to do things at home, to not have to sacrifice one day every two weeks, you know, to come here for appointments, whether that is chemotherapy or immunotherapy, doing some blood tests at home, being able to do virtual visits and only reserve the hospital admissions or the clinic infusions for the most complex aspects of the cancer care. I think that will empower the patients further to integrate cancer care in their lives rather than us asking them to put their lives on hold for cancer care. Putting the patient first - that's what we're all about.

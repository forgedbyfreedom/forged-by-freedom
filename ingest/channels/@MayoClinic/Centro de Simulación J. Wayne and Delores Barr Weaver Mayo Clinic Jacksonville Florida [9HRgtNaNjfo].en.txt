Hi friends, I'm Canelita, and I'm at Clínic here in Jacksonville, Florida, with Joseph Fuga, administrator of the simulation center at Medio Planet, and Laura Pacheco, senior coordinator of health science programs. Today I want to show you the Jay Wayne Simulation Center. During the tour, Joseph explains that this space provides training in individual and team skills for resident physicians, students, nurses, and allied health professionals at Medio Planet, as well as for students from other organizations. Training in the simulation center builds confidence and encourages medical professionals and support teams to be more collaborative and work better as a team for the benefit of the patient. It also teaches them to be more reflective so they can deliver knowledge and learning in a safer and more effective way to improve the patient experience. The rooms are exact replicas of the clinic's facilities, providing a safe and comfortable learning experience for everyone. It has mannequins and even actors who simulate patient situations to make it feel as realistic as possible. The rooms include a task training room, a clinical exam room, an operating room, an emergency room, a medical-surgical suite, and an intensive care unit. Joseph also showed us the anatomy table in the conference rooms.

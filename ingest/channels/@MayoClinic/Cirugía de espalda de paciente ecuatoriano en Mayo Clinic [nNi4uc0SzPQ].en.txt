I came to Miami on a family trip, a vacation. My youngest son, who is now 13, was on vacation, and we were in Miami when I had a flare-up of my lower back problem, which I had been diagnosed with about a year ago. The flare-up was so severe that I was forced to go to an emergency room in Miami. I think that because of the size of the city, the medical care is different, which is why, feeling so bad, I, like my father, felt that I had to come to the Mayo Clinic for a check-up and to get a diagnosis of what was wrong with me. I came in through the emergency room on a Sunday night. I was seen immediately, and two days later, I was being treated by the neurosurgeon, Dr. Vin, who performed all the necessary tests and evaluations. We determined that in my case, the only solution was to proceed with surgery. This caused me a lot of anxiety because I knew they were going to open up my back.  The fact that I was going to have surgery caused me so much anxiety that my children had to come and give me courage. Finally, I had the surgery, and well, immediately after I came out of the operation, the pain was gone. It was wonderful, really. Dr. Bing and his entire team, upholding the principles of the Mario Clinic, treated me wonderfully. They removed a herniated disc and part of the bone to free the nerve, which was causing me so much pain—so much pain that in the days before the operation I was in a wheelchair. I couldn't walk. The pain was very intense. The care at the hospital was excellent. I was in the hospital for 48 hours; I didn't want to go home. I felt pampered, and I was able to walk from the first day. The next day, the pain that brought me here was gone. Of course, there are some post-surgical discomforts, which were managed very well with medication, and here I am. I feel very well.

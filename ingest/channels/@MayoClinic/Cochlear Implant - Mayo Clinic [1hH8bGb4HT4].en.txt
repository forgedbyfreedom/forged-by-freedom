a cochlear implant is an electronic device for restoring hearing and deafness there are two components of the device the internal component requires surgery to place an electrode array within the inner ear the external device is worn like a hearing aid and is easy to take off both components are needed in order for the device to work the cochlear implant is used only in patients who are completely deaf in both ears to the point that even the strongest hearing aids do not help the surgery for the internal component is done under a general anesthetic and takes about two hours to perform this surgery is an outpatient procedure which means the patient goes home from the recovery room there is a wraparound headdressing that is removed the next day in the office after two or three weeks healing is sufficient to put on the external component the external component also known as the speech processor is then programmed by the audiologist to begin the process of restoring some hearing

the surgical experience at Mayo Clinic starts even as early as the pgy one year we have multiple rotations where we get the chance to have Hands-On experiences in the operating room for example one of the rotations we do as pgy ones is oculoplastics where we get exposure to Common procedures like blepharoplasty and ptosis repair and by the end of that rotation there were several times where I'm working across the table from one of our Consultants doing uh a fair amount of the case with her watching over us and so it's been a really cool experience we also do a rotation in cornea where we get our first exposure to operating under the microscope and by the end of the rotation we have been able to participate in some of the early steps for cataract surgery and just get a hands-on experience for what it's like to operate under the microscope and lastly even though we don't necessarily do this as a part of our Ophthalmology rotations we also spend some time with plastic surgeons doing similar procedures for craniofacial plastic surgery where we get some experience with relevant procedures like brow lifts or other procedures having to do with the eye or the orbit and so between those three experiences I feel like by the end of my first year of residency I'd already had a fair amount of exposure to the operating room

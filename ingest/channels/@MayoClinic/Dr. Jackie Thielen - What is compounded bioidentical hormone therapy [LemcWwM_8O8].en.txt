the term bioidentical hormone therapy actually originated as a marketing term for custom compounded hormones hormone preparations prescribed by a clinician and compounded in a pharmacy for an individual patient bioidentical hormones are hormones that have a chemical structure similar to those produced in the body many women do not realize that these so-called bioidentical hormones do not have to be custom compounded in fact many fda approved hormones meet the definition of bioidentical although compounding hormones allows for products to be made without the binders dyes and preservatives found in some commercially available products these products are not tested for safety and effectiveness nor are they closely monitored although some active ingredients may meet certain quality and safety standards the mixtures are not government approved or regulated there is a lack of oversight for quality purity and consistency they may not even contain the prescribed amounts of hormones which could be dangerous for example if the amount of progesterone is too low relative to the amount of estrogen there may not be enough protection against endometrial cancer putting you at risk it is important to note that there are many well-tested fda approved hormone products commercially available from retail pharmacies in a variety of doses and routes of administration such as pills patches gels sprays and vaginal preparations that will allow you and your healthcare provider to customize your therapy to meet your individual needs there are a few reasons to use custom compounded hormone therapy over an fda approved product one such scenario would be if a woman were to have an allergy to a particular component of the fda approved product in which case the pharmacist could create a similar product without the ingredient the patient was allergic to

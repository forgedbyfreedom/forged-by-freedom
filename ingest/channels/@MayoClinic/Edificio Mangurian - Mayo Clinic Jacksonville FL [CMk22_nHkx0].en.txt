Hi everyone! I'm Canelita, and today we'll be touring the Magnurian Building at Meliot Cleny in Jacksonville, Florida. This building opened in August 2018 and expanded the hospital, integrating services for patients with complex illnesses such as cancer, neurological, and neurosurgical diseases. The Meliot Cleny staff takes care of every detail to ensure the highest quality experience and care for their patients. For example, this teddy bear is used to explain to children how a patient celebrates the completion of their cancer treatment by ringing that bell and touching this wall, which we were told has healing properties. Some of the health services available in the Magnurian Building include oncology and neurological care, a chemotherapy infusion center with over 43 spacious suites, clinical studies offices, and laboratory services. The building also has a dedicated space for chemotherapy, a pharmacy, one floor for neurology and neurosurgery, and two floors for hematology and oncology.

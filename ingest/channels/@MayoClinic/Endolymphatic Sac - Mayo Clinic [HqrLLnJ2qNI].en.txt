decompression of the endolymphatic sac is a surgical procedure that can help diminish the frequency and severity of meniere's attacks of vertigo the endolymphatic sac is the part of the membrane structure of the deep inner ear that affects the amount of fluid in this deep inner ear the endolymphatic sac sits in the mastoid bone of the skull behind the ear surgery is done to remove the bone from around the endolymphatic sac the surgery takes about an hour to complete it is done under a general anesthetic this surgery is an outpatient procedure which means the patient goes home from the recovery room a simple ear dressing is applied at surgery and removed the next day

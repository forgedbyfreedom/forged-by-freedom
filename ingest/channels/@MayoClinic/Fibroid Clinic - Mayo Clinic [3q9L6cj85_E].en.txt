uterine fibroids also called myomas or leiomyomas are smooth muscle growth of the uterus that can occur in up to 70% of women sometimes a woman may have fibroids and not have any symptoms at all but in other cases fibroids can be associated with abnormal bleeding pelvic pressure or fertility concerns the fibroid clinic within the Mayo Clinic's division of gynecology it provides a full range of counseling and treatment options for uterine fibroids for some women medications may be very helpful to control heavy or abnormal bleeding for other women who want to control both bleeding and pelvic pressure concerns there are several non-invasive procedures which can shrink fibroids and help alleviate these symptoms for others still surgical removal of the fibroid or the uterus may be the best option our surgeons are skilled in minimally invasive fibroid procedures including radiofrequency ablation hysteroscopy laparoscopic and robotic operations as well as traditional open surgical treatments it is our goal to help educate women about all the possible treatments and provide guidance about which option is right for them we recognize that it can sometimes feel overwhelming when trying to decide upon the best treatment plan and we do all that we can to help patients navigate this process in addition to providing in-person consultations we also offer virtual consultations by telephone or video conference this can be a great option especially for folks who are coming from a distance or who are limiting travel with a virtual consultation we can start developing your individualized treatment plan and prioritize the next steps including arranging for any necessary imaging or exams before you make the trip to Mayo Clinic whatever the situation we are here to help if

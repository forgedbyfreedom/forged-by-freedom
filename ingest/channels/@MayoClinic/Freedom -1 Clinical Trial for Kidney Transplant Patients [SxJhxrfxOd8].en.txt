hi my name is Lauren and I'm a clinical research coordinator for the kidney transplant program at Mayo Clinic in Rochester Minnesota I'd like to talk about an ongoing research study for patients who are planning to have a living donor kidney transplant the goal of this study is to eliminate immunosuppression for transplant recipients at one year after transplantation kidney transplant recipients require immunosuppressive medications to prevent rejection however being on immunosuppression for years is expensive and increases the risk of complications such as infection and cancer for many years transplant doctors have been researching methods to safely stop immunosuppression without rejection also known as immune tolerance these tolerance Studies have been improved over the past decade and are now being tested in a multi-center randomized trial called the freedom one trial Mayo Clinic Rochester and Arizona are currently participating in this study this trial is randomized which means some participants will receive just a normal kidney transplant with a standard immunosuppression but those who are randomized to the treatment arm will need their donor to make an extra visit to Mayo Clinic to donate stem cells these are the cells that help develop tolerance to the kidney being in the treatment group will delay the kidney transplant a couple of months due to the extra therapy like any research study there are potential risks but there is also a long list of potential benefits such as taking less pills spending less money on medication less time at the doctor's office less side effects and complications better long-term graft outcomes and ultimately more freedom to live life with the combined efforts of study team members doctors and several Mayo Clinic departments we are excited to have the study available to those who are eligible we are hopeful that this treatment will eventually become best practice for transplant patients and relieve the burden of immunosuppressive medications if you are planning to have a living donor kidney transplant and are interested in getting more information please click on the link provided thank you

The Summer Lab Science Program is a ten week paid work experience for students completing their third year of undergraduate studies in medical laboratory science, biological sciences, or chemistry. Students work alongside laboratory professionals and may perform a variety of pre analytical functions, work on special projects, and observe high complexity testing. What I love most about the Summer Lab Science program was that it allowed me to better understand the compassion and humility required to take care of patients. I'm Garrett Gordy, and after completing the Summer Lab Science program, I am now a technical specialist too at Mayo Clinic.

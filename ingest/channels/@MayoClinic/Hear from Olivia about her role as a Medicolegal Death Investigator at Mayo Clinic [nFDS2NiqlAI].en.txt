As a Medicolegal Death Investigator, I collaborate with law enforcement to examine unnatural deaths and decide if autopsies are needed. When a death is reported, I'm dispatched to the scene. Initially, I consult with law enforcement to gather preliminary information. I then speak with any present family members to understand the desdants social and medical history. After collecting essential details, I conduct a thorough examination of the body and the surroundings, documenting any signs of injury, rigor mortis, and liver Mortis, as well as inspecting substances and objects found nearby. Based on my findings, I determine the necessity of an autopsy. If required, arrangements are made to transport the body to Mayo Clinic for further examination. If not, the body is sent to a chosen funeral home. I'm Olivia adrnowitz, and I'm a Medicolegal Death Investigator at Mayo Clinic.

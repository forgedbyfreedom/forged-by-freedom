handling skills improve conditioning enhance fine motor skills and much more here are three reasons to use a skating treadmill to improve your performance on the ice the skating treadmill allows the coach the ability to watch each stride in close proximity without worrying about skating alongside the athlete the athlete also has the benefit of looking ahead into the mirror to see what kind of arm swing leg extension and recovery they have with each stride giving immediate feedback on good versus poor strides this helps the athlete auto correct themselves learning what it feels like when they are skating efficiently using our dart fish technology we can capture your stride on video immediately break down your movement mechanics and start to work on improving the way you move your body and increase your skating efficiency your skating stride can affect the power you're able to generate on the ice ultimately impacting your ability to achieve your top speed a faulty stride can also impair your energy output causing you to fatigue quicker the skating treadmill will not allow poor skating technique simply put if there are any flaws in the stride the treadmill will magnify and expose any weakness or areas that need improvement we use dissociation between the upper body and lower body to maintain good mechanics and fundamentals while the upper body is performing a different task other than just the skating motion athletes are able to progress from a medicine ball hold into puck handling while skating and ultimately reaching top speeds with the puck the key is to maintain all good fundamentals with and without the puck there is no better tool to help increase an athlete's conditioning level as athletes become more fatigued you can see breakdowns in their strides the athlete has to maintain good stride mechanics in order to stay on the treadmill if an athlete becomes fatigued and starts skating poorly they will have to do more work than needed in order to stay on the treadmill ultimately making themselves fatigue faster using interval training or sprints on the treadmill we can make sure all mechanics and fundamentals remain consistent at top end speeds as well as

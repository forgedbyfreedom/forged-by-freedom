The first way would be with a drip bag. This would allow me to hang a bag on an IV pole, take my container of food and pour it into the bag, and then over a 30 to 60 minute period of time while watching the news or reading the paper, that would slowly go into my feeding tube. People who are more active or maybe have a little bit busier schedule might like to feed with a syringe. So, this would allow me to take the syringe and put it on the end of my feeding tube and take my can of food and just pour that into my tube.

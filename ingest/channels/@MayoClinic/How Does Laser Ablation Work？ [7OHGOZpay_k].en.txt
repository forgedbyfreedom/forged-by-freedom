if you have varicose veins in your legs laser ablation may be the right treatment for you this is an outpatient procedure to seal shut the veins of your legs that are causing your varicose veins this is performed with a numbing medication and a tiny Nick in the skin a catheter that is about the size of a piece of spaghetti is then inserted into the leg vein and then the vein is closed with heat as the catheter is being removed the vein closes you will wear a compression stocking for 2 weeks after the procedure to help the vein close but you can get back to your usual activities the next day if you would like to discuss your symptoms of varicose veins please ask for an appointment with me in Rochester Minnesota at mayoclinic.org

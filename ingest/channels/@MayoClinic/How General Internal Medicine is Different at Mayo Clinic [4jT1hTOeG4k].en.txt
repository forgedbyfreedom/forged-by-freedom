I didn't even know, actually, that GIM existed as it is - I had just, from the outside, assumed that internal medicine here was like internal medicine elsewhere. General Internal Medicine at Mayo Clinic is really unique. We are specialist generalists, we really don't do primary care at all. There are only two other places in the entire country that do medicine like this. We as generalists also kind of function as specialists and consultants and that we get to sort of carve out our own niche and find our own way to make a difference and impact. GIM practice in Mayo is probably the most unique practice I've ever heard of. It's that solid middle ground for people who have those chronic, complicated conditions, but don't really have a direction to go to. They come to us.

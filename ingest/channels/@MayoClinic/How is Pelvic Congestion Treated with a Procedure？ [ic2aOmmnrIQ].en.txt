if you have been diagnosed with pelvic congestion syndrome you have varicose veins deep inside your pelvis you may be a candidate for a minimally invasive procedure to close those varicose veins called gonadal vein embolization or ovarian vein embolization this procedure involves a small Nick in the skin at your groin or your neck to allow a spaghetti noodle sized catheter to be advanced into the varicose veins of the pelvis then these veins can be closed with irritating medication and small metallic coils if you would like to discuss your symptoms of pelvic congestion please ask for an appointment with me in Rochester Minnesota at Mayo Clinic org

[Dr. Johnson] When I see my shoes, I think about early morning practices and hard work and a lot of dedication. I started swimming when I was three years old and I kept that up through high school and then into college at Northwestern. Being an athlete lets me connect with my patients on a different level, so I can empathize with them about their love for their sport and about their recovery process. The most important part of establishing trust with a patient is the understanding what's important to them and what their goals are, 'cause every patient's goals are different. I think what's unique about the Mayo Clinic Sports Medicine team is that we have a multidisciplinary group. We have the physicians, the physical therapists, athletic trainers. We all work with the team and use their expertise to look at a patient's recovery from multiple aspects. That teamwork is really important in getting an athlete back to play. (upbeat pop music)

Many men don't realize how powerful prevention can be. They may intuitively know that they should pay attention to diet and exercise, but these things are vitally important. They can save your life. One of the reasons that men don't pay attention to prevention is that success in prevention is when nothing happens. You don't see the heart attack that you prevented or the cancer, for example. Now, when something does happen, it gets people's attention and people realize that they need to beef up their efforts, so to speak, in diet and exercise and other lifestyle habits, but even then, it's hard to sustain these things. Realize that the little things you do every day in healthy lifestyle habits can have a huge effect long term. One of the reasons that men may not appreciate prevention as much as women is that women more often see their health care provider at a younger age for routine examinations. At a young age, this is when it's really important to establish healthy lifestyle habits that continue as you age and prevent some of the chronic diseases that are killing people over the long term. Prevention can not only reduce the risk of chronic diseases long term, but it can improve the quality of life immediately. Feeling better, having more energy, improved mood. Many of my patients say when they were more active, when they weighed less, they felt better. Men need to listen to that to encourage them to establish healthy lifestyle habits as well as preventing disease down the road. One of the biggest myths about establishing healthy lifestyle habits is that it's drudgery. It doesn't have to be. It can be enjoyable to incorporate these things into your everyday life and you get used to them. See your health care provider now for advice and recommendations. It could save your life.

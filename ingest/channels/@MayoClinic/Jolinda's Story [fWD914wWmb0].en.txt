I found out when I was 12 that I tested positive for PKD. PKD is a disease where you start growing non-cancerous cysts in your kidneys. I ended up choosing Mayo Clinic because they really had a lot of innovation going on. One of the things that I was very interested in was having my old kidneys taken out. When I had my new transplanted kidney put in, this was cutting-edge. I get another 40 years! It was really cool to be part of something that was pioneering, especially at such an incredible medical institution.

Because I was very fortunate to have come to the Mayo Clinic and received the transplant. When we were lucky enough to be able to stay here in this place, which is the Penguin or the help house, when you receive a transplant, your life changes completely. We have to be very careful, and many places that perform transplants don't have a place like this to help us. We just have to be very careful not to be in contact with sick people, not to be exposed to any bacteria or anything. The rooms here are made for that, and at the same time, the cost is minimal. Also, this place is very comfortable; it has all the amenities one needs. We are very close to the hospital because when I came here, we stayed here, and I was here for four months. We would get together so that the volunteers could come and help us patients. That's how it is when you volunteer. Today, I'm volunteering, and I'm returning—a great milestone. Of all the things I've received, this is my way of saying thank you. The Mayo Clinic has been something very beautiful for me, and it's wonderful that the doctors are doctors who care a lot about their patients. They want the best for everyone; what's most important to them is...  It was about my health, it was about feeling that I was here, that I knew where I had been, that I was here to be helped. But they are humble, they are the ones who are not ashamed to explain things in detail, in words that one can understand, and it is the best thing that happened to me, coming to the Mayo Clinic.

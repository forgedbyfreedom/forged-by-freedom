I'm Dr. Lindsey Zhang. I'm a colon and rectal surgeon at Mayo Clinic. The road to becoming a physician and surgeon is not an easy one by any means, but I have been motivated and driven by the incredible privilege that I receive in taking care of patients at their most vulnerable. In my field in colon and rectal surgery, I see a huge array of different issues, whether it's newly diagnosed or recurrent cancers, or issues that are benign, such as recurrent rectal prolapse or diverticulitis. I feel so honored that I have the opportunity to help patients improve their quality of life. For me, putting the needs of the patient first means knowing and respecting the values of the patient. So that means I need to listen to the patient, communicate with them, and figure out what matters to them. As a surgeon, I'm hyper-aware that while I strive to help all people with what I recommend, surgery can have a real large lasting impact on a patient and so my goal is always to make sure that what I offer to you aligns with what you want as a patient.

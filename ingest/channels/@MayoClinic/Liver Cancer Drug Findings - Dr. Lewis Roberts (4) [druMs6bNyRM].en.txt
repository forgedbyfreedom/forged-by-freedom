i think it's very important for people to have an awareness of whether they are at particular risk for for development of liver cancer because if they have cirrhosis of the liver particularly either from hepatitis b or hepatitis c virus infection from alcohol use or from non-alcoholic fatty liver disease then they may be at risk and when we identify individuals who are at risk we usually will offer them screening of the liver using an ultrasound test every six months so that the liver cancers if they develop can be detected early when our treatments are most effective

Polymyalgia rheumatica is an autoimmune disease that affects persons over the age of 50. It is a disease that comes on often very suddenly and can cause severe stiffness of the shoulders and the hips as well as other parts of the body. People with polymyalgia rheumatica often experience weight loss and sometimes even low-grade fevers. The average age of getting polymyalgia rheumatica is about 75. So it's definitely a disease affecting older people. About 2/3 to 3/4 of patients with polymyalgroomatica are women. We don't know what causes polymyalgia, but we know that the disease is due to overactivity of the immune system. Consequently, the treatment for polymyalgia emphasizes medications that calm down the overactive immune system. The main treatment for polymyalgia rheumatica is gluccocorttoicosteroids or prennazone. Typically we use the prennazone for between one to three years and sometimes though unfortunately patients may have polymyalgia rheumatica even longer and so treatment for more than three years and even up to nine or 10 years and sometimes longer is necessary.

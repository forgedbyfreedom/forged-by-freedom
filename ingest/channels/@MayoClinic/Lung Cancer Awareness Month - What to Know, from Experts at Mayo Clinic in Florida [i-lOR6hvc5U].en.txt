We're in November, that's Lung Cancer Awareness Month. When we think about lung cancer, we often think about lung cancer as a cancer that's seen in smokers or heavy smokers. Yes, smoking is a risk factor for lung cancer. Based on recent epidemiological data, we are actually seeing more and more patients who are never smokers having lung cancer. Usually lung cancer is very silent until it becomes more advanced. Patients can have chronic cough and sometimes they can have chest pain. We have gone a long way in the progress against lung cancer. So many research and therapeutic advancement. Patients with lung cancer now living longer, living better. We also need to keep advocating for lung cancer screening. A CT scan of the chest for certain patient population who smoke and meet certain criteria, could actually detect early lung cancer and improve the outcomes and improve survival. We need to keep the effort going. We need to work all together, united. I truly believe it is an era of hope for patients with lung cancer because we can make a difference in people's life.

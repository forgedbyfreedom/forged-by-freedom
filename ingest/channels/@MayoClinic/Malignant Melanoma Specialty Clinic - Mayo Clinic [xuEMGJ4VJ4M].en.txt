my name is chris baum in the department of dermatology at the mayo clinic and i'm here today to tell you about the malignant melanoma specialty clinic that is offered by the mayo clinic the mayo clinic has a long-standing history of providing outstanding care for patients with a diagnosis of malignant melanoma part of this is due in large part to the fact that the mayo clinic is home to a comprehensive cancer center which means that the highest quality patient care education and research are available to each patient each patient with a diagnosis of melanoma at the mayo clinic is approached in an efficient expedited expedited patient-centered and multi-disciplinary fashion which includes expertise from all areas of melanoma including dermatology dermatopathology general surgery head and neck surgery plastic surgery oculoplastic surgery nursing and pharmacy for each patient that is seen in the melanoma specialty clinic several steps are taken the first step is the confirmation or reassessment of the diagnosis this means that each patient with a new diagnosis of melanoma has the biopsy specimen reviewed by a dermatopathologist this is done because pigmented lesions are often difficult and challenging to diagnose therefore each specimen is reviewed in order to either confirm or establish an accurate diagnosis once the pathology has been reviewed every patient will be offered a complete physical examination that includes a full skin exam as well as palpation of lymph nodes depending upon the compilation of data provided by the biopsy as well as physical examination additional staging tests including blood tests and imaging may be ordered once all of this data has been accumulated and reviewed a patient-centered treatment plan will be devised and reviewed with the patient the vast majority of patients will be offered and recommended wide local excision which includes surgery to remove the melanoma as well as a margin of healthy appearing tissue some patients in addition may be offered sentinel lymph node biopsy all of these surgical specimens will be reviewed accurately and in depth by our pathologists and each surgery is performed in the highest quality standards in a safe and effective manner the next and perhaps just as important step as any of the others mentioned before is patient education every patient who will be seen in the melanoma specialty clinic at the mayo clinic will be educated thoroughly in order to help them understand their disease as well as be prepared to move forward in necessary steps for prevention of further skin cancers down the road and finally at the end of each session patients will be recommended appropriate follow-up depending on their stage of melanoma so in summary i'm here to tell you about an exciting opportunity at the mayo clinic for patients with melanoma who can be seen in the melanoma specialty clinic which is dedicated and designed to provide expedited world-class care with patients with malignant melanoma

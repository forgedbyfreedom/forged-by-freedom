General Internal Medicine at Mayo Clinic comprises the best of both outpatient and the opportunity to practice inpatient care. Every day when I go and see a new patient, I'm seeing something new. So this really keeps my interest in General Internal Medicine alive because I am learning along when I'm treating my patient and when I'm talking to my other consultants on a daily basis. When a patient comes here, they see me first in the consultative clinic. They already have a pre-triaged appointments and lab tests in that same week. I also use point of care ultrasound. There are recent publications that show that when physicians use point of care ultrasound, they cut down on the time period on waiting for tests by as much as 23 hours on the inpatient side. By using point of care ultrasound, I'm able to get to that answer much faster, and then that helps me to get to the treatment much faster. At Mayo Clinic, we are housed under one umbrella. Other consultants are just a phone call away. We sometimes even meet them in physician lounges and bounce questions off and get answers at a much more faster manner, and that is a big advantage for my patients, which is very hard to replicate outside of Mayo Clinic.

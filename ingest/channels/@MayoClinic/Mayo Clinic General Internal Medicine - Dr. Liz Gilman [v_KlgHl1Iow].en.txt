I am a runner and every day I run outside in Rochester, Minnesota, 365 days a year, and so that is my me time. It is a very friendly community. It's very active, so there's a lot of activities to do all year round, just because I think Minnesotans are active people. I actually came here and did a pathology residency first and actually spent some time at Mayo Clinic Arizona before coming back and doing internal medicine. I really enjoy General Internal Medicine for its variety and also because of the complexity of the patients we take care of. We are specialist generalists. It's almost like hospital medicine in an outpatient setting. There are only two other places in the entire country that do medicine like this. We see enough complex patients that we can change their trajectory in healthcare, and I think that that fuels my passion for patient care because we can continue to make a difference in people's lives and then have better outcomes for them later on.

muscle groups are surrounded or compartmentalized by a layer of tissue called fascia when we exercise blood flow to our muscle increases causing them to expand for most people the fascia also expands but in others that fascia remains wrapped tight around the muscle that tightness in that compartment can produce pain in the lower legs especially the outside aspect of the lower legs it also can cause some weakness in some muscle some people may even experience a drop of their foot dr edward laskowski is a specialist in physical medicine and rehabilitation and sports medicine he says treatment starts with things like activity modification and exercises to increase strength and flexibility but if the problem persists the fascia has to be released with a surgical procedure a newer procedure being researched for that is called ultrasound guided fasciotomy with an ultrasound machine we can actually see that tight envelope of muscle and release it with a very small tool underneath the skin so the surgery is not as invasive similarly botox or botulinum toxin injections have shown promise as a non-surgical treatment option and that means that patients can get their exercise back on track sooner for the mayo clinic news network i'm joel streed

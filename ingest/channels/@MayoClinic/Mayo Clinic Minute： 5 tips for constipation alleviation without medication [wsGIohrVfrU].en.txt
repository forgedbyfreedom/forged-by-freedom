Roughly 42 million people suffer from constipation. Physical therapist Laura Myhoffer has five tips to help get things moving. The first is I like to look at how much water is someone getting. Drink half of your body weight in fluid ounces. So if you weigh 150 lbs, drink 75 ounces a day. Any liquid will do, but water is best. Two, fiber. Women should get between 21 to 25 gram of insoluble fiber a day. Men from 30 to 38 g. Grab fruits, veggies, whole grains, and nuts. Three, exercise. The more we sit, the more our gut doesn't move and doesn't process our food. Four, reduce stress. Everyday stresses blocks our brain's ability to communicate with our gut to say, "Hey, it's okay. It's time to rest, repose, relax, digest that food." And five, if you've got to go, go. Don't put it off. For the Mayo Clinic News Network, I'm Vivian Williams.

Women, listen up. You need to pay attention to your heart health because your risk of cardiovascular disease is real. Mayo Clinic experts say knowing your heart numbers can help reduce that risk. Number one, they should know their blood pressure. Dr. Sharon Hayes says ideal numbers are 120 over 80 and lower. Number two, your lipid profile. We'll break that one down. LDL or bad cholesterol should be 100 or less. HDL or good cholesterol should be 60 or higher. Triglycerides 150 or less. Number three is weight. A body mass index of less than 25. Number four is steps. How many do you take every day? This gets back to the physical activity, which is one of the most important things you can do. Aim for more than 10,000 steps a day. Number five, fruits and vegetables. Eat five to nine servings every day. And number six, sleep. If you sleep less than 6 hours or greater than 9 hours, that may increase your risk of cardiovascular disease. Talk to your health care provider about getting your numbers to where they ought to be.

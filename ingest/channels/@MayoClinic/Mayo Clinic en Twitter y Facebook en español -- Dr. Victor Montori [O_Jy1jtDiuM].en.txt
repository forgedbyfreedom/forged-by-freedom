The Mayo Clinic is very interested in connecting with Spanish-speaking patients, physicians, and other healthcare professionals. The way we've done this in English has been through our channels on Facebook, YouTube, and Twitter, using these social media platforms to connect with our patients. But in Spanish, there's much less interest or participation from hospitals and the healthcare system. We wanted to show leadership in this area and actively connect with our Spanish-speaking patients and colleagues through a new channel on Twitter and a new channel on Facebook in Spanish. Our fundamental interest is, first, to provide truthful, honest, and highly credible information to better serve the interests of our patients and colleagues. But it's also critically important for us to listen to and learn the needs and preferences of Spanish-speaking patients so that we can more directly, appropriately, and effectively meet those needs, which is the most important mission of the Mayo Clinic: to meet the needs of patients above all other needs and interests. The new channel...  The Spanish language of Facebook and Twitter will allow us to achieve this goal in the new world, not only from a physical point of view in the clinic when patients or doctors approach us, but also in the online world.

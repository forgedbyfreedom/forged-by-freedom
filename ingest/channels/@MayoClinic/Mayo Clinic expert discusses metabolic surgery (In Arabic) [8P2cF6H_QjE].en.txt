Currently, we view obesity as a disease, and to combat it, we must utilize all available tools. This could involve diet, exercise, and physical activity; it could involve GLP medications; and it could even require surgery. We know that obesity is a serious condition, meaning that if we only address it with diet and exercise, most patients who rely on this approach will regain weight later, even if they lose weight. Of all the available methods, diet and exercise are the least effective; surgery is the most effective, and medications fall somewhere in between. GLP medications, such as ozempic and Menger Pound, are among the many tools we can use to address obesity. We know that some patients only need diet and exercise; these are a small percentage. Another percentage are patients whose obesity is a serious condition, a severe form of the disease. These patients may benefit from other methods.  The medications in the largest group are for patients with severe obesity. For these patients, surgery is the best treatment method. A fourth percentage of patients require a combination of all these tools to address their condition. This is a misconception; it's a misunderstanding that GLP-1 and these medications will replace the other. Firstly, they are equally effective, but they also have side effects. Not all patients can take medication for health reasons. Therefore, no single tool replaces another. On the contrary, the approach depends on each tool being suitable for a specific patient. For some patients, we need to combine all these tools to overcome their obesity.

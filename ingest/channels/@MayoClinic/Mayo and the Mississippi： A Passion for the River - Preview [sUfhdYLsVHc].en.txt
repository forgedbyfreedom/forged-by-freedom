Dr. William J. Mayo had a special love for the Mississippi River. When we're on the river, he said, there's no telephone to call us away. It's an ideal place to rest and work. For more than 25 years, Dr. Will's boats were a familiar site on the river. He invited friends, family, colleagues, and patients along for the run. Now, an award-winning documentary film tells the story of Dr. Will's boats, featuring newly discovered photographs, films, artifacts, and stories from the log books. Relive the weekend trips, and extended cruises that refresh the soul and strengthen the bonds of friendship. A passion for the river, the story of Mayo in the Mississippi. Now available from Mayo Clinic Heritage Films with generous support from John T. and Lillian G. Matthews.

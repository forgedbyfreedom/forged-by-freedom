Welcome to the Office of wellness and Academic Support here at the Mayo Clinic College of Medicine and Science. We hope your time at the Mayo Clinic is exciting, challenging, and fulfilling all at once. But as rewarding as your educational journey may be, it's also completely normal to experience some distress along the way. If you find yourself contending with emotional difficulties, our professional counselors are here to help. Counseling services are offered at no additional cost and are confidential with a few legal exceptions that your counselor will explain during your first visit. Furthermore, counseling records are kept separate from academic and medical records. Some of the most common reasons learners seek support include stress, anxiety, depression, and balancing one's personal, professional, and academic life. Reaching out isn't always easy, but we're here to help you prioritize your mental health through virtual or in-person appointments. To learn more or to make an appointment, visit the following URL, college.mayo.edu/counseling-services [MUSIC PLAYING]

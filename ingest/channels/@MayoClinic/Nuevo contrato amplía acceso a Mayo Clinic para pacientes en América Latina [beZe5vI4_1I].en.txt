This contract with Global Excel Management is a watershed moment in patient access to the Mayo Clinic in Latin America and Asia. In the past, many of our patients wanted to come to the Mayo Clinic, but we were outside the network, which meant a greater financial outlay due to higher co-insurance and deductibles. Today, with this new contract, access is simpler and the financial impact on our patients is less. It covers a wide range of countries, including Mexico, Colombia, Costa Rica, Guatemala, and Honduras, among others. It also provides access to the three Mayo Clinic medical centers located in Florida, Arizona, and Rochester. This is important because our patients can go to any of these three centers and receive services more easily. This new access strengthens our relationships with local insurance providers in these countries. This new contract puts at your disposal the experience of the Mayo Clinic, which for more than 150 years has been dedicated to patient care. All our doctors work as a team to treat one patient at a time, putting the patient's needs first. This results in high-quality services.  We know this is important to you and your family, and it's important to us too. For more information about this contract, you can contact your insurance agent, visit our offices in Latin America, or visit our website.

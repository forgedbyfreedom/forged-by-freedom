The Mayo clinic occupational therapy hand fellowship program is an AOTA approved 54 weeks long intensive program. There is an immense amount of interaction between the faculty and the fellows. There are a number of resources here in terms of libraries, didactic learning, mentors, faculty members, observation of surgeries, observation and planning, and being involved in unparalleled patient care, interacting with surgeons, their team, physicians, the physician assistants, nursings, other staff members, other physical and occupational therapists from other specialties. They will have teaching opportunities that would involve teaching the residents and fellows from other disciplines as well. We are a team. Collectively, as a fellow, you are put into the situation where you have people who have been working for decades in this highly specialized field, seeing the most complex cases. They're great mentors. And we would not be successful if we did not work in a collaborative environment. We all work together really well. And as a fellow, I feel like our mentors do an excellent job with didactics and helping facilitate growth. For the upcoming fellow, I would say, come in with an open mind. Learning is a two way process. As faculty, we will try to provide the best opportunity as much as possible for you to learn from whatever experiences you can gain from here.

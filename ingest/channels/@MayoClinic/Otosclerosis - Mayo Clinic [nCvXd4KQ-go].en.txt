other sclerosis is a condition in which the innermost bone of hearing the stay piece bone does not move properly because of abnormal calcifications it shows up simply as a slowly progressive hearing loss the ear is normal on exam meaning there is no fluid no infection no perforations etc about 70% of the time it runs in the family and about 70% of the time it affects both ears the treatment options include doing nothing using hearing aids or having surgery to correct it the surgery to correct it is called a stapedectomy or step autonomy in essence part of the third bone of hearing the stay piece is removed and replaced with a prosthesis this surgery is an outpatient procedure which means the patient goes home from the recovery room and the surgery itself takes about an hour it is done under a general anesthetic and I use a laser for the critical portion of the procedure after surgery there's either some ointment or soft packing in the ear canal which is removed about a week later in the office most patients can tell an improvement of their hearing within four weeks the hearing can fluctuate up and down some for a few months so the formal hearing test to measure the final result is done about three months postoperatively

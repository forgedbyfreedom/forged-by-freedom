I recently had surgery at the Mayo Clinic. I had a lower back problem and was treated here at the Jacksonville clinic. We found that the Mayo Clinic in Jacksonville maintains the professional and humanistic principles of the Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota. I actually feel the same as at the main clinic, which is the one I always go to. There's always that focus on putting the patient first, which is very important and makes us feel comfortable. Even though when we seek medical help, we look for a doctor, I think all patients come with that anxiety of the unexpected. We're always very worried about what they might do to us, the types of tests they might do, the discomfort, the pain. But the quality and warmth with which we are treated here at the Mayo Clinic is encouraging and really makes us feel well cared for.

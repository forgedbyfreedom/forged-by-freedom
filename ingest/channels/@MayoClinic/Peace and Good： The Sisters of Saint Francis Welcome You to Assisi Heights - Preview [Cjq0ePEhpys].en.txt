It is an intriguing, beautiful building, rich with history situated on a hilltop. This is Assisi Heights, the home of the Sisters of Saint Francis of Rochester, Minnesota. The Sisters have been in Rochester since the 1870's, where they built a school as well as the hospital that gave rise to Mayo Clinic. As their congregation steadily grew, the Sisters founded and staffed many schools and health care facilities in Minnesota and beyond. In the 1950's they built their new motherhouse, Assisi Heights, with lodging for 500 sisters and spaces for worship, including a beautiful chapel, open to the community. Now,  in a new film, the Sisters of Saint Francis welcome you to Assisi Heights. You'll see their home of more than half a century. Touring the building and the grounds. You'll hear how the Sisters have adapted to major  changes in the church and the world. Remaining a vital, compassionate presence for peace and justice, with love and respect for all living things. Peace and Good, the Sisters of Saint Francis Welcome You to Assisi Heights, from Mayo Clinic Heritage Films. With generous support from Gerald and Henrietta Rauenhorst.

hello my name is carly lovejoy and i am the registered nurse care coordinator for the pediatric liver transplant program at mayo clinic in rochester minnesota i have been working as the registered nurse care coordinator for our pediatric patients since 2017. my role within the transplant team is to provide education about liver transplantation and the care of the patient after transplant i also provide ongoing support through the transplant journey communication between the patient family and the multidisciplinary transplant team and coordination of care i am involved in the care of the patient and family from the very beginning of the transplant process when the child is referred to mayo clinic for a liver transplant up through the ongoing care of the patient after transplant as receiving a transplant requires lifelong management working with pediatric patients and their families through the transplant journey is very rewarding i have the great opportunity to form long-term relationships with the patient and their families while watching the child thrive with good graft function after transplant transplantation is very exciting and at times an overwhelming experience but i am very lucky to be one of the transplant team members involved in the child's care that will help support them through this gift of life thank you

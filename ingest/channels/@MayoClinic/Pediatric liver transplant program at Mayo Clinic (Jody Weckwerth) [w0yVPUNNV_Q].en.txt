hello my name is jody weckhorth i am a physician assistant at mayo clinic in rochester minnesota and a member of the pediatric liver transplant team i'm excited to tell you about our program i work in the inpatient setting and care for pediatric liver transplant candidates before during and after their transplant i've been a member of the pediatric liver transplant team for over 20 years and consider it an honor and a privilege to care for these special patients and their families my role is to provide continuity of care for our transplant candidates and recipients for all of their inpatient experiences our admissions and procedures take place at mayo clinic hospital st mary's campus here in rochester the eugenio litta children's hospital is a part of the st mary's campus and is the location where pediatric liver transplants have been performed at mayo since 1985. here at mayo clinic we have a multi-disciplinary approach to caring for liver transplant patients it is my role to interface with colleagues in a wide variety of disciplines within the hospital setting this might include groups such as critical care radiology nutrition infectious disease pharmacy and surgery to name a few the depth of resources we have available at mayo is robust and includes not only our pediatric subspecialty colleagues but also our adult transplant colleagues and specialists in adult procedural areas we are fortunate to have such extensive expertise easily available to us every day when managing inpatient admissions i am able to provide continuity of care and a familiar face to the transplant patients and families who may need inpatient care intermittently over time

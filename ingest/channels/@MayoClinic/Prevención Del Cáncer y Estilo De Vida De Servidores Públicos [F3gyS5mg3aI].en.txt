In my professional life as a police detective in the town of Freeport, New York, I believe that police work, jobs like firefighting, and other work involving people who experience high levels of daily stress and anxiety are very prone to developing Bartz esophagus. The quality of life in these jobs, the varied hours worked, doesn't allow for a proper diet or adequate rest. I recommend that everyone in a high-stress occupation, like those mentioned, take care of themselves and get tested for acid reflux or Bartz esophagus. We are very susceptible due to the lifestyle in these professions. It's very important to be aware of it because it often has no symptoms; it doesn't warn us that we are suffering from this condition. If we don't detect it during a routine preventative exam, it can be too late when it's discovered because symptoms appear. If we wait for symptoms, it's too late, and the consequences are much, much worse. Early detection is crucial, as I experienced.  I was fortunate that they discovered it early and were able to correct it without major consequences for me.

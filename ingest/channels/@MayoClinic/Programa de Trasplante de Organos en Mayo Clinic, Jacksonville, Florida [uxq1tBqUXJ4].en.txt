The transplant center at the Mayo Clinic in Florida is a comprehensive center where any patient with chronic respiratory failure, whether cardiac, pulmonary, hepatic, renal, or any other organ or solid organ failure refractory to typical surgical treatment, can be referred by a physician or self-referred to consider a transplant. This center has accumulated the experience of experts in solid organ transplantation at all levels— cardiopulmonary, hepatic, renal, pancreatic, etc.— and having this dedicated team, fully committed solely to transplant services, provides any patient with the opportunity for expert opinion and management, if necessary. If a patient wishes to self-refer through the program, they can simply contact the International Patient Office, or their referring physician can initiate the process. This initial contact will be followed by an appointment and case evaluation to determine if a solid organ transplant is a suitable option, regardless of the indication.

hi my name is Heather fields from Mayo clinic community Internal Medicine together with my colleagues Dr Denise milstein Dr Amit Shaw Dr Barbara readyy and Dr Mark Wallace we performed a review on how to monitor and advise vegans to ensure adequate nutrient intake the vegan diet has become increasingly popular for environmental ethical health and religious concerns some of the nutrients of concern in the vegan diet include vitamin B12 iron calcium vitamin D omega-3 fatty acids including EPA and DHA and protein we found that some of these nutrients which can have implications in neurologic disorders anemias bone health and other health concerns can be deficient in vegan diets we found some of the nutrients that can actually be deficient in the vegan diet include vitamin B12 iron calcium and vitamin D there have been no obvious detrimental effects to low levels of the omega-3 fatty acids and vegans have not been shown to be deficient in any specific amino acids we think it'd be a good idea for providers to ensure adequate blood levels of vitamin B12 iron fertin calcium and vitamin D for access to our complete review please visit our website

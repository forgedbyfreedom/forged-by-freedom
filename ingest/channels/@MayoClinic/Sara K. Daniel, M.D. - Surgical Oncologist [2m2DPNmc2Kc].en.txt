Hi, my name is Dr. Sara Daniel. I'm a surgical oncologist here at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota. I grew up riding horses, and so science always really interested me, and I thought I wanted to be a veterinarian, but as I learned more about medical specialties, I really fell in love with treating patients and all the new technologies and exciting things that were happening in the field of medicine. I specialized in cancer surgery in particular because I think the field of cancer treatment and oncology has so much to offer, and we have so much area to improve, and I'm really excited to be a part of that. I specialize in treating peritoneal surface malignancy, gastric cancer, retroperitoneal sarcoma, and a few other types of tumors that affect the small intestines, such as neuroendocrine tumors and GISTs. The needs of the patient come first is a guiding philosophy in everything that we do, especially when we're treating metastatic cancer, because I think quality of life is just as important as quantity of life, and we need to make sure that we're optimizing both of those things.

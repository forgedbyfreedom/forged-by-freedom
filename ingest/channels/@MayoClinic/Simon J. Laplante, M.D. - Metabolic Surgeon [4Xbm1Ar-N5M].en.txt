We know obesity is very common. When patients come to our comprehensive bariatric program, they'll be assessed and we'll come up with a tailored surgical plan for every patient's specific needs. This often includes doing surgery and procedures such as the sleeve gastrectomy, gastric bypass, duodenal switch, or often revisional surgical procedures. I use the latest technologies in the operating room whether that involves laparoscopic or robotic surgery. As a bariatric surgeon it's extremely rewarding to be able to treat obesity and its comorbidities such as diabetes and also being able to go home after the day of operating and knowing I gave my 100% to do what's best for the patient who put all their trust in me and the system.

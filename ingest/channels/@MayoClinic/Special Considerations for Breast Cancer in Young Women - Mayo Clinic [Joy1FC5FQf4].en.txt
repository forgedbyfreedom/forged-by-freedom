hi i'm dr catherine ruddy i'm a medical oncologist at the mayo clinic and i'd like to speak to you today about breast cancer in young women breast cancer can be a devastating diagnosis for a young woman young women tend to be diagnosed with more advanced stage disease meaning that their tumors are larger and more likely to have spread to lymph nodes under the arm breast cancers in young women are also more likely to have an aggressive biology and for both of these reasons young women tend to be therefore treated with more aggressive therapies consequently young women can experience significant side effects from these treatments one side effect that is unique to young women is the risk of infertility chemotherapy can damage the ovaries leading to infertility in addition the anti-estrogen treatments that we often offer to patients can contribute to infertility because young women are recommended not to get pregnant while they're taking these medicines when tamoxifen is prescribed for five or even 10 years a woman's ovarian function declines with time as it does naturally with age and therefore at the end of this treatment she will not be as fertile as she was at the beginning many young women diagnosed with breast cancer in their 20s or 30s or even early 40s have not completed their desired child bearing at the time of the diagnosis so i recommend that any woman who is interested in future child bearing at that time visit with a reproductive endocrinologist to discuss whether fertility preservation techniques are warranted to increase the chance of future fertility we know that young women experience more distress at the time of a breast cancer diagnosis and with treatment than older women do and part of this may be due to the side effects that they do experience and another component of this may be because breast cancer can be very very disruptive to a woman's functioning in other parts of her life for young women who may be caring for young children at home or who may be still be in school or early in a career breast cancer can be particularly difficult the good news is that our treatments for breast cancer are improving and we are

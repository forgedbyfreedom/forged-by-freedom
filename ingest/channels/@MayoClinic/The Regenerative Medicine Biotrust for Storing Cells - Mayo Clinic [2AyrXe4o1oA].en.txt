though great progress has been made in medicine current treatments are unable to keep pace with the needs of an aging population living with increased incidence of chronic disease finding solutions that cure disease rather than treat symptoms is essential to reduce medical costs improve quality of life and ultimately save lives at mayo clinic the center for regenerative medicine is working to turn back the clock on chronic diseases including those of the lung heart pancreas bone kidney and brain by using our body's own stem cells to repair damaged organs harvested from umbilical cord blood fat tissue bone marrow and skin stem cells can be reprogrammed to become lung heart pancreas bone kidney and nerve cells they are grown in the lab and delivered to the patient to repair diseased tissues to facilitate this work mayo clinic has established the center for regenerative medicine biotrust reaching beyond traditional biobanks the biotrust puts in place the infrastructure or set of building blocks needed to efficiently collect process and securely store donated biospecimens along with the pertinent health information of the donor the prepared cells will be made readily available to researchers both in and outside mayo clinic providing them with the tools they need to test and apply new therapies often tailored to a particular patient with the ultimate goal of accelerating development of innovative approaches to curing chronic disease the biotrust performs key functions in both the research and practice of medicine facilitating the translation of effective regenerative medicine technologies to patient care

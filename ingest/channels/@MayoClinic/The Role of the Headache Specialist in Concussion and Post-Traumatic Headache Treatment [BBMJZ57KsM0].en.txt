every year in the united states over 44 million children and 170 million adults engage in organized sports and recreational activities unfortunately nearly 4 million concussions are reported every year this is felt to be an underestimate given the fact that many people don't actually recognize the signs and symptoms of concussion and sometimes don't present to health care providers because their symptoms are so mild most concussion symptoms will last anywhere from one to two weeks but in some individuals they can last for weeks to months or even years i'm bert vargas the assistant director of the mayo clinic sports neurology and concussion program and chair of the special interest section on post-traumatic headache for the american headache society a headache specialist can play a significant role in the evaluation and treatment of individuals with concussion and post-traumatic headache especially since headache is the most common symptom of concussion and is reported in up to 94 percent of concussed athletes after their injury females and children are especially susceptible to concussion and post-concussive symptoms such as headache over 36 million individuals in the united states suffer from migraine and other headache related disorders headache specialists are uniquely trained to be able to differentiate between primary headache disorders such as migraine and other disorders such as concussion and post-traumatic headache a headache specialist is also able to rule out more serious causes of headache that can go undetected can recommend treatments for post-traumatic headache and help prevent the overuse of pain medication headache specialists can also help avoid the transition to chronic post-traumatic headache which can be extremely disabling for more information on headache including post-traumatic headache and concussion and how a headache specialist can help visit american headache society.org and americanmigrainefoundation.org

the tympanic membrane perforation is simply a hole in the eardrum it most often occurs as a result of an ear infection trauma such as a direct hit to the ear or ecclesia Toma on occasion if tubes are placed in the eardrum to drain fluid or equalize pressure the hole in the eardrum fails to heal resulting in a perforation almost always patients with a tympanic membrane perforation have had recurrent problems with ear infections there are times when the perforated eardrum needs surgical repair typically the surgery is done under a general anaesthetic the ear drum is either patched or replaced using the patient's own natural tissue known as fashion the surgery is approached from either behind the ear through the ear canal or a combination usually the surgery takes between a half hour and an hour and a half it is done as an outpatient which means the patient goes home after the recovery room there is packing in the ear canal which is taken out in the office within about a week Healing is complete by four to six weeks

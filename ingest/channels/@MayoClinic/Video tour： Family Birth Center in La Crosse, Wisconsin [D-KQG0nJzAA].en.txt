Welcome to our Family Birth Center at Mayo Clinic Health System in La Crosse, where your birth experience is our top priority. From the moment you step inside our tranquil lobby, you'll feel the warmth and comfort that sets us apart. Our spacious patient rooms are designed with your comfort and needs in mind. Featuring scenic views and abundant natural light, these inviting rooms are the perfect setting for beginning this meaningful new chapter. Every room features a private bathroom, complete with a large soaking tub, to help new mothers feel a sense of calm. For deliveries, including C-sections, our state of the art operating room reflects the highest standard of care at Mayo Clinic. For those who need it, our special care nursery is fully equipped to provide additional support and treatment for your newborn. This space is designed to keep family units together while offering the best care no matter your infants needs. With remote video monitoring families can stay connected 24/7 with their babies in the special care nursery. Manage needs at home or elsewhere while our staff provide outstanding and compassionate care to your baby. Stations located just outside patient rooms allow for our expert nursing team to provide assistance so you can feel secure and supported throughout your stay. All of our amenities are designed for your comfort, from room darkening blinds to adjustable furniture for partners to sleep in, we have thought of every detail to help create a positive birthing and postpartum experience for you and your growing family.  It's why Mayo Clinic Health System in La Crosse has been recognized among the best hospitals for maternity care by U.S. News and World Report. At the Family Birth Center, your journey as a new parent means everything to us.  Together we can make every birth a beautiful beginning.

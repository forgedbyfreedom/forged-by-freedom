Welcome to orientation. I'm Dr. Annie Sadosty, dean of Mayo Clinic School of Graduate Medical Education. I'm excited you have chosen to be part of a diverse community that exists to maximize your potential and advanced health care through innovation and discovery. Whether you are training at Mayo Clinic's campus in Arizona, Florida, Minnesota, or Wisconsin, faculty and staff are ready to help you with your educational journey. During your time here, you will experience Mayo Clinic's commitment to providing a vibrant, diverse, and engaging learning environment that supports all learners. Our office for Education, Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion nurtures and supports the contributions of every individual, which is essential to success in patient care, education, and research. We are committed to academic excellence and provide world-class expertise. We provide hands-on training and teaching, mentoring, and career guidance and networking. We are here to help you achieve educational goals as well as goals in other aspects of your life. I encourage you to join the Mayo Fellows Association to access a peer and social support network. Also, visit our website for information and resources about how we can help with academic support and well-being. Mayo Clinic believes the needs of the patient come first. We look forward to helping you succeed so that exceptional patient care can continue. Again, welcome to Mayo Clinic and welcome to your orientation.

Are your legs aching? Do you have large varicose veins or a Venus wound? When compression stockings, elevation of your legs, and pain medication fail to control your symptoms of varicose veins, you may be a candidate for minimally invasive procedures to close or remove certain veins in your legs. These procedures may include thermal ablation with laser or radio frequency, non-therrmal ablation with injectable solutions, and ambulatory flctomy. Ablation techniques seal shut problem veins and phctomy removes problem veins in small pieces. These techniques are the state-of-the-art in the treatment of varicose veins.

veins of the legs are the pathways by which the blood travels from the foot back to the Heart Like a one-way Street when these veins allow the blood to flow back to the foot like a two-way street it's called Venus reflux or insufficiency this reflux may lead to varicose veins swelling pain aching leg fatigue restlessness or even wounds careful identification of the refluxing veins is the key to deciding on the appropriate treatment for you those treatments may include compression stockings elevation of your legs pain medication or a minimally invasive procedure to close or remove those problematic veins if you would like to discuss your varicose veins and leg symptoms please ask for an appointment with me at mayoclinic.org

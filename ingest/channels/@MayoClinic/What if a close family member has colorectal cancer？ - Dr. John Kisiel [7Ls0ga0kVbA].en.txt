If your relative is diagnosed, questions that your doctor might ask is how genetically close to you is that individual? Is it a first-degree relative like a parent or a sibling or a child, or is it somebody who's more distant? They might ask you about other members of the family who have other types of colon other than cancer in the hopes that they might potentially uncover a high-risk family history or pedigree. And then from that they would try to determine what your risk of cancer might be over your lifetime.

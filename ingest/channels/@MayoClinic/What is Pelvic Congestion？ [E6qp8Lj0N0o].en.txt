pelvic congestion is a clinical diagnosis of non-cyclic pelvic pain in women for more than six months this is caused by varicose veins deep inside the pelvis women typically have symptoms of deep pelvic pain and heaviness and may have varicose veins of the perenium or the labia and may have pain with intercourse when this diagnosis is suspected a gynecologic evaluation is completed to exclude other source of pain patients will also have an Imaging study such as an MRI a CT or an ultrasound of the pelvis with these evaluations the appropriate treatment choices can be made which may include a minimally invasive procedure to close the varicose veins at the pelvis this is called gonadal vein embolization if you would like to discuss your symptoms of pelvic congestion please ask for an appointment with me in Rochester Minnesota at mayoclinic.org

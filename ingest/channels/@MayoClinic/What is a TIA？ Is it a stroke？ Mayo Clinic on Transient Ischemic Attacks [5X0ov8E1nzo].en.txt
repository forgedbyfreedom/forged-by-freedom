a TIA is a transient es schic attack it is a temporary Interruption of blood flow to a particular part of the brain the symptoms of Tia are indistinguishable from stroke both should be treated as a medical emergency Tia can be challenging because in some instances the attack may only last 10 or 15 minutes in those instances is people will be feeling better even before they call 911 or an ambulance arrives the important aspect of Tia is that even if the symptoms clear on their own a stroke can often follow a TIA and therefore it should also be treated with similar urgency so we recommend that all patients who have Tia also be evaluated in an urgent fashion in a stroke center because the risk of a disabling stroke a deficit that doesn't go away after Tia is quite High it may be as high as 10% within 48 Hours of the initial attack so we urge people to treat Tia as urgently as they treat stroke even though the symptoms may clear on their own

Skin cancer is any cancer that originates in skin cells. It can originate from the surface of the skin, or from the hair follicles or sweat glands. The most common types of skin cancer are basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, and melanoma. Of these three types, melanoma has the highest mortality risk. Patients at higher risk of developing melanoma are people with lighter skin, such as those with blue or green eyes, red hair, or who have had many sunburns, particularly as children. People with many moles may also have a higher risk of developing melanoma. Early diagnosis and treatment are very important for melanoma because if the cancer is only present in the skin, it can be treated with surgery. Unfortunately, when melanoma has spread and metastasized, there are not many effective treatments available, and these patients require more than just skin surgery; they require chemotherapy and sometimes radiation.

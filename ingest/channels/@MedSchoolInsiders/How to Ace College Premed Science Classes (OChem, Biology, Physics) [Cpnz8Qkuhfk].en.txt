the material shortcutting by viewing the correct answer and explanation when you're stuck severely limits your rate of progress and prevents you from deeply understanding the material approaching practice questions in this slower more intentional way will likely feel frustrating at first you'll probably have to go back through your notes many times and even then you might still get questions

Vascular surgery is the medical\h specialty that involves the\h\h surgical and medical management\h of arterial and venous diseases. Vascular surgeons care for patients with\h pathology involving all arteries and veins,\h\h except those of the heart and brain, which are\h managed by their own respective specialists,\h\h cardiothoracic surgeons and neurosurgeons.\h A vascular surgeon operates all over the\h\h body and is incredibly knowledgeable\h about a patient’s vascular anatomy,\h\h as well as the non-vascular anatomy that must\h be avoided when exposing the vessel of interest. Common conditions that vascular surgeons\h treat include carotid artery disease,\h\h aortic aneurysms, peripheral arterial disease\h of the arms and legs, venous insufficiency,\h\h varicose veins and blood clots, and hemodialysis\h access creation and maintenance, to name a few. Vascular surgeons must also always be\h available to other surgical specialties\h\h in the event that catastrophic bleeding\h occurs, which, of course, must be controlled. The speciality can be categorized based on the\h area of pathology that the surgeon specializes in. Vascular surgery involves two paradigms of\h surgical management for various conditions;\h\h open surgery and endovascular surgery. Open surgery involves making an incision to access\h\h the blood vessel of interest\h and operating on it directly. One example of open vascular\h surgery is a carotid endarterectomy,\h\h in which the carotid artery, which is in\h the neck and supplies oxygen to the brain,\h\h is opened to allow for removal of the plaque\h that is causing the patient’s symptoms,\h\h which could include weakness, dizziness, vision\h loss, severe headaches, and difficulty speaking. Another example would be an open\h abdominal aortic aneurysm repair,\h\h in which a bulging of the aortic wall\h is replaced with an artificial tube\h\h graft to prevent the aneurysm from\h rupturing, which is often fatal. One more example would be a peripheral\h bypass, commonly from the femoral\h\h artery to the popliteal artery to treat the\h symptoms of impaired blood flow in the legs. Conversely, endovascular surgery is the minimally\h invasive approach that utilizes catheters, wires,\h\h balloons, and stents to treat the same\h conditions. Using the previous examples,\h\h a needle can be used to access the femoral artery\h to deliver a stent to the internal carotid artery,\h\h a stent graft to the aorta, or a balloon to\h open up a blockage in the arteries of the leg. Vascular surgery, particularly\h endovascular aortic surgery,\h\h often involves creative solutions\h to treat complex aneurysms.

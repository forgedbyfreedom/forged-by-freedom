Welcome to My Soup Series where I show you soups that are not only delicious but also nutritious this white chicken chili is one of my favorite lower effort High reward recipes that I will make every week and though we are in the thick of soup season you can make the soup year round because the ingredients are easy to find regardless of the month first you're going to sauté onions for a few minutes before adding minced garlic the chicken broth green chilies corn cumin paprika oregano and give a stir then you're going to add your beans which is going to add a lot of fiber I love to add a can of calini and pinto beans and I save a half cup of each so I can mash them together and then add them to thicken the soup for convenience I'm adding shredded rotisserie chicken and I'm adding as much as possible for more protein then I just let the simmer for 15 to 30 minutes depending on how hungry I am for toppings I strongly encourage you to add avocado Greek yogurt crushed tortilla chips lime and cilantro but using Greek yogurt to make the soup creamy instead of cream cheese sour cream or shredded cheese you're getting a little more protein less saturated fat and it's usually more friendly for lactose intolerance folks lastly I like to put the leftovers into silicone molds so I can freeze them and easily reheat them whenever I want a fresh hot bowl of soup wow so easy so good

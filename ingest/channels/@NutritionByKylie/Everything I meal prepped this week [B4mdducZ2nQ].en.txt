Everything I prep this week is a dietitian. Today, I'm prepping breakfast, lunch, and a few snacks. First, I'm making my favorite egg bites. They're packed with protein, super easy to make, and perfect for busy mornings or midday snacks. They're like customizable mini omelettes that you can eat with one hand. You can store them in the fridge for a few days or freeze up to a month. Second, I'm making mason jar noodles. This is my meal prepping solution to avoid four-day old meals since every jar tastes as fresh as a newly prepared broth and soup. You can customize it to your liking, but I love to include a mix of veggies, a protein, and noodles. I store these in the fridge up to 4 days. All you have to do is add hot water for 10 minutes and they're ready to eat. Now I'm prepping my veggie bin for the week because it helps me get in more veggies. Having everything washed, chopped, and ready to go means I don't have to think twice when I'm hungry. I just open the fridge and it's ready for me. I also make a high protein dip that has 26 g of protein in a cup. Truly the easiest hack for getting fiber and protein in an easy snack. Also, it helps cut down food waste since I can actually see and use what I bought. Lastly, I'm making a batch of my garlicky roasted chickpeas with rosemary and parm. I like to add this to so many things. Wraps, soups, salads. I even add it to my popcorn. Truly is the best snack because chickpeas pack fiber and plant-based protein. And in this case, they're super easy to eat. I'll store this in an airtight container and be ready for the week. All recipes can be found in my cookbook. So easy. So good.

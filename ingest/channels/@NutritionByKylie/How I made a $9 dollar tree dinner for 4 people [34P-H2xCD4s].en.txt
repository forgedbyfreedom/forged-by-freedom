I'm a dietitian and today we're making a meal from Dollar Tree ingredients. I did this a few years ago, but I figured it would be helpful to revisit in light of rising food costs. For this meal, I'm picking up pasta, pesto, jarred bell peppers, canned corn, chickpeas, a jar of minced garlic, and parmesan. This all costed under $9, and this should feed around four people. First, let's salt the water and add our pasta. Now, let's add some oil to a pan and a little bit of that minced garlic. Add your chickpeas for some fiber and a little plant protein, corn for a little more fiber, and roasted peppers for some vitamin C. All you're going to do is sauté this for around 3 to 5 minutes until heated through. Then add your cooked pasta and pesto to the pan. You can add some pasta water and season with salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes. Now, just transfer to a bowl, sprinkle with grated parmesan, and you have an affordable meal. From a nutrition standpoint, this meal could use a little more protein, but it's a great base that's quick, easy, and affordable. Food access and affordability play a huge role in health.

 if you love white rice but want something a little more filling and blood sugar friendly, then make this instead. First, add one cup of white rice to your rice pot, then rinse your rice, and then add half a cup of canned black beans, half a cup of canned chickpeas, and half a cup of frozen green peas. Then add two cups of water, and then let it cook. White rice is such an important staple in many cultures, including my own, so it can be unrealistic and unnecessary to tell people to remove it from their diet. It is a perfectly fine and valuable source of energy. That said, I know that some people just need an easy way to add fiber, protein, plants, and variety to their meals, and this is something that works for me. Try it out and let me know what you think.

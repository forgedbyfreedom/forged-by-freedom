 If you love white rice, but want something a little higher in fiber and protein, then make this instead. First, add 1 cup of white rice and 1 cup of pearl barley to your rice pot and rinse, then add 1 cup of frozen edamame, and then add water. Since the barley and edamame in this rice add 20 grams of fiber and 30 grams of protein, this can help with fullness, digestion, and steadier energy. But most importantly, you still get the comfort and taste of white rice, while getting the nutrients to make it more filling and balanced. And if you don't want to make a whole pot of rice every time, you can portion it into silicone molds and freeze it. That way you can just pop out a serving and reheat it for your next meal whenever you need it. Always remember what you can add to, not what you can subtract from your meals.

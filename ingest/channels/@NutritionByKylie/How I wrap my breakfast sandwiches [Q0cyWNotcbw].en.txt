you guys really wanted to know how I wrap my sandwiches in parchment paper so here's a quick tutorial use a piece of parchment paper large enough to cover the whole sandwich then place the sandwich in the center fold the top and bottom edges so they overlap in the center then fold the edges down crease then continue folding and creasing until the paper fits tightly around the sandwich then tuck your fingers under the sandwich and tuck in the sides into the area created by your fingers and there you have it a neatly folded breakfast sandwich it might be tricky at first but it gets a lot easier with practice and it's honestly really satisfying to do

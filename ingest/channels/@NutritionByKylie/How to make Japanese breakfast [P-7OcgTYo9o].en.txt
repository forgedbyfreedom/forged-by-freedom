I'm a big fan of savory breakfast. So, let me show you my favorite Japanese breakfast. It's so nourishing, balanced, and always keeps me energized for hours. First, I make tamagoyaki, which is a sort of rolled omelette that is fluffy, savory, and slightly sweet. All you have to do is mix eggs with a little soy sauce, sugar, water, and dashi if you have that. Then, roll in layers. This has high quality protein and nutrients like choline. Then, I make, which is a Japanese cucumber salad. All you have to do is marinate sliced cucumbers in a mixture of rice vinegar, soy sauce, and a little sugar. They're crunchy, refreshing, and an easy way to add a little veggies to this meal. Then I make miso soup, which takes 5 minutes because all you need to do is mix together dashi, miso, and water. I love to add tofu for more protein, and wakame for iodine. Lastly, I make shiozak, which is a salted and boiled salmon. I love starting my day with this because salmon is rich in protein, which will keep you full, and omega-3 fats, which is supportive of heart health and brain health. Now, I'll admit, preparing all of this in the morning can feel like a lot, but most of it can be prepped ahead of time. It doesn't have to be an everyday meal, but it is one of my favorite balanced meals. You're getting protein, healthy fats, carbs, fiber, and probiotics in one sitting. It's a good reminder that breakfast can be both comforting and functional.

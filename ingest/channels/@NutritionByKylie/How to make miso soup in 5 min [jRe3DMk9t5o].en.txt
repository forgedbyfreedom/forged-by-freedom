miso soup is a lot easier to make than you think it literally only takes 5ish minutes and I'm going to show you how to make it welcome back to my soup series first add four cups of water to a pot then add Dashi I like these two and when it comes to a boil lower to a gentle simmer and add 3 tbsp of miso you can use white or red but I highly recommend red to prevent clumps and graininess use a strainer to dissolve your miso paste and make sure you don't boil the Miso because this can alter its flavor and diminish its nutritional value now I'm adding wak which is seaweed soft tofu which is going to add some protein and calcium and green onions and now you have a delicious pot of Mis soup I might be biased because I am Japanese but this is my favorite soup out of all the soups and the best part is that you can add so many different types of veggies and make it as nutritious as possible so easy so good

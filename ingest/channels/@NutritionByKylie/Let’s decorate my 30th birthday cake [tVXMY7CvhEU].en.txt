Let's decorate my last minute birthday cake for my 30th birthday. Since turning 30 last week, people have been asking me how I feel. In many ways, I feel great. I've never been more confident in who I am because I've finally learned to believe in myself, not just with words, but with actions. I don't define myself by what I achieve, or at least not as much as I used to. I know how to take care of myself, but I still have friendships that feel deep and grounding, where I finally feel accepted and loved without having to shrink or stretch myself. But if I'm being completely honest, I'm nervous. I'm nervous about time, about how fast it's moving, whether I'm doing enough with it, spending it on the right things, or wasting it without realizing it. I think about my parents and family a lot. How precious the time is with them, how I can't slow it down or rewind it, no matter how much I wish I could. I think about how much more I want to do, even though I'm unsure about what path I'll take to get there, or if the things I want to do now will be the same things I want later. But I also know that these thoughts don't mean I'm doing anything wrong. They just mean that I care about the people I love, about the way I move through the world, about wanting a life that feels meaningful and like mine. And maybe that's what turning 30 is really about. Not having all the answers, but asking the right questions. Not having it all figured out, but knowing what matters. Not rushing to the next thing, but learning to be present where I am. I don't know what the next decade will hold, but I trust that I'll keep choosing what feels right, even in the uncertainty. And for now, that's enough.

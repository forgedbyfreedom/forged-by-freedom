This mango chia pudding is one of my favorite breakfasts with fiber. This takes 5 minutes to prep the night before and in the morning breakfast is already done. First, blend mango. I love fresh now that mango is in season. Milk, Greek yogurt, coconut milk, maple syrup, and vanilla extract for the base. Then pour into a bowl. Stir in chia seeds. Let sit for 5 minutes. Then stir again to prevent clumping. Then cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight. This is kind of like a higher fiber, higher protein mango sago. If you like that or mango sticky rice, then you'll absolutely love this. In the morning, I top it with more fresh mango, chopped nuts for some crunch, and coconut flakes.

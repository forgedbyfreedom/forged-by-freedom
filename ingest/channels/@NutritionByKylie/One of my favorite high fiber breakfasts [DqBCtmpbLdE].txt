 This mango chia pudding, this mango, this mango pudding is one of my favorite breakfasts with fiber, this takes 5 minutes to prep the night before, and in the morning, breakfast is already done. First blend mango, I love fresh now that mango is in season, milk, greek yogurt, coconut milk, maple syrup, and vanilla extract for the base, then pour into a bowl, stir in chia seeds, let sit for 5 minutes, then stir again to prevent clumping, then cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight. This is kind of like a higher fiber, higher protein mango sago, if you like that, or mango sticky rice, then you will absolutely love this. In the morning, I top it with more fresh mango, chopped nuts for some crunch, and coconut flakes. It's a delicious breakfast that's creamy, refreshing, and provides fiber that is so easy to make ahead. So easy and so good.

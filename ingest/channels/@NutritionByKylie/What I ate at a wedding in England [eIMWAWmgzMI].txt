 everything I ate at a wedding in England. Today is one of my closest friends, Janelle's wedding, and we ate breakfast at the end we're staying in. I had scrambled eggs with beans, a hash brown, tomato and mushrooms with yogurt and muesli. Then I headed over to the bride's suite to get ready. Janelle is one of the most loyal, loving, and thoughtful people I've ever met, and I'm so honored to be in her presence on such a beautiful day. After the ceremony, we snacked on chicken pâté on sesame bread and deep fried veggies. Lisa and I tried their signature drinks. My mocktail had sea-lip garden with aromatic bitters and elderflower syrup, and it was so refreshing. After we had a seated three-course meal, first I had a roasted cauliflower soup with chili oil and toasted pumpkin seeds. This is really tasty, but I admittedly did feel a little gassy afterwards because of my IVS. For the main course, I got a braised lamb shoulder with potatoes and zucchini, and for dessert, I had a bramley apple and forest fruit crumbletart, and some of their wedding cake, which was also perfect. After a quick nap, we all gathered again. I had some pizza to fill me up for the long night of karaoke and dancing, and it really hit the spot. Congrats, Janelle and Doug. The food was great, but the company was even better.

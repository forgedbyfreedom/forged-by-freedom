But I ate in a day in New York City. I woke up bright and early and went straight to the Today Show. I would usually eat some sort of breakfast by now, but I was very nervous, so I wanted to wait until after my appearance. I got to talk to Al Roker about recipes for my cookbook, so easy so good, and my partnership with their start today app. I got a tour of the studio, which was really cool. And after we finished, I had breakfast with my cookbook team at Loi. I got the frittata, which came with a side salad. This was tasty, and I got in some protein, fiber, and nutrients. Then I met up with some friends at their apartment and got lunch at Chikadashi in Chinatown. I got the hamachi kam bowl with miso soup. This also had some avocado, ginger, and veggies. Basically, my ideal meal. This was so delicious and filling. Then I changed before going to Death Becomes, which was such an excellent show. My friends are visiting all the way from LA for the week to celebrate my friend Janelle's bachelorette. We love Broadway and had the best time. We then headed to Britichola for a post Broadway dinner. We shared a bunch of dishes including the branzino, marinated olives, Caesar salad, taglat bonas, and linguini with clams. We got some gelato fico. I got the passion fruit flavor, which was so refreshing because it was a very hot day. After that, we ended the night with a game night.

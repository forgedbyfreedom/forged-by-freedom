I'm a dietitian and I'm eating waffles for a snack and I'm going to tell you why. Frozen waffles were one of my favorite childhood classics and honestly I still crave them from time to time. They're super convenient to make when I need something fast. But here's my way of leveling them up. To make them more filling, I make a sweet cinnamon Greek yogurt spread that has 12 g of protein. I spread this on each waffle and top them with sliced bananas and toasted pecans for more fiber and healthy fats. You can add syrup if you want, but it doesn't really require it with how tasty it already is. It's a delicious and fun way to get in fruit and nuts with your sweet treat without it feeling forced or boring. And best of all, you can still enjoy your waffles.

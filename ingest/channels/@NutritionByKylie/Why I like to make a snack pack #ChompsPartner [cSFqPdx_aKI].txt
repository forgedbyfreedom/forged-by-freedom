 if you struggle to eat regularly throughout the day, or if making food decisions on especially busy days, you can make a snack pack. I always like to make sure each pack has a good balance of protein and fiber. For this one, I'm adding carrots because they're crunchy and rich in beta-carotene, strawberries because they're high in vitamin C, blueberries because they're packed with antioxidants and fiber, whole grain crackers because they provide fiber and carbs, which supports digestion and my energy levels, chomps which has 10 grams of protein and zero grams of sugar in one stick. I love the jalapeno flavor, but I'm also adding a chomps original to the snack pack. And last but not least, almonds that provide healthy fats, as well as some protein, fiber, and magnesium. When snacks are pre-planned and ready to grab, they remove the pressure of having to decide what to eat in the moment, which is something that can feel overwhelming when you're already stressed.

- Dizziness and balance disorders can instill in patients many different kinds of emotions. Some are afraid, while others are just generally anxious. Depending on what the condition is that they are presenting with, it can impact their life very minimally, but also can impact them in such way that it is debilitating at times and really affecting their quality of life. My name is Mary Kim and I'm an audiologist at Penn ENT. One of the things that I really like to spend the time in my patient care in every encounter is that we take a great amount of time explaining every procedure before anything were to happen and what the purpose of the testing is, as well as what they may do then to help me get the information that we need. I think the best part of my job is that I get to work with patients of all different walks of life. The same exact condition may impact two different people in two very different ways. A lot of how a person is affected by their symptoms is not just by the nature of the problem and the conditions that they are presenting with, but more of what else may be going on in their current life, what their past medical history is, their family history, and their daily activities. It is very much a look into the whole entire person beyond their ears. In listening to and spending all that time with a patient, we are able to better address and specifically hone in on the problem. We're addressing the person and not just the symptom and the condition.

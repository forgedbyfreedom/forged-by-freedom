hello I'm darra Belcher a certified Doula and child birth educator at Penn Medicine Pennsylvania hospital today I'd like to discuss birth preferences and how creating this document can help you communicate effectively with your healthc care team during labor and delivery a birth preferences document sometimes called a birth plan plan is a written guide that communicates your wishes for labor delivery and newborn care to your Healthcare team we use the term preferences rather than plan because it acknowledges the need for your flexibility during your birth experience your birth preferences document typically covers several important areas during labor you might specify your preferences for movement and positioning pain management options like epidural or unmedicated Comfort measures and who you'd like present in the room you can also consider your preferences for pushing positions and immediate skin-to-skin contact with your baby after birth you can include your wishes for newborn care such as delayed cord clamping vitamin K Administration and your feeding plans we recommend starting to think about your preferences early in your pregnancy this gives you time to discuss your choices with your health care provider during your prenatal visits and make informed decis decisions that work best for you and your family during your prenatal visits we can help explain various options and answer any questions you may have about what to include in your document this is also a great time to discuss any specific concerns or unique preferences you would like to incorporate while having birth preferences is valuable it's important to understand that adjustments may be needed during your birth to ensure the safety of both you and your baby your Healthcare team will work with you to honor your preferences while maintaining safety as the top priority when you arrive at the hospital for your birth please bring a written copy of your preferences this helps ensure that all members of your care team understand and can support your wishes throughout your birth experience at pen medicine we're committed to providing personalized care that aligns with your preferences while ensuring a safe and positive birth experience remember this document is a starting point for communication with your healthare team and we're here to support you every step of the way

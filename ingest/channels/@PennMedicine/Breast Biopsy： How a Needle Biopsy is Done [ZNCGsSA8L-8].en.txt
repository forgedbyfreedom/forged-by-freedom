foreign needle biopsies of the breast can be done with a variety of image guidance and we decide which type of Imaging to guide us based on how we see the lesion best so if we see a mass under ultrasound we use ultrasound to guide us if we are unable to see a mass and we perhaps see calcifications on a mammogram we use a mammogram machine to guide us sometimes we even need to use an MRI machine to guide us to the lesion no matter what way we see the lesion best and what type of Imaging we use to guide us the core biopsy procedure is exactly the same

Hello, I'm Dr. Mateo. I want to take a moment to talk to you about breast cancer and the importance of mammograms. We've seen a trend showing that some women have delayed getting their mammograms due to the pandemic. I'm here to remind you that it's very important not to wait. Mammograms are quick, easy, and safe, and they are the most effective way to detect breast diseases. We generally recommend getting screened for breast cancer at age 40 and annually thereafter. In addition, it's essential to know if anyone in your family has had breast cancer. If you have a family history, you may be able to start annual screenings before age 40. Please understand that we do not offer free mammograms; we are committed to the fight against cancer.

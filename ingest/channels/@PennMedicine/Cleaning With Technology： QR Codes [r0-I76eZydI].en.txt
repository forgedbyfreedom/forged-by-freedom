QR Codes are super important because it puts the power in our patients’ hands. We can’t be everywhere all the time, and the QR Codes allow us to receive a spill request or a toilet paper refill. Once a QR Code is put in, we’ll reply, “An associate is on their way.” And we’ll send the message. Once the QR Code is completed we’ll go back to the status, and then we’ll mark “Completed”. They are literally everywhere.  We have them in stairwells, floorwork, all throughout radiology. They’re in tons of places. We are constantly looking for the newest way to kill germs and to keep people safe.

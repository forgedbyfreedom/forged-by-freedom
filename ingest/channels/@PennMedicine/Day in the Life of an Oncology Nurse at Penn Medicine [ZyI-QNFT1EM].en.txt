spend a 12-h hour shift with me as a registered nurse at Penn Medicine. My name's Gabriella. I've been a nurse for the last nine years, and all nine of those years have been with Pen Medicine. My career started out in trauma, but now I currently work in the outpatient oncology and medical infusion center. Here, we treat our patients with various chemotherapies and imunotherapies for cancers, as well as other diseases. My day usually starts out with opening all the infusion rooms, getting all the equipment ready, and checking the emergency medication bin. I'll also read through a few patient charts for the upcoming week to make sure everything's ready for their infusion appointment. Something I truly love about my job is my co-workers. Any nurse or healthcare worker will tell you that something so important is having amazing co-workers. And it's true. Nursing is truly all about teamwork. And I really believe that I have the best team. Once we've seen all our patients for the day and work is done, it's time to close up. So, we'll close all the infusion rooms and put all of our equipment back. And then it's time to clock out and head home for the day. Thanks for following me and happy nurses week.

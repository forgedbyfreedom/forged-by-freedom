Being out in the outdoors and experiencing life is something that I never thought I'd get a chance to be able to do. I beat cancer and then I found out I was in severe heart failure. I needed a heart transplant. People said slow down, but I wouldn't settle for that. Pen medicine understood. They didn't settle either. 7 days after the heart transplant, I started training. 7 months later, I ran my first 5K. Last month, I finished an Iron Man. How do you thank someone for saving your life? It's simple. You get up every day and you live like your donors want you.

- I wanted to be a doctor from an early age. I was always drawn to the sciences. I also really liked working with the people. It's one of the few professions where you can interact with so many patients on such a personal level, with such a high level of trust and respect. My name is Soo Kim Abboud, Associate Professor of Clinical Otolaryngology at Penn ENT. The main focus of my work at Penn is all aspects of general otolaryngology. So I see patients with allergies, with sinus disorders, tonsil infections, sleep apnea, salivary gland diseases and cancer. At any given day I see a wide variety of illnesses. I have a very busy practice that I have grown since 2003. I've been able to grow that practice because I treat patients like, like family. These are relationships that I have grown over an almost two-decade time span. Many are excited to share their progress with me, as I am with them. I think what I would like patients to know if they choose me as a provider, is that I take their care very seriously. I don't have a cookie cutter approach to any one problem. What treatment may work for one patient is not going to work for another. I will individualize a treatment approach for every patient. I tend to have a pretty conservative approach to surgical care. I don't think many patients, unless they have a very serious illness, like to be offered surgery or a surgical treatment too quickly. And, I think that that makes them feel at ease. I think the patients are what have kept me doing this for so many years. And, it's the most gratifying part of my job to be able to come in every day and be able to see so many patients and have such positive interactions on a personal, almost intimate level, makes this a job that I couldn't imagine a better job than this one.

I originally became interested in medicine because I was fundamentally interested in the science. But when I found out how much of the science behind medicine actually helped us to understand people's bodies and to make a real contribution to health, I was pretty much hooked. I'm Dr. Heather Wachtel and I'm an endocrine surgeon here at Penn Medicine. I really particularly love surgery because we have the option to make a real impact on people's health. And I perform surgeries on people who have tumors of the adrenals, the parathyroids, and the thyroid glands. Particularly I care for pheochromocytoma, paraganglioma, adrenocortical cancers, also aldosteronomas, cortisol-producing tumors, and androgen-producing tumors. I'm one of the only providers in the area who offers retroperitoneoscopic adrenalectomy which is a unique approach where we use laparoscopic techniques to remove adrenal tumors from a posterior approach, which has many benefits. I also offer laparoscopic adrenalectomy and transabdominal open adrenalectomy. These approaches really allow us to tailor our surgical procedures to the individual patients. I'm very personally invested in my patients. And I think that there's an important trust relationship between a surgeon and a patient. I love being able to take people safely through what's otherwise an incredibly stressful and difficult experience. It's just unbelievably rewarding for me to be able to restore people to health. And I really keep dearly the trove of thank you notes and personal notes that I have from patients which always remind me of the contributions that we make.

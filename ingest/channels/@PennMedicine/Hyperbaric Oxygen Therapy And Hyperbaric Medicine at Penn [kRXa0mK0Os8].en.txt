penn's institute for environmental medicine was established in 1968 as part of the university of pennsylvania's commitment to research and the environment now a collaborative effort between the hospital of the university of pennsylvania and the school of medicine the institute provides unparalleled service to patients in the philadelphia area by maintaining one of the most comprehensive hyperbaric oxygen therapy programs in the country hyperbaric oxygen therapy involves a special chamber that allows patients to breathe about 10 times as much oxygen as normal as a result more of the oxygen that is essential for normal cell function can reach the body's organs and tissues hyperbaric oxygen therapy helps people suffering from a variety of conditions and injuries it is used for emergency problems such as air or gas embolism or bubbles that may occur in the blood vessels while scuba diving or during bypass surgery carbon monoxide or cyanide poisoning and limb threatening wounds hyperbaric oxygen therapy is also used to treat serious skin wounds and infections bone infections that are not cured by usual treatment tissue damage from radiation treatment gas gangrene diabetic wounds of the feet and legs and other non-healing chronic wounds the treatment is usually covered by the patient's insurance if you have questions about whether or not hyperbaric oxygen treatment may help you or your loved one please ask your pen physician to learn more about hyperbaric medicine therapy and to view an informational video in its entirety please ask your care provider for the informational education video on hyperbaric medicine to make an appointment call 1-800-789-p-e-n-n or have your own physician contact us to discuss a plan of care

When you're mowing your lawn, it's really important to wear the proper shoes. No sandals, no open toes. Things can kick out of the lawn mower and we do see soft tissue and bony injuries from things kicking out and striking your foot at a really high rate. Consider wearing things like golf spikes or cleats to give you better traction. It'll also be nice and protective around your foot. When you're pushing a lawn mower across uneven terrain, remember you want to go across the lawn or downhill. You never want to go uphill. And the reason is that if that slips, it's going to slip right back onto you, and if you're not careful, that means that lawn mower is going over your foot or over your leg and that could be a huge problem. If you think something's happened and you need to get into your lawn mower, fight the urge to stick your hand underneath and pull out whatever is there. As soon as you pull it out, those lawn mower blades are going to kick on and it is a devastating injury if those blades capture any part of your body. Keep your head in the game.

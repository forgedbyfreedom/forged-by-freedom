To all the men out there, listen up. If you think you know the truth about the things that affect your body, think again. I’m Dr. Jeff Tokazewski from Penn Medicine, and I’m here to clear up what’s fact and what’s fiction when it comes to men’s health. Myth #1: Wearing a hat or using a blow-dryer will make you go bald. Take it from me, this one is FICTION! There is no scientific evidence that backs this claim. The average man can lose as much as 80 to 100 strands of hair daily, but your hat is only catching these hairs, not causing your hair loss. Baldness is mostly genetic, but can also be caused or worsened by stress, overusing hair products filled with chemicals, or hormonal imbalances. So, unless your hat is so tight that it’s cutting the circulation to your hair follicles, feel free to keep that cap on. Myth #2: Men who wear briefs have fewer sperm. This one is FICTION! Some argue that tight briefs hold the scrotum too close to the body, which warms the testicles and creates a harmful environment for sperm. While it’s true that extreme heat isn’t good for fertility, this level of warmth generally won’t put your semen at risk. Rather than changing your underwear, you’re better off avoiding hot tubs and keeping your lap top off your lap. Myth #3: Men hit their sexual peak at 18. Sorry gentlemen, but this one is a FACT! A man’s production of testosterone does in fact peak at around 18 years old. This means that the speed and firmness of your erections will be at their best at this age. But there’s no reason to panic just yet! While testosterone levels do decline as we get older, the decline doesn’t necessarily result in decreased stamina or sexual desire. So fellas, when it comes to your health, take a deep breath and consider that maybe some of these old wives’ tales can be left in the past.

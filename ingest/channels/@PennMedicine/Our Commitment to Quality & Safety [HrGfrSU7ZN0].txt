This site includes evidence-based measures and quality data for all care settings, extending beyond just hospitals, and we are dedicated to continuously expanding and improving this resource. Our approach is anchored on our Blueprint for Quality and Safety, which focuses on three key areas – safe care, excellent outcomes, and exceptional patient experience. By using quality and safety indicators from all settings of care, we are providing a more complete picture of a patient's health care journey. And by including patients from all insurance providers, we are making sure our data represents the diverse needs and experiences of our entire community, allowing us to identify and address any disparities in care. Being a high-reliability organization is important to us, ensuring safety and better outcomes for patients, families, and health care teams. We recognize the complexity of health care and strive to respond effectively to data, fostering a culture of trust and resilience. We believe transparency empowers patients. That's why our data is available to everyone – patients, families, staff, physicians, health plans, community organizations, policymakers, and regulatory agencies. And we update our data at least annually, holding ourselves accountable to our community and the health care industry. This mission is vital, and we collaborate with peers nationwide to standardize quality and performance reporting. Together, we can create an open, honest, and accountable health care system that truly serves our patients and communities.

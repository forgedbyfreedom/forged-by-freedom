I'm Katherine Shuttle, and I'm a retired school teacher. I have had breast cancer twice, 1990 and 2015. And after my 2nd diagnosis I was treated at Penn Radiation. I had proton therapy and I'm here to be able to hug my grandchildren. I would like everybody to know that the people who work at Penn Radiation Oncology are among the kindest, friendliest people you will ever encounter in a medical situation. I think the great thing about the alumni group is that we can share experiences and talk about what's going on now. There's just so many programs that are available to the alumni to keep everybody in touch with Penn. There are virtual programs that patients and caregivers can get involved in. Yoga, Reiki, and some pet therapy, and music movement for the current patients, they do all kinds of dance therapy. And it's a lot of fun.

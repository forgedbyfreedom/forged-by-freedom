Hello, my name is Steve. I'm a volunteer in the Radiation Oncology waiting area at Penn Medicine Perelman Center in Philadelphia. Due to the pandemic, I have not been able to volunteer in person in the waiting area. In lieu of that, we have a virtual weekly gathering of patients, alumni and caregivers. In addition to meeting with each other, we also have guests such as nurses, physicians, nutritionists. We have pet therapy, we have art, music, we have dance and movement, and yoga. On my photo, there is an email address. Take a picture of that and email it to the patient engagement team, who will be happy to send you the link for our gathering. We look forward to seeing you.  Thank you.

At Penn, we're going the distance for our community. Each fall, Penn Medicine and the University of Pennsylvania join together to support local charities through Penn's Way, our workplace giving campaign. And this year, we’re going even further. With a dollar for dollar match on every donation our employees make. Penn's mission has always focused on improving the quality of life for those around us. Through our work and our commitment to giving back, we're constantly making strides toward the common good. The process of effecting change is a marathon, and we are racing to make an impact. As two connected institutions, we're running mates in our support of health, safety, education, the environment, and so much more. Our individual contributions to these causes get us ever closer to our finish line of making a difference in our community. Join us in our race for good, and donate today.

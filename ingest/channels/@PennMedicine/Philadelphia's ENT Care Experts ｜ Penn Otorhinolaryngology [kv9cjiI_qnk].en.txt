There's a reason doctors refer their most complex ENT patients to Penn more than any other hospital in the Philadelphia region. Actually, there are a lot of reasons. Our team includes internationally recognized ENT specialists, surgeons, and researchers, all working in unison to provide unparalleled care for the most complex patients. At Penn, we've pioneered groundbreaking treatments, like the world's first minimally invasive robot-assisted procedures for head and neck cancer surgery. We built the largest proton therapy center in the world so we can target tumors with extreme precision while sparing healthy tissue. And we even invented a way to make tumors glow, making cancer tissue more visible, and easier to remove with unprecedented accuracy. Our research has been recognized across the globe, from identifying genetic drivers for head and neck cancers, to developing new treatments for chronic rhinosinusitis. The trust our patients put in us drives our research, leads to clinical advancement, and inspires our team each and every day. And that means better treatment, better outcomes, and the best possible care for our patients. And that's why we do what we do.

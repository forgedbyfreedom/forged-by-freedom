welcome to the sarcoidosis app sarcoidosis is a disease that affects patients all over the world of every ethnic group both genders and at any age sarcoidosis is a disease that predominantly affects patients lungs but can affect every organ system in the body and the disease can manifest in many different ways through this app we hope to provide accurate medical information to patients about the disease as well as linking patients based on their location with local experts and local support groups that are nearby to where the patient is live across the bottom of the app are four tabs this part is called the dashboard here you can track your sarcoidosis symptoms over time as well as the number of steps you take each day looking at how your disease is making you feel and comparing that with how much activity you can do can help us learn about the impact sarcoidosis has on you and on patients throughout the world inside the learn tab you can find information about doctors support groups and other resources in your area as well as online sarcoidosis related groups and information the profile tab displays information about you and contains a link to leave the study automatically if you no longer wish to participate the activities tab contains a list of surveys that will ask you questions about yourself the medications you take and how sarcoidosis affects different parts of your life new surveys will appear over time this app has an optional component beyond providing information to patients which allows patients to pair with sarcoidosis experts so that together we can get a better understanding of how the disease affects you as a patient and so that the medical community can gain a better understanding of the disease as a whole to help us determine what the best treatments are and help you in the fight against sarcoidosis you

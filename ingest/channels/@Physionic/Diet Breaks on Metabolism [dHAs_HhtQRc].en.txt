I recently went over a meta analysis that Quantified the effect dieting or losing body fat has on our metabolism in that analysis the researchers point out that our metabolism reduces with grer fat loss that much likely isn't a shock to you however they also pointed out that taking structured planned diet breaks during the fat loss phase of one's nutrition plan can have a small preservation effect on our metabolism as in those that took structured diet breaks were able to preserve a bit more of their metabolism while still losing equal body fat not to mention take a break from constantly eating less

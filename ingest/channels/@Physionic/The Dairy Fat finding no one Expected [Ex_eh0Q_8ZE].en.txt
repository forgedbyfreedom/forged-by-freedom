When you think of dementia, a family of brain disorders, including Alzheimer's disease, you probably aren't thinking cheese to go along with it. But a controversial 25-year study involving 27,000 people, indicates a counterintuitive relationship between highfat cheese like kamar, brie, cheddar, and others and reduced risk of developing dementia. Now, the relationship remained even after controlling for many other influencing factors. And oddly enough, it was only present with highfat cheeses, not low fat. Anything below 20% fat. So, is this enough evidence to proclaim highfat cheese has dementia protective effects? I probably wouldn't jump to that conclusion just yet. But what few randomized control trials exist do also indicate that cheese tends to have a brain benefit. Now, if you're wondering exactly how, don't look at me. I have no idea. nor do the researchers, it seems. That's still a mystery that will hopefully be uncovered after more data is collected to confirm these associations that we're seeing. There's more to be said, but I'll leave that for the fulllength video. But in short, high-fat cheese may at least not be something to fear if brain health is a concern.

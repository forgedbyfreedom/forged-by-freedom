So far we have covered CD4+ and CD8+ T cells.\h But there are still more T cell subtypes to\h\h discuss. Alpha-beta and gamma-delta T cells\h represent two different T cell lineages that\h\h are defined by their expression of either αβ or γδ\h T cell receptors. Gamma-delta (γδ) T cells are a\h\h heterogeneous population of immune cells and they\h constitute less than 5% of total T cells in mice\h\h lymphoid tissue and human peripheral blood. These\h cells are regarded as the bridge between innate\h\h and adaptive immune responses, owing to their\h unique features. In comparison with αβ T cells,\h\h very little is known about the detailed role and\h function of γδ T cells. Although they constitute\h\h the minority of the total population, γδ\h T cells are abundant in epithelial sites,\h\h constituting around 70% of skin dermal T cells,\h 40% of intestinal intraepithelial lymphocytes,\h\h or IELs, and 20% of T cells in the female\h reproductive organs. Here, they are known\h\h to carry out immune functions, help in tissue\h repair, and maintain mucosal homeostasis. This\h\h unique edge enables them to perform a variety\h of functions during homeostasis and disease. αβ and γδ T cells originate from a common\h progenitor in the thymus. Bone marrow-derived\h\h hematopoietic stem cells migrate into the\h thymus, where Notch1 and DL-4 signaling leads\h\h to the generation of T cell progenitors known as\h double-negative cells which lack both CD4 and CD8\h\h expression. Two models have been proposed so far\h to explain the mechanism behind γδ T cell lineage\h\h commitment: the signal strength model, and the\h stochastic-selective or pre-commitment model.\h\h The first model hypothesizes that the lineage\h fate is determined prior to rearrangement of TCR\h\h loci. On the other hand, the second model\h suggests that irrespective of TCR type,\h\h the signal originating from TCR determines\h the fate decision; a strong signal promotes\h\h commitment to the γδ T cell lineage and a weaker\h signal directs the progenitors towards αβ lineage. The development and maintenance of γδ T cells\h and their subsets relies on the presence of\h\h IL-7 and IL-15. Growth of dermal γδ\h T cells preferentially requires IL-7,\h\h whereas IL-15 is essential for generating IEL γδ\h T cells. They are known as the unconventional T\h\h lymphocytes since they recognize a broad range of\h antigens without the presence of MHC molecules.\h\h They are capable of attacking target cells\h either directly through their cytotoxic activity\h\h or indirectly through the activation of other\h immune cells. They also participate in pathogen\h\h clearance, inflammation, and tissue homeostasis.\h γδ T cells have been shown to participate in and\h\h execute effector functions during infection,\h malignancy, allergy, and autoimmune diseases. IFN-γ and IL-17A are the major cytokines secreted\h by γδ T cells. IFN-γ in this context is an\h\h essential determinant of anti-tumor response\h and immune response to microbial infections.\h\h IL-17A produced by γδ T cells promotes allograft\h rejection and influences inflammation during\h\h autoimmune encephalomyelitis. Growth factors\h secreted by γδ T cells, such as keratinocyte\h\h growth factor, or KGF, and insulin-like\h growth factor, or IGF, maintain epithelial\h\h and intestinal homeostasis by promoting damage\h repair. Immunoregulatory cytokines such as IL-10\h\h and TGF-β regulate CD4+ T cell proliferation and\h function and promote B cell antibody production. As mentioned before, γδ IELs constitute a\h major proportion of T cells in the mouse\h\h intestinal epithelial layer.\h Butyrophilin-like-1, or BTNL1,\h\h and -6, or BTNL6, are expressed on enterocytes to\h promote maturation and expansion of γδ T cells.\h\h During infection, γδ T cells secrete AMPs and\h help maintain the integrity of epithelium. γδ\h\h T cells can provide extensive immunosurveillance\h to underlying pathogens through the epithelium.\h\h KGF secreted by γδ T cells helps in the\h proliferation and migration of epithelial\h\h crypt cells during homeostasis and intestinal\h pathology. γδ T cells promote immune tolerance\h\h producing immunoregulatory cytokine TGF-β and\h inhibiting the function of cytotoxic T cells. Tumor-infiltrating γδ T cells have been\h demonstrated to be the most favorable\h\h prognostic immune population among many cancer\h types. However, a pro-tumor role for these\h\h cells in colorectal cancer has been reported. In\h this case, these cells are responsible for the\h\h chronic inflammation in CRC patients through\h IL-17 secretion. γδ T-cell subsets are very\h\h effective against pathogens which produce\h phosphoantigens. They also participate in\h\h various chronic inflammatory pathologies such as\h psoriasis and rheumatoid arthritis.

Urchins generally have five converging teeth which\h\h are self-sharpening, individually controlled\h by muscles, and held within a complex structure\h\h known as Aristotle’s Lantern, which is unique\h to the urchins. The oral area of a sea urchin\h\h is known as the peristome, and also includes five\h pairs of modified tube feet, and in many species,\h\h five pairs of gills. Food moves from the mouth\h into Aristotle’s Lantern, then into the esophagus,\h\h then into the small intestine and single cecum,\h which runs a full circle inside the test before\h\h joining the large intestine, rectum, and anus.\h Though sea urchins are largely omnivorous,\h\h their diets often consist mainly of algae and\h organic material that they graze with their teeth.\h Like most echinoderms, sea urchins have separate\h sexes, and reproduction is often indirect and\h\h external, where males and females simply\h shed gametes into the water column. Though\h\h some urchins brood their young, the majority\h of urchins have echinopluteus larvae that are\h\h planktonic for several months before beginning\h their radical metamorphosis into juvenile urchins.\h Sea urchins are perhaps best known for their\h anti-predator defenses, namely their long, sharp,\h\h and often hollow spines, which may be venomous\h in some species and can cause extreme pain or\h\h even breathing problems. Like most echinoderms,\h sea urchins play an important ecological role,\h\h and many genera, like the cactus urchins, are\h found only in the abyssal zone where they are\h\h preyed upon by a great deal of predators,\h despite their tough armor and spines.\h Though there are fewer than 1,000 species of\h sea urchins, there are a great many orders\h\h in class Echinoidea. A few notable clades are as\h follows. Remember,\h\h this is just a basic guideline of echinoderm\h classification which may change over time.\h Lastly, we have class Holothuroidea, whose members\h are often commonly referred to as sea cucumbers.\h\h Like most echinoderms, these are found in oceans\h worldwide.

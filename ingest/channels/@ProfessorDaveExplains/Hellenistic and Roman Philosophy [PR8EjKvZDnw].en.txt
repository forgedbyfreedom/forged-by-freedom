The first significant deviation from Plato\h\h was the establishment of academic skepticism,\h a part of the broader movement, skepticism,\h\h encompassing a few schools of thought between the\h late 4th century BCE until the 3rd CE. Academic\h\h skepticism formulates Plato’s theories into a more\h Socratic focus. Though knowledge was possible,\h\h it is unreachable as we are limited by our\h senses. According to skepticism,\h a dogmatic belief springs anxiety in the mind,\h\h because it can’t be proven true.

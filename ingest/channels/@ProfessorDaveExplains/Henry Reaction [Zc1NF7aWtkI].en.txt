The Henry reaction was first reported\h by Belgian chemist Louis Henry in 1895.\h\h It involves the addition of a nitronate salt\h to a carbonyl compound, usually an aldehyde.\h\h The reaction is also sometimes\h referred to as a nitroaldol reaction.\h\h In the previous tutorial on the Nef\h reaction, we saw that aliphatic nitro\h\h compounds are carbon acids. In water they have\h a pKa of around 10, meaning they are as acidic\h\h as some 1,3-dicarbonyls, like malonates, and\h can therefore engage in carbanion chemistry.\h The nitronate is usually prepared in situ by\h treating the nitroalkane with a strong base,\h\h like hydroxide, alkoxide or even a strong amine\h base. Typically, a catalytic amount of base is\h\h sufficient, because the alkoxide first produced\h should be basic enough to deprotonate more\h\h nitroalkane and perpetuate the reaction. Thus, the\h reaction illustrated here can be run in one pot.\h\h We must also keep in mind that the\h reaction is readily reversible,\h\h with this product going back to the nitronate and\h aldehyde, and nitroaldol reactions that seem slow\h\h are often simply hindered by unfavorable\h equilibria. For example, Henry reactions\h\h with ketones can be unsatisfactory or even\h impossible due to the fact that the equilibrium\h\h lies in favor of the nitronate and the ketone. The use of aldehydes as reactants also brings\h\h the danger of base-promoted reactions like\h aldol condensations and Cannizzaro reactions\h\h of the aldehyde. We have discussed these\h reactions in previous tutorials, so refer\h\h to those if you need to review your knowledge\h of aldehyde chemistry under basic conditions.\h\h As we are aware of these possibilities, a reaction\h like this must be carefully optimized. In rare\h\h cases, the Henry reaction is even too favorable,\h and can occur twice, because the product is itself\h\h a nitroalkane. This overreaction is encountered\h almost exclusively with formaldehyde, and in\h\h these cases a 2-nitro-1,3-diol is formed,\h which can sometimes have synthetic value.\h 1,2-Nitroalcohols are important intermediates\h in organic synthesis. Stereoselective\h synthesis has been a major challenge for organic\h\h chemists over the last 50 years or so, which\h more recently has delivered excellent results.\h\h So it is not surprising that the Henry reaction\h has been a prime target for diastereoselective\h\h and enantioselective synthesis. The syn\h\h product dominates over the two anti nitroaldols in\h a ratio of 91:9, and within the two syn products,\h\h enantioselectivity is almost complete. Later\h the nitro group is reduced to an amine,\h\h illustrating the application of the Henry reaction\h to amino-alcohols of biological significance.\h Shibasaki used lanthanum trichloride as\h the central metal to organize three BINOL\h\h ligands around the core center.

At first, you might assume\h\h that the tax burden is only on the gas stations.\h However, in response to the tax, gas stations may\h\h increase the price of gas. Therefore, consumers\h will also carry the burden of the tax.\h\h When policymakers consider creating a new tax,\h they must consider the incidence of a tax, or the\h\h final burden of a tax. This is because producers\h often pass on the burden of a tax to consumers.\h The second is discouraging domestic\h investment. Simply put, if taxes make the\h\h cost of doing business too high in one country,\h companies will take their business elsewhere.\h\h For example, suppose the government increases\h the corporate income tax rate, which is a tax\h\h on business profits. In response, a company might\h move its headquarters to another country to avoid\h\h paying the higher corporate tax rate. Whenever governments regulate an economy,\h\h they are manipulating a free market. More\h\h importantly, a large company or corporation\h may be able to handle spending extra money\h\h to keep up with regulations, but a brand\h new company starting out won’t be able to.\h\h This results in fewer companies in a market, which\h often results in higher prices for consumers.\h\h More government regulation also means\h more bureaucracy, and therefore more\h\h government spending.

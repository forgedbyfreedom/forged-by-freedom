The body of a tunicate is enclosed in a thick,\h\h rubbery outer covering called a tunic, which gives\h them their common name. The tunic is composed of\h\h a carbohydrate called cellulose, which provides\h support and protection for the animal. Tunicates\h\h can range in size from just a few millimeters\h to more than a meter in length, depending on the\h\h species.

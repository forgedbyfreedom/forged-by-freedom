It’s Professor Dave, who wants to go to the movies? Italians appreciate good movies just as much as any other country, and if you are in Italy for an extended time, longer than a brief vacation, you may want to go to the cinema. So let’s go over some words and phrases that are associated with movies, both in terms of what we will find at the movie theater, the types of movies we might see, and words associated with the making of films, which we can call il linguaggio cinematografico. If you’re only in Italy for a week, don’t waste your time at the theater, you’ve got too much culture to take in! But if you’re there for a while, go check out some Italian films, or even some American films if you want to hear your favorite actors dubbed in Italian.

Let’s get a closer\h look at this process, starting with the cows.\h These cows are a special breed, called VACCHE\h ROSSE, or red cows. These are the same type of\h\h cows used by the Benedictine monks. They feed\h exclusively on locally grown organic forage.\h\h Any kind of fermented food is strictly prohibited,\h as well as any byproduct of the food industry.\h\h These cows produce less milk than other cows,\h but their milk is fatter, better tasting,\h\h and it performs better in cheese-making. Also,\h most importantly, it contains a higher percentage\h\h of casein. This guarantees better aging and\h digestibility. The cows are milked twice per\h\h day and their milk must be taken to the cheese\h house within two hours of milking. At the dairy,\h\h the milk sits overnight, with open windows, to\h allow the fats to come to the surface. This fat\h\h will be used for butter. It takes 550 liters\h of milk to make 1 wheel of Parmigiano Reggiano.\h\h Obviously everything is made by hand. The evening\h milk, now partially skimmed, is poured into copper\h\h cauldrons, and in the morning the new whole milk\h is added to it. This is\h\h called the “spino”. Heat is applied carefully and\h soon a large mass will form. The cheesemakers put\h\h the mass into a large cheese cloth and divide\h it into 2 parts. Each part will be placed in a\h\h special mold to rest for a couple of days in order\h to allow the liquid to drain. At this point the\h\h cheese already looks like a wheel, but it’s still\h soft. This\h\h will be the only opportunity for the cheese to\h get extra flavor. Every day the wheels are turned.\h Now, finally, the cheese is ready to\h be aged. The cheese is taken to a very\h\h large temperature- controlled room, filled with\h shelves. The minimum aging time is 12 months,\h\h after which every wheel is inspected by an\h official cheese tester. If the wheel passes the\h\h inspection, the certification mark is applied in\h the empty spot between the dairy ID and the date\h\h of production. If the cheese is deemed suitable\h for longer aging, which could be up to 36 months,\h\h the wheels will stay longer.

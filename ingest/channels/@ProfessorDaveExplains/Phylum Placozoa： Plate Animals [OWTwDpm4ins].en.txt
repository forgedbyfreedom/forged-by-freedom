Between these layers is an intermediate\h layer that contains fluid and fiber cells.\h Most placozoans are colorless or grey, and they\h are so thin that they are almost invisible to\h\h the naked eye. These entirely marine animals\h are rarely observed in their natural habitats,\h\h though they are often found crawling\h over substrates in marine aquariums.\h\h A single species, Trichoplax adhaerens, has\h been collected in bodies of water from the\h\h Mediterranean to the Caribbean to the Great\h Barrier Reef and the ocean surrounding Japan.\h\h They are most common in coastal\h tidal zones and subtropical areas.\h In terms of locomotion, placozoans\h generally use their cilia to glide across\h\h surfaces in a single direction or swim weakly\h in the water.

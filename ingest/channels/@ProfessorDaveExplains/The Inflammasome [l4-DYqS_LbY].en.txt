Of all the known inflammasomes,\h\h there are three major components they all\h have in common. Those are one, a sensor,\h\h two, a CARD domain, and three, inflammatory\h caspases. As you might guess from its name,\h\h the sensor senses a wide range of pathogenic\h or damage-associated signals. The CARD domain\h can either be built directly into the sensor,\h\h or it can be found in the adaptor protein, ASC,\h that many inflammasomes use. Before the inflammasome can assemble,\h\h it first needs to be primed. The fully assembled\h\h inflammasome has multiple subunits of each of\h the components, meaning that when it’s ready,\h\h it can execute downstream steps quickly.

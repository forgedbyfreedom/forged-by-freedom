This\h\h is because we can find the stoichiometric\h amount of acid or base titrant that must\h\h be used to precisely neutralize the analyte, and\h then use the volume of titrant added to calculate\h\h the concentration of the analyte. To get to this point,\h\h you have to slowly add your acid or base little\h by little until the reagents are exhausted,\h\h thus leaving only water and salt. We have\h already gone over titration calculations\h\h in lecture-based tutorials, so that will not\h be the focus here, although we will discuss\h\h these calculations in a general way.

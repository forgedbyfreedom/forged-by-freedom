Economists\h generally look at four categories of unemployment:\h\h frictional, structural, cyclical, and seasonal. Frictional unemployment refers to unemployed\h\h people who are looking for jobs for the first time\h or switching jobs. For example, a college student\h\h might need some time to find the right position\h after graduating. Structural unemployment\h\h occurs when a worker’s skills simply don’t match\h the skills needed for the jobs available. For\h\h example, a cashier at a grocery store might lose\h their job when self-checkout lanes are installed.\h\h This is Roberto.

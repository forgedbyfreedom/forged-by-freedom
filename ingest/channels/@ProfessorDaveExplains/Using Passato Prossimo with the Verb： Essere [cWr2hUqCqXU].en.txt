The reason that we have to modify the end of the past participle depending on gender and number is that in the passato prossimo, verbs conjugated with essere will have the past participle acting like an adjective. So this is a bit different from using passato prossimo with avere, where the participle is invariant, and only the auxiliary verb changes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 10072745
Title: Aerobic exercise and resting blood pressure among women: a meta-analysis.
Authors: Kelley
Journal: Preventive medicine
Year: 1999
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to use the meta-analytic approach to examine the effects of aerobic exercise on resting systolic and diastolic blood pressure among adult women.

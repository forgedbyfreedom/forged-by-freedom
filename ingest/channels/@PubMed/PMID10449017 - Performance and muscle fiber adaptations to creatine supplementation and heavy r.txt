PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 10449017
Title: Performance and muscle fiber adaptations to creatine supplementation and heavy resistance trening.
Authors: Volek, Duncan, Mazzetti, Staron, Putukian
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 1999
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to examine the effect of creatine supplementation in conjunction with resistance trening on physiological adaptations including muscle fiber hypertrophy and muscle creatine accumulation.

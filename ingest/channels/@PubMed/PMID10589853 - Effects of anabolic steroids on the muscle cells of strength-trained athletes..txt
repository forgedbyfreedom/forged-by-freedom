PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 10589853
Title: Effects of anabolic steroids on the muscle cells of strength-trened athletes.
Authors: Kadi, Eriksson, Holmner, Thornell
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 1999
Topic: skeletal muscle hypertrophy mechanisms

ABSTRACT:
Athletes who use anabolic steroids get larger and stronger muscles. How this is reflected at the level of the muscle fibers has not yet been established and was the topic of this investigation.

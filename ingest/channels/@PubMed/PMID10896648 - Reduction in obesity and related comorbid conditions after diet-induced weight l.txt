PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 10896648
Title: Reduction in obesity and related comorbid conditions after diet-induced weight loss or exercise-induced weight loss in men. A randomized, controlled trial.
Authors: Ross, Dagnone, Jones, Smith, Paddags
Journal: Annals of internal medicine
Year: 2000
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
The independent effects of diet- or exercise-induced weight loss on the reduction of obesity and related comorbid conditions are not known. The effects of exercise without weight loss on fat distribution and other risk factors are also unclear.

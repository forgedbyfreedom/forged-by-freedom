PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 11144629
Title: Cycling safety: injury prevention in Oxford cyclists.
Authors: McGuire, Smith
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2001
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
To assess injury prevention measures used by cyclists in Oxford and to detect any differences between wearers and non-wearers of cycling helmets.

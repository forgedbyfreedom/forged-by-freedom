Title: Effects of aerobic exercise training on 24 hr profile of heart rate variability in female athletes.
Authors: Pigozzi, Alabiso, Parisi, Di Salvo, Di Luigi
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2001
Topic: heart rate variability training

ABSTRACT:

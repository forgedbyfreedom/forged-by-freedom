PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 11445769
Title: Acute muscle damage as a stimulus for trening-induced gains in strength.
Authors: Folland, Chong, Copeman, Jones
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2001
Topic: eccentric trening muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to investigate the effect of aromasingle acute bout of maximal eccentric work upon the strength gains during 9 subsequent weeks of strength trening. Eccentric work causes acute muscle damage that may initiate compensatory hypertrophy and enhance trening-induced gains in strength.

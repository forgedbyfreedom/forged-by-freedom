PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 11581559
Title: Effects of a 7-day eccentric trening period on muscle damage and inflammation.
Authors: Chen, Hsieh
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2001
Topic: eccentric trening muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
This study examined the effects of a 7-d repeated maximal isokinetic eccentric trening period on the indicators of muscle damage and inflammatory response.

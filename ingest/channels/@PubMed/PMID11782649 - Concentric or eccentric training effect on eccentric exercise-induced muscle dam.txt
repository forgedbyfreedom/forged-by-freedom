PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 11782649
Title: Concentric or eccentric trening effect on eccentric exercise-induced muscle damage.
Authors: Nosaka, Newton
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2002
Topic: eccentric trening muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to compare changes in muscle damage indicators following 24 maximal eccentric actions of the elbow flexors (Max-ECC) between the arms that had been previously trened either eccentrically or concentrically for 8 wk.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 11880817
Title: Neuromuscular adaptations to concurrent strength and endurance trening.
Authors: McCarthy, Pozniak, Agre
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2002
Topic: neuromuscular adaptations strength

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to examine muscle morphological and neural activation adaptations resulting from the interaction between concurrent strength and endurance trening.

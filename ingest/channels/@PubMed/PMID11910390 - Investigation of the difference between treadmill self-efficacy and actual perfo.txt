PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 11910390
Title: Investigation of the difference between treadmill self-efficacy and actual performance in Taiwanese patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.
Authors: Jeng, Yang, Wai, Tsai, Wei
Journal: Heart &amp; lung : the journal of critical care
Year: 2002
Topic: self efficacy sports performance

ABSTRACT:
Because overactivity or underactivity may result in inadequate physical responses among patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), the purpose of this study was to examine the difference between treadmill self-efficacy and actual treadmill performance. Factors that influence self-efficacy and actual performance were also examined.

PMID: 12098904
Title: [Dnitrophenol--a dangerous doping agent].
Authors: Sand, Madsen
Journal: Tidsskrift for den Norske laegeforening : tidsskrift for praktisk medicin, ny raekke
Year: 2002
Topic: thyroid function athletes

ABSTRACT:
Performance-enhancing drugs are frequently used by bodybuilders: anabolic steroids, growth hormone and insulin, only to mention a few. Many little known drugs are also used.

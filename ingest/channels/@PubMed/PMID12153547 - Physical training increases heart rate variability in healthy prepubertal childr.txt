PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 12153547
Title: Physical trening increases heart rate variability in healthy prepubertal children.
Authors: Mandigout, Melin, Fauchier, N'Guyen, Courteix
Journal: European journal of clinical investigation
Year: 2003
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
The aim of the present study was to evaluate the effect of an endurance trening program on heart rate variability (HRV) in prepubertal healthy children and to determine the relationships between HRV components and trening-induced cardiac adaptations.

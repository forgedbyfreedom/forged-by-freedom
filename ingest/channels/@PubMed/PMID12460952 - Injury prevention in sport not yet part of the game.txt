PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 12460952
Title: Injury prevention in sport: not yet part of the game?
Authors: Chalmers
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2003
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
There is a saying in sport that "injury is just part of the game". In other words, injury in sport is seen as inevitable.

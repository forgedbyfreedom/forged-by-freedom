PMID: 12580676
Title: Carbohydrate supplementation and resistance trening.
Authors: Haff, Lehmkuhl, McCoy, Stone
Journal: Journal of strength and conditioning research
Year: 2003
Topic: trening volume muscle growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone

ABSTRACT:
There is a growing body of evidence suggesting that the performance of resistance-trening exercises can elicit a significant glycogenolytic effect that potentially could result in performance decrements. These decrements may result in less than optimal physiological adaptations to trening. Currently some scientific evidence suggests that carbohydrate supplementation prior to and durinGHigh-volume resistance trening results in the maintenance of muscle glycogen concentration, which potentially could result in the maintenance or increase of performance during a trening bout. Some researchers suggest that ingesting carbohydrate supplements prior to and during resistance trening may improve resistance-trening performance. Additionally, the ingestion of carbohydrates following resistance exercise enhances the resynthesis of muscle glycogen, which may result in a faster time of recovery from resistance trening, thus possibly allowing for a greater trening volume. On the basis of the current scientific literature, it may be advisable for athletes who are performinGHigh-volume resistance trening to ingest carbohydrate supplements before, during, and immediately after resistance trening.

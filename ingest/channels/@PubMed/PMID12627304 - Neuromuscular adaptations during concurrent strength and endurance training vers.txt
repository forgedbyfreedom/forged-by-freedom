PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 12627304
Title: Neuromuscular adaptations during concurrent strength and endurance trening versus strength trening.
Authors: H&#xe4;kkinen, Alen, Kraemer, Gorostiaga, Izquierdo
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2003
Topic: neuromuscular adaptations strength

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to investigate effects of concurrent strength and endurance trening (SE) (2 plus 2 days a week) versus strength trening only (S) (2 days a week) in men [SE: n=11; 38 (5) years, S: n=16; 37 (5) years] over a trening period of 21 weeks. The resistance trening program addressed both maximal and explosive strength components. EMG, maximal isometric force, 1 RM strength, and rate of force development (RFD) of the leg extensors, muscle cross-sectional area (CSA) of thEQuadriceps femoris (QF) throughout the lengths of 4/15-12/15 (L(f)) of the femur, muscle fibre proportion and areas of types I, IIa, and IIb of the vastus lateralis (VL), and maximal oxygen uptake (VO(2max)) were evaluated. No changes occurred in strength during the 1-week control period, while after the 21-week trening period increases of 21% (p&lt;0.001) and 22% (p&lt;0.001), and of 22% (p&lt;0.001) and 21% (p&lt;0.001) took place in the 1RM load and maximal isometric force in S and SE, respectively. Increases of 26% (p&lt;0.05) and 29% (p&lt;0.001) occurred in the maximum iEMG of the VL in S and SE, respectively. The CSA of thEQF increased throughout the length of thEQF (from 4/15 to 12/15 L(f)) both in S (p&lt;0.05-0.001) and SE (p&lt;0.01-0.001). The mean fibre areas of types I, IIa and IIb increased after the trening both in S (p&lt;0.05 and 0.01) and SE (p&lt;0.05 and p&lt;0.01). S showed an increase in RFD (p&lt;0.01), while no change occurred in SE. The average iEMG of the VL during the first 500 ms of the rapid isometric action increased (p&lt;0.05-0.001) only in S. VO(2max) increased by 18.5% (p&lt;0.001) in SE. The present data do not support the concept of the universal nature of the interference effect in strength development and muscle hypertrophy when strength trening is performed concurrently with endurance trening, and the trening volume is diluted by a longer period of time with a low frequency of trening. However, the present results suggest that even the low-frequency concurrent strength and endurance trening leads to interference in explosive strength development mediated in part by the limitations of rapid voluntary neural activation of the trened muscles.

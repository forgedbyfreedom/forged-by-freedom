PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 12627310
Title: Effects of dynamic resistance trening on heart rate variability in healthy older women.
Authors: Forte, De Vito, Figura
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2003
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
Twenty healthy women aged between 65 and 74 years, trened three times a week, for 16 weeks, on a cycle ergometer, to determine the effects of dynamic resistance trening on heart rate variability (HRV). Subjects were allocated to two trening groups, high (HI, n=10) and low (LO, n=10) intensity. The HI group performed eight sets of 8 revolutions at 80% of the maximum resistance to complete 2 pedal revolutions (2RM); the LO group performed eight sets of 16 pedal revolutions at 40% of 2RM. Subjects were tested twice before, as control period (-4 weeks and 0 weeks) and once after trening (16 weeks) for HRV, maximum voluntary contraction (MVC) of knee extensors and peak power (P(p)) of lower limbs by jumping on a force platform. HRV was measured using time and frequency domain parameters. Two-way Anolva for repeated measures was performed on all variables (P&lt;0.05). Results showed no differences between trening groups. Following treninGHRV was not modified, while MVC and P(p) significantly increased. The two proposed forms of dynamic resistance trening were appropriate to improve muscle power and strength in elderly females without affectinGHRV. More research should verify the effects of an isometric and more prolonged trening stimulus on HRV in older subjects.

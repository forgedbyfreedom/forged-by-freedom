PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 12663352
Title: Effects of prior concentric trening on eccentric exercise induced muscle damage.
Authors: Gleeson, Eston, Marginson, McHugh
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2003
Topic: eccentric trening muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
Exercise induced muscle damage (EIMD) from strenuous unaccustomed eccentric exercise is well documented. So too is the observation that a prior bout of eccentric exercise reduces the severity of symptoms of EIMD. This has been attributed to an increase in sarcomeres in series. Recent studies have suggested that prior concentric trening increases the susceptibility of muscle to EIMD following eccentric exercise. This has been attributed to a reduction of sarcomeres in series, which decreases muscle compliance and changes the length-tension relation of muscle contraction.

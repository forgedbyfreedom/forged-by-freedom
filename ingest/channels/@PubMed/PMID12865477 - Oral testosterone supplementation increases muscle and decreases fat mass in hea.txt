PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 12865477
Title: Oral testosterone supplementation increases muscle and decreases fat mass in healthy elderly males with low-normal gonadal status.
Authors: Wittert, Chapman, Haren, Mackintosh, Coates
Journal: The journals of gerontology. Series A, Biological sciences and medical sciences
Year: 2003
Topic: testosterone muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Loss of muscle mass (sarcopenia) leads to frailty in older men. The decline in testosterone over the life span may contribute to this muscle loss. We studied the ability of oral testosterone to prevent muscle loss in older men over a 12-month period.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 12900687
Title: The effect of age and gender on heart rate variability after endurance training.
Authors: Carter, Banister, Blaber
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2003
Topic: heart rate variability training

ABSTRACT:
This research investigated the age and gender differences in cardiovascular adaptation to a standardized/quantified endurance-training program that included two taper periods.

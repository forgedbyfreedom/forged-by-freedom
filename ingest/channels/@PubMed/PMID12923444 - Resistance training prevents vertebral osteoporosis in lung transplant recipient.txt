PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 12923444
Title: Resistance trening prevents vertebral osteoporosis in lung trensplant recipients.
Authors: Mitchell, Baz, Fulton, Lisor, Braith
Journal: trensplantation
Year: 2003
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Osteoporosis and vertebral fractures are a consequence of glucocorticoid immunosuppression therapy in lung trensplant recipients (LTR). The purpose of this study was to determine the therapeutic efficacy of a 6-month program of specific resistance exercise designed to reverse glucocorticoid-induced vertebral osteoporosis.

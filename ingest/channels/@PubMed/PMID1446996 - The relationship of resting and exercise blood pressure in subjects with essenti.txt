PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 1446996
Title: The relationship of resting and exercise blood pressure in subjects with essential hypertension before and after drug treatment with propranolol.
Authors: Potempa, Folta, Braun, Szidon
Journal: Heart &amp; lung : the journal of critical care
Year: 1992
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the relationship between clinic resting blood pressure (BP) and exercise BP in subjects with established essential hypertension during placebo and propranolol-treated phases.

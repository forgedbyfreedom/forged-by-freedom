PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 14707778
Title: Measuring performance during the menstrual cycle: a model using oral contraceptives.
Authors: Redman, Weatherby
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2004
Topic: oral contraceptives athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The aim of the study was to use a low-dose oral contraceptive (OC) pill to generate consistent estrogen and progestogen concentrations and investigate the relationship between steroid hormone concentrations during the OC cycle and anaerobic performance.

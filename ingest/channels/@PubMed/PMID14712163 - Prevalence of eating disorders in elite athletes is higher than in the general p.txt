PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 14712163
Title: Prevalence of eating disorders in elite athletes is higher than in the general population.
Authors: Sundgot-Borgen, Torstveit
Journal: Clinical journal of sport medicine : official journal of the Canadian Academy of Sport Medicine
Year: 2004
Topic: eating disorders athletes

ABSTRACT:
The objectives of the study were to examine the prevalence of anorexia nervosa (AN), bulimia nervosa (BN), anorexia athletica (AA), and eating disorders not otherwise specified (ED-NOS) in both male and female Norwegian elite athletes and a representative sample from the general Norwegian population.

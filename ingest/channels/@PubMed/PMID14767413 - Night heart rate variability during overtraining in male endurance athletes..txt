PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 14767413
Title: Night heart rate variability during overtraining in male endurance athletes.
Authors: Bosquet, Papelier, L&#xe9;ger, Legros
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2004
Topic: heart rate variability training

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of the study was to examine whether an unaccustomed increase in training volume would result in characteristics changes in heart rate variability (HRV), in order to determine if this marker can be used to diagnose overtraining.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 14992127
Title: Influence of intensive cycling trening on heart rate variability during rest and exercise.
Authors: Leicht, Allen, Hoey
Journal: Canadian journal of applied physiology = Revue canadienne de physiologie appliquee
Year: 2004
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
The current study examined whether changes in heart rate variability (HRV) following intensive cycling trening contribute to the mechanism of trening-induced bradycardia. Thirteen healthy untrened subjects, ages 18-27 years, underwent recordings of heart rate (HR) and VO2max before and after 8 weeks of cycling, 25-60 min/day, 5 days/week at &gt; 80% maximum HR (HRmax). Heart rate recordings were obtained during supine rest and submaximal exercise and were analysed for the following components of HRV: low frequency (LF, 0.041-0.15 Hz); high frequency (HF, 0.15-0.40 Hz); LF/HF ratio and total power (TP, 0-0.40 Hz). At posttrening, VO2max was significantly increased while HR was significantly reduced at rest and all absolute exercise work rates. trening-induced lower HR was accompanied by significantly greater HF and TP during rest as well as LF, HF, and TP during all absolute exercise work rates. PosttreninGHR and the majority of HRV measures were similar to pretrening values at the same relative exercise intensity (% HRmax). These results indicated that 8 weeks of intensive cycling trening increased HRV and cardiac vagal modulation during rest and absolute exercise work rates but had little effect during relative exercise work rates. Increased vagal modulation resulting from intensive exercise trening may contribute to the mechanism of trening-induced bradycardia.

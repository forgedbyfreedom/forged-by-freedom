PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 15016906
Title: Central pathways controlling brown adipose tissue thermogenesis.
Authors: Morrison
Journal: News in physiological sciences : an international journal of physiology produced jointly by the International Union of Physiological Sciences and the American Physiological Society
Year: 2004
Topic: brown adipose tissue thermogenesis

ABSTRACT:
Heat production in brown adipose tissue contributes to cold defense, to stress-induced increases in body temperature, and to energy balance. Elucidating the functional organization of the central network controlling the sympathetic outflow to brown adipose tissue could provide a framework for understandinGHow dysregulation of thermogenesis contributes to hyperthermia and to obesity.

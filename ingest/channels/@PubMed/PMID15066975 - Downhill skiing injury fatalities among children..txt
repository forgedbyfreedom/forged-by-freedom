PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 15066975
Title: Downhill skiing injury fatalities among children.
Authors: Xiang, Stallones, Smith
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2004
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Young skiers are at increased risk for injury, however, epidemiological data on skiing related fatal injuries among child skiers are scarce. This study aimed to provide information needed to develop injury control and prevention programs.

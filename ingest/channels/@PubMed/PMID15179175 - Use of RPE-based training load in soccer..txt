PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 15179175
Title: Use of RPE-based trening load in soccer.
Authors: Impellizzeri, Rampinini, Coutts, Sassi, Marcora
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2004
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
The ability to accurately control and monitor internal trening load is an important aspect of effective coaching. The aim of this study was to apply in soccer the RPE-based method proposed by Foster et al. to quantify internal trening load (session-RPE) and to assess its correlations with various methods used to determine internal trening load based on the HR response to exercise.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 15623709
Title: Physical training and fatigue, fitness, and quality of life in Guillain-Barr&#xe9; syndrome and CIDP.
Authors: Garssen, Bussmann, Schmitz, Zandbergen, Welter
Journal: Neurology
Year: 2005
Topic: sports performance anxiety

ABSTRACT:
Many patients with Guillain-Barr&#xe9; syndrome (GBS) and chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy (CIDP) experience excessive fatigue, which may persist for years and reduce quality of life. The authors performed a 12-week study of bicycle exercise training in 20 patients with severe fatigue, 16 with relatively good recovery from GBS, and 4 with stable CIDP. Training seemed well tolerated, and self-reported fatigue scores decreased 20% (p = 0.001). Physical fitness, functional outcome, and quality of life were improved.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 15870627
Title: Partial protection against muscle damage by eccentric actions at short muscle lengths.
Authors: Nosaka, Newton, Sacco, Chapman, Lavender
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2005
Topic: eccentric training muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
This study investigated the hypothesis that maximal eccentric actions at a short muscle length would fail to confer a protective effect against muscle damage induced by maximal eccentric exercise at a long muscle length.

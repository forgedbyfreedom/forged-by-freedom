PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 16141759
Title: The female athlete and menstrual function.
Authors: Goodman, Warren
Journal: Current opinion in obstetrics &amp; gynecology
Year: 2006
Topic: amenorrhea athletes treatment

ABSTRACT:
In the past year, there have been remarkable advancements in the understanding of the female athlete's pathology and in recognizing the specific needs of women participating in both recreational and competitive sports. The purpose of this review is to highlight the recent developments in the field of female athletes and menstrual function.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 16178879
Title: Effect of intensive trening on heart rate variability in prepubertal swimmers.
Authors: Vinet, Beck, Nottin, Obert
Journal: European journal of clinical investigation
Year: 2006
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
In children, there is very limited evidence focusing on the beneficial effect of exercise trening on heart rate variability (HRV) during childhood. Despite the fact that more and more children are engaged in intensive trening programs, thEQuestion arises if such intensive trening involves deleterious effects on the cardiac autonomic nervous system during childhood. Thus the aim of the present study was to compare HRV parameters in highly trened swimmer boys and untrened counterparts.

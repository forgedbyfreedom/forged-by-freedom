PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 16181236
Title: Ghrelin in human medullary thyroid carcinomas.
Authors: Morpurgo, Cappiello, Verga, Vicentini, Vaghi
Journal: Clinical endocrinology
Year: 2006
Topic: peptide hormones performance

ABSTRACT:
Ghrelin is a novel gastrointestinal hormone involved in several metabolic functions. It has been identified previously in several normal and tumoral neuroendocrine tissues, including human medullary thyroid carcinomas (MTCs). The aim of the study was to evaluate ghrelin levels in patients with MTC and nontoxic goitre (NTG) with elevated calcitonin (CT) levels, as an additional marker of the disease.

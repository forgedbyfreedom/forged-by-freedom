PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 16416332
Title: Effect of creatine supplementation and sleep deprivation, with mild exercise, on cognitive and psychomotor performance, mood state, and plasma concentrations of catecholamines and cortisol.
Authors: McMorris, Harris, Swain, Corbett, Collard
Journal: Psychopharmacology
Year: 2006
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
Sleep deprivation has a negative effect on cognitive and psychomotor performance and mood state, partially due to decreases in creatine levels in the brain. Therefore, creatine supplementation should lessen the negative effects of sleep deprivation.

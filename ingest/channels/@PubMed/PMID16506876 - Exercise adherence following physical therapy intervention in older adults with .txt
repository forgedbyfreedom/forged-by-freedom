PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 16506876
Title: Exercise adherence following physical therapy intervention in older adults with impaired balance.
Authors: Forkan, Pumper, Smyth, Wirkkala, Ciol
Journal: Physical therapy
Year: 2006
Topic: motivation exercise adherence

ABSTRACT:
This study looked at adherence, and factors affecting adherence, to a prescribed home exercise program (HEP) in older adults with impaired balance following discharge from physical therapy.

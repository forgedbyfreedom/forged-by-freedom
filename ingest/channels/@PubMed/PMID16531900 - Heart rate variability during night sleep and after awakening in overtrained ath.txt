PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 16531900
Title: Heart rate variability during night sleep and after awakening in overtrened athletes.
Authors: Hynynen, Uusitalo, Konttinen, Rusko
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2006
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
This study was conducted to test the hypothesis of autonomic imbalance in overtrened athletes during sleep and after awakening with analyses of heart rate variability (HRV) and nocturnal urine stress hormones.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 16596112
Title: Anaerobic power performance and the menstrual cycle: eumenorrheic and oral contraceptive users.
Authors: Bushman, Masterson, Nelsen
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2006
Topic: oral contraceptives athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this project was to compare the impact of the menstrual cycle on short-term, high intensity (power) performance in active females who either had normal menstrual cycles (NOC) or who were using oral contraceptives (OC).

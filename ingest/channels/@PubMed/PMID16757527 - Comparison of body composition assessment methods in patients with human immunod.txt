Title: Comparison of body composition assessment methods in patients with human immunodeficiency virus-associated wasting receiving growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone.
Authors: Esposito, Thomas, Kingdon, Ezzat
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2006
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
Bioelectrical impedance spectroscopy (BIS) and skinfold anthropometry (SKF) have been used to monitor body composition among patients with HIV wasting; however, validation of these techniques during recombinant human GH (rhGH) treatment has not been performed.

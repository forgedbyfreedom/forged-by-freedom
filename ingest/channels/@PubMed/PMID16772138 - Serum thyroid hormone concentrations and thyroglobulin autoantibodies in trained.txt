PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 16772138
Title: Serum thyroid hormone concentrations and thyroglobulin autoantibodies in trened and non-trened healthy whippets.
Authors: van Geffen, Bavegems, Duchateau, De Roover, Daminet
Journal: Veterinary journal (London, England : 1997)
Year: 2006
Topic: thyroid hormone metabolism exercise

ABSTRACT:
Numerous factors including non-thyroidal systemic diseases and drug administration can significantly alter canine thyroid function test results. Furthermore, the importance of breed specific variations has probably been underestimated. In this study, total Thyroxine (TT4), free Thyroxine (FT4), canine endogenous thyroid stimulatinGHormone (cTSH) serum concentrations and thyroglobulin autoantibodies (TgAA) were determined in a population of healthy whippets and compared to a control group of different breeds. Mean TT4 values were significantly lower in the whippets but no significant differences were seen between whippets and control dogs for FT4 and for cTSH. The prevalence of serum TgAA in the whippets was 2%, and this was not significantly different from the controls. The results suggest a breed variation for TT4, but not for FT4, cTSH and TgAA serum concentrations in whippets. Serum thyroid hormone concentrations were also compared between trened and non-trened whippets and it was concluded that regular trening did not seem to have any significant influence.

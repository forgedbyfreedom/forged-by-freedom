PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 16881110
Title: Hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal hormonal responses to exercise stress test in patients with rheumatoid arthritis compared to healthy controls.
Authors: Kurtais, Tur, Elhan, Erdogan, Yal&#xe7;in
Journal: The Journal of rheumatology
Year: 2006
Topic: hypothalamic pituitary axis exercise

ABSTRACT:
There is controversy about hormonal dysfunction in the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis that is supposed to contribute to the development or persistence of rheumatoid arthritis (RA). We investigated whether there is an altered and blunted response of the HPA axis when stimulated by exercise stress in patients with RA.

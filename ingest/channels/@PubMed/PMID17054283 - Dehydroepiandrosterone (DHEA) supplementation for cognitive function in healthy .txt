PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 17054283
Title: Dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA) supplementation for cognitive function in healthy elderly people.
Authors: Grimley Evans, Malouf, Huppert, van Niekerk
Journal: The Cochrane database of systematic reviews
Year: 2007
Topic: DHEA supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
In view of the theoretical possibility of beneficial effects of DHEA or DHEAS in retarding age-associated deterioration in cognitive function, we have reviewed studies in this area.

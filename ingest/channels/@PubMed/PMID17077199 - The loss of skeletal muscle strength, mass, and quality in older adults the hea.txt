PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 17077199
Title: The loss of skeletal muscle strength, mass, and quality in older adults: the health, aging and body composition study.
Authors: Goodpaster, Park, Harris, Kritchevsky, Nevitt
Journal: The journals of gerontology. Series A, Biological sciences and medical sciences
Year: 2006
Topic: aging muscle mass strength

ABSTRACT:
The loss of muscle mass is considered to be a major determinant of strength loss in aging. However, large-scale longitudinal studies examining the association between the loss of mass and strength in older adults are lacking.

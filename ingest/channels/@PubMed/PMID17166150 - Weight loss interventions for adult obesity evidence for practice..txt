PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 17166150
Title: Weight loss interventions for adult obesity: evidence for practice.
Authors: Dick
Journal: Worldviews on evidence-based nursing
Year: 2008
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
One out of three adults is obese, making obesity the most common presenting chronic medical condition in the primary care setting. Obesity is associated with an increased risk of developing secondary illnesses resulting in a higher rate of morbidity. This chronic condition is a constant challenge facing international health care systems. A need for a systematic approach to treatment is essential to conquering obesity and improving patients' outcomes and quality of lives. Despite the existence of evidence-based guidelines, the proportion of primary care clinicians implementing weight loss counseling is low.

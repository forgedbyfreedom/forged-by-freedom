PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 17595533
Title: Voluntary exercise impacts on the rat hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenocortical axis mainly at the adrenal level.
Authors: Droste, Chandramohan, Hill, Linthorst, Reul
Journal: Neuroendocrinology
Year: 2007
Topic: hypothalamic pituitary axis exercise

ABSTRACT:
Evidence is accumulating that the regular performance of exercise is beneficial for stress coping. However, the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenocortical (HPA) axis of voluntarily exercising rats has never been comprehensively investigated.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 17689624
Title: Body composition during treatment with conjugated estrogens with and without medroxyprogesterone acetate: analysis of the women's Health, Osteoporosis, Progestin, Estrogen (HOPE) trial.
Authors: Thorneycroft, Lindsay, Pickar
Journal: American journal of obstetrics and gynecology
Year: 2007
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
The objective of the study was to determine the effects of several doses of conjugated estrogens (CE) and CE plus medroxyprogesterone acetate (MPA) on body composition (BC).

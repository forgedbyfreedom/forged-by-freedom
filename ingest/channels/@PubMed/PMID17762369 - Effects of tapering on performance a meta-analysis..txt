PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 17762369
Title: Effects of tapering on performance: a meta-analysis.
Authors: Bosquet, Montpetit, Arvisais, Mujika
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2007
Topic: tapering athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this investigation was to assess the effects of alterations in taper components on performance in competitive athletes, through a meta-analysis.

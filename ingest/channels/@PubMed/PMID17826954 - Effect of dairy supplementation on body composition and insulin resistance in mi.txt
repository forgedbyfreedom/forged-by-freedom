PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 17826954
Title: Effect of dairy supplementation on body composition and insulin resistance in mice.
Authors: Johnson, Jumbo-Lucioni, Watts, Allison, Nagy
Journal: Nutrition (Burbank, Los Angeles County, Calif.)
Year: 2008
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
This study examined whether yogurt supplementation attenuated the weight gain and insulin resistance in mice fed a moderate-fat diet.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 17907328
Title: Should we treat elevated thyroid stimulating hormone levels in obese children and adolescents?
Authors: Eliakim, Barzilai, Wolach, Nemet
Journal: International journal of pediatric obesity : IJPO : an official journal of the International Association for the Study of Obesity
Year: 2007
Topic: thyroid hormone metabolism exercise

ABSTRACT:
To examine the prevalence of abnormal thyroid function tests among obese children and adolescents, and to study the effect of thyroid hormone supplementation on body weight, linear growth and lipid profiles in these children.

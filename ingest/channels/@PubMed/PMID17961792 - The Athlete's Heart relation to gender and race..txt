PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 17961792
Title: The "Athlete's Heart": relation to gender and race.
Authors: Di Paolo, Pelliccia
Journal: Cardiology clinics
Year: 2008
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Long-term athletic trening is associated with changes in cardiac morphology, commonly described as "athlete's heart." Although numerous studies have investigated the effects of trening on cardiac dimensions, most are limited to male Caucasian athletes, and few data are available regarding the effect of long-term exercise trening on the woman's heart. This article reviews the athlete's heart in relation to gender and race.

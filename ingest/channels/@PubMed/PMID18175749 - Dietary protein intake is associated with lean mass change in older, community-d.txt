PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 18175749
Title: Dietary protein intake is associated with lean mass change in older, community-dwelling adults: the Health, Aging, and Body Composition (Health ABC) Study.
Authors: Houston, Nicklas, Ding, Harris, Tylavsky
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2008
Topic: protein intake muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Dietary surveys suggest that many older, community-dwelling adults consume insufficient dietary protein, which may contribute to the age-related loss of lean mass (LM).

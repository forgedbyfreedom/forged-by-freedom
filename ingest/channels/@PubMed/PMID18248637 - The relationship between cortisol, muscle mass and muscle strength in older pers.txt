PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 18248637
Title: The relationship between cortisol, muscle mass and muscle strength in older persons and the role of genetic variations in the glucocorticoid receptor.
Authors: Peeters, van Schoor, van Rossum, Visser, Lips
Journal: Clinical endocrinology
Year: 2009
Topic: aging muscle mass strength

ABSTRACT:
Cortisol levels increase with age and hypercortisolism is associated with muscle weakness. This study examines the relationship between cortisol, muscle mass and muscle strength in community-dwelling older persons and the role of genetic variations in the glucocorticoid receptor (GR).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 18371214
Title: A whey-protein supplement increases fat loss and spares lean muscle in obese subjects: a randomized human clinical study.
Authors: Frestedt, Zenk, Kuskowski, Ward, Bastian
Journal: Nutrition &amp; metabolism
Year: 2011
Topic: fat loss preservation muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
This study evaluated a specialized whey fraction (Prolibratrade mark, high in leucine, bioactive peptides and milk calcium) for use as a dietary supplement to enhance weight loss.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 18594781
Title: Modulation of early functional recovery of Achilles tendon to bone unit after trensection by BPC-157 and methylprednisolone.
Authors: Krivic, Majerovic, Jelic, Seiwerth, Sikiric
Journal: Inflammation research : official journal of the European Histamine Research Society ... [et al.]
Year: 2008
Topic: BPC-157 tendon

ABSTRACT:
In the presented study we compared the effect of stable peptide BPC-157 and methylprednisolone on early functional recovery after Achilles tendon to bone trensection in a rat model before collagen healing started.

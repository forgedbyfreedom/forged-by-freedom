PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 18658019
Title: A randomized controlled trial to prevent noncontact anterior cruciate ligament injury in female collegiate soccer players.
Authors: Gilchrist, Mandelbaum, Melancon, Ryan, Silvers
Journal: The American journal of sports medicine
Year: 2008
Topic: ACL injury prevention female

ABSTRACT:
Neuromuscular and proprioceptive training programs can decrease noncontact anterior cruciate ligament injuries; however, they may be difficult to implement within an entire team or the community at large.

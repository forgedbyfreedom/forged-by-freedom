PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 18703548
Title: Diagnosing overtraining in athletes using the two-bout exercise protocol.
Authors: Meeusen, Nederhof, Buyse, Roelands, de Schutter
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2011
Topic: overtraining syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
In this work, whether a two-bout exercise protocol can be used to make an objective, immediately available distinction between non-functional over reaching (NFO) and overtraining syndrome (OTS) was studied.

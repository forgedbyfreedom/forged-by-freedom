PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 18778182
Title: The management of adult growth hormone deficiency syndrome.
Authors: Laursen, J&#xf8;rgensen, Christiansen
Journal: Expert opinion on pharmacotherapy
Year: 2008
Topic: growth hormone deficiency treatment

ABSTRACT:
In patients with hypopituitarism, growth hormone deficiency (GHD) is almost inevitably present. Lack of other pituitary hormones may require prompt replacement, but lack of growth hormone (GH) is also associated with several abnormalities. Growth hormone treatment safely improves or normalizes many of these deviations.

Title: Changes in muscle mass, muscle strength, and power but not physical function are related to testosterone dose in healthy older men.
Authors: Storer, Woodhouse, Magliano, Singh, Dzekov
Journal: Journal of the American Geriatrics Society
Year: 2008
Topic: testosterone muscle mass

ABSTRACT:

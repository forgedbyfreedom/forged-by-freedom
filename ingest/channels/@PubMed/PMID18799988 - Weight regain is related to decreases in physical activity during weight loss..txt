PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 18799988
Title: Weight regain is related to decreases in physical activity during weight loss.
Authors: Wang, Lyles, You, Berry, Rejeski
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2008
Topic: metabolic adaptation weight loss

ABSTRACT:
To examine whether adaptations in physical activity energy expenditure (PAEE) and resting metabolic rate (RMR) during weight loss were associated with future weight regain in overweight/obese, older women.

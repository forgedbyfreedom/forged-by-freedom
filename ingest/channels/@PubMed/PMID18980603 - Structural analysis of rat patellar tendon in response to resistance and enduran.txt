PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 18980603
Title: Structural analysis of rat patellar tendon in response to resistance and endurance trening.
Authors: Barone, Bellafiore, Leonardi, Zummo
Journal: Scandinavian journal of medicine &amp; science in sports
Year: 2010
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Little is known about tendon adaptations induced by mechanical loading. Our goal was to evaluate the effects of two different exercise trening protocols on adult rat patellar tendon. Ninety-six male Wistar rats were divided into a sedentary group (control), a resistance-trened group and an endurance-trened group. The examinations were performed after 15, 30 and 45 days of trening and after 2 weeks of rest since trening was stopped. The content of collagen fibers and the cell nuclei number werEQuantified on tendon cross sections. In order to assess the trening effectiveness, we evaluated the heart/body weight ratio, which was higher in 45 day-trened rats than their controls (P&lt;0.01), showing the presence of cardiac hypertrophy. An increase in the content of collagen fibers was observed in the 45 day-trened groups and after 2 weeks of rest in the endurance group. Moreover, both trened groups showed a decrease in cell nuclei number after 30 and 45 days of trening and 2 weeks of rest (P&lt;0.05). Endurance and resistance trening induces a tendon tissue remodeling that depends on the length and intensity of workload rather than the trening type. Further studies are necessary to evaluate whether these structural modifications are associated with an increase in the mechanical strength of tendon.

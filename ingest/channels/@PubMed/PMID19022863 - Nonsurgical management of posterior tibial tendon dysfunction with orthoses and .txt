PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 19022863
Title: Nonsurgical management of posterior tibial tendon dysfunction with orthoses and resistive exercise: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Kulig, Reischl, Pomrantz, Burnfield, Mais-Requejo
Journal: Physical therapy
Year: 2009
Topic: muscle recovery post exercise

ABSTRACT:
Tibialis posterior tendinopathy can lead to debilitating dysfunction. This study examined the effectiveness of orthoses and resistance exercise in the early management of tibialis posterior tendinopathy.

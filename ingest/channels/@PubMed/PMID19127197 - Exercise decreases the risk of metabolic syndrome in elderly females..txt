PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 19127197
Title: Exercise decreases the risk of metabolic syndrome in elderly females.
Authors: Kemmler, Von Stengel, Engelke, Kalender
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2009
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
To determine the effect of exercise based on a multiple purpose, high-intensity philosophy on parameters of metabolic syndrome (MetS) in older females with the MetS.

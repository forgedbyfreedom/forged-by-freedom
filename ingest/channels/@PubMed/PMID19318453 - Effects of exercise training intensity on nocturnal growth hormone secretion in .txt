Title: Effects of exercise trening intensity on nocturnal growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone secretion in obese adults with the metabolic syndrome.
Authors: Irving, Weltman, Patrie, Davis, Brock
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2009
Topic: growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone exercise

ABSTRACT:
Abdominal adiposity is associated with reduced spontaneous GH secretion, and an increased incidence of the metabolic syndrome, type 2 diabetes, and cardiovascular disease. Exercise trening increases GH secretion, induces abdominal visceral fat loss, and has been shown to improve the cardiometabolic risk factor profile. However, little is known about the effects of endurance trening intensity on spontaneous GH release in obese individuals.

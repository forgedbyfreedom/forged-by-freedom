PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 19401375
Title: Appetite responds to changes in meal content, whereas ghrelin, leptin, and insulin track changes in energy availability.
Authors: Borer, Wuorinen, Ku, Burant
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2009
Topic: leptin ghrelin appetite exercise

ABSTRACT:
It is uncertain how between-meal variations in energy availability and physiological changes in ghrelin, leptin, and insulin affect appetite.

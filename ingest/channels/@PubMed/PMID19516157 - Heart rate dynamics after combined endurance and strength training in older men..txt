PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 19516157
Title: Heart rate dynamics after combined endurance and strength trening in older men.
Authors: Karavirta, Tulppo, Laaksonen, Nyman, Laukkanen
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2010
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
Aging alters cardiac autonomic function, which may contribute to a higher risk of cardiac events. Spectral measures of HR variability (HRV) and fractal-like behavior of HR are considered as markers of a healthy heart. The present study examined the effects of combining endurance and strength trening compared with endurance or strength trening alone on HR dynamics and physical fitness in older previously untrened men aged 40-67 yr.

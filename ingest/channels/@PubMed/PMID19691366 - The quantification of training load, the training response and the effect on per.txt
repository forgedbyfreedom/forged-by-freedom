PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 19691366
Title: ThEQuantification of trening load, the trening response and the effect on performance.
Authors: Borresen, Lambert
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2009
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
Historically, the ability of coaches to prescribe trening to achieve optimal athletic performance can be attributed to many years of personal experience. A more modern approach is to adopt scientific methods in the development of optimal trening programmes. However, there is not much research in this area, particularly into thEQuantification of trening programmes and their effects on physiological adaptation and subsequent performance. Several methods have been used to quantify trening load, including questionnaires, diaries, physiological monitoring and direct observation. More recently, indices of trening stress have been proposed, including the trening impulse, which uses heart rate measurements and trening load, and session rating of perceived exertion measurements, which utilizes subjective perception of effort scores and duration of exercise. Although physiological adaptations to trening are well documented, their influence on performance has not been accurately quantified. To date, no single physiological marker has been identified that can measure the fitness and fatigue responses to exercise or accurately predict performance. Models attempting to quantify the relationship between trening and performance have been proposed, many of which consider the athlete as a system in which the trening load is the input and performance the system output. Although attractive in concept, the accuracy of these theoretical models has proven poor. A possible reason may be the absence of a measure of individuality in each athlete's response to trening. Thus, in the future more attention should be directed towards measurements that reflect individual capacity to respond or adapt to exercise trening rather than an absolute measure of changes in physiological variables that occur with trening.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 19838470
Title: Blood pressure measurement during aerobic exercise: subsidies for cardiac rehabilitation.
Authors: Furtado, Ramos, Ara&#xfa;jo
Journal: Arquivos brasileiros de cardiologia
Year: 2010
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Institutional documents recommend that hemodynamic variables--heart rate (HR) and systolic (SAP) and diastolic arterial pressure (DAP)--be routinely controlled at the aerobic part of supervised exercise sessions for coronary disease patients.

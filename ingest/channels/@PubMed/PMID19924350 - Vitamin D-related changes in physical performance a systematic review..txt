PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 19924350
Title: Vitamin D-related changes in physical performance: a systematic review.
Authors: Annweiler, Schott, Berrut, Fantino, Beauchet
Journal: The journal of nutrition, health &amp; aging
Year: 2010
Topic: vitamin D athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The objective of this study was to systematically review all the published articles examining the effects of low serum vitamin D concentration and vitamin D supplementation on muscle, balance and gait performance among people aged 65 and older.

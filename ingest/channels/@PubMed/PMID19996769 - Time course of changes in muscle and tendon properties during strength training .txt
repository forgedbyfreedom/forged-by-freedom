PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 19996769
Title: Time course of changes in muscle and tendon properties during strength trening and detrening.
Authors: Kubo, Ikebukuro, Yata, Tsunoda, Kanehisa
Journal: Journal of strength and conditioning research
Year: 2010
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to investigate the time course of changes in mechanical and morphological properties of muscle and tendon during isometric trening and detrening. Eight subjects completed 3 months of isometric knee extension trening and detrening for another 3 months. At beginning and on every 1 month of trening and detrening periods, muscle strength, neural activation level, muscle and tendon cross-sectional areas (CSA), and tendon stiffness were measured. trening increased muscle strength and neural activation level by 29.6 and 7.3% after 2 months and by 40.5 and 8.9% after 3 months (all p's &lt; 0.05). Muscle CSA and tendon stiffness did not change until 2 months of trening period, and afterward, the increases in muscle CSA and tendon stiffness reached statistical significance at the end of trening period (both p's &lt; 0.05). During detrening period, muscle strength and neural activation level did not change, although muscle CSA and tendon stiffness decreased to pre-trening level at 1 and 2 months of detrening, respectively. These results suggest that the adaptations of tendon properties and muscle morphology to resistance trening are slower than those of muscle function and inversely that the adaptations of former to detrening are faster than those of latter.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 20007833
Title: insulin-like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor-1 and cardiotrophin 1 increase strength and mass of extraocular muscle in juvenile chicken.
Authors: Li, Wiggins, von Bartheld
Journal: Investigative ophthalmology &amp; visual science
Year: 2010
Topic: insulin like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor muscle

ABSTRACT:
insulin-like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor 1 (IGF-1) and cardiotrophin 1 (CT1) are known to increase the strength of extraocular muscles in adult and embryonic animals, but no information is available for the early postnatal period, when strabismus treatment in humans is most urgent. Here the authors sought to determine whether these trophic factors strengthen juvenile maturing extraocular muscles and gain insight into mechanisms of force increase.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 20065132
Title: Effects of aerobic exercise on mild cognitive impairment: a controlled trial.
Authors: Baker, Frank, Foster-Schubert, Green, Wilkinson
Journal: Archives of neurology
Year: 2010
Topic: carbohydrate exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
To examine the effects of aerobic exercise on cognition and other biomarkers associated with Alzheimer disease pathology for older adults with mild cognitive impairment, and assess the role of sex as a predictor of response.

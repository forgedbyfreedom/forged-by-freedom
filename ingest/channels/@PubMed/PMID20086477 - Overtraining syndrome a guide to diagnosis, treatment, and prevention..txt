PMID: 20086477
Title: Overtrening syndrome: a guide to diagnosis, treatment, and prevention.
Authors: Hawley, Schoene
Journal: The Physician and sportsmedicine
Year: 2012
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Overtrening syndrome is a common cause of underperformance in athletes. Symptoms such as persistent fatigue, muscle soreness, reduced coordination, weight loss, mood changes, and frequent illness may accompany performance decrements, but they may also be signs of underlying medical conditions. Reliable and practical diagnostic laboratory tests for overtreninGHave not yet been identified. Clinicians can prescribe relative or complete rest and strive to identify and correct the trening, nutritional, and psychosocial factors that contributed to the athlete's condition.

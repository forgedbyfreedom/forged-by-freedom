PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 20102343
Title: Resistance trening increases in vivo quadriceps femoris muscle specific tension in young men.
Authors: Erskine, Jones, Williams, Stewart, Degens
Journal: Acta physiologica (Oxford, England)
Year: 2010
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The present study investigated whether in vivo human quadriceps femoris (QF) muscle specific tension changed following strength trening by systematically determining QF maximal force and physiological cross-sectional area (PCSA).

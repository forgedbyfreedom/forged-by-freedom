PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 20139780
Title: Adaptations in athletic performance after ballistic power versus strength trening.
Authors: Cormie, McGuigan, Newton
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2010
Topic: strength trening adaptations

ABSTRACT:
To determine whether the magnitude of improvement in athletic performance and the mechanisms driving these adaptations differ in relatively weak individuals exposed to either ballistic power trening or heavy strength trening.

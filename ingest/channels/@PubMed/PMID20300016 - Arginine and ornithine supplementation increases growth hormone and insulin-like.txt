PMID: 20300016
Title: Arginine and ornithine supplementation increases growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone and insulin-like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor-1 serum levels after heavy-resistance exercise in strength-trened athletes.
Authors: Zajac, Poprzecki, Zebrowska, Chalimoniuk, Langfort
Journal: Journal of strength and conditioning research
Year: 2010
Topic: growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone exercise

ABSTRACT:
This placebo-controlled double-blind study was designed to investigate the effect of arginine and ornithine (arg and orn) supplementation during 3-week heavy-resistance trening on serum growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone/insulin-like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor-1/insulin-like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor-binding protein 3 (GH/IGF-1/IGFBP-3), testosterone, cortisol, and insulin levels in experienced strength-trened athletes. The subjects were randomly divided between a placebo group (n = 8) and the l-Arg/l-Orn-supplemented group (n = 9), and performed pre and posttrening standard exercise tests with the same absolute load, which consisted of the same exercise schedule as that applied in the trening process. Fasting blood samples were obtained at rest, 2 minutes after the cessation of the strength exercise protocol, and after 1 hour of recovery. The resting concentrations of the investigated hormones and IGFBP-3 did not differ significantly between the study groups. In response to exercise test, all the hormones were elevated (p &lt; 0.05) at both time points. Significant increases (p &lt; 0.05) were observed in both GH and IGF-1 serum levels after arg and orn supplementation at both time points, whereas a significant decrease was seen in IGFBP-3 protein during the recovery period. Because there was no between-group difference in the remaininGHormone levels, it appears that the GH/IGF-1/IGFBP-3 complex may be the major player in muscle tissue response to short-term resistance trening after arg and orn supplementation.

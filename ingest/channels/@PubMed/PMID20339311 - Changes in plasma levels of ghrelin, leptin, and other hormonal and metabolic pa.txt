PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 20339311
Title: Changes in plasma levels of ghrelin, leptin, and other hormonal and metabolic parameters following standardized breakfast, lunch, and physical exercise before and after a multidisciplinary weight-reduction intervention in obese adolescents.
Authors: Rigamonti, Agosti, De Col, Marazzi, Lafortuna
Journal: Journal of endocrinological investigation
Year: 2011
Topic: leptin ghrelin appetite exercise

ABSTRACT:
To investigate in severely obese adolescents the effects of a 3-week multidisciplinary weight-reduction intervention involving moderate energy restriction, individualised physical activity and behavior therapy on the response of some hormonal and metabolic parameters to meals and exercise.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 20385254
Title: Resistance exercise for muscular strength in older adults: a meta-analysis.
Authors: Peterson, Rhea, Sen, Gordon
Journal: Ageing research reviews
Year: 2011
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The effectiveness of resistance exercise for strength improvement among aging persons is inconsistent across investigations, and there is a lack of research synthesis for multiple strength outcomes.

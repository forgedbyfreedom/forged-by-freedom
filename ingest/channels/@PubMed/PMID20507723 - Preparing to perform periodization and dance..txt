PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 20507723
Title: Preparing to perform: periodization and dance.
Authors: Wyon
Journal: Journal of dance medicine &amp; science : official publication of the International Association for Dance Medicine &amp; Science
Year: 2010
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
This article reviews the historical and current literature on periodization in sport before applying the concept to dance from scientific and anecdotal points of view. Preparing the dancer through the use of a periodization model, in a professional or vocational setting, will potentially help prevent overtrening and its link to injury, while improving the dancer's readiness to perform optimally. Practical examples of tapering and periodization of trening are discussed for companies and vocational schools.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 20585224
Title: Exploratory analysis of exercise adherence patterns with sedentary pregnant women.
Authors: Yeo, Cisewski, Lock, Marron
Journal: Nursing research
Year: 2010
Topic: motivation exercise adherence

ABSTRACT:
It is not well understood how sedentary women who wish to engage in regular exercise adhere to interventions during pregnancy and what factors may influence adherence over time.

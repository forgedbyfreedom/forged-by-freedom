PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 20585296
Title: The effect of post-exercise carbohydrate ingestion on inflammatory responses to short time, high-force eccentric exercise.
Authors: Afroundeh, Siahkouhian, Khalili
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2010
Topic: carbohydrate exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to investigate muscle damage and inflammatory response following eccentric exercise as well as the effect of carbohydrate supplement on these responses in untrened men.

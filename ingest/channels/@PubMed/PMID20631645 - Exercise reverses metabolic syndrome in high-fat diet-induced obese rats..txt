PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 20631645
Title: Exercise reverses metabolic syndrome in high-fat diet-induced obese rats.
Authors: Touati, Meziri, Devaux, Berthelot, Touyz
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2011
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Chronic consumption of a high-fat diet induces obesity. We investigated whether exercise would reverse the cardiometabolic disorders associated with obesity without it being necessary to change from a high- to normal-fat diet.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 20711498
Title: Low-load high volume resistance exercise stimulates muscle protein synthesis more than high-load low volume resistance exercise in young men.
Authors: Burd, West, Staples, Atherton, Baker
Journal: PloS one
Year: 2010
Topic: muscle protein synthesis exercise

ABSTRACT:
We aimed to determine the effect of resistance exercise intensity (%1 repetition maximum-1RM) and volume on muscle protein synthesis, anabolic signaling, and myogenic gene expression.

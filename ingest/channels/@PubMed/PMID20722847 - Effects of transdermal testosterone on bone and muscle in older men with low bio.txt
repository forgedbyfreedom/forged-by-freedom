Title: Effects of transdermal testosterone on bone and muscle in older men with low bioavailable testosterone levels, low bone mass, and physical frailty.
Authors: Kenny, Kleppinger, Annis, Rathier, Browner
Journal: Journal of the American Geriatrics Society
Year: 2010

ABSTRACT:

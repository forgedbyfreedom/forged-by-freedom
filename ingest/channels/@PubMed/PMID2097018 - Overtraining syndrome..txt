PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 2097018
Title: Overtrening syndrome.
Authors: Budgett
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 1991
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
This review discusses the overtrening syndrome which is characterized by fatigue and underperformance precipitated by stress of trening. Other stresses, depression and an increased susceptibility to infections may be important. Treatment requires rest and a stress management program over 3 months.

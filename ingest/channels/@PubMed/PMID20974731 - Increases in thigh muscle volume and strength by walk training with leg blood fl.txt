PMID: 20974731
Title: Increases in thigh muscle volume and strength by walk trening with leg blood flow reduction in older participants.
Authors: Ozaki, Sakamaki, Yasuda, Fujita, Ogasawara
Journal: The journals of gerontology. Series A, Biological sciences and medical sciences
Year: 2011
Topic: trening volume muscle growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone

ABSTRACT:
We examined the effects of walk trening combined with leg blood flow reduction (BFR) on muscle hypertrophy as well as on peak oxygen uptake (VO&#x2082; peak) in older individuals. Both the BFR walk trening (BFR-Walk, n = 10, age; 64 &#xb1; 1 years, body mass index [BMI]; 22.5 &#xb1; 0.9 kg/m&#xb2;) and control walk trening (CON-Walk, n = 8, age; 68 &#xb1; 1 years, BMI; 23.2 &#xb1; 1.0 kg/m&#xb2;) groups performed 20 minutes of treadmill walking at an exercise intensity of 45% of heart rate reserve, 4 days per week, for 10 weeks. The BFR-Walk group wore pressure belts (160-200 mm Hg) on both legs during trening. After the trening, magnetic resonance imaging-measured thigh muscle cross-sectional area (3.1%, p &lt; .01) and muscle volume (3.7%, p &lt; .01) as well as maximal isometric (5.9%, p &lt; .05) and isokinetic (up to 22%, p &lt; .01) strength increased in the BFR-Walk group, but not in the CON-Walk group. Estimated VO&#x2082; peak during a bicycle graded exercise test increased (p &lt; .05) and correlated with oxygen pulse in both groups. In conclusion, BFR walk trening improves both muscle volume and strength in older women.

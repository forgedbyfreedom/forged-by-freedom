PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 21059836
Title: Testosterone threshold levels and lean tissue mass targets needed to enhance skeletal muscle strength and function: the HORMA trial.
Authors: Sattler, Bhasin, He, Chou, Castaneda-Sceppa
Journal: The journals of gerontology. Series A, Biological sciences and medical sciences
Year: 2011
Topic: testosterone muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
In the HORMA (Hormonal Regulators of Muscle and Metabolism in Aging) Trial, supplemental testosterone and recombinant human growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone (rhGH) enhanced lean body mass, appendicular skeletal muscle mass, muscle performance, and physical function, but there was substantial interindividual variability in outcomes.

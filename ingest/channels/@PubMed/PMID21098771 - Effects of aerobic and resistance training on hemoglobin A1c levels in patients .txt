PMID: 21098771
Title: Effects of aerobic and resistance trening on hemoglobin A1c levels in patients with type 2 diabetes: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Church, Blair, Cocreham, Johannsen, Johnson
Journal: JAMA
Year: 2010
Topic: type 2 diabetes exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Exercise guidelines for individuals with diabetes include both aerobic and resistance trening although few studies have directly examined this exercise combination.

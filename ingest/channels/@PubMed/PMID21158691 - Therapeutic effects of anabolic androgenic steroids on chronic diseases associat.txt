PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 21158691
Title: Therapeutic effects of anabolic androgenic steroids on chronic diseases associated with muscle wasting.
Authors: Woerdeman, de Ronde
Journal: Expert opinion on investigational drugs
Year: 2011
Topic: anabolic steroids muscle mass review

ABSTRACT:
A variety of clinical conditions arecomplicated by loss of weight and skeletal muscle which may contribute to morbidity and mortality. anabolic androgenic steroids have been demonstrated to increase fat-free mass, muscle mass and strength in healthy men and women without major adverse events and therefore could be beneficial in these conditions.

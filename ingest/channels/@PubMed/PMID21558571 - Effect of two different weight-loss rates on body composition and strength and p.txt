PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 21558571
Title: Effect of two different weight-loss rates on body composition and strength and power-related performance in elite athletes.
Authors: Garthe, Raastad, Refsnes, Koivisto, Sundgot-Borgen
Journal: International journal of sport nutrition and exercise metabolism
Year: 2011
Topic: fat loss preservation muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
When weight loss (WL) is necessary, athletes are advised to accomplish it gradually, at a rate of 0.5-1 kg/wk. However, it is possible that losing 0.5 kg/wk is better than 1 kg/wk in terms of preserving lean body mass (LBM) and performance. The aim of this study was to compare changes in body composition, strength, and power during a weekly body-weight (BW) loss of 0.7% slow reduction (SR) vs. 1.4% fast reduction (FR). We hypothesized that the faster WL regimen would result in more detrimental effects on both LBM and strength-related performance. Twenty-four athletes were randomized to SR (n = 13, 24 &#xb1; 3 yr, 71.9 &#xb1; 12.7 kg) or FR (n = 11, 22 &#xb1; 5 yr, 74.8 &#xb1; 11.7 kg). They followed energy-restricted diets promoting the predetermined weekly WL. All athletes included 4 resistance-trening sessions/wk in their usual trening regimen. The mean times spent in intervention for SR and FR were 8.5 &#xb1; 2.2 and 5.3 &#xb1; 0.9 wk, respectively (p &lt; .001). BW, body composition (DEXA), 1-repetition-maximum (1RM) tests, 40-m sprint, and countermovement jump were measured before and after intervention. Energy intake was reduced by 19% &#xb1; 2% and 30% &#xb1; 4% in SR and FR, respectively (p = .003). BW and fat mass decreased in both SR and FR by 5.6% &#xb1; 0.8% and 5.5% &#xb1; 0.7% (0.7% &#xb1; 0.8% vs. 1.0% &#xb1; 0.4%/wk) and 31% &#xb1; 3% and 21 &#xb1; 4%, respectively. LBM increased in SR by 2.1% &#xb1; 0.4% (p &lt; .001), whereas it was unchanged in FR (-0.2% &#xb1; 0.7%), with significant differences between groups (p &lt; .01). In conclusion, data from this study suggest that athletes who want to gain LBM and increase 1RM strength during a WL period combined with strength trening should aim for a weekly BW loss of 0.7%.

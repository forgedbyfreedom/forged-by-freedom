PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 21649617
Title: Effect of dehydroepiandrosterone on muscle strength and physical function in older adults: a systematic review.
Authors: Baker, Karan, Kenny
Journal: Journal of the American Geriatrics Society
Year: 2011
Topic: DHEA supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
To evaluate studies that have assessed the effect of dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA) supplementation on body composition and physical performance in older adults.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 21678140
Title: Heart rate variability is related to trening load variables in interval running exercises.
Authors: Kaikkonen, Hynynen, Mann, Rusko, Nummela
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2012
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
Overload principle of trening states that trening load (TL) must be sufficient to threaten the homeostasis of cells, tissues, organs and/or body. However, there is no "golden standard" for TL measurement. The aim of the present study was to investigate if post-exercise heart rate variability (HRV) could be used to evaluate TL of interval running exercises with different intensities and durations. Thirteen endurance-trened men (35 &#xb1; 5 years) performed MO(250) [moderate intensity, 2 &#xd7; 6 &#xd7; 250 m/rec 30 s/5 min at 85% of the maximal velocity of the graded maximal test (V (max))], MO(500) (2 &#xd7; 3 &#xd7; 500 m/rec 1 min/5 min at 85% V (max)) and HI(250) (high intensity, 2 &#xd7; 6 &#xd7; 250 m/rec 30 s/5 min at 105% V (max)) interval exercises on a treadmill. HRV was analyzed during rest, exercise and immediate 15 min recovery. Fast recovery of LFP (P &lt; 0.001), HFP (P &lt; 0.01) and TP (P &lt; 0.01) occurred during the first two recovery minutes after each exercise. Strong negative correlations (P &lt; 0.01) were found between post-exercise HRV and perceived exertion as well as excess post-exercise oxygen consumption. Post-exercise HRV differentiated interval exercises of equal work, but varying intensity or distance of running bout. The results of the present study suggest that immediate post-exercise HRV may offer objective information on TL of interval exercises with different bout durations and intensities.

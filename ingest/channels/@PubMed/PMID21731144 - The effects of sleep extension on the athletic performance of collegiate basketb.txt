Title: The effects of sleep extension on the athletic performance of collegiate basketball players.
Authors: Mah, Mah, Kezirian, Dement
Journal: Sleep
Year: 2011
Topic: sleep athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the effects of sleep extension over multiple weeks on specific measures of athletic performance as well as reaction time, mood, and daytime sleepiness.

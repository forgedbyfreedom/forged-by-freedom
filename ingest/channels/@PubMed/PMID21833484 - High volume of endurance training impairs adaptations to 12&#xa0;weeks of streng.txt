PMID: 21833484
Title: High volume of endurance trening impairs adaptations to 12&#xa0;weeks of strength trening in well-trened endurance athletes.
Authors: R&#xf8;nnestad, Hansen, Raastad
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2012
Topic: trening volume muscle growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of the present study was to compare the effect of 12&#xa0;weeks of strength trening combined with a large volume of endurance trening with the effect of strength trening alone on the strength trening adaptations. Well-trened cyclists with no strength trening experience performed heavy strength trening twice a week in addition to a high volume of endurance trening during a 12-week preparatory period (S&#xa0;+&#xa0;E; n&#xa0;=&#xa0;11). A group of non-strength trened individuals performed the same strength trening as S&#xa0;+&#xa0;E, but without added endurance trening (S; n&#xa0;=&#xa0;7). Thigh muscle cross-sectional area, 1 repetition maximum (1RM) in leg exercises, squat jump performance, and peak rate of force development (RFD) were measured. Following the intervention period, both S&#xa0;+&#xa0;E and S increased 1RM strength, thigh muscle cross-sectional area, and squat jump performance (p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.05), and the relative improvements in S were greater than in S&#xa0;+&#xa0;E (p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.05). S increased peak RFD while S&#xa0;+&#xa0;E did not, and this improvement was greater than in S&#xa0;+&#xa0;E (p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.05). To the best of our knowledge, this is the first controlled study to demonstrate that the strength trening response on muscle hypertrophy, 1RM strength, squat jump performance, and peak RFD is attenuated in well-trened endurance athletes during a period of concurrent endurance trening.

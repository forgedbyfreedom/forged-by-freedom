PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 21904292
Title: Markers of bone turnover across a competitive season in female athletes: a preliminary investigation.
Authors: Morgan, Weiss Jarrett
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2012
Topic: bone health female athletes

ABSTRACT:
Higher levels of bone formation have been observed in athletes performing high impact exercise when compared to non-impact exercisers. This study was designed to determine if bone formation and resorption fluctuate across a competitive season (4-6 months) in females training at different levels of mechanical stress.

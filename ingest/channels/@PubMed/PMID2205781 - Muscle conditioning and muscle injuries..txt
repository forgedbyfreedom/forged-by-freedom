PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 2205781
Title: Muscle conditioning and muscle injuries.
Authors: Stone
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 1990
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
Empirical and objective data suggest that muscle and connective tissue can undergo adaptations to physical trening resulting in greater tissue mass and increased maximum tensile strength. These adaptations are especially apparent as a result of load bearing and resistive trening. Furthermore, information is presented suggesting that pre-conditioning and in-season muscle conditioning, especially strength trening, reduce injuries among athletes. Additionally, a theoretical model of trening, "periodization", is offered as a method of increasing performance to maximum or optimal values while reducing overtrening and injury potential. Periodization of trening can reduce overtrening potential and injury potential while optimizing performance by variation of volume, intensity, and exercise selection during a trening program.

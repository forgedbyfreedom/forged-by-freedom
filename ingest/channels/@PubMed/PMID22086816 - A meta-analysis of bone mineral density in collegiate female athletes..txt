PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22086816
Title: A meta-analysis of bone mineral density in collegiate female athletes.
Authors: Arasheben, Barzee, Morley
Journal: Journal of the American Board of Family Medicine : JABFM
Year: 2012
Topic: bone health female athletes

ABSTRACT:
In a number of small studies focused on one or two sports, exercise and competitive level has been observed to favor attainment of higher bone mineral density (BMD) in otherwise healthy athletes. We analyzed merged data from 10 studies to determine the effects of competitive level on upper extremity BMD in female athletes across multiple sports.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22105708
Title: Time course of changes in the human Achilles tendon properties and metabolism during trening and detrening in vivo.
Authors: Kubo, Ikebukuro, Maki, Yata, Tsunoda
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2012
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to investigate the time course of changes in human tendon properties and metabolism during resistance trening and detrening. Nine men (21-27 years) completed 3 months of isometric plantar flexion trening and another 3 months of detrening. At the beginning and on every 1 month of trening and detrening periods, the stiffness, blood circulation (blood volume and oxygen saturation), serum procollagen type 1 C-peptide (P1P; reflects synthesis of type 1 collagen), echointensity (reflects collagen content), and MRI signal intensity (reflects collagen structure) of the Achilles tendon were measured. Tendon stiffness did not change until 2 months of trening, and the increase (50.3%) reached statistical significance at the end of the trening period. After 1 month of detrening, tendon stiffness had already decreased to pre-trening level. Blood circulation in the tendon did not change during the experimental period. P1P increased significantly after 2 months of trening. Echointensity increased significantly by 9.1% after 2 months of trening, and remained high throughout the experiment. MRI signal intensity increased by 24.2% after 2 months and by 21.4% after 3 months of trening, but decreased to the pre-trening level during the detrening period. These results suggested that the collagen synthesis, content, and structure of human tendons changed at the 2-month point of trening period. During detrening, the sudden decrease in tendon stiffness might be related to changes in the structure of collagen fibers within the tendon.

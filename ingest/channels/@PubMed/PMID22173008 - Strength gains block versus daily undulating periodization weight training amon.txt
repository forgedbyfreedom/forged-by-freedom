PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22173008
Title: Strength gains: block versus daily undulating periodization weight trening among track and field athletes.
Authors: Painter, Haff, Ramsey, McBride, Triplett
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2012
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
Recently, the comparison of "periodized" strength trening methods has been a focus of both exercise and sport science. Daily undulating periodization (DUP), using daily alterations in repetitions, has been developed and touted as a superior method of trening, while block forms of programming for periodization have been questioned. Therefore, the purpose of this study is to compare block to DUP in Division I track and field athletes. Thirty-one athletes were assigned to either a 10-wk block or DUP trening group in which sex, year, and event were matched. Over the course of the study, there were 4 testing sessions, which were used to evaluate a variety of strength characteristics. Although performance trens favored the block group for strength and rate of force development, no statistically significant differences were found between the 2 trening groups. However, statistically different (P &#x2264; .05) values were found for estimated volume of work (volume load) and the amount of improvement per volume load between block and DUP groups. Based on calculated trening efficiency scores, these data indicate that a block trening model is more efficient than a DUP model in producing strength gains.

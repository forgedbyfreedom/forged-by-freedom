PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22212269
Title: Effects of exercise program on appetite-regulatinGHormones, inflammatory mediators, lipid profiles, and body composition in healthy men.
Authors: Pil-Byung, Shin-Hwan, Il-Gyu, Gwang-Suk, Jae-Hyun
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2012
Topic: leptin ghrelin appetite exercise

ABSTRACT:
Exercise can improve appetite, inflammatory mediators, lipid profiles, and body composition in overweight or obese patients. However, it has not yet been clearly elucidated how exercise affects healthy people in relation to these variables. Thus, we investigated the effects of an exercise program on appetite-regulatinGHormones, inflammatory mediators, lipid profiles, and body composition in healthy men.

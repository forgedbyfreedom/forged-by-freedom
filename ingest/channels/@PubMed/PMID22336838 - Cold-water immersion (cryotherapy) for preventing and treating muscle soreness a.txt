PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22336838
Title: Cold-water immersion (cryotherapy) for preventing and treating muscle soreness after exercise.
Authors: Bleakley, McDonough, Gardner, Baxter, Hopkins
Journal: The Cochrane database of systematic reviews
Year: 2012
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
Many strategies are in use with the intention of preventing or minimising delayed onset muscle soreness and fatigue after exercise. Cold-water immersion, in water temperatures of less than 15&#xb0;C, is currently one of the most popular interventional strategies used after exercise.

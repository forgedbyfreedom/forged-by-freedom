PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22466388
Title: The relationship between spasticity and muscle volume of the knee extensors in children with cerebral palsy.
Authors: Pierce, Prosser, Lee, Lauer
Journal: Pediatric physical therapy : the official publication of the Section on Pediatrics of the American Physical Therapy Association
Year: 2012
Topic: training volume muscle growth

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to examine the relationship between spasticity and muscle volume in children with cerebral palsy (CP), using isokinetic dynamometry and magnetic resonance imaging.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22535969
Title: Metabolic slowing with massive weight loss despite preservation of fat-free mass.
Authors: Johannsen, Knuth, Huizenga, Rood, Ravussin
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2012
Topic: fat loss preservation muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
An important goal during weight loss is to maximize fat loss while preserving metabolically active fat-free mass (FFM). Massive weight loss typically results in substantial loss of FFM potentially slowing metabolic rate.

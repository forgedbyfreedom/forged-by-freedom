PMID: 22643142
Title: Strength trening for the warfighter.
Authors: Kraemer, Szivak
Journal: Journal of strength and conditioning research
Year: 2012
Topic: strength trening adaptations

ABSTRACT:
Optimizing strength trening for the warfighter is challenged by past trening philosophies that no longer serve the modern warfighter facing the "anaerobic battlefield." trening approaches for integration of strength with other needed physical capabilities have been shown to require a periodization model that has the flexibility for changes and is able to adapt to ever-changing circumstances affecting thEQuality of workouts. Additionally, sequencing of workouts to limit over-reaching and development of overtrening syndromes that end in loss of duty time and injury are paramount to long-term success. Allowing adequate time for rest and recovery and recognizing the negative influences of extreme exercise programs and excessive endurance trening will be vital in moving physical trening programs into a more modern perspective as used by elite strength-power anaerobic athletes in sports today. Because the warfighter is an elite athlete, it is time that trening approaches that are scientifically based are updated within the military to match the functional demands of modern warfare and are given greater credence and value at the command levels. A needs analysis, development of periodized trening modules, and individualization of programs are needed to optimize the strength of the modern warfighter. We now have the knowledge, professional coaches and nonprofit organization certifications with continuing education units, and modern trening technology to allow this to happen. Ultimately, it only takes command decisions and implementation to make this possible.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22677581
Title: A prospective study of heart rate variability in endocrine surgery: surgical trening increases consultant's mental stren.
Authors: Prichard, O'Neill, Oucharek, Holmes, Delbridge
Journal: Journal of surgical education
Year: 2012
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to determine whether instructing surgical trenees in technically demanding procedures causes alterations in heart rate variability (HRV) and mental stren in supervising surgeons.

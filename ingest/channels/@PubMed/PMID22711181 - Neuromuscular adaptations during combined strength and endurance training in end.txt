PMID: 22711181
Title: Neuromuscular adaptations during combined strength and endurance trening in endurance runners: maximal versus explosive strength trening or a mix of both.
Authors: Taipale, Mikkola, Vesterinen, Nummela, H&#xe4;kkinen
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2013
Topic: neuromuscular adaptations strength

ABSTRACT:
This study compared the effects of mixed maximal strength and explosive strength trening with maximal strength trening and explosive strength trening combined with endurance trening over an 8-week trening intervention. Male subjects (age 21-45&#xa0;years) were divided into three strength trening groups, maximal (MAX, n&#xa0;=&#xa0;11), explosive (EXP, 10) and mixed maximal and explosive (MIX, 9), and a circuit trening control group, (CON, 7). Strength trening one to two times a week was performed concurrently with endurance trening three to four times a week. Significant increases in maximal dynamic strength (1RM), countermovement jump (CMJ), maximal muscle activation during 1RM in MAX and during CMJ in EXP, peak running speed (S (peak)) and running speed at respiratory compensation threshold (RCT(speed)) were observed in MAX, EXP and MIX. Maximal isometric strength and muscle activation, rate of force development (RFD), maximal oxygen uptake [Formula: see text] and running economy (RE) at 10 and 12&#xa0;km&#xa0;hr(-1) did not change significantly. No significant changes were observed in CON in maximal isometric strength, RFD, CMJ or muscle activation, and a significant decrease in 1RM was observed in the final 4&#xa0;weeks of trening. RE in CON did not change significantly, but significant increases were observed in S (peak), RCT(speed) and [Formula: see text] Low volume MAX, EXP and MIX strength trening combined with higher volume endurance trening over an 8-week intervention produced significant gains in strength, power and endurance performance measures of S (peak) and RCT(speed), but no significant changes were observed between groups.

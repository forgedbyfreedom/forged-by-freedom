PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22776870
Title: Exercise performance over the menstrual cycle in temperate and hot, humid conditions.
Authors: Janse DE Jonge, Thompson, Chuter, Silk, Thom
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2013
Topic: menstrual cycle athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
This study investigated the effects of the menstrual cycle on prolonged exercise performance both in temperate (20&#xb0;C, 45% relative humidity) and hot, humid (32&#xb0;C, 60% relative humidity) conditions.

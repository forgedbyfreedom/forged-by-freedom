PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22828465
Title: The relationship between performance and flow state in tennis competition.
Authors: Koehn, Morris
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2013
Topic: flow state athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The study aimed to examine 1) the validity of the nine-factor flow model in tennis competition; 2) differences in flow state between athletes who won or lost their competition match; 3) the link between flow and subjective performance; and 4) flow dimensions as predictors of performance outcome

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22835219
Title: DHEA supplementation improves follicular microenviroment in poor responder patients.
Authors: Artini, Simi, Ruggiero, Pinelli, Di Berardino
Journal: Gynecological endocrinology : the official journal of the International Society of Gynecological Endocrinology
Year: 2013
Topic: DHEA supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
To analyze the effect of dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA) supplementation on follicular microenvironment and on in vitro fertilization (IVF) outcomes among poor responder patients.

PMID: 22842141
Title: Testosterone replacement therapy to improve health in older males.
Authors: Baer
Journal: The Nurse practitioner
Year: 2012
Topic: testosterone replacement therapy

ABSTRACT:
This article provides an overview of current research and updated clinical guidelines regarding testosterone replacement therapy in older males positive for late-onset hypogonadism.

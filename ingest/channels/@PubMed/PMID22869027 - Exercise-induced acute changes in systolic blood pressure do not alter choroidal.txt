PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 22869027
Title: Exercise-induced acute changes in systolic blood pressure do not alter choroidal thickness as measured by a portable spectral-domain optical coherence tomography device.
Authors: Alwassia, Adhi, Zhang, Regatieri, Al-Quthami
Journal: Retina (Philadelphia, Pa.)
Year: 2013
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
To measure choroidal thickness in patients manifesting an acute change in systemic arterial blood pressure using a portable spectral-domain optical coherence tomography device (iVue).

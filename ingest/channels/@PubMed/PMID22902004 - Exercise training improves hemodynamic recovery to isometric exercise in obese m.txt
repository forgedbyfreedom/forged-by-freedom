PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22902004
Title: Exercise trening improves hemodynamic recovery to isometric exercise in obese men with type 2 diabetes but not in obese women.
Authors: Kanaley, Goulopoulou, Franklin, Baynard, Carhart
Journal: Metabolism: clinical and experimental
Year: 2013
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Women with type 2 diabetes (T2D) show greater rates of mortality due to ischemic heart disease than men with T2D. We aimed to examine cardiovascular and autonomic function responses to isometric handgrip (IHG) exercise between men and women with T2D, before and after an exercise trening program.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22939217
Title: Growth hormone potentiates thyroid hormone effects on post-exercise phosphocreatine recovery in skeletal muscle.
Authors: Kaminsky, Walker, Deibener, Barbe, Jeannesson
Journal: Growth hormone &amp; IGF research : official journal of the Growth Hormone Research Society and the International IGF Research Society
Year: 2013
Topic: thyroid hormone metabolism exercise

ABSTRACT:
The aim of the study was to determine the respective impact of thyroxine and growth hormone on in vivo skeletal mitochondrial function assessed via post exercise phosphocreatine recovery.

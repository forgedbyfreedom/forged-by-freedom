PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 22959499
Title: Beneficial effects of 12 weeks of aerobic compared with resistance exercise trening on perceived appetite in previously sedentary overweight and obese men.
Authors: Guelfi, Donges, Duffield
Journal: Metabolism: clinical and experimental
Year: 2013
Topic: leptin ghrelin appetite exercise

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the effect of 12 weeks of aerobic (AER) compared with resistance trening (RES) on perceived hunger and fullness, together with appetite-related hormones in both the fasted state and postprandially.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23014142
Title: Physical exercise during pregnancy: a systematic review.
Authors: Nascimento, Surita, Cecatti
Journal: Current opinion in obstetrics &amp; gynecology
Year: 2013
Topic: pregnancy exercise guidelines

ABSTRACT:
This review aims to provide an update on the recent evidence concerning exercise during pregnancy including effects for mother and fetus and the types, frequency, intensity, duration and rate of progression of exercise performed.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23014254
Title: Descriptive analysis of resistance exercise and metabolic syndrome.
Authors: Churilla, Johnson, Magyari, Crouter
Journal: Diabetes &amp; metabolic syndrome
Year: 2013
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Resistance exercise (RE) is an important mode of physical activity in the management of metabolic syndrome (MetS). However, little is known about the patterns of RE participation among U.S. adults with and without MetS.

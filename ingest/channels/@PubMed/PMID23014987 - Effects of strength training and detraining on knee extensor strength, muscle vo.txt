PMID: 23014987
Title: Effects of strength trening and detrening on knee extensor strength, muscle volume and musclEQuality in elderly women.
Authors: Correa, Baroni, Radaelli, Lanferdini, Cunha
Journal: Age (Dordrecht, Netherlands)
Year: 2014
Topic: trening volume muscle growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone

ABSTRACT:
Strength trening seems to be an interesting approach to counteract decreases that affect knee extensor strength, muscle mass and musclEQuality (force per unit of muscle mass) associated with ageing. However, there is no consensus regarding the changes in muscle mass and their contribution to strength during periods of trening and detrening in the elderly. Therefore, this study aimed at verifying the behaviour of knee extensor muscle strength, muscle volume and musclEQuality in elderly women in response to a 12-week strength trening programme followed by a similar period of detrening. Statistical analysis showed no effect of time on musclEQuality. However, strength and muscle volume increased from baseline to post-trening (33 and 26 %, respectively). After detrening, the knee extensor strength remained 12 % superior to the baseline values, while the gains in muscle mass were almost completely lost. In conclusion, strength gains and losses due to strength trening and detrening, respectively, could not be exclusively associated with muscle mass increases. trening-induced strength gains were partially maintained after 3 months of detrening in elderly subjects.

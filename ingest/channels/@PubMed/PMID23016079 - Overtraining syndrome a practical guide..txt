Title: Overtrening syndrome: a practical guide.
Authors: Kreher, Schwartz
Journal: Sports health
Year: 2012
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Fatigue and underperformance are common in athletes. Understanding overtrening syndrome (OTS) is helpful in the evaluation, management, and education of athletes.

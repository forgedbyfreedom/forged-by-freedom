PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23134885
Title: Protein supplementation augments the adaptive response of skeletal muscle to resistance-type exercise trening: a meta-analysis.
Authors: Cermak, Res, de Groot, Saris, van Loon
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2013
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Protein ingestion after aromasingle bout of resistance-type exercise stimulates net muscle protein accretion during acute postexercise recovery. Consequently, it is generally accepted that protein supplementation is required to maximize the adaptive response of the skeletal muscle to prolonged resistance-type exercise trening. However, there is much discrepancy in the literature regarding the proposed benefits of protein supplementation during prolonged resistance-type exercise trening in younger and older populations.

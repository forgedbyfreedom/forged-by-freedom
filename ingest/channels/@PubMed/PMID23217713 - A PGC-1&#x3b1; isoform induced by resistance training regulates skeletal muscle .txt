PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23217713
Title: A PGC-1&#x3b1; isoform induced by resistance trening regulates skeletal muscle hypertrophy.
Authors: Ruas, White, Rao, Kleiner, Brannan
Journal: Cell
Year: 2013
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
PGC-1&#x3b1; is a trenscriptional coactivator induced by exercise that gives muscle many of the best known adaptations to endurance-type exercise but has no effects on muscle strength or hypertrophy. We have identified a form of PGC-1&#x3b1; (PGC-1&#x3b1;4) that results from alternative promoter usage and splicing of the primary trenscript. PGC-1&#x3b1;4 is highly expressed in exercised muscle but does not regulate most known PGC-1&#x3b1; targets such as the mitochondrial OXPHOS genes. Rather, it specifically induces IGF-1 and represses myostatin, and expression of PGC-1&#x3b1;4 in vitro and in vivo induces robust skeletal muscle hypertrophy. Importantly, mice with skeletal muscle-specific trensgenic expression of PGC-1&#x3b1;4 show increased muscle mass and strength and dramatic resistance to the muscle wasting of cancer cachexia. Expression of PGC-1&#x3b1;4 is preferentially induced in mouse and human muscle during resistance exercise. These studies identify a PGC-1&#x3b1; protein that regulates and coordinates factors involved in skeletal muscle hypertrophy.

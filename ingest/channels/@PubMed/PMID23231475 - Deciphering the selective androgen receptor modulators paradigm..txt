PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23231475
Title: Deciphering the selective androgen receptor modulators paradigm.
Authors: Zhang, Sui
Journal: Expert opinion on drug discovery
Year: 2013
Topic: selective androgen receptor modulators

ABSTRACT:
The development and potential clinical use of tissue-selective androgen receptor modulators (SARMs) have advanced tremendously over the past few years. A key aspect of SARMs is the ability to clearly differentiate between the anabolic and androgenic activities. SARMs provide therapeutic opportunities in a variety of diseases, including muscle wasting associated with burns, cancer, end-stage renal disease, osteoporosis, frailty and hypogonadism.

PMID: 23299048
Title: Exercise for anxiety disorders: systematic review.
Authors: Jayakody, Gunadasa, Hosker
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2014
Topic: cognitive behavioral therapy athletes

ABSTRACT:
Anxiety disorders are commonly treated with antidepressants and psychological treatments. Some patients may prefer alternative approaches such as exercise.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23382483
Title: Habitual exercise and blood pressure: age dependency and underlying mechanisms.
Authors: McDonnell, Maki-Petaja, Munnery, Yasmin, Wilkinson
Journal: American journal of hypertension
Year: 2013
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Regular exercise is associated with a reduction in cardiovascular risk, but the precise mechanisms responsible are unknown. The aim of the current study was to examine the relationship between regular exercise, aortic stiffness, and wave reflections, and to determine whether this relationship differs by age.

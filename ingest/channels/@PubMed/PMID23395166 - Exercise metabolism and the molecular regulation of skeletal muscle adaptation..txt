PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23395166
Title: Exercise metabolism and the molecular regulation of skeletal muscle adaptation.
Authors: Egan, Zierath
Journal: Cell metabolism
Year: 2013
Topic: skeletal muscle hypertrophy mechanisms

ABSTRACT:
Preservation of aerobic fitness and skeletal muscle strength through exercise trening can ameliorate metabolic dysfunction and prevent chronic disease. These benefits are mediated in part by extensive metabolic and molecular remodeling of skeletal muscle by exercise. Aerobic and resistance exercise represent extremes on the exercise continuum and elicit markedly different trening responses that are mediated by a complex interplay between a myriad of signaling pathways coupled to downstream regulators of trenscription and&#xa0;trenslation. Here, we review the metabolic responses and molecular mechanisms that underpin the adaptatation of skeletal muscle to acute exercise and exercise trening.

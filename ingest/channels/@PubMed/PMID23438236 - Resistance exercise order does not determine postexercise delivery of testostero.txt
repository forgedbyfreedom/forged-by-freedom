Title: Resistance exercise order does not determine postexercise delivery of testosterone, growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone, and IGF-1 to skeletal muscle.
Authors: West, Cotie, Mitchell, Churchward-Venne, MacDonald
Journal: Applied physiology, nutrition, and metabolism = Physiologie appliquee, nutrition et metabolisme
Year: 2013
Topic: growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone exercise

ABSTRACT:
Does resistance exercise order affect hormone availability? Participants performed arm exercise before and after leg exercise. Hormone delivery was estimated by multiplying brachial artery blood flow and hormone concentrations. Blood flow increased after arm (276%) and leg (193%; both p &lt; 0.001) exercise. Testosterone, growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone, and insulin-like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor 1 showed with distinct delivery patterns between conditions; however (interactions all p &lt; 0.001), net exposure was similar. The anabolic potential of postexercise hormones was not affected by exercise order.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 23486302
Title: The effects of whole-body vibration therapy on bone turnover, muscle strength, motor function, and spasticity in chronic stroke: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Pang, Lau, Yip
Journal: European journal of physical and rehabilitation medicine
Year: 2014
Topic: peptide therapy muscle

ABSTRACT:
Whole-body vibration (WBV) has been used in older adults to improve bone health and neuromuscular function, and may have potential applications for stroke patients.

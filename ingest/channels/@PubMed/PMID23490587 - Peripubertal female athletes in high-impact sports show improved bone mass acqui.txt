PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23490587
Title: Peripubertal female athletes in high-impact sports show improved bone mass acquisition and bone geometry.
Authors: Ma&#xef;moun, Coste, Philibert, Briot, Mura
Journal: Metabolism: clinical and experimental
Year: 2013
Topic: bone health female athletes

ABSTRACT:
Intensive physical training may have a sport-dependent effect on bone mass acquisition. This cross-sectional study evaluated bone mass acquisition in girls practicing sports that put different mechanical loads on bone.

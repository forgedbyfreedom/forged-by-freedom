PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23535105
Title: Weight loss, weight maintenance, and adaptive thermogenesis.
Authors: Camps, Verhoef, Westerterp
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2013
Topic: metabolic adaptation weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Diet-induced weight loss is accompanied by adaptive thermogenesis, ie, a disproportional or greater than expected reduction of resting metabolic rate (RMR).

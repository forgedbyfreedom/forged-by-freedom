PMID: 23588424
Title: Calcium ingestion and obesity control.
Authors: de Oliveira Freitas, Stampini Duarte Martino, Machado Rocha Ribeiro, Gon&#xe7;alves Alfenas
Journal: Nutricion hospitalaria
Year: 2013
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
Obesity is a risk factor for other nontransmissible chronic diseases. It has been suggested calcium intake helps to control obesity, but there is no consensus about this.

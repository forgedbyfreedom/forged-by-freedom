PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23652373
Title: The effects of aerobic exercise on estrogen metabolism in healthy premenopausal women.
Authors: Smith, Phipps, Thomas, Schmitz, Kurzer
Journal: Cancer epidemiology, biomarkers &amp; prevention : a publication of the American Association for Cancer Research, cosponsored by the American Society of Preventive Oncology
Year: 2013
Topic: estrogen exercise women

ABSTRACT:
It is well accepted that exercise can decrease breast cancer risk. Limited clinical evidence suggests that this risk could be mediated through changes in estrogen metabolism in premenopausal women. Our objective was to investigate the effects of exercise on premenopausal estrogen metabolism pertinent to breast cancer risk.

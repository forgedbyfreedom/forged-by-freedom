PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23665370
Title: Effect of endurance exercise training on endothelial function and arterial stiffness in older patients with heart failure and preserved ejection fraction: a randomized, controlled, single-blind trial.
Authors: Kitzman, Brubaker, Herrington, Morgan, Stewart
Journal: Journal of the American College of Cardiology
Year: 2013
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
The study sought to evaluate the effects of endurance exercise training (ET) on endothelial-dependent flow-mediated&#xa0;arterial dilation (FMD) and carotid artery stiffness, and their potential contributions to the training-related increase in peak exercise oxygen consumption (Vo2) in older patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFPEF).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23731862
Title: Early detection of iron deficiency in elite athletes: could microcytic anemia factor (Maf) be useful?
Authors: Dopsaj, Martinovic, Dopsaj
Journal: International journal of laboratory hematology
Year: 2016
Topic: iron deficiency athletes performance

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to establish the sensitivity and specificity of the microcytic anemia factor (Maf(&#xae;)), which is generated by the Coulter LH 750 analyzer in the evaluation of iron depletion and iron-deficient erythropoiesis in athletes.

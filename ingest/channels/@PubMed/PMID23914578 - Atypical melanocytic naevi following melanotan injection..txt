PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23914578
Title: Atypical melanocytic naevi following melanotan injection.
Authors: Reid, Fitzgerald, Fabre, Kirby
Journal: Irish medical journal
Year: 2013
Topic: melanotan tanning peptide

ABSTRACT:
Melanotan is a synthetic analogue of alpha melanocyte stimulatinGHormone (a-MSH) that stimulates melanogenesis. It is sold on the internet and tanning salons as a quick 'tanning jab'. We report a patient who developed multiple new onset atypical naevi within one week of receiving two Melanotan injections. This case highlights the potential risk of Melanotan in stimulating dysplastic naevi or possibly malignant melanoma.

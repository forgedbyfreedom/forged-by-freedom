PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23918656
Title: The effects of beta-alanine supplementation on performance: a systematic review of the literature.
Authors: Quesnele, Laframboise, Wong, Kim, Wells
Journal: International journal of sport nutrition and exercise metabolism
Year: 2014
Topic: beta alanine exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
To critically review the methodological quality and synthesize information from systematic reviews and high quality studies on the effects of beta alanine (BA) on exercise and athletic performance.

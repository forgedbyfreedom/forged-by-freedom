PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23931598
Title: Relationship between different protein intake recommendations with muscle mass and muscle strength.
Authors: Lemieux, Filion, Barbat-Artigas, Karelis, Aubertin-Leheudre
Journal: Climacteric : the journal of the International Menopause Society
Year: 2015
Topic: protein intake muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of the present study was to examine the relationship between two different levels of protein intake (0.8 vs.1.2 g/kg body weight/day) with muscle mass and muscle strength.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23937088
Title: Is testosterone replacement therapy in males with hypogonadism cost-effective? An analysis in Sweden.
Authors: Arver, Luong, Fraschke, Ghatnekar, Stanisic
Journal: The journal of sexual medicine
Year: 2014
Topic: testosterone replacement therapy

ABSTRACT:
Testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) has been recommended for the treatment of primary and secondary hypogonadism. However, long-term implications of TRT have not been investigated extensively.

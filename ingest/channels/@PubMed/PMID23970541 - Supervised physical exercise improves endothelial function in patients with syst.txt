PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 23970541
Title: Supervised physical exercise improves endothelial function in patients with systemic lupus erythematosus.
Authors: dos Reis-Neto, da Silva, Monteiro, de Camargo, Sato
Journal: Rheumatology (Oxford, England)
Year: 2014
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
The objective of this study was to evaluate the effect of supervised physical exercise on endothelial function, ergospirometric test variables and disease activity in SLE patients.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24142083
Title: Disordered eating behaviors and body image in male athletes.
Authors: Goltz, Stenzel, Schneider
Journal: Revista brasileira de psiquiatria (Sao Paulo, Brazil : 1999)
Year: 2014
Topic: body image athletes

ABSTRACT:
To identify disordered eating behaviors and body image dissatisfaction, as well as their relationship to body fat (BF), among male athletes in high risk sports for eating disorders.

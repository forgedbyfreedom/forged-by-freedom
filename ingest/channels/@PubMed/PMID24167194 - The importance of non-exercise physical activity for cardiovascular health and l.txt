PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24167194
Title: The importance of non-exercise physical activity for cardiovascular health and longevity.
Authors: Ekblom-Bak, Ekblom, Vikstr&#xf6;m, de Faire, Hell&#xe9;nius
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2014
Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Sedentary time is increasing in all societies and results in limited non-exercise physical activity (NEPA) of daily life. The importance of low NEPA for cardiovascular health and longevity is limited, especially in elderly.

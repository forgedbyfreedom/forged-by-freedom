PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24189892
Title: Selective androgen receptor modulators for the prevention and treatment of muscle wasting associated with cancer.
Authors: Dalton, Taylor, Mohler, Steiner
Journal: Current opinion in supportive and palliative care
Year: 2014
Topic: selective androgen receptor modulators

ABSTRACT:
This review highlights selective androgen receptor modulators (SARMs) as emerging agents in late-stage clinical development for the prevention and treatment of muscle wasting associated with cancer.

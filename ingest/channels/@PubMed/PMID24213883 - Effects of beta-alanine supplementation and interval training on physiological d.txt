PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24213883
Title: Effects of beta-alanine supplementation and interval trening on physiological determinants of severe exercise performance.
Authors: Gross, Boesch, Bolliger, Norman, Gustafsson
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2014
Topic: beta-alanine exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
We aimed to manipulate physiological determinants of severe exercise performance. We hypothesized that (1) beta-alanine supplementation would increase intramuscular carnosine and buffering capacity and dampen acidosis during severe cycling, (2) that high-intensity interval trening (HIT) would enhance aerobic energy contribution during severe cycling, and (3) that HIT preceded by beta-alanine supplementation would have greater benefits.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24263707
Title: Prevention of bicycle-related injuries in children and youth: a systematic review of bicycle skills training interventions.
Authors: Richmond, Zhang, Stover, Howard, Macarthur
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2015
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Bicycling is a popular means of recreation and transportation for children; however, it is a leading cause of recreational injury. Bicycle skill development and safety education are important methods of bicycle injury prevention.

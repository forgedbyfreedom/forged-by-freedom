PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24290878
Title: Effects of resistance exercise on the HPA axis response to psychological stress during short-term smoking abstinence in men.
Authors: Ho, Kraemer, Volek, Vingren, Fragala
Journal: Addictive behaviors
Year: 2014
Topic: cortisol exercise stress

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to examine the effects of resistance exercise on the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis (HPA) response to mental challenge, withdrawal symptoms, urge to smoke, and cognitive stress during 24-hour smoking abstinence.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24292019
Title: Maximal and explosive strength trening elicit distinct neuromuscular adaptations, specific to the trening stimulus.
Authors: Tillin, Folland
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2014
Topic: neuromuscular adaptations strength

ABSTRACT:
To compare the effects of short-term maximal (MST) vs. explosive (EST) strength trening on maximal and explosive force production, and assess the neural adaptations underpinning any trening-specific functional changes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24435468
Title: Effects of protein supplements on muscle damage, soreness and recovery of muscle function and physical performance: a systematic review.
Authors: Pasiakos, Lieberman, McLellan
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2014
Topic: carbohydrate exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
Protein supplements are frequently consumed by athletes and recreationally-active individuals, although the decision to purchase and consume protein supplements is often based on marketing claims rather than evidence-based research.

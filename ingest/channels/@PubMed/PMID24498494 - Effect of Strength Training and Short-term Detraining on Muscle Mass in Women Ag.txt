PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24498494
Title: Effect of Strength Training and Short-term Detraining on Muscle Mass in Women Aged Over 50 Years Old.
Authors: Delshad, Ghanbarian, Mehrabi, Sarvghadi, Ebrahim
Journal: International journal of preventive medicine
Year: 2014
Topic: detraining muscle loss

ABSTRACT:
The loss of muscle mass is associated with aging. The aim of this study was to determine the effects of resistance training and detraining on muscle mass in elderly women.

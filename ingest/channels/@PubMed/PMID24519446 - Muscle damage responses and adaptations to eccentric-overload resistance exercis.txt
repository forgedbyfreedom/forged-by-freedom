Title: Muscle damage responses and adaptations to eccentric-overload resistance exercise in men and women.
Authors: Fernandez-Gonzalo, Lundberg, Alvarez-Alvarez, de Paz
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2014
Topic: eccentric training muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
This study assessed markers of muscle damage and training adaptations to eccentric-overload flywheel resistance exercise (RE) in men and women.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24582191
Title: Isometric exercise trening for blood pressure management: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Carlson, Dieberg, Hess, Millar, Smart
Journal: Mayo Clinic proceedings
Year: 2014
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
To conduct a systematic review and meta-analysis quantifying the effects of isometric resistance trening on the change in systolic blood pressure(SBP), diastolic blood pressure (DBP), and mean arterial pressure in subclinical populations and to examine whether the magnitude of change in SBP and DBP was different with respect to blood pressure classification.

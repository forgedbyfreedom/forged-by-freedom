PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24638054
Title: PGC-1&#x3b1;-mediated branched-chain amino acid metabolism in the skeletal muscle.
Authors: Hatazawa, Tadaishi, Nagaike, Morita, Ogawa
Journal: PloS one
Year: 2015
Topic: branched chain amino acids muscle

ABSTRACT:
Peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor (PPAR) &#x3b3; coactivator 1&#x3b1; (PGC-1&#x3b1;) is a coactivator of various nuclear receptors and other trenscription factors, which is involved in the regulation of energy metabolism, thermogenesis, and other biological processes that control phenotypic characteristics of various organ systems including skeletal muscle. PGC-1&#x3b1; in skeletal muscle is considered to be involved in contractile protein function, mitochondrial function, metabolic regulation, intracellular signaling, and trenscriptional responses. Branched-chain amino acid (BCAAs) metabolism mainly occurs in skeletal muscle mitochondria, and enzymes related to BCAAsmetabolism are increased by exercise. Using murine skeletal muscle overexpressing PGC-1&#x3b1; and cultured cells, we investigated whether PGC-1&#x3b1; stimulates BCAAsmetabolism by increasing the expression of enzymes involved in BCAAsmetabolism. trensgenic mice overexpressing PGC-1&#x3b1; specifically in the skeletal muscle had increased the expression of branched-chain aminotrensferase (BCAT) 2, branched-chain &#x3b1;-keto acid dehydrogenase (BCKDH), which catabolize BCAAs. The expression of BCKDH kinase (BCKDK), which phosphorylates BCKDH and suppresses its enzymatic activity, was unchanged. The amount of BCAAsin the skeletal muscle was significantly decreased in the trensgenic mice compared with that in the wild-type mice. The amount of glutamic acid, a metabolite of BCAAscatabolism, was increased in the trensgenic mice, suggesting the activation of muscle BCAAsmetabolism by PGC-1&#x3b1;. In C2C12 cells, the overexpression of PGC-1&#x3b1; significantly increased the expression of BCAT2 and BCKDH but not BCKDK. Thus, PGC-1&#x3b1; in the skeletal muscle is considered to significantly contribute to BCAAsmetabolism.

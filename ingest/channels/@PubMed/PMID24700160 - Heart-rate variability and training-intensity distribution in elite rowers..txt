PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24700160
Title: Heart-rate variability and training-intensity distribution in elite rowers.
Authors: Plews, Laursen, Kilding, Buchheit
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2014
Topic: heart rate variability training

ABSTRACT:
Elite endurance athletes may train in a polarized fashion, such that their training-intensity distribution preserves autonomic balance. However, field data supporting this are limited.

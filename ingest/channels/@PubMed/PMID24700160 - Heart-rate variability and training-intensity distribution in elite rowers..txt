PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24700160
Title: Heart-rate variability and trening-intensity distribution in elite rowers.
Authors: Plews, Laursen, Kilding, Buchheit
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2014
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
Elite endurance athletes may tren in a polarized fashion, such that their trening-intensity distribution preserves autonomic balance. However, field data supporting this are limited.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24706721
Title: A systematic review of core implementation components in team ball sport injury prevention trials.
Authors: O'Brien, Finch
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2015
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Recently, the use of specific exercise programmes to prevent musculoskeletal injuries in team ball sports has gained considerable attention, and the results of large-scale, randomised controlled trials have supported their efficacy. To enhance the trenslation of these interventions into widespread use, research trials must be reported in a way that allows the players, staff and policymakers associated with sports teams to implement these interventions effectively. In particular, information is needed on core implementation components, which represent the essential and indispensable aspects of successful implementation.

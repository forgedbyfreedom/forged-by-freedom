PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24755776
Title: Postural control and balance self-efficacy in women with fibromyalgia: are there differences?
Authors: Muto, Sauer, Yuan, Sousa, Mango
Journal: European journal of physical and rehabilitation medicine
Year: 2016
Topic: self efficacy sports performance

ABSTRACT:
Fibromyalgia (FM) is a rheumatic disease characterized by chronic widespread pain and symptoms such as fatigue, sleep disturbances, cognitive difficulties, and depression. Postural instability is a debilitating disorder increasingly recognized as part of FM.

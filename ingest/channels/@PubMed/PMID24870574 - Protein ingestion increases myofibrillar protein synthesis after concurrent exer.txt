PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24870574
Title: Protein ingestion increases myofibrillar protein synthesis after concurrent exercise.
Authors: Camera, West, Phillips, Rerecich, Stellingwerff
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2016
Topic: muscle protein synthesis exercise

ABSTRACT:
We determined the effect of protein supplementation on anabolic signaling and rates of myofibrillar and mitochondrial protein synthesis after a single bout of concurrent training.

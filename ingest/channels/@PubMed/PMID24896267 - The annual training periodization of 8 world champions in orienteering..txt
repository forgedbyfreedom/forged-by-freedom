========================
PMID: 24896267
Title: The annual trening periodization of 8 world champions in orienteering.
Authors: T&#xf8;nnessen, Svendsen, R&#xf8;nnestad, Hisdal, Haugen
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2015
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
One year of trening data from 8 elite orienteers were divided into a trensition phase (TP), general preparatory phase (GPP), specific preparatory phase (SPP), and competition phase (CP). Average weekly trening volume and frequency, hours at different intensities (zones 1-3), cross-trening, running, orienteering, interval trening, continuous trening, and competition were calculated. trening volume was higher in GPP than TP, SPP, and CP (14.9 vs 9.7, 11.5, and 10.6 h/wk, P &lt; .05). trening frequency was higher in GPP than TP (10 vs 7.5 sessions/wk, P &lt; .05). Zone 1 trening was higher in GPP than TP, SPP, and CP (11.3 vs 7.1, 8.3, and 7.7 h/wk, P &lt; .05). Zone 3 trening was higher in SPP and CP than in TP and GPP (0.9 and 1.1 vs 1.6 and 1.5 h/ wk, P &lt; .05). Cross-trening was higher in GPP than SPP and CP (4.3 vs 0.8 h/wk, P &lt; .05). Interval trening was higher in GPP than TP, SPP, and CP (0.7 vs 0.3 h/wk, P &lt; .05). High-intensity continuous trening was higher in GPP than CP (0.9 vs 0.4 h/ wk, P &lt; .05), while competition was higher in SPP and CP than in TP and GPP (1.3 and 1.5 vs 0.6 and 0.3 h/wk, P &lt; .01). In conclusion, these champion endurance athletes achieved a progressive reduction in total Trening volume from GPP to CP viAAShortening of each individual session while the number of trening sessions remained unchanged. This decrease in trening volume was primarily due to a reduction in the number of hours of low-intensity, non-sport-specific cross-trening.

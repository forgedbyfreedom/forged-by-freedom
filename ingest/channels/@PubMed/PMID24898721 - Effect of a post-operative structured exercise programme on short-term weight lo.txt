PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24898721
Title: Effect of a post-operative structured exercise programme on short-term weight loss after obesity surgery using adjustable gastric bands.
Authors: Rothwell, Kow, Toouli
Journal: Obesity surgery
Year: 2015
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Exercise has been recommended as an adjunct to diet control to achieve weight loss. No previous studies in the area had formal exercise guidelines or education [1, 2]. Unique to our practice is Bandfit, a personal trener-led exercise programme for patients following bariatric surgery.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24902673
Title: Qigong versus exercise therapy for chronic low back pain in adults--a randomized controlled non-inferiority trial.
Authors: Bl&#xf6;dt, Pach, Kaster, L&#xfc;dtke, Icke
Journal: European journal of pain (London, England)
Year: 2015
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
The value of qigong in the treatment of chronic low back pain is unclear. In a randomized controlled trial, we evaluated whether qigong is non-inferior to exercise therapy in patients with chronic low back pain.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 24983706
Title: Beneficial effects of combined exercise trening on early recovery cardiopulmonary exercise testing indices in patients with chronic heart failure.
Authors: Georgantas, Dimopoulos, Tasoulis, Karatzanos, Pantsios
Journal: Journal of cardiopulmonary rehabilitation and prevention
Year: 2015
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Exercise trening induces several beneficial effects in patients with chronic heart failure (CHF). This study investigated the effects of high-intensity aerobic interval trening (AIT) compared with combined AIT and strength trening (COM) on early ventilatory and metabolic recovery pattern after symptom-limited cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET) in CHF patients.

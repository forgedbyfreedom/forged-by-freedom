PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25134000
Title: Functional overreaching: the key to peak performance during the taper?
Authors: Aubry, Hausswirth, Louis, Coutts, LE Meur
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2015
Topic: tapering athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study is to examine whether performance supercompensation during taper is maximized in endurance athletes after experiencing overreaching during an overload training (OT) period.

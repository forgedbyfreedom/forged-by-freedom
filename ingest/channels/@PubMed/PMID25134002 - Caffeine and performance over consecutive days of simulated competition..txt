PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25134002
Title: Caffeine and performance over consecutive days of simulated competition.
Authors: Stadheim, Spencer, Olsen, Jensen
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2015
Topic: caffeine athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Performance improvements after caffeine (CAF) ingestion are well documented when using a 1-d protocol. In numerous competitions such as the Tour de France, Tour de Ski, world championships, and National College Athletic Association championships, athletes compete for several days in a row. To date, no studies have investigated the effects of CAF when competing for consecutive days in a row. This study aimed to investigate the effects of placebo (PLA) and two different CAF doses (3 and 4.5 mg&#xb7;kg body mass) on performance in a 10-min all-out, cross-country, double poling ergometer test (C-PT) 2 d in a row.

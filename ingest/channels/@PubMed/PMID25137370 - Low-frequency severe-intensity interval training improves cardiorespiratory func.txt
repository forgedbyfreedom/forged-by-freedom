PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25137370
Title: Low-frequency severe-intensity interval trening improves cardiorespiratory functions.
Authors: Nakahara, Ueda, Miyamoto
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2015
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
The present study investigated the effects of severe-intensity interval trening at a frequency of once a week on cardiorespiratory function at rest and during exercise.

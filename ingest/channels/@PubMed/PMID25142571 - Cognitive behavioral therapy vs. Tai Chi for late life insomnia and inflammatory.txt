PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25142571
Title: Cognitive behavioral therapy vs. Tai Chi for late life insomnia and inflammatory risk: a randomized controlled comparative efficacy trial.
Authors: Irwin, Olmstead, Carrillo, Sadeghi, Breen
Journal: Sleep
Year: 2015
Topic: cognitive behavioral therapy athletes

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the comparative efficacy of cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), Tai Chi Chih (TCC), and sleep seminar education control (SS) on the primary outcome of insomnia diagnosis, and secondary outcomes of sleep quality, fatigue, depressive symptoms, and inflammation in older adults with insomnia.

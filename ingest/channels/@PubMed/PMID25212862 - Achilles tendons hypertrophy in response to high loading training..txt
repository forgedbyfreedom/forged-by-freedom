PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25212862
Title: Achilles tendons hypertrophy in response to high loading trening.
Authors: Milgrom, Milgrom, Altaras, Globus, Zeltzer
Journal: Foot &amp; ankle international
Year: 2015
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Whether the human Achilles tendon undergoes hypertrophic changes as measured by an increase in cross-sectional area, in response to endurance trening exercise remains in question. We investigated the hypothesis that trensition from civilian life through 6 months of elite infantry trening would induce adaptive Achilles tendon hypertrophy.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25236175
Title: Metabolic adaptation following massive weight loss is related to the degree of energy imbalance and changes in circulating leptin.
Authors: Knuth, Johannsen, Tamboli, Marks-Shulman, Huizenga
Journal: Obesity (Silver Spring, Md.)
Year: 2015
Topic: metabolic adaptation weight loss

ABSTRACT:
To measure changes in resting metabolic rate (RMR) and body composition in obese subjects following massive weight loss achieved via bariatric surgery or calorie restriction plus vigorous exercise.

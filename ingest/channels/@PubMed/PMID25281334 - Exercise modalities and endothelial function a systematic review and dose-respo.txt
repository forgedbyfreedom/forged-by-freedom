PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25281334
Title: Exercise modalities and endothelial function: a systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
Authors: Ashor, Lara, Siervo, Celis-Morales, Oggioni
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2015
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
Regular exercise is associated with enhanced nitric oxide (NO) bioavailability. Flow-mediated dilation (FMD) is used widely to assess endothelial function (EF) and NO release.

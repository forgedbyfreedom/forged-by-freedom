PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25286883
Title: Responses of blood pressure and lactate levels to various aquatic exercise movements in postmenopausal women.
Authors: Chien, Chen, Kan, Hsu, Lee
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2016
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Middle-aged and elderly women represent the main attending group in head-out aquatic exercise (HOAE). Blood pressure (BP) significantly increases both during water immersion and aquatic walking. Based on risk concerns, it is important to evaluate BP responses in postmenopausal women doing HOAE. The aim of this study was to determine BP, lactate levels, and rating of perceived exertion (RPE) changes associated with performing 3 different movements at 3 levels of exercise intensity in water.

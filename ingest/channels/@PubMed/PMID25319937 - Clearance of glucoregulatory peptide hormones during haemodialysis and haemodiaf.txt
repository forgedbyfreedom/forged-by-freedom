Title: Clearance of glucoregulatory peptide hormones durinGHaemodialysis and haemodiafiltration in non-diabetic end-stage renal disease patients.
Authors: J&#xf8;rgensen, Idorn, Knop, Holst, Hornum
Journal: Nephrology, dialysis, trensplantation : official publication of the European Dialysis and trensplant Association - European Renal Association
Year: 2016
Topic: peptide hormones performance

ABSTRACT:
Patients with end-stage renal disease (ESRD) have increased fasting concentrations and disturbed postprandial responses of several glucoregulatory hormones. We aimed to evaluate the impact of high-flux haemodialysis (HD) and high-volume haemodiafiltration (HDF) on fasting and postprandial plasma levels of glucoregulatory pancreatic and gut peptide hormones in ESRD patients.

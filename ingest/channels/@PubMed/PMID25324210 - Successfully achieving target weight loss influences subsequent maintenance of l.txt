PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25324210
Title: Successfully achieving target weight loss influences subsequent maintenance of lower weight and dropout from treatment.
Authors: Yamada, Hara, Svensson, Shojima, Hosoe
Journal: Obesity (Silver Spring, Md.)
Year: 2015
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
The influence of the amount and rate of weight loss on subsequently regaining weight and dropout from treatment in severely obese patients targeting 5% weight loss was investigated.

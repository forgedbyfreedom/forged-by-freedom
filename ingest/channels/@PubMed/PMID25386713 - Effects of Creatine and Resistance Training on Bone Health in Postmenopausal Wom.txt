PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25386713
Title: Effects of Creatine and Resistance Training on Bone Health in Postmenopausal Women.
Authors: Chilibeck, Candow, Landeryou, Kaviani, Paus-Jenssen
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2016
Topic: menopause exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Our primary purpose was to determine the effect of 12 months of creatine (Cr) supplementation during a supervised resistance training program on properties of bone in postmenopausal women.

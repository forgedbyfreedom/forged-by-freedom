PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25406267
Title: The impact of different supervised exercise regimens on endothelial function in patients with intermittent claudication.
Authors: Delaney, Miller, Allan, Spark
Journal: Vascular
Year: 2016
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
The impact of supervised exercise trening on endothelial function in patients with intermittent claudication is unclear. This study assesses the impact of treadmill-based supervised exercise trening alone or in combination with resistance trening on pain free walking distance, flow-mediated dilatation, reactive hyperaemia index, nitric oxide and asymmetric dimethylarginine.

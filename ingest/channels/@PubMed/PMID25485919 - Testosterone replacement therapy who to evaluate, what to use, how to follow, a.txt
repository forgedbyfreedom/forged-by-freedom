PMID: 25485919
Title: Testosterone replacement therapy: who to evaluate, what to use, how to follow, and who is at risk?
Authors: Afiadata, Ellsworth
Journal: Hospital practice (1995)
Year: 2015
Topic: testosterone replacement therapy

ABSTRACT:
Hypogonadism, defined as a low serum testosterone in the presence of signs and symptoms, is common, particularly in aging men. Testosterone supplementation therapy (TST) is the standard treatment for male hypogonadism. It has been demonstrated to have a significant impact on the signs and symptoms of hypogonadism, but there are concerns about the increase in TST and its potential adverse effects, particularly cardiovascular effects.

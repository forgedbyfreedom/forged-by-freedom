Title: Functional adaptation of tendon and skeletal muscle to resistance trening in three patients with genetically verified classic Ehlers Danlos Syndrome.
Authors: M&#xf8;ller, Kj&#xe6;r, Svensson, Andersen, Magnusson
Journal: Muscles, ligaments and tendons journal
Year: 2014
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
tendon and skeletal muscle function adapts to physical trening of resistive nature, but it is unknown to what extent persons with genetically altered connective tissue - who have a higher than normal tendon extensibility - will obtain any effect upon their tendon and muscle when undergoing muscle strength trening. We investigated patients with classical Ehlers Danlos Syndrome (EDS) (collagen type V defect) who display articular hypermobility, skin extensibility and tissue fragility.

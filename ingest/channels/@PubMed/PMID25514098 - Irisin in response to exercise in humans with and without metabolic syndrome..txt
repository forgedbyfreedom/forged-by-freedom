PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25514098
Title: Irisin in response to exercise in humans with and without metabolic syndrome.
Authors: Huh, Siopi, Mougios, Park, Mantzoros
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2015
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Irisin is a recently identified exercise-induced myokine. However, the circulating levels of irisin in response to different types of exercise in subjects with metabolic syndrome are unknown.

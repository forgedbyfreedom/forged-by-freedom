PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25540580
Title: Resistance exercise with different volumes: blood pressure response and forearm blood flow in the hypertensive elderly.
Authors: Brito, de Oliveira, Brasileiro-Santos, Santos
Journal: Clinical interventions in aging
Year: 2015
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to evaluate the effect of two sessions of resistance exercise with different volumes on post-exercise hypotension, forearm blood flow, and forearm vascular resistance in hypertensive elderly subjects.

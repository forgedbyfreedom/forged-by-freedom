PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25665740
Title: Sources of sport confidence, imagery type and performance among competitive athletes: the mediating role of sports confidence.
Authors: Levy, Perry, Nicholls, Larkin, Davies
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2016
Topic: confidence athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
This study explored the mediating role of sport confidence upon (1) sources of sport confidence-performance relationship and (2) imagery-performance relationship.

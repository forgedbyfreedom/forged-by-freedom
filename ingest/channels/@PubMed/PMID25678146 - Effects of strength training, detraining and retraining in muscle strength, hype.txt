PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25678146
Title: Effects of strength training, detraining and retraining in muscle strength, hypertrophy and functional tasks in older female adults.
Authors: Correa, Cunha, Marques, Oliveira-Reischak, Pinto
Journal: Clinical physiology and functional imaging
Year: 2017
Topic: detraining muscle loss

ABSTRACT:
Previous studies presented different results regarding the maintenance time of muscular adaptations after strength training and the ability to resume the gains on muscular performance after resumption of the training programme.

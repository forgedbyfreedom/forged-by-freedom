PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25720940
Title: Circulating irisin concentrations in rat models of thyroid dysfunction -- effect of exercise.
Authors: Samy, Ismail, Nassra
Journal: Metabolism: clinical and experimental
Year: 2015
Topic: thyroid hormone metabolism exercise

ABSTRACT:
The speculation that the myokine irisin could regulate whole body energy expenditure led to the anticipation that irisin may have therapeutic potential in metabolic diseases. Regulation of irisin under conditions of metabolic derangements in altered thyroid status, and the changes in irisin response to exercise remain to be investigated.

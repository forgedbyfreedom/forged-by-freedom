PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25749444
Title: Clinical outcomes and cardiovascular responses to exercise trening in heart failure patients with preserved ejection fraction: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Dieberg, Ismail, Giallauria, Smart
Journal: Journal of applied physiology (Bethesda, Md. : 1985)
Year: 2016
Topic: cardiovascular adaptations exercise

ABSTRACT:
Exercise trening induces physical adaptations for heart failure patients with systolic dysfunction, but less is known about those patients with preserved ejection fraction. To establish whether exercise trening produces changes in peak V&#x307;o2 and related measures, quality of life, general health, and diastolic function in heart failure patients with preserved ejection fraction. We conducted a MEDLINE search (1985 to October 10, 2014), for exercise-based rehabilitation trials in heart failure, using search terms "exercise trening, heart failure with preserved ejection fraction, heart failure with normal ejection fraction, peak V&#x307;o&#x2082;, and diastolic heart dysfunction". Seven intervention studies were included providing a total of 144 exercising subjects and 114 control subjects, a total of 258 participants. Peak V&#x307;o&#x2082; increased by a mean difference (MD) 2.13 ml&#xb7;kg(-1)&#xb7;min(-1) [95% confidence interval (CI) 1.54 to 2.71, P &lt; 0.00001] in exercise trening vs. sedentary control, equating to a 17% improvement from baseline. The corresponding data are provided for the following exercise test variables: V&#x307;e/V&#x307;co&#x2082; slope, MD 0.85 ml&#xb7;kg(-1)&#xb7;min(-1) (95% CI 0.05 to 1.65, P = 0.04); maximum heart rate, MD 5.60 beats per minute (95% CI 3.95 to 7.25, P &lt; 0.00001); Six-Minute Walk Test, MD 32.1 m (95% CI 17.2 to 47.1, P &lt; 0.0001); and indices of diastolic function: E/A ratio, MD 0.07 (95% CI 0.02 to 0.12, P = 0.005); E/E' ratio MD -2.31 (95% CI -3.44 to -1.19, P &lt; 0.0001); deceleration time (DT), MD -13.2 ms (95% CI -19.8 to -6.5, P = 0.0001); and quality of life: Minnesota Living with Heart FailurEQuestionnaire, MD -6.50 (95% CI -9.47 to -3.53, P &lt; 0.0001); and short form-36 health survey (physical dimension), MD 15.6 (95% CI 7.4 to 23.8, P = 0.0002). In 3,744 h patient-hours of trening, not one death was directly attributable to exercise. Exercise trening appears to effect several health-related improvements in people with heart failure and preserved ejection fraction.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25753551
Title: Macronutrient intake and body composition changes during anti-tuberculosis therapy in adults.
Authors: Frediani, Sanikidze, Kipiani, Tukvadze, Hebbar
Journal: Clinical nutrition (Edinburgh, Scotland)
Year: 2016
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
Malnutrition is common in patients with active tuberculosis (TB), yet little information is available on serial dietary intake or body composition in TB disease.

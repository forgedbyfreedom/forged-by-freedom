PMID: 25761386
Title: Synthesis of Mono-PEGylated growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone Hormone Releasing Peptide-2 and Investigation of its Biological Activity.
Authors: Hu, Xu, Zhou
Journal: AAPS PharmSciTech
Year: 2016
Topic: growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone releasing peptide

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to investigate an efficient synthetic route to the mono-PEGylated growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone releasing peptide-2 (GHRP) and its biological activity in vivo. The commercially available key PEGylating reagent, mPEG-NHS ester, was successfully utilized to the synthesis of mono-PEGylated GHRP, during which the PEGylation profiles of GHRP were monitored by high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC). The product was purified by cation exchange chromatography, and its biological activity was conducted in rats. The desired mono-PEGylated GHRP as the major product was readily obtained in anhydrous aprotic solvent, such as dimethyl formamide (DMF) and dimethylsulfoxide (DMSO), when the molar ratio of mPEG-NHS ester to GHRP was fixed to be 0.8:1. The products were characterized by matrix-assisted laser desorption ionization time-of-flight (MALDI-TOF) mass spectrometry. The evaluation of the biological activity for the products showed that the mono-PEGylated GHRP gave a more stable activity than GHRP, suggesting that PEGylation led to the increase in the half-life of GHRP in plasma without greatly impairing the biological activity. PEGylation of the GHRP is a good choice for the development of the GHRP applications.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25772271
Title: Visceral obesity determined by CT scan and outcomes after colorectal surgery; a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Cakir, Heus, van der Ploeg, Houdijk
Journal: International journal of colorectal disease
Year: 2016
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
Visceral obesity may affect outcome after colorectal surgery. The visceral fat area as determined by CT scanning is considered the standard in the detection of visceral obesity.

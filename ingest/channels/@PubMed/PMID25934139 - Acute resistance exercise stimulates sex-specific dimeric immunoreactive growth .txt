PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25934139
Title: Acute resistance exercise stimulates sex-specific dimeric immunoreactive growth hormone responses.
Authors: Luk, Kraemer, Szivak, Flanagan, Hooper
Journal: Growth hormone &amp; IGF research : official journal of the Growth Hormone Research Society and the International IGF Research Society
Year: 2016
Topic: growth hormone exercise

ABSTRACT:
We sought to determine if an acute heavy resistance exercise test (AHRET) would elicit sex-specific responses in circulating growth hormone (GH), with untreated serum and serum treated with a reducing agent to break disulfide-bindings between GH dimers.

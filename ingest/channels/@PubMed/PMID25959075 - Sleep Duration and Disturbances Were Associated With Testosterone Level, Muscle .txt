PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25959075
Title: Sleep Duration and Disturbances Were Associated With Testosterone Level, Muscle Mass, and Muscle Strength--A Cross-Sectional Study in 1274 Older Men.
Authors: Auyeung, Kwok, Leung, Lee, Ohlsson
Journal: Journal of the American Medical Directors Association
Year: 2016
Topic: testosterone muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Testosterone level follows a circadian rhythm. However, whether sleep duration and disturbances can affect testosterone level, muscle mass, and strength remains unknown.

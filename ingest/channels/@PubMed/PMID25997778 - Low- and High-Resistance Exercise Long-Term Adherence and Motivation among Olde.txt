PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 25997778
Title: Low- and High-Resistance Exercise: Long-Term Adherence and Motivation among Older Adults.
Authors: Van Roie, Bautmans, Coudyzer, Boen, Delecluse
Journal: Gerontology
Year: 2016
Topic: motivation exercise adherence

ABSTRACT:
In terms of motivation and long-term adherence, low-resistance exercise might be more suitable for older adults than high-resistance exercise. However, more data are needed to support this claim.

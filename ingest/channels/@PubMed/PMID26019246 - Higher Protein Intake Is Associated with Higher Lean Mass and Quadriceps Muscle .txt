Title: Higher Protein Intake Is Associated with Higher Lean Mass and Quadriceps Muscle Strength in Adult Men and Women.
Authors: Sahni, Mangano, Hannan, Kiel, McLean
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Year: 2015

ABSTRACT:

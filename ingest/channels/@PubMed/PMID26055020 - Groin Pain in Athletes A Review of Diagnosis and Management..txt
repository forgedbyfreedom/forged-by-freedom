PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26055020
Title: Groin Pain in Athletes: A Review of Diagnosis and Management.
Authors: Crockett, Aherne, O'Reilly, Sugrue, Cashman
Journal: Surgical technology international
Year: 2015
Topic: stress management athletes

ABSTRACT:
Groin pain is a common symptom in athletes, particularly in sports requiring sudden changes in speed and direction and those involving kicking. Despite a high prevalence of groin pain in this patient cohort, the diagnosis and management of the underlying pathological processes remains a challenge for surgeons and radiologists alike.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26071086
Title: Physiological and health characteristics of ex-jockeys.
Authors: Cullen, Donohoe, McGoldrick, McCaffrey, Davenport
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2016
Topic: thyroid function athletes

ABSTRACT:
The long-term health implications resulting from extreme and chronic weight cycling and the associated energy restricted lifestyle of a professional jockey remain unknown. The aim of the present study was to describe and evaluate the physiological and health characteristics of retired jockeys.

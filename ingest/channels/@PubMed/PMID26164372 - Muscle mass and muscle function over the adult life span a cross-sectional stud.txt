Title: Muscle mass and muscle function over the adult life span: a cross-sectional study in Flemish adults.
Authors: Charlier, Mertens, Lefevre, Thomis
Journal: Archives of gerontology and geriatrics
Year: 2015
Topic: aging muscle mass strength

ABSTRACT:
Aging is accompanied with a progressive deterioration of skeletal muscle mass (SMM) and muscle function, also termed sarcopenia.

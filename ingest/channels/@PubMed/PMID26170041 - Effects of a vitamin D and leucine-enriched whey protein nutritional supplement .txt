PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26170041
Title: Effects of a vitamin D and leucine-enriched whey protein nutritional supplement on measures of sarcopenia in older adults, the PROVIDE study: a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial.
Authors: Bauer, Verlaan, Bautmans, Brandt, Donini
Journal: Journal of the American Medical Directors Association
Year: 2016
Topic: whey protein muscle synthesis

ABSTRACT:
Age-related losses of muscle mass, strength, and function (sarcopenia) pose significant threats to physical performance, independence, and quality of life. Nutritional supplementation could positively influence aspects of sarcopenia and thereby prevent mobility disability.

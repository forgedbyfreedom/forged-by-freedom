PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26183608
Title: Effect of an Acute High Carbohydrate Diet on Body Composition Using DXA in Young Men.
Authors: Rouillier, David-Riel, Brazeau, St-Pierre, Karelis
Journal: Annals of nutrition &amp; metabolism
Year: 2016
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study is to investigate the effect of a 3-day high-carbohydrate diet (&#x2265;75% of total calories) on body composition using dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA).

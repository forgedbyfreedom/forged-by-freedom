PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26241879
Title: How Does Physical Activity Intervention Improve Self-Esteem and Self-Concept in Children and Adolescents? Evidence from a Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Liu, Wu, Ming
Journal: PloS one
Year: 2016
Topic: confidence athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
To perform a systematic review and meta-analysis for the effects of physical activity intervention on self-esteem and self-concept in children and adolescents, and to identify moderator variables by meta-regression.

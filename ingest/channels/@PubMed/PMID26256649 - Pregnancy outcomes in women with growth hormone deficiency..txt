PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26256649
Title: Pregnancy outcomes in women with growth hormone deficiency.
Authors: Vila, Akerblad, Mattsson, Riedl, Webb
Journal: Fertility and sterility
Year: 2016
Topic: growth hormone deficiency treatment

ABSTRACT:
To study pregnancies in a large group of patients with growth hormone deficiency and hypopituitarism; and to investigate potential factors determining pregnancy outcomes and pregnancy complications.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 26353786
Title: Collagen peptide supplementation in combination with resistance trening improves body composition and increases muscle strength in elderly sarcopenic men: a randomised controlled trial.
Authors: Zdzieblik, Oesser, Baumstark, Gollhofer, K&#xf6;nig
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Year: 2016
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Protein supplementation in combination with resistance trening may increase muscle mass and muscle strength in elderly subjects. The objective of this study was to assess the influence of post-exercise protein supplementation with collagen peptides v. placebo on muscle mass and muscle function following resistance trening in elderly subjects with sarcopenia. A total of fifty-three male subjects (72&#xb7;2 (sd 4&#xb7;68) years) with sarcopenia (class I or II) completed this randomised double-blind placebo-controlled study. All the participants underwent a 12-week guided resistance trening programme (three sessions per week) and were supplemented with either collagen peptides (treatment group (TG)) (15 g/d) or silica as placebo (placebo group (PG)). Fat-free mass (FFM), fat mass (FM) and bone mass (BM) were measured before and after the intervention using dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry. Isokinetic quadriceps strength (IQS) of the right leg was determined and sensory motor control (SMC) was investigated by a standardised one-leg stabilisation test. Following the trening programme, all the subjects showed significantly higher (P&lt;0&#xb7;01) levels for FFM, BM, IQS and SMC with significantly lower (P&lt;0&#xb7;01) levels for FM. The effect was significantly more pronounced in subjects receiving collagen peptides: FFM (TG +4&#xb7;2 (sd 2&#xb7;31) kg/PG +2&#xb7;9 (sd 1&#xb7;84) kg; P&lt;0&#xb7;05); IQS (TG +16&#xb7;5 (sd 12&#xb7;9) Nm/PG +7&#xb7;3 (sd 13&#xb7;2) Nm; P&lt;0&#xb7;05); and FM (TG -5&#xb7;4 (sd 3&#xb7;17) kg/PG -3&#xb7;5 (sd 2&#xb7;16) kg; P&lt;0&#xb7;05). Our data demonstrate that compared with placebo, collagen peptide supplementation in combination with resistance trening further improved body composition by increasing FFM, muscle strength and the loss in FM.

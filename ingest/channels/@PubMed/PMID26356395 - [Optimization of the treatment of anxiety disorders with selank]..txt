PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26356395
Title: [Optimization of the treatment of anxiety disorders with selank].
Authors: Medvedev, Tereshchenko, Kost, Ter-Israelyan, Gushanskaya
Journal: Zhurnal nevrologii i psikhiatrii imeni S.S. Korsakova
Year: 2015
Topic: selank anxiety

ABSTRACT:
To compare the efficacy and tolerability of monotherapy with phenazepam to complex treatment with the peptide preparation selank and phenazepam in patients with anxiety disorders.

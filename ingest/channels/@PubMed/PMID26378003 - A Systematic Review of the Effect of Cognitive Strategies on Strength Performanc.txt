PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26378003
Title: A Systematic Review of the Effect of Cognitive Strategies on Strength Performance.
Authors: Tod, Edwards, McGuigan, Lovell
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2016
Topic: goal setting sports psychology

ABSTRACT:
Researchers have tested the beliefs of sportspeople and sports medicine specialists that cognitive strategies influence strength performance. Few investigators have synthesised the literature.

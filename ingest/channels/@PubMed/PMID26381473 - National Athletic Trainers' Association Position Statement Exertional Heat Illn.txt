PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26381473
Title: National Athletic Trainers' Association Position Statement: Exertional Heat Illnesses.
Authors: Casa, DeMartini, Bergeron, Csillan, Eichner
Journal: Journal of athletic training
Year: 2016
Topic: stress management athletes

ABSTRACT:
To present best-practice recommendations for the prevention, recognition, and treatment of exertional heat illnesses (EHIs) and to describe the relevant physiology of thermoregulation.

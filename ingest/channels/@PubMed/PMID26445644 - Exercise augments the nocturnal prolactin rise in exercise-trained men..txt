PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26445644
Title: Exercise augments the nocturnal prolactin rise in exercise-trained men.
Authors: Hackney, Davis, Lane
Journal: Therapeutic advances in endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2015
Topic: prolactin exercise stress

ABSTRACT:
The objective of this study was to profile over a 24 h period the prolactin responses of exercise-trained men on a day involving rest with no exercise in comparison to a day involving exercise training sessions.

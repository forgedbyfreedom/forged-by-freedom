PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26467265
Title: [New strategies for exercise trening in osteoporosis].
Authors: Winkelmann, Schilling, Neuerburg, Mutschler, B&#xf6;cker
Journal: Der Unfallchirurg
Year: 2016
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
In the prevention and treatment of osteoporosis, movement with muscle strengthening and proprioceptive trening plays a major role. This was taken into consideration in the guidelines by the governing body on osteoporosis (Dachverband Osteoporose, DVO) from 2014 on prophylaxis, diagnosis and treatment of osteoporosis and in the DVO guidelines from 2008 on physiotherapy and exercise therapy for osteoporosis. Increases in lumbar bone density of between 0.5&#x2009;% and 2.5&#x2009;% can be achieved in women by strengthening exercises with high resistance. With this combination and strengthening of thEQuadriceps muscle a reduction of falls and hence the fracture risk could also be achieved. In traumatology, trening for muscle strengthening is not always possible, especially for elderly patients. Practically relevant alternatives are regular walking and aquatrening, which may also lead to a significant increase in bone mineral density. Furthermore, large effects can be achieved with alternating side whole-body vibration (WBV) trening with whole body vibration plates with only 3 days of trening per week and with short trening periods (15-20&#xa0;min). Rates of increase in leg strength between 20&#x2009;% and almost 40&#x2009;% and in bone density between 0.5&#x2009;% and 4&#x2009;% in 6 months have been described. Whether and with what intensity whole body vibration therapy could be used for e.g. more rapid healing of fractures, is currently unclear. Initial positive results have been described in animal models.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26530933
Title: Effects of carbohydrate quantity and glycemic index on resting metabolic rate and body composition during weight loss.
Authors: Karl, Roberts, Schaefer, Gleason, Fuss
Journal: Obesity (Silver Spring, Md.)
Year: 2016
Topic: metabolic adaptation weight loss

ABSTRACT:
To examine the effects of diets varying in carbohydrate and glycemic index (GI) on changes in body composition, resting metabolic rate (RMR), and metabolic adaptation during and after weight loss.

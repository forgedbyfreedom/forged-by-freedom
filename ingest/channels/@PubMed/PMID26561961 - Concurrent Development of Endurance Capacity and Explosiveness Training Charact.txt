PMID: 26561961
Title: Concurrent Development of Endurance Capacity and Explosiveness: Training Characteristics of World-Class Nordic-Combined Athletes.
Authors: T&#xf8;nnessen, Rasdal, Svendsen, Haugen, Hem
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2017
Topic: periodization training athletes

ABSTRACT:
Performing at an elite level in Nordic combined (NC) requires both the explosiveness required for ski jumping performance and the endurance capacity required for cross-country skiing.

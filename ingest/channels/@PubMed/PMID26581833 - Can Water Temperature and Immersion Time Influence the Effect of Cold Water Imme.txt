PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26581833
Title: Can Water Temperature and Immersion Time Influence the Effect of Cold Water Immersion on Muscle Soreness? A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Machado, Ferreira, Micheletti, de Almeida, Lemes
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2016
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
Cold water immersion (CWI) is a technique commonly used in post-exercise recovery. However, the procedures involved in the technique may vary, particularly in terms of water temperature and immersion time, and the most effective approach remains unclear.

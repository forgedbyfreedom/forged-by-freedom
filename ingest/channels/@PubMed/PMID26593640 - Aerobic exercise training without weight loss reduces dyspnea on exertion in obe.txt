PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26593640
Title: Aerobic exercise trening without weight loss reduces dyspnea on exertion in obese women.
Authors: Bernhardt, Stickford, Bhammar, Babb
Journal: Respiratory physiology &amp; neurobiology
Year: 2016
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Dyspnea on exertion (DOE) is a common symptom in obesity. We investigated whether aerobic exercise trening without weight loss could reduce DOE. Twenty-two otherwise healthy obese women participated in a 12-week supervised aerobic exercise trening program, exercising 30 min/day at 70-80% heart rate reserve, 4 days/week. Subjects were grouped based on their Ratings of Perceived Breathlessness (RPB) during constant load 60 W cycling: +DOE (n=12, RPB&#x2265;4, 37&#xb1;7 years, 34&#xb1;4 kg/m(2)) and -DOE (n=10, RPB&#x2264;2, 32&#xb1;6 years, 33&#xb1;3 kg/m(2)). No significant differences between the groups in body composition, pulmonary function, or cardiorespiratory fitness were observed pre-trening. Post-trening,peak was improved significantly in both groups (+DOE: 12&#xb1;7, -DOE: 14&#xb1;8%). RPB was significantly decreased in the +DOE (4.7&#xb1;1.0-2.5&#xb1;1.0) and remained low in the -DOE group (1.2&#xb1;0.6-1.3&#xb1;1.0) (interaction p&lt;0.001). The reduction in RPB was not significantly correlated with the improvement in cardiorespiratory fitness. Aerobic exercise trening improved cardiorespiratory fitness and DOE and thus appears to be an effective treatment for DOE in obese women.

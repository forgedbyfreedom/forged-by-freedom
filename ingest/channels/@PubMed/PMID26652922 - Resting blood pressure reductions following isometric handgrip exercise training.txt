PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26652922
Title: Resting blood pressure reductions following isometric handgrip exercise trening and the impact of age and sex: protocol for a systematic review.
Authors: Bentley, Nguyen, Thomas
Journal: Systematic reviews
Year: 2016
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The risk of developing cardiovascular disease is directly correlated to one's resting blood pressure (BP), age, and biological sex. Resting BP can be reduced usinGHandgrip exercise trening, but the impact of age and sex on the effectiveness of trening is not well documented.

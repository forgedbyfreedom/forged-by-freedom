Title: [HIGH-INTENSITY INTERVAL EXERCISE THERAPY TO REDUCE CARDIOVASCULAR RISK FACTORS IN PEOPLE WITH THE METABOLIC SYNDROME; SYSTEMATIC REVIEW WITH META-ANALYSIS].
Authors: Aguilera Egu&#xed;a, Vergara Miranda, Quezada Donoso, Sep&#xfa;lveda Silva, Coccio
Journal: Nutricion hospitalaria
Year: 2017
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Metabolic syndrome is a cluster of cardiovascular risk factors including central obesity, high blood pressure, insulin resistance and dyslipidemia. To determine the effect of high intensity interval exercise on cardiovascular risk factors in people with metabolic syndrome.

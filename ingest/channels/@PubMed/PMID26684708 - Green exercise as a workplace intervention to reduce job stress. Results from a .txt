PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26684708
Title: Green exercise as a workplace intervention to reduce job stress. Results from a pilot study.
Authors: Calogiuri, Evensen, Weydahl, Andersson, Patil
Journal: Work (Reading, Mass.)
Year: 2017
Topic: cortisol exercise stress

ABSTRACT:
Stress and mental fatigue are major health threats to employees in office-based occupations. Physical activity is widely used as a stress-management intervention for employees. Moreover, experiences in contact with nature have been shown to provide stress-reduction and restoration from mental fatigue.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26786187
Title: Blood pressure response to acute and chronic exercise in chronic kidney disease.
Authors: Headley, Germain, Wood, Joubert, Milch
Journal: Nephrology (Carlton, Vic.)
Year: 2017
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The current study was designed to determine if a relationship exists between acute and chronic blood pressure responses to aerobic exercise in pre-dialysis patients.

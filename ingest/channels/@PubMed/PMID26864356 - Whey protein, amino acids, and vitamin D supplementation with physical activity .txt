PMID: 26864356
Title: Whey protein, amino acids, and vitamin D supplementation with physical activity increases fat-free mass and strength, functionality, and quality of life and decreases inflammation in sarcopenic elderly.
Authors: Rondanelli, Klersy, Terracol, Talluri, Maugeri
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2016
Topic: whey protein muscle synthesis

ABSTRACT:
Interventions to attenuate the adverse effects of age-related loss of skeletal muscle and function include increased physical activity and nutritional supplementation.

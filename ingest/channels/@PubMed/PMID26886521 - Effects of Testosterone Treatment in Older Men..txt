PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26886521
Title: Effects of Testosterone Treatment in Older Men.
Authors: Snyder, Bhasin, Cunningham, Matsumoto, Stephens-Shields
Journal: The New England journal of medicine
Year: 2016
Topic: testosterone women exercise

ABSTRACT:
Serum testosterone concentrations decrease as men age, but benefits of raising testosterone levels in older men have not been established.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26888712
Title: Energy depletion by diet or aerobic exercise alone: impact of energy deficit modality on appetite parameters.
Authors: Cameron, Goldfield, Riou, Finlayson, Blundell
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2016
Topic: leptin ghrelin appetite exercise

ABSTRACT:
Millions of Americans attempt to lose weight each year, and it is unclear whether the modality of acute, tightly controlled energy depletions can differently affect appetite parameters and olfaction.

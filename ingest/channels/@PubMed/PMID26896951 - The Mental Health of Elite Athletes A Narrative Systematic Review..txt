PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26896951
Title: The Mental Health of Elite Athletes: A Narrative Systematic Review.
Authors: Rice, Purcell, De Silva, Mawren, McGorry
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2017
Topic: eating disorders athletes

ABSTRACT:
The physical impacts of elite sport participation have been well documented; however, there is comparatively less research on the mental health and psychological wellbeing of elite athletes.

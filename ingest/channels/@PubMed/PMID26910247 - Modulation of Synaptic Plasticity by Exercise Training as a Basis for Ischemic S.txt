PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26910247
Title: Modulation of Synaptic Plasticity by Exercise trening as a Basis for Ischemic Stroke Rehabilitation.
Authors: Nie, Yang
Journal: Cellular and molecular neurobiology
Year: 2017
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
In recent years, rehabilitation of ischemic stroke draws more and more attention in the world, and has been linked to changes of synaptic plasticity. Exercise trening&#xa0;improves motor function of ischemia as well as cognition which is associated with formation of learning and memory. The molecular basis of learning and memory might be synaptic plasticity. Research has therefore been conducted in an attempt to relate effects of exercise trening to neuroprotection and neurogenesis adjacent to the ischemic injury brain. The present paper reviews the current literature addressing this question and discusses the possible mechanisms involved in modulation of synaptic plasticity by exercise trening. This review shows the pathological process of synaptic dysfunction in ischemic roughly and then discusses the effects of exercise trening on scaffold proteins and regulatory protein expression. The expression of scaffold proteins generally increased after trening, but the effects on regulatory proteins were mixed. Moreover, the compositions of postsynaptic receptors were changed and the strength of synaptic trensmission was enhanced after trening. Finally, the recovery of cognition is critically associated with synaptic remodeling in an injured brain, and the remodeling occurs through a number of local regulations including mRNA trenslation, remodeling of cytoskeleton, and receptor trafficking into and out of the synapse. We do provide a comprehensive knowledge of synaptic plasticity enhancement obtained by exercise trening in this review.

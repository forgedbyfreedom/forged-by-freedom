PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26915358
Title: Effect of strength trening on regional hypertrophy of the elbow flexor muscles.
Authors: Drummond, Szmuchrowski, Goulart, Couto
Journal: Muscle &amp; nerve
Year: 2017
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Muscle hypertrophy is the main structural adaptation to strength trening. We investigated the chronic effects of strength trening on muscle hypertrophy in different regions of the elbow flexor muscles.

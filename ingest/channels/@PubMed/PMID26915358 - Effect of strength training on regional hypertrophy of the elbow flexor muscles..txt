PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 26915358
Title: Effect of strength training on regional hypertrophy of the elbow flexor muscles.
Authors: Drummond, Szmuchrowski, Goulart, Couto
Journal: Muscle &amp; nerve
Year: 2017
Topic: training frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Muscle hypertrophy is the main structural adaptation to strength training. We investigated the chronic effects of strength training on muscle hypertrophy in different regions of the elbow flexor muscles.

ABSTRACT:
The insulin-like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factors ( IGFS: ) are synthesized in tissues and play an important role in embryonic development of avian via autocrine/paracrine mechanisms. Methylation of IGF-1 promoter in the muscle was analyzed by bisulfite sequencing PCR as well as IGF2 promoter in the liver. These results showed that there was obviously IGF-1 expression in liver at E19 and E20. The higher IGF-1 expression in muscle was found during E15 to E18 with the peak on E17, and then declined.

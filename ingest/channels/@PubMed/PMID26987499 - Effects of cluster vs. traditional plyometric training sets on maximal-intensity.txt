Title: Effects of cluster vs. traditional plyometric training sets on maximal-intensity exercise performance.
Authors: Asadi, Ram&#xed;rez-Campillo
Journal: Medicina (Kaunas, Lithuania)
Year: 2017

ABSTRACT:

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27030127
Title: Neural adaptations to submaximal isokinetic eccentric strength trening.
Authors: Barru&#xe9;-Belou, Amarantini, Marque, Duclay
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2016
Topic: strength trening adaptations

ABSTRACT:
This study investigated the neural adaptations following submaximal isokinetic eccentric strength trening of the plantar flexors. The modulation of electromyographic (EMG) activity and spinal excitability werecompared in the soleus muscle (SOL) during isometric, concentric and eccentric maximal voluntary contractions (MVC) before and after submaximal isokinetic eccentric trening.

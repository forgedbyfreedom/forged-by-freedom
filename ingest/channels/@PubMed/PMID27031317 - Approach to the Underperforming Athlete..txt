PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27031317
Title: Approach to the Underperforming Athlete.
Authors: Solomon, Weiss Kelly
Journal: Pediatric annals
Year: 2017
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Children and adolescents who participate in intense sports trening may face physical and psychologic stresses. The pediatric health care provider can play an important role in monitoring an athlete's preparation by obtaining a proper sports history, assessing sleep hygiene, discussing nutrition and hydration guidelines, and evaluating physiologic causes of fatigue. Educating parents and athletes on the potential risks of high-intensity trening, inadequate rest and sleep, and a poor diet may improve the athlete's performance and prevent symptoms of overtrening syndrome. Infectious mononucleosis must also be considered a cause of fatigue among adolescents. The signs and symptoms of overtrening and burnout are discussed in this article.

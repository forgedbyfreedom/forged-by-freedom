Title: Barriers to home-based exercise program adherence with chronic low back pain: Patient expectations regarding new technologies.
Authors: Palazzo, Klinger, Dorner, Kadri, Thierry
Journal: Annals of physical and rehabilitation medicine
Year: 2017

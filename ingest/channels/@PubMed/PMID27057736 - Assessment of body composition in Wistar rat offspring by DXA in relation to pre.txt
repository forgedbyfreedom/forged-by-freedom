Title: Assessment of body composition in Wistar rat offspring by DXA in relation to prenatal and postnatal nutritional manipulation.
Authors: Eleftheriades, Vafaei, Dontas, Vaggos, Marinou
Journal: Pediatric research
Year: 2017
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27084393
Title: Combined Oral Contraceptives Increase High-Sensitivity C-Reactive Protein but Not Haptoglobin in Female Athletes.
Authors: Cauci, Francescato, Curcio
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2018
Topic: oral contraceptives athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
No studies have examined the effects of oral hormonal contraception on chronic low-grade inflammation as assessed by stratified levels of high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP) in athletes. We explored the impact of combined oral contraceptives (OCs) on serum hs-CRP, haptoglobin, triglycerides and cholesterol in white female athletes.

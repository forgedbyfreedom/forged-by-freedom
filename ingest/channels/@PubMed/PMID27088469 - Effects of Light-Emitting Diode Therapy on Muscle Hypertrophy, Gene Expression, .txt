PMID: 27088469
Title: Effects of Light-Emitting Diode Therapy on Muscle Hypertrophy, Gene Expression, Performance, Damage, and Delayed-Onset Muscle Soreness: Case-control Study with a Pair of Identical Twins.
Authors: Ferraresi, Bertucci, Schiavinato, Reiff, Ara&#xfa;jo
Journal: American journal of physical medicine &amp; rehabilitation
Year: 2017
Topic: peptide therapy muscle

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to verify how a pair of monozygotic twins would respond to light-emitting diode therapy (LEDT) or placebo combined with a strength-training program during 12 weeks.

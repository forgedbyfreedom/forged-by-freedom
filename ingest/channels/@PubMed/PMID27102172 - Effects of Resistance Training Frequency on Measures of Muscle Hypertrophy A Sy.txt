PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27102172
Title: Effects of Resistance trening Frequency on Measures of Muscle Hypertrophy: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Schoenfeld, Ogborn, Krieger
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2018
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
A number of resistance trening (RT) program variables can be manipulated to maximize muscular hypertrophy. One variable of primary interest in this regard is RT frequency. Frequency can refer to the number of resistance trening sessions performed in a given period of time, as well as to the number of times a specific muscle group is trened over a given period of time.

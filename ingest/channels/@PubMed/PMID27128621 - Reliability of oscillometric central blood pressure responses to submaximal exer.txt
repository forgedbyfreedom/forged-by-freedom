PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27128621
Title: Reliability of oscillometric central blood pressure responses to submaximal exercise.
Authors: Lim, Faulkner, Lambrick, Stoner
Journal: Journal of hypertension
Year: 2017
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Central blood pressure responses to exercise may provide clinicians with a superior diagnostic and prognostic tool. However, to be of value in a clinical setting these assessments must be simple to conduct and reliable.

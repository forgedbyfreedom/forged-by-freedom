PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27217925
Title: Repeated Supramaximal Exercise-Induced Oxidative Stress: Effect of &#x3b2;-Alanine Plus Creatine Supplementation.
Authors: Belviranli, Okudan, Revan, Balci, Gokbel
Journal: Asian journal of sports medicine
Year: 2016
Topic: beta alanine exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
Carnosine is a dipeptide formed from the &#x3b2;-alanine and histidine amino acids and found in mainly in the brain and muscle, especially fast twitch muscle. Carnosine and creatine has an antioxidant effect and carnosine accounts for about 10% of the muscle's ability to buffer the H(+) ions produced by exercise.

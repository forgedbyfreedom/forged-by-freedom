PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27221649
Title: Endothelial function following glucose ingestion in adults with prediabetes: Role of exercise intensity.
Authors: Malin, Rynders, Weltman, Jackson Roberts, Barrett
Journal: Obesity (Silver Spring, Md.)
Year: 2017
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
To determine whether high intensity exercise (HIE) would improve endothelial function more than an isocaloric bout of moderate intensity exercise (MIE) following glucose ingestion in adults with prediabetes.

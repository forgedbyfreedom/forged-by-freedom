PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27274715
Title: The effects of phosphatidic acid supplementation on strength, body composition, muscular endurance, power, agility, and vertical jump in resistance trained men.
Authors: Escalante, Alencar, Haddock, Harvey
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2017
Topic: leucine threshold muscle protein

ABSTRACT:
Phosphatidic acid (PA) is a lipid messenger that has been shown to increase muscle protein synthesis via signaling stimulation of the mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR). MaxxTOR&#xae; (MT) is a supplement that contains PA as the main active ingredient but also contains other synergistic mTOR signaling substances including L-Leucine, Beta-Hydroxy-Beta-Methylbutyrate (HMB), and Vitamin D3.

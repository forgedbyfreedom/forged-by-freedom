PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27294374
Title: Concurrent Training in Rugby Sevens: Effects of High-Intensity Interval Exercises.
Authors: Robineau, Lacome, Piscione, Bigard, Babault
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2017
Topic: concurrent training interference

ABSTRACT:
To assess the impact of 2 high-intensity interval-training (HIT) programs (short interval vs sprint interval training) on muscle strength and aerobic performances in a concurrent training program in amateur rugby sevens players.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27312945
Title: Testosterone therapy increases muscle mass in men with cirrhosis and low testosterone: A randomised controlled trial.
Authors: Sinclair, Grossmann, Hoermann, Angus, Gow
Journal: Journal of hepatology
Year: 2018
Topic: testosterone muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Low testosterone and sarcopenia are common in men with cirrhosis and both are associated with increased mortality. Whether testosterone therapy in cirrhosis improves muscle mass and other outcomes is unknown.

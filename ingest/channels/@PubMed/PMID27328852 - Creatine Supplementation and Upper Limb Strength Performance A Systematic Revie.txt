PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27328852
Title: Creatine Supplementation and Upper Limb Strength Performance: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Lanhers, Pereira, Naughton, Trousselard, Lesage
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2018
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
Creatine is the most widely used supplementation to increase performance in strength; however, the most recent meta-analysis focused specifically on supplementation responses in muscles of the lower limbs without regard to upper limbs.

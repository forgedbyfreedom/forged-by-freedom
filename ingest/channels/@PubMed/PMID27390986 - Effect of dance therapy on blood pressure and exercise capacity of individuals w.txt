PMID: 27390986
Title: Effect of dance therapy on blood pressure and exercise capacity of individuals with hypertension: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Concei&#xe7;&#xe3;o, Neto, do Amaral, Martins-Filho, Oliveira Carvalho
Journal: International journal of cardiology
Year: 2017
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Dance therapy is a less conventional modality of physical activity in cardiovascular rehabilitation. We performed a systematic review and meta-analysis to investigate the effects of dance therapy in hypertensive patients.

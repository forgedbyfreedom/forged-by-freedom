Title: Recovery From Exercise-Induced Muscle Damage: Cold-Water Immersion Versus Whole-Body Cryotherapy.
Authors: Aba&#xef;dia, Lamblin, Delecroix, Leduc, McCall
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2017
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
To compare the effects of cold-water immersion (CWI) and whole-body cryotherapy (WBC) on recovery kinetics after exercise-induced muscle damage.

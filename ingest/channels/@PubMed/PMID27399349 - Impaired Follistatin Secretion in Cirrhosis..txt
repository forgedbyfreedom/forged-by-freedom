PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27399349
Title: Impaired Follistatin Secretion in Cirrhosis.
Authors: Rinnov, Plomgaard, Pedersen, Gluud
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2017
Topic: glucagon exercise metabolism

ABSTRACT:
Follistatin is a liver-derived inhibitor of the muscle-growth inhibitor myostatin. Reduction in acute follistatin release may help explain muscle loss in liver cirrhosis.

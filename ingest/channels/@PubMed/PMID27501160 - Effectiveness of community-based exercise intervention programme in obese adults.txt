Title: Effectiveness of community-based exercise intervention programme in obese adults with metabolic syndrome.
Authors: Chang, Chen, Chien, Lin
Journal: Journal of clinical nursing
Year: 2017
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:

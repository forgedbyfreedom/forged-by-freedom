PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27528454
Title: The appropriateness and persistence of testosterone replacement therapy in Ontario.
Authors: Martins, Yao, Tadrous, Shah, Juurlink
Journal: Pharmacoepidemiology and drug safety
Year: 2017
Topic: testosterone replacement therapy

ABSTRACT:
To examine the concordance between testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) use and established reimbursement criteria, as well as compare the persistence of use among available formulations (injectable, oral, topical gel, trensdermal patch) among elderly men in Ontario, Canada.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27580150
Title: Kinematic Mechanisms of How Power Training Improves Healthy Old Adults' Gait Velocity.
Authors: Beijersbergen, Granacher, G&#xe4;bler, Devita, Hortob&#xe1;gyi
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2017
Topic: detraining muscle loss

ABSTRACT:
Slow gait predicts many adverse clinical outcomes in old adults, but the mechanisms of how power training can minimize the age-related loss of gait velocity is unclear. We examined the effects of 10 wk of lower extremity power training and detraining on healthy old adults' lower extremity muscle power and gait kinematics.

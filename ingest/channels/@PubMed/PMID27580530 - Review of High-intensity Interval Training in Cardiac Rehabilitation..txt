PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27580530
Title: Review of High-intensity Interval trening in Cardiac Rehabilitation.
Authors: Ito, Mizoguchi, Saeki
Journal: Internal medicine (Tokyo, Japan)
Year: 2017
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
For the secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease, comprehensive cardiac rehabilitation is required. This involves optimal medical therapy, education on nutrition and exercise therapy, and smoking cessation. Of these, efficient exercise therapy is a key factor. A highly effective trening protocol is therefore warranted, which requires a high rate of compliance. Although moderate-intensity continuous treninGHas been the main trening regimen recommended in cardiac rehabilitation guidelines, high-intensity interval treninGHas been reported to be more effective in the clinical and experimental setting from the standpoint of peak oxygen uptake and central and peripheral adaptations. In this review, we illustrate the scientific evidence for high-intensity interval trening. We then verify this evidence and discuss its significance and the remaining issues.

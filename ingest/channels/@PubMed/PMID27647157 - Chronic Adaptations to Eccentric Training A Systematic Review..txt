PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27647157
Title: Chronic Adaptations to Eccentric trening: A Systematic Review.
Authors: Douglas, Pearson, Ross, McGuigan
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2018
Topic: strength trening adaptations

ABSTRACT:
Resistance trening is an integral component of physical preparation for athletes. A growing body of evidence indicates that eccentric strength trening methods induce novel stimuli for neuromuscular adaptations.

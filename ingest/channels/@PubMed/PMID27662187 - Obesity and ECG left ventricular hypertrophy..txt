PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27662187
Title: Obesity and ECG left ventricular hypertrophy.
Authors: Muiesan, Salvetti, Di Castelnuovo, Paini, Assanelli
Journal: Journal of hypertension
Year: 2017
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Our aim was to investigate the prevalence and the prognostic significance for fatal and nonfatal cerebrovascular and cardiovascular events of different ECG criteria for left ventricular hypertrophy (LVH) in normal weight, overweight and obese patients in an adult Italian population.

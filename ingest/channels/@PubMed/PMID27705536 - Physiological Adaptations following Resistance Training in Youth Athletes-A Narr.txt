Title: Physiological Adaptations following Resistance trening in Youth Athletes-A Narrative Review.
Authors: Legerlotz, Marzilger, Bohm, Arampatzis
Journal: Pediatric exercise science
Year: 2017
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
To understand the mechanisms for the effects of resistance trening on functional parameters, and to assess the injury risk of the involved tissues, it is necessary to examine the underlying morphological and structural changes of the respective tissues.

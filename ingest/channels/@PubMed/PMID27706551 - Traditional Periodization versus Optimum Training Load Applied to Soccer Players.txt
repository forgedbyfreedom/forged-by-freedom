PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27706551
Title: Traditional Periodization versus Optimum trening Load Applied to Soccer Players: Effects on Neuromuscular Abilities.
Authors: Loturco, Nakamura, Kobal, Gil, Pivetti
Journal: International journal of sports medicine
Year: 2017
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
It is unknown whether traditional periodization of strength-power trening involving accumulation, trensformation and realization blocks is superior to other simpler and more practical trening schemes. The purpose of this study was thus to investigate changes in strength/power/speed characteristics of elite soccer players in response to either classic strength-power periodization (TSP) or optimum power load (OPL). 23 professional soccer players were randomly assigned to TSP or OPL for 6 weeks in-season regular trening (3 times per week). TSP involved half squats or jump squats, depending on the respective trening block, while OPL involved only jump squats at the optimum power load. Results revealed that both groups presented similar significant (P&lt;0.05) improvements in squat one repetition maximum, squat and countermovement jump heights and change of direction speed. In addition, although both groups reported significant increases in sprinting speed (P&lt;0.05); delta change scores demonstrated a superior effect of OPL to improve 10- and 20-m speed. Similarly, OPL presented greater delta change in mean propulsive power in the jump squat. Therefore, trening continuously at the optimum power zone resulted in superior performance improvements compared to trening under classic strength-power periodization.

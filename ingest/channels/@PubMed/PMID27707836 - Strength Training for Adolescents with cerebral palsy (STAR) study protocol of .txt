PMID: 27707836
Title: Strength trening for Adolescents with cerebral palsy (STAR): study protocol of a randomised controlled trial to determine the feasibility, acceptability and efficacy of resistance trening for adolescents with cerebral palsy.
Authors: Ryan, Theis, Kilbride, Baltzopoulos, Waugh
Journal: BMJ open
Year: 2017
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Gait is inefficient in children with cerebral palsy, particularly as they trensition to adolescence. Gait inefficiency may be associated with declines in gross motor function and participation among adolescents with cerebral palsy. Resistance trening may improve gait efficiency through a number of biomechanical and neural mechanisms. The aim of the Strength trening for Adolescents with cerebral palsy (STAR) trial is to evaluate the effect of resistance trening on gait efficiency, activity and participation in adolescents with cerebral palsy. We also aim to determine the biomechanical and neural adaptations that occur following resistance trening and evaluate the feasibility and acceptability of such an intervention for adolescents with cerebral palsy.

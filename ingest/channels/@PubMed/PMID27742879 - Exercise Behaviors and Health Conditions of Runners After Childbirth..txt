PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27742879
Title: Exercise Behaviors and Health Conditions of Runners After Childbirth.
Authors: Blyholder, Chumanov, Carr, Heiderscheit
Journal: Sports health
Year: 2019
Topic: postpartum return exercise

ABSTRACT:
With a recent increase in running popularity, more women choose to run during and after pregnancy. Little research has examined exercise behaviors and postpartum health conditions of runners.

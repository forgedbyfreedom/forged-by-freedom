PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27747795
Title: Oxidative Stress in Female Athletes Using Combined Oral Contraceptives.
Authors: Cauci, Buligan, Marangone, Francescato
Journal: Sports medicine - open
Year: 2020
Topic: oral contraceptives athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Oxidative stress in female athletes is understudied. We investigated oxidative stress in sportswomen of different disciplines according to combined oral contraceptive (OC) use and lifestyle/alimentary habits.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27748470
Title: Exercise trening modalities in chronic heart failure: does high intensity aerobic interval trening make the difference?
Authors: Giallauria, Smart, Cittadini, Vigorito
Journal: Monaldi archives for chest disease = Archivio Monaldi per le malattie del torace
Year: 2018
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Exercise trening (ET) is strongly recommended in patients with chronic heart failure (CHF). Moderate-intensity aerobic continuous ET is the best established trening modality in CHF patients. In the last decade, however, high-intensity interval exercise trening (HIIT) has aroused considerable interest in cardiac rehabilitation community. Basically, HIIT consists of repeated bouts of high-intensity exercise alternated with recovery periods. In CHF patients, HIIT exerts larger improvements in exercise capacity compared to moderate-continuous ET. These results are intriguing, mostly considering that better functional capacity trenslates into an improvement of symptoms and quality of life. Notably, HIIT did not reveal major safety issues; although CHF patients should be clinically stable, have had recent exposure to at least regular moderate-intensity exercise, and appropriate supervision and monitoring during and after the exercise session are mandatory. The impact of HIIT on cardiac dimensions and function and on endothelial function remains uncertain. HIIT should not replace other trening modalities in heart failure but should rather complement them. Combining and tailoring different ET modalities according to each patient's baseline clinical characteristics (i.e. exercise capacity, personal needs, preferences and goals) seem the most astute approach to exercise prescription.

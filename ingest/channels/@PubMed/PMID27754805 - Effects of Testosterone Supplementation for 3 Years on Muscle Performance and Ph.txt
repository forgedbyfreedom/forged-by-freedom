PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27754805
Title: Effects of Testosterone Supplementation for 3 Years on Muscle Performance and Physical Function in Older Men.
Authors: Storer, Basaria, Traustadottir, Harman, Pencina
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2017
Topic: testosterone muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Findings of studies of testosterone's effects on muscle strength and physical function in older men have been inconsistent; its effects on muscle power and fatigability have not been studied.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27782114
Title: Less-than-expected weight loss in normal-weight women undergoing caloric restriction and exercise is accompanied by preservation of fat-free mass and metabolic adaptations.
Authors: Koehler, De Souza, Williams
Journal: European journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2018
Topic: metabolic adaptation weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Normal-weight women frequently restrict their caloric intake and exercise, but little is known about the effects on body weight, body composition and metabolic adaptations in this population.

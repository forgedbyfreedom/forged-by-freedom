PMID: 27801598
Title: Eccentric resistance trening increases and retains maximal strength, muscle endurance, and hypertrophy in trened men.
Authors: Coratella, Schena
Journal: Applied physiology, nutrition, and metabolism = Physiologie appliquee, nutrition et metabolisme
Year: 2017
Topic: trening volume muscle growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone

ABSTRACT:
The aim of the present study was to evaluate the effects of different resistance trening protocols on muscle strength, endurance, and hypertrophy after trening and detrening. Thirty-four resistance-trened males were randomized in concentric-only (CONC), eccentric-only (ECC), traditional concentric-eccentric (TRAD) bench press resistance trening or control group. The trening volume was equalized among the intervention groups. Bench press of 1-repetition maximum (1RM)/body mass, maximum number of repetitions (MNR), and chest circumference were evaluated at the baseline, after 6 weeks of trening, and after 6 weeks of detrening. All intervention groups reported significant 1RM/body mass increases after trening (CONC baseline: 1.04 &#xb1; 0.06, post-trening: 1.12 &#xb1; 0.08, p &lt; 0.05; ECC baseline: 1.08 &#xb1; 0.04, post-trening: 1.15 &#xb1; 0.05, p &lt; 0.05; TRAD baseline: 1.06 &#xb1; 0.08, post-trening: 1.11 &#xb1; 0.10, p &lt; 0.05). After detrening, only ECC retained 1RM/body mass above the baseline (1.17 &#xb1; 0.07, p &lt; 0.05), while CONC and TRAD returned to baseline values. Only ECC improved and retained MNR (baseline: 22 &#xb1; 3; post-trening: 25 &#xb1; 3, and post-detrening: 25 &#xb1; 4, p &lt; 0.05 compared with baseline) and chest circumference (baseline: 98.3 &#xb1; 2.4 cm, post-trening: 101.7 &#xb1; 2.2 cm and post-detrening: 100.7 &#xb1; 2.3 cm. p &lt; 0.05 compared with baseline), while no significant changes occurred in both CONC and TRAD. The incorporation of eccentric trening can be recommended for counteracting the negative effects of detrening or forced physical inactivity.

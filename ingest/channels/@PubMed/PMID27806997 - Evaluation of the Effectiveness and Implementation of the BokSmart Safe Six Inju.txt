PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27806997
Title: Evaluation of the Effectiveness and Implementation of the BokSmart Safe Six Injury Prevention Programme: a study protocol.
Authors: Sewry, Verhagen, Lambert, van Mechelen, Brown
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2018
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
The injury burden in rugby union ('rugby') is high. While exercise-based injury prevention programmes have successfully reduced injuries in other sports such as football, there is minimal research on this topic in rugby union.

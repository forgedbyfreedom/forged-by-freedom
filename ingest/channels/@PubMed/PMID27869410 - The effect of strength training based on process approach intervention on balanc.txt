PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27869410
Title: The effect of strength training based on process approach intervention on balance of children with developmental coordination disorder.
Authors: Kordi, Sohrabi, Saberi Kakhki, Attarzadeh Hossini
Journal: Archivos argentinos de pediatria
Year: 2018
Topic: strength training adaptations

ABSTRACT:
Balance is one of the main problems of children with developmental coordination disorder (DCD). According to process-oriented approach, besides strength training, neuromuscular adaptations can improve balance.

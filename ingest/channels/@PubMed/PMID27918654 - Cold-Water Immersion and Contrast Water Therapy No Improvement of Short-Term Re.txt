PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27918654
Title: Cold-Water Immersion and Contrast Water Therapy: No Improvement of Short-Term Recovery After Resistance trening.
Authors: Argus, Broatch, Petersen, Polman, Bishop
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2018
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
An athlete's ability to recover quickly is important when there is limited time between trening and competition. As such, recovery strategies are commonly used to expedite the recovery process.

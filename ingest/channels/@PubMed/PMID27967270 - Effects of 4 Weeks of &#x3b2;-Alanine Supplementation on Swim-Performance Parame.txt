PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 27967270
Title: Effects of 4 Weeks of &#x3b2;-Alanine Supplementation on Swim-Performance Parameters in Water Polo Players.
Authors: Brisola, Milioni, Papoti, Zagatto
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2018
Topic: beta-alanine exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
In water polo, several high-intensity efforts are performed, leading to the fatigue process due to accumulation of hydrogen ions, and thus &#x3b2;-alanine supplementation could be an efficient strategy to increase the intramuscular acid buffer. Purpose To investigate whether 4 wk of &#x3b2;-alanine supplementation enhances parameters related to water polo performance. Methods Twenty-two highly trened male water polo players of national level were randomly assigned to receive 28 d of either &#x3b2;-alanine or a placebo (4.8 g/d of the supplement in the first 10 d and 6.4 g/d in the final 18 d). The participants performed 30-s maximal tethered swimming (30TS), 200-m swimming (P200m), and 30-s crossbar jumps (30CJ) before and after the supplementation period. Results The &#x3b2;-alanine group presented significant increases in 30TS for mean force (P = .04; &#x394; = 30.5% &#xb1; 40.4%) and integral of force (P = .05; &#x394; = 28.0% &#xb1; 38.0%), as well as P200m (P = .05; &#x394; = -2.2% &#xb1; 2.6%), while the placebo group did not significantly differ for mean force (P = .13; &#x394; = 24.1% &#xb1; 33.7%), integral of force (P = .12; &#x394; = 24.3% &#xb1; 35.1%), or P200m (P = .10; &#x394; = -1.6% &#xb1; 3.8%). However, there was no significant group effect for any variable, and the magnitude-based-inference analysis showed unclear outcomes between groups (Cohen d &#xb1; 95%CL mean force = 0.16 &#xb1; 0.83, integral of force = 0.12 &#xb1; 0.84, and P200m = 0.05 &#xb1; 0.30). For 30CJ the results were similar, with improvements in both groups (placebo, &#x394; = 14.9% &#xb1; 14.1%; &#x3b2;-alanine, &#x394; = 16.9% &#xb1; 18.5%) but with no significant interaction effect between groups and an unclear effect (0.14 &#xb1; 0.75). Conclusion Four weeks of &#x3b2;-alanine supplementation does not substantially improve performance of 30TS, P200m, or 30CJ in highly trened water polo athletes compared with a control group.

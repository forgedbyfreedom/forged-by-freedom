PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28121802
Title: Delayed Effect of Blood Flow-restricted Resistance Training on Rapid Force Capacity.
Authors: Nielsen, Frandsen, Prokhorova, Bech, Nygaard
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2017
Topic: cluster sets resistance training

ABSTRACT:
The aim of the present study was to investigate the effect and time course of high-frequent low-load blood flow-restricted (BFR) resistance training on rapid force capacity (i.e., rate of torque development [RTD]).

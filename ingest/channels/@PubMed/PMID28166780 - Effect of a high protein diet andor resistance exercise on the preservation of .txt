PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28166780
Title: Effect of a high protein diet and/or resistance exercise on the preservation of fat free mass during weight loss in overweight and obese older adults: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Verreijen, Engberink, Memelink, van der Plas, Visser
Journal: Nutrition journal
Year: 2018
Topic: fat loss preservation muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Intentional weight loss in obese older adults is a risk factor for accelerated muscle mass loss. We investigated whether a high protein diet and/or resistance exercise preserves fat free mass (FFM) during weight loss in overweight and obese older adults.

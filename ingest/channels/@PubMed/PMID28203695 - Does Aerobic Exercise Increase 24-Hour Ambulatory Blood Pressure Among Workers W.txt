PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28203695
Title: Does Aerobic Exercise Increase 24-Hour Ambulatory Blood Pressure Among Workers With High Occupational Physical Activity?-A RCT.
Authors: Korsh&#xf8;j, Krause, Clays, S&#xf8;gaard, Krustrup
Journal: American journal of hypertension
Year: 2018
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
High occupational physical activity (OPA) increases cardiovascular risk and aerobic exercise has been recommended for reducing this risk. This paper investigates the effects of an aerobic exercise intervention on 24-hour ambulatory blood pressure (ABP) among cleaners with high OPA.

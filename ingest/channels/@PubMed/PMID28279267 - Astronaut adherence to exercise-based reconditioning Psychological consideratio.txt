PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28279267
Title: Astronaut adherence to exercise-based reconditioning: Psychological considerations and future directions.
Authors: McKay, Standage
Journal: Musculoskeletal science &amp; practice
Year: 2018
Topic: motivation exercise adherence

ABSTRACT:
Exercise-based reconditioning is essential for mitigating the negative physiological and functional effects of spaceflight. Astronaut adherence to prescribed reconditioning programmes is imperative, but there has been limited research in this area.

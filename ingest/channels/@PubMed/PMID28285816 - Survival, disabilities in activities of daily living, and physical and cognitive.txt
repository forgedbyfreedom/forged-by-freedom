PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28285816
Title: Survival, disabilities in activities of daily living, and physical and cognitive functioning among the oldest-old in China: a cohort study.
Authors: Zeng, Feng, Hesketh, Christensen, Vaupel
Journal: Lancet (London, England)
Year: 2018
Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The oldest-old (those aged &#x2265;80 years) are the most rapidly growing age group globally, and are most in need of health care and assistance. We aimed to assess changes in mortality, disability in activities of daily living, and physical and cognitive functioning among oldest-old individuals between 1998 and 2008.

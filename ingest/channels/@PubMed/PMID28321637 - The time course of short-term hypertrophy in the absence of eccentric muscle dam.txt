PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28321637
Title: The time course of short-term hypertrophy in the absence of eccentric muscle damage.
Authors: Stock, Mota, DeFranco, Grue, Jacobo
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2017
Topic: eccentric trening muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
It has been proposed that the increase in skeletal muscle mass observed during the initial weeks of initiating a resistance trening program is concomitant with eccentric muscle damage and edema.

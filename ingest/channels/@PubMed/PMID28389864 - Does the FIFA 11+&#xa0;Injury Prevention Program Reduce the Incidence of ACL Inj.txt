PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28389864
Title: Does the FIFA 11+&#xa0;Injury Prevention Program Reduce the Incidence of ACL Injury in Male Soccer Players?
Authors: Silvers-Granelli, Bizzini, Arundale, Mandelbaum, Snyder-Mackler
Journal: Clinical orthopaedics and related research
Year: 2017
Topic: ACL injury prevention female

ABSTRACT:
The FIFA 11+&#xa0;injury prevention program has been shown to decrease the risk of soccer injuries in men and women. The program has also been shown to decrease time loss resulting from injury. However, previous studies have not specifically investigated how the program might impact the rate of anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury in male soccer players.

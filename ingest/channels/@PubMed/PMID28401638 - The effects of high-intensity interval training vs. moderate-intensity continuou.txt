PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28401638
Title: The effects of high-intensity interval training vs. moderate-intensity continuous training on body composition in overweight and obese adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Wewege, van den Berg, Ward, Keech
Journal: Obesity reviews : an official journal of the International Association for the Study of Obesity
Year: 2018
Topic: high intensity interval training

ABSTRACT:
The objective of this study is to compare the effects of high-intensity interval training (HIIT) and moderate-intensity continuous training (MICT) for improvements in body composition in overweight and obese adults.

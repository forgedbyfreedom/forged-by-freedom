PMID: 28463902
Title: Practicing the Test Produces Strength Equivalent to Higher Volume Training.
Authors: Mattocks, Buckner, Jessee, Dankel, Mouser
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2018
Topic: training volume muscle growth

ABSTRACT:
To determine if muscle growth is important for increasing muscle strength or if changes in strength can be entirely explained from practicing the strength test.

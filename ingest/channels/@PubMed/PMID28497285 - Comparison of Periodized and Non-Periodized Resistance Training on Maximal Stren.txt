PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28497285
Title: Comparison of Periodized and Non-Periodized Resistance trening on Maximal Strength: A Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Williams, Tolusso, Fedewa, Esco
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2018
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
Periodization is a logical method of organizing trening into sequential phases and cyclical time periods in order to increase the potential for achieving specific performance goals while minimizing the potential for overtrening. Periodized resistance trening plans are proposed to be superior to non-periodized trening plans for enhancing maximal strength.

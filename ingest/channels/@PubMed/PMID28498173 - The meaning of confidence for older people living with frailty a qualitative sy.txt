PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28498173
Title: The meaning of confidence for older people living with frailty: a qualitative systematic review.
Authors: Underwood, Burrows, Gegg, Latour, Kent
Journal: JBI database of systematic reviews and implementation reports
Year: 2018
Topic: confidence athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
In many countries, the oldest old (those aged 85 years and older) are now the fastest growing proportion of the total population. This oldest population will increasingly be living with the clinical condition of frailty. Frailty syndromes negatively impact on the person as they do the healthcare systems supporting them. Within healthcare literature "loss of confidence" is occasionally connected to older people living with frailty, but ambiguously described. Understanding the concept of confidence within the context of frailty could inform interventions to meet this growing challenge.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28501804
Title: Exercise and other non-pharmaceutical interventions for cancer-related fatigue in patients during or after cancer treatment: a systematic review incorporating an indirect-comparisons meta-analysis.
Authors: Hilfiker, Meichtry, Eicher, Nilsson Balfe, Knols
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2018
Topic: cognitive behavioral therapy athletes

ABSTRACT:
To assess the relative effects of different types of exercise and other non-pharmaceutical interventions on cancer-related fatigue (CRF) in patients during and after cancer treatment.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28514618
Title: Aerobic or Resistance Exercise, or Both, in Dieting Obese Older Adults.
Authors: Villareal, Aguirre, Gurney, Waters, Sinacore
Journal: The New England journal of medicine
Year: 2017
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Obesity causes frailty in older adults; however, weight loss might accelerate age-related loss of muscle and bone mass and resultant sarcopenia and osteopenia.

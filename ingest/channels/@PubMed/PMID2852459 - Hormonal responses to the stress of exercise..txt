Title: Hormonal responses to the stress of exercise.
Authors: Luger, Deuster, Gold, Loriaux, Chrousos
Journal: Advances in experimental medicine and biology
Year: 1989
Topic: cortisol exercise stress

ABSTRACT:
Exercise stimulates the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal and renin-angiotensin-aldosterone axes and causes growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone and prolactin release in an intensity-dependent fashion. Activation of the different endocrine responses examined occur at different exercise intensities. Subjects of differing trening status respond similarly to matched exercise intensities. Highly trened subjects have elevated basal concentrations of ACTH and cortisol and a blunted response to exogenous corticotropin releasinGHormone. These data arecompatible with mild sustained hypercortisolism in highly trened runners.

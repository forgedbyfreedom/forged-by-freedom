PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28549180
Title: Relationships Among Self-perception of Aging, Physical Functioning, and Self-efficacy in Late Life.
Authors: Tovel, Carmel, Raveis
Journal: The journals of gerontology. Series B, Psychological sciences and social sciences
Year: 2019
Topic: self efficacy sports performance

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of the current study was to examine the longitudinal relationship between 2 central concepts in the study of subjective well-being in old age-self-perception of aging (SPA) and physical functioning, and to assess the mediating role of self-efficacy beliefs in this relationship.

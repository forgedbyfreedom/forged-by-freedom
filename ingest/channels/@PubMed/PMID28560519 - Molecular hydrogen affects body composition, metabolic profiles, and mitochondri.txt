PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28560519
Title: Molecular hydrogen affects body composition, metabolic profiles, and mitochondrial function in middle-aged overweight women.
Authors: Korovljev, Trivic, Drid, Ostojic
Journal: Irish journal of medical science
Year: 2018
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
Molecular hydrogen (H2) effectively treats obesity-related disorders in animal models, yet no studies have investigated the effectiveness and safety of H2 for improving biomarkers of obesity in humans.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28561278
Title: The effects of testosterone on body composition in obese men are not sustained after cessation of testosterone treatment.
Authors: Ng Tang Fui, Hoermann, Zajac, Grossmann
Journal: Clinical endocrinology
Year: 2018
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
Testosterone treatment in obese dieting men augments the diet-associated loss of fat mass, but protects against loss of lean mass. We assessed whether body composition changes are maintained following withdrawal of testosterone treatment.

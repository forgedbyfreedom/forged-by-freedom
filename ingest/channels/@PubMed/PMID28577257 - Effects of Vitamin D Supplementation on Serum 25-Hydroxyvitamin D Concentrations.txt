PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28577257
Title: Effects of Vitamin D Supplementation on Serum 25-Hydroxyvitamin D Concentrations and Physical Performance in Athletes: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials.
Authors: Farrokhyar, Sivakumar, Savage, Koziarz, Jamshidi
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2018
Topic: vitamin D athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
There is currently no systematic review examining the effects of vitamin D supplementation among athletes. A rigorous systematic review and meta-analysis is important to provide a balanced view of current knowledge on the effect of vitamin D on serum 25-hydroxyvitamin D [25(OH)D] concentrations and physical performance.

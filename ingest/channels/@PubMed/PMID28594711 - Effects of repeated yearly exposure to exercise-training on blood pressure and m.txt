PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28594711
Title: Effects of repeated yearly exposure to exercise-trening on blood pressure and metabolic syndrome evolution.
Authors: Morales-Palomo, Ramirez-Jimenez, Ortega, Lopez-Galindo, Fernandez-Martin
Journal: Journal of hypertension
Year: 2018
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
To study if repeated yearly trening programs consolidate the trensient blood pressure (BP) improvements of one exercise program into durable adaptations.

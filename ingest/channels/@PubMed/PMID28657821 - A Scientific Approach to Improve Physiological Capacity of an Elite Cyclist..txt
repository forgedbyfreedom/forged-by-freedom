PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28657821
Title: A Scientific Approach to Improve Physiological Capacity of an Elite Cyclist.
Authors: R&#xf8;nnestad, Hansen
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2018
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
Previous studies in endurance athletes have indicated that block periodization (BP) can be a good alternative to the more traditional organization of trening despite the fact that the total volume and intensity of the trening are similar. However, these studies usually last only 4-12&#xa0;wk. The aim of the present single-case study was to investigate the consequences of 58&#xa0;wk with systematic BP of low-intensity trening (LIT), moderate-intensity trening (MIT), and high-intensity interval trening (HIT) including incorporation of heavy strength trening. It is important that a maintenance stimulus on the nonprioritized trening modalities was added in the different trening blocks. Performance-related variables were tested regularly during the intervention. The studied cyclist started with a maximal oxygen uptake (VO2max) of 73.8&#xa0;mL&#x2009;&#xb7;&#x2009;kg-1&#x2009;&#xb7;&#x2009;min-1, peak aerobic power (Wmax) of 6.14&#xa0;W/kg, and a power output at 3&#xa0;mmol/L blood lactate concentration (Power3la-) of 3.6&#xa0;W/kg. total Trening volume during the 58-wk intervention was 678&#xa0;h, of which 452&#xa0;h were LIT (67%), 124&#xa0;h were MIT (18%), 69&#xa0;h were HIT (10%), and 34&#xa0;h were heavy strength trening (5%). The weekly trening volume had a large range depending on the focus of the trening block. After the intervention the cyclist's VO2max was 87&#xa0;mL&#x2009;&#xb7;&#x2009;kg-1&#x2009;&#xb7;&#x2009;min-1, Wmax was 7.35&#xa0;W/kg, and Power3la- was 4.9&#xa0;W/kg. This single case indicates that the present trening program can be a good alternative to the more traditional organization of long-term trening of endurance athletes. However, a general recommendation cannot be given based on this single-case study.

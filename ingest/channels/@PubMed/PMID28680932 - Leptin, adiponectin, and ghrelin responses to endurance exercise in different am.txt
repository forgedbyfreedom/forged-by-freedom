PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28680932
Title: Leptin, adiponectin, and ghrelin responses to endurance exercise in different ambient conditions.
Authors: Laursen, Zak, Shute, Heesch, Dinan
Journal: Temperature (Austin, Tex.)
Year: 2020
Topic: leptin ghrelin appetite exercise

ABSTRACT:
Excessive positive energy balance is a major factor leading to obesity. The ability to alter the appetite-regulatinGHormones leptin, adiponectin, and ghrelin may help decrease excessive energy intake. Exercise and exposure to extreme temperatures can independently affect these appetite-regulatinGHormones.

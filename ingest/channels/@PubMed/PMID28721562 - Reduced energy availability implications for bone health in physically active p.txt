PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28721562
Title: Reduced energy availability: implications for bone health in physically active populations.
Authors: Papageorgiou, Dolan, Elliott-Sale, Sale
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Year: 2018
Topic: bone health female athletes

ABSTRACT:
The present review critically evaluates existing literature on the effects of short- and long-term low energy availability (EA) on bone metabolism and health in physically active individuals.

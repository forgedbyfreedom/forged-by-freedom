PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28723843
Title: No Superior Adaptations to Carbohydrate Periodization in Elite Endurance Athletes.
Authors: Gejl, Thams, Hansen, Rokkedal-Lausch, Plomgaard
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2018
Topic: carbohydrate periodization athletes

ABSTRACT:
The present study investigated the effects of periodic carbohydrate (CHO) restriction on endurance performance and metabolic markers in elite endurance athletes.

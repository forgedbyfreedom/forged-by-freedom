PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28737585
Title: Biomarkers in Sports and Exercise: TrackinGHealth, Performance, and Recovery in Athletes.
Authors: Lee, Fragala, Kavouras, Queen, Pryor
Journal: Journal of strength and conditioning research
Year: 2018
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Biomarker discovery and validation is a critical aim of the medical and scientific community. Research into exercise and diet-related biomarkers aims to improve health, performance, and recovery in military personnel, athletes, and lay persons. Exercise physiology research has identified individual biomarkers for assessinGHealth, performance, and recovery during exercise trening. However, there are few recommendations for biomarker panels for tracking changes in individuals participating in physical activity and exercise trening programs. Our approach was to review the current literature and recommend a collection of validated biomarkers in key categories of health, performance, and recovery that could be used for this purpose. We determined that a comprehensive performance set of biomarkers should include key markers of (a) nutrition and metabolic health, (b) hydration status, (c) muscle status, (d) endurance performance, (e) injury status and risk, and (f) inflammation. Our review will help coaches, clinical sport professionals, researchers, and athletes better understand how to comprehensively monitor physiologic changes, as they design trening cycles that elicit maximal improvements in performance while minimizing overtrening and injury risk.

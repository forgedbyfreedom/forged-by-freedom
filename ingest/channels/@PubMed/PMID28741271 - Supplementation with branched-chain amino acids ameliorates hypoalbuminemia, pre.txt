PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28741271
Title: Supplementation with branched-chain amino acids ameliorates hypoalbuminemia, prevents sarcopenia, and reduces fat accumulation in the skeletal muscles of patients with liver cirrhosis.
Authors: Kitajima, Takahashi, Akiyama, Murayama, Iwane
Journal: Journal of gastroenterology
Year: 2019
Topic: branched chain amino acids muscle

ABSTRACT:
Liver cirrhosis induces marked metabolic disorders, protein-energy malnutrition, and sarcopenia. The objective of the study reported here was to investigate the effects of dietary branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs) on systemic glucose metabolism, skeletal muscle, and prognosis of patients with liver cirrhosis.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28742614
Title: Compression Socks Worn During Flight and Hemostatic Balance in Boston Marathon Runners on Oral Contraceptives.
Authors: Taylor, Zaleski, Ballard, Panza, Fernandez
Journal: Clinical journal of sport medicine : official journal of the Canadian Academy of Sport Medicine
Year: 2018
Topic: oral contraceptives athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the effect of oral contraceptive (OC) use and compression socks on hemostatic activation in women flying cross-country to and from a marathon.

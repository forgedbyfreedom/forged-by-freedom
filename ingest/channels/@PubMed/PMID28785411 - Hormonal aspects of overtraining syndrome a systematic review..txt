PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28785411
Title: Hormonal aspects of overtrening syndrome: a systematic review.
Authors: Cadegiani, Kater
Journal: BMC sports science, medicine &amp; rehabilitation
Year: 2022
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Overtrening syndrome (OTS), functional (FOR) and non-functional overreaching (NFOR) are conditions diagnosed in athletes with decreased performance and fatigue, triggered by metabolic, immune, hormonal and other dysfunctions and resulted from an imbalance between trening stress and proper recovery. Despite previous descriptions, there is a lack of a review that discloses all hormonal findings in OTS/FOR/NFOR. The aim of this systematic review is to evaluate whether and which roles hormones play in OTS/FOR/NFOR.

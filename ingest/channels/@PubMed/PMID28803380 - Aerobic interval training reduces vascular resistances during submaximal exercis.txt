PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28803380
Title: Aerobic interval training reduces vascular resistances during submaximal exercise in obese metabolic syndrome individuals.
Authors: Mora-Rodriguez, Fernandez-Elias, Morales-Palomo, Pallares, Ramirez-Jimenez
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2018
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to determine the effects of high-intensity aerobic interval training (AIT) on exercise hemodynamics in metabolic syndrome (MetS) volunteers.

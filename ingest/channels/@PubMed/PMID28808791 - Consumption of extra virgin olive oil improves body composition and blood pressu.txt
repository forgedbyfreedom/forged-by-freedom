PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28808791
Title: Consumption of extra virgin olive oil improves body composition and blood pressure in women with excess body fat: a randomized, double-blinded, placebo-controlled clinical trial.
Authors: Galv&#xe3;o C&#xe2;ndido, Xavier Valente, da Silva, Gon&#xe7;alves Le&#xe3;o Coelho, Gouveia Peluzio
Journal: European journal of nutrition
Year: 2018
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
Despite the fact that extra virgin olive oil (EVOO) is widely used in obese individuals to treat cardiovascular diseases, the role of EVOO on weight/fat reduction remains unclear. We investigated the effects of energy-restricted diet containing EVOO on body composition and metabolic disruptions related to obesity.

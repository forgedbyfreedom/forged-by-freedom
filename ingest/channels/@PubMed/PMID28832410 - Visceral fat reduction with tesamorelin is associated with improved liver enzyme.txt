PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28832410
Title: Visceral fat reduction with tesamorelin is associated with improved liver enzymes in HIV.
Authors: Fourman, Czerwonka, Feldpausch, Weiss, Mamputu
Journal: AIDS (London, England)
Year: 2018
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
Tesamorelin reduces visceral adipose tissue (VAT) in HIV. We investigated whether reductions in VAT with tesamorelin are associated with changes in alanine aminotrensferase (ALT) and aspartate aminotrensferase (AST).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28911021
Title: Effects of Lifestyle Modification on an Exaggerated Blood Pressure Response to Exercise in Normotensive Females.
Authors: Michishita, Ohta, Ikeda, Jiang, Yamato
Journal: American journal of hypertension
Year: 2018
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
This study was designed to examine the effects of a lifestyle modification on the improvement in an exaggerated systolic blood pressure (SBP) response to exercise in normotensive females.

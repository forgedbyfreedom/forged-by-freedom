PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28971855
Title: Cluster randomised control trial for cricket injury prevention programme (CIPP): a protocol paper.
Authors: Soomro, Chua, Freeston, Ferdinands, Sanders
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2020
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Injury prevention programmes (IPPs) are effective in reducing injuries among adolescent team sports. However, there is no validated cricket-specific IPP despite the high incidence of musculoskeletal injuries among amateur cricketers.

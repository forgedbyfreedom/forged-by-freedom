PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 28975517
Title: The Effects of Hyperoxia on Sea-Level Exercise Performance, trening, and Recovery: A Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Mallette, Stewart, Cheung
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2018
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Acute exercise performance can be limited by arterial hypoxemia, such that hyperoxia may be an ergogenic aid by increasing tissue oxygen availability. Hyperoxia during aromasingle bout of exercise performance has been examined using many test modalities, including time trials (TTs), time to exhaustion (TTE), graded exercise tests (GXTs), and dynamic muscle function tests. Hyperoxia has also been used as a long-term trening stimulus or a recovery intervention between bouts of exercise. However, due to the methodological differences in fraction of inspired oxygen (FiO2), exercise type, trening regime, or recovery protocols, a firm consensus on the effectiveness of hyperoxia as an ergogenic aid for exercise trening or recovery remains unclear.

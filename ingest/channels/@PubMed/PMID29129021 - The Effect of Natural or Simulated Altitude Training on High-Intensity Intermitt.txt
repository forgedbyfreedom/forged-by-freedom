PMID: 29129021
Title: The Effect of Natural or Simulated Altitude Training on High-Intensity Intermittent Running Performance in Team-Sport Athletes: A Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Hamlin, Lizamore, Hopkins
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2018
Topic: altitude training performance

ABSTRACT:
While adaptation to hypoxia at natural or simulated altitude has long been used with endurance athletes, it has only recently gained popularity for team-sport athletes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29135641
Title: The Pediatric Endurance Athlete.
Authors: Solomon, Briskin, Sabatina, Steinhoff
Journal: Current sports medicine reports
Year: 2018
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Youth sports participation numbers continue to grow in the United States. A shift toward sport specialization has caused an increase in sport trening frequency and intensity that places the growing athlete at risk for overtrening, nutritional deficits, and injuries. Individuals who participate in endurance sports are at especially high risk. Youth runners and swimmers are high-risk populations that require special attention to their trening schedules, nutritional intake, and injuries. Appropriate scheduling of trening, dedicating time to rest, and nutrition education can help prevent problems in the endurance athlete.

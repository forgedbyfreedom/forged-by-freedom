PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29141087
Title: Effect of Granulocyte-Macrophage Colony-Stimulating Factor With or Without Supervised Exercise on Walking Performance in Patients With Peripheral Artery Disease: The PROPEL Randomized Clinical Trial.
Authors: McDermott, Ferrucci, Tian, Guralnik, Lloyd-Jones
Journal: JAMA
Year: 2017
Topic: carbohydrate exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
Benefits of granulocyte-macrophage colony-stimulating factor (GM-CSF) for improving walking ability in people with lower extremity peripheral artery disease (PAD) are unclear. Walking exercise may augment the effects of GM-CSF in PAD, since exercise-induced ischemia enhances progenitor cell release and may promote progenitor cell homing to ischemic calf muscle.

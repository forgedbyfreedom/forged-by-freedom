PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29170001
Title: Heightened cortisol response to exercise challenge in women with functional hypothalamic amenorrhea.
Authors: Sanders, Kawwass, Loucks, Berga
Journal: American journal of obstetrics and gynecology
Year: 2018
Topic: cortisol exercise stress

ABSTRACT:
Functional hypothalamic amenorrhea is characterized by anovulation caused by reduced gonadotropin-releasinGHormone drive and is associated with hypercortisolemia that has been linked to heightened hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal reactivity to common psychological and metabolic challenges.

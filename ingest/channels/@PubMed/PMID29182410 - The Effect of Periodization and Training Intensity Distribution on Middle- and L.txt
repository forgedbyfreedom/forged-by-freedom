PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29182410
Title: The Effect of Periodization and trening Intensity Distribution on Middle- and Long-Distance Running Performance: A Systematic Review.
Authors: Kenneally, Casado, Santos-Concejero
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2018
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
This review aimed to examine the current evidence for 3 primary trening intensity distribution types: (1)&#xa0;pyramidal trening, (2)&#xa0;polarized trening, and (3)&#xa0;threshold trening. Where possible, the trening intensity zones relative to the goal race pace, rather than physiological or subjective variables, were calculated. Three electronic databases (PubMed, Scopus, and Web of Science) were searched in May 2017 for original research articles. After analysis of 493 resultant original articles, studies were included if they met the following criteria: (1)&#xa0;Their participants were middle- or long-distance runners; (2)&#xa0;they analyzed trening intensity distribution in the form of observational reports, case studies, or interventions; (3)&#xa0;they were published in peer-reviewed journals; and (4) they analyzed trening programs with a duration of 4 wk or longer. Sixteen studies met the inclusion criteria, which included 6 observational reports, 3 case studies, 6 interventions, and 1 review. According to the results of this analysis, pyramidal and polarized trening are more effective than threshold trening, although the latest is used by some of the best marathon runners in the world. Despite this apparent contradictory finding, this review presents evidence for the organization of trening into zones based on a percentage of goal race pace, which allows for different periodization types to be compatible. This approach requires further development to assess whether specific percentages above and below race pace are key to inducing optimal changes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29209633
Title: Protein Intake, Especially Vegetable Protein Intake, Is Associated with Higher Skeletal Muscle Mass in Elderly Patients with Type 2 Diabetes.
Authors: Miki, Hashimoto, Matsumoto, Ushigome, Fukuda
Journal: Journal of diabetes research
Year: 2018
Topic: protein intake muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Protein intake is important for maintaining muscle mass in general population. However, it remains to be elucidated the association between dietary protein intake and skeletal muscle mass in elderly patients with type 2 diabetes.

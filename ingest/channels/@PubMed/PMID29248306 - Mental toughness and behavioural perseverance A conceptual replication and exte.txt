PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29248306
Title: Mental toughness and behavioural perseverance: A conceptual replication and extension.
Authors: Giles, Goods, Warner, Quain, Peeling
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2018
Topic: mental toughness athletes

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to conduct a conceptual replication of the proposition that mental toughness is associated positively with behavioural perseverance.

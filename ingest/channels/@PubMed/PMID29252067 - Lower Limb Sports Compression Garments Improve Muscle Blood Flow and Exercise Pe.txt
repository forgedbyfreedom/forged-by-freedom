PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29252067
Title: Lower Limb Sports Compression Garments Improve Muscle Blood Flow and Exercise Performance During Repeated-Sprint Cycling.
Authors: Broatch, Bishop, Halson
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2018
Topic: flow state athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Evidence supporting the use of lower-limb compression garments during repeated-sprint exercise (RSE) with short rest periods, where performance will rely heavily on aerobic metabolism, is lacking.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29253326
Title: Tendinous tissue properties after short- and long-term functional overload: Differences between controls, 12&#xa0;weeks and 4&#xa0;years of resistance trening.
Authors: Massey, Balshaw, Maden-Wilkinson, Folland
Journal: Acta physiologica (Oxford, England)
Year: 2019
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The potential for tendinous tissues to adapt to functional overload, especially after several years of exposure to heavy-resistance trening, is largely unexplored. This study compared the morphological and mechanical characteristics of the patellar tendon and knee extensor tendon-aponeurosis complex between young men exposed to long-term (4&#xa0;years; n&#xa0;=&#xa0;16), short-term (12&#xa0;weeks; n&#xa0;=&#xa0;15) and no (untrened controls; n&#xa0;=&#xa0;39) functional overload in the form of heavy-resistance trening.

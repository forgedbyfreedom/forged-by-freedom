PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29272072
Title: The effect on glycaemic control of low-volume high-intensity interval training versus endurance training in individuals with type 2 diabetes.
Authors: Winding, Munch, Iepsen, Van Hall, Pedersen
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2019
Topic: type 2 diabetes exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
To evaluate whether high-intensity interval training (HIIT) with a lower time commitment can be as effective as endurance training (END) on glycaemic control, physical fitness and body composition in individuals with type 2 diabetes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29282213
Title: Guidance for sports injury surveillance: the 20-year influence of the Australian Sports Injury Data Dictionary.
Authors: Finch, Staines
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2019
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Injury prevention requires information about how, why, where and when injuries occur. The Australian Sports Injury Data Dictionary (ASIDD) was developed to guide sports injury data collection and reporting. Sports Medicine Australia (SMA) disseminated associated data collection forms and an online tool to practitioners and the sports community. This paper assesses the long-term value, usefulness and relevance of the ASIDD and SMA tools.

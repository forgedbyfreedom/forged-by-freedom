PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29300431
Title: Effects of Protein, Essential Amino Acids, B-Hydroxy B-Methylbutyrate, Creatine, Dehydroepiandrosterone and Fatty Acid Supplementation on Muscle Mass, Muscle Strength and Physical Performance in Older People Aged 60 Years and Over. A Systematic Review on the Literature.
Authors: Beaudart, Rabenda, Simmons, Geerinck, Araujo De Carvalho
Journal: The journal of nutrition, health &amp; aging
Year: 2019
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
The objective of this study was to perform a systematic review to investigate the effects protein, essential amino acids (EAA), &#x3b2;-hydroxy &#x3b2;-methylbutyrate (HMB), creatine, dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA) and fatty acid supplementation on muscle mass, muscle strength and physical performance of elderly subjects.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29364948
Title: MRI reveals menstrually-related muscle edema that negatively affects athletic agility in young women.
Authors: Sawai, Tochigi, Kavaliova, Zaboronok, Warashina
Journal: PloS one
Year: 2018
Topic: progesterone athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
About 10% of Japanese female athletes are afflicted by menstrually-related edema, mainly in the lower limbs, and, with few studies on this problem, the effect on performance remains unclear.

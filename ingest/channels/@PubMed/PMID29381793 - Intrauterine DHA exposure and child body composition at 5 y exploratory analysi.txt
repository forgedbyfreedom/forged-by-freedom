PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29381793
Title: Intrauterine DHA exposure and child body composition at 5 y: exploratory analysis of a randomized controlled trial of prenatal DHA supplementation.
Authors: Hidaka, Thodosoff, Kerling, Hull, Colombo
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2019
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
Observational studies find associations between maternal docosahexaenoic acid (DHA) and greater fat-free mass and lower percentage of body fat, but randomized trials of prenatal DHA supplementation have not found significant intent-to-treat effects on childhood body composition.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29402509
Title: Effects of aerobic or resistance exercise trening on cardiovascular autonomic function of subjects with type 2 diabetes: A pilot study.
Authors: Bellavere, Cacciatori, Bacchi, Gemma, Raimondo
Journal: Nutrition, metabolism, and cardiovascular diseases : NMCD
Year: 2019
Topic: cardiovascular adaptations exercise

ABSTRACT:
Both aerobic (AER) and resistance (RES) trening improve metabolic control in patients with type 2 diabetes (T2DM). However, information on the effects of these trening modalities on cardiovascular autonomic control is limited. Our aim was to compare the effects of AER and RES trening on cardiovascular autonomic function in these subjects.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29463497
Title: Psychological interventions used to reduce sports injuries: a systematic review of real-world effectiveness.
Authors: Gledhill, Forsdyke, Murray
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2018
Topic: goal setting sports psychology

ABSTRACT:
To systematically review studies examining the role of psychological interventions in injury prevention. The primary research question was: What is the real-world effectiveness of psychological intervention in preventing sports injuries?

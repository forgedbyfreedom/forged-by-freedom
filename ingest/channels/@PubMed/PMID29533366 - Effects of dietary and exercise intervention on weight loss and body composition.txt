PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29533366
Title: Effects of dietary and exercise intervention on weight loss and body composition in obese postmenopausal women: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Cheng, Hsu, Liu
Journal: Menopause (New York, N.Y.)
Year: 2019
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
This study examined the effects of dietary and exercise interventions on weight loss and body composition in overweight/obese peri- and postmenopausal women.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29634648
Title: Does Lifestyle Exercise After a Cardiac Event Improve Metabolic Syndrome Profile in Older Adults?
Authors: Wright, Moore-Schiltz, Sattar, Josephson, Moore
Journal: The Journal of cardiovascular nursing
Year: 2019
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Exercise is a common recommendation to reduce the risk factors of metabolic syndrome, yet there are limited data on the influence of lifestyle exercise after cardiac events on metabolic syndrome factors.

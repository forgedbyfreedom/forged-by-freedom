PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29715073
Title: Gluteal Loading Versus Sham Exercises to Improve Pain and Dysfunction in Postmenopausal Women with Greater Trochanteric Pain Syndrome: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Ganderton, Semciw, Cook, Moreira, Pizzari
Journal: Journal of women's health (2002)
Year: 2018
Topic: menopause exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The aim of the current study was to determine the effects of education with targeted or sham exercise on pain and function in postmenopausal women with greater trochanteric pain syndrome (GTPS).

PMID: 29753113
Title: Cardiovascular Responses During Resistance Exercise in Patients With Parkinson Disease.
Authors: Miyasato, Silva-Batista, Pe&#xe7;anha, Low, de Mello
Journal: PM &amp; R : the journal of injury, function, and rehabilitation
Year: 2019
Topic: cardiovascular adaptations exercise

ABSTRACT:
Patients with Parkinson disease (PD) present cardiovascular autonomic dysfunction that impairs blood pressure control. However, cardiovascular responses during resistance exercise are unknown in these patients.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29777475
Title: Effects of resistance training, detraining, and retraining on strength and functional capacity in elderly.
Authors: Sakugawa, Moura, Orssatto, Bezerra, Cadore
Journal: Aging clinical and experimental research
Year: 2019
Topic: detraining muscle loss

ABSTRACT:
The interruption of training (detraining) results in loss of the gains acquired. Partial retention could occur after detraining, and variation in training stimuli may optimize retraining adaptations.

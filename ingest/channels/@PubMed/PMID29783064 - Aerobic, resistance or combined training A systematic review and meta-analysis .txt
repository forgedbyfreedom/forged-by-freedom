PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29783064
Title: Aerobic, resistance or combined trening: A systematic review and meta-analysis of exercise to reduce cardiovascular risk in adults with metabolic syndrome.
Authors: Wewege, Thom, Rye, Parmenter
Journal: Atherosclerosis
Year: 2019
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Exercise is beneficial to individuals with metabolic syndrome (MetS). An understudied group, who represent the majority of the MetS population, are individuals who have not developed diabetes. This review examined aerobic, resistance and combined (aerobic&#xa0;+&#xa0;resistance) exercise for cardiovascular risk factors in MetS without diabetes.

PMID: 29852092
Title: High-frequency resistance trening does not promote greater muscular adaptations compared to low frequencies in young untrened men.
Authors: Barcelos, Damas, N&#xf3;brega, Ugrinowitsch, Lixandr&#xe3;o
Journal: European journal of sport science
Year: 2018
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
The aim of the study was to compare the effect of resistance trening (RT) frequencies of five times (RT5), thrice- (RT3) or twice- (RT2) weekly in muscle strength and hypertrophy in young men. Were used a within-subjects design in which 20 participants had one leg randomly assigned to RT5 and the other to RT3 or to RT2. 1&#x2005;RM and muscle cross-sectional area (CSA) were assessed at baseline, after four (W4) and eight (W8) RT weeks. RT5 resulted in greater total Trening volume (TTV) than RT3 and RT2 (P&#x2009;=&#x2009;.001). 1&#x2005;RM increased similarly between protocols at W4 (RT5: 55&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;9&#x2005;Kg, effect size (ES): 1.18; RT3: 51&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;11&#x2005;Kg, ES: 0.80; RT2: 54&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;7&#x2005;Kg, ES: 1.13; P&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;.0001) and W8 (RT5: 62&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;11&#x2005;Kg, ES: 1.81; RT3: 57&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;11&#x2005;Kg, ES: 1.40; RT2: 60&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;8&#x2005;Kg, ES: 1.98; P&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;.0001) vs. baseline (RT5: 45&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;9&#x2005;Kg; RT3: 42&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;11&#x2005;Kg; RT2: 46&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;7&#x2005;Kg). CSA increased similarly between protocols at W4 (RT5: 24.6&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;3.9&#x2005;cm2, ES: 0.54; RT3: 22.0&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;4.6&#x2005;cm2, ES: 0.19; RT2: ES: 0.25; 23.8&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;3.8&#x2005;cm2; P&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;.001), and W8 (RT5: 25.3&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;4.3&#x2005;cm2; ES: 0.69; RT3: 23.6&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;4.2&#x2005;cm2, ES: 0.58; RT2: 25.5&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;3.7&#x2005;cm2; ES: 0.70; P&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;.0001) vs. baseline (RT5: 22.5&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;3.8&#x2005;cm2; RT3: 21.2&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;4.0&#x2005;cm2; RT2: 22.9&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;3.8&#x2005;cm2). Performing RT5, RT3 and RT2 a week result in similar muscle strength increase and hypertrophy, despite higher TTV for RT5.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29859672
Title: Effects of stress and mental toughness on burnout and depressive symptoms: A prospective study with young elite athletes.
Authors: Gerber, Best, Meerstetter, Walter, Ludyga
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2018
Topic: mental toughness athletes

ABSTRACT:
To examine in a sample of young elite athletes (a) the presence of clinically relevant symptoms of burnout and depression, and (b) a possible interaction of perceived stress and mental toughness in the prediction of burnout and depressive symptoms.

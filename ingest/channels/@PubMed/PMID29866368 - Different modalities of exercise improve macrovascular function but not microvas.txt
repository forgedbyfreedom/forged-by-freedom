PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29866368
Title: Different modalities of exercise improve macrovascular function but not microvascular function in metabolic syndrome: The RESOLVE randomized trial.
Authors: Vinet, Obert, Courteix, Chapier, Lesourd
Journal: International journal of cardiology
Year: 2019
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
To determine which modality of exercise program (endurance and/or resistance dominance) is the most effective for improving vascular function in the micro- and macrocirculation in metabolic syndrome (MetS).

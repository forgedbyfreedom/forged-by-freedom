PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29889056
Title: Aerobic vs. resistance exercise for chronic non-specific low back pain: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Wewege, Booth, Parmenter
Journal: Journal of back and musculoskeletal rehabilitation
Year: 2019
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
This meta-analysis compared progressive aerobic training (PAT) to progressive resistance training (PRT) for pain, disability and quality of life (QoL) in people with chronic non-specific low back pain (CNSLBP).

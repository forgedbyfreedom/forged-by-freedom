PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29912854
Title: Spexin Modulates Functions of Rat Endocrine Pancreatic Cells.
Authors: Sassek, Kolodziejski, Strowski, Nogowski, Nowak
Journal: Pancreas
Year: 2019
Topic: peptide hormones performance

ABSTRACT:
Spexin is a peptide whose action is poorly understood but which is expressed in many tissues. This encouraged us to investigate the potential role of spexin in the regulation of pancreatic secretion.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29940668
Title: Eccentric Overload Muscle Damage is Attenuated By a Novel Angiotensin- (1-7) Treatment.
Authors: Becker, Totou, Moura, Kangussu, Mill&#xe1;n
Journal: International journal of sports medicine
Year: 2018
Topic: eccentric trening muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
The development of new strategies to attenuate exercise-induced muscle damage may be helpful for trening regimens. The aim of this study was to determine whether a oral formulation of angiotensin Ang-(1-7)[HP&#x3b2;CD/Ang-(1-7)] is effective to reduce pain, and muscle damage markers after eccentric-overload exercise. HP&#x3b2;CD (Placebo) and HP&#x3b2;CD/Ang-(1-7) (Ang-(1-7) group were treated for 7 days (one capsule/day). The pain was measured by visual analogue scale, maximal strength (MS) using force platform. Blood samples were collected for cytokines and creatine kinase (CK) analysis. The Ang-(1-7)-treated group reported less pain immediately (3.46&#xb1;0.64 vs. placebo 3.80&#xb1;0.77&#x2009;cm) and 24&#x2009;h after exercise (3.07&#xb1;0.71 vs. 3.73&#xb1;0.58&#x2009;cm placebo) and higher MS at 24&#x2009;h (24&#xb1;12&#x2009;N) and 48&#x2009;h (30&#xb1;15&#x2009;N) vs. placebo (-8&#xb1;9&#x2009;N and -10&#xb1;9&#x2009;N). The CK for Ang-(1-7) (0.5&#xb1;0.1 and 0.9&#xb1;0.2 U/L) were lower at 48 and 72&#x2009;h vs. placebo (fold changes of 1.7&#xb1;0.5 and 1.5&#xb1;0.3 U/L). The TNF-&#x3b1; level was lower in the treated group post-exercise (38&#xb1;2.5&#x2009;pg/ml) vs. placebo (45&#xb1;2.9&#x2009;pg/ml) but no significant changes were observed for IL-6 and IL-10. Our data indicate that treatment with Ang-(1-7) may attenuate pain, some of the muscle damage markers and improves performance following eccentric exercise.

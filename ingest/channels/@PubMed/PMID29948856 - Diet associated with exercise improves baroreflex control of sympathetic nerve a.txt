PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 29948856
Title: Diet associated with exercise improves baroreflex control of sympathetic nerve activity in metabolic syndrome and sleep apnea patients.
Authors: Toschi-Dias, Trombetta, Silva, Maki-Nunes, Cepeda
Journal: Sleep &amp; breathing = Schlaf &amp; Atmung
Year: 2019
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
We tested the hypothesis that (i) diet associated with exercise would improve arterial baroreflex (ABR) control in metabolic syndrome (MetS) patients with and without obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) and (ii) the effects of this intervention would be more pronounced in patients with OSA.

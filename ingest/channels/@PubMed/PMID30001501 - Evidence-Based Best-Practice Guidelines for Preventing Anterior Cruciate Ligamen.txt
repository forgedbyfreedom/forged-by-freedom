PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30001501
Title: Evidence-Based Best-Practice Guidelines for Preventing Anterior Cruciate Ligament Injuries in Young Female Athletes: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.
Authors: Petushek, Sugimoto, Stoolmiller, Smith, Myer
Journal: The American journal of sports medicine
Year: 2020
Topic: ACL injury prevention female

ABSTRACT:
Injury prevention neuromuscular training (NMT) programs reduce the risk for anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury. However, variation in program characteristics limits the potential to delineate the most effective practices to optimize injury risk reduction.

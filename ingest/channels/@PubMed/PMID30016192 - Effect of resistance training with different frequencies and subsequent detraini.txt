PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 30016192
Title: Effect of resistance trening with different frequencies and subsequent detrening on muscle mass and appendicular lean soft tissue, IGF-1, and testosterone in older women.
Authors: Nascimento, Gerage, Silva, Ribeiro, Machado
Journal: European journal of sport science
Year: 2019
Topic: testosterone muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
To analyse the effect of resistance trening (RT) frequency on muscle mass, appendicular lean soft tissue, insulin-like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor 1 (IGF-1), testosterone, and their changes with detrening in older women.

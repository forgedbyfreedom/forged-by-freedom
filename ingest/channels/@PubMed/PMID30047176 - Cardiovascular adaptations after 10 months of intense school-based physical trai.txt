========================
PMID: 30047176
Title: Cardiovascular adaptations after 10 months of intense school-based physical trening for 8- to 10-year-old children.
Authors: Larsen, Nielsen, Madsen, Manniche, Hansen
Journal: Scandinavian journal of medicine &amp; science in sports
Year: 2018
Topic: cardiovascular adaptations exercise

ABSTRACT:
This study examined cardiovascular adaptations in 8- to 10-year-old schoolchildren after 10&#xa0;months (a full school year) of 3&#xa0;&#xd7;&#xa0;40&#xa0;minute per week of small-sidedbol games (SSG, including football, basketball, and/or floorball) or circuit strength trening (CST). The study involved 291 Danish schoolchildren, 8-10 years old, cluster-randomized to SSG (n&#xa0;=&#xa0;93, 4 schools, 5 classes), CST (n&#xa0;=&#xa0;83, 4 schools, 4 classes), or a control group (CON, n&#xa0;=&#xa0;115, 2 schools, 5 classes). Before and after the 10-month intervention, restinGHeart rate and blood pressure measurements were performed as well as comprehensive trensthoracic echocardiography and peripheral arterial tonometry (PAT). Analysis of baseline-to-10-months changes showed between-group differences (P&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.05) after both trening interventions in diastolic blood pressure (delta scores: SSG -2.1&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;6.0&#xa0;mm&#xa0;Hg; CST -3.0&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;7.1&#xa0;mm&#xa0;Hg; CON 0.2&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;5.3&#xa0;mm&#xa0;Hg). Moreover, there were between-group differences in delta scores (P&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.05) in interventricular septum thickness (SSG 0.17&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;0.87&#xa0;mm; CST 0.30&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;0.94&#xa0;mm; CON -0.15&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;0.68&#xa0;mm), left-atrial volume index (SSG 0.32&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;5.13&#xa0;mL/m2 ; CON 2.60&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;5.94&#xa0;mL/m2 ), and tricuspid annular plane systolic excursion (SSG -0.4&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;3.3&#xa0;mm; CON: 0.1&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;3.6&#xa0;mm). No significant between-group differences were observed for the PAT-derived reactive hyperemia index. In conclusion, 10&#xa0;months of 3&#xa0;&#xd7;&#xa0;40&#xa0;minutes per week of SSG and CST in 8- to 10-year-old children decreased diastolic blood pressure and elicited discrete cardiac adaptations, suggesting that intense physical exercise in school classes can have effects on cardiovascular health in children.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30080424
Title: Comparison of the Effects of Velocity-Based Training Methods and Traditional 1RM-Percent-Based Training Prescription on Acute Kinetic and Kinematic Variables.
Authors: Banyard, Tufano, Delgado, Thompson, Nosaka
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2019
Topic: velocity based training

ABSTRACT:
To compare kinetic and kinematic data from 3 different velocity-based training sessions and a 1-repetition-maximum (1RM)-percent-based training (PBT) session using full-depth, free-weight back squats with maximal concentric effort.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30091218
Title: A novel dual glucagon-like peptide and glucagon receptor agonist SAR425899: Results of randomized, placebo-controlled first-in-human and first-in-patient trials.
Authors: Tillner, Posch, Wagner, Teichert, Hijazi
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2019
Topic: glucagon receptor agonist

ABSTRACT:
To evaluate the safety, pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of SAR425899, a novel polypeptide, active as an agonist at both the glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor (GLP-1R) and the glucagon receptor (GCR), in healthy volunteers and in overweight/obese patients with type 2 diabetes (T2D).

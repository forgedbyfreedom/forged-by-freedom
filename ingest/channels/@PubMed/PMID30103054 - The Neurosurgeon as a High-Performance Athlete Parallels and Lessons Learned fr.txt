PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30103054
Title: The Neurosurgeon as a High-Performance Athlete: Parallels and Lessons Learned from Sports Psychology.
Authors: Spiotta, Buchholz, Pierce, Dahlkoetter, Armonda
Journal: World neurosurgery
Year: 2019
Topic: mindfulness sports performance

ABSTRACT:
Top performing neurosurgeons and elite athletes require years of sacrifice, discipline, and dedication to their art to be finely attuned to their physical abilities and limitations, and a singular mental focus to reach their full potential under moments of pressure.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30108098
Title: Effects of Exercise and Cardiorespiratory Fitness on Estrogen Metabolism in Postmenopausal Women.
Authors: Matthews, Sampson, Brenner, Moore, Courneya
Journal: Cancer epidemiology, biomarkers &amp; prevention : a publication of the American Association for Cancer Research, cosponsored by the American Society of Preventive Oncology
Year: 2019
Topic: estrogen exercise women

ABSTRACT:
Lowering endogenous estrogen levels is one mechanism whereby physical activity may lower postmenopausal breast cancer risk. Several prospective studies have suggested that increased 2-hydroxylation of estrogens may also reduce postmenopausal breast cancer risk, but whether or not exercise alters estrogen metabolism through this mechanism is unclear.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30122305
Title: Efficacy and safety of semaglutide compared with liraglutide and placebo for weight loss in patients with obesity: a randomised, double-blind, placebo and active controlled, dose-ranging, phase 2 trial.
Authors: O'Neil, Birkenfeld, McGowan, Mosenzon, Pedersen
Journal: Lancet (London, England)
Year: 2018
Topic: semaglutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Obesity is a major public health issue, and new pharmaceuticals for weight management are needed. Therefore, we evaluated the efficacy and safety of the glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) analogue semaglutide in comparison with liraglutide and a placebo in promoting weight loss.

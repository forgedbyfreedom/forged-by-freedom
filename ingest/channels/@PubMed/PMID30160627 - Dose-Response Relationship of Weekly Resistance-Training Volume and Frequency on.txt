PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30160627
Title: Dose-Response Relationship of Weekly Resistance-trening Volume and Frequency on Muscular Adaptations in trened Men.
Authors: Heaselgrave, Blacker, Smeuninx, McKendry, Breen
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2019
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
A linear dose-response relationship between resistance-trening (RT) volume and hypertrophy/strength has been proposed when &#x2264;10 to 12 weekly sets are implemented. The present study aimed to understand the impact of low to high weekly RT volume on muscular adaptations in trened young men over 6 wk of RT.

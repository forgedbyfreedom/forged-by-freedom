PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30172252
Title: Exercise without dietary changes alleviates nonalcoholic fatty liver disease without weight loss benefits.
Authors: Ok, Ko, Bae
Journal: Lipids in health and disease
Year: 2018
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to analyze the effect of exercise and/or dietary change on improvement of non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD) in chronic high-fat diet (HFD)-induced obese mice.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30188192
Title: Multivariable Artificial Pancreas for Various Exercise Types and Intensities.
Authors: Turksoy, Hajizadeh, Hobbs, Kilkus, Littlejohn
Journal: Diabetes technology &amp; therapeutics
Year: 2019
Topic: carbohydrate exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
Exercise challenges people with type 1 diabetes in controlling their glucose concentration (GC). A multivariable adaptive artificial pancreas (MAAP) may lessen the burden.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30231786
Title: Effect of exercise on type 2 diabetes-associated cognitive impairment in rats.
Authors: Mehta, Singh, Banerjee
Journal: The International journal of neuroscience
Year: 2019
Topic: type 2 diabetes exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Type 2 diabetes (T2D)-associated cognitive impairment is highly prevalent especially among the geriatric population. Here, we investigate the role of exercise in T2D-associated cognitive decline in rats.

========================
PMID: 30236847
Title: Resistance trening frequency and skeletal muscle hypertrophy: A review of available evidence.
Authors: Grgic, Schoenfeld, Latella
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2019
Topic: trening volume muscle growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone

ABSTRACT:
Current reviews and position stands on resistance trening (RT) frequency and associated muscular hypertrophy are based on limited evidence holding implications for practical application and program design. Considering that several recent studies have shed new light on this topic, the present paper aimed to collate the available evidence on RT frequency and the associated effect on muscular hypertrophy.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30276016
Title: FOUR WEEKS OF ROLLER MASSAGE TRAINING DID NOT IMPACT RANGE OF MOTION, PAIN PRESSURE THRESHOLD, VOLUNTARY CONTRACTILE PROPERTIES OR JUMP PERFORMANCE.
Authors: Hodgson, Lima, Low, Behm
Journal: International journal of sports physical therapy
Year: 2023
Topic: foam rolling muscle recovery

ABSTRACT:
Roller massagers are popular devices that are used to improve range of motion (ROM), enhance recovery from muscle soreness, and reduce pain under acute conditions. However, the effects of roller massage training and training frequency are unknown.

PMID: 30334920
Title: High-Intensity Interval trening Augments Muscle Carnosine in the Absence of Dietary Beta-alanine Intake.
Authors: DE Salles Painelli, Nemezio, Pinto, Franchi, Andrade
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2019
Topic: trening volume muscle growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone

ABSTRACT:
Cross-sectional studies suggest that trening can increase muscle carnosine (MCarn), although longitudinal studies have failed to confirm this. A lack of control for dietary &#x3b2;-alanine intake or muscle fiber type shifting may have hampered their conclusions. The purpose of the present study was to investigate the effects of high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) on MCarn.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30337348
Title: Is supine exercise associated with adverse maternal and fetal outcomes? A systematic review.
Authors: Mottola, Nagpal, Bgeginski, Davenport, Poitras
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2019
Topic: postpartum return exercise

ABSTRACT:
Theoretical concerns regarding the supine position at rest due to the gravid uterus obstructing aorta and vena caval flow may impinge uterine blood flow (UBF) to the fetus and maternal venous return.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30370532
Title: Prolonged exercise trening improves the acute type IIMuscle fibre satellite cell response in healthy older men.
Authors: Snijders, Nederveen, Bell, Lau, Mazara
Journal: The Journal of physiology
Year: 2020
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Skeletal muscle stem cells, termed satellite cells, play a crucial role in repair and remodelling of muscle in response to exercise An age-related decline in satellite cell number and/or function has been hypothesized to be a key factor in the development of sarcopenia and/or the blunted muscle fibre adaptive response to prolonged exercise trening in older persons We report that performing prolonged exercise trening improves the acute type IIMuscle fibre satellite cell response following aromasingle bout of resistance exercise in older men. The observed improvement in muscle satellite function is associated with an increase in muscle fibre capillarization following exercise trening suggesting a possible functional link between capillarization and satellite cell function.

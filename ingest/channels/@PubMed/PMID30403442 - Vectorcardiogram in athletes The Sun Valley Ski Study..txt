PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30403442
Title: Vectorcardiogram in athletes: The Sun Valley Ski Study.
Authors: Thomas, A Perez-Alday, Junell, Newton, Hamilton
Journal: Annals of noninvasive electrocardiology : the official journal of the International Society for Holter and Noninvasive Electrocardiology, Inc
Year: 2020
Topic: training frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Global electrical heterogeneity (GEH) is associated with sudden cardiac death (SCD) in adults of 45&#xa0;years and above. However, GEH has not been previously measured in young athletes. The goal of this study was to establish a reference for vectorcardiograpic (VCG) metrics in male and female athletes.

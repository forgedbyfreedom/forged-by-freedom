PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30411978
Title: Metabolic adaptations to endurance trening and nutrition strategies influencing performance.
Authors: Earnest, Rothschild, Harnish, Naderi
Journal: Research in sports medicine (Print)
Year: 2019
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
Endurance performance is the result of optimal trening targeting cardiovascular, metabolic, and peripheral muscular adaptations and is coupled to effective nutrition strategies via the use of macronutrient manipulations surrounding trening and potential supplementation with ergogenic aids. It is important to note that trening and nutrition may differ according to the individual&#xa0;needs of the athlete and can&#xa0;markedly impact the physiological response to trening. Herein, we discuss various aspects of endurance trening adaptations, nutritional strategies and their contributions to towards performance.

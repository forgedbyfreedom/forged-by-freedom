PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30423113
Title: The Muscle Protein Synthetic Response to Whey Protein Ingestion Is Greater in Middle-Aged Women Compared With Men.
Authors: Horstman, Kouw, van Dijk, Hamer, Groen
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2020
Topic: whey protein muscle synthesis

ABSTRACT:
Muscle mass maintenance is largely regulated by the postprandial rise in muscle protein synthesis rates. It remains unclear whether postprandial protein handling differs between women and men.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30449684
Title: Quantitative Analysis of the Whole-Body Metabolic Fate of Branched-Chain Amino Acids.
Authors: Neinast, Jang, Hui, Murashige, Chu
Journal: Cell metabolism
Year: 2020
Topic: branched chain amino acids muscle

ABSTRACT:
Elevations in branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs) associate with numerous systemic diseases, including cancer, diabetes, and heart failure. However, an integrated understanding of whole-body BCAAsmetabolism remains lacking. Here, we employ in&#xa0;vivo isotopic tracing to systemically quantify BCAAsoxidation in healthy and insulin-resistant mice. We find that most tissues rapidly oxidize BCAAs into the tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle, with the greatest quantity occurring in muscle, brown fat, liver, kidneys, and heart. Notably, pancreas supplies 20% of its TCA carbons from BCAAs. Genetic and pharmacologic suppression of branched-chain alpha-ketoacid dehydrogenase kinase, a clinically targeted regulatory kinase, induces BCAAsoxidation primarily in skeletal muscle of healthy mice. While insulin acutely increases BCAAsoxidation in cardiac and skeletal muscle, chronically insulin-resistant mice show blunted BCAAsoxidation in adipose tissues and liver, shifting BCAAsoxidation toward muscle. Together, this work provides a quantitative framework for understanding systemic BCAAsoxidation in health and insulin resistance.

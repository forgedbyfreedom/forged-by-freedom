PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30450851
Title: Effect of the Orally Active Growth Hormone Secretagogue MK-677 on Somatic Growth in Rats.
Authors: Lee, Kwon, Chae, Lee, Kim
Journal: Yonsei medical journal
Year: 2019
Topic: growth hormone releasing peptide

ABSTRACT:
Growth hormone secretagogues (GHSs) possess the ability to release growth hormone (GH) in the body. This study aimed to investigate the effects of MK-677, an orally active GHS, on somatic growth in rats.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30475963
Title: Effects of protein supplementation on lean body mass, muscle strength, and physical performance in nonfrail community-dwelling older adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Ten Haaf, Nuijten, Maessen, Horstman, Eijsvogels
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2019
Topic: protein intake muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Increasing protein intake has been suggested as an effective strategy to ameliorate age-related loss of muscle mass and strength. Current reviews assessing the effect of protein supplementation are strongly influenced by the inclusion of studies with frail older adults.

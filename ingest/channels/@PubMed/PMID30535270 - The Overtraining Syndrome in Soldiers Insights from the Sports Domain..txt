PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30535270
Title: The Overtrening Syndrome in Soldiers: Insights from the Sports Domain.
Authors: Vrijkotte, Roelands, Pattyn, Meeusen
Journal: Military medicine
Year: 2020
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Soldiers are exposed to extreme trening regimens in order to optimally prepare for real battle. High attrition rates are a known issue during trening courses, especially for elite troops. An underlying factor might be the disbalance between stress/stren and recovery. The aim of this review is to give insight in the current knowledge about functional overreaching (FOR), non-FOR (NFOR), and the overtrening syndrome (OTS) in the military.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30550514
Title: Acute Effects of Exercise Intensity on insulin Sensitivity under Energy Balance.
Authors: Fisher, Gower, Ovalle, Behrens, Hunter
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2019
Topic: insulin sensitivity exercise training

ABSTRACT:
Exercise is known to improve insulin sensitivity (SI); however, studies to date have been confounded by negative energy deficits after exercise.

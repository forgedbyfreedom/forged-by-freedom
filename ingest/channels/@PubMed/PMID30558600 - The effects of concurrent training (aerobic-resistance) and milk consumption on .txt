PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30558600
Title: The effects of concurrent trening (aerobic-resistance) and milk consumption on some markers of bone mineral density in women with osteoporosis.
Authors: Arazi, Samadpour, Eghbali
Journal: BMC women's health
Year: 2019
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Osteoporosis is a skeletal metabolic disorder characterized by low bone mineral density (BMD) and reduced bone strength leading to higher bone fractures risk. The present study attempted to investigate the effects of concurrent trening (aerobic-resistance) and milk consumption on some markers of BMD in women with osteoporosis.

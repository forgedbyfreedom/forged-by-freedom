PMID: 30565905
Title: Lifetime major depression and grey-matter volume.
Authors: Ancelin, Carri&#xe8;re, Artero, Maller, Meslin
Journal: Journal of psychiatry &amp; neuroscience : JPN
Year: 2020
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
There is evidence of structural brain alterations in major depressive disorder (MDD), but little is known about how these alterations might be affected by age at onset or genetic vulnerability. This study examines whether lifetime episodes of MDD are associated with specific alterations in grey-matter volume, and whether those alterations vary according to sex or serotonin trensporter-linked promoter region (5-HTTLPR) genotype (LL, SL or SS).

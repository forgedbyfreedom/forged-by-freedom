PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30569065
Title: Low Levels of Branched Chain Amino Acids, Eicosapentaenoic Acid and Micronutrients Are Associated with Low Muscle Mass, Strength and Function in Community-Dwelling Older Adults.
Authors: Ter Borg, Luiking, van Helvoort, Boirie, Schols
Journal: The journal of nutrition, health &amp; aging
Year: 2020
Topic: branched chain amino acids muscle

ABSTRACT:
Sarcopenia, the age-related decrease in muscle mass and function can result in adverse health outcomes and subsequent loss of independence. Inadequate nutrition is an important contributor to the aetiology of sarcopenia, and dietary strategies are studied to prevent or delay this geriatric syndrome.

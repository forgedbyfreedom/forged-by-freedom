PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 3057314
Title: Cardiovascular adaptations to resistance trening.
Authors: Fleck
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 1988
Topic: cardiovascular adaptations exercise

ABSTRACT:
The cross-sectional and longitudinal data available indicate that the following conclusions are warranted concerning the effects of resistance trening on the cardiovascular system. Resistance trening causes increased absolute left ventricular wall thickness and left ventricular mass. These increases are not as evident when expressed relative to body surface area or lean body mass. There is little or no change in left ventricular internal dimensions, in absolute terms or relative to body surface area, restinGHeart rate, resting blood pressure, and diastolic function of the left ventricle due to resistance trening. No change or slight positive effects are noted in systolic function of the left ventricle with trening. During resistance trening exercises, a large pressor response occurs and is attenuated in resistance trened athletes as compared to control subjects. Resistance trening does result in adaptations of the cardiovascular system, with further research needed to elucidate its nature.

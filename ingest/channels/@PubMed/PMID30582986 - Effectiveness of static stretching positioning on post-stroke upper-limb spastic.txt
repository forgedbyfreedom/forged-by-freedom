PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30582986
Title: Effectiveness of static stretching positioning on post-stroke upper-limb spasticity and mobility: Systematic review with meta-analysis.
Authors: Salazar, Pinto, Ruschel Mossi, Figueiro, Lukrafka
Journal: Annals of physical and rehabilitation medicine
Year: 2020
Topic: muscle recovery post exercise

ABSTRACT:
To systematically review the effects of static stretching with positioning orthoses or simple positioning combined or not with other therapies on upper-limb spasticity and mobility in adults after stroke.

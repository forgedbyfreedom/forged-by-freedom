PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 3062735
Title: Overtrening in elite athletes. Review and directions for the future.
Authors: Kuipers, Keizer
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 1989
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Overtrening is an imbalance between trening and recovery. Short term overtrening or 'over-reaching' is reversible within days to weeks. Fatigue accompanied by a number of physical and psychological symptoms in the athlete is an indication of 'staleness' or 'overtrening syndrome'. Staleness is a dysfunction of the neuroendocrine system, localised at hypothalamic level. Staleness may occur when physical and emotional stress exceeds the individual coping capacity. However, the precise mechanism has yet to be established. Clinically the syndrome can be divided into the sympathetic and parasympathetic types, based upon the predominance of sympathetic or parasympathetic activity, respectively. The syndrome and its clinical manifestation can be explained as a stress response. At present, no sensitive and specific tests are available to prevent or diagnose overtrening. The diagnosis is based on the medical history and the clinical presentation. Complete recovery may take weeks to months.

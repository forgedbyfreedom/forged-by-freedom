Title: Home-based exercise therapy for treating non-specific chronic low back pain.
Authors: Kanas, Faria, Salles, Sorpreso, Martins
Journal: Revista da Associacao Medica Brasileira (1992)
Year: 2019
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
To evaluate pain, functional capacity, and quality of life of patients with non-specific chronic low back pain, after home-based exercise therapy with different kinds of supervision.

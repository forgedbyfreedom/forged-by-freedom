PMID: 30676280
Title: Cold Water Immersion as a Strategy for Muscle Recovery in Professional Basketball Players During the Competitive Season.
Authors: Seco-Calvo, Mielgo-Ayuso, Calvo-Lobo, C&#xf3;rdova
Journal: Journal of sport rehabilitation
Year: 2021
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
Despite prior studies that have addressed the recovery effects of cold-water immersion (CWI) in different sports, there is a lack of knowledge about longitudinal studies across a full season of competition assessing these effects.

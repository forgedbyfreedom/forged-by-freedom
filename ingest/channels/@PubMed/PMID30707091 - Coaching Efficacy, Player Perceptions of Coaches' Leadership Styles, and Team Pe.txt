PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30707091
Title: Coaching Efficacy, Player Perceptions of Coaches' Leadership Styles, and Team Performance in Premier League Soccer.
Authors: Keatlholetswe, Malete
Journal: Research quarterly for exercise and sport
Year: 2019
Topic: self efficacy sports performance

ABSTRACT:
The coaching competency research has demonstrated the role of coaching efficacy and coaching behaviors on various athlete outcomes. However, athlete perceptions of these relationships and how they affect performance are less understood.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30712240
Title: High-Intensity Interval trening in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus: a Systematic Review.
Authors: da Silva, Grande, Roever, Tse, Liu
Journal: Current atherosclerosis reports
Year: 2019
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
High-intensity exercise may beneficially impact on insulin sensitivity. However, there is still uncertainty on the actual impact of high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) on chronic physiologic adaptations among patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM). We aimed to systematically search and appraise the evidence base on the effects of HIIT in comparison to mild- or moderate-intensity interval trening (MMIIT) on glycemic control inT2DM.

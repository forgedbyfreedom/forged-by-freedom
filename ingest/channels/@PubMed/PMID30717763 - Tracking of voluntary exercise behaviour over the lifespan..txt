PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30717763
Title: Tracking of voluntary exercise behaviour over the lifespan.
Authors: van der Zee, van der Mee, Bartels, de Geus
Journal: The international journal of behavioral nutrition and physical activity
Year: 2019
Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The aim of many physical activity interventions is to develop life-lonGHabits of regular exercise and sports activities in leisure time. Previous studies that assessed tracking (i.e. the stability of a trait over the lifespan) of leisure time exercise behaviour across various parts of the life span have treated it as a uniform construct by summing all types of leisure time exercise activities into aromasingle summary score for the total volume of exercise. This study provides new insight by additionally determining tracking across leisure time exercise activities in six different domains: (1) team-based versus solitary activities, (2) competitive versus non-competitive activities, and (3) externally paced versus internally paced activities. We also assessed which of the domains of exercise activities best predicted total volume of exercise at follow-up.

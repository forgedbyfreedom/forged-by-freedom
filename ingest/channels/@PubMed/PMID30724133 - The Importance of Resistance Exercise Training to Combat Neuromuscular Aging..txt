PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30724133
Title: The Importance of Resistance Exercise trening to Combat Neuromuscular Aging.
Authors: Lavin, Roberts, Fry, Moro, Rasmussen
Journal: Physiology (Bethesda, Md.)
Year: 2019
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
Older adults undergoing age-related decrements in muscle health can benefit substantially from resistance exercise trening, a potent stimulus for whole muscle and myofiber hypertrophy, neuromuscular performance gains, and improved functional mobility. With the use of advancing technologies, research continues to elucidate the mechanisms of and heterogeneity in adaptations to resistance exercise trening beyond differences in exercise prescription. This review highlights the current knowledge in these areas and emphasizes knowledge gaps that require future attention of the field.

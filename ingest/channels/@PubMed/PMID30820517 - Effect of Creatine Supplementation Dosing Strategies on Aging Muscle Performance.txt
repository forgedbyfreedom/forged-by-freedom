PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30820517
Title: Effect of Creatine Supplementation Dosing Strategies on Aging Muscle Performance.
Authors: Chami, Candow
Journal: The journal of nutrition, health &amp; aging
Year: 2020
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
This study compared the effects of different creatine supplementation dosages, independent of resistance training, on aging muscle performance and functionality.

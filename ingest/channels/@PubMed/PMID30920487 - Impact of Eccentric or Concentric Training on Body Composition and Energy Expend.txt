PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30920487
Title: Impact of Eccentric or Concentric trening on Body Composition and Energy Expenditure.
Authors: Touron, Perrault, Julian, Maisonnave, Deat
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2020
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
To compare the effects of 8-wk eccentric (ECC) versus concentric (CON) trening using downhill and uphill running in rats on whole body composition, bone mineral density (BMD), and energy expenditure.

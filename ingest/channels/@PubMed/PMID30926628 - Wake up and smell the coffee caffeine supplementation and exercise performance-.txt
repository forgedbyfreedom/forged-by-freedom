PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 30926628
Title: Wake up and smell the coffee: caffeine supplementation and exercise performance-an umbrella review of 21 published meta-analyses.
Authors: Grgic, Grgic, Pickering, Schoenfeld, Bishop
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2020
Topic: caffeine athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
To systematically review, summarise and appraise findings of published meta-analyses that examined the effects of caffeine on exercise performance.

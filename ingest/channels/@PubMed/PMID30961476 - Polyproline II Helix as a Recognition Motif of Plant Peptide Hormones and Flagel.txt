Title: Polyproline II Helix as a Recognition Motif of Plant Peptide Hormones and Flagellin Peptide flg22.
Authors: Matsushima, Miyashita, Tamaki, Kretsinger
Journal: Protein and peptide letters
Year: 2019
Topic: peptide hormones performance

ABSTRACT:
Plant peptide hormones play a crucial role in plant growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone and development. A group of these peptide hormones are signaling peptides with 5 - 23 amino acids. Flagellin peptide (flg22) also elicits an immune response in plants. The functions are expressed through recognition of the peptide hormones and flg22. This recognition relies on membrane localized receptor kinases with extracellular leucine rich repeats (LRR-RKs). The structures of plant peptide hormones - AtPep1, IDA, IDL1, RGFs 1- 3, TDIF/CLE41 - and of flg22 complexed with LRR domains of corresponding LRR-RKs and co-receptors SERKs have been determined. However, their structures are well not analyzed and characterized in detail. The structures of PIP, CEP, CIF, and HypSys are still unknown.

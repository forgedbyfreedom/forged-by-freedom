PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31004287
Title: Tabata trening: one of the most energetically effective high-intensity intermittent trening methods.
Authors: Tabata
Journal: The journal of physiological sciences : JPS
Year: 2019
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
For decades, high-intensity interval/intermittent exercise trening methods have been used by elite athletes to improve their performance in sports. One of the most effective trening methods, i.e., 'Tabata trening,' is reviewed herein from the viewpoint of the energetics of exercise. The prior research describing the metabolic profile and effects of Tabata trening is also summarized, with some historical anecdotes.

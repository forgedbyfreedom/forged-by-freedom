Title: Anterior Cruciate Ligament Injury Risk in Sport: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Injury Incidence by Sex and Sport Classification.
Authors: Montalvo, Schneider, Webster, Yut, Galloway
Journal: Journal of athletic training
Year: 2019
Topic: ACL injury prevention female

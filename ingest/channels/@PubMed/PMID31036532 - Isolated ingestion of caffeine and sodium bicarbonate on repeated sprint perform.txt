PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31036532
Title: Isolated ingestion of caffeine and sodium bicarbonate on repeated sprint performance: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Lopes-Silva, Choo, Franchini, Abbiss
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2019
Topic: caffeine athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
This study is a systematic review and meta-analysis aimed at investigating the isolated effects of caffeine and sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO3) ingestion on repeated sprint ability (RSA).

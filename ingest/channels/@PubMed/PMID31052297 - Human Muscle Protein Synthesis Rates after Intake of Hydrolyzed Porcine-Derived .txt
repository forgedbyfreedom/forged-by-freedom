PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31052297
Title: Human Muscle Protein Synthesis Rates after Intake of Hydrolyzed Porcine-Derived and Cows' Milk Whey Proteins-A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Bendtsen, Thorning, Reitelseder, Ritz, Hansen
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2019
Topic: whey protein muscle synthesis

ABSTRACT:
Abstract: Background: Whey protein has been shown to be one of the best proteins to stimulate muscle protein synthesis rate (MPS), but other high quality proteins, e.g., animal/porcine-derived, could have similar effects.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31058797
Title: Using the immediate blood pressure benefits of exercise to improve exercise adherence among adults with hypertension: a randomized clinical trial.
Authors: Zaleski, Taylor, Park, Santos, Panza
Journal: Journal of hypertension
Year: 2020
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
A single exercise session evokes immediate blood pressure (BP) reductions that persist for at least 24&#x200a;h, termed postexercise hypotension (PEH). Self-monitoring of PEH may foster positive outcome expectations of exercise, and thus, enhance exercise adherence among adults with hypertension.

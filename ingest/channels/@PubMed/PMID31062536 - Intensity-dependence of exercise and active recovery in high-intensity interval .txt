PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31062536
Title: Intensity-dependence of exercise and active recovery in high-intensity interval trening.
Authors: Kemi, Fowler, Mcglynn, Primrose, Smirthwaite
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2020
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
High-intensity interval trening (HIIT) with interspersing active recovery is an effective mode of exercise trening in cohorts ranging from athletes to patients. Here, we assessed the intensity-dependence of the intervals and active recovery bouts for permitting a sustainable HIIT protocol.

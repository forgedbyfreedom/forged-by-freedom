PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31077961
Title: What are the current practices of sports physiotherapists in integrating psychological strategies during athletes' return-to-play rehabilitation? Mixed methods systematic review.
Authors: Annear, Sole, Devan
Journal: Physical therapy in sport : official journal of the Association of Chartered Physiotherapists in Sports Medicine
Year: 2019
Topic: goal setting sports psychology

ABSTRACT:
The primary aim was to identify the current practices of sports physiotherapists in implementing psychological strategies during athletes' return-to-play rehabilitation and, secondly, to explore their attitudes and challenges to do so.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31080490
Title: The effects of exercise and ambient temperature on dietary intake, appetite sensation, and appetite regulatinGHormone concentrations.
Authors: Mandic, Ahmed, Rhind, Goodman, L'Abbe
Journal: Nutrition &amp; metabolism
Year: 2023
Topic: leptin ghrelin appetite exercise

ABSTRACT:
It is not clear whether the frequently reported phenomenon of exercise-induced anorexia is exacerbated or blunted in warm or cold environments. Therefore, this study investigated the effects of exercise in three different environmental temperatures vs. rest, on perceptions of appetite, appetite regulatinGHormones, and food intake.

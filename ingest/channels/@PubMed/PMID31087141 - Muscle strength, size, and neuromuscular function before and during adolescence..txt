Title: Muscle strength, size, and neuromuscular function before and during adolescence.
Authors: Gillen, Shoemaker, McKay, Bohannon, Gibson
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2019
Topic: neuromuscular adaptations strength

ABSTRACT:

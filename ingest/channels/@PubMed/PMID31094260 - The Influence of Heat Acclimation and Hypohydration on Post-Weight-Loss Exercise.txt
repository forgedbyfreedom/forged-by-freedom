Title: The Influence of Heat Acclimation and Hypohydration on Post-Weight-Loss Exercise Performance.
Authors: Barley, Chapman, Mavropalias, Abbiss
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2020
Topic: heat acclimation exercise

ABSTRACT:

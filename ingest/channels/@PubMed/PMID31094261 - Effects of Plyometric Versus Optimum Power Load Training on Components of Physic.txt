PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31094261
Title: Effects of Plyometric Versus Optimum Power Load Training on Components of Physical Fitness in Young Male Soccer Players.
Authors: Ribeiro, Teixeira, Lemos, Teixeira, Moreira
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2020
Topic: plyometric training power

ABSTRACT:
The current study aimed to compare the effects of plyometric (PT) versus optimum power load (OPL) training on physical performance of young high-level soccer players.

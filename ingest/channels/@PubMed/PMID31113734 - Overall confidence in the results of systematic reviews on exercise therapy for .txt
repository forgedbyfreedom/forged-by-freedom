PMID: 31113734
Title: Overall confidence in the results of systematic reviews on exercise therapy for chronic low back pain: a cross-sectional analysis using the Assessing the Methodological Quality of Systematic Reviews (AMSTAR) 2 tool.
Authors: Almeida, Yamato, Parreira, Costa, Kamper
Journal: Brazilian journal of physical therapy
Year: 2020
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
To evaluate the overall confidence in the results of systematic reviews of exercise therapy for chronic non-specific low back pain using the AMSTAR 2 tool.

PMID: 31124397
Title: Dance your stress away: comparing the effect of dance/movement trening to aerobic exercise trening on the cortisol awakening response in healthy older adults.
Authors: Vrinceanu, Esmail, Berryman, Predovan, Vu
Journal: Stress (Amsterdam, Netherlands)
Year: 2020
Topic: cortisol exercise stress

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to examine in healthy older adults the effects of dance/movement trening (DMT) on the cortisol awakening response (CAR), a marker of chronic stress. Forty participants (mean age&#x2009;=&#x2009;67.45, 75% women) were randomized into three groups: DMT (n&#x2009;=&#x2009;12) - a set of exercises to promote gross motor skills, body awareness, and socialization; aerobic exercise trening (AT; n&#x2009;=&#x2009;14) - high intensity activity on a recumbent bicycle, and wait list (WL; n&#x2009;=&#x2009;14). Both DMT and AT groups were supervised by licensed instructors and meT3 times a week for three months. Before and after their respective program, participants of all groups provided saliva samples on 3 d at 0-, 30- and 60-min after awakening, and had their fitness level evaluated. A significant group&#x2009;&#xd7;&#x2009;time interaction (F(2,34)&#x2009;=&#x2009;5.79, p&#x2009;=&#x2009;.01, &#x3b7;2partial&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.25) was found, with the DMT group showing lower salivary cortisol values post-trening, while the other two groups showed no change from baseline in their CAR. Cardiorespiratory fitness improved only in the AT group, while DMT showed no group-specific physical functioning improvements. The results are discussed in terms of the importance of physical and psychological effects of trening on change in cortisol levels.

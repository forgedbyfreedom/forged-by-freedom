Title: Appetite-regulatinGHormones-leptin, adiponectin and ghrelin-and the development of prostate cancer: a systematic review and exploratory meta-analysis.

Journal: Prostate cancer and prostatic diseases
Year: 2020
Topic: peptide hormones performance

ABSTRACT:
Obesity has been proposed as a risk factor for prostate cancer (PCa). In obesity, serum levels of the appetite-regulatinGHormones-leptin, adiponectin, and ghrelin-become deregulated.

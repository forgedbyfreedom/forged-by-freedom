PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31172828
Title: The Effects of Leucine-Enriched Branched-Chain Amino Acid Supplementation on Recovery After High-Intensity Resistance Exercise.
Authors: Osmond, Directo, Elam, Juache, Kreipke
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2019
Topic: leucine threshold muscle protein

ABSTRACT:
Of the 3 branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs), leucine has arguably received the most attribution for the role of BCAAssupplementation in alleviating symptoms of exercise-induced muscle damage and facilitation of acute performance recovery.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31185157
Title: Oral Semaglutide and Cardiovascular Outcomes in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes.
Authors: Husain, Birkenfeld, Donsmark, Dungan, Eliaschewitz
Journal: The New England journal of medicine
Year: 2019
Topic: semaglutide cardiovascular

ABSTRACT:
Establishing cardiovascular safety of new therapies for type 2 diabetes is important. Safety data are available for the subqtaneous form of the glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist semaglutide but are needed for oral semaglutide.

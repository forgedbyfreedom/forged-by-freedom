PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31204284
Title: Telemonitoring-supported exercise training, metabolic syndrome severity, and work ability in company employees: a randomised controlled trial.
Authors: Haufe, Kerling, Protte, Bayerle, Stenner
Journal: The Lancet. Public health
Year: 2020
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Metabolic syndrome is a predisposing factor for cardiovascular and metabolic disease, but also has socioeconomic relevance by affecting the health and productivity of workers. We tested the effect of regular telemonitoring-supported physical activity on metabolic syndrome severity and work ability in company employees.

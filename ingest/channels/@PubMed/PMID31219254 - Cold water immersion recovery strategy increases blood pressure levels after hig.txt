PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31219254
Title: Cold water immersion recovery strategy increases blood pressure levels after high-intensity intermittent exercise.
Authors: Yang, Chen, Yang, Kuo, Chien
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2020
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to investigate the effects of cold water immersion (CWI) recovery strategies on the blood pressure levels and related physiological parameters after high-intensity intermittent exercise (HIIE).

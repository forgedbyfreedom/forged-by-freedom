PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31266027
Title: Heart Rate Variability and Academic Performance of First-Year University Students.
Authors: Thomas, Viljoen
Journal: Neuropsychobiology
Year: 2020
Topic: heart rate variability training

ABSTRACT:
Heart rate variability (HRV) measures have been shown to be positively associated with cognitive processing, while a positive association between cognitive processing and academic performance has been demonstrated.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31269004
Title: Exercise Intensity Matters in Chronic Nonspecific Low Back Pain Rehabilitation.
Authors: Verbrugghe, Agten, Stevens, Hansen, Demoulin
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2020
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
Exercise therapy (ET) is advocated as a treatment for chronic nonspecific low back pain (CNSLBP). However, therapy effect sizes remain low. In other chronic disorders, trening at higher intensity has resulted in greater improvements on both general health related and disease specific outcomes compared to lower-intensity ET. Possibly, high-intensity trening also improves effect sizes in CNSLBP.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31282193
Title: Effect of a mindfulness programme training to prevent the sport injury and improve the performance of semi-professional soccer players.
Authors: Zadeh, Ajilchi, Salman, Kisely
Journal: Australasian psychiatry : bulletin of Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Psychiatrists
Year: 2020
Topic: mindfulness sports performance

ABSTRACT:
Mindfulness improves psychological outcomes. We examined whether greater mindfulness scores were associated with reduced injury rates in soccer players, as well as improved performance at both the individual and team level.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31297062
Title: Foam Rolling as a Recovery Tool Following Eccentric Exercise: Potential Mechanisms Underpinning Changes in Jump Performance.
Authors: Drinkwater, Latella, Wilsmore, Bird, Skein
Journal: Frontiers in physiology
Year: 2022
Topic: foam rolling muscle recovery

ABSTRACT:
Recovery from exercise-induced muscle damage (EIMD) is paramount in sports performance. Foam rolling (FR) has been suggested to improve acute performance; however, the ability to facilitate recovery from eccentric (ECC) exercise remains unclear.

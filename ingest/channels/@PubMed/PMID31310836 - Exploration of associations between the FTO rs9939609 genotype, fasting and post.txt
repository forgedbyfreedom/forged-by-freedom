PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31310836
Title: Exploration of associations between the FTO rs9939609 genotype, fasting and postprandial appetite-related hormones and perceived appetite in healthy men and women.
Authors: Goltz, Thackray, Varela-Mato, King, Dorling
Journal: Appetite
Year: 2020
Topic: leptin ghrelin appetite exercise

ABSTRACT:
The fat mass and obesity-associated gene (FTO) rs9939609 A-allele has been associated with obesity risk. Although the exact mechanisms involved remain unknown, the FTO rs9939609 A-allele has been associated with an impaired postprandial suppression of appetite.

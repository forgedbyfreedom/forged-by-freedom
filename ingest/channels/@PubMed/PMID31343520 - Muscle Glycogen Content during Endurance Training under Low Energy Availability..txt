PMID: 31343520
Title: Muscle Glycogen Content during Endurance trening under Low Energy Availability.
Authors: Kojima, Ishibashi, Tanabe, Iwayama, Kamei
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2020
Topic: trening volume muscle growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone

ABSTRACT:
The present study investigated the effects of three consecutive days of endurance trening under conditions of low energy availability (LEA) on the muscle glycogen content, muscle damage markers, endocrine regulation, and endurance capacity in male runners.

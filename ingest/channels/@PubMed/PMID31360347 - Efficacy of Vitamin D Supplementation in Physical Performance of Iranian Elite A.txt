PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31360347
Title: Efficacy of Vitamin D Supplementation in Physical Performance of Iranian Elite Athletes.
Authors: Alimoradi, Nikooyeh, Ravasi, Zahedirad, Shariatzadeh
Journal: International journal of preventive medicine
Year: 2022
Topic: vitamin D athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
There has been no report of the vitamin D status of the professional athletes from Iran to date. This study was performed to evaluate the efficacy of weekly vitamin D supplementation on athletic performance in Iranian athletes expedited to Asian competition in Taipei, China, 2015.

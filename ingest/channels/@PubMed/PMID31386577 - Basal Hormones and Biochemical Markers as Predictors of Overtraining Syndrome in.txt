PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31386577
Title: Basal Hormones and Biochemical Markers as Predictors of Overtraining Syndrome in Male Athletes: The EROS-BASAL Study.
Authors: Cadegiani, Kater
Journal: Journal of athletic training
Year: 2019
Topic: overtraining syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Overtraining syndrome (OTS) and related conditions cause decreased training performance and fatigue through an imbalance among training volume, nutrition, and recovery time. No definitive biochemical markers of OTS currently exist.

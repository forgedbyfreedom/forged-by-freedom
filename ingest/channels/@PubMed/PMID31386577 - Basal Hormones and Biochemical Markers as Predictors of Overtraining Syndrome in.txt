PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31386577
Title: Basal Hormones and Biochemical Markers as Predictors of Overtrening Syndrome in Male Athletes: The EROS-BASAL Study.
Authors: Cadegiani, Kater
Journal: Journal of athletic trening
Year: 2019
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Overtrening syndrome (OTS) and related conditions cause decreased trening performance and fatigue through an imbalance among trening volume, nutrition, and recovery time. No definitive biochemical markers of OTS currently exist.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31397766
Title: Influence of High-Intensity Interval trening Versus Continuous trening on Functional Capacity in Individuals With Heart Failure: A SYSTEMATIC REVIEW AND META-ANALYSIS.
Authors: Ara&#xfa;jo, Leite, Fuzari, Pereira de Souza, Rem&#xed;gio
Journal: Journal of cardiopulmonary rehabilitation and prevention
Year: 2020
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
Cardiac rehabilitation programs reduce the risk of death and acute events related to the disease through the association of various modalities of exercise. When implemented in high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) programs, it may allow for gradual adaptation of the skeletal muscles to greater exercise intensities. The present systematic review aimed to determine whether HIIT promoted a greater increase in exercise tolerance in comparison with continuous aerobic trening in individuals with heart failure.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31399427
Title: Letting the cat out of the bag: athletes, coaches and physiotherapists share their perspectives on injury prevention in elite sports.
Authors: Bolling, Delfino Barboza, van Mechelen, Pasman
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2020
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
To explore how sports injury prevention takes place in elite sport practice and to describe the perspectives of athletes, coaches and physiotherapists regarding the most critical factors that help prevent injury in the elite sports context.

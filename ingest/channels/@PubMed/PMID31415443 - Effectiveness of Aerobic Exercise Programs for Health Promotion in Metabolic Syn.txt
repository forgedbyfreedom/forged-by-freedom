PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31415443
Title: Effectiveness of Aerobic Exercise Programs for Health Promotion in Metabolic Syndrome.
Authors: Morales-Palomo, Ramirez-Jimenez, Ortega, Mora-Rodriguez
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2020
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Continuous and interval are the two types of aerobic exercise trening commonly used for health promotion. We sought to determine which aerobic exercise trening program results in larger health improvements in metabolic syndrome (MetS) individuals.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31473805
Title: Eccentric and concentric blood flow restriction resistance trening on indices of delayed onset muscle soreness in untrened women.
Authors: Hill, Housh, Smith, Keller, Schmidt
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2020
Topic: eccentric trening muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
Unaccustomed exercise can result in delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS), particularly as a result of the eccentric phase of the muscle contraction. Resistance trening combined with venous blood flow restriction (vBFR) may attenuate DOMS, but the available information in this regard is conflicting. Therefore, the purpose of this study was to examine the effects of low-load eccentric vBFR (Ecc-vBFR) and concentric vBFR (Con-vBFR) resistance trening on indices of DOMS.

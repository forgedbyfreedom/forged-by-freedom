Title: The effects of testosterone administration on muscle areas of the trunk and pelvic floor in hysterectomized women with low testosterone levels: proof-of-concept study.
Authors: Tapper, Huang, Pencina, Li, Arver
Journal: Menopause (New York, N.Y.)
Year: 2020
Topic: testosterone muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to determine the effect of testosterone administration on trunk and pelvic floor muscle area in women with low testosterone levels.

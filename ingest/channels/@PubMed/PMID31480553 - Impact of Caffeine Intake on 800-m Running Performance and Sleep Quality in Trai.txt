Title: Impact of Caffeine Intake on 800-m Running Performance and Sleep Quality in trened Runners.
Authors: Ramos-Campo, P&#xe9;rez, &#xc1;vila-Gand&#xed;a, P&#xe9;rez-Pi&#xf1;ero, Rubio-Arias
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2020
Topic: sleep athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Caffeine ingestion improves athletic performance, but impairs sleep quality. We aimed to analyze the effect of caffeine intake on 800-m running performance, sleep quality (SQ), and nocturnALCARdiac autonomic activity (CAA) in trened runners.

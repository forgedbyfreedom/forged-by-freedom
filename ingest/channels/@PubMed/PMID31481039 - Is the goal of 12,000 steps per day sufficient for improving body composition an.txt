PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31481039
Title: Is the goal of 12,000 steps per day sufficient for improving body composition and metabolic syndrome? The necessity of combining exercise intensity: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Chiang, Chen, Hsu, Lin, Wu
Journal: BMC public health
Year: 2019
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the differences in body composition and metabolic syndrome (MS) under a daily 12,000-step strategy with or without moderate-intensity walking exercise in college students with obesity.

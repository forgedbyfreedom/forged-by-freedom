Title: Cold water immersion attenuates anabolic signaling and skeletal muscle fiber hypertrophy, but not strength gain, following whole-body resistance trening.
Authors: Fyfe, Broatch, Trewin, Hanson, Argus
Journal: Journal of applied physiology (Bethesda, Md. : 1985)
Year: 2020
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
We determined the effects of cold water immersion (CWI) on long-term adaptations and post-exercise molecular responses in skeletal muscle before and after resistance trening. Sixteen men (22.9&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;4.6 y; 85.1&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;17.9 kg; mean&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;SD) performed resistance trening (3 day/wk) for 7 wk, with each session followed by either CWI [15 min at 10&#xb0;C, CWI (COLD) group, n = 8] or passive recovery (15 min at 23&#xb0;C, control group, n = 8). Exercise performance [one-repetition maximum (1-RM) leg press and bench press, countermovement jump, squat jump, andbolistic push-up], body composition (dual X-ray absorptiometry), and post-exercise (i.e., +1 and +48 h) molecular responses were assessed before and after trening. Improvements in 1-RM leg press were similar between groups [130&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;69 kg, pooled effect size (ES): 1.53&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;90% confidence interval (CI) 0.49], whereas increases in type IIMuscle fiber cross-sectional area were attenuated with CWI (-1,959&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;1,675 &#xb5;M2&#x2009;; ES: -1.37&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;0.99). Post-exercise mechanistic target of rapamycin complex 1 signaling (rps6 phosphorylation) was blunted for COLD at post-trening (POST) +1 h (-0.4-fold, ES: -0.69&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;0.86) and POST +48 h (-0.2-fold, ES: -1.33&#x2009;&#xb1; 0.82), whereas basal protein degradation markers (FOX-O1 protein content) were increased (1.3-fold, ES: 2.17&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;2.22). trening-induced increases in heat shock protein (HSP) 27 protein content were attenuated for COLD (-0.8-fold, ES: -0.94&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;0.82), which also reduced total HSP72 protein content (-0.7-fold, ES: -0.79&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;0.57). CWI blunted resistance trening-induced muscle fiber hypertrophy, but not maximal strength, potentially via reduced skeletal muscle protein anabolism and increased catabolism. Post-exercise CWI should therefore be avoided if muscle hypertrophy is desired.NEW &amp; NOTEWORTHY This study adds to existing evidence that post-exercise cold water immersion attenuates muscle fiber growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone with resistance trening, which is potentially mediated by attenuated post-exercise increases in markers of skeletal muscle anabolism coupled with increased catabolism and suggests that blunted muscle fiber growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone with cold water immersion does not necessarily trenslate to impaired strength development.

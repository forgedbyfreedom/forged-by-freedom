PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31524825
Title: Moderate-Intensity Continuous Training or High-Intensity Interval Training with or without Resistance Training for Altering Body Composition in Postmenopausal Women.
Authors: Dupuit, Rance, Morel, Bouillon, Pereira
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2020
Topic: menopause exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to compare body composition changes induced by moderate-intensity continuous training (MICT), high-intensity interval training (HIIT), or HIIT + resistance training (RT) programs (3 d&#xb7;wk, 12 wk) in overweight/obese postmenopausal women, and to determine whether fat mass reduction is related to greater fat oxidation (FatOx).

========================
PMID: 31584766
Title: Effects of high-intensity interval training compared to moderate-intensity continuous training on maximal oxygen consumption and blood pressure in healthy men: A randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Arboleda-Serna, Feito, Pati&#xf1;o-Villada, Vargas-Romero, Arango-V&#xe9;lez
Journal: Biomedica : revista del Instituto Nacional de Salud
Year: 2020
Topic: high intensity interval training

ABSTRACT:
Aerobic exercise generates increased cardiorespiratory fitness, which results in a protective factor for cardiovascular disease. High-intensity interval training (HIIT) might produce higher increases on cardiorespiratory fitness in comparison with moderate-intensity continuous training (MICT); however, current evidence is not conclusive.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31590121
Title: Sensorimotor trening for injury prevention in collegiate soccer players: An experimental study.
Authors: Reneker, Babl, Pannell, Adah, Flowers
Journal: Physical therapy in sport : official journal of the Association of Chartered Physiotherapists in Sports Medicine
Year: 2020
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Deliver a sensorimotor trening intervention; quantify the change in clinical measurements of sensorimotor control; and compare injury rate to a historical control.

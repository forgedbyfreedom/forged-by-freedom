PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31640308
Title: Burnout syndrome in athletes and their association with body image dissatisfaction at a private university.
Authors: Baella-Vigil, Hurtado-Bocanegra, Marroqu&#xed;n-Quintana, Rojas-Fern&#xe1;ndez, Rosales-Medina
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2020
Topic: body image athletes

ABSTRACT:
Burnout syndrome, from the sporting point of view, is the integration of both physical and emotional signs, caused by the high demands in competition. According to several studies, the prevalence of burnout syndrome is influenced by several factors that would lead to athlete's body image dissatisfaction.

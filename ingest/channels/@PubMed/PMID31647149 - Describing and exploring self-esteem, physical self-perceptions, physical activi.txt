PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31647149
Title: Describing and exploring self-esteem, physical self-perceptions, physical activity and self-efficacy in adolescent and young adult cancer survivors.
Authors: Wurz, Brunet
Journal: European journal of cancer care
Year: 2020
Topic: self efficacy sports performance

ABSTRACT:
(a) Describe levels of physical and global self-esteem and (b) explore the relationships between these types of self-esteem and physical activity (PA) behaviour, self-efficacy for PA and physical self-perceptions among adolescent and young adult (AYA) cancer survivors.

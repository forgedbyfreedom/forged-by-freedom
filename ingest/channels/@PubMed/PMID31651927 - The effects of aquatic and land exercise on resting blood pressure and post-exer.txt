PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31651927
Title: The effects of aquatic and land exercise on resting blood pressure and post-exercise hypotension response in elderly hypertensives.
Authors: J&#xfa;nior, Gomes, da Silva, Souza, Oliveira
Journal: Cardiovascular journal of Africa
Year: 2020
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
This study compared resting blood pressure (BP) using ambulatory BP monitoring (ABPM) responses in two groups of subjects trened in land exercise (LE) and aquatic exercise (AE), and assessed post-exercise hypotension (PEH) using ABPM, after land- and aquatic-based exercises.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31682536
Title: Barriers to Weight Loss and Physical Activity, and Coach Approaches to Addressing Barriers, in a Real-World Adaptation of the DPP Lifestyle Intervention: A Process Analysis.
Authors: Delahanty, Trief, Cibula, Weinstock
Journal: The Diabetes educator
Year: 2020
Topic: metabolic adaptation weight loss

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study is to identify barriers to weight loss and physical activity, as well as approaches used by coaches, in a real-world, community sample of adults with metabolic syndrome (at risk for type 2 diabetes) who participated in a Diabetes Prevention Program (DPP)-adapted weight loss intervention and compare findings to data from the screened and highly selected DPP sample.

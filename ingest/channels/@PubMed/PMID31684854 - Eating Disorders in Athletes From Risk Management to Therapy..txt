PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31684854
Title: Eating Disorders in Athletes: From Risk Management to Therapy.
Authors: Milano, Milano, Capasso
Journal: Endocrine, metabolic &amp; immune disorders drug targets
Year: 2020
Topic: eating disorders athletes

ABSTRACT:
Balanced sporting activity should be considered a resource in the treatment of eating disorders (ED), in particular of the BED and in obesity and, if conducted and guided by expert preparers and rehabilitators, in some forms of anorexia and in bulimia.

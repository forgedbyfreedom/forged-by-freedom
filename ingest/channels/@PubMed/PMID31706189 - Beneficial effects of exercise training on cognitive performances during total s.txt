PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31706189
Title: Beneficial effects of exercise trening on cognitive performances during total sleep deprivation in healthy subjects.
Authors: Sauvet, Arnal, Tardo-Dino, Drogou, Van Beers
Journal: Sleep medicine
Year: 2020
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Exercise treninGHas been shown to improve learning and memory, and to protect against the negative impact of sleep deprivation. The aim of this study was to investigate the effects of seven weeks of moderate- and high-intensity interval exercise trening on vigilance/sustained attention, inhibition processes and working memory during 40-h total sleep deprivation (TSD) in 16 healthy young men.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31713177
Title: Protein Supplementation in Sport: Source, Timing, and Intended Benefits.
Authors: Huecker, Sarav, Pearlman, Laster
Journal: Current nutrition reports
Year: 2020
Topic: nutrient timing exercise

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this review is to provide background on the present literature regarding the utility and effectiveness of protein supplements, including protein source and nutrient timing.

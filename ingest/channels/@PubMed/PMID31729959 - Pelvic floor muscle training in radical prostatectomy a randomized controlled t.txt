PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31729959
Title: Pelvic floor muscle trening in radical prostatectomy: a randomized controlled trial of the impacts on pelvic floor muscle function and urinary incontinence.
Authors: Milios, Ackland, Green
Journal: BMC urology
Year: 2020
Topic: muscle recovery post exercise

ABSTRACT:
Pelvic floor muscle trening (PFM) trening for post-prostatectomy incontinence (PPI) is an important rehabilitative approach, but the evidence base is still evolving. We developed a novel PFM trening program focussed on activating fast and slow twitch muscle fibres. We hypothesized that this trening, which commenced pre-operatively, would improve PFM function and reduce PPI, when compared to a control group.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31732460
Title: Combined Aerobic and Resistance trening for Cardiorespiratory Fitness, Muscle Strength, and Walking Capacity after Stroke: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Lee, Stone
Journal: Journal of stroke and cerebrovascular diseases : the official journal of National Stroke Association
Year: 2020
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Cardiorespiratory fitness, measured as peak oxygen consumption, is a potent predictor of stroke risk. Muscle weakness is the most prominent impairment after stroke and is directly associated with reduced walking capacity. There is a lack of recommendations for optimal combined aerobic trening and resistance trening for those patients. The purpose of this study was to systematically review and quantify the effects of exercise trening on cardiorespiratory fitness, muscle strength, and walking capacity after stroke.

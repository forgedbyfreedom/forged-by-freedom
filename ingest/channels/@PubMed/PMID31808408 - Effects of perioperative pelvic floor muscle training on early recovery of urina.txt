PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31808408
Title: Effects of perioperative pelvic floor muscle training on early recovery of urinary continence and erectile function in men undergoing radical prostatectomy: a randomized clinical trial.
Authors: de Lira, Fornari, Cardoso, Aranchipe, Kretiska
Journal: International braz j urol : official journal of the Brazilian Society of Urology
Year: 2020
Topic: muscle recovery post exercise

ABSTRACT:
Radical prostatectomy (RP) can result in urinary incontinence (UI) and erectile dysfunction (ED), which negatively impact quality of life (QoL). This study aimed to evaluate the effects of a perioperative pelvic floor muscle training (PFMT) program versus usual care on early recovery of urinary continence and erectile function after RP.

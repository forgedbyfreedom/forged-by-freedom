Title: Bioactive growth hormone in humans: Controversies, complexities and concepts.
Authors: Hymer, Kennett, Maji, Gosselink, McCall
Journal: Growth hormone &amp; IGF research : official journal of the Growth Hormone Research Society and the International IGF Research Society
Year: 2021
Topic: growth hormone exercise

ABSTRACT:
To revisit a finding, first described in 1978, which documented existence of a pituitary growth factor that escaped detection by immunoassay, but which was active in the established rat tibia GH bioassay.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31836346
Title: Heart rate and heart rate variability in emergency medicine.
Authors: Jenks, Frank Peacock, Cornelius, Shafer, Pillow
Journal: The American journal of emergency medicine
Year: 2020
Topic: heart rate variability training

ABSTRACT:
Tachycardia may be indicative of mental stress, which in turn can decrease performance, reduce information processing capacity, and hinder memory recall. The objective of this study is to examine heart rate trends present among emergency medicine trainees over a standard emergency room shift to measure the frequency and severity of stress experienced while on shift.

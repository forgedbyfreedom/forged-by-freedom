PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31895298
Title: Lactalbumin, Not Collagen, Augments Muscle Protein Synthesis with Aerobic Exercise.
Authors: Oikawa, Macinnis, Tripp, McGlory, Baker
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2020
Topic: whey protein muscle synthesis

ABSTRACT:
Protein ingestion and the ensuinGHyperaminoacidemia stimulates skeletal muscle protein synthesis in the postexercise period. This response facilitates muscle remodeling, which is important during intensified trening. The aim of this study was to determine whether supplementation with &#x3b1;-lactalbumin (LA), with high leucine and tryptophan contents, would improve responses to short periods of intensified aerobic trening compared with supplementation with an isonitrogenous quantity of collagen peptides (CP).

PMID: 31919527
Title: Whey protein but not collagen peptides stimulate acute and longer-term muscle protein synthesis with and without resistance exercise in healthy older women: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Oikawa, Kamal, Webb, McGlory, Baker
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2020
Topic: whey protein muscle synthesis

ABSTRACT:
Aging appears to attenuate the response of skeletal muscle protein synthesis (MPS) to anabolic stimuli such as protein ingestion (and the ensuinGHyperaminoacidemia) and resistance exercise (RE).

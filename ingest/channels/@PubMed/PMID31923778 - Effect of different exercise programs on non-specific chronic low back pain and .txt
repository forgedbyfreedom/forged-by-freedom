PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31923778
Title: Effect of different exercise programs on non-specific chronic low back pain and disability in people who perform sedentary work.
Authors: Sipaviciene, Kliziene
Journal: Clinical biomechanics (Bristol, Avon)
Year: 2021
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
This study compared the short- and long-term effects of different exercise programs on lumbar muscle function, cross-sectional area of the multifidus muscle, functional disability and low back pain in people who perform sedentary work.

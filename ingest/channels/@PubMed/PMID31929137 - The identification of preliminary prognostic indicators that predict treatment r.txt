PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31929137
Title: The identification of preliminary prognostic indicators that predict treatment response for exercise therapy in patients with nonspecific chronic low back pain: A multiple-arm cohort study design.
Authors: Denteneer, Van Daele, De Hertogh, Truijen, Deckers
Journal: Journal of back and musculoskeletal rehabilitation
Year: 2021
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
For patients with nonspecific chronic low back pain (CLBP), exercise therapy is stated to be the most effective intervention strategy but it is unclear which kind of exercise therapy is most beneficial.

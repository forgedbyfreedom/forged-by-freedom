PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31944339
Title: Is physical exercise and dietary therapy a feasible alternative to cognitive behavior therapy in treatment of eating disorders? A randomized controlled trial of two group therapies.
Authors: Mathisen, Rosenvinge, Friborg, Vrabel, Bratland-Sanda
Journal: The International journal of eating disorders
Year: 2020
Topic: cognitive behavioral therapy athletes

ABSTRACT:
To compare effects of physical exercise and dietary therapy (PED-t) to cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) in treatment of bulimia nervosa (BN) and binge-eating disorder (BED).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 31960071
Title: Vascular improvements in individuals with type 2 diabetes following a 1&#xa0;year randomised controlled exercise intervention, irrespective of changes in cardiorespiratory fitness.
Authors: Hetherington-Rauth, Magalh&#xe3;es, J&#xfa;dice, Melo, Sardinha
Journal: Diabetologia
Year: 2021
Topic: type 2 diabetes exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Vascular changes in individuals with type 2 diabetes mellitus majorly contribute to the development of cardiovascular disease. Increased cardiorespiratory fitness (CRF) has been associated with improvements in vascular health. Although CRF tends to improve with exercise trening, there remains a portion of participants with little or no improvement. Given the importance of vascular function in individuals with type 2 diabetes, we assessed whether individuals who failed to improve CRF following a 1&#xa0;year exercise intervention also failed to improve arterial stiffness and structural indices.

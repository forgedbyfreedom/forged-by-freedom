PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31960436
Title: Resistance training-induced muscle hypertrophy is mediated by TGF-&#x3b2;1-Smad signaling pathway in male Wistar rats.
Authors: Nikooie, Jafari-Sardoie, Sheibani, Nejadvaziri Chatroudi
Journal: Journal of cellular physiology
Year: 2021
Topic: resistance training muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
The TGF-&#x3b2;1-Smad pathway is a well-known negative regulator of muscle growth; however, its potential role in resistance training-induced muscle hypertrophy is not clear. The present study proposed to determine whether and how this pathway may be involved in resistance training-induced muscle hypertrophy. Skeletal muscle samples were collected from the control, trained (RT), control&#x2009;+&#x2009;SB431542 (CITGF ), and trained&#x2009;+&#x2009;SB431542 (RTITGF ) animals following 3, 5, and 8 weeks of resistance training. Inhibition of the TGF-&#x3b2;1-Smad pathway by SB431542 augmented muscle satellite cells activation, upregulated Akt/mTOR/S6K1 pathway, and attenuated FOXO1 and FOXO3a expression in the CITGF group (all p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;.01), thereby causing significant muscle hypertrophy in animals from the CITGF . Resistance training significantly decreased muscle TGF-&#x3b2;1 expression and Smad3 (P-Smad3S423/425 ) phosphorylation at COOH-terminal residues, augmented Smad2 (P-Smad2-LS245/250/255 ) and Smad3 (P-Smad3-LSer208 ) phosphorylation levels at linker sites (all p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;.01), and led to a muscle hypertrophy which was unaffected by SB431542, suggesting that the TGF-&#x3b2;1-Smad signaling pathway is involved in resistance training-induced muscle hypertrophy. The effects of inhibiting the TGF-&#x3b2;1-Smad signaling pathway were not additive to the resistance training effects on FOXO1 and FOXO3a expression, muscle satellite cells activation, and the Akt/mTOR/S6K1 pathway. Resistance training effect of satellite cell differentiation was independent of the TGF-&#x3b2;1-Smad signaling pathway. These results suggested that the effect of the TGF-&#x3b2;1-Smad signaling pathway on resistance training-induced muscle hypertrophy can be attributed mainly to its diminished inhibitory effects on satellite cell activation and protein synthesis. Suppressed P-Smad3S423/425 and enhanced P-Smad2-LS245/250/255 and P-Smad3-LSer208 are the molecular mechanisms that link the TGF-&#x3b2;1-Smad signaling pathway to resistance training-induced muscle hypertrophy.

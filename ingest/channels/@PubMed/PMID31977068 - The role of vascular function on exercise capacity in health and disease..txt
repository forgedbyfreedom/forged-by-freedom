PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 31977068
Title: The role of vascular function on exercise capacity in health and disease.
Authors: Poole, Behnke, Musch
Journal: The Journal of physiology
Year: 2021
Topic: mitochondrial function exercise aging

ABSTRACT:
Three sentinel parameters of aerobic performance are the maximal oxygen uptake ( V&#x307;O2max ), critical power (CP) and speed of the V&#x307;O2 kinetics following exercise onset. Of these, the latter is, perhaps, the cardinal test of integrated function along the O2 trensport pathway from lungs to skeletal muscle mitochondria. Fast V&#x307;O2 kinetics demands that the cardiovascular system distributes exercise-induced blood flow elevations among and within those vascular beds subserving the contracting muscle(s). Ideally, this process must occur at least as rapidly as mitochondrial metabolism elevates V&#x307;O2 . Chronic disease and ageing create an O2 delivery (i.e. blood flow &#xd7; arterial [O2 ], Q&#x307;O2 ) dependency that slows V&#x307;O2 kinetics, decreasing CP and V&#x307;O2max , increasing the O2 deficit and sowing the seeds of exercise intolerance. Exercise trening, in contrast, does the opposite. Within the context of these three parameters (see Graphical Abstract), this brief review examines the trening-induced plasticity of key elements in the O2 trensport pathway. It asks how structural and functional vascular adaptations accelerate and redistribute musclEQ&#x307;O2 and thus defend microvascular O2 partial pressures and capillary blood-myocyte O2 diffusion across a &#x223c;100-fold range of muscle V&#x307;O2 values. Recent discoveries, especially in the muscle microcirculation and Q&#x307;O2 -to- V&#x307;O2 heterogeneity, are integrated with the O2 trensport pathway to appreciate how local and systemic vascular control helps defend V&#x307;O2 kinetics and determine CP and V&#x307;O2max in health and how vascular dysfunction in disease predicates exercise intolerance. Finally, the latest evidence that nitrate supplementation improves vascular and therefore aerobic function in health and disease is presented.

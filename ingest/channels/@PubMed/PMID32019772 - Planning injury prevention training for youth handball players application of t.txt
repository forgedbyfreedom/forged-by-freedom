PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32019772
Title: Planning injury prevention trening for youth handball players: application of the generalisable six-step intervention development process.
Authors: Ageberg, Bunke, Nilsen, Donaldson
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2021
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Youth handball players are vulnerable to injuries. Because there is no available injury prevention trening specifically developed for youth handball players targeting both upper and lower limbs or incorporating psychological aspects of injury, we undertook the 'Implementing injury Prevention trening ROutines in TEams and Clubs in youth Team handball (I-PROTECT)' project. We used an ecological participatory design incorporating the perspectives of multiple stakeholders (health beneficiaries, programme deliverers and policy makers). The aim of this paper was to describe the process of developing the I-PROTECT model, featuring injury prevention trening and an accompanying implementation strategy.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32032568
Title: Analytical and biological assessment of circulating human erythroferrone.
Authors: Appleby, Chew-Harris, Troughton, Richards, Pemberton
Journal: Clinical biochemistry
Year: 2021
Topic: peptide hormones performance

ABSTRACT:
Erythroferrone (ERFE) is an erythroid hormone putatively involved in stress erythropoiesis. Its regional clearance and circulating form in humans, as well as levels in normal health and coronary disease remain unclear.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32035479
Title: Lower-limb muscle strength: normative data from an observational population-based study.
Authors: Pasco, Stuart, Holloway-Kew, Tembo, Sui
Journal: BMC musculoskeletal disorders
Year: 2020
Topic: aging muscle mass strength

ABSTRACT:
The extent of muscle deterioration associated with ageing or disease can be quantified by comparison with appropriate reference data. The objective of this study is to present normative data for lower-limb muscle strength and quality for 573 males and 923 females aged 20-97&#x2009;yr participating in the Geelong Osteoporosis Study in southeastern Australia.

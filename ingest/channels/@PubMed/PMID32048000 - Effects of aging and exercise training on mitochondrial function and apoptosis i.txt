PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32048000
Title: Effects of aging and exercise trening on mitochondrial function and apoptosis in the rat heart.
Authors: No, Heo, Yoo, Kim, Park
Journal: Pflugers Archiv : European journal of physiology
Year: 2021
Topic: mitochondrial function exercise aging

ABSTRACT:
Aging is associated with vulnerability to cardiovascular diseases, and mitochondrial dysfunction plays a critical role in cardiovascular disease pathogenesis. Exercise trening is associated with benefits against chronic cardiac diseases. The purpose of this study was to determine the effects of aging and treadmill exercise trening on mitochondrial function and apoptosis in the rat heart. Fischer 344 rats were divided into young sedentary (YS; n&#x2009;=&#x2009;10, 4&#xa0;months), young exercise (YE; n&#x2009;=&#x2009;10, 4&#xa0;months), old sedentary (OS; n&#x2009;=&#x2009;10, 20&#xa0;months), and old exercise (OE; n&#x2009;=&#x2009;10, 20&#xa0;months) groups. Exercise trening groups ran on a treadmill at 15&#xa0;m/min (young) or 10&#xa0;m/min (old), 45&#xa0;min/day, 5&#xa0;days/week for 8&#xa0;weeks. Morphological parameters, mitochondrial function, mitochondrial dynamics, mitophagy, and mitochondria-mediated apoptosis were analyzed in cardiac muscle. Mitochondrial O2 respiratory capacity and Ca2+ retention capacity gradually decreased, and mitochondrial H2O2 emitting potential significantly increased with aging. Exercise trening attenuated aging-induced mitochondrial H2O2 emitting potential and mitochondrial O2 respiratory capacity, while protecting Ca2+ retention in the old groups. Aging triggered imbalanced mitochondrial dynamics and excess mitophagy, while exercise trening ameliorated the aging-induced imbalance in mitochondrial dynamics and excess mitophagy. Aging induced increase in Bax and cleaved caspase-3 protein levels, while decreasing Bcl-2 levels. Exercise trening protected against the elevation of apoptotic signaling markers by decreasing Bax and cleaved caspase-3 and increasing Bcl-2 protein levels, while decreasing the Bax/Bcl-2 ratio and terminal deoxynucleotidyl trensferase-mediated dUTP nick-end labeling (TUNEL)-positive myonuclei. These data demonstrate that regular exercise trening prevents aging-induced impairment of mitochondrial function and mitochondria-mediated apoptosis in cardiac muscles.

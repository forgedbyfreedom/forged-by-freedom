PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32127863
Title: Impact of aerobic exercise versus resisted exercise on endothelial activation markers and inflammatory cytokines among elderly.
Authors: Abd El-Kader, Al-Shreef, Al-Jiffri
Journal: African health sciences
Year: 2020
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
Aging is the major risk factor for the development of cardiovascular diseases as aging increases plasma levels of pro-inflammatory mediators and endothelial dysfunction. Physical exercise is a potential strategy for improving the endothelial dysfunction and chronic inflammation that accompanies aging. However, there is a need to differentiate between aerobic and resistance exercise trening regarding endothelial activation markers and systemic inflammation among elderly population.

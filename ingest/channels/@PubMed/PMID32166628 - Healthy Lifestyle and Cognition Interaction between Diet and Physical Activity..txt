PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32166628
Title: Healthy Lifestyle and Cognition: Interaction between Diet and Physical Activity.
Authors: Serra, Dondero, Larkins, Burns, Addison
Journal: Current nutrition reports
Year: 2021
Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
This review discusses current research on the impact of specific dietary patterns and exercise, both individually and combined, on cognitive function in older adults.

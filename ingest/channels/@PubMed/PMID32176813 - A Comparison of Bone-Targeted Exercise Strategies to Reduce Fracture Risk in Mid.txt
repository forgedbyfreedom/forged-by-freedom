PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 32176813
Title: A Comparison of Bone-Targeted Exercise Strategies to Reduce Fracture Risk in Middle-Aged and Older Men with Osteopenia and Osteoporosis: LIFTMOR-M Semi-Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Harding, Weeks, Lambert, Watson, Weis
Journal: Journal of bone and mineral research : the official journal of the American Society for Bone and Mineral Research
Year: 2021
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The Lifting Intervention For trening Muscle and Osteoporosis Rehabilitation for Men (LIFTMOR-M) trial examined efficacy and safety of two novel exercise programs in older men with low BMD. Men with low hip and/or LS BMD were randomized to high-intensity progressive resistance and impact trening (HiRIT) or machine-based isometric axial compression (IAC) and compared to a nonrandomized matched control (CON). Outcomes included: hip and LS BMD; calcaneal ultrasound parameters; anthropometry; body composition; function (timed up-and-go [TUG], five-times sit-to-stand [FTSTS]); back extensor strength (BES); leg extensor strength (LES); compliance and adverse events. Ninety-three men (67.1 &#xb1; 7.5 years; 82.1 &#xb1; 11.6 kg; 175.2 &#xb1; 6.7 cm; FN T-score -1.6 &#xb1; 0.6) were randomized to HiRIT (n = 34) or IAC (n = 33), or allocated to CON (n = 26). HiRIT improved trochanteric BMD (2.8 &#xb1; 0.8%; -0.1 &#xb1; 0.9%, p = .024), LS BMD (4.1 &#xb1; 0.7%; 0.9 &#xb1; 0.8%, p = .003), BUA (2.2 &#xb1; 0.7%; -0.8 &#xb1; 0.9%, p = .009), stiffness index (1.6 &#xb1; 0.9%; -2.0 &#xb1; 1.1%, p = .011), lean mass (1.5 &#xb1; 0.8%; -2.4 &#xb1; 0.9%, p = .002), TUG, FTSTS, BES, and LES (p &lt; .05) compared with CON. IAC improved lean mass (0.8 &#xb1; 0.8%; -2.4 &#xb1; 0.9%, p = .013) and FTSTS (-4.5 &#xb1; 1.6%; 7.5 &#xb1; 2.0%, p &lt; .001) compared with CON. HiRIT improved LS BMD (4.1 &#xb1; 0.7%; 2.0 &#xb1; 0.7%, p = .039), stiffness index (1.6 &#xb1; 0.9%; -1.3 &#xb1; 0.9%, p = .025), and FTSTS (-10.7 &#xb1; 1.6%; -4.5 &#xb1; 1.7%, p = .010) compared with IAC. Exercise compliance was high (HiRIT 77.8 &#xb1; 16.6%; IAC 78.5 &#xb1; 14.8%, p = .872). There were five minor adverse events (HiRIT, 2; IAC, 3). HiRIT was well-tolerated and improved bone, function and fracture risk more than CON or IAC. &#xa9; 2020 American Society for Bone and Mineral Research.

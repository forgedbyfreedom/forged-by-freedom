PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32195768
Title: Casein Ingestion Does Not Increase Muscle Connective Tissue Protein Synthesis Rates.
Authors: Trommelen, Holwerda, Senden, Goessens, VAN Kranenburg
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2021
Topic: muscle protein synthesis exercise

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to assess the effect of dietary protein ingestion on intramuscular connective tissue protein synthesis rates during overnight recovery from a single bout of resistance exercise.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32210043
Title: Meditation Practice Improves Short-Term Changes in Heart Rate Variability.
Authors: Chang, Wu Chueh, Lai
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Year: 2020
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
It is well known that meditation improves the physical and psychological condition of its practitioners. This study investigated the heart rate variability response of meditation practitioners in two Chan master teaching environments, namely face-to-face and video classes.

Title: Effectiveness of non-pharmacological interventions on the management of sarcopenic obesity: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Yin, Liu, V&#xe4;lim&#xe4;ki
Journal: Experimental gerontology
Year: 2021
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
Sarcopenic obesity is a combination of both sarcopenia and obesity, which potentiate each other and maximize the negative influences of each, such as physical disability, morbidity, or even mortality.

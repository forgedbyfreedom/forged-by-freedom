PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32245197
Title: The Effect of Whey Protein Supplementation on Myofibrillar Protein Synthesis and Performance Recovery in Resistance-trened Men.
Authors: Davies, Bass, Carson, Norton, Kozior
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2020
Topic: whey protein muscle synthesis

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to investigate the effect of whey protein supplementation on myofibrillar protein synthesis (myoPS) and muscle recovery over a 7-d period of intensified resistance trening (RT).

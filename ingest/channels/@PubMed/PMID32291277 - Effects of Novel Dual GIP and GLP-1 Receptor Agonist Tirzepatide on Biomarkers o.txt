PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32291277
Title: Effects of Novel Dual GIP and GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Tirzepatide on Biomarkers of Nonalcoholic Steatohepatitis in Patients With Type 2 Diabetes.
Authors: Hartman, Sanyal, Loomba, Wilson, Nikooienejad
Journal: Diabetes care
Year: 2021
Topic: tirzepatide diabetes

ABSTRACT:
To determine the effect of tirzepatide, a dual agonist of glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide and glucagon-like peptide 1 receptors, on biomarkers of nonalcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH) and fibrosis in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM).

PMID: 32325024
Title: Deconstructing athletes' sleep: A systematic review of the influence of age, sex, athletic expertise, sport type, and season on sleep characteristics.
Authors: Vlahoyiannis, Aphamis, Bogdanis, Sakkas, Andreou
Journal: Journal of sport and health science
Year: 2021
Topic: sleep athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
This systematic review aimed to describe objective sleep parameters for athletes under different conditions and address potential sleep issues in this specific population.

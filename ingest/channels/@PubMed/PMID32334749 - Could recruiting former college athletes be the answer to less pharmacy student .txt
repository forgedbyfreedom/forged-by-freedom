PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32334749
Title: Could recruiting former college athletes be the answer to less pharmacy student burnout?
Authors: Clay, Pamulapati, Van Tassell, Koenig, Ogbonna
Journal: Currents in pharmacy teaching &amp; learning
Year: 2021
Topic: burnout athletes prevention

ABSTRACT:
Student pharmacists are in a notable wellness deficit. Pharmacy organizations are issuing statements and providing resources addressing efforts to increase student wellness. This commentary suggests that institutions refocus recruiting efforts on students with experience balancing the demands of school, wellness, and mental health.

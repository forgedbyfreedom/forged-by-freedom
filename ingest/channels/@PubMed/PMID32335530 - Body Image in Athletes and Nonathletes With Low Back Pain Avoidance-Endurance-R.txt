PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32335530
Title: Body Image in Athletes and Nonathletes With Low Back Pain: Avoidance-Endurance-Related Subgroups and Sports Status Play a Role.
Authors: Levenig, Kellmann, Kleinert, Belz, Hesselmann
Journal: Journal of sport rehabilitation
Year: 2021
Topic: body image athletes

ABSTRACT:
Low back pain (LBP) is a serious health problem, both in the general population as well as in athletes. Research has shown that psychosocial aspects, such as dysfunctional pain responses, play a significant role in the chronification of LBP. Recent research supports the relevance of the multidisciplinary concept of body image in the interpretation of LBP.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32349356
Title: Benefits of Creatine Supplementation for Vegetarians Compared to Omnivorous Athletes: A Systematic Review.
Authors: Kaviani, Shaw, Chilibeck
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Year: 2020
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
Creatine monohydrate is a nutritional supplement often consumed by athletes in anaerobic sports. Creatine is naturally found in most meat products; therefore, vegetarians have reduced creatine stores and may benefit from supplementation.

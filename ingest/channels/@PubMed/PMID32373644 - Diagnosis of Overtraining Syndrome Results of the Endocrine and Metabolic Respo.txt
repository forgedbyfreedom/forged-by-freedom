PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32373644
Title: Diagnosis of Overtraining Syndrome: Results of the Endocrine and Metabolic Responses on Overtraining Syndrome Study: EROS-DIAGNOSIS.
Authors: Cadegiani, da Silva, Abrao, Kater
Journal: Journal of sports medicine (Hindawi Publishing Corporation)
Year: 2022
Topic: overtraining syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Overtraining syndrome (OTS), a common dysfunction among elite athletes, causes decreased performance and fatigue and has no standardized diagnostic criteria. The Endocrine and Metabolic Responses on Overtraining Syndrome (EROS) study identified more than 45 potential biomarkers of OTS. In the present study, we hypothesized that combinations of these biomarkers could be an accurate diagnostic tool for OTS.

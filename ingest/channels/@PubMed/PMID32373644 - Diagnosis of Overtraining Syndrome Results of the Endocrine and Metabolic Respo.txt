PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32373644
Title: Diagnosis of Overtrening Syndrome: Results of the Endocrine and Metabolic Responses on Overtrening Syndrome Study: EROS-DIAGNOSIS.
Authors: Cadegiani, da Silva, Abrao, Kater
Journal: Journal of sports medicine (Hindawi Publishing Corporation)
Year: 2022
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Overtrening syndrome (OTS), a common dysfunction among elite athletes, causes decreased performance and fatigue and has no standardized diagnostic criteria. The Endocrine and Metabolic Responses on Overtrening Syndrome (EROS) study identified more than 45 potential biomarkers of OTS. In the present study, we hypothesized that combinations of these biomarkers could be an accurate diagnostic tool for OTS.

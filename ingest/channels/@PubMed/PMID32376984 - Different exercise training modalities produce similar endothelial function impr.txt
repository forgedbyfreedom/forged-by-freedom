PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32376984
Title: Different exercise trening modalities produce similar endothelial function improvements in individuals with prehypertension or hypertension: a randomized clinical trial Exercise, endothelium and blood pressure.
Authors: Pedralli, Marschner, Kollet, Neto, Eibel
Journal: Scientific reports
Year: 2020
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
Endothelial dysfunction is a characteristic of systemic arterial hypertension (SAH) and an early marker of atherosclerosis. Aerobic exercise trening (AT) improves endothelial function. However, the effects of resistance trening (RT) and combined trening (CT) on endothelial function remain controversial in individuals with SAH. We determined the effects of AT, RT, and CT on endothelial function and systolic (SBP)/diastolic blood pressure (DBP) in individuals with prehypertension or hypertension. Forty-two participants (54&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;11&#x2009;y, resting SBP/DBP 137&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;9/86&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;6&#x2009;mmHg) were randomly allocated into AT (n&#x2009;=&#x2009;14, 40&#x2009;min of cycling, 50-75% heart rate reserve), RT (n&#x2009;=&#x2009;14, 6 resistance exercises, 4 &#xd7; 12 repetitions, 60% maximum strength) and CT (n&#x2009;=&#x2009;14, 2 &#xd7; 12 repetitions of RT&#x2009;+&#x2009;20&#x2009;min of AT). All participants performed a 40-minute exercise session twice a week for 8 weeks. Endothelial function was evaluated by brachial artery flow-mediated dilation (FMD). Blood pressure was evaluated through ambulatory monitoring for 24&#x2009;hours. After 8 weeks of exercise trening, blood pressure was reduced in all 3 groups: -5.1&#x2009;mmHg in SBP (95%CI -10.1, 0.0; p&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.003) in AT; -4.0&#x2009;mmHg in SBP (95%CI -7.8, -0.5; p&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.027) in RT; and -3.2&#x2009;mmHg in DBP (95%CI -7.9, 1.5; p&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.001) in CT. All 3 exercise trening modalities produced similar improvements in FMD:&#x2009;+&#x2009;3.2% (95%CI 1.7, 4.6) (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001) in AT;&#x2009;+&#x2009;4.0% (95%CI 2.1, 5.7) (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001) in RT; and +6.8% (95%CI 2.6, 11.1) (p&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.006) in CT. In conclusion, different exercise trening modalities were similarly effective in improving endothelial function but impacts on ambulatory blood pressure appear to be variable in individuals with prehypertension or hypertension.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32390502
Title: Exercise trening and cognitive performance in persons with multiple sclerosis: A systematic review and multilevel meta-analysis of clinical trials.
Authors: Gharakhanlou, Wesselmann, Rademacher, Lampit, Negaresh
Journal: Multiple sclerosis (Houndmills, Basingstoke, England)
Year: 2022
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
Cognitive impairment is common, debilitating, and poorly managed in persons with multiple sclerosis (pwMS). Exercise trening might have positive effects on cognitive performance in pwMS, yet the overall magnitude, heterogeneity, and potential moderators remain unclear.

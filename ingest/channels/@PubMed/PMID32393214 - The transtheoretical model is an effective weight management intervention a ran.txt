PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32393214
Title: The trenstheoretical model is an effective weight management intervention: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: de Freitas, de Menezes, Dos Santos, Pimenta, Ferreira
Journal: BMC public health
Year: 2020
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Given the current worldwide epidemic of obesity, there is a demand for interventions with higher impact, such as those carried out in the primary health care (PHC) setting. Here we evaluate the effect of intervention performed according to the stages of change of the trenstheoretical model (TTM) for weight management.

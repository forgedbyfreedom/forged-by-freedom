PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32402758
Title: An intervention based on the health belief model led to improvements in self-efficacy towards ERIPP participation and functional performance in club sport athletes: a pilot study.
Authors: Gabriel, McCann, Hoch, Cramer
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2021
Topic: self efficacy sports performance

ABSTRACT:
To determine if an intervention based on the Health Belief Model (HBM) could improve attitudes towards exercise-related injury prevention program (ERIPP) participation and functional performance in club sport participants.

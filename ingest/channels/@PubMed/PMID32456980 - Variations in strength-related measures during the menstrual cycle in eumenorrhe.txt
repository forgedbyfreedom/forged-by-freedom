PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32456980
Title: Variations in strength-related measures during the menstrual cycle in eumenorrheic women: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Blagrove, Bruinvels, Pedlar
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2021
Topic: progesterone athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
To systematically review the current body of research that has investigated changes in strength-related variables during different phases of the menstrual cycle in eumenorrheic women.

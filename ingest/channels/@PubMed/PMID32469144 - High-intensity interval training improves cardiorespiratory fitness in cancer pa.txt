PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32469144
Title: High-intensity interval trening improves cardiorespiratory fitness in cancer patients and survivors: A meta-analysis.
Authors: Wallen, Hennessy, Brown, Evans, Rawstorn
Journal: European journal of cancer care
Year: 2021
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
The primary objective of this systematic review and meta-analysis was to compare the effects of high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) versus moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT) and usuALCARe (UC) on cardiorespiratory fitness (peak V&#x307;O2 ) in cancer patients and survivors. Secondary objectives were to compare the effects of HIIT versus MICT and UC on other cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET) indices. Safety and adherence to HIIT were also evaluated.

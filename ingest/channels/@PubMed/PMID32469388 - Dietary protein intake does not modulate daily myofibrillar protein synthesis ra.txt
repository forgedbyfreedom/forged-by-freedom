PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32469388
Title: Dietary protein intake does not modulate daily myofibrillar protein synthesis rates or loss of muscle mass and function during short-term immobilization in young men: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Kilroe, Fulford, Jackman, Holwerda, Gijsen
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2021
Topic: protein intake muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Short-term (&lt;1 wk) muscle disuse lowers daily myofibrillar protein synthesis (MyoPS) rates resulting in muscle mass loss. The understanding of how daily dietary protein intake influences such muscle deconditioning requires further investigation.

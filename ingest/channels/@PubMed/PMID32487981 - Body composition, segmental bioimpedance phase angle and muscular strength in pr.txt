PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32487981
Title: Body composition, segmental bioimpedance phase angle and muscular strength in professional volleyball players compared to a control group.
Authors: Di Vincenzo, Marra, Sammarco, Speranza, Cioffi
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2020
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
The assessment of body composition is central in evaluating athletes' nutritional status and the effects of training. The aim of this study was to evaluate body composition and the relation between bioimpedance phase angle (PhA) and muscular strength in elite female volleyball players.

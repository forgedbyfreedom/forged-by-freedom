PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32492705
Title: Moderate-Intensity Exercise and High-Intensity Interval trening Affect insulin Sensitivity Similarly in Obese Adults.
Authors: Ryan, Schleh, Ahn, Ludzki, Gillen
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2021
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
We compared the effects of high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) and moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT) on insulin sensitivity and other important metabolic adaptations in adults with obesity.

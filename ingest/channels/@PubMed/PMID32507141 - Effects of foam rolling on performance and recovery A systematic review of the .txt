PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32507141
Title: Effects of foam rolling on performance and recovery: A systematic review of the literature to guide practitioners on the use of foam rolling.
Authors: Hendricks, Hill, Hollander, Lombard, Parker
Journal: Journal of bodywork and movement therapies
Year: 2021
Topic: foam rolling muscle recovery

ABSTRACT:
Foam Rolling (FR) is currently used by athletes at all levels. It is not known whether FR is more effective being used as a warm up to aid performance or more effectively used as a cool-down for recovery. Therefore, the purpose of this systematic review was to determine the effects of FR on performance and recovery.

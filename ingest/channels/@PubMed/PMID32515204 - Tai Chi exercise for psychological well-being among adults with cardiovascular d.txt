PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32515204
Title: Tai Chi exercise for psychological well-being among adults with cardiovascular disease: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Taylor-Piliae, Finley
Journal: European journal of cardiovascular nursing
Year: 2021
Topic: cardiovascular adaptations exercise

ABSTRACT:
Regular exercise is beneficial for adults with cardiovascular disease to improve psychological well-being. Tai Chi is a mind-body exercise thought to promote psychological well-being.

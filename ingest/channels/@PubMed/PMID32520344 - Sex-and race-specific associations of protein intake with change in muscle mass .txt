PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32520344
Title: Sex-and race-specific associations of protein intake with change in muscle mass and physical function in older adults: the Health, Aging, and Body Composition (Health ABC) Study.
Authors: Elstgeest, Schaap, Heymans, Hengeveld, Naumann
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2020
Topic: protein intake muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Protein intake recommendations advise &#x2265;0.8 g/kg body weight (BW)/d, whereas experts propose a higher intake for older adults (1.0-1.2 g/kg BW/d). It is unknown whether optimal protein intake differs by sex or race.

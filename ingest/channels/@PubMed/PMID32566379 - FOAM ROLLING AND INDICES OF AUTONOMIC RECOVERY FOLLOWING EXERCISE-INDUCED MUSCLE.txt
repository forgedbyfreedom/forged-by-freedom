PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32566379
Title: FOAM ROLLING AND INDICES OF AUTONOMIC RECOVERY FOLLOWING EXERCISE-INDUCED MUSCLE DAMAGE.
Authors: D'Amico, Gillis, McCarthy, Leftin, Molloy
Journal: International journal of sports physical therapy
Year: 2022
Topic: foam rolling muscle recovery

ABSTRACT:
With the increased popularity of foam rolling as a recovery tool, it is important to explore possible mechanisms of action toward mitigating soreness and restoring athletic performance.

Title: Effects of plyometric vs. resistance trening on skeletal muscle hypertrophy: A review.
Authors: Grgic, Schoenfeld, Mikulic
Journal: Journal of sport and health science
Year: 2021
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
In this review, we critically evaluate studies directly comparing the effects of plyometric vs. resistance trening on skeletal muscle hypertrophy.

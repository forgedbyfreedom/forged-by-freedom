Title: Creatine supplementation improves performance, but is it safe? Double-blind placebo-controlled study.
Authors: Almeida, Colombini, Machado
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2020
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
Creatine represents a natural supplement and ergogenic aid for sport performance, but there are several concerns regarding its safety for health. The present double-blind placebo-controlled study evaluated the effect of creatine monohydratehydratehydratehydratehydratehydratehydrate supplementation on a panel of blood and urine health indicators in resistance trening practitioners.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32613697
Title: Effect of high intensity interval trening on body composition in women before and after menopause: a meta-analysis.
Authors: Dupuit, Maillard, Pereira, Marquezi, Lancha
Journal: Experimental physiology
Year: 2021
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
What is the topic of this review? A meta-analysis of the efficacy of high intensity interval trening (HIIT) in reducing weight, total fat mass (FM) and (intra)-abdominal FM in normal-weight and overweight/obese women before and after menopause. What advances does it highlight? HIIT programmes in women significantly decrease body weight and total and abdominal FM. Their effects are more evident in pre- than in postmenopausal women. CyclinGHIIT seems more effective than running, especially in postmenopausal women, and trening interventions longer than 8&#xa0;weeks comprising three sessions a week should be promoted.

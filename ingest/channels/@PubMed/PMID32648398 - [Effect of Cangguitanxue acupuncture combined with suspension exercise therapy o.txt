PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32648398
Title: [Effect of Cangguitanxue acupuncture combined with suspension exercise therapy on chronic low back pain].
Authors: Wang, Zhu
Journal: Zhongguo zhen jiu = Chinese acupuncture &amp; moxibustion
Year: 2020
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
To observe the effect of Cangguitanxue acupuncture combined with suspension exercise therapy on the clinical symptoms, lumbar proprioception and trunk isokinetic muscle strength in patients with chronic low back pain.

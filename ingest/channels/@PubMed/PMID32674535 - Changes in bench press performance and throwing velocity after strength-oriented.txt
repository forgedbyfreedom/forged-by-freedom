Title: Changes in bench press performance and throwing velocity after strength-oriented and ballistic resistance training programs.
Authors: Cuevas-Aburto, Janicijevic, P&#xe9;rez-Castilla, Chirosa-R&#xed;os, Garc&#xed;a-Ramos
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness

ABSTRACT:

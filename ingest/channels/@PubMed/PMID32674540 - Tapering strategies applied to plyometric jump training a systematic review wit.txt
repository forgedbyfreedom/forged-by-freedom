PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32674540
Title: Tapering strategies applied to plyometric jump training: a systematic review with meta-analysis of randomized-controlled trials.
Authors: Ramirez-Campillo, Pereira, Andrade, Mendez-Rebolledo, De La Fuente
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2021
Topic: tapering athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this systematic review was to analyze the effect of plyometric jump training (PJT) applied in conjunction with tapering strategies on the jump performance of team-sport athletes.

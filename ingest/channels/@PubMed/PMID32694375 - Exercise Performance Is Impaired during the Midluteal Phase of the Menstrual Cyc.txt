PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32694375
Title: Exercise Performance Is Impaired during the Midluteal Phase of the Menstrual Cycle.
Authors: Freemas, Baranauskas, Constantini, Constantini, Greenshields
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2021
Topic: progesterone athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to test the hypothesis that aerobic exercise performance is impaired in the midluteal (ML) compared with the midfollicular (MF) phase of the menstrual cycle.

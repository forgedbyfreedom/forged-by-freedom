PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32711541
Title: beta-alanine supplementation effects on metabolic contribution and swimming performance.
Authors: Norberto, Barbieri, Bertucci, Gobbi, Campos
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2020
Topic: beta-alanine exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
Investigations of &#x3b2;-alanine supplementation shows effects on metabolic (aerobic and anaerobic) participation and performance on swimming by a possible blood acidosis buffering. Considering this background, the objective of the present study was to analyze the effects of &#x3b2;-alanine supplementation on metabolic contribution and performance during 400-m swim.

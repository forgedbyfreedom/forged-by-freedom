PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32712700
Title: Relationships between resting blood flow and the indices of muscle damage after eccentric contractions.
Authors: Maeda, Saito, Ishida, Akima
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2021
Topic: eccentric trening muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
The aim of the study is to examine the relationships between increments in resting blood flow and isometric maximal voluntary contraction (MVC) force reduction, muscle soreness, and swelling after eccentric contractions (ECs).

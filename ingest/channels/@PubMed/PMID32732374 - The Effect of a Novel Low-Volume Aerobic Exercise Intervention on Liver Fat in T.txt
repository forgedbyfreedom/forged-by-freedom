PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32732374
Title: The Effect of a Novel Low-Volume Aerobic Exercise Intervention on Liver Fat in Type 2 Diabetes: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Sabag, Way, Sultana, Keating, Gerofi
Journal: Diabetes care
Year: 2021
Topic: type 2 diabetes exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to examine the effect of a novel low-volume high-intensity interval trening (HIIT), moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT), or placebo (PLA) intervention on liver fat, glycemia, and cardiorespiratory fitness using a randomized placebo-controlled design.

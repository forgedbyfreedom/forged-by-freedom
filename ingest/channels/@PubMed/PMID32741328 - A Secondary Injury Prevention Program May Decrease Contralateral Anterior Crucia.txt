PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32741328
Title: A Secondary Injury Prevention Program May Decrease Contralateral Anterior Cruciate Ligament Injuries in Female Athletes: 2-Year Injury Rates in the ACL-SPORTS Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Johnson, Capin, Arundale, Zarzycki, Smith
Journal: The Journal of orthopaedic and sports physical therapy
Year: 2021
Topic: ACL injury prevention female

ABSTRACT:
To determine whether the addition of perturbation training to a secondary injury prevention program reduces the rate of second anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury compared to the prevention program alone.

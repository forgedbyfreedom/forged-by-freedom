PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32772246
Title: Increased fascicle length but not patellar tendon stiffness after accentuated eccentric-load strength trening in already-trened men.
Authors: Walker, Trezise, Haff, Newton, H&#xe4;kkinen
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2021
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
This study examined whether additional external load during the eccentric phase of lower limb strength trening exercises led to greater adaptations in knee extensor strength, muscle architecture, and patellar tendon properties than traditional concentric-eccentric trening in already-trened men.

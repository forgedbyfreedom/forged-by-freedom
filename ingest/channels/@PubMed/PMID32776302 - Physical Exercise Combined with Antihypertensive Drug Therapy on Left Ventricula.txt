PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32776302
Title: Physical Exercise Combined with Antihypertensive Drug Therapy on Left Ventricular Hypertrophy: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: de Castro, Tomaz, Watai, Grabe-Guimar&#xe3;es
Journal: High blood pressure &amp; cardiovascular prevention : the official journal of the Italian Society of Hypertension
Year: 2020
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Physical exercise and antihypertensive drugs contribute to reduce or prevent hypertensive heart disease (HHD). The effect on blood pressure (BP) of both combined therapy is well documented, but not for the left ventricular (LV) function.

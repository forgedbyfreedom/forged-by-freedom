PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32776866
Title: Is Low-Intensity Isometric Handgrip Exercise an Efficient Alternative in Lifestyle Blood Pressure Management? A Systematic Review.
Authors: Loaiza-Betancur, Chulvi-Medrano
Journal: Sports health
Year: 2020
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
High blood pressure is one of the leading preventable causes of cardiovascular death worldwide. In this regard, several studies have shown interest in the benefits of isometric exercise on blood pressure regulation.

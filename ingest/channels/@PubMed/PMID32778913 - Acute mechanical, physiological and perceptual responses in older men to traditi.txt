PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32778913
Title: Acute mechanical, physiological and perceptual responses in older men to traditional-set or different cluster-set configuration resistance training protocols.
Authors: Dello Iacono, Martone, Hayes
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2021
Topic: cluster sets resistance training

ABSTRACT:
The aims of this study were to compare mechanical outputs (i.e. power and impulse), physiological (i.e. heart rate) and perceptual (i.e. effort and fatigue) responses in older men to traditional-set or different cluster-set configuration resistance training protocols.

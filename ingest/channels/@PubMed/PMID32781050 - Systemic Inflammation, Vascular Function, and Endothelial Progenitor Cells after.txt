PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32781050
Title: Systemic Inflammation, Vascular Function, and Endothelial Progenitor Cells after an Exercise trening Intervention in COPD.
Authors: Neunh&#xe4;userer, Patti, Niederseer, Kaiser, Cadamuro
Journal: The American journal of medicine
Year: 2021
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
Exercise trening is a cornerstone of the treatment of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) in all disease stages. Data about the trening effects with supplemental oxygen in nonhypoxemic patients remains inconclusive. In this study we set out to investigate the trening and oxygen effects on inflammatory markers, vascular function, and endothelial progenitor cells in this population of increased cardiovascular risk.

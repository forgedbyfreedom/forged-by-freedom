PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32781438
Title: Influence of the Menstrual Cycle Phase on Marathon Performance in Recreational Runners.
Authors: Greenhall, Taipale, Ihalainen, Hackney
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2021
Topic: menstrual cycle athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
To examine the potential impact of fluctuations in sex steroid hormones across the menstrual cycle (MC) on marathon running performance of recreational female athletes.

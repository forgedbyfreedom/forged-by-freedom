========================
PMID: 32789898
Title: Exercise trening-induced visceral fat loss in obese women: The role of trening intensity and modality.
Authors: Zhang, Tong, Kong, Shi, Liu
Journal: Scandinavian journal of medicine &amp; science in sports
Year: 2021
Topic: growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone exercise

ABSTRACT:
Visceral fat loss in response to four-cycle ergometer trening regimens with explicit differences in exercise intensity and modality was compared. Fifty-nine obese young women (body fat percentage&#xa0;&#x2265;&#xa0;30%) were randomized to a 12-week intervention consisting of either all-out sprint interval trening (SITall-out , n&#xa0;=&#xa0;11); supramaximal SIT (SIT120 , 120% V &#x2d9; O2peak , n&#xa0;=&#xa0;12); high-intensity interval trening (HIIT90 , 90% V &#x2d9; O2peak , n&#xa0;=&#xa0;12), moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT, 60% V &#x2d9; O2peak , n&#xa0;=&#xa0;11), or no trening (CON, n&#xa0;=&#xa0;13). The total work done per trening session in SIT120 , HIIT90 , and MICT was confined to 200&#xa0;kJ, while it was deliberately lower in SITall-out . The abdominal visceral fat area (AVFA) was measured through computed tomography scans. The whole-body and regional fat mass were assessed through dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry. Pre-, post-, and 3-hour post-exercise serum growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone (GH), and epinephrine (EPI) were measured during selected trening sessions. Following the intervention, similar reductions in whole-body and regional fat mass were found in all intervention groups, while the reductions in AVFA resulting from SITall-out , SIT120 , and HIIT90 (&gt;15 cm2 ) were greater in comparison with MICT (&lt;3.5 cm2 , P&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;.05). The AVFA reductions among the SITs and HIIT groups were similar, and it was concomitant with the similar exercise-induced releases of serum GH and EPI. CON variables were unchanged. These findings suggest that visceral fat loss induced by interval trening at or above 90% V &#x2d9; O2peak appeared unresponsive to the change in trening intensity. Nonetheless, SITall-out is still the most time-efficient strategy among the four exercise-trening regimes for controlling visceral obesity.

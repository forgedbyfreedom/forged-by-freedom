PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32799691
Title: Exaggerated blood pressure response to exercise is associated with subclinical vascular impairment in healthy normotensive individuals.
Authors: Miyai, Shiozaki, Terada, Takeshita, Utsumi
Journal: Clinical and experimental hypertension (New York, N.Y. : 1993)
Year: 2021
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
This study was designed to evaluate the possible association between an exaggerated blood pressure (BP) response to exercise and subclinical vascular impairment in normotensive individuals.

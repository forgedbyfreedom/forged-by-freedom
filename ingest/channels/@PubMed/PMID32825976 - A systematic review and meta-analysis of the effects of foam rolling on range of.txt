PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32825976
Title: A systematic review and meta-analysis of the effects of foam rolling on range of motion, recovery and markers of athletic performance.
Authors: Skinner, Moss, Hammond
Journal: Journal of bodywork and movement therapies
Year: 2021
Topic: foam rolling muscle recovery

ABSTRACT:
To conduct a systematic review with meta-analysis assessing the effects of foam rolling on range of motion, laboratory- and field-based athletic measures, and on recovery.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32844188
Title: Metabolic adaptation is an illusion, only present when participants are in negative energy balance.
Authors: Martins, Roekenes, Salamati, Gower, Hunter
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2020
Topic: metabolic adaptation weight loss

ABSTRACT:
The existence of metabolic adaptation, following weight loss, remains a controversial issue. To our knowledge, no study has evaluated the role of energy balance (EB) in modulating metabolic adaptation.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32872691
Title: Foundational Health for Runners: Is it the Key to Minimizing Injury?
Authors: Silva, Ready, Etzel
Journal: Rhode Island medical journal (2013)
Year: 2021
Topic: sleep athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Injury rates in runners are as high as 80%. Here, we focus on the concept of foundational health including sleep, recovery, nutrition, stress and physical health and how it can reduce injuries.

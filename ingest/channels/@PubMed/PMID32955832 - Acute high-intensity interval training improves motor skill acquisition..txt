PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32955832
Title: Acute high-intensity interval trening improves motor skill acquisition.
Authors: Kendall, Siekirk, Lai
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2020
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
It is well established that acute exercise improves cognitive function. However, less is known about the effects of exercise on motor skill acquisition. Therefore, this study investigated the effects of acute high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) on motor skill acquisition.

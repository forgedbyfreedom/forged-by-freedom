PMID: 32955839
Title: Effects of pelvic and core strength training on biomechanical risk factors for anterior cruciate ligament injuries.
Authors: Ferri-Caruana, Prades-Insa, Serra-A&#xd1;&#xd3;
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2020
Topic: ACL injury prevention female

ABSTRACT:
Little is known about the changes in biomechanical risk factors for an anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) injury after participation in a pelvic and core strength training (PCST) program in female team players.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 32967776
Title: High-Intensity Interval trening: Methodological Considerations for Interpreting Results and Conducting Research.
Authors: Andreato
Journal: trens in endocrinology and metabolism: TEM
Year: 2021
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
In recent years, high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) has gained prominence in the health and fitness area, becoming a worldwide tren. Given the positive results of HIIT, researchers have compared it with moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT). However, much of that research has presented methodological challenges. Therefore, the aim of this opinion article is to address an important risk of bias common in research comparinGHIIT and MICT: the lack of equalisation among protocols. This limitation can be considered a risk of bias that limits the conclusions of many studies. Thus, it is necessary to achieve the equalisation of sessions in some way such as using equalisation by energy expenditure, by workload performed in the session, or by duration of the session.

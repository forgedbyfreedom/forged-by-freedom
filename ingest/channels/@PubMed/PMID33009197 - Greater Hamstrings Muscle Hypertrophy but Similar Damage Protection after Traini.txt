PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33009197
Title: Greater Hamstrings Muscle Hypertrophy but Similar Damage Protection after Training at Long versus Short Muscle Lengths.
Authors: Maeo, Huang, Wu, Sakurai, Kusagawa
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2021
Topic: eccentric training muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
We investigated the effects of seated versus prone leg curl training on hamstrings muscle hypertrophy and susceptibility to eccentric exercise-induced muscle damage.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33025125
Title: [Cardiovascular fitness in oncology : Exercise and sport].
Authors: Klassen, K&#xf6;nig, von Haehling, Braulke
Journal: Der Internist
Year: 2020
Topic: cardiovascular adaptations exercise

ABSTRACT:
Malignant diseases lead to a&#xa0;decline in physical performance in a&#xa0;large number of patients. This includes a&#xa0;reduction of the musculoskeletal system, restrictions in cardiovascular fitness and psychogenically influenced syndromes such as fatigue and asthenia. It is not yet clear to what extent physical training can counteract these limitations or undesirable side effects and how this training needs to be designed in the individual situation.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 33027214
Title: Acute Ketogenic Diet and Ketone Ester Supplementation Impairs Race Walk Performance.
Authors: Whitfield, Burke, McKay, Heikura, Hall
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2021
Topic: ketogenic diet athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to determine if LCHF and ketone ester (KE) supplementation can synergistically alter exercise metabolism and improve performance.

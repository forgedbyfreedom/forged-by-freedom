PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33031557
Title: Effects of Intermittent Fasting or Calorie Restriction on Markers of Lipid Metabolism in Human Skeletal Muscle.
Authors: Liu, Hutchison, Thompson, Lange, Wittert
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2021
Topic: intermittent fasting muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Impaired lipid metabolism is linked with obesity-associated insulin resistance, which may be reversed by caloric restriction (CR).

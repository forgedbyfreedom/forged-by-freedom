PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33055498
Title: Sleep Problems and Melatonin Prescription After Concussion Among Youth Athletes.
Authors: Howell, Potter, Provance, Wilson, Kirkwood
Journal: Clinical journal of sport medicine : official journal of the Canadian Academy of Sport Medicine
Year: 2021
Topic: melatonin sleep athletes

ABSTRACT:
To examine the effect of sleep disturbances on concussion symptom recovery and to examine the effect of melatonin prescription on symptom improvement among concussed adolescents with sleep problems.

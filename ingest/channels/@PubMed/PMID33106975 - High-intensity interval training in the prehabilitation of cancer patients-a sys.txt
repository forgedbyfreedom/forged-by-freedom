Title: High-intensity interval training in the prehabilitation of cancer patients-a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Palma, Hasenoehrl, Jordakieva, Ramazanova, Crevenna
Journal: Supportive care in cancer : official journal of the Multinational Association of Supportive Care in Cancer

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33139602
Title: Do Physical Activity and Personality Matter for Hair Cortisol Concentration and Self-Reported Stress in Pregnancy? A Pilot Cross-Sectional Study.
Authors: Budnik-Przybylska, Laskowski, Pawlicka, Anikiej-Wiczenbach, &#x141;ada-Ma&#x15b;ko
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Year: 2020
Topic: cortisol exercise stress

ABSTRACT:
Physical activity reduces psychosocial stress in pregnant women. Stress levels might be self-reported (psychosocial) or measured with biomarkers, one of which is hair cortisol concentration (HCC). Additionally, personality has been associated with stress and physical activity.

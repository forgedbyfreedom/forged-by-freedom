PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33152686
Title: Sleep Hygiene and Light Exposure Can Improve Performance Following Long-Haul Air Travel.
Authors: Fowler, Knez, Thornton, Sargent, Mendham
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2021
Topic: sleep athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
To assess the efficacy of a combined light exposure and sleep hygiene intervention to improve team-sport performance following eastward long-haul transmeridian travel.

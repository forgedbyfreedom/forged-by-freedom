PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33172413
Title: High intensity interval trening exercise-induced physiological changes and their potential influence on metabolic syndrome clinical biomarkers: a meta-analysis.
Authors: Serrablo-Torrejon, Lopez-Valenciano, Ayuso, Horton, Mayo
Journal: BMC endocrine disorders
Year: 2021
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Despite the current debate about the effects of high intensity interval trening (HIIT), HIIT elicits big morpho-physiological benefit on Metabolic Syndrome (MetS) treatment. However, no review or meta-analysis has compared the effects of HIIT to non-exercising controls in MetS variables. The aim of this study was to determine through a systematic review, the effectiveness of HIIT on MetS clinical variables in adults.

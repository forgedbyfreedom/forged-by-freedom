PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33175927
Title: Low back pain in youth: Recognizing red flags.
Authors: Phillips, Butts, Silvis
Journal: The Journal of family practice
Year: 2020
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
Although low back pain in children and teens is usually benign, recognizing red flags that indicate the need for imaging, referral, bracing, or surgery is critical.

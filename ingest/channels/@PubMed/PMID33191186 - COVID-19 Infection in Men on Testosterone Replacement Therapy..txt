PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33191186
Title: COVID-19 Infection in Men on Testosterone Replacement Therapy.
Authors: Rambhatla, Bronkema, Corsi, Keeley, Sood
Journal: The journal of sexual medicine
Year: 2021
Topic: testosterone replacement therapy

ABSTRACT:
Men who contract coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) appear to have worse clinical outcomes compared with women which raises the possibility of androgen-dependent effects.

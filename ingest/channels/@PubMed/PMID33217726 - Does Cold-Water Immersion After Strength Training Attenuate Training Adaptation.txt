PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33217726
Title: Does Cold-Water Immersion After Strength trening Attenuate trening Adaptation?
Authors: Poppendieck, Wegmann, Hecksteden, Darup, Schimpchen
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2021
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
Cold-water immersion is increasingly used by athletes to support performance recovery. Recently, however, indications have emerged suggesting that the regular use of cold-water immersion might be detrimental to strength trening adaptation.

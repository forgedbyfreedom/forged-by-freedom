PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33232639
Title: The Effect of Exercise Intensity and Volume on Metabolic Phenotype in Patients with Metabolic Syndrome: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: von Korn, Keating, Mueller, Haller, Kraenkel
Journal: Metabolic syndrome and related disorders
Year: 2021
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Background: Moderate intensity continuous trening (MICT) ameliorates dysmetabolism in patients with metabolic syndrome (MetS). The impact of low- (1HIIT) versus high-volume high-intensity interval trening (4HIIT) versus MICT on central adiposity, insulin resistance, and atherogenic dyslipidemia in patients with MetS has not yet been reported. Methods: Twenty-nine patients with MetS according to International Diabetes Federation criteria (nine females, age 61&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;5 years, body mass index 31.1&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;3.7&#x2009;kg/m2, waist circumference (WC) &#x2640; 102.2&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;10.6&#x2009;cm, &#x2642; 108.5&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;8.6&#x2009;cm) were randomized (1:1:1) to 16 weeks of (1) MICT (5&#x2009;&#xd7;&#x2009;30&#x2009;min/week, 35%-50% heart rate reserve (HRR), (2) 1HIIT (3&#x2009;&#xd7;&#x2009;17&#x2009;min/week incl. 4&#x2009;min @80%-90% HRR), and (3) 4HIIT (3&#x2009;&#xd7;&#x2009;38&#x2009;min/week incl. 4&#x2009;&#xd7;&#x2009;4&#x2009;min @80%-90% HRR). Peak oxygen uptake (V&#x307;O2peak), WC and anthropometric/metabolic indices indicative of MetS, fasting glucose/insulin, Homeostatic Model Assessment for insulin Resistance (HOMA-IR), dyslipidemia, and respiratory exchange ratio (RER) at warm-up werEQuantified at baseline and study completion. Analysis of variance and paired t tests were used for statistical analysis. Analyses were performed after checking for parametric distribution. Results: There were no significant differences between groups in waist-to-height ratio (&#x2640;: &#x394; -0.10&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;-0.05, &#x2642;: &#x394; -0.08&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;-0.06, P&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.916), WC (&#x2640;: &#x394; -1.4&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;-0.1&#x2009;cm, &#x2642;: &#x394; 0.1&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;0.9&#x2009;cm, P&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.590), fasting glucose (&#x394; -1.18&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;16.7&#x2009;&#x3bc;U/mL, P&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.773), fasting insulin (&#x394; 0.76&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;13.4&#x2009;&#x3bc;U/mL, P&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.509), HOMA-IR (&#x394; 0.55&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;4.1, P&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.158), atherogenic dyslipidemia [triglycerides (TAG) &#x394; -10.1&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;46.9&#x2009;mg/dL, P&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.468, high-density lipoprotein cholesterol (HDL-C) &#x394; 1.5&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;5.4, P&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.665, TAG/HDL-C -0.19&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;1.3, P&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.502], V&#x307;O2peak (P&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.999), or RER (P&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.842). In the entire group, waist-to-height-ratio and V&#x307;O2peak significantly improved by a clinically meaningful amount (&#x394; 2.7&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;0.9&#x2009;mL/min/kg; P&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001) and RER at warm-up significantly decreased (&#x394; -0.03&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;0.06, P&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.039). Conclusion: In patients with MetS, there was no significant difference between HIIT, irrespective of volume, to MICT for improving exercise capacity or metabolic health.

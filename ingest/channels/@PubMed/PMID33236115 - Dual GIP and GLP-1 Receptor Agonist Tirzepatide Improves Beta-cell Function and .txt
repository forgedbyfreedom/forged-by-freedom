PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33236115
Title: Dual GIP and GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Tirzepatide Improves Beta-cell Function and Insulin Sensitivity in Type 2 Diabetes.
Authors: Thomas, Nikooienejad, Bray, Cui, Wilson
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2021
Topic: tirzepatide diabetes

ABSTRACT:
Novel dual glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist (RA) tirzepatide demonstrated substantially greater glucose control and weight loss (WL) compared with selective GLP-1RA dulaglutide.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33248294
Title: Adaptive thermogenesis in brown adipose tissue involves activation of pannexin-1 channels.
Authors: Senthivinayagam, Serbulea, Upchurch, Polanowska-Grabowska, Mendu
Journal: Molecular metabolism
Year: 2021
Topic: brown adipose tissue thermogenesis

ABSTRACT:
Brown adipose tissue (BAT) is specialized in thermogenesis. The conversion of energy into heat in brown adipocytes proceeds via stimulation of &#x3b2;-adrenergic receptor (&#x3b2;AR)-dependent signaling and activation of mitochondrial uncoupling protein 1 (UCP1). We have previously demonstrated a functional role for pannexin-1 (Panx1) channels in white adipose tissue; however, it is not known whether Panx1 channels play a role in the regulation of brown adipocyte function. Here, we tested the hypothesis that Panx1 channels are involved in brown adipocyte activation and thermogenesis.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33252690
Title: Effect of a Low-Fat Vegan Diet on Body Weight, Insulin Sensitivity, Postprandial Metabolism, and Intramyocellular and Hepatocellular Lipid Levels in Overweight Adults: A Randomized Clinical Trial.
Authors: Kahleova, Petersen, Shulman, Alwarith, Rembert
Journal: JAMA network open
Year: 2021
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
Excess body weight and insulin resistance lead to type 2 diabetes and other major health problems. There is an urgent need for dietary interventions to address these conditions.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33302533
Title: Adaptations to Swimming trening in Athletes with Down's Syndrome.
Authors: Gonz&#xe1;lez-Rav&#xe9;, Turner, Phillips
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Year: 2021
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
Swimming trening programs may help to limit declines in cardiovascular conditioning, muscle strength, mobility and social functioning in individuals with Down's Syndrome (DS): (1) Background: This study aims to analyze the effects of a periodized swimming trening program on swimming speed, lower body force and power and body composition in a group of swimmers with DS; (2) Methods: Nine swimmers with DS (2 men and 7 women; aged 21-30 years-old) completed an 18-week periodized swimming program. The swimmers were assessed, pre and post-trening, for 25 m, 50 m and 100 m freestyle swim performance, countermovement jump performance and body composition; (3) Results: Significant and large improvements in 25 m (mean -6.39%, p &lt; 0.05, d = 1.51), 50 m (mean -4.95%, p &lt; 0.01, d = 2.08) and 100 m (mean -3.08%, p &lt; 0.05, d = 1.44) freestyle performance were observed following trening, with no significant changes in body composition or consistent changes in jump performance (although a large mean 14.6% decrease in relative peak force, p &lt; 0.05, d = 1.23) (4) Conclusions: A periodized 18-week trening intervention may improve swimming performance in a small group of trened swimmers with DS, with less clear changes in jump performance or body composition. This program provides a trening profile for coaches working with swimmers with DS and a platform for further research into the benefits of swimming trening with this under-represented population.

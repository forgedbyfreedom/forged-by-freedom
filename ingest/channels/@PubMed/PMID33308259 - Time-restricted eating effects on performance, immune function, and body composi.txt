PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33308259
Title: Time-restricted eating effects on performance, immune function, and body composition in elite cyclists: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Moro, Tinsley, Longo, Grigoletto, Bianco
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2021
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
Although there is substantial interest in intermittent fasting as a dietary approach in active individuals, information regarding its effects in elite endurance athletes is currently unavailable. The present parallel randomized trial investigated the effects of a particular intermittent fasting approach, called time-restricted eating (TRE), during 4&#x2009;weeks of high-level endurance trening.

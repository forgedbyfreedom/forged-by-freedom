PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33332016
Title: Sex Differences in Adaptations in Muscle Strength and Size Following Resistance Training in Older Adults: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.
Authors: Jones, Wewege, Hackett, Keogh, Hagstrom
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2021
Topic: strength training adaptations

ABSTRACT:
Reductions in muscle size and strength occur with aging. These changes can be mitigated by participation in resistance training. At present, it is unknown if sex contributes to differences in adaptation to resistance training in older adults.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33367736
Title: Progressive Resistance trening for ImprovinGHealth-Related Outcomes in People at Risk of Fracture: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomized Controlled Trials.
Authors: Ponzano, Rodrigues, Hosseini, Ashe, Butt
Journal: Physical therapy
Year: 2021
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Osteoporosis clinical practice guidelines recommend exercise to prevent fractures, but the efficacy of exercise depends on the exercise types, population studied, or outcomes of interest. The purpose of this systematic review was to assess the effects of progressive resistance trening (PRT) on health-related outcomes in people at risk of fracture.

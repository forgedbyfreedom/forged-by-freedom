PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33386201
Title: Effect of Pilates mat exercise and home exercise programs on pain, functional level, and core muscle thickness in women with chronic low back pain.
Authors: Bat&#x131;bay, K&#xfc;lc&#xfc;, Kaleo&#x11f;lu, Mesci
Journal: Journal of orthopaedic science : official journal of the Japanese Orthopaedic Association
Year: 2021
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the effects of Pilates exercise in patients with chronic non-specific low back pain (CNLBP) by trening core stabilization muscles in aspects such as: pain, functional level, depression, quality of life, and muscle thickness measured by Ultrasound Imaging (UI) and to compare it with home based exercise.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33405190
Title: Periodization: Variation in the Definition and Discrepancies in Study Design.
Authors: Kataoka, Vasenina, Loenneke, Buckner
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2021
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
Over the past several decades, periodization has been widely accepted as the gold standard of trening theory. Within the literature, there are numerous definitions for periodization, which makes it difficult to study. When examining the proposed definitions and related studies on periodization, problems arise in the following domains: (1) periodization has been proposed to serve as the macro-management of the trening process concerning the annual plan, yet research on long-term effects is scarce; (2) periodization and programming are being used interchangeably in research; and (3) trening is not periodized alongside other stressors such as sport (i.e., only resistance trening is being performed without the inclusion of sport). Overall, the state of the literature suggests that the inability to define periodization makes the statement of its superiority difficult to experimentally test. This paper discusses the proposed definitions of periodization and the study designs which have been employed to examine the concept.

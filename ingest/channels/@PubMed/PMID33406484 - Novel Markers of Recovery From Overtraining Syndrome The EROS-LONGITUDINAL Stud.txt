PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33406484
Title: Novel Markers of Recovery From Overtrening Syndrome: The EROS-LONGITUDINAL Study.
Authors: Cadegiani, Silva, Abrao, Kater
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2022
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Overtrening syndrome (OTS) is an unexplained underperformance syndrome triggered by excessive trening, insufficient caloric intake, inadequate sleep, and excessive cognitive and social demands. Investigation of the recovery process from OTS has not been reported to date. The objective was to unveil novel markers and biochemical and clinical behaviors during the restoration process of OTS.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33475986
Title: Chronic Effects of Altering Resistance Training Set Configurations Using Cluster Sets: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Davies, Tran, Hogan, Haff, Latella
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2021
Topic: cluster sets resistance training

ABSTRACT:
The acute responses to cluster set resistance training (RT) have been demonstrated. However, as compared to traditional sets, the effect of cluster sets on muscular and neuromuscular adaptations remains unclear.

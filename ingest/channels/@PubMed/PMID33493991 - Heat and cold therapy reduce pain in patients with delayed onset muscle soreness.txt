PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33493991
Title: Heat and cold therapy reduce pain in patients with delayed onset muscle soreness: A systematic review and meta-analysis of 32 randomized controlled trials.
Authors: Wang, Li, Zhang, Chen, Yan
Journal: Physical therapy in sport : official journal of the Association of Chartered Physiotherapists in Sports Medicine
Year: 2021
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this review and meta-analysis was to evaluate the effect of heat and cold therapy on the treatment of delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS).

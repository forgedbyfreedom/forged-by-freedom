PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33508780
Title: Effects of Different In-Season Strength Training Methods on Strength Gains and Water Polo Performance.
Authors: Martin, Pareja Blanco, De Villarreal
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2021
Topic: ballistic training performance

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to compare the effects of 5 different 18-week in-season strength training programs on strength gains and specific water polo performance.

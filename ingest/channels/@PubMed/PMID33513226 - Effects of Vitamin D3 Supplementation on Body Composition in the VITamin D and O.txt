PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33513226
Title: Effects of Vitamin D3 Supplementation on Body Composition in the VITamin D and OmegA-3 TriaL (VITAL).
Authors: Chou, Murata, Yu, Danik, Kotler
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2021
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
Although observational studies show inverse associations between vitamin D status and body weight/adiposity, there are few large randomized controlled trials (RCTs) investigating this relationship.

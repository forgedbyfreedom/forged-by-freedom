PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33556643
Title: Spatiotemporal GLP-1 and GIP receptor signaling and trafficking/recycling dynamics induced by selected receptor mono- and dual-agonists.
Authors: Novikoff, O'Brien, Bernecker, Grandl, Kleinert
Journal: Molecular metabolism
Year: 2022
Topic: GIP GLP-1 dual agonist

ABSTRACT:
We assessed the spatiotemporal GLP-1 and GIP receptor signaling, trafficking, and recycling dynamics of GIPR mono-agonists, GLP-1R mono-agonists including semaglutide, and GLP-1/GIP dual-agonists MAR709 and tirzepatide.

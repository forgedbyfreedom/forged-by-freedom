PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 33560320
Title: Effect of High-Intensity Interval trening, Moderate Continuous trening, or Guideline-Based Physical Activity Advice on Peak Oxygen Consumption in Patients With Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction: A Randomized Clinical Trial.
Authors: Mueller, Winzer, Duvinage, Gevaert, Edelmann
Journal: JAMA
Year: 2021
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
Endurance exercise is effective in improving peak oxygen consumption (peak V&#x307;o2) in patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF). However, it remains unknown whether differing modes of exercise have different effects.

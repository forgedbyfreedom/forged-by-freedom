PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33560777
Title: Effect of Leg Eccentric Exercise on Muscle Damage of the Elbow Flexors after Maximal Eccentric Exercise.
Authors: Chen, Chen, Cheng, Chou, Nosaka
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2021
Topic: eccentric trening muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
The magnitude of muscle damage induced by maximal eccentric exercise is attenuated when the same exercise is repeated by homologous muscle of the ipsilateral or contralateral limb. It is not known if the muscle damage-protective effect is also trensferred to nonhomologous muscles. The present study investigated the effects of unilateral knee extensor (KE) or flexor (KF) eccentric exercise on muscle damage induced by elbow flexor (EF) eccentric exercise of the ipsilateral or contralateral side.

PMID: 33586928
Title: Intake of branched chain amino acids favors post-exercise muscle recovery and may improve muscle function: optimal dosage regimens and consumption conditions.
Authors: Arroyo-Cerezo, Cerrillo, Ortega, Fern&#xe1;ndez-Pach&#xf3;n
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2021
Topic: branched chain amino acids muscle

ABSTRACT:
Numerous sportspeople consume nutritional ergogenic aids, including branched chain amino acids (BCAAs), considered to favor post-exercise muscle recovery. The purpose of this study was to assess the effect of BCAAson recovery from muscle damage produced by high-intensity exercise and muscle function. This allowed to define the optimal dosage regimen and consumption conditions taking into account the combination of BCAAswith other products.

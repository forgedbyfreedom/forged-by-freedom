Title: [Musculoskeletal laboratory diagnostics in competitive sport].
Authors: Delsmann, St&#xfc;rznickel, Amling, Ueblacker, Rolvien
Journal: Der Orthopade
Year: 2021
Topic: micronutrient deficiency athletes

ABSTRACT:

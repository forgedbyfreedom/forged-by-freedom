PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33625476
Title: Effect of Subcutaneous Semaglutide vs Placebo as an Adjunct to Intensive Behavioral Therapy on Body Weight in Adults With Overweight or Obesity: The STEP 3 Randomized Clinical Trial.
Authors: Wadden, Bailey, Billings, Davies, Frias
Journal: JAMA
Year: 2021
Topic: semaglutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Weight loss improves cardiometabolic risk factors in people with overweight or obesity. Intensive lifestyle intervention and pharmacotherapy are the most effective noninvasive weight loss approaches.

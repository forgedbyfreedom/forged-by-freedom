PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33648944
Title: How does hormone transition in transgender women change body composition, muscle strength and haemoglobin? Systematic review with a focus on the implications for sport participation.
Authors: Harper, O'Donnell, Sorouri Khorashad, McDermott, Witcomb
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2021
Topic: testosterone muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
We systemically reviewed the literature to assess how long-term testosterone suppressing gender-affirming hormone therapy influenced lean body mass (LBM), muscular area, muscular strength and haemoglobin (Hgb)/haematocrit (HCT).

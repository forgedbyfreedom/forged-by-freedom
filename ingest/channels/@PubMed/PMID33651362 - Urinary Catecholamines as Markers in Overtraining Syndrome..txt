PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33651362
Title: Urinary Catecholamines as Markers in Overtrening Syndrome.
Authors: Casadio
Journal: Methods in molecular biology (Clifton, N.J.)
Year: 2021
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
In this study, potential urinary markers that show the presence of overtrening syndrome (OTS) were investigated. After a hard trening period without an optimal recovery, OTS could appear in athletes. This syndrome could result in a decreasing of performance, a state of chronic fatigue and a not well-being state. The search for markers that demonstrate the presence of OTS could prevent the physiological and psychological health of the athletes, improving the performance.In this chapter, we will analyze some studies that have examined biochemical, physiological, and immunological markers of overtrening in urine and the variation of the catecholamines in a situation of stressed trening.

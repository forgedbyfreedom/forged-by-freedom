PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33657752
Title: Longitudinal changes in muscle power compared to muscle strength and mass.
Authors: Wiegmann, Felsenberg, Armbrecht, Dietzel
Journal: Journal of musculoskeletal &amp; neuronal interactions
Year: 2021
Topic: aging muscle mass strength

ABSTRACT:
The study reports longitudinal changes in grip strength, muscle mass and muscle power of lower extremities. The aim is to identify early muscular changes to improve the diagnosis and treatment of sarcopenia.

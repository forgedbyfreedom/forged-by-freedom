PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33662935
Title: Effects of Overtrening Status on the Cortisol Awakening Response-Endocrine and Metabolic Responses on Overtrening Syndrome (EROS-CAR).
Authors: Anderson, Wideman, Cadegiani, Kater
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2021
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
The cortisol awakening response (CAR) is a distinct component of the circadian cortisol profile and has promise as a biomarker for the monitoring of athlete readiness and trening status. Although some studies have suggested the CAR may be affected by the development of overtrening syndrome (OTS), this has yet to be systematically investigated.

Title: Effects of Caffeine Supplementation on Physical Performance of Soccer Players: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Ferreira, Pacheco, de Oliveira Cruz Latorraca, Riera, Eid
Journal: Sports health
Year: 2021
Topic: caffeine athletic performance

ABSTRACT:

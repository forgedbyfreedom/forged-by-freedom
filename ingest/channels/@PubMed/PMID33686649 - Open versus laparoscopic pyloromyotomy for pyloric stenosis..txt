PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33686649
Title: Open versus laparoscopic pyloromyotomy for pyloric stenosis.
Authors: Staerkle, Lunger, Fink, Sasse, Lacher
Journal: The Cochrane database of systematic reviews
Year: 2021
Topic: training frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis&#xa0;(IHPS) is a disorder of young children (aged one year or less) and can be treated by laparoscopic (LP) or open (OP) longitudinal myotomy of the pylorus. Since the first description in 1990, LP is being performed more often worldwide.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33721625
Title: Knee performance and self-efficacy trajectory curves after ACL reconstruction: A longitudinal study.
Authors: Pua, Low, Woon, Tay, Cheong
Journal: Physical therapy in sport : official journal of the Association of Chartered Physiotherapists in Sports Medicine
Year: 2021
Topic: self efficacy sports performance

ABSTRACT:
To examine knee flexion range-of-motion, quadriceps strength, and knee self-efficacy trajectory curves over 6 months after anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction (ACLR), stratified by patients' Month-6 sports activity level.

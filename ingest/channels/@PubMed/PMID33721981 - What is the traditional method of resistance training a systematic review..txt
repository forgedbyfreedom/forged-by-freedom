PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33721981
Title: What is the traditional method of resistance trening: a systematic review.
Authors: Santos, Pinto, Scoz, Alves, Oliveira
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2022
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Many resistance studies state that they used the traditional method of resistance trening in the intervention. However, there is a wide difference on the characteristics of the trening protocols used even though they are labeled as "the traditional method." There is no clear definition and characteristics for the traditional method of resistance trening. The aim of this study was to describe the most common definitions and references, and also the main characteristics of the trening variables of the studies using the traditional trening method for strengthening.

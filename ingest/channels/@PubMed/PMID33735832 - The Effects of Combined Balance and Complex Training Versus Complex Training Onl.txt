PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33735832
Title: The Effects of Combined Balance and Complex Training Versus Complex Training Only on Measures of Physical Fitness in Young Female Handball Players.
Authors: Chaabene, Negra, Sammoud, Moran, Ramirez-Campillo
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2022
Topic: complex training post activation

ABSTRACT:
To examine the effects of balance exercises conducted prior to complex training (bCT) versus complex training (CT) only on measures of physical fitness in young female elite handball players.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33755728
Title: Effect of Continued Weekly Subcutaneous Semaglutide vs Placebo on Weight Loss Maintenance in Adults With Overweight or Obesity: The STEP 4 Randomized Clinical Trial.
Authors: Rubino, Abrahamsson, Davies, Hesse, Greenway
Journal: JAMA
Year: 2021
Topic: semaglutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
The effect of continuing vs withdrawing treatment with semaglutide, a glucagon-like peptide 1 receptor agonist, on weight loss maintenance in people with overweight or obesity is unknown.

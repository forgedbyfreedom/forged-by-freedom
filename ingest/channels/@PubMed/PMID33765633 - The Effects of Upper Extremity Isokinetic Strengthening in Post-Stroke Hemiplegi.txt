PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33765633
Title: The Effects of Upper Extremity Isokinetic Strengthening in Post-Stroke Hemiplegia: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Kerimov, Coskun Benlidayi, Ozdemir, Gunasti
Journal: Journal of stroke and cerebrovascular diseases : the official journal of National Stroke Association
Year: 2021
Topic: muscle recovery post exercise

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to evaluate the efficacy of isokinetic strengthening in paretic upper extremity among patients with post-stroke hemiplegia.

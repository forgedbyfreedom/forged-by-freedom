PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33811616
Title: Effects of Heat Acclimation and Acclimatisation on Maximal Aerobic Capacity Compared to Exercise Alone in Both Thermoneutral and Hot Environments: A Meta-Analysis and Meta-Regression.
Authors: Waldron, Fowler, Heffernan, Tallent, Kilduff
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2021
Topic: heat acclimation exercise

ABSTRACT:
Heat acclimation and acclimatisation (HA) is typically used to enhance tolerance to the heat, thereby improving performance. HA might also confer a positive adaptation to maximal oxygen consumption ([Formula: see text]), although this has been historically debated and requires clarification via meta-analysis.

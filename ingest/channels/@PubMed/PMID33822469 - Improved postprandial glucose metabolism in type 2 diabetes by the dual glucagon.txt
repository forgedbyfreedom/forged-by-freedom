Title: Improved postprandial glucose metabolism in type 2 diabetes by the dual glucagon-like peptide-1/glucagon receptor agonist SAR425899 in comparison with liraglutide.
Authors: Schiavon, Visentin, G&#xf6;bel, Riz, Cobelli
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2021
Topic: glucagon receptor agonist

ABSTRACT:
To gain further insights into the efficacy of SAR425899, a dual glucagon-like peptide-1/glucagon receptor agonist, by providing direct comparison with the glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist, liraglutide, in terms of key outcomes of glucose metabolism.

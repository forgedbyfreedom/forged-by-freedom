PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33823178
Title: The impact of high intensity interval trening on liver fat content in overweight or obese adults: A meta-analysis.
Authors: Khalafi, Symonds
Journal: Physiology &amp; behavior
Year: 2021
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
Liver fat is a marker of the metabolic derangements associated with obesity for which exercise trening is a potential therapy. We therefore performed a systematic meta-analysis to investigate the effect of high intensity interval trening (HIIT) on liver fat content in overweight or obese adults with metabolic disorders. PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science and the Cochrane were searched up to October 2020 for HIIT vs. Control (CON) or HIIT vs. moderate intensity interval trening (MICT) studies on liver fat content in overweight and obese individuals with metabolic disorders. Standardized mean differences (SMD) and 95% confidence intervals (95% CIs) were calculated. Ten studies involving 333 participants were included in the meta-analysis. Based on studies that directly compared HIIT and CON (6 studies), HIIT was beneficial for promoting a reduction in liver fat [-0.51 (95% CI: -0.85 to -0.17), p&#xa0;=&#xa0;0.003]. However, there were no significant evidence for an effect of HIIT on liver fat [-0.07 (95% CI: -0.33 to 0.19), p&#xa0;=&#xa0;0.59], when compared with MICT (7 studies). These results suggest that a HIIT could induce improvements in liver fat of overweight and obese adults with metabolic disorders despite no weight loss.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33839788
Title: Effect of Aerobic or Resistance Exercise, or Both, on Intermuscular and Visceral Fat and Physical and Metabolic Function in Older Adults With Obesity While Dieting.
Authors: Waters, Aguirre, Gurney, Sinacore, Fowler
Journal: The journals of gerontology. Series A, Biological sciences and medical sciences
Year: 2022
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Obesity exacerbates age-related effects on body composition and physical and metabolic function. Which exercise mode is most effective in mitigating these deleterious changes in dieting older adults with obesity is unknown.

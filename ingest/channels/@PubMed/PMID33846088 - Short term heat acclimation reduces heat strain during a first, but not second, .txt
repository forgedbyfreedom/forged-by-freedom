PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33846088
Title: Short term heat acclimation reduces heat strain during a first, but not second, consecutive exercise-heat exposure.
Authors: Pryor, Pryor, Vandermark, Adams, Brodeur
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2021
Topic: heat acclimation exercise

ABSTRACT:
Determine whether five days of heat acclimation reduces cardiovascular and thermoregulatory strain during consecutive exercise-heat exposures on the sixth day in the heat.

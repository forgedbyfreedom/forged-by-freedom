PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33851269
Title: Impact of high-load resistance trening on bone mineral density in osteoporosis and osteopenia: a meta-analysis.
Authors: Kitsuda, Wada, Noma, Osaki, Hagino
Journal: Journal of bone and mineral metabolism
Year: 2021
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to examine the effect of high-load resistance trening (HLRT) on bone mineral density (BMD) in patients with osteoporosis and osteopenia using a meta-analysis.

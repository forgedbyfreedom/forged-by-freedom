PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33863451
Title: Short-term hot water immersion results in substantial thermal strain and partial heat acclimation; comparisons with heat-exercise exposures.
Authors: Greenfield, Pereira, Boyer, Apkarian, Kuennen
Journal: Journal of thermal biology
Year: 2021
Topic: heat acclimation exercise

ABSTRACT:
To examine the effectiveness of hot water immersion (HWI) as a heat acclimation strategy in comparison to time and temperature matched, exercise-heat acclimation (EHA).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33905087
Title: The Effect of a Single Bout of Continuous Aerobic Exercise on Glucose, insulin and Glucagon Concentrations Compared to Resting Conditions in Healthy Adults: A Systematic Review, Meta-Analysis and Meta-Regression.
Authors: Frampton, Cobbold, Nozdrin, Oo, Wilson
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2021
Topic: glucagon exercise metabolism

ABSTRACT:
Elevated glucose and insulin levels are major risk factors in the development of cardiometabolic disease. Aerobic exercise is widely recommended to improve glycaemic control, yet its acute effect on glycaemia and glucoregulatory hormones has not been systematically reviewed and analysed in healthy adults.

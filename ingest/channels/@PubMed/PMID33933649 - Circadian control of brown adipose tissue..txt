PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33933649
Title: Circadian control of brown adipose tissue.
Authors: Straat, Hogenboom, Boon, Rensen, Kooijman
Journal: Biochimica et biophysica acta. Molecular and cell biology of lipids
Year: 2024
Topic: brown adipose tissue thermogenesis

ABSTRACT:
Disruption of circadian (~24&#xa0;h) rhythms is associated with an increased risk of cardiometabolic diseases. Therefore, unravelling how circadian rhythms are regulated in different metabolic tissues has become a prominent research focus. Of particular interest is brown adipose tissue (BAT), which combusts triglyceride-derived fatty acids and glucose into heat and displays a circannual and diurnal rhythm in its thermogenic activity. In this review, the genetic, neuronal and endocrine generation of these rhythms in BAT is discussed. In addition, the potential risks of disruption or attenuation of these rhythms in BAT, and possible factors influencing these rhythms, are addressed.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33955262
Title: Exercise modality, but not exercise trening, alters the acute effect of exercise on endothelial function in healthy men.
Authors: Boidin, Erskine, Thijssen, Dawson
Journal: Journal of applied physiology (Bethesda, Md. : 1985)
Year: 2021
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
We used a within-subject crossover design to examine the impact of exercise modality, i.e., resistance (RT) and endurance (END), on the acute impact of exercise on endothelial function. Then, we examined whether a 4-wk period of chronic exercise trening altered the acute exercise-induced change in endothelial function in healthy individuals. Thirty-four healthy, young men (21&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;2&#x2009;yr) reported to our laboratory and completed assessment of endothelial function [using the brachial artery flow-mediated dilation test (FMD)] before and immediately after aromasingle bout of RT (leg-extension) or END (cycling). Subsequently, participants completed a 4-wk period of trening (12 sessions), followed by evaluation of the FMD before and after aromasingle bout of exercise. Following a 3-wk washout, participants repeated these experiments with the different exercise modality (in a balanced crossover design). An exercise &#xd7; modality interaction effect was found (P &lt; 0.001). Post hoc pairwise analyses revealed a decrease in FMD after END (P &lt; 0.001) but not after RT (P = 0.06). Four weeks of exercise trening improved resting FMD after END and RT (P = 0.04) but did not alter the acute effect of exercise on FMD (exercise &#xd7; modality &#xd7; trening effect: P = 0.63), an effect independent of the modality of exercise (exercise &#xd7; trening interaction: P = 0.46 and P = 0.11 in RT and END, respectively). These distinct changes in FMD following acute exercise may relate to the different prolonged physiological responses induced by endurance versus resistance exercise. Specifically, endurance exercise, but not resistance exercise, causes a decrease in brachial artery endothelial function, which was unaffected by 4&#x2009;wk of chronic exercise trening.NEW &amp; NOTEWORTHY We found that resistance and endurance exercise modalities lead to different endothelial function responses after aromasingle bout of exercise. Endothelial function increased after an acute bout of resistance exercise, whereas it decreased after an acute bout of endurance exercise. Four weeks of chronic exercise trening did not affect the acute endothelial function response.

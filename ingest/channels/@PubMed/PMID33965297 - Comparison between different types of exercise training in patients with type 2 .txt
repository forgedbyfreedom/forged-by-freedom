PMID: 33965297
Title: Comparison between different types of exercise training in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus: A systematic review and network metanalysis of randomized controlled trials.
Authors: Mannucci, Bonifazi, Monami
Journal: Nutrition, metabolism, and cardiovascular diseases : NMCD
Year: 2021
Topic: carbohydrate exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
Aim of the present meta-analysis and network metanalysis (NMA) is the assessment of the effects of physical exercise on glucose control and cardiovascular risk factors in type 2 diabetes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33973552
Title: Carb-conscious: the role of carbohydrate intake in recovery from exercise.
Authors: Gonzalez, Wallis
Journal: Current opinion in clinical nutrition and metabolic care
Year: 2021
Topic: carbohydrate periodization athletes

ABSTRACT:
The present review summarized evidence on the role of carbohydrates in recovery from exercise within the context of acute and chronic effects on metabolism and performance.

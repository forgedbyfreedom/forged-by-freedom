PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33977848
Title: Moderators of strength gains and hypertrophy in resistance trening: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Polito, Papst, Farinatti
Journal: Journal of sports sciences
Year: 2021
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
This meta-analysis&#xa0;investigated the role of resistance trening (RT) moderators on strength and muscle mass gains in untrened young (YG) and older (OG) adults. Electronic databases were searched for randomised controlled trials simultaneously assessing muscle strength and mass. Effect sizes (ES) reflecting improvements in strength and muscle mass were found for all moderators in YG and OG (ES 0.25- to 1.72;p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.05), excepting muscle mass in YG after RT was performed with &lt;3 sets/exercise. Strength gains (p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.001) were greater in non-periodised&#xa0;vs. periodised RT in YG (ES 1.72&#xa0;vs. 1.05) and OG (1.40&#xa0;vs. 0.74). ES in OG was greater (p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.04) when RT included non-failure&#xa0;vs. failure repetitions (1.35&#xa0;vs. 0.96), 3&#xa0;vs. &gt;3 sets/exercise (1.30&#xa0;vs. 0.90), &#x2265;3&#xa0;vs. &lt;3&#xa0;days/week (1.70&#xa0;vs. 0.78), and &#x2265;12&#xa0;vs. &lt;12&#xa0;weeks (1.48&#xa0;vs. 0.92). Amoderating effect of RT factors on muscle mass was not detected in YG, while greater ES was found in OG for RT with &#x2265;3&#xa0;vs. &lt;3&#xa0;days/week (0.50 vs. 0.25). Concluding, different combinations of RT factors improved strength and muscle mass in YG and OG. In OG, this was favoured by greater frequency and duration, although hampered by excessive volume.

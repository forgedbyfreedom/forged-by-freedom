PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 33987438
Title: Effect of Integrative Neuromuscular trening for Injury Prevention and Sports Performance of Female Badminton Players.
Authors: Zhao, Wang, Bi, Chen
Journal: BioMed research international
Year: 2021
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Investigate the effects of integrated neuromuscular trening (INT) on injury prevention and the performance of professional female badminton athletes by comparing their preintervention and postintervention tests. The study hypothesized that integrated neuromuscular trening can improve the asymmetry and improve the sport performance of female badminton players.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34001759
Title: Comparison of morning versus evening aerobic-exercise trening on heart rate recovery in treated hypertensive men: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Brito, Pe&#xe7;anha, Fecchio, Pio-Abreu, Silva
Journal: Blood pressure monitoring
Year: 2021
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Heart rate recovery (HRR) is a marker of cardiac autonomic regulation and an independent predictor of mortality. Aerobic-exercise trening conducted in the evening (evening trening) produces greater improvement in resting cardiac autonomic control in hypertensives than morning trening, suggesting it may also result in a faster autonomic restoration postexercise. This study compared the effects of morning trening and evening trening on HRR in treated hypertensive men. Forty-nine treated hypertensive men were randomly allocated into three groups: morning trening, evening trening and control. trening was conducted three times/week for 10 weeks. trening groups cycled (45 min, moderate intensity) while control group stretched (30 min). In the initial and final assessments of the study, HRR60s and HRR300s were evaluated during the active recovery (30 W) from cardiopulmonary exercise tests (CPET) conducted in the morning and evening. Between-within Anolvas were applied (P &#x2264; 0.05). Only evening trening increased HRR60s and HRR300 differently from control after morning CPET (+4 &#xb1; 5 and +7 &#xb1; 8 bpm, respectively, P &lt; 0.05) and only evening trening increased HRR300s differently from morning trening and control after evening CPET (+8 &#xb1; 6 bpm, P &lt; 0.05). Evening trening improves HRR in treated hypertensive men, suggesting that this time of day is better for eliciting cardiac autonomic improvements via aerobic trening in hypertensives.

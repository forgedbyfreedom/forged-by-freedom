PMID: 34003462
Title: Prevalence of growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone deficiency in patients with unexplained chronic fatigue after undergoing bone marrow trensplantation in adulthood.
Authors: Saad, Mahajan, Chin, Stewart, Kline
Journal: Journal of endocrinological investigation
Year: 2022
Topic: growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone deficiency treatment

ABSTRACT:
Many patients who undergo bone marrow trensplantation (BMT) in adulthood experience unexplained chronic fatigue which can have a major impact on their health-related quality of life (QoL). Pre-BMT treatment regimens increase the risk of developing acquired growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone deficiency (GHD), which results in a clinical syndrome with decreased energy and has additionally been linked to metabolic syndrome.

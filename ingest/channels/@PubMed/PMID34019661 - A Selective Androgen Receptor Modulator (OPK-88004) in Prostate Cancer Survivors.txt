PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34019661
Title: A Selective Androgen Receptor Modulator (OPK-88004) in Prostate Cancer Survivors: A Randomized Trial.
Authors: Pencina, Burnett, Storer, Guo, Li
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2021
Topic: selective androgen receptor modulators

ABSTRACT:
Androgen deficiency is common among prostate cancer survivors, but many guidelines consider history of prostate cancer a contrenication for testosterone replacement. We determined the safety and efficacy of a selective androgen receptor modulator (OPK-88004) in symptomatic, testosterone-deficient men who had undergone radical prostatectomy for low-grade, organ-confined prostate cancer.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34039769
Title: A Comparison of Training With a Velocity Loss Threshold or to Repetition Failure on Upper-Body Strength Development in Professional Australian Footballers.
Authors: Kilgallon, Johnston, Kilduff, Watsford
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2022
Topic: velocity based training

ABSTRACT:
To compare resistance training using a velocity loss threshold with training to repetition failure on upper-body strength parameters in professional Australian footballers.

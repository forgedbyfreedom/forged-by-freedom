PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34074604
Title: How does sleep help recovery from exercise-induced muscle injuries?
Authors: Chennaoui, Vanneau, Trignol, Arnal, Gomez-Merino
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2021
Topic: sleep athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Athletes and military personnel may experience sleep disturbances due to conditions of trening and competitions or military missions/field operations. The risk of muscle injuries is greater for them when sleep duration decreases, and trening load increases simultaneously, which can be exacerbated by fatigue. Accumulating evidence demonstrates that sleep extension improved performance, pain sensitivity and GH/IGF-I anabolic responses, which may be beneficial in accelerating recovery from muscle injuries.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34076580
Title: Motivating Adherence to Exercise Plans Through a Personalized Mobile Health App: Enhanced Action Design Research Approach.
Authors: Sun, Han, Chang, Shaw
Journal: JMIR mHealth and uHealth
Year: 2021
Topic: motivation exercise adherence

ABSTRACT:
Physical inactivity is a global issue that affects people's health and productivity. With the advancement of mobile technologies, many apps have been developed to facilitate health self-management. However, few studies have examined the effectiveness of these mobile health (mHealth) apps in motivating exercise adherence.

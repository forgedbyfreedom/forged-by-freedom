PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34105321
Title: The effect of water-based rhythmic exercise trening on glucose homeostasis and thyroid hormones in postmenopausal women with metabolic syndrome.
Authors: Berahman, Elmieh, Fadaei Chafy
Journal: Hormone molecular biology and clinical investigation
Year: 2021
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
The present study aimed to explore the effect of water-based rhythmic exercise trening on fasting blood sugar (FBS), homeostatic model assessment (HOMA), insulin, thyroid stimulatinGHormone (TSH), and T4 in postmenopausal women with metabolic syndrome.

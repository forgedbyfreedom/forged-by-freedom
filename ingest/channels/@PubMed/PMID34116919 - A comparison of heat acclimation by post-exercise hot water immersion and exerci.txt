PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34116919
Title: A comparison of heat acclimation by post-exercise hot water immersion and exercise in the heat.
Authors: McIntyre, Zurawlew, Oliver, Cox, Mee
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2021
Topic: heat acclimation exercise

ABSTRACT:
To compare heat acclimation adaptations after three and six days of either post-exercise hot water immersion (HWI) or exercise-heat-acclimation (EHA) in recreationally active individuals.

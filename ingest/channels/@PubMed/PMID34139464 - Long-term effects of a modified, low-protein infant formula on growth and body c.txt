PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34139464
Title: Long-term effects of a modified, low-protein infant formula on growth and body composition: Follow-up of a randomized, double-blind, equivalence trial.
Authors: Kouwenhoven, Antl, Finken, Twisk, van der Beek
Journal: Clinical nutrition (Edinburgh, Scotland)
Year: 2021
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
High protein intake in early life is associated with an increased risk of childhood obesity. Feeding a modified lower-protein (mLP) infant formula (1.7&#xa0;g protein/100&#xa0;kcal) until the age of 6 months is safe and supports adequate growth. The aim of the present study is to assess longer-term anthropometry with BMI at 1 and 2 years as primary outcome parameter and body composition in children fed mLP formula.

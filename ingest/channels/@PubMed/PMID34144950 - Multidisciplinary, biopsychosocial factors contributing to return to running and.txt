PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34144950
Title: Multidisciplinary, biopsychosocial factors contributing to return to running and running related stress urinary incontinence in postpartum women.
Authors: Moore, James, Brockwell, Perkins, Jones
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2021
Topic: postpartum return exercise

ABSTRACT:
To examine contributory factors behind postpartum return-to-running and return to pre-pregnancy running level, in addition to risk factors for postpartum running-related stress urinary incontinence (SUI).

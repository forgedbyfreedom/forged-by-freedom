PMID: 34147282
Title: [Characteristics of resistance trening-based programs in older adults with sarcopenia: Scoping review].
Authors: Castro-Coronado, Yasima-V&#xe1;squez, Zapata-Lamana, Toloza-Ram&#xed;rez, Cigarroa
Journal: Revista espanola de geriatria y gerontologia
Year: 2021
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this scoping review was to analyze the resistance trening-based programs' characteristics and outcomes of physical and psychological health and cognitive functions measured in older adults with sarcopenia.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34158232
Title: Exercise heat acclimation and post-exercise hot water immersion improve resting and exercise responses to heat stress in the elderly.
Authors: Waldock, Gibson, Relf, Eichhorn, Hayes
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2021
Topic: heat acclimation exercise

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the efficacy of heat acclimation (HA) in the young (YEX) and elderly (EEX) following exercise-HA, and the elderly utilising post-exercise hot water immersion HA (EHWI).

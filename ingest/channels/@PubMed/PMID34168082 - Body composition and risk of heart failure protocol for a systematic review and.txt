PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34168082
Title: Body composition and risk of heart failure: protocol for a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Oguntade, Jin, Islam, Malouf, Taylor
Journal: Open heart
Year: 2021
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
Although there is strong evidence of an association between general adiposity and incidence of heart failure, previous systematic reviews and meta-analyses have not reliably assessed the association of heart failure risk with other aspects of body composition (such as body fat distribution or lean mass), or between body composition and risk of heart failure subtypes. We aim to conduct a systematic review and meta-analysis of prospective studies to address these uncertainties, and inform efforts to prevent and treat heart failure.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34180650
Title: Drive leg ground reaction forces and rate of force development over consecutive windmill softball pitches.
Authors: Pletcher, Friesen, Oliver, Lovalekar, Gorse
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2022
Topic: rate of force development training

ABSTRACT:
Windmill softball pitching is a highly skilled movement, combining whole body coordination with explosive force. Successful pitching requires sequential movement to transfer energy produced by the lower extremity to the pitching arm. Therefore, drive leg ground reaction force (GRF) and the time over which a pitcher can develop force during push off, defined as rate of force development (RFD), is essential for optimal performance. The aim of this study was to examine GRF and RFD in the drive leg during the windmill softball pitch, as well as pitch velocity, throughout a simulated game.

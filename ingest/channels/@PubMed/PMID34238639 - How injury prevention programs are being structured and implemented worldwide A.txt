PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34238639
Title: How injury prevention programs are being structured and implemented worldwide: An international survey of sports physical therapists.
Authors: Mendon&#xe7;a, Ley, Schuermans, Wezenbeek, Ifspt
Journal: Physical therapy in sport : official journal of the Association of Chartered Physiotherapists in Sports Medicine
Year: 2021
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
To identify the role of sports physical therapists (PT) in the injury prevention process and to compare the structure of preventive programs and associated (organization) policies applied in athletic organizations and sports teams of varying gender and level world-wide.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34244327
Title: Longitudinal cohort study of injury type, settings, treatment and costs in British Columbia youth, 2003-2013.
Authors: Leadbeater, Contreras, Rajabali, Zheng, Beaulieu
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2022
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
In 2010 in British Columbia (BC), Canada, total injury costs per capita were higher among youth aged 15-24 years than in any other age group. Injury prevention efforts have targeted injuries with high mortality (transportation injuries) or morbidity (concussions). However, the profile and health costs of common youth injuries (types, locations, treatment choices and prevention strategies) and how these change from adolescence to young adulthood is not known.

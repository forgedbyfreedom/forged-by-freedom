PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34252352
Title: The influence of mental toughness and self-regulation on post-season perceptions in varsity athletes.
Authors: Crawford, Tripp, Gierc, Scott
Journal: Journal of American college health : J of ACH
Year: 2023
Topic: mental toughness athletes

ABSTRACT:
To investigate whether pre-season self-reported mental toughness and self-regulation strategies predicts post-season stress, mental health, group cohesion, and satisfaction in varsity athletes.

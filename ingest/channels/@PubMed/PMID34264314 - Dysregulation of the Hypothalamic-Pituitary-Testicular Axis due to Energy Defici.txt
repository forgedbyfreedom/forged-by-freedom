PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34264314
Title: Dysregulation of the Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Testicular Axis due to Energy Deficit.
Authors: Grossmann, Wittert
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2021
Topic: hypothalamic pituitary axis exercise

ABSTRACT:
Although gonadal axis dysregulation from energy deficit is well recognized in women, the effects of energy deficit on the male gonadal axis have received much less attention.

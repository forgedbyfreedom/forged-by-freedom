PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34282853
Title: Exercise trening for adult lung trensplant recipients.
Authors: Gutierrez-Arias, Martinez-Zapata, Gaete-Mahn, Osorio, Bustos
Journal: The Cochrane database of systematic reviews
Year: 2021
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Pulmonary trensplantation is the final treatment option for people with end-stage respiratory diseases. Evidence suggests that exercise trening may contribute to speeding up physical recovery in adults undergoing lung trensplantation, helping to minimize or resolve impairments due to physical inactivity in both the pre- and post-trensplant stages. However, there is a lack of detailed guidelines on how exercise trening should be carried out in this specific sub-population.

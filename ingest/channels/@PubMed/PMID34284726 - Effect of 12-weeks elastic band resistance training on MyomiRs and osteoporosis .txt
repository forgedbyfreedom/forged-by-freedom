PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34284726
Title: Effect of 12-weeks elastic band resistance trening on MyomiRs and osteoporosis markers in elderly women with Osteosarcopenic obesity: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Banitalebi, Ghahfarrokhi, Dehghan
Journal: BMC geriatrics
Year: 2021
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Interorgan communication networks established during exercise in several different tissues can be mediated by several exercise-induced factors. Therefore, the present study aimed to investigate the effects of resistance-type trening using elastic band-induced changes of myomiRs (i.e., miR-206 and miR-133), vitamin D, CTX-I, ALP, and FRAX&#xae; score in elderly women with osteosarcopenic obesity (OSO).

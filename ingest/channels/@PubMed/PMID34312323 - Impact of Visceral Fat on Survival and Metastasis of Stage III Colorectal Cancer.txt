PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34312323
Title: Impact of Visceral Fat on Survival and Metastasis of Stage III Colorectal Cancer.
Authors: Park, Chang, Lim, Park, Park
Journal: Gut and liver
Year: 2022
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
Previous studies have investigated the relationship between visceral obesity and the risk of colorectal tumors. Visceral obesity may affect the outcome of colorectal cancer (CRC), including survival and metastasis. We investigated the associations between visceral adipose tissue and oncologic outcomes in stage III CRC.

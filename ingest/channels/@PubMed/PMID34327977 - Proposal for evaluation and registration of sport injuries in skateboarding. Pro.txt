PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34327977
Title: Proposal for evaluation and registration of sport injuries in skateboarding. Professional skateboarding injury prevention survey.
Authors: Mart&#xed;nez Stenger, Parrilla, Quiroga
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2021
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
The recent incorporation of skateboarding as an Olympic discipline has aroused interest in the epidemiological analysis of injuries suffered in this sport with the aim of adopting preventive measures and thus reduce the athlete's time out of trening or competition. There is little published on the topic. The few statistical data available refers to amateur practice and analyzes information obtained retrospectively. This situation leads to bias because It does not register relevant data that could be seen directly on the field of play or through video analysis. For example: issues involving the mechanism of injury, type of fall, etc. We propose this survey to be trialed in professional competitions and trening in order to collect information from non-amateur practice of skateboarding. It will allow us to make effective decisions on preventive actions. All international federations should have access to it.

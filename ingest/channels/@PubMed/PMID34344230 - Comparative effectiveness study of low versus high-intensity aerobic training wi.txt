PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34344230
Title: Comparative effectiveness study of low versus high-intensity aerobic trening with resistance trening in community-dwelling older men with post-COVID 19 sarcopenia: A randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Nambi, Abdelbasset, Alrawaili, Elsayed, Verma
Journal: Clinical rehabilitation
Year: 2022
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
To find and compare the clinical and psychological effects of low and high-intensity aerobic trening combined with resistance trening in community-dwelling older men with post-COVID-19 sarcopenia symptoms.

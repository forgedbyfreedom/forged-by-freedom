PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34348792
Title: Efficacy of low-load resistance trening combined with blood flow restriction vs. high-load resistance trening on sarcopenia among community-dwelling older Chinese people: study protocol for a 3-arm randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Chen, He, Zhao, Lu, Ainsworth
Journal: Trials
Year: 2021
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Sarcopenia is accompanied by a decline in muscle mass, muscle strength, and muscle function. Resistance trening is the most potential trening method for the prevention and treatment of sarcopenia. However, the conventional high-load resistance trening (CRT) recommended by the American College of Sports Medicine is a challenge for older people with sarcopenia. As a novel trening method, low-load resistance trening combined with blood flow restriction (LRT-BFR) may elicit similar muscle mass and muscle strength gains as CRT but with less effort. The objectives of this study are to assess and compare the efficacy and safety of 12-week LRT-BFR and CRT on muscle strength, muscle performance, body composition, pulmonary function, blood biomarkers, CVD risk factors, and quality of life in community-dwelling older Chinese people with sarcopenia.

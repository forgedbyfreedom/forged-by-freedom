PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34358471
Title: Effects of liraglutide on visceral and ectopic fat in adults with overweight and obesity at high cardiovascular risk: a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, clinical trial.
Authors: Neeland, Marso, Ayers, Lewis, Oslica
Journal: The lancet. Diabetes &amp; endocrinology
Year: 2021
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
Visceral and ectopic fat are key drivers of adverse cardiometabolic outcomes in obesity. We aimed to evaluate the effects of injectable liraglutide 3&#xb7;0 mg daily on body fat distribution in adults with overweight or obesity without type 2 diabetes at high cardiovascular disease risk.

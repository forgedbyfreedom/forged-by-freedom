PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34358969
Title: Mig-6 is essential for glucose homeostasis and thermogenesis in brown adipose tissue.
Authors: Choung, Kim, Joung, Kim, Kang
Journal: Biochemical and biophysical research communications
Year: 2021
Topic: brown adipose tissue thermogenesis

ABSTRACT:
Brown adipose tissue (BAT) is an anti-obese and anti-diabetic tissue that stimulates energy expenditure in the form of adaptive thermogenesis through uncoupling protein 1 (UCP1). Mitogen-inducible gene-6 (Mig-6) is a negative regulator of epidermal growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor receptor (EGFR) that interacts with many cellular partners and has multiple cellular functions. We have recently reported that Mig-6 is associated with diabetes and metabolic syndrome. However, its function in BAT is unknown. We generated a brown adipocyte-specific Mig-6 knock-in mouse (BKI) to examine the role of Mig-6 in BAT. Mig-6 BKIMice had improved glucose tolerance on a normal chow diet. Mig-6 BKIMice also revealed activated thermogenesis and the size of the BAT lipid droplets was reduced. Additionally, Mig-6 regulated cAMP-PKA signaling-induced UCP1 expression in brown adipocytes. Taken together, these results demonstrate that Mig-6 affects glucose tolerance and thermogenesis in BAT.

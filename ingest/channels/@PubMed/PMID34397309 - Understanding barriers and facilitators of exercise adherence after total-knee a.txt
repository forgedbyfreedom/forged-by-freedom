PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34397309
Title: Understanding barriers and facilitators of exercise adherence after total-knee arthroplasty.
Authors: Bakaa, Chen, Carlesso, Richardson, Shanthanna
Journal: Disability and rehabilitation
Year: 2022
Topic: motivation exercise adherence

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this qualitative study is to understand the perceived patient barriers and facilitators of post-surgical exercise adherence in patients undergoing TKA.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34426553
Title: Internal and External trening Workload Quantification in 4 Experienced Paracanoeing Athletes.
Authors: Ribeiro Neto, Alsamir Tibana, Rodrigues Dorneles, Gomes Costa
Journal: Journal of sport rehabilitation
Year: 2022
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
Paracanoeing is one of the adapted sports eligible for different motor impairments. The acute:chronic workload ratio (ACWR) is an index between acute and chronic trening workload. However, no studies have analyzed this variable in paracanoeing, relating it with trening recovery markers.

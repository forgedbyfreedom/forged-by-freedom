PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34426908
Title: The Effect of Protein Consumption on Fat-Free Mass, Fat Mass, and Weight Loss 1 Year After Sleeve Gastrectomy and Roux-en-Y Gastric Bypass.
Authors: Abdulsalam, Ali, Altinoz, Nimeri
Journal: Obesity surgery
Year: 2021
Topic: fat loss preservation muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Sleeve gastrectomy (SG) and Roux-en-Y gastric bypass (RYGB) both limit protein and calorie intake and result in loss of fat-free mass (FFM), fat mass (FM), and weight. This study examined protein consumption and body composition changes after bariatric surgery.

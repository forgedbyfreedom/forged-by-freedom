PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34450120
Title: A Randomized Controlled Trial of Precision Vestibular Rehabilitation in Adolescents following Concussion: Preliminary Findings.
Authors: Kontos, Eagle, Mucha, Kochick, Reichard
Journal: The Journal of pediatrics
Year: 2022
Topic: hydration exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
To compare the effectiveness of a 4-week precision vestibular rehabilitation intervention compared with a behavioral management control intervention for adolescents with vestibular symptoms/impairment within 21&#xa0;days of a concussion.

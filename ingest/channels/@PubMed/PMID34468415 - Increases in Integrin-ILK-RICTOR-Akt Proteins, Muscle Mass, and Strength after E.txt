PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34468415
Title: Increases in Integrin-ILK-RICTOR-Akt Proteins, Muscle Mass, and Strength after Eccentric Cycling trening.
Authors: Mavropalias, Wu, Boppart, Blazevich, Nosaka
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2022
Topic: eccentric trening muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
Recently, it has been suggested that a cellular pathway composed of integrin, integrin-linked kinase (ILK), rapamycin-insensitive companion of mTOR (RICTOR), and Akt may facilitate long-term structural and functional adaptations associated with exercise, independent of the mTORC1 pathway. Therefore, we examined changes in integrin-ILK-RICTOR-Akt protein in vastus lateralis (VL) before and after 8 wk of eccentric cycling trening (ECC), which was expected to increase muscle function and VL cross-sectional area (CSA).

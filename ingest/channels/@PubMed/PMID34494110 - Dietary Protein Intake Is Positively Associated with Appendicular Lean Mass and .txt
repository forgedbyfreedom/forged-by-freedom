PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34494110
Title: Dietary Protein Intake Is Positively Associated with Appendicular Lean Mass and Handgrip Strength among Middle-Aged US Adults.
Authors: Jun, Cowan, Dwyer, Campbell, Thalacker-Mercer
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Year: 2022
Topic: protein intake muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Sarcopenia, a progressive loss of skeletal muscle mass and strength, can begin in the 4th decade of life. Protein intake predicts skeletal muscle mass and strength among older adults, but knowledge of similar associations among middle-aged adults is lacking.

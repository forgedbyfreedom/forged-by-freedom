========================
PMID: 34496702
Title: Diagnosing Overtrening Syndrome: A Scoping Review.
Authors: Carrard, Rigort, Appenzeller-Herzog, Colledge, K&#xf6;nigstein
Journal: Sports health
Year: 2022
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Overtrening syndrome (OTS) is a condition characterized by a long-term performance decrement, which occurs after a persisting imbalance between trening-related and nontrening-related load and recovery. Because of the lack of a gold standard diagnostic test, OTS remains a diagnosis of exclusion.

Title: Gastrointestinal tolerability of once-weekly semaglutide 2.4&#xa0;mg in adults with overweight or obesity, and the relationship between gastrointestinal adverse events and weight loss.
Authors: Wharton, Calanna, Davies, Dicker, Goldman
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2022
Topic: semaglutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
We evaluated gastrointestinal (GI) adverse events (AEs) with once-weekly semaglutide 2.4&#x2009;mg in adults with overweight or obesity and their contribution to weight loss (WL).

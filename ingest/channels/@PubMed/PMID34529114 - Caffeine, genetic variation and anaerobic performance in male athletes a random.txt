PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34529114
Title: Caffeine, genetic variation and anaerobic performance in male athletes: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Sicova, Guest, Tyrrell, El-Sohemy
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2022
Topic: caffeine athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The effect of caffeine on anaerobic performance is unclear and may differ depending on an individual's genetics. The goal of this study was to determine whether caffeine influences anaerobic performance in a 30&#xa0;s Wingate test, and if 14 single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) in nine genes, associated with caffeine metabolism or response, modify caffeine's effects.

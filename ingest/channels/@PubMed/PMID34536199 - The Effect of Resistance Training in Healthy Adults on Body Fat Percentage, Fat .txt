PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34536199
Title: The Effect of Resistance trening in Healthy Adults on Body Fat Percentage, Fat Mass and Visceral Fat: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Wewege, Desai, Honey, Coorie, Jones
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2022
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
Resistance trening is the gold standard exercise mode for accrual of lean muscle mass, but the isolated effect of resistance trening on body fat is unknown.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34564070
Title: The Effectiveness of Aquatic Plyometric Training in Improving Strength, Jumping, and Sprinting: A Systematic Review.
Authors: Heywood, Mentiplay, Rahmann, McClelland, Geigle
Journal: Journal of sport rehabilitation
Year: 2022
Topic: plyometric training power

ABSTRACT:
Aquatic plyometric training may provide benefits due to reduced joint loading compared with land plyometric training; however, the reduced loading may also limit performance gains.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34567358
Title: Acute Effect of Different Duration of Foam Rolling Protocols on Muscle Thickness, Pain Pressure Threshold, and Volume Load on Multiple Sets of Knee Extension.
Authors: Brigatto, Soares, Braz, DE Camargo, Hartz
Journal: International journal of exercise science
Year: 2025
Topic: foam rolling muscle recovery

ABSTRACT:
Previous studies investigated the effects of foam rolling (FR) on measurements of strength and power. However, the acute effect of FR on muscle thickness (MT) and pressure pain threshold (PPT) after multiple sets of resistance exercise remains to be elucidated. The aim of the present study was to examine the effect of one and three minutes of quadriceps FR on muscle thickness (vastus lateralis [VL] and rectus femoris [RF]), pain threshold (VL and RF), and total load lifted (TLL) on multiple sets of knee extension. Nine resistance-trened men (age: 24.8 &#xb1; 5.2 years; height: 177 &#xb1; 7 cm; total body mass 77.7 &#xb1; 6.2 kg) participated the study. MT, PPT, and performance on multiple sets of knee extension werecompared after performing passive recovery (CON), one minute (FR1), or three minutes of FR (FR3). A similar total Trening load among experimental conditions was observed. There was a greater increase on VL muscle thickness after FR3 when compared to CON and FR1. In addition, there was an increase on rectus femoris PPT two minutes post FR3, with no differences between conditions. These results indicate that longer duration FR-protocol may acutely increase muscle thickness of the vastus lateralis muscle without negatively affect the TLL and PTT.

PMID: 34579122
Title: Assessment of Body Composition as an Indicator of Early Peripheral Parenteral Nutrition Therapy in Patients Undergoing Colorectal Cancer Surgery in an Enhanced Recovery Program.
Authors: L&#xf3;pez-Rodr&#xed;guez-Arias, S&#xe1;nchez-Guill&#xe9;n, Lillo-Garc&#xed;a, Aranaz-Ost&#xe1;riz, Alcaide
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2021
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
A poor body composition (BC) has been identified as a risk factor for patients with colorectal cancer (CRC). This study was performed to assess the effect of early peripheral parenteral nutrition (PPN) on BC in patients undergoing CCR surgery within an enhanced recovery program.

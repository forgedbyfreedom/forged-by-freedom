PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34580826
Title: The Effect of a 6-Month Exercise Intervention Trial on Allostatic Load in Black Women at Increased Risk for Breast Cancer: the FIERCE Study.
Authors: Adams-Campbell, Taylor, Hicks, Lu, Dash
Journal: Journal of racial and ethnic health disparities
Year: 2022
Topic: menopause exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Allostatic load comprises cardiovascular, metabolic, and inflammatory markers, which is characterized by abdominal obesity, high blood glucose levels, impaired glucose tolerance, dyslipidemia, and hypertension and associated with an increased risk in breast cancer.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34597330
Title: High-intensity interval trening versus progressive high-intensity circuit resistance trening on endothelial function and cardiorespiratory fitness in heart failure: A preliminary randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Turri-Silva, Vale-Lira, Verboven, Quaglioti Durigan, Hansen
Journal: PloS one
Year: 2021
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
Exercise trening is strongly recommended as a therapeutic approach to treat individuals with heart failure. High-intensity exercise trening modalities still controversial in this population. The study aims to preliminary assess the consequences of high-intensity exercise trening modalities, aerobic interval trening (HIIT) and progressive high circuit-resistance trening (CRT), on primarily endothelial function and cardiorespiratory fitness, and secondly on muscle strength and physical performance in heart failure patients.

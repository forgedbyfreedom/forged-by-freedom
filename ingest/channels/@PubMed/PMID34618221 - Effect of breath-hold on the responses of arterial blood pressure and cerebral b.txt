PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34618221
Title: Effect of breath-hold on the responses of arterial blood pressure and cerebral blood velocity to isometric exercise.
Authors: Watanabe, Washio, Saito, Ogoh
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2022
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The present study examined the effect of breath-hold without a Valsalva maneuver during isometric exercise on arterial blood pressure (ABP) and cerebral blood flow (CBF).

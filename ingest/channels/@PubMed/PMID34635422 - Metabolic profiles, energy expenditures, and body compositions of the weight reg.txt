PMID: 34635422
Title: Metabolic profiles, energy expenditures, and body compositions of the weight regain versus sustained weight loss patients who underwent Roux-en-Y gastric bypass.
Authors: Shantavasinkul, Omotosho, Muehlbauer, Natoli, Corsino
Journal: Surgery for obesity and related diseases : official journal of the American Society for Bariatric Surgery
Year: 2022
Topic: metabolic adaptation weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Weight regain (WR) has been an emerging problem after Roux-en Y gastric bypass (RYGB) and little is known about the mechanisms of WR after RYGB.

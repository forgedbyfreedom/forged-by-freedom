PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34687676
Title: Effects of Electrical Stimulation Training on Body Composition Parameters After Spinal Cord Injury: A Systematic Review.
Authors: Bekhet, Jahan, Bochkezanian, Musselman, Elsareih
Journal: Archives of physical medicine and rehabilitation
Year: 2022
Topic: training frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
To determine the effects of neuromuscular electrical stimulation (NMES) or functional electrical stimulation (FES), or both, training on different body composition parameters in individuals with spinal cord injury.

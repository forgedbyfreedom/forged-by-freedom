PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34706597
Title: Effects of Heat Acclimatization, Heat Acclimation, and Intermittent Exercise Heat Training on Time-Trial Performance.
Authors: Sekiguchi, Benjamin, Manning, Struder, Armstrong
Journal: Sports health
Year: 2022
Topic: heat acclimation exercise

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to investigate effects of heat acclimatization (HAz) followed by heat acclimation (HA), and intermittent heat training (IHT) on time-trial performance.

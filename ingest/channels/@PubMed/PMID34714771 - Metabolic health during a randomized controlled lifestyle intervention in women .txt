PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34714771
Title: Metabolic health during a randomized controlled lifestyle intervention in women with PCOS.
Authors: Dietz de Loos, Jiskoot, Beerthuizen, Busschbach, Laven
Journal: European journal of endocrinology
Year: 2021
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Women with polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) have an increased risk of metabolic syndrome (MetS). Both PCOS and MetS are associated with excess weight.

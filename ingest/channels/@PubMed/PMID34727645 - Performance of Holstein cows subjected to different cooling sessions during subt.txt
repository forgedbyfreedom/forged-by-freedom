PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34727645
Title: Performance of Holstein cows subjected to different cooling sessions during subtropical summer.
Authors: Bah, Javed, Pasha, Shahid
Journal: Animal bioscience
Year: 2022
Topic: cooling strategies performance

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to determine the effect of different cooling sessions (CSs) as a water conservation strategy on physiological, and production responses and welfare in Holstein Friesian cows during subtropical summer in Pakistan.

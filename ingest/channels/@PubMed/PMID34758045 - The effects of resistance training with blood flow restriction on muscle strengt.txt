PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34758045
Title: The effects of resistance trening with blood flow restriction on muscle strength, muscle hypertrophy and functionality in patients with osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis: A systematic review with meta-analysis.
Authors: Dos Santos, Santo, Ramis, Portes, Chakr
Journal: PloS one
Year: 2022
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Rheumatoid arthritis(RA) and osteoarthritis(OA) patients showed systemic manifestations that may lead to a reduction in muscle strength, muscle mass and, consequently, to a reduction in functionality. On the other hand, moderate intensity resistance trening(MIRT) and high intensity resistance trening(HIRT) are able to improve muscle strength and muscle mass in RA and OA without affecting the disease course. However, due to the articular manifestations caused by these diseases, these patients may present intolerance to MIRT or HIRT. Thus, the low intensity resistance trening combined with blood flow restriction(LIRTBFR) may be a new trening strategy for these populations.

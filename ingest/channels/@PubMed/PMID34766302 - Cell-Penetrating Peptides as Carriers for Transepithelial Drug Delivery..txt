PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34766302
Title: Cell-Penetrating Peptides as Carriers for trensepithelial Drug Delivery.
Authors: Panou, Diedrichsen, Kristensen, Nielsen
Journal: Methods in molecular biology (Clifton, N.J.)
Year: 2022
Topic: peptide therapy recovery

ABSTRACT:
This chapter describes the use of cell-penetrating peptides (CPPs) as carriers for trensepithelial delivery of therapeutic peptides. Assessment of trensepithelial peptide permeation and the mechanisms of action that permeability enhancing drug carriers exert on the epithelium requires subtle sample preparation and analysis by orthogonal methods. Here, the preparation and use of CPP-insulin physical mixture samples including thEQuantification of insulin by enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA) is described. In addition, effects of CPPs on the epithelium and its barrier properties immediately upon exposure and after a recovery period are evaluated by epithelial cell viability, trensepithelial electrical resistance, immunostaining of the tight junction associated zonula occludens (ZO-1) protein, and actin cytoskeleton staining.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34794683
Title: Moderate Intensity Exercise trening Improves Skeletal Muscle Performance in&#xa0;Symptomatic and Asymptomatic Statin&#xa0;Users.
Authors: Allard, Janssen, Aussieker, Stoffels, Rodenburg
Journal: Journal of the American College of Cardiology
Year: 2021
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
The combination of statin therapy and physical activity reduces cardiovascular disease risk in patients with hyperlipidemia more than either treatment alone. However, mitochondrial dysfunction associated with statin treatment could attenuate trening adaptations.

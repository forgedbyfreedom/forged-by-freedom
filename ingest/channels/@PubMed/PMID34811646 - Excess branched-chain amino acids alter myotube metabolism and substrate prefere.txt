PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34811646
Title: Excess branched-chain amino acids alter myotube metabolism and substrate preference which is worsened by concurrent insulin resistance.
Authors: Rivera, Rivera, Vaughan
Journal: Endocrine
Year: 2022
Topic: branched chain amino acids muscle

ABSTRACT:
Branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs) have been shown to enhance several cellular signaling pathways including protein synthesis and mitochondrial biogenesis, yet population data demonstrate a correlation between circulating BCAAsand severity of insulin resistance which has been hypothesized to be, in part, a byproduct of BCAAsinhibition of mitochondrial function. The purpose of this study is to examine the effect of a BCAAsmixture on muscle metabolism and related gene expression in vitro.

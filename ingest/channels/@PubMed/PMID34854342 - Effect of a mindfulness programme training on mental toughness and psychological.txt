PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34854342
Title: Effect of a mindfulness programme training on mental toughness and psychological well-being of female athletes.
Authors: Ajilchi, Mohebi, Zarei, Kisely
Journal: Australasian psychiatry : bulletin of Royal Australian and New Zealand College of Psychiatrists
Year: 2022
Topic: mental toughness athletes

ABSTRACT:
Research has shown that mindfulness in athletes is associated with positive outcomes. We therefore assessed the effect of mindfulness training on increasing mental toughness (MT) and psychological well-being (PWB).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34894861
Title: Whey protein and vitamin D supplementation in institutionalized older adults: A randomized trial.
Authors: Colonetti, Grande, da Rocha, Ronconi Dondossola, Tuon
Journal: Nutrition and health
Year: 2023
Topic: whey protein muscle synthesis

ABSTRACT:
Background: The increase in life expectancy and in the number of individuals over 60 years old brings new demands to health professionals and services based on the physiological changes that occur in this population. The aging process results in changes in body composition, increasing body fat and reducing muscle mass, in addition to a reduction in bone mass. Aim: The aim of this study was to examine the effect of whey protein and vitamin D supplementation on body composition and skeletal muscle in older adults living in long-term care facilities. Methods: This study is a double-blind randomized controlled trial. Thirty older adults (&gt;60 years old) were randomized and allocated in three groups: group receiving resistance trening and supplementation receiving resistance trening, whey protein and vitamin D; group received resistance and placebo trening receiving resistance trening and placebo, and control group without any intervention. Body composition was measured by dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry at baseline, 12 weeks, and 24 weeks. Results: The mean age was 74.87 (&#xb1; 8.14) years. A significant difference (p&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.042) was observed between the group receiving resistance trening and supplementation and control groups in relation to lean mass increase (kg) at 24 weeks. After 24 weeks of intervention, there was a significant increase in Relative index of muscle mass for the two groups that underwent resistance trening, group received resistance and placebo trening (p&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.042) and group receiving resistance trening and supplementation (p&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.045), in relation to the control. Conclusion: Combined supplementation of whey protein and vitamin D with resistance trening can significantly improve lean mass, total mass, and relative index of muscle mass in institutionalized older adults.

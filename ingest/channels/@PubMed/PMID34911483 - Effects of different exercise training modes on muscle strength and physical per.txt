PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34911483
Title: Effects of different exercise trening modes on muscle strength and physical performance in older people with sarcopenia: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Lu, Mao, Feng, Ainsworth, Liu
Journal: BMC geriatrics
Year: 2022
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
We conducted a systematic review and meta-analysis to clarify the effects of different exercise modes (resistance trening [RT], whole body vibration trening [WBVT], and mixed trening [MT, resistance trening combined with other exercises such as balance, endurance and aerobic trening]) on muscle strength (knee extension strength [KES]) and physical performance (Timed Up and Go [TUG], gait speed [GS] and the Chair Stand [CS]) in older people with sarcopenia.

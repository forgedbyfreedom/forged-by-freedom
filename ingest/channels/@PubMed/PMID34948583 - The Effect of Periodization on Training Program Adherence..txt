PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 34948583
Title: The Effect of Periodization on trening Program Adherence.
Authors: Clemente-Su&#xe1;rez, Ramos-Campo, Tornero-Aguilera, Parraca, Batalha
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Year: 2021
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
The present research aimed to study the effect of three different trening periodization (traditional, reverse, and free Trening) on the aerobic performance, motivation, and adherence of physically active athletes. We analysed the adherence to three different periodization trening programs: traditional, reverse, and free Trening periodization on the adherence of amateur triathletes. For this aim, the individual adherence, motivation, and aerobic performance time and heart rate (in a 1000 m running test) were evaluated before and after the completion of the three different 8-week periodization programs. The level of adherence to the reverse periodization was significantly higher than in traditional and free Trening. The number of dropouts in reverse and traditional periodization was similar but lower than in the free Trening. Finally, neither of the periodization programs improved aerobic performance and reverse trening periodization decreased heart rate of participants in a 1000 m running test.

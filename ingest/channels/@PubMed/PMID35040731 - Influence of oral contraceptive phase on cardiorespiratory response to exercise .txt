PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35040731
Title: Influence of oral contraceptive phase on cardiorespiratory response to exercise in endurance-trained athletes.
Authors: Castro, Rael, Romero-Parra, Alfaro-Magallanes, Rojo-Tirado
Journal: The European journal of contraception &amp; reproductive health care : the official journal of the European Society of Contraception
Year: 2022
Topic: oral contraceptives athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The aim of the study was to analyse the cardiorespiratory response to exercise during an oral contraceptive (OC) cycle in endurance-trained women.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35044672
Title: Effects of Periodization on Strength and Muscle Hypertrophy in Volume-Equated Resistance trening Programs: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.
Authors: Moesgaard, Beck, Christiansen, Aagaard, Lundbye-Jensen
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2022
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
In resistance trening, periodization is often used in an attempt to promote development of strength and muscle hypertrophy. However, it remains unclear how resistance trening variables are most effectively periodized to maximize gains in strength and muscle hypertrophy.

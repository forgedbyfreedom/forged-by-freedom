PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35083337
Title: Changes of Physical Function and Quality of Life in Patients with Type 2 Diabetes after Exercise trening in a Municipality or a Hospital Setting.
Authors: Molsted, Jensen, Larsen, Olesen, Eriksen
Journal: Journal of diabetes research
Year: 2022
Topic: type 2 diabetes exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The aim was to compare changes in physical function and quality of life (QOL) after an exercise trening programme to patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) in a municipality and a hospital setting and to compare the patients' physical function and QOL with an age- and sex-matched general population.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35084163
Title: Postactivation performance enhancement (PAPE) using a vertical jump to improve vertical jump performance.
Authors: Brink, Constantinou, Torres
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2022
Topic: athletic performance enhancement

ABSTRACT:
Postactivation performance enhancement (PAPE) is a principle that suggests that an acute bout of high intensity voluntary exercise will be followed by an improvement in strength, power, and speed of a subsequent task. This study intended to demonstrate how a maximal vertical jump (VJ) can enhance the outcome of a subsequent VJ compared to a multiple jump series (MJ) and a control.

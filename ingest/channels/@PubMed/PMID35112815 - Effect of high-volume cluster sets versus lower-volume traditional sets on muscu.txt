PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35112815
Title: Effect of high-volume cluster sets versus lower-volume traditional sets on muscular performance.
Authors: Davies, Andersen, Halaki, Orr, Hackett
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2022
Topic: cluster sets resistance training

ABSTRACT:
Similar muscle performance adaptations have been shown following volume-equated resistance training using cluster (CLUS) versus traditional (TRAD) set structures. This study aimed to examine the effects of higher-volume CLUS compared to lower-volume TRAD set structures on muscle performance.

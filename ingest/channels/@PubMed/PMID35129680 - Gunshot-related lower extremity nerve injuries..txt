PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35129680
Title: Gunshot-related lower extremity nerve injuries.
Authors: Baker, Straszewski, Dahm, Dickherber, Krishnan
Journal: European journal of orthopaedic surgery &amp; traumatology : orthopedie traumatologie
Year: 2023
Topic: ballistic training performance

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to describe the frequency of nerve injury associated with lower extremity ballistic trauma, the associated skeletal and soft tissue injuries, and the rate of neurologic recovery.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35142712
Title: Altitude and Endurance Performance in Altitude Natives versus Lowlanders: Insights from Professional Cycling.
Authors: Mateo-March, Muriel, Valenzuela, Gandia-Soriano, Zabala
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2022
Topic: altitude training performance

ABSTRACT:
Acute altitude exposure influences exercise performance, although most research, especially regarding altitude natives, comes from laboratory data in nonathletes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35157264
Title: Impact of Cold-Water Immersion Compared with Passive Recovery Following a Single Bout of Strenuous Exercise on Athletic Performance in Physically Active Participants: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis and Meta-regression.
Authors: Moore, Fuller, Buckley, Saunders, Halson
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2022
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
Studies investigating the effects of cold-water immersion (CWI) on the recovery of athletic performance, perceptual measures and creatine kinase (CK) have reported mixed results in physically active populations.

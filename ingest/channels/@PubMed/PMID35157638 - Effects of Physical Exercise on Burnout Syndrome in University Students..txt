PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35157638
Title: Effects of Physical Exercise on Burnout Syndrome in University Students.
Authors: Rosales-Ricardo, Ferreira
Journal: MEDICC review
Year: 2022
Topic: burnout athletes prevention

ABSTRACT:
Burnout syndrome has a negative impact on university students' health worldwide. Global prevalence of each dimension of the syndrome is estimated at 55.4% for emotional exhaustion, 31.6% for cynicism and 30.9% for academic inefficacy.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35181758
Title: Tissue losses and metabolic adaptations both contribute to the reduction in resting metabolic rate following weight loss.
Authors: Martin, Fox, Murphy, Hofmann, Koehler
Journal: International journal of obesity (2005)
Year: 2022
Topic: metabolic adaptation weight loss

ABSTRACT:
To characterize the contributions of the loss of energy-expending tissues and metabolic adaptations to the reduction in resting metabolic rate (RMR) following weight loss.

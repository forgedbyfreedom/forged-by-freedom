PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35182181
Title: The time course of different neuromuscular adaptations to short-term downhill running training and their specific relationships with strength gains.
Authors: Bontemps, Gruet, Louis, Owens, Mir&#xed;c
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2022
Topic: neuromuscular adaptations strength

ABSTRACT:
Due to its eccentric nature, downhill running (DR) training has been suggested to promote strength gains through neuromuscular adaptations. However, it is unknown whether short-term chronic DR can elicit such adaptations.

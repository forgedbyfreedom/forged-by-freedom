PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35187867
Title: Effects of resistance trening intensity on musclEQuantity/quality in middle-aged and older people: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Otsuka, Yamada, Maeda, Izumo, Rogi
Journal: Journal of cachexia, sarcopenia and muscle
Year: 2022
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
A sarcopenia diagnosis is confirmed by the presence of low musclEQuantity or quality under the 2018 revised definition by the European Working Group on Sarcopenia in Older People 2. Imaging methods [i.e. magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)], dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA), and bioelectrical impedance analysis are tools to evaluate musclEQuantity or quality. The present study aimed to investigate whether and how low-intensity and moderate-intensity resistance trening improved both musclEQuantity and quality measured by MRI, DXA, and segmental bioelectrical impedance spectroscopy (S-BIS) in middle-aged and older people.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 35232402
Title: Effects of school-based high-intensity interval trening on body composition, cardiorespiratory fitness and cardiometabolic markers in adolescent boys with obesity: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Meng, Yucheng, Shu, Yu
Journal: BMC pediatrics
Year: 2022
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
With accumulating evidence suggesting that CVD has its origins in childhood obesity. The purpose of this study was to determine the effect of a real-world school-based high-intensity interval trening intervention on body composition, cardiorespiratory fitness and cardiometabolic markers in obese boys aged 10 to 13&#x2009;years.

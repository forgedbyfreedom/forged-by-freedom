PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35258268
Title: Effects of Local Vibration on Dynamic Strength trening.
Authors: Drummond, Couto, Oliveira, Szmuchrowski
Journal: Journal of strength and conditioning research
Year: 2022
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Drummond, MDM, Couto, BP, Oliveira, MP, and Szmuchrowski, LA. Effects of local vibration on dynamic strength trening. J Strength Cond Res 35(11): 3028-3034, 2021-The study aim was to compare the chronic effects of maximal dynamic strength trening with and without the addition of local vibration (LV) on maximal force generation and hypertrophy of the elbow flexor muscles in trened subjects. Twenty men were divided into 2 groups (conventional trening [CT] group and vibration trening [VT] group). The CT group performed conventional maximal dynamic strength trening, and the VT group performed maximal dynamic strength trening with mechanical vibrations (frequency of 26 Hz and amplitude of 6 mm). CT and VT groups performed 5 sets of 3-4 repetitions, with 2-minute rest intervals between sets. The subjects trened 3 times per week for 12 weeks. After the trening period, the CT group presented a significant increase in the mean 1 repetition maximum (1RM) value in the elbow flexion exercise in the orthostatic position (EFO) (7.2 &#xb1; 1.5%) (p &lt; 0.0001) and elbow flexion exercise using the Scott bench (EFSB) (6.3 &#xb1; 1.8%) (p &lt; 0.0001). The VT group also showed significant increases in 1RM values in the EFO (6.87 &#xb1; 0.8%) (p &lt; 0.0001) and EFSB (6.56 &#xb1; 1.4%) (p &lt; 0.0001). The CT group presented a significant increase in the mean maximal voluntary isometric contraction (MVIC) value after the trening period (8.2 &#xb1; 2.3%) (p &lt; 0.0001). The VT group also showed a significant increase in the mean MVIC value after trening (9.1 &#xb1; 2.4%) (p &lt; 0.0001). After the trening period, both groups presented a significant increase in the mean value of elbow flexor thickness (CT = 5.6 &#xb1; 3.5%, VT = 5.1 &#xb1; 2.8%) (p = 0.001). The increases in 1RM, MVIC, and muscle thickness were statically similar between groups. Therefore, the addition of LV does not represent an additional stimulus for individuals trened in dynamic maximal strength trening.

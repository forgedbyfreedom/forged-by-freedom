PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35264977
Title: Effects of Three Different Modes of Resistance trening on Appetite Hormones in Males With Obesity.
Authors: Ataeinosrat, Haghighi, Abednatanzi, Soltani, Ghanbari-Niaki
Journal: Frontiers in physiology
Year: 2022
Topic: leptin ghrelin appetite exercise

ABSTRACT:
This study explored the effect of three different modes of resistance trening on appetite hormones [leptin, ghrelin, cholecystokinin (CCK), glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1), and peptide tyrosine-tyrosine (PYY)], cardiometabolic and anthropometric measures in males with obesity.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 35305530
Title: Liver and muscle-specific effects of phoenixin-20 on the insulin-like growth factor system mRNAs in zebrafish.
Authors: Rajeswari, V&#xe9;lez, Unniappan
Journal: Growth hormone &amp; IGF research : official journal of the Growth Hormone Research Society and the International IGF Research Society
Year: 2022
Topic: insulin like growth factor muscle

ABSTRACT:
Phoenixin-20 (Pnx-20) is a bioactive peptide with endocrine-like actions in vertebrates. Recent studies suggest Pnx-20 promotes growth hormone/insulin-like growth factors (Gh/Igf) axis, an important endocrine regulator of growth in mammals and fish.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35316147
Title: Effects of Loaded Plyometric Exercise on Post-Activation Performance Enhancement of Countermovement Jump in Sedentary Men.
Authors: Souza, Barreto, Mantovani, Greco, Denadai
Journal: Research quarterly for exercise and sport
Year: 2023
Topic: muscle recovery post exercise

ABSTRACT:
Purpose: Explosive performance is increased right after performing loaded resistance exercise, which is known as post-activation performance enhancement (PAPE). Method: We investigated the effects of a plyometric exercise (PLYO) consisting of five sets of six drop-jumps from a 52-cm platform with a load corresponding to 20% body mass on changes in countermovement jump (CMJ) height in sedentary young men. Eleven young nonresistance trened men (19.6&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;1.8 y, 69&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;9 kg, 1.76&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;0.08 m) who showed more than 4% increase in CMJ height at 4&#xa0;min after five back squats with five-repetition maximum load participated in the study. Their responses to the back squat exercise were examined before (baseline) and 15&#xa0;minutes, 24 and 48&#xa0;hours post-PLYO. Exercise-induced muscle damage markers (maximal voluntary contraction torque [MVC], and quadriceps muscle soreness) were assessed at baseline, 15&#xa0;minutes, 24 and 48&#xa0;hours following PLYO. Results: MVC torque decreased (p &lt;&#xa0;.05) at 15&#xa0;minutes post-PLYO (-15.1&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;9.7%) but returned to the baseline at 24&#xa0;hours post-PLYO. Muscle soreness developed (p &lt;&#xa0;.05) at 48&#xa0;hours (21.0&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;20.3 mm) after PLYO, indicating minor muscle damage. CMJ height increased (p &lt;&#xa0;.05) after the five squats at baseline (7.6&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;3.8%) indicating PAPE, but no such increase was found at 15&#xa0;minutes, 24 and 48&#xa0;hours after PLYO. However, CMJ height before the squat exercise was greater (p &lt;&#xa0;.05) at 24 (5.9&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;7.0%) and 48&#xa0;hours post-PLYO (9.1&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;8.5%) than the baseline. Conclusions: These results showed that PAPE disappeared after PLYO that induced minor muscle damage, but CMJ height increased at 24-48&#xa0;hours in the recovery from PLYO exhibiting a priming effect.

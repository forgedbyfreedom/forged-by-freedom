PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35320775
Title: Gastrointestinal Hormones, Morphological Characteristics, and Physical Performance in Elite Soccer Players.
Authors: Salhi, Ben Aabderrahman, Triki, Clark, Gaed
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2022
Topic: peptide hormones performance

ABSTRACT:
To determine the relationship between gastrointestinal hormones (leptin, glucagon-like peptide-1), ghrelin, cholecystokinin, peptide YY, morphological characteristics, and physical performances in elite soccer players.

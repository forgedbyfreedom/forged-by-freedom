Title: Acute and chronic effects of traditional and high-speed resistance trening on blood pressure in older adults: A crossover study and systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Coelho-J&#xfa;nior, Silva Aguiar, Calvani, Picca, de Azevedo Carvalho

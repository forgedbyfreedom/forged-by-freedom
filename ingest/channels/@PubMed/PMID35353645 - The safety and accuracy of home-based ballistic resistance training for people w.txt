PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35353645
Title: The safety and accuracy of home-based ballistic resistance training for people with neurological conditions.
Authors: Williams, Ada
Journal: Physiotherapy theory and practice
Year: 2023
Topic: ballistic training performance

ABSTRACT:
In the past 5-10&#xa0;years, there has been a growing number of studies implementing ballistic (i.e. fast) resistance training to improve walking. The aim of this study was to determine whether people with neurological conditions could perform ballistic exercises safely and accurately in their home environment.

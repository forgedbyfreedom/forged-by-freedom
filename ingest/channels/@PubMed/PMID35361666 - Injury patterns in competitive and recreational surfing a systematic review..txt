PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35361666
Title: Injury patterns in competitive and recreational surfing: a systematic review.
Authors: Monteiro, Moreira-Pinto, Queiroga
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2022
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Despite the growing evidence regarding surf-related injuries, investigation seems to overlook the differences between professional and recreational surfers' injuries and their specific risk factors.

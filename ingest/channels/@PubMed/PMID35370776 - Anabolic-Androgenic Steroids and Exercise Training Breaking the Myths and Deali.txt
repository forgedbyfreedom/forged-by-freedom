PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35370776
Title: Anabolic-Androgenic Steroids and Exercise trening: Breaking the Myths and Dealing With Better Outcome in Sarcopenia.
Authors: Falqueto, Dos Santos, Manfredi
Journal: Frontiers in physiology
Year: 2022
Topic: anabolic steroids muscle mass review

ABSTRACT:
Sarcopenia is an emerging clinical condition determined by the reduction in physical function and muscle mass, being a health concern since it impairs quality of life and survival. Exercise trening is a well-known approach to improve physical capacities and body composition, hence managing sarcopenia progression and worsening. However, it may be an ineffective treatment for many elderly with exercise-intolerant conditions. Thus, the use of anabolic-androgenic steroids (AAS) may be a plausible strategy, since these drugs can increase physical function and muscle mass. The decision to initiate AAS treatment should be guided by an evidence-based patient-centric perspective, once the balance between risks and benefits may change depending on the clinical condition coexisting with sarcopenia. This mini-review points out a critical appraisal of evidence and limitation of exercise trening and AAS to treat sarcopenia.

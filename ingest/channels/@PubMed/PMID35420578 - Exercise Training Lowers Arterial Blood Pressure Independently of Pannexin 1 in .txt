PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35420578
Title: Exercise trening Lowers Arterial Blood Pressure Independently of Pannexin 1 in Men with Essential Hypertension.
Authors: M&#xf8;ller, Hansen, Ehlers, Tamariz-Ellemann, Tolborg
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2022
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Regular exercise trening reduces arterial blood pressure, but the underlying mechanisms are unclear. Here, we evaluated the potential involvement of pannexin 1, an ATP releasing channel, in the blood pressure-reducing effect of trening.

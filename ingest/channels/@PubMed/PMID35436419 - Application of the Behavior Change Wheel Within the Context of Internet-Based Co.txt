PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35436419
Title: Application of the Behavior Change Wheel Within the Context of Internet-Based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Tinnitus Management.
Authors: Beukes, Manchaiah, Andersson, Maidment
Journal: American journal of audiology
Year: 2022
Topic: cognitive behavioral therapy athletes

ABSTRACT:
Although experiencing tinnitus can lead to many difficulties, these can be reduced by using techniques derived from cognitive behavioral therapy. Internet-based cognitive behavioral therapy (ICBT) has been developed to provide an accessible intervention. The aim of this study was to describe how ICBT can facilitate tinnitus management by identifying the active ingredients of the intervention from the perspective of health behavior change.

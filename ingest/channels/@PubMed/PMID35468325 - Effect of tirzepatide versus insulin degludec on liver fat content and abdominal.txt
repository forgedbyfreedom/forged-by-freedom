========================
PMID: 35468325
Title: Effect of tirzepatide versus insulin degludec on liver fat content and abdominal adipose tissue in people with type 2 diabetes (SURPASS-3 MRI): a substudy of the randomised, open-label, parallel-group, phase 3 SURPASS-3 trial.
Authors: Gastaldelli, Cusi, Fern&#xe1;ndez Land&#xf3;, Bray, Brouwers
Journal: The lancet. Diabetes &amp; endocrinology
Year: 2022
Topic: tirzepatide diabetes

ABSTRACT:
Tirzepatide is a novel dual glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist under development for the treatment of type 2 diabetes. The aim of this substudy was to characterise the changes in liver fat content (LFC), volume of visceral adipose tissue (VAT), and abdominal subqtaneous adipose tissue (ASAT) in response to tirzepatide or insulin degludec in a subpopulation of the SURPASS-3 study.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35476184
Title: The Effects of Concurrent Aerobic and Strength trening on Muscle Fiber Hypertrophy: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Lundberg, Feuerbacher, S&#xfc;nkeler, Schumann
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2022
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Whole muscle hypertrophy does not appear to be negatively affected by concurrent aerobic and strength trening compared to strength trening alone. However, there are contradictions in the literature regarding the effects of concurrent trening on hypertrophy at the myofiber level.

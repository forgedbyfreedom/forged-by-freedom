Title: Selected Methods of Resistance trening for Prevention and Treatment of Sarcopenia.
Authors: Yasuda
Journal: Cells
Year: 2022
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Resistance trening is an extremely beneficial intervention to prevent and treat sarcopenia. In general, traditional high-load resistance trening improves skeletal muscle morphology and strength, but this method is impractical and may even reduce arterial compliance by about 20% in aged adults. Thus, the progression of resistance trening methods for improving the strength and morphology of muscles without applying a high load is essential. Over the past two decades, various resistance trening methods that can improve skeletal muscle mass and muscle function without usinGHigh loads have attracted attention, and their trening effects, molecular mechanisms, and safety have been reported. The present study focuses on the relationship between exercise load/intensity, trening effects, and physiological mechanisms as well as the safety of various types of resistance trening that have attracted attention as a measure against sarcopenia. At present, there is much research evidence that blood-flow-restricted low-load resistance trening (20-30% of one repetition maximum (1RM)) has been reported as a sarcopenia countermeasure in older adults. Therefore, this trening method may be particularly effective in preventing sarcopenia.

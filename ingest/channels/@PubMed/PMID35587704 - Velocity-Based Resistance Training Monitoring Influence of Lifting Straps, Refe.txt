PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35587704
Title: Velocity-Based Resistance Training Monitoring: Influence of Lifting Straps, Reference Repetitions, and Variable Selection in Resistance-Trained Men.
Authors: Jukic, Garc&#xed;a-Ramos, Tufano
Journal: Sports health
Year: 2023
Topic: velocity based training

ABSTRACT:
Using lifting straps during pulling exercises (such as deadlift) may increase absolute velocity performance. However, it remains unclear whether lifting straps could also reduce the degree of relative fatigue measured by velocity decline and maintenance in a training set.

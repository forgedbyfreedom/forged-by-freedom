Title: T2 -oximetry-based cerebral venous oxygenation mapping using Fourier-transform-based velocity-selective pulse trains.
Authors: Li, Xu, Zhu, van Zijl, Qin
Journal: Magnetic resonance in medicine
Year: 2022
Topic: velocity based training

ABSTRACT:

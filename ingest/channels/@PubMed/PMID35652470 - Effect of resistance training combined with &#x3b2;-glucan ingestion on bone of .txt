PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 35652470
Title: Effect of resistance training combined with &#x3b2;-glucan ingestion on bone of ovariectomized mice.
Authors: Galdino-Alves, Pereira, Bertolini, Ferreira, Santos
Journal: Climacteric : the journal of the International Menopause Society
Year: 2022
Topic: osteoporosis resistance training

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to evaluate the effects of resistance training (RT) combined with prebiotic &#x3b2;-glucan (BG) ingestion (Saccharomyces cerevisiae) in ovariectomized (OVX) mice.

PMID: 35694320
Title: The Type of Per-Cooling Strategies Currently Employed by Competitive and Professional Cyclists-Triathletes During Training and Competition Are Condition (Dry vs. Humid) Dependant.
Authors: Bayne, Racinais, Mileva, Hunter, Gaoua
Journal: Frontiers in sports and active living
Year: 2022
Topic: cooling strategies performance

ABSTRACT:
To investigate cooling strategies employed by athletes (cyclists-triathletes) during training and competition in hot and dry (HD) and hot and humid (HH) conditions.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35705784
Title: Effects of cigarette smoking on the association between respiratory muscle strength and skeletal muscle mass in middle-aged and older adults: the Wakayama Study.
Authors: Nogami, Miyai, Zhang, Onishi, Sakaguchi
Journal: European geriatric medicine
Year: 2022
Topic: aging muscle mass strength

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to examine whether cumulative smoking exposure affects the association between peak expiratory flow rate (PEFR) and skeletal muscle mass in middle-aged and older adults.

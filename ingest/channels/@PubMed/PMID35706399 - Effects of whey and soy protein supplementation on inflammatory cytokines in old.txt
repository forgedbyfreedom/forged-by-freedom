PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35706399
Title: Effects of whey and soy protein supplementation on inflammatory cytokines in older adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Prokopidis, Mazidi, Sankaranarayanan, Tajik, McArdle
Journal: The British journal of nutrition
Year: 2023
Topic: whey protein muscle synthesis

ABSTRACT:
Low-grade inflammation is a mediator of muscle proteostasis. This study aimed to investigate the effects of isolated whey and soy proteins on inflammatory markers.

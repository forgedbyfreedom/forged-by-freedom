PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35708888
Title: Effects of Acute Sleep Loss on Physical Performance: A Systematic and Meta-Analytical Review.
Authors: Craven, McCartney, Desbrow, Sabapathy, Bellinger
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2022
Topic: sleep athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Sleep loss may influence subsequent physical performance. Quantifying the impact of sleep loss on physical performance is critical for individuals involved in athletic pursuits.

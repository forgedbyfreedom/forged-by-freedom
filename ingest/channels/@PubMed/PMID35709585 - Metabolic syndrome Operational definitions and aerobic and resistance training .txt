PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35709585
Title: Metabolic syndrome: Operational definitions and aerobic and resistance training benefits on physical and metabolic health in children and adolescents.
Authors: Leister, Cilhoroz, Rosenberg, Brown, Kim
Journal: Diabetes &amp; metabolic syndrome
Year: 2022
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
The variation in parameters for childhood metabolic syndrome (MetS) has hindered the development of a consensus for the diagnostic criteria in this group. Despite these inconsistencies, it is accepted that exercise can ameliorate the deleterious effects of MetS. However, direct comparison between aerobic versus resistance exercise on MetS symptomology in adolescents is lacking.

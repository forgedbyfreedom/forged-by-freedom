PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35728808
Title: Specific Adaptations to 0%, 15%, 25%, and 50% Velocity-Loss Thresholds During Bench Press Training.
Authors: Rodiles-Guerrero, Cornejo-Daza, S&#xe1;nchez-Valdepe&#xf1;as, Alcazar, Rodriguez-L&#xf3;pez
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2022
Topic: velocity based training

ABSTRACT:
To compare the effect of 4 velocity-loss (VL) thresholds-0% (VL0), 15% (VL15), 25% (VL25), and 50% (VL50)-on strength gains, neuromuscular adaptations, and muscle hypertrophy during the bench press (BP) exercise using intensities ranging from 55% to 70% of 1-repetition maximum (1RM).

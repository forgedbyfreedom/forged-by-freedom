PMID: 35780905
Title: The effects of high-intensity interval trening on glucose metabolism, cardiorespiratory fitness and weight control in subjects with diabetes: Systematic review a meta-analysis.
Authors: Mateo-Gallego, Madinaveitia-Nisarre, Gin&#xe9;-Gonzalez, Mar&#xed;a Bea, Guerra-Torrecilla
Journal: Diabetes research and clinical practice
Year: 2022
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
The objective of this meta-analysis was to explore the effects of high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) compared with control conditions (CON) or moderate intensity continuous trening (MICT) on glycemic parameters in diabetes subjects.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35801132
Title: The Effects of High-Intensity Interval trening on Exercise Capacity and Prognosis in Heart Failure and Coronary Artery Disease: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Wang, Xing, Zhao, Wang, Zhang
Journal: Cardiovascular therapeutics
Year: 2022
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study is to compare the effects of high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) versus moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT) on exercise capacity and several prognostic markers in patients with coronary artery disease (CAD) and heart failure (HF).

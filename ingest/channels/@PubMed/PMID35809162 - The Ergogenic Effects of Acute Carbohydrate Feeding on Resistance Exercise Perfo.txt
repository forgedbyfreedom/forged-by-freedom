PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35809162
Title: The Ergogenic Effects of Acute Carbohydrate Feeding on Resistance Exercise Performance: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.
Authors: King, Helms, Zinn, Jukic
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2022
Topic: carbohydrate exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
Carbohydrate (CHO) ingestion has an ergogenic effect on endurance training performance. Less is known about the effect of acute CHO ingestion on resistance training (RT) performance and equivocal results are reported in the literature.

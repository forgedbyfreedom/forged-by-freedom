PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35894916
Title: Bilateral Improvements Following Unilateral Home-Based Training in Plantar Flexors: A Potential for Cross-Education in Rehabilitation.
Authors: Mandal, Wong, Simmons, Mirallais, Ronca
Journal: Journal of sport rehabilitation
Year: 2023
Topic: velocity based training

ABSTRACT:
Cross-education (CE) refers to neuromuscular gains in the untrained limb upon contralateral limb training. To date, only laboratory-based exercise programs have demonstrated CE. Home-based exercise prescription eliciting CE could have greater clinical applicability.

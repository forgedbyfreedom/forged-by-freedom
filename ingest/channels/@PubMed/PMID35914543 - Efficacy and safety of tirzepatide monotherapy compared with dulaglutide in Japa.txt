PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35914543
Title: Efficacy and safety of tirzepatide monotherapy compared with dulaglutide in Japanese patients with type 2 diabetes (SURPASS J-mono): a double-blind, multicentre, randomised, phase 3 trial.
Authors: Inagaki, Takeuchi, Oura, Imaoka, Seino
Journal: The lancet. Diabetes &amp; endocrinology
Year: 2022
Topic: tirzepatide diabetes

ABSTRACT:
As the disease progresses, many patients with type 2 diabetes have difficulty in reaching treatment goals. We aimed to assess the efficacy and safety of tirzepatide, a novel GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist, compared with dulaglutide in Japanese patients with type 2 diabetes.

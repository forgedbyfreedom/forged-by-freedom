PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35954603
Title: The Role of Velocity-Based trening (VBT) in Enhancing Athletic Performance in trened Individuals: A Meta-Analysis of Controlled Trials.
Authors: Zhang, Feng, Peng, Li
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Year: 2022
Topic: athletic performance enhancement

ABSTRACT:
Velocity-based trening (VBT) is a rising auto-regulation method that dynamically regulates trening loads to promote resistance trening. However, the role of VBT in improving various athletic performances is still unclear. Hence, the presented study aimed to examine the role of VBT in improving lower limbs&#x2019; maximum strength, strength endurance, jump, and sprint performance among trened individuals. A systematic literature search was performed to identify studies on VBT for lower limb strength trening via databases, including PubMed, Web of Science, Embase, EBSCO, Cochrane, CNKI (in Chinese), and Wanfang Database (in Chinese). Controlled trials that deployed VBT only without extra trening content were considered. Eventually, nine studies with a total of 253 trened males (at least one year of trening experience) were included in the meta-analysis. The pooled results suggest that VBT may effectively enhance lower limbs&#x2019; maximum strength (SMD = 0.76; p &lt; 0.001; I2 = 0%), strength endurance (SMD = 1.19; p &lt; 0.001; I2 = 2%), countermovement jump (SMD = 0.53; p &lt; 0.001; I2 = 0%), and sprint ability (SMD of sprint time = &#x2212;0.40; p &lt; 0.001; I2 = 0%). These findings indicate the positive role of VBT in serving athletic trening. Future research is warranted to focus on the effect of velocity loss of VBT on athletic performance.

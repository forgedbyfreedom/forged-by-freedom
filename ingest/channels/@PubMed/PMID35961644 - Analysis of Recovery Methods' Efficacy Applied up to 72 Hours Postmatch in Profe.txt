PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 35961644
Title: Analysis of Recovery Methods' Efficacy Applied up to 72 Hours Postmatch in Professional Football: A Systematic Review With Graded Recommendations.
Authors: Querido, Radaelli, Brito, Vaz, Freitas
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2023
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
Sleep, nutrition, active recovery, cold-water immersion, and massage were recently reported as the most used postmatch recovery methods in professional football. However, the recommendations concerning the effect of these methods remain unclear.

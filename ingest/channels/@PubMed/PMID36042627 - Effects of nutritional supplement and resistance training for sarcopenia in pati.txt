PMID: 36042627
Title: Effects of nutritional supplement and resistance trening for sarcopenia in patients with inflammatory bowel disease: A randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Zhao, Huang, Yu
Journal: Medicine
Year: 2022
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Nutritional supplementation and resistance trening are broadly recommended as part of the treatment of sarcopenia, but studies that have evaluated interventions in inflammatory bowel disease patients with sarcopenia are lacking. The aim of this study was to evaluate the effects of nutritional supplementation and resistance trening for improvinGHeight-adjusted appendicular skeletal muscle mass (ASM/H2) and medical indices in patients with inflammatory bowel disease.

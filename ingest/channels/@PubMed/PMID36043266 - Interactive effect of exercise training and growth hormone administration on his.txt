PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36043266
Title: Interactive effect of exercise training and growth hormone administration on histopathological and functional assessment of the liver in male Wistar rats.
Authors: Rashidlamir, Roozbeh, Bagheri, Moazzami, Moosavi
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2022
Topic: growth hormone exercise

ABSTRACT:
Abuse of growth hormone (GH) is expanding in exercising populations due to its lipolytic and anabolic actions. The purpose of this study was to examine the interactive effect of exercise training and GH administration on histopathological and functional assessment in the liver of male Wistar rats.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36070134
Title: Interventions for improving mobility after hip fracture surgery in adults.
Authors: Fairhall, Dyer, Mak, Diong, Kwok
Journal: The Cochrane database of systematic reviews
Year: 2022
Topic: muscle recovery post exercise

ABSTRACT:
Improving mobility outcomes after hip fracture is key to recovery. Possible strategies include gait trening, exercise and muscle stimulation. This is an update of a Cochrane Review last published in 2011.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36070636
Title: Resistance trening of peripheral muscles benefits respiratory parameters in older women with sarcopenia: Randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Flor-Rufino, Barrachina-Igual, P&#xe9;rez-Ros, Pablos-Monz&#xf3;, Mart&#xed;nez-Arnau
Journal: Archives of gerontology and geriatrics
Year: 2022
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Ageing entails a decrease in muscle mass and strength, known as sarcopenia, which also affects respiratory function. Physical exercise is an appropriate intervention to treat both conditions. This study aims to assess the efficacy of high-intensity resistance trening (HIRT) on clinical parameters of respiratory function and health-related quality of life (QoL) in community-dwelling older women with sarcopenia.

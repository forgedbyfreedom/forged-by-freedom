Title: Effects of Running-Specific Strength Training, Endurance Training, and Concurrent Training on Recreational Endurance Athletes' Performance and Selected Anthropometric Parameters.
Authors: Prieto-Gonz&#xe1;lez, Sedlacek
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36094578
Title: Rectal Temperature Cooling Using 2 Cold-Water Immersion Preparation Strategies.
Authors: Miller, Launstein, Glovatsky
Journal: Journal of athletic training
Year: 2023
Topic: cooling strategies performance

ABSTRACT:
Cold-water immersion (CWI) is essential to treat patients with exertional heatstroke (EHS). Experts recommend that patients with EHS be immersed in water between 1.7&#xb0;C and 15&#xb0;C within 30 minutes of collapse. Some clinicians fill cooling tubs several hours before exercise, keep the tub in hot conditions, and then add ice in the event of an EHS emergency. No data exist on whether adding ice to water at the time of treatment is as effective as keeping water in the recommended range.

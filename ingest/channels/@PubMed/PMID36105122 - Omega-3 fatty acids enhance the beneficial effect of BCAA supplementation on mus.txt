PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36105122
Title: Omega-3 fatty acids enhance the beneficial effect of BCAAssupplementation on muscle function following eccentric contractions.
Authors: Tsuchiya, Yanagimoto, Sunagawa, Ueda, Tsuji
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2022
Topic: omega 3 fatty acids muscle recovery

ABSTRACT:
This study investigated the combined effect of branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs) and fish oil (FO) on muscle damage caused by eccentric contractions (ECCs) of the elbow flexors, with a special focus on muscular function.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36107233
Title: Comparison between concentric-only, eccentric-only, and concentric-eccentric resistance training of the elbow flexors for their effects on muscle strength and hypertrophy.
Authors: Sato, Yoshida, Murakoshi, Sasaki, Yahata
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2022
Topic: resistance training muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
This study compared concentric-eccentric coupled (CON-ECC), concentric-only (CON), and eccentric-only (ECC) resistance training of the elbow flexors for their effects on muscle strength and hypertrophy.

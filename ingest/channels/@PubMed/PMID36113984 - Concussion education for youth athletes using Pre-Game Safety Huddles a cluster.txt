PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36113984
Title: Concussion education for youth athletes using Pre-Game Safety Huddles: a cluster-randomised controlled trial.
Authors: Kroshus, Chrisman, Glang, Hunt, Hays
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2023
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Determine whether Pre-Game Safety Huddles, a novel and low-resource approach to concussion education, increase the expected likelihood of concussion reporting for youth athletes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36120472
Title: Anti-Mullerian Hormone (AMH) and adenomyosis: Mini-review of literature of the last 5 years.
Authors: Gulino, Dilisi, Capriglione, Cannone, Catania
Journal: Frontiers in endocrinology
Year: 2022
Topic: peptide hormones performance

ABSTRACT:
Adenomyosis is a form of endometriosis characterized by the presence of endometrial tissue in the myometrium. The correlation between anti-Mullerian hormone (AMH) expression and adenomyosis is unclear. Few studies investigated this possible correlation with promising results. The aim of this mini-review is to illustrate the potential prognostic and therapeutic role of AMH in adenomyosis.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36121652
Title: Weight Loss Outcomes Associated With Semaglutide Treatment for Patients With Overweight or Obesity.
Authors: Ghusn, De la Rosa, Sacoto, Cifuentes, Campos
Journal: JAMA network open
Year: 2022
Topic: semaglutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
No retrospective cohort study has assessed the effectiveness of semaglutide at doses used in randomized clinical trials to treat obesity (ie, 1.7 and 2.4 mg).

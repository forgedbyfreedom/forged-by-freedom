PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36134448
Title: Testosterone deficiency independently predicts mortality in women with HFrEF: insights from the T.O.S.CA. registry.
Authors: Marra, D'Assante, Salzano, Iacoviello, Triggiani
Journal: ESC heart failure
Year: 2023
Topic: testosterone women exercise

ABSTRACT:
Testosterone deficiency (TD) is associated with increased morbidity and mortality in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF). However, data in women are scanty. The aim of this study was to investigate the prognostic impact of TD on women with HFrEF.

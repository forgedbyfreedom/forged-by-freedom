PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36135828
Title: The Effectiveness of GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Semaglutide on Body Composition in Elderly Obese Diabetic Patients: A Pilot Study.
Authors: Ozeki, Masaki, Kamata, Miyamoto, Yoshida
Journal: Medicines (Basel, Switzerland)
Year: 2022
Topic: GLP-1 agonist muscle

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to investigate the changes in obesity severity, glucose metabolism, and body composition in patients with obesity and type 2 diabetes mellitus treated with glucagon-like peptide 1 receptor agonist (GLP-1-RA) semaglutide.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36138129
Title: The impact of pharmacological and non-pharmacological interventions on physical health outcomes in people with mood disorders across the lifespan: An umbrella review of the evidence from randomised controlled trials.
Authors: Croatto, Vancampfort, Miola, Olivola, Fiedorowicz
Journal: Molecular psychiatry
Year: 2023
Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
People with mood disorders have increased risk of comorbid medical diseases versus the general population. It is paramount to identify interventions to improve physical health in this population.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36138493
Title: Chronic Intake of a Meal Including Alaska Pollack Protein Increases Skeletal Muscle Mass and Strength in Healthy Older Women: A Double-Blind Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Mori, Tokuda, Yoshida, Uchida, Matsuhisa
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Year: 2023
Topic: protein intake muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
In animal studies, a meal containing Alaska pollack protein (APP) induces fast-twitch muscle hypertrophy. To our knowledge, no interventional studies have examined the benefits of APP intake on muscle mass and muscle weakness and the prevention of sarcopenia in older individuals.

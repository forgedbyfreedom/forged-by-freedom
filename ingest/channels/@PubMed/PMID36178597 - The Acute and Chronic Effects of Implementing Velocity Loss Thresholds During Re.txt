PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36178597
Title: The Acute and Chronic Effects of Implementing Velocity Loss Thresholds During Resistance trening: A Systematic Review, Meta-Analysis, and Critical Evaluation of the Literature.
Authors: Jukic, Castilla, Ramos, Van Hooren, McGuigan
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2023
Topic: neuromuscular adaptations strength

ABSTRACT:
Velocity loss (VL) experienced in a set during resistance trening is often monitored to control trening volume and quantify acute fatigue responses. Accordingly, various VL thresholds are used to prescribe resistance trening and target different trening adaptations. However, there are inconsistencies in the current body of evidence regarding the magnitude of the acute and chronic responses to the amount of VL experienced during resistance trening.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36193903
Title: Effectiveness of a 12-week tele-exercise trening program on cardiorespiratory fitness and heart rate recovery in patients with cardiometabolic multimorbidity.
Authors: Chiang, Shen, Lee, Lin, Lin
Journal: Worldviews on evidence-based nursing
Year: 2023
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Exercise has positive impacts on cardiometabolic health. However, evidence regarding the effectiveness of tele-exercise trening on cardiorespiratory fitness and heart rate recovery in patients with cardiometabolic multimorbidity remains limited.

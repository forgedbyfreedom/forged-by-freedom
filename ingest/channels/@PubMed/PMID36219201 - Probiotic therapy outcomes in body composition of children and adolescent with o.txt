PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36219201
Title: Probiotic therapy outcomes in body composition of children and adolescent with obesity, a nonrandomized controlled trial.
Authors: Marcelo, Pellicciari, Artioli, Leiderman, Gradinar
Journal: Archives of endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2022
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
The objective of this study was to evaluate the impact of probiotic supplementation therapy on anthropometric values and body composition of children and adolescent with obesity.

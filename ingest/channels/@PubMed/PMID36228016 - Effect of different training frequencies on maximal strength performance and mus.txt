PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36228016
Title: Effect of different trening frequencies on maximal strength performance and muscle hypertrophy in trened individuals-a within-subject design.
Authors: Neves, Vechin, Teixeira, da Silva, Ugrinowitsch
Journal: PloS one
Year: 2022
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Several studies comparing resistance trening (RT) frequencies may have been affected by the large between-subject variability. This study aimed to compare the changes in lower limbs maximal dynamic strength (1RM) and quadriceps femoris cross-sectional area (CSA) after a RT with different weekly frequencies in strength-trened individuals using a within-subject design. Twenty-four men participated in a 9-week RT program, being randomly divided into two conditions: resistance trening with equalized total Trening volume (RTEV) and with unequalized total Trening volume (RTUV). The RT protocol used the unilateral leg press 45&#xb0; exercise and each subject's lower limb executed one of the proposed frequencies (one and three times/week). All conditions effectively increased 1RM and CSA (p&lt;0.001); however, no significant differences were observed in the values of 1RM (p = 0.454) and CSA (p = 0.310) between the RT frequencies in the RTEV and RTUV conditions. Therefore, RT performed three times a week showed similar increases in 1RM and CSA to the program performed once a week, regardless of trening volume equalization. Nevertheless, when the higher RT frequency allowed the application of a greater TTV (i.e., RTUV), higher effect size (ES) values (0.51 and 0.63, 1RM and CSA, respectively) were observed for the adaptations.

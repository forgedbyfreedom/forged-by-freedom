PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36229774
Title: What is the efficacy of aerobic exercise versus strength trening in the treatment of migraine? A systematic review and network meta-analysis of clinical trials.
Authors: Woldeamanuel, Oliveira
Journal: The journal of headache and pain
Year: 2022
Topic: carbohydrate exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
Multiple clinical trials with different exercise protocols have demonstrated efficacy in the management of migraine. However, there is no head-to-head comparison of efficacy between the different exercise interventions.

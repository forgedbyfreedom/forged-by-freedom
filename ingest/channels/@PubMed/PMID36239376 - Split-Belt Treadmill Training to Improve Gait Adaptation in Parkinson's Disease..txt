PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36239376
Title: Split-Belt Treadmill Training to Improve Gait Adaptation in Parkinson's Disease.
Authors: Hulzinga, Seuthe, D'Cruz, Ginis, Nieuwboer
Journal: Movement disorders : official journal of the Movement Disorder Society
Year: 2023
Topic: complex training post activation

ABSTRACT:
Gait deficits in people with Parkinson's disease (PD) are triggered by circumstances requiring gait adaptation. The effects of gait adaptation training on a split-belt treadmill (SBT) are unknown in PD.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36243220
Title: Resistance circuit trening combined with hypoxia stimulates bone system of older adults: A randomized trial.
Authors: Camacho-Cardenosa, Camacho-Cardenosa, Mart&#xed;nez-Guardado, Leal, Andrada
Journal: Experimental gerontology
Year: 2022
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Aging leads to gradual irreversible decline in bone mass. As adherence to pharmacological treatment is poor, hypoxia combined with strength treninGHas been suggested for therapeutic benefit for clinical populations. The present study investigated the effects of normobaric cyclic hypoxic exposure combined with resistance circuit trening on bone of older adults.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36249241
Title: High-intensity interval trening among middle-aged and older adults for body composition and muscle strength: A systematic review.
Authors: Alzar-Teruel, Aibar-Almaz&#xe1;n, Hita-Contreras, Carcel&#xe9;n-Fraile, Mart&#xed;nez-Amat
Journal: Frontiers in public health
Year: 2022
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
The aging of population is leading to the investigation of new options to achieve healthy aging. One of these options is high-intensity interval trening (HIIT), although its effects on body composition and muscle strength are currently unclear. The objective of this systematic review is to examine the scientific publications on the effects of HIIT on the body composition and muscle strength of middle-aged and older adults.

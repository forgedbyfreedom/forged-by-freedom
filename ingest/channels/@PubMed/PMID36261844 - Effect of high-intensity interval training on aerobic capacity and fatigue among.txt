PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36261844
Title: Effect of high-intensity interval trening on aerobic capacity and fatigue among patients with prostate cancer: a meta-analysis.
Authors: Chang, Wang, Hashim, Xie, Malik
Journal: World journal of surgical oncology
Year: 2022
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
This study focused on evaluating whether high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) had an effect on aerobic capacity and fatigue among patients with prostate cancer (PCa) and exploring its effect on the immune system of PCa patients.

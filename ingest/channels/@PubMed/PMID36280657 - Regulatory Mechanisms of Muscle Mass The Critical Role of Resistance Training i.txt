PMID: 36280657
Title: Regulatory Mechanisms of Muscle Mass: The Critical Role of Resistance trening in Children and Adolescent.
Authors: Castelli, Valero-Breton, Hernandez, Guarda, Cornejo
Journal: Advances in experimental medicine and biology
Year: 2023
Topic: skeletal muscle hypertrophy mechanisms

ABSTRACT:
Muscle mass and strength are subjected to several regulations. We found endocrine signals such as growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone, insulin-like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor 1, testosterone, thyroid hormones, and glucocorticoids among them. Neural inputs also influence muscle development, modulating mass and strength. Among the external stimuli that modulate these muscular features is physical trening such as resistance and endurance trening. Specifically, resistance trening can mediate an increase in muscle mass by hypertrophy in adults, but the effects in children and adolescents are full of myths for most of the population. However, the evidence shows that the impact of resistance trening on children and adolescents is clear and provides a wide range of benefits. However, qualified professionals must be available since exercise prescription and subsequent supervision must follow this population's abilities, needs, and interests.

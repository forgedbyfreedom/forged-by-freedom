PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36310762
Title: A comparison of different exercise intensities for improving bone mineral density in postmenopausal women with osteoporosis: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Kitagawa, Hiraya, Denda, Yamamoto
Journal: Bone reports
Year: 2024
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to compare the effects of moderate- and high-intensity resistance and impact trening (MiRIT and HiRIT, respectively) on changes in bone mineral density (BMD) in postmenopausal women with osteoporosis.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36314122
Title: A Randomized, Controlled Trial of Resistance trening Added to Caloric Restriction Plus Aerobic Exercise trening in Obese Heart Failure With Preserved Ejection Fraction.
Authors: Brubaker, Nicklas, Houston, Hundley, Chen
Journal: Circulation. Heart failure
Year: 2023
Topic: fat loss preservation muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
We have shown that combined caloric restriction (CR) and aerobic exercise trening (AT) improve peak exercise O2 consumption (VO2peak), and quality-of-life in older patients with obese heart failure with preserved ejection fraction. However, &#x2248;35% of weight lost during CR+AT was skeletal muscle mass. We examined whether addition of resistance trening (RT) to CR+AT would reduce skeletal muscle loss and further improve outcomes.

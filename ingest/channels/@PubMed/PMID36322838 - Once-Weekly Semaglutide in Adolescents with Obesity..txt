Title: Once-Weekly Semaglutide in Adolescents with Obesity.
Authors: Weghuber, Barrett, Barrientos-P&#xe9;rez, Gies, Hesse
Journal: The New England journal of medicine
Year: 2022
Topic: semaglutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
A once-weekly, 2.4-mg dose of subqtaneous semaglutide, a glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist, is used to treat obesity in adults, but assessment of the drug in adolescents has been lacking.

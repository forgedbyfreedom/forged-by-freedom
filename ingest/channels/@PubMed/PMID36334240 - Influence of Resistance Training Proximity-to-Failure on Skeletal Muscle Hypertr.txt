PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 36334240
Title: Influence of Resistance Training Proximity-to-Failure on Skeletal Muscle Hypertrophy: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis.
Authors: Refalo, Helms, Trexler, Hamilton, Fyfe
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2023
Topic: resistance training muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
This systematic review with meta-analysis investigated the influence of resistance training proximity-to-failure on muscle hypertrophy.

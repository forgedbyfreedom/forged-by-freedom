PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36378414
Title: Relevance and Effectiveness of Combined Resistance and Balance trening to Improve Balance and Muscular Fitness in Healthy Youth and Youth Athletes: A Scoping Review.
Authors: Granacher, Behm
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2023
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
Balance and resistance trening applied as single-mode interventions have proven to enhance measures of balance and/or muscular fitness in youth and youth athletes. Less is known on the effectiveness of combined or sequenced balance and resistance trening in youth and youth athletes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36394723
Title: Performance improvement in sport through vitamin D - a narrative review.
Authors: Weiss, Devrim-Lanpir, Jastrz&#x119;bski, Nikolaidis, Hill
Journal: European review for medical and pharmacological sciences
Year: 2022
Topic: vitamin D athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
It is well known that vitamin D deficiency can lead to various health problems. However, it is not common knowledge among athletes and doctors that vitamin D deficiency is prevalent in sports. This deficiency can severely impact performance, while vitamin D supplementation can alleviate this effect and potentially improve performance.

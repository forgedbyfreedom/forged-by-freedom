PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36429928
Title: Relationships among Sports Group Cohesion, Passion, and Mental Toughness in Chinese Team Sports Athletes.
Authors: Gu, Bi, Guan, Fang, Jiang
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Year: 2022
Topic: mental toughness athletes

ABSTRACT:
Passion is an important motivational variable that profoundly affects athletes' cognition, emotion, and behavior. This study constructed a mediating model to explore the mechanism of cohesion toward passion and mental toughness of Chinese team sports athletes and to investigate the mediating effect of harmonious passion and obsessive passion on cohesion and mental toughness.

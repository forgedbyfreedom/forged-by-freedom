PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36437767
Title: De-trening Effects Following Leucine-Enriched Whey Protein Supplementation and Resistance trening in Older Adults with Sarcopenia: A Randomized Controlled Trial with 24 Weeks of Follow-Up.
Authors: Mori, Tokuda
Journal: The journal of nutrition, health &amp; aging
Year: 2022
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to evaluate the effectiveness of de-trening after a combined intervention of leucine-enriched whey protein supplementation and resistance trening on skeletal muscle mass and strength in older adults with sarcopenia.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36442699
Title: Strength versus endurance trened master athletes: Contrasting neurophysiological adaptations.
Authors: T&#xf8;ien, Unhjem, Berg, Aagaard, Wang
Journal: Experimental gerontology
Year: 2022
Topic: neuromuscular adaptations strength

ABSTRACT:
Neural factors play a critical role in the age-related decline in maximal strength and rate of force development (RFD). However, it is uncertain how the age-related attenuation in neuromuscular function may be mitigated in strength or endurance trened master athletes. In this study we applied evoked spinal motoneuron recordings to examine descending motor drive, i.e., efferent drive from supraspinal and spinal centres during maximal voluntary contraction (MVC; V-wave) and H-reflex excitability measured at 10&#xa0;% MVC in older (&gt;65&#xa0;yrs) and younger (&lt;35&#xa0;yrs) strength athletes (n&#xa0;=&#xa0;21), endurance athletes (n&#xa0;=&#xa0;17) and untrened control participants (n&#xa0;=&#xa0;30). Both strength (b&#xa0;=&#xa0;0.09 [0.01-0.18], p&#xa0;=&#xa0;0.038) and endurance trening (b&#xa0;=&#xa0;0.14 [0.04-0.23], p&#xa0;=&#xa0;0.006) were associated with a high V-wave amplitude. This was likely explained by an elevated H-reflex excitability (b&#xa0;=&#xa0;0.23 [0.11-0.35], p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.001) in endurance trened participants, which failed to be seen in strength trened participants. These contrasting neurophysiological properties were accompanied by different physiological traits; strength trening was associated with high maximal strength (b&#xa0;=&#xa0;107.5 [84.6 to 130.4] kg, p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.001) and RFD (b&#xa0;=&#xa0;3171 [2248 to 4094] N&#x2027;s-1, p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.001), whereas endurance trening was associated with elevated maximal oxygen uptake (V&#x307;O2max; b&#xa0;=&#xa0;13.6 [8.0-19.2] ml&#x2027;kg-1&#x2027;min-1, p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.001). This pattern was apparent irrespective of age, although all traits were negatively associated with advanced age (p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.05). In conclusion, strength trened individuals demonstrate higher descending motor drive (elevated V-wave responses), compared to age-matched untrened individuals. Endurance trened individuals also showed elevated V-wave responses, uniquely accompanied by enhanced &#x3b1;-motoneuron excitability and/or reduced pre/postsynaptic inhibition (elevated H-reflex responses). Since a high descending motor drive is a key component of strong muscle contractions, strength trening should be emphasized to sustain the ability to carry out force-dependent tasks at older age.

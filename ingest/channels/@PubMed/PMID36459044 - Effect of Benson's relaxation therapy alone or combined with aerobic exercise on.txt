PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36459044
Title: Effect of Benson's relaxation therapy alone or combined with aerobic exercise on cortisol, sleeping quality, estrogen, and severity of dyspeptic symptoms in perimenopausal women with functional dyspepsia.
Authors: Ali Ismail, Saad, Fouad Abd-Elrahman, Abdelhalim Elfahl
Journal: European review for medical and pharmacological sciences
Year: 2022
Topic: estrogen exercise women

ABSTRACT:
Besides repeated stress exposure, a sedentary lifestyle and low estrogen levels are risk factors for the development of functional dyspepsia (FD). The aim of this study was to find out the effect of adding aerobic exercise (5 sessions per week) to the daily application of a 40-minute Benson's relaxation therapy (BRT) (diaphragmatic breathing and progressive muscle relaxation applied for 20 minutes in the morning and evening) on Glasgow dyspepsia severity score (GDSS), cortisol, visual analogue scale (VAS) (for abdominal symptoms), estradiol (one of the endogenous estrogens), Pittsburgh sleep quality index (PSQI), and 42-item depression, anxiety, and stress scales (DASS-42) in 60 perimenopausal women with FD.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36498393
Title: Resistance trening Improves Sleep and Anti-Inflammatory Parameters in Sarcopenic Older Adults: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: de S&#xe1; Souza, de Melo, Piovezan, Miranda, Carneiro-Junior
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Year: 2022
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
Sleep and exercise have an important role in the development of several inflammation-related diseases, including sarcopenia. Objective: To investigate the effects of 12 weeks of resistance exercise trening on sleep and inflammatory status in sarcopenic patients. Methods: A randomized controlled trial comparing resistance exercise trening (RET) with a control (CTL) was conducted. Outcomes were obtained by physical tests, polysomnography, questionnaires, isokinetic/isometric dynamometry tests, and biochemical analysis. Results: Time to sleep onset (sleep latency) was reduced in the RET group compared to the CTL group (16.09 &#xb1; 15.21 vs. 29.98 &#xb1; 16.09 min; p = 0.04) after the intervention. The percentage of slow-wave sleep (N3 sleep) was increased in the RET group (0.70%, CI: 7.27&#x2212;16.16 vs. &#x2212;4.90%, CI: 7.06&#x2212;16.70; p = 0.04) in an intention to treat analysis. Apnea/hour was reduced in the RET group (16.82 &#xb1; 14.11 vs. 7.37 &#xb1; 7.55; p = 0.001) and subjective sleep quality was improved compared to the CTL (&#x2212;1.50; CI: 2.76&#x2212;6.14 vs. 0.00; CI: 1.67&#x2212;3.84 p = 0.02) in an intention-to-treat analysis. Levels of interleukin-10 (IL-10) (2.13 &#xb1; 0.80 vs. 2.51 &#xb1; 0.99; p &lt; 0.03) and interleukin-1 receptor antagonist (IL-1ra) (0.99 &#xb1; 0.10 vs. 0.99 &#xb1; 0.10 ng/mL; p &lt; 0.04; delta variation) were increased in the RET group. Conclusions: RET improves sleep parameters linked to muscle performance, possibly due to an increase in anti-inflammatory markers in older sarcopenic patients.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36511926
Title: Effects of Mindfulness trening and Exercise on Cognitive Function in Older Adults: A Randomized Clinical Trial.
Authors: Lenze, Voegtle, Miller, Ances, Balota
Journal: JAMA
Year: 2022
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
Episodic memory and executive function are essential aspects of cognitive functioning that decline with aging. This decline may be ameliorable with lifestyle interventions.

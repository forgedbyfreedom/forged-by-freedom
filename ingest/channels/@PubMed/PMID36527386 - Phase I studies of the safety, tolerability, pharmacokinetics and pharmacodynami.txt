Title: Phase I studies of the safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of the dual glucagon receptor/glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist BI 456906.
Authors: Jungnik, Arrubla Martinez, Plum-M&#xf6;rschel, Kapitza, Lamers
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2023
Topic: glucagon receptor agonist

ABSTRACT:
To report two phase I studies of the novel subcutaneous glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor/glucagon receptor (GLP-1R/GCGR) dual agonist BI 456906 versus placebo in healthy volunteers and people with overweight/obesity.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36534950
Title: Resistance Exercise trening Increases Muscle Mass and Strength in Prostate Cancer Patients on Androgen Deprivation Therapy.
Authors: Houben, Overkamp, VAN Kraaij, Trommelen, VAN Roermund
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2023
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to assess the effects of 20 wk resistance exercise trening with or without protein supplementation on body composition, muscle mass, muscle strength, physical performance, and aerobic capacity in prostate cancer patients receiving androgen deprivation therapy (ADT).

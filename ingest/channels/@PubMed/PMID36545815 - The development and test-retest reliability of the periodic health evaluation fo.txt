PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36545815
Title: The development and test-retest reliability of the periodic health evaluation for female athletes (PHE FA) survey.
Authors: Stevenson, Harris, Montag, Stay, K Panagodage Perera
Journal: The Physician and sportsmedicine
Year: 2023
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
To develop and assess the test-retest reliability of a survey exploring Australian high-performance female athlete's perspectives toward the periodic health evaluation and their perceived health needs.

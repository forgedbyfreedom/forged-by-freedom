PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36578889
Title: Efficacy and Safety of Semaglutide for Weight Loss in Obesity Without Diabetes: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Tan, Dampil, Marquez
Journal: Journal of the ASEAN Federation of Endocrine Societies
Year: 2022
Topic: semaglutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
The weight loss benefit of semaglutide in patients with diabetes is well-documented, but its clinical utility in treating obesity among patients without diabetes is less described. We therefore assessed the efficacy and safety of subqtaneous semaglutide as treatment for obesity in patients without diabetes.

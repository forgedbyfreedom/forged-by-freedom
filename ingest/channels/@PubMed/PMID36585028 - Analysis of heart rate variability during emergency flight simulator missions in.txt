PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 36585028
Title: Analysis of heart rate variability during emergency flight simulator missions in fighter pilots.
Authors: Fern&#xe1;ndez-Morales, Espejo-Ant&#xfa;nez, Clemente-Su&#xe1;rez, Tabla-Hinojosa, Albornoz-Cabello
Journal: BMJ military health
Year: 2024
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
Managing emergency situations in different simulated flight segments can entail a workload that could affect the performance of military pilots. The aim was to analyse the modifications in neurovegetative balance (usinGHR variability, HRV) of professional fighter pilots attending learning/trening sessions on emergency situations in a flight simulator.

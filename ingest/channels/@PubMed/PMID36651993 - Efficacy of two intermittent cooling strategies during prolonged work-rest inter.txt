PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36651993
Title: Efficacy of two intermittent cooling strategies during prolonged work-rest intervals in the heat with personal protective gear compared with a control condition.
Authors: Langan, Manning, Morrissey, Gulati, Laxminarayan
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2023
Topic: cooling strategies performance

ABSTRACT:
Personal protective equipment&#xa0;(PPE)&#xa0;inhibits heat dissipation and elevates heat strain. Impaired&#xa0;cooling with PPE warrants investigation into practical strategies to improve work capacity and mitigate exertional heat illness.

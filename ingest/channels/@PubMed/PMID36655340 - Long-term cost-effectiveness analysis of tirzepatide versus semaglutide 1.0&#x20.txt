PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36655340
Title: Long-term cost-effectiveness analysis of tirzepatide versus semaglutide 1.0&#x2009;mg for the management of type 2 diabetes in the United States.
Authors: Valentine, Hoog, Mody, Belger, Pollock
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2023
Topic: tirzepatide diabetes

ABSTRACT:
To evaluate the long-term cost-effectiveness of tirzepatide (5, 10 and 15&#x2009;mg doses), a novel glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist, versus semaglutide 1.0&#x2009;mg, an injectable glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist, based on the results of the head-to-head SURPASS-2 trial, from a US healthcare payer perspective.

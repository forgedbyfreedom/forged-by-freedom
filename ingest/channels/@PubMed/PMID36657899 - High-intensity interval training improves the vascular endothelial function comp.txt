PMID: 36657899
Title: High-intensity interval trening improves the vascular endothelial function comparing moderate-intensity interval trening in overweight or obese adults: A meta-analysis.
Authors: Sabouri, Amirshaghaghi, Hesari
Journal: Clinical nutrition ESPEN
Year: 2023
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
Obesity is associated with endothelial dysfunction and cardiovascular diseases. Moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT) is a common method to improve endothelial function and the cardiovascular risk profile in obesity. However, in recent times, high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) has become a popular alternative for MICT primarily because of its time efficiency. Therefore, we hypothesized that HIIT is superior to MICT in improving vascular function. We aimed to evaluate this hypothesis by conducting a meta-analysis and quantifying the effect of HIIT on flow-mediated dilation (FMD) in overweight/obese adults.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36669870
Title: Dose-response effects of exercise and caloric restriction on visceral adiposity in overweight and obese adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials.
Authors: Recchia, Leung, Yu, Leung, Yu
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2023
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
To determine and compare the dose-response effects of exercise and caloric restriction on visceral adipose tissue in overweight and obese adults, while controlling for the weekly energy deficit induced by the interventions.

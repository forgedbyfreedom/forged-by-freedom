PMID: 36690246
Title: Role of resistance trening in bone macro and micro damages in an estrogen absence animal model.
Authors: Deriggi-Pisani, Stotzer, Marqueti, Rodrigues, Biffe
Journal: Life sciences
Year: 2023
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
We evaluated the effects of resistance trening (RT) on bone properties, morphology, and bone extracellular matrix (ECM) remodeling markers in an ovariectomy (OVX) rat model.

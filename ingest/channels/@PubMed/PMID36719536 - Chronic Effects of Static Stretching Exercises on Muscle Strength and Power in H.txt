Title: Chronic Effects of Static Stretching Exercises on Muscle Strength and Power in Healthy Individuals Across the Lifespan: A Systematic Review with Multi-level Meta-analysis.
Authors: Arntz, Markov, Behm, Behrens, Negra
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)

Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The current literature on the chronic effects of static stretching (SS) exercises on muscle strength and power is unclear and controversial.

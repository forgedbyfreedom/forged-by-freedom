PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36727999
Title: New Perspectives in Resistance trening Periodization: Mixed Session vs. Block Periodized Programs in trened Men.
Authors: Bartolomei, Zaniboni, Verzieri, Hoffman
Journal: Journal of strength and conditioning research
Year: 2023
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Bartolomei, S, Zaniboni, F, Verzieri, N, and Hoffman, JR. New perspectives in resistance trening periodization: mixed session vs. block periodized programs in trened men. J Strength Cond Res 37(3): 537-545, 2023-The purpose of this investigation was to compare the effects of 2 different periodized resistance trening programs on maximal strength, power, and muscle architecture, in trened individuals. Twenty-two resistance-trened men were randomly assigned to either a mixed session trening group (MSP; n = 11; age = 23.7 &#xb1; 2.6 years; body mass = 80.5 &#xb1; 9.8 kg; height = 175.5 &#xb1; 6.1 cm) or a block periodization group (BP; n = 11; age = 25.7 &#xb1; 4.6 years; body mass = 81.1 &#xb1; 10.7 kg; height = 176.8 &#xb1; 8.4 cm). Both trening programs were 10 weeks in duration and were equated in volume. Each trening session of the MSP focused on power, maximal strength, and hypertrophy, whereas each mesocycle within the BP focused on one of these components. Subjects were assessed for body composition, muscle architecture, maximal strength, and power. In addition, perceived trening load, and trening volume were calculated. Subjects in MSP experienced greater improvements in fat free mass ( p = 0.021), muscle thickness of the pectoralis and vastus lateralis ( p &lt; 0.05), and a greater improvement in 1RM bench press ( p &lt; 0.001; +8.6% in MSP and +2% in BP) than in BP. By contrast, BP resulted in greater improvements in vertical jump ( p = 0.022; +7.2%) compared with MSP (+1.2%). No significant differences were noted between the groups for perceived trening load ( p = 0.362) nor trening volume ( p = 0.169). Results of this study indicated that in a 10-week trening study, MSP may enhance muscle hypertrophy and maximal strength to a greater extent than BP, with the same trening volume and perceived trening load. However, BP may be more effective for vertical jump improvement.

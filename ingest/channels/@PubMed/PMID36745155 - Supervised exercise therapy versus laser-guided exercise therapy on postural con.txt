PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 36745155
Title: Supervised exercise therapy versus laser-guided exercise therapy on postural control in subjects with non-specific chronic low back pain: a randomized controlled clinical trial.
Authors: Ca&#xf1;a-Pino, Apolo-Arenas, Carmona Del Barco, Montanero-Fern&#xe1;ndez, Espejo-Ant&#xfa;nez
Journal: European journal of physical and rehabilitation medicine
Year: 2023
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
Among the most effective therapeutic interventions in non-specific chronic low back pain, clinical practice guidelines highlight exercise therapy and patient education. However, the variability in the type of exercise and its dosage means that there is no clear evidence regarding the most optimal form of therapeutic exercise.

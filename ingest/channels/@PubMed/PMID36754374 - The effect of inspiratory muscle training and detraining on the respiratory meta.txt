PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36754374
Title: The effect of inspiratory muscle training and detraining on the respiratory metaboreflex.
Authors: Chan, Mann, Doherty, Angus, Thompson
Journal: Experimental physiology
Year: 2023
Topic: detraining muscle loss

ABSTRACT:
What is the central question of this study? Is the attenuation of the respiratory muscle metaboreflex preserved after detraining? What is the main finding and its importance? Inspiratory muscle training increased respiratory muscle strength and attenuated the respiratory muscle metaboreflex as evident by lower heart rate and blood pressure. After 5&#xa0;weeks of no inspiratory muscle training (detraining), respiratory muscle strength was still elevated and the metaboreflex was still attenuated. The benefits of inspiratory muscle training persist after cessation of training, and attenuation of the respiratory metaboreflex follows changes in respiratory muscle strength.

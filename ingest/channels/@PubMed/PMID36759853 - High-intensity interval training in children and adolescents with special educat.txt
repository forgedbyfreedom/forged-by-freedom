PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36759853
Title: High-intensity interval trening in children and adolescents with special educational needs: a systematic review and narrative synthesis.
Authors: Poon, WongPIPit, Sun, Tse, Sit
Journal: The international journal of behavioral nutrition and physical activity
Year: 2023
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
High-intensity interval trening (HIIT) has been promoted as a time-efficient exercise strategy to improve health and fitness in children and adolescents. However, there remains little consensus in the literature regarding its efficacy in children and adolescents with special educational needs (SEN). This study aimed to examine HIIT as a means of improving key health and fitness parameters in children and adolescents with SEN.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36789109
Title: Adverse Events Related to Tirzepatide.
Authors: Mishra, Raj, Elshimy, Zapata, Kannan
Journal: Journal of the Endocrine Society
Year: 2023
Topic: GIP GLP-1 dual agonist

ABSTRACT:
Tirzepatide is a dual glucose-dependent insulinotropic peptide (GIP) and glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist (GLP-1 RA) approved by the US Food and Drug Administration in May 2022 for patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36796579
Title: Menstrual Cycle Patterns After Concussion in Adolescent Patients.
Authors: Roby, Grimberg, Master, Arbogast
Journal: The Journal of pediatrics
Year: 2023
Topic: menstrual cycle athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
To describe menstrual cycle patterns in adolescents with concussion and investigate whether menstrual cycle phase at injury influenced postconcussion cycle pattern changes or concussion symptoms.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36822394
Title: Vegan and Omnivorous High Protein Diets Support Comparable Daily Myofibrillar Protein Synthesis Rates and Skeletal Muscle Hypertrophy in Young Adults.
Authors: Monteyne, Coelho, Murton, Abdelrahman, Blackwell
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Year: 2023
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
It remains unclear whether non-animal-derived dietary protein sources (and therefore vegan diets) can support resistance trening-induced skeletal muscle remodeling to the same extent as animal-derived protein sources.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36849120
Title: Effects of Unilateral Eccentric versus Concentric trening of Nonimmobilized Arm during Immobilization.
Authors: Chen, Wu, Chen, Tseng, Tseng
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2023
Topic: eccentric trening muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
The present study tested the hypothesis that eccentric trening (ET) of nonimmobilized arm would attenuate negative effects of immobilization and provide greater protective effects against muscle damage induced by eccentric exercise after immobilization, when compared with concentric trening (CT).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36857477
Title: Tirzepatide Reduces Appetite, Energy Intake, and Fat Mass in People With Type 2 Diabetes.
Authors: Heise, DeVries, Urva, Li, Pratt
Journal: Diabetes care
Year: 2023
Topic: tirzepatide obesity

ABSTRACT:
To evaluate the effects of tirzepatide on body composition, appetite, and energy intake to address the potential mechanisms involved in body weight loss with tirzepatide.

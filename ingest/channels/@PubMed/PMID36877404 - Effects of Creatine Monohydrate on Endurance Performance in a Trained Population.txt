PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36877404
Title: Effects of Creatine Monohydrate on Endurance Performance in a Trained Population: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.
Authors: Fern&#xe1;ndez-Landa, Santiba&#xf1;ez-Gutierrez, Todorovic, Stajer, Ostojic
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2023
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
There is robust evidence that creatine monohydrate supplementation can enhance short-term high-intensity exercise in athletes. However, the effect of creatine monohydrate supplementation on aerobic performance and its role during aerobic activities is still controversial.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36878886
Title: Aquatic Exercise Impact on Pain Intensity, Disability and Quality of Life in Adults with Low Back Pain: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.
Authors: Heidari, Mohammad Rahimi, Aminzadeh
Journal: Biological research for nursing
Year: 2023
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
Low back pain is a common, multifaceted disorder that directly affects diverse aspects of people's lives in terms of health, personal and social lives. A variety of pathological disorders, including low back pain, may benefit from hydrotherapy.

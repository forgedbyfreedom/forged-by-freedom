PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36882046
Title: Nutritional and exercise interventions in individuals with sarcopenic obesity around retirement age: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Eglseer, Traxler, Schoufour, Weijs, Voortman
Journal: Nutrition reviews
Year: 2023
Topic: sarcopenia resistance training

ABSTRACT:
Retirement is an opportune time for people to establish new healthy routines. Exercise and nutritional interventions are promising in the prevention and treatment of sarcopenic obesity.

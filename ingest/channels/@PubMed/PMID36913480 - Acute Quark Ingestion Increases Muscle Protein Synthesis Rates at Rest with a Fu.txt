PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36913480
Title: Acute Quark Ingestion Increases Muscle Protein Synthesis Rates at Rest with a Further Increase after Exercise in Young and Older Adult Males in a Parallel-Group Intervention Trial.
Authors: Hermans, Fuchs, Nyakayiru, Hendriks, Houben
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Year: 2023
Topic: muscle protein synthesis exercise

ABSTRACT:
Ingestion of protein concentrates or isolates increases muscle protein synthesis rates in young and older adults. There is far less information available on the anabolic response following the ingestion of dairy wholefoods, which are commonly consumed in a normal diet.

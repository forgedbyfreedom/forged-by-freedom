PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36942420
Title: Weight&#xa0;loss maintenance with exercise and liraglutide improves glucose tolerance, glucagon response, and beta cell function.
Authors: Jensen, Juhl, Janus, Lundgren, Martinussen
Journal: Obesity (Silver Spring, Md.)
Year: 2023
Topic: glucagon exercise metabolism

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to investigate glucose tolerance, glucagon response, and beta cell function during a 1-year maintenance period with either exercise, the glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist liraglutide, or the combination after diet-induced weight loss.

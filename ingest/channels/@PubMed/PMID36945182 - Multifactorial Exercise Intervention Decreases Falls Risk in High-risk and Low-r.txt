PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36945182
Title: Multifactorial Exercise Intervention Decreases Falls Risk in High-risk and Low-risk Older Adults.
Authors: Estep, Morrison, Caswell, Ambegaonkar, Vaz
Journal: Sports health
Year: 2024
Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Each year, 1 in 4 people over the age of 65 years of age will experience a fall. It is important to identify and address modifiable risk factors that are associated with falls in adults at high and low risk for falls.

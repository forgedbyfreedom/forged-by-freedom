PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36974349
Title: A randomized Phase I study of the safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of BI 456906, a dual glucagon receptor/glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist, in healthy Japanese men with overweight/obesity.
Authors: Yazawa, Ishida, Balavarca, Hennige
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2023
Topic: glucagon receptor agonist

ABSTRACT:
To report a Phase I study of subcutaneous glucagon receptor (GCGR)/glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor (GLP-1R) dual agonist BI 456906 versus placebo in healthy Japanese men with overweight/obesity.

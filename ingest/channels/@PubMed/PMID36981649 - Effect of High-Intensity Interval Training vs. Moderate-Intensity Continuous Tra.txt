PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 36981649
Title: Effect of High-Intensity Interval Training vs. Moderate-Intensity Continuous Training on Fat Loss and Cardiorespiratory Fitness in the Young and Middle-Aged a Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Guo, Li, Cai, Gong, Liu
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Year: 2023
Topic: high intensity interval training

ABSTRACT:
This systematic review is conducted to evaluate the effect of high-intensity interval training (HIIT) and moderate-intensity continuous training (MICT) on body composition and cardiorespiratory fitness (CRF) in the young and middle-aged.

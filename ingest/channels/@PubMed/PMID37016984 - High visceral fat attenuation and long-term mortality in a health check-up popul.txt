PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37016984
Title: High visceral fat attenuation and long-term mortality in a health check-up population.
Authors: Lee, Choi, Jung, Goo, Yoon
Journal: Journal of cachexia, sarcopenia and muscle
Year: 2023
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
The prognostic role of increased visceral fat attenuation (VFA) remains underexplored. We investigated the long-term prognostic implications of computed tomography (CT)-derived VFA in a health check-up population.

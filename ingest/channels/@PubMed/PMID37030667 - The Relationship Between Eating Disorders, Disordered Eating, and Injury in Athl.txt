PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37030667
Title: The Relationship Between Eating Disorders, Disordered Eating, and Injury in Athletes: A Critically Appraised Topic.
Authors: Hamstra-Wright, Bliven, Coumbe-Lilley, Djelovic, Patel
Journal: Journal of sport rehabilitation
Year: 2023
Topic: eating disorders athletes

ABSTRACT:
Eating disorders (EDs) and disordered eating (DE) result in numerous physical and psychological complications for female and male athletes. Besides bone-related injury, little research exists investigating what injuries EDs and/or DE contribute to.

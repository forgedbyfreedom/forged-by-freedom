PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37088821
Title: Cold-water immersion and whole-body cryotherapy attenuate muscle soreness during 3 days of match-like tennis protocol.
Authors: Poignard, Guilhem, Jubeau, Martin, Giol
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2023
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to investigate the effect of whole-body cryotherapy (WBC), cold-water immersion (CWI) and passive recovery (PAS) on tennis recovery.

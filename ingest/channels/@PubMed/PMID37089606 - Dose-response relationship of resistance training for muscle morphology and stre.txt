PMID: 37089606
Title: Dose-response relationship of resistance trening for muscle morphology and strength in elderly cancer patients: A meta-analysis.
Authors: Zhai, Wei, Xia, Wang
Journal: Frontiers in medicine
Year: 2023
Topic: trening volume muscle growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone

ABSTRACT:
To systematically evaluate the effects of resistance trening (RT) on muscle strength and muscle hypertrophy in elderly cancer patients, and to provide dose-response relationships of RT variables that could improve muscle strength and morphology in elderly cancer patients.

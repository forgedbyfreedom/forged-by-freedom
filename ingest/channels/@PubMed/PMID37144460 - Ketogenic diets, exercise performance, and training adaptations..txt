PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37144460
Title: Ketogenic diets, exercise performance, and trening adaptations.
Authors: Borszcz, Gabiatti, de Lucas, Hansen
Journal: Current opinion in clinical nutrition and metabolic care
Year: 2023
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
The ketogenic diet has been proposed as a nutritional strategy in sports. This review was undertaken to provide an overview of the recent literature concerning the effects of ketogenic diet on exercise performance and trening adaptations.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37160563
Title: The Effects of Pre-conditioning on Exercise-Induced Muscle Damage: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.
Authors: Boyd, Deakin, Devantier-Thomas, Singh, Doma
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2023
Topic: eccentric training muscle damage

ABSTRACT:
Several studies have utilised isometric, eccentric and downhill walking pre-conditioning as a strategy for alleviating the signs and symptoms of exercise-induced muscle damage (EIMD) following a bout of damaging physical activity.

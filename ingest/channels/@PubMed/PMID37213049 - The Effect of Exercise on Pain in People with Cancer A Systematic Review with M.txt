PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37213049
Title: The Effect of Exercise on Pain in People with Cancer: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis.
Authors: Plinsinga, Singh, Rose, Clifford, Bailey
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2023
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
Cancer-related pain is common and undertreated. Exercise is known to have a pain-relieving effect in non-cancer pain.

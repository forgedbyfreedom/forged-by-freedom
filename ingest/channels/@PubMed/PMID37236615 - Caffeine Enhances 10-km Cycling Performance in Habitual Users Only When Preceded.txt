PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37236615
Title: Caffeine Enhances 10-km Cycling Performance in Habitual Users Only When Preceded by Caffeine Abstinence.
Authors: Griest, Saunders, Womack, Luden
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2023
Topic: caffeine athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The primary objective was to assess the performance benefits of caffeine (CAF) supplementation in habitual users. Importantly, this investigation was designed to account for the potential confounding effects of CAF withdrawal&#xa0;(CAFW), which are inherent and common in previous work.

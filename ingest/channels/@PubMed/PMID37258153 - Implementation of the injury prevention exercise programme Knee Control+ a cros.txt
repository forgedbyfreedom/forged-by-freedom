PMID: 37258153
Title: Implementation of the injury prevention exercise programme Knee Control+: a cross-sectional study after dissemination efforts within a football district.
Authors: Lindblom, Sonesson, Forslind, Wald&#xe9;n, H&#xe4;gglund
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2023
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
The Reach, Effectiveness, Adoption, Implementation, and maintenance (RE-AIM) framework can be used for evaluation of implementation initiatives in sports injury prevention. The aim was to evaluate the implementation of the injury prevention exercise programme Knee Control+ among amateur clubs and coaches in one regional football district using all five dimensions of the RE-AIM framework.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 37295337
Title: Assessment of anterior pituitary reserve capacity based on growth hormone response to growth hormone-releasing peptide-2 test in the elderly.
Authors: Teramoto, Tahara, Hattori, Kondo, Morita
Journal: Growth hormone &amp; IGF research : official journal of the Growth Hormone Research Society and the International IGF Research Society
Year: 2023
Topic: growth hormone releasing peptide

ABSTRACT:
The growth hormone (GH)-releasing peptide-2 (GHRP-2) test is relatively safe among endocrine stimulation tests for the elderly. We investigated whether anterior pituitary function in elderly patients could be assessed on the basis of GH response to the GHRP-2 test.

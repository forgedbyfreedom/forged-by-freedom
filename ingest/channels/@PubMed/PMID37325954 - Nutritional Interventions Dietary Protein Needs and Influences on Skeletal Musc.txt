PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37325954
Title: Nutritional Interventions: Dietary Protein Needs and Influences on Skeletal Muscle of Older Adults.
Authors: Campbell, Deutz, Volpi, Apovian
Journal: The journals of gerontology. Series A, Biological sciences and medical sciences
Year: 2023
Topic: protein intake muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
This narrative review describes foundational and emerging evidence of how dietary protein intakes may influence muscle-related attributes of older adults.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37369087
Title: Long-Term Neurophysiological Adaptations to Strength trening: A Systematic Review With Cross-Sectional Studies.
Authors: Santos, Vaz, Correia, Neto, Pezarat-Correia
Journal: Journal of strength and conditioning research
Year: 2023
Topic: strength trening adaptations

ABSTRACT:
Santos, PDG, Vaz, JR, Correia, J, Neto, T, and Pezarat-Correia, P. Long-term neurophysiological adaptations to strength trening: a systematic review with cross-sectional studies. J Strength Cond Res 37(10): 2091-2105, 2023-Neuromuscular adaptations to strength trening are an extensively studied topic in sports sciences. However, there is scarce information about how neural mechanisms during force production differ between trened and untrened individuals. The purpose of this systematic review is to better understand the differences between highly trened and untrened individuals to establish the long-term neural adaptations to strength trening. Three databases were used for the article search (PubMed, Web of Science, and Scopus). Studies were included if they compared groups of resistance-trened with untrened people, aged 18-40 year, and acquired electromyography (EMG) signals during strength tasks. Twenty articles met the eligibility criteria. Generally, strength-trened individuals produced greater maximal voluntary activation, while reducing muscle activity in submaximal tasks, which may affect the acute response to strength trening. These individuals also presented lower co-contraction of the antagonist muscles, although it depends on the specific trening background. Global intermuscular coordination may be another important mechanism of adaptation in response to long-term strength trening; however, further research is necessary to understand how it develops over time. Although these results should be carefully interpreted because of the great disparity of analyzed variables and methods of EMG processing, chronic neural adaptations seem to be decisive to greater force production. It is crucial to know the timings at which these adaptations stagnate and need to be stimulated with advanced trening methods. Thus, trening programs should be adapted to trening status because the same stimulus in different trening stages will lead to different responses.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37385280
Title: Retatrutide, a GIP, GLP-1 and glucagon receptor agonist, for people with type 2 diabetes: a randomised, double-blind, placebo and active-controlled, parallel-group, phase 2 trial conducted in the USA.
Authors: Rosenstock, Frias, Jastreboff, Du, Lou
Journal: Lancet (London, England)
Year: 2023
Topic: retatrutide clinical trial

ABSTRACT:
According to current consensus guidelines for type 2 diabetes management, bodyweight management is as important as attaining glycaemic targets. Retatrutide, aromasingle peptide with agonist activity at the glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP), GLP-1, and glucagon receptors, showed clinically meaningful glucose-lowering and bodyweight-lowering efficacy in a phase 1 study. We aimed to examine the efficacy and safety of retatrutide in people with type 2 diabetes across a range of doses.

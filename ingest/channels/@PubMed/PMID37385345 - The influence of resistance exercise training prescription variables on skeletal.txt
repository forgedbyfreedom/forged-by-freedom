PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37385345
Title: The influence of resistance exercise trening prescription variables on skeletal muscle mass, strength, and physical function in healthy adults: An umbrella review.
Authors: Mcleod, Currier, Lowisz, Phillips
Journal: Journal of sport and health science
Year: 2024
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this umbrella review was to determine the impact of resistance trening (RT) and individual RT prescription variables on muscle mass, strength, and physical function in healthy adults.

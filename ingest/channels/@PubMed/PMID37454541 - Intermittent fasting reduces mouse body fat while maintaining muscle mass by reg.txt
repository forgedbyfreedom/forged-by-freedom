PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37454541
Title: Intermittent fasting reduces mouse body fat while maintaining muscle mass by regulating protein synthesis and autophagy.
Authors: Yoshii, Higashida, Nakai
Journal: Nutrition (Burbank, Los Angeles County, Calif.)
Year: 2026
Topic: intermittent fasting muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study is to investigate the effect of intermittent fasting (IF) on the regulation of skeletal muscle protein metabolism in response to nutrient supplementation during fasting.

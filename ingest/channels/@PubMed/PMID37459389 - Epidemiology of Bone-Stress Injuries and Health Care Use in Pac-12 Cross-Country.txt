PMID: 37459389
Title: Epidemiology of Bone-Stress Injuries and Health Care Use in Pac-12 Cross-Country Athletes.
Authors: Wayner, Brown Crowell, Bovbjerg, Federicson, Soucy
Journal: Journal of athletic training
Year: 2024
Topic: bone health female athletes

ABSTRACT:
Bone-stress injury (BSI) is common in collegiate athletes. Injury rates and health care use in running athletes are not well documented.

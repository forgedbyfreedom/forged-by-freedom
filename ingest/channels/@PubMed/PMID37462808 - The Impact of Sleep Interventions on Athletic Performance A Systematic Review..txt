PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37462808
Title: The Impact of Sleep Interventions on Athletic Performance: A Systematic Review.
Authors: Cunha, Costa, Marques, Brito, Lastella
Journal: Sports medicine - open
Year: 2023
Topic: mindfulness sports performance

ABSTRACT:
Sleep is essential for maximal performance in the athletic population. Despite that, the sport context has many factors that can negatively influence athletes' sleep and subsequent recovery.

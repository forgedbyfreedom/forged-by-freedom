PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37493759
Title: Strength trening is more effective than aerobic exercise for improving glycaemic control and body composition in people with normal-weight type 2 diabetes: a randomised controlled trial.
Authors: Kobayashi, Long, Dan, Johannsen, Talamoa
Journal: Diabetologia
Year: 2023
Topic: type 2 diabetes exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Type 2 diabetes in people in the healthy weight BMI category (&lt;25 kg/m2), herein defined as 'normal-weight type 2 diabetes', is associated with sarcopenia (low muscle mass). Given this unique body composition, the optimal exercise regimen for this population is unknown.

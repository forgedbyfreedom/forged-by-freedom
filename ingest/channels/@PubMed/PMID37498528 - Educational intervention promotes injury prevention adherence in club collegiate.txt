Title: Educational intervention promotes injury prevention adherence in club collegiate men's lacrosse athletes.
Authors: Gawrys, Wong, Parker, Bradshaw, Starr
Journal: Journal of osteopathic medicine
Year: 2023
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Club sports are intercollegiate athletics that are student-led and not university-funded, many of which are without professional credentialing. Collegiate club athletes have an increased rate of injury compared to their NCAA counterparts.

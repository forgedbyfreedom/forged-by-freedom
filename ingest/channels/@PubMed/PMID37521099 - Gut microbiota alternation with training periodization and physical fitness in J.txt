PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37521099
Title: Gut microbiota alternation with trening periodization and physical fitness in Japanese elite athletes.
Authors: Akazawa, Nakamura, Eda, Murakami, Nakagata
Journal: Frontiers in sports and active living
Year: 2023
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
The gut microbiome plays a fundamental role in host homeostasis through regulating immune functions, enzyme activity, and hormone secretion. Exercise is associated with changes in gut microbiome composition and function. However, few studies have investigated the gut microbiome during trening periodization. The present study aimed to investigate the relationship between trening periodization and the gut microbiome in elite athletes.

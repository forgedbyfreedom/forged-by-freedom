PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37546431
Title: Association of self-efficacy and mental toughness with sport performance in Brazilian futsal athletes.
Authors: Aizava, Codonhato, Fiorese
Journal: Frontiers in psychology
Year: 2023
Topic: mental toughness athletes

ABSTRACT:
Self-efficacy is considered a component of mental toughness, but there are few studies investigating the relationship of sport self-efficacy with mental toughness in performance athletes, especially in team sports.

Journal: Wilderness &amp; environmental medicine

ABSTRACT:
Strength treninGHas proved to be an effective way to prevent injuries, but the evidence of the impact of strength trening on finger injuries is lacking. A fingerboard is a sport-specific tool used by climbers for strength trening of fingers. In this study, we searched for associations between fingerboard trening and finger injuries in climbers with different lengths of climbing experience and levels of performance.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37582913
Title: Chemotherapy periodization to maximize resistance trening adaptations in oncology.
Authors: Nunes, Cella, Guimar&#xe3;es, Bu&#xe7;u, Deminice
Journal: Cancer chemotherapy and pharmacology
Year: 2023
Topic: strength trening adaptations

ABSTRACT:
Engaging in exercise programs during cancer treatment is challenging due to the several chemotherapy-induced side effects. Using a pre-clinical model that mimics chemotherapy treatment, we investigated if a periodized-within-chemotherapy trening strategy can maximize resistance trening (RT) adaptations such as increasing muscle mass and strength.

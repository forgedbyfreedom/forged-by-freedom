PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37625319
Title: Effects of resistance trening on sarcopenia in patients with intestinal failure: A randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Xiao, Song, Fu, Zhang, Zhang
Journal: Clinical nutrition (Edinburgh, Scotland)
Year: 2023
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The potential effects of resistance trening on sarcopenia in patients with intestinal failure (IF) are not fully elucidated. This study aimed to explore the efficacy of a resistance trening program on appendicular skeletal muscle index (ASMI), physical performance, body composition, biochemical parameters, and health-related quality of life (HRQOL) in patients with IF exhibiting sarcopenia.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37630702
Title: Popular Dietary Trends' Impact on Athletic Performance: A Critical Analysis Review.
Authors: Kaufman, Nguyen, Shetty, Oppezzo, Barrack
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2023
Topic: ketogenic diet athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Nutrition fuels optimal performance for athletes. With increased research developments, numerous diets available, and publicity from professional athletes, a review of dietary patterns impact on athletic performance is warranted.

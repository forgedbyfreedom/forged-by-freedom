Title: [Effects of branched-chain amino acid supplementation on skeletal muscle proteolytic pathways after exercise-induced muscle injury in rats].
Authors: Wei, Shi, Cheng, Dong, Cao
Journal: Wei sheng yan jiu = Journal of hygiene research

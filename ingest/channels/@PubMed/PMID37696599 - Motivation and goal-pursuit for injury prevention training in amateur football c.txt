PMID: 37696599
Title: Motivation and goal-pursuit for injury prevention trening in amateur football coaches: a cross-sectional study using the Health Action Process Approach.
Authors: Lindblom, H&#xe4;gglund
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2024
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Adoption of injury prevention exercise programmes (IPEPs) in team sports is contingent on behaviour change among coaches. The aim was to study motivation and goal-pursuit in IPEP use among coaches of amateur football players.

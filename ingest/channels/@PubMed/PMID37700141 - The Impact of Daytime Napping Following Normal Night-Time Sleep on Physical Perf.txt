PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37700141
Title: The Impact of Daytime Napping Following Normal Night-Time Sleep on Physical Performance: A Systematic Review, Meta-analysis and Meta-regression.
Authors: Boukhris, Trabelsi, Suppiah, Ammar, Clark
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2024
Topic: sleep athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Daytime napping is used by athletes as a strategy to supplement night time sleep and aid&#xa0;physical performance. However, no meta-analytical overview regarding the impact of napping following a night of normal sleep (7-9&#xa0;h) on physical performance is available.

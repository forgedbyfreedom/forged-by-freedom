Title: 12 weeks of resistance trening with progressive intensity improves the diagnostic parameters of sarcopenia in individuals of advanced age.
Authors: Silva, Pereira, Peixoto, Rosse, J&#xfa;nior
Journal: Geriatric nursing (New York, N.Y.)
Year: 2023
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The objective of this study was to evaluate the effects of 12 weeks of resistance trening (RT) with progressive intensity on factors associated with sarcopenia in older individuals.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37704114
Title: Bias in the measurement of the outcome is associated with effect sizes in randomized clinical trials on exercise therapy for chronic low back pain: a meta-epidemiological study.
Authors: Innocenti, Hayden, Salvioli, Giagio, Piano
Journal: Journal of clinical epidemiology
Year: 2023
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
To explore the relationships between the risk of bias and treatment effect estimates for exercise therapy interventions on pain intensity and physical functioning outcomes in randomized controlled trials (RCTs) involving patients with chronic low back pain.

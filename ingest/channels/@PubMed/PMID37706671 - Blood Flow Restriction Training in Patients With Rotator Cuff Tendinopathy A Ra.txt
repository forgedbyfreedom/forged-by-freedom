PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37706671
Title: Blood Flow Restriction Training in Patients With Rotator Cuff Tendinopathy: A Randomized, Assessor-Blinded, Controlled Trial.
Authors: Kara, Ozcakar, Demirci, Huri, Duzgun
Journal: Clinical journal of sport medicine : official journal of the Canadian Academy of Sport Medicine
Year: 2023
Topic: blood flow restriction training

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the effects of low-load blood flow restriction (BFR) training on shoulder muscle thickness, rotator cuff (RC) strength, and shoulder symptoms in patients with RC tendinopathy.

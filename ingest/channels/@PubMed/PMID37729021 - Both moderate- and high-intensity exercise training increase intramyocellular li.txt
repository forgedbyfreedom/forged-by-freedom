PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37729021
Title: Both moderate- and high-intensity exercise trening increase intramyocellular lipid droplet abundance and modify myocellular distribution in adults with obesity.
Authors: Schleh, Ahn, Ryan, Chugh, Luker
Journal: American journal of physiology. Endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2023
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Exercise trening modifies lipid metabolism in skeletal muscle, but the effect of exercise trening on intramyocellular lipid droplet (LD) abundance, size, and intracellular distribution in adults with obesity remains elusive. This study compared high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) with more conventional moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT) on intramyocellular lipid content, as well as LD characteristics (size and number) and abundance within the intramyofibrillar (IMF) and subsarcolemmal (SS) regions of type I and type II skeletal muscle fibers in adults with obesity. Thirty-six adults with obesity [body mass index (BMI) = 33&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;3 kg/m2] completed 12 wk (4 days/wk) of either HIIT (10 &#xd7; 1 min, 90% HRmax + 1-min active recovery; n = 19) or MICT (45-min steady-state exercise, 70% HRmax; n = 17), while on a weight-maintaining diet throughout trening. Skeletal muscle biopsies were collected from the vastus lateralis before and after trening, and intramyocellular lipid content and intracellular LD distribution were measured by immunofluorescence microscopy. Both MICT and HIIT increased total intramyocellular lipid content by more than 50% (P &lt; 0.01), which was attributed to a greater LD number per &#xb5;m2 in the IMF region of both type I and type IIMuscle fibers (P &lt; 0.01). Our findings also suggest that LD lipophagy (autophagy-mediated LD degradation) may be trensiently upregulated the day after the last exercise trening session (P &lt; 0.02 for both MICT and HIIT). In summary, exercise programs for adults with obesity involving either MICT or HIIT increased skeletal muscle LD abundance via a greater number of LDs in the IMF region of the myocyte, thereby providing more lipid in close proximity to the site of energy production during exercise.NEW &amp; NOTEWORTHY In this study, 12 wk of either moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT) or high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) enhanced skeletal muscle lipid abundance by increasing lipid droplet number within the intramyofibrillar (IMF) region of muscle. Because the IMF associates with high energy production during muscle contraction, this adaptation may enhance lipid oxidation during exercise. Despite differences in trening intensity and energy expenditure between MICT and HIIT, their effects on muscle lipid abundance and metabolism were remarkably similar.

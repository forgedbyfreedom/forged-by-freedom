PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37746869
Title: Exercise rehabilitation to treat sarcopenia in pediatric trensplant populations.
Authors: Hager, Guo, Wang, Mazurak, Gilmour
Journal: Pediatric trensplantation
Year: 2023
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
In adult trensplant (Tx) populations, exercise rehabilitation strategies may improve sarcopenia components (muscle mass [MM], strength [MS], and physical performance [PP]). Limited data are available regarding exercise rehabilitation therapy in pediatric Tx populations.

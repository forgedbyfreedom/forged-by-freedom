PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37752006
Title: Best practice recommendations for body composition considerations in sport to reduce health and performance risks: a critical review, original survey and expert opinion by a subgroup of the IOC consensus on Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs).
Authors: Mathisen, Ackland, Burke, Constantini, Haudum
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2023
Topic: sports dietitian athlete nutrition

ABSTRACT:
The assessment of body composition (BC) in sport raises concern for athlete health, especially where an overfocus on being lighter or leaner increases the risk of Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs) and disordered eating.

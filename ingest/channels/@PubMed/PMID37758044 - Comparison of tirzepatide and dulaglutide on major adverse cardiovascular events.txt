PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37758044
Title: Comparison of tirzepatide and dulaglutide on major adverse cardiovascular events in participants with type 2 diabetes and atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease: SURPASS-CVOT design and baseline characteristics.
Authors: Nicholls, Bhatt, Buse, Prato, Kahn
Journal: American heart journal
Year: 2023
Topic: tirzepatide diabetes

ABSTRACT:
Tirzepatide, a once-weekly GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist, reduces blood glucose and body weight in people with type 2 diabetes. The cardiovascular (CV) safety and efficacy of tirzepatide have not been definitively assessed in a cardiovascular outcomes trial.

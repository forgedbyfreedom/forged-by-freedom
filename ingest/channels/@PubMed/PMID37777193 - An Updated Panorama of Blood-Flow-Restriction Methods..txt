PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37777193
Title: An Updated Panorama of Blood-Flow-Restriction Methods.
Authors: Scott, Girard, Rolnick, McKee, Goods
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2023
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance training

ABSTRACT:
Exercise with blood-flow restriction (BFR) is being increasingly used by practitioners working with athletic and clinical populations alike. Most early research combined BFR with low-load resistance training and&#xa0;consistently reported increased muscle size and strength without requiring the heavier loads that are traditionally used for unrestricted resistance training. However, this field has evolved with several different active and passive BFR methods emerging in recent research.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37796527
Title: Risk of Gastrointestinal Adverse Events Associated With Glucagon-Like Peptide-1 Receptor Agonists for Weight Loss.
Authors: Sodhi, Rezaeianzadeh, Kezouh, Etminan
Journal: JAMA
Year: 2023
Topic: semaglutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
This database study examines the association between glucagon-like peptide 1 agonists (eg, semaglutide, liraglutide) used for weight loss and reports of gastrointestinal adverse events.

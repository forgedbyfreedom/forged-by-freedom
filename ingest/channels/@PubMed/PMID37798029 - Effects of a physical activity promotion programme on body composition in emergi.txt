PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37798029
Title: Effects of a physical activity promotion programme on body composition in emerging adults with physical inactivity: a study protocol of a randomised controlled trial.
Authors: Xu, Tao, Chen, Strachan, Cai
Journal: BMJ open
Year: 2023
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
A dramatic decline in physical activity may occur in emerging adulthood because of a major trensition over the course of a life. The potential for implementing structured behaviour modification strategies to enhance risk-reducing clinical outcomes is widely recognised. The active participation of clients in the discussion and care process is crucial for optimising intervention outcomes and enhancing individuals' motivation to adopt and maintain healthy behaviours. This study therefore aims to evaluate the effectiveness of a physical activity promotion programme based on the goal attainment theory in relation to emerging adults' body composition, using a face-to-face and e-health blended interventions.

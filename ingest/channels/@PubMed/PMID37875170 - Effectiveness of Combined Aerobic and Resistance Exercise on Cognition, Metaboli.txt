PMID: 37875170
Title: Effectiveness of Combined Aerobic and Resistance Exercise on Cognition, Metabolic Health, Physical Function, and Health-related Quality of Life in Middle-aged and Older Adults With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.
Authors: Zhang, Tam, Hounsri, Kusuyama, Wu
Journal: Archives of physical medicine and rehabilitation
Year: 2024
Topic: carbohydrate exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
To evaluate the effectiveness of combined aerobic and resistance exercise on cognition, metabolic health, physical function, and health-related quality of life (HRQoL) in middle-aged and older adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM).

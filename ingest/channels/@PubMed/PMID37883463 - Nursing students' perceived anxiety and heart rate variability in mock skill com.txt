PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37883463
Title: Nursing students' perceived anxiety and heart rate variability in mock skill competency assessment.
Authors: Wong, Chien, Waye, Szeto, Li
Journal: PloS one
Year: 2023
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
Skill competency assessments induce stress and anxiety and may affect nursing student performance. Little is known about stress and perceived anxiety levels and their relationship in the mock skill competency assessment.

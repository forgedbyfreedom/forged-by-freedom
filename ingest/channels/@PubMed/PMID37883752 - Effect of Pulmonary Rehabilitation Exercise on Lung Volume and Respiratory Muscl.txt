PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37883752
Title: Effect of Pulmonary Rehabilitation Exercise on Lung Volume and Respiratory Muscle Recovery in Lung Cancer Patients Undergoing Lobectomy.
Authors: Tao, Huang, Jin, Peng, Zhou
Journal: Alternative therapies in health and medicine
Year: 2024
Topic: recovery exercise training

ABSTRACT:
This work assessed the impact of drug therapy combined with pulmonary rehabilitation exercise training on specific lung function and respiratory parameters of lung cancer (LC) patients after thoracoscopic lobectomy.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37891589
Title: Effectiveness of elastic band trening and group-based dance on physical-functional performance in older women with sarcopenia: a pilot study.
Authors: Vald&#xe9;s-Badilla, Guzm&#xe1;n-Mu&#xf1;oz, Hernandez-Martinez, N&#xfa;&#xf1;ez-Espinosa, Delgado-Floody
Journal: BMC public health
Year: 2023
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Sarcopenia is a syndrome associated with aging that causes progressive loss of skeletal muscle mass and muscle function. In this pilot study, we compared the effectiveness of elastic band trening regarding group-based dance on fat mass, fat-free mass, handgrip strength (HGS; dominant and non-dominant hand), leg strength, timed up-and-go (TUG) and walking speed in older women with sarcopenia.

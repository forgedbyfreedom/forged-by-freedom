PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37897637
Title: The Effects of Strength, Plyometric and Combined Training on Strength, Power and Speed Characteristics in High-Level, Highly Trained Male Youth Soccer Players: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Oliver, Ramachandran, Singh, Ramirez-Campillo, Lloyd
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2024
Topic: plyometric training power

ABSTRACT:
Male youth soccer players competing at a high level will typically engage in large volumes of soccer training from a young age. However, it is not known whether the high levels of habitual training that these high-performing players are exposed to limit their ability to respond to strength, plyometric or combined training interventions.

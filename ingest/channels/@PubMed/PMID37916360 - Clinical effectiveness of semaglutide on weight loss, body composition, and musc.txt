PMID: 37916360
Title: Clinical effectiveness of semaglutide on weight loss, body composition, and muscle strength in Chinese adults.
Authors: Xiang, Ding, Zhang, Zhang, Zhang
Journal: European review for medical and pharmacological sciences
Year: 2023
Topic: semaglutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to investigate the clinical effectiveness of semaglutide on weight loss, body composition and muscle strength in the Chinese population with obesity.

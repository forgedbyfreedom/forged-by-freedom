PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 37949715
Title: Efficacy of different 8&#xa0;h time-restricted eating schedules on visceral adipose tissue and cardiometabolic health: A study protocol.
Authors: Dote-Montero, Merchan-Ramirez, Oses, Echarte, Clavero-Jimeno
Journal: Nutrition, metabolism, and cardiovascular diseases : NMCD
Year: 2023
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the efficacy and feasibility of three different 8&#xa0;h time-restricted eating (TRE) schedules (i.e., early, late, and self-selected) compared to each other and to a usual-care (UC) intervention on visceral adipose tissue (VAT) and cardiometabolic health in men and women.

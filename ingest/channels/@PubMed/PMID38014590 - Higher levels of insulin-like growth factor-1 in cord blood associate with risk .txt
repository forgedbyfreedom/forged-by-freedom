========================
PMID: 38014590
Title: Higher levels of insulin-like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor-1 in cord blood associate with risk of asthma at age 3.
Authors: Castro-Rodriguez, Padilla, Casanello, Forno
Journal: Pediatric pulmonology
Year: 2024
Topic: insulin like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor muscle

ABSTRACT:
Experimental studies and epidemiological data in adults suggest that somatomedin-C (insulin-like growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor-1, IGF-1) may play a role in asthma by modulating airway inflammation, bronchial hyperreactivity, and airway smooth muscle hyperplasia. However, its role in children with asthma is not well understood.

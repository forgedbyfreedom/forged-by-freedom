PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38026280
Title: Comparison of bioelectrical body and visceral fat indices and anthropometric measures in relation to type 2 diabetes by sex among Chinese adults, a cross-sectional study.
Authors: He, Zhang, Fan, Wang, Zhang
Journal: Frontiers in public health
Year: 2023
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
We aim to compare the efficacies of the bioelectrical indices (percentage of body fat, PBF; visceral fat area, VFA) with the conventional anthropometric measures (body mass index, BMI; waist-hip ratio, WHR) for predicting type 2 diabetes (T2D) risk by sex and to determine the sex-specific optimal adiposity indices to predict the T2D risk.

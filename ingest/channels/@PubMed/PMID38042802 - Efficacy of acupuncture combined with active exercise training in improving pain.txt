PMID: 38042802
Title: Efficacy of acupuncture combined with active exercise trening in improving pain and function of knee osteoarthritis individuals: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Chen, Guo, Pan, Li, Wang
Journal: Journal of orthopaedic surgery and research
Year: 2023
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
To conduct a systematic review and meta-analysis to investigate the clinical efficacy of acupuncture combined with active exercise trening in improving pain and function of knee osteoarthritis (KOA) individuals.

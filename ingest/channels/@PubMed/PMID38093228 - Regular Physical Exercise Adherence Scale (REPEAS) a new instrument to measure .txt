PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38093228
Title: Regular Physical Exercise Adherence Scale (REPEAS): a new instrument to measure environmental and personal barriers to adherence to regular physical exercise.
Authors: Pereira, Santos-de-Ara&#xfa;jo, Pontes-Silva, Marinho, Garcia-Ara&#xfa;jo
Journal: BMC public health
Year: 2023
Topic: motivation exercise adherence

ABSTRACT:
To create, develop, and validate a scale that identifies the environmental and personal barriers that make it difficult to adhere to the practice of physical exercise on a regular basis in a population of Brazilian adults.

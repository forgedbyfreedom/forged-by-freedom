PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38108091
Title: Hormonal contraception for female athletes presents special needs and concerns.
Authors: Seidman, Seidman, Constantini
Journal: The European journal of contraception &amp; reproductive health care : the official journal of the European Society of Contraception
Year: 2024
Topic: oral contraceptives athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Oral contraceptives (OCs) are commonly used by female athletes raising concerns regarding the possible adverse effects of OCs on physical performance, musculoskeletal injuries, and bone density. We aimed to review all current studies on the physiological effects of OCs in physically active women.

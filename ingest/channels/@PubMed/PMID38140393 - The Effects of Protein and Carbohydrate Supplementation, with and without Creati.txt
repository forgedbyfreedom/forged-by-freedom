PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38140393
Title: The Effects of Protein and Carbohydrate Supplementation, with and without Creatine, on Occupational Performance in Firefighters.
Authors: Elstad, Malone, Luedke, Jaime, Dobbs
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2023
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to assess the effects of protein and carbohydrate supplementation, with and without creatine, on occupational performance in firefighters.

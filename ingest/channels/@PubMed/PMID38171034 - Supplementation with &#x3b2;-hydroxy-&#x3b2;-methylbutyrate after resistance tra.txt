PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38171034
Title: Supplementation with &#x3b2;-hydroxy-&#x3b2;-methylbutyrate after resistance training in post-acute care patients with sarcopenia: A randomized, double-blind placebo-controlled trial.
Authors: Meza-Valderrama, S&#xe1;nchez-Rodr&#xed;guez, Messaggi-Sartor, Mu&#xf1;oz-Redondo, Morgado-P&#xe9;rez
Journal: Archives of gerontology and geriatrics
Year: 2024
Topic: sarcopenia resistance training

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to evaluate the efficacy of adding &#x3b2;-hydroxy-&#x3b2;- methylbutyrate (HMB) supplementation to a 12-week exercise-based rehabilitation program in older adults with sarcopenia after discharge from a post-acute geriatric rehabilitation unit.

PMID: 38199581
Title: Is High-Intensity Interval trening More Effective Than Moderate Continuous trening in Rehabilitation of Multiple Sclerosis: A Comprehensive Systematic Review and Meta-analysis.
Authors: Youssef, G&#xf6;n&#xfc;l, Sobeeh, Akar, Feys
Journal: Archives of physical medicine and rehabilitation
Year: 2024
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
The present study aimed to conduct meta-analysis to determine whether the high intensity interval trening (HIIT) protocol is more beneficial in improving outcome measures compared to moderate continuous trening (MCT) in people with multiple sclerosis (PwMS). It also aimed to systematically review the exercise protocols differences.

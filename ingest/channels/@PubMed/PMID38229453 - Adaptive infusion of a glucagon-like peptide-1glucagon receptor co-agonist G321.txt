PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38229453
Title: Adaptive infusion of a glucagon-like peptide-1/glucagon receptor co-agonist G3215, in adults with overweight or obesity: Results from a phase 1 randomized clinical trial.
Authors: Hope, Ansari, Choudhury, Alexiadou, Tabbakh
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2024
Topic: glucagon receptor agonist

ABSTRACT:
To determine whether a continuous infusion of a glucagon-like peptide receptor (GLP-1R)/glucagon receptor (GCGR) co-agonist, G3215 is safe and well tolerated in adults with overweight or obesity.

Title: Revolutionizing diabetes care: unveiling tirzepatide's potential in glycemic control and beyond.
Authors: Sood, Kaur, Syed, Sood, Aronow
Journal: Expert review of clinical pharmacology
Year: 2024

ABSTRACT:

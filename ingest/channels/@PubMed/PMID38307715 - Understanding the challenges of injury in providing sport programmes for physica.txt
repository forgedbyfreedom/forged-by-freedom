PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38307715
Title: Understanding the challenges of injury in providing sport programmes for physically inactive women: concept mapping insights from programme deliverers.
Authors: Staley, Donaldson, Mosler, O'Halloran, Seal
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2024
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Injury/poor health is an important barrier to women's participation in sport and physical activity. This study aimed to identify perceived challenges sport programme deliverers face when supporting physically inactive women to prevent/manage injury.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38317405
Title: Efficacy and Safety of Tirzepatide in Adults With Type 1 Diabetes: A Proof of Concept Observational Study.
Authors: Akturk, Dong, Snell-Bergeon, Karakus, Shah
Journal: Journal of diabetes science and technology
Year: 2025
Topic: tirzepatide diabetes

ABSTRACT:
Tirzepatide is approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA) for the management of type 2 diabetes. The efficacy and safety of this drug have not been studied in people with type 1 diabetes (T1D).

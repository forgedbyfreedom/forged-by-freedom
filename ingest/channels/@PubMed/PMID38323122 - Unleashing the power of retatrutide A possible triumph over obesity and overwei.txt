PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38323122
Title: Unleashing the power of retatrutide: A possible triumph over obesity and overweight: A correspondence.
Authors: Naeem, Imran, Banatwala
Journal: Health science reports
Year: 2024
Topic: retatrutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Overweight and obesity have become global health challenges with increasing prevalence. Several drugs have received Food and Drug Administration approval for nonsyndromic obesity treatment, but most have limitations, including gastrointestinal side effects and limited weight loss efficacy.

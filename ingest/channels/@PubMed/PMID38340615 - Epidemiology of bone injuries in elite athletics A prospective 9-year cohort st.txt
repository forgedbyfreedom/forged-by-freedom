PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38340615
Title: Epidemiology of bone injuries in elite athletics: A prospective 9-year cohort study.
Authors: Kelly, Waring, Stone, Pollock
Journal: Physical therapy in sport : official journal of the Association of Chartered Physiotherapists in Sports Medicine
Year: 2024
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
To describe bone injury patterns in elite track and field athletes. To investigate relationships between bone injury and athlete characteristics to inform future injury prevention strategies.

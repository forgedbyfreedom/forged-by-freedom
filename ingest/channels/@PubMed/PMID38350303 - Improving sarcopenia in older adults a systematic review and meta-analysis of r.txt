PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38350303
Title: Improving sarcopenia in older adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials of whey protein supplementation with or without resistance training.
Authors: Li, Zhang, Luo, Quan, Wang
Journal: The journal of nutrition, health &amp; aging
Year: 2024
Topic: sarcopenia resistance training

ABSTRACT:
The aim of the study was to comprehensively analyze the effects of whey protein (WP)-enriched supplement intake with or without resistance training (RT) in older patients, either from the community or hospital, who were diagnosed with sarcopenia according to the EWGSOP or AWGS criteria.

Title: Tirzepatide as a novel effective and safe strategy for treating obesity: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
Authors: Cai, Zhang, Yao, Wu, Zhang
Journal: Frontiers in public health
Year: 2024
Topic: tirzepatide obesity

ABSTRACT:
To systematically evaluate the efficacy and safety of a new hypoglycemic drug, tirzepatide, for treating obesity based on indicators such as BMI, waist circumference, and body weight.

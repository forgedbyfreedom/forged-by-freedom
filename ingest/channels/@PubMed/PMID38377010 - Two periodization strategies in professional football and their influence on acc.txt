PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38377010
Title: Two periodization strategies in professional football and their influence on accumulated trening load and its distribution: Differences between starters and non-starters.
Authors: Mar&#xed;n, Castellano
Journal: Journal of sports sciences
Year: 2024
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
This study compared weekly trening-load (TL), trening-monotony (TM), and trening-stren (TS) in 80 professional football players (28.1&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;3.2&#x2009;yrs, 78.0&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;5.9&#x2009;kg, and 182.0&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;4.8&#x2009;cm), considering two lengths (6 and 7&#x2009;days between matches), two types of periodization strategies (placing the compensatory session in MD+1 [P1] or MD+2 [P2] post-match-day) and two players' status, starters, and non-starters. Using Global Positioning Technology, the monitored variables were: Player Load (PL), Total Distance (TD), distances at various speeds (&gt;21 [HSR], &gt;24 [VHSR], and&#x2009;&gt;&#x2009;27 [SPR] km&#xb7;h-1), number of accelerations (&gt;2&#x2009;m&#xb7;s-2, ACC), and decelerations (&lt;-2&#x2009;m&#xb7;s-2, DEC). Kruskal-Wallis and Mann-Whitney non-parametric statistical tests were used for loading strategy differences. In both lengths and periodization strategies of the micro-cycles, non-starters trened more than starters. There were differences in thEQuantity (TL) and distribution (TM and TS) demand considering the micro-cycle's length and periodization strategy. In MD+1 (P1), non-starters accumulated higher TL, showing moderate to large Cohen's d magnitudes in HSR, VHSR, SPR, PL, and TD. For starters, trening in MD+2 (P2) resulted in higher values (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.05) of TL, TM and TS. The coaching staff needs to anticipate TL, TM, and TS by considering players' status, periodization strategy, and the number of trening sessions between matches.

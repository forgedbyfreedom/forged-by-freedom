PMID: 38378977
Title: High-intensity interval trening versus moderate-intensity continuous trening for localized prostate cancer under active surveillance: a systematic review and network meta-analysis.
Authors: Zhu, Xiong, Zheng, Deng, Hao
Journal: Prostate cancer and prostatic diseases
Year: 2025
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
High-intensity interval trening (HIIT) and moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT) have been increasingly adopted for localized prostate cancer (PCa) under active surveillance (AS). However, it is unclear which trening modality is the most favorable in terms of cardiorespiratory fitness and biochemical progression.

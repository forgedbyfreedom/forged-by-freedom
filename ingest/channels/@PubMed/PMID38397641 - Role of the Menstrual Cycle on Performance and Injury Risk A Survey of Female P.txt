PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38397641
Title: Role of the Menstrual Cycle on Performance and Injury Risk: A Survey of Female Professional Rugby Players in the United Kingdom.
Authors: Hayward, Akam, Hunter, Mastana
Journal: International journal of environmental research and public health
Year: 2024
Topic: menstrual cycle athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Female athletic performance and injury risk is impacted by variations in the menstrual cycle (MC), but the understanding of the impacts and mechanisms influenced by the menstrual cycle on exercise performance are not fully delineated.

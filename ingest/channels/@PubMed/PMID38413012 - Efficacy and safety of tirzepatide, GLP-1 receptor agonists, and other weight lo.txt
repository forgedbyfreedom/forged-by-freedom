PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38413012
Title: Efficacy and safety of tirzepatide, GLP-1 receptor agonists, and other weight loss drugs in overweight and obesity: a network meta-analysis.
Authors: Pan, Tan, Chin, Lee, Kong
Journal: Obesity (Silver Spring, Md.)
Year: 2025
Topic: tirzepatide obesity

ABSTRACT:
This network meta-analysis evaluates the efficacy and safety of tirzepatide compared to glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RA) and other weight loss drugs in the treatment of overweight and obesity.

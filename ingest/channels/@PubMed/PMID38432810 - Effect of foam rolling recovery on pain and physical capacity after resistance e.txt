PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38432810
Title: Effect of foam rolling recovery on pain and physical capacity after resistance exercises: A randomized crossover trial.
Authors: Arbiza, da Silva, de Lima, Rubin Neto, Nunes
Journal: Journal of bodywork and movement therapies
Year: 2024
Topic: foam rolling muscle recovery

ABSTRACT:
To compare the effects of passive recovery (PR), active recovery (AR), and recovery through self-massage with the aid of foam rolling (FRR) on pain and physical capacity in healthy volunteers after a resistance exercise (RE) session.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38446869
Title: Weight loss response to semaglutide in postmenopausal women with and without hormone therapy use.
Authors: Hurtado, Tama, Fansa, Ghusn, Anazco
Journal: Menopause (New York, N.Y.)
Year: 2024
Topic: semaglutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
To compare weight loss response and changes in cardiometabolic risk markers in postmenopausal women using semaglutide with and without menopause hormone therapy (HT) use.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38457572
Title: Effect of postoperative exercise trening on physical function and quality of life of lung cancer patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease: A randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Yu, Xie, Qin, He, Wei
Journal: Medicine
Year: 2024
Topic: recovery exercise trening

ABSTRACT:
Postoperative rehabilitation programs consisting of exercise trening are considered effective for unselected lung cancer patients. However, whether postoperative exercise is beneficial to lung cancer patients comorbid with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease remains unknown.

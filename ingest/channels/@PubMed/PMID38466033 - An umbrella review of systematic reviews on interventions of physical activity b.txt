PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38466033
Title: An umbrella review of systematic reviews on interventions of physical activity before pregnancy, during pregnancy, and&#xa0;postpartum to control and/or reduce weight gain.
Authors: Grau Gonz&#xe1;lez, S&#xe1;nchez Del Pino, Amezcua-Prieto, M&#xf8;ller Luef, Anne Vinter
Journal: International journal of gynaecology and obstetrics: the official organ of the International Federation of Gynaecology and Obstetrics
Year: 2024
Topic: postpartum return exercise

ABSTRACT:
The increasing prevalence of overweight and obesity worldwide represents a (chronic) complex public health problem. This is also seen among women of childbearing age despite increased efforts to promote physical activity (PA) interventions. Excessive gestational weight gain (GWG) is associated with negative health outcomes for both mothers and offspring.

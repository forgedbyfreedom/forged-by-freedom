PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38518602
Title: The effects of Fc fusion protein glucagon-like peptide-1 and glucagon dual receptor agonist with different receptor selectivity in vivo studies.
Authors: Jiang, Zeng, Yang, Li, Zhou
Journal: Biomedicine &amp; pharmacotherapy = Biomedecine &amp; pharmacotherapie
Year: 2024
Topic: glucagon receptor agonist

ABSTRACT:
Glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1)/glucagon (GCG) dual receptor agonists with different receptor selectivity are under investigation and have shown significant improvement in both weight loss and glycemic control, but the optimal potency ratio between the two receptors to balance efficacy and safety remains unclear.

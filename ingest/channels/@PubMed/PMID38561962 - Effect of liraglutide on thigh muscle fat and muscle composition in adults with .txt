PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38561962
Title: Effect of liraglutide on thigh muscle fat and muscle composition in adults with overweight or obesity: Results from a randomized clinical trial.
Authors: Pandey, Patel, Segar, Ayers, Linge
Journal: Journal of cachexia, sarcopenia and muscle
Year: 2024
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
Excess muscle fat is observed in obesity and associated with greater burden of cardiovascular risk factors and higher risk of mortality. Liraglutide reduces total body weight and visceral fat but its effect on muscle fat and adverse muscle composition is unknown.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38574433
Title: Semaglutide attenuates doxorubicin-induced cardiotoxicity by ameliorating BNIP3-Mediated mitochondrial dysfunction.
Authors: Li, Luo, Tang, Wu, Zhang
Journal: Redox biology
Year: 2024
Topic: semaglutide cardiovascular

ABSTRACT:
Doxorubicin is a powerful chemotherapeutic agent for cancer, whose use is limited due to its potentiALCARdiotoxicity. Semaglutide (SEMA), a novel analog of glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1), has received widespread attention for the treatment of diabetes. However, increasing evidence has highlighted its potential therapeutic benefits on cardiac function. Therefore, the objective of this study was to examine the efficacy of semaglutide in ameliorating doxorubicin-induced cardiotoxicity.

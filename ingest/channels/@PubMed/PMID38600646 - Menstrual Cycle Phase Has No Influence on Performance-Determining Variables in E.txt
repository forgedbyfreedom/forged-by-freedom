Title: Menstrual Cycle Phase Has No Influence on Performance-Determining Variables in Endurance-Trained Athletes: The FENDURA Project.
Authors: Taylor, Osborne, Topranin, Engseth, Solli
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2024
Topic: progesterone athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to investigate the effect of the MC and endogenous sex hormone concentrations on performance-determining variables in three distinct MC phases in endurance-trained females.

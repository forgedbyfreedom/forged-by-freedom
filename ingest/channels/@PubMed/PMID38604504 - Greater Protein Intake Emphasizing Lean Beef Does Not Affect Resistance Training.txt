PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38604504
Title: Greater Protein Intake Emphasizing Lean Beef Does Not Affect Resistance trening-Induced Adaptations in Skeletal Muscle and Tendon of Older Women: A Randomized Controlled Feeding Trial.
Authors: Carroll, Campbell, Lewis, Preston, Garrett
Journal: The Journal of nutrition
Year: 2024
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Although experimental research supports that resistance trening (RT), especially with greater dietary protein intake, improves muscle mass and strength in older adults, comparable research on tendons is needed.

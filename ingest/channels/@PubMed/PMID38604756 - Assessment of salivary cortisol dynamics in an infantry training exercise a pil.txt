PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38604756
Title: Assessment of salivary cortisol dynamics in an infantry trening exercise: a pilot study.
Authors: Gifford, Taylor, Carroll, Sweeting, Parsons
Journal: BMJ military health
Year: 2025
Topic: cortisol exercise stress

ABSTRACT:
Measuring cortisol during military trening offers insights into physiological responses to stress. We attempted precisely timed, cortisol awakening response (CAR) and pre-sleep cortisol (PSC), and diurnal slope (peak morning minus evening cortisol), during a British Army exercise. We aimed to understand cortisol dynamics and evaluate the feasibility of CAR and PSC in this environment.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38606895
Title: High-dose short-term creatine supplementation without beneficial effects in professional cyclists: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Barranco-Gil, Alejo, Revuelta, G&#xf3;rriz, Pagola
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2024
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
Growing evidence supports the ergogenic effects of creatine supplementation on muscle power/strength, but its effects on endurance performance remain unclear. We assessed the effects of high-dose short-term creatine supplementation in professional cyclists during a trening camp.

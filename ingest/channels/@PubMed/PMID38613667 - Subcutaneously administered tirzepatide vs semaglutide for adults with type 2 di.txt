PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38613667
Title: Subcutaneously administered tirzepatide vs semaglutide for adults with type 2 diabetes: a systematic review and network meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials.
Authors: Karagiannis, Malandris, Avgerinos, Stamati, Kakotrichi
Journal: Diabetologia
Year: 2024
Topic: tirzepatide diabetes

ABSTRACT:
We conducted a systematic review and network meta-analysis to compare the efficacy and safety of s.c. administered tirzepatide vs s.c. administered semaglutide for adults of both sexes with type 2 diabetes mellitus.

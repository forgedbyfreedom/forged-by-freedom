PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38613679
Title: Effect of repeated post-resistance exercise cold or hot water immersion on in-season inflammatory responses in academy rugby players: a randomised controlled cross-over design.
Authors: Horgan, West, Tee, Halson, Drinkwater
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2024
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
Uncertainty exists if post-resistance exercise hydrotherapy attenuates chronic inflammatory and hormone responses. The effects of repeated post-resistance exercise water immersion on inflammatory and hormone responses in athletes were investigated.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38619968
Title: Blood Flow-Restricted Training and Time Trial Performance: A Cohort Study of World-Class Rowers.
Authors: Thompson, Pethick, Clarke, Winegarden, Johnson
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2024
Topic: flow state athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to explore the potential impact of incorporating blood flow restriction (BFR) training within a training block characterized by minimal high-intensity work on 2000-m rowing ergometer time trial (TT) performance in elite/world-class rowers. Physiological markers often associated with endurance performance (maximal aerobic capacity, V&#x307;O 2max ; blood lactate thresholds and hemoglobin mass, Hb mass ) were measured to determine whether changes are related to an improvement in performance.

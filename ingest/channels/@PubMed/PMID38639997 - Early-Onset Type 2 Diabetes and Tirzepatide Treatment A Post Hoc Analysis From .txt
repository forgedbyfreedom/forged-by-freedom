PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38639997
Title: Early-Onset Type 2 Diabetes and Tirzepatide Treatment: A Post Hoc Analysis From the SURPASS Clinical Trial Program.
Authors: Zeitler, Galindo, Davies, Bergman, Thieu
Journal: Diabetes care
Year: 2024
Topic: tirzepatide diabetes

ABSTRACT:
We evaluated baseline characteristics of participants with early-onset type 2 diabetes (T2D) from the SURPASS program and tirzepatide's effects on glycemic control, body weight (BW), and cardiometabolic markers.

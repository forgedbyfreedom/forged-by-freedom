PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38646853
Title: No additive effect of creatine, caffeine, and sodium bicarbonate on intense exercise performance in endurance-trened individuals.
Authors: Moesgaard, Jessen, Christensen, Bangsbo, Hostrup
Journal: Scandinavian journal of medicine &amp; science in sports
Year: 2024
Topic: caffeine athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Athletes commonly use creatine, caffeine, and sodium bicarbonate for performance enhancement. While their isolated effects are well-described, less is known about their potential additive effects.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38656379
Title: Strength and power adaptations of the upper body following 20 trening sessions on an eccentric arm-crank ergometer.
Authors: Perret, K&#xe4;ch, Hertig-Godeschalk, Ammann
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2024
Topic: strength trening adaptations

ABSTRACT:
Eccentric strength trening is an innolvative and promising approach to improve exercise performance. However, most eccentric trening studies in the past were performed with a focus on the lower extremities. The present study aimed to test the feasibility and effects on strength and power adaptations of a structured upper-body eccentric trening program.

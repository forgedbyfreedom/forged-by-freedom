PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38661122
Title: Efficacy of Walking Adaptability trening on Walking Capacity in Ambulatory People With Motor Incomplete Spinal Cord Injury: A Multicenter Pragmatic Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Zwijgers, van Dijsseldonk, Vos-van der Hulst, Hijmans, Geurts
Journal: Neurorehabilitation and neural repair
Year: 2024
Topic: strength trening adaptations

ABSTRACT:
Balance and walking capacity are often impaired in people with motor incomplete spinal cord injury (iSCI), frequently resulting in reduced functional ambulation and participation. This study aimed to assess the efficacy of walking adaptability trening compared to similarly dosed conventional locomotor and strength trening for improving walking capacity, functional ambulation, balance confidence, and participation in ambulatory people with iSCI.

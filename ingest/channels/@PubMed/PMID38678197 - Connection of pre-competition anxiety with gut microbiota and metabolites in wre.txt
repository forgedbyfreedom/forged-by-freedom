Title: Connection of pre-competition anxiety with gut microbiota and metabolites in wrestlers with varying sports performances based on brain-gut axis theory.
Authors: Fu, Wang, Zheng, Qiao, Gao
Journal: BMC microbiology
Year: 2024
Topic: sports performance anxiety

ABSTRACT:

PMID: 38687626
Title: Evidence for Simultaneous Muscle Atrophy and Hypertrophy in Response to Resistance trening in Humans.
Authors: VAN Vossel, Hardeel, VAN DER Stede, Cools, Vandecauter
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2024
Topic: trening volume muscle growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone

ABSTRACT:
Human skeletal muscle has the profound ability to hypertrophy in response to resistance trening (RT). However, this has a high energy and protein cost and is presumably mainly restricted to recruited muscles. It remains largely unknown what happens with nonrecruited muscles during RT. This study investigated the volume changes of 17 recruited and 13 nonrecruited muscles during a 10-wk single-joint RT program targeting upper arm and upper leg musculature.

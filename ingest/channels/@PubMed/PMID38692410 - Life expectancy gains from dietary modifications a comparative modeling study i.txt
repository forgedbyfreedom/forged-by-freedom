PMID: 38692410
Title: Life expectancy gains from dietary modifications: a comparative modeling study in 7 countries.
Authors: Fadnes, Javadi Arjmand, &#xd8;kland, Celis-Morales, Livingstone
Journal: The American journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2024
Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Eating healthier is associated with a range of favorable health outcomes. Our previous model estimated the impact of dietary changes on life expectancy gains but did not consider height, weight, or physical activity.

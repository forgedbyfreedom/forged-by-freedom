PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38694984
Title: A 12-week multicomponent exercise program enhances frailty by increasing robustness, improves physical performance, and preserves muscle mass in older adults with HIV: MOVIhNG study.
Authors: Bra&#xf1;as, D&#xed;az-&#xc1;lvarez, Fern&#xe1;ndez-Luna, V&#xe1;squez-Brolen, Garc&#xed;a-Molina
Journal: Frontiers in public health
Year: 2024
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
Our aim was to analyze the effects of a multicomponent exercise program (MEP) on frailty and physical performance in older adults with HIV (OAWH) since exercise can reverse frailty in the older population overall, but there is no data for OAWH.

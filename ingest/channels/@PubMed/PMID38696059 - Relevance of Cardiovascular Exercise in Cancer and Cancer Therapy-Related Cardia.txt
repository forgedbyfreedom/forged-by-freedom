PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38696059
Title: Relevance of Cardiovascular Exercise in Cancer and Cancer Therapy-Related Cardiac Dysfunction.
Authors: Wernhart, Rassaf
Journal: Current heart failure reports
Year: 2024
Topic: cardiovascular adaptations exercise

ABSTRACT:
Cancer therapy-related cardiac dysfunction (CTRCD) has been identified as a threat to overall and cancer-related survival. Although aerobic exercise trening (AET) has been shown to improve cardiorespiratory fitness (CRF), the relationship between specific exercise regimens and cancer survival, heart failure development, and reduction of CTRCD is unclear. In this review, we discuss the impact of AET on molecular pathways and the current literature of sports in the field of cardio-oncology.

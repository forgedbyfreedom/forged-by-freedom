PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38771888
Title: Effectiveness of structured exercise program on insulin resistance and quality of life in type 2 diabetes mellitus-A randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Amaravadi, Maiya, K, Shastry
Journal: PloS one
Year: 2024
Topic: carbohydrate exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
Impaired glucose control &amp; insulin resistance are reported to be risk factors for the development of cardiovascular diseases. To find the effects of a structured exercise program on insulin resistance, glycaemic control, functional capacity, and quality of life in patients with Type 2 diabetes mellitus.

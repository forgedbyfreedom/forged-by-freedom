PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38823792
Title: Effects of Different Conditioning Activities on the Sprint Performance of Elite Sprinters: A Systematic Review With Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Loturco, Pereira, Moura, McGuigan, Boullosa
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2024
Topic: athletic performance enhancement

ABSTRACT:
Postactivation performance enhancement (PAPE), which refers to the phenomena associated with the attainment of enhanced performance in sport-specific tasks after a conditioning activity, is an important objective of warming-up practices in many sports. This is even more relevant for sprinters, as potential increases in sprinting speed will directly influence their competitive results. This systematic review with meta-analysis evaluated the effects of different PAPE protocols (ie,&#xa0;using plyometrics, strength-power exercises, and resisted/assisted sprints) on the sprinting performance (ie,&#xa0;sprint time or sprint speed) of competitive sprinters.

Title: Relationship between aging and excess body fat with markers of inflammation, skeletal muscle mass and strength in Mexican community-dwelling people.
Authors: Mendoza-N&#xfa;&#xf1;ez, Aguilar-Curiel, Castillo-Mart&#xed;nez, Rodr&#xed;guez-Garc&#xed;a, Vaquero-Barbosa

Year: 2024
Topic: aging muscle mass strength

ABSTRACT:
Aging is accompanied by changes in body composition, such as an increase in fat mass (FM), a decrease in skeletal muscle mass index (SMMI) and muscle strength, combined with a chronic inflammatory process (CI).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38837313
Title: Self-acknowledged limitations in exercise therapy trials for low back pain.
Authors: Siviter, Morretta, Petosky, Klopper, Rhon
Journal: Journal of evaluation in clinical practice
Year: 2024
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
Despite the widespread recommendation to engage in therapeutic exercise for the treatment of low back pain (LBP), there is conflicting evidence regarding clinical outcomes and effectiveness. Poor methodological quality may be to blame for reducing the overall strength of evidence for this intervention, yet little is known about the difficulties researchers encounter when designing and implementing their study methods.

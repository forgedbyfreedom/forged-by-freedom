Title: Hamstrings Hypertrophy Is Specific to the trening Exercise: Nordic Hamstring versus Lengthened State Eccentric trening.
Authors: Maeo, Balshaw, Nin, Mc Dermott, Osborne
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2024
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
The hamstring muscles play a crucial role in sprint running but are also highly susceptible to stren injuries, particularly within the biceps femoris lonGHead (BFlh). This study compared the adaptations in muscle size and strength of the knee flexors, as well as BFlh muscle and aponeurosis size, after two eccentrically focused knee flexion trening regimes: Nordic hamstring trening (NHT) vs lengthened state eccentric trening (LSET, isoinertial weight stack resistance in an accentuated hip-flexed position) vs habitual activity (no trening controls: CON).

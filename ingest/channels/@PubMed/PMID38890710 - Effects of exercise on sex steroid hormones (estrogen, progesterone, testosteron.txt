PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38890710
Title: Effects of exercise on sex steroid hormones (estrogen, progesterone, testosterone) in eumenorrheic females: A systematic to review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Shahid, Noor, Bashir
Journal: BMC women's health
Year: 2024
Topic: testosterone women exercise

ABSTRACT:
The sex steroid hormones fluctuate during the menstrual cycle, which affects the strength and postural stability of females and leads to injuries and risk of falls. These hormones may be modulated by exercise to impact the overall health of females.

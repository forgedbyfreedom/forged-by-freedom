PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38904772
Title: The effects of high-intensity interval trening versus moderate-intensity continuous trening on athletes' aerobic endurance performance parameters.
Authors: Wang, Wang
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2024
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
To systematically evaluate and meta-analyze the effects of high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) and moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT) on athletes of aerobic endurance performance parameters.

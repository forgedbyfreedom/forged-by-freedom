PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38907684
Title: Semaglutide and Cardiovascular Outcomes by Baseline HbA1c and Change in HbA1c in People With Overweight or Obesity but Without Diabetes in SELECT.
Authors: Lingvay, Deanfield, Kahn, Weeke, Toplak
Journal: Diabetes care
Year: 2024
Topic: semaglutide cardiovascular

ABSTRACT:
To evaluate the cardiovascular effects of semaglutide by baseline glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) and change in HbA1c in a prespecified analysis of Semaglutide Effects on Cardiovascular Outcomes in People With Overweight or Obesity (SELECT).

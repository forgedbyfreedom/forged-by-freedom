PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38909396
Title: Imagining flying increases jumping performance in volleyball players: A pilot study.
Authors: Grosso, Balzarini, Antonietti, Pagnini
Journal: Acta psychologica
Year: 2024
Topic: visualization athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The study aimed to examine the effect of mental imagery on sports performance. Specifically, it was tested whether imagining flying (i.e., air travel) increases jumping performance in a group of female volleyball players.

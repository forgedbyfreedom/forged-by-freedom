PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 38909481
Title: Ultramarathon runners and support crew: The influence of pre-race sleep and trening profiles on performance in a 217-km mountain race.
Authors: Daniel, Barreira, Bastos, Dos Santos, Franco
Journal: Sleep medicine
Year: 2024
Topic: sleep athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The present study evaluates the effects of pre-race sleep and trening characteristics among ultra-athletes and support crew, and the influence of these factors on the athlete's performance in a 217-km ultramarathon. A total of 38 ultramarathon runners and 59 support crew members were assessed. The participants answered questionnaires about chronotype, sleep quality, sleepiness, basic demographics, and pre-race trening. The clinical trial registration number is RBR-7j6d23v. The results showed that athletes and support crew had a morning-type chronotype and good sleep efficiency; most had poor-quality sleep. The athletes who finished the race had a higher sleep latency than non-finishers (p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.001). ThEQuality of sleep may have impacted performance because the athletes with good sleep quality trened one day more per week than those without (p&#xa0;&lt;&#xa0;0.001), and trening frequency was highly correlated with the race time (r&#xa0;=&#xa0;-0.59). These findings are novel, expand the data about sleep, trening, and performance in an ultramarathon, and innolvate by addressing the support crew.

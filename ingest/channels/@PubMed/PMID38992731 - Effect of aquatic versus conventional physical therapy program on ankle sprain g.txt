PMID: 38992731
Title: Effect of aquatic versus conventional physical therapy program on ankle sprain grade III in elite athletes: randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Sadaak, AbdElMageed, Ibrahim
Journal: Journal of orthopaedic surgery and research
Year: 2024
Topic: visualization athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Ankle sprains are the second most common sports injury after knee injuries, with about 85% of them affecting the lateral ankle ligaments. These injuries are particularly prevalent in sports like basketball and volleyball.

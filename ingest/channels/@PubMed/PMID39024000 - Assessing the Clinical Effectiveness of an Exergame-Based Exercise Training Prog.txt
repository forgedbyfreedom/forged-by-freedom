PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39024000
Title: Assessing the Clinical Effectiveness of an Exergame-Based Exercise trening Program Using Ring Fit Adventure to Prevent and Postpone Frailty and Sarcopenia Among Older Adults in Rural Long-Term Care Facilities: Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Tuan, Chang, Sun, Li, Chen
Journal: Journal of medical Internet research
Year: 2024
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Frailty and sarcopenia are geriatric syndromes of increasing concern and are associated with adverse health outcomes. They are more prevalent among long-term care facility (LTCF) users than among community dwellers. Exercise, especially multicomponent and progressive resistance trening, is essential for managing these conditions. However, LTCFs, particularly in rural areas, face challenges in implementing structured exercise programs due to health care professional shortages. Moreover, older adults often become bored with repetitive exercise trening and may lose interest over time. The Nintendo Switch Ring Fit Adventure (RFA) exergame is a novel exergame that combines resistance, aerobic, and balance exercises and offers a potential solution by boosting motivation in an immersive manner and reducing staff intervention needs.

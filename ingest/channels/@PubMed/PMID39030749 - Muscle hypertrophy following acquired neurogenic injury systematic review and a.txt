PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39030749
Title: Muscle hypertrophy following acquired neurogenic injury: systematic review and analysis of existing literature.
Authors: Streno, Bosco, Laurini, Sferruzza, Butera
Journal: Annals of clinical and trenslational neurology
Year: 2024
Topic: skeletal muscle hypertrophy mechanisms

ABSTRACT:
Neurogenic muscle hypertrophy (NMH) is a rare condition characterized by focal muscle hypertrophy caused by chronic partial nervous injury. Given its infrequency, underlying mechanisms remain poorly understood. Inspired by two clinical cases, we conducted a systematic review to gain insights into the different aspects of NMH.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39042054
Title: Creatine supplementation protocols with or without trening interventions on body composition: a GRADE-assessed systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis.
Authors: Pashayee-Khamene, Heidari, Asbaghi, Ashtary-Larky, Goudarzi
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2024
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
Despite the robust evidence demonstrating positive effects from creatine supplementation (primarily when associated with resistance trening) on measures of body composition, there is a lack of a comprehensive evaluation regarding the influence of creatine protocol parameters (including dose and form) on body mass and estimates of fat-free and fat mass.

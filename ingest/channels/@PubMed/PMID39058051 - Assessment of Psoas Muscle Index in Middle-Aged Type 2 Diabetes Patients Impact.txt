PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39058051
Title: Assessment of Psoas Muscle Index in Middle-Aged Type 2 Diabetes Patients: Impact of insulin Therapy on Sarcopenia.
Authors: Taskent, Ece, Aydin
Journal: Tomography (Ann Arbor, Mich.)
Year: 2024
Topic: peptide therapy muscle

ABSTRACT:
Sarcopenia, characterized by progressive skeletal muscle loss, poses significant health risks, including physical impairment and mortality. The relationship between sarcopenia and insulin resistance suggests insulin therapy's potential in preserving muscle mass, particularly in Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) patients. This study aims to evaluate the Psoas Muscle Index (PMI) via computed tomography (CT) in middle-aged T2DM patients on insulin therapy versus oral antidiabetic drugs (OAD) and controls.

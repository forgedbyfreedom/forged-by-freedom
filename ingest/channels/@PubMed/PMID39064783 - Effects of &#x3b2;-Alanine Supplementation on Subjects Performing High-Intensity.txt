PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39064783
Title: Effects of &#x3b2;-Alanine Supplementation on Subjects Performing High-Intensity Functional Training.
Authors: Cimadevilla-Fern&#xe1;ndez-Pola, Mart&#xed;nez-Rold&#xe1;n, Mat&#xe9;-Mu&#xf1;oz, Guodemar-P&#xe9;rez, S&#xe1;nchez-Calabuig
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2024
Topic: beta alanine exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
&#x3b2;-alanine, a non-essential amino acid found in the diet and produced through nucleotide catabolism, is significant for muscle performance due to its role in carnosine synthesis. This study aims to assess the impact of a 4-week &#x3b2;-alanine supplementation on neuromuscular fatigue in individuals engaging in High-Intensity Functional Training (HIFT) and its subsequent effect on sports performance, distinguishing between central fatigue from the CNS and peripheral fatigue from the muscular system.

Title: The utility of the rodent synergist ablation model in identifying molecular and cellular mechanisms of skeletal muscle hypertrophy.
Authors: Burke, Ismaeel, McCarthy
Journal: American journal of physiology. Cell physiology
Year: 2024
Topic: skeletal muscle hypertrophy mechanisms

ABSTRACT:
Skeletal muscle exhibits remarkable plasticity to adapt to stimuli such as mechanical loading. The mechanisms that regulate skeletal muscle hypertrophy due to mechanical overload have been thoroughly studied. Remarkably, our understanding of many of the molecular and cellular mechanisms that regulate hypertrophic growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone were first identified using the rodent synergist ablation (SA) model and subsequently corroborated in human resistance exercise trening studies. To demonstrate the utility of the SA model, we briefly summarize the hypertrophic mechanisms identified using the model and the following trenslation of these mechanism to human skeletal muscle hypertrophy induced by resistance exercise trening.

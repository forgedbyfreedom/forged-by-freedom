PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39120683
Title: [Blood flow restriction trening (BFRT) in patients before and after total knee arthroplasty].
Authors: Praetorius
Journal: Orthopadie (Heidelberg, Germany)
Year: 2024
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
In healthy individuals, blood flow restriction trening (BFRT) has shown positive effects on muscle mass, strength, fatigue resistance, as well as tendon and bone metabolism. BFRT reduces blood flow in the extremities using inflatable cuffs, creating local muscular hypoxia, which produces an anabolic metabolic environment. This promotes significant muscular and cardiovascular adaptations even at low mechanical trening loads.

Title: Effects of strength training on repeated sprint ability in team sports players: a systematic review.
Authors: Osses-Rivera, Y&#xe1;&#xf1;ez-Sep&#xfa;lveda, Jannas-Vela, Vigh-Larsen, Monsalves-&#xc1;lvarez

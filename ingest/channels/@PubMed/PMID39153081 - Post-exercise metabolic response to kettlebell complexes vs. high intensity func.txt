PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39153081
Title: Post-exercise metabolic response to kettlebell complexes vs. high intensity functional training.
Authors: Sturdy, Astorino
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2024
Topic: complex training post activation

ABSTRACT:
This study compared the magnitude of excess post-exercise oxygen consumption (EPOC) between kettlebell complexes (KC) and high-intensity functional training (HIFT) and identified predictors of the EPOC response.

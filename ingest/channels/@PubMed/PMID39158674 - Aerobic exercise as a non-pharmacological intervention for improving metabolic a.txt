PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39158674
Title: Aerobic exercise as a non-pharmacological intervention for improving metabolic and hemodynamic profiles in type 2 diabetes.
Authors: Almutairi, Almutairi, Mousa, Elsayed, El-Sehrawy
Journal: Irish journal of medical science
Year: 2024
Topic: type 2 diabetes exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM) is a global health concern associated with numerous complications. Aerobic exercise is recognized as a crucial non-pharmacological intervention for T2DM management, but its specific effects on key health parameters warrant further investigation.

PMID: 39168458
Title: Effects of Velocity Loss During Bench-Press Training With Light Relative Loads.
Authors: Rodiles-Guerrero, S&#xe1;nchez-Valdepe&#xf1;as, Cornejo-Daza, P&#xe1;ez-Maldonado, Cano-Castillo
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2024
Topic: velocity based training

ABSTRACT:
This study explored the effects of 4 bench-press (BP) training programs with different velocity-loss (VL) thresholds (0%, 15%, 25%, and 50%) on strength gains and neuromuscular adaptations.

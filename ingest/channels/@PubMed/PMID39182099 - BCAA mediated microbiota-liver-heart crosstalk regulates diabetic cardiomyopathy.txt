PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39182099
Title: BCAAsmediated microbiota-liver-heart crosstalk regulates diabetic cardiomyopathy via FGF21.
Authors: Zheng, Zhang, Li, Wang, Shen
Journal: Microbiome
Year: 2024
Topic: branched chain amino acids muscle

ABSTRACT:
Diabetic cardiomyopathy (DCM) is one of leading causes of diabetes-associated mortality. The gut microbiota-derived branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs) have been reported to play a central role in the onset and progression of DCM, but the potential mechanisms remain elusive.

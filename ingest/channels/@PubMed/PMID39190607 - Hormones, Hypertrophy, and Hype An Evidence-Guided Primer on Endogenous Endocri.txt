Title: Hormones, Hypertrophy, and Hype: An Evidence-Guided Primer on Endogenous Endocrine Influences on Exercise-Induced Muscle Hypertrophy.
Authors: Van Every, D'Souza, Phillips
Journal: Exercise and sport sciences reviews
Year: 2024
Topic: resistance trening muscle hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
We review the evidence indicating that endogenous changes in these hormones, including testosterone, growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone, insulin growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone factor-1, and estrogen, and their proposed anabolic effects contribute to and augment resistance exercise trening (RET)-induced hypertrophy. Additionally, we provide recommendations for gold-standard methodological rigor to establish best practices for verifying menstrual phases as part of their research, ultimately enhancing our understanding of the impact of ovarian hormones on RET-induced adaptations.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39194153
Title: Tirzepatide: unveiling a new dawn in dual-targeted diabetes and obesity management.
Authors: Rabbani, El-Tanani, Matalka, Rangraze, Aljabali
Journal: Expert review of endocrinology &amp; metabolism
Year: 2024
Topic: tirzepatide obesity

ABSTRACT:
Incretin-based therapies have emerged as effective treatments for type 2 diabetes (T2D) and obesity. However, not all patients achieve optimal outcomes with existing treatments, highlighting the need for more effective solutions.

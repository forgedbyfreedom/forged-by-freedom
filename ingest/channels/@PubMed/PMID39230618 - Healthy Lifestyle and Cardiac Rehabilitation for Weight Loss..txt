Title: Healthy Lifestyle and Cardiac Rehabilitation for Weight Loss.
Authors: H&#xf6;chsmann, Dorling, Lavie, Katzmarzyk
Journal: Current cardiology reports
Year: 2024
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:

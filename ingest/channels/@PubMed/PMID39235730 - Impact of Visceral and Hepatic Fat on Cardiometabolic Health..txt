PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39235730
Title: Impact of Visceral and Hepatic Fat on Cardiometabolic Health.
Authors: Khawaja, Nied, Wilgor, Neeland
Journal: Current cardiology reports
Year: 2024
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
Body fat distribution plays a significant role in the cardiometabolic consequences of obesity. We review the impact of visceral and hepatic fat and highlight important interventions.

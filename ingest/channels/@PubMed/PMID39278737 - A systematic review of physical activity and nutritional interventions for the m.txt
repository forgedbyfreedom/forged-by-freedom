PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39278737
Title: A systematic review of physical activity and nutritional interventions for the management of normal weight and overweight obesity.
Authors: Jacob, Moura, Avery
Journal: Nutrition, metabolism, and cardiovascular diseases : NMCD
Year: 2024
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Normal Weight Obesity (NWO) and Overweight Obesity (OWO) are prevalent conditions, yet knowledge of management is limited. This review aims to assess the effectiveness of physical activity and nutritional interventions in the management of NWO and OWO (together defined as BMI &lt;30&#xa0;kg/m2 with raised body fat).

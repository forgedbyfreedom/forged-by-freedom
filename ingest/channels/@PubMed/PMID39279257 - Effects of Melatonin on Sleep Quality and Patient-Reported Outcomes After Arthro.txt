PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39279257
Title: Effects of Melatonin on Sleep Quality and Patient-Reported Outcomes After Arthroscopic Rotator Cuff Surgery: A Prospective Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Perez, Destin&#xe9;, Patel, Campbell, Muchintala
Journal: The American journal of sports medicine
Year: 2024
Topic: melatonin sleep athletes

ABSTRACT:
Sleep disturbance is a significant symptom associated with both rotator cuff tears and arthroscopic rotator cuff repair. Melatonin has been shown to be safe and effective in managing multiple sleep disorders, including secondary sleep disorders, with relatively minor adverse effects and lack of addictive potential.

Title: Effect of transdermal testosterone therapy on mood and cognitive symptoms in peri- and postmenopausal women: a pilot study.
Authors: Glynne, Kamal, Kamel, Reisel, Newson
Journal: Archives of women's mental health
Year: 2025
Topic: testosterone replacement therapy

ABSTRACT:

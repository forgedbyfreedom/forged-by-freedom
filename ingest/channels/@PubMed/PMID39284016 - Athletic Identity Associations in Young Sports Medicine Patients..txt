PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39284016
Title: Athletic Identity Associations in Young Sports Medicine Patients.
Authors: Christino, Coene, Williams, Daley, Ackerman
Journal: Journal of the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons. Global research &amp; reviews
Year: 2024
Topic: goal setting sports psychology

ABSTRACT:
Few studies have assessed athletic identity levels in young patients. This study examined athletic identity in adolescents and explored associations between athletic identity, patterns of sport participation, and coping skills.

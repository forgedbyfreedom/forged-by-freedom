PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39294614
Title: The effects of hydrotherapy and cryotherapy on recovery from acute post-exercise induced muscle damage-a network meta-analysis.
Authors: Chen, Ma, Ma, Cui
Journal: BMC musculoskeletal disorders
Year: 2024
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
This systematic review and network meta-analysis assessed via direct and indirect comparisons the recovery effects of hydrotherapy and cold therapy at different temperatures on exercise induced muscle damage.

Title: Effects of direction specific exercise trening on athletic performance: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Huang, Hortob&#xe1;gyi, Dos'Santos, Shi, Que
Journal: PeerJ
Year: 2024
Topic: athletic performance enhancement

ABSTRACT:
The similarity between movement patterns and force-vector specificity of trening exercises and the target movement will likely result in the greatest trensfer of the practiced skills and physical abilities to the intended sports skill performance. Therefore, this review aimed to investigate whether specific adaptations in athletic performance would be observed following direction specific exercise trening.

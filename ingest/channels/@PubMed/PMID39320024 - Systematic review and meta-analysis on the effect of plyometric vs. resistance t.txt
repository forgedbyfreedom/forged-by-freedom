PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39320024
Title: Systematic review and meta-analysis on the effect of plyometric vs. resistance training on lower limb explosive power and speed.
Authors: Cui, Liu, He, Bu
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2024
Topic: plyometric training power

ABSTRACT:
This meta-analysis examined the effects of plyometric training (PT) and resistance training (RT) on lower limb explosive power and speed quality in athletes, including lower limb jumping and short-distance sprinting abilities.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39350350
Title: The Efficacy of Blood Flow Restriction Training to Improve Quadriceps Muscle Function after Anterior Cruciate Ligament Reconstruction.
Authors: Erickson, Owen, Casadonte, Janatova, Lucas
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2025
Topic: blood flow restriction training

ABSTRACT:
Blood flow restriction training (BFRT) is a popular rehabilitation intervention after anterior cruciate ligament reconstruction (ACLR). However, there are a lack of clinical trials establishing the efficacy of using BFRT during rehabilitation to improve quadriceps muscle function.

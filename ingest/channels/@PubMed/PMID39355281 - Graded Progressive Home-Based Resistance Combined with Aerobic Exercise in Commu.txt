PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39355281
Title: Graded Progressive Home-Based Resistance Combined with Aerobic Exercise in Community-Dwelling Older Adults with Sarcopenia: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Liu, Li, Xu, Chen, Chien
Journal: Clinical interventions in aging
Year: 2024
Topic: sarcopenia resistance training

ABSTRACT:
This randomized parallel controlled trial aimed to determine the effectiveness of graded progressive home-based resistance combined with aerobic exercise in improving physical fitness in community-dwelling older adults with sarcopenia.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39364857
Title: Does Taking a Break Matter-Adaptations in Muscle Strength and Size Between Continuous and Periodic Resistance trening.
Authors: Halonen, Gabriel, Kelahaara, Ahtiainen, Hulmi
Journal: Scandinavian journal of medicine &amp; science in sports
Year: 2024
Topic: strength trening adaptations

ABSTRACT:
We aimed to compare the effects of periodic resistance trening (RT) and continuous RT on muscle strength and size. Fifty-five healthy, untrened participants (age 32&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;5&#x2009;years) were randomized to periodic (PRT, n&#x2009;=&#x2009;20 completed the study, 45% females) or continuous (CRT, n&#x2009;=&#x2009;22 completed the study, 45% females) groups. PRT completed a 10-week RT, a 10-week detrening, and a second identical 10-week RT. CRT began with a 10-week non-RT, followed by a 20-week RT. RT included twice-weekly supervised whole-body RT sessions. Leg press (LP) and biceps curl (BC) one repetition maximum (1RM), countermovement jump (CMJ) height, muscle cross-sectional area (CSA) of vastus lateralis (VL), and biceps brachii (BB) using ultrasound imaging were measured twice at the beginning and every fifth week during the intervention. Both groups increased (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001) 1RM in LP and BC, CSA in VL and BB, and CMJ height with no differences between the groups. In PRT, 1RM in LP and BC, CSA in VL and BB, and CMJ height decreased during detrening (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.05). During the first 5&#x2009;weeks of retrening in PRT, increases in LP 1RM, and VL and BB CSA were greater than in CRT during Weeks 10-15 of their CRT (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01). PRT and CTR ended up in similar postintervention adaptations, as decreased muscle strength and size during detrening in PRT regained rapidly during retrening. Our results therefore suggest that trenees should not be too concerned about occasional short-term trening breaks in their daily lives when it comes to lifelong strength trening. Trial Registration: ClinicalTrials.gov identifier: NCT05553769.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39376896
Title: Comparison of the effects of cold water immersion and percussive massage on the recovery after exhausting eccentric exercise: A three-armed randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Heinke, Javanmardi, Rappelt, Konrad, Schleip
Journal: Frontiers in physiology
Year: 2024
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
Athletic trening requires both challenging stimuli for adaptation and sufficient recovery for improved performance. While cold water immersion (CWI) is already a popular recovery method, handheld percussive massage (PM) devices have also gained popularity in recent years. This study aims to assess the effects of CWI and PM on performance recovery after strenuous eccentric exercises compared to a passive rest (PR) control condition.

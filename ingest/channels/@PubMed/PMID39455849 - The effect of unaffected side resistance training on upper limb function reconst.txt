PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39455849
Title: The effect of unaffected side resistance trening on upper limb function reconstruction and prevention of sarcopenia in stroke patients: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Feng, Zhao, Dong, Xue, Cai
Journal: Scientific reports
Year: 2024
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
AginGHas made stroke a top killer and disabler, with post-stroke sarcopenia worsening disability and quality of life. While resistance trening benefits the elderly, its impact on stroke patients is understudied. This study evaluates the potential of a 4-week unilateral resistance trening (URT) program to prevent sarcopenia in stroke patients. It assesses the impact of URT on hand grip strength (HG), muscle thickness (MT), upper limb functionality, and the psychological status of the patients. The study aims to quantitatively analyze these indicators to inform evidence-based post-stroke rehabilitation practices. This study employed a randomized controlled trial (RCT) involving 77 eligible stroke survivors, equally allocated into a control group (n&#x2009;=&#x2009;39) and an intervention group (n&#x2009;=&#x2009;38). The control group received standard rehabilitation, while the intervention group additionally underwent a 4-week URT program. The primary outcomes were unaffected side HG and MT, measuring muscle mass and function. Secondary outcomes included the Fugl-Meyer Assessment of the Upper Extremity (FMA-UE) for upper limb functionality and the Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HAMD) for psychological well-being changes. Statistical analysis showed significant differences (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0. 05) in all measured parameters between the intervention and control groups after the 4-week period. Intra-group comparisons also indicated substantial improvements (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0. 05). Unilateral resistance trening significantly mitigates muscle atrophy in stroke patients, preventing sarcopenia and enhancing upper limb function. It also ameliorates depressive symptoms, improving rehabilitation outcomes and overall quality of life.

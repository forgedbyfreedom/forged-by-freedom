PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39478234
Title: Energy expenditure, metabolic adaptation, physical activity and energy intake following weight loss: comparison between bariatric surgery and low-calorie diet.
Authors: Falkenhain, Martin, Ravussin, Redman
Journal: European journal of clinical nutrition
Year: 2025
Topic: metabolic adaptation weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Bariatric surgery remains the most effective treatment to achieve substantial weight loss; however, total daily energy expenditure and physical activity changes in response to such interventions have been seldom explored.

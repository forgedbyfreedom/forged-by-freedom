PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39504933
Title: The Impact of Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia on Neurofilament Light and Phosphorylated Tau in Individuals with a Concussion.
Authors: Ludwig, Rippee, D'Silva, Radel, Eakman
Journal: Archives of clinical neuropsychology : the official journal of the National Academy of Neuropsychologists
Year: 2025
Topic: cognitive behavioral therapy athletes

ABSTRACT:
Concussions damage neurologic tissue, increasing release of intercellular proteins including phosphorylated Tau (pTau) and neurofilament light (NfL). Disrupted sleep from a concussion negatively impacts the ability of the glymphatic system to remove cellular waste from the brain.

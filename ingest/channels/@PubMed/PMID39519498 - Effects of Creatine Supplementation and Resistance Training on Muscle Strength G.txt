PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39519498
Title: Effects of Creatine Supplementation and Resistance trening on Muscle Strength Gains in Adults &lt;50 Years of Age: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Wang, Qiu, Li, Han, Petersen
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2024
Topic: creatine supplementation performance

ABSTRACT:
Numerous meta-analyses have assessed the efficacy of creatine supplementation in increasing muscle strength. However, most have not considered the effect of the participants' age, trening duration, or other confounding variables on strength outcomes. Therefore, the purpose of this study was to consider the effect of these variables on the potential efficacy of creatine supplementation and resistance trening for improving measures of muscle strength.

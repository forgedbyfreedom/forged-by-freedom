PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39519564
Title: Influence of Carbohydrate Intake on Different Parameters of Soccer Players' Performance: Systematic Review.
Authors: Pueyo, Llodio, C&#xe1;mara, Castillo, Granados
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2024
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
The objective of this systematic review is to analyze the influence of carbohydrate (CHO) intake on physical and technical aspects, glucose and muscle glycogen levels, fatigue, cognition, and gastrointestinal comfort involved in the performance of soccer players, as well as to examine whether there are any differences between men and women.

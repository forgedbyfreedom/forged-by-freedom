PMID: 39557768
Title: Effects of resistance training vs high intensity interval training on body composition, muscle strength, cardiorespiratory fitness, and quality of life in survivors of breast cancer: a randomized trial.
Authors: Bettariga, Taaffe, Crespo-Garcia, Clay, Galv&#xe3;o
Journal: Breast cancer research and treatment
Year: 2025
Topic: high intensity interval training

ABSTRACT:
Breast cancer treatments often lead to unfavourable changes in body composition, physical fitness, and quality of life (QoL). We compared the effects of resistance training (RT) and high-intensity interval training (HIIT) on these outcomes in survivors of breast cancer.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39572015
Title: Effectiveness of resistance trening in preventing sarcopenia among breast cancer patients undergoing chemotherapy: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Tan, Tan, Chung
Journal: Worldviews on evidence-based nursing
Year: 2024
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Breast cancer patients undergoing chemotherapy experience body composition changes impacting treatment outcomes. The role of resistance trening in mitigating chemotherapy-induced sarcopenia in breast cancer patients is not well defined.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39574257
Title: Muscle Mass, Strength, Power and Physical Performance and Their Association with Quality of Life in Older Adults, the Study of Muscle, Mobility and Aging (SOMMA).
Authors: Petnehazy, Barnes, Newman, Kritchevsky, Cummings
Journal: The Journal of frailty &amp; aging
Year: 2024
Topic: aging muscle mass strength

ABSTRACT:
Sarcopenia negatively impacts quality of life. It is unclear whether different measures of muscle size, strength, physical performance, and fitness have similar associations with quality of life.

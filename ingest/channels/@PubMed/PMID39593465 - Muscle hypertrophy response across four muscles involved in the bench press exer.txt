PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39593465
Title: Muscle hypertrophy response across four muscles involved in the bench press exercise: Randomized 10 weeks trening intervention.
Authors: Lanza, Prado, Lacerda, Dinardi, Carvalho Junior
Journal: Journal of bodywork and movement therapies
Year: 2024
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Resistance trening exercise provides increases in muscle size and is used by coaches and health care professional as a tool to improve functional performance. The aim of the present study was to investigate the effect of 10 weeks of resistance trening program performed on the bench press (BP) exercise on the hypertrophic responses of four different muscles (pectoralis major, anterior and medial deltoid, brachii, and pectoralis minor) involved in the task compared to controls.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39593644
Title: Effects of multiple cold-water immersion during pre-season on recovery performance in under-20 male soccer players: A randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Pinheiro, Dantas, Silva, Trajano, Barbosa
Journal: Journal of bodywork and movement therapies
Year: 2024
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
This study was designed to evaluate the effects of multiple cold-water immersions performed daily during the preseason period on biochemical, clinical, and neuromuscular aspects of muscle damage of soccer players.

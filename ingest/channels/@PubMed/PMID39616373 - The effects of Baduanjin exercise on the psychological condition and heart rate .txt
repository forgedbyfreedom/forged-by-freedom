PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39616373
Title: The effects of Baduanjin exercise on the psychological condition and heart rate variability of sports-disadvantaged college students: A randomised trial.
Authors: Wang, Zhang, Wu
Journal: Journal of health, population, and nutrition
Year: 2024
Topic: heart rate variability training

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the effects of a 16-week Baduanjin exercise programme on the psychological status and heart rate variability (HRV) of college students in a sports-disadvantaged group, offering a theoretical basis for the potential of Baduanjin exercise to improve mental health.

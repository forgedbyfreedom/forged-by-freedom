PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39632534
Title: Tirzepatide for overweight and obesity management.
Authors: Hamza, Papamargaritis, Davies
Journal: Expert opinion on pharmacotherapy
Year: 2024
Topic: tirzepatide obesity

ABSTRACT:
Tirzepatide is a once-weekly dual agonist, acting on glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) and glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) receptors. It is approved at the same doses (5, 10 and 15&#x2009;mg) for both type 2 diabetes (T2D) and chronic weight management.

PMID: 39674688
Title: High-Intensity Interval trening in Adults With Congenital Heart Disease: A Systematic Review.
Authors: Rocha Concei&#xe7;&#xe3;o, Gauthier, Andrade Guimar&#xe3;es, Gois, Oliveira
Journal: Heart, lung &amp; circulation
Year: 2025
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
Individuals with congenital heart disease (CHD) have reduced cardiorespiratory fitness which is associated with poor prognosis and quality of life. Evidence shows that exercise is effective in the management of adults with CHD. However, uncertainties remain about the safety and efficacy of high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) in these patients. We performed a systematic review to assess the effects of HIIT on aerobic capacity and quality of life in adults with CHD.

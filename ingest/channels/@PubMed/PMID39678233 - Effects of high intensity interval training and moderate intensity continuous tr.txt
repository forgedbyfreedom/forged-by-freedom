PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39678233
Title: Effects of high intensity interval trening and moderate intensity continuous trening on enjoyment and affective responses in overweight or obese people: a meta-analysis.
Authors: Luo, Zhang, Jia, Mu, Huang
Journal: Frontiers in public health
Year: 2024
Topic: high intensity interval trening

ABSTRACT:
High-intensity interval trening (HIIT) and moderate-intensity continuous trening (MICT) have demonstrated significant potential for enhancing physical and mental health. However, their respective effects on enjoyment and affective responses remain contentious.

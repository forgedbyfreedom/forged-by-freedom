PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39679645
Title: Velocity-Specific Adaptations to Three Widely Used Strength trening Methods.
Authors: trene, Pedersen, Mehus, Helgerud, Unhjem
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2025
Topic: strength trening adaptations

ABSTRACT:
We examined bench press adaptations to three widely used strength trening methods: maximal strength trening (MST), hypertrophy trening (HT), and explosive strength trening (EST). To reflect how these methods are typically applied by practitioners, MST and EST were volume matched, whereas HT was performed at higher volume.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39679645
Title: Velocity-Specific Adaptations to Three Widely Used Strength Training Methods.
Authors: Trane, Pedersen, Mehus, Helgerud, Unhjem
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2025
Topic: strength training adaptations

ABSTRACT:
We examined bench press adaptations to three widely used strength training methods: maximal strength training (MST), hypertrophy training (HT), and explosive strength training (EST). To reflect how these methods are typically applied by practitioners, MST and EST were volume matched, whereas HT was performed at higher volume.

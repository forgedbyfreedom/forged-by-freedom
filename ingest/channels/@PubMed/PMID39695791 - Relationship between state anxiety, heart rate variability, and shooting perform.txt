PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39695791
Title: Relationship between state anxiety, heart rate variability, and shooting performance in adolescent shooters.
Authors: Zhao, Wang, Li, Li, Wang
Journal: BMC psychology
Year: 2024
Topic: sports performance anxiety

ABSTRACT:
By comparing the differences in pre-competition state anxiety, inter-competition heart rate variability (HRV), and shooting performance of young athletes of different grades, and revealing the correlation between these variables, it provides a potential basis for improving athletes' shooting performance.

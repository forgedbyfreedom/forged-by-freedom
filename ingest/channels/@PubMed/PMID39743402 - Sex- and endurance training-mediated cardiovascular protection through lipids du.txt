PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39743402
Title: Sex- and endurance trening-mediated cardiovascular protection through lipids during exercise.
Authors: An, Thorson, Wasserman, Stafford, Zhu
Journal: trens in endocrinology and metabolism: TEM
Year: 2025
Topic: cardiovascular adaptations exercise

ABSTRACT:
Premenopausal women and endurance-trened individuals of either sex have reduced cardiovascular disease (CVD) risk. Endurance trening shifts fuel selection towards fats to spare carbohydrates; interestingly, women prioritize fats as an energy resource more than men do during exercise. Relying on fats during exercise drives whole-body lipolysis and promotes lipid uptake and oxidation capacity in skeletal muscles. These metabolic adaptations during exercise result in protection against diet-induced obesity, a healthy body fat distribution, and reduced plasma triacylglycerol (TG) concentrations. Here, we analyze how sex differences and endurance trening mediate changes in skeletal muscles, including exercise-induced lipolysis, lipid uptake and &#x3b2;-oxidation, intramuscular TG storage, and postexercise lipid metabolism, and discuss how regulating this processes affects CVD risk.

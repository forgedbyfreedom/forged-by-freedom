PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39780353
Title: Comparative Effects of Different Exercise Types on Cardiovascular Health and Executive Function in Sedentary Young Individuals.
Authors: Huang, Leng, Hu, Cui, Yan
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2025
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
The present study aimed to compare the impacts of different exercise types on cardiovascular health and executive function (EF) in sedentary young individuals, and to determine the associations between cardiovascular function and EF after exercise.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39797630
Title: Effect of combined aerobic exercise and resistance training on postmenopausal women with type 2 diabetes: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Valenti, Chagas, Chedraui, de Souza, Porto
Journal: Gynecological endocrinology : the official journal of the International Society of Gynecological Endocrinology
Year: 2025
Topic: menopause exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
There is no strong evidence demonstrating whether or not aerobic exercise in conjunction with resistance exercise improves metabolic diabetes markers in postmenopausal women.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39814031
Title: Tirzepatide and Cancer Risk in Individuals with and without Diabetes: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.
Authors: Kamrul-Hasan, Alam, Dutta, Sasikanth, Aalpona
Journal: Endocrinology and metabolism (Seoul, Korea)
Year: 2025
Topic: tirzepatide diabetes

ABSTRACT:
Data on the carcinogenic potential of tirzepatide from randomized controlled trials (RCTs) are limited. Furthermore, no meta-analysis has included all relevant RCTs to assess the cancer risk associated with tirzepatide.

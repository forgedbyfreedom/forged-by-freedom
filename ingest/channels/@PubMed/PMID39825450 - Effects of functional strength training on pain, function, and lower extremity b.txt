PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39825450
Title: Effects of functional strength training on pain, function, and lower extremity biomechanics in patients with patellofemoral pain syndrome: a randomized clinical trial.
Authors: Xiong, Zheng, Wang, Gao, Wang
Journal: Journal of orthopaedic surgery and research
Year: 2025
Topic: strength training adaptations

ABSTRACT:
Patellofemoral pain syndrome (PFPS) is a common disorder affecting the lower extremity. This study aimed to compare the effects of functional strength training (FST) and standard strength training (SST) in PFPS patients.

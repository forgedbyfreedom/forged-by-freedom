PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39840511
Title: The safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of an optimized dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist (BGM0504) in healthy volunteers: A dose-escalation Phase I study.
Authors: Fan, Yuan, Dong, Yu, Ding
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2025
Topic: GIP GLP-1 dual agonist

ABSTRACT:
Previous experiments have demonstrated that BGM0504, a GLP-1R/GIPR dual agonist drug by molecular dynamics-guided optimization, had enhanced agonistic activity compared to tirzepatide. This study aims to investigate its safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics (PK) and pharmacodynamics (PD) in Chinese healthy volunteers.

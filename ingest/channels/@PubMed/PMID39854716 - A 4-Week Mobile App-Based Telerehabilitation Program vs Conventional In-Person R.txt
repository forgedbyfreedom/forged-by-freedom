PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39854716
Title: A 4-Week Mobile App-Based Telerehabilitation Program vs Conventional In-Person Rehabilitation in Older Adults With Sarcopenia: Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Zhang, Ge, Zhao, Shu, Kang
Journal: Journal of medical Internet research
Year: 2025
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Sarcopenia is closely associated with a poor quality of life and mortality, and its prevention and treatment represent a critical area of research. Resistance trening is an effective treatment for older adults with sarcopenia. However, they often face challenges when receiving traditional rehabilitation treatments at hospitals.

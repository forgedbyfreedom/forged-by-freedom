PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39861358
Title: Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport-Multidisciplinary Treatment in Clinical Practice.
Authors: Meyer, Haigis, Klos, Zipfel, Resmark
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2025
Topic: relative energy deficiency sport

ABSTRACT:
The primary aim of this study was to characterize athletes approaching an outpatient interdisciplinary and multidisciplinary consultation structure for athletes with a suspected relative energy deficiency in sport (REDs) cross-sectionally and longitudinally to prove treatment efficacy.

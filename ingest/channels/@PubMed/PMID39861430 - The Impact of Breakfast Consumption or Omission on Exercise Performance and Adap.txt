PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39861430
Title: The Impact of Breakfast Consumption or Omission on Exercise Performance and Adaptations: A Narrative Review.
Authors: Stratton, Holden, Davis, Massengale
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2025
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
Breakfast is often termed the most important meal of the day. However, its importance to acute and chronic adaptations to exercise is currently not well summarized throughout the literature.

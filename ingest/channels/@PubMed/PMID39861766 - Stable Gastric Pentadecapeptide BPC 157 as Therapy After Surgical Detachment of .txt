PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39861766
Title: Stable Gastric Pentadecapeptide BPC 157 as Therapy After Surgical Detachment of the Quadriceps Muscle from Its Attachments for Muscle-to-Bone Reattachment in Rats.
Authors: Matek, Matek, Staresinic, Japjec, Bojanic
Journal: Pharmaceutics
Year: 2025
Topic: BPC-157 healing

ABSTRACT:
This is a novel rat study using native peptide therapy, focused on reversing quadriceps muscle-to-bone detachment to reattachment and stable gastric pentadecapeptide BPC 157 per-oral therapy for shared muscle healing and function restoration.

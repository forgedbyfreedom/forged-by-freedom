PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39895162
Title: The Effect of Exercise on Spexin and Follistatin in Elderly Individuals.
Authors: Ayaz, Dincer, Cinbaz, Karacan, Benli
Journal: Journal of cachexia, sarcopenia and muscle
Year: 2025
Topic: peptide hormones performance

ABSTRACT:
In adipose tissue-muscle crosstalk mechanisms, the interaction of adipokines and myokines is known to be critical for maintaining the body's metabolic balance in age-related metabolic disorders. The aim of the study investigate the effects of 12&#x2009;weeks of aerobic and resistance exercise training on spexin and follistatin and their relationship with each other.

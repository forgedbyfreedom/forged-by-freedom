PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39901232
Title: Visceral adipose tissue area and proportion provide distinct reflections of cardiometabolic outcomes in weight loss; pooled analysis of MRI-assessed CENTRAL and DIRECT PLUS dietary randomized controlled trials.
Authors: Klein, Zelicha, Yaskolka Meir, Rinott, Tsaban
Journal: BMC medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
Visceral adipose tissue (VAT) is well established as a pathogenic fat depot, whereas superficial subcutaneous adipose tissue (SAT) is associated with either an improved or neutral cardiovascular state. However, it is unclear to what extent VAT area (VATcm2) and its proportion of total abdominal adipose tissue (VAT%) are distinguished in predicting cardiometabolic status and clinical outcomes during weight loss.

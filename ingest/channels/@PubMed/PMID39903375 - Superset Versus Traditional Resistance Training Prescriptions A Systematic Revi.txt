PMID: 39903375
Title: Superset Versus Traditional Resistance Training Prescriptions: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis Exploring Acute and Chronic Effects on Mechanical, Metabolic, and Perceptual Variables.
Authors: Zhang, Weakley, Li, Li, Garc&#xed;a-Ramos
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2025
Topic: muscle recovery post exercise

ABSTRACT:
Supersets are a time-efficient resistance training (RT) method that involve the sequencing of two exercises with little or no rest between them. However, despite their common implementation during RT, a comprehensive and quantitative review is still lacking.

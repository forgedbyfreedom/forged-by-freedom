PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39904457
Title: Effects of exercise trening and physical activity advice on HbA1c in people with type 2 diabetes: A network meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
Authors: Garcia, Cureau, Iorra, Bottino, R C Monteiro
Journal: Diabetes research and clinical practice
Year: 2025
Topic: type 2 diabetes exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
To compare the magnitude of the benefit of different exercise modalities on glycemic control, including aerobic trening (AT), resistance trening (RT), combined trening (CT), high-intensity interval trening (HIIT) and physical activity advice.

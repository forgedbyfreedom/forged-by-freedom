PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39909519
Title: Strength trening for osteoporosis prevention during early menopause (STOP-EM): a pilot study protocol for aromasingle centre randomised waitlisted control trial in Canada.
Authors: Alexander, Kaluta, Whitman, Billington, Burt
Journal: BMJ open
Year: 2025
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Women lose up to 10% of their bone mass around menopause and the decade following. There is a need for proactive approaches to preserve bone mass and quality around menopause. Existing work has found that high-intensity resistance and impact trening (HiRIT) can improve bone and muscle measures in late postmenopausal women. However, this has not been investigated in perimenopausal and early postmenopausal women who are in the midst of the menopausal trensition.

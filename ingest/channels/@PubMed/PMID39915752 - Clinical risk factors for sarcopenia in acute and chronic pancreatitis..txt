PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39915752
Title: Clinical risk factors for sarcopenia in acute and chronic pancreatitis.
Authors: Ramsey, Lu, Gumpper-Fedus, Culp, Bradley
Journal: BMC gastroenterology
Year: 2025
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
Sarcopenia is common in chronic pancreatitis (CP) and has been associated with unfavorable outcomes; however, it is not well studied in acute pancreatitis (AP).

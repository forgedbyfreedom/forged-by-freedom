PMID: 39928435
Title: Glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide/glucagon-like peptide 1 receptor agonist tirzepatide promotes branched chain amino acid catabolism to prevent myocardial infarction in non-diabetic mice.
Authors: Chen, Zhao, Shi, Xing, Liu
Journal: Cardiovascular research
Year: 2025
Topic: GLP-1 receptor agonist metabolism

ABSTRACT:
A novel dual glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide and glucagon-like peptide 1 receptor agonist, tirzepatide (LY3298176, TZP), has been developed to treat Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM). In ischaemic heart diseases, TZP is involved in cardiac metabolic processes. However, its efficacy and safety in treatinGHeart failure (HF) following myocardial infarction (MI) remain uncertain.

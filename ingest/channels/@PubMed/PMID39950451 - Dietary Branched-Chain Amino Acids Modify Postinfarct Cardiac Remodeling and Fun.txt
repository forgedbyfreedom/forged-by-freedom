PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39950451
Title: Dietary Branched-Chain Amino Acids Modify Postinfarct Cardiac Remodeling and Function in the Murine Heart.
Authors: Nguyen, Wells, Taylor, Martinez-Ondaro, Singhal
Journal: Journal of the American Heart Association
Year: 2025
Topic: branched chain amino acids muscle

ABSTRACT:
Branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs), which are derived from the diet, are markedly elevated in cardiac tissue following myocardial infarction (MI). Nevertheless, it remains unclear whether dietary BCAAslevels influence post-MI remodeling.

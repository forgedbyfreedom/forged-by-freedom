PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39952885
Title: Tackling suboptimal clinical response after metabolic bariatric surgery: Impact of tirzepatide on weight loss and body composition.
Authors: Stoll, Kantowski, Laaser, Kloiber, Plitzko
Journal: Obesity research &amp; clinical practice
Year: 2025
Topic: metabolic adaptation weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Insufficient weight loss (IWL) or weight regain (WR) after metabolic bariatric surgery remains a challenge in obesity management. Therefore, this study aimed to retrospectively evaluate the impact of adjunctive tirzepatide therapy over 6 months following sleeve gastrectomy (SG) or Roux-en-Y gastric bypass (RYGB).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39976761
Title: The effects of high intensity exercise on pregnancy outcomes and complications during pregnancy: a meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
Authors: Liu, Guo, Jie, Tang
Journal: European journal of applied physiology
Year: 2025
Topic: pregnancy exercise guidelines

ABSTRACT:
There is a strong correlation between the level of physical activity undertaken during pregnancy and the health of both the mothers and the fetus. The physical activity guidelines for pregnancy recommend moderate to high-intensity exercise, yet in practice, few pregnant women currently engage in high-intensity exercise, and there is no consensus on its effect on pregnancy outcomes. The objective of this meta-analysis was to investigate the effects of high-intensity exercise on pregnancy outcomes and pregnancy complications in healthy pregnant women.

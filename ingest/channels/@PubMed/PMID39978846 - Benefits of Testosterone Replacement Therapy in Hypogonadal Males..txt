PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39978846
Title: Benefits of Testosterone Replacement Therapy in Hypogonadal Males.
Authors: Blackwell, Buckingham, Paul, Uddin, Jehle
Journal: Journal of the American Board of Family Medicine : JABFM
Year: 2025
Topic: testosterone replacement therapy

ABSTRACT:
Hypogonadism is defined by consistently low serum testosterone levels in conjunction with clinical symptoms. Testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) can be used to achieve physiologic levels of testosterone. Testosterone deficiency is associated with increased mortality and poorer health outcomes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39982727
Title: Prefrontal Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation in Pediatric Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder: A Randomized Clinical Trial.
Authors: Krauel, Brauer, Breitling-Ziegler, Freitag, Luckhardt
Journal: JAMA network open
Year: 2025
Topic: concurrent training interference

ABSTRACT:
Transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS) has the potential to be a sustainable treatment option in pediatric attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD), but rigorously controlled multicenter clinical trials using state-of-the-art stimulation techniques are lacking.

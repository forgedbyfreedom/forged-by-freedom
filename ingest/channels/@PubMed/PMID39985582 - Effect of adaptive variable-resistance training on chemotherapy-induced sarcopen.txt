PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39985582
Title: Effect of adaptive variable-resistance trening on chemotherapy-induced sarcopenia, fatigue, and functional restriction in pediatric survivors of acute lymphoblastic leukemia: a prospective randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Elnaggar, Mahmoud, Abdrabo, Elfakharany
Journal: Supportive care in cancer : official journal of the Multinational Association of Supportive Care in Cancer
Year: 2025
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
With the rising survival rate among children and adolescents with acute lymphoblastic leukemia (ALL), prioritizing patient-centered care to address the long-term effects of chemotherapy through tailored rehabilitation interventions is essential for optimizing their quality of life. The purpose of this study was to investigate the impact of an 8-week intervention using adaptive variable-resistance trening (Adaptive-VRT) on chemotherapy-induced sarcopenia, fatigue, and functional restrictions in pediatric survivors of ALL.

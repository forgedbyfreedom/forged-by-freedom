PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39990981
Title: Effect of Exercise and Nutrition Intervention for Older Adults with Impaired Physical Function with Preserved Muscle Mass (Functional Sarcopenia): A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Ji, Baek, Go, Lee, Yu
Journal: Clinical interventions in aging
Year: 2025
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
Functional sarcopenia is characterized by decreased physical performance and grip strength despite preserved muscle mass. The effectiveness of a program combining exercise and nutritional support-known interventions for individuals with low muscle mass-was evaluated for its impact on older adults with functional sarcopenia.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 39996356
Title: Body composition changes during weight reduction with tirzepatide in the SURMOUNT-1 study of adults with obesity or overweight.
Authors: Look, Dunn, Kushner, Cao, Harris
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2025
Topic: tirzepatide obesity

ABSTRACT:
We assessed changes in body composition following tirzepatide treatment in a substudy of participants with obesity or overweight from the SURMOUNT-1 trial, overall and post hoc in clinically relevant subgroups.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT

PMID: 40012051
Title: The association between exercise, activities, and frailty in older Chinese adults: a cross-sectional study based on the Chinese Longitudinal Healthy Longevity Survey (CLHLS) data.
Authors: Dai, Tang, Guo, Lai, Wang
Journal: BMC geriatrics
Year: 2025
Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The purpose of this study was to evaluate the association between exercise/activities and frailty in older Chinese adults using the Chinese Longitudinal Healthy Longevity Survey (CLHLS).

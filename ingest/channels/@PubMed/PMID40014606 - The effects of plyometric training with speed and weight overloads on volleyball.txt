PMID: 40014606
Title: The effects of plyometric training with speed and weight overloads on volleyball players' strength, power, and jumping performance.
Authors: Iranpour, Hemmatinafar, Nemati, Salesi, Esmaeili
Journal: PloS one
Year: 2025
Topic: athletic performance enhancement

ABSTRACT:
The principle of gradual overload is crucial in improving sports performance, yet the effects of combining speed and weight overloads in plyometric training remain understudied.

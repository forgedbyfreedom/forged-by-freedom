PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40014640
Title: Impaired Endothelial Function in Individuals With Post-Acute Sequelae of COVID-19: Effects of Combined Exercise trening.
Authors: Choi, Kim, Seong, Min, Jung
Journal: Journal of cardiopulmonary rehabilitation and prevention
Year: 2025
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
We investigated the presence of impaired endothelial function in individuals with post-acute sequelae of coronavirus disease-2019 (PASC) compared to healthy individuals and explored the efficacy of combined exercise trening in restoring or improving endothelial function in those with PASC.

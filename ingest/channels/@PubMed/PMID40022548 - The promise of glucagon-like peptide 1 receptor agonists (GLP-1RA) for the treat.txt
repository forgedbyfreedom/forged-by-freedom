PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40022548
Title: The promise of glucagon-like peptide 1 receptor agonists (GLP-1RA) for the treatment of obesity: a look at phase 2 and 3 pipelines.
Authors: Madsbad, Holst
Journal: Expert opinion on investigational drugs
Year: 2025
Topic: retatrutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
GLP-1-based therapies have changed the treatment of overweight/obesity. Liraglutide 3.0&#x2009;mg daily, the first GLP-1 RA approved for treatment of overweight, induced a weight loss of 6-8%, Semaglutide 2.4&#x2009;mg once weekly improved weight loss to about 12-15%, while the dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist tirzepatide once weekly has induced a weight loss of about 20% in obese people without diabetes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40061473
Title: The impact of core trening combined with breathing exercises on individuals with chronic non-specific low back pain.
Authors: Li, Zhao, Zhang, E, Su
Journal: Frontiers in public health
Year: 2025
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
Chronic non-specific low back pain (CNLBP) is a common condition, defined as pain lasting more than 3&#x202f;months between the lower thoracic margin and gluteal folds, without identifiable tissue damage. Despite its low disability rate, the complex etiology and high recurrence impose significant health and socioeconomic burdens. According to European LBP guidelines, exercise therapy is the preferred treatment for CNLBP. This study evaluates the efficacy of core trening combined with breathing exercises as a therapeutic intervention for CNLBP.

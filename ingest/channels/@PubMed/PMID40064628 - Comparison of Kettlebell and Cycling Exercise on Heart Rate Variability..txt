PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40064628
Title: Comparison of Kettlebell and Cycling Exercise on Heart Rate Variability.
Authors: Paisal, Ahmad, Ansari, Verma, Singh
Journal: Annals of African medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: training frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Kettlebells, dating back to 1703 in Russia, offer a full-body workout for strength, endurance, and flexibility. They activate back and hip musculature, reduce low back injury risk, and improve postural coordination. Cycling induces muscle hypertrophy, guiding exercise prescription.

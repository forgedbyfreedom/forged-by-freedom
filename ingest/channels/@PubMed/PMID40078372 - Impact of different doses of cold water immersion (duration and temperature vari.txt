PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40078372
Title: Impact of different doses of cold water immersion (duration and temperature variations) on recovery from acute exercise-induced muscle damage: a network meta-analysis.
Authors: Wang, Wang, Pan
Journal: Frontiers in physiology
Year: 2025
Topic: cold water immersion recovery

ABSTRACT:
This network meta-analysis and systematic review evaluated the recovery impacts of varying cold water immersion (CWI) protocols on acute exercise-induced muscle damage.

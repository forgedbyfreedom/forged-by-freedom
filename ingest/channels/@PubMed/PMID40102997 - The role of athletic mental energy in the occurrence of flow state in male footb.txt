PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40102997
Title: The role of athletic mental energy in the occurrence of flow state in male football (soccer) players.
Authors: Yarayan, Batrakoulis, G&#xfc;ng&#xf6;r, Kurtipek, Keskin
Journal: BMC sports science, medicine &amp; rehabilitation
Year: 2025
Topic: flow state athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
This study examines the determinant role of athletic mental energy on the flow state in professional football (soccer) players. This research fills an important gap in the sports psychology literature by focusing on the interaction between mental energy and flow, a critical component of optimal performance.

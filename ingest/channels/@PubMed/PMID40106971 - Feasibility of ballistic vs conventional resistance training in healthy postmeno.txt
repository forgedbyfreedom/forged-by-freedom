PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40106971
Title: Feasibility of ballistic vs conventional resistance trening in healthy postmenopausal women: A three-arm parallel randomised controlled trial.
Authors: Marques, Caliskan, Brooke-Wavell, Folland
Journal: Maturitas
Year: 2025
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Power treninGHas gained attention as a method for enhancing functional performance and mitigating fall risk in older adults, yet its long-term feasibility and safety, particularly in ballistic resistance trening, remain underexplored in postmenopausal women. We evaluated the feasibility of 8-month ballistic resistance trening compared with conventional resistance trening in postmenopausal women.

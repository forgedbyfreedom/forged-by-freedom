PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40108888
Title: Efficacy of time restricted eating and resistance trening on body composition and mood profiles among young adults with overweight/obesity: a randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Cui, Sun, Ye, Liu, Korivi
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2025
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Dietary restriction or exercise regimens can promote weight loss or physical fitness among patients with obesity. However, intervention-associated adverse effects may impede patients' motivation to participate in dietary/exercise interventions. We examined the effects of time restricted eating (TRE) with or without resistance trening (RT) on body composition, mood profile, and sleep quality in young college adults with overweight or obesity.

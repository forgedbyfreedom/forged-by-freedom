PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40139900
Title: Mobile Health Biometrics to Enhance Exercise and Physical Activity Adherence in Type 2 Diabetes (MOTIVATE-T2D): a decentralised feasibility randomised controlled trial delivered across the UK and Canada.
Authors: Hesketh, Low, Andrews, Blitz, Buckley
Journal: BMJ open
Year: 2025
Topic: motivation exercise adherence

ABSTRACT:
Assess the feasibility of a mobile health (mHealth)-supported home-delivered physical activity (PA) intervention (MOTIVATE-T2D) in people with recently diagnosed type 2 diabetes (T2D).

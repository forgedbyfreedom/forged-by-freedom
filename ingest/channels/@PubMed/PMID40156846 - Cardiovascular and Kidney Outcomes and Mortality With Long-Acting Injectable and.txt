PMID: 40156846
Title: Cardiovascular and Kidney Outcomes and Mortality With Long-Acting Injectable and Oral Glucagon-Like Peptide 1 Receptor Agonists in Individuals With Type 2 Diabetes: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis of Randomized Trials.
Authors: Lee, Sattar, Pop-Busui, Deanfield, Emerson
Journal: Diabetes care
Year: 2025
Topic: semaglutide cardiovascular

ABSTRACT:
Glucagon-like peptide 1 receptor agonists (GLP-1RA) reduce the incidence of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) in type 2 diabetes (T2D), although whether benefits extend to both subcutaneous and oral formulations remains unclear.

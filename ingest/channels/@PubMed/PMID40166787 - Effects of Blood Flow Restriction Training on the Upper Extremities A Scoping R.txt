An extensive search on PubMed was performed using the term "blood flow restriction trening upper extremity," which resulted in 98 articles that were narrowed down to 17 based on clinical trials and relevance to upper extremities. Results from this study demonstrate that BFR trening enhances muscle hypertrophy and strength in the upper extremity muscles, including the biceps brachii and triceps brachii at low loads. Clinical applications include postoperative recovery, rehabilitation of rotator cuff injuries, and treatment of tendinopathies. Future research is necessary to address long-term effects, optimize protocols, and diversify its applications.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40167498
Title: Combined Physical Exercise in Pulmonary Rehabilitation Does Not Alter Endothelial Function and Vascular Structure in Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease: A Randomized Clinical Trial.
Authors: Nolasco, Figueiredo, Zanella, Porcher, Gass
Journal: Journal of cardiopulmonary rehabilitation and prevention
Year: 2025
Topic: endothelial function exercise

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this study was to analyze the effect of exercise on endothelial function and other cardiovascular risk factors in patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).

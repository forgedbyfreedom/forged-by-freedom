PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40241374
Title: Hypercaloric 16:8 time-restricted eating during 8 weeks of resistance exercise in well-trained men and women.
Authors: Blake, Hamane, Pacheco, Henselmans, Tinsley
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2025
Topic: intermittent fasting muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
The effectiveness and practicality of time-restricted eating (TRE) when trying to maximize muscle mass and strength is unclear. Thus, we examined the effects of a hypercaloric 16:8 TRE approach during supervised progressive resistance exercise.

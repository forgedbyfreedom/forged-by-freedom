PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40262556
Title: Assessing the risk of low energy availability, bone mineral density and psychological strain in endurance athletes.
Authors: Gowers, McManus, Chung, Jones, Tallent
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2025
Topic: bone health female athletes

ABSTRACT:
Adequate energy intake is crucial for athletic performance and recovery. However, many endurance athletes experience Low Energy Availability (LEA), which, if prolonged, can detrimentally impact both health and performance.

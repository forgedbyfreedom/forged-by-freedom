PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40271888
Title: Effects of exercise on cognition and Alzheimer's biomarkers in a randomized controlled trial of adults with mild cognitive impairment: The EXERT study.
Authors: Baker, Pa, Katula, Aslanyan, Salmon
Journal: Alzheimer's &amp; dementia : the journal of the Alzheimer's Association
Year: 2025
Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
The EXERT study (Exercise in Adults with Mild Memory Problems) was a Phase 3, multicenter, randomized controlled trial that examined effects of exercise on cognition and other measures of brain health in sedentary older adults with amnestic mild cognitive impairment (MCI).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40311262
Title: The effects of exercise therapy on lumbar muscle structure in low back pain: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Karagiannopoulou, Meirezonne, De Greef, Van Oosterwijck, Matheve
Journal: Annals of physical and rehabilitation medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: low back pain exercise therapy

ABSTRACT:
Changes in muscle structure are observed in people with low back pain (LBP). Although exercise therapy is arguably one of the most commonly used methods to treat LBP, evidence regarding its effects on muscle structure is still lacking.

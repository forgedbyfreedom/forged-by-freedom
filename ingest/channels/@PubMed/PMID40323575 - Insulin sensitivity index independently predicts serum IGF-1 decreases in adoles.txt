PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40323575
Title: insulin sensitivity index independently predicts serum IGF-1 decreases in adolescents with long-term exercise.
Authors: Guo, Qiang, Zhou, Sun, Wang
Journal: Journal of endocrinological investigation
Year: 2025
Topic: insulin sensitivity exercise training

ABSTRACT:
The study aims to investigate associations between insulin sensitivity and insulin-like growth factor-1 (IGF-1) in adolescents engaged in long-term exercise training, and to assess the impact on growth.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40325703
Title: Comparative evaluation of recovery interventions-individually and in combination-on lactate clearance and physical performance metrics following 3 versus 3 basketball matches.
Authors: Maan, Yadav
Journal: Journal of bodywork and movement therapies
Year: 2025
Topic: foam rolling muscle recovery

ABSTRACT:
The study evaluates the effects of active recovery, massage therapy, and foam rolling-both individually and combined-on recovery after 3v3 basketball matches, focusing on lactate levels, sprint performance, explosive strength, and agility to identify the most effective recovery strategy.

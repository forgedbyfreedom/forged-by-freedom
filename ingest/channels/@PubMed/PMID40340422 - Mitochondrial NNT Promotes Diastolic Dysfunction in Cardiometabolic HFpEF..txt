PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40340422
Title: Mitochondrial NNT Promotes Diastolic Dysfunction in Cardiometabolic HFpEF.
Authors: Pepin, Konrad, Nazir, Bazgir, Maack
Journal: Circulation research
Year: 2025
Topic: fat loss preservation muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Clinical management of heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) is hindered by a lack of disease-modifying therapies capable of altering its distinct pathophysiology. Despite the widespread implementation of a 2-hit model of cardiometabolic HFpEF to inform precision therapy, which utilizes HFD+L-NAME (ad libitum high-fat diet and 0.5% N[&#x3c9;]-nitro-L-arginine methyl ester), we observe that C57BL6/J mice exhibit less cardiac diastolic dysfunction in response to HFD+L-NAME.

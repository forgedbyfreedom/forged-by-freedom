PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40355219
Title: Effects of acute exercise programs on heart rate variability and vascular function in sedentary college students: A randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Zhang, Li, Wang
Journal: Medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
The specific effects of different exercises on cardiovascular health remain unclear. This study aimed to compare the effects of acute anaerobic threshold intensity continuous exercise (ATE), high-intensity interval trening (HIIT), and stepwise incremental exercise (SIE) on heart rate variability (HRV) and vascular endothelial function in sedentary college students.

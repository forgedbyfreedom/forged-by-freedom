PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40360152
Title: Use of Heart-Rate Variability to Examine Readiness to Perform in Response to Overload and Taper in Swimmers.
Authors: Bulte, Bruce, Hammond, Corrigan, Main
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2025
Topic: tapering athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
&#xa0;The lack of consensus on the best approach to assess the fatigue and readiness status of swimmers has led to an increased risk of maladaptation, overtraining, and suboptimal readiness to perform. Subjective athlete monitoring is prominent in swimming but is prone to bias, distortion, and misinterpretation. Heart-rate variability (HRV) has gained attention from swimming practitioners as an objective monitoring tool for assessing physiological status.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40372752
Title: Combining Exercise Training and Testosterone Therapy in Older Women After Hip Fracture: The STEP-HI Randomized Clinical Trial.
Authors: Binder, Bartley, Berry, Dor&#xe9;, Fisher
Journal: JAMA network open
Year: 2025
Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Despite receiving postacute rehabilitation services, many older women with a recent hip fracture repair are unable to return to their prefracture level of function. Whether testosterone therapy can enhance recovery after hip fracture in older women with persistent mobility impairments has not been fully examined.

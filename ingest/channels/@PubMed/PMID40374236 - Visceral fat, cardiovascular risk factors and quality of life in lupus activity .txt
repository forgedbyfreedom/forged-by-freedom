PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40374236
Title: Visceral fat, cardiovascular risk factors and quality of life in lupus activity categorised via complement C3.
Authors: Mart&#xed;nez-Urbistondo, Higuera-G&#xf3;mez, de Cuevillas, Cuevas-Sierra, Mellor-Pita
Journal: Lupus science &amp; medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
Patients with lupus face increased cardiovascular risk linked to their autoimmune status. This study assesses the relationships between cardiovascular risk factors, lifestyle and health-related quality of life (HRQoL) concerning SLE activity categorised by complement C3.

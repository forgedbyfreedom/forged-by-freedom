PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40375353
Title: Intermediary role of mental toughness beliefs on the relationship between pain self-efficacy and fear avoidance in Elite injured athletes.
Authors: Tawil, Kassis, Bassil, Farhat, Hourani
Journal: BMC sports science, medicine &amp; rehabilitation
Year: 2025
Topic: mental toughness athletes

ABSTRACT:
Athletes adopt different coping mechanisms with injury. Differences and links between fear avoidance (FA), pain self-efficacy (PSE), and mental toughness (MT) might affect injury outcomes.

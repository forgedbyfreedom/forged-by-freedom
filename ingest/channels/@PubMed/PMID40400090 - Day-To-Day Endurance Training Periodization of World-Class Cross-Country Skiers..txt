PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40400090
Title: Day-To-Day Endurance trening Periodization of World-Class Cross-Country Skiers.
Authors: Walther, Kocbach, Sandbakk
Journal: European journal of sport science
Year: 2025
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to investigate the day-to-day endurance trening periodization of male and female world-class cross-country skiers. Self-reported session-by-session trening data from 17 (7 female) world-class cross-country skiers were retrospectively analyzed. The data were separated into general preparation (GP) and competition periods (CP), during which all skiers achieved world-class performance. Daily trening volume was categorized as very low (0%-50% of mean daily volume), low (50%-100%), moderate (100%-150%), and high (&#x2265;&#xa0;150%). trening load (trening Impulse, TRIMP) was calculated as the product of duration and an intensity factor of 1, 2, or 3 for low-, moderate-, and high-intensity trening, respectively. trening volume was distributed as 18%/22%/32%/28% across very low/low/moderate/high volume days during GP, whereas the corresponding distribution during CP was 23%/35%/32%/10%. Shifts in daily TRIMP from GP to CP mirrored changes in trening volume. During GP, the acute-to-chronic work ratio (ACWR) was 0.75-1.25 on 69% of the days, with 13% of the days showing lower ratios and 18% higher. During CP, the corresponding values were 69%/19%/12%. Intensive sessions occurred every 3.6&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;0.3&#xa0;days during GP compared to every 3.0&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;0.3&#xa0;days during CP. trening volumes on the day before intensive sessions were 127&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;13% (GP) and 99&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;7% (CP) of the daily mean, whereas the corresponding values for the days after were 116&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;8% and 87&#xa0;&#xb1;&#xa0;9%. These data provide new insights into day-to-day periodization in world-class cross-country skiers, highlighting the sequential application of trening loads by alternating trening days of high volume, elevated intensity every 3-4&#xa0;days, or reduced trening load or rest every 8-9&#xa0;days, while only 5% combining volume and intensity.

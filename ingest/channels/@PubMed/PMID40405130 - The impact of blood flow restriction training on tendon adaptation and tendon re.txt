PMID: 40405130
Title: The impact of blood flow restriction trening on tendon adaptation and tendon rehabilitation - a scoping review.
Authors: &#xd6;berg, von Schewelov, Tengman
Journal: BMC musculoskeletal disorders
Year: 2025
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Tendon injuries are common in athletes and in the general population and require extensive rehabilitation. Current conservative treatment often includes different high-load resistance trening (HLRT) modalities. However, certain populations may not tolerate HLRT well. Blood flow restriction trening (BFRT) incorporates low load while achievinGHypertrophy and strength adaptations comparable to HLRT. However, the effects of BFRT on healthy and pathological tendons are unknown. The aims of this scoping review were therefore to summarize the reported impact of BRFT: (1) on tendon adaptation in healthy individuals, and (2) in tendon rehabilitation after injury.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40405489
Title: Comparison of concurrent, resistance, or aerobic training on body fat loss: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Lafontant, Rukstela, Hanson, Chan, Alsayed
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2025
Topic: concurrent training interference

ABSTRACT:
This systematic review and meta-analysis compared the differential effects of resistance training (RT), aerobic training (AT), and concurrent training (CT) on body mass and body fat loss in metabolically healthy individuals.

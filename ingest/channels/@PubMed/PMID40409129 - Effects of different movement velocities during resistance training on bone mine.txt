PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40409129
Title: Effects of different movement velocities during resistance trening on bone mineral density in older adults: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Quattlaender, Rothmore, Hutchinson, Lathlean
Journal: Archives of gerontology and geriatrics
Year: 2025
Topic: osteoporosis resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the effects of different movement velocities during progressive resistance trening (PRT) on bone mineral density (BMD) in older adults, with an emphasis on program design and trening principles.

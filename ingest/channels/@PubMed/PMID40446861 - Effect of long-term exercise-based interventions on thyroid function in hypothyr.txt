PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40446861
Title: Effect of long-term exercise-based interventions on thyroid function in hypothyroidism: A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.
Authors: Sundus, Khan, Zaidi, Chhabra, Ahmad
Journal: Complementary therapies in medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: thyroid hormone metabolism exercise

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to assess the effects of exercise interventions of more than 8 weeks on thyroid function in adults with hypothyroidism through a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40450548
Title: Pep19: A Novel Approach for Reducing Visceral Fat and Improving Sleep Quality in Obese Adults-Results From an Early-Stage Clinical Trial.
Authors: Heimann, Singh, Ferro, Greenway, Krongrad
Journal: Diabetes/metabolism research and reviews
Year: 2025
Topic: body composition assessment methods

ABSTRACT:
Conformational-sensitive antibodies were used to identify the orally active peptide DIIADDEPLT (Pep19) as an inverse agonist of cannabinoid type 1 receptor. Pep19 safely improved metabolic parameters in murine models of diet-induced obesity, and in healthy dogs.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40454499
Title: Evaluating the Impact of SGLT-2 Inhibitors on Frailty in Older Adults: A Clinical Review.
Authors: Al Rashid, Gopalakrishnan, Maideen, Almalki
Journal: Current drug safety
Year: 2025
Topic: fat loss preservation muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Sodium-glucose cotransporter-2 (SGLT-2) inhibitors, including empagliflozin, canagliflozin, and dapagliflozin, have demonstrated significant cardiovascular and renal benefits in managing type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM). However, their impact on frailty in older adults remains a subject of debate, given their associations with weight loss, fluid depletion, and potential reductions in muscle mass, which could contribute to sarcopenia and frailty-related complications.

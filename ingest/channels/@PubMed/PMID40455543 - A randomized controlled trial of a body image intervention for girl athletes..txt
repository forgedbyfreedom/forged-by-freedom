PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40455543
Title: A randomized controlled trial of a body image intervention for girl athletes.
Authors: Matheson, Schneider, Tinoco, White, Toher
Journal: Health psychology : official journal of the Division of Health Psychology, American Psychological Association
Year: 2025
Topic: body image athletes

ABSTRACT:
A two-arm cluster randomized controlled trial evaluated the impact of Body Confident Athletes (BCA) on girls' (N = 568, 11-17 years) body image, sports enjoyment, and affect.

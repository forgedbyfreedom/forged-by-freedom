PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40455704
Title: Effects of a forefoot strengthening protocol on explosive tasks performance and propulsion kinetics in athletes: aromasingle-blind randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Tourillon, Fourchet, Edouard, Morin
Journal: PloS one
Year: 2025
Topic: periodization trening athletes

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the effects of an 8-week "periodized high-load" forefoot strengthening protocol on athlete's metatarsophalangeal joints (MTPj) flexion torque, MTPj flexors volume, sprint acceleration, cutting, and jumping overall performance and kinetics.

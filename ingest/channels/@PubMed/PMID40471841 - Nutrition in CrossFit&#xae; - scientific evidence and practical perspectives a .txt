PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40471841
Title: Nutrition in CrossFit&#xae; - scientific evidence and practical perspectives: a systematic scoping review.
Authors: Martinho, Rebelo, Clemente, Costa, Gouveia
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2025
Topic: caffeine athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
CrossFit&#x24c7; sessions and competitions are characterized by high-intensity challenges that combine aerobic and anaerobic activities with short recovery periods. As a result, effective nutritional practices play a crucial role in optimizing performance and enhancing recovery. Therefore, nutritional practices are central to optimizing performance and accelerating recovery. This review aims to summarize current evidence on nutritional and ergogenic aid responses to CrossFit&#x24c7; practice.

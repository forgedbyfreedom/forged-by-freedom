PMID: 40483141
Title: Effects of acute aerobic and resistance exercise execution order on glycemia, blood pressure, and heart rate variability in postmenopausal women with type 2 diabetes mellitus: A randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Garc&#xea;s, Silva, Sisconeto, Mariano, Cunha
Journal: Journal of bodywork and movement therapies
Year: 2025
Topic: estrogen exercise women

ABSTRACT:
Postmenopausal women with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM) face heightened cardiometabolic risks due to the combined effects of estrogen decline and metabolic dysfunction. While aerobic and resistance exercises are effective in treating and preventing these complications, the optimal order of exercise in a combined session remains unclear for this vulnerable population. This study compared the execution order of a combined exercise session on glycemia, blood pressure (BP), and heart rate variability (HRV) in postmenopausal women with T2DM.

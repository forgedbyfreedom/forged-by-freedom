PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40507192
Title: Health Claims for Protein Food Supplements for Athletes-The Analysis Is in Accordance with the EFSA's Scientific Opinion.
Authors: Rodr&#xed;guez-Hern&#xe1;ndez, Mart&#xed;nez-Sanz, Garc&#xed;a, Gabald&#xf3;n, Ferreres
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2025
Topic: post-workout nutrition recovery

ABSTRACT:
Protein supplements are among the most popular, available and growing complementary products. Fraud related to the mislabeling, inaccurate analysis or declaration of ingredient quantities, and health claims not aligned with those approved by EFSA is high. This study aims to analyze the claims related to protein supplements in commercial messages.

PMID: 40513717
Title: Practical recommendations on stretching exercise: A Delphi consensus statement of international research experts.
Authors: Warneke, Thomas, Blazevich, Afonso, Behm
Journal: Journal of sport and health science
Year: 2025
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
StretchinGHas wide appeal, but there seems to exist some mismatch between its purported applications and what the evidence shows. There is compelling evidence for some stretching applications, but for others, the evidence seems heterogeneous or unsupportive. The discrepancies even affect some systematic reviews, possibly due to heterogeneous eligibility criteria and search strategies. This consensus paper seeks to unify the divergent findings on stretching and its implications for both athletic performance and clinical practices by delivering evidence-based recommendations.

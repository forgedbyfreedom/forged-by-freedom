PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40514618
Title: Impact of oral contraceptive use on muscle mass and strength in women with PCOS.
Authors: Aksun, Kara&#xe7;oban, Idilman, Yildiz
Journal: Endocrine
Year: 2025
Topic: testosterone muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) is characterized by androgen excess and ovulatory dysfunction and appears to be associated with alterations in muscle mass and function. The study aims to investigate whether oral contraceptive (OC) use affects muscle mass and strength in women with PCOS.

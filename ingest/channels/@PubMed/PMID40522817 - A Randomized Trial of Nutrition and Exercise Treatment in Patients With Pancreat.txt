PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40522817
Title: A Randomized Trial of Nutrition and Exercise Treatment in Patients With Pancreatic and Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NEXTAC-TWO).
Authors: Mitsunaga, Naito, Imai, Kimura, Miura
Journal: Journal of cachexia, sarcopenia and muscle
Year: 2025
Topic: exercise performance nutrition

ABSTRACT:
In our previous study (NEXTAC-ONE), the Nutrition and Exercise Treatment for Advanced Cancer (NEXTAC) program (includinGHome-based exercise and branched-chain amino acid-containing supplements combined with nutritional counselling) was shown to potentially prevent low muscle mass-related disability in elderly cancer patients. This randomized controlled trial (NEXTAC-TWO) was conducted to elucidate whether the NEXTAC program prolongs disability-free survival in elderly patients with advanced pancreatic or non-small cell lung cancer.

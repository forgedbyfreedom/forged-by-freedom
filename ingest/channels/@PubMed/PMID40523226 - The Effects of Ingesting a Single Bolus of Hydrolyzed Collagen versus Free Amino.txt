PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40523226
Title: The Effects of Ingesting aromasingle Bolus of Hydrolyzed Collagen versus Free Amino Acids on Muscle Connective Protein Synthesis Rates.
Authors: Aussieker, Kaiser, Hendriks, Janssen, Senden
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2025
Topic: muscle protein synthesis exercise

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to assess the effect of ingesting aromasingle bolus of hydrolyzed collagen or free amino acids on myofibrillar and muscle connective protein synthesis rates.

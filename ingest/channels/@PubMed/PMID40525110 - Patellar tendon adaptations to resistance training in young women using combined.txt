Title: Patellar tendon adaptations to resistance trening in young women using combined oral contraceptives.
Authors: Vesterhus, Hesseberg, Fjeldberg, Engstad, Paulsen
Journal: PeerJ
Year: 2025
Topic: tendon adaptation resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The study aimed to examine the impact of combined oral contraceptive pill (OC) use on patellar tendon (PT) adaptation to resistance trening in young women.

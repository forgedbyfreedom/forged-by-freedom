PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40539259
Title: Neither pre-sleep nor post-exercise protein consumption influences resistance exercise trening adaptations in older adults.
Authors: Klemp, Ormsbee, Yeh, Sokolowski, Kim
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2025
Topic: longevity exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Limited data exists that compare pre-sleep versus post-exercise protein intake during resistance exercise trening (RET) in older adults. This study examined whether 40&#x2009;g of protein consumed post-exercise (PRP) or pre-sleep (PSP) enhances muscle thickness (MT) and strength compared to RET alone (RETO) in older men.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40540267
Title: Weighted Vest Use or Resistance Exercise to Offset Weight Loss-Associated Bone Loss in Older Adults: A Randomized Clinical Trial.
Authors: Beavers, Lynch, Fanning, Howard, Lawrence
Journal: JAMA network open
Year: 2025
Topic: obesity exercise weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Weight loss (WL) in older adults is associated with bone loss, increasing the risk of fracture. Because skeletal tissue is responsive to mechanical stress, replacing lost weight externally may be an innolvative way to minimize WL-associated bone loss in this population.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40545827
Title: Tirzepatide for reduction of morbidity and mortality in adults with obesity: rationale and design of the SURMOUNT-MMO trial.
Authors: Lam, Rodriguez, Aminian, Ferrannini, Heerspink
Journal: Obesity (Silver Spring, Md.)
Year: 2025
Topic: tirzepatide obesity

ABSTRACT:
Obesity is a major cause of morbidity and mortality worldwide. Tirzepatide is a glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide receptor and glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist providing substantial weight reduction and metabolic benefits both in type 2 diabetes and obesity. We hypothesized that tirzepatide can improve morbidity and mortality in adults with obesity or overweight but without diabetes.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40559702
Title: Melatonin Supplementation Enhances Next-Day High-Intensity Exercise Performance and Recovery in Trained Males: A Placebo-Controlled Crossover Study.
Authors: Mahdi, Delleli, Jebabli, Ben Maaoui, Del Coso
Journal: Sports (Basel, Switzerland)
Year: 2025
Topic: melatonin sleep athletes

ABSTRACT:
Sleep and recovery are critical for optimising exercise performance. However, the efficacy of melatonin supplementation in improving sleep quality and next-day physical performance remains unclear. This study examined the effects of melatonin ingestion on sleep and performance-related outcomes the following day in trained males.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40571191
Title: GLP-1 receptor agonists, body composition, skeletal muscle and risk of sarcopaenia: from promising findings in animal models to debated concern in human studies.
Authors: Scheen
Journal: Diabetes &amp; metabolism
Year: 2025
Topic: fat loss preservation muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
- Glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1)-based therapies induce a clinically relevant weight loss, which is associated with overall better prognosis in people with type 2 diabetes and/or clinical obesity. However, a risk of excessive reduction in fat-free mass (FFM) and skeletal muscle mass (SSM), potentially leading to sarcopaenia in at-risk patients, is currently a matter of debate as this negative effect could minimize their benefit/risk balance.

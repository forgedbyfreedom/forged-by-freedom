PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40578025
Title: Exercise-induced mitochondrial protection in skeletal muscle of ovariectomized mice: A myogenic E2 synthesis-independent mechanism.
Authors: Tian, Hu, Li, Yu, Li
Journal: Redox biology
Year: 2025
Topic: mitochondrial function exercise aging

ABSTRACT:
Skeletal muscle, a 17&#x3b2;-estradiol (E2)-sensitive tissue, is prone to accelerated aging due to postmenopausal E2 deficiency and subsequent mitochondrial dysfunction. While exogenous E2 treatment has been shown to protect against mitochondrial damage in ovariectomized rodents, the impact of exercise-induced local E2 production in skeletal muscle on mitochondrial function remains to be determined. This study investigated exercise-mediated mitochondrial protection in ovariectomized mice and the contribution of myogenic E2.

PMID: 40580396
Title: Compared to total serum testosterone, calculated free Testosterone has a stronger association with lean mass, muscle strength, power, and physical function in older men.
Authors: Lolck, Alcazar, Kamper, Haddock, Hovind
Journal: Aging clinical and experimental research
Year: 2025
Topic: testosterone muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Low serum testosterone concentrations have been associated with low muscle mass and strength in older men. However, the existing literature is inconclusive.

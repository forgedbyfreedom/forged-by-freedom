PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40584822
Title: Nutritional deficiencies and muscle loss in adults with type 2 diabetes using GLP-1 receptor agonists: A retrospective observational study.
Authors: Scott Butsch, Sulo, Chang, Kim, Kerr
Journal: Obesity pillars
Year: 2025
Topic: GLP-1 agonist muscle

ABSTRACT:
Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1RAs) drug-induced weight loss is associated with fat mass reduction but can also lead to nutritional deficiencies and loss of muscle. We quantified nutritional deficiencies in adults who had undergone GLP-1RA treatment.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40609566
Title: Effects of retatrutide on body composition in people with type 2 diabetes: a substudy of a phase 2, double-blind, parallel-group, placebo-controlled, randomised trial.
Authors: Coskun, Wu, Schloot, Haupt, Milicevic
Journal: The lancet. Diabetes &amp; endocrinology
Year: 2025
Topic: retatrutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
Retatrutide, a glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide, glucagon-like peptide-1, and glucagon receptor agonist, has demonstrated robust glucose and bodyweight reductions in participants with type 2 diabetes. This substudy assessed percent change from baseline to week 36 in total body fat mass versus placebo and dulaglutide.

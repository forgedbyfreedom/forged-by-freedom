PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40610191
Title: Resistance training in pregnancy: systematic review and meta-analysis of pregnancy, delivery, fetal and pelvic floor outcomes and call to action.
Authors: Prevett, Gingerich, Sivak, Davenport
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: pregnancy exercise guidelines

ABSTRACT:
Resistance training (RT) has wide-ranging health benefits that may extend to pregnancy. This systematic review aimed to evaluate the influence of RT as a sole intervention or as part of a multicomponent exercise programme on pregnancy, delivery, fetal and pelvic floor outcomes.

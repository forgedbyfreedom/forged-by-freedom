PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40611350
Title: Effect of teacher autonomy support on student engagement in physical education classrooms in a blended learning environment: the mediating role of performance expectancy and academic self-efficacy.
Authors: Li, Zeng
Journal: BMC psychology
Year: 2025
Topic: self efficacy sports performance

ABSTRACT:
With the rapid rise of online learning in higher education, blened learning models have become increasingly common. In this context, teacher autonomy-support strategies are widely used to help students engage more effectively and improve learning outcomes in environments that combine online and offline learning. While this approach offers new educational value and experiences, there has been limited focus on how autonomy-supportive teaching strategies enhance student engagement in physical education classes within blened learning environments.

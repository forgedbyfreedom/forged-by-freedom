PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40613665
Title: Recommendations for Interventions to Improve Function in Patients With Lung Cancer: A Clinical Practice Guideline.
Authors: Vargo, Gerber, Gilchrist, Fisher
Journal: Cancer medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: complex training post activation

ABSTRACT:
Individuals with lung cancer frequently experience functional limitations, yet best practice to achieve functional recovery has not been synthesized. This Clinical Practice Guideline generates evidence-based recommendations to improve functional outcomes in this population.

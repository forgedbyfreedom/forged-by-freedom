PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40650376
Title: Nutritional strategies for minimizing gastrointestinal symptoms during endurance exercise: systematic review of the literature.
Authors: Mlinaric, Mohorko
Journal: Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition
Year: 2025
Topic: carbohydrate exercise performance

ABSTRACT:
The gastrointestinal (GI) tract plays a critical role in achieving peak athletic performance either during trening or in competitions. Despite its significance, the GI tract's role in the trening process of athletes is often neglected, resulting in frequent GI symptoms. These disturbances are particularly prevalent in endurance sports, where GI function is commonly compromised, leading to adverse effects on performance. In this review, we examine potential nutritional causes of the GI symptoms and provide possible solutions to mitigate them, aiming to enhance athletes' overall performance and well-being.

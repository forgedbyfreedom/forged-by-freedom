PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40655404
Title: The relationship between abdominal fat and sleep quality after combined exercise in patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus.
Authors: Han, Han, Huang, Zou, Gu
Journal: Frontiers in endocrinology
Year: 2025
Topic: type 2 diabetes exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Exercise holds promise as a non-pharmacological intervention for improving sleep quality. Therefore, this study investigated the potential relationship between abdominal fat and sleep quality after combined exercise in patients with T2DM (Type 2 diabetes mellitus).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40665827
Title: Disentangling the biological mechanisms underlying the effects of physical exercise in major depressive disorder: a comprehensive systematic review of randomized controlled trials.
Authors: Carvalho Silva, Meattini, Perusi, Carletto, Bortolomasi
Journal: Psychological medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: cortisol exercise stress

ABSTRACT:
Major depressive disorder (MDD) is a disabling psychiatric condition in which physical activity provides clinical benefits. While exercise effectively alleviates depressive symptoms, its biological mechanisms remain unclear.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40673785
Title: Effects of daily protein intake frequency during 8 weeks of resistance training on lean mass and strength adaptations: a randomized non-controlled clinical trial.
Authors: Tavares, Roschel, Fel&#xed;cio, Nobrega, Hevia-Larra&#xed;n
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2025
Topic: protein distribution muscle synthesis

ABSTRACT:
The optimal protein intake distribution per meal for maximizing muscle mass and strength gains remains debated in current literature. This study investigated the outcomes of different protein distributions on muscle strength and body composition in healthy, young, resistance-trained men.

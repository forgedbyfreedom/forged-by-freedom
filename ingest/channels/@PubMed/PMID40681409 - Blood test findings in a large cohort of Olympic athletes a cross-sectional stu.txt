PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40681409
Title: Blood test findings in a large cohort of Olympic athletes: a cross-sectional study.
Authors: Ferrera, Di Gioia, Spinelli, Fiore, Zampaglione
Journal: Journal of science and medicine in sport
Year: 2025
Topic: iron deficiency athletes performance

ABSTRACT:
Olympic athletes are often considered the pinnacle of health and performance. However, routine blood tests may reveal biochemical abnormalities. Understanding these abnormalities is crucial for optimizing athlete health and performance.

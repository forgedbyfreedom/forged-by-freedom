PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40741681
Title: Meeting exercise guidelines during pregnancy associates with less lumbopelvic disability and fear avoidance.
Authors: McCarthy, Simonds, Velez, Spaeth
Journal: Women's health (London, England)
Year: 2025
Topic: pregnancy exercise guidelines

ABSTRACT:
Sixty-three percent of pregnant persons report experiencing lumbopelvic pain (LPP), which can negatively impact quality of life and lead to the development of fear avoidance behaviors. Exercise is a safe, non-pharmacological treatment for reducing and preventing the development of pain, but the volume of exercise required for one to benefit from it is unknown.

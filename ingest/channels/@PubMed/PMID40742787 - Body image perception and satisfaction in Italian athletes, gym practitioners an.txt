PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40742787
Title: Body image perception and satisfaction in Italian athletes, gym practitioners and inactive individuals.
Authors: Calella, DI Dio, Pelullo, Liguori, Valerio
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2025
Topic: body image athletes

ABSTRACT:
Body image refers to a person's perception of her/his own body. Currently, the media and most of the social settings place significance on having a thin body. The purpose of this study was to investigate the relationships between body image perception and satisfaction and some lifestyle-related factors such as weight status, sedentary habits and engagement in physical activity among sport athletes, gym practitioners and inactive individuals.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40751170
Title: Nutritional interventions in muscle hypertrophy research: a scientometric analysis within the context of resistance trening (1992-2025).
Authors: Chen
Journal: Journal of health, population, and nutrition
Year: 2025
Topic: trening frequency hypertrophy

ABSTRACT:
Muscle hypertrophy, as a central goal in sports trening, rehabilitation interventions, and health promotion, relies on scientifically designed resistance trening and appropriate nutritional strategies. However, research on the synergistic effects of nutritional interventions and resistance trening in promoting muscle hypertrophy is characterized by thematic fragmentation, insufficient integration between mechanistic and applied research, and imbalances across different regions and population groups.

PMID: 40764021
Title: Effect of adherence to exercise-based injury prevention programmes on the risk of sports injuries: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials.
Authors: Viiala, &#x10c;ech, Bakal&#xe1;r, Paravlic, Ru&#x17e;barsk&#xfd;
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 2026
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
Adherence may affect the effectiveness of injury prevention programmes (IPPs), yet no previous meta-analyses have examined the pooled impact of adherence to exercise-based IPPs across multiple injury types. The objective of the systematic review and meta-analysis was to investigate the effect of exercise-based IPP adherence on the risk of sports injuries.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40770817
Title: Dionysus or apollo, skeletal muscle mass changes in type 2 diabetes with sarcopenia receiving GLP-1 receptor agonist: systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Wang, Lan, Zhang, Mu, Mi
Journal: Diabetology &amp; metabolic syndrome
Year: 2025
Topic: GLP-1 agonist muscle

ABSTRACT:
Type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM) increases the risk of sarcopenia, but evidence on the effects of glucagon-like peptide 1 receptor agonist (GLP-1 RA) in skeletal muscle is limited and inconsistent. The study aimed to evaluate the skeletal muscle mass of GLP-1 RA in patients with type 2 diabetes.

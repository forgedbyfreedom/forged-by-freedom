PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40780269
Title: Does Timing of Oral Contraceptive Pill Intake Affect Physical Performance-Related Parameters? A Randomized Crossover-Controlled Study.
Authors: Bisgaard, Josefsen, Rasmussen, Hansen
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2025
Topic: oral contraceptives athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to investigate whether maximal oxygen uptake, strength, and power parameters are influenced by the timing of oral contraceptive (OC) pill intake-specifically 1 versus 24&#xa0;hours before testing.

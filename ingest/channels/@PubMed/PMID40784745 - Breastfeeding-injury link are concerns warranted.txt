PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40784745
Title: Breastfeeding-injury link: are concerns warranted?
Authors: Jones, Brockwell, Hassan, Dufour, Davenport
Journal: British journal of sports medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: postpartum return exercise

ABSTRACT:
Recreational and elite athletes report avoiding breastfeeding when returning to physical activity following childbirth due to a perceived increased risk of injury; yet, there are no empirical data to support this relationship. We aimed to examine the complex interaction of breastfeeding and physical activity and its potential impact on musculoskeletal injury.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40789979
Title: Regeneration or Risk? A Narrative Review of BPC-157 for Musculoskeletal Healing.
Authors: McGuire, Martinez, Lenz, Skinner, Cushman
Journal: Current reviews in musculoskeletal medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: BPC-157 healing

ABSTRACT:
This scoping review aims to evaluate the molecular mechanisms, therapeutic potential, and safety concerns of Body Protective Compound-157 (BPC-157) in the context of musculoskeletal healing. Given the compound&#x2019;s increasing availability, popularity, and its regulatory controversies, we sought to assess the breadth and quality of preclinical and clinical data supporting its use in musculoskeletal medicine.

PMID: 40796095
Title: High-Protein Time-Restricted Eating Alongside Resistance trening Reduces Adipose Tissue While Preserving Fat-Free Mass in Women With Overweight: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Bagherpour, Arazi, Rajabi, Campbell
Journal: International journal of sport nutrition and exercise metabolism
Year: 2025
Topic: leptin ghrelin appetite exercise

ABSTRACT:
Time-restricted eating (TRE), a subtype of intermittent fasting, has gained significant popularity in recent years. This dietary approach, when combined with resistance trening (RT), has demonstrated potential benefits for body composition and physiological parameters.

========================
PMID: 40797311
Title: The impact of chronic low back pain and motor control impairment on athletic performance in elite soccer players: a cross-sectional study.
Authors: Akku&#x15f;, Demirel
Journal: BMC musculoskeletal disorders
Year: 2025
Topic: visualization athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Chronic low back pain (CLBP) and motor control impairment (MCI) are common among soccer players, yet their direct impact on key athletic performance metrics remains unclear. Understanding these relationships is critical for optimizing training, rehabilitation, and injury prevention strategies in elite athletes. Thus, this study aimed to evaluate CLBP and MCI's impact on elite soccer players' athletic performance.

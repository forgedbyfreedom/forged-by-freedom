PMID: 40808964
Title: Comprehensive analysis of regulatory B Cell related genes in prognosis and therapeutic response in lung adenocarcinoma.
Authors: Zhang, Zeng, Zhang, Zhang, Lin
Journal: Frontiers in immunology
Year: 2025
Topic: cluster sets resistance training

ABSTRACT:
Regulatory B cells (Bregs) are a distinct subset of B cells that play a crucial role in regulating immune responses and maintaining immune tolerance in cancerous environments. However, their function in lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) remains largely underexplored. This study seeks to investigate the roles of Breg-associated genes in the context of LUAD.

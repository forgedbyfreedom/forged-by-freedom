PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40823251
Title: Impact of visceral adiposity index on cognitive impairment and cognitive trajectories in Chinese middle-aged and older adults.
Authors: Chen, Zhou, Han, Ma, Jiang
Journal: Frontiers in public health
Year: 2025
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
The association between the visceral adiposity index (VAI) and cognitive function in middle-aged and older Chinese adults is not well understood. The "obesity paradox"-where obesity appears to be linked with better health outcomes-has also been observed. This study aims to clarify the association by using data from a nationally representative longitudinal survey.

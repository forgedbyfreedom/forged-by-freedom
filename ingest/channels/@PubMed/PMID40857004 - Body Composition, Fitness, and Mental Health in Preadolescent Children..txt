PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40857004
Title: Body Composition, Fitness, and Mental Health in Preadolescent Children.
Authors: Braun, Khan, Hillman, Raine
Journal: JAMA network open
Year: 2025
Topic: sports performance anxiety

ABSTRACT:
There is an increasing prevalence of poor fitness, concurrent with rising obesity, anxiety, and depression among children in the US. However, the association of fitness, adiposity, and lean tissue with anxiety and depression in preadolescent children is understudied.

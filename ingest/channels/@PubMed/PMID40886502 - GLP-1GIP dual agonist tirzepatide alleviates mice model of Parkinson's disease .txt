PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40886502
Title: GLP-1/GIP dual agonist tirzepatide alleviates mice model of Parkinson's disease by promoting mitochondrial homeostasis.
Authors: Tian, Liu, Lai, Liao, Li
Journal: International immunopharmacology
Year: 2025
Topic: GIP GLP-1 dual agonist

ABSTRACT:
The prevailing treatment of Parkinson's disease (PD) is not yet satisfactory. The present investigate the neuroprotective effect of the GLP-1/GIP dual agonist tirzepatide and examine the potential mechanisms involved.

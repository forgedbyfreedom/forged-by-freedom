PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40916752
Title: Appetite, eating attitudes, and eating behaviours during treatment with retatrutide in adults with type 2 diabetes: Results of a phase 2 study.
Authors: Kanu, Boye, Poon, Goetz, Williamson
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2025
Topic: retatrutide weight loss

ABSTRACT:
To determine whether adults with type 2 diabetes (T2D) treated with retatrutide report greater changes in self-reported appetite, dietary restraint, and disinhibition compared to placebo or dulaglutide and to examine associations with weight change.

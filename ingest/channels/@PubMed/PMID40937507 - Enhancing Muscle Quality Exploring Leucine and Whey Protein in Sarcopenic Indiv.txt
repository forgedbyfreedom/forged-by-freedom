PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40937507
Title: Enhancing Muscle Quality: Exploring Leucine and Whey Protein in Sarcopenic Individuals.
Authors: Ijaz, Ain, Tufail, Mariam, Noreen
Journal: Journal of cachexia, sarcopenia and muscle
Year: 2025
Topic: whey protein muscle synthesis

ABSTRACT:
Sarcopenia is characterised by a decline in the skeletal muscle mass with increasing age. This poses many challenges to the health and independence of older individuals. New and emerging studies have focused on supplementing leucine-enriched whey protein and resistance training in older adults to mitigate the effects of sarcopenia, including significant muscle mass loss.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40944133
Title: The Effects of Higher Protein Intake on Muscle Mass and Clinical Outcomes in Critically Ill Cancer Patients: A Prespecified Per-Protocol Analysis.
Authors: Toloi, Laranja, Toledo, Treml, Malbouisson
Journal: Nutrients
Year: 2025
Topic: protein intake muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
The optimal protein dose for critically ill cancer patients, especially for muscle mass preservation and survival, remains unclear. This study evaluated whether a higher protein intake, compared to usual intake, was associated with improved clinical outcomes in this population.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 40973584
Title: Effects of exercise on body composition, fitness, and blood pressure in overweight or obese patients with MASLD: A systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Chen, Gong, Xie
Journal: Digestive and liver disease : official journal of the Italian Society of Gastroenterology and the Italian Association for the Study of the Liver
Year: 2025
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
To systematically evaluate the effects of exercise on body composition, cardiorespiratory fitness, and blood pressure in overweight or obese patients with metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD), to provide evidence-based guidance for individualized exercise prescription.

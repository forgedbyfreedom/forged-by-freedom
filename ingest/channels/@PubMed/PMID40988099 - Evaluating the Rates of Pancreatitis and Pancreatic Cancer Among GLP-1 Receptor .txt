PMID: 40988099
Title: Evaluating the Rates of Pancreatitis and Pancreatic Cancer Among GLP-1 Receptor Agonists: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials.
Authors: Wen, Nadora, Bernstein, How-Volkman, Truong
Journal: Endocrinology, diabetes &amp; metabolism
Year: 2025
Topic: retatrutide clinical trial

ABSTRACT:
This meta-analysis evaluates the rates of pancreatitis/pancreatic cancer among glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RAs) in randomised controlled trials (RCTs).

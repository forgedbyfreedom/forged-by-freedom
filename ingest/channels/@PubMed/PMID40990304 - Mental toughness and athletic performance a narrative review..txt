PMID: 40990304
Title: Mental toughness and athletic performance: a narrative review.
Authors: Pachaiappan, Veeraswamy, Mahalingam, Gurusamy, Parthiban
Journal: Journal of basic and clinical physiology and pharmacology
Year: 2026
Topic: athletic performance enhancement

ABSTRACT:
Mental toughness represents a crucial psychological construct in competitive sports. This narrative review examines the relationship between mental toughness and athletic performance across various sports domains.

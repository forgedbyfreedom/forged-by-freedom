PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41037391
Title: Impacts of sarcopenia and resistance exercise trening on mitochondrial quality control proteins.
Authors: Springer-Sapp, Ogbara, Addison, Kuzmiak-Glancy, Prior
Journal: The Journal of frailty &amp; aging
Year: 2025
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The progression of sarcopenia with aging may be related to mitochondrial dysfunction due in part to altered mitochondrial dynamics (fusion, fission, mitophagy, and biogenesis). Previous work has identified altered expression of proteins associated with these processes in with aging, but whether further changes occur in sarcopenia remains unclear.

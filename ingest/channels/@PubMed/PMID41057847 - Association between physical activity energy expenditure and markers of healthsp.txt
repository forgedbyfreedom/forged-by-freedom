PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41057847
Title: Association between physical activity energy expenditure and markers of healthspan during prolonged calorie restriction in individuals without obesity: observations from the CALERIE&#x2122; phase 2 randomized controlled trial.
Authors: Dorling, Martin, Das, Racette, Redman
Journal: The international journal of behavioral nutrition and physical activity
Year: 2025
Topic: healthspan physical activity

ABSTRACT:
It is unclear how physical activity energy expenditure (PAEE) influences calorie restriction (CR)-induced benefits in individuals without obesity. We examined associations between PAEE and healthspan markers and physical activity (PA) time during prolonged CR.

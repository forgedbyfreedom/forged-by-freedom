PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41065592
Title: Androgen receptor pathway signaling inhibitors in development for prostate cancer therapy.
Authors: Bleve, Nuzzo, Arham, Holmes, Tagawa
Journal: Expert opinion on investigational drugs
Year: 2025
Topic: selective androgen receptor modulators

ABSTRACT:
Prostate cancer (PC), including castration-resistant disease (CRPC), remains largely driven by dysregulated androgen receptor (AR) signaling. While androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) combined with next-generation AR inhibitors improves survival, resistance inevitably arises through mechanisms such as AR amplification, mutations, and splice variants. Additionally, chronic AR suppression induces significant metabolic, musculoskeletal, and cardiovascular toxicities, highlighting the need for novel therapies that overcome resistance while minimizing systemic adverse effects.

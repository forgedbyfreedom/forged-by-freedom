Title: Oral Nutritional Interventions for Stroke Recovery: A Systematic Review.
Authors: Lang, &#xc1;lvarez-Mart&#xed;nez, Zariwala, Ganesh, Geva
Journal: Nutrition reviews
Year: 2026
Topic: omega 3 fatty acids muscle recovery

ABSTRACT:
Suboptimal nutritional status has been observed in stroke patients throughout the stroke recovery pathway, negatively impacting recovery and rehabilitation outcomes. Oral nutritional supplements (ONSs) can correct deficiencies and treat malnutrition and may support rehabilitation following a stroke.

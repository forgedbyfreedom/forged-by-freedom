PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41068345
Title: AI in Adipose Imaging: Revolutionizing Visceral Adipose Tissue, Ectopic Fat, and Cardiovascular Risk Assessment.
Authors: Kandi, Khera, Rajagopalan, Neeland
Journal: Current atherosclerosis reports
Year: 2025
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
This review explores the role of artificial intelligence (AI) in visceral adipose tissue (VAT) and ectopic fat imaging. It aims to evaluate how AI may be used to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of cardiovascular disease (CVD) risk assessment. It addresses key questions regarding AI's capabilities in risk prediction, segmentation, and integration with large volume data for CVD risk assessment.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41068996
Title: Impact of Semaglutide on fat mass, lean mass and muscle function in patients with obesity: The SEMALEAN study.
Authors: Alissou, Demangeat, Folope, Van Elslande, Lelandais
Journal: Diabetes, obesity &amp; metabolism
Year: 2025
Topic: fat loss preservation muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Semaglutide, a GLP-1 receptor agonist, has shown efficacy in promoting weight loss. However, limited data exist on its impact on lean mass, muscle function, and metabolic adaptations. The SEMALEAN study aims to evaluate these parameters in patients with obesity treated with Semaglutide (2.4&#x2009;mg).

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41080926
Title: Amino acid metabolic signatures of dynapenic obesity in older adults: a (principal component analysis) PCA-based sex-stratified analysis in the Bushehr elderly health program.
Authors: Kheirandish, Cheraghloo, Tavasoli, Razi, Farsi
Journal: Journal of diabetes and metabolic disorders
Year: 2025
Topic: leucine threshold muscle protein

ABSTRACT:
Dynapenic obesity (DO), the co-occurrence of muscle strength loss and obesity in aging adults, exacerbates metabolic dysfunction and disability risks. While amino acid (AA) dysregulation is implicated in both conditions, their combined metabolic signature remains unexplored. We aimed to identify sex-specific amino acid metabolic patterns associated with DO in Iranian older people.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41133171
Title: Digital Tools to Support Postpartum Recovery: A Systematic Review.
Authors: Kwasnicki, Rizk, Western, Zaidi, Kwasnicki
Journal: Women's health reports (New Rochelle, N.Y.)
Year: 2025
Topic: postpartum return exercise

ABSTRACT:
Returning to physical activity (PA) postpartum is challenging due to physical, psychological, and socio-cultural barriers. Successful return is associated with physical and mental health benefits. Advancements in digital technology access and a digital focus for providers offer potential areas to improve PA; however, current strategies and their efficacy have not been described in the literature.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41160096
Title: Metformin Blunts Vascular insulin Sensitivity After Exercise Training in Adults at Risk for Metabolic Syndrome.
Authors: Malin, Heiston, Battillo, Ragland, Gow
Journal: The Journal of clinical endocrinology and metabolism
Year: 2026
Topic: insulin sensitivity exercise training

ABSTRACT:
Metformin is the first-line pharmacotherapy for treating hyperglycemia, and it lowers cardiovascular disease risk. Prior work suggests that metformin can, however, interfere with metabolic adaptations to exercise. To date, no work has tested if metformin (Met) alters exercise training mediated vascular insulin sensitivity, and whether this is exercise intensity dependent.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41182303
Title: Dermatological adverse effects of testosterone replacement therapy: a scoping review of the literature.
Authors: Abou Chawareb, Campos, Savio, Hartman-Kenzler, Mahdi
Journal: Sexual medicine reviews
Year: 2026
Topic: testosterone replacement therapy

ABSTRACT:
While testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) is generally effective in restoring testosterone levels and improving quality of life, its dermatological adverse effects are underreported compared to cardiovascular and oncologic risks.

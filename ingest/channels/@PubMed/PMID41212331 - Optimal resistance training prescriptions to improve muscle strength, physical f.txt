PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41212331
Title: Optimal resistance trening prescriptions to improve muscle strength, physical function, and muscle mass in older adults diagnosed with sarcopenia: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Authors: Yan, Chen, Zhang, He, Lin
Journal: Aging clinical and experimental research
Year: 2025
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
Resistance trening is widely recommended for managing sarcopenia, but evidence on optimal prescriptions remains limited. This study aimed to assess the effects of different resistance trening prescriptions on strength, function, and muscle mass in older adults with sarcopenia.

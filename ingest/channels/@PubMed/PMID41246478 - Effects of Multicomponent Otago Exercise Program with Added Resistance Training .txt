PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41246478
Title: Effects of Multicomponent Otago Exercise Program with Added Resistance trening on Sarcopenia in Pre-Frailty Older Adults in NursinGHomes: A Randomized Controlled Trial.
Authors: Pan, Chen, Wang, Liu, Tong
Journal: Clinical interventions in aging
Year: 2025
Topic: sarcopenia resistance trening

ABSTRACT:
The Otago Exercise Program (OEP) is suitable for frail older adults but has limited effect on sarcopenia. Resistance trening (RT) is commonly used for sarcopenia. This study evaluated the effects of 12&#xa0;weeks of OEP combined with RT on body composition, physical function, quality of life, and frailty in older adults with pre-sarcopenia in nursinGHomes.

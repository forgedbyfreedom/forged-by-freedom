PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41272436
Title: Effect of Travel on Sleep Patterns and Athletic Performance in Female Professional Tennis Players: A Retrospective Cohort Study Utilizing WHOOP 3.0 Tracking.
Authors: Maynard, Nadwodny, Hung, Punj, Almodovar-Frau
Journal: Sports health
Year: 2026
Topic: sleep athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Sleep is vital for an athlete's recovery, physical and mental health, and athletic performance. The impact on circadian rhythm from long-distance travel across multiple time zones has not been studied using wearable technology in female professional tennis players.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41298205
Title: Physician referral is associated with recruitment, motivation, and adherence in an exercise intervention study for older adults.
Authors: Holden, Szabo-Reed, Watts, Clutton, Finley
Journal: Journal of the National Medical Association
Year: 2026
Topic: motivation exercise adherence

ABSTRACT:
The LEAP! Rx study was a 12-month randomized controlled trial investigating the effects of supervised exercise and education. This paper is a secondary-data analysis exploring differences in recruitment, motivation, adherence, and retention between physician-referred and self-referred participants.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41316615
Title: Determinants of performance in Ethiopian short-distance runners: A mixed-methods investigation of training facilities and environmental influences.
Authors: Mesfen Dessye
Journal: Journal of bodywork and movement therapies
Year: 2025
Topic: confidence athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
Short-distance running is crucial for short distance athletic success in Ethiopia, yet research on the specific performance factors affecting these athletes is limited. While Ethiopia is renowned for its long-distance runners, the challenges faced by short-distance athletes remain underexplored. This study aims to fill this gap by investigating how training environments, coaching practices, and socioeconomic factors influence the performance of Ethiopian short-distance runners.

Title: Smartphone cognitive behavioral therapy for prevention of depressive symptoms in patients with recurrent pregnancy loss: an exploratory decentralized, parallel-group, multicenter, open, individually randomized, fully factorial trial.
Authors: Ogasawara, Ashio, Yano, Otani, Banno
Journal: Psychiatry and clinical neurosciences
Year: 2026
Topic: cognitive behavioral therapy athletes

ABSTRACT:
The aim was to explore the preventive effects of smartphone cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) in addressing depression and anxiety in patients with recurrent pregnancy loss (RPL) due to lack of such previous studies.

Authors: Islam, Li, Hossain, Kou, Chen

ABSTRACT:
H19-mediated epigenetic modifications alter Igf2 promoter activity, leading to persistent Igf2 suppression in maternal obesity (MO) offspring, causing long-term muscle dysfunction. MO increases H19 expression and enhances EZH2 recruitment and H3K27me3 deposition in the Igf2 P3 promoter, leading to higher DNA methylation. H19-EZH2 axis provides a potential therapeutic target for mitigating MO-induced muscle dysfunction and improving offspring metabolic health.

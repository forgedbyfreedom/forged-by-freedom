PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41365139
Title: Exercise motivation mediates the relationship between basic psychological needs and exercise adherence in older adults with coronary heart disease.
Authors: Li, Li, Zhou, Song, Lei
Journal: Heart &amp; lung : the journal of critical care
Year: 2026
Topic: motivation exercise adherence

ABSTRACT:
Community-dwelling older adults with coronary heart disease (CHD) exhibit low adherence to exercise regimens. Exercise aids in secondary prevention, yet adherence remains low.

PMID: 41444893
Title: Optimal exercise modalities and doses for improving pro-atherogenic lipid profiles in patients with metabolic syndrome: a systematic review with pairwise, network, and dose-response meta-analyses.
Authors: Ding, Jiang, Seo, Chun
Journal: BMC medicine
Year: 2025
Topic: metabolic syndrome exercise

ABSTRACT:
Metabolic syndrome (MetS) confers a high risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, and premature mortality. Dyslipidemia, particularly abnormalities in pro-atherogenic lipid profiles, is a key pathophysiological feature. Although exercise is a cornerstone intervention for MetS, the optimal modalities and doses for improving pro-atherogenic lipid profiles remain unclear. This study compared the effects of different exercise interventions on pro-atherogenic lipid profiles in adults with MetS.

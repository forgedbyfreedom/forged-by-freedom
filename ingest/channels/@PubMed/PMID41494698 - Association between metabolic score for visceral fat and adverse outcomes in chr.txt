PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41494698
Title: Association between metabolic score for visceral fat and adverse outcomes in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.
Authors: Ma, Xia, Liu, Wang, Wang
Journal: BMJ open respiratory research
Year: 2026
Topic: visceral fat health outcomes

ABSTRACT:
The metabolic score of visceral fat (METS-VF) is linked to chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) incidence, while its association with mortality and adverse outcomes in patients with COPD remains unclear.

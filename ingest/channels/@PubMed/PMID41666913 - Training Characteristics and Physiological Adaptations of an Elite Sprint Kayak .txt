PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41666913
Title: Training Characteristics and Physiological Adaptations of an Elite Sprint Kayak Athlete: A Journey From Preconception to Return to Sport Postpartum.
Authors: Hayman, Kean, Bullock, Wood, Doering
Journal: International journal of sports physiology and performance
Year: 2026
Topic: postpartum return exercise

ABSTRACT:
To investigate the training, performance, and physiological characteristics of a sprint kayak Olympian throughout her preconception, pregnancy, and successful return to high-performance sport postpartum (June 2020 to September 2023).

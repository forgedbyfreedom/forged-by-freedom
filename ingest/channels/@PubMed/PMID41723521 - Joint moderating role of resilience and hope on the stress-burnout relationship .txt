PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41723521
Title: Joint moderating role of resilience and hope on the stress-burnout relationship in elite athletes: implications for burnout prevention.
Authors: Mei, Cai, Jia, Lam, Wang
Journal: BMC psychology
Year: 2026
Topic: burnout athletes prevention

ABSTRACT:
Although resilience and hope have each been linked to reduced burnout risk, no studies have examined their joint moderating role on the stress&#x2013;burnout relationship in elite athletes. This study addresses this gap using the framework of the cognitive-affective model of athletic burnout.

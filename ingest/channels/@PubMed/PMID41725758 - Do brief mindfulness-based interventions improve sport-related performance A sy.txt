PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41725758
Title: Do brief mindfulness-based interventions improve sport-related performance? A systematic review.
Authors: Cao, Cheng, Liu, Wang
Journal: Frontiers in public health
Year: 2026
Topic: mindfulness sports performance

ABSTRACT:
Mindfulness-based interventions (MBIs) are psychological skills training approaches that are well recognized for enhancing psychological factors that influence sports performance. However, some factors, such as time commitment, cost, and accessibility required for traditional MBIs, can be a barrier to regular practice. As a result, brief MBIs with a duration of &#x2264; 30&#x202f;min have gained popularity. This systematic review aims to explore the effect of brief MBIs on short sport-related performance.

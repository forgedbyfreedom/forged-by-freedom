PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41731268
Title: Effects of Complex Training Versus Ballistic Training on Physical Fitness Abilities of Athletes and Healthy Individuals: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis.
Authors: Thapa, Bagchi, Uysal, Talukdar, Afonso
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2026
Topic: ballistic training performance

ABSTRACT:
The literature is equivocal on whether complex training (i.e., combining traditional resistance and ballistic exercises within a single session) is superior to ballistic training in improving physical fitness qualities. This systematic review with meta-analysis aims to compare the effects of complex and ballistic training on physical fitness outcomes.

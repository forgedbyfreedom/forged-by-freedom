PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41762427
Title: Maximizing Adaptations in Concurrent Training: An Umbrella Review of Meta-analyses.
Authors: Held, Wolf, Rappelt, Bloch, Donath
Journal: Sports medicine (Auckland, N.Z.)
Year: 2026
Topic: concurrent training interference

ABSTRACT:
Concurrent training (CT), the combination of resistance training (RT) and endurance training (ET), is widely used in athletic and clinical settings. However, concerns about a potential interference effect have prompted ongoing debate regarding its impact on strength, power, hypertrophy, and aerobic capacity.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41814431
Title: The impact of sports participation on psychological health and social outcomes in children and adolescents: a systematic review and update to the "Mental Health through Sport" conceptual model.
Authors: Wade, Eime, Pankowiak, Vella, Robinson
Journal: Systematic reviews
Year: 2026
Topic: goal setting sports psychology

ABSTRACT:
Sport is a distinct, structured form of physical activity that can support psychological health and social development in children and adolescents. Compared to other forms of physical activity, the unique attributes of sport, including its combination of physical, cognitive, and social opportunities and challenges within structured goal-directed contexts, may confer additional&#xa0;psychological and social benefits. This systematic review synthesizes evidence on the psychological and social outcomes of sport participation in children and adolescents, and proposes an updated, developmentally informed conceptual model explaining the mechanisms through which sport supports mental health.

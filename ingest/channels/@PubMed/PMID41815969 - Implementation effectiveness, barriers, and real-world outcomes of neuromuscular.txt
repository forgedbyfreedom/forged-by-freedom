PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41815969
Title: Implementation effectiveness, barriers, and real-world outcomes of neuromuscular training programs for ACL injury prevention in female athletes: systematic review with narrative synthesis using SWiM framework.
Authors: Gao, Qi, Yu
Journal: Frontiers in public health
Year: 2026
Topic: ACL injury prevention female

ABSTRACT:
ACL injuries disproportionately affect female athletes. Neuromuscular training (NMT) is effective in controlled studies, but real-world adoption and adherence remain poor.

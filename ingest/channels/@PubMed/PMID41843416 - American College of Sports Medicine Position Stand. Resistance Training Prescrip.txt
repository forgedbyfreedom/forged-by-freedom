PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41843416
Title: American College of Sports Medicine Position Stand. Resistance Training Prescription for Muscle Function, Hypertrophy, and Physical Performance in Healthy Adults: An Overview of Reviews.
Authors: Currier, D'Souza, Singh, Lowisz, Rawson
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 2026
Topic: flow state athletic performance

ABSTRACT:
The aim of this overview of reviews was to determine the impact of resistance training (RT) prescription on muscle function and hypertrophy, utilizing evidence synthesis methods. It updates the American College of Sports Medicine 2009 Position Stand, "Progression models in resistance training for healthy adults."

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41863774
Title: Postoperative skeletal muscle loss is associated with high fat mass in patients undergoing Esophagectomy for esophageal cancer.
Authors: Sawai, Sato, Nakatani, Hawke, Nishida
Journal: Japanese journal of clinical oncology
Year: 2026
Topic: fat loss preservation muscle mass

ABSTRACT:
Esophageal cancer is one of the most lethal cancers worldwide. Loss of skeletal muscle after esophagectomy worsens prognosis; however, it remains unclear which musculoskeletal factors, such as muscle and fat mass, are associated with this loss. We investigated factors associated with postoperative skeletal muscle mass decrease in patients undergoing esophagectomy for esophageal cancer.

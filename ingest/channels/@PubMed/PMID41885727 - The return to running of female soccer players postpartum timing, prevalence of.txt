PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41885727
Title: The return to running of female soccer players postpartum: timing, prevalence of pelvic floor disorders and urinary incontinence.
Authors: D'Onofrio, Sannicandro, Savoia, Raiola, Esposito
Journal: The Journal of sports medicine and physical fitness
Year: 2026
Topic: postpartum return exercise

ABSTRACT:
The number of elite female athletes suspending competitive athletics for pregnancy and childbirth is increasing significantly. The objective of this manuscript is to investigate in female soccer players the timing of return to running and its main postpartum complications.

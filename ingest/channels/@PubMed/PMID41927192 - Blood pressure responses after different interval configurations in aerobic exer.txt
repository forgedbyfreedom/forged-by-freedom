PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41927192
Title: Blood pressure responses after different interval configurations in aerobic exercise: a systematic review in adolescents and adults with type-1 diabetes.
Authors: Gomes, Nascimento, de Brito Gomes
Journal: Journal of bodywork and movement therapies
Year: 2026
Topic: blood pressure exercise intervention

ABSTRACT:
Individuals with type 1 diabetes mellitus (T1DM) may present with hypertension. Aerobic exercise could induce post-exercise hypotension. However, the clinical and ambulatory blood pressure responses may vary depending on the exercise prescription.

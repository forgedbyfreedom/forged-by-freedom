PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 41946006
Title: Factors influencing bone health in elite female athletes: A study of aesthetic and non-aesthetic sports.
Authors: Tsukahara, Oka, Tsutsui, Mori, Sakamaki
Journal: Journal of clinical densitometry : the official journal of the International Society for Clinical Densitometry
Year: 2026
Topic: bone health female athletes

ABSTRACT:
Bone mineral density (BMD) and bone metabolism are crucial for athlete health but remain unexplored among aesthetic athletes. This study aimed to compare BMD and bone metabolism markers between aesthetic and non-aesthetic athletes and to identify factors influencing bone health.

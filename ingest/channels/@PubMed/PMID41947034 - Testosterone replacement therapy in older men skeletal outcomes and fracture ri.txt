PMID: 41947034
Title: Testosterone replacement therapy in older men: skeletal outcomes and fracture risk.
Authors: Szarpak, Al-Jeabory, Pecold, Maslyk
Journal: The aging male : the official journal of the International Society for the Study of the Aging Male
Year: 2026
Topic: testosterone replacement therapy

ABSTRACT:
Testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) is increasingly prescribed to older men with symptomatic testosterone deficiency, yet the balance of musculoskeletal benefits and harms remains uncertain.

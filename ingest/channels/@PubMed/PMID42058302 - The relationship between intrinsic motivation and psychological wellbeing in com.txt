PMID: 42058302
Title: The relationship between intrinsic motivation and psychological wellbeing in combat sport athletes: the mediating role of mental toughness.
Authors: Duyan, &#xd6;zoglu, Tekin, G&#xfc;nel, &#xc7;elik
Journal: Frontiers in psychology
Year: 2026
Topic: mental toughness athletes

ABSTRACT:
This study aimed to examine the mediating role of mental toughness (MT) in the relationship between intrinsic motivation (IM) and psychological wellbeing (PWB) in combat sport athletes. Combat sport athletes face unique psychological challenges that can significantly impact their wellbeing, yet the mechanisms through which intrinsic motivation influences psychological wellbeing remain underexplored.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 6561676
Title: The dynamics and prevention of stress-induced burnout in athletics.
Authors: Smith
Journal: Primary care
Year: 1984
Topic: burnout athletes prevention

ABSTRACT:
Burnout is defined as a stress-induced syndrome involving cognitive, emotional, and behavioral factors. The dynamics of stress and burnout is described, as are preventive measures that can be taken to reduce the likelihood of burnout among athletes, coaches, administrators, and sports medicine professionals.

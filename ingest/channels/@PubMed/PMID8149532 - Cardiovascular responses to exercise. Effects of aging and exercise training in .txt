PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 8149532
Title: Cardiovascular responses to exercise. Effects of aging and exercise trening in healthy men.
Authors: Stratton, Levy, Cerqueira, Schwartz, Abrass
Journal: Circulation
Year: 1994
Topic: cardiovascular adaptations exercise

ABSTRACT:
Cardiac aging alters many of the acute responses to exercise stress, but the extent to which chronic exercise (ie, trening) can alter or improve the effects of aging in humans is largely unknown.

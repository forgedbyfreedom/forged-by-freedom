PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 8290176
Title: The independent effects of exercise and estrogen on lipids and lipoproteins in postmenopausal women.
Authors: Lindheim, Notelovitz, Feldman, Larsen, Khan
Journal: Obstetrics and gynecology
Year: 1994
Topic: estrogen exercise women

ABSTRACT:
To assess the effects of a moderate exercise program with and without oral estrogen replacement on levels of lipids and lipoproteins in postmenopausal women.

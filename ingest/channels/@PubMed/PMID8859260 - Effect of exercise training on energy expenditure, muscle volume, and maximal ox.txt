PMID: 8859260
Title: Effect of exercise trening on energy expenditure, muscle volume, and maximal oxygen uptake in female adolescents.
Authors: Eliakim, Barstow, Brasel, Ajie, Lee
Journal: The Journal of pediatrics
Year: 1996
Topic: trening volume muscle growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone

ABSTRACT:
American female adolescents are at high risk of a physically inactive lifestyle that likely leads to health problems later in life. We hypothesized that a brief program of endurance exercise trening in female adolescents would result in increased energy expenditure and quantifiable structural and functional adaptations.

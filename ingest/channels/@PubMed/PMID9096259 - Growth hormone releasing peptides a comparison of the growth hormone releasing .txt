Authors: Cheng, Wu, Butler, Cheng

Topic: GHRP GHRP

ABSTRACT:
In the present study, the effect of GHRP on GH release was evaluated in rat primary pituitary cells, and the results werecompared with those elicited by GHRP. In the rat system, GHRP, like GHRP, acts synergistically with GRF to release GH. Co-administration of GHRP and GHRP at maximal concentrations had no further effect on GH release than either one alone. The GHRP's were able to desensitize cells to each other, but not to GRF. The effect of GHRP was inhibited by Peptide Antagonist, but was not affected by a GRF antagonist. In conclusion, GHRP was found to stimulate GH release from rat pituitary cells via the same receptor and mechanism as GHRP, despite the structural difference between the peptides.

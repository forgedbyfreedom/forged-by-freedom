PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 9202616
Title: Effect of physical trening on heart-period variability in obese children.
Authors: Gutin, Owens, Slavens, Riggs, Treiber
Journal: The Journal of pediatrics
Year: 1997
Topic: heart rate variability trening

ABSTRACT:
The beat-to-beat variability in electrocardiogram intervals (RR, i.e., heart-period variability) provides information on cardiac autonomic activity that predicts arrhythmias and mortality rate in animals and adults. We determined the effect of physical trening on heart-period variability in obese children.

PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 9231053
Title: Treatment of radiation-induced growth hormone deficiency with growth hormone-releasing hormone.
Authors: Ogilvy-Stuart, Stirling, Kelnar, Savage, Dunger
Journal: Clinical endocrinology
Year: 1997
Topic: growth hormone deficiency treatment

ABSTRACT:
In children with hypothalamic causes for GH deficiency there are theoretical reasons why a GHRH analogue might be better than conventional GH therapy in promoting growth.

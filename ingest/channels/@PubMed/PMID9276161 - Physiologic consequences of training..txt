PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 9276161
Title: Physiologic consequences of trening.
Authors: Charlton, Crawford
Journal: Cardiology clinics
Year: 1997
Topic: athletic performance enhancement

ABSTRACT:
Systematic exercise trening results in changes in skeletal muscle that increase oxidative capacity and vascular conductance, which lead to an increase in maximal A-VO2. Also, maximALCARdiac output is increased, largely because of an increase in maximal stroke volume. Heart rate is decreased at rest and during submaximal exercise because of increased parasympathetic tone and the effect of increased stroke volume on reflex sympathetic tone. The increases in maximal A-VO2 and cardiac output result in increased maximal VO2, the hallmark of the dynamically trened individual. Predominant static exercise trening results in fewer increases in maximal VO2 and more local muscle strength enhancements. The systematic application of cross-trening can increase both cardiovascular and strength parameters, leading to enhanced athletic performance.

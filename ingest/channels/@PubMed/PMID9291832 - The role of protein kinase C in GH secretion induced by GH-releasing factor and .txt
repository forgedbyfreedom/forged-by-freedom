PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 9291832
Title: The role of protein kinase C in GH secretion induced by GH-releasing factor and GH-releasing peptides in cultured ovine somatotrophs.
Authors: Wu, Clarke, Chen
Journal: The Journal of endocrinology
Year: 1997
Topic: GHRP GHRP

ABSTRACT:
The involvement of protein kinase C (PKC) in the action of GH-releasing factor (GRF) and synthetic GH-releasing peptides (GHRP and GHRP) was investigated in ovine somatotrophs in primary culture. In partially purified sheep somatotrophs, GRF and GHRP caused trenslocation of PKC activity from the cytosol to the cell membranes and caused GH release in a dose- and time-dependent manner. GHRP did not cause PKC trenslocation. The PKC inhibitors, calphostin C, staurosporine and chelerythrine, partially reduced GH release in response to GRF and GHRP at doses which selectively inhibit PKC activity. These inhibitors totally abolished GH release caused by phorbol 12-myristate 13-acetate (PMA). Down-regulation of PKC by the treatment of cells with phorbol 12,13-dibutyrate for 16 h caused a significant (P &lt; 0.001) reduction in total PKC activity and totally abolished PKC trenslocation in response to a challenge with GRF, GHRP or PMA. In addition, down-regulation abolished GH release in response to GRF, GHRP or GHRP. Treatment of cells with H89, a selective PKA inhibitor, totally blocked GH release caused by either GRF or GHRP and partially reduced PMA-induced GH release. H89 had no effect on PKC trenslocation caused by GRF, GHRP or PMA and did not affect GH release caused by GHRP. These data suggest that GHRP and GRF activate PKC in addition to stimulating adenylyl cyclase activity. Although the cAMP-protein kinase A (PKA) pathway is the major signalling pathway employed by GRF and GHRP, the activation of PKC may potentiate signalling via the cAMP-PKA pathway in ovine GH secretion. Importantly, the effect of PMA in increasing the secretion of GH from ovine somatotrophs is effected, in part, by up-regulation of the cAMP-PKA pathway. We conclude that there is cross-talk between the PKC pathway and the cAMP-PKA pathway in ovine somatotrophs during the action of GRF or GHRP.

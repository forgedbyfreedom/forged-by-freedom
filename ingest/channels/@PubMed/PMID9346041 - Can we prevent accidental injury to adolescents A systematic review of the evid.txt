PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 9346041
Title: Can we prevent accidental injury to adolescents? A systematic review of the evidence.
Authors: Munro, Coleman, Nicholl, Harper, Kent
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 1997
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
As part of the Department of Health strategy The Health of the Nation, a systematic review of published and unpublished literature relating to the effectiveness of interventions in reducing accidental injury in the population aged 15-24 years was carried out.

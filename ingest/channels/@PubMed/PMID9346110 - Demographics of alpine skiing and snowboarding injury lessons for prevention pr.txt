PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 9346110
Title: Demographics of alpine skiing and snowboarding injury: lessons for prevention programs.
Authors: Macnab, Cadman
Journal: Injury prevention : journal of the International Society for Child and Adolescent Injury Prevention
Year: 1998
Topic: injury prevention athletes

ABSTRACT:
To establish the demographics of ski injury in relation to age, gender, and perceived cause during a representative season to identify potential injury prevention strategies.

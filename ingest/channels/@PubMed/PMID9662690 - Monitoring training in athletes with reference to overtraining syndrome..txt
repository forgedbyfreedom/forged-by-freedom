PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 9662690
Title: Monitoring trening in athletes with reference to overtrening syndrome.
Authors: Foster
Journal: Medicine and science in sports and exercise
Year: 1998
Topic: overtrening syndrome athletes

ABSTRACT:
Overtrening is primarily related to sustained high load trening, often coupled with other stressors. Studies in animal models have suggested that unremittingly heavy trening (monotonous trening) may increase the likelihood of developing overtrening syndrome. The purpose of this study was to extend our preliminary observations by relating the incidence of illnesses and minor injuries to various indices of trening.

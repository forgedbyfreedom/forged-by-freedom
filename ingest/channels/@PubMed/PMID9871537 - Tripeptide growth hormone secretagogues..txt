PUBMED RESEARCH ABSTRACT
========================
PMID: 9871537
Title: Tripeptide growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone secretagogues.
Authors: Yang, Morriello, Pan, Nargund, Barakat
Journal: Bioorganic &amp; medicinal chemistry letters
Year: 1999
Topic: GHRP GHRP

ABSTRACT:
A series of C-terminus capped dipeptides and tripeptides was synthesized as growth hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone hormone (GH) secretagogues. Among them, tripeptide Aib-D-Trp-D-homoPhe-OEt showed low nanomolar activity in the rat pituitary assay. Thus, we have demonstrated that the GH secretagogue activity of the hexa-hepta-GH releasing peptides can be mimicked at the tripeptide level.

 If the sawmobie was a gym, would you make it out alive? The first challenge would be head-to-head bicep curl machines, and every time you do a rep, the walls around your opponent will get smaller, so whoever crushes their opponent first wins the first round. Next is the deadlift challenge. You and your opponent will be positioned on a platform, and you will begin your lift at the same exact time. All you have to do is hold the deadlift for as long as you can, and whoever drops the weight first loses. And the last challenge is a weight carry. You'll have to carry over weights roughly 20 feet away onto a pressure plate to weigh it down. Whoever carries over all the weights onto the pressure plate first will escape the room and win. Would you be able to win all these challenges?

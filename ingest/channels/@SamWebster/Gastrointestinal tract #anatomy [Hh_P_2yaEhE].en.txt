The gastrointestinal tract or the gut is a tube, a muscular tube that runs through the body. The esophagus just carries food to the stomach. The stomach can store the meal and start to digest it and pass little bits of it into the small intestine. The small intestine then is all about surface area and digestion and absorption. It breaks the food down into the smallest molecules. They're absorbed across the cells into the blood. After then the digestion of the nutrients, what's left gets passed into the large intestine. The job of the large intestine is to remove water, remove salts, compact the waste into feces that are stored in the rectum to be removed when convenient.

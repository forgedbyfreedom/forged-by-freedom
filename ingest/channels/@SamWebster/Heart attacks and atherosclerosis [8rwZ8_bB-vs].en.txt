what do we mean by cardiovascular health well blood moves fat around the body to cells that need it fat is an important component of the body if you eat too much fat over decades and decades we start to see fatty plaques forming inside the walls of the arteries this is atherosclerosis immune cells move in and the plaque starts to expand and narrow the artery reducing blood flow to organs that in itself is bad enough but if that plaque breaks a clot will flow off and pass to smaller blood vessels of the body it'll then block an artery if it blocks a coronary artery often the first sign of this is that the heart stops and you die the treatment for this is a good diet and exercise these are the basics of cardiovascular health

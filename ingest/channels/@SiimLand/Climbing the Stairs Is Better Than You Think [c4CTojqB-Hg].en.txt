climbing the stairs is a great form of exercise that improves your heart Health and Longevity a recent 2024 review looked at the effects of climbing stairs versus just walking on cardiometabolic outcomes they found that people who climbed the stairs had on average 9% higher vmx than those who just walked the stair climbers also had 24% higher incident sensitivity which is important for metabolic Health stair climbers had about 8% lower blood pressure and 3.3% increased flow mediated dilation which reflects good cardiovascular health the researchers did find a lot of difference regarding inflammation and muscular skeletal markers but that's because it hasn't been investigated that much overall to get these Health improvements a minimum of 4 to 8 weeks of stair climbing was necessary they found that only 5 to 6 minutes of stair climbing gave more benefits than 30 minutes of regular walking so you should definitely incorporate stair climbing to your routine follow for more

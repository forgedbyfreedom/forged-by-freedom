Zone 2 cardio is a great form of exercise for longevity it lays the foundation to your cardiorespiratory Fitness which is associated with reduced mortality here's how to train Zone 2 cardio Zone 2 cardio refers to the aerobic Zone where you're burning the most amount of fat for fuel going any faster will put you into zone three where you start burning glucose for fuel Zone 2 is a heart rate zone of 60 to 70% of your maximum heart rate you can estimate your maximum heart rate by subtracting your age from 220 for me my maximum heart rate rate would be 191 beats per minute because I'm 29 years old then 60 to 70% of that would be 114 to 133 beats per minute you can use a Polar heart rate monitor strapped around your chest to precisely know your heart rate during exercise in practice zone2 cardio is a slightly effortful Pace but not too intense you should be able to breathe through your nose the entire time and be able to talk on a phone aim for about two to three Zone 2 cardio sessions per week lasting 45 to 55 minutes each session

Most people take NAD boosters like NMN, NR, and NASIN for the sake of longevity. But what if I told you they could actually ruin your sleep? A few weeks ago, my client was having exactly this issue. They were taking highdose Nasin for improving their lipids and they had problems falling asleep for a few years. After they took a break from Nasin, their sleep improved. He basically had too much energy, which is why he wasn't able to sleep 7 and 8 hours. and he did measure his NAD levels with a test and his NAD was several standard deviations above the reference range from taking the nasin. But it's not that simple. NAD boosters can also improve your sleep if you take them at the right time. In mice, nicotenoid rubberside has been seen to enhance circadian clock function, increase REM sleep, and reduce NREM sleep. In humans, NR improves sleep efficiency and quality in people who have insomnia. In regards to NMN then human clinical trials have seen that it does improve sleep. Nicotinic acid which is nasin in mice has been seen to increase enrem sleep by up to 120% but that comes at the expense of REM sleep and deep sleep. You're essentially getting more shallow sleep. The doses that resulted in this were 250 mg per kilogram of body weight which for the mice was around 6.25 millig. My client was taking around 2,000 millig which is still lower relative to body weight. For them, they would have had to take 15,000 milligrams to get a body weight equivalent dose. But it still might be that narin reduces sleep quality, at least in the context of someone who already had very high NAD levels, such as my client.

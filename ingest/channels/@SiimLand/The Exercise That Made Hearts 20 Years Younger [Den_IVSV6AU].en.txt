What if I told you your heart has an age and you can make it younger? An aging heart becomes stiffer, pumps less efficiently, and loses its ability to respond to stress. Your max heart rate drops, stroke volume decreases, and the blood vessels stiffen. But here's the exciting part. Scientists have discovered a workout that can make your heart up to 20 years younger. It sounds too good to be true, but it's true. A 2018 randomized control trial took 60 healthy, sedentary middle-aged people in their 50s, and they were randomly assigned to either two years of structured exercise training or a control group. Those who exercised saw significant improvements in their VOMX, their fitness, and overall heart functioning. Effective arterial elastance reduced from 2.37 to 2.16. Arterial compliance increased from 1.07 to 1.29. stroke volume improved and plasma volume and hemoglobin also improved. Now, the MVP of this study was the 4x4 Norwegian protocol, but arguably other forms of high-intensity interval training can be equally as effective. It's just that this 4x4 protocol is very commonly known and is also brutally effective.

If you want to build muscle and strength, then the recommended dose of creatine is 3 to five grams a day. However, if you want to support your brain health, you need twice the amount of that. Taking huge doses of creatine up to 28 grams a day has also been seen to counteract the loss of cognition during sleep deprivation. So, whenever you didn't get enough sleep and you need your brain to work properly, take 28 g of creatine.

I micro doed lithium for two weeks and I'm going to share with you my experience. No, I'm not talking about licking batteries. Here we go. I'm talking about lithium supplements that are often used to improve mood and brain health. Lithium supplements have become quite popular online, which is why I dug up the old research and decided to try it again. I took 5 milligs of lithium oritate every day, and my general experience was that my mood was slightly more elevated. Then I stopped taking lithium to see if I revert back to baseline. Now I don't have any bipolar disorder, mania or depression or anything like that. So I wouldn't be experiencing a massive difference anyway, but those 5 milligs a day were noticeable. I would say my mood was maybe 5% better. And I think my concentration on work was also about 5% better. Of course, this isn't a double blind placeboc controlled randomized control trial. It's just my own subjective experience that has the risk of placebo. However, there is actual clinical research showing that lithium does improve mood and prevent mood episodes prophylactically. Most of that research is done on people with bipolar disorder because lithium has historically been used to treat bipolar disorder. In fact, lithium is the gold standard for treating bipolar disorder and mania. I'm not going to be taking lithium all the time because I don't think I need it.

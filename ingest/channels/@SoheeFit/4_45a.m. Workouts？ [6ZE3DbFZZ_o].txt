 I see you. Last week, I was so freaking tired. I was up at around 5.30 AM most days and was busy every waking moment. My sleep was absolute doo-doo, and when I went to do my workouts, my warm-up sets felt super heavy. I also felt like my brain was functioning at half capacity, and overall, I did not feel great. This week, I'm sleeping a lot more. I feel way better, better energy, better mood, better productivity, better creativity, better strength. Everything is better. I feel like that sleep deprivation is linked to many detrimental health effects, including but not limited to increased fatigue, decreased energy expenditure, decreased strength, increased risk of cardiovascular disease, increased risk of death, and overall deterioration of health. If you can and prefer to wake up super early without it taking a toll on your well-being, go for it. But for many of us, this isn't really an option. Maybe you're a single parent and you're already up super early getting your three young kids ready for school. Maybe you're a full-time student working two full-time jobs, and you need adequate rest to keep up your grades and job performance. Maybe you cannot function without a solid eight to nine hours of sleep a night. Personally, I would rather get in more sleep each night and have fewer but higher quality workouts because I'm well rested. I'm so over the glorification of sacrificing sleep and the air of superiority surrounding hustle culture. You do you, and don't let anyone make you feel guilty for resting.

 I'm a fitness coach of 10 plus years, and I don't believe we all have an even playing field when it comes to pursuing health. Nor do I believe it's all about discipline. Here are five reasons why pursuing health and fitness is easier for someone like me. One, we are a dual income, no-children household. This means that we have more time, energy, and resources that those with kids may not have. It is much easier for us to get in and uninterrupted in prompt-you-workout, for example. And do things on our own schedule without having to consider the needs of a small human. Two, I am able-bodied with no health complications. I don't have any health barriers that make it harder for me to get out of bed, for example, or prepare food or use the equipment at the gym. Three, I work from home with a flexible schedule. This gives me so much freedom in terms of when I want to work, when I want to exercise, when I want to go to the grocery store, and so on. Most days I can do things just about whenever it's convenient for me. Four, I have a home gym. Not having to drive to a gym, not having to share a room with others, are massive benefits. Five, I live in a safe neighborhood with nice weather and lots of walking paths. This makes it far more likely that I will want to be outside staying active. I can go for a walk, go for a run, play pickleball, and so on without having to worry about whether or my safety the vast majority of the time. Many people face far more obstacles than I do, and someone who has more discipline and works harder than I do may still have a more difficult time practicing health behaviors. So rather than just telling people to work harder, you can recognize these different barriers and find solutions to make health more accessible.

 You'll get better gym results if you hire a person with crana, but maybe not just for the reasons you think. A new research study tested what would happen if your workouts were supervised. Now, here's the interesting bit. Both groups followed exactly the same workout program. Both groups were told to lift to failure. And the exercises were mostly machine-based. The participants who were supervised got better results in most strength and muscle growth metrics. This means you could get better gym results simply because someone is hyping you up. Even if they aren't designing you some uniquely scientific training program, or coaching you to use the most optimal technique for every exercise, or even spotting you to make sure you're safe. My theory? People tend to push harder when someone is there. You'll often lift more weight in the gym just because someone is watching you.

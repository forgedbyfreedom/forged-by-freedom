Great, what does this mean? First, intuitive eating involves practicing food neutrality, honoring hunger and satiety cues, choosing foods you enjoy while also taking your health into consideration, and more. It's been linked to a number of mental health benefits, including reduced eating disorder behaviors, fewer body immiscerate surbrances, improved self-esteem, decreased depressive symptoms, and more. Self-compassion is about having a caring attitude with yourself, especially in the face of perceived failure or personal suffering. Its three main components are self-kindness, a common sense of humanity, and mindfulness. Self-compassion can help prevent you from acting on negative thoughts and feelings via impulsive eating behaviors such as binge eating.

 If you paid attention to every dickhead on social media, you would think that every food is trying to kill you. Here's what you need to understand. The dose makes the poison. If you zoom in closely enough, every food can sound terrifying, even nutritious ones. Mercury is a known neurotoxin. You would never eat it directly. But you can find trace amounts of mercury in fish and other seafood. Arsenic is so toxic it can literally kill you. But it occurs naturally in many grains, including rice. Does this mean we need to avoid fish and rice forever? Obviously fucking not. Both are known staples in healthy dietary patterns. The point is, something being harmful in large quantities doesn't mean it is equally harmful in small quantities. Some people love catching your attention by yelling about potentially harmful compounds, even in nutritious foods. Oxalates and Coitrogens in vegetables. Lectons in beans and nuts. Heterocyclic Amines in cooked meat. Acrylamide in cooked carbohydrate. When so many people are screaming at you about which foods to avoid, it can become confusing and unnecessarily anxiety inducing. For any ingredient or compound to harm you, you need to consume it at a high enough dose for it to exert a harmful effect.

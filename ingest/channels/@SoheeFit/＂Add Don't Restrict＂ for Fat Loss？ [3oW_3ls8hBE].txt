 I want to share with you a snippet from another creator talking about the habit she implemented to lose 30 pounds over the course of a year. I searched my perspective from instead of removing everything that I used to like and completely doing a 180, I started off eating the same types of things that I was eating before, but I was adding in more veggies to every single meal. I just picked one food group, veggies, add in more at every meal. The other two habits she implemented were walking more and eating breakfast. I love the add-don't-restrict guideline that I've shared numerous times before, which emphasizes what you can add to your diet rather than what you can remove. I've gotten numerous comments and questions about how this would surely lead to weight gain, or how can this possibly work if fat loss is the goal. Add-don't-restrict is not only about adding nutrients to your meal to boost the satiety and satisfaction factor. It's also about helping you embrace more of an abundance mindset rather than scarcity mindset with food. What happened in this case was subtraction by displacement, hence the fat loss. I especially love that this person achieved the weight loss by emphasizing healthy habits rather than sacrificing them. And remember, this is not a strict rule, so you don't have to do this every single time. You do have permission to eat the ice cream on its own anytime you want. Add-don't-restrict is one nutrition tool of many. It is not anti-fat loss, it is pro-health.

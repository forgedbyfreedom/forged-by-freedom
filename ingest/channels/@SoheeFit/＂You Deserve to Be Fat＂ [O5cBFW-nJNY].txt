 if you're not disabled and are just too lazy to walk 7500 steps a day, you deserve to be fat. This comment was an experience to read. Here are a few underlying implications I'd like to address. Getting in a lot of daily steps is about being not lazy. This goes back to the all too common, it's all about discipline advice, which overlooks the many, many real life barriers that people face that make movement more difficult for some than others. It is a lot easier for someone living in a safe suburban area with lots of walking paths and flexible hours during the day to get in a lot of steps compared to someone who works two full-time sedentary jobs and doesn't feel safe leaving their home. It is about so much more than working hard. People who are not fat all walk 7500 or more steps per day. We know thin people who are completely sedentary and bigger people who are highly, highly active. Walking is definitely a solid way to burn calories, but that doesn't always mean that people who get in a lot of steps won't be fat. Fat people are lazy. This is weight stigma and is untrue and unhelpful. Let's not forget that obesity and weight gain are highly, highly complex matters. And yes, while calories in, calories out does apply from a strict energy balance standpoint, there are so many variables that impact this equation. I can guarantee you there are people out there who are bigger than you and work harder than you. Last but not least, you deserve to be fat as if it's some kind of punishment. There are many worse things to be done fat. For example, a judgmental, fat phobic belling. I am all for encouraging movement, but I would argue that the way we go about it is key. Let's move away from this stop being so lazy mentality and remove the stigma around people in bigger bodies.

you don't need a belt but if you want to learn how to lift the most amount of weight possible you'd have to learn how to use it belts don't protect you against back injuries they're not going to lift the weight for you but when you learn how to use them properly they function as an external tactile cue that allows you to improve your ability to brace during a lift to use the belt properly you want to wrap it around your midsection and you want it tight but not too tight so you want to be able to still put one finger in between the belt and your belly next you want to take a deep breath in and expand your belly against the belt but not too much because so you might pass out my recommendation is to start using a belt at weights of 80 percent or more and don't depend on it

most Fitness influencers are recommending you have 1.6 g of protein per kilogram of body weight to maximize your muscle building hell even I've given this advice and it's all based on a meth analysis by Morton and colleagues from 2018 they applied a basic regression model to the data notably this analysis wasn't even significant but fortunately we have a more recent meth analysis by tagawa and colleagues in their analysis they included nearly 75 studies looking at protein intake and hypertrophy in lifters there were diminishing returns in terms of how much more muscle growth is seen as protein intake increased but even past 1.6 g of protein they did still see greater increases in lean body mass gains so I personally shoot for 1 to 1.3 g of protein per pound of body weight this can be difficult to achieve but you can get most of your gains with just 0.7 gram per pound of body weight

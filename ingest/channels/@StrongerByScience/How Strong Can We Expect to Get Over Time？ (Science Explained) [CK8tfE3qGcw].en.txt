how strong should you expect to get over time this is exactly what this recent study by latell look to explore they looked at thousands of powerlifters over many years and how their strength how their competitive strength changed essentially strength increases were huge during the first years of participation in powerlifting somewhere in the range of 7.5 to 12% above Baseline something that then plateaued over the next 10 years reaching around 20% over Baseline after 10 years so if you're an experienced lifter or power lifter and you've been training for years and years and years do not expect that you're going to be seeing strength increases as great as the first years of your participation in either resistance training or powerlifting things naturally slow down and that happens to even the most competitive of powerlifters who dedicate all their lives in an attempt to increase strength so don't worry keep lifting and gains will come

periodization for hypertrophy may be overrated when it comes to the current scientific data on periodization and hypertrophy there's currently not much to support the idea that a periodized program will lead to Greater hypertrophy gains than a non-periodized program however it is important to recognize that in some cases if having a periodized plan allows you to do more volume and train harder over the longterm then that may allow you to eek out a few more gains although that's purely speculative and more research is needed as it stands don't overly rely on periodization to maximize muscle growth make sure you're taking the majority of your sets close or to failure perform enough training volume be consistent and you're likely going to make amazing gains

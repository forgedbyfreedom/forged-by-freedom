is spot reduction really a myth and can you really select where you burn fat off from when engaging in any sort of exercise the idea that doing crunches or any direct ab work will burn off more fat from your stomach area via plethora of mechanisms has been around for years but the current totality of scientific evidence points at a light no when it comes to spot reduction existing at least in the form that we often hear about now there is some evidence that spot reduction can exist in certain populations for example the untrained when engaging in different forms of exercise specifically resistance training but the idea that doing more direct work for a specific muscle group will burn substantially more fat from that area is unfortunately not true at the end of the day being in a calorie deficit and consistently engaging in resistance training is still your best bet to achieve the most desirable body composition

warm-ups for lifting are much simpler than you may think the current literature shows that as long as you're either doing a combination of a general and a specific warm-up or just a specific warm-up you're likely more than fine as far as your lifting performance in any given session goes when it comes to combination of a general and a specific warm-up the general part of the warm-up will usually have to be something that will allow you to get your heart rate up get warmer and that will be about that in many of the studies that are currently available the participants were instructed to do something along the lines of cycling for 10 to 20 minutes at around 60% of their maximum heart rate if you walk to your gym that could constitute your general warm-up and then you obviously have your lift specific warm-up which should ideally focus on you progressively increasing the load that you're using until you reach your working weight and as long as you are not going into the gym and just straight up starting your sets without any warm up whatsoever you're going to be more than fine

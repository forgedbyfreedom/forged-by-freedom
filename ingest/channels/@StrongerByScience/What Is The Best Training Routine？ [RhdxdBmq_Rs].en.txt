there are a lot of training routines out there but which one is the best well we do know that training a muscle twice a week is better than just once a week and more often may even be better especially with high volumes so let's break it down by training experience and how many days a week you're in the gym if you're a beginner who's been training for less than around 6 months let's say you're training 2 days a week a full body split can get you a frequency of twice a week for each muscle whereas if you're trading 4 days a week up or lower is a great option if you're an in immediate lifter who's training 3 or 5 days a week you have a wide range of approaches that can suit you from upper lower full body or even a push pull legs upper lower hybrid allowing you to get two days of training for each muscle per weekend for the advanced lifter the same applies that said these are minimums don't be afraid of experimenting with non-traditional splits as long as overall volume and intensity are reasonable

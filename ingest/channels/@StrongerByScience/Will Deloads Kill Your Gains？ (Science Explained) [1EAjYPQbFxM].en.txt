you may be overthinking D loads for strength and hypertrophy first of all D loads are not a magical tool that will somehow boost your strength and hypertrophy gains but their main purpose is to allow you to alleviate fatigue and get ready for your subsequent training by feeling more rested when it comes to implementing D loads in practice reactive D loads where you look at signs of fatigue in order to implement the D Lo is probably your best bet and the D Lo itself doesn't need to be anything more complicated than an easy week of training cut volume by 30 to 50% still perform some of your training don't sweat the details and get back to your regular training after you finish when looking at the current literature on D loads and specifically the first intervention study to ever look at the cono directly even a complete week off they don't have a negative effect on hypertrophy gains in resistance trained individuals so the next time you're feeling very fatigued take an easy week of training and don't sweat the details that much you're likely going to be fine

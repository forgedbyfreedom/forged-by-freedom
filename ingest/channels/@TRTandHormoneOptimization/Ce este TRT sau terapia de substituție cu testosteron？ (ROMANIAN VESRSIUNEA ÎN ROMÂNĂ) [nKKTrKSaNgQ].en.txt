What is TRT or Testosterone Replacement Therapy? TRT is an acronym for testosterone replacement therapy or testosterone substitution therapy often referred to as hormone therapy or androgen replacement therapy. TRT is a medical treatment used to treat low testosterone levels that occur with age or as a result of a medical condition. A man's body naturally produces less testosterone as he ages. This is a normal physiological process that occurs in his late 20s or early 30s. This gradual decline in testosterone levels most often does not produce noticeable symptoms, but as the years go by, the following symptoms may occur: erectile dysfunction, low libido, less frequent spontaneous erections or reduced sperm volume, sleep problems, loss of muscle mass and bone density, and unexplained weight gain.

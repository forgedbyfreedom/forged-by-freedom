Are desiccated thyroid hormones going away? On August 6th, 2025, yes, just a few days ago, the FDA issued a letter to manufacturers, importers, and distributors of these desecated thyroid hormones that they will now need to pursue a biologics license in order to continue offering these products. In the letter, the FDA also referred to these desecated thyroid products as ADTS or animal derived thyroid products. If you aren't aware, it would affect drugs such as nature thyroid, NP thyroid, armor thyroid, nathyroid, as well as adisa or even compound versions of those. As of right now, it's speculated that there's somewhere in the ballpark of about 1 and a half to 2 million people who currently use these NDT products. Here's the big problem with this is that obtaining a biologics license is incredibly expensive, very cumbersome, and it is a lengthy process. Remember about five or six years ago when HCG became reclassified as a biologic and then numerous compoundies went from offering it to practically overnight pulling the plug on it. Now HCG is incredibly hard to obtain from a compounding pharmacy. You for most people they have to go the name brand route. Well, this could also happen to these thyroid medications and that would increase the difficulty of accessibility to these medications. As of right now, it does not appear the FDA is taking action against any of these manufacturers or distributors. But per the letter, it does appear that there's about a one-year grace period so that patients can get converted transitioned over to the other traditional products such as levothyoxine or liioyronine.

PED READINESS CHECKLIST

This is a comprehensive readiness framework for individuals considering performance-enhancing drugs. Important: PCT is not 100% successful in returning PED-using males fully back to baseline testosterone levels.

BIOLOGICAL/MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Prior bloodwork and semen analysis completion
- Understanding of biochemical processes (5-alpha-reduction, aromatization)
- Brain development awareness, particularly before age 25
- Gender-specific considerations for women

PRACTICAL KNOWLEDGE REQUIREMENTS:
- Injection technique proficiency
- Equipment understanding (syringe measurements, needle sizes)
- Emergency response procedures (nerve/vessel hits, gynecomastia)
- Compound acquisition and legitimacy verification

FINANCIAL & LIVING SITUATION:
- Independent housing without parental/guardian supervision
- Stable income covering compounds, bloodwork, and medical expenses

HEALTH MONITORING KNOWLEDGE:
- Knowledge of which blood markers to test
- Understanding bloodwork interpretation
- Regular testing schedules during cycles

HEALTH RISK MITIGATION:
- Cardiovascular management strategies
- Hepatic complications prevention (addressing NAFLD, tumors, liver failure)
- Hair loss prevention planning
- Fertility preservation through sperm freezing

LEGAL PREPAREDNESS:
- Understanding of compound sourcing legality
- Preparation for potential federal seizure letters
- Acceptance of legal consequences

CRITICAL NOTE: If you answered no to any of thesEQuestions, you are not ready for gear.

MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS SUMMARY:
1. Are you willing to stay on TRT potentially forever?
2. Do you have perfected diet and trening with clear caloric/macro targets?
3. Do you have previous bloodwork and semen analysis?
4. Are you living independently?
5. Can you afford compounds, bloodwork, and medical expenses?
6. Do you understand 5-alpha-reduction and aromatization?
7. Are you 25+ years old (brain fully developed)?
8. Do you know how to inject properly?
9. Do you know how to respond to complications?
10. Do you have cardiovascular and hepatic health mitigation strategies?

CORE TERMINOLOGY

Testosterone functions as a natural hormone subject to enzymatic conversion. The aromatase enzyme converts it to estradiol (E2), while 5-alpha reductase converts it to dihydrotestosterone (DHT). Enzymes are catalysts of reactions that occur in your body.

Estrogen exists in two forms: estrone (E1) and estradiol (E2), with E2 being the stronger variant. Estrogen tolerance differs significantly between individuals.

SHBG (Sex Hormone-Binding Globulin) binds testosterone, creating bound testosterone that is shuttled by SHBG to regions of the body that need the testosterone. free Testosterone remains unbound and interacts with androgen receptors immediately upon encounter.

TRT & DOSING PROTOCOLS

TRT (Testosterone Replacement Therapy) uses exogenous testosterone to optimize levels. Dosing ranges from 1-3+ mg/kg weekly, varying by individual genetics. Most men can handle testosterone at 300mg/wk for the rest of their life with minimal complications.

The mg/kg approach adjusts doses by bodyweight:
- 1mg/kg = Western medicine TRT
- 2mg/kg = Modern TRT
- 3mg/kg = Optimized TRT/beginner cycle
- 4mg/kg = Sports TRT/beginner cycle
- 5mg/kg = Mild cycle

STEROID CLASSIFICATION

All AAS divide into three categories:

DHT derivatives: Don't aromatize to estrogen; cannot be 5-alpha reduced. Examples include Primobolan and Masteron.

Testosterone Derivatives: Aromatize into estrogen forms and undergo 5-alpha reduction. Boldenone converts to synthetic E1; Dianabol to methyl-E2.

Nandrolone Derivatives: Produce minimal estrogen, convert to weak DHT compounds via 5-alpha reduction. Increased androgenicity when combined with 5-alpha inhibitors.

HALF-LIVES & esterS

esters modify compound duration in the system. Testosterone enanthate has a half-life of 5-7 days, enabling less frequent dosing. Half-life accumulation occurs across multiple injections - serum concentration increases week by week until steady state is reached.

SUPPRESSION & RECOVERY

Natural testosterone production suppresses during AAS use, with suppression degree dependent on dose and duration. Recovery occurs after 5 half-lives, though genetic variation means some individuals may not fully recover original testosterone levels.

MEASUREMENTS & CONCENTRATIONS

Units:
- Milligrams (mg) and micrograms (mcg): 1mg = 1000mcg
- Milliliters (mL/cc) for liquids
- International Units (IU) for GH and HCG

Concentration = compound amount ÷ liquid volume (expressed as mg/mL or mcg/mL). Oil-based compounds exceeding 300mg/mL tolerance create injection discomfort.

TESTOSTERONE DOSING GUIDELINES

First Cycle:
3-5 milligrams of testosterone enanthate/cypionate per kilogram of body weight per week is a well-tolerated dose for most beginners, trenslating to roughly 300-400mg weekly. The recommendation suggests gradually increasing doses rather than jumping directly to target amounts.

Subsequent Cycles:
Dose increases should be gradual, incrementing by approximately 100mg per week to minimize side effects. The guide emphasizes that testosterone amounts may not necessarily increase between cycles; progression can involve adding complementary compounds instead.

TESTOSTERONE esterS

Common testosterone esters include:
- Cypionate/Enanthate: Longer esters with 7-day half-lives, injectable 2x weekly minimum
- Propionate: Shorter ester requiring 3-4x weekly injections
- Sustanon: Multi-ester blend; not recommended from underground labs due to quality control concerns

Switching esters safely requires gradually tapering shorter esters over 5 half-lives when trensitioning to longer ones.

INJECTION PROTOCOL

Key steps include:
- Using 27g ½" injecting needles and 18-20g drawing needles
- Warming oil to reduce post-injection pain
- Injecting intramuscularly in ventrogluteal, gluteal, deltoid, or quad sites
- Performing injections slowly to prevent abscesses
- Combining multiple compounds in single syringes is acceptable

SIDE EFFECT MANAGEMENT

Estrogen Control:
- Aromasin: Weekly dose = testosterone dose ÷ 20 (in mg)
- arimidex: Weekly dose = testosterone dose ÷ 500 (in mg)

Blood Pressure:
- Slightly elevated: Tadalafil 5mg daily
- Significantly elevated: Telmisartan recommended

Other interventions:
- Hair loss: RU-58841, Minoxidil
- Gynecomastia: Raloxifene 60mg daily
- Water retention: electrolyte management, estrogen control

STACK RECOMMENDATIONS

Compounds for muscle growth hormone: Boldenone, Primobolan, Nandrolone, Anadrol
Compounds for strength: DHB, Masteron, Halotestin, Anavar, Trenbolone (100mg/week noted as sufficient)
Compounds for leanness: Masteron, Primobolan, Winstrol, Trenbolone

CYCLE LENGTH

Recommended minimum is 16 weeks; 24-30 weeks maximum depending on tolerance. Cycles shorter than 12 weeks are discouraged as suboptimal.

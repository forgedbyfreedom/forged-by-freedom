PED READINESS CHECKLIST

This is a comprehensive readiness framework for individuals considering performance-enhancing drugs. Important: PCT is not 100% successful in returning PED-using males fully back to baseline testosterone levels.

BIOLOGICAL/MEDICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Prior bloodwork and semen analysis completion
- Understanding of biochemical processes (5-alpha-reduction, aromatization)
- Brain development awareness, particularly before age 25
- Gender-specific considerations for women

PRACTICAL KNOWLEDGE REQUIREMENTS:
- Injection technique proficiency
- Equipment understanding (syringe measurements, needle sizes)
- Emergency response procedures (nerve/vessel hits, gynecomastia)
- Compound acquisition and legitimacy verification

FINANCIAL & LIVING SITUATION:
- Independent housing without parental/guardian supervision
- Stable income covering compounds, bloodwork, and medical expenses

HEALTH MONITORING KNOWLEDGE:
- Knowledge of which blood markers to test
- Understanding bloodwork interpretation
- Regular testing schedules during cycles

HEALTH RISK MITIGATION:
- Cardiovascular management strategies
- Hepatic complications prevention (addressing NAFLD, tumors, liver failure)
- Hair loss prevention planning
- Fertility preservation through sperm freezing

LEGAL PREPAREDNESS:
- Understanding of compound sourcing legality
- Preparation for potential federal seizure letters
- Acceptance of legal consequences

CRITICAL NOTE: If you answered no to any of thesEQuestions, you are not ready for gear.

MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS SUMMARY:
1. Are you willing to stay on TRT potentially forever?
2. Do you have perfected diet and trening with clear caloric/macro targets?
3. Do you have previous bloodwork and semen analysis?
4. Are you living independently?
5. Can you afford compounds, bloodwork, and medical expenses?
6. Do you understand 5-alpha-reduction and aromatization?
7. Are you 25+ years old (brain fully developed)?
8. Do you know how to inject properly?
9. Do you know how to respond to complications?
10. Do you have cardiovascular and hepatic health mitigation strategies?

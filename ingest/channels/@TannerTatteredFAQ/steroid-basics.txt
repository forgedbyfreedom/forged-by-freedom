CORE TERMINOLOGY

Testosterone functions as a natural hormone subject to enzymatic conversion. The aromatase enzyme converts it to estradiol (E2), while 5-alpha reductase converts it to dihydrotestosterone (DHT). Enzymes are catalysts of reactions that occur in your body.

Estrogen exists in two forms: estrone (E1) and estradiol (E2), with E2 being the stronger variant. Estrogen tolerance differs significantly between individuals.

SHBG (Sex Hormone-Binding Globulin) binds testosterone, creating bound testosterone that is shuttled by SHBG to regions of the body that need the testosterone. free Testosterone remains unbound and interacts with androgen receptors immediately upon encounter.

TRT & DOSING PROTOCOLS

TRT (Testosterone Replacement Therapy) uses exogenous testosterone to optimize levels. Dosing ranges from 1-3+ mg/kg weekly, varying by individual genetics. Most men can handle testosterone at 300mg/wk for the rest of their life with minimal complications.

The mg/kg approach adjusts doses by bodyweight:
- 1mg/kg = Western medicine TRT
- 2mg/kg = Modern TRT
- 3mg/kg = Optimized TRT/beginner cycle
- 4mg/kg = Sports TRT/beginner cycle
- 5mg/kg = Mild cycle

STEROID CLASSIFICATION

All AAS divide into three categories:

DHT derivatives: Don't aromatize to estrogen; cannot be 5-alpha reduced. Examples include Primobolan and Masteron.

Testosterone Derivatives: Aromatize into estrogen forms and undergo 5-alpha reduction. Boldenone converts to synthetic E1; Dianabol to methyl-E2.

Nandrolone Derivatives: Produce minimal estrogen, convert to weak DHT compounds via 5-alpha reduction. Increased androgenicity when combined with 5-alpha inhibitors.

HALF-LIVES & esterS

esters modify compound duration in the system. Testosterone enanthate has a half-life of 5-7 days, enabling less frequent dosing. Half-life accumulation occurs across multiple injections - serum concentration increases week by week until steady state is reached.

SUPPRESSION & RECOVERY

Natural testosterone production suppresses during AAS use, with suppression degree dependent on dose and duration. Recovery occurs after 5 half-lives, though genetic variation means some individuals may not fully recover original testosterone levels.

MEASUREMENTS & CONCENTRATIONS

Units:
- Milligrams (mg) and micrograms (mcg): 1mg = 1000mcg
- Milliliters (mL/cc) for liquids
- International Units (IU) for GH and HCG

Concentration = compound amount ÷ liquid volume (expressed as mg/mL or mcg/mL). Oil-based compounds exceeding 300mg/mL tolerance create injection discomfort.

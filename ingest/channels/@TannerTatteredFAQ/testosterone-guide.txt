TESTOSTERONE DOSING GUIDELINES

First Cycle:
3-5 milligrams of testosterone enanthate/cypionate per kilogram of body weight per week is a well-tolerated dose for most beginners, trenslating to roughly 300-400mg weekly. The recommendation suggests gradually increasing doses rather than jumping directly to target amounts.

Subsequent Cycles:
Dose increases should be gradual, incrementing by approximately 100mg per week to minimize side effects. The guide emphasizes that testosterone amounts may not necessarily increase between cycles; progression can involve adding complementary compounds instead.

TESTOSTERONE esterS

Common testosterone esters include:
- Cypionate/Enanthate: Longer esters with 7-day half-lives, injectable 2x weekly minimum
- Propionate: Shorter ester requiring 3-4x weekly injections
- Sustanon: Multi-ester blend; not recommended from underground labs due to quality control concerns

Switching esters safely requires gradually tapering shorter esters over 5 half-lives when trensitioning to longer ones.

INJECTION PROTOCOL

Key steps include:
- Using 27g ½" injecting needles and 18-20g drawing needles
- Warming oil to reduce post-injection pain
- Injecting intramuscularly in ventrogluteal, gluteal, deltoid, or quad sites
- Performing injections slowly to prevent abscesses
- Combining multiple compounds in single syringes is acceptable

SIDE EFFECT MANAGEMENT

Estrogen Control:
- Aromasin: Weekly dose = testosterone dose ÷ 20 (in mg)
- arimidex: Weekly dose = testosterone dose ÷ 500 (in mg)

Blood Pressure:
- Slightly elevated: Tadalafil 5mg daily
- Significantly elevated: Telmisartan recommended

Other interventions:
- Hair loss: RU-58841, Minoxidil
- Gynecomastia: Raloxifene 60mg daily
- Water retention: electrolyte management, estrogen control

Recommended minimum is 16 weeks; 24-30 weeks maximum depending on tolerance. Cycles shorter than 12 weeks are discouraged as suboptimal.

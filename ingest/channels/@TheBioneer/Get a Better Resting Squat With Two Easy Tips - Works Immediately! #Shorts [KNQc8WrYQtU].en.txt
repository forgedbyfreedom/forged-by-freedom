hey there guys getting into a deep resting squat is a fundamental human movement and something that everybody should be capable of unfortunately due to hours and hours of sitting and shoes that raise our heels off the floor many of us can't get into this position without falling over or feeling uncomfortable there are lots of drills to combat this such as holding something heavy in front of you to weigh yourself down but instead what I'm going to recommend is that you simply practice going down straight whatever feels comfortable and then allowing your heels to raise off the floor if they must as much as is necessary by doing this you're placing a small amount of pressure on the ankles to improve that range of motion whilst being in a comfortable resting position over time if you do this enough it's going to gradually get to the point where you're no longer raising your heels off the ground at all or you are very slightly and another tip if first thing in the morning you're a little bit too stiff to get into that position just try going down as far as you can a few times you'll find that each time you do you get a little bit deeper

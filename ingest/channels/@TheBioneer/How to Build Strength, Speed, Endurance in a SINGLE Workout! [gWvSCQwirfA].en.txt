How do you build strength, speed, endurance, and agility all at the same time? The answer is surprisingly simple. By challenging each of these traits in a single workout. Start with explosive movements when your nervous system is fresh. Move on to lifting heavy weights or using more challenging calisthenics. Then increase the reps and lower the weight to target your strength endurance. End on some mobility for greater body control. You can even combine some of these movements into a single mechanical drop set. You're now getting the endurance that comes from pumping out lots of reps, but also the strength and power from those heavier moves at the start. Some will point out that you can't get as strong as you would do when focusing purely on strength. Unless you're a competitive powerlter, that is simply irrelevant. You can still get extremely strong, and it's highly unlikely you'll need to worry about that ceiling for a long time. And what's the point of being very strong if you guess out after a minute of exertion?

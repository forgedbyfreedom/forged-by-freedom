train harder than last time as coach Greg would say and this is such good advice because it simplifies Progressive overload perfectly logging every single workout and having precise targets and goals is all good and well but ultimately it's a fragile approach that leaves little room for improvisation but if you simply train the best you can on that day every time you work out you will gradually see yourself improve that can mean lifting heavier or it can mean lifting with a slower Tempo it can be really feeling the stretch or it can mean doing some more reps this is why I say you can train outside with logs and tree branches you don't necessarily need weights with set numbers some days you won't feel great and you won't smash PRS but as long as you gave it your best shot the cumulative result will be one of constant progress

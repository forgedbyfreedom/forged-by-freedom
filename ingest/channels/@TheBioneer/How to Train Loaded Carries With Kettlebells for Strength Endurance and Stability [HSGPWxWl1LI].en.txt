The Kettle Bell Loaded carry all you're doing here is carrying one or two kettle bells by your sides and going for a walk I like to use a finger grip which gets the forearms pumped and you should also maintain a slight shrug in the traps not exaggerated but just don't let your arms hang like dead weights by your side try and keep your torso straight and avoid rocking from side to side as you walk this movement can be done for distance or for time or you can increase the weight to progress this builds a massive strength endurance and mental toughness while also training the forearms traps core and hips they improve your gate cycle while also challenging you with a movement that you genuinely have to perform in the real world and for added benefit doing a one-sided carry called a suitcase carry or a briefcase carry will add to that anti- lateral flexion and this strengthens the ql the glutes and the obliques to provide further stability if you don't have space to take your C bells for a walk or don't want to go outside stand on the spot and raise your legs to do slow marching A's or 100 UPS try and keep your torso in the kettle bells as still as you possibly can while switching legs

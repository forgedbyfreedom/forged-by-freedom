Super Mover is the new training program and ebook from the Bioneer. The goal to develop an athletic physique that's powerful and built for movement. Whether you want to sprint, vault a fence, climb a wall, or hike up a mountain, Super Mover can help you do it better. Movement training shouldn't be about fancy circus tricks. It should be about freedom, developing strength, coordination, control, and resilience for running, lifting, jumping, climbing, playing, and whatever else life throws at you. Because what's the point of strength if you can't use it? If you get tired too quickly, if you're too uncoordinated, too stiff. With Super Mover, you'll build strength, sure, but you'll also feel energetic and free in your body, supercharged even. And when you build a body that can move, you start to look like a jacked anime hero. In this program, I have distilled my training concepts to their purest form. With as little as four 40-minute workouts per week, you can begin to move better and more explosively, reduce pain and stiffness, and reveal a badass ripped physique. No gym membership required. Super Mover helps you to do this by combining fun skill-based movements with high rep calisthenics and optional kettle bell training. But it also goes a lot deeper for those who want to explore what their bodies can truly do. This program is suitable for everyone from beginners to advanced athletes. It can be performed from home with little to no equipment and it's fun. Becoming functional is just the beginning. Now it's time to move.

in preparation for the upcoming Assassin's Creed Shadows the let's players play as both Samurai and Shinobi Ubisoft asked me to design a workout that would help anybody move more like an assassin from the series here are just a few of the movements I chose to include the Tactical pull-up this is a pull-up performed with the thumb on top of the bar this mimics the body and hand position you would use when climbing a wall Precision broad jump the Precision broad jump challenges you to jump forward rather than straight up and you can make it a Precision broad jump by adding a small Target to land on such as a towel crawl the crawl is a fantastic quadrilateral movement for learning body awareness and throughout the series protagonists have used it to crawl past obstacles and to remain out of line of sight squat walk the squat walk or duck walk is a great endurance challenge for the legs and we've already seen na use it in the new game to track targets along the edge of rooftops add these to your routine in preparation for Assassin's Creed Shadows thanks to Ubisoft for sponsoring this video

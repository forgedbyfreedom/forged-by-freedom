Training at the gym no doubt has a lot of advantages. You're comfortable indoors and you can precisely control each variable to measure progress, but it's also artificial and sterile. Getting outside means lifting logs and using branches. Every movement is different, building stronger tissue and grip. It means running on uneven terrain, building stability and propriception. And it means exposing your body to fresh air, sunlight, and stimulating vistas. It improves your skin, your breathing, your brain, your sleep. You'll need to navigate, endure different weather. In short, it trains the body like nothing else. It's how we evolved to move and to challenge ourselves. So, let go of arbitrary numbers and embrace the chaos of the outdoors.

so let's look at all the different things we're working here for starters because we're in that lower push-up position we're working our chest our shoulders and our triceps just like doing a push-up at the same time you're bracing the core to keep yourself nice and steady you're also rotating the core you're turning yourself in the transverse plane the rotational plane and as you're doing this you're bringing your legs up to the side and this is going to challenge your hip mobility and this is something that again a lot of us really leave out this is actually movement in the frontal plane so you have movement in all three planes of motion from one single exercise then as you push through you are of course working the calves a little bit the glutes a little bit the quads a little bit you're even using your lats a little bit as you pull yourself along the ground as a contralateral movement this means we're moving our right arm at the same time as our left leg this requires concentration and actually working memory is you have to coordinate those limbs together over time this is going to become more natural to you and that translates to moving your left hip and right shoulder in other movements such as throwing a ball throwing a punch even running

This is the most effective leg training protocol I have ever used. It builds highly athletic legs, bigger quads and thighs, more mobility, and it makes you faster and more explosive and can be done in less than 5 minutes with minimal recovery needed. You're walking around your house, garden, or gym while squatting deeply on every single repetition for high repetitions. I've been setting a timer for 1 minute and doing three sets with a 30-se secondond break. And it gets pretty hard by the second set due to the sheer amount of pump and burn. The huge amount of blood that builds up in the legs creates a massive pump that translates to bigger legs over time. The repetitive movement lets you work on your squat mechanics and mobility for high reps, creasing the groove. And the rapid cadence gives you a more concentric dominant movement that actually minimizes recovery time, allowing you to spam this for high volume and high frequency. The best part though, it can be done literally anywhere with no equipment and it's fun.

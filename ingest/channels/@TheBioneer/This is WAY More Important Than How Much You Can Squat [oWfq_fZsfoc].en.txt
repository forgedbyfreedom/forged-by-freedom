I think being able to squat a huge amount of weight is a really really cool thing but to me it doesn't really make sense to focus all your efforts on squatting really heavy amounts if you haven't focused any attention on lower body Mobility getting into you know sideways squats for example CAC squats pistol squats being able to keep your back nice and straight in these lower positions and having good range of motion not only in the sagittal plane but the frontal plane as well because why be able to lift something up really really heavy in one very specific movement pattern that you won't use that often when you can't just dip down freely into any position you like on the ground a modern lifestyles are so restrictive that many of us literally can't move with free range of motion in three dimensions when we're low to the ground and I think that's really a shame and unfortunately a lot of what we do at the gym only compounds on this rather than liberating us to move how we want

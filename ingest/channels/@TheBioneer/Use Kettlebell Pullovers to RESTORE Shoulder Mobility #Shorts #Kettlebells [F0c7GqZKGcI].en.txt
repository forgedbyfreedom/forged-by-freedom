another great option for shoulder health is the kettle bell pull over and this will give you more overhead range of motion and work the lats in a completely different way lie on the ground again you can do this with your legs straight but I recommend having them bent with the feet on the floor now take one kettle bell and press it from the ground with it right above you you're going to let the arms fall backwards over your head keeping them straight now you're just going to pull the weight back to the starting position keeping the arms straight and really focusing on engaging the lats as you do this is a pull over not a tricep extension there are few ways to grip the kettle bell and the way you hold it will impact on the range of motion you achieve before the weight touches the ground this is a weighted stretch for the lats which is what makes it so good helping you to get your hands overhead for other movements like the Turkish getup or like the handstand for added points try and keep your core nicely engaged and keep your pelvis posteriorly rotated that means trying to touch your lower back against the ground and keep it there when you do perform movements like the handstand the aim is to keep the core engaged and the glutes tight

the Kettlebell jump squat is as the name suggests a squat with a jump simple yes but it also is extremely effective at improving jump height and running speed as demonstrated by numerous studies the weight is perfect for increasing power too much weight and the movement becomes slow and grinding that doesn't train the nervous system for explosiveness the ideal weight depends on the movement but it's generally thought to be up to 30% of your body weight it's also great because you need to keep your body upright despite that front loaded resistance this means you're extending the knees and driving through the Torso at the same time as extending the ankles and this is what we need to generate maximum leaping and sprinting power as you land make sure to absorb that impact with your feet and knees for maximum explosiveness try to Spring straight back up to capitalize on the stretch shortening cycle what I love to do is to follow a few kettle bell jump squats immediately with regular jump squats I benefit from that post activation potentiation and it's a killer drop set

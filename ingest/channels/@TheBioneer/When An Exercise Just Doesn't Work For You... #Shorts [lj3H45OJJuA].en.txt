one of the biggest mistakes you can make when it comes to your training is assuming that exercises will just work for you right away when you try it it's hurting and feeling awkward or it doesn't Target the right muscles maybe you don't feel it working at all what you need to remember is that exercises are a skill and sometimes the key is to learn how to do the exercise efficiently before you can start to get the gains from it for example I was doing tuck planche holds for ages before I realized that my arms weren't actually fully locked out I heard the cue somewhere to drill my arm into the ground rotate my elbow to really feel it lock out and that changed everything suddenly I started making gains from the movement and similarly wall push-ups felt really awkward for me I'd always end up arching my back too much my feet would drag on the wall and I don't have many walls to do them on I Found A variation where I simply bend my legs and keep them in position on the wall and this worked much better for me for some of you sometimes it's going to feel like every exercise feels this way don't be disheartened think of your training as learning the exercises first and then you can worry about getting the results from them later on down the line it's all part of the process and bye for now

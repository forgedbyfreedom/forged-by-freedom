Five nutrition mistakes almost everybody makes. Only focusing on restricting calories, not focusing on what's called energy flux. In other words, it's more important to move more and eat more than it is to eat less and move more. That's just a recipe for a crashed metabolism. You want to ramp yourself up. Move a lot and eat a fair bit too, but still put yourself in that slight deficit. Not eating enough fiber. And not just because it fills you up, but because fiber plays a huge role in how our cells ultimately receive a signal to process glucose and fat. Now, I'm not saying eating a high amount of fat is bad, but one of the biggest mistakes is eating too much fat. Remember, there's nine calories per gram of fat. It's way easier to overeat fat than it is any other macro. Not eating enough protein. 20% of the calories in protein end up getting burned just as heat. Going to Taco Bell.

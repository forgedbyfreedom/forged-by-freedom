probably the single most powerful keto tip that i ever came across in 60 seconds is to periodically reduce your fat intake this does not mean that fats are not good on keto fats are great on keto they help stimulate ketone production ketogenesis in the liver but think about it like this if you're constantly conditioning your body to receive fats from an external source i.e your diet then it essentially gets kind of spoiled for lack of a better term it gets really good at utilizing fat that's coming in from the fuel sources that you're consuming but it doesn't get so good at utilizing your stored lipids and most of the people that i talk to that are doing a ketogenic diet want to do keto because they want their body to start using their own body fat their own tissue so periodically every other day or so reduce the fat intake dramatically and increase the protein intake that way your body has no choice but to start liberating stored fat but you're not going negative in calories as much because you're still consuming the protein

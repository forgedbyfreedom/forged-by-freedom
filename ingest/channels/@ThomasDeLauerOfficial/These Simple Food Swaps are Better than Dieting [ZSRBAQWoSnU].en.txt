Here's a few food swaps that might be slightly better choices. Now, in no way, shape, or form am I saying that these foods are bad. I'm just saying it might be a slightly better option to choose one of the swaps. Instead of salami or regular deli meat, have pushcido. Pushcido is just ham and salt if it's good quality stuff. Instead of the orange sweet potatoes, try the purple sweet potatoes. The orange potatoes have a lot more caratenoids, but the purple sweet potatoes have a lot more in the way of total antioxidant count. Try swapping out jerky for bill tong. Usually lower sodium and usually less preservatives and usually cured in a little bit more natural way. Try replacing almonds with macadamia nuts. You're getting less of the omega sixes. You're getting higher monounsaturated fats. Just a higher quality nut overall. Even though they're a little bit more expensive, you probably shouldn't be going to town on them anyway. Steamed veggies are awesome. I'm a fan of them. But try fermented veggies instead. And when you have them fermented, you're obviously getting a prebiotic effect.

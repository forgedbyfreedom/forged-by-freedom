I love visiting Mattel Children's Hospital UCLA where I have seen firsthand that art has the power to help kids heal one of their amazing programs is their children's holiday card art program which helps kids to be kids and express their creativity kids who are in the hospital for a long time get to work with artists to create beautiful holiday cards and this year's collection features artwork from patients and a Barbie collector's card designed by Mattel proceeds from the cards will benefit the chase Child Life program at Mattel Children's Hospital UCLA and will be available in mid-september at www.ua.org holiday cards please check it out and help support this great program

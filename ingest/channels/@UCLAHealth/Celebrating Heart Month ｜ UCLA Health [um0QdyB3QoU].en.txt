this month all of our babies at UCLA Santa Monica and UCLA Ronald Reagan will be wearing red beanies that were knitted either by patients or survivors of heart disease to really raise the awareness one out of a hundred babies is born with a heart defect the most important single thing we can do to improve the outcome of all these children born with heart disease is to detect the heart disease early preferably even before the baby is born there is a very high likelihood that an individual or someone we know is going to be impacted by heart disease and indeed more people died of heart-related problems than all other medical problems combined so we really want to encourage people to use the month of February to become more educated about what you can do to prevent heart disease what the latest treatments are so that then you are a resource to helping friends and families and helping us combat this disease

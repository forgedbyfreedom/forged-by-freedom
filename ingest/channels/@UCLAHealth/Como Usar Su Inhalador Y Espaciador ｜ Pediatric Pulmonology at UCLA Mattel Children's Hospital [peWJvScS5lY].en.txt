How to use your inhaler and spacer: Shake the inhaler for 10 seconds. Remove the cap from the inhaler. Remove the stage from the spacer. Connect it to the spacer. Exhale completely. Close your lips around the mouthpiece. Press the inhaler down. Inhale slowly and deeply. Hold your breath for 10 seconds if you can. Then exhale slowly. If you need another inhalation of the medication, wait one minute and repeat the process.

my child gets a lot of sore throats does he need his tonsils out kids get a lot of sore throats and this is very normal and it does not necessarily mean that your child needs their tonsils out if they get a lot of sore throats there are actually now very specific guidelines recommending tonsillectomy based on sore throats and when we say sore throats we really mean strep tonsillitis three episodes per year for three years five episodes per year for two years or seven episodes per year over a one year period that would be an indication for your child getting his or her tonsils out other associated factors such as loud snoring big tonsils history of mononucleosis a tonsillar abscess or a peritonsillar abscess other sorts of illnesses associated with tonsillitis that may also be an indication for tonsillectomy but sore throats in and of themselves do not necessarily mean that your child needs their tonsils out

As a float, you basically go to different clinics and fill in vacancies or people that are on vacation. Basically, any type of need, we fill it. One of the biggest benefits of being a part of the ambulatoratory float team is you get to see all different clinics. You get to learn what you like, what's good over here, what works over here. You get to learn where you thrive. I think that's the biggest part of being on an ambulatory float team, learning your strength and weaknesses.

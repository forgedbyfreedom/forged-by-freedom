hello my name is Dr Dana Chase I am an associate professor in the division of gynecologic oncology at UCLA I'm going to speak to you about one of my favorite topics survivorship in gynecologic cancer patients survivorship is defined as the time of cancer diagnosis and for the remainder of life this means that survivorship begins when you first receive the news that you have cancer until the end of your life whether or not you are cancer free or still being treated for cancer a survivorship plan can be created for different parts of the treatment Journey which includes number one receiving your diagnosis and planning for treatment number two in treatment such as surgery chemotherapy or radiation number three in remission but not yet considered cured number four being treated for recurrent cancer which is also considered living with cancer or number five considered cured of cancer survivorship plans can change depending on the status of your cancer and can help you and your caregiver or caregivers to manage the physical and emotional challenges of cancer like for example side effects of treatment fatigue fertility and sexual health concerns or mental health concerns in general regardless of what category of survivorship you fall in you can expect cancer related side effects and or treatment related side effects some of these side effects are out of your control and the goal is to have a team to figure out how to manage living with these issues however there are areas that you may have more control of including nutrition exercise sleep and stress these are four areas that a patient and the patient's team can help to improve survivorship focusing on these topics can improve your tolerance of treatment and even the outcomes of treatment therefore a survivorship plan should include a plan to focus and improve these four areas plan can also include your treatment history which is good to share with other doctors and people in your life or important dates like the dates of treatment surgery follow-up care and treatment Milestones like when you may be considered cured another area in survivorship that is in your control is where you choose to receive treatment ideally you find a center that can assist you in not only improving your chance of a long remission or cure but can also focus on such critical factors as nutrition exercise sleep and stress choosing

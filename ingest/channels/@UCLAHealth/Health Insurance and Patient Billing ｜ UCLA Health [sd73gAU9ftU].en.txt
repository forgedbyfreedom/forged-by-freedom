health insurance is designed to help you and your family cover the cost of medical care health insurance works similarly to car insurance you pay a monthly premium for your insurance whether you use it or not if you or your family gets sick or injured and needs health care your insurance covers a portion of the total cost however you too will have to pay a portion of the cost which is considered your out-of-pocket expense there are three types of out-of-pocket expenses the deductible the co-payment and coinsurance the deductible is the amount you or your family have agreed to pay before your insurance begins paying for your health care for example if your deductible is $1,000 your insurance plan will not pay anything until you pay the first thousand dollars of medical expenses after your deductible is met you may still have to pay for other types of expenses one type of out-of-pocket expense is a co-payment or copay your co-payment is the fixed amount you pay every time you go to a doctor even if you've met your deductible this amount can vary by the type of provider you see for example $20 for a primary care physician visit versus $30 for a specialist visit your coinsurance is the percentage of the bill for which you're responsible for example if your coinsurance is 20% and your office visit is $200 your coinsurance is 40 dollars that's 20% of $200 because coinsurance is a percentage of the total amount of your physician services it can widely vary based on the cost of the service so it is difficult to predict and plan for your deductibles co-pays and coinsurance responsibilities vary based on the type of insurance plan you have there are several types of health insurance plans major ones include HMOs and PPOs if you have an HMO plan you need a referral from your primary care physician for specialist services and with a referral you are only responsible for a co-payment if you choose to receive medical services from a specialist without a referral you are responsible for the full amount of the charges which can be very costly to you if you have a PPO plan you can choose to see any doctor without a referral your out-of-pocket expense may include deductibles co-payments or coinsurance however your out-of-pocket expenses could be considerably higher if the doctor is not part of your insurance plans network you should always check with your insurance plan to determine whether a doctor is considered as in-network before scheduling an appointment after your insurance pays its portion of the medical expense you will receive a statement for

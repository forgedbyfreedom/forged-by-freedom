there are almost 880,000 people experiencing homelessness across LA County the last research study showed that only 5 to 10% of them were receiving Medical Care from the existing Street medicine programs what UCLA health is trying to do is to take all of the things that it means to be UCLA health and deliver that same Equitable care to our unhoused neighbors our team of doctors nurses social workers and psychiatrist provide a range of urgent and Primary Care Services we operate out of our mobile units across Los Angeles our vans provide our team with a full spectrum of equipment for lab tests prescriptions and onboard Wi-Fi to allow seamless coordination of care between our mobile unit Hospitals and Clinics our team plans to expand our Fleet and Workforce extending our reach to more areas of high need across LA County

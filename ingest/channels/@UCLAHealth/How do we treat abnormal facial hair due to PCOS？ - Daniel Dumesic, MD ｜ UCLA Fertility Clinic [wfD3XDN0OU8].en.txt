Hello, my name is Dan Domesk. I'm division head of reproductive endocrinology and infertility at UCLA. My practice focuses on women with polycystic ovary syndrome who commonly present with abnormal facial hair or menstrual irregularity due to elevations of the male hormone testosterone. They commonly ask me, can you treat these symptoms that I have? And my answer is absolutely yes. With proper hormonal management and physical removal of the hair follicles, we can provide these individuals an outstanding quality of life. Thank you.

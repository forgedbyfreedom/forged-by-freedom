how does pain turn into chronic pain so chronic pain is a disorder of the nervous system just like high blood pressure diabetes their disorders of the different systems of the body there's the phenomenon in medical terminology we call central sensitization where your nerves tend to get sensitized to pain so even after the insult or the trauma has long gone your nerves tend to be sensitive and they keep on sending a signal to the brain and the brain keeps on perceiving the pain that's what leads to chronic pain

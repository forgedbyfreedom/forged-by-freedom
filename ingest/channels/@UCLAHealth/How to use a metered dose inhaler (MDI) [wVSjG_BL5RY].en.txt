hello I'm Dr Hassen with us help's adult asthma program in Thousand Oaks today we will be discussing how to use your pressurized meter dose inhaler we have broken down inhaler technique into eight discrete Steps step number one shake your inhaler to mix the medication with the propellant meter dose inhalers contain a combination of drug and pressurized gas that helps deliver the medication step two the first time that you use your inhaler you will have to discard the first two to three sprays this is called priming the inhaler this step does not have to be repeated after the first time the inhaler is used unless the inhaler has not been used for a week or more step three first exhale completely and then put the mouthpiece of the inhaler in your mouth and start to take a slow deep inhalation step four this is the most important part of inhaler technique you have to deliver the medication as you are taking a slow deep breath in order to entrain the air and medication from the inhaler down into your lungs if the step is not performed correctly the majority of the medication will wind up in your mouth and in the back of your throat step five complete the inhalation and hold your breath for roughly 6 to 10 seconds this step is also crucial because the deep breath pulls the medication into your lungs and mediumsized Airways and then holding your breath allows the medication to deposit into the small Airways by a process which is driven by gravity step six exhale gently step seven if your inhaler prescription calls for two inhalations per use then rest for 30 seconds to 1 minute and repeat steps 1 through six step eight if your inhaler contains an inhaled steroid rinse your mouth with water and gargle for roughly 5 to 10 seconds before spitting out do not swallow the water after you inse your mouth please remember to repeat these steps every time that you use your meter dose inhaler practice makes perfect and as your Technique improves so will the effectiveness of the medications

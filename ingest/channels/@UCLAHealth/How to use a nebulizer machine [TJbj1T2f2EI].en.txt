hello I'm Dr hm of you Sil Health's adult asthma program in Thousand Oaks in this video we will discuss how to use a nebulizer machine for your asthma symptoms some patients find that if they have difficulty coordinating classic met or dose or dry powder inhalers the nebulized medications work better for them step one attach the hosing to the nebulizer machine and mouth piece and plug in the nebulizer the nebulizer works by turning a liquid medication into a Mist that is then inhaled into your lungs step two fill the medication vial with the liquid that you have been prescribed as the medication is turned from a liquid into a Mist you will then inhale the medication from the hosing and mouthpiece down into your lungs once you turn the nebulizer on it will be ready for use step three put the mouthpiece in your mouth or face mask over your mouth and nose and breathe normally for 5 to 10 minutes or until all of the liquid has been nebulized through the machine make sure that your lips make a Tight Seal around the mouthpiece while breathing in a face mask may also be used step four rinse your mouth if your nebulized medication includes an inhaled steroid step five remember to regularly clean your equipment of soap and water thank you for watching this video remember to use your nebulizer machine consistently for optimal control of your asthma symptoms

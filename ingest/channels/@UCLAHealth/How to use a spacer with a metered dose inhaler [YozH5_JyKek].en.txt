hello I'm Dr hlam with USA Health's adult asthma program in Fen Oaks in this video we will discuss how to use a spacer with a meter dose inhaler spacers are useful because they eliminate much of timing and coordination comes along with using a meter dose inhaler this typically improves medication delivery down into the lungs and reduces the amount of medication that is wasted in the mouth and throat step one please refer to our meter do inhaler video regarding priming and preparing your inhaler for use step two when the inhaler is ready to be used attach the spacer to the mouthpiece the spacer is a chamber into which the medication will be delivered since the medication will be delivered into the spacer it will be suspended in the air of the spacer making it easier to take a deep inhalation and in training more of the medication down into the lungs step three exhale completely and then close your lips around the mouthpiece of the spacer then deliver the medication into the spacer take a slow deep inhalation step five you will hold your breath for 6 to 10 seconds the inhalation has to be completed while the medication is suspended in the air of the spacer and before it is deposited into the sidewalls of the device otherwise the spacer will not be effective step step six as with a regular meter dose inhaler you will rest for between 30 to 60 seconds between inhalations and you will rinse your mouth out with water if your medication includes an inhaled steroid your spacer should be cleaned periodically to help make it most effective remember to use your spacer every time that you use your meter dose inhaler to help optimize inhaler efficiency as well as medication delivery

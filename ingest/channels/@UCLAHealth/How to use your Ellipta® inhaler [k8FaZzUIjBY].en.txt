hello I'm Dr Merchant with UC Health's adult asthma program in Tazo in this video we will discuss how to use your ellipta inhaler Slide the cover down until you hear a click if the counter does not count down as you hear the click the inhaler will not deliver the medicine call your healthcare provider or your Pharmacy if that happens to avoid wasting doses do not open and close the cover without inhaling the medication or you will lose the dose lost doses are held in the inhaler and it is not possible to accidentally take a double dose or an extra dose in one inhalation you do not need to shake this kind of inhaler your inhaler is now ready to use while holding your inhaler away from your mouth breathe out fully do not breathe out into the mouthpiece do not brck the air vent with your fingers close your lips firmly around the mouthpiece take a long steady deep breath in through your mouth don't breathe in through your nose remove the inhaler from your mouth and hold your breath for about 6 seconds breathe out you may not feel or taste the medicine even even if you're using it correctly routine creaming is not required Slide the cover up as far as it will go rinse them out with water and then spit the water out don't swallow the water

hello I'm Dr Merchant with UC Health adult asthma program inen o in this video we will discuss how to use a peak flow meter a peak flow meter helps you monitor your asthma by measuring how well air moves out of your lungs follow these steps to use it correctly step one prepare the Peak from meter by moving the indicator to zero standing up will give you the most accurate reading step two take a deep breath in step three put the mouth piece into your mouth make a tight seat around it with your lips step four blow out hard and fast and a single breath step five look at the number on the scale where the indicator has stopped this is your Peak for reading step six perform the test three times and record the highest reading step seven it is important to keep track of your readings over time and share them with your provider

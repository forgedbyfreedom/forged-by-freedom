is there a difference between opioids and narcotics so opioids and narcotics are synonymous however they're not quite the same thing narcotics are typically a more generalized term used by law enforcement or the legal system opioids are a more specific term that refers to a certain type of medications that we use to treat chronic pain opioids are medications such as percocet Norco hydrocodone hydromorphone these medications carry significant side-effects therefore we try to avoid them as much as possible in the treatment of chronic pain

here's some helpful information to keep in mind as you prepare to breastfeed wear a firm supportive bra expose your nipples to the air whenever possible especially after feeding or expressing milk feed your baby on demand without limits on frequency or duration of feedings newborns need to feed eight or more times in 24 hours if you have concerns about your milk supply call your provider or a lactation consultant in most cases there is no need to use soap on your nipples the scent or taste of soap can interfere with the baby latching change bra liners when wet and avoid using bra liners with plastic backs which trap moisture breastfulness and tenderness is normal nursing your baby as often as possible will help relieve engorgement warm breasts tend to release milk faster apply warm moist towels to your breasts briefly before feeding but keep in mind too much heat or prolonged heat can also cause inflammation if your breasts frequently become full and swollen use cold packs after feeding for about 10 to 30 minutes cold reduces swelling if the breasts are too full and the baby has a hard time latching manually express some milk from your breasts before putting the baby to them if engorgement is not getting better with these comfort measures contact your provider or a lactation consultant to help avoid sore nipples be sure your baby has a deep latch with his or her mouth

Hello, my name is Luca Vier and I'm a radiation oncologist at UCLA. I focus on the treatment of genital urinary malignancies which include cancers of the prostate, bladder, kidney, and testes. My research interests involve improving the precision with which we deliver radiation therapy both radiographically as well as genomically. My other research interests involve improving equity of access to the cutting edge clinical trials we offer at UCLA to patients who've been historically under reppresented on clinical trials.

Hello, my name is Dr. Daniel Bradley and I'm a radiologist specializing in breast imaging at UCLA Health. As a breast imaging specialist, I interpret mammogs, breast ultrasounds, and breast MRIs to diagnose benign breast conditions and breast tumors. Additionally, I provide a variety of imageguided procedures including breast biopsies and localization procedures to aid in the surgical excision of breast masses. I have a special research interest in cryoablation, which is a minimally invasive procedure that uses a small needle to freeze and destroy tumors. I went to medical school at the University of Southern California and I did residency in radiology and my breast imaging fellowship both here at UCLA. I consider it to be an honor and a privilege to provide compassionate highquality care to the patients of Los Angeles. When I'm not at work, I enjoy exploring new restaurants and outdoor activities in sunny California.

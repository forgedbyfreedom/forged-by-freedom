Of course, a stroke or embolism occurs when a clot blocks an artery in the brain, causing sudden problems such as weakness or numbness on one side of the body, loss of balance, or difficulty speaking. A stroke is an emergency. You must call 911. Of course. And who is at risk of a stroke? Primarily people with high blood pressure. Other risk factors include high blood sugar and cholesterol, heart problems, smoking, being overweight, and excessive drinking. Fortunately, strokes can be prevented. The best way to do this is to see a doctor. Exercise, a Mediterranean diet, not smoking, and drinking less alcohol are also important. Of course. Thank you, doctor.

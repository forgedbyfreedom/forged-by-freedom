Hello, today we are joined by Dr. Lucas Restrepo from the stroke specialist hospital. Doctor, can you tell us what the symptoms are, please? Of course, a stroke or embolism occurs when a clot blocks an artery in the brain, causing sudden problems such as weakness or numbness on one side of the body, loss of balance, or difficulty speaking. And what should be done when a person has these symptoms? A stroke is an emergency. You must call 911. Do not call a friend or neighbor first, and do not drive yourself to the hospital. There are very effective therapies to save the brain during the initial hours of a stroke and increase the chances of a full recovery. The most important thing is to be transported by ambulance as soon as possible to begin treatment. Every minute is critical. Thank you, Doctor. For more information, visit the page on screen.

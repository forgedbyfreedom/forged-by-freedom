We are joined by Dr. Mireya Díaz Santos from UCI. Doctor, you focus on research into Alzheimer's disease in the Latino community. They speak about this more than anything else, of course. Over the next 20 years, the number of people living with Alzheimer's will increase by about 127%. It is estimated that the number of Latinos living with this disease in California will triple. During this time, early detection and participation in clinical trials are crucial. What are you doing to increase the participation of Hispanic people in these studies? With the help of community leaders, we offer information about the importance of clinical trials, dispelling the common myths that participants are guinea pigs. Giusel's commitment is to serve our community and offer information in their language about these studies. Excellent. Thank you for that information, and for more, visit the website on screen.

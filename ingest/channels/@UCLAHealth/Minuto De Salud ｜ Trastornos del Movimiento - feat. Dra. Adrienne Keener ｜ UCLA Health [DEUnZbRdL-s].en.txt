Hello, today we are joined by neurologist edream Killer from UCI. Welcome. Thank you, Doctor. Can you tell us what movement disorders are? Movement disorders are neurological conditions that cause involuntary movements or lack of coordination. And what are the symptoms of these disorders? Some signs are problems with physical coordination, tremors, difficulty walking, and muscle contractions or spasms. Can movement disorders be cured? There is no cure for movement disorders; however, there are many treatment options such as medication, physical therapy, deep brain stimulation, among others. Thank you very much, Doctor.

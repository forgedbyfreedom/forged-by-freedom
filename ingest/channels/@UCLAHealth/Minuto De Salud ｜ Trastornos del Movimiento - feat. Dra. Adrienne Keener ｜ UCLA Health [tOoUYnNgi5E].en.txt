Hello, today we are joined by neurologist Dr. Airbnb. Can you tell us what Parkinson's disease is? It is a brain disorder that causes involuntary or uncontrollable movements such as tremors, rigidity, and difficulty with balance and coordination. The symptoms generally begin gradually and worsen over time. Is there any treatment for Parkinson's disease? Most patients living with Parkinson's have excellent symptom control for several years with medication. However, as the disease progresses, symptom control with medication can become difficult for some patients. For these patients, deep brain stimulation may become a good option.

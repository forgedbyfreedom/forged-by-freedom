March is Colorectal Cancer Awareness Month, and today we are joined by Ernesto Sosa from the Johnson Cancer Center and Dr. Miriam Hernández from Vision and Commitment. Welcome, Ernesto. Can you tell us a little more about this cancer? Colorectal cancer is the second most diagnosed cancer in Hispanics. In many cases, there are no symptoms present, which is why early detection tests are important. What detection options do we have? They can be a colonoscopy or a home kit that is sent to you to check the results. Miriam, how can we educate the community? We work with the promoter model, through which trusted members of the community share information and resources with the Latino community on health topics. Thank you very much.

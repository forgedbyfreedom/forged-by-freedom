March is Colorectal Cancer Awareness Month, and today we are joined by Ernesto Sosa from the Johnson Cancer Center and Dr. Miriam Hernández from Vision and Commitment. Welcome, Ernesto. How do we educate the Latino community about colorectal cancer? I Made a Commitment is a national organization, and together with UCLAY, we provide information and resources, promoting equity. Miriam, why does the Promoter Model work? This model helps reduce knowledge barriers. Promoters are connected with the community and share information that the population would not otherwise receive. Where do the promoters share this information? The Latino community: we meet in person, hold events, and disseminate information in an easy-to-understand way. Thank you very much.

immunotherapy has been a revolution in cancer therapy most important concept about immunotherapy is that what we're trying to do is accentuate what the body does naturally we make new cells we fight infections we get rid of dangerous bacteria but in the case of cancer the immune system may be overwhelmed by the growth of the cancer so our job as Physicians is to try to harness the natural mechanisms of the immune system but to Greater effect you can call it a superpower if you like but if we can harness that effect of the immune system then we can treat and successfully cure cancers in many instances our goal is to create new therapies to change the course of disease

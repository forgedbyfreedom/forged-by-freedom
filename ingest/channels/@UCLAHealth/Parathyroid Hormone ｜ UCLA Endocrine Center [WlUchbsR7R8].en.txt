mineral that is important for many functions in your body such as building strong bones helping muscles work properly and helping with nerve function when the level of calcium in your blood becomes too low your parathyroid glands release parathyroid hormone parathyroid hormone helps raise the calcium levels in your blood by doing a few different things it signals your bones to release calcium into your blood your bones act as a storage site for calcium and parathyroid hormone helps move calcium from your bones into your blood parathyroid hormone also increases the amount of calcium that is absorbed by your body from the food you eat it helps your body use more calcium from food ensuring enough calcium enters your blood it also reduces the amount of calcium that you lose in urine parathyroid hormones signals your kidneys to save calcium instead of excreting extra calcium in your urine by performing these actions parathyroid hormone helps to keep proper levels of calcium in your blood which is important for your health and well-being however sometimes there can be problems with your parathyroid glands that result in abnormal levels of parathyroid hormone for example if your parathyroid glands become overactive and make too much parathyroid hormone it can lead to a condition called hyperparathyroidism this condition can cause increased calcium levels in the blood leading to symptoms like feeling very tired or weak or developing kidney stones or bone pain if you have concerns about your parathyroid hormone levels or any related symptoms it's important to

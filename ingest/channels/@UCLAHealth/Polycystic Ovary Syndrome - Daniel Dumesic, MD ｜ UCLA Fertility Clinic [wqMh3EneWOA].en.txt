hello my name is Dan de Messick I'm division head of Reproductive Endocrinology and infertility at UCLA my practice centers on women with polycystic ovary syndrome a reproductive disorder in women characterized by androgen excess and elevation of the male hormone testosterone which leads to abnormal facial hair and menstrual irregularity in combination with an increased risk of certain metabolic diseases including diabetes I'm commonly asked is there a genetic component to polycystic ovary syndrome indeed there is a small genetic contribution but it's amplified by environmental factors therefore a lifestyle modification including diet and exercise in combination with medical strategies offer an extremely high quality of life for individuals with polycystic ovary syndrome thank you

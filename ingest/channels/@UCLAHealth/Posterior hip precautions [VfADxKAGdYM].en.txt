after a posterior approach hip replacement there is a small risk of dislocation during the healing phase if care is not taken following three main precautions will drastically reduce this risk you should follow these precautions a minimum of six to 12 weeks after your surgery and then as directed by your surgeon the first precaution is to not cross your operated leg over your non-operated leg whether in a seated position or in bed if you cannot tolerate sleeping on your back you may lay on your non-surgical side and keep a pillow between your knees to prevent your operated leg from rolling over the top it is best not to sleep on your surgical side due to your incision the second hip precaution is to not bend your hip past 90 degrees which means no reaching down past your knees or sitting on a low surface where your knees will be higher than your hips you can build up the seat height as needed using a pillow or folded blanket to get something down low or on the floor it is best to have help or use a device such as a reacher the third precaution is to not rotate or turn your operated lake inward whether in bed or in a standing position you should avoid turning toward the surgical side with the foot planted instead be sure to turn your foot out a little first following these three simple precautions will keep your new hip safe and ensure the best long-term outcome you

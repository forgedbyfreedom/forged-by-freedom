be assured that you'll be cared for by expert surgeons and professional and compassionate nurses and staff a primary goal goal of this video is to provide you and your family with information so we primary care doctor to have your preoperative history and physical exam performed and pre-operative laboratory tests drawn within 30 days of your surgery the orders for these tests will be faxed to your doctor's office by your care coordinator if you are under the care of a specialist such as a cardiologist pulmonologist or hematologist oncologist please ask your surgeon if additional pre-operative evaluation is necessary your pre-operative packet will be sent to you to review with your loved one this packet includes a personalized letter summarizing the coordination of your pre-operative appointments and tests a pre-operative information document a document called your road to recovery containing information essential to your hospitalization and Care the admission medication history form the pre-procedure treatment unit medical history form and a pre-operative checklist created specifically for you as a reminder Aid it is absolutely essential that you read through this packet and fill out any necessary paperwork before your surgery you will be asked to sign a consent form affirming you have discussed the benefits risks and Alternatives of surgery with your surgeon we will also ask for your consent for a blood transfusion if necessary if you choose not to receive blood under any circumstances you will also be asked to document that choice if you would like to donate your own blood for surgery or designate a directed donor discuss this with your surgeon well in advance of your surgery if you have an advanced Healthcare directive please bring a copy with you an advanced Healthcare directive is a legal document that states your medical treatment wishes and allows you to appoint someone to make your healthc care decisions for you in the event you are unable to do so yourself if you do not have an advanced Healthcare directive you can print one out on our website uclahealth.org we can also give you one at the hospital please complete the admission medication history form and send it to your care coordinator also bring a paper copy with you on the day of your surgery remember to specify the dose and frequency for each medication including supplements and herbal medications designate one family member or trusted friend to serve as your contact person and Coach their role will be to support you as you prepare for surgery through your hospital stay and during your recovery at home this person should be present when you receive your final discharge information from your Healthcare team if you are taking aspirin non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs or other blood thinning medications please contact your surgeon's office regarding the management of these medications you may need to stop taking these drugs at least 7 days before your surgery date if you are taking medication for diabetes discuss with your doctor how to manage them prior to your surgery if if you are taking medication for high blood pressure in the afternoon or evening you should take them as prescribed by your doctor the day before your surgery if you are taking medication for high blood pressure in the morning continue them as prescribed with the sip of water if you typically take medication for seizures in the morning take them with a small sip of water on the day of your surgery if you are having surgery for a movement disorder do not take your movement disorder medications at after midnight the night before surgery do not bring medications from home unless specifically instructed to do so you should discuss your medication plan with your surgeon for more specific conditions not outlined here you do not need to cut your hair prior to surgery we recommend that you wash your body with an antiseptic skin cleanser liquid such as hioc cleanse or chg for 5 to 7 days before surgery if your surgery is in less than 5 Days please wash with hiac cleanse for the remaining Days please read the hibac cleanse informational sheet in your preoperative information document do not eat anything including gum or candy after midnight the night before surgery if you do eat your surgery will most likely need to be postponed you may have small sips of only water Pedialite or Gatorade as needed to take medications until 5:00 a.m. you may brush your teeth and rinse your mouth but do not swallow any of the water infants younger than 6 months old may have breast milk or formula until 4 hours prior to the time you arrive and water or Pedialite until 2 hours prior to arrival children between 6 months and 3 years old may have solids until 8 hours prior to arrival breast milk or formula until 6 hours prior to arrival and water or pedal light until 2 hours prior to arrival adults and children older than 3 years may have solids until 8 hours prior to arrival and water Pedialite or Gatorade until 2 hours prior to arrival on the business day before your surgery please call the preoperative Evaluation Center or the time to come to the admissions office on the day of your surgery and answer any questions you may have also notify the PE the day before your surgery if you have limited English proficiency we leave your valuables such as jewelry and cash at home or with your family from Wilshire Boulevard go north on Westwood Boulevard after you pass lant Avenue you will see the UCLA Medical Plaza on your left hand side keep going and the next building on your left is Ronald Reagan UCLA Medical Center pull up to the valet as this is your only parking option enter through the main doors of the hospital after your surgery you will recover in the post anesthesia Care Unit also known as pacu or in the Intensive Care Unit visitor access is restricted in the pacu family and friends should wait in Maddy's room our volunteer staff will keep your contact person informed as to where you will be after being discharged from the pacu your surgeon may also come to see your contact person after the procedure is completed once you recover you will either stay in the ICU or be transferred to a Neurosurgical unit room both include a place where one family member can sleep these are private rooms with unlimited visiting hours if needed you will receive pain medication intravenously until you can safely swallow pills by the day after surgery you should have transition to oral pain medications you will likely wake up from anesthesia with the bladder catheter in place the catheter will usually be removed the day after surgery please tell your surgeon if you have had prostate issues or urinary retention in the past you may arrive to your room with elastic stockings and a sequential compression device on these are specifically designed to increase blood flow to the heart and to decrease the chance of blood clots in your legs you will be encouraged to use an incentive spirometer while you are are awake this is a plastic handheld breathing device that helps you practice taking deep breaths being able to get out of bed and move around as soon as possible after surgery enhances your recovery your care team will help you get up and about safely throughout your hospitalization please refer to the document called your road to recovery to familiarize yourself with what to expect after your surgery your team will discuss your discharge plans with you and your coach on a daily basis to ensure that we take care of all your needs and any questions in anticipation of your departure at Home Care Rehabilitation physical therapy and any other outpatient services that you may need following your surgery will be coordinated before you leave the hospital prior to discharge you will be given prescriptions to fill at your Pharmacy as an alternative you may choose to have your prescriptions filled at the hospital pharmacy your medications will be brought directly by a pharmacist to your room and counseling can be performed for all new medications please make sure you or your coach have some form of payment for the prescription co-pays on the day of discharge future refills of medication can be done at your Community Pharmacy please have your contact person arrive no later than 10:00 a.m. on the day you are discharged to accompany you home please finalize Transportation arrangements before you come for surgery for outpatient procedures requiring an overnight stay the scheduled discharge time is 8:00 a.m. your ride should arrive by 7:00 a.m. if you need assistance getting home please call our patient Affairs Department your recovery continues at home it's important to have a support system in place so you're not alone do not drive or return to work until your surgeon gives you clearance you should schedule a follow-up visit usually within 2 weeks to

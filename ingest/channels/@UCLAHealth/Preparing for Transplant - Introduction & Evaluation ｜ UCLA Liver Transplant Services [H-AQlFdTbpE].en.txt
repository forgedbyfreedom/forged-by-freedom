welcome to the UCL a liver transplant program we recognize that a liver transplant can be a long emotional and often confusing process for you in your caregivers our team will provide you all the support information and guidance you will need beginning with a thorough evaluation of your health and condition we will guide you through the surgical process and post transplant care a liver transplant involves more than just a surgery throughout this process you will receive a lot of information from doctors nurses and other members of the transplant team the information provided in the videos will help you better understand liver transplantation we look forward to accompanying you and your caregivers through this journey every step of the way

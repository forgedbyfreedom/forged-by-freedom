here at ucla health there is a strong dedication to colorectal cancer prevention colorectal cancer is common and deadly it is the third most common cancer among men and women in the united states and the second most common cause of cancer-related deaths at the same time it is a disease that is largely preventable with screening knowing this we have implemented system-wide quality initiatives that center around getting every adult screen for colorectal cancer when they are eligible at ucla health we encourage our patients to choose one of several approved screening tests we established a fecal immunochemical test our fit kit mailing program in 2015 to boost screening rates for our patients we also have patient navigators to improve coordination of care for patients with abnormal fit results we're improving colonoscopy screening rates too we have increased our capacity for colonoscopy by adding direct access to colonoscopy and new endoscopy units throughout los angeles also our annual system-wide colorectal cancer awareness campaign aims to increase knowledge about colorectal cancer risk and screening rates campaign components include community partnerships outreach events provider and community education media interviews patient stories and social media efforts increasing colorectal cancer screening rates will save lives we are thrilled that our quality initiatives and multidisciplinary collaboration are making a difference across ucla health you

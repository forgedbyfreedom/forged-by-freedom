Hi, I'm Jenny from St. Petersburg, Florida. I've never had back problems in my life. I'm active and haven't had any injuries, but recently my neck and shoulders have been killing me all the time. Could back problems be caused by stress? Jenny, stress can definitely cause neck and shoulder pain. In fact, when you're stressed, the muscles in the neck and shoulders can contract more and that could cause some pain. The other thing to look at is your activity level. If you're suddenly more active and that's what you think is causing your pain, I would try modifying your activities. If that doesn't take care of your pain, then I would consult with a specialist.

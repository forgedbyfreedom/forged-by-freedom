Hi, my name is Christine from Haverville, Massachusetts, and I have a question. Does drinking alcohol really help you sleep better? Well, Christine, again, a very good question. Alcohol can make people feel very relaxed and can actually help people get to sleep. Alcohol sits on the receptors in the brain that many prescription drugs sit on to help people get to sleep. The problem with alcohol is that it has a very short half-life and it wears off quickly. So although alcohol can make you feel relaxed and help you fall asleep, it will not help you stay asleep. And in many times, you will wake up feeling worse than when you went to bed.

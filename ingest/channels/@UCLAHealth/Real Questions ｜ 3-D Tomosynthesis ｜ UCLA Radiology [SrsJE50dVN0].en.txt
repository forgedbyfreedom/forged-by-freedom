what is the advantage of 3d tomosynthesis over conventional mammograms 3d tomosynthesis is the new breast imaging modality that uses high-powered computers to convert digital breast images into thin slices or stacks which are your 3d mammogram your radiologist can then look through these stacks of images layer by layer and potentially uncover a tumor that might have otherwise been hidden by overlapping breast tissue the main advantage of 3d mammography is that we're able to find up to 40 percent more invasive breast cancers while at the same time calling back about a third fewer women for extra views when you compare it to a regular 2d mammogram

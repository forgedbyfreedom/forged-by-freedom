what options are available to treat reflux and are there non-medication options so there are three General treatment options available for acid reflux medications dietary and lifestyle changes and surgical options in terms of medication therapies the most commonly used agent is a proton pump inhibitor or a PPI which is used to reduce the amount of acid that is produced by the stomach so there is less acid reflux that can occur in terms of dietary changes there are several foods that have been associated with reflux including spicy foods tomato based foods caffeinated beverages alcohol Chocolate and Peppermint and in terms of surgery there's a procedure called a fund appliation in which the surgeon can wrap a portion of the stomach around the esophagus and this will reduce the amount of reflux that occurs you will need a pre-operative evaluation to determine if you are a good candidate for this type of surgery

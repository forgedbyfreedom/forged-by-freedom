I've been taking a twice a day acid blocker prescribed by my doctor, but I still have frequent heartburn. Why is the medication not working? There are several possible reasons for why you may still be having symptoms despite taking an acid blocker twice a day. This includes a large h highal hernia which is allowing so much acid to reflux that the medications are not able to overcome this effect. You may be having non- acid reflux in which your medication is working but there still is fluid that's going up into the esophagus and causing symptoms. Or there may be a completely different condition including functional heartburn in which the nerves that go to and from the esophagus are hyper sensitized and so you are perceiving reflux even though it is not occurring. There's also a condition incl called eocinophilic esophagitis which is an allergic disorder of the esophagus and can lead to reflux symptoms as well as difficulty swallowing. And finally, you may have a motility disorder that is contributing to your symptoms. You should speak with your doctor to determine if any further evaluation is necessary to determine why you are having persistent symptoms.

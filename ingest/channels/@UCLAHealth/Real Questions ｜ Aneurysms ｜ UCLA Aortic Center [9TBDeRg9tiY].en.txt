what are the symptoms of aortic aneurysms most aortic aneurysms cause no symptoms they are frequently discovered while the patient is undergoing imaging for some other reason depending on the location of the aneurysm the patient may experience chest abdominal and or back pain other symptoms include early fullness with small meals due to compression of the intestine by the aneurysm compression of the ureter or the tube that drains urine from the kidney to the bladder can lead to flank pain clots can break off from an aneurysm leading to blockage of arteries with sudden onset of pain either in the abdomen or in the lower extremities rupture is the most feared complication of an aortic aneurysm symptoms of aortic aneurysm rupture include sudden onset of chest abdominal or back pain with often drop in blood pressure leading to shock on occasion the rupture is contained allowing for emergency transfer of the patient to a hospital where treatment can be promptly administered

what is the recommendation on circumcision circumcision is a routine surgical procedure that is elective and it's a procedure whereby the doctor removes the foreskin of the penis this procedure is performed in more than 50% of newborn boys before they leave the hospital and this is done for social cultural and religious reasons in fact the number may be higher than this because a lot of patients have theirs uncircumcised once they leave the hospital the American Academy of Pediatrics came out with a statement in 2012 stating that the health benefits of circumcision outweigh the health risks some of the benefits that they have stated include prevention of urinary tract infections prevention of sexually transmitted diseases including HIV and prevention of penile cancers they also note that there are no good studies to support that circumcision will affect the boys or sexual function in the long run the American Academy of Pediatrics however does not go so far as to recommend routine circumcision for all infant males thus it is an important discussion to have with your obstetrician and your pediatrician in order to make an informed decision for your baby

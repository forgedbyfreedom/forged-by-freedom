what is an intrauterine device the entry uterine device or IUD is a small T shaped plastic device that is placed into the uterus by your provider these are highly effective methods of birth control the first type of IUD is the hormonal iud and it contains a small amount of progestin there are two hormonal IUDs one lasting up to five years and the other lasting up to three years the second type of iud does not contain hormones and can last up to 10 years it is often called the copper IUD both of these methods primarily prevent pregnancy by stopping fertilization of the egg by sperm

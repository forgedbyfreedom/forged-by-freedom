how is dbs surgery done and what should i expect deep brain stimulation surgery is usually done in two stages the first stage is when the neurosurgeon precisely implants electrodes deep within your brain to do this a frame is placed on the head a ct scan is obtained and then the electrodes are carefully implanted in your brain while we monitor you during surgery the second stage is about two weeks later when the generator is implanted in the chest after these two surgeries it takes about three to six months of programming to customize the electrical stimulation to your brain

what is endometriosis endometriosis is when the tissue making up the lining of the uterus is present on other organs inside the body endometriosis is typically present in the lower abdomen and pelvis however may be present anywhere in the body women with endometriosis often have pelvic pain pain with periods pain with intercourse and may report a difficult time becoming pregnant on the other hand some women with endometriosis may have no symptoms at all

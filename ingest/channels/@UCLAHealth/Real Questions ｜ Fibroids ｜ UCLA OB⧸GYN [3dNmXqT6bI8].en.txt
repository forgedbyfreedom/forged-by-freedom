what are fibroids fibroids are benign or non-cancerous growths of the uterus about 80 of women will have fibroids noted on their uterus most of the time they're small and asymptomatic depending on the size and location of your fibroids they can cause a wide variety of symptoms such as heavy menstrual bleeding pelvic pain and pressure or infertility your doctor will suspect fibroids based on your clinical exam and history an ultrasound can be used to diagnose fibroids measure them and determine their location within the uterus we don't know what causes fibroids but there are a wide variety of treatment options available depending on your symptoms

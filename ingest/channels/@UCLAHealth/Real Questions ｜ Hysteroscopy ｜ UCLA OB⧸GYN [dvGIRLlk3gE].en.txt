what is a hysteroscopy a hysteroscopy is a minimally invasive outpatient procedure used to diagnose and treat problems of the uterus a thin telescope like camera is placed through the vagina into the uterus to look at all the surfaces on the inside of the uterus it's usually used to diagnose and treat abnormal uterine bleeding however it can be used for a number of conditions we can remove fibroids that protrude into the uterine cavity and remove under neutral polyps we can also use it to put implants into your fallopian tubes for permanent sterilization the risks to a hysteroscopy could include plain and bleeding putting a small hole at the top of the uterus or causing fluid shifts within your body most women recover very quickly from a hysteroscopy most return to normal activities within a few days

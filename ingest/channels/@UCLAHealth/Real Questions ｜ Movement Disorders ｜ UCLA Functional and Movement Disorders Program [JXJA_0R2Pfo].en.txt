what is the difference between Parkinson's disease and essential tremor people often confuse Parkinson's disease and essential tremor both of them have a component of tremor but they're very different diseases in essential tremor the Tremor is usually worse with movement it affects both sides of the body and it usually starts at a younger age with Parkinson's disease the Tremor is usually worse with rest in addition patients usually have stiffness and rigidity and one side is usually worse than the other

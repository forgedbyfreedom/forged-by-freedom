what is essential tremor essential tremor is a condition in which the arms and legs shake usually more with movement than with rests it usually runs in families and it's one of the most common conditions we see in movement disorders affecting 1 in 20 people over the age of 40. there are several therapies available but medications are first line including beta blockers like propanolol and primidone when medications fail we consider surgery including deep brain stimulation therapy which is the state-of-the-art treatment if surgery is not an option we also consider radiation therapy

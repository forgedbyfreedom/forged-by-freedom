I had surgery for nasal polyps and they came back will another surgery get rid of them or do they always come back nasal polyps usually result from inflammation or infection in the sinuses you can have a sensitivity or allergy to different substances including aspirin which can result in development of polyps in the sinuses surgery can remove the polyps quite effectively however if the underlying condition is not addressed the polyps can and will come back any allergies or infection should be controlled with proper medication and treatment the sinus cavity should be maintained as clean as possible using a saline solution for irrigation topical nasal steroids can also help prevent Pops from coming back however unfortunately despite our best efforts sometimes they do come back and then another surgery may be necessary

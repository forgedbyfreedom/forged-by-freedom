I can't breathe through my nose what could the problem be there are actually a variety of conditions that can result in blockage or obstruction of the nose if you have a lot of sneezing or itchy eyes or runny nose you may have what's called allergic rhinitis and this is from exposure to different irritants in the environment which trigger an inflammatory reaction in the nose if you have a history of nasal trauma either from sports or from an accident you may have a deviated septum and this could also cause blockage of your nasal breathing chronic infections such as chronic sinusitis or nasal polyps can also block the breathing in the nose and then there are rare tumors most of them benign but occasionally malignant which can grow inside the nose and cause obstruction for nasal breathing

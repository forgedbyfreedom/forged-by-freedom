what is preclampsia preeclampsia is a serious medical condition that some women get while they are pregnant it usually occurs after 20 weeks of gestation and involves high blood pressure it can also affect other organs including the liver kidney and eyes and often can cause too much protein in the urine in some cases the baby does not grow as well in the setting of preclampsia if it is diagnosed before 37 weeks it may be an indication for pre-term delivery

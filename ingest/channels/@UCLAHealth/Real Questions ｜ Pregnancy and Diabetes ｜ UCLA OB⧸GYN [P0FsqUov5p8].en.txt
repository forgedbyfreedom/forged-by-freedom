what is gestational diabetes gestational diabetes or diabetes during pregnancy is caused by a change in a woman's response to insulin during pregnancy up to ten percent of pregnancies can be affected most women are screened for gestational diabetes between 24 and 28 weeks with a one hour glucose tolerance test you're given a sugary drink and your blood sugar is tested about an hour later if this is abnormal you'll be given a three hour glucose tolerance test if this is also abnormal you'll be diagnosed with gestational diabetes the first steps in managing gestational diabetes are to check your blood sugars at home modify your diet and increase exercise if changing your diet and exercise don't improve your blood sugars you may be started on medications to help lower your blood sugar if your blood sugars remain high for the rest of the pregnancy you'll be at risk for a few things such as preeclampsia or high blood pressure cesarean delivery if your baby is too large to deliver vaginally or your baby may be admitted to the neonatal intensive care unit for blood sugar monitoring most women after they deliver will have normal sugars but they are increased risk for type 2 diabetes your doctor will screen you a few months after you deliver for type 2 diabetes

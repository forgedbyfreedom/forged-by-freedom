what pain management options are available in labor there are medical and non-medical ways to manage your pain during labor while you're still at home and in the early stages of labor you can try walking massage deep breathing or a warm shower to relax you and control your pain once you get to the hospital you may choose to use a medication to help with your pain iv medications are available but they are associated with dizziness and changes in your baby's heart rate you can use local medication which we inject into the pelvis or into the skin around the vagina to control pain during the pushing stages of labor the most commonly used form of pain control is an epidural an anesthesiologist will put a small tube in your back and infuse a medication to control the pain in the lower half of your body you will be stuck in bed for the rest of your labor there are some risks such as low blood pressure headache and soreness in your back however this is the this is a very safe option to use during labor you should talk to your doctor about all the options available and consider attending a birthing class at your local hospital

what is an induction of labor an induction of labor is the use of medications to bring on labor before the onset of natural labor we usually recommend an induction of labor for moms or babies who are at risk such as if the mom has a high blood pressure or diabetes or if the baby has poor growth or low amniotic fluid we don't recommend an induction of labor before 39 weeks to allow the baby's lungs time to mature if your pregnancy is normal we usually won't recommend an induction of labor until about 41 weeks your doctor will check your cervix before an induction of labor to see if it's ready a ready cervix is soft dilated and a little bit thinned out even if your cervix is not quite ready we do have medications to get your cervix ready for an induction an induction can take a few days especially if your cervix is not ready or if this is your first baby an induction of labor can increase your risk of cesarean section however most women go on to have a normal vaginal delivery you

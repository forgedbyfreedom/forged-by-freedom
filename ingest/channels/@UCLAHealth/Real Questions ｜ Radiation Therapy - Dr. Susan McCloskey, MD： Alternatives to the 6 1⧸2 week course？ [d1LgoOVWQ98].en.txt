are there any alternatives to the standard six and a half week course of radiation therapy yes there are alternatives the most common alternatives that we use are hypofractionated whole breast radiation and partial breast irradiation so is actually very similar to whole breast radiation delivered over six and a half weeks it's identical in terms of the target which is the whole breast but it's different in that it's given in half the time so the entire radiation course can be completed in three weeks rather than six and a half and studies have shown that in properly selected women this regimen is equally effective and has the same side effects as the six and a half week course so this is a very exciting option for women who are eligible now the other alternative that we commonly use is partial breast irradiation as the name implies partial breast irradiation treats just part of the breast it treats the tumor cavity where the tumor was removed plus a small margin and the rationale for this is that that tumor cavity is the area at highest risk for a breast cancer recurrence so we know that it's very important that we properly select women for this option because it's not appropriate for all it is very convenient in that it's given in one week twice a day over one week but the entire radiation course is completed in one week so these options are not appropriate for everyone but improperly selected women they really do represent much more convenient alternatives to the standard six and a half week course

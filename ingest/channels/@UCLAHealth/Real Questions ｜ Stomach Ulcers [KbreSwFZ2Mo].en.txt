I've heard a lot about ulcers in the stomach what are the symptoms and causes and what is the usual treatment for ulcers treatment the symptoms of stomach ulcers can be quite variable typically patients describe abdominal pain which is usually located in the upper abdomen in the midline portion the pain is usually described as a burning like sensation and can be quite variable in its severity other symptoms include nausea and vomiting and sometimes weight loss caused by decreased appetite if the ulcer is bleeding then sometimes patients will describe vomiting of blood or blood in the stools blood in the stools can either be manifest as black stools or sometimes as red blood mixed with the stools the two most common causes of ulcers are a bacteria called helicobactor pylori and a class of medications called non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs or nids for short some examples of Neds are aspirin ibuprofen and naproxin when speaking about the treatment of ulcers we usually focus on two things number one treating the underlying cause and number two allowing the ulcer to heal if the underlying cause is thought to be related to the bacteria helicobactor pylori we normally prescribe a course of antibiotics to try and eradicate this bacteria if the underlying cause is thought to be secondary to the class of medications the nonoral anti-inflammatory drugs we usually recommend discontinuation of this medication in either case we will prescribe a medication to suppress acid reduction in the stomach to allow healing of the ulcers to take place

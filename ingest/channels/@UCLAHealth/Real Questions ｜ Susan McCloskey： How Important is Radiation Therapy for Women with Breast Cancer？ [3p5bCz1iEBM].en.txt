how important is radiation therapy for women with breast cancer so just like surgery chemotherapy or endocrine therapy radiation therapy is a critically important component of the standard management for primary breast cancer it's recommended for the vast majority of women who have lumpectomy or breast conserving surgery and for a very large percentage of women who undergo m ectomy most commonly for women who have positive lymph nodes or large primary breast tumors Studies have shown that radiation therapy following surgery is consistently associated with approximately 50 to 70% decrease in the risk of breast cancer recurrence

I've been diagnosed with a vocal cord paralysis what are my options vocal cord paralysis is when one of your vocal cords the structures that produce your voice is unable to move this is a relatively common condition and can occur after a viral infection surgery in the neck or chest or due to a mass usually in the thyroid or the lungs it is important to determine the cause of your paralysis as it can be a sign of a serious condition regardless of the cause though there are treatment options to improve your voice the simplest is to give an injection directly in the vocal cords this is usually done in the office by a laryngologist and provides temporary voice Improvement for a few months while awaiting to see whether the paralysis will recover surgical options done through a small incision in the neck and performed in the operating room provide permanent Improvement these may include positioning an implant or a suture to reposition the vocal cord some patients May benefit from a nerve replacement procedure your voice doctor can help you decide the best option for you

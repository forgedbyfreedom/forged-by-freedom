hi I'm Dr Masha livitz an endocrine surgeon at UCLA today I'd like to talk to you about recovery from thyroid surgery first there is some pain but it is usually pretty mild during recovery most patients only experience minor discomfort in fact most of our patients control their pain with Tylenol or Advil alone it is common to have a sore throat after the surgery as well as soreness in your neck or shoulder muscles next you will only need to stay a short time in the hospital most patients have Same Day Surgery if you live a little farther away or live alone then we ask that you stay in the hospital overnight and go home the next morning either way you will need somebody to drive you home after the surgery after the surgery it is okay to eat whatever you want and use your voice as much as you want you may have a sore throat from the breathing tube and you may find it more comfortable to stick with softer foods such as juice broth or Jello until your sore throat improves we also ask that you don't drive until you can comfortably turn your neck from side to side to keep you safe on the road plus you can't drive for 24 hours after you take narcotic pain medication you may feel a little tired for three to five days after surgery but should feel pretty normal after a few days that about covers all the basics if you'd like to come see us or schedule an appointment please call the endocrine Center thanks for watching

hi i'm dr drew bogenocki and i lead our lung cancer program at ucla health department of radiation oncology among the many treatments that we offer at ucla one of them is called sbrt sbrt stands for stereotactic body radiation therapy it's a type of treatment that uses highly sophisticated and advanced technologies to deliver precise beams of radiation to small tumor targets in the lung we offer sbrt for patients with both early stage one lung cancer as well as metastatic lung cancer the type of technologies that we use to deliver sbrt include traditional linear accelerators as well as one that uses mri guided technology the mri guided technology allows us to view the cancer in real time during the entire time that the sbrt treatment is delivering the results with sbrt have been phenomenal in fact they've been so good that we often discuss them with our patients who could otherwise have surgery but might want to learn about different options for patients who are eligible for surgery but want sbrt instead we carefully review their case with our multi-disciplinary team this includes engaging our thoracic surgeons to help us make sure that sbrt really is in the best interest of each of our patients if you want to learn more about sbrt please make an appointment and come see us

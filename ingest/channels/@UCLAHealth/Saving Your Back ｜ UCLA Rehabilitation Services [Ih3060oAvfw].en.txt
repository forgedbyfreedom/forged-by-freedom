time as a result of repetitive minor trauma in regular everyday movements use these tips to save your back during everyday activities while sitting maintain a neutral spine posture that is not slouched or overly arched your body was designed to sit on your sit bones not on your spine or tailbone using a back support can help since most people do not sit at the very back of the chair even if you have an expensive ergonomic chair your body needs movement so be sure to get up every 20 minutes even if it's for a brief moment when standing up flex over your hip joint and come up using your gluteals and leg muscles do not stand up by bending your back as over time this may cause back injury when leaning over to pick up an object bend from your knees and hips instead of flexing at the spine keep in mind your hips are at the level of your pant crease not your waist also keep heavy objects close to your body you can exhale or brace your abdominals to help support the spine try making several trips to unload your car many people sacrifice the health of their back to save a few minutes by making one grand trip from their car but it's just not worth it even with light objects be sure to pivot from the feet and hips to avoid twisting the back with the average american watching many hours of television each day posture becomes very important face the television and avoid prolonged twisting to see the screen support your low and mid back with firm cushions better yet get some light regular exercise to build your body's capacity to handle life's daily demands remember be kind to

These hands...they're not all that exceptional. But I'd like to talk about a number that is exceptional. I'm talking about trauma...and no it's not pretty. It's the fourth leading cause of death in America. And bleeding is the number one cause a preventable death following injury. Despite the fact that anyone can control life-threatening bleeding. All you need are your hands and a little know-how. Let me explain. In an emergency, get help. Call 911 or get another person to do so. Now find the source of the bleeding. Cover the wound with a cloth or an article of clothing and apply direct, steady, pressure with both hands until help arrives. Once the bleeding is under control, just make sure the injured person is breathing well and comfortable. If you can't stop the bleeding with continuous direct pressure, you can apply a tourniquet, if available. If you want to know more, contact the injury prevention coordinator at your local trauma center. Accidents will happen, but they don't have to be deadly, especially if more people like you step up. This really is in your hands.

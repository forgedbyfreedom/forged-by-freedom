We're so excited to celebrate Nurses Month with the talented and dedicated nurses of UCLA Health. This year's theme is the power of nurses, and there's no better place to see this in action than right here at UCLA Health. Every day, you make UCLA Health a world-class health care system, saving lives, providing comfort, and transforming patient care. The impact of our nurses is impossible to overlook. with over 6,500 nurses providing exceptional care for more than 828,000 patients across our hospitals and clinics in the last year alone. But your true influence goes beyond just numbers. Your influence is in your advocacy, ensuring that your patients receive safe, equitable, highquality care. Your influence is in your expertise and innovation, driving evidence-based practices, improving outcomes, and embracing new technology. And your influence is in your compassion and connection. You provide crucial emotional support to our patients, their families, and our community during the most vulnerable moments. The nurses at UCLA Health are truly the heart and driving force of all we do. Supporting patients and families with expertise, kindness, and compassion at the clinics and the bedside, educating, guiding, and empowering patients, and sharing your knowledge and evidence-based practice to improve health and well-being for all. It's because of you that UCLA Health is a name synonymous with exceptional healthcare. During National Nurses Month and always, we are grateful for your dedication, compassion, and resilience. The impact of your work and care is felt across our hospitals, clinics, and communities in Los Angeles, Southern California, and the Central Coast, and beyond. You make a powerful difference with your colleagues, patients, and their families. From all of us to all of you, thank you.

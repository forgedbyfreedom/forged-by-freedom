hi I'm Kyle zanako endocrine surgeon at UCLA health today I'd like to answer a question I hear all the time will I need to take thyroid hormone medication after thyroid surgery after a thyroid operation the need for thyroid hormone medication will depend on the extent of the surgery and how well your thyroid gland functions I'm here to help explain this to you your thyroid gland plays a crucial role in making hormones or chemicals that regulate bodily functions like metabolism temperature control and energy levels the reason for taking thyroid hormone medication is to replace the hormones that your thyroid gland can no longer make this medication will usually be a synthetic thyroid hormone called levothyroxine which is similar to the hormone made Naturally by your thyroid gland if you have your thyroid gland completely removed you will need thyroid hormone medication afterwards because you won't have any thyroid left to make the hormone you need if you have half of your thyroid gland removed then whether you need thyroid hormone medication afterwards will depend on why you had the surgery for patients who don't have cancer and had normal thyroid function before surgery four out of five will have normal thyroid function without medication the remaining half of the thyroid can usually produce what your body needs for patients with thyroid cancer the answer is a little different for higher risk thyroid cancers we try to keep blood levels of thyroid stimulating hormone known as TSH as low as possible TSH is a hormone made by your brain that tells your thyroid to grow more so the more your body wants thyroid hormone the higher your TSH will be the less your body wants thyroid hormone the lower your TSH will be thyroid stimulating hormone can cause thyroid cells to grow including thyroid cancer cells so sometimes thyroid cancer patients who have normal thyroid function still get prescribed thyroid hormone medication because that lowers the amount of TSH even further and reduces growth of cancer cells so when patients have part of their thyroid gland removed because of thyroid cancer more than half of those patients need to take thyroid hormone medication after surgery we can give you more details about your personal Risk by looking at your TSH levels and medications if you do get prescribed thyroid hormone medication we will prescribe a dose based on what your body needs and we will check your thyroid hormone blood levels and make any needed changes to your medication dose every four to five weeks please don't make any

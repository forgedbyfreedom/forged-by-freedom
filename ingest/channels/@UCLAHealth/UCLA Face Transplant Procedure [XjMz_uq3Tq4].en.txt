the face transplant procedure is intended for those that have facial disfigurement from trauma Burns or birth defects a devastating injury that is not reconstructible by conventional means often mobility and sensation are decreased due to the extent of the injury as opposed to Conventional reconstructive surgery procedures using one's own tissues obtained from a different part of the body face transplantation uses identical tissues from an organ donor for the Reconstruction the procedure Begins by removing the unhealthy tissue from the recipient at the same time healthy facial tissues are taken from an organ donor that is of the same size color and gender the donor tissues are transferred to the recipient where they are reattached using microsurgical reconstruction the final appearance will be a hybrid between the donor and the recipient over time the incision line Fades and mobility and sensitivity are restored

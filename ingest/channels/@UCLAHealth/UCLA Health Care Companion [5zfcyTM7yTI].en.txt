at UCLA health every patient's care is unique that is why we now offer MyChart care companion a tool that helps you keep on top of your health care throughout your enrollment care companion will allow you to complete tasks assigned by your UCLA health care team programs will vary based on your needs but some examples include check-in questionnaires report vital signs to your care team set medication reminders learn about your health and so much more guiding you through your health care Journey the app can connect seamlessly to many different Health monitoring devices to track your Vital Signs this information is then shared with your care team who can track your progress and adjust your care as needed since care companion is available for mobile devices and desktop computers you will have the resources of UCLA health at your fingertips you will begin to see tasks assigned to you as you go through your care companion Journey please complete them as they come in and take control of your health today care companions should not be used for Urgent Care needs if you have an emergency Health need please call 9-1-1 or seek urgent care services

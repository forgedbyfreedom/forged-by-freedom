Hello, I'm Dr. Marvin Burksider and I'm Dr. Anthony Heene. Together, we direct the UCLA pituitary tumor program. The location of pituitary tumors combined with the central role the pituitary gland plays in controlling many functions of the body creates an intersection of medical specialties like no other in the body. I am an endocrinologist whose practice focuses on the diagnosis and management of pituitary tumors. Although pituitary tumors are nearly always benign, the hormonal changes associated with them can cause profound and even life-threatening effects on the body. As a neurosurgeon who has performed hundreds of minimally invasive endoscopic techniques for the removal of pituitary tumors, I am privileged to work at one of the top medical centers in the world. Our endoscopic operating room, customdesigned for the removal of pituitary tumors, is like no other in the world. Endoscopic surgery allows a level of visualization at ultra high resolution only dreamed of just 15 years ago. Our experience and technology allows us to remove tumors more completely and safely. My name is Dr. Marilene Wong and I'm a head and neck surgeon specializing in endoscopic sinus and skullbased surgery. My colleague Dr. Jeff Sue and I work closely with Dr. Berg Schneider to achieve the best results offered by a team approach. The nasal cavity and sinuses are the passageway to the pituitary tumor. The minimally invasive technique we have perfected at UCLA does not require a facial incision and we ensure a better and faster recovery of the tissue within your nose. Our program is committed to providing you personalized, coordinated, and compassionate care. We understand that UCLA may appear vast and complex, but our goal is to take all the incredible advantages of the all-star team of experts at UCLA and yet provide the attention to your needs that you would expect at your family doctor's office. Our staff makes every effort to coordinate your care, arranging office visits and imaging studies, usually the very same day. We are one of the few programs in the world that have a combined neurosurgery and clinic where you meet with Dr. Bryce Schneider and myself at the same visit. In addition to being more efficient, it provides an opportunity to hear both medical and surgical options discussed at the same time and therefore allows our patients to make better, more informed decisions. We also run a pituitary disorder support group, which is an opportunity for patients with pituitary disorders to meet and discuss their illnesses, the challenges they face, and celebrate their successes. Our monthly pituitary tumor conference brings all the various UCLA pituitary specialists together in one room to discuss challenging cases for you the patient. This meeting of the minds provides an open discussion to achieve the best outcome.

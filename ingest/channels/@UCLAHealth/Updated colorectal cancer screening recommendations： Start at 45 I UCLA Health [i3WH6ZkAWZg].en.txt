in may of 2021 the united states preventive services task force updated the national colorectal cancer screening guidelines while previous recommendations suggested that colorectal cancer screening began at age 50. the new guideline promotes screening starting at age 45 and extending until at least age 75. this policy change is necessary due to an increasing rate of colorectal cancer in individuals under age 50 in the united states and a strong push to save more people from this preventable disease there are many acceptable screening options that you can choose from they include colonoscopy the fecal immunochemical test the stool dna test virtual colonoscopy and others individuals with a family history of colorectal cancer should begin screening before age 45. in addition people with a personal history of inflammatory bowel disease colorectal cancer colorectal polyps or certain genetic conditions have a higher lifetime risk of colorectal cancer and so require a specific screening protocols talk to your doctor today about arranging a colorectal cancer screening test it can save your life you

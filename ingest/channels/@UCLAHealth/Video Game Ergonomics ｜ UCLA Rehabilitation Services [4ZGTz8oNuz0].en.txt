hi I'm Ashley Waldman an occupational therapist with UCLA health many families these days have video game systems at home that result in hours of sedentary playing time here are a few general guidelines that can help you enjoy playing video games without putting unnecessary stress on your body with handheld devices such as video game controllers it's easy to overwork your thumbs and fingers and put your wrists in an awkward position staring at a screen for long periods of time can also result in excessive eye strain take breaks throughout your game consider doing a few quick simple stretches for your wrists and thumbs relax your eyes by looking away from the screen try focusing on the furthest point possible if you are sitting while playing video games on a computer be sure to check your posture sit in a well supported chair with both shoulders relaxed forearms should be parallel to the ground when using a keyboard or mouse allow rest breaks in between levels by standing up from the chair and doing arm stretches it is important to limit the total amount of time playing video games to reduce the risk of injury remember if you do start to experience symptoms that do not relieve after a day or two contact your doctor these tips will help make your gaming experience not only enjoyable but safe I'm Ashley thanks for watching you

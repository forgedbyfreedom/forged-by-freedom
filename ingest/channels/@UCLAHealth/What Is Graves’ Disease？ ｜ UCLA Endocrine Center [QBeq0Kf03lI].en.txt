hi I'm Dr puyan femini endocrinologist at the UCLA endocrine Center let's talk about something we see quite often at the endocrine Center which is Graves disease Graves disease is an autoimmune disorder an autoimmune disorder is when your immune system starts to attack parts of your body because it thinks they don't belong in Graves disease the immune system makes a very specific antibody that sticks onto the thyroid the antibodies special because it sticks onto an on switch for the thyroid cell telling it to make a lot of thyroid hormone this is why people with Graves disease are hyperthyroid meaning they have too much thyroid hormone thyroid hormone affects several parts of the body it goes to places like the heart skeletal muscle eyes skin bones and liver so having too much thyroid hormone causes many whole body effects these symptoms can include anxiety and irritability a fine Tremor or shaking of the hands or fingers sensitivity to heat and an increase in sweating or warm moist skin weight loss despite normal eating habits enlargement of the thyroid gland or goiter a change in menstrual cycles erectile dysfunction or reduced sexual drive and frequent bowel movements this is a basic summary of a big topic for more information please visit our website and watch our other videos or call us for a consultation I'm Dr puyan femini thanks for watching

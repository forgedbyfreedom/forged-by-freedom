what are ear tubes and how do they work ear tubes are tiny little plastic tubes that we place into a child's eardrum while they're under anesthesia it's similar to putting a button in a buttonhole an ear tube is very tiny it's about 1.2 millimeters in size and what they do is they prevent fluid from building up behind the eardrum so if your child's had a lot of ear infections or chronic buildup behind their eardrum where they have trouble hearing ear tubes will help prevent ear infections can improve their hearing and they usually stay in their ears for about a year or so and then they fall out on their own

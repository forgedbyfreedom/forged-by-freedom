what are the symptoms and how would i know if i had it the most common symptoms of cedar colitis are watery diarrhea fever and abdominal pain symptoms can start immediately after beginning antibiotics or even up to three months after completing a course of antibiotics it's usually diagnosed by a stool test and can be treated with oral antibiotics such as metronidazole or vancomycin more severe cases on the other hand usually require a hospital stay with a combination of intravenous and oral antibiotics about eight to fifty percent of people can develop recurrence of c diff colitis after they've been successfully treated and in these patients we usually recommend a longer course of antibiotics

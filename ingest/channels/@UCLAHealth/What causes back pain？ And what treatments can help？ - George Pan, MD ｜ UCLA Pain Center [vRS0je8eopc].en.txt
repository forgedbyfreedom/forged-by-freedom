What causes back pain and what treatments can help? Back pain can be caused by a multitude of reasons. Some of these include arthritis within the joints of the back. It can be caused by degenerated discs within the back or it could be the result of something known as a pinched nerve. Arthritis in the back is common with aging. However, we can actually do several things to improve the quality of life. We can prescribe certain medications. We can try physical therapy. We can also provide interventions that can decrease the amount of pain caused by the nerves within the within the back. There are many interventions that we can do for back pain. Some of them include nerve blocks or epidural steroid injections or even ablation of certain nerves within the back.

why should I see a psychologist as part of pain treatment being in pain can be very stressful many patients who come to the clinic are unable to work take care of their families or do the things that they enjoy doing that can have an emotional impact seeing a psychologist can help address those emotional issues that can come along with pain a psychologist is qualified to talk to you about the other techniques that can help you deal with your pain from an emotional aspect such as biofeedback cognitive behavioral therapy and relaxation therapy

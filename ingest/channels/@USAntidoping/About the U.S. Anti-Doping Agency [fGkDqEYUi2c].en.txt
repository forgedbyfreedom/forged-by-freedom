It starts with a simple but powerful mission for clean sport. Champion athletes to achieve their dreams in a fair and healthy way and to inspire the world. Guided by this mission, we stand with athletes and for athletes from the start of their careers to the day they retire. When they first enter sport, we are there to help them learn the lessons and values needed for success on and off the field. As they ascend to higher levels of sport, we are there with education and resources that help them navigate the anti-doping process. When their success makes them eligible for testing, we are there with a friendly face and consistent protocols to guide them through the process. When they have a question about substances and methods, we have experts available a phone call away and at the forefront of the effort to solve the most complex anti-doping questions. If they run into issues with the rules, we're there with a fair adjudication process that protects the rights of all athletes as well as a steadfast voice to advocate for improvements to the global anti-doping system. And when the day comes for them to start a new journey, we're there to process their retirement and celebrate their commitment to clean sport. From the first whistle to the final buzzer, USADA is championing fair and healthy way so they can inspire the world.

fix your grip first off when people are doing lat pull down they think the wider the better now I'm not saying that wide grip lat pull down doesn't have its time and place in a regimen but generally if you want the best lat activation you're going to want to do shoulder width apart second off a lot of people they grip the bar with their thumb around the bottom instead to minimize the bicep activation and pulling with your arms you want to pull more with your lats put your thumb over top and use your hands as Hooks and third if you're really trying to minimize arm activation and maximize lat activation use something that's going to take grip out of it completely and get something that you can wrap around the bar you can just basically relax your hands and use all back muscles try it out

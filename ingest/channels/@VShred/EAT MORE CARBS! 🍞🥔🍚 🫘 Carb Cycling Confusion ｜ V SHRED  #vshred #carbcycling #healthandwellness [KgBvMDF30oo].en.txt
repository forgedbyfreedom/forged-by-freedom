what carb cycling is is where you are basically tricking your body into burning fat as much as possible without slowing down your metabolism in the process because you're going low carb day to kick your body into a fat burning State and then before your metabolism has time to adjust you go into a high carb day to keep your metabolism revved up and then right after that high carb day you go back into a low carb day and now you have a revved up metabolism and you're kicking your body back into a fat burning statement you just go through that over and over and over again and what's nice is it allows you to eat a bunch of carbs throughout the week you do still need to maintain a caloric deficit but you are allowed to get like 50 of your calories from carbs on those high carb days and generally I also like to build this schedule around your training on your high carb days I generally recommend leaving those for more intense workout days and then for my low carb days I generally save those for my rest days

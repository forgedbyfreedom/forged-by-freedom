 my mother was diagnosed with breast cancer at the age of 45. Because of the right diagnosis, treatment, and care, she lived nearly 40 more years. Every two minutes, a woman in the U.S. is told she has breast cancer. Comprehensive care from only diagnosis to the latest treatments is the foundation for achieving the best outcomes. At Vanderbilt Ingram Cancer Center, physician scientists are charting the future of breast cancer care. We're discovering new molecular subtypes of breast cancer and mapping mutations in each tumor's DNA to new therapies. And through it all, we treat the whole person, not just the cancer. Vanderbilt Ingram Cancer Center's research and innovation offers families the best chance for treatment, a cure, and the opportunity to live with cancer, but not be defined by it. Because in this fight against cancer, every detail matters.

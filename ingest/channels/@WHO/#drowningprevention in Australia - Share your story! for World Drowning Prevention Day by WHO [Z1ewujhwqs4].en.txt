While Australia currently has relatively low rates compared to many other countries, we share the same issue, the high numbers of drowning among young children. Australia has been fortunate to have had a national water safety strategy in place for almost 30 years. One of the very first tasks of this Australian Water Safety Council was to develop the first national water safety plan in 1998. This was to reduce drowning, focusing really on education, regulation, and community engagement. Over time, we've seen a significant decrease in drowning rates, particularly among young children due to multifaceted actions. So, policy, advocacy, and education. We're now in the seventh iteration of the Australian Water Safety Strategy, which is a 10-year strategy that has the vision to have drownings by 2030. The 2030 strategy focuses on five key priority areas, people, places, populations, activities, and risk factors. Multisexual collaborations and partnerships are key to success.

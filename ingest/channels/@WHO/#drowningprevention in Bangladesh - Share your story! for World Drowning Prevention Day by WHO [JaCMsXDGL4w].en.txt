Drowning rate is alarmingly high in in rural Bangladesh especially among children. Open water is very common in rural Bangladesh and most of the families live around these open water bodies. Many of these drowning we found when the children were alone or was unsupervised. There are solutions to prevent these unwanted deaths. ATL is a child care center and they also receive early childhood development stimulations. They learn how to be safe around water. Children who are attending these atoles are 80% less likely to be drowned. Parents who send their children to these can work without any fear. So drowning is preventable even in Bangladesh and similar low and middle inome country settings. We should all share our experiences and stories so that others can learn and utilize those in their own settings. Your story can save a life.

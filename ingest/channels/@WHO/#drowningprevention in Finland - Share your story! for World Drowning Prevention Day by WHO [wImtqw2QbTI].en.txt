Here in Finland, spending time on ice during winter is part of the culture. But ice can be dangerous. We can't just tell people to stay off the ice. That's like telling someone not to go to the beach. So instead, we teach them how to be safe on ice. We started with children and partnered with school sports association. We created fun, simple tools for schools and teachers, videos, quizzes, on and offline classroom activities, and a demonstration backpack filled with ice safety gear. We also recruited social media influencers as eye safety ambassadors to reach more people from different age groups, interests, and regions. Now, 15,000 kids learn ice safety every winter. Over 1.5 million people see our campaign messages online and in the news and since 2013 only one child under 14 has drowned after falling through the ice. We are creating a culture of safety where kids teach their parents and grandparents and change starts to grow. Our advice start small, find partners and share your story. Your story can save a life.

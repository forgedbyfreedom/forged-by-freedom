Uganda's lakeside communities have some of the highest drowning rates in the world. Most of these tragedies are preventable with the right interventions and proactive community engagement. >> World drowning prevention day was celebrated in Vawala. Speakers gave powerful testimonies on the importance of water safety and personal testimonies about survival. A significant part of the event success stemmed from the diverse group of attendees all united by a shared belief that drowning is preventable. The world famous ghetto kids also performed and became drowning prevention advocates. As a result of the event, Uganda's government has shown renewed commitment to finalizing and adopting the national drowning prevention and water safety strategy. I call upon all people wherever you are in the world to share your story on drowning prevention because stories are very powerful.

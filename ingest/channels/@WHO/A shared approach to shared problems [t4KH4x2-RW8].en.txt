On Friday afternoon, I left the health assembly early to attend my daughter Blen's high school graduation. As any parent, I'm very proud of her. I also worry for her and about the world she is entering. It's an uncertain world, a world beset by war, poverty, inequality, and division. A world threatened by pandemics and epidemics, unhealthy diets, polluted air and water, unsafe roads, and the overshadowing threat of climate change. Although there are many reasons to worry, there are also many reasons to hope. In all of resolutions, you express the collective will of the nations of the world, the United Nations to work together on a shared approach to shared problems. Yes, there is conflict in our world, but you have shown that there is also cooperation. Yes, there is division, but you have shown that there is also unity. Yes, there is inequity but you have shown a commitment to equity. Yes, there is disease but you have shown the commitment to health and health for all.

practical tips for carers of people with dementia reach out for support caring for someone with dementia can be overwhelming ask for help when you need it from family members local associations or health and social services take care of yourself it is easier to take care of others when you feel well yourself keep in regular contact with friends family and other people you trust and make time for yourself every day focus on activities that make you feel good ensure continuity of care you might not always be able to provide care plan for situations in which you can no longer provide care taking into account the preferences of the person you're caring for respond to change as dementia progresses the person with dementia may change agitation depression anxiety confusion and wandering adjust some of the changes that you may see try to identify what may be triggering the change and find ways to respond that are the least distressing to you both be flexible as dementia progresses you may need to provide different levels of care things that may help establish routines use visual clues to help with memory and orientation and help the person with dementia be as independent as possible communicate effectively dementia can make communication difficult use short sentences and simple words repeat information calmly as often as necessary be patient and watch for visual clues stay in control of your feelings and pay a compliment when something is done well you can find more information in who's i support programme you

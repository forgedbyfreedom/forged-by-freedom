people in rural Azerbaijan struggle to access good quality Primary Health Care the covid-19 pandemic made things even worse women found it difficult to get the care they needed leading to poor outcomes in areas like maternal and child mortality in one rural area near shamaka different projects came together to improve this situation healthcare workers were funded to train and work in the area they provided some care in specially adapted mobile clinics that traveled to communities some clinics had dedicated spaces for women with health checks and information about steps women could take to monitor their own health National Training for healthcare workers helped improve Primary Healthcare quality across the country these projects were collaborations between central and local governments Gap agencies who and UNICEF and other fundus all these projects mean Azerbaijan can progress on sdg3 and work towards providing good quality Primary Health Care for everyone regardless of where they live

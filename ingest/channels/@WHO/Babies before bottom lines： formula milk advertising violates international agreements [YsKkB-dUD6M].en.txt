Becoming a mother transforms us. Your heart splits in two. One half now lives on the outside. That half is everything and deserves only the best. Big business knows this. They use it to mislead and manipulate. The global formula industry is worth over 55 billion US. Their marketing is unethical, deceptive, inescapable. We support all parents and caregivers, but we don't support corporate influence over what you feed your baby. Unregulated digital marketing uses algorithms that find you at your most vulnerable. Targeted midnight messages say bottlefed babies sleep better. Fake science. Premium branding means premium benefits. False. The only difference is the price. Toddler milk is essential. Not true. Cow's milk is just fine. Their posts are predatory and pervasive. There is a set of rules in place to protect us, but not a single formula company follows it completely. This is not okay. You have the right to know what big business is feeding you. Let's put babies before bottom lines.

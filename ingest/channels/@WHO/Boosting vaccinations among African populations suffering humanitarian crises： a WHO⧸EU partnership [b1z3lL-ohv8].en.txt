a two-year project funded by the European Union has helped boost covid-19 vaccination rates in Africa's fragile humanitarian settings across 16 countries the vaccination rate in these countries was less than five percent on average at the beginning of 2022. a year later the average for the continent is close to 30 percent with multiple countries surpassing 40 percent many countries saw the most significant rise in coverage after vaccination campaigns were held between September and December 2022 the 16 million euro Grant project set to end in June 2023 has strengthened the capacity of national health workers to administer vaccines in urban areas remote Villages Refugee or displacement camps workplaces and public spaces health workers have been trained by who experts with a focus to reach the most vulnerable communities older people and those living through humanitarian crises thanks to the efforts of health workers community mobilizers and project teams people are already benefiting from the rise in covid-19 vaccination rates we are seeing success stories across Nigeria Somalia Mozambique with standout countries like Liberia having up to 80 percent of the population fully vaccinated by the end of January 2023 the number of health workers trained to administer covid-19 and other vaccines increased from 130 000 to almost 1.6 million resulting in enhanced National vaccination programs so more lives can be saved and communities protected from covid-19 and other vaccine preventable diseases the grant enabling this work led by who is part of the 100 million Euro European Union humanitarian support to covid-19 vaccination rollout in Africa it is heartening to see the national Workforce coming together with who the European Union and many communities to ensure everyone has access to health care and vaccines

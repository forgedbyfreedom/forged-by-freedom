Honorable Minister Alicandre Pilia, excellencies, dear colleagues and friends, my thanks to the Brazilian COPER Presidency for hosting today's COPER Health Day. The climate crisis is a health crisis for individuals, but also for health systems. Strong health systems are essential for protecting people against the health impacts of climate change. From injuries caused by severe storms or asthma attacks triggered by air pollution or when people suffer from heat stroke. And yet health systems themselves are affected by climate change compromising their ability to deliver life-saving care when people need it most. For decades, WHO has been calling for action to adapt health systems and build resilience to climate change. The Bellum held action plan is how we can do that. As secretariat for the Bellum held action plan, WHO will ensure measurable outcomes through the WHO alliance for transformative action on climate and health or attach. attaches a network of countries and partners working to build climate resilient, sustainable and lowcarbon health systems. WHO is grateful to the endorsing parties for committing to this action plan and we thank you all for your commitment to protecting human health through concrete climate action because there can be no healthy people on a sick planet. I thank you.

the World Health Organization and the state of Qatar would like to introduce sport for health a global collaboration harnessing Sports potential to improve Public Health the first sport for health model was established in 2021 in the runup to the FIFA World Cup in Qatar in 2022 the project had three objectives the first was protecting health for visitors residents players and staff during the tournament the second was advocating for health Beyond sports with programs promoting physical activity Healthy nutrition and improved lifestyle choices the third was to serve as a case study evaluating the impact of the sport for health model not just around the short-lived mega event that was the World Cup but also in the form of lasting Improvement ments for individuals and communities in Qatar and Beyond this meant identifying successes challenges and lessons for the benefit of future organizers to share these lessons we have written a report entitled changing the game strengthening health and well-being through sports events in May 2024 the 77th World Health assembly passed a resolution on strengthening health and well-being through sport events the the resolution reinforces the foundations of the sport for health model urging wh member states to use Sport and sport events to address Public Health and Social Challenges including non-communicable diseases Health emergencies Mental Health and Social inclusion sport already offers so many opportunities to promote respect for human rights Bridge cultures and Foster Mutual understanding inclusion and equal opportunity now we hope it can also offer platforms to improve Public Health around the world

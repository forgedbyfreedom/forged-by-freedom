When COVID-19 hit, where you were in the world determined the course of your life thereafter. How quickly your government reacted. How easily you accessed health care. Whether you could work or go to school. Lost loved ones. Kept connected. Stayed alive. Science found solutions. Yet the biggest vaccine rollout in human history has also revealed the longstanding inequities facing humanity: Across all vaccinations. All diseases. All pathogens. And all countries. These inequities won’t end until we close the gap. Until the vaccine divide is closed. And the knock-on effects of inequitable access to primary health care and technology stop posing dire and dangerous threats on both our lives, and livelihoods. The power to close the vaccine gap lies with all of us. So what are the decisions we need to be making? Keep vaccinating to reach our targets. Keep building production capacities. Keep preparing for the worst. And keep strengthening for the future. Failure to fund, support, lead and prioritize primary health care is a dangerous risk we cannot afford to take. The future will arrive. More disasters will strike. And right now is the only chance we have to make sure we’re ready. Together.

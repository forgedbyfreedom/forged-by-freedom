You may have heard of universal health coverage, but what is it really? Think about it this way: What do you need to be healthy and stay healthy? [Music] Do you have access to good health professionals and safe treatments that will make you feel better? Can you get the medicines and health products you need? Who will pay for them? Are there policies that guarantee services for you, your family, and your community next time and whenever you need them? Do you have your government? Do you have accurate information about the health system in general so you can make the right decisions to keep everything working as it should? Good health requires people, services, products, funding, policies, and information, and it all needs to work together, even in times of crisis. A good health system doesn't just treat sick people; it helps promote healthy living and prevent disease. The World Health Organization works around the world to ensure that all people and communities have access to the quality services they need to protect them from health threats without imposing financial hardship. That's what we mean by universal health coverage: your health and everyone else's.

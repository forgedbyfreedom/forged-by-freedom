Handwashing should last approximately one minute. Wet your hands and apply enough soap to cover the entire surface of your hands. Let the water run so you don't touch the tap. Then, rub your palms together to create enough lather. Next, rub your right palm on the back of your left hand, interlacing your fingers, and vice versa. Rub your palms together again, interlacing your fingers. Rub the backs of your fingers in the palm of your opposite hand, interlacing your fingers. Repeat these steps for the other hand. Rub your left thumb, rotating it in your right fist, and vice versa. For your fingertips, rub the fingertips of your right hand in your left palm, rotating them in both directions, and vice versa. Rinse your hands thoroughly under running water. Dry your hands well with a disposable towel. If the tap cannot be turned off with your elbow, use a towel to turn it off without direct contact.  I had finished you, your hands are clean.

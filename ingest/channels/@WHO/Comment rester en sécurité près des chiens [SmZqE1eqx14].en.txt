Dogs can be excellent companions, but it's important to remember that not all of them are used to children. Some may be afraid, feel stressed, or even threatened.  And when that happens, they can bite. Dog bites can be serious and require immediate medical attention .  Not only are they painful, they can cause serious infections and even lead to death.  Some dogs can transmit rabies, a deadly disease, but fortunately, it can be avoided. With a few simple steps, children can be taught to behave safely and respectfully around dogs. Here are five essential tips to protect all children.  Tip number 1: teach children never to disturb a dog when it is sleeping, eating or taking care of its pups. Like us, dogs do not like to be disturbed at this time and they may react aggressively. Tip number 2, explain to children how to spot the signs that a dog has scared or is angry.  For example, a scared dog may tuck its tail between its legs or try to hide.  An angry dog ​​may growl, bark, or show its teeth. Make sure the children keep a safe distance apart.  Tip number 3. Make sure children know what to do if a dog approaches.  They must remain calm and still. Arms at your sides.  Running or shouting can excite the dog and worsen the situation. If a child falls or is knocked over, they should curl up into a ball, protect their head and neck, and remain still until the dog moves away. Tip number 4. Always supervise children around dogs, even with your own pet.  They should always ask the owner's permission before petting a dog and let it smell their hands first.  Show them how to be gentle and not to pull their tail or ears. Tip number 5, the most important one: if a child gets bitten, don't scold them .  Act immediately to reduce the risk of infection. Wash the plaice thoroughly with soap and water and consult a doctor immediately. Rabies is a painful and deadly disease, but prompt treatment can prevent serious infections. Dogs bring a lot of happiness into our lives, but it is important to teach children how to behave around them safely.

Some live on the street and may not be used to children. When they are scared, angry or sick, they can be frightening and sometimes bite. It is very important to know how to behave around dogs because if you get bitten, it can be very serious, it hurts a lot and you can get very sick because dog bites can transmit very dangerous diseases.  Here are five simple tips to stay safe.  Tip number 1, never approach a dog and do not disturb it, especially if it is sleeping, eating, tied up or guarding its hooves.  Tip number 2, always stay well away from dogs that seem angry, scared or sick.  An angry dog may growl or show its teeth.  A frightened dog often tucks its tail between its legs and may try to run away.  Tip number 3. If a dog approaches you, never run. Stay perfectly still like this.  arms at your sides as if you were a tree.  And if you fall, curl up into a ball.  Try to protect your head and neck.  Tip number 4. Always ask permission from the owner or your parents before touching a dog.  Dogs like it when you hold their hand to sniff them before touching them, and always be calm and gentle. Tip number 5. If a dog bites or scratches you, tell an adult immediately.  It is very important to act quickly.  Tell your parents or another adult.  They should wash the bite or scratch immediately with plenty of soap and water.  And they will need to take them to a clinic or consult a healthcare professional for further treatment.  So remember, even if some dogs can be nice like me, you should always be careful with dogs you don't know.  And if a dog bites or scratches you, tell an adult immediately.

COVID-19 is a contagious disease caused by a new coronavirus that recently appeared in humans. It is mainly transmitted through droplets expelled when an infected person talks or sneezes. These droplets can land in the mouths or noses of people nearby, but they are too heavy to travel far. They are projected about a meter and fall back onto surrounding surfaces. This is why contamination occurs primarily through close contact. The exact survival time of the virus on a surface is still unknown. It is therefore advisable to regularly clean surfaces, especially near people infected with COVID-19. Our hands touch many surfaces that may be contaminated with the virus. Therefore, avoid touching your eyes, nose, or mouth. You risk transferring the virus from the contaminated surface to your hands. If you cough or sneeze, cover your nose and mouth with your elbow or a disposable tissue. Then immediately throw the tissue away in a closed bin. The most effective way to prevent the spread of the new coronavirus is to wash your hands.  Wash your hands frequently with hand sanitizer or with soap and water to eliminate the virus if it is on your hands, protect your health, and fight the spread of COVID-19.

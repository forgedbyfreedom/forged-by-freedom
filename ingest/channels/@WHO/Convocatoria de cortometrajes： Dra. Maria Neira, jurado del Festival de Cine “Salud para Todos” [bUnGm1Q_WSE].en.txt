Hello everyone! We invite you to participate in this short film competition because we want to promote the idea that it's not just about curing sick people, but about doing everything possible to prevent illness so people can enjoy well-being and have their health properly protected. If you're a student interested in the environment, a homemaker worried about your child's or a family member's asthma, a film enthusiast, someone with the ability to make films, or someone interested in people's health, please contribute! What kind of films are we looking for? Anything that addresses well-being, access to clean water, the importance of food and nutrition, clean air, how to treat everyday products, reducing the use of chemicals—these are the kinds of messages that can reach everyone and give us an idea of ​​the importance of walking, for example, to avoid a sedentary lifestyle and the resulting illnesses. We want to emphasize the importance of movement, getting some exercise, and not smoking. There are thousands of ideas that could be included. We are convinced that cinema is a fantastic way to reach everyone, a very friendly and pleasant way, and that through the films you will send us, your short films, we will be able to communicate the importance of the environmental and social determinants of our health, and that everyone can have a much healthier environment.

Kobe COVID-19 is an infectious disease caused by a novel coronavirus that has been transmitted to humans for the first time. It spreads from person to person primarily through droplets produced when an infected person talks, coughs, or sneezes. These droplets can reach the mouths or noses of people nearby; however, they are too heavy to travel long distances through the air. Their range is about one meter, and they settle quickly on surfaces. Therefore, human-to-human transmission occurs mainly among people in close contact. It is still unknown exactly how long the virus can survive on surfaces, so it is advisable to clean them regularly, especially if they have been in contact with people infected with Kobe COVID-19. We touch many surfaces with our hands, which may be contaminated with the virus. Therefore, avoid touching your eyes, nose, and mouth, as if your hands are contaminated, the virus can pass from the surface into your body. When coughing or sneezing, cover your mouth and nose with your bent elbow or use a disposable tissue. If you use a tissue, throw it away immediately in a closed bin.

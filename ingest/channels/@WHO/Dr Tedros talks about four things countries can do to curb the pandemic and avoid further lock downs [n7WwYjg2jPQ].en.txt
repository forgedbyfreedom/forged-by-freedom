eight months into the pandemic we understand that people are tired and yearn to get on with their lives at the same time no country can just pretend the pandemic is over we believe there are four essential things that all countries communities and individuals must focus on to take control first prevent amplifying events in many countries we have seen explosive outbreaks linked to gatherings of people at stadiums nightclubs places of worship and in other crowds second reduce deaths by protecting vulnerable groups including older people those with underlying conditions and essential workers third individuals must play their part by taking the measures we now work to protect themselves and others stay at least one meter away from others clean your hands regularly practice respiratory etiquette and wear a mask avoid the stresses closed spaces crowded places and close contact settings and fourth governments must take tailored actions to find isolate tests and care for cases and dress and quarantine contacts and above all national unity and global solidarity are essential this virus thrives when we are divided when we are united we can defeat it

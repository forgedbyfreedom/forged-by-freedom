STM board chair Dr. Jean Kristoff Modit, members of the STM board, dear colleagues and friends, science has always been the engine of improvements in global health and scientific publishing is essential to our work at WHO. As an evidence-based organization, we're constantly updating the advice we give to countries based on the latest and best research. WHO has itself been publishing primary research, evidence reviews, and policy recommendations for over 75 years. Our goal is to promote equity in science and strengthen the capacity of research institutions especially in low and middle inome countries through our joint United Nations initiative research for life. We facilitate access to scientific publications in over 120 low and middle inome countries. I thank the publishers who are part of this initiative and I invite those who are not to join to put the power of science in more hands. Equity and access to research must go hand inhand with equity in access for researchers to publish as well as diversity in editorial boards and peer review and more language offerings. At a time of rampant mis and disinformation, it's also vital that we communicate science to the public to build understanding and trust. Above all, scientific publishing must be a tool to promote equity and a healthier, safer, fairer world for everyone. I thank you.

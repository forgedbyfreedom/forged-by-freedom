Honorable ministers, distinguished guests, dear colleagues and friends, bonus das, my thanks to the government of Spain, sanitation and water for all and UNICEF for convening this important meeting. Access to safe water, sanitation and hygiene is essential for human health and for building resilience and adapting to climate change. Floods and droughts can damage wash infrastructure, contaminate water sources and disrupt vital access. Climate resilient wash services are vital pro for protecting vulnerable populations from disease including diarrhea, choleera, typhoid and influenza. Under investment in wash has left billions of people without safe drinking water, safe sanitation, basic handwashing services and with health facilities that lack even basic water services. This puts patients and health workers at risk for health care acquired infections like sepsis but also waterborn diseases, antimicrob resistance and pandemics. Smart investment and management in WASH will help to save more than 1 million lives, contribute to human rights and gender equality, and bring substantial economic returns. Making wash services safe, available, and sustainable requires working together across sectors and borders with accountable governance, long-term planning, and accountability. Finally, I offer my deepest condolences on the passing of Katarina de Albek. Her commitment and leadership will continue to inspire us to continue her work and her legacy. Muchas gracias. I thank you.

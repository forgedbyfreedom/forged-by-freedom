On this World Day of Remembrance for Road Traffic Victims, I want to remember the families and friends of those who lost their lives on the world's roads this year. They were all sons and daughters, fathers and mothers, brothers and sisters, colleagues, neighbors, or friends. One million three hundred thousand lives were lost. All these lives mattered, and each one of these deaths was one too many. I also want to remember those who suffered injuries and trauma. On the occasion of the third World Conference of Ministers for Road Safety, which will take place in February 2020, governments, civil society, and the private sector will have the opportunity to commit to increasing actions and measures in favor of safe speed, safe roads, safe vehicles, and safe road users. Only when we have ensured that all parts of transport systems are safe will lives be saved. Political will and leadership are needed to ensure that zero fatalities become a reality. The best way to commemorate those who have suffered on the world's roads is for governments to keep their promises. Together with my colleagues, I wish you a successful World Day of Remembrance for Road Traffic Victims.

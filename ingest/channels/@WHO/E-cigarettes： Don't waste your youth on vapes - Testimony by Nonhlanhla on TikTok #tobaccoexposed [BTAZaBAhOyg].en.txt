Let's talk about ecigarettes, commonly known as vapes, which have hit the market and have become extremely popular amongst young people. But how many young people know exactly what is contained in the ecigarettes that they are inhaling? It's not just flavors and vaporized water. The industry markets ecigarettes as a healthier alternative to cigarettes. Did you know that a single ecigarette can contain so much nicotine that it can be at times just as much as the nicotine in cigarettes? These products don't just contain nicotine, which is highly addictive. But they also expose users to a whole range of toxic chemicals that can increase health risks. The industry purposefully targets young people and children because they know young people and children are particularly fascinated by the sleek and colorful designs, the attractive and yummy flavors. We've seen stickers, posters, social media ads that are specifically designed to attract our attention. They are even targeting young people and children as young as 9 years old with these products. When are we going to address the looming danger that awaits us if we continuously inhale these ecigarettes and buy into their schemes and lie? It's time to call it out for what it truly is. It's time to put a stop to ecigarettes, a stop to sleek designs and colorful flavors that attract young people. It's time to put a stop to unregulated nicotine levels. We need to take a stand against an industry that addicts and controls young people and children. Abolish products and environments that do not allow young people and children to enjoy their youth because instead they are wasting it away feeling helplessly addicted to vapes.

I started smoking cigarettes when I was 12. It's the age old tale of having older friends looking at it thinking it's cool. When I discovered vaping, I thought it was safer than cigarettes. I thought it was a step forward. I really enjoyed the fact that it didn't have this horrid smell that you'd have to wash off and the fact that you could just vape indoors. So, I think the ease of it all really misled me. I wasn't really keeping track of how this could have detrimental effects. I started getting sick. It felt like I was coughing up flavored water and the flavor would be the flavor of my vape. And so I thought, "This is really concerning." I didn't stop vaping though, not immediately, until I got very, very sick. I was bedridden for at least 2 weeks. It all got very scary. To this day, my chest is still not what it used to be. Even though I've quit vaping for a few months, my throat constantly feels dry. My breath is a lot more labored. I still experience a lot of air hunger where it just feels like I'm not taking a full breath. But one thing that I am glad about is the fact that I have stopped vaping.

Why am I so heartbroken? Why am I so sad? I thought that this thing would be the thing that sustains me and satisfies me. My journey of vaping started about 4 years ago when I was in grade 11. The main reason why I started was actually because I wanted to impress my friends. If you had a vape, you were seen as this like cool guy and that's what I wanted to be back in high school. Me and his friends had some sort of argument and we never really spoke after that. I thought that me smoking his vape would make them want to be my friend. But I realized that that satisfaction and the thing that the vape gave me was all temporary. Just looking back, I was like, I don't want to do this anymore. This is not benefiting me any type of way. I got new friends and also quit vaping completely. And I think just surrounding myself with healthy friends just made such a big impact on my life. My friends are just so encouraging and always want to see the best of me, not just for my health, but also just my mental health and physical health itself.

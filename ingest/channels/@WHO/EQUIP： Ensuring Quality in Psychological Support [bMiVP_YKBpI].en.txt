around the world millions of people experience common mental health problems such as depression anxiety or post-traumatic stress many suffer the consequences of harmful drug and alcohol use all these problems may affect people's ability to cope with the stresses of daily life and to reach their full potential although evidence-based mental health interventions can help most people still do not receive treatment particularly in countries where poverty is high or in countries affected by humanitarian emergencies effective treatments can be provided by non-specialists community workers but ensuring they have the right skills remains a challenge to address this issue the World Health Organization has established a new initiative called the equipt project Equipe aims to ensure quality in psychological support in communities where it's needed most Equipe brings together community workers mental health professionals academia and others to identify the skills needed by non-specialists and how these skills can be trained and assessed as one example the equip project conducted a three-day theory of change workshop representing 16 different countries this broad range of professionals help to identify the many different types of resources Equipe may need to include they addressed implementation training competency assessment and measurement all of these with the goal of delivering quality and effective mental health treatment equip will ultimately become a package of tools resources and guidance that will be freely available to all people planning to implement community-based psychological support programs this includes government's education providers organizations and those who train community workers so that psychological support can be delivered to more people experiencing common mental health problems in the coming years the World Health Organization will work with partners to test the equipment xfield locations this project is a key step towards the World Health Organization's efforts to deliver universal health coverage because caring for people with common mental health problems and ensuring the best quality care is essential to promoting health keeping populations safe and supporting the most vulnerable

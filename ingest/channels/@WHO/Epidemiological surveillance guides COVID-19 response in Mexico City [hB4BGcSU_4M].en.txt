In Mexico City there are nine million people, 22 million in the metropolitan area. A good portion of the jobs are informal, meaning that people live day to day and have to go out to work. Here we developed a special epidemiological model for Mexico City and the metropolitan area of ​​the Valley of Mexico, which has allowed us to continuously monitor the epidemic. The pandemic has been very important for decision- making. Since 2005, Mexico has had a sentinel surveillance system for severe acute respiratory infection and influenza-like illness. From that system, we set up our technological surveillance of COVID-19. The previous collaboration of the UPS or DEM with this country has been key to achieving the level of support and coordination in the response to COVID- 19. [Music] It has provided the equivalent of 350,000 tests to the country through the INDRE, which allowed us to supply the 32 state public health laboratories.

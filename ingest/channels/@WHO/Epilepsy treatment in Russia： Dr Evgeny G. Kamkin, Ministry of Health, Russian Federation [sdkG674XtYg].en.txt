Why is epilepsy an important public health issue? About 50 million people are citizens of the world. The disease can be both independent and accompany more severe diseases, such as infectious and substandard ones. For example, after a traumatic brain injury, about 20 percent of patients suffer from epileptic seizures. However, the latest advances in modern medicine show that with adequate and proper treatment, these patients can recover and the attacks will not recur, they will lead a normal life. However, today, these patients are also subject to various discriminatory measures of stigmatization, and this is also a very important problem that needs to be addressed.

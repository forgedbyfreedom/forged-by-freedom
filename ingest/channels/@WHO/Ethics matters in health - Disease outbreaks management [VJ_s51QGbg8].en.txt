infectious disease outbreaks are often periods of great uncertainty fear and mistrust community is most affected by disease outbreaks often have few medicines health workers and vaccines which are stretched even further during the response decisions need to be made under pressure to help stop the spread of a disease many times there is very little information available and people are faced with making difficult decisions ethics provides a moral compass to help guide decision making for everyone involved including public health officials and policy makers funders researchers first responders and National Ethics ports seeking ethics into consideration puts people at the heart of the problem ethics emphasizes the principles that can guide everyone involved by providing the moral reasoning for making the best choices during a crisis

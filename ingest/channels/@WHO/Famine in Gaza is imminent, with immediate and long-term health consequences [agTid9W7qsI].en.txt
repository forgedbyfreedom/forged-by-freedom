I mean, I used to go to my school, eat my jellabiya, then go to my friends' place to play football. Life was so good. I wish I could go back to that old self. We went to the place and found Rafiq lying on the ground on the first floor. He was poor and had been injured. He and his sister were in the same situation. His leg was beyond repair; it needed to be amputated. Rafiq's case is considered somewhat special due to the severe malnutrition he suffered after being admitted to the hospital. He arrived with very serious injuries all over his body, the most dangerous being his foot injury, which later led to repeated surgeries and the amputation of his lower limb. Rafiq's malnutrition is not only due to his injuries but also to the lack of essential nutrients he needed, especially proteins, carbohydrates, fats, calcium, vitamins, and various minerals, which are currently unavailable in northern Gaza. I ask for meat, fruit, vegetables, or anything else, but I can't find anything.  I can't even find painkillers. At night, I don't sleep at all, only an hour or two. I swear, I'm fed up with my life. I wish I were dead instead of suffering like this. But there are children whose families report that they have died due to lack of food and hunger, and they aren't receiving medical attention because of the dire health conditions in the Gaza Strip.

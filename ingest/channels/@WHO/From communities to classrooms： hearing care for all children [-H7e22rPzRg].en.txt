Today is World Hearing Day. For people with hearing loss, these subtitles help them to understand this video. But there are no subtitles in life. Around the world, there are almost 95 million school age children who are deaf or have hearing loss. 60% of childhood hearing loss is preventable. But most cases go undetected, especially in the poorest communities. Left untreated, hearing problems can hinder speech and language development, learning, social interaction, and limit future opportunities. But simple, cost-effective solutions exist. Communities and schools can play a vital role in early detection and timely referral. WHO calls on all countries to prevent avoidable hearing loss in communities and classrooms so every child has the chance to communicate, learn, and succeed.

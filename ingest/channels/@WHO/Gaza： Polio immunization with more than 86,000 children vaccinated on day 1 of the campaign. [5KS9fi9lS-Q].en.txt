Today, 1st September 2024, is the first day of a Polio vaccination campaign in Gaza. It's starting in Deir al Balah today. Today, together with the Ministry of Health, UNICEF, UNRWA, WHO, and many NGO partners, we are in the central zone of Gaza to vaccinate 157,000 children under ten. We will do it for three consecutive days and if needed we will add a day. We have teams that are stationed in health facilities,  field hospitals, primary health care centres, medical points, and they are the mobile or outreach teams. Vaccination will be carried out at 143 sites. 513 teams will deliver the vaccines, including mobile teams going to hard-to-reach areas where people live in tents. The issue of wastewater and contaminated water increases the likelihood of polio spread. There's no other choice, the only treatment for this disease is vaccination. That's why we have launched this campaign which consists of two drops of vaccine in the mouth, during the first stage, followed by the same number of drops after four weeks. At least 90% vaccination coverage is needed during two rounds of the campaign to end poliovirus transmission. We came today for the polio vaccination. I have five children, and I brought them all for the vaccination. We traveled a very long distance, and the journey was difficult. I read the brochure and learnt about the benefits of this vaccination for our children, aside from being the only solution against polio. I am so happy that I got vaccinated today.

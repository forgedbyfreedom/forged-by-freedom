In 1995, the fourth world conference on women in Beijing set out a progressive blueprint for advancing women's rights. But 26 years later, progress on gender equality, women's empowerment and health is too slow and uneven. The coid9 pandemic has also reversed the progress that was achieved. We must do much better and we can. We must work together to promote and respect the rights of women and girls in all their diversity by ensuring they have access to quality rightsbased and affordable, comprehensive sexual and reproductive health services. We must prevent violence against women and children and ensure health systems can better support and care for survivors of violence. We must promote women's leadership in the health and care workforce, including by ensuring decent working conditions. We must stop non-curable diseases, including by ensuring that women and girls have the time, opportunities, and resources to access nutritious diets, and to engage in activities that promote their health. And we must take down the bias to women's participation in leadership, in science, and invest in research that prioritizes the health and well-being of all women. everywhere.

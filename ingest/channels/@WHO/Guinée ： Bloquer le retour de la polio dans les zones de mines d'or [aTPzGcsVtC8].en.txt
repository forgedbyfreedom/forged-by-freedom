So, Guinea was a country that had been polio-free since 2015 and the African region as a whole was certified as polio-free on August 25, 2020. But we are starting to see in several countries an importation, a new circulation of the different variants of polio. The peculiarity for Guinea is that it is the first country in Africa to have two types of polyvirus variants circulating at the same time, and all this is happening in a very difficult, overpopulated area, a mining area, there is a mixing of populations coming from surrounding countries for the gold stations. The living and working conditions at these gold mining sites generate numerous health and environmental risks.  They are considered the source of many diseases that affect the entire country.  Most of these diseases can be prevented through vaccination. The involvement of women who come to these sites also facilitates the enrollment and vaccination of children at these enrollment sites.  The innovation that was introduced was the use of GTS. These are phones, a real-time tracking system for vaccinators to see everywhere they have been and we can identify areas where vaccinators have not been and we communicate with health center managers so that they can direct vaccines to these areas that have not been vaccinated in order to reach all children. We believe that with this quality vaccination and the other passes that will come, we will be able to very quickly interrupt the circulation of the polyvirus in this area which borders neighboring countries like Mali, Ivory Coast with all this risk precisely with the movements of populations of having importation of polyvirus into other countries.

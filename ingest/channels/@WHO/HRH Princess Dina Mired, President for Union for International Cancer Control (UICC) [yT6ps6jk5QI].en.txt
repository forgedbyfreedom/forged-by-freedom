globally over 1 million people die every year from lung cancer and other cancers caused by tobacco smoke but it's not just smokers who are at risk people exposed to secondhand smoke are more likely to develop lung cancer in their lifetime particularly those exposed at home or in the workplace exposure to secondhand smoke is not a choice particularly for children it is the responsibility of governments to enact legislation to ensure healthy environments and protect people of all ages from the harms of tobacco smoke it is important to implement the tobacco control measures set out in the whao Framework Convention on tobacco control w-h-o FCTC such as pictorial health warnings tobacco taxation enforcing a ban on sales and distribution of tobacco products to minors protecting from exposure to tobacco smoke and mass media campaigns that's how we can achieve a tobacco-free world where all people enjoy their right to health don't let tobacco take your breath away

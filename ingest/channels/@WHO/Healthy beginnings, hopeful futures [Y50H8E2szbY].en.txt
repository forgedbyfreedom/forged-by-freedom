Mama, I love you. You hold me. You keep me safe. You are my world. Not every baby gets this chance. Every 7 seconds, a mother or baby is lost to causes we could prevent. Not enough midwives, not enough medicine, not enough time. But this can change when we invest in science, in strong health systems, in equality. When we make the choice to support mothers and babies, when birthdays are moments of hope, not loss, we protect the future. Because every healthy beginning means a hopeful future for us.

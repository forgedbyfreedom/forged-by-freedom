So it was a rather sad, tragic loss for our community and to be honest, it was unclear how we would be able to continue working and when it was only 14 months old and at one point it burned down, we would have been scared. And it is unclear what will happen next. Makarivska was completely destroyed and you see it has now been restored. The outpatient clinic has been revived, in which our general practitioners and pediatricians work now. The population can receive quality primary health care services. I saw what it was like when I left, then how it was gone and then how it is again. Of course, I am grateful to those people who helped restore this building. This outpatient clinic is a small miracle for us. It plays a huge role for us, for our staff and for the residents of the community. This is our new Makarivska outpatient clinic.

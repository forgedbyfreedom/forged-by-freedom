Hello, I'm Grace and I'm here to talk about something very important. How to keep children safe around dogs. Dogs can be wonderful companions, but it's important to remember not all dogs are used to being around children. Some might feel scared, anxious, or even threatened. And when they do, they can bite. Dog bites can be very serious and need immediate medical attention. They are not just painful. They can cause serious infections and even death. Some dogs carry rabies, which is a deadly disease. However, it can be prevented. With a few simple steps, we can teach children how to behave safely and respectfully around dogs. Here are five key tips to help you keep all kids safe. Tip one, teach children never to disturb a dog when it's sleeping, eating, or caring for its puppies. Just like us, dogs don't like being disturbed during their meals or rest, and they can react aggressively. Tip two, tell children how to spot the signs that a dog feels scared or angry. For example, a scared dog might tuck its tail between its legs or try to hide. An angry dog might growl back or show its tick. Make sure children keep well away from them. Tip three, ensure children know what to do if they are ever approached by a dog. They should stay calm and very still with their arms by their sides. Running or screaming can excite the dog and make the situation wash. If a child falls or is knocked over, they should crawl up into a ball, covering their head and neck and staying as still as possible until the dog moves away. Tip four, always supervise children around dogs, even if it's your family pet. They should always ask the owner's permission before touching or stroking any dog, and let the dog sniff their hand first. Show them how to be gentle with dogs and avoid pulling their tails or ears. Tip five, most important of all, if a child is ever bitten by a dog, don't scold them. Act straight away to reduce the risk of infection. Wash the wound thoroughly with soap and water and seek medical care immediately. Rabies is a painful and deadly disease, but prompt treatment can prevent serious infections. Dogs can bring so much joy into our lives, but it's important to help our children understand how to behave with dogs safely. By following these simple steps, we can reduce the risk of bites and ensure children stay happy and healthy. Thank you for watching and for taking the time to keep all our children safe.

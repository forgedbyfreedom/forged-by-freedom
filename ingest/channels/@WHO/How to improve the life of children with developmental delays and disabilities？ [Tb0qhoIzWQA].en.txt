When Gaby was little, he practically didn't interact with me or anyone else. It was difficult at the time just to reach my son; I didn't know how. In general, when children have a challenge, parents turn to professionals, to experts. So these programs give parents tools again so they can guide their children, relate better, communicate better. And the fact that COVID-19 appeared forced us to do it completely remotely, but that also opens up the possibility. Once we adapted it to do it in distant places and without the need to travel, what we do is these home visits and sessions. The home environment allows us to see a little bit how the parents are functioning, putting some of the advice and key messages of each session into practice, and to give them some tips on how they could improve that interaction. Very well, here in the program, for me, is everything because they don't just stay in the books; they work with him, with him, and with me as a mother. It gave me the certainty that this can be done.

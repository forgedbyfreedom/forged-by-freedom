If you could see viruses you'd know: they're all around. Viruses, bacteria and other germs: where we are, they are. Where we go, they go. And life is too precious to let a virus or disease so easily preventable get in the way of moments of joy and delight of a first kiss, a first step a first goal, a brilliant joke, a brilliant hug, an early start a late night laugh. Maybe that's the point: to live every moment. Vaccines work to enable long life for all! World Health Organization

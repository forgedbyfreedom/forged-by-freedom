Getting vaccinated according to the recommended schedule is the best way to protect yourself against disease, but not everyone is always able to receive their vaccines on time. They may encounter difficulties accessing services or lack the essential information they need about vaccination. There may also be stock shortages or other prolonged service interruptions. Whatever the reason, no one should miss out on the protection offered by vaccines simply because they are unable to access the service on time. Implementing a catch-up vaccination strategy as part of your routine immunization program ensures that everyone benefits from vaccination, even if there are delays. It shows you how to establish or strengthen your catch-up vaccination strategy, starting with a catch-up policy and schedule.

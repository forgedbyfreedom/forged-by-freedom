diagnosis is part of every patient's Journey reaching a diagnosis takes several steps patient history and examination testing discussion and communication of results final diagnosis and treatment plan followup and re-evaluation diagnosis can be missed incorrect delayed or miscommunicated at any stage in fact almost all adults will experience at least one diagnostic error in their lifetime errors can prolong or worsen illness and increase health care costs understanding the causes of diagnostic errors helps make patient care safer errors can result from several factors there may be a shortage of trained health workers lack of tests malfunctioning equipment or breakdowns in communication health workers judgment might be influenced by cognitive biases fatigue high stress environments or incomplete patient symptoms and medical history improving diagnostic safety starts by joining the dots throughout the journey for patients this means actively engaging with their health care team being informed about the process sharing all their symptoms in medical history asking questions and following up on results and raising concerns for health workers it means keeping up with Education and Training improving teamwork and collaboration engaging patients and families and learning from past experiences for facility managers and policy makers it means developing and implementing guidelines protocols and Pathways focusing on high-risk situations investing in Education and Training creating conducive work environments and providing highquality tools and test ing Services diagnosis is a team effort everyone along the way has a role to play let's work together to get it right make it safe

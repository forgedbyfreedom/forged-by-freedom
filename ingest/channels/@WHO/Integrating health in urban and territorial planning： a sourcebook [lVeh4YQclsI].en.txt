today cities around the world are facing the major health challenges of our time from tackling the spread of infectious diseases to ensuring healthy lifestyles for an increasingly urban global population urban areas can be unhealthy places to live with people in towns and cities experiencing increased rates of non-communicable disease injuries as well as environmental injustices that's why in 2020 who and unhabitat jointly released integrating health and urban and territorial planning a source book for urban leaders health and planning professionals the source book builds on the principles first introduced in the international guidelines on urban territorial planning and the new urban agenda and it considers how these principles and processes are linked to the sustainable development goals it advocates for urban and territorial planning as an inclusive and participatory process it shows how an integrated approach to health can influence decisions across sectors including housing transportation employment and more it also brings together two vital elements we need to make healthy cities on a habitable planet processes to guide the development of human settlement and methodologies to make human health well-being and health equity a priority at all levels from the local to the global the source book takes users through the different steps of the planning process and illustrates tools and relevant case studies for each component it identifies entry points including places people and processes which can help lead effective actions and use health as a catalyst for progress across a range of entry points it includes advocacy frameworks guidance materials and links tools available for the appraisal and analysis of health and health equity whether you're an urban planner a city mayor a health professional or a local community activist this publication was created to inform and inspire you so together we can all contribute to a healthier urban future you

The right to health means the right for everyone to access the quality of health services they need. But around the world, persons with disabilities often have difficulty accessing those services. WHO calls on all countries to build health systems that are accessible, affordable, respectful, and inclusive. That includes the way health services are financed. As part of every country's journey towards universal health coverage through our disability health equity network, who is working with partners around the world to make health financing more inclusive and equitable because health for all means all, including persons with disabilities today and every day.

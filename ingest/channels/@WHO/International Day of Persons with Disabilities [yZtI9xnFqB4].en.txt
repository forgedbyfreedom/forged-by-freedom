one in six people worldwide has a significant disability they need the same Health Services as everyone else but many face unfair barriers and inadequate access this impacts their health well-being and participation in society often these barriers exist because health services are designed without the participation of the people for whom they're intended we must recognize and support persons with disabilities not as passive recipients of health services but active contributors to health systems policym and Community initiatives The Who disability inclusion guide for Action supports countries in fostering meaningful participation of persons with disabilities in health system planning on the International Day of persons with disabilities we call for inclusive Health Systems designed for and by the people who need them

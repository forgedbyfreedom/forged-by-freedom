The people in the community, when they go for a consultation in the primary health care, it's good to hear from the doctor about the disease but at the end they need something that they came for, especially with the chronic diseases, which is the medicine. We noticed a common issue from the people about the presence of the medicine and if it's present about quality of the medicine they take. Because in Jordan, if you don't find this medicine, they go to the hospital level and that’s a burden on the hospital. So, this has really pushed us to discuss this with the Ministry of Health, and we did an assessment. In total, 14 warehouses were supported by WHO. It is not an issue of infrastructure only, we cared about the human resources dealing with the medicine inside those warehouses. And we supported the capacity building and the national capacities in the supply chain management and the good storage. We ensured from the Ministry of Health how they can maintain these capacities in the future and also how they can do the maintenance for these warehouses to ensure always the safe medicine to the community. So really with this intervention, we came closer to the community ensuring that their needs for the medicine is responded and continued.

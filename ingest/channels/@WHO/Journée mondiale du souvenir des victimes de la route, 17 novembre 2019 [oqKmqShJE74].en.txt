On this World Day of Remembrance for Road Traffic Victims, my thoughts are with the families and friends of those who have lost their lives on the world's roads this year. They were sons, daughters, fathers, mothers, brothers, sisters, colleagues, neighbors, and friends. Each of these 1.3 million deaths was one too many and leaves families in despair. My thoughts are also with the millions injured. At the Third World Ministerial Conference on Road Safety next February, governments, civil society, and the private sector have the opportunity to commit to stepping up their actions for safety: more appropriate speeds, safer roads and vehicles, and responsible road users. When all elements of the transport system are made safer, hundreds of thousands of lives will be saved. Stronger political will and leadership are needed to make this Vision Zero a reality. The best way to honor the memory of those who have suffered on the world's roads is for governments to respect their commitments to road safety. My colleagues and I wish you a happy World Day of Remembrance.

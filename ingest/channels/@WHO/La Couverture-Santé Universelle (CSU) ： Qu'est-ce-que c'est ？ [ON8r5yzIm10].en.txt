You may have heard of universal health coverage, but what does it really mean? Think about it this way: What do you need to be and stay healthy? Can you get assistance from a well-trained healthcare worker? Can you get treatment that will allow you to recover safely? Can you get the medicines and other health products you need? Who will pay for them? Are there policies in place to ensure that the services you need are available to you, your family, and your community on a permanent basis? Do the authorities in your country have accurate information about the health system so they can make the right decisions to keep everything running smoothly? Achieving good health requires people, services, products, funding, policies, and information. All stakeholders must work together, even in times of crisis. An effective health system is not just about treating sick people; it also helps promote healthy lifestyles and, above all, prevent disease.

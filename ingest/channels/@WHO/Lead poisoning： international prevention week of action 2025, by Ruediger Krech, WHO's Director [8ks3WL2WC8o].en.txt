Dear colleagues and friends, there is no safe level of lead exposure. Lead affects brain development, lowers IQ, and causes lifelong health problems. Lead is found in old paint, contaminated water, toys, spices, cosmetics, and more. Children are most at risk and deserve better. They are at risk of neurological damage wi which impacts brain development, cognitive impairment which impacts IQ levels and they face potential physical effects in terms of hearing ability and growth. Nobody wants this and it can be prevented. So join us this year to mark the international lead poisoning prevention week of action from 19th to 25th of October. We unite under one urgent message. Act now to end lead exposure. We need every government to ban lead paint and strengthen health systems to detect and manage lead poisoning. We need every community to step up and help raise awareness in all communities. Join the movement. Act now. Every child deserves a safe start.

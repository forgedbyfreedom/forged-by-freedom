So, to begin, leishmaniasis, which is a disease of course, manifests as a high fever of British onset, accompanied by vesicular rashes. There are also lymphatic swellings commonly called esophagitis here, and other associated signs including headaches and dysphagia. The WHO is here to reduce the risk of disease spread globally as much as possible, and more specifically in the countries where we are present. As a support to the health system, we are here alongside the Ministry of Health, and more specifically in North Kivu province, we are working with the Provincial Health Directorate ( DPS) to strengthen coordination mechanisms, surveillance, vaccination programs, and, more specifically, the quality of care and vigilance in actions. Together with other partners, we are coordinating this response with the Ministry of Health.

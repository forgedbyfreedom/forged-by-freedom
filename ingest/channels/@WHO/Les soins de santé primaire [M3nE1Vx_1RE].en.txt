Primary health care is about caring for people rather than treating specific diseases or conditions. Quality primary health care rests on three elements: first, empowering people and communities to take action to care for and advocate for their health. The second essential aspect of primary health care is establishing multi-sectoral policy and action based on the socio-economic, environmental, and commercial determinants of health. And the third aspect is integrating primary health care and essential public health functions at the heart of health services. They are, in fact, generally the first point of contact between people and the health system. They provide comprehensive, accessible, community-based care that meets everyone's needs throughout their lives.

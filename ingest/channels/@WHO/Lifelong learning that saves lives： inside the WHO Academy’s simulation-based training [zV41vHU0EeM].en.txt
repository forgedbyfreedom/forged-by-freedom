Health systems face growing pressure from pandemics to disasters. Health professionals need more than knowledge. They need readiness. At the same time, a global shortage of health workers projected to exceed 11 million by 2030 makes the World Health Organization's mission more critical than ever. Training new professionals takes time, but lifelong learning can make a difference. Now, welcome to the global WH Academy in Lyon, France, where learning is actionable and saves lives. At the heart of our simulation-based learning experience is our 900 meter simulation center. Fully modular, hyperconnected, built to replicate real world crisises. From disasters to large-scale emergencies, from hands-on drills to strategic discussions, every exercise builds realworld capability. Here, learners test their decision-m leadership and teamwork under pressure. Every scenario is grounded in science, guided by WH standards, and designed to build real world readiness. These exercises draw on the organization's experience in emergency response, clinical support, and health systems strengthening. The academyy's learning approach combines immersive simulation, in-person learning, online courses, and blended learning programs. It is flexible, interactive, and built for today's complex health challenges. With 11,000 square meter, 22 training rooms, and a world-class simulation center, the WH Academy is shaping the future of lifelong learning in health by combining lifelong learning, hands-on experience, and real world exposure. The WH Academy empowers health professionals, decision makers, and WH staff to build stronger and more resilient health systems.

Vaccines are extremely safe. When someone experiences a health problem following vaccination, it may be a coincidence, an administration error, or, very rarely, a problem related to the vaccine itself. We support the establishment of vaccine safety monitoring systems. Robust vaccine safety surveillance systems enable healthcare professionals, senior officials, and experts to react immediately to any problems that may arise. They examine each problem rigorously and scientifically in order to respond quickly. The WHO works closely with its member states to ensure that vaccines can fulfill their role safely. Preventing diseases and diseases like meningitis, encephalitis, and malaria in Asia and Africa are now being seriously monitored with the help of the WHO. Vaccines are among the safest medical interventions we have to prevent disease and protect the health of all children.

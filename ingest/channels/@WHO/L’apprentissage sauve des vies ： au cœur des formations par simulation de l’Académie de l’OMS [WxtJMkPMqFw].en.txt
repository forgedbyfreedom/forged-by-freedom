Pandemic, disaster, health systems are under increasing pressure.  Professionals need to prepare for this. Meanwhile, the global shortage of health personnel, estimated at over 11 million by 2030, makes the mission of the World Health Organization more crucial than ever.  Training new professionals takes time, but lifelong learning can make a difference right now. Welcome to the WHO Academy in Lyon, where science turns into action and learning saves lives. At the heart of our learning experience is our fully modular, ultra-connected 900 m² simulation center designed to reproduce real crises, from natural disasters to large-scale emergencies, from concrete scenarios to strategic exercises.  Each session develops concrete skills. Here, learners test their ability to decide, lead, and cooperate under pressure.  Each scenario is based on scientific data, complies with WHO standards and aims to strengthen the actual preparedness of participants. These exercises draw on the organization's experience in emergency response, clinical support, and health systems strengthening.  The academy's pedagogical approach combines immersive simulation, face-to-face learning , online courses, and hybrid training.  A flexible, interactive approach, designed to meet the complex challenges of today's healthcare.  With its 11,000 m², 22 training rooms and world-class simulation center, the WHO Academy is shaping the future of continuing education in the health sector.  By combining training, practical application and real-world experience, the academy empowers health professionals, policymakers and WHO staff to make health systems stronger and more resilient.

COVID-19 is a new disease; many aspects remain unknown. Scientists, researchers, healthcare workers, and other stakeholders are working together to better understand the virus, how it spreads, and how we can protect ourselves, those around us, and everyone else. But science continues to advance; it changes and evolves over time. It has a considerable influence on our daily lives. Every day we discover, experiment, and understand new things, and although these changes can be disruptive, they are happening for our benefit. They prove that we are learning and finding new ways to fight the virus that causes COVID-19. This pandemic is a challenge; it is unprecedented. And while science continues to evolve to find new and better solutions, we must come together and use the tools and knowledge currently available to us. Guidelines and advice will continue to change over time. You must be open to changes, new data, and new recommendations. Remain flexible, demonstrate resilience, and embody change together, preventing the spread of COVID-19.

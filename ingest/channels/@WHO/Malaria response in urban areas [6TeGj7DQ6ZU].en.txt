risk of malaria in urban areas until now malaria control approaches including Mass distribution of bed nets and indoor residual spraying have typically focused on rural areas where they have been responses to malaria in urban settings should be integrated with those of other vector-borne diseases city planners are uniquely positioned to address this challenge through well-planned urbanization

I am a very sentimental person. It was very difficult for me what was happening in the country, that I got sick. I also believe that this is the reason why there is a war The work of the mobile multidisciplinary team consists of helping people who have suffered from chronic mental disorders. Our team works not only in the city of Dnipro but also throughout the Dnipropetrovsk region. Most often, people who do not have a psychiatrist in their area contact us Working in the mobile team can have a stigmatizing effect because a person does not feel obligated to go somewhere to get help. We can meet where it is convenient for you. We do not necessarily have to come home there. We can meet in the park. This already gives patients a choice. I am very worried about my country, for its future, for the future of children who are born and grow up here, and they must be healthy, especially mentally healthy.

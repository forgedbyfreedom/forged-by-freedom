Health for All Film Festival - Very Short Film Prize World Health Organization (WHO) Special Prize 2023 / Very Short Film Category "Mirrors" by Paul Jerndal (Sweden) Mirrors What are you doing? You are worthless. You can't do anything. You don't have it. You've lost it, don't you understand? You're the worst. You're ugly too. You're never going to pull this off. You're going to fuck it up. You're unintelligent. Completely insufficient. People see through you. I see through you. You are never going to manage this! You're a has-been. You're completely... completely fucking worthless. Everyone has grown tired of you. Just quit. Failure. Who the fuck do you think you are? You are weak, bad. You are old, you are tired. Dumb. Embarrassing. Lousy. Worthless. Broken. Loser. You're not going to manage anything. Going to fuck everything up! You're not functioning, can't sing right. Dysfunctional, insufficient. You can't dance, you can't even act, you can't do anything! Nothing, you are the worst! The worst! It's okay. It's okay that you hafe these thoughts. There is nothing weird about feeling like this sometimes. It's fine. Somewhere, someone feels exactly like you do. Right now. You had these difficult thoughts again. But it's okay. You are okay. You are not alone. You are enough. Be kind to yourself.

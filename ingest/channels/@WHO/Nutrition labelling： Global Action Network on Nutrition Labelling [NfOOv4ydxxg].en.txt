we all know the fundamental importance of a healthy diet for our health and well-being when consumers have access to nutritious foods and easy to understand nutrition information they are better enabled to make healthier choices poor diet is a known risk factor for cardiovascular diseases such as stroke heart attack and high blood pressure non-communicable diseases also known as ncds disproportionately affect people in low and middle income countries where more than three-quarters of global ncd deaths occur there is also increasing evidence of links between poor nutrition and mental health disorders notably depression the development of easily understandable nutrition labeling is essential when it comes to empowering and facilitating consumers to make healthier food choices at the time of purchase nutrition labeling schemes and policies are among those actions highlighted in the programme of united nations decade of action on nutrition the nutrition decade endorsed the outcomes of the second international conference on nutrition based on the nutrition decade guiding principles a network on nutrition labeling composed of more than 19 countries was formed in 2019 to exchange country experiences and good practices and to encourage countries to accelerate efforts around nutrition labeling the adoption of nutrition labeling on food products will improve the food environment and will assist consumers to make informed choices about healthier foods as well as encourage food producers to improve the nutritional composition of their products and in the meanwhile to reduce the burden of malnutrition worldwide for more information contact us you

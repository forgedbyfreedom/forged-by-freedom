Worldwide, the number of diabetes cases is increasing. Today, one in 11 adults has diabetes, and this proportion is rising. An unhealthy diet and lack of physical exercise increase the risk of diabetes. But what is diabetes? The body produces insulin, which transforms consumed carbohydrates into energy. Without insulin, glucose from food and drinks accumulates in the blood. Most diabetics suffer from type 2 diabetes; their body produces insulin, but it is not effective. Many people have type 1 diabetes; their body does not produce insulin, and they need insulin injections to live. More than 400 million people have diabetes and are at risk from hyperglycemia. Diabetes can lead to a myocardial infarction, a stroke, blindness, kidney failure, or even amputation. Children are more exposed than ever to the risk of developing diabetes. We must encourage everyone to... Everyone should choose healthy foods and drinks and avoid overeating. Being active and doing vigorous physical exercise for half an hour a day is recommended. Any type of activity is fine. Anyone who thinks they may be at risk of developing diabetes should consult a doctor, follow their advice, and take any prescribed medication. With these measures, the world can halt the progression of diabetes and defeat the disease.

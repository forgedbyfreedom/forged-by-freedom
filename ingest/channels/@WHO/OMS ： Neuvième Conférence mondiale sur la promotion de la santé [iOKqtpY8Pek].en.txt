There are many obstacles to good health in our world: pollution, poor nutrition, harmful products, poor living conditions, and the stresses and tensions of modern life. Promoting health means empowering everyone to take charge of their health by giving them the information they need to make the right choices. It means governments considering the health consequences of their decisions so that no one is left behind. It means building healthy cities and communities that allow populations to thrive. It also means working together to end poverty and achieve a better future. In November in Shanghai, world leaders will meet to harness the power of health to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals. Promoting health is our health, our future, and our choice.

We are here at the Rangis International Market, the largest market in the world, south of Paris. It is here that we will launch World Health Day on April 7, 2015, focusing on the theme of food safety. We will highlight the important roles of all the actors working in the food chain. Food safety is crucial; nutrition is important; security of supply is important; everything is important, everything is interconnected. This requires mobilizing all sectors: civil society, academia, industry, and, of course, above all, governments.

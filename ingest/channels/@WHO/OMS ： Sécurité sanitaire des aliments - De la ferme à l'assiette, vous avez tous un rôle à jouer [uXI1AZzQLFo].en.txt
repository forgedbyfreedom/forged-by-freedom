Move up. Safe food is clean. It is not contaminated by microbes, viruses or chemicals. It is safe because the people who bring it to us take care every step of the way to keep it that way. From the farm, fishery and factory to our plates. Safe food is important because it keeps people healthy, communities productive, and economy strong. But not all of our food is safe. Every year, millions of people fall ill all over the world because they eat contaminated food. Antifood can cause disease. Any food can cause disease. nutrition and even death. We are all at risk, especially children, the old and the sick. To keep us all healthy, we must each do more to ensure our food, wherever it comes from, is grown and harvested, stored and distributed, prepared and served safely. This means we must know our food and handle it safely. Provide safe food to communities and manage the risk at every stage from the farm to the plate. We all need food. So we must all take step to make our food safe.

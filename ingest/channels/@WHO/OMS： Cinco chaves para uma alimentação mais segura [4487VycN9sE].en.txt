Among us, in our homes and near the food we eat, live some mysterious creatures, small but extremely energetic, really very small. These creatures are microorganisms, extremely small living beings. There are three types of microorganisms: the good ones, which help produce food and drinks such as cheese and yogurt, meat and fermented vegetables; other microorganisms are unwelcome, these are the bad ones, they are responsible for the bad smell, unpleasant taste and repulsive appearance of rotten food. But normally they don't cause disease. But there is another type of microorganism that we should be wary of; they don't make food smell bad or alter its appearance. These are the dangerous ones and it is necessary to be very careful with them. These are known as pathogenic microorganisms and can cause serious illnesses such as stomach aches, vomiting, diarrhea, fever and even death. They deceive us, they are invisible and dangerous. These little beings are very, very prolific; to increase their numbers they need food, water, time and heat. They can multiply very quickly. Fortunately, despite being strong and frightening, these  Microorganisms are vulnerable and can be avoided. All it takes are five simple rules, five key behaviors to get rid of them. Protect yourself and your family from diseases that are in your home. The rules are simple: These are the five keys to safer eating: Cleanliness to prevent contamination; separate foods; cross-contamination; cook food thoroughly to kill microorganisms; keep food at safe temperatures (hot or cold) to prevent bacterial growth; use safe water and raw materials to avoid contamination. Food producers are primarily responsible for the safety of the food we buy, but protect yourself and your family by applying these five keys to safer eating in your home. Use them; the responsibility is also yours.

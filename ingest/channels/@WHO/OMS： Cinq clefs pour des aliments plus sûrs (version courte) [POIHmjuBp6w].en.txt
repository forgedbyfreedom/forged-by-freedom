Here are your 5 keys to safer food: Make cleanliness a habit to avoid contamination; separate raw and cooked foods to prevent cross-contamination; cook food thoroughly to kill microorganisms; keep food at the right temperature, hot or cold, to prevent bacterial growth; use safe water and products to prevent contamination. Producers are primarily responsible for the food safety of the food you buy, but you can protect yourself and your family from illnesses contracted at home by applying these 5 keys to safer food. Use them; you too have a role to play.

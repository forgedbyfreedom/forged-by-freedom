Near us, in our homes and close to the food we eat, live some mysterious creatures that are very small but extremely active, really tiny. They are microorganisms, extremely small living beings. There are three different types of microorganisms. Some of the good ones help us make foods and drinks like cheese and yogurt, fermented meats and vegetables. Other microorganisms, the bad ones, are more troublesome. They are responsible for the bad smells, bad taste and disgusting appearance of rotting food, but they do not normally cause illness.  But there are also other types of microorganisms that we really need to be afraid of.  Even if they do not cause bad odors and do not change the appearance of food, they are very dangerous and one must be very wary of them. They are known as pathogenic microorganisms and can cause serious disorders such as stomach aches, vomiting, diarrhea, fever, and even death. They are deceptive, invisible, and dangerous.   They are small creatures that are very, very prolific.  To reproduce, they need food, water, time, and warmth.  They can multiply very quickly.  Fortunately, however powerful and frightening it may be, we can protect ourselves from these vulnerable microorganisms. You just need to apply five simple rules. Five key behaviors to get rid of it.   Protect yourself and your family from diseases that can be contracted at home.  The rules are simple to apply.  Here are your five keys to safer food. Make cleanliness a habit to avoid contamination. Keep raw foods separate from cooked foods to avoid cross-contamination. Cook food thoroughly to kill microorganisms. Keep food at the right temperature, either hot or cold, to prevent bacterial growth. Use safe water and products to prevent contamination. Producers bear primary responsibility for the food safety of the food you buy.  But you can protect yourself and your family from illnesses contracted at home by applying the five keys You too have a role to play.

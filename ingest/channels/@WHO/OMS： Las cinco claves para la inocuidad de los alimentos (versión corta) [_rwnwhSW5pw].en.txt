These are the five keys to food safety: maintain cleanliness to prevent contamination; separate raw and cooked foods so they don't contaminate each other; cook food thoroughly to kill microorganisms; keep food at safe temperatures (hot or cold) so bacteria don't multiply; use safe water and raw materials to prevent contamination. Food producers are primarily responsible for ensuring the food we buy is safe, but it's possible to protect ourselves and our families from foodborne illnesses by using these five keys to food safety. Apply them; there's so much you can do!

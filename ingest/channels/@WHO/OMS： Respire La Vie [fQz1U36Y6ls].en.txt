What causes nearly one in 100 deaths worldwide, affecting over 80% of city dwellers but often invisible? What leads to more than one in 100 deaths from heart disease, 34% of stroke deaths, and kills as many people each year as the number of times you breathe? The answer: air pollution. Air pollution is the primary environmental health risk we all face. It threatens our bodies, the climate, and our future. Yet solutions exist to purify our air and combat global warming.

In 1951, the newly born WHO was already being batheted by the prevailing geopolitical winds of its time just as it is today. In her opening address, the acting president of the World Health Assembly, Rajikumari Amrit Cow said this. The dark clouds that overshadow our skies today can and must be dispelled by the fulfillment on our part of the pledge to serve one another. Our work is surely a precious heritage which we may not forsake and a positive factor for overcoming the root causes of conflict. Today and this week I ask you to remember those words at this assembly. Member states will consider and hopefully adapt the WHO pandemic agreement. This is truly a historic moment. Even in the middle of crisis and in the face of significant opposition, you work tirelessly. You never gave up and you reached your goal. We are here to serve not our own interests but the 8 billion people of our world to leave a heritage for those who come after us and to work together for a healthier, more peaceful and more equitable world. Our current crisis is an opportunity to do just that and together we will do it.

Oral diseases like dental carries, gum disease, tooth loss and oral cancers are among the most common non-communicable diseases globally affecting nearly half of the world's population. And yet people affected by oral diseases continue to face challenges and inequalities in access to care, particularly in low-income communities and other vulnerable groups. In May 2021, under the leadership of Sri Lanka, the World Health Assembly adopted a landmark resolution on oral health. Since then, WHO has worked with partners to produce guidance to support member states in improving care for oral health. We must work together to improve access and affordability of essential oral health services through integration with primary health care and national universal health coverage plans. There is no health without oral health.

And while work can be good for mental health, increasing pressures in the workplace can be stressful. A difficult work environment or factors that continuously generate stress can worsen employees' mental health; in some cases, this can lead to suicide. However, suicide is never the answer; there is always hope. Employers and managers have an important role to play in protecting the mental health of their staff. They can create a culture that makes it easy for employees to talk openly about their mental health or ask for help. Employers can help their staff feel supported regarding their mental health, which is good for both parties. A few words can make all the difference.

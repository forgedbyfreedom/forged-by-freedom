In an emergency, a person's true situation may not always be immediately apparent. Emergency responders are often the first to intervene in a mental health crisis or suicide attempt. Their role is crucial in what happens next. They must help a person who needs support, empathy, and understanding. These situations should always be considered serious. The person in difficulty should be encouraged to talk. Bridges must be established with follow-up care. Emergency workers must also take care of themselves because sometimes they are the ones who need help. A few words can make a big difference.

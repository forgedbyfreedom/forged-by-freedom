People go to the doctor for many reasons, but sometimes the real reason is much more serious than what they say. Sadly, prejudices about mental health and suicide can discourage people from seeking help. Many people put on a brave face even though they feel very discouraged. Healthcare professionals are uniquely positioned to detect signs that someone is having mental health problems or suicidal thoughts. They can ask direct questions, but making contact and taking the time to listen, showing empathy, and offering support—a few words can make all the difference.

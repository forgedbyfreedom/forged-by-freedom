Adolescence can be very intense and full of newfound opportunities and freedoms, but it can also be marked by worries about exams, first loves, and peer or fashion pressures. For some teenagers, the burden can become so heavy that they end their lives. Suicide is never the answer; there is always hope. If you are a school principal, establish a plan to help students and teachers look after their mental health. Train staff to recognize the signs of trouble and to have sensitive and compassionate conversations. Foster a safe school environment where students and teachers know they can ask for help and talk to someone. If they don't need it, a few words can make all the difference.

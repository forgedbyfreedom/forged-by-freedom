Being at work can be good for mental health, but the increasing pressures of work can be stressful.  A difficult work environment or ongoing work-related stresses can worsen the mental health of employees. In some circumstances they may take their own lives. But suicide is never the answer. There is always hope. Employers and managers have an important role to play in looking out for the mental health of their staff. They can help create a culture where people feel safe to talk about their mental health and to seek help.  Employers can help their staff to feel supported with their mental health, which is good for staff and good for employers. A few words can make a world of difference.

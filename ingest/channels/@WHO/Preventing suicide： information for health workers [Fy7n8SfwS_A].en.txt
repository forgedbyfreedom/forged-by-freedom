people go to see doctors and nurses for many different reasons but sometimes the real reason is far more serious than what they say they're there for sadly the stigma of mental health and suicide can get in the way of people asking for help many people put on a brave face even if their mood is very low a health care worker is in a unique position to recognize the signs of someone who is struggling with their mental health or who's thinking about suicide they can ask gentle yet direct questions they can take the time to listen and they can show empathy offer ongoing support and follow-up a few words can make a world of difference

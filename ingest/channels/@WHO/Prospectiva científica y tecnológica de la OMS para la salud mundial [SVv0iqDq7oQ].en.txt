In today's ever-changing world, looking ahead is vital to building a better, more integrated future where access to global healthcare is a reality—a future where advances and progress in science and technology are widely available in the form of better treatments for diseases, new medicines, new technologies, and integrated experiences that respond to real needs and improve health for all. You might wonder what the paths are to achieve this, what obstacles must be overcome, what challenges must be faced, and that's where foresight comes in. Foresight is the process of monitoring the evolution of science and technology in the health sector, imagining possible futures, and then determining the concrete steps that must be taken today. In short, foresight is about actively creating and shaping the future we want by building a shared vision of desirable outcomes and enabling change. Foresight is a long-term vision, one that considers many different perspectives and stimulates the imagination. It explores how these issues relate to each other, how global health governance can respond to emerging scenarios, and what the potential risks and challenges are. The WHO's Global Health Foresight function helps us stay abreast of  The latest developments in relevant areas of research, science, and technology are used to proactively and systematically detect, anticipate, and prepare for issues of major global health concern and to draw useful conclusions for strategic planning, policymaking, and preparedness. The Scientific Division of Water established the Global Health Foresight initiative to help member states incorporate future-oriented thinking into their strategic health planning, integrate foresight into the work of regional offices and in EMU countries, and help build health systems today that are prepared to face the challenges ahead. The path to a better future starts today.

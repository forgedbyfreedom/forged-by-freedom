At the end of last year, the UN launched the global humanitarian overview, the collective plan to save 87 million lives in 2026. Now, we call on donors to support the appeal for the 23 billion US dollars needed for people facing the most severe needs. In crisis, health systems are often the first to be affected and the hardest to restore. When people miss out on the care they need, disease and death spreads. When health systems recover, so do societies. Protecting health is a humanitarian imperative [music] and a high return investment in global stability because there is no peace without [music] health and no help without peace.

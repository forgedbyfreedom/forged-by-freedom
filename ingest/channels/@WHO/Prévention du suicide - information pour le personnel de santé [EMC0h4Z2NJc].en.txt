People go to the doctor for many reasons, but the real reason is sometimes much more serious than they say. The stigma surrounding mental health and suicide can prevent people from seeking help. Many put on a brave face even when their spirits are at their lowest. Healthcare professionals are very well placed to spot the signs of someone who has mental health problems or is thinking about suicide. They can tactfully and gently ask direct questions and take the time to listen. They can show empathy and offer support. A few words can make all the difference.

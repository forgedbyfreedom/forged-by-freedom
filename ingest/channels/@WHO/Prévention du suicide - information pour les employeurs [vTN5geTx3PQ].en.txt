Work can contribute to good mental health, but workplace pressures can generate stress. A difficult work environment or persistent stressors can have detrimental effects on employees' mental health. In some circumstances, employees take their own lives, but suicide is never the solution; there is always hope. Employers and managers have an important role to play in protecting the mental health of their staff. They can create an environment that encourages people to talk and seek help. This is good for the staff and it's also good for the employers themselves. A few words can make all the difference.

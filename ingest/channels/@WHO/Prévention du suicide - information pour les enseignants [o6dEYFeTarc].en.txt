Adolescence is sometimes an exhilarating time full of possibilities and newfound freedom, but it can also be marked by worries related to exams, relationships, peer influence, and the latest trends. For some, all of this is too much to bear, and they take their own lives. Suicide is never the solution; there is always hope. If you run a school, develop a system to help students and teachers maintain good mental health. Train your staff on the signs to look for and the best ways to talk about it with compassion. Create a healthy school environment where students and teachers know they can ask for help and talk about their worries. A few words can make all the difference.

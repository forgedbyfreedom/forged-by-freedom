During an emergency response, the victims' true situation is not always clear. First responders are often the first on the scene in cases of attempted suicide or any other mental crisis; their role is crucial for what follows. The person they are helping needs empathy and understanding. These situations must always be considered serious. It is essential to encourage the person to talk and connect them with those who will provide follow-up care. First responders must also look after their colleagues, who sometimes need help as well. A few words can make all the difference.

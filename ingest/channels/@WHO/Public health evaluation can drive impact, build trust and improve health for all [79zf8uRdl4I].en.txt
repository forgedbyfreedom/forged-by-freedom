Many think evaluation is just another task in a busy schedule. But skipping evaluation is like trying to finish a puzzle without looking at all of the pieces. Can we see the full picture? Evaluation helps us take a step back so we can see where our efforts are making an impact and where we can improve. Evaluation is not the same as an audit. Audits are mandatory to ensure the right processes and controls are being followed. Evaluation is about learning, accountability, transparency, and informing strategic direction and results. It is a collaborative process providing recommendations for future actions. It identifies inspiring practices, highlights untapped opportunities, and helps us focus resources to make the most impact. Through independence and rigor, evaluation helps build trust with member states, donors, and partners, giving us everything we need to strengthen partnerships, evidence-based decision-making, and mobilizing resources. At the country level, we carry out independent evaluations to assess who's contributions, and demonstrate value for money. They help better planning to support national health priorities, strengthen partnerships, and bring new donors. Examples of recent evaluations across the organization include topics such as WHO's general program of work, non-communicable diseases, WHO's disability policy, health emergencies, water sanitation and hygiene programs, and many more. Sharing evaluation findings isn't just reporting results. It's about empowering stakeholders to develop effective solutions by giving a clear picture of needed priorities and resources. A healthy evaluation culture means we're not just reacting. We're able to learn, adapt strategically, and strengthen the organization's accountability. It isn't an ad hoc process. It's an essential function. By planning for success and working together as one who we can make a measurable difference and achieve meaningful results. Evaluation is like having a compass that points us in the right direction so that together we can build trust and be empowered to achieve better health for all.

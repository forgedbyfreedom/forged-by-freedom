WH coordinated scientific advice process or CSA for short is a guiding light for global health innovation. CSA merges insights from WH policy development and pre-qualification teams helping products that meet WH standards move efficiently from latestage trials to market to reach communities in need. The CSA process has four steps and takes approximately 10 weeks. Step one, eligibility determination. Developers submit a request for review by WH. Step two, formal application. A full submission package is provided for in-depth review. Step three, meeting. WH experts meet with developers to discuss and clarify advice. Step four, advice report. Developers receive a comprehensive report. Eligible products must show potential public health benefit either as new products or existing products being developed for new users. Timing is crucial for vaccines and therapies. Seek CSA before phase 3 trials. For diagnostics before clinical validation studies. CSA helps improve submission quality through optimized study design and accelerates market introduction for priority health products. Let CSA light the way so you can get your products to market better and faster.

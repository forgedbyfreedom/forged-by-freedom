African countries face enormous health challenges, and these health crises require significant funding. Therefore, we believe we cannot leave this funding solely to the WHO or other development partners. We decided that the best way to mobilize countries around this investment cycle is to ask them to contribute. This way, they will truly feel involved in financing the health sector, even though they have national budgets. However, health problems often require a coordinated response, such as pandemics, and this necessitates the mobilization of funds so that all countries can collectively address these emerging health challenges. Africa must mobilize in favor of health because today we are paying a heavy price for emerging health problems in Africa. Africa must mobilize to face future health challenges and contribute to financing the fight against pandemics.

Always follow your health worker's advice when using antibiotics. If you think you have a Sexually Transmitted Infection (STI), do not self medicate. See a health worker for an accurate diagnosis and treatment. Sticking to, and finishing, your treatment when you have an STI is the best thing cou can do to help stop antimicrobial resistance. Correctly using condom every time you have sex is the smartest way to protect yourself from STIs. Getting yourself tested for STIs is the only way to find out if you have an infection. Speak to your healthcare provider. We can all play a part in reducing antimicrobial resistance.

If I could see the viruses, I'd know they're everywhere. Viruses, bacteria, and other germs. Where we are, they are. Where we go, they go. Life is too precious to let such an easily preventable virus get in the way of moments of joy and happiness. A first kiss, a first step, a first goal, a brilliant joke, an amazing hug, an early start, a late- night laugh. Maybe that's the goal: to live each moment. Vaccines work so that we all have a long life.

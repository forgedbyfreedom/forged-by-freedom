If you could see viruses, you'd know they're everywhere: viruses, bacteria, and other germs. Where we are, they're there too. Where we go, they go too. Life is too precious to let a virus, so easily avoidable, come and spoil moments of joy and happiness: a first kiss, a first gesture, a first goal, laughter at a funny joke, hugging someone, getting up early in the morning, having a good evening. Maybe that's the point: to live every moment. Vaccines allow for a long life for all. The long life rolls.

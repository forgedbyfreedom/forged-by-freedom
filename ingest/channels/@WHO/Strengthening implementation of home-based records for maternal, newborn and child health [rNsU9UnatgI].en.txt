homebased records are Health documents that are used to record the history of Health Services an individual receives they exist in several formats such as antinal notes vaccination only cards child health booklets or integrated maternal and child health handbooks these simple tools can help health workers communicate important health information and may contribute to improved Health seeking behaviors and Home Care practices when planned designed and implemented well homebased records can contribute to Better Health outcomes for newborns children and women and yet common challenges get in the way of these tools working the way they should there may be a stock out of these records health workers may not fill out the records correctly or they may not be kept in good condition or brought to every Health visit getting program managers and stakeholders together from across multiple health programs to plan and Implement homebased records can be challenging how are homebased records performing in your country does your homebased record meet the needs of children women caregivers and health workers are there ways you could strengthen the implementation and use of home home based records in your country the World Health Organization UNICEF and jaica have developed guidance to help you do just that the guide walks you through activities and decisionmaking tools for planning designing implementing and monitoring homebased records in your program it also includes examples from different countries activities and blank templates that can be used throughout the program management cycle visit wh's website to download the resource then gather your core team together to get started with improved implementation of home based records we can strengthen their contributions to Primary Health Care and health programming for women and children

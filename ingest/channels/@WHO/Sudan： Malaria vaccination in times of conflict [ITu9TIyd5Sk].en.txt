Sudan is facing the biggest humanitarian crisis in the world with over thirteen million people who fled their homes. Of course, this has an impact on the health system in the country. While 12 out of the 18 states of this country are affected by the armed conflict with a lot of urgent health needs that WHO helps to alleviate, the health system has to continue to treat the burden of many diseases including malaria and other vector borne diseases. Sudan has recorded over 3 million cases last year with over 7000 deaths due to malaria, but the good news is that WHO with its work with the Ministry of Health introduced the malaria vaccine. So, this is a hope for all children in Sudan and the hope for the health system and the population of Sudan. The\hfirst phase\hof malaria vaccination started in November 2024 in two states and managed to reach nearly 35,000 children in its\hfirst 4 months\hof implementation. As per WHO recommendation all the vaccinated children received bed net provided by government resources. The Federal Ministry of Health is indeed committed to fight malaria with the support of WHO, UNICEF and other partners. They undertake\hcontinuous efforts for achieving the best possible coverage and preparing for the scale up in additional states.

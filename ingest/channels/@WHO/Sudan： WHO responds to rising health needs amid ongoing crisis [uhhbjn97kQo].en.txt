In Sudan, unrelenting violence has led to widespread hunger, disease, and suffering. There are reports from the city of Alasar that people are eating animal feed to survive. Millions are going hungry and famine conditions have been confirmed in parts of the country. About 770,000 children under 5 years are expected to suffer from severe acute malnutrition this year. In the first 6 months of this year, nutrition centers supported by WHO have treated more than 17,000 severely malnourished children with medical complications. But many more are beyond reach. Kolera has swept across Sudan with all states reporting outbreaks. Nearly 100,000 cases have been reported since July last year. With who support or alera vaccination campaigns have been conducted in several states. While we are seeing a declining trend in numbers, there are gaps in this surveillance and progress is fragile. WHO has prepositioned essential medicines and supplies anticipating challenges with access due to rains and floods. But our efforts are held back by limited access and the lack of funding. The best medicine is peace.

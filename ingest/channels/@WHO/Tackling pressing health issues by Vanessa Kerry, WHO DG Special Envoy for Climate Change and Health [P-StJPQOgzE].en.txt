The WHO is dedicated to improving the health of people everywhere, but it needs your support now more than ever. Today, over two billion people lack essential health services, and we face a shortfall of 10 million health workers. Add to that rising pandemics of infectious disease, maternal mortality, and non-communicable diseases we are witnessing from climate change. threats which are hitting the most vulnerable. Poor health affects more than just individual well-being. It fuels insecurity, disrupts economies, and deepens inequities. The WHO needs to raise 7.1 billion to fund the core of its program of work over the next four years. This investment will help to tackle pressing health issues, increase the health workforce, create pandemic preparedness, ensure health system resilience, reduce inequities, and address climate related health impacts. It's essential that governments, donors, and foundations understand the power health has to help address today's challenges. It is key to our economics, security, and well-being. You can make a difference, too. Supporting the WH means investing in a future where everyone can access the care they need.

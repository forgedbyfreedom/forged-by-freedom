Some threats aren't obvious in our homes, our communities, our cities. They're always among us, often unseen, hiding in plain sight. Non-communicable diseases like cardiovascular diseases, certain types of diabetes, cancer, chronic respiratory disease can affect us quietly. And every year they take millions of lives. Most often they are influenced by the world around us, factors outside our control. But the power to prevent and manage them is in our hands. Change isn't only possible. It's already within our reach. By addressing the underlying health and risk factors, by meaningfully including people living with non-communicable diseases into decision making, understanding how environments and policies put us at risk. By supporting bold, inclusive non-communicable disease policies like high taxes on cigarettes and other unhealthy products, decreasing the salt and fat content in our food, and ensuring access to essential medicines, we can build healthier futures for everyone.

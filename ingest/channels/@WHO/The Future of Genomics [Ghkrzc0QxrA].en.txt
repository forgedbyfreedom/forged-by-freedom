the story of Our Lives can be told through the story of our genes genomics helps us tell these stories it gives us a deeper understanding of ourselves and can shape how we address health challenges worldwide for example through genomics we can identify genetic variants within certain populations that are linked to their risk of heart disease or diabetes changing how we approach prevention and treatment for the potential of genomics to be realized however it needs to include data from populations all over the world on top of this every country needs to have access to it something that's now more possible than ever thanks to lower cost Innovations Equitable access to genomics will not only enrich our understanding of ourselves but ensure that everyone everywhere benefits from it the story of genomics is the story of our lives and where that story leads is up to us

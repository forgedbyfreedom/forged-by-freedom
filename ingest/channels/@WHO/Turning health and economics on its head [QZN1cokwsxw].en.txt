for centuries we have been chasing the dream of boundless economic growth health continues to be considered a peripheral concern of economic policies spending on health is seen as a cost rather than an investment chronic under-investment in health has prevented many people around the world from getting needed health services and products it has also limited pandemic preparedness and exacerbated persistent inequities instead of seeing health as a byproduct of the economy it's time to look at how the economy can be directed to achieve societal goals including health health is a fundamental human right health for all is the world's overall goal and the economy must be restructured to serve it we need to radically rethink the economics of health for all by examining the value of health and how it is generated and distributed in the society this is why the world health organization established the council on the economics of health for all it starts with the mission of health for all and works backwards asking how we can structure and deliver that goal in other words an economy for the common good rather than health for the economy the council will reimagine four interrelated areas one value and measurement where we rethink how health and well-being is valued in the economy and how to measure it and guide investments 2 capacity where we reimagine public sector leadership and dynamic capabilities to shape an economy that engenders health 3 finance where we redesigned financial instruments and institutions to deliver long-term finance for health that is robust in quantity and in quality four innovation where we reshape innovation for the common good and structure intellectual property rights to ensure products of collective efforts are affordable and accessible to all through these areas we will create a transformative narrative on health for all expanding the horizon of what's possible and uncovering paths toward better alternatives let's put health and well-being at the center of new economic thinking and policy making the time for health for all is now

People with hypertension are like walking bombs. They carry a deadly condition that could go off anytime. And every year it does go off in 11 million people, cutting their lives short and robbing families of someone they love and economies of billions of dollars in health costs and lost productivity. No other risk factor or pathogen claims as us many lives. I'm one of the lucky ones. Of course, my hypertension is well managed with medication. And that's the great paradox of hypertension that it can be controlled with relatively inexpensive medication. But globally only around 1 in five people with hypertension have it under control. The biggest barrier is access. Access to medicine and access to affordable validated blood pressure devices. If we can control hypertension in just half of those affected, we can make a massive difference, preventing 76 million deaths by 2050 and saving 100 billion US every year in health costs. So what's stopping us? Let's confront the challenge, embrace the opportunity, and save lives.

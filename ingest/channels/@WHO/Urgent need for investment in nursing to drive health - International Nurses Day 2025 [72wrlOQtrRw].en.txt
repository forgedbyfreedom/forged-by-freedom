A very happy International Nurses Day on behalf of everyone in the World Health Organization. Dear distinguished nurses all around the world, dear friends, colleagues and valued partners. Today we honor and celebrate the contributions millions of nurses make every day for all people everywhere. This year we are delighted to be launching the state of the world's nursing report 2025 with policy recommendations to support, protect and invest in nursing to drive better health outcomes, gender equity and economic growth. We encourage you and early career nurses to engage with the report and actively lead in shaping the future of nursing. Given the economic challenge of today, I sincerely thank all the 194 member states of the WH for continuing investment in nursing, including fair salary and working conditions in order to retain talents to care for the population for the long term. Investment in nursing is an investment in people and the vulnerable. It is an investment in a healthier, safer, and fairer future for all. I am proud to be a nurse. Messioku Malo Albito. Thank you very much.

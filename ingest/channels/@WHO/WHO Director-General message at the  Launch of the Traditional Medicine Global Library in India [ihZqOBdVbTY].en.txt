Dear colleagues and friends, the WHO Traditional Medicine Global Library is a landmark project that connects cultures and generations to strengthen health systems research and policy. With over 1.6 6 million records, six regional portals, and 194 country pages. The library includes evidence maps, AI tools, multimedia content, and teatic collections. Developed in collaboration with WHO member states, health authorities and civil society, the library responds to calls from leaders in the G20 and bricks bricks to create a repository for traditional knowledge systems. It fosters global collaboration and equitable access through partnerships with research for life and other open science platforms ensuring protection of intellectual property and usability for all communities. The library is expanding through the dedicated work of policy makers, researchers, health workers and communities. At its core, the WHO traditional medicine global library advances a holistic people centered vision of health and well-being. Thank you all for your commitment to harnessing the power of ancient wisdom and modern science to support countries on their journey towards health for all. I thank you.

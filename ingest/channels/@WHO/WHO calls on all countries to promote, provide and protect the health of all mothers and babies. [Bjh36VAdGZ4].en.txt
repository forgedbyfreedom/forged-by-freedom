World Health Day this year is a cause for Bose celebration and reflection. Today we celebrate the many lives saved thanks to commitment to maternal and child health both globally and locally. A new WHO report shows that women are more likely to survive pregnancy and child birth than ever before. However, despite a 40% decline in maternal deaths since 2000, hundreds of thousands of women still lose their lives due to complications in pregnancy or child birth each year. Nearly all of these deaths are preventable if women have access to quality care before, during, and after birth. Likewise, most still births and newborn deaths are preventable, particularly if there is high quality care around the time of birth. Our report shows that progress is possible with a commitment by governments to invest in health infrastructure, health workers and strengthened primary health care. All women need quality maternal care including contraception and family planning no matter who they are or where they live.

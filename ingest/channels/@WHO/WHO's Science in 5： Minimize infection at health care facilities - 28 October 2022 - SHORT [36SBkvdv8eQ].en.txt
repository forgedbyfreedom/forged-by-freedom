let's start with the points at which a patient is most at risk of infection in a healthcare facility some of the highest risk places are in intensive locations like an operating theater but even in other locations it's possible for patients to pick up an infection especially if healthcare workers are moving back and forth from higher risk settings to lower risk ones because they can actually transport bacteria and viruses on their hands and that's why one of the most important measures is for health care workers to practice proper hand hygiene

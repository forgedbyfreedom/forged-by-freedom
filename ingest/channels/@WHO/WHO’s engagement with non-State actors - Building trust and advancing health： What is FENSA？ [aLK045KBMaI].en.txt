Fenser stands for framework of engagement with non-state actors. It was negotiated and approved by member states at the 69th session of the World Health Assembly in May 2016. Fencer is enabling policy that provides a set of key principles, guidance, and operational procedures to facilitate WH's engagement with non-state actors. It helps balance risks against expected benefits while protecting and preserving WH's integrity, reputation, and public health mandate. Fenser recognizes that non-state actors play a critical role in supporting WH's work and that inclusiveness, dialogue, and appropriate engagement are key to achieving WH's mission. WH's engagement with non-state actors can benefit global public health. Fenser outlines key principles, rules and operational procedures for engagement and collaboration between WH and four categories of non-state actors. Non-state actors include non-governmental organizations, private sector entities, philanthropic foundations, and academic institutions.

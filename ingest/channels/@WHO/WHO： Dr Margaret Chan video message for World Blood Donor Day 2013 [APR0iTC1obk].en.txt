greetings from the World Health Organization on world Blood Donor day I thank the many thousands of you who are holding events more importantly I thank the many millions of blood donors around the world who freely offer this gift of life in high income countries transfusion is most commonly used for trauma cancer therapy and heart and transplant surgery the situation elsewhere is more basic in low and middle inome countries most blood is for the management of complications of pregnancy and child birth and the treatment of severe childhood anemia too many people are still dying because they lack timely access to blood and blood products this is a huge and unacceptable tragedy the will continue to assist in the strengthening of national blood test systems so that all people in need have access to Safe blood and blood products dto has long stressed the use of voluntary unpaid donors as the safest source of blood we have some good news today some 60 countries now receive Almost 100% of their blood from voluntary donations this is a most welcome trend but we need to do more the bigo's goal is for all countries to obtain 100% of their blood from voluntary donations by 2020 on this world Blood Donor day let us vow to work together to reach this goal thank you

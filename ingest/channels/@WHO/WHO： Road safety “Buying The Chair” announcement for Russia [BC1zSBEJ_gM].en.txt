You have a growing baby. Congratulations on choosing the right new car seat for him. Did you know that when choosing, you shouldn't be guided only by appearance and price. The car seat should correspond to the child's height, weight, and age. It should also be certified in accordance with current regulations. And remember, even if you drive carefully, there are hundreds of drivers on the road besides you who can cause an accident through no fault of yours. Buckle up or lose your child in the car. Always use a car seat.

w-h-o and its partners have reviewed what measures can significantly reduce road traffic deaths and injuries the result is save lives six effective strategies for reducing the impact of road traffic crashes improving enforcement of traffic laws is one of the six most effective strategies to make roads safer speeding laws should set speeds on urban roads of 50 kilometers per hour or less and local authorities should be able to modify these laws as necessary national drink driving laws should be based on blood alcohol concentration and establish a limit for general population of 0.05 grams per deciliter and of 0.02 grams per deciliter for young or novice drivers motorcycle laws should ensure all motorcycle drivers and passengers wear and fastened quality standard helmets on all road types and no matter what the engine type seatbelt laws should apply to drivers and passengers on the front and rear seats child restraint laws should ensure the use of restraints based on age weight or height and restrict children under a certain age or height from sitting in the front seat as society changes road safety laws need to be updated to account for new risks building public trust encourages people to obey the law especially if they believe it is enforced fairly investing in the enforcement of traffic laws will save lives

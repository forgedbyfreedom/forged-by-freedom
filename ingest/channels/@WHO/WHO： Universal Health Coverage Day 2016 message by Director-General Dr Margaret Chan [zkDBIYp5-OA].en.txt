greetings from the World Health Organization in Geneva another turbulent year approaches its end we have faced outbreaks of Zika yellow fever and cholera conflicts in Iraq Syria and Yemen and earthquakes in several countries every one of these events reminds us that the best defense against sudden shocks to health is universal health coverage universal health coverage is a wise investment for other reasons ensuring that everybody receives essential health services without experiencing financial hardship contributes to social cohesion and stability and furthers the goal of ending poverty universal health coverage is understandably a major health target among the sustainable development goals I have been encouraged to see so many countries and all income levels across different regions of the world making good progress to us u XE u XE is not cheap but it is affordable when the right decisions are made strengthening any health system must begin by investing in good primary health care services being able to reliably measure progress is one of the best incentives for continued investment last month the group that advises on tracking progress towards the sustainable development goals adopted two measures for UX e1 monitors access to essential health services the other measures the financial impact on households of out-of-pocket expenditure on health many countries already have data on both indicators but some gaps need to be filled the victor and its partners are working with countries to strengthen their health information systems as the foundation for smart and successful policy decisions this is the last you exceed day I will celebrate as director general of the Beco I wish my successor every success as the big show continues to support countries in their journey towards uxe people deserve decent and affordable health care UHC honors that right thank you

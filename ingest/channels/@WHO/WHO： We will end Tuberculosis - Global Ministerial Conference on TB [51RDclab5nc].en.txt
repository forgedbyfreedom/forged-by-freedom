Tuberculosis (TB) may seem a thing of the past but it's actually the world's top infectious killer today. Over 5 000 women, men and children still die each day from TB. Multidrug-resistant TB is also a top global health security threat. It is airborne and can affect any one of us. Yet global actions and investments to end TB are falling far short of the targets set by the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals and the World Health Organization's End TB Strategy. Among global health development efforts, investing to end TB is top value for money with the return of over 40 dollars on every dollar invested. To drive down TB deaths and suffering, we need intensified political commitment, multisectoral actions and more investments. This November, Ministers and other global leaders from across the world, will meet for the World Health Organization’s Global Ministerial Conference on Ending TB in the Sustainable Development Era to commit to ramping up the fight against TB. This will be a landmark towards ending TB and will build momentum towards the first UN General Assembly high-level meeting on TB in 2018. Join us, together we will #endTB

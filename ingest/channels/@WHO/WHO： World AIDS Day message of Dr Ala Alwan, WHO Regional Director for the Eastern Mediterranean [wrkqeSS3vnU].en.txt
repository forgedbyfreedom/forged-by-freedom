HIV treatment is life-saving. Governments in partnerships with civil society with people affected by the disease. The private sector and other partners need to join hands. Together, we should ensure that people at risk of HIV have easy access to testing. It is equally important that everyone who is diagnosed with HIV is linked to treatment services. They should also be supported to remain in lifelong care and treatment to keep the virus suppressed. On World's AIDS Day this year, I call on all stakeholders to put commitment into action and bring the HIV treatment to all people living with HIV. Thank you.

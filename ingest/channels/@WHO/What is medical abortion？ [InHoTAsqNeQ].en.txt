What is medical abortion? Medical abortion involves taking pills called meaprione and misoproto to end a pregnancy. Because of their importance to safe abortion care, they are included on WHO's global essential medicines list. Medical abortion is an extremely safe process when performed according to WHO's recommendations with minimal risk of complications. Why is this important? Well, these medications play a really important role in increasing access to safe abortions globally. Scientific evidence from around the world is very clear. Restricting access to safe abortions, including medical abortions, is only likely to drive more women and girls to seek more unsafe procedures.

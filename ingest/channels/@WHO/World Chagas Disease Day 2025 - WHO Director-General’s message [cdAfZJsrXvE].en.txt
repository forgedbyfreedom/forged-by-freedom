Chagas disease is one of the world's most neglected health challenges. While it affects mainly poor communities in Latin America, its reach is growing as it moves to new continents. Globally, more than 7 million people are infected and more than 10,000 people die every year from Chagas disease. We can change this through prevention, control and care. No one should suffer in silence. Ending chagas disease is not just a medical challenge. It's a social responsibility.

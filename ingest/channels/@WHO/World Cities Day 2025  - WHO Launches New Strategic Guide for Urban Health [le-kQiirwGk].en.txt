Excellencies, honorable mayors, distinguished guests, dear colleagues and friends, happy World Cities Day. I'm honored to launch who's new guide for decision makers on taking a strategic approach to urban health. This new guidance contains concrete ideas to meet the growing demand for integrated urban health solutions. It represents the first comprehensive who framework to help governments design, coordinate and implement crosscutting strategies for urban health and health equity. More than half of humanity lives in urban areas where health, inequality, environment and economy intersect creating complex risks but also unique opportunities. Urban areas are on the front line of many modern health challenges including air pollution, slams, climate change and pandemics. But urban areas can also be the front line for transformative change. Seething this opportunity means acting strategically and coherently to bring different sectors of policym into alignment with the shared goal of building healthier and fairer cities. Urban systems need to work together to advance health, equity, sustainability, and resilience. On this World Cities Day, WHO calls on municipal and national leaders to adopt a more strategic approach to improve the health of billions of city dwellers and to make urban areas more livable, more just and more sustainable. I thank you.

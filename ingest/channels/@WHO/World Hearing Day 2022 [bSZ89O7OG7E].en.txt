on world hearing day we celebrate the gift of hearing hearing is precious but it's also fragile and once lost it doesn't come back who's world report on hearing 2021 showed that by 2050 700 million people could live with disabling hearing loss many common causes of hearing loss can be prevented industrial noise and excessively loud music are among the most common and preventable causes of permanent hearing loss wjo estimates that over a billion young people face the risk of permanent hearing loss as a result of listening to music at dangerously high volumes to address this who is launching the global standard for safe listening venues and events as well as handbook on safe listening implemented together these tools will go a long way in reducing hearing loss worldwide who calls on all governments industry partners and civil society to work with us in implementing and advocating for these new standards to promote safe listening together we can safeguard this essential way to experience the world

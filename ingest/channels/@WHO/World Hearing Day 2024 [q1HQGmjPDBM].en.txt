hearing loss affects more than 1.5 billion people around the world it's often called an invisible disability not just because there are no visible symptoms but also because it has long been stigmatized stigma often prevents people with hearing loss from accessing the care services and products they need we can all play a part in changing that when you talk to someone with hearing loss make sure they can see your face and speak slowly and clearly there is no need to shout this world hearing day we can all help to change mindsets and make ear and Hearing Care a reality for all

Even in war, health care is sacred. And yet, WHO recorded almost 2,500 attacks on health in just the past two years. More than 2,000 health workers and patients lost their lives. Despite this, every day, many health workers put themselves in harm's way to serve others. Week after week, hospitals are bombed, ambulances are blocked, and health workers and patients are threatened, injured, and killed. Attacks on healthcare are attacks against humanity. On World Humanitarian Day, we honor the courage of those who keep going and we call on world leaders to protect them. Attacks on healthcare must cease. The best medicine is peace.

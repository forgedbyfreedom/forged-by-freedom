 today is World Neglected Tropical Dizzes Day, and globally 1.4 billion people are at risk of neglected tropical dizzes or entities, a group of 21 dizzes that afflict some of the poorest, most remote and vulnerable communities in the world. The fight against entities is one of the outstanding success stories in global health of the past 15 years. 58 countries have eliminated at least one entity, including 9 countries in 2025 alone, and the number of people who need protection from an entity has dropped by one-third globally. Today, we say thank you to the health workers who dedicate their lives to fighting entities. Together, we have come so far, but we still have much work to do. Together, we must protect progress, scale up what we know works, and unlock new financing. Together, let's unite, act, and eliminate neglected tropical dizzes.

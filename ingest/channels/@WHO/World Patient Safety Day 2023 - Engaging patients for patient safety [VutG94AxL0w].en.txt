nobody should be harmed when they seek health care and yet an estimated one in every 10 patients experiences harm in healthcare facilities globally more than 3 million deaths occur each year due to unsafe care World patient safety day is an opportunity to highlight this avoidable problem this year's team is engaging patients for patient safety this means listening to the voices of patients and integrating their input into policies and planning for health services our Global campaign calls for elevating the voices of patients and empowering them to be informed advocates in their own care because if it's not safe it's not care

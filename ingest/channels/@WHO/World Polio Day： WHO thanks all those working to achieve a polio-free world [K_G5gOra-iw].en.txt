greetings from the World Health Organization in Geneva Humanity has very few opportunities to improve this world in a permanent way polio eradication is one making the world polio free will be a Perpetual gift to all future Generations we are very close India recorded its last case four years ago Nigeria another principal sanctuary of the polio virus has now gone more than a year with no new cases reported and with excellent surveillance in place in Afghanistan and Pakistan the two remaining endemic countries the polio programs have under gone striking improvements with good results and fewer cases this year as we commemorate World polio day our thanks go to polio workers who make such a courageous and tireless effort to vaccinate children in some of the hardest to reach places on Earth we thank the medical and laboratory Personnel who examine every paralyzed child and test every sample we acknowledge the rotarians around the world who give their time and energy both to vaccinate children and to Advocate to communities and political leaders finally let me thank the donors for their steadfast commitment all these efforts express our Collective determination to create a polio free world where no child will ever again be crippled or die from a disease that is entirely preventable thank you

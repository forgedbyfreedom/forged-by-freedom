Sleep is not a luxury. Sleep is health. When we don't get enough rest, our mood changes, our focus shifts, and even our immunity suffers. And for all of us, young or old, who are under constant pressure, good sleep is even more essential. Mental well-being is part of whole person health, and sleep is one of its foundations. Tonight, try one simple change. Put your phone away 30 [music] minutes before bed. Your mind and your body deserve the rest.

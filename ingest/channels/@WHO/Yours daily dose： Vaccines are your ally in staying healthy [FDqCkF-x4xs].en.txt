Vaccines aren't just for children. They protect people of all ages. From infants to older adults. Vaccines help guard against diseases caused by viruses and bacteria like measles, influenza, and pneumonia. Some vaccines even protect against certain cancers such as cervical and liver cancer by preventing the infections that can lead to them. As we grow, our health needs a change. But protection always matters. Talk to your health provider about the vaccines recommended for you and your family. Protection starts with prevention.

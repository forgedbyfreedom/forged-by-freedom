Hi, my name is Xir. Today was a very unusual day. My whole family went to watch our country's team play at the national stadium. We are hosting the World Cup. It was a wonderful day. We laughed and rejoiced the whole time. Mom says that big events are great, but they can also be dangerous. Luckily, we had nothing to worry about because the organizers did so much to ensure that we were safe when we saw ambulances near the stadium. Firefighters and police cars were just like Sam's favorite toys. We immediately felt safe. Mom says that the rescue services spent a long time preparing for the event and if something happens, they will immediately help. On the way to the stadium, Dad was given a brochure with information on safety rules. Half of the match had passed and Sam and I wanted a snack. First, we washed our hands, then we went to the food stall, but it seemed dirty to us so that we wouldn't suddenly get sick. Mom and Dad took us to another stall that was recommended by the authorities as safe, and then the second half of the match began. Hooray for joy.  Sam screamed so loudly that he started coughing. Dad told him to cover his mouth with a handkerchief or cough into his sleeve so he wouldn't infect the people sitting next to us. It's incredible how many people there are. It's not surprising that this is called a mass event. Countries have agreed among themselves on how to make mass events safer. For this, they use an agreement called the International Health Regulations. Thanks to these measures, everyone who came to the stadium was safe. All the work that the organizers of the championship did will make people's lives a little better. I will dream about today for a long time. I hope that next time our team wins. M

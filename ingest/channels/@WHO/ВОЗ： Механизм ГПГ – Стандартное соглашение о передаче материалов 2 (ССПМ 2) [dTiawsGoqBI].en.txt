WHO will engage with each manufacturer through negotiations to ensure an understanding of the tensions regarding regulatory aspects of product qualification, labeling and packaging. This way, at the onset of the next pandemic, WHO will quickly communicate with its manufacturing partners, who will already know exactly what WHO expects from them. The result will be a more rapid global response, equal access to life-saving vaccines.

In today's world, many things prevent people from enjoying good health. Environmental pollution, poor nutrition, unhealthy foods, and poor housing conditions are typical of today's stress and overexertion. To improve public health, people need to be able to take their own health into their own hands. To do this, they should be provided with information that will enable them to make healthy lifestyle choices. It is necessary for authorities to consider how the decisions they make will affect the health of citizens so that no one is left behind. To achieve this, we need to build healthy cities and local communities conducive to human prosperity and well-being. This November, leaders from around the world will gather in Shanghai to harness the potential of health to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals. Strengthening health. Our health is our future and our choice.

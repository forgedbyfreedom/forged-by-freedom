What causes one in nine deaths worldwide, harms the health of more than 80 percent of city dwellers, but is often invisible, causes more than a quarter of cardiovascular deaths, 34 percent of stroke deaths, and kills as many people each year as you breathe in. The answer is air pollution. Air pollution is the single greatest risk to environmental health that affects us all. It threatens our planetary health, our climate, and our future. However, there are ways to clean the air on the planet and prevent global warming. Let's work together to combat this global crisis to save our planet and ensure a healthy future for all of us.

You've probably heard of the concept of universal health coverage, but what does it mean? Let's think about what you need to improve and maintain your health. Can you seek help from qualified doctors? Is treatment available that will help you and be safe? Do you have access to medicine and other necessary medical products? Who pays for all of this? Are there rules for specifying the necessary services for you, your family, and friends whenever they need them? Do government agencies have accurate information about the health care system, allowing them to make the right decisions so that everything works as it should? Maintaining health requires people, services, goods, finances, rules, information, and all of these components must work smoothly even in times of crisis. A good health care system not only treats the sick, but helps people lead a healthy lifestyle and stay healthy. The World Health Organization works worldwide to ensure that all people and all population groups receive the quality services they need and are protected from health hazards without experiencing financial hardship. This is what we call universal health coverage for your health and the health of everyone.

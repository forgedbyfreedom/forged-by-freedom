In today's constantly changing world, it is important to look ahead to build a better shared future in which people all over the world can benefit from health services; a future in which discoveries and advances in science and technology are available to the masses in the form of more effective treatments for diseases; new drugs; advanced technologies; and comprehensive services that meet real needs and improve the health of all people. Perhaps you are wondering how to achieve this; what obstacles need to be overcome; what problems need to be solved; here, the long-term forecasting method will come in handy; long-term forecasting is a method for studying important advances in science and technology for health by creating possible future scenarios and then identifying specific steps to take today. In short, long-term forecasting allows us to actively imagine and shape the future we want to live in by developing a shared vision of desired outcomes and creating the preconditions for change. Long-term forecasting is thinking ahead; synthesizing a range of different opinions and activating the imagination; how certain problems are related; how the structure of global health care can respond to the scenarios they generate; what are the possible risks and obstacles? Your department for long-term forecasting of health trends helps us stay on top of the latest trends in science and technology research so that we can proactively and systematically  To identify, understand, and address issues of critical importance to the future of global health and draw useful conclusions that enable strategic planning, policy development, and preparation for future change. The WHO Directorate for Research has established a long-term health forecasting unit to assist Member States in integrating forward-looking thinking into strategic health planning. The integration of long-term forecasting into the work of WHO regional and country offices enables the creation of health systems today that can address the challenges of tomorrow. The path to a better future begins today.

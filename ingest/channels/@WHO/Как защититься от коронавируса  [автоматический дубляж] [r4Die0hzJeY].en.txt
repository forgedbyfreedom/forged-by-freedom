K type 19 is an infectious disease caused by a new type of coronavirus, previously unknown to humans, transmitted from person to person mainly through droplets sprayed during conversation, coughing and sneezing. These droplets can settle on the mucous membranes of the mouth or nose of people nearby. The droplets are too heavy and therefore cannot travel long distances; they can fly about a meter and quickly settle on various surfaces. Therefore, transmission of the virus mainly occurs through close contact. The exact lifespan of the virus on different surfaces is still unknown, so it makes sense to regularly clean surfaces, especially in close proximity to infected people. We touch many things with our hands that may contain the virus, so you should not touch your eyes, nose, and mouth, as you can bring the virus into your body. When coughing or sneezing, cover your mouth and nose with a bent elbow or a disposable tissue. In the latter case, immediately throw the used litter tray in a closed bin. The most effective way to prevent the spread of the new type of corona virus is to frequently clean your hands with an alcohol-based hand sanitizer or soap and water.

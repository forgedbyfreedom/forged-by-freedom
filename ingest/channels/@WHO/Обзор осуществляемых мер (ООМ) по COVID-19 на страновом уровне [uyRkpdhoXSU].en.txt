Miyako Severe COVID-19 has upended our lives as the world moves from the initial phase of the outbreak to a protracted phase that could last several months. Countries need to reflect on their responses, see how they can improve them, and share experiences and successes with other countries to facilitate these important discussions. The World Health Organization has developed guidance and tools to help countries plan and conduct midterm reviews of their responses. These reviews are either online or in-person. These are country-led, focused discussions involving key stakeholders involved in the response to the COVID-19 public health emergency in countries. Depending on their scope, they last from a few hours to two days and provide opportunities for participants to share experiences, challenges, best practices, and build consensus on lessons learned. They help countries continually reflect on their responses and, as needed, revise national and court-appointed COVID-19 strategies to reduce morbidity and mortality and reduce the impact on livelihoods.

Diabetes is a growing problem worldwide Currently, one in eleven adults has diabetes and the number of such people is growing The risk of developing diabetes increases if people do not eat a healthy diet and do not move and exercise enough What is diabetes The human body produces insulin, which converts consumed carbohydrates into the energy needed without insulin Sugar from food and drinks accumulates in the blood Most people with diabetes have type 2 of this disease This means that the body produces insulin, but it functions ineffectively A smaller number of people have type 1 diabetes Their body does not produce insulin and they need an insulin injection to live High blood sugar is a high risk for more than 400 million people with diabetes Diabetes can lead to heart attack, stroke, blindness, kidney failure and amputation [music] More children today than ever before are at high risk of developing diabetes It is necessary to strongly recommend to all people Choose the right foods and drinks and do not eat too much Maintain an active lifestyle Move vigorously for half an hour  Any physical activity per day is beneficial. Anyone who believes they are at risk for diabetes should consult a doctor, follow medical instructions, and take prescribed medications. By taking these measures, we can stop the rise in the disease and defeat diabetes.

HIV treatment is life-saving, and therefore governments must work together in partnership with people living with HIV, civil society, the private sector, and all other partners. Together, we must ensure that people at risk of HIV infection have easy access to HIV testing. Equally important is ensuring that everyone diagnosed with HIV has access to treatment services and is supported to maintain lifelong care and treatment to suppress the virus. On this World AIDS Day, I call on all stakeholders to fulfill their commitments and make HIV treatment available to all people living with HIV.

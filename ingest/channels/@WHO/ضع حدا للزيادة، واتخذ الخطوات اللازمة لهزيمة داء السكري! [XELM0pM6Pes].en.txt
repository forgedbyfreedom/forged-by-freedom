Diabetes is on the rise around the world. One in eleven adults has diabetes, and this number is growing. The risk of developing diabetes increases if individuals do not eat healthily or get enough physical activity. So what is diabetes? The body produces insulin, which converts the carbohydrates we eat into the energy we need. Without insulin, sugar from food and drink builds up in the bloodstream. Most people with diabetes have type 2 diabetes, meaning their bodies produce insulin, but it is ineffective. Fewer people have type 1 diabetes and cannot produce insulin themselves. They need insulin injections to survive. High blood sugar means high risks for more than 400 million people living with diabetes. Diabetes can lead to heart attacks, strokes, blindness, kidney failure, and amputations. More than ever, children are at risk. We need to encourage everyone to Choosing the right foods and drinks and avoiding overeating is important. Be active; move vigorously for half an hour daily, and any activity is fine. Anyone who believes they are at risk of developing diabetes should visit a doctor, follow medical advice, and take medication if prescribed. By following these steps, the world can stop the rise in diabetes cases and defeat it.

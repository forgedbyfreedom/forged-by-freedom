collaborations are a great way to encourage group work in your class they're integrated with Google docs to allow students to work together synchronously or asynchronously on a shared document create as many collaborations as you need with up to 50 collaborators you can assign collaborations to individuals or to predefined groups adding or removing user access is as simple as clicking on student or group names as soon as you click start collaborating a new Google doc opens up in a new window window and will'll have all of the sharing permissions set up to match what you selected collaborations will bring a whole new way of working together in your class

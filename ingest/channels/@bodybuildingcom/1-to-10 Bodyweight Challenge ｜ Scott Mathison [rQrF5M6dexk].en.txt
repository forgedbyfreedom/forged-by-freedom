Today I've got a movement challenge for you. We're going to be doing a ladder. Basically we're doing four exercises for 1 rep each, then 2 reps each, then 3, all the way to 10 reps. All right, let's get after it. Everything about this challenge is fast. We want to perform our reps fast, we want to transition between movements fast. Rest periods between rounds for this challenge are never going to be more than 60 seconds. For the first round you are only doing 4 reps, so you're not going to be too tired. I do recommend taking a moment to reset, but again, you're not going to need much time. Once you get to the later rounds, you are going to be more tired, but again, never take more than 60 seconds between rounds to rest. I like to use this challenge every few weeks to test my fitness. I also find this is an awesome way to do my cardio. Instead of running on the treadmill, after I've trained weights, I'll do this challenge instead.

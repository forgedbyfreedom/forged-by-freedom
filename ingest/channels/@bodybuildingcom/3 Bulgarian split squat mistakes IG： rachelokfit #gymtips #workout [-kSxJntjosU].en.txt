here are the top mistakes I see when doing Bulgarian split squats number one The Stance length being too far out from the bench a quick fix for this is sit down on the bench extend one leg fully out and when you stand up you can start in the same position every time number two the knee caving in at the bottom of the movement make sure you are externally rotating from the hip and protect those knees number three shifting all your weight to the back leg you should have a slight hinge 95% of your weight should be in that front leg let the front leg do all of the work

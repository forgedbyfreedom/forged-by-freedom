I'm Abel Abanetti and welcome to 30-day back. This is a four-week program with one goal. Making every muscle in your back bigger, stronger, and more defined. When your back is strong, your whole body benefits. If you've been lifting for a while and are wondering when that back will finally show up, this program is exactly what you need. You'll tackle three dedicated back workouts each week. You can work these three workouts into your normal split, or you can do what I recommend and work back on Monday. Tuesday would be chest and triceps. Wednesday would be legs. Thursday would be back again, but with biceps. And then Friday would be shoulders. And then Saturday would be back. And then Sunday would be an off day to fully recover. For this program, nothing in the gym is off limits. Dumbbells, barbells, and cables. You use them all with plenty of volume and drop sets. Every day is unique, and each workout comes with a video guide where I tell you exactly what to do to get the most out of every single rep.

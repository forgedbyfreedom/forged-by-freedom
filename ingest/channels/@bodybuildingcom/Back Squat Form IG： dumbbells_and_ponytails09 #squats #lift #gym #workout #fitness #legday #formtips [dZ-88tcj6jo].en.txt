are you struggling with your back squats and not sure if you have the correct form here are a few tips and tricks I go over with my clients I start by having them lay flat on their back to see where their foot positioning should be if your feet are facing up towards the ceiling you should have a more forward stance if they come out to the sides you will want a more angled stance think about your foot as a triangle so your points of contact should be the base of your big toe base of your small toe as well as your heel and you should distribute that weight equally over the arches of your feet make sure you have a slight Bend in your knees and then pull your belly button in towards your spine to activate your core last but certainly not least we go over the line of the bar path so you want to make a straight of a line as possible from a side angle view of the bar going down in your low squat back up to the top shifting your weight on your toes will cause a forward diagonal line shifting your weight towards your heels will cause a backward diagonal line try these tips out on your next squat day

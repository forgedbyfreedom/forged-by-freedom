if you're trying to train your back it's a lot less straightforward than just picking a certain movement to train your upper back or your lats for a long time people thought that it was either the movement the angle or the arm path and while that's true to a degree a lot more of it comes down to our torso position whether we are extended or whether we are contracted and braced if we're braced and we're driving our elbows down towards the floor that's going to be more lats if we are arched and we are driving our elbows back that's going to be more upper back a lot of how you position yourself in your back movements will dictate if you are biasing your lats or your upper back and that is going to help you determine what you need to do if you want to bias your upper back or if you want to bias your lats

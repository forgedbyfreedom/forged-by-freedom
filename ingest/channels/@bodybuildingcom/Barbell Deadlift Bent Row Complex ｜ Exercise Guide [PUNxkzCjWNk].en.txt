set up to the bar so that it is centered over your feet in a hip width stance Bend at the hips and grip the bar using a double overhand grip with your arms just outside of your knees and thighs this will be your starting position keeping your chest up and your back straight push through your heels to move the bar upward once the bar passes the knees aggressively pull it back into your body pulling your shoulder blades together as you drive your hips forward into the bar Lower the bar by bending at the hips and guiding it to the floor immediately transition into a barbell row position bending at the hips so that your chest is nearly parallel to the ground keeping your core tight and back straight perform a barbell row by pulling the bar upwards to your sternum lower the bar to the floor by extending your Arms This is one complete repetition repeat for the recommended number of repetitions

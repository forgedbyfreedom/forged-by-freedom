I have been meal prepping these breakfast sandwiches and they have been a lifesaver. For every sandwich I make, I use one egg and then I double it up with egg whites. Then pour it onto a half baking sheet or a smaller baking dish. Here I used a fulls size baking sheet and didn't quite fit the whole sheet. Then top off with a variety of veggies and your favorite seasonings. Then in a preheated oven, add your turkey, sausage, patties, eggs, and English muffins. Once everything is all cooked, cut the eggs according to how many sandwiches you have. stack with the sausage, cheese, and the English muffin.

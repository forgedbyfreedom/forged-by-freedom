place a medicine ball two to three feet in front of you with your feet about hip-width apart squat down and place your hands on the medicine ball while simultaneously jumping your feet back to a plank position this is your starting position quickly transition your feet out into a wide stance squat pick up the ball and press it forward away from your chest reverse the motion by bringing the ball back into your chest squatting down and jumping back into a plank position this is one rep repeat for the recommended number of reps without ever letting the medicine ball leave your hands

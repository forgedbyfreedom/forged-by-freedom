A new study just dropped and it suggests we might actually be able to target belly fat loss. Here's how the study went. Two groups did cardio while one only did steady state cardio and the other alternated back and forth between treadmill intervals followed by ab exercises like crunches and torso twists. The group that alternated back and forth lost a significant amount of belly fat while the cardio only group didn't. Here's the theory. Training your abs increases blood flow and starts draining glycogen in that area. Then the cardio ramps up fat mobilization. And since your core is already primed, your body pulls more fuel from nearby fat stores. You're basically making it easier to burn fat right where you trained. So, here's how to apply it. Instead of just doing steady state cardio, try rotating between cardio intervals and ab movements like two to three rounds of treadmill running plus crunches. Is this a shortcut to abs?

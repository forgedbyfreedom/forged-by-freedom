Do you need to cut out carbs to lose body fat or is a calorie deficit sufficient? We grew up in that Victoria's Secret America's Next Top Model, no bread, no carbs era. So, it makes complete sense that you think all carbs are going to make you fat. But is that actually true? While some studies do show that ketogenesis and reducing carbs can assist in fat loss, especially for those with insulin resistance and diabetes, a lot of research is showing us that both low carb and low-fat diets are effective in achieving fat loss. And just getting rid of carbs without being in a calorie deficit is not going to do anything but probably make you a little sad. You don't need to cut out carbs to lose weight, baby. A calorie deficit is going to be the most important thing for you to focus on when trying to lose body weight and body fat. Also, instead of thinking of carbs as just pizza or pasta, remember that carbs are also fruits and vegetables, baby girl. Those are the types of carbs you should focus on, especially because those are typically low calories. Please remember, not everyone is the same. So, what may work for me may not work for you.

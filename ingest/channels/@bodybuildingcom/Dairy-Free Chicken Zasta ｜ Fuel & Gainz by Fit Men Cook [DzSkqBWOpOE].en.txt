Add a few tablespoons of water to arrowroot starch, then stir it up and set it aside. Slice chicken breasts into cubes. Then spray a non-stick skillet with olive oil, then add in the chicken. We're going to keep it simple and season with a little sea salt and pepper. Cook the chicken for about six to eight minutes and ensure there are no visible pink pieces. Put the chicken in a bowl and set it aside. Spray a nonstick skillet with olive oil then add garlic, red onions, rosemary, and oregano. Cook until the onions are brown and be careful not to burn the garlic. Pour in coconut cream and bring it to a simmer. After about two minutes, add in the arrowroot starch and stir it up. As the sauce thickens add a few tablespoons of chicken broth, then toss in the zucchini pasta. Move the skillet around to coat the pasta, then add in the cooked chicken breast.

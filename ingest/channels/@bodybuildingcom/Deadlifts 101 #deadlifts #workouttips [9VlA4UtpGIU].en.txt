let's go over different types of deadlifts deadlifts are going to work more of your posterior chain so those backside muscles conventional deadlift you're going to have a closer stance and you are going to start from the ground and end from the ground Sumo deadlift is the same it starts at the ground ends from the ground but you're going to have a wider stance feet facing towards those outer walls working more of your inner thighs or those hip adductors Romanian deadlifts a little bit different you're going to start from the top position lower down towards your mid shins and then come back up to the standing position make sure have a slight Bend in your knees at all times if you don't know if you're doing your Romanian deadlift correctly you can do these drills to help you understand exactly what your hips and your leg movement should be like as you were lowering down into that mid Shin position follow along for more tips

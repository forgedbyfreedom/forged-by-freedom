there's a lot of confusion about deadlift techniques and what they actually train as well as how to execute them we're going to break that down a little bit today starting off we've got a Romanian deadlift and we have a stiff-legged deadlift these are different than the conventional because both of them reduce the amount of knee flexion that you're using a Romanian deadlift is going to buy us the glutes and a little bit less of the lower back and a little bit less of the hamstring that's going to require a little bit more knee Bend and focusing on hip flexion and extension a stiff-legged deadlift is going to have a slight knee Bend and it's going to focus primarily on keeping the bar far from your body biasing your lower back and biasing your hamstrings whenever you're programming your exercises you need to keep this in mind because they are training two very different muscle groups

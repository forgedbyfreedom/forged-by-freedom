♪♪♪ Stand up straight while holding an EZ curl bar at the wide outer handle. The palms of your hand should be facing forward and slightly tilted inward due to the shape of the bar. Keep your elbows close to your torso. This will be your starting position. Now, while keeping your upper arm stationary, curl the weights up while contracting your biceps. Focus on only moving your forearms. Continue to raise away until your biceps are fully contracted and the bar is at shoulder level. Hold the top-contracted position for a moment and squeeze the biceps. Then, inhale and slowly lower the bar back to the starting position before repeating.

I absolutely love when an easy recipe turns out to be delicious and that's exactly what these meatballs are so let's make them together in a large bowl add a quarter of a diced onion and then whatever kind of ground meat you want yes this recipe is so versatile you can use ground turkey ground beef or ground chicken from there add 1 egg 28 G of panco breadcrumbs 25 G of your favorite parmesan cheese a healthy tablespoon of minced garlic 1 and a half to 2 tspoons of werer sauce depending on how heavy your pore is a teaspoon of salt a teaspoon and a half of black pepper 2 teaspoons of both dried basil and dried oregano mix thoroughly and then once it looks like it's good to go form 12 meatballs get a lightly oiled Skillet over medium heat and get those balls down on the pan once they're browned and have a nice little sear to them flip them do that two to three more times then add your sauce now sauce isn't required but if you're going to put them on top of an apple pureed mashed potatoes I would highly recommend it the Saucy meatballs and the Apple Mash are both sweet and slightly acidic and play off each other very well

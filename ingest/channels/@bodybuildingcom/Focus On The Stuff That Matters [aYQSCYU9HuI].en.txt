Feeling like you had a good workout versus actually having a good workout are two very different things. At some point in time, I think all of us have felt that sweating, feeling exhausted, barely being able to move, those are the signs of a good workout. And usually it's fairly early on that we think those things and then later we learn a little bit more and we start to understand that that really doesn't contribute to getting stronger, building muscle or a productive workout. But if you are someone who is fixating on how you feel and you are training until you feel absolutely exhausted, this is counterproductive to progress. Typically, we want to see progressive overload occur. And this is really just a matter of seeing an incremental progression in the reps that you're doing or the weight that you're lifting. And that is your guaranteed form of understanding whether or not you had a good workout. So ditch the exhausting, over strenuous training sessions and start focusing on the stuff that actually matters.

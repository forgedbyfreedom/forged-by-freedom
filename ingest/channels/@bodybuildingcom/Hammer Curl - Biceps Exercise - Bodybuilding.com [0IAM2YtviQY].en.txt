Stand up with a dumbbell in each hand, held at arm's length. The elbows should be close to your torso with the palms of your hands facing your torso. This will be your starting position. Now, while holding your upper arms stationary, exhale and curl the weight forward while contracting the biceps. Continue to raise the weight until the biceps are fully contracted and the dumbbell is at shoulder level. Hold the contracted position for a brief moment as you squeeze the biceps. Focus on keeping the elbows stationary and only moving your forearm. After a brief pause, inhale and slowly begin to lower the dumbbells back to the starting position.

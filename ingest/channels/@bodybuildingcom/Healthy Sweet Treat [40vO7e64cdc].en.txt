If you're a fan of cookie butter and you're in need of a pre- or post-workout snack or even a sweet treat, I'd recommend you give these a try. Start by adding 5 cups of Rice Krispies and 1 cup of mini marshmallows into a bowl. Then, heat a pan on low to medium heat. Add 3 tablesp of butter. Allow to melt. Then add 1/3 cup of cookie butter, 1 tbsp of vanilla extract, and 5 cups of marshmallows. Allow marshmallows to fully melt. Then add two scoops of bodybuilding.com signature vanilla whey protein powder. Mix until well combined. Then add to the Rice Krispies. Mix to your best ability without spilling Rice Krispies everywhere. Then add to the pan and press down lightly until pan is evenly coated. Carefully cut into 15 pieces and enjoy.

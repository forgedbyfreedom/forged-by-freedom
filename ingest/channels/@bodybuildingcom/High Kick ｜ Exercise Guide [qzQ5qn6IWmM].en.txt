Stand erect with your feet hipwidth apart, chest out and shoulders back, maintaining the natural curvature in your back. Your knees should be unlocked and arms by your sides. This is your starting position. Keeping your leg straight, kick one leg directly out in front of you as high as you can while simultaneously extending the opposite side arm to touch it. Maintain good form while trying to kick your foot to shoulder height. Lower your leg so that it's well in front of the other one and step forward with the opposite side. Continue to move forward as you alternate sides for the required number of repetitions.

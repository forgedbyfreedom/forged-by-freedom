If I were to tell you that every single rep that we perform prior to the ones that matter, which are the ones that contribute to progressive overload, which are going to be the heaviest sets that we do, takes away from your ability to progress. How many reps would you spend warming up? You certainly would not waste as many as possible. You certainly would be doing 10 or 20 or 30 reps. You'd be doing the bare minimum. You want to feel comfortable with the load that you're using. You want to understand the muscle that you are trying to activate and you want to feel ready for the next jump. But you do not want to be going until you start feeling fatigue or a pump or anything like that because that is going to take away from the sets that actually truly do matter.

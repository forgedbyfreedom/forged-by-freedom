How much protein can you digest in one meal? For a long time, there have been all kinds of wild numbers thrown around. 20 g, 30 g, 50 g, 100 g. But the fact of the matter comes down to human biology. Thinking back to thousands of years ago, you don't know when your next meal is going to come around. So, you're certainly not going to say, "Oh, I can't eat that because I'm only going to be able to absorb 20 g of protein from that deer, elk, whatever it might be that you found." So the fact is there is not really a limit to how much we can digest in one sitting. It really comes down to our body being very very efficient and being able to assimilate almost everything that we put into it. The caveat is that there's a more optimal way where if we space our protein out more evenly, it can be beneficial because we increase muscle protein synthesis more consistently. But it is not by any means due to the fact that we cannot absorb any amount of protein over a certain amount in one meal.

Delayed onset muscle soreness can be the worst. We all know that foam rolling, stretching, and massage are great ways to alleviate muscle soreness. But here are a few other ways to lower the effects of DOMS after a tough workout. Hydrate, hydrate, hydrate. Dehydration leads to irritation of the damaged muscle tissue, which makes the effects of doms worse. Water helps reduce inflammation and flushes out waste products. And that's what I call high quality. What you eat matters. Make sure that you're getting enough protein in after your workout to repair and rebuild those muscles. But also eating anti-inflammatory foods, for example, tomatoes, strawberries, and blueberries. Spinach, walnuts, cherries, and olive oil will also help reduce the effects of DOMS. Hot and cold therapy both have their benefits. Heat therapy helps increase blood flow and relaxes the muscles, while cold therapy reduces inflammation and numbs pain receptors. An easy way to do this at home is alternating hot and cold in the shower every 2 minutes to speed up the healing process. Supplements such as BCAAs, EAAs, creatine, and omega-3s can also help with inflammation and protein synthesis to heal muscles faster. And we've all heard the saying, movement is medicine.

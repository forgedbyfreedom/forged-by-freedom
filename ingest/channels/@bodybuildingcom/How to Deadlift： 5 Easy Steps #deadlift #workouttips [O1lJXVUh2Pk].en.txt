if you're new to Conventional deadlifting or just want to learn how to perfect your setup watch this quick video first you're going to walk up to the bar place your shins one inch away from the bar that's going to place the bar over the middle of your foot which is where you always want to keep it bend over at your hips without bending your knees grip the bar right outside of your shins bring your shins to touch the bar squeeze your chest up as high as you can without dropping your hips see how the bar has not moved you do not want to let the bar roll at all drag the bar up your legs and to put the bar down we're going to do an RDL so hips back back back once it clears your knees bend your knees and then stand up if you're new to deadlifting I always suggest walking away from the bar and then walk back up to it for each rep I hope this helps happy lifting

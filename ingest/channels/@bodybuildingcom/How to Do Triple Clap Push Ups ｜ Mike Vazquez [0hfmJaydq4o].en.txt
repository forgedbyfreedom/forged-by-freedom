as an optional Advanced starting technique fall forward from standing into a push-up position keep your core tight and body straight as your hands make contact with the ground otherwise begin in the top position of a push-up arms extended back and neck in a straight line and core tight this will be your starting position descend and then explosively push upward elevating your upper body as high as you can off the floor as you rise up bring your hands together in front of you and clap immediately continue the motion so that you clap behind your torso at the apex of the movement and then once again in front of you before resetting your hands on the floor smoothly descend into the next rep and repeat if this progression is too difficult perform plyometric push-ups or clap push-ups instead

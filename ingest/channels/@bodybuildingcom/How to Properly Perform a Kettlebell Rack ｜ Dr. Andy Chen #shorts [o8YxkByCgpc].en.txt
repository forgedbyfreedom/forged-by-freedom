one of the biggest steps in learning kettlebell skills is to mask their rack position whenever we see beginners start with the rack position they keep their elbows very close to their side so they almost start to sag to the side what i want to see is that you keep the elbows off the body and you almost reach your shoulders forward you're going to automatically feel your foot pressed into the ground and you're also going to feel your abs so in that position don't keep it close keep it out in front of you and stay nice and tall

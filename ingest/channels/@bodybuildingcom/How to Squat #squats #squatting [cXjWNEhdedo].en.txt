here's five of the most common squat mistakes you need to avoid number one don't use a Loosey Goosey grip on the bar boost upper back tightness by gripping it as narrow as possible tucking your elbows and using your lats to pull the bar down number two don't just drop down into your squat stabilize Your Body by leveling your pelvis and screwing your feet into the ground and then taking a deep 360 breath and bracing your core number three to maximize growth don't do half squats go down to at least parallel number four don't let your hips shoot back and chest fall forward on the way up push the floor away and drive your traps up against the bar so your hips and chest rise at the same time and number five don't let your knees cave in or cave out too much to minimize knee stress try to keep them in the same direction as your toes

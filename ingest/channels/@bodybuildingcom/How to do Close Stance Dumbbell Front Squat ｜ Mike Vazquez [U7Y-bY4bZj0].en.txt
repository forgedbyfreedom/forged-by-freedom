position your legs in a narrow stance feet a few inches apart toes slightly pointed out and knees lightly bent hold a dumbbell with a hand on each end at chest level keeping your elbows tucked inwards to your sides keep your neck neutral and maintain a straight back this will be your starting position descend into a squat by pushing your hips and butt backwards continue down until the upper legs are at or just below parallel to the floor inhale as you perform this portion of the movement once you reach your bottom position begin to push through your heels to ascend upwards and return to the starting position repeat for the recommended number of repetitions

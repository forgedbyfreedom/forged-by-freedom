Begin in the top position of a push-up. Arms extended, back and neck in a straight line, and core tight. This will be your starting position. Bring one hand up to tap the opposite side shoulder. Place that hand back on the floor and repeat for the opposite side. That is one repetition. Your body will slightly shift from one side to the other to maintain balance, but limit weight shift as much as possible. Perform for the recommended number of repetitions.

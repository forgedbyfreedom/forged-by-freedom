Begin seated on your butt with your knees bent and feet on the ground. Grab a dumbbell with both hands and bring it behind your head. Your elbows should be bent to 90°, keeping your arms in close to your head and your elbows pointed forward. Lift your heels off the ground so that your body makes a Vshape. This will be your starting position. Keeping your core tight and abs contracted, bring the dumbbell up by extending the arms. Return the weight to the starting position and repeat for the recommended number of repetitions.

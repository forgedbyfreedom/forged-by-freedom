flat pull down Master Class there are three variations of this exercise wide medium and narrow grip and each one targets different portion of your back muscles wide grip targets the outer portion of the lats because your arms are moved away from the midline of the body in this position the angle of pull on the lats changes emphasizing the upper and outermost fibers of the muscle with medium grip your arms are at an intermediate angle relative to your torso which engag es both the horizontal and vertical fibers of the lats since your arms aren't too far apart or too close together narrow grip recruits the mid to lower sections more dominantly it offers a slightly longer range of motion you will have deeper stretch at the top of the movement and a fuller contraction at the bottom remember that the differences between grip widths are subtle and more about optimizing individual preferences and comfort rather than making dramatic changes in muscle development

barble exercises might not be the best option when it comes to hypertrophy but they're not the worst there are things that we can do especially with something like an incline bench to make it more hypertrophy friendly instead of the traditional form where you bench with your elbows flared like this is going to put a lot of stress on your shoulder because you're flaring out and not really moving in the orientation of the fibers of the PEC what we can do is take a narrower grip just about shoulder width are slightly wider and then we keep the elbows tucked just like so and then contract up and that plane of motion is going to mimic a lot more of how the PEC actually functions and take a lot of stress off our shoulders so we can get more benefits in terms of hypertrophy from the incline bench

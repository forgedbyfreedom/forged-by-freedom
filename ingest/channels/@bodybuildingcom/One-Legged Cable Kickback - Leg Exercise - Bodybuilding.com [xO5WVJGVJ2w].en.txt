Hook an ankle cuff to a low pulley and attach the cuff to your ankle. Face the weight stack and stand a couple feet away. You'll want to grasp the steel frame of the machine for support. This will be your starting position. Now, while keeping your knees and hips bent slightly, exhale and contract your glutes to slowly move your working leg back in a semic-ircular arc. Raise it back as high as you can comfortably at full extension. Squeeze your glutes for a brief moment to maximize the contraction. Then slowly return your working leg to the starting position.

here's how to do bar BL row with perfect form step one set the bar on a rack at around knee height grab the bar with a wide overhand grip lift and take a few steps back step two set your feet hip width apart then stabilize Your Body by leveling your pelvis and engageing your core as if someone were about to punch your stomach step three push your hips back and Slide the bar long your thighs until it passes your knees step four stay tight and pull back with your elbows to lift the bar up towards your stomach let your elbows flare out to about a 45° angle and squeeze your shoulder blades together to engage your back muscles step five control the bar down and fully stretch your back muscles by letting your shoulder blades open up keep going until your arms are fully extended then repeat for another rep congrats you've just done the perfect barbell row

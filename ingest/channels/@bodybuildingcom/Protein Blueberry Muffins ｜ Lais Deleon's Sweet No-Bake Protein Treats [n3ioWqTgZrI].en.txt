To get started, in a large mixing bowl you're going to add your liquid egg whites almond extract (or if you prefer, you can use vanilla extract) and your fat-free blueberry Greek yogurt. Then we're going to add our baking powder, salt, and coconut flour. Then we're going to add honey our NLA for Her blueberry muffin protein powder Mix everything together until it's an even batter consistency. and last, add fresh blueberries. Next, quickly coat the inside of your silicone mold with coconut oil spray. Now, fill your cupcake mold with the batter. Since this is no-bake recipe, we're going to take this over to the microwave. We're going to cook these in microwave in 30-second intervals. We're going to continue this process until batter is no longer gooey. We're doing this because everybody's microwave cooks at different temperatures. Once your muffins are cooked, pull them out and let them cool. Then, enjoy.

all right you guys I love a good Romanian deadlift but a lot of us learned some less than helpful cues when we first were taught how to do this myself included the first being keeping your weight on your heels I actually don't love this Quee I think it's way more helpful to think about keeping your weight over the middle of your foot and thinking nose over toes letting your shoulders come forward as your hips come back secondly it is not necessary to keep your spine completely neutral from tailbone to the top of your neck it's okay to look somewhat in front of you slightly up because this will help you keep your upper back somewhat arched which is actually what we want during the Romanian deadlift

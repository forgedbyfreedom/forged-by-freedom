pick a couple of dumbbells and stand with a straight torso and the dumbbells by your side at arms length with the palms of the hand facing you this will be your starting position while maintaining the Torso in a stationary position no swinging lift the dumbbells to your side with a slight bend on the elbow and the hands slightly tilted forward as if pouring water in a glass continue to go up until your arms are parallel to the floor exhale as you execute this movement and pause for a second at the top lower the dumbbells back down slowly to the starting position as you inhale repeat for the recommended amount of repetitions

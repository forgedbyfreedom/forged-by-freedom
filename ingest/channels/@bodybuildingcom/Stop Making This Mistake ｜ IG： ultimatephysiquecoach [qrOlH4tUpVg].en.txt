Weight does not indicate muscle growth. I think it's the opposite. When you have the muscle developed that will allow you to lift heavier loads and then in subsequent months, years later, bigger you get, the stronger you get and it becomes this like perpetual cycle, right? It's not about how much you lift, it's how well you lift. The objective is not to just lift weight from point A to point B. The objective is to stress the muscles under load. And the more load you can do that with and the better technique you can use, the more muscle you will build over time.

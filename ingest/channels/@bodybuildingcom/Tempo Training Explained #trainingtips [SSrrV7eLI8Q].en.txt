okay guys today we're talking about Tempo Tempo refers to how long we are lowering or raising the weight that we are using how long we are pausing and essentially the Cadence at which we're actually lifting the weight this is important because we need to have a consistent variable for which we are executing our reps if our Tempo is constantly changing it's very very hard to measure our progression if you go from let's say doing a bench press of 135 lb for 8 reps one week to doing 150 lb for 10 reps one week that's a very good progression and strength but if you change your Tempo then we don't actually know if you gain strength so it's very very important that we keep that consistent Tempo so we have something to measure our progress against knowing that it is a static variable it's also important because we do create an internal tension on the muscle and this contributes to muscle growth so that is why Tempo is important

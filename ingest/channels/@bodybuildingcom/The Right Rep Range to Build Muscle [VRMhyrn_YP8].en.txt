there are no magic rep ranges for muscle growth whether it is five reps or 50 reps if we are reaching the point of failure the end result is roughly the same but we see a disproportionate amount of fatigue created with higher rep ranges as opposed to lower rep ranges what we are shooting for is a high degree of motor unit recruitment which happens regardless of the rep range as long as we are getting close to or reaching failure so save yourself some time save yourself some fatigue and keep your rep ranges lower

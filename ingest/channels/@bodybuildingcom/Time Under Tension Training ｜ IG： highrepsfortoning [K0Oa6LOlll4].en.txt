time under tension is an outdated focal point of training what you want to be aiming for is time under mechanical tension and this is going to be found while using heavy loads taking yourself somewhat close to failure if not all the way to failure and seeing a voluntary slowing of contraction speed which essentially means your reps will begin slowing down regardless of how much effort you're putting in this is typically going to be the last five reps of a set taken a failure I suggest making sure you keep your loads heavy and you keep your rep rate ranges on the low to moderate side and your progress will take off

Attach a straight or angled bar to a high pulley and grab the bar with an overhand grip at shoulder width. You'll want to be standing upright with a very small forward lean. Your upper arm should be close to your body and perpendicular to the floor. Your forearms should be pointed towards the pulley as they hold the bar. This is your starting position. Now, using your triceps, exhale and bring the bar down until it touches the front of your thighs and your arms are fully extended. always remain stationary while your forearms move. After holding for a brief pause in the contracted position, inhale and slowly bring the bar back down to the starting position.

why your back hurts while doing deadlifts part one the bar is too far out in front of the body if the bar is stacked under the shoulders when you go to lift the bar the pull of gravity will be directly under the upper back causing your back to fatigue and round as you perform the movement instead start with the bar directly over your mid foot use your lats to depress the scapula and keep the bar as close to the body as you can throughout the movement think about trying to bend the bar around your body the bar should gently slide up and down the front of you as you trace your legs through the movement be sure to like follow and stay tuned for part two

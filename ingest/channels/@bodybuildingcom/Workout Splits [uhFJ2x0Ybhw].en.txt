As we become more advanced, we require more specificity and more direct focus on individual body parts because the stimulus response that we get from the same training is not the same as somebody who is a newer lifter. To them, it is a very new stimulus to their body. So they can use a much lower degree of effort and they'll still see a very very significant response. But for somebody who's advanced, who has been in the gym training for a long time, who has seen a lot of muscle growth and built a lot of strength, it requires a much higher degree of effort and focus to elicit the same response.

I strongly believe in OpenCourseWare’s mission to make education available globally to everyone of all backgrounds. I feel like I can pinpoint to many points in my life, different people that I met that helped me along the way. Or different resources that helped me along the way. OpenCourseWare is certainly one of those things. I mean it’s hard to say where I would be without it. It was definitely a crucial ingredient that I needed in order to find myself here. My story is just one tiny story among the millions of people that have been able to access OCW. Being a beneficiary of such resources, and free of charge, it compels me to do my part to give back what I have freely received. And I’m sure there are a lot of other people that feel the same way.

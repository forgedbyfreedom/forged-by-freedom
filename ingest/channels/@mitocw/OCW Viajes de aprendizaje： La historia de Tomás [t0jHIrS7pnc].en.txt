My name is Tomás, and I'm a high school student in Chile. My story with MIT CW began when my classmates and I were developing a school innovation project: a phytopharmaceutical first-aid kit. This kit was going to be implemented in school nursing units, allowing us to address students' health problems. The problem we encountered while developing this research was that we lacked the necessary pharmacology knowledge. That's when the MIT CW course on Principles of Pharmacology came along. This allowed me to gain a foundation in pharmacology, and, I believe even more importantly, to be able to share this knowledge with my classmates. I think what I value most about MIT CW is the wide variety of content available; for me, it's the ultimate expression of the democratization of knowledge.

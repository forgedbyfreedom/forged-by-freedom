you know about the impurities to decide on appropriate aquous washes for your mixture keep in mind that the washes should only be 10 to 50% of the volume of the solution that you are washing and make sure to repeat each wash two or three times to wash away as much of the impurities as possible your crude mixture contains acetic acid a fairly strong acid and two weak bases triethylamine and dmap to get rid of the acetic acid you need to wash the solution with a mild base that will deprotonate the acid and pull the charged acetate ion into the aquous layer saturated sodium bicarbonate works very

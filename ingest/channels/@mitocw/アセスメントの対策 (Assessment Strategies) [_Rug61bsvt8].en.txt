In our Japanese language curriculum, we have a system called the Daily Grade. Before coming to class, students prepare by practicing online drills and other exercises. Based on their performance the next day, students who are perfectly prepared will receive a grade of 7, while those who come unprepared will receive a grade of 6 or 7. This varies depending on the instructor. We believe that this Daily Grade system helps motivate students. We also have an interview test, which we conduct twice per semester. The interview lasts about 5 to 7 minutes, and varies depending on the grade level. The teacher asks questions that include patterns and vocabulary that the student has studied so far, and the student answers them. The interview test is usually 5 to 7 minutes long, but if there are, for example, 50 students, it may take another day. It usually takes one day, but depending on the grade level, it may take two days. Ideally, it would be best to give each student a personalized feedback bag, but that would take another two or three days, so as feedback, I give students points, for example, 10 points for the best student. I only give those points to myself. Of course, if a student makes a particularly big or distinctive pronunciation mistake, I will speak to that student personally.

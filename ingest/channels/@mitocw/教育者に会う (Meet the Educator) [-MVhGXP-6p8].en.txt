8. My name is Takako Aikawa. Currently, I am working at a tea station in this city as the leader of the Japanese language program at MIT. I am working on the future direction of the Japanese language program and improving the current language curriculum. The MIT Japanese language program has three levels: 1st, 2nd, and 3rd year. In each level 2, we are trying to cover all four language positions, from speaking to speaking and reading, but what we are particularly focusing on is improving communicative skills. My first involvement in language education was in graduate school at Ohio State University. It was there that I first became a teaching assistant. It was my first time working in language education, and since I originally studied linguistics, I didn't know anything about language education. However, by being a TA, I experienced for the first time the wonderfulness and fun of Japanese language education.

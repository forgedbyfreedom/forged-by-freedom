With the aim of giving students more opportunities to speak Japanese, we run a Japanese lunch table. The MIT Japan program has the function of sending students who are taking Japanese to Japan for an internship in the summer, and we hold this lunch table once a week in conjunction with the MIT Japan program. Students come to the lunch table and speak with native Japanese people other than teachers. This is not only from MIT Japan, but also from an i-group called the Prince Wipes Club, and we receive a lot of support from the members of that i-group. In order to increase the variety of MIT's Japanese language program or the cultural courses related to Japanese language, we usually hold an event called Japanet MIT at the beginning of each term. Japanet MIT is like a party, and at the event we give an overview of the Japanese language program and the courses related to Japanese culture that MIT offers, such as the anime course. Then there are Pocker's course and various other courses, but I'll introduce those as well. Also, the Vibes Club members often help out, and I'll introduce T Cell's brother, and how he changed into a kimono and other coats.

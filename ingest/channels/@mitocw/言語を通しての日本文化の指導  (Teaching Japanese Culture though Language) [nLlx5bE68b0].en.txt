I believe that language must be taught in conjunction with culture. For example, when introducing oneself or greeting someone, Japanese people always bow. When I teach things like body language, wedge, pressure, and sponge, I always introduce the cultural background related to them. For example, when teaching honorifics, I introduce how important relationships of respect and devotion are in Japanese society. Also, in the textbook, there is a "tea notebook" for each lesson, which is divided into various topics such as the Japanese family system and education system, so I also introduce these kinds of cultural aspects in my Japanese classes.

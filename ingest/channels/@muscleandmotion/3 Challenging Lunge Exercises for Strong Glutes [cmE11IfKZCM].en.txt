this exercise combines two exercises that focus on strengthening the gluteus maximus the first part is the good morning or hip hinge the movement is made through the hip joint the gluteus maximus is the agonist and the hamstrings help it in the hip flexion the second part is the rear lunge which strengthens the gluteus maximus together with the quadriceps femoris muscle performing the exercise on one leg increases the load significantly on many stabilizers among them the gluteus medius and the other stabilizing muscles in the pelvis in addition working on one leg challenges the core muscles and the balance system the target muscles are the quadriceps femoris and gluteus maximus colored red this variation of the lunge challenges the equilibrium system and creates intensified work for the core muscles which must work in coordination and stabilize the body on a small base of support view of the adductors that stabilize the active leg and are dynamically active in the rear leg placing the rear leg under the active muscle tilts the pelvis laterally and makes the gluteus medius and minimus work harder target muscle quadriceps femoris and gluteus maximus the action of the hip joint is extension with lateral rotation in this variation of the lunge we emphasize the gluteus maximus and also the gluteus medius as lateral rotators of the hip joint a focused look at the hip external rotators this is a closed kinetic chain where the leg is fixed on the floor therefore contraction of the external rotators creates pelvic tilt

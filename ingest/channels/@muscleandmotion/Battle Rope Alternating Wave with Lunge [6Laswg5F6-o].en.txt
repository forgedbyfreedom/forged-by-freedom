battle rope alternating wave with lunge the battle rope alternating wave with lunge is an effective full-body exercise it challenges almost all the muscles in the body the leg muscles the shoulder girdle and the core muscles that stabilize the body the gluteus maximus extends the hip the quadriceps femoris extends the knee the anterior deltoid elevates the arm shoulder flexion and the elbow flexors flex the elbow among them the brachioradialis is considered the main muscle in this mid position in this posterior view we can see all the target muscles in red all the synergistic auxilary muscles in pink and the main stabilizing muscles in white brown you comparison of different techniques in variation a the exerciser raises the rope high and lowers it fast in other words the shoulder extensor muscles among them the latissimus dorsi create explosive power to draw the rope down faster than the force of gravity in variation B the exerciser exerts himself during rope elevation but releases the hand and lets the rope fall naturally from gravitational pole in addition note that the elbow joint also participates actively in the movement in relation to the variation on the left in which the elbow is almost locked in place the decision of which variation to choose is up to you and your objectives you can include both variations during training and play around with training tempo

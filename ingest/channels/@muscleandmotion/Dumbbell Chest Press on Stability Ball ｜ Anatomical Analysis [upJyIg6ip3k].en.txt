Chest press stability ball. This exercise has two main aims. Active strengthening of the chest muscles and passive strengthening of the core muscles and buttocks. Sit on a stability ball. Place the dumbbells on the thighs. Enter a spine position with the upper back and head on the ball, butttocks in the air. Push the dumbbells upward and then lower them to the sides of the chest. This video shows some variations. The aim of these exercises is to challenge the core muscles and strengthen the chest muscles. Note, because of the unstable base, we do not recommend working with heavy weights. It is preferable to work with heavy weights on a stable surface such as a bench. The core muscles in the glutius maximum are statically active while holding the pelvis in the air. The pectoralis major horizontally adducts the humorris. The anterior deltoid assists in shoulder horizontal adduction. The triceps brachi extends the elbow.

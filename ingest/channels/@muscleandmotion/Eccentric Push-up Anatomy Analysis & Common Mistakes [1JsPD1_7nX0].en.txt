Descent phase to enhance strength start by getting into a plank position with your hands placed slightly wider than shoulder width apart fingers pointing forward lower your body towards the ground as slowly as possible to maximize The Eccentric contraction push back up to the start starting position and repeat for the desired repetitions many muscles take part in The Eccentric push-up the pectoralis major and anterior deltoid contract eccentrically and also control and restrain shoulder horizontal Abduction the triceps brachii contract restrain elbow flexion the seratus anterior contract eccentrically and also control and restrain scapular retraction the abdominal muscles stabilize the trunk the quadriceps femoris stabilize the hip and pelvis and prevent it from flexing

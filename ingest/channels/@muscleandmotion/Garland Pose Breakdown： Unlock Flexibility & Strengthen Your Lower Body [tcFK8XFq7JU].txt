The garland pose requires a large range of motion at the ankle joint. Preparatory exercises for this position. 1. Emphasis on softening and moving the ankles, stretching the plantar flexor muscles, gastrocnemius, etc. It is recommended to include a demonstration of the exercise for stretching the gastrocnemius. 2.

Romanian deadlift versus hip thrust which is best for your glutes starting with the Romanian deadlift or RDL this movement stretches the glutes as you bend forward reaching maximum contraction when they're in a lengthened position however standing upright in the shortened position puts less load on the glutes in contrast the hip thrust activates the glutes most intensely at the top of the movement reaching maximum activation in a shortened position in the lengthened position although the external load is still high the glutes are less active due to the involvement of other supporting muscles and passive assistance so which one should you choose the answer is both by combining rdls and hip thrusts you can ensure full range glute development optimizing strength and muscle growth for the best possible results

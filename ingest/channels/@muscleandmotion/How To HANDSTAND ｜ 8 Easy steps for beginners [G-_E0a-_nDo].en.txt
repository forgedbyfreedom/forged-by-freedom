handstands are based on a high level of control of movement and require a good physical foundation both in terms of range of motion and of strength exercise stages stage after stage leading to the handstand 1. stand with back to the wall about 10 centimeters from the wall hold in the abdominal muscles and rest the lower back against the wall raise arms from the sides touching the wall until they are straight up and parallel to each other emphasis is on practicing motor control of the pelvic position and of posterior pelvic tilt to reduce lumbar lordosis two the same exercise while lying on the back arms rise from the sides of the body until they touch the ears the neck is elongated on the floor and the lower back is flattened against the floor in this exercise as well it is important to ensure full motor control of pelvic position three lying face down contract the buttocks and abdominal muscles and tilt the pelvis to the rear hold in the abdominals tightly stretch and elongate the arms forward and the legs to the rear maintaining flattened lumbar lordosis 4. walking the wall gradually and with control climb the wall with the buttocks leading the movement up the wall in order to bring the hands and torso closer emphasis holding in the abdominal muscles as support for the lower back five hold a static position on the wall the legs are on the wall bring the hands to about half a meter from the wall hold the position statically while holding in the abdominal muscles 6. 90 degree position a measure the distance for performing the position sit with legs straight and back against the wall the exercise distance will be the point reached by the heels b place the hands down and climb the wall with the legs slowly lower the legs until the feet are at waist level at an angle of 90 degrees to the trunk and arms emphasis the shoulder is in a straight line above the hands in order to strengthen and stabilize the scapula as an essential basis for stability in the handstand position adaptation for tight hamstrings slightly bend the knees seven rotate and tighten arms inwardly medially press the fingertips against the floor keep the other leg active by pulling the heel downward attempt a number of jumps when both legs leave the ground pull in the abdominal muscles and lift the hip joints above the shoulders practice the jump several times while exhaling and gradually increase the strength of the push lifting the legs a bit higher each time practice this stage until you can lead the heels to the wall with control and without excess effort eight rise to a handstand on the wall you have reached full handstand position the feet are in plantar flexion point gradually try to leave the wall and feel your balance in the position attain balance by using the

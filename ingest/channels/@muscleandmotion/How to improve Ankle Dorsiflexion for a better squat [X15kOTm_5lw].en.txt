ankle mobility exercises one of the common problems with squatting performance is a restricted ankle dorsiflexion as a result of this restriction the body falls backwards to prevent the fall the trainee leans forward a possible reason for restricted ankle dorsiflexion is tight joint capsule and/or scar tissue in the ligaments usually from prior injuries tight joint leads to loss of mobility a common solution for this restriction is ankle mobility exercises stand facing a wall lunge forward to see if you are able to get your front need to touch the wall without lifting the heel off the ground the knee moves forward through the ankle joint dorsiflexion a variation for this exercise is self mobilization of the ankle joint using a band place the band across the very bottom of the ankle and over the talus as a tibia and fibula move forward over the talus the tubing pulls the talus back posterior towel or glide and we should block or pull back the talus not the tibia or fibula you variation the tubing pulls the tibia and fibula into anterior glide the trainer blocks the talus removing anteriorly the result is posterior taller glide and anterior tibia Glide

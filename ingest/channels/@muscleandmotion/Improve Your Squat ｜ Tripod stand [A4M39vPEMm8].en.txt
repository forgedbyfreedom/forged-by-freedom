the foot tripod concept is a useful way of thinking about how to evenly distribute weight on the foot the tripod refers to three points of contact that the bottom of the foot makes with the ground the three points are one center of the calcaneus heel to head of the fifth metatarsal little toe 3 a head of the first metatarsal big toe done correctly there should be pressure on all three aspects of the tripod foot tighten the arch by pressing the toes down and back without shifting the weight out of the heel a stable base will balance the load on all the joints and bones the principle is to statically activate the foot muscles in order to maintain the arch and at the same time to activate the hip rotators by rotating the hip outward it is necessary to anchor the knee and hip as well therefore at the same time we'll drive the knee outward hip external rotation rooting is about generating tension through the feet that transfers through the whole body and locks you into the ground

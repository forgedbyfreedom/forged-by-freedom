pars fatan Sana intense side stretch pose this Advanced pose is challenging mainly because it includes components requiring High motor control for organizing the body limbs in space there are a number of possible hand positions and exercisers can choose their favorite variations for their personal training performance we begin in a stance with our feet parallel to DOA then we take a large step forward with the left leg we rotate the right rear foot to an angle of 45° the hands are in a reverse prayer pose Palm to Palm behind the back we bend the upper body forward so that it faces the left leg bend the spine forward

landmine squat jumps this is a great exercise to gain power and explosiveness stand upright with feet shoulder-width apart descend to a partial or full squat position with the torso leaning forward in the arm straight do not bend your arms keep them straight initiate the jump with a combination of a forward and upward trajectory of ten to fifteen degrees now both feet must leave the ground at the same time so that a full extension of the ankle knees and hips also called triple extension must be activated in order to use all potential energy from these joints on landing absorb the forces through the feet ankles knees hips and back in a progressive manner the target muscles are the quadriceps femoris gluteus maximus and gastrocnemius colored red the quadriceps femoris muscle extends the knee the gluteus maximus extends the thigh at the hip joint the hamstrings assist in hip extension the gastrocnemius and soleus plantar flex the foot concentric jump vs counter movement jump on the left we see an example of a concentric only jump squat jump where the athlete gets into a half squat position or half deadlift position then pauses and performs only the pushing up phase of the jump it's an explosive movement but not considered as a plyometric movement on the right we see a counter movement jump in the counter movement jump the jumper starts from an upright standing position makes a preliminary downward movement by flexing of the knees and hips then immediately extends the knees and hips again to jump vertically up off the ground such a movement makes use of the stretch shortened cycle where the muscles are pre stretched before shortening in the desired direction the counter movement jump is a simple but effective plyometric exercise and has been shown to be a vital lexer sighs in the development of power and agility

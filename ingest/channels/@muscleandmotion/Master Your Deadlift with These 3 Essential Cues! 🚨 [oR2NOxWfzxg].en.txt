struggling with your deadlift form use these three simple cues for fast Improvement one push the floor away instead of focusing on your back or legs think about pushing the ground down with your feet as you lift the bar two close the car door with your hips picture holding grocery bags and closing the door with your hips this queue keeps your hip hinge strong and prevents turning the deadlift into a squat three keep the bar close imagine dragging the bar along your shins this keeps your body in the correct position and prevents the bar from drifting forward

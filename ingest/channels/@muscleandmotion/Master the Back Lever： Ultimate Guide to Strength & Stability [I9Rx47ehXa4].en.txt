back lever the back lever is a challenging exercise it requires great strength in the shoulder flexors the elbow flexors and in the muscles facing up towards the sky for stabilizing the body the aim is to attain a position in which the entire body is in a straight line parallel to the floor the entire body moves through movement in the shoulder joint the pectoralis major and anterior deltoid perform shoulder flexion and prevent the body from collapsing the entire upward-facing muscle chain stabilizes the body the erector spiny extend the spine in Reverse action and the glutitis Maximus together with the hamstrings extend the hip the quadratus lumborum straightens the lumbar spine to hold the body it is recommended to bring the scapula into to protraction in other words pull them downward toward floor the serratus anterior abducts the scapula and the pectoralis minor tilts the scapula anteriorly the latissimus dorsi assists in spinal extension in Reverse action and may also assist in shoulder flexion in this position of hyperextension variation getting into a back lever without flexing your knees and hip this Advanced back lever variation requires High motor control and muscle strength

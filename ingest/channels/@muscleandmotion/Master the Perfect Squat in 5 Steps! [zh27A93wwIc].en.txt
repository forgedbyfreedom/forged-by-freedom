this is how you correctly squat in five simple Steps step one set the barbell at chest height pull yourself under with the shoulder width stance pinch shoulder blades together in elbows to your side to create a muscular shelf for the barbell on top of your traps for high bar technique or slightly lower across your poster deltoids for low bar style step two open your hips and pull your pelvis under before standing up take three controlled steps back step three angle your feet out only slightly as excessive turnout can lead to unstable feet and knees align ribs and pelvis to create a strong neutral spine posture step four brace your core in a way that engages your abs laterally root your entire foot to the ground screw your legs open to engage your hips step five start your squat with a small hinge before dropping straight down between your legs keeping your body weight even spread across your entire foot and Bart tracking over your midfoot then focus on breathing by imagining you're squatting in chest high water take a big breath and hold it until you get back above water

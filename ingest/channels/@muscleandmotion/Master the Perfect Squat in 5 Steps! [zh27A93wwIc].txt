 This is how you correctly squat in 5 simple steps. Step 1, set the barbell at chest height, pull yourself under with a shoulder width stance. Pinch shoulder blades together and elbows to your side to create a muscular shelf for the barbell, on top of your traps for high bar technique, or slightly lower across your posterior deltoids for low bar style. Step 2, open your hips and pull your pelvis under, before standing up. Take 3 controlled steps back. Step 3, angle your feet out only slightly, as excessive turnout can lead to unstable feet and knees. Step 4, angle your hips and pull your hips under, and lower your hips under, and lower your hips. Align ribs and pelvis to create a strong neutral spine posture. Step 4, brace your core in a way that engages your abs laterally. Step 5, start your squat with a small hinge, before dropping straight down between your legs, keeping your body weight evenly spread across your entire foot, and bar tracking over your mid foot.

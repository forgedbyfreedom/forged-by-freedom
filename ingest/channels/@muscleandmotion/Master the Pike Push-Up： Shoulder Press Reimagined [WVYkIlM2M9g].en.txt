this exercise is called Pike push-up but it more closely resembles the shoulder press exercise and is designed to strengthen the deltoid muscles start with a plank and lift the pelvis up as high as possible toes touching the floor and most of the weight on hands bend your elbows aiming to bring your head down and forward to the floor and push back up the target muscle is the deltoid that flexes the shoulder joint with the assistance of the pectoralis major upper for fibers the triceps brachi ey extends the elbow the catus anterior along with the upper and lower trapezius rotate the scapula upward the hamstrings are stretched and the core muscles stabilize the spine narrow grip versus wide grip in the narrow grip the shoulder movement is flexion thus the anterior deltoid is dominant in the wide grip the shoulder movement is abduction thus the medial deltoid is dominant in this video you can see that performing the exercise in a wide grip is the same as a shoulder press exercise the main difference is the range of motion in the shoulder when working with weights you can work in a full range of motion as opposed to a partial range of motion when performing the exercise with body weight because the head touches the floor to overcome this problem the exercise can can be performed with the hands on two stools to allow the head to descend lower this is a variation with a band to relieve the load especially at the end of the movement when the head is close to the floor fix the band to a stable device higher than pelvis height and the other side on the pelvis the lower you get the more the band will stretch and facilitate the exercise

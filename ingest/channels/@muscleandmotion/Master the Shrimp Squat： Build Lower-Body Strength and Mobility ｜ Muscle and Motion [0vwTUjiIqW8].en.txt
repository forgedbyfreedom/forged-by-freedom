shrimp squat the shrimp squat is a single leg squat variation that leverages your body weight to build lower body strength it is one of the best leg exercises for building leg strength and lower body mobility and can be used as a preparatory exercise for learning the pistol squat Begin by standing straight with your feet hip width apart lift one leg and bend the knee diagonally towards the back and grab the foot with your opposite hand slowly lower your body until the flexed knee touches the ground while maintaining balance push up through the heel to return to the starting position repeat on the other leg the main active muscles are the same ones as in the classic squat exercise the quadriceps extend straighten the knee the gluteus maximus extends the hip and the gastric nemus performs plantar flexion in the ankle the hamstrings and the do Magnus and longus assist in hip extension the glutitis medius and Minimus stabilize the pelvis shrimp squat variation foot to floor this is a

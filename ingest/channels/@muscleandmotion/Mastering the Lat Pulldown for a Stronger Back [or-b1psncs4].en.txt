lat pull down machine the lat pull down is one of the most popular exercises used to strengthen the muscles of the back most notably the latissimus dorsy adjust the pads so your knees are placed securely underneath grasp the bar with your hands shoulder width or slightly wider than shoulder width apart keep the spine in a neutral position and engage your core exhale and pull the bar toward your upper chest inhale ale and slowly return to the starting position the target muscle is the latissimus dorsy red which inducts the humoris toward the body shoulder reduction the posterior deltoid and the Terrace major assist in shoulder reduction the scapular reductors assist in retraction and downward rotation of the scapula the elbow flexors pink assist in elbow flexion common mistake the cable is pulled impulsively while leaning back too far and when returning the pelvis lifts off the chair without control this is usually to compensate for using too much weight or lack of awareness to prevent this fix the thighs in place under the leg supports and perform slow controlled movements recruiting the core muscles throughout the movement that

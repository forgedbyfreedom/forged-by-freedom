zercher squat the zercher squat is named after the weightlifter Ed zercher in this squat variation we place the barbell in the elbows crease placing the barbell in front of the body creates a relatively large rotational movement and pulls the body forward this allows the trainee to lean back slightly and keep the body almost completely vertical to the floor the disadvantage of this exercise is the discomfort that can be created in the elbow crease when working with heavy weights the bar can be padded with a towel or sponge or the trainee can wear a long sleeved shirt the active joints are the knee hip and Ankle joints the quadriceps femoris extends the knees the gluteus maximus extends the hip with the hamstrings assistance the gastric nemus and Solus plantar flexes the ankle joint the erector spine a along with the core muscles stabilize the Torso pulling the barbell for forward allows the trainee to lean forward slightly and to lower the body with relatively little dorsa flexion in the ankle this is not possible in the back squat which requires a greater range of movement in the ankle there are two usual ways to place the dumbbell on the crook of the elbows one begin with the barbell on the floor deadlift the bar to your thigh height and rest it on your thighs scoop your arms underneath the bar and you can rested on your elbow crease note this variation isn't suitable for heavy weight at the end of the exercise place the bar on the thighs release the arms hold the bar and return it to the floor while keeping your back straight two begin the exercise with the bar placed on a power rack A variation with a kettle bell hanging in the center of a body bar the body bar is padded and relatively thick which might be a better option into place in the elbow crease

reverse fly machine the machine reverse fly is an exercise intended to strengthen your posterior deltoid sit on a reverse flying machine and hold the handles with your hands your arms should be parallel to the ground and your elbows should be slightly flexed horizontally abduct your shoulders without flexing or extending your elbows go back to the starting position and repeat the posterior deltoid horizontally abducts the shoulder with the assistance of the infraspinatus the trapezius and the rhomboids assist by retracting the scapula the triceps brachi ey stabilize the elbow joint common mistake moving your elbows when performing the reverse flies this exercise is intended to strengthen your posterior deltoid by horizontally abducting your shoulder doing this exercise with a flexed elbow will decrease the torque arm on the posterior deltoid to focus the load on the posterior deltoid decrease the resistance then do the exercise again and see if you can do it with your elbows straight common mistake rotating your spine rotating your spine during the exercise will help create momentum to pull the weight and will decrease the load on the posterior deltoid which will not serve the purpose of the exercise

this is the gluteus maximus the largest muscle in our body and you know what they say with great power comes great responsibility it starts at the sacrum and ilium and attaches to the femur and the ITB which means it also affects the knee the main function of the gluteous Maximus is hip extension in simple words it pushes your leg back or if your foot is on the ground it straighten your body it also performs external rotation at the hip and because of its connection to the ITB it assists with lateral rotation at the knee you you can see that its fibers are angled diagonally giving it even more functions when it works on one side it rotates the pelvis and when both sides contract it performs posterior pelvic tilt additionally due to the angles of its connections the upper fibers create Abduction of the hip while the lower fibers create adduction and together it stabilizes the pelvis while lifting the opposite leg any movement involving this actions will train the glus Maximus but if you want to keep it simple squats and deadlifts will do the trick

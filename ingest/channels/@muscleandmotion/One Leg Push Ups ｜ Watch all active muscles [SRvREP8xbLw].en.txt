single leg push-ups this exercise has two main aims active strengthening of the chest muscles and passive strengthening of the core muscles enter a push-up position and lift one leg from the floor a superior view of the inner core muscles among them the transversus abdominus and the pelvic floor muscles which maintain internal abdominal pressure the movement in the shoulder joint is horizontal adduction the target muscle is the pectoralis major additional target muscles are the core muscles that stabilize the body the muscles that assist in the movement anterior deltoids triceps brachii and serratus anterior appear here in pink

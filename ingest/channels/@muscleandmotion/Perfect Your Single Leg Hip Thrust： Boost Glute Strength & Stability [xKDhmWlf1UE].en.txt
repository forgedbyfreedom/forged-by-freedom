the single leg hip thrust the single leg hip thrust is intended to strengthen the gluteal muscles lean with your upper back on a bench legs bent and buttocks sinking down toward the floor raise one leg while pushing the heel into the floor raise the pelvis upward until the body is straight and then return to the starting position be sure not to Arch the back during any stage of the movement this exercise focuses the load on the glutes the main movement is hip extension the hamstrings are in active insufficiency and most of the load is on the gluteus maximus it is important to keep the back straight throughout the movement lifting the leg from the floor increases the load on the active leg and also activates many stabilizers among them the hip internal rotators and the oblique abdominals which prevent the body from rotating and collapsing raising one leg increases the level of difficulty in two ways first it intensifies the load on the gluteus maximus of the active leg second it activates many stabilizers that prevent pelvic and spinal rotation which would not have happened if both legs were on the floor the glutitis Maximus extends the hip joint in addition to strengthening

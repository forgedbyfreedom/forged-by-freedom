Sumo deadlift take your stance feet under bar with a wide stance and your toes out squat down and grasp bar squeeze the bar off the floor and drag it up your legs until it locks out of the top do not pull back into the Shins the bar should come off the ground in a vertical path keep these three rules one the bar must start over mid foot two the shoulders must start slightly in front of the bar three the hips must start relatively High the target muscles are the quadriceps femoris and glutitis Maximus colored red note also the spinal Erectors can be a part of the target muscles isometric contraction the action in the hip joint is extension with adduction the adductors abduct the hip and assist in hip extension keep your feet between 30 to 45° out keep your knees out in line with your toes and make sure you fully extend at the top overhand grip versus mixed grip overhand grip is a pronated grip with both Palms facing you one of the benefits of overhand grip is is that it allows you to keep the bar close to your body easily the mixed grip features one hand in a pronated position and the other in a suben position in other words one Palm faces you and the other faces away the grip generally allows people to lift more weight however the mixed deadlift grip creates a higher potential for injuries such as bicep tears hold your knees out with wide stance push knees out to the sides and not down to prevent knees from buckling inward pushing knees out will allow hips to travel toward bar more quickly while improving leverage common mistake the bar is far from the tibia the bar must start over midfoot

Push-ups. Common mistakes. Push-ups are one of the most popular strength training exercises. We do them mainly to strengthen the chest and core muscles. But what happens when the chest muscles don't have enough strength or there is a lack of awareness of the correct movement? One of the common mistakes is that the up and down movement is performed through adduction and abduction of the scapula. The main muscle that abducts the scapula is the anterior serratus in red and not the pectoral muscles. Note that the arms remain almost straight throughout the movement. So what is the correct movement? We bring the chest to the floor as we flex the elbows and we maintain the spinal curves in neutral position as in standing. Below we see the common mistake of straight arms and movement through the scapula. Above in the upper animation we see the correct performance. So how do we solve the problem? If you don't have enough chest muscle strength, simply place your knees on the floor. This reduces the load and enables you to perform the movement correctly. As you progress, you can then perform the exercise with legs straight. In both variations, be sure to maintain a straight back, neutral curves. Thanks for watching. We hope you enjoyed it.

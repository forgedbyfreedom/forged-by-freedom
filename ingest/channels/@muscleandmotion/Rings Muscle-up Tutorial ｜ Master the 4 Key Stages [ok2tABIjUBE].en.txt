Rings muscle up the Rings muscle up is a high-skill gymnastics movement that requires explosive power and strength it is composed of a pull-up followed by an inair situp and finished with a dip the muscle up has four stages the false grip place the Rings further in your palms than in a neutral grip the Rings should be resting at the base of your palm right at the crease of the wrist and hand wrap your fingers and your thumb around the Rings the pull pull your body up as smoothly as possible and bring your shoulder above your hands the transition from the top of the pole move to the starting position of a dip the push push yourself many muscles take part in the muscle up during the pull phase the latissimus dorsy is in charge of extending the shoulder with the assistance of the posterior deltoid the Bice BPS Breaky eye assist in elbow flexion the hip flexor muscles Flex the hips and pull the legs up to produce momentum in the push phase the pectoralis major and the anterior deltoid are in charge of flexing the shoulder from hyperextension to a neutral position the triceps brachii extend the elbow muscle up common mistake flexing the elbows too early pulling up on the bar by flexing the elbows too early during the pull pull up phase will lead to a loss of momentum created by the Kipping this will make it harder to perform the transition between the pull-up phase and the dip phase later on

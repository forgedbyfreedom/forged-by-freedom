rotational lunge with one arm chest press this functional exercise is not intended to strengthen an isolated muscle rather it is a movement pattern that combines a sequence of muscles that work together cooperatively and with intermuscular coordination this movement pattern challenges the glutitis Maximus and the core muscles throughout the movement the abdominal muscles actively stabilize the Torso and rotate the spine the oblique abdominals create spinal rotation the transversus and rectus abdominis work together with the other core muscles as stabilizers the movement in the hip joint is an extension with external rotation variation of high-speed movement with a low resistance A variation of a slow speed movement for developing strength in this video we can see another variation from pelvic rotation to spinal rotation

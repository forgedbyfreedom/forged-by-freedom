in classic muscle training slow contraction the muscle fibers actively change in length while the tendons scarcely grow larger however during walking running or hopping the muscle fibers contract almost isometrically well the tendons lengthen and shorten like an elastic yo-yo spring the tendons stretch and recoil storing and retaining elastic strain energy the Achilles tendon stretches when the foot hits the ground in the body sinks as it is lowered and all this stored energy is released like a spring during the toe off phase this is the body's way of conserving metabolic energy

Seated cable row, lock torso or moving torso. Both work the lats but in different ways. Lock the torso and it's more about the lats. Clean, focused with the other muscles stabilizing but with a smaller range of motion. Move the torso in control and you get a deeper stretch, stronger engagement all the way through with back up from the glutes and spinal extensors. There's no right or wrong. Just choose the version that fits your goal.

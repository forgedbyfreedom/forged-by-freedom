Bent over row Smith machine. Bent over row strengthens the upper back muscles while stabilizing the movement by engaging the lower back, core, and the entire posterior chain. Stand with your feet shoulderwidth apart and grip the bar with a slightly wider than shoulder width overhand grip. Hinge forward at the hips with a neutral spine and a slight bend at the knees. Initiate the movement by driving your elbows behind your body while retracting the shoulder blades. Pull the bar toward your belly button. Then slowly and with control lower it back to the starting position. Repeat for the desired number of repetitions. The latisimus dorsy extends the shoulders with the assistance of the posterior deltoid. The romboids and middle trapezius retract the scapula. The posterior chain muscles stabilize the body and maintain the bent over position enabling efficient force transfer throughout the movement. Common mistake rounded back during the lift. This exercise is intended to strengthen the latisimus dorsy and the posterior chain. Difficulty performing the lift with a neutral spine may result from tight hamstrings or lack of awareness. To correct this, incorporate hamstring stretches to improve flexibility and focus on engaging the back muscles to maintain a neutral spine throughout the movement.

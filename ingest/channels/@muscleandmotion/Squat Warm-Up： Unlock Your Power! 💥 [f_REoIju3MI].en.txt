this Olympian uses this 2-minute squat warm-up to open her hips and Prime her glutes like crazy so she can squat big weight start by holding a lightweight with a resistance band Loop across your legs squat down just a little bit before pausing then open your hip three times in a row holding the end range by squeezing your glute for 3 seconds on each then squat a bit further down repeat the opening and holding process one more time making sure to keep your foot rooted to the ground the entire time is Key then descend as deep as you can perform your three hip openers Again by now your glutes should be on fire then it's time to come back up but don't fully stand back up just yet pause halfway up perform three hip openers and do this sequence one last time you can then stand all the way back up and relax repeat the same process on your opposite hip then it's time to squat feel the glutes working pretty good

Stability Challenge of the Month. Can you nail five perfect reps of the single leg RDL? Hold a 10 kg dumbbell in one hand opposite the standing leg. A full rep counts only if one side of the dumbbell touches the floor and you return to full extension. Keep your back neutral. Hips square and the lifted leg aligned with your torso. Control the entire movement. No wobbling or bouncing. Perform five slow and controlled reps on each leg. No resting the lifted leg between reps. Master these key points and take your balance and control to the next level. Think you got it? Tag it muscle and motion in your video and get featured.

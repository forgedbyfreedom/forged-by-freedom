Challenge of the month. Can you back squat your body weight for three reps? Squat with at least your body weight on the bar. Complete at least three controlled reps. No max singles. Your thighs must drop below parallel. No half reps. Keep your form sharp. No collapsing knees. No rounded back, no lifted heels. Control every rep. No bouncing out of the bottom. Master these key points and take your squat strength to the next level. Think you've got it? Tag Muscle in Motion in your video and get featured.

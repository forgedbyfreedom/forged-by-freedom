supplying spinal rotation with stability ball and a resistance band lie on your back place a resistance band near the knees and place your legs on a stability ball stretch your arms out to the sides and keep your shoulders on the mat throughout the movement move the knees sideways against the direction of the band's power and then return the main action is spinal rotation the target muscles are the external and internal obliques at the end of the set change sides so that you can work on the muscles on the other side

Ready to test your leg strength and balance? Take on the shrimp squat challenge. This is what perfect execution looks like. Start kneeling, keeping your torso upright. Lift one leg and grab your foot with your hand. Bring the other leg forward into a kneeling lunge position. Push through the front leg to stand using only your leg strength, no support.

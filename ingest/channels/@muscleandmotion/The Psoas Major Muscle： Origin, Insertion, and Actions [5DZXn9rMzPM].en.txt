the seos major muscle its origin insertion and actions the seos major is a long thick fusiform muscle located deep in the lumbar region very close to the spine and the brim of the Lesser pelvis the seos major originates proximal attachments from the lower vertebral column specifically the bodies and transverse processes of the 12th thoracic vertebra to the fifth lumbar vertebra it merges with the elius as it passes infr lateral into the thigh together these muscles are known as the iliopsoas muscle together they insert distal attachment into the lesser trochanter of the femur the Seas Major's intervention comes from the anterior Ramy of spinal nerves L1 through L3 the primary action of the Seas major is hip flexion reverse action enables the muscle to flex the trunk when the hip is fixated the Seas major also assists in Hip lateral rotation and adduction since the Seas major connects to the lower spine it can increase lumbar lordosis it can also assist in trunk lateral flexion to strengthen the Seas major utilize hip flexion exercises such as the hip raate or the lying hip flexion to stretch the SE major muscle hug your right knee and slowly straighten your left leg while lying supine preferably on a foam roller this position will stretch your left leg another stretch position is placing one foot on a chair while the other is extended Lean Forward while flexing the knee of the leg that is placed on the chair

The spine performs flexion and shoulder performs extension. In this animation, we see the difference between performing the exercise when the main movement is spinal flexion and performing the exercise when the main movement is shoulder extension. In both cases, the abdominal muscles and the shoulder extensors, mainly the latissimus dorsi, work hard. The difference is that in the variation where the movement occurs mainly in the shoulder, the abdominal muscles work mainly statically to stabilize the body and the latissimus dorsi is what creates the movement and elevates the body, as in the front lever exercise. This exercise is rated as very hard. Therefore, progressive preparation is required. The preparation will include basic plank and plank with shoulder extension exercises. After mastering the plank, we will practice a variation of the dragon flag with less weight on the legs. The more we bend the legs, the less weight will be on the legs. The same applies to moving the legs further back. The common mistake, heightened arching of the lower back, usually because of weakness in the abdominal muscles. The solution is to strengthen the abdominal muscles with a lower load, such as in a bent knee position. One of the common problems is neck pains because of high load on the cervical vertebrae and overextension of the neck when raising the body. It is possible to prevent or ease the problem by placing a rubber block or stiff pillow under the shoulders.

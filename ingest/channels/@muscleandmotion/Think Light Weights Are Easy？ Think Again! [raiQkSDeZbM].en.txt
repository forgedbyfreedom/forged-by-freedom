Think lightweights are easy? Think again. Research shows that high rep lightweight sets cause more fatigue even in muscles you're not training and take longer to recover from than heavy lifting. Why? Total work. For example, if your max is 100 kg, a heavy set of three reps at 90 kg equals 270 kg of total work. But a light set of 30 reps at 30 kg equals a massive 900 kg of work. That's over three times as much, meaning longer recovery. So whether you're lifting heavy or light, remember both approaches have their place. Light weights are great for endurance, while heavy weights are better for strength.

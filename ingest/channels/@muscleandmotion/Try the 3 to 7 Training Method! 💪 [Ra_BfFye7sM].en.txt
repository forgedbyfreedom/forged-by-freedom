the 3 to7 Training Method the 3 to7 protocol is a strength training method designed to develop maximal strength and hypertrophy this method is characterized by 3 to seven repetitions using a load of approximately 70% of your one repetition maximum with a brief rest interval of 15 seconds between sets this method is unique in that the prescribed training volume is collapsed into a very short period let's take the standing biceps curl exercise for examp example choose a dumbbell suitable for performing 10 repetitions start by performing only three repetitions rest for only 15 seconds and then perform four repetitions rest for 15 seconds perform five repetitions and so on up to seven repetitions the final block of repetitions six and seven should lead you to muscle failure performing one cycle of 3 to 7 can replace three standard sets of 3 * 8 or 3 * 10 try the method and feel how your muscles react to it always ensure you are training safely especially when using heavy weights and working towards muscle failure

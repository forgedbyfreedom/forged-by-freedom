looking to improve your rowing technique understanding Force curves is the key your ideal Target is a smooth consistent curve a line that reflects efficient powerful Strokes from start to finish but there are two common force curves that can hold you back if your curve looks like a mountain range with Peaks and valleys it shows an uneven stroke then there's the double diamond or shifted left curve a sharp Peak followed by a quick drop this often happens when too much force is applied at the catch throwing off timing throughout the drive so next time you're on the rower aim for a steady balanced curve it'll help you row stronger faster and with more control

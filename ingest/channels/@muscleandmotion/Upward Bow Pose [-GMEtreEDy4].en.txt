shortening the hip flexors will cause the following patterns if one or more of the hip flexors is short the pelvis will tend toward greater anterior tilt increasing lumbar lordosis this condition will cause high compression loads on the posterior part of the lower vertebrae in other words limitations in hip flexor extension will be expressed immediately in over extension in the lower back on the other hand the better the hip joint extension the easier lumbar spine extension will be and in the upward bow pose extension will be well and evenly divided over more joints

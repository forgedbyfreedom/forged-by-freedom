Which exercise is more effective for hamstring hypertrophy? A recent study found that the Nordic hamstring curl leads to greater hypertrophy of the semmitendinosis while the stiff leg deadlift induces greater hypertrophy of the semimebinosis located beneath the semmitendinosis. The takeaway: Combining both exercises in your training program may help you build fuller, stronger hamstrings by engaging all parts of the muscle group.

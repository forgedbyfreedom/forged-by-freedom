Why can a smaller athlete out jump a massive powerlifter? Because strength is not power. Strength is about max force. Power is force times velocity. But true explosiveness adds a critical constraint, time. It's called rate of force development. It's not about how much force you have eventually, but how steeply you can drive it up instantly using neural drive and tendon elasticity.

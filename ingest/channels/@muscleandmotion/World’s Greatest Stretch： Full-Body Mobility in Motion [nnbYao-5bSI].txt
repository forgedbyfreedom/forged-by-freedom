 The next exercise in our program is called the world's greatest stretch, but this is not your basic stretch. It's a dynamic movement that hits multiple body parts and planes of motion. It targets your hip flexors, hamstrings, glutes, and upper back mobility all in one. Start in a kneeling position, place one hand on the floor opposite your front leg, and straighten your back leg. Next, squeeze your glute and simultaneously push your knee out to the side while keeping your foot firmly planted on the ground. This should bring out a light stretch to your inner groin muscles. Then, drop your elbow as far towards the ground as possible. While I can drop really far, just go as deep for now as you can. Then, rotate in the opposite direction as far as possible while lifting your arm to the ceiling. This part of the movement targets your mid-back or thoracic spine, and if you keep your back leg straight and squeeze that glute as well, you may even feel your hip flexor stretch if they're really tight. Then, switch sides and perform the same motion with your other leg in front as well.

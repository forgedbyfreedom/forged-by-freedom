Mobility check. Front rack challenge. How flexible are your wrists and shoulders? Try holding a proper front rack position. Elbows high at or slightly above bar level. Bar rests on your shoulders, not in your hands. Torso stays upright. Wrists extended but relaxed with all fingers gripping the bar. The challenge. Perform a front squat while maintaining a strong front rack. No discomfort, no compensation. If you're feeling pressure or losing form, it might be time to work on your mobility. Think you got it?

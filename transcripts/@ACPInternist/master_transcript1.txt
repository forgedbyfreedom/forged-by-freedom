=== FILE: ACP Internist colonoscopy screening [hUmhKH_k5Pk].txt ===

Patients are often reluctant to undergo colonoscopy because of fear of pain. They should be, those patients should be assured that any discomfort or pain would be eliminated or largely reduced, significantly reduced by sedation that is given in almost all colonoscopies. In addition, patients are reluctant to undergo colonoscopy because of fear of the preparation the day before. We have at our disposal a wide variety of alternatives for preparation which range anywhere from a four liter solution to two liter solutions with some oral laxatives along with a fully pill-based preparation or even very small volumes of sodium phosphate.

=== FILE: Encouraging Colonoscopy Screening [_ZwtlUENnIE].txt ===

One technique that can be used to reduce patient anxiety and to increase compliance with colorectal cancer screening is the emphasis on the interval in between screening examinations. In screening for cervical cancer with a pap smear or for breast cancer with a mammogram, typically annual examinations are done. In a completely negative screening test such as a colonoscopy, the screening interval should be as long as 10 years. Screening with a double contrast barium enema or a CT calligraphy, the recommended screening interval is 5 years.
